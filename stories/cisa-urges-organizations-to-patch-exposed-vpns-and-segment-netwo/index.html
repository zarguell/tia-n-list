    CISA Urges Organizations to Patch Exposed VPNs and Segment Networks Against Gunra Ransomware

    gbhackers.com · 14h ago · August 11th, 2026

    CISA and international law-enforcement partners have issued a joint #StopRansomware advisory warning that Gunra ransomware affiliates are exploiting exposed edge infrastructure, including VPN gateways, firewall appliances and RDP-accessible systems, to breach enterprise networks. The advisory positions Gunra as an increasingly organized ransomware-as-a-service operation whose affiliates combine data theft, credential compromise and rapid encryption to pressure victims into payment. The group uses a conventional but damaging double-extortion model: attackers exfiltrate sensitive corporate data before encrypting files, then threaten to publish or sell the material through a Tor-hosted data-leak site if negotiations fail. Its victim base now spans healthcare, finance, manufacturing, transport, government, utilities, education, retail and professional services across several regions. The immediate defensive priority is remediation of internet-facing systems. Investigators observed Gunra operators exploiting FortiOS and FortiProxy authentication-bypass flaws tracked as CVE-2024-55591 and CVE-2025-24472. Korean authorities also reported exploitation of credential-exposure and SSH access-control weaknesses affecting public-facing VPN gateways.
