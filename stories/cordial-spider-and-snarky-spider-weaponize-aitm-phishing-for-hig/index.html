    Cordial Spider and Snarky Spider weaponize AiTM phishing for high-speed SaaS data theft

    cyberpress.org · 101d ago · May 2nd, 2026

    New technical details have emerged on the Scattered Spider-affiliated groups profiled yesterday. CrowdStrike's Falcon Shield telemetry reveals that both groups begin with voice phishing — impersonating internal IT support to manufacture urgency — then direct victims to adversary-in-the-middle phishing pages that perfectly clone corporate SSO portals. Once session tokens are captured, the attackers register their own MFA devices (CORDIAL SPIDER uses Windows Quick Emulator, SNARKY SPIDER prefers Genymobile Android emulators), delete existing legitimate MFA devices, and create inbox rules that automatically trash security alert emails containing keywords like "alert," "incident," "MFA," and "security warning." SNARKY SPIDER begins data exfiltration within one hour of initial compromise. The groups specifically hunt for SSNs, financial contracts, VPN credentials, and confidential business documents across SharePoint, HubSpot, and Google Workspace. Commercial VPN services (Mullvad, Oxylabs, NetNut) and residential proxy networks disguise traffic as legitimate residential internet activity. The attacks exploit customer misconfigurations — overly permissive access controls and lack of phishing-resistant MFA — rather than software vulnerabilities. Organizations using SaaS platforms should enforce phishing-resistant MFA, monitor for MFA device enrollment anomalies, and audit inbox rules for security keyword suppression.
