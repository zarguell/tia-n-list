    Anthropic Claude Opus 5 Shows 98% Resistance to Indirect Prompt Injection Attacks

    cyberpress.org · 1d ago · August 10th, 2026

    Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5 has reduced successful indirect prompt injection attacks to 2% within 15 attempts, according to the company’s latest system card, marking the strongest result among models evaluated on the Gray Swan IPI benchmark. Indirect prompt injection is a critical risk for AI agents that read emails, browse websites, process documents, or invoke tools. An attacker can hide malicious instructions within otherwise legitimate content, attempting to get an AI agent to execute them. The potential impacts include sensitive data exfiltration, destructive actions, system compromise, and unauthorized financial activity. Anthropic Claude Opus 5 Shows 98% Resistance On the Indirect Prompt Injection (IPI) benchmark, Claude Opus 5 recorded a 2.0% probability that an attacker could achieve a successful compromise after 15 attempts. Put another way, it resisted 98% of attacks in that testing condition. The result improves significantly on Claude Opus 4.8 , where the attacker success probability reached 5.5% after 15 attempts. Opus 5 also reduced one-attempt attack success from 0.5% to 0.2%. Anthropic said the 28-scenario IPI benchmark was created with Gray Swan, the UK AI Security Institute, the US Center for AI Standards and Innovation, and other model developers. Anthropic described Opus 5 as the most robust model tested in the IPI evaluation.
