    Pwn2Own Berlin 2026 final tally — $1.3M awarded for 47 zero-days, DEVCORE wins with $505K

    bleepingcomputer.com · 85d ago · May 18th, 2026

    The three-day Pwn2Own Berlin 2026 hacking contest at OffensiveCon concluded with researchers earning $1,298,250 for 47 unique zero-day vulnerabilities. DEVCORE Research Team won the competition with 50.5 Master of Pwn points and $505,000 in rewards, highlighted by Orange Tsai's $200,000 Microsoft Exchange RCE chain (3 bugs to SYSTEM) and $175,000 Microsoft Edge sandbox escape. Other significant wins: STARLabs SG ($242,500), IBM X-Force's Valentina Palmiotti ($70,000), and successful exploits against VMware ESXi, Windows 11, Red Hat Enterprise Linux, and multiple AI coding agents including Claude Code and Cursor.
