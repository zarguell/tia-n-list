    REMUS Infostealer — Rapid Evolution into Full MaaS Ecosystem with Session Theft Focus

    bleepingcomputer.com · 87d ago · May 16th, 2026

    Flare's analysis of 128 underground posts (February–May 2026) reveals REMUS evolving from a basic credential stealer into a mature Malware-as-a-Service platform emphasizing authenticated session continuity over traditional password theft. The operator claims ~90% callback rate with proper crypting. Key developments: SOCKS5 proxy support for token restoration, IndexedDB collection targeting 1Password and LastPass browser extensions, restore-token functionality for session persistence, multi-operator dashboards with worker tracking. The campaign mirrors broader trends where stolen cookies and authenticated sessions — which bypass MFA entirely — have become more valuable than passwords on underground markets. The operator's development cadence, versioned updates, and customer support structure increasingly resemble legitimate software businesses. This marks a significant professionalization of infostealer operations from smash-and-grab credential collection toward persistent access monetization.
