    AI autonomous cyber benchmarks shattered — Mythos and GPT-5.5 double capability every ~4 months

    cyberscoop.com · 89d ago · May 14th, 2026

    The UK's AI Security Institute reports that Claude Mythos Preview and GPT-5.5 have significantly exceeded the autonomous cyber capability doubling trend tracked since late 2024 — previously estimated at five months, now closer to four. Mythos Preview became the first model to complete both AISI cyber ranges: solving "The Last Ones" (32-step network attack) in 6/10 attempts and "Cooling Tower" (previously unsolved) in 3/10 attempts. Separately, Palo Alto Networks found that Claude Mythos and GPT-5.5-Cyber identified 26 CVEs representing 75 issues across 130+ products in a single month — compared to a typical volume of fewer than five. In a parallel move, OpenAI launched "Daybreak," its cybersecurity initiative combining GPT-5.5 with the Codex agentic framework in three access tiers. The House Homeland Security Committee held a closed-door Anthropic briefing with a live Mythos demonstration, and a hearing is being planned.
