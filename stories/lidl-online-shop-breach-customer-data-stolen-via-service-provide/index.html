    Lidl Online Shop Breach — Customer Data Stolen via Service Provider Hack

    bleepingcomputer.com · 28d ago · July 14th, 2026

    German supermarket chain Lidl notified customers in Germany, Belgium, and the Netherlands that attackers stole personal information (name, phone, email, date of birth, customer number) from a separately stored customer data file at a service provider. Lidl confirmed that passwords, payment information, addresses, and customer accounts were not affected, and the online shop systems themselves were not breached. The service provider has filed a police report.
