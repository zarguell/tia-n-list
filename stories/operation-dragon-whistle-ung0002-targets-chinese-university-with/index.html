    Operation Dragon Whistle — UNG0002 Targets Chinese University with Cobalt Strike via Fitness-Testing Lure

    cyberpress.org · 77d ago · May 26th, 2026

    Seqrite Labs uncovered a highly targeted spear-phishing campaign dubbed "Operation Dragon Whistle" targeting Changzhou University in China. The attackers, identified with medium-high confidence as the UNG0002 group, exploit the mandatory 2026 fitness testing cycle as a social engineering lure — creating a severe sense of urgency since failing the assessment impacts graduation. The attackers demonstrate deep institutional knowledge, using real staff names, phone numbers, and official seals in decoy documents.

    The infection chain uses a ZIP attachment containing a double-extension LNK file disguised as a PDF, which triggers a VBScript that weaponizes the legitimate Bandizip archiving tool for DLL sideloading. The malware checks for debugging tools and security environments before deploying a Cobalt Strike Beacon entirely in memory. Infrastructure is hosted on Alibaba Cloud with rotated hosting providers to evade ASN-based blocking.
