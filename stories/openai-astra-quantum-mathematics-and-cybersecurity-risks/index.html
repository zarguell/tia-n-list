The Astra Profile Astra’s performance in non-cyber domains — specifically high-level mathematics and theoretical science — serves as the primary lead indicator for its cybersecurity potential. Recent benchmarks indicate that an internal version of Astra has solved 10 major “open math problems,” some of which have remained unresolved for decades. The model has demonstrated unprecedented proficiency in quantum complexity, extremal combinatorics, lattice cryptography, and the intricacies of quantum parallel repetition .
