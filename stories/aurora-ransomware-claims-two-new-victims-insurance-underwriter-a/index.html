    Aurora Ransomware Claims Two New Victims — Insurance Underwriter and Dutch Civil Engineering Firm

    malware.news · 49d ago · June 23rd, 2026

    The Aurora ransomware operation added NationsBuilders Insurance Services (NBIS, US crane/rigging insurance MGU) and NTP B.V. Civil Engineering Construction (Netherlands) to its leak site, both on June 22. NBIS reported 2.7 million filetree entries across 24 shares including claims, HR, finance, IT, and M&A diligence data. NTP B.V. reported "10+ years of operations, every employee's personal files, complete HR/payroll system exports, and every network device configuration."

    Aurora has been steadily accumulating victims — previous targets include ALS Global (June 20), and the group's tactics align with double-extortion ransomware operations. The NBIS attack is particularly concerning due to the sensitive insurance underwriting data and the parent company ownership (Align Financial / DUAL North America / Howden Group).
