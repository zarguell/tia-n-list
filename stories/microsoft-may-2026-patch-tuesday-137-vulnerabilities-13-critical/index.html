    Microsoft May 2026 Patch Tuesday — 137 vulnerabilities, 13 critical, first zero-day-free Patch Tuesday since June 2024

    krebsonsecurity.com · 90d ago · May 13th, 2026

    Microsoft addressed 137 vulnerabilities across its product ecosystem with no zero-days actively exploited or publicly disclosed — the first such "clean" Patch Tuesday in nearly two years. Notable critical fixes include CVE-2026-41089 (CVSS 9.8): a stack-based buffer overflow in Windows Netlogon allowing unauthenticated RCE on domain controllers with no user interaction required; CVE-2026-41096: critical RCE in the Windows DNS client; CVE-2026-41103 (CVSS 9.1): elevation of privilege in the Microsoft SSO Plugin for Jira & Confluence allowing forged credential bypass of Entra ID authentication; and four critical Microsoft Word RCE vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-40361/40364/40366/40367, all CVSS 8.4) exploitable via the Preview Pane. This month's Patch Tuesday is notably the first to reflect the impact of Anthropic's "Project Glasswing" AI vulnerability discovery initiative — Krebs on Security reports that Apple shipped fixes for 52 iOS vulnerabilities (triple its average), Mozilla patched 271 in Firefox 150, and Oracle addressed 450+ including 300+ remotely exploitable flaws, all attributed to AI-assisted discovery.
