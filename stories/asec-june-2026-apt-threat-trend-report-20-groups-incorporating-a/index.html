    ASEC June 2026 APT Threat Trend Report — 20 Groups Incorporating AI, Cloud, and MaaS

    asec.ahnlab.com · 28d ago · July 14th, 2026

    AhnLab's ASEC published its June 2026 APT trend report tracking 20 state-sponsored groups. Key cross-regional findings:

    North Korea: APT38 (Sapphire Sleet) compromised the Mastra npm supply chain to inject malicious dependencies into 140+ npm packages. Kimsuky shifted to malicious LNK files, Dropbox API, GitHub Releases, and Google Drive for C2. Lazarus distributed brand-jacking and typosquatting packages via npm. TA-RedAnt deployed NarwhalRAT for keylogging and screen capture. UNK_DeadDrop exploited GitHub/GitLab repos and VS Code/Cursor for credential theft and crypto wallet theft from developers.

    China: FishMonger enhanced stealth with SprySOCKS, WINDRV, and WINPLUS. Mustang Panda exploited Zoho WorkDrive as C2. UNC6508 compromised REDCap servers for credential theft. Multiple groups showed pronounced cloud and OAuth token exploitation.

    Russia: APT28 evolved PixyNetLoader with PNG steganography and cloud C2. GreyVibe utilized generative AI and LLMs throughout operations. Turla deployed STOCKSTAY (.NET backdoor) for long-term surveillance of Ukrainian and European diplomatic targets. Gamaredon actively exploited legitimate cloud services and tunneling infrastructure.

    Iran: MuddyWater leveraged CastleRAT MaaS from the TAG-150 criminal ecosystem. Nimbus Manticore used .NET AppDomain hijacking with Azure infrastructure.

    Action: The trend of APT groups exploiting legitimate developer tools (npm, VS Code, Cursor) and cloud services as C2 infrastructure demands enhanced monitoring of outbound traffic from development environments and CI/CD pipelines.
