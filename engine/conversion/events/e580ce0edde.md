Gunra ransomware has undergone a significant operational transformation, transitioning from a Conti-based locker to a fully independent Ransomware-as-a-Service model with its own payload builder targeting both Windows and Linux. At least 32 organizations confirmed as victims as of March 2026. The group operates in dark web communities (RAMP, Rehub, Tierone, Darkforums) with a feature-rich affiliate panel supporting negotiation, payload deployment, and brand customization — affiliates can operate under their own branding, making attribution harder. Notable: Gunra imposes no restrictions on target industries or geographies, and research by S2W has identified cryptographic weaknesses in the Linux variant that could aid defensive decryption. The group maintains a low public profile with consistent activity during Asian business hours.
