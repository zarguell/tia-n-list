The 2026 Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report — analyzing 31,000+ incidents and 22,000+ confirmed breaches (nearly double 2024) — reveals a fundamental shift: exploitation of vulnerabilities has overtaken credential abuse as the leading known initial access vector for the first time. Network asset breaches (VPNs, firewalls, routers) surged from 1.5% to 5% — a 3× increase — and now match user-device targeting frequency. JPMorganChase CISO Pat Opet noted at RSAC 2026: "The very devices that are supposed to be our frontline defense are becoming our greatest weakness." The report highlights a widening remediation gap: only 26% of KEV-listed vulnerabilities were fully remediated (down from 38%), median time-to-fix rose to 43 days (from 32), and vulnerability instances in the dataset grew from 68.7M (2022) to 527M (2025). AI-assisted vulnerability discovery is accelerating: Anthropic's Mythos found 271 Firefox vulnerabilities, and Microsoft is on pace to exceed its annual CVE record.
