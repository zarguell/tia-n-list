Detailed technical analysis of the ongoing cPanel/WHM exploitation campaign reveals a sophisticated operation by the Mr_Rot13 group, active for over six years with exceptionally low detection rates. Beyond the initial CVE-2026-41940 (CVSS 9.8) authentication bypass, the attackers deploy a custom Go-based payload infector that modifies root passwords, implants SSH public keys labeled `cpanel-updater`, drops a Python webshell, and injects malicious JavaScript into cPanel login interfaces to harvest admin credentials in real-time. Stolen data is exfiltrated via ROT13-obfuscated domains and Telegram channels. A cross-platform remote control Trojan (`filemanager`) provides persistent web-based GUI access with bcrypt authentication. The group's infrastructure links back to a 2022 PHP backdoor targeting WordPress that maintained zero detection for years. Ctrl-Alt-Intel reported that the campaign was used to infiltrate Southeast Asian government and military networks, exfiltrating 4 GB of sensitive data.
