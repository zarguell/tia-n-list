North Korea's Lazarus Group is using ClickFix social engineering lures to deliver a new macOS malware kit dubbed "Mach-O Man," targeting the fintech and cryptocurrency sectors. The malware steals credentials, Keychain secrets, and session tokens, providing attackers with a foothold into corporate environments. The kit represents Lazarus's continued investment in macOS-specific tooling after years of Windows-focused campaigns, reflecting the group's targeting shift toward Apple-using professionals in financial services.
