Today (May 12) marks ShinyHunters' deadline for Instructure and affected schools to negotiate over the stolen Canvas data — 3.6 TB covering 275 million records across 8,809 school systems. The House Homeland Security Committee has sent a letter to Instructure CEO Steve Daly demanding a briefing by May 21, citing "serious questions about the company's incident response capabilities" and the recurrence of intrusion within days of the initial disclosure. Daly apologized over the weekend for "inconsistent communication and deficient public response." The second breach on May 7 exploited XSS vulnerabilities in the Free-For-Teacher (FFT) program to deface ~330 Canvas login portals with extortion messages. CrowdStrike has reviewed indicators and found no evidence of current threat actor access. CISA is providing voluntary support. The FFT program — which offered low-friction accounts on shared production infrastructure — has been permanently shut down. Halcyon's Ransomware Research Center calls this "at or near the top for education-sector impact" among 2026 incidents.
