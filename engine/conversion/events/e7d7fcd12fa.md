The New York State Department of Financial Services has reached a $2.25 million settlement with Delta Dental stemming from the 2023 Clop ransomware gang exploitation of the MOVEit transfer zero-day vulnerability. Delta Dental was among many organizations whose patient data was exposed when Clop exploited the vulnerability to acquire client data. The settlement reflects regulators' continued enforcement action against organizations that failed to adequately protect against or respond to the MOVEit supply-chain attack, which affected thousands of organizations globally.
