Canvas LMS is back online after Instructure took the platform offline to contain the ShinyHunters breach, which exploited the Free-For-Teacher (FFT) account program to access production data across the multi-tenant SaaS environment. The timing during finals week at thousands of universities amplified the disruption — students at the University of Maryland, University of New Mexico, and multiple UT system schools faced extended deadlines and lost coursework access. ShinyHunters claims 3.6 TB of data covering 275 million users across ~9,000 schools, including names, email addresses, student IDs, and private messages. The FFT program — which offered low-friction accounts with no institutional verification running on the same production infrastructure as paying institutions — has been permanently shut down. The extortion deadline of May 12 remains active, with ShinyHunters pressuring individual schools to negotiate directly via Tox messaging. Instructure has not confirmed whether ransom demands will be met or how individual breach notifications will be handled.
