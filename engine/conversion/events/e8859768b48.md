DragonForce ransomware group claimed responsibility on May 16 for an attack against AdvancedHEALTH, a US healthcare sector organization. The group's extortion post claims 2,300,000 lines of "FULL patient data, partner agreements, management, payroll and HR files" and has begun releasing data in chunks. The attack underscores the continued targeting of US healthcare providers by ransomware groups, with patient records remaining a high-value extortion commodity.
