CISA has added CVE-2026-41940 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, requiring federal agencies to patch within four days. The escalation comes as SecurityWeek reports over 40,000 servers likely compromised worldwide, with Shadowserver confirming 44,000 unique IPs scanning honeypots — numbers that spiked sharply after watchTowr Labs' public disclosure on April 28 but began dropping as of May 3 as organizations apply patches. New details from GBHackers reveal that threat actors weaponized the public PoC within days of disclosure to target government and military organizations across Southeast Asia, including the Philippine Coast Guard and the Lao Ministry of National Defense. The attackers combined cPanel exploitation with a separate custom exploit chain against an Indonesian defense-sector training portal, ultimately exfiltrating over 4 GB of documents including Chinese railway infrastructure data containing national identification numbers, bank account details, and phone numbers. After gaining access, the attackers deployed OpenVPN and Ligolo tunneling tools disguised as Linux services for persistent network pivoting.
