SonicWall has disclosed three vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-0204, CVE-2026-0205, CVE-2026-0206) spanning its entire Gen6, Gen7, and Gen8 firewall product lines. The most critical, CVE-2026-0204 (CVSS 8.0), is a weak authentication flaw allowing adjacent-network attackers to bypass access controls on the management interface. CVE-2026-0205 (CVSS 6.8) is a post-auth path traversal enabling file access, and CVE-2026-0206 (CVSS 4.9) is a post-auth stack-based buffer overflow in the SSL-VPN component that crashes the firewall entirely, causing full denial-of-service. Discovered by CrowdStrike's Advanced Research Team. SonicWall urges administrators to immediately disable HTTP/HTTPS-based firewall management and SSL-VPN access until patches are applied.
