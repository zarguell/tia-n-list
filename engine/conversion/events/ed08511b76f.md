The Trump administration is redirecting the CyberCorps Scholarship For Service program — which requires recipients to work in government — toward artificial intelligence. The Office of Personnel Management and National Science Foundation have renamed it "CyberAI SFS," alarming current scholars who entered the program for cybersecurity career pathways. The shift reflects the administration's broader prioritization of AI over traditional cybersecurity workforce development at a time when federal agencies face critical security staffing shortages.
