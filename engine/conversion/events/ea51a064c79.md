Anthropic broadened Project Glasswing to ~150 organizations across 15 countries, including previously underrepresented power, water, healthcare, communications, and hardware sectors. Since April, the restricted **Claude Mythos Preview** model has discovered **10,000+ high- or critical-severity vulnerabilities**, including 2,000 bugs in Cloudflare's critical-path systems (400 rated high/critical), 271 vulns fixed in Firefox 150 (10x the previous model's output), and a 90%+ validation rate on independently reviewed findings across 1,000+ open-source projects.

**Industry tension:** Anthropic explicitly stated it will not release Mythos-class models to the general public due to misuse risk. The bottleneck has shifted from vulnerability discovery to human capacity for triage, disclosure, and patching — a challenge the Cloud Security Alliance, SANS, and OWASP jointly concluded will leave organizations "overwhelmed" by AI-accelerated exploitation.
