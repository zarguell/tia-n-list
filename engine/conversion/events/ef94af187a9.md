The ransomware group Lamashtu claimed responsibility on May 16 for an attack against Parle Agro (parleagro.com), a leading Indian non-alcoholic beverage and packaged foods company behind well-known brands including Frooti, Appy/Appy Fizz, Bailley, and Hippo. The group has threatened to release sensitive data unless demands are met. Lamashtu is a relatively less-documented ransomware operation; this represents an escalation into targeting major Indian enterprises in the consumer goods sector. Parle Agro has not publicly commented.
