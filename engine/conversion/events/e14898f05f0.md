The ShinyHunters extortion group claimed responsibility for a cyberattack against **IC Security (icsecurity.com)**, a U.S.-based cybersecurity firm, with a threat to leak **2.7 million compromised records** unless demands are met by June 22, 2026. The group published a "FINAL WARNING" on June 19 demanding payment. IC Security provides physical security systems (access control, surveillance) and cybersecurity services — the compromise of a cybersecurity vendor's own systems raises supply chain concerns for its client base. The attackers claim to have exfiltrated "over 2.7 million records and other internal corporate data."
