DINUM disclosed that the Tchap breach (covered June 10) affects 73,467 accounts — 9% of 825,000 registered users. Private E2EE conversations were protected, but the attacker scraped public chat rooms for names, emails, organizations, and avatars. The threat actor claims to have stolen 650,000 messages and 13.5 GB of documents/media, plus hardcoded LDAP credentials found in a shared PowerShell script.
