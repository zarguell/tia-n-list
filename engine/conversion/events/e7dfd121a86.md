A CRLF injection vulnerability (June 1, GHSA-5vg9-5847-vvmq) in Laravel's email handling accepts user-supplied addresses without sanitizing line-feed characters, allowing attackers to inject arbitrary SMTP headers and manipulate outbound email — enabling mail relay abuse, phishing via compromised Laravel applications, and email spoofing. Applications using `Illuminate\Mail` with user-controllable `$to`, `$cc`, or `$bcc` parameters are at risk.
