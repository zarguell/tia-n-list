Iran-aligned threat operators have conducted an extensive espionage campaign against Omani government networks, compromising at least 11 entities including the Ministry of Justice and Legal Affairs, Royal Oman Police, Royal Fleet of Oman, and the Tax Authority. The operation was exposed through a poorly secured C2 server — a RouterHosting VPS in the UAE — that hosted the full toolkit including ASP.NET webshells, Python exploit scripts, Chisel tunneling binaries, and exfiltrated data. Attackers leveraged ProxyShell exploitation (CVE-2021-34473/34523/31207) against Exchange servers and password brute-force attacks against government portals. The primary target, the Ministry of Justice, suffered large-scale data theft including 26,596 DotNetNuke user records with staff credentials, Windows SAM and SYSTEM registry hives, and judicial case data. National ID records containing citizen names, birthdates, and nationality data were also extracted. The campaign's infrastructure — spread across a "dubai-#" cluster on the same ASN and linked to cloned Iranian diaspora media sites — strongly suggests a Ministry of Intelligence and Security (MOIS) nexus. Organizations should audit for the provided IOCs and review Exchange server patching status.
