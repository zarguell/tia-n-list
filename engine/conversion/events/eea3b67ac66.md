Instructure has confirmed that the Canvas LMS breach exposed user names, email addresses, student ID numbers, and private messages between users. The company states it has found no evidence that passwords, dates of birth, government identifiers, or financial information were compromised. ShinyHunters, the extortion group behind the September 2025 Instructure breach, has claimed responsibility for the new incident, alleging 3.65 TB of data stolen spanning 275 million individuals across nearly 9,000 institutions worldwide, including access to Instructure's Salesforce instance. Instructure has deployed security patches, rotated application keys (customers must re-authorize API access), revoked privileged credentials, and restored Canvas Data 2 and Beta services. The company has not confirmed the attacker's scale claims or whether extortion is being attempted.
