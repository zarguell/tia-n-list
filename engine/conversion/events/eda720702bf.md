Varonis disclosed **SearchLeak**, a three-stage attack chain chaining **parameter-to-prompt injection**, an **HTML rendering race condition**, and a **Bing SSRF** to exfiltrate email content, calendar events, OneDrive/SharePoint documents with a single click on a crafted URL. Microsoft addressed the vulnerability (assigned CVE-2026-42824, critical severity) earlier this month. The attack demonstrates how legacy bug classes (SSRF, HTML injection) become more dangerous in AI contexts where prompt injection enables new attack surfaces.
