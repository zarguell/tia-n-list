CVE-2026-20182, the seventh actively exploited Cisco SD-WAN vulnerability in 2026, now has a public Metasploit module released by Rapid7, dramatically lowering the barrier to widespread exploitation. The flaw — discovered March 9 but only disclosed May 14 — allows unauthenticated attackers to bypass peering authentication in the control connection handshake by claiming to be a vHub device. Success grants administrative access to NETCONF and the ability to manipulate routing and policy across the entire SD-WAN fabric. Rapid7's Douglas McKee described it as "the cybersecurity version of a Jedi mind trick." UAT-8616 remains the primary threat actor, but Cisco Talos has identified 10 additional threat clusters exploiting the broader CVE-2026-20122/20128/20133 chain since February. CISA's remediation deadline is May 17 — tomorrow. There are no workarounds. Cisco advises collecting admin-tech data before upgrading to preserve forensic evidence, auditing auth.log for unauthorized SSH keys, and escalating suspected compromises to Cisco TAC.
