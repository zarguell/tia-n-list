OpenVPN released an emergency update for its macOS client (version 3.8.2, build 6009, May 25) addressing a critical vulnerability in the **privileged helper component** that could allow attackers to inject arbitrary commands with elevated privileges. Discovered by researchers Ismael Esquilichi, Pablo Redondo, and Lê Đức Ninh, the flaw stems from improper input validation in the component responsible for executing operations requiring elevated permissions. No active exploitation has been confirmed, but the high CVSS 9.4 score and the widespread deployment of VPN clients in enterprise environments make patching urgent. Users should verify the SHA-256 checksum (`8d50036510859956d20d1f50e43171be53417431104c6befb1352ff5187c248a`) before installation.
