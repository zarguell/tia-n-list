IMA Diligence Services, a financial consulting subsidiary of IMA Financial Group, is notifying **525,306 individuals** that their personal information was stolen in a December 2025 breach attributed to the Genesis ransomware group. The attackers exfiltrated **700 GB of data** from a legacy server managed by a third party.

Compromised data includes names, SSNs, driver's license numbers, financial account numbers, credit card numbers, health insurance data, passport numbers, and taxpayer IDs. IMA is offering 12 months of free credit monitoring and identity restoration.
