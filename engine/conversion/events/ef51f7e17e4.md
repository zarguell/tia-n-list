Qilin, the most active ransomware group globally with 338 victims in Q1 2026 (per Check Point Research), claimed an attack against CLINICA AVELLANEDA MEDICAL CENTER (clinicaavellaneda.com.ar), a healthcare provider in Argentina, on May 16. The group has threatened to publish full patient medical data unless negotiations are initiated. Healthcare continues to be the most targeted sector due to operational complexity and downtime sensitivity, consistent with the broader ransomware landscape tracked throughout Q1 2026.
