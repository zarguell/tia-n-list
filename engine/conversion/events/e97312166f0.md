UK authorities charged five people in connection with **Russian Coms**, a major caller ID spoofing platform used to make over 1.8 million scam calls across 107 countries since 2020. The platform, operated via web app and handset, offered encrypted calls, voice-changing services, and instant device wipes. Scammers spoofed bank phone numbers to defraud victims of an average of £9,400 each. The platform was dismantled in March 2024 as part of Operation Henhouse, which led to 290 arrests.
