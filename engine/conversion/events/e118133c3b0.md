Multiple U.S. Army internet subdomains were defaced in a 404 hijacking campaign, CyberScoop confirmed. Error pages on oil.army.mil (Army's Open Innovation Lab) and ai2c.army.mil (Artificial Intelligence Integration Center) displayed defacement messages denigrating President Donald Trump and U.S. Ambassador to Türkiye Tom Barrack, reading "FREE KURDISTAN" and "Kurdish sr was here." The 404 hijacking technique exploits custom error page configuration to inject defacement content.
