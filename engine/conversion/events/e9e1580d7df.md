Owe Martin Andresen, 49, the alleged main administrator ("Speedstepper") of Dream Market — once the largest dark web marketplace after AlphaBay's takedown — has been arrested in Germany and indicted by a U.S. federal grand jury on 12 money laundering counts carrying up to 20 years each. German authorities seized approximately $1.7 million in gold bars purchased with Dream Market commission funds and shipped to Andresen's home address, plus $23,000 in cash and cryptocurrency wallets holding $1.2 million. The marketplace facilitated the sale of over 450kg of cocaine, 90kg of heroin, and 36kg of fentanyl before its 2019 shutdown. Andresen was identified after accessing dormant Dream Market wallets containing millions in cryptocurrency in late 2022. The arrest follows a series of recent dark web takedowns including the Crimenetwork reboot (May 11 digest) and the Incognito Market operator's 30-year sentence.
