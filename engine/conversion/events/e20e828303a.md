Microsoft announced that beginning **September 1, 2026**, Entra ID will make passkeys the default authentication method, auto-enabling users currently on SMS or voice MFA. On **February 1, 2027**, Microsoft-provided SMS and voice authentication will be retired entirely. Organizations requiring SMS or voice must select a third-party telecom provider through the Microsoft Security Store.

Microsoft cited AI-powered phishing campaigns reaching **54% click-through rates** (vs. 12% for traditional campaigns) as the driver for the transition. The company recommended organizations begin planning passkey rollout immediately, identify users still on phishable methods, and prepare user communications.
