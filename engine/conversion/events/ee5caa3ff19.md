The "Trapdoor" malvertising and ad fraud campaign encompasses 455 malicious Android applications and 183 C2 domains, generating up to 659 million bid requests per day at its peak. Applications accumulated over 24 million downloads from Google Play. The operation uses a sophisticated two-stage distribution model: users initially download utility apps (PDF readers, cleanup tools) that pass Play Store checks and exhibit no malicious behavior, then are targeted with deceptive advertisements claiming the app is outdated — leading to installation of a secondary weaponized payload. Trapdoor's anti-analysis measures include device environment scanning (root detection, debugging tools, active VPNs), native packing, code virtualization, and fake class structures impersonating legitimate ad SDKs.
