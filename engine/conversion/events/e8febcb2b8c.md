Google is rolling out a new Android security feature that detects and blocks AI deepfake impersonation calls in real time. When both caller and recipient use Phone by Google, a silent encrypted signal is exchanged via RCS. If the signal is missing, the recipient's device pings the contact's actual phone to verify. Available on Android 12+ starting with Pixel devices, enabled by default.

The FTC reported $2.95 billion in impersonation scam losses in 2024; INTERPOL's March 2026 assessment flagged impersonation fraud as a leading threat contributing to $440+ billion in global losses.
