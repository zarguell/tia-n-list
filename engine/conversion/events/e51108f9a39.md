Securonix has published analysis of a sophisticated attack framework dubbed "Veil#Drop" that abuses compromised websites, Google's Blogspot platform, PowerShell, and fileless techniques to evade detection and deploy the PureLog information stealer. The multi-stage delivery chain uses Blogspot-hosted payloads that bypass traditional allowlists of trusted domains, with PowerShell-based in-memory execution leaving minimal forensic footprint. The campaign demonstrates threat actors' continued pivot toward trusted cloud platforms as malware distribution infrastructure.
