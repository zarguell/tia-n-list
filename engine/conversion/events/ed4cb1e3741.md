Matthew Bathula, 41, of Clarksville, Maryland, faces federal charges including two counts of unauthorized access to a protected computer and one count of aggravated identity theft stemming from unauthorized access while employed as a pharmacist at the University of Maryland Medical Center. The case highlights the persistent risk of insider threats in healthcare environments, where employees with legitimate system access can exploit their privileges to access sensitive patient data. Healthcare remains the most frequently targeted sector for insider breaches due to the high value of medical records and the broad access granted to clinical staff.
