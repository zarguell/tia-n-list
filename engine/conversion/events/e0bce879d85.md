NVIDIA has confirmed that GeForce NOW user information was exposed in a data breach limited to the Armenian regional partner GFN.am, which operates the cloud gaming service across Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Moldova, Ukraine, and Uzbekistan. A threat actor using the ShinyHunters nickname — believed to be an imposter — posted the stolen database for $100,000 on a hacker forum (now removed). The breach occurred between March 20 and 26 and exposed full names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, and usernames. No passwords were exposed. NVIDIA's own network was not impacted.
