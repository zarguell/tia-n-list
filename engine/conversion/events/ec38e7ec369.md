Splunk disclosed a critical pre-authentication remote code execution vulnerability (CVE-2026-20253, CVSS 9.8) in Splunk Enterprise on June 10. The flaw allows unauthenticated attackers to achieve RCE on the platform that sits at the heart of many security operations centers. Given Splunk's central role in log ingestion and detection, successful exploitation gives attackers full access to sensitive security telemetry.
