Europol and Bulgarian authorities led "Operation Kratos 2," a seven-month operation resulting in **29 arrests** and the dismantling of **nine organized crime groups** supporting illegal streaming networks. Authorities seized 27,000+ URLs across 169 domains infringing on nearly 850,000 media titles.

The operation conducted 148 house searches, identified 86 suspects, and referred 59 cases for prosecution. Investigators identified 4,400 new domains and 18,000+ IP addresses linked to piracy. Supported by UEFA, La Liga, beIN Media Group, and 14 countries including the US and UK.
