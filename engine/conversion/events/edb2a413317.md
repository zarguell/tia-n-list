Managed hosting provider KnownHost reports that CVE-2026-41940 was actively exploited in the wild prior to public disclosure, with targeted zero-day attacks potentially starting as early as February 23, 2026 — over two months before cPanel's April 28 emergency patch. Security firm watchTowr has published a technical analysis and proof-of-concept exploit demonstrating the CRLF injection technique that allows unauthenticated attackers to inject `user=root` into session files, bypassing encryption on the `whostmgrsession` cookie. Shodan enumerates approximately 1.5 million cPanel instances exposed to the internet. The vulnerability affects all versions after 11.40; fix requires manual execution of `/usr/local/cpanel/scripts/upcp` as automatic update channels do not deliver it.
