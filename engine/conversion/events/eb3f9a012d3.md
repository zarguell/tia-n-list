CyberScoop's comprehensive investigation into TeamPCP's four-month rampage reveals: **1,000+ packages compromised** across npm and PyPI, affecting ~500M weekly downloads combined. Google Threat Intelligence Group traces the core operator to **South Africa** (residential/mobile IPs). Palo Alto Networks tracks "ResoluteXBF" as the lead handler, with two additional members ("diencracked," "Shinigami"). The group listed ~4,000 private code repositories for sale at $95,000. Victims include Bitwarden, LiteLLM, Checkmarx, SAP, TanStack, Mistral AI, Red Hat. TeamPCP is motivated more by notoriety than money — approximately $90K in extortion proceeds — but has fundamentally eroded trust in open-source registries. The group collaborated with Lapsus$, ShinyHunters, Vect, DragonForce.
