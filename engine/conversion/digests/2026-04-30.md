The bug dates to a 2017 optimization in algif_aead.c that places page-cache pages into a writable scatterlist, producing a controlled four-byte write into in-memory setuid binaries such as /usr/bin/su. Because corrupted pages are never marked dirty, on-disk files stay untouched and checksum-based integrity tools see nothing. It is confirmed on Ubuntu, Amazon Linux, RHEL, and SUSE, and it escapes containers.
