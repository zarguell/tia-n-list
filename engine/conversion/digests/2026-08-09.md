Progress Kemp LoadMaster joined the CISA KEV catalog under active exploitation of CVE-2026-8037, an unauthenticated command injection with telemetry logging 792 attempts from 65 unique IPs over 41 days. Federal agencies face an August 10 remediation deadline; treat every internet-facing LoadMaster as a priority patch. CERT Polska's follow-up on December's Poland energy attacks surfaced a previously unobserved vector: an attacker-operated private APN into the OT network, enabled by a misconfiguration that let any device on the APN reach the plant. Head Mare trojanized TrueConf installers to push PhantomCore backdoors, so anyone who pulled a client from a compromised server, including partner employees, is exposed.

Watchlist: LoadMaster patching before the deadline, private-APN client isolation for OT deployments, and installer verification against vendor checksums.
