3. **Duplicate suspects function performance**: `duplicate_suspects()` in audit_checks.py is O(n²) on the active story pool. With 1,026 stories in a 7-day window, it exceeds 5 minutes. The audit ran it with a 3-day cutoff (348 stories) to complete. This limits the function's utility for detecting near-duplicates in a growing store. Consider indexing by title tokens or using LSH for scalability.

## Recommendations

1. **Fix language bleed**: Remove the Chinese characters ("突破") from `coloc-mos-o-agente-numa-sandbox-o-que-poderia-correr-mal-agentes.md` line 1 — replace with "break through" or similar.

2. **Review flood entrant sources**: Evaluate RSS feeds for `www.valtersit.com` and `www.beaconbeagle.com`. If they're content farms, consider adding them to a skip list or reducing their source weight in score.py.

3. **Optimize duplicate_suspects**: The O(n²) pairwise comparison doesn't scale. Consider:
   - LSH (Locality-Sensitive Hashing) on title tokens
   - Blocking by first-2-words or source
   - Limiting to stories with overlapping sources before Jaccard comparison

4. **Ghost story cleanup**: 3,945 eventless ghosts exist (informational, not rendered). Most are young (<90d). Consider a periodic cleanup job for ghosts >90d with no references.
