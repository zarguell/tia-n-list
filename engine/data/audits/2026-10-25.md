## Recommendations

1. **Reject raw reference IDs at the analysis publish gate** (repeat of yesterday's Rec 1, now with a front-page example). Extend `prose_lint.lint_analysis` to fault `rd:`/`masto:`/`mf:`/`desk:`/`rss:`/`x:` shapes as hard violations, then strip the 11 existing files. Content is editorial, so I did not touch it.
2. **Codify the "covered and no new development" demotion.** The PHP story at 9.10 with 339 events is the proof that score saturation, not editor taste, drives ~10 demotions a day. Either exclude digest-covered stories whose newest events add no CVE/actor/victim count/IOC, or feed the existing `stale_demoted` delta test into `score.zero_delta` so a re-report flood halves the score the way 48h of silence does. A gate that admits 22 and publishes 12 with 0 promotes in 12 days is not a ranking any more.
3. **Skip `Remotely` and host `jobicy.com` now.** 108 lifetime events, 0 kept, 100% drop rate; the 30-decision/2-day bar is calibrated for ambiguous fleets, not for a job board. Deterministic, cheap, zero recall risk.
4. **Fix or retire the four dead feeds.** Verify a working cybereason feed URL (or drop the site), send a browser-like UA or accept the 403 for tripwire, replace or drop cyberpress, and back off the Reddit `netsec` poll to stop the hourly 429. Four failures an hour for five days is a standing tax and a silently dead signal path.
5. **Sort the IOC pages by status then recency and drain the candidate backlog.** Expired observables should never lead a "Corroborated" table; 734 unprocessed candidates against 323 published means the extraction step, not the page, is the bottleneck.
6. **Prune orphan analysis files on merge.** `triage.py apply` (or the hourly cleanup path) should move `<loser>.md` into an archive when a story is merged away, so analysis count keeps meaning distinct incidents.
7. **Unescape HTML entities when writing CTI references**, then sweep the 22 affected records so STIX/CSV consumers get usable URLs.
8. **Raise the entity-probe cap or run it store-wide off-cycle.** Three genuine duplicate pairs are still outstanding after today's budget; the per-run cap of 10 on an 11k-story store guarantees a multi-day queue even when every dismissal is easy.

Budget note: this audit is now the slowest it has been - `audit.py` takes ~6 minutes wall because both duplicate probes are O(n²) over 11k+ stories and 13.4k story files. Nothing times out today; the number to watch is store growth (56% of story files already render nothing).
