## Issues found

1. **Deduplication probe missed the Binance split** (`duplicate_suspects` clean while two active stories carried the same $6.3B theft for a week, one of them demoted six consecutive days as "zero delta" while the other was digest headliner). Title-jaccard on "Binance links $6.3 billion crypto theft to compromised DevOps laptop" vs "Binance says fake job interview helped North Korean hackers steal $6.3 billion in crypto" falls under the probe threshold despite identical victim, victim amount, date and actor. Fixed by merge this run; the detection gap remains (see Recommendations 1).
2. **Prose gate is warning-noise at analysis level**: 16 analyses in every run's log emit `triage-bookkeeping (kept)` warnings for phrases like "The event references…". Harmless but it trains readers to ignore the gate, and on a real FAIL it will be buried in ~16 lines of expected noise.

## Recommendations

1. **Widen the dedup probe beyond title jaccard** in `engine/audit.py` / `merge.py`: when two active stories share the same CVE set or the same (actor, victim-amount) tuple and their newest events are within 7 days, probe them even if titles diverge. That would have caught the Binance pair on Nov 3 instead of the audit catching it a day later, and would catch the recurring "<vendor> links <amount> theft…" vs "<vendor> says <method> led to <amount> theft" headline pattern.
2. **Codify the 15-demotes-a-day pattern in `score.py`**: extend the `zero_delta` test to fire when the newest N events in a hot story add no new CVE, actor, IOC, or victim count (the demote rationales describe exactly this condition 15 times in one digest). A working `zero_delta_penalty` there would have shrunk today's override list to the one genuine promote.
3. **Fix the analysis prose prompt**, not the analyses: the `triage-bookkeeping` warnings and the "tracked by CISA and multiple vendors" / "warranting full forensic review" filler both come from the analysis instructions inviting meta-commentary and closing advice. Ask for a closing line only when it names a concrete detection or patch action.
4. **Prune `needs-analysis.json` when the analysis consumer writes a file**, rather than only on the next `merge.py` emit. Right now the queue can read 14 pending when every one of the 14 has an analysis on disk, which makes `analysis_queue` a lagging indicator and briefly double-counted work in today's audit.
