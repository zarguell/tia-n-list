## Recommendations

1. **Codify the re-report demotion in `score.py`.** 569 of 1232 logged demotes across 202 digests say the same thing in different words: the newest items restate a story a previous digest covered and add no new fact. That is a mechanical test — same story already covered within N days, no new CVE, no new actor attribution, no new victim count, no new IOC, no advisory or KEV movement — and it is the single largest class of agent judgement being spent per run (30 today). A "covered, no delta" penalty would take that off the agent's plate and make the digests cheaper and more consistent.
2. **Add an IOC shape gate before publication.** A 32/40/64-hex length check on any string presented as an MD5/SHA-1/SHA-256 in digest or analysis prose would have caught Issue 1 deterministically. Truncated hashes should fail the specificity/grounding stage the same way a missing CVE does.
3. **Tighten the `entity_dup_suspects` probe.** 10 of 10 pairs came back at jaccard 0.07–0.21, and 9 share exactly one stoplist-worthy token (`api`, `fake`, `county`, `wordpress`). Requiring two distinct shared entities, or jaccard ≥ 0.3, would have left only the one pair that mattered (the CA v. OpenAI fragment) and made the probe worth reading.
4. **Flag or clamp future-dated events at ingest.** Reject or annotate `published_at` more than 24h ahead of ingest time (Issue 3), and make `hourly_fresh` treat a future max-timestamp as its own failure rather than as `-12h old`. Right now a bad feed date silently disables the check.
5. **Decide whether the emoji headline is house style or slop.** 191 of 202 digests carry an emoji-per-item headline and today's is `🔌 Zyxel 9.8 unauth router RCE, 🛒 Optimizely CDN skimmer…`. It is consistent and clearly deliberate, so I changed nothing, but it is the one thing on the digest page that reads as newsletter rather than threat-intel report. An editorial call, not an audit finding.
6. **Give triage an analysis-move path on merge.** Issue 2 becomes a routine merge the moment `absorb_cti_on_merge` has an analysis-file equivalent; the "keep the richer analysis" heuristic would need deciding at the same time.
