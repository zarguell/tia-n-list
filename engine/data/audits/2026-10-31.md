## Issues found

1. **The digest's promote/wildcard channel is dead.** 18 consecutive digests (10-14 → 10-31) with zero
   promotes and zero wildcards, while the same handful of slugs is demoted 4-6 times each. Whatever the
   digest agent's judgment is doing, the escape hatch that exists to catch under-scored novel stories is
   not firing, so inclusion is now purely the score.py proxy plus demotions. That is a substantive
   editorial risk, not a style nit: the story class "significant but under-ranked" (a DOJ domain seizure,
   a novel TTP with no CVE) has no path into the digest.
2. **Four OPML sources are dead and have been for weeks.** `cybereason.com/blog/rss.xml` now serves the
   blog HTML page (breaking since at least 2026-10-01, ~23 failures/day), `tripwire.com/blogs.xml` returns
   a Cloudflare 403 to `tia-storyline/1.0`, `vanimpe.eu/feed` redirects to an HTML page, and
   `cyberpress.org/category/cyber-security-news/feed/` times out (>25s) while the site's main feed works.
   Each burns a fetch and a log line every hour.
3. **Reddit `r/netsec` RSS failed with HTTP 429 on every hourly run for the last three days** (36/36,
   `r/cybersecurity` succeeds). Reddit rate-limits this client's back-to-back requests; `ingest_reddit`
   has no inter-request delay and no retry, so the netsec signal layer is simply missing. Not a
   correctness break (the signal is advisory), but it silently disables half the Reddit input.
4. **Analysis depth has decoupled from significance.** 115 analyses were written in the last three days;
   6 are under 600B and 17 under 900B, and the thin ones include score-6.0 stories (Ivanti ICS
   CVE-2026-104935 with 30 sources: 406B; Cisco Secure Firewall CVE-2026-76418: 590B), while score-0.6
   stories get 454-578B. The 200-6000 char prose-lint gate cannot tell the difference. The 07:11-07:12Z
   batch of four thin files points at a fallback model in the rung ladder being used for analysis, not
   just for triage.
5. **Ghost stories are 46.5% of the store and growing.** 5,403 eventless non-redirect stories (5,383
   yesterday, +20), 25-35 minted per day, 30-48% of each day's "new story" count. They do not render,
   but they inflate the new-story trend and every store-wide count, and nothing ages them out.
6. **Ingest noise sits just under the check threshold.** The `ingest_noise` invariants need ≥30 decisions
   per source and ≥35% share per author, so nothing trips, but six handles at ≥80% drop rate consumed
   14.3% of the 838 attributed triage decisions in two days (~57 wasted drops/day):
   `hypedupcat@infosec.exchange` 42 decisions/98% dropped, `ninjaintelgroup` 19/84%,
   `bobe_bot@mastobot.ping.moi` 17/88%, `blueteamjobs@infosec.exchange` 15/100% (job listings),
   `redhotcyber@mastodon.bida.im` 14/93%, `noname57bot@social.circl.lu` 13/100%. Sources:
   `blueteamjobs.com` 15/100%, `www.yazoul.net` 15/80%, `github.com` 19/58%, `thecybersecguru.com` 17/53%.
7. **Concurrent automations commit over the audit.** 577 files were dirty in the working tree when the
   audit opened, and the 12:00Z hourly wrote its own commit at 11:25Z/12:53Z while the audit was repairing
   the store. This run it caught the audit's own fixes: the two story merges, the triage decisions file and
   the `sweep_exclusions.json` additions all landed inside `a372239e4bf "hourly update 2026-10-31T12:53Z"`
   (577 files) rather than under an audit commit. Content is intact and verified in HEAD, but the store's

8. **Yesterday's "URL-identity invariant" recommendation would not work as written.** A URL-identity

## Recommendations

1. **Fix the promote channel before it is unrecoverable.** 18 digests with zero promotes means the
   digest prompt's promote/wildcard instruction has stopped landing. Either the brief no longer offers
   the agent an under-scored candidate pool (check whether the bench is being filtered out of the
   candidates file), or the instruction is being read as "when in doubt demote". Codifying the
   `stale_demoted` streak as a negative score.py term would remove the daily re-demotion work and leave
   the agent's attention for the promote decision.
2. **Codify the demote streak that `stale_demoted` already computes** (9 stories today; 3 at streak 3) as a
   `score.py` penalty rather than 24 fresh overrides a day, and surface the opposite case — a story with
   rising score and zero demotes — as the digest's promote pool.
3. **Retire the four dead feeds and treat feed health as a first-class signal**: drop or replace
   `cybereason.com/blog/rss.xml`, `tripwire.com/blogs.xml`, `vanimpe.eu/feed`,
   `cyberpress.org/category/...`, and log all feed errors instead of `errors[:3]` (three of six were
   invisible in the log). PR #171 already recovered two of the six; the remaining four are dead at the
   publisher.
4. **Add a delay + single retry with backoff between the two Reddit feeds** in `ingest_reddit`, and log
   the 429 as degraded-input rather than a per-run stderr line nobody reads.
5. **Make analysis length a function of significance**: require ≥3 paragraphs / ≥900 chars for stories
   scoring ≥5.0, and pin the analysis stage's model ladder so a fallback rung cannot write a 400-byte
   analysis for a 30-source zero-day.
6. **Merge the NetamLoader pair next run** (over today's cap): `hackers-target-asian-governments-with-new-
   netamloader-malware-hi` → `unknown-apt-uses-netamloader-to-attack-a-win10-os-customized-for`. Then
   review the Shai-Hulud cluster (19 files) with the question "installment or incident?" — the
   Tensorlake/Trinitite pair overlaps by incident today.
7. **Make `malware.news` a resolution step, not a source**: it minted both of today's duplicates as
   `/t/<slug>/<id>` mirrors. Resolving its link targets before clustering (or refusing to mint a story
   when the target article is already an event) would have caught both mechanically.
8. **Age out ghosts**: delete or archive eventless non-redirect stories older than N days, and stop
   minting until triage keeps at least one event, so the new-story trend measures incidents rather than
   mints.
9. **Skip the six worthless handles** named in Issue 6 at the ingest layer rather than spending ~57
   triage decisions a day re-dropping them.
10. **Widen the `ingest_noise` invariants** to fire on total waste (decisions × drop rate) in addition to
    share, so a 100%-dropped job feed cannot hide under the 30-decision floor forever.
