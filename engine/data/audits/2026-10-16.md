1. **Finish consolidating GhostAction** into
   `the-campaign-that-never-stopped-tracking-ghostaction-from-2025-t` (or
   whichever slug the owner prefers as canonical), including the digest-linked
   fake-font-files slug. The probe only surfaced one pair of a four-slug cluster;

2. **Codify re-report/CATCH-UP down-ranking in `score.py`.** The digest agent
   demotes ~14 stories/day (124 demotes vs 10 promotes over nine digests), almost
   all "already covered, no new delta / outlet re-report". A mechanical
   "event title ≈ existing event title, no new CVE/actor" penalty would remove
   most manual demotion work.
3. **Boost non-CVE TTP stories.** All three recent wildcards are novel-technique
   / no-CVE stories (Pwn2Own, credential-stealer loaders) the proxy under-ranks.
   A small floor for high-novelty, multi-source TTP coverage would reduce
   wildcard dependence.
4. **Split the Palo Alto catch-all.** `palo-alto-networks-patches-13-
   vulnerabilities` (first seen Jul 10, last 10-16) absorbs many separate PAN-OS
   advisories (AV26-905, AV26-1041, the CVE-2026-0322 zero-day); `distinct_
   advisory_ids` did not exonerate it against AV26-905 in the suspect probe. An
   advisory-code-based guard (or splitting per advisory) would stop the
   catch-all from masking distinct advisories.
