## Recommendations

1. **Codify re-report curation in `score.py` (third repeat).** 651 overrides,
   83% demotes, and the digest agent is doing the same work by hand every day:
   (a) score an event with no new CVE/actor/victim-count/IOC as zero-delta
   against its story; (b) prefer the newest *clean* event title, never a raw
   toot, when selecting a story title. Fixing (b) retires Issues 1 and 3
   mechanically.
2. **Add a title lint with a hard gate on the hot/all listing**: reject titles
   matching `#\w`, `https?://`, or ending mid-URL, and prefer the newest
   non-stub event title. The FreeRDP fix happened manually yesterday and the
   same blemish reappeared within 24h on a higher-scoring story.
3. **Extend duplicate detection beyond title similarity.** The BragJack cluster
   (four live stories, one research disclosure) and the boilerplate-prefix
   over-merge are invisible to both current detectors. A URL-host + first-40-
   token-boilerplate check would catch the aggregator series; a "same disclosure,
   same week, ≥3 shared tokens" check would catch BragJack.
4. **Stale-story lifecycle.** Issue 2 exists because a story can be demoted
   forever without being corrected. A "demoted 2+ digests with no new fact"
   signal should surface the story to the desk for a rewrite or a
   supersede-merge into the newer Chrome 155 story.
5. **Refresh or drop the `securityonline.info` Plex reference** on the CTI case
   page once it is reachable; today it is an unverifiable link in published
   content.
