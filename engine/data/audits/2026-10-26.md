## Recommendations

1. **Add a deploy-freshness guard to cronman jobs.** Before PHASE2, compare the repo's HEAD against `origin/main` (fetch, cheap) and log or refuse to compose when the checkout is behind, naming the commit it is missing. Five days of merged fixes sat unshipped while every job reported DONE; a one-line staleness assertion would have caught it on 10-22.
2. **Codify the five wildcard classes in `score.py`** instead of re-deriving them per digest: supply-chain package compromise, novel TTP with no CVE (agentic/ClickFix-style), same-vendor mass-disclosure bundles (≥6 CVEs, one day), named-actor campaign resumption, and regulatory/standards milestones. Today's 43-for-43 demote slate and 13 days without a promote or wildcard says the proxy is not surfacing what the editor keeps having to add.
3. **Merge the ConnectWise cluster and the Mythos pair next run** (4 slugs into 1, 2 into 1), then re-point any digest links. Until then, the digest will keep picking the thin slug because title similarity, not event count, decides.
4. **Make "The event references…" a hard prose violation, not a WARN,** or have the analysis prompt forbid it outright. 17 files in one lint run is the pipeline describing itself to readers.
5. **Schedule `engine/cleanup_ghosts.py`** on a cadence. 5,257 ghosts against 11,132 active stories (56% of all story files do not render) will otherwise keep growing the store and every full scan.
6. **Add a rendered-provenance assertion to the site deploy** (like the snapshot pin): fail the deploy if any `/daily/<date>/` page contains `Generated by `, and add the phrase to `prose_lint`'s banned strings as belt and braces on top of PR #170.
7. **Stop ranking expired observables above live ones on `/cti/iocs/`** (sort by status then last-seen), and either work the 736-candidate backlog or cap what the page claims.
