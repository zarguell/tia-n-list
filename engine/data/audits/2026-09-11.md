## Recommendations

1. **Consolidate Patch Tuesday clusters**: Add a post-Patch-Tuesday consolidation step that merges same-family+month stories after the initial ingest settles (48h window). The 12 Microsoft stories should become 1–2 canonical entries.
2. **Harden repair_dedupe.py same-base check**: Exclude advisory-series slugs (pattern: `*-av26-\d+`) from the same-base duplicate detector. Advisory numbers are sequential IDs, not duplicate suffixes.
3. **Validate merge targets in triage apply**: Before executing a merge, check that the target story exists and is not itself merged. Skip with a warning if invalid.
4. **Schedule ghost story cleanup**: The 1662 ghost stories are dead weight. A periodic cleanup (monthly) that removes eventless non-redirect stories older than 90 days would keep the store lean.
