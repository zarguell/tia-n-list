## Issues found

1. **Today's digest is factually wrong about the GitLab CVE-2026-110264 affected range.** Body text: "touches every release from 18.11 through 19.5". Primary source (docs.gitlab.com, stored event `masto:117480750107262877`): "affects every GitLab release from 7.0 up to the fixed versions". The published analysis and the digest's own headline ("twelve years of releases") both agree with 7.0, so the digest body is internally contradictory and understates exposure by about a decade. This is content quality, outside my fix license — it needs the digest agent's editor or a correction pass.
2. **Publisher auto-shares are being scored as story development.** `swallowtail-russian-espionage-group-...` (score 6.2) and `pan-os-cve-2026-0257-cisa-kev-...` (4.1) both had `zero_delta: False` today on the strength of events like `masto:117483586576564599`: a Jetpack auto-share (`utm_source=mastodon&utm_medium=jetpack_social`) of the publisher's own already-stored securityonline.info article, whose content_md is the headline plus "via Mastodon". These are echoes, not developments, but they clear the zero-delta gate and refresh the recency anchor, which is why the digest had to demote both by hand. Store-wide, 30 of 83 recent digest demotes are this class.
3. **Source chips on story cards surface scraper domains.** `php-warns-admins-of-two-critical-flaws-emergency-patches-coming` carries 115 "sources" including vectorwire.ai, fixgamingchannel.com, newtqnia.com, abgeo.dev, desnips.nl, techupscale.com, zubiqo.com, calif.io, arint.info, cybersecurefox.com — AI content-farm republishers. Scoring impact is bounded (breadth caps at n_sources=4, authority is a max, pickup caps at 0.6), but the card renders "cyberplace.social +113 more", which reads badly.
4. **`engine/audit.py` needs ~13 minutes at 13,143 stories** (network-bound on `live_site` and the 500KB snapshot bundle). A 300s bounded harness timeout returns an empty file with no error, which is indistinguishable from a broken audit.
5. Front-page #1 hot slot is CISA AA26-293a SafePay (score 10.0) while the digest demoted the same story today as covered-and-only-re-reported. Not a bug, but the two editorial surfaces disagree in public.

## Recommendations

1. **Classify publisher auto-shares as echoes in `score.py`.** Concrete rule: an event whose URL matches an already-stored event's URL modulo tracking params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `jetpack_social`, `ref`, `fbclid`), or whose host equals the story's already-seen publisher host and whose content_md is headline-only plus "via Mastodon", must not enter `dev_dts` and must not clear `zero_delta`. That alone would remove the largest recurring digest-demote class (30/83 over six digests) and stop stories re-heating at 6.2 on their own publisher's automated share.
2. **Add a deny/low-authority list for AI content-farm domains** (vectorwire.ai, fixgamingchannel.com, newtqnia.com, abgeo.dev, desnips.nl, techupscale.com, zubiqo.com, implicator.ai, calif.io, arint.info, cybersecurefox.com, sammy.hk) and prefer publisher domains over social bridges in the card source chips. Low urgency for scores (all capped), high value for how the site reads.
3. **Surface the digest's editorial ranking on the front page, or at least suppress stories the digest demoted that day.** Right now the site's #1 is a story the newsletter called a re-report.
4. **Raise the audit harness timeout to at least 20 minutes**, or parallelize the `live_site`/`snapshot_pin` curl checks in `audit.py` — at current store size the run cannot finish inside 300s and fails silently with an empty JSON file.
5. **Run `engine/cleanup_ghosts.py` on a schedule** (or fold it into the weekly job): ghosts grew +273 in 13 days (4,914 → 5,187). Harmless today, but it is unbounded growth in a store the site has to stat.
6. Consider retiring the `1-basic-information-original-title-bragjack-...` cluster, whose own analysis admits it aggregates a rolling series of one author's dev.to reposts.
