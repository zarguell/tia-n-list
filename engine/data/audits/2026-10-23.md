## Recommendations

1. **Make `audit.py`'s `duplicate_suspects` probe consult `engine/sweep_exclusions.json`** before reporting a pair (order-insensitive). Five of seven suspects today were pairs already adjudicated by the Oct 18-21 audits; the exclusion file exists precisely to stop this and the probe ignores it, so each audit re-litigates settled dismissals.
2. **Add a coherence guard for event sinks.** Either (a) a score penalty when a story's event titles share no discriminators with the story title (the same `_norm_tokens` intersection `match_scores` already computes), (b) refuse to attach an event whose title shares zero discriminators with the story title unless a CVE/advisory code matches, or (c) a hard cap on `n_sources`/`events` before the story is split for review. Any of the three would have prevented today's Issue 1. Note the sink kept `zero_delta: false` and full `breadth: 2.0`, so breadth is rewarding incoherence.
3. **Codify the two recurring digest demote patterns in `score.py`:** a re-report-only penalty that actually sets `zero_delta` for stories whose newest events merely restate a covered advisory (the field is present but false on all seven such stories), and a single-source-leak-claim penalty (n_sources == 1 plus leak-site/actor-claim vocabulary).
4. **Skip `jobicy.com` (and job-ad-shaped titles, `⚡ Remote ... Apply here`) at ingest.** They are not cyber news in any sense and they are contributing score mass to a rendered story.
5. **Force an immediate analysis refresh for the lead story** rather than waiting for the hourly queue, and consider a minimum substance bar (say, 150 words including a CVE and one action) before an analysis is published for a story scoring above ~9 — the day's lead story is exactly where a 291-byte stub does the most damage.
6. **Publish the CVE-2026-115547 version correction** (2026.10.2, per the Okta advisory) with the next Okta analysis refresh.
