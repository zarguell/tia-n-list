## Recommendations

1. **Codify the sibling-slug demotion in `score.py`.** 32% of all logged demotions (289/909) exist only because a second or third slug for an already-digested incident outranks real news. A penalty for "shares >= 2 CVEs or a primary event with a story digested in the last N days" would remove the single largest manual decision class from the digest agent.
2. **Make old-development stories fall below the digest cut mechanically.** 69 CATCH-UP demotions in 73 digests, most of them "N-day-old development, nothing new today" on stories still scoring above the line. The recency term needs to bite harder on `dev` age than on event recency.
3. **Write story json atomically** (temp file plus `os.replace`, ideally a shared `store.write_json`). That kills the torn-read crash class and removes the most plausible mechanism for issue 1, where a concurrent stage writes back a pre-merge snapshot.
4. **Editorial decision on the SonicWall canonical** (issue 3): either restore the SecurityWeek roundup as its own story, or retitle the canonical and accept the CVE union. Also decide whether the AuthPoint CVEs belong on both stories or only on `watchguard-security-advisory-av26-1125`.
5. **Finish the duplicates the merge cap deferred:** `google-llmjacking-attacks-rack-up-1-million-in-cloud-ai-charges` into the Mandiant canonical, and the Talos Q3 IR pair (`cisco-talos-reports-ransomware-rise-with-help-desk-social-engine` into `cisco-talos-q3-2026-ir-trends-help-desk-impersonation-and-remote`). The ARTEX pair needs a read first: the gbhackers piece may be the same South Korean banking campaign the CrowdStrike story already carries.
6. **Unescape CTI reference URLs** at write time (`&amp;` to `&`), and reconcile `dark-caracal-...` `detections.yara` with the file on disk.
