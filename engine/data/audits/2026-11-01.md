## Recommendations

1. **Make story writes atomic.** Write to `<path>.tmp` in the same directory and `os.replace()` it, in
   `triage.py` (the per-story dump in `apply`), `merge.py` and `store.py`. That removes the
   13:06Z class of `JSONDecodeError` at the source; every reader in the pipeline (ssg, cti, audit,
   snapshots) is exposed to it as long as a truncate is observable. Pin it with a test that reads a
   story file from a second process while `apply` rewrites it.
2. **Drop or repair the 551 bare-domain CTI references** and add a lint: a `references` entry must have
   a path segment longer than a bare `/`. Until then the 12:55Z ref-liveness numbers (130 dead / 132
   walled) cannot be read as link rot.
3. **Give the digest the same substance floor the analysis prompt has** — at least three concrete
   artifacts per entry (CVE id, fixed version, victim count, or IOC), and treat a number that appears on
   the case page but not in the digest as a defect. Today's Drupal and Northflank entries are the
   worked examples.
4. **Codify the `stale_demoted` streak as a `score.py` penalty** (4 stories at streak 2-3 today; eight
   slugs account for most of the 815 lifetime demotes) and surface the inverse — rising score, zero
   demotes — as the digest's standing promote pool. That is now the cheaper half of the promote
   problem: the channel works, the candidates still have to be found by hand each morning.
5. **Teach the `ingest_noise` flood detector about the collector's skip list.** A source skipped at the
   collector should stop counting toward `flood entrant` immediately, or the audit spends its one FAIL
   on a fixed source for the tail of the 7-day window. Today's FAIL is otherwise a false positive.
6. **`engine/audit.py` needs an incremental read path.** It took 8m20s detached and read 831MB to
   produce 268KB of JSON: it `json.load`s all 9,473 stories three times (`store_invariants` twice,
   `fragmentation` once) before `load_all_stories` does it a fourth time, and the score-0 sweep then
   compares every pair. Precedent exists (the 2026-10-21 sigma fix, same symptom). Reuse one load, and
   a bounded caller stops seeing a false timeout.
7. **Merge queue for next run, in order**: `synology-warns-of-ransomware-attacks-on-nas-devices-running-
   outd` → `synology-nas-encrypted-by-c3rb3r-ransomware` (needs a decision about its 830B analysis),
   then `tricks-from-the-past-...` → `check-point-reveals-yoba-malware-campaign-using-signed-ai-binari`
   (needs a decision about the two digest links). Then read the threataft Oray article and settle the
   Oray pair one way or the other.
8. **Give the store an explicit ownership boundary**: the hourly and desk jobs should stage only the
   paths their flow wrote (cronman's `_publish_pathspec` already does this for CTI). Third day of
   reports on the same defect, and it is the reason the audit's own output cannot be attributed.
