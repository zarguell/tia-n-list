## Recommendations

1. **Add a handle skip for `secquill@infosec.exchange` / `secquill.com`.** 70% of its events are dropped, its kept minority duplicates bigger outlets (DreamHost today, VAPOR PANDA, GhostMap), and it is one of the two sources in a hard FAIL. This is a clean skip, not a demotion.
2. **Do not hard-skip `infonewscyber.fr`; demote it to low-trust** (may add an event only to a story another source opened) or cap it at 2-3 events/day. It is the sole source on 13 French regional breach stories that no other feed carries; the 67% drop rate is the cost of the 18 that land.
3. **Make the flood detector fair to new sources** (repeat of yesterday's Recommendation 2, still not actioned): "100% of lifetime in the window" is true by definition for any newly followed source, so detector 4 fires on every new entrant regardless of behaviour. Require `lifetime >= 2 × flood_days` before the lifetime-share arm counts, and let the drop-rate and stub arms carry the real signal. Otherwise this check will keep failing for a week every time a source is added, and the real gamefan-class signal will be lost in that noise.
4. **Validate reference URLs at write time.** A CTI reference should be the event's own `url` unless a HEAD returns 200 before it is persisted — that would have caught the 80-character gbhackers/cyberpress synthesis outright. Separately, treat gbhackers.com as reference-unstable and prefer the primary vendor source in records that cite both.
5. **Codify the echo-ring penalty in `score.py`** (third audit running): 21 demotes a day, all "aggregator echo, no new fact", is a scoring bug wearing an editorial costume. Raise the `rereports`/`zero_delta` weight for mastodon relay handles that re-post an already-covered disclosure.

Also move the stray French EFS event out of the White House proxy story into the canonical EFS story.
