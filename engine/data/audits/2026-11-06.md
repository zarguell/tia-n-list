## Recommendations

1. **Deal with `infonewscyber.fr`.** Either add a handle skip (`infonewscyber@infosec.exchange`) or demote it to a low-trust source that can only contribute an event to a story already opened by another source. A hard skip loses the French local breach coverage; a rate-limit or low-trust flag keeps it without the 70% triage cost. Do not leave it tripping the detector every week.
2. **Make `ingest_noise` fair to new sources.** A handle followed for the first time can only ever show "100% of lifetime in window" — the detector cannot distinguish a flood from a new entrant. Require `lifetime >= 2x window` before the 100%-of-lifetime signal counts, and keep the drop-rate arm (70% here) as the real signal.
3. **Codify the echo-ring penalty in `score.py`.** 35 digest demotes a day, all "aggregator echo, no new fact", is a scoring bug wearing an editorial costume. Raise the `rereports`/`zero_delta` weight for mastodon relay handles that re-post an already-covered disclosure, and the digest agent stops hand-demoting ~30 clusters per day.
4. **Merge the remaining same-story pairs** (2 per run until done): SonicWall GMS first (it is on the site twice and today's digest linked one of them), then EFS blood bank, VAPOR PANDA, BOSSAI, Sogou.
5. **Normalise all-lowercase titles at ingest** (sentence-case the first character when the source headline has no capitals), so the H1 and card never render a lowercase headline. Applies to 20 active stories, one of them today's lead.
6. **Kill or rewrite the BragJack aggregator analysis.** Its story has 0 events and the prose aggregates five unrelated campaigns; either re-home those paragraphs to their own stories or retire the analysis. This is the analysis-side twin of the fragmentation problem.
7. **Have `triage.py` archive the analysis `.md` alongside the CTI record on merge** (same `.archive/` convention), so merges do not leave orphaned analyses behind.
8. **Alert on a streak of `CHECK: UNKNOWN` verdicts**, not just on FAIL. Two consecutive UNKNOWNs should page; the nine-day blind spot was invisible precisely because UNKNOWN is the designed non-blocking path.
