## Recommendations

1. Codify the digest demote streak in `score.py`: `stale_demoted` already computes streaks (3 stories
   today, `adobe-campaign-classic-...` at 3 days). A trailing demote penalty would stop the digest agent
   spending 26 slots a day re-demoting the same slugs, and would surface the promote side of the ledger.
2. Add a URL-identity invariant to `audit_checks.dedup_invariants`: no normalized URL may appear as
   events in two active stories (today's Polymarket pair would have failed it deterministically).
3. Treat `malware.news` as a relay/mirror source in the registry: it licenses 20-32 new stories/day that
   are mirrors of publisher articles. Resolving its `/t/<slug>/<id>` links to the original URL before
   clustering would collapse a large share of the fragmentation trend, exactly as the crawl-derived
   duplicates suggest.
4. Merge next run (2 available): INJ3CTOR3 FreePBX pair, Cloudflare Q3 DDoS report pair,
   Crime Stoppers breach pair.
5. When a merge empties a story that owns an analysis, decide one home for the prose (move it to the
   canonical or drop it) so the store does not carry two analyses for one incident and the digest stops
   citing shells.
6. Consider an ingest skip for the clearly worthless handles named in issue 7 (`blueteamjobs@`,
   `noname57bot@`, `scanmalware@`, `redhotcyber@`) rather than burning triage decisions on them hourly.
