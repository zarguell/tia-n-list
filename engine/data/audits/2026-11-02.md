## Recommendations

1. **Codify the single-source-stub demote in `score.py`.** 19 of 293 demotes in the last 14 digests
   (4 of today's 16) are stories whose only events are `radar.offseq` or `cve.org` social stubs with
   no vendor advisory, no exploitation evidence, and sometimes conflicting fixed versions. A
   confidence gate on `n_sources == 1 and source in {radar.offseq.com, cve.org}` would remove most
   of the digest agent's manual demote work.
2. **Penalise zero-delta accumulation.** `stale_demoted` shows the same stories demoted 2, 3 and 4
   consecutive days (`binance-links-*`, `polymarket-halts-*`) because recap events keep arriving
   with no new CVEs, actors, victim counts or IOCs. A "no new facts in N days" decay term would stop
   them from wasting a slot in the bench every day.
3. **Make the publish chains tolerate a dirty worktree** (Issue 2): align `cti_pass` and `story_desk`
   with the `-c rebase.autoStash=true` form used by `daily_digest`, `tia_kev`, `bedrock_models`,
   `kev_burndown` and `audit`, and abort cleanly (record + log) instead of leaving a half-rebased
   tree when the stash cannot be reapplied.
4. **CTI pass should skip `merged_into` stories** (Issue 5) and attach authored records to the
   canonical chain target; the 130 existing shell records are worth a one-off remap.
5. **Ingest watchlist, below the FAIL thresholds.** No detector tripped, but: `infonewscyber.fr` (18
   decisions in 2 days, 72% dropped, all 19 lifetime events inside the 7-day flood window) is a
   brand-new low-yield source; `justpaste.in`/author `justpaste` is 82% dropped over 11 decisions;
   and `0daybeats@epsilon.social` consumed 13 triage slots for 13 drops (100%) in the window. A
   handle skip on `0daybeats@epsilon.social` and a host skip on `justpaste.in` would return ~24
   slots per 2 days. `security_crawler_carl@infosec.exchange` (76% dropped, 17 decisions) is the
   next candidate.
6. **Strip the "The event references" boilerplate from the 26 flagged analyses** (Issue 6) in a
   single content pass, and promote that lint from WARN to error once the backlog is clear.
