Recurring across all 564 logged overrides, so worth a deliberate tune.
2. **Refresh `cti-queue.json` after PHASE2 (or have the audit subtract covered entries)**: re-run `cti_pass.py` PHASE1 as the last step of PHASE3, or commit the post-PHASE2 queue. This makes the committed "uncovered" count honest and stops spurious backlog re-runs.
