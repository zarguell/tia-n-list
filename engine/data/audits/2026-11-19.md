## Recommendations

1. **Watch source freshness, not just global freshness.** Add a per-source staleness leg to `audit.py` (e.g. `reddit.json` newest post older than 12h → FAIL, and the same for any OPML source silent >72h). Three days of dead Reddit went unseen precisely because `hourly_fresh` only looks at the store-wide newest event.
2. **Decide Reddit's fate:** OAuth-authenticated access (a registered app + PRAW-style token) or drop both subs from `ingest.py` and blank the community term in `score.py`. What must not continue is a frozen snapshot quietly feeding scores; if the subs are gone (r/cybersecurity now redirects to login), the honest move is removal.
3. **Codify the re-report penalty** in `score.py`: 444 of 1,444 digest overrides (31%) are "re-report/restate/translation of something already covered", and the `stale_demoted` streak data is already emitted by `audit.py`. A negative term for a story re-surfacing after N days with no new CVE/actor/victim/IOC would remove the digest agent's single largest manual judgment class.
4. **Backfill the 21 therecord.media stubs** now that PR #214's full-text path works: a one-off re-fetch for events whose `.md` is <200 B, skipping `rd:` stubs. Priority: the three stories the digest and analyses cite.
5. **Merge the UAT-12643/Overlord pair** on the next run (cap-limited today) and reconcile the two analysis files into one rather than stranding either.
6. **Either refresh or unpublish the 22 warn-stage legacy analyses**, at minimum the four named in Issue 5.
7. **Correct the Overlord CTI record's ATT&CK list** (drop T1542 unless a boot component is documented) and consider a gate that requires the technique name to appear in, or be plainly implied by, the event text.
