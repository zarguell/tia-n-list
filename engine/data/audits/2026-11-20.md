3. **Digest override volume.** 43 demotes in one digest (and 39, 40, 30, 27

   `kevrichment/data/epss-history.json` is modified and
   `kevrichment/data/quarantine/` is untracked in `~/repos/tia-n-list` before

5. **Specificity gate warn count is drifting up.** The publish lint
   (`stage=warn`) reports 23 of ~3800 shipped analyses would "newly fail" the
   prose-specificity gate — 8 more than yesterday's log. Pre-existing prose
   is not a today defect, but the count is monotonically increasing, which
   means the gate is being tightened without a backfill plan.

## Recommendations

1. **Merge the UAT-12643 pair with artefact cleanup.** Add a small,
   idempotent step (or an audit-approved triage `merges` entry plus a
   follow-up pass) that, when the canonical story for a merge already owns a
   CTI record, retargets the absorbed slug's CTI record and its `.sigma`/
   `.yara` to the canonical story instead of orphaning them — then merge
   `north-korea-linked-hackers-hide-overlord-rat-server-addresses-in` into
   `uat-12643-suspected-north-korea-aligned-actor-blends-blockchain`.
2. **Codify the re-report signal in `score.py`.** The digest demotes ~40
   stories a day with "covered / no new / re-report / CATCH-UP" reasoning.
   A penalty for stories whose newest events add no new discriminators
   (CVEs, actors, victim counts, IOCs) after the story was already covered by
   a digest would remove most of that hand work and shrink the override log.
3. **Gate the promote pool on a named entity.** 8 of today's promote-pool
   candidates were rejected for naming no victim, CVE, actor or vector. Reject
   them at pool-build time instead of at digest time.
4. **Watch `kbytechnologies.com`.** 27 lifetime events, all 27 in the last 7
   days, 39% dropped, already appearing as a secondary source on the vCenter
   and Equinor stories. Ingest-noise arms 1 and 4 cannot see it yet (18
   decisions; lifetime age < 14 days), so it is the next firehose candidate.
   `malware.news` (90 decisions/2d, 38% dropped) and `gbhackers.com` (33
   decisions, 42% dropped) remain the volume leaders but stay well under the
   80% junk thresholds.
5. **Backfill or scope the specificity gate.** Either grandfather the 23
   currently-shipped analyses that would fail or add the gate to the hourly
   repair path, so the warn list stops growing audit over audit.
