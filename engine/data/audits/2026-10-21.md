## Recommendations

1. **Retire the generator footer at publish.** Strip a trailing `Generated by
   <model>. Input tokens: ...` line in the digest publish step (or drop it from
   the digest prompt) and clean the 45 historical pages. Publishing per-run
   token cost is the single most embarrassing thing currently on the site.
2. **Merge the eMudhra pair tomorrow** (canonical:
   `the-internet-goes-on-high-alert-after-discovery-of-8-compromised`, 18
   sources, digest-linked) and move the CTI record of the loser onto the
   canonical rather than leaving a case page behind a redirect. Same for the
   ConnectWise pair (canonical `connectwise-investigating-potential-cloud-backup-breach`).
   Generalize the merge path so a `merged_into` story's CTI record is absorbed
   or retargeted automatically: today, merging two CTI-covered stories quietly
   leaves two records and two case pages for one event.
3. **Codify the editorial signal instead of hand-demoting it.** A "covered
   before and newest event adds no new fact" penalty plus a "single-source novel
   TTP" allowance in `score.py` would remove ~75% of daily demote overrides and
   most promote overrides (KEV-dated-today is already modelled). The digest
   agent's override log is the training data.
4. **Batch the sigma gate.** Replace the per-rule `sigma check` loop with one
   `sigma check <dir>` (or a pySigma parse pass), or cache results by rule
   mtime. Cuts audit wall clock from ~11 min to well under 2, which keeps it
   inside ordinary harness timeouts.
5. **Add source/handle skips for the standing noise floor:** `www.yazoul.net`,
   `www.redhotcyber.com` (both 100% dropped over the last 2 days) and the
   `hypedupcat@infosec.exchange` handle (97% of 32 decisions dropped). The
   ingest_noise check passes, but every one of those is paid triage on junk.
6. **Re-check the unreachable CTI reference** and prefer sources that answer
   without a bot wall; add `cybersecuritynews.com` to the ref-liveness cache's
   dead list if it stays dark.
7. **Analysis quality floor:** require either one named CVE/actor/tool or one
   IOC before an analysis file is accepted for a hot story, so 500-byte
   "watch for vendor updates" stubs cannot ship as the newest analysis.
