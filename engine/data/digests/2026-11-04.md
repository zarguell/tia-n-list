[NEW] OpsShield's cPGuard web security suite suffered a supply chain compromise of its update channel at 02:06 UTC on November 2, when an attacker using compromised credentials pushed malicious versions 5.88.12 through 5.88.19 through the update distribution CDN onto customer Linux servers in the [cPGuard supply chain compromise](stories/cpguard-opsshield-llp-breach/).
