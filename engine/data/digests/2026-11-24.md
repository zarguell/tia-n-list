[NEW] CISA turned six exploited flaws into patch-now orders with its November 20 and November 23 Known Exploited Vulnerabilities additions, in the [CISA KEV exploitation bundle](stories/cisa-warns-hackers-are-actively-exploiting-cisco-vmware-sap-and/). The set spans Cisco Expressway CVE-2026-76466, VMware vCenter CVE-2026-123890, SAP NetWeaver CVE-2025-42884, Apache Roller CVE-2026-129448, MONAI Label CVE-2026-133968, and N-able MailAssure CVE-2026-134624. Field detail is strongest on Expressway, where attackers used a public proof of concept against a Russian company to gain root on X15.5.3 devices and pivot through two internet-facing appliances, with roughly 14,900 exposed hosts counted. Restrict Expressway ports 4371 and 4372 to trusted cluster hosts, limit vCenter port 2012 exposure, and work the bundle by federal deadlines rather than as one campaign.

[NEW] N-able disclosed CVE-2026-134624, a CVSS 9.3 server-side request forgery in MailAssure and SpamExperts found while investigating the breach of its hosted email filtering service, in the [N-able MailAssure flaw](stories/n-able-discloses-critical-mail-assure-vulnerability-found-during/). [NEW] ASUS published CVE-2026-114203 on November 24, a CVSS 4.0 9.3 OS command injection in GPU Tweak III versions V.1.6.9.4 through v2.2.1.1, in the [ASUS GPU Tweak III flaw](stories/asus-gpu-tweak-iii-cve-2026-114203-critical-unauthenticated-comm/). A crafted request to a service HTTP endpoint executes arbitrary OS commands, and a matching October researcher write-up describes the Mobile Module listener on 0.0.0.0:5001 accepting POSTs to /arrange_page_setting where the bIsSupport and iPosIndex fields are interpolated into a reg add command line run by cmd.exe. The service typically runs as administrator, and Access-Control-Allow-Origin with a wildcard means a malicious site can drive the loopback listener at 127.0.0.1:5001 even when inbound firewall rules block other hosts. ASUS names no fixed release, so inventory installs in the affected range, stop the GT3 mobile service until a patched build appears, and hunt for cmd.exe and reg.exe spawned by the mobile service plus the proof-of-concept marker C:\pwn_lan.txt.

[NEW] Apache's November 23 batch fixes 33 flaws across nine projects, with the critical cluster in Jena Fuseki, in the [Apache November patch batch](stories/apache-updates-fix-33-flaws-across-nine-projects-including-criti/). CVE-2026-86155 is a CVSS 4.0 10.0 server-side request forgery in Fuseki's SHACL validation service affecting releases through 6.2.0 and fixed in 6.3.0, and CVE-2026-124324 is a companion bypass of localhost-only administration controls through SERVICE operations, alongside CVE-2026-86156 path traversal and further Jena advisories. Fuseki's documented default leaves SPARQL endpoints publicly reachable while administration paths under /$/ stay localhost-bound, so the pair should be read as control-plane risk on any reachable SHACL endpoint rather than an outbound-request nuisance. Upgrade Jena and Fuseki to 6.3.0, restrict /ds/shacl and SPARQL Update until patched, and hunt proxy logs for POSTs carrying SPARQL SERVICE terms in submitted shapes.

[NEW] A threat actor pushed backdoored releases of six WordPress plugins from WP connect on November 22, including Air WP Sync, WP Sync for Notion, the Brevo for Gravity Forms add-on, and Contact Form 7 add-ons for Airtable and Notion, in the [WP connect supply-chain backdoors](stories/supply-chain-compromise-backdoors-six-wp-connect-plugins-blocked/). [NEW] Microsoft closed an Azure API Management Classic flaw on November 19 after identifying unauthorized access on November 18, as Norway's National Security Authority warned operators to assume secrets were exposed, in the [Azure APIM secret theft](stories/azure-flaw-exploited-to-target-secrets-at-norwegian-energy-firms/). The flaw let an unauthorized party reach management functions and secrets in Classic-tier instances not set to Microsoft's recommended guidance, including named values, identity provider client secrets, subscription keys, and direct management API tokens, while v2 tiers were unaffected. Equinor and Skagerak Energi were among the affected Norwegian organizations, with impact reported in other sectors and countries, and Equinor detected its incident on a Tuesday without full containment until Friday while production continued. Rotate every named value, client secret, subscription key, and management token in scope from before November 19, hunt for reuse of those credentials in downstream backends, and move toward private endpoints with Key Vault-backed named values.

[NEW] CISA advisory ICSA-26-323-02 covers Elecbrakes EB2 controllers through 3.0.4.2.0, VM1 below 1.3.4.2.1, and all ELBC2000 units, whose missing authentication and replayable Bluetooth commands let any nearby radio apply trailer brakes, in the [Elecbrakes Bluetooth brake flaws](stories/elecbrakes-trailer-brake-controllers-accept-unauthenticated-blue/). CVE-2026-96805 and CVE-2026-125273 carry CVSS 9.6 for the unauthenticated, unprotected brake commands, and CVE-2026-124281 carries CVSS 6.1 for a firmware-update channel whose integrity rests only on CRC32 after Just Works pairing. Element Digital researchers showed a four-byte write to the brake control characteristic setting force from 0x00 to 0xFF with confirmation feedback and no sequence numbers or nonces, producing stronger deceleration at higher values on a closed road, with 10 to 100 metres of range. Fixed EB2 3.0.4.4.3 and VM1 1.3.4.2.1 builds shipped July 6 through user-initiated app updates with 60 to 70 percent of connecting controllers current, while the discontinued ELBC2000 has no fix, so verify versions in the app and treat any powered ELBC2000 as unmitigated.

[REVISIT] United States agencies joined the United Kingdom response with a joint CISA, FBI, HHS, USDA, and DOT fact sheet warning that internet-facing refrigeration and commercial temperature controllers can be altered to change temperatures, in the [refrigeration controller exposure](stories/ncsc-operation-disconnects-thousands-of-refrigeration-controller/). The underlying Whitehall and NCSC operation, reported by the BBC, moved thousands of Resource Data Management controllers at major supermarkets and food suppliers off the public internet, covering tens of thousands of fridges and freezers, after Neil McCourt of Omega Solutions and validating Pen Test Partners researchers found the weaknesses. RDM issued advisory RDM-SA-2026-001 on November 2 and revised it November 3, released new firmware, and drew criticism from Pen Test Partners for thin impact detail, while many thousands of units at smaller stores were still reported exposed. Remove controllers from the public internet, apply updated firmware, use VPN or private APN paths for cellular modems, and watch for exposed RDM units at smaller cold-chain operators or any field report of tampering.
