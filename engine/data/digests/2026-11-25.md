CVE-2026-123633 triggers when a client sends only part of a HEADERS frame header and a later upstream allocation drains the still-live connection, rated 9.2 under CVSS 4.0 and 8.1 under 3.1, with affected Open Source branches from 1.19.7 through 1.30.6 and 1.31.0 through 1.31.8 plus Plus builds back to R33.
