[NEW] The TU Berlin Hello Again paper presented at ACM CCS 2026 breaks the anti-hammering assumption behind firmware TPMs, in [the Hello Again PIN bypass](stories/attack-bypasses-tpm-lockout-to-crack-windows-hello-and-bitlocker/).
