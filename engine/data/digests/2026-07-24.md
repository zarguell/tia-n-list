The Laundry Bear campaign is the day's defining story because of its reach. A zero-click Zimbra flaw exploited since July 2025 has been stealing email archives, passwords and 2FA tokens from government, defense and energy organizations across NATO states, Ukraine and Africa. [Laundry Bear](stories/laundry-bear-void-blizzard-zimbra-zero-click-espionage-joint-int/) is now the subject of a joint CISA-FBI advisory, and the pattern of Ukrainian targeting first and global deployment second matches previous Russian campaigns. Any Zimbra instance unpatched since November 2025 should be treated as compromised.

Clop runs a parallel extortion campaign against PTC Windchill and FlexPLM, exploiting a KEV-listed RCE with JSP webshells and demands sent from spoofed support addresses. [The Windchill campaign](stories/first-ever-exploitation-of-ptc-windchill-vulnerability-cve-2026/) targets internet-exposed product lifecycle systems where stolen design data carries competitive value.
