The COLDCARD hardware-wallet RNG flaw explains the $88.6 million Bitcoin theft from 4,585 wallets in three automated sweeps. A faulty check in firmware makes the random number generator fall back to a deterministic algorithm seeded with observable device values, so attackers could reconstruct seeds offline and match derived addresses against the blockchain. Updating firmware does not repair an exposed seed; affected users must generate and migrate to a new one.
