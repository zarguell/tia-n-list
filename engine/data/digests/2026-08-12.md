Zoom's "Zoomsday" zero-click RCE is the day's defining story. [CVE-2026-53413](stories/zoom-zero-click-zoomsday-flaw-lets-meeting-participants-execute/), a CVSS 8.3 flaw in the Zoom Client annotation engine, lets a meeting participant execute code on another attendee's device with no interaction beyond joining the call, so fleet-wide updates are the only mitigation.

Microsoft shipped one of its largest Patch Tuesday releases on record, [roughly 400 CVEs including three zero-days](stories/microsoft-patches-398-windows-flaws-including-actively-exploited/). Priority is CVE-2026-68820, the WinSock [AFD.sys privilege escalation flaw Lazarus exploits for SYSTEM](stories/windows-afd-sys-zero-day-exploited-by-lazarus-hackers-to-gain-sy/), plus RCEs in QUIC, DNS Server, and [Outlook via malicious Office files](stories/microsoft-outlook-rce-flaw-lets-attackers-execute-code-via-malic/). Microsoft's AI-assisted discovery pipeline [expects volumes like this to keep coming](stories/microsoft-expects-more-windows-security-updates-from-ai-discover/), making patch triage the binding constraint.

Perimeter appliances kept taking fire. [Cisco patched an ASA/FTD SSL VPN zero-day](stories/cisco-patches-firewall-zero-day-exploited-for-dos-attacks/) that lets unauthenticated attackers crash firewalls with a crafted HTTP request; CISA added it to KEV on August 11, federal deadline August 14. [Ivanti EPM](stories/ivanti-epm-update-patches-remotely-exploitable-flaws/) and the [ICS Patch Tuesday roundup](stories/ics-patch-tuesday-vulnerabilities-fixed-by-siemens-schneider-pho/) from Siemens, Schneider, and Phoenix Contact fill out the appliance queue.

CERT-UA tied a [trojanized WireGuard client delivered through fake IT job interviews](stories/sandworm-fake-job-interviews-push-trojanized-wireguard-vpn-to-in/) to a Sandworm-adjacent cluster targeting system administrators, while a [ClickFix chain ends in CNCMachineRMS](stories/one-clickfix-lure-can-give-hackers-a-persistent-remote-shell-thr/), an undocumented no-import-table implant. The [Kimwolf botnet rebuilt itself](stories/kimwolf-botnet-rebuilt-to-survive-takedowns-researchers-say/) around HTTP/2 floods with full Chrome fingerprints, and [CopyEscape](stories/docker-copyescape-vulnerability-enables-host-file-overwrite-and/) lets a malicious container overwrite host files via docker cp. The Unsafe group claimed [Presentations.AI](stories/unsafe-ransomware-group-compromises-presentations-ai/), and DEF CON attendees ran an [evil twin phishing attack on in-flight Wi-Fi](stories/def-con-attendees-allegedly-jam-plane-wi-fi-launch-evil-twin-phi/). Watchlist: verify Zoom patching, deploy the AFD.sys fix, audit internet-facing ASA/FTD, distrust docker cp from untrusted containers, and treat unsolicited interview VPN installers as malicious.
