The password spray campaign against Azure CLI is the day's defining story. Huntress logged 81 million login attempts across twelve days against Microsoft 365 tenants through the deprecated OAuth ROPC flow, which mints tokens without an interactive MFA prompt; 78 accounts across 64 organizations were compromised, and eight victims had no MFA at all. Policies that only cover browser sign-ins do not see this traffic, so ROPC usage and conditional access coverage of OAuth flows need an explicit audit. The OT story carries the public safety risk. Canada's CSE disclosed that NoName gained access to a Quebec town's water treatment plant, including the ability to manipulate control pumps and chlorine dosing. The shift from DDoS to ICS manipulation is the escalation that matters for every utility with remote access into OT, and the fix set is standard: segmentation, MFA on HMI and SCADA access, and review of remote entry points.
