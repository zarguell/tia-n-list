Today adds eight new items led by offline benefits systems in Britain, a confirmed password manager compromise and an intrusion into a U.S. Air Force cloud platform, plus a news agency outage, live AI hacking campaigns, a pre-auth Linux kernel flaw, a medical breach notice and signed installer abuse, with two updates on Drupal and supply chain installers and a revisit on exploited geospatial flaws added to the federal catalog.

[NEW] Britain's Department for Work and Pensions took benefits systems offline on November 27 after identifying a cyber incident, and pension and benefits services were still down on November 28, in the [British DWP outage](stories/british-government-takes-services-offline-following-cyber-incide/). Claimants cannot sign in to Universal Credit accounts, apply online for Personal Independence Payment or the State Pension, or use Tell Us Once to report a death, though the department says regular benefit and pension payments will be paid as normal. DWP has not disclosed the incident type, said whether personal data was accessed, or given a restoration time, and it is investigating with the National Cyber Security Centre and the Government Cyber Coordination Centre.

[NEW] pCloud confirmed a cyberattack against pCloud Pass after customers reported new-device alerts for an unfamiliar device labeled "pd" at 45.131.244.7 in Luxembourg, in the [pCloud Pass hack](stories/pcloud-confirms-password-manager-hack-after-suspicious-login-ale/). Some alerts arrived despite two-factor authentication while other recipients had never used pCloud Pass, and the reporting establishes exposed email addresses and login attempts without confirming theft of passwords or vault contents. The company says the incident was quickly contained and limited to Pass, with Cloud Storage unaffected. Users should review active sessions and rotate reused passwords while pCloud explains the device label, the Luxembourg address, and the initial access vector.

[NEW] Reuters reported on November 27 that hackers breached Cloud One, the US Air Force flagship cloud platform, in the [Cloud One breach](stories/hackers-breach-us-air-force-cloud-one-platform-putting-military/). The intrusion surfaced during upgrade work within the previous month, and the response paused application migrations, blocked software updates, and revoked some administrator rights, with US Cyber Command called in to assess the impact. The attacker, the entry point, the full scope, and whether data was stolen remain unconfirmed, which matters because the platform hosts Defense Department applications supporting operations, personnel, logistics, and munitions tracking across 210 worldwide locations.

[REVISIT] Exploitation pressure on GeoServer hardened into catalog action as CISA added CVE-2026-120705 and CVE-2026-129243 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 27, in the [GeoServer exploitation wave](stories/hackers-exploit-two-geoserver-flaws-as-researchers-warn-of-unaut/). watchTowr reported active error-based probing through the unauthenticated WFS flaws while Eye Security chained five issues from XML injection through forged sessions to code execution via SQLite JDBC load_extension on default builds of 3.0.1 and 2.28.5, with fixed releases 3.0.2, 2.28.6, and 2.27.7. Eye Security counts more than 3,000 internet-accessible instances, so operators should patch and hunt stored-query creation or invocation under wfs/query paths. Last covered November 26.

[NEW] The open-source AI hacking framework ARTEX is now documented in live campaigns by state-linked groups, in the [ARTEX campaign assessment](stories/ai-hacking-tool-artex-spreads-as-its-code-is-copied-everywhere/). CrowdStrike found it in a late-September to early-October campaign against South Korean financial organizations using DeepSeek v4.1-flash plus GLM-5.3 and Grok 4.6, and Microsoft tied ARTEX-driven activity to the Storm-0940 ConnectWise supply-chain compromise and Storm-3358 operations alongside WISP, vshell, and AdaptixC2. Planner and worker agents drive Nmap, curl, and follow-on tooling from reconnaissance through exfiltration, and more than 1,000 public consoles plus over 1,000 copied repositories keep it circulating after the original repository was removed on October 8. Defenders should keep affected ConnectWise application connections disabled, inventory listed application IDs including Cloud Backup 506e08f9-6e9a-4da4-9029-a09903644ae, and hunt Entra sign-in anomalies plus CloudKeyVault secret-read bursts.

[NEW] Agence France-Presse took its AFP Forum client platform offline after detecting an intrusion around 01:30 Paris time overnight into November 26, in the [AFP Forum outage](stories/afp-hit-by-cyberattack-afp-forum-content-platform-taken-offline/). The shutdown disrupted the global distribution of dispatches, photographs, and video to media subscribers, with alternative delivery arranged and the platform still showing a maintenance notice on November 27. AFP has not named an actor or entry point and has not said whether sensitive information was accessed, with external experts assisting the investigation.

[UPDATE] Drupal admins face a second patch trigger alongside the exploited installer RCE: a November 28 advisory SA-CORE-2026-019 for CVE-2026-136716, an access bypass scored 21/25 that is reported as exploited in the wild and lets attackers hijack sites, in the [Drupal core flaws](stories/drupal-core-rce-cve-2026-132165-and-gadget-chain-cve-2026-132166/).

[UPDATE] The SoftwareOK supply-chain incident gained scoping detail with affected builds now pinned to Q-Dir 12.83 through 12.85 and DesktopOK 12.82 through 12.85, in the [SoftwareOK installer tampering](stories/software-supply-chain-attack-via-the-official-softwareok-website/). Both direct downloads and in-program updates can deliver the Python-based remote access trojan, which persists under C:\ProgramData\Microsoft\DeviceSync\b2 and communicates with 8.210.69.51:9876.

[NEW] Linux administrators face a pre-authentication root path in the in-kernel NFS server under CVE-2026-99362, CVSS 9.8, in the [Linux nfsd flaw](stories/cve-2026-99362-pre-auth-root-in-linux-nfsd/). A single TCP client can shrink session reply buffers and overflow the heap with a crafted COMPOUND tag, corrupting kernel memory before authentication whenever NFSv4.1 or NFSv4.2 listens on port 2049 outside loopback, a condition present since Linux 3.16. Fixed kernels include 5.10.275, 5.15.226, 6.1.193, 6.6.163, 6.12.117, 6.18.61, 7.3.2, and 7.4-rc1, with Debian carrying 6.12.117-1~deb12u1 while Ubuntu had no packaged fix as of November 25. No public exploit or in-the-wild use was reported, so patch and reboot now, or restrict TCP port 2049 to trusted storage networks and stop unneeded nfs-server instances meanwhile.

[NEW] Boston Scientific is notifying 117,789 people after voice phishing gave an attacker access to cloud infrastructure, in the [Boston Scientific breach](stories/boston-scientific-data-breach-exposes-personal-medical-info/). The company learned of unauthorized access on May 12 and determined on November 5 that a breach had occurred, exposing personal and medical information while remote-monitoring, connected-device, and clinical workflow data stayed out of scope. Manufacturing, ordering, distribution, and patient services were not disrupted.

[NEW] eSentire documented a Microsoft-signed installer named Snipping-Tool.exe served from snipp.codes-team[.]com that delivers the Vinci remote access trojan, in the [Vinci RAT delivery](stories/microsoft-store-app-found-delivering-vinci-rat-capable-of-remote/). The valid Microsoft signature lets the payload inherit the trust users and allow-lists extend to genuine tooling inside the Store ecosystem, arriving without the usual malicious attachment or sideloaded binary. Hunt proxy and DNS logs for the domain and inspect the signature chain on any snipping utility not pulled from the genuine vendor page.
