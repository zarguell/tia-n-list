[REVISIT] Singapore's Cyber Security Agency confirmed active exploitation of the NGINX2Root lead flaw on November 26, in the [NGINX HTTP/2 integer underflow](stories/cve-2026-123007-critical-nginx-http-2-integer-underflow-leads-to/). CVE-2026-123007 is an HTTP/2 flow-control underflow rated 9.2 under CVSS v4 that refunds too much send credit and turns a later copy into remote code execution with worker privileges, and both Rapid7 and Wiz reproduced code execution against NGINX 1.31.8 in lab conditions. Fixed builds are 1.30.7 and 1.31.9 with Plus fixes in the 37.x branches, and disabling HTTP/2 on affected listeners is the interim measure while Censys counts 74.2 million web properties on affected lines.

[REVISIT] The refrigeration controller campaign now has named KEV defects and a fourth Danfoss advisory, in the [refrigeration hacks](stories/us-agencies-warn-of-refrigeration-hacks-amid-suspected-attacks-o/). CISA added CVE-2025-41450, CVE-2023-25915, and CVE-2023-25914 in Danfoss AK-SM 800A controllers to KEV on November 25, and a November 26 CISA industrial notice covers CVE-2026-129713, a CVSS 9.8 unauthenticated stack buffer overflow in the same 8xxA line. The documented damage stands at about 700 French supermarkets pushed to 50 degrees Celsius on October 11, a second wave over 100 stores across seven more countries on November 2, blood-service cold rooms driven toward 110 degrees before staff intervened, and suspected late-August freezer failures at roughly six US military commissaries that France attributes to the IRGC unit Shahid Kaveh.

[NEW] Treasury's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network took systems offline after a cybersecurity incident with direct impact on Bank Secrecy Act filing, in the [FinCEN intrusion](stories/treasury-s-fincen-takes-systems-offline-after-cybersecurity-inci/). FinCEN disclosed unauthorized access to a limited subset of systems on November 25 without naming the entry point, the dwell time, or whether data was taken, while batch filers report failed BSA submissions starting about 17:45 ET on November 18. Order FIN-2026-R002 moved deadlines for affected Secure Data Transfer Mode reports and beneficial ownership filings in the November 18 to December 29 window out to December 30, and filers who refiled through the portal were told to switch the filing type from Amended to Initial to avoid duplicates.

[NEW] An attacker used a compromised employee GitHub account to push malicious code into backend services and pull down customer data, in the [1Password breach](stories/1password-says-a-hacker-used-an-employee-s-github-account-to-tak/). 1Password says the intrusion began around November 19, reached private repositories and the CI/CD pipeline, and exposed names, email addresses, account and user identifiers, creation and last sign-in times, public keys, and SRP parameters and tokens from invitation, provisioning, and recovery emails. The company says vault contents and their protecting keys were not accessed, lengthened emailed codes from six digits to 10 alphanumeric characters, and engaged outside experts while warning customers to expect targeted phishing built on the stolen contact data.

[NEW] Attackers are probing two GeoServer flaws that chain toward unauthenticated remote code execution, in the [GeoServer exploitation](stories/hackers-exploit-two-geoserver-flaws-as-researchers-warn-of-unaut/). watchTowr reports exploitation attempts against CVE-2026-120705, an unauthenticated WFS XML injection that reads local files or fetches attacker URLs, and CVE-2026-129243, an 8.8 dispatcher bypass that allows anonymous stored-query creation and deletion. Rapid7 researcher Jonah Burgess described a five-part chain adding path disclosure, remember-me cookie forgery with the default geoserver key, and JDBC deserialization for code execution, with Eye Security counting more than 3,000 internet-facing instances and fixes in 3.0.2, 2.28.6, and 2.27.7.

[NEW] Microsoft Threat Intelligence published a November 25 assessment of ARTEX, the AI penetration testing framework released in July, in the [ARTEX defenders guide](stories/what-defenders-need-to-know-about-artex-the-agentic-ai-framework/). Microsoft observes state-sponsored and financially motivated actors including Storm-0940, Storm-3358, Onyx Sleet, Pearl Sleet, Crystal Typhoon, and Salt Typhoon using it for reconnaissance, credential attacks, and web exploitation, while Unit 42 counted 609 exposed ARTEX hosts in October and researchers report more than 1,600 exposed consoles in November with the console on port 8787 and a trojanized Docker image in circulation. Detection has to rest on behavior such as one-to-14 request bursts with 25 to 31 second pauses and self-labeling pentest task counters, since forks and rebrands defeat file signatures and shared tooling is a weak attribution signal.

[REVISIT] Dragos tracked roughly two million prompts carrying canary URLs, DNS names, and AWS keys through AI routers and marketplaces from July to November and found providers reading or forwarding customer traffic, in the [Dragos canary-token study](stories/dragos-canary-token-study-tracks-where-two-million-ai-prompts-le/). Prompts marked Zero Data Retention were still fetched or forwarded, including secret URLs accessed from a Chinese AI lab system, and seeded keys were used for tens of thousands of AWS calls spanning S3 listing, Secrets Manager, Parameter Store, Bedrock enablement, and IAM user creation. OpenRouter removed Together AI, Phala, and Novita, pulled Zero Data Retention eligibility from 125 endpoints, and notified affected customers, while a companion honeypot showed a median 37 minutes from key theft to first use.

[NEW] Jeremiah Fowler's November 24 report describes an unprotected repository holding about 2 billion credential records across 8,471 plaintext folders, in the [open credential database](stories/open-database-exposed-roughly-2-billion-credential-records-for-m/). The set mixes passwords with browser cookies, session tokens, autofill data, device details, and wallet material, including 608 million Gmail records, 114.2 million Google service records, 88 million Facebook records, 61.4 million .gov entries, and per-victim malware output folders, with no owner identified. Because live session tokens allow replay without guessing a password, rotation alone is inadequate until the originating infostealer hosts are isolated and rebuilt, sessions revoked, and MFA moved to an authenticator app or security key.

[NEW] Socket found 13 malicious NuGet packages in 28 versions across four publisher accounts aimed at Windows .NET developers through fake take-home assessments, in the [malicious NuGet packages](stories/13-malicious-nuget-packages-turn-fake-net-job-assessments-into-m/). Most posed as WinUI 3 and WPF button controls, a build analyzer dropped OskSupport.dll beside a copied osk.exe for side-loading, and WinUI.Button 1.0.0 fetched a payload through the GitHub contents API with about 160 downloads before removal. Restoring a package alone does not prove compromise, and hunts should center on restore logs with repository-local sources, dotnet.exe under MSBuildAnalyzer.dll, and GitHub contents traffic with MyUpdater and CppHttp user agents.

[NEW] HPE advisory HPESBNW05186 lists 23 CVEs in AirWave 8.3.0.0 through 8.3.0.7 with 8.3.0.8 as the fix, in the [HPE AirWave patches](stories/hpe-airwave-8-3-0-8-fixes-23-flaws-including-cve-2026-133973-rce/). Three are Critical at 9.8: CVE-2026-133971 and CVE-2026-133972, web and API authentication bypasses, and CVE-2026-133973, unauthenticated command injection in the web management interface, with HPE stating no known exploit code at release. Until patched, HPE's workaround isolates CLI and web management on a dedicated layer 2 segment with layer 3 filtering, and accounting logs should be reviewed for administrative sessions from unexpected networks since an attacker reaching the management plane needs no second entry point.

[NEW] VirtueMart RCE CVE-2026-133335 is exploited in the wild per the Joomla CNA record and fixed in 4.8.10, in the [VirtueMart exploitation](stories/virtuemart-rce-cve-2026-133335-actively-exploited-patched-in-4-8/). The flaw is an unauthenticated local file inclusion in the storefront controller loader affecting 4.0.0 through 4.8.7, scored 9.2 with ATTACKED maturity, where one request can run PHP as the web user and read configuration.php with its database credentials and order data. A forum user reported an unauthenticated RCE in 4.6.4 on September 8 and confirmed it in 4.8.2 on September 12, more than two months before the November 19 fix, so stores should install the full 4.8.10 package rather than stopping at 4.8.8 and reconcile PayPal Checkout orders against captures.

[NEW] Okta Threat Intelligence assesses the vishing cluster O-UNC-053 very likely shifted its extortion brand from ShinyHunters to INCEPTION, in the [Okta INCEPTION report](stories/okta-links-shinyhunters-associated-vishing-group-to-new-inceptio/).
