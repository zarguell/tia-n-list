[NEW] The day's defining development is a break in confidential computing, detailed in the [Mior and Skyfall attacks](stories/mior-and-skyfall-attacks-let-rogue-cloud-hosts-steal-secrets-fro/). Mior (CVE-2026-0462, CVSS 4.0 5.9, ETH Zurich, ACM CCS 2026) lets a malicious hypervisor with BIOS control and a PCIe device read and modify SEV-SNP guest memory and forge signed attestation reports via uninitialized RMP entries on MMIO redirect, tested on EPYC 9135 (Zen 5) with Zen 3 and 4 considered affected, with SEV firmware dated August 12 and platform initialization fixes due December 2026. Skyfall (CVE-2026-40671, CVSS 4.9, plus EDK II CVE-2026-18448, CVSS 5.6, ETH Zurich and Google, IEEE S and P 2027) leaks guest register state and disk encryption keys through Transient Scheduler Attacks side channels from incomplete VMRUN error cleanup, tested on EPYC 7313, 9124 and 9334, with microcode dated August 12 and embedded fixes running into April 2027. Only SEV-SNP is affected, and tenants of confidential VMs should apply AMD microcode and firmware, verify SNP attestation TCB values, and apply the TSA mitigations from AMD-SB-7029, since a rogue host operator is now in a position to read secrets and fake the proof the guest is intact.

[NEW] US aviation stopped nationwide Thursday in the [FAA ground stop](stories/faa-ground-stop-issued-due-to-extraordinary-circumstances/). The FAA halted all departures for more than four hours starting November 13 after a telecommunications outage hit all 22 air route traffic control centers, disrupting roughly 7,000 flights with about 4,042 airborne at issuance, and lifted the order at 8:15 pm ET in what sources call only the third nationwide moratorium after September 11, 2001 and January 11, 2023. Officials report no evidence of a cyberattack, with Homeland Security, NSA, DoD, CISA and the FBI involved and the FBI examining whether an L3Harris private-network software update caused reboots, while Transportation blames legacy switches and routers. Delays were expected for days, so treat this as infrastructure fragility with security-process implications rather than an intrusion, and watch whether the update hypothesis is confirmed.

[NEW] A defense supply-chain intrusion grounded military drones in the [Quantum Systems attack](stories/cyberattack-on-quantum-systems-disables-bundeswehr-drones-via-ma/). Quantum Systems confirmed a late-October intrusion through a poisoned QBase ground-control and mission-planning update pushed from a compromised distribution server, so one artifact reached every customer at once, with the Vector reconnaissance drone in German service as Falke among affected missions and the unscheduled update itself raising the alarm. [NEW] Carrier signaling is the interception point in [the SilentAir campaign](stories/silentair-hijacks-asian-telecom-networks-to-intercept-sms-2fa-co/). [NEW] MSP trust is the attack path in [the SCKit worm](stories/sckit-abuses-screenconnect-to-deploy-remote-access-and-target-te/). Microsoft Threat Intelligence and ConnectWise describe a November 11 campaign against cloud-hosted ScreenConnect instances where commands through legitimate remote-support infrastructure silently install an identical client pointed at attacker infrastructure, followed by a batch launcher, a PowerShell decoder keyed to device material, scheduled-task and Run-key persistence, and RelayTrap positioned to serve payloads to connecting technicians. The worm then targets technician workstations and rides the victim's own console out to every downstream device that technician administers, so deleting one rogue client clears neither persistence nor exposed sessions. ConnectWise is investigating an RMM plus extension flaw in versions 1.0.31 and 1.0.32 accepting an empty access key when none is configured, so rotate session and access credentials, audit extension versions and key configuration, and hunt RelayTrap with the documented persistence until ConnectWise publishes a CVE and fixed builds.

[NEW] The hypervisor fell without a hypervisor bug in the [BlackNevas engagement](stories/ransomware-at-the-hypervisor-layer-how-attackers-use-esxi-and-vc/). Kaspersky GERT reconstructs a ten-day ransomware operation that destroyed four ESXi hosts, every virtual machine on them, both vCenter Server Appliances and all connected backup infrastructure using legitimate VMware management interfaces, with Babuk-derived ESXi encryptors reflecting the September 2021 source leak reused by at least ten families. Access came through a PRTG Network Monitor platform with broad host reach and a root password reused across nodes, giving SSH to every host, with backups destroyed first through the same plane and guest EDR blind to hypervisor-side VMDK changes. Isolate vCenter and ESXi management interfaces, restrict SSH to jump hosts, give every host unique root credentials, strip reusable credentials from monitoring platforms, and treat backup infrastructure as a separate security boundary verified unreachable from the virtualization plane.
