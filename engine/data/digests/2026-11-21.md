[NEW] The FBI is investigating an apparent intrusion involving the National Crime Information Center, the federal database behind arrest warrants and background checks, in [the NCIC breach probe](stories/fbi-investigates-apparent-breach-of-national-crime-information-c/). CNN first reported the investigation on November 21 with Reuters carrying the same account, and neither the method, the actor, nor any victim count has been disclosed. The sensitivity comes from what the system holds, with warrants and criminal history data potentially exposed, so watch for an FBI statement on scope and whether records were read, altered, or copied.

[NEW] Amazon Threat Intelligence found an exposed Cairn control server showing Chinese-linked operators using autonomous AI agents to discover and exploit a zero-day, in [the Cairn AI intrusion](stories/amazon-says-chinese-hackers-used-cairn-ai-tool-to-discover-appar/). Prompts were written in Simplified Chinese and the agents, given only IP addresses and a goal of obtaining shell access, planned and adjusted attacks on their own, eventually finding an unknown path traversal in the MONAI Label medical imaging app, tracked as CVE-2026-133968, that gave full control of a university server running a release through 0.8.5 with authentication disabled by default. Unit 42 ties the same tooling to roughly 25 operations against at least 19 organizations with over a million records taken at one victim, and Google reports a Cairn-linked intrusion in the US aerospace sector, so hunt for exposed AI agent consoles and patch or isolate MONAI Label instances.

[REVISIT] CISA added the Apache Roller deserialization flaw CVE-2026-129448 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 20, in [the Roller RCE story](stories/apache-roller-pre-auth-rce-cve-2026-82384-cvss-9-8/). The CVSS 9.8 bug lets unauthenticated attackers execute code through the XML-RPC endpoint on Roller 4.0.0 through 6.1.5, which Apache confirmed under active exploitation on November 17. Upgrade to 6.1.6 or disable the extension types and remove the xmlrpc servlet mapping, since blocking XML-RPC in the admin UI alone does not stop exploitation.

[UPDATE] The n0n extortion gang reportedly leaked JPMorgan Chase, Visa, and Mastercard customer data stolen from third-party providers after its 48-hour deadline expired, in [the card-brand vendor case](stories/jpmorgan-says-third-party-vendors-were-targeted-in-cyberattack/).

[NEW] Federal prosecutors charged ransomwarehelp.com co-founder and CTO Juan Ricardo Palacio Escobar with three wire fraud counts, in [the RansomwareHelp indictment](stories/cto-of-ransomwarehelp-indicted-for-wire-fraud/). An FBI undercover operation played both a Rhode Island victim and the demanding gang, finding the firm charged a $2,750 assessment and $13,260 for recovery while paying about $6,003 in bitcoin for the decryption key and presenting the work as its own, with a Kansas business billed $720,000 in similar fashion. The case puts recovery firms that secretly pay ransoms while claiming cryptographic expertise on notice.

[NEW] Sean Cairncross is out as National Cyber Director after just over a year, in [the cyber leadership shakeup](stories/cyber-director-cairncross-out-as-white-house-resets-cyber-leader/). Accounts split between firing and resignation, with reporting that chief of staff Stephen Miller long wanted him out, and Pentagon CIO Kirsten Davies steps in as acting director pending Senate confirmation. The change lands amid a run of disruptive outages that officials say show no sign of malicious foreign activity, with CISA nominee Michael LoSapio testifying the same day.

[NEW] Bell Canada confirmed an agentic intruder breached its systems on November 17 and stole enterprise customer data, in [the Bell breach](stories/bell-canada-says-agentic-hacker-stole-enterprise-customer-data/). The attacker reached a pre-production Maximo environment used to order and provision circuits plus additional systems, taking business contact details, ticket titles, and some network element IP addresses, but no credentials, highly sensitive personal information, or file attachments. The provisioning data maps circuits to the people who manage them, which is useful material for targeted follow-on contact, so review Maximo access paths and separation between order management and production circuit data.

[NEW] UK teams pulled thousands of refrigeration controllers off the public internet in a preemptive operation, in [the refrigeration controller story](stories/ncsc-operation-disconnects-thousands-of-refrigeration-controller/). Omega Solutions found a weak-password flaw in Resource Data Management controllers with Pen Test Partners validating further issues under advisory RDM-SA-2026-001, while the related Danfoss controllers hit in France on October 11 and 12 relied on a date-seeded backdoor account. A single RDM data manager can control dozens of fridges across multiple sites, so verify no controller management interface is internet reachable, move remote access to VPN or private APN, and apply the latest firmware.

[NEW] Recorded Future links the October 11 and 12 attack that changed defrost settings at more than 700 French retail locations, causing fires and tens of millions in food losses, to the ARTEX offensive AI framework, in [the industrial AI attack analysis](stories/ai-joins-the-industrial-attack-workflow/). France attributed the incident on November 9 to Shahid Kaveh, a unit of Iran's IRGC Cyber Electronic Command tracked by the authors as GreenJuliett, whose infrastructure kept scanning internet-facing PLCs through at least October 30. The write-up frames tools like ARTEX as filling an operator role with humans still in control, and further GreenJuliett scanning is the thing to watch.

[REVISIT] Europol and partners in nine countries seized 235 servers and 257 domains behind Gootloader, CastleRAT, and StealC and recovered 6.4 million victim datasets, in [the Endgame takedown](stories/police-dismantle-gootloader-castlerat-stealc-malware-infrastruct/). An FBI FLASH summarized on November 20 puts the Gootloader WordPress footprint above 1,000 infected sites feeding access tied to more than $28 million in ransom payments, and HaveIBeenPwned received 180,000 emails and 893,000 passwords from the seized infrastructure. Rotate exposed credentials with MFA and expect displacement rather than disappearance, since earlier Endgame phases hit overlapping services without ending them.

[NEW] ThreatFabric describes Bazuka, found in late October during an NFC card relay investigation, as a full Android fraud framework rather than a single trojan, in [the Bazuka report](stories/bazuka-the-fraud-framework-that-can-do-it-all/). A dropper and downloader feed a core with 23 modules for overlays, keylogging, 2FA and SMS interception, remote device control, crypto address swapping, and contactless card interaction, fronted by a panel that uses large language models to manage victims at scale. Published indicators include a TikTok 18-plus themed dropper and control infrastructure under brokerok[.]org and gargona[.]org, with no victim count or attribution so far.

[NEW] A Reuters investigation found Meta's Muse personal agent can access private Signal texts without using its official Connector, in [the Muse privacy story](stories/meta-s-muse-ai-agent-accesses-private-signal-messages-without-us/). Reproduction with Signal and AI Now Institute staff showed Android notification permission and macOS accessibility and screenshot permissions bypassing the Connector while iPhones resisted, and TIME found hourly dossiers covering 4 million users including people who never installed Muse. Revoke Muse's notification and accessibility grants, disconnect its connectors where possible, and inspect the audit trail for context no user supplied.

[NEW] SonicWall told customers to retire Generation 6 firewalls immediately and stop leaving them at the internet edge, in [the Gen 6 warning](stories/sonicwall-warns-gen-6-firewalls-won-t-get-security-patches-even/). The company says automation and AI now help attackers find and exploit unsupported edge devices faster, and there is no patch path even for critical flaws, so replacement is the only remedy. Inventory Gen 6 units and move services off them, starting with anything exposing a management interface to public networks.

[NEW] Silent Push exposed a fake Lynchburg, Virginia construction firm called The Great House run by North Korean remote workers seeking contracts rather than delivering malware, in [the fake firm finding](stories/silent-push-exposes-north-korean-fake-construction-company/). The domain thegreathouse[.]us was registered with an address from a known 1,400-email North Korean IT worker cluster, and a second video call showed real-time deepfake layering before the feed cut. The shift pushes deepfake interview tradecraft into procurement and vendor onboarding, so treat camera presence as insufficient verification and watch for more domains on the same email cluster.

[NEW] US prosecutors charged Russian national Igor Andreevich D'iu with acting as an unregistered foreign agent, stalking, and identity document fraud over surveillance of employees of a tech and defense company whose software Ukraine uses, in [the Palantir spying case](stories/russian-charged-with-spying-on-palantir-employees-for-moscow/). The indictment says he was recruited, directed, and funded by Moscow over Telegram, entered illegally from Mexico after traveling via Istanbul, and photographed laptop screens in coffee shops near the company's Palo Alto offices while compiling dossiers on staff and their families. Watch for court filings on how the dossiers were built and whether any company systems were accessed.
