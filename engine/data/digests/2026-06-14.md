Operation Highland is the day's defining story: Sygnia details how the Chinese Velvet Ant cluster maintained undetected access to an air-gapped critical infrastructure network for a decade, from 2016 to 2026, by hijacking the organization's entire authentication stack. [The campaign](stories/operation-highland-velvet-ant-chinese-apt-hijacks-authentication/) replaced pam_unix.so with nine distinct backdoored variants and trojanized OpenSSH components to harvest credentials and log every administrative session, while a modified Nginx server proxied crafted POST requests through fcgiwrap to bridge the isolated network. Password changes and session terminations changed nothing, and remediation required a testing lab just to validate replacement binaries without locking out legitimate administrators.
