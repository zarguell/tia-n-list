One missing MFA control turned a credential-spraying attempt into a near-complete [Akira ransomware intrusion](stories/one-missing-mfa-control-lets-credential-spray-become-full-akira/): an exposed VPN with valid credentials, then domain-wide reconnaissance, data theft, and encryption. A second incident shows the same affiliates [rebooting Windows into Safe Mode with Networking](stories/akira-ransomware-reboots-windows-into-safe-mode-to-disable-edr-a/) to disable EDR and Defender; encryption stalled on memory errors, but credentials and data were already exfiltrated. MFA-less remote access is the choke point.

The extortion pipeline stayed busy. Clop posted five victims, including fintech [Fiserv](stories/clop-ransomware-strikes-financial-tech-leader-fiserv/) and health technology [Philips](stories/clop-ransomware-strikes-health-technology-leader-philips/), and MajinaHanashi listed five more, including an Italian [healthcare provider holding 135,000 files](stories/majinahanashi-ransomware-strikes-cda-in-italy/). [FulcrumSec dumped the rest of Novo Nordisk's AI/ML stack](stories/more-novo-nordisk-data-dumped-by-fulcrumsec/), 30 models and 70 datasets, and ransomware hit doors and HVAC at a [Winnipeg hospital](stories/ransomware-attack-disables-canadian-hospital-s-doors-hvac/). [Gunra follow-up reporting](stories/gunra-ransomware-uses-up-to-100-chacha20-threads-to-rapidly-encr/) adds a Linux encryptor running up to 100 ChaCha20 threads and describes [primary and disaster-recovery backups deleted before encryption](stories/gunra-targets-primary-and-disaster-recovery-backups-before-file/).

Platform exploitation continues. [City-Forum](stories/stealthy-city-forum-attacks-target-salesforce-and-servicenow-wit-2/) is the first observed in-the-wild abuse of Salesforce's guest UI-API surface and a lightly documented ServiceNow search endpoint; one tenant logged 560,000 guest enumeration events, and the fix is configuration, not patching. [VMware vCenter CVE-2026-59310](stories/critical-vmware-vcenter-vulnerability-in-attackers-crosshairs/), a CVSS 9.8 directory traversal, exploited since August 3, reaching 361 victim IPs in 47 countries via reverse_ssh backdoors. Adobe rated [ColdFusion APSB26-90](stories/adobe-coldfusion-critical-vulnerabilities-enable-arbitrary-code/) Priority 1, [Commerce CVE-2026-71362](stories/critical-adobe-commerce-flaw-lets-unauthenticated-attackers-esca/) was blocked by WAFs around disclosure, and [Phantom Stealer](stories/phishing-cracked-software-and-discord-links-spread-phantom-steal/) spread via phishing, cracked software, and Discord.

Watchlist: MFA on all remote-access surfaces, immutable backups given Gunra's deletion behavior, guest-visible data audits on Experience Cloud and ServiceNow portals, and patching of vCenter, ColdFusion, and Commerce.
