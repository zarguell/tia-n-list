Suspected Chinese-speaking hackers are actively exploiting CVE-2026-59310, a critical directory-traversal flaw in VMware vCenter Server's Syslog Server component (CVSS 9.8), writing malicious cron jobs to /etc/cron.d/ that download and execute payloads granting root access. From that foothold, the attackers steal Active Directory credentials, deploy Babuk-derived ransomware on ESXi hypervisors, and establish multiple backdoors. QUIRSO security researchers identified 361 unique victim IP addresses across 47 countries, with Germany, the United States, Turkey, Iran, and France most affected. A parallel campaign exploiting CVE-2026-59309, another vCenter RCE, appears to be operating simultaneously, possibly by the same or a related actor. VMware vCenter is ubiquitous in enterprise virtualization environments, and the combination of unauthenticated RCE, credential theft, and ransomware deployment makes this immediately exploitable at scale. Organizations running vCenter should verify they have applied the July 29 patches for both CVE-2026-59309 and CVE-2026-59310.
