The cPanel/WHM authentication bypass is the day's defining story. [CVE-2026-41940](stories/cpanel-whm-critical-authentication-bypass-cve-2026-41940-cvss-9/) carries a CVSS 9.8 rating and lets an unauthenticated attacker reach the hosting control panel on every supported version except the latest, and the fix is not delivered through normal update channels: administrators must run the upcp script manually. Namecheap blocked cPanel ports at the network level to protect customers. A hosting control panel is the key to every site and mailbox on the box, making this the highest-value mass target of the week.

The [GitHub RCE](stories/github-rce-cve-2026-3854-could-have-exposed-millions-of-private/) in the git push pipeline is the other critical disclosure. A single crafted push could have granted read and write access to private repositories on GitHub.com and Enterprise Server, and the fix shipped within two hours of Wiz's report. Separately, the LiteLLM SQL injection was exploited within 36 hours of disclosure, with attackers extracting API keys through the proxy's error-handling path. VECT 2.0 ransomware carries a nonce-handling flaw that corrupts files instead of encrypting them, so paying achieves nothing.

Two supply-chain threads deserve attention. The SAP npm campaign has passed 1,000 compromised repositories with a self-propagating strain that steals GitHub tokens from CI, and the Vercel breach shows the shadow OAuth risk of AI apps bridged to Google Workspace. Forescout counted 670 VNC servers exposing ICS and OT systems without authentication.

Watchlist: run the cPanel upcp fix, rotate CI tokens and audit SAP packages, inventory OAuth grants to AI apps, and take any internet-facing VNC or RDP off the edge.
