[NEW] CISA's newest KEV add is already being exploited, tracked in [the UnrealIRCd command-injection flaw](stories/cisa-adds-unrealircd-cve-2026-114713-to-kev-after-hot-patch-to-6/). CVE-2026-114713 (CVSS 8.6) lets any unauthenticated user on a network with two or more linked servers inject newlines through the labeled-response module and run commands with server authority, including KILL and GLINE, and maintainer Bram Matthys calls it the most critical UnrealIRCd issue in fifteen years. CISA listed it on November 13 with a federal remediation deadline of November 16, and the maintainer reports exploitation in progress, so the patch window effectively closes tomorrow. Operators who cannot restart can apply the no-downtime 6.2.7.1 hot-patch bundle covering 6.0.0 through 6.2.7 or a network-wide SPAMFILTER workaround, while end-of-life 5.x hosts get only a blacklist-module workaround, and defenders should hunt for unexpected GLINE and KILL activity plus unknown linked servers rather than relying on the score.

[REVISIT] Cursor has now confirmed how its marketplace was poisoned, detailed in [the trojanized Claude Code extension](stories/malicious-claude-code-vs-code-extension-installs-persistent-ssh/). An access key Cursor issued to Fireworks AI was exposed, and attackers used it to copy historical 2025 coding-agent traces from a read-only exchange bucket, where some transcripts held unredacted API credentials that opened further access inside Cursor. That foothold produced the tampered anthropic.claude-code 2.1.312 and 2.1.313 builds served for about eleven hours on October 29, carrying a 66-line JavaScript loader that adds an attacker SSH key and opens a reverse tunnel with platform startup persistence, and Microsoft estimates up to 5,000 developer systems received the build. Removing 2.1.313 from the marketplaces stops new installs but cleans nothing, so treat any host that ran it as compromised, hunt unauthorized authorized_keys entries and outbound tunnel processes, and rotate every secret that ever appeared in a coding-agent transcript.

[NEW] End-of-life D-Link routers are stacking unpatched pre-authentication flaws, in [the DIR-600L stack overflow](stories/d-link-dir-600l-firmware-2-17-2-17b02-critical-stack-buffer-over/) and [the DIR-850L hard-coded credentials](stories/d-link-dir-850l-hard-coded-console-credentials-cve-2026-129445/). CVE-2026-129353 scores a perfect 10.0 for an unauthenticated stack buffer overflow in the DIR-600L authentication handler on firmware 2.17 and 2.17b02, but the underlying artifact demonstrates only a child-process crash under emulation, so remote code execution stays alleged while denial of service is confirmed, and a sibling overflow rides along as CVE-2026-129354 with no fixed firmware in sight. The DIR-850L entry is blunter, with CVE-2026-129445 (CVSS 9.3) exposing hard-coded console credentials baked into firmware 1.20WWb03, which no patch can remove. Retire exposed units or isolate their management interfaces at the edge and hunt for oversized authentication.cgi parameters, since neither line has a vendor advisory or KEV entry.

[REVISIT] A remote kernel flaw found by a machine is still waiting on distribution plumbing, in [the Linux TCP use-after-free](stories/linux-kernel-tcp-flaw-with-9-8-cvss-score-affects-ubuntu-red-hat/). CVE-2026-98432 is a race in tcp_v4_syn_recv_sock reachable by an unauthenticated remote sender, scored 9.8 by the kernel team, and an unreleased internal Claude model demonstrated a lab chain from kernel-pointer leak to root in under an hour against multipath TCP listeners. Upstream fixed it October 15, but as of November 15 Ubuntu fixes sit in the -proposed pocket rather than default updates while Red Hat lists 5.9 against a machine-readable 8.1 and Amazon lists 7.0, so fleet owners cannot trust a single scanner verdict. Confirm the running kernel rather than the installed package, disable multipath TCP listeners where feasible, and treat unpatched internet-facing hosts as exposed.

[NEW] Japan's October surge has a million-person sequel in [the Osaka breach notice](stories/city-of-osaka-warns-of-data-breach-impacting-1-million-as-japan/). The city confirmed about one million of its 2.8 million residents are affected through the Arara Message email-delivery compromise, whose exposure runs near 77 million addresses across its customer base, and told residents to treat unexpected city mail as suspicious. The intrusion was at the vendor rather than municipal systems, but attackers holding a verified list of city-mail recipients can craft pretexting that passes casual checks. Watch whether follow-on reporting ties Osaka's tenant to tax, health-insurance or child-benefit correspondence, and expect the next disclosure to name another shared-platform customer rather than a new technique.

[NEW] Mercenary spyware has its first forensic confirmation on journalists' iPhones in [the Paragon Graphite findings](stories/citizen-lab-finds-paragon-graphite-spyware-used-in-zero-click-at/). Citizen Lab found with high confidence that Paragon's Graphite targeted two journalists through sophisticated zero-click iMessage attacks, with the ATTACKER1 iMessage account figuring in device logs. Apple blocked the underlying exploit in February's iOS 18.3.1 release, catalogued as CVE-2025-43200, though reported compromise windows run from January into early spring. The finding lands a year after WhatsApp notified roughly 90 accounts targeted with Paragon spyware, and it keeps newsroom iPhones inside the threat model for no-interaction compromise.
