[UPDATE] Tenable published a Brotli2Shell FAQ confirming public proofs of concept and a sprawling footprint across Node.js, .NET, Bun, Envoy, Python HTTP clients and PHP for Windows, PHP shipped rebuilt Windows binaries 8.2.35-1 through 8.5.12-1 bundling Brotli 1.2.1, and CyberScoop reported researchers still see no active exploitation even as Wiz warns 91 percent of cloud environments hold vulnerable resources, in the [Node.js Brotli decoder flaw](stories/node-js-brotli-decoder-vulnerability-cve-2026-111497/).

[UPDATE] CISA added both PHP2Shell flaws (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619) to its KEV catalog on October 26 and BleepingComputer confirmed the first successful in-the-wild remote code execution on a honeypot, with Perplexity researcher Adel Karimi reporting 35 shells across three honeypots, in the [PHP critical flaws](stories/php-warns-admins-of-two-critical-flaws-emergency-patches-coming/).

[NEW] Microsoft reports Storm-0940, a China-linked APT active since 2021, used the open source AI penetration testing framework Artex to automate its mid-October ConnectWise intrusion campaign, attempting more than 2,900 sign-ins per minute across 300 accounts and over 2,250 tenants through password spraying and OAuth application abuse. ConnectWise suspended Cloud Backup, SaaS Security and Cloud Management in response, while Huntress observed compromised ConnectWise application credentials reaching hundreds of Microsoft 365 tenants including more than 300 Entra tenants, in the [AI-driven ConnectWise intrusions](stories/china-apt-uses-ai-pentest-tool-artex-in-connectwise-attacks/).

[NEW] Sansec tracks a Magecart style campaign that has infected 953 online stores, mostly Magento, since July 2025 with a JavaScript loader hidden in the cms_block table and spread across five loader domains, the newest (deepveindrilling.com) hiding behind Cloudflare. The March 2026 PolyShell flaw (CVE-2026-48356), an unauthenticated REST API file upload in Magento and Adobe Commerce with no production patch beyond the 2.4.9 pre-release, injected 471 of those stores, in the [infected online stores](stories/953-online-stores-infected-in-long-lasting-malware-campaign/).

[NEW] Hackers accessed a University of Copenhagen test environment containing personal data for 525,000 people, including full names, Danish national identification numbers, phone numbers and email addresses, plus PIN codes for another 20,000 staff and students. The intrusion was detected October 20 and disclosed October 23, with the university reporting to the Danish Data Protection Authority and temporarily restricting campus physical access, in the [University of Copenhagen data breach](stories/university-of-copenhagen-data-breach-exposes-data-of-525-000-people/).

[NEW] Palo Alto confirmed two buffer overflows: CVE-2026-0310, a September 9 XML processing flaw letting unauthenticated attackers reach root code execution on PA-Series firewalls via management or dataplane interfaces, and CVE-2026-111497, the Brotli decoder overflow confirmed October 26 in PAN-OS 12.2, 12.1 and 11.2. No exploitation is reported for either, so restrict management interface exposure and upgrade to the fixed releases, in the [PAN-OS buffer overflows](stories/palo-alto-pan-os-buffer-overflow-lets-attackers-execute-arbitrar/).

[REVISIT] Eye Security sinkholed the abandoned update infrastructure of the Time4Popcorn fork, whose updater fetched unsigned executables over HTTP and ran them as SYSTEM on Windows and root on macOS, and observed 2.5 to 3 million Windows systems plus 250,000 to 350,000 macOS clients phoning home, including government and military networks. The flaw is tracked as CVE-2026-30612, and the researcher takeover removed the execution risk, though other abandoned updater domains may remain unclaimed, in the [abandoned Popcorn Time updater](stories/abandoned-popcorn-time-app-exposed-millions-of-device-to-rce-att/).

[NEW] Wiz Research reports the Gheist campaign has planted more than 13,500 branches across over 850 compromised GitHub tokens since September 15, using stolen tokens to create ci-sync branches and extract private repository secrets from Actions logs without direct repository access. No CVEs apply, so audit workflow runs and rotate tokens, in the [Gheist GitHub campaign](stories/gheist-campaign-automates-with-stolen-github-tokens-to-create-br/).

[NEW] Rapid7 disclosed 17 Zimbra Collaboration Suite flaws including CVE-2026-10632 and CVE-2026-93640, spanning cross-site scripting to root privilege escalation. Review the advisory batch and patch exposed collaboration hosts, in the [Zimbra flaws](stories/rapid7-discloses-17-zimbra-collaboration-suite-flaws/).

[NEW] The University of New Mexico Health System shut clinics and diverted ambulances on October 27 during an active cybersecurity incident, temporarily leaving the state without its only Level 1 trauma center across a network handling about 900,000 outpatient and 100,000 emergency visits a year. Employees reported on-screen messages claiming theft of student, employee and patient data while the FBI assists the investigation, though the health system has not confirmed exfiltration, in the [New Mexico hospital disruption](stories/cybersecurity-issue-forces-a-major-new-mexico-hospital-to-divert/).

[NEW] CERT-UA warns attackers are remotely hijacking Dahua IP cameras and DVRs in Ukraine through P2P access paths and weak authentication to pull live feeds. Audit Dahua P2P settings, disable unneeded remote access and enforce stronger credentials, in the [Dahua camera hijacking](stories/cert-ua-alerts-on-remote-hijack-of-dahua-ip-cameras-and-dvrs-in/).

[NEW] Huntress tracked an adversary in the middle token replay campaign against Microsoft 365 that produced 497 successful sign-ins across 97 accounts at 79 organizations between July 14 and September 22, replaying MFA established session tokens from German hosted blocks 193.26.115.221 and 193.26.115.199 within windows as short as four minutes. Revoke active tokens, enforce continuous access evaluation and hunt the 48 fake Entra device registrations, in the [Microsoft 365 token replay campaign](stories/aitm-token-replay-campaign-against-microsoft-365-tracked-by-hunt/).
