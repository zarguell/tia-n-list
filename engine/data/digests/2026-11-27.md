[NEW] SAP confirmed on November 26 that it found suspicious activity in its IT environment and took protective measures, in the [SAP GitHub intrusion](stories/sap-hit-by-suspected-cyberattack-hackers-reportedly-steal-github/). Internal communications place the activity in GitHub, and German reporting on the incident dubbed Project Granite says attackers stole employee authentication tokens and used them to copy and alter SAP source code. Handelsblatt reports that SAP restricted GitHub access, suspended automated development workflows, and replaced signing keys. SAP told SecurityWeek and BleepingComputer that its investigation found no evidence of unauthorized access to customer business data, and no report shows modified code reaching delivered products. The reported use of agentic AI remains an insider-sourced claim, and SAP has not confirmed the token theft or named the affected repositories.

[NEW] FIFA confirmed on November 26 that hackers breached its systems and accessed what it called a limited set of personal and business information, in the [FIFA breach disclosure](stories/fifa-says-hackers-accessed-limited-set-of-personal-and-business/). The organization gave no attack date, no categories or number of people affected, and did not answer follow-up questions. The Handsome Boy Hacking Club claimed responsibility, said it stole more than 12 GB of data, and published material on a cybercriminal forum, describing identity and immigration documents alongside financial, legal, and governance files, though those volume and content claims remain unverified. OffSet7 logged a leak-site countdown on November 20 and Escudo Digital reported the extortion listing on November 21, before FIFA confirmed.

[NEW] Researcher ek0ms savi0r published 4HTTP2SHELL, a four-request guest-to-command chain against Cacti 1.2.31, in the [Cacti command chain](stories/unpatched-cacti-zero-day-enables-rce-in-just-four-http-requests/). An anonymous guest rewrites a setting, a stacked UPDATE through the graph_view.php preview LIMIT clause alters poller_item.arg1, and graph_realtime.php reaches popen, with the demonstration running the web tier as root. Previdian independently verified the chain in a lab and tracks it as VULN-2026-0010. There is no CVE and no official patch in the reviewed material, so sites with guest access enabled should disable the guest user until a vendor fix or confirmed mitigation appears.

[NEW] Accenture APEX Labs found evidence that the five MiCollab flaws in Mitel advisory MISA-2026-0012 were exploited before the November 16 advisory, in the [MiCollab pre-advisory exploitation](stories/accenture-apex-labs-assesses-mitel-micollab-flaws-were-exploited/). In one observed attack a web shell landed on November 15, the actor gained privileged access to a MiCollab server, reached identity infrastructure quickly, and stole business information. The set spans CVE-2026-133275, CVE-2026-133303, CVE-2026-133311, CVE-2026-133323, and CVE-2026-133324, all added to CISA KEV on November 25. Upgrading to 10.3.0.18 alone is not enough because the separate patch must also be applied, and Mitel advises restricting external access to the conferencing component meanwhile.

[NEW] The Swiss National Cyber Security Centre confirmed five AI-agent-assisted cyberattacks, four of which succeeded, in the [Swiss AI-agent intrusions](stories/switzerland-confirms-ai-assisted-cyberattacks-breached-critical/). Three attacks targeted critical-infrastructure operators and two of those succeeded, described as the country's first confirmed cases of attackers using AI agents this way, with ARTEX and CyberStrikeAI named as examples. Elastic separately found five public agents appearing in intrusions with none enforcing authorization in code by default. The change is operational tempo and tool selection rather than a new exploit class, and the NCSC urges cutting internet exposure, patching critical flaws within 24 hours, and MFA or passkeys with behavioral monitoring.

[NEW] The North Korea-linked PolinRider campaign added a 58-line Python loader that hides in a utils package or conftest.py and resolves its command server through Ethereum transaction calldata, in the [PolinRider repository poisoning](stories/north-korea-linked-polinrider-poisons-over-7-600-github-reposito/). Apiiro counts 7,609 repositories under 2,330 accounts while SafeDep measured 375 repositories under 260 accounts on November 24, with commits rewritten to preserve the original author, message, and date. Cloning a poisoned repository and running the test suite, or opening the folder in VS Code, executes the loader, and later stages take environment secrets, browser passwords, cookies, and wallet data. Detection should rest on the blockchain query pattern and the import-time execution path rather than the rotating wallets, and suspect hosts must be treated as compromised without running the suite to confirm.

[NEW] AhnLab ASEC confirmed that some Q-Dir and DesktopOK installers distributed through the official SoftwareOK website were tampered with to carry malicious files, in the [SoftwareOK installer tampering](stories/software-supply-chain-attack-via-the-official-softwareok-website/). Malicious files appeared both in direct downloads from the official page and during the in-program update path, while the programs themselves ran normally. ASEC traces the files to the official distribution path rather than third-party mirrors, and GBHackers describes the payload as remote-control malware. No actor, CVE, or affected build list was given in the reviewed material.

[NEW] SOCRadar attributes the RedOps campaign to the Vietnam-sponsored WeaveLotus actor and dates it to August and September 2026, in the [RedOps espionage campaign](stories/redops-weavelotus-targets-southeast-asian-governments-for-south/). Five intrusions in Thailand and the Philippines yielded gigabytes of documents, plans, and credentials, while activity against more than 30 Indonesian government organizations stayed at reconnaissance. Entry came through GeoServer CVE-2024-36401, JasperReports default credentials, and osTicket CVE-2021-42235, all stale commodity weaknesses rather than novel exploits. Patching GeoServer to the fixed branches, moving osTicket to 1.15.4 or 1.14.8 or newer, and replacing JasperReports defaults close the reported paths.

[NEW] Bitdefender tracks a SYS010 campaign, active since the first malicious ad on October 16 with hundreds of ads, that marries malvertising to browser management, in the [SYS010 browser takeover](stories/sys010-malware-abuses-browser-management-to-spy-on-gmail-outlook/). A working wallpaper app enrolls the browser in attacker-controlled enterprise management and forces the Chrome Docs Helper 1.0.25 extension, which reads up to 50 recent Gmail or Outlook messages per task and permanently deletes selected mail while also stealing cookies and Meta business data. One Meta advertisement reached 6,275 EU users, and campaign traffic arrived from five continents. Victims should remove unexpected browser management and extensions, revoke sessions, and audit Meta ad permissions alongside mailbox rules and delegation.

[NEW] Flight Centre Travel Group told the ASX on November 26 of unusual activity in a third-party accommodation booking system, in the [Flight Centre supplier breach](stories/australia-s-flight-centre-data-breach-exposes-customer-records-a/). The affected estate covers the Flight Centre brand, Travel Associates, Cruiseabout, and some independent agents, while flight bookings and corporate travel run elsewhere and are unaffected. Mandiant is engaged alongside the unnamed vendor, and most records reviewed so far pair names with basic travel information, with a small share holding limited passport details, email addresses, phone numbers, and birth dates, and no card or bank data identified. The company says a significant number of records may have been accessed without knowing how many were extracted, a hedge that favors assuming full extraction of the name layer.

[NEW] Todoist disclosed that a stolen version-control credential was used on November 22 and 23 to alter automated workflows in three repositories, obtain an employee administrative credential, and enter its cloud infrastructure, in the [Todoist cloud intrusion](stories/todoist-breach-exposes-legacy-password-hashes-and-api-tokens/). The attacker exported email addresses, account creation dates, and salted SHA1 password hashes for about 6 percent of users, plus API and Slack tokens for about 0.003 percent, and searched tasks and connected Outlook mailboxes for credential-like material. Stolen API tokens were then used against the sync endpoint to read project, task, and comment data. Todoist retired SHA1, revoked tokens, ended sessions, disabled the Outlook add-in, and shortened token lifetimes, while customers should reset passwords, replace tokens, and rotate any secret ever stored in a task or comment.

[NEW] SOCRadar reports a Russian-speaking crew testing ransomware builds against Microsoft Defender before deployment, in the [LostTrust Eclipse revival](stories/losttrust-revival-tied-to-eclipse-ransomware-targets-us/). The operators' own material names 17 organizations, 14 in the United States including a federal agency, three energy cooperatives, and a healthcare provider, though those names are crew claims rather than confirmed victims. In the lab an October 16 build was isolated while a reworked build five days later reached its server silently, with an AI agent disabling tracing and antimalware scanning on its third tool call. SOCRadar links the build server and the MetaEncryptor and LostTrust platform to an Eclipse ransomware server with high confidence.

[NEW] Kaspersky findings describe Android phones arriving with malware embedded in firmware, in the [Keenadu preinstallation](stories/preinstalled-keenadu-malware-turns-up-on-220-000-android-devices/). Monthly detections of such devices are up more than 2.5 times over the past year, and Keenadu accounts for more than 220,000 of them. A buyer can receive a compromised device without installing anything suspicious, which moves the problem upstream of normal app hygiene. Watch for affected models and firmware paths, and fold firmware provenance into acceptance checks for new stock.

[UPDATE] BleepingComputer reports that any site that left its installer remotely accessible after SA-CORE-2026-017 may already be compromised, in the [Drupal installer compromise risk](stories/drupal-core-rce-cve-2026-132165-and-gadget-chain-cve-2026-132166/). Admins should confirm the user 1 address is one they control, consider resetting it, and review logs for unauthorized access, since exploitation has run since November 19 and the affected states carry no visible warning in the interface.
