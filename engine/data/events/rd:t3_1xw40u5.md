Turns out I wasn't off the mark - there's an entire body of research exploring how LLMs can be backdoored and how hard it actually is to detect a poisoned model. So I decided to try building one myself. I implemented a backdoor that only triggers when the model is running inside pi (making it harness-specific) and is asked a specific question. It was way easier than I expected: just a 2-step fine-tuning process and a few hours of compute on the exact same GPU I use for local inference.
