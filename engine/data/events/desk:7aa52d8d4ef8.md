IP Crime reported on 20 November 2026 that Mitel had patched five AWV flaws in MiCollab, including CVSS 10.0 unauthenticated command injection tracked as MTLVULN-1864. It stressed that 10.3.0.18 alone is not sufficient because Mitel requires upgrading to that release and applying a separate patch, or deploying a later release. The article lists the five MTLVULN identifiers with their severities and cites the Canadian Cyber Centre notice from 19 November.
