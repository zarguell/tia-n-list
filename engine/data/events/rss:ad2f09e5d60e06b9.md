Every connection your devices make starts with a DNS lookup, answered by a resolver that translates domain names into IP addresses and sees every domain you request. Choosing one is a security decision: the right resolver keeps lookups private and blocks known phishing and malware domains before a connection is made. Among the best DNS servers in 2026, Quad9 stands out for security and Cloudflare for speed and audited privacy, with more specialized options for custom filtering and regional needs. This guide compares the leading public resolvers on threat blocking, privacy, encryption and resilience, then covers what DNS filtering cannot stop, how organizations keep DNS visibility without giving up privacy, how DNS hijacking works, and how to keep your own domains from being taken over.
