A new version of the SYS01 infostealer malware delivered through Meta ads is enrolling browsers into enterprise-style management to force the installation of an extension that steals sensitive data, including Gmail and Outlook emails. The ads lure Windows users into downloading free animated wallpapers that work as advertised but also deploy malware in the background during installation. The campaign targets Facebook and Meta business accounts and gives the operator access to recent Gmail and Outlook messages in Chrome, and the ability to permanently delete emails. Bitdefender Labs tracks the campaign as SYS010 and says the malware is likely a new version of the SYS01 infostealer , observed since at least 2022 in malvertising activity on Facebook . The name reflects the researchers' assessment that this variant represents a major improvement over its predecessor, leading them to tag it as version 2, represented in binary as 010. Researchers identified a malicious SYS010 ad on October 16 and found that the campaign was still ongoing, with hundreds of ads created. Company telemetry shows that requests to the threat actor’s servers came from several countries in Asia, Europe, North America, Africa, and South America. Meta Ad Library' transparency page shows that one advertisement alone reached 6,275 users in the European Union, targeting anyone above 18.
