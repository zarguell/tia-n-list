The CVE record was published on 18 November 2026 at 02:02:35 UTC with ASUS as the assigning CNA and Emilio Galle / emilloc credited as the finder. It describes a missing authorization flaw (CWE-862) in Control Center Datacenter 4.0.0.2 and earlier and Control Center Enterprise 3.1.0.8 and earlier that lets a remote authenticated Viewer change an Administrator password through a crafted password update request and gain administrative privileges. The record scores CVSS 4.0 at 9.4 with vector AV:N/AC:L/AT:N/PR:L/UI:N/VC:H/VI:H/VA:H/SC:H/SI:H/SA:H and lists the ASUS security advisory as its only external reference.
