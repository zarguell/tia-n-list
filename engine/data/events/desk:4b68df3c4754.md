MikroTik's forum announcement of the emergency RouterOS release (fix in 7.25 beta 3, 7.24.2, 7.23.4, and 6.49.21 and newer) tells administrators the device will set a "Flagged" status written to the log if compromise indicators are found after upgrading, and to inspect configurations for unknown scripts or users regardless. MikroTik staff advised not exposing Winbox, WebFig, or SSH to the internet, using firewall rules or a VPN such as WireGuard instead, and confirmed that many years of RouterOS versions were affected with fixes only from the listed releases. Replies in the same thread carry the earliest publicly posted attack logs, which CERT Polska later dated to September 2, before the patches were released.
