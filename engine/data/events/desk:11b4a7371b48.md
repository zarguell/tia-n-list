FinCEN issued order FIN-2026-R002 on November 24, granting temporary, limited exceptive relief for a service disruption that was hindering filers from meeting reporting obligations. The order moved deadlines for certain BSA reports filed through Secure Data Transfer Mode and for Beneficial Ownership Information Reports or FinCEN ID updates from November 18 through December 29 to December 30. It said the Financial Institutions Hotline was not affected and that filers still had to comply with other BSA obligations, including ongoing monitoring for suspicious transactions.
