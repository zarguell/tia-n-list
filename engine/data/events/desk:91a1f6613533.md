CybersecurityNews.ca reported that attackers were targeting Drupal's install.php script to reinstall and take over live sites and that the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security urged patching. It states that CVE-2026-132165 lets anonymous users take over a site, had been exploited since 19 November, and was fixed in 11.4.13, 11.3.22 and 10.6.22, with no patch for end-of-life branches including Drupal 8 and 9. Its key points also advise blocking install.php even after patching.
