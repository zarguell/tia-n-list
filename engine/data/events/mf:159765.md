According to Straight Arrow News , the online game Drone Battle: Ukraine is linked to Russia’s Alabuga Special Economic Zone in Tatarstan, a site associated with the production of attack drones used in the war against Ukraine. Investigators say the game is the latest part of a broader campaign that has used major social media platforms, including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and X to reach young people. The game is just the lure. Investigators say it forms the entry point to a recruitment funnel that markets education, careers, travel, community, and technological opportunity to young people before ultimately steering some recruits toward jobs assembling drones at Alabuga.
