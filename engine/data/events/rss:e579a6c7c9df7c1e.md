In “Confidentiality, Quantum Evidence, AI-Quantum Systems, and Human Judgment,” Ralph Losey discusses the intersection of quantum technology and the legal landscape. He highlights the transition from Schrödinger’s Cat as a thought experiment to a practical challenge in law, emphasizing the critical need for legal professionals to adapt to quantum advancements. Key issues involve confidentiality risks with the potential of quantum computers breaching current encryption methods and the complexities of validating quantum-generated evidence in courts. As quantum technology evolves, lawyers must grasp its implications, manage risks associated with sensitive data, and maintain human judgment in legal decision-making processes, ensuring reliability in the face of uncertainty.
