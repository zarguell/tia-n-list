Stadtwerke Landsberg KU, a German municipal public utility, disclosed a ransomware attack that occurred on September 1, 2026. On September 7, the company published an official GDPR Article 34 incident notification on its website, informing customers of the cyberattack.

The incident involved ransomware that encrypted central IT systems. The organization responded by cutting all internet connections and shutting down systems to stabilize the situation. Critical infrastructure including the electrical grid, water supply, wastewater treatment, district heating, fiber network, EV charging stations, swimming pool, and parking facilities were not affected. However, possible exfiltration of personal data including names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and banking information cannot be excluded.

The company activated its crisis plan, engaged external cybersecurity specialists and IT forensic investigators, notified relevant authorities, and filed a criminal complaint. MITRE ATT&CK techniques observed include T1486 (Data Encrypted for Impact) and T1657 (Financial Theft). No threat actor has been named.
