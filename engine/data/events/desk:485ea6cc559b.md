Researcher Smitty van Bodegom (loops) disclosed a one-click universal XSS in the Firefox MV2 uBlock Origin element picker, plus a less severe variant in MV3 uBO Lite. The chain recovers the per-install extension UUID through a WAR-backed fetch hook, embeds the picker as a hidden `iframe`, and drives it with `postMessage` messages named `epickerStart`, `showDialog` and `resultsetDetails`. A newline in the `opt` field turns the displayed network filter into a second trusted scriptlet filter for another domain; the writeup credits GPT-5.6 Sol with finding the bug and Claude with most of the PoC code. The example relies on the default `lootdest.*##+js(no-fetch-if, adsbygoogle, war:googlesyndication_adsbygoogle.js)` rule and a click on a transparent Create control.
