Citizen Lab's investigation found that Russian authorities used **Cellebrite's UFED** phone-cracking technology to access the device of imprisoned human rights activist Andrey Pivovarov around June 2021 — after Cellebrite had canceled its contract with Russia.

The historic architecture (offline mode, legacy hardware continued functioning after updates ceased) made it difficult to cut off problematic customers. Information from the phone may have been used to surveil fellow dissident Anastasiya Burakova.
