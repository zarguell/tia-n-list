OpenAI and more than 100 organizations have issued an urgent call for coordinated global cyber defense as artificial intelligence becomes increasingly capable of supporting both attackers and defenders. The coalition, spanning technology, cybersecurity, cloud computing, finance, semiconductors, manufacturing, and critical infrastructure, says organizations must act before advanced AI makes cyberattacks faster, cheaper, more scalable, and harder to contain. Its open letter warns that hospitals, water systems, local governments, and the infrastructure underpinning the internet could face heightened danger if current defensive gaps remain unresolved. OpenAI and 100+ Organizations Call for Global Cyber Defense The signatories argue that this is a defining moment for operational cybersecurity preparedness and resilience worldwide. Published Thursday as “A Call for Collective Action on Cyber Defense,” the statement says AI-enabled attacks may become far more widespread and sophisticated in coming months as model capabilities improve. Signatories include Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, Amazon Web Services, Oracle, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, IBM, Adobe, AMD, Arm, SAP, Dell Technologies, Visa, Mastercard, Capital One, Hugging Face , Fortinet, and Check Point. Initial reporting placed the roster at roughly 118 organizations, with more expected to join.
