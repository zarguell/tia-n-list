South Korea's Financial Services Commission convened an emergency response meeting on October 2, 2026 with the Financial Supervisory Service, the Financial Security Institute, six banks and three card companies after the September 30 Shinhan Bank data leak and follow-on attacks on KB Kookmin Bank and other financial firms. The commission ordered immediate security reviews of internet-exposed systems and services, checks of authentication and access control, and rapid sharing of attacker IPs and methods with agencies including KISA. The statement did not name ARTEX.
