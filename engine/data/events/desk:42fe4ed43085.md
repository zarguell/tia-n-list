Patchstack published the CVE-2026-124251 advisory, crediting Greg Otto with discovering the CSRF flaw in WPIDE versions up to and including 3.6.4 and listing 3.6.5 as the patched release. The entry gives CVSS 8.8 and notes that a privileged user must perform an action for exploitation. Its timeline shows the report on 30 October 2026, an early warning to Patchstack customers and publication on 10 November 2026, with practical priority marked low because user interaction is required.
