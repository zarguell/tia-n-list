Huntress published the original disclosure of this campaign on September 28, 2026, one day before BleepingComputer's coverage, credited to Mark O'Halloran and Jonathan Semon. It documents two attacker-created custom GPTs titled "Plus 5.6" (g-6ab595ad6554819181b686d4876efb80 and g-6ab6ba039440819185ed491740b11cf8), the ClickFix lure at sites.google.com/view/antibot172881, and an eight-stage chain from a decimal-IP stager host (1614733393, resolving to 96.62.224.81) through ISOSimple.msi and Canon-signed COTFileReadApp.exe sideloading to a RAT in a custom encrypted archive. The Huntress SOC responded to at least 40 related incidents with two confirmed custom GPT infections, published full IoCs including 45.140.205.28 and sample SHA256s, and described a second wave using a Stardock-signed host (DeElevate64.exe, IconEdit2Turb.msi, "Stardock DeElevation Tool" persistence).
