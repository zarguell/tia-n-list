Three CVEs on MikroTik RouterOS — equipment massively deployed across ISPs, enterprises, and embedded networks. MikroTik is regularly targeted by threat actors. Three vulnerabilities tracked as CVE-2026-67276, CVE-2026-67277, and CVE-2026-86060 affect RouterOS. CISA added CVE-2026-67277 and CVE-2026-86060 to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on September 10. Organizations should update RouterOS immediately and check for indicators of compromise.
