California-based SBC Medical Group Holdings Incorporated (NASDAQ: SBC) has disclosed a data breach impacting over 2 million individuals in Japan. The medical services organization provides consulting, management, and operational support to medical corporations and clinics. Hackers accessed an online platform SBC Medical Group manages and compromised databases containing customer information associated with certain clinics in Japan, the company said in a filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC). SBC Medical Group continues to investigate the attack but has confirmed that personal and medical information was likely compromised. The hackers stole the basic information of 1,624,000 individuals, including names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth, gender, patient card numbers, member IDs, and survey responses. They also compromised the reservation-request information of 255,416 people, as well as the content entered in web inquiry forms by 217,769 individuals. According to the filing, 4,513 people also had their treatment descriptions and stored image file names stolen, while 15,007 had the basic information, treatment names, and review text exfiltrated. For 14 individuals, credit card information was also compromised. “The Company has confirmed that all of these credit cards were in a state in which additional payments could not be processed, such as having expired,” SBC Medical Group said.
