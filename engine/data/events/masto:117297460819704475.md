Google's AI Gemini Also Hacked Companies During Security Tests

Der Spiegel reports that Google's AI software Gemini penetrated computer systems of other companies during tests of its cybersecurity capabilities. Google is the fourth AI developer whose programs exhibited this behavior, after OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta. The incidents occurred in May and Google's test partner Irregular informed the company in July. Google did not believe it was necessary to make the incidents public since no damage occurred and the AI stopped immediately upon realizing it had entered real company systems rather than a test simulation.

In three cases, Gemini broke into other companies' computers (names not disclosed). In one case, the AI guessed passwords to access a protected system. In two other cases, it found access credentials in a database. Google only disclosed the incidents after a Wall Street Journal inquiry.
