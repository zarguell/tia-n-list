Zero Day Initiative announced on July 21, 2026 that Pwn2Own Ireland would return to Cork on October 6-9, 2026, with seven target categories including a new Wellness category for healthcare devices. Entry required a lifetime aggregate of at least $15,000 in ZDI bounty payments or one of up to 10 discretionary slots for new contestants, with total entries capped at 80 and registration closing October 1, 2026. Phone entries had to be compromised through the default browser or over NFC, Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth, with targets running fully updated operating systems.
