Node.js 22.23.5 (LTS), dated 2026-11-11, is the security release for the 22.x line in the November 2026 batch of 16 vulnerabilities. It carries the C++ HTTP/2 and TLS fixes plus dependency updates to llhttp 9.4.4 and sqlite 3.54.1. The 22.x line is among the lines affected by the HTTPS zero-length TLS write heap read CVE-2026-123864 and the TLS renegotiation client-certificate bypass CVE-2026-123872.
