Yandex data centres are central to Russia’s sovereign internet infrastructure. Like most modern digital infrastructure, they host data and applications used for a range of civilian and military purposes. One targeted facility in Sasovo reportedly hosted two of Yandex’s three supercomputers, which were used to train the YandexGPT large language model.[2] The attacks followed an increase in Russian jet-powered drone strikes against Ukrainian data centers, which disrupted mobile and internet services for thousands of people. Russia may face greater difficulty restoring affected services because its internet infrastructure is more restricted. While Ukraine can rely on international partners for cloud services, Russia has fewer comparable options. [3] Unverified sources claim that Yandex may be unable to replace the destroyed infrastructure before 2028.
