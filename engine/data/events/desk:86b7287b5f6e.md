Fireworks updated its October 2026 incident bulletin on November 9, 2026, reporting that a third-party cloud provider hosting Fireworks R&D machines experienced a security incident on October 23 and that on October 24 an unauthorized party used it to reach Fireworks development machines and run an unauthorized workload. The update adds account and billing information, including billing and shipping addresses, invoice and payment history, account balance, and credit usage, plus incomplete model weights and datasets for certain customers, while Fireworks says it does not store payment card numbers. Fireworks links the activity to a financially motivated cybercriminal group pursuing LLMJacking and says the investigation is ongoing.
