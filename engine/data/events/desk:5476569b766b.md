VMware published advisory VMSA-2021-0028 on 10 December 2021 (initial publication 8 December 2021), covering CVE-2021-44228 and CVE-2021-45046 across vCenter Server, Horizon, HCX, NSX-T and other products, with a note that exploitation attempts in the wild had been confirmed by 10 December 2021. The response matrix fixes vCenter Server 7.x in 7.0U3c, 6.7.x in 6.7 U3q and 6.5.x in 6.5 U3s, with workaround guidance in KB87081 and KB87096. Any vCenter still on a build below those patch levels, such as the 7.0.3 deployment later exploited in the BO Team case, was running a known and fully remediated remote code execution path.
