Joomla Center published coverage of the 6.2.1 and 5.4.11 releases with a source timestamp of 24 November 2026 at 00:00 +03:00, or 21:00 UTC on 23 November. It reports 58 fixes split into four critical, 23 high, 29 medium and two low advisories, and explains that CVE-2026-129393 stems from insufficient escaping in generated XML so field settings can alter the form definition rather than ordinary image viewing. It advises checking for a leftover installation folder, moving to 6.2.1 or 5.4.11, and notes that no separate Joomla 3 or 4 packages were issued in this release.
