Huntress reported that on November 11, 2026 an operator used access to multiple cloud-hosted ScreenConnect panels to push commands to groups of managed endpoints, installing attacker-controlled rogue clients and staging additional payloads. The campaign compromised at least 6 managed service providers and 288 hosts across 48 organizations, hitting all 288 hosts within roughly 90 minutes. The toolkit, which Huntress calls SCKit, provides persistent backdoor access, intercepts session cookies, and self-replicates by waiting for a technician to connect to a compromised endpoint before infecting that technician's workstation and spreading to other sessions and panels.
