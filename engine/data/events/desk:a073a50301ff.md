ThreatLocker Threat Intelligence reproduced FastUnlock on 19 November 2026 and confirmed that the bypass reaches the non-administrator WSIAccount without a sign-in. The test required physical access, a Microsoft account in the recovery flow, and Narrator navigation with Caps Lock and the arrow keys; the physical keyboard could not be used while navigating Narrator, so the testers used the on-screen keyboard and an external drive. ThreatLocker says a BitLocker preboot PIN mitigates the technique on a powered-off or hibernated machine because the drive must unlock before Windows starts.
