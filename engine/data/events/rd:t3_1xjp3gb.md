Fujitsu has launched a retainer-based incident response service in Japan that uses a threat-analysis AI system powered by frontier AI for 24/7 forensic investigation, malware analysis, and suspicious-URL analysis. The company says customers can upload suspicious files and device data through a dedicated portal. It aims to provide threat-analysis reports in as little as one business day and forensic reports in two business days, with Fujitsu security experts checking the results and taking over triage or containment when needed. The practical detail is the human review. This is AI-assisted incident response with an expert handoff, not a claim that a model can replace the response team.
