DATA BREACH: Monceau Fleurs, a cyberattack exposes customer data of four retail chains of the Emova group.

A cyberattack on the French retail group Emova exposed customer data belonging to four of its retail banners, including Monceau Fleurs. The incident is under investigation and the group has not published the number of affected customers or the data categories taken.
