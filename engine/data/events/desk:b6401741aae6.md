Mandiant's advisory describes an unkeyed ObjectStateFormatter deserialization sink in Idunno.AntiCsrf.AntiCsrfModule PreRequestHandlerExecute: a single unauthenticated POST with any non-empty CSRF cookie and a Base64 gadget chain in the form field runs code as w3wp.exe. It states that active exploitation in the wild has been observed, rates exploitability High, and records FLARE root-cause analysis on 4 November and CVE assignment initiated on 11 November. The project is defunct with no official patch, and the advisory publishes hashes and sizes for three public DLL builds plus IIS log hunting guidance.
