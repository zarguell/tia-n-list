Kaspersky disclosed Operation ShadowHammer on March 25, 2019, identifying a sophisticated supply chain attack that leveraged ASUS Live Update software between June and November 2018. Over 57,000 Kaspersky users downloaded the backdoored version; Kaspersky estimated the real scale could exceed one million users worldwide. The attackers hardcoded over 600 unique MAC addresses to surgically target specific victims. The malicious updaters were signed with legitimate ASUS certificates and hosted on official ASUS update servers. Kaspersky linked evidence to the ShadowPad incident and the BARIUM APT actor.
