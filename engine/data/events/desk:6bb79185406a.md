The New York Times live blog, which began at 4:30 PM EST on November 13, 2026, reported that before the nationwide advisory the FAA had already halted flights to Chicago's O'Hare and Midway airports citing equipment problems, and had logged equipment-related departure delays at Newark, Las Vegas, Teterboro, Orlando, Houston and Phoenix. More than 30,000 departures and arrivals were scheduled for the remainder of the day, and the paper noted the stop was only the third nationwide moratorium on flights in recent history, after September 11, 2001, and January 11, 2023.
