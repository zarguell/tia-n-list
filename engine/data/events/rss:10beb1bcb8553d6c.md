TOP ATTACKS AND BREACHES Japanese cloud hosting provider Onamae Desktop Cloud has confirmed a cyberattack in which intruders compromised its infrastructure and installed malware on customer servers. More than 26,000 servers were potentially affected. The provider began shutting down customer environments on October 21 to contain the intrusion and prevent further unauthorized activity. India’s state-run State Bank of India, serving more than 500 million customers, disclosed a data breach after 3,897 customer records appeared online. The exposed information reportedly included contact details, residential addresses, birth dates, bank profiles, loan records, and identity-verification documents. The bank reported no disruption to core services. WordPress backup developer TeamUpdraft has suffered a cyberattack after intruders accessed its website and potentially exposed customer information and management-service connection keys.
