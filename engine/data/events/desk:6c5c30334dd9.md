TRM Labs reported that on 27 October 2026 the FBI seized the domain of Virtualine, a Russian-speaking bulletproof hosting provider. The firm noted Virtualine received the most illicit-attributed value in 2026 to date among tracked bulletproof hosts, with a Russian drug marketplace accounting for roughly 44% of direct payments, a sextortion scheme for nearly 17%, a cryptocurrency mixer for almost 13%, ransomware for nearly 5%, and malware for 2%.
