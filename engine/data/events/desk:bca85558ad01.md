UkrMedia reported that Ukrainian law enforcement and the FBI uncovered a hacking group that attacked corporate networks, stole data and demanded multimillion-dollar ransoms. At least 28 companies paid more than $40.5 million, about UAH 1.8 billion, and the group used AI-generated voice messages while calling staff as technical support. The report describes 66 simultaneous searches in Kyiv and 11 regions, four detentions and seizures of computers, phones, bank cards, nearly $370,000 and EUR 20,000. It says the organization had at least 27 members, a broader network of more than 60 people in eight jurisdictions including former Conti members, and one US victim that lost 1.4 TB and paid $5 million against a $10 million demand.
