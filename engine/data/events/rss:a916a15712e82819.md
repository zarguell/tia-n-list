Frankfurt, 26 October 2026 – Christian Lindner has invested in QuoIntelligence, the Frankfurt-based cyber threat intelligence company named Europe’s Most Promising Cybersecurity Startup 2026 by the European Cyber Security Organisation (ECSO). The investment follows QuoIntelligence’s €7.3 million Series A financing round completed in April 2026. Financial terms were not disclosed. Lindner’s investment reflects his confidence in QuoIntelligence’s business model, team and market position. He sees the company as well positioned to scale its European cybersecurity business from an already established base. Christian Lindner said: “Europe needs more technology companies that can compete internationally in strategically important markets. QuoIntelligence combines human expertise with AI-driven technology in an area where demand is growing rapidly. I am pleased to support the company as an investor in its next phase of European growth.” As cybersecurity becomes an increasingly strategic issue for European organizations, the ability to rely on European technology and infrastructure is gaining importance.
