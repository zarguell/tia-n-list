IBM published the CVE-2026-84244 record at 19:08 UTC on 8 October 2026. It describes a CVSS 9.3 stored cross-site scripting flaw in the Quick Search results grid of Guardium Data Protection 12.2, where an unauthenticated attacker who can influence monitored database traffic can execute script in an authenticated user's browser. The record lists SqlGuard_12.0p233_FixPack as the fix and references IBM bulletin 7288035.
