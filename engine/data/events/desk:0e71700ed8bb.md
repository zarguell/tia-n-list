CISA published ICS advisory ICSA-26-323-02 on 19 November 2026 for Elecbrakes EB2 through version 3.0.4.2.0, VM1 below 1.3.4.2.1 and all ELBC2000 controllers. It lists CVE-2026-96805 and CVE-2026-125273 at CVSS 3.1 9.6 for missing authentication and replayable BLE commands, plus CVE-2026-124281 at 6.1 for an unprotected firmware-update channel. The advisory says exploitation could let an attacker control trailer brakes, modify firmware, disable brake lights or other safety features, and that no public exploitation had been reported to CISA at release.
