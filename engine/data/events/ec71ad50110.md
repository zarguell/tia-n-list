Cisco Talos has disclosed UAT-8302, a China-nexus APT group targeting government entities in South America since late 2024 and southeastern Europe throughout 2025. The group deploys a suite of custom backdoors including NetDraft (a .NET variant of the FINALDRAFT/SquidDoor family using Microsoft Graph API and OneDrive for C2), CloudSorcerer version 3 (delivered via DLL side-loading with process injection into explorer.exe and spoolsv.exe), and VSHELL (delivered through SNOWLIGHT/SNOWRUST loaders). Notably, UAT-8302's malware overlaps with tools used by other China-linked clusters including Jewelbug, REF7707, and CL-STA-0049 — pointing to a shared ecosystem of capabilities. The group also deploys the Hades HIDS kernel-mode driver for system monitoring and concealment. For lateral movement, the operators rely on open-source tools (Impacket, nbtscan, naabu, PortQry) and conduct extensive AD enumeration using adconnectdump.py and SharpGetUserLoginIPRP. Cisco has released updated ClamAV signatures and Snort rules.
