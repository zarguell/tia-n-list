Executive Summary Cyble has continued tracking GhostBat RAT activity targeting Android users in India since its initial identification in October 2025 . Recent investigations into malicious mParivahan and eChallan-themed applications revealed a broader operator-controlled ecosystem that we track as GhostX. Analysis of the exposed GhostX backend identified administrative and user-management structures, device-control records, SMS-delivery instructions, call-forwarding activity, and continuously changing phishing content , indicating active campaign management. Impressions The latest GhostBat RAT variant represents a significant evolution in both delivery and post-compromise capabilities. The malware now abuses the Android Accessibility Service to support Ghost Overlay and broader PIN/key-input capture targeting financial applications used across India. Ghost Overlay transparently intercepts PIN keypad interactions in Google Pay and MobiKwik. It replays the same touches into the legitimate application, allowing the malware to capture PIN information without presenting a conventional phishing interface.
