Vendor advisory by Check Point Research (Lotem Finkelstein) dated September 22, 2026 confirming a wave of exploitation attempts against Spark customers starting September 12, originating from VPN and proxy anonymization infrastructure and using certificates with subjects CN=vpn, OU=users, O=global and two close variants. Recommends hunting anomalous certificate-based Mobile Access logins followed by internal port and service scans, and confirms the management zero-day CVE-2026-93616 was used in pinpointed attacks on July 23, 2026.
