This incident record reports a 21 November 2026 public disclosure by Tower Records Japan after unauthorized access to Tower Records Online was confirmed at 17:10 on 19 November. It gives counts of 10,214,652 login records, 2,784,514 member records and 776,953 delivery records, with no credit card data and no confirmed secondary damage as of 21 November. The report says the intrusion route was not stated in the company notice.
