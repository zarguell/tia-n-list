The Hacker News reported on November 11, 2026 that of the 37,571 Grok-built apps Claroc found online, 35,578 were open to anyone without a sign-in, including 24 apps built by staff at X and SpaceXSI. Most of the open apps were games, landing pages, and calculators, but about one in ten contained internal business or personal data, such as a confidential onboarding plan, a sales territory map of 594 accounts, and an X advertising proposal to a U.S. federal agency marked confidential. SpaceXSI removed all 24 staff apps after Claroc's report but not other users' apps, and open apps contained internal data from 30 other organizations.
