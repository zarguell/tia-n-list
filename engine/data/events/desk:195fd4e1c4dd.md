Fortinet published advisory FG-IR-24-015 on February 8, 2024, documenting CVE-2024-21762, a critical out-of-bounds write vulnerability in FortiOS and FortiProxy SSL-VPN (sslvpnd) with CVSS 9.6. The advisory lists affected versions from FortiOS 6.0.x through 7.4.2 and FortiProxy 1.0.x through 7.4.2, with upgrades to 6.0.18, 6.2.16, 6.4.15, 7.0.14, 7.2.7, 7.4.3, and FortiProxy 2.0.14 or above. CISA added the vulnerability to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on February 9, 2024, confirming in-the-wild exploitation.
