DreamHost brought its File Manager back online after an incident that disrupted several services, though some customers remained locked out of email or FTP accounts until passwords were reset. The first sign of trouble came on the afternoon of October 23, 2026, when connectivity problems hit the File Manager interface. DreamHost described the resets as precautionary and said affected users cannot sign in until the account owner resets their passwords. The company said all affected account owners had been emailed instructions by October 25.
