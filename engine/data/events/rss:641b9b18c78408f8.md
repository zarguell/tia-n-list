Financial technology company Abrigo has restored its Sageworks risk management application, except for borrower-facing capabilities, following an outage triggered by "irregular activity" in its environment. On October 30, the company began emergency maintenance and took the Sageworks application offline to investigate suspicious activity detected earlier that day, with the outage continuing through the weekend. Abrigo provides cloud-based software and analytics solutions for community banks and credit unions across the United States. According to the company, it serves more than 2,400 entities . Its Sageworks product is a software platform that helps lenders process loan applications, analyze borrowers’ finances, assess creditworthiness, and monitor loan portfolios. Due to its nature, Sageworks handles sensitive information, including banking customer details. "We identified irregular activity in our environment, and, out of abundance of caution, we disabled the Sageworks application at approximately 11:25 am ET," the company said on Friday. In status updates over the weekend, Abrigo said its investigation was ongoing but did not disclose details about the nature of the incident. The incident impacted the following services: Online Portal Now, Sageworks Analyst, and Sageworks API.
