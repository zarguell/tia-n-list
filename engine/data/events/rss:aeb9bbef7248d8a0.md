German software giant SAP confirmed a cybersecurity incident following reports that hackers infiltrated its software development infrastructure, potentially gaining access to the company’s source code. In a statement to Recorded Future News on Friday, SAP said it had identified suspicious activity and immediately taken steps to protect its customers and secure its services. The company did not disclose the nature or extent of the intrusion. “Based on our investigation to date, we have found no evidence of unauthorized access to customer business data,” SAP said. “We will continue to strengthen our security to provide the secure and resilient environment our customers depend on.” German magazine Der Spiegel first reported the incident Thursday, citing internal company communications and sources familiar with the matter. According to the magazine, hackers targeted SAP’s software development environment hosted on GitHub, obtaining employee authentication tokens that allowed them to copy and modify the company’s source code. The German Press Agency (dpa), which independently reviewed portions of internal SAP communication, reported that the company had alerted employees to suspicious activity within its GitHub environment several days before the incident became public. Employees were advised to assess whether they needed to change their login credentials or take additional security precautions.
