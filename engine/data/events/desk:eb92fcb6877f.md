TBS NEWS DIG reported at 17:39 JST on November 9, 2026 that Nitori Holdings had confirmed unauthorized access by a third party to a system it manages on November 6, and that up to about 37.62 million records of member information may have leaked. The report says the affected data includes customer names, addresses and telephone numbers from store and online purchases, and that credit card information and passwords are not included. Nitori warned customers to be wary of suspicious email and telephone calls impersonating the company.
