We ask an assistant why the sky is blue and get a correct explanation, but at the same time the system makes a request to an external website that the user never made. This is the phenomenon at the center of a new CERT-AGID paper titled "La backdoor attende il suo turno. Un comportamento appreso nei pesi attivato dalla presenza di un server MCP" ("The backdoor waits its turn. A behavior learned in the weights and triggered by the presence of an MCP server").
