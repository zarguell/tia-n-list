Cook County Health and Hospitals System terminated its relationship with medical transcription vendor Perry Johnson & Associates and notified patients after PJ&A reported a data security incident. CCH said PJ&A informed it on July 21, 2023 that an unauthorized individual had accessed its systems in April 2023, and CCH was notified on July 26 that patient information might be affected. The notice says 1.2 million CCH patients were impacted. This is the earliest public reporting found in this search, about three weeks before PJ&A filed with HHS.
