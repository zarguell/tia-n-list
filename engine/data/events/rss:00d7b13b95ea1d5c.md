Your SOC pays for multiple threat intelligence feeds, yet fresh malicious domains and IPs can still appear in telemetry as unknown. When feeds rely on overlapping sources, more subscriptions may deliver the same indicators while leaving emerging threats uncovered. Analysts must fill these gaps manually, checking indicator reputations, comparing sources, and gathering evidence. Every extra lookup consumes valuable time, increases the triage queue, and puts response service-level agreements (SLAs) at risk. While the team works to establish whether an alert is malicious, attackers gain more time to operate. The answer isn’t necessarily another feed. It’s intelligence that brings fresh indicators and investigative context into your existing security workflows, helping SOC and MSSP teams detect threats earlier and investigate with less manual effort.
