TOP ATTACKS AND BREACHES Denmark’s government has disclosed a breach involving its Central Register of Persons after unauthorized individuals exploited access provided to a private supplier. The incident reportedly exposed personal information, including names, addresses, and identification numbers, associated with approximately 8.8 million individuals in the national registry. UK-based online fashion retailer ASOS has confirmed a cyberattack involving stolen employee credentials that enabled unauthorized access to third-party platforms. Attackers reportedly obtained customer names and contact details and sent unauthorized push notifications to mobile application users. The threat group Xuanye claimed responsibility for the incident. ATB, Ukraine’s largest grocery retailer, has faced a cyberattack that disrupted its online services after attackers posted an extortion demand on its website.
