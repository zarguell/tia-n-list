Law enforcement agencies from nine countries shut down 235 servers and seized 257 domains used by three malicious programs: Gootloader, CastleRAT and StealC.

Part of Operation Endgame: Europol and partners in Bulgaria, Canada, Denmark, Germany, Moldova, the Netherlands, Poland, the UK and the US took down the infrastructure behind the Gootloader loader, the CastleRAT remote-access trojan and the StealC infostealer, identifying 6.4 million stolen credential sets tied to more than 40,000 victims. HaveIBeenPwned recovered 180,000 email addresses and 893,000 unique passwords.
