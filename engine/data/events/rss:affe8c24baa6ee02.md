Patti Degnan’s journey is unusual. She didn’t have an early plan to become a CISO; but perhaps life events made it inevitable. Patti Degnan’s first encounter with security shaped her future interest: her identity was stolen in 2006. “That was my first introduction into safeguarding data. It piqued my interest in how to better protect my own information. I wanted to understand what had happened, so I tried to discover what information about me was held by different agencies. The intent was seeing if my personal data was safe – but the effect was learning the concepts of privacy and personal data protection.” It wasn’t until joining Symantec in 2012 that this underlying interest began to rise to the surface. “I was in internal audit, focusing on the operational and IT perspective, and began to focus on data privacy.” This was pre-Snowden and years before GDPR. She was combining audit knowledge, PCI understanding and privacy knowledge at a time its importance was growing. Symantec had made several acquisitions. “We probably had close to 60 different backend infrastructure components that were all payment gateways. I worked hard trying to figure out what that complex environment looked like, and how we were deploying controls around it – and that was my first serious foray into security.” She left Symantec in 2015, having become IT audit and compliance manager, and moved to LendingClub as senior manager in IT compliance and assurance.
