Original answers and English translations are provided below. What is Stadia? Stadia is hard to assign to a precise malware class; we even put "RAT" in the name just so customers would understand it better. Technically Stadia is a hybrid resident bot in the style of classic Zeus/Andromeda updated for modern times, combined with an infostealer in a single piece of software. Why this is not a RAT in the classic sense: we don't use a persistent TCP connection like the vast majority of RATs; our traffic goes over HTTP/S with our own packet data system on JSON/RC4 (actually we'll be dropping RC4 in upcoming updates in favor of something else).
