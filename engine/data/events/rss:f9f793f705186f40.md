A leader of the Scattered Spider cybercrime collective has been sentenced to six years in prison in the United States for stealing millions in cryptocurrency and conspiring to hack a dozen companies. US prosecutors charged 24-year-old British man Tyler Robert Buchanan and four other suspects in November with wire fraud and aggravated identity theft in relation to a series of phishing attacks between September 2021 and April 2023. According to court documents , their victims include companies across a wide range of industries, such as telecommunications, technology, business process outsourcing (BPO), entertainment, and information technology (IT) suppliers, as well as virtual currency providers, cloud communications providers, and individuals. Buchanan and his accomplices sent hundreds of SMS phishing messages to victim companies' employees' phones (while impersonating the companies or a contracted IT or BPO supplier) to trick them into entering sensitive information (including credentials and personally identifiable information) into phishing pages that looked like their employers' legitimate websites. The stolen information was used to steal over $11.6 million of virtual currency after compromising victims' cryptocurrency accounts and wallets via Subscriber Identity Module (SIM) swaps (also known as SIM swapping attacks).
