CERT-EU advisory 2026-020, release date printed on the page as 12-11-2026 17:20:53 Brussels time (UTC+1 in November, hence 16:20:53Z). It states that on 12 November 2026 Cloud Software Group published bulletins covering 28 memory flaws in NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway, that CVE-2026-107412 (CVSS 9.5) affects appliances configured as a Gateway (SSL VPN, ICA Proxy, CVPN or RDP Proxy) or an AAA virtual server and can be reached without authentication according to the vendor, and that CVE-2026-120868 (CVSS 9.3) affects every deployment including the default configuration. It records that Citrix reports no exploitation and that no public proof of concept has been confirmed, notes ten of the flaws are scored high on confidentiality, integrity and availability while the vendor describes the impact only as unpredictable behavior or denial of service, and recommends upgrading internet-facing Gateway and AAA appliances first. Affected versions listed are 14.1 before 14.1-73.57, 13.1 before 13.1-64.39, ADC FIPS before 14.1-73.58 FIPS, and ADC FIPS/NDcPP before 13.1-37.293.
