TOP ATTACKS AND BREACHES CenterPoint Energy, a US utility company serving Texas and several other states, has disclosed a data breach affecting more than 1 million Texas residents. Unauthorized access to an external system exposed names, addresses, Social Security information, and driver’s license numbers, according to regulatory notifications filed by the company. Burger King Japan, operator of the international fast-food chain in Japan, has disclosed a breach affecting approximately 11.18 million application members. Unauthorized access between October 2 and 7 exposed contact information, birth dates, gender, and regional details. The company found no evidence of compromised passwords or payment cards. ConnectWise Cloud Backup, a Microsoft 365 backup service used by managed service providers, has suffered a credential compromise enabling unauthorized access to customer environments.
