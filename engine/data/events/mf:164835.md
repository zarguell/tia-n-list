Galago, a newly observed ransomware operation, claims to work with the Panzer group. A shared prefix in their Tor leak-site addresses supports a naming link, but does not by itself prove shared operators, malware, or access to the same systems. Researchers first flagged Galago on 9 September 2026 after an open-source alert alleged an attack on an Icelandic healthcare organization. They began monitoring Galago’s dark leak site (DLS) on 15 September and found it inactive, with no victims listed. As of the reported observation, Galago has not publicly substantiated an intrusion through its own leak site. Galago’s site description claims an operational partnership with Panzer. Its reported Tor address begins pnzr4del , while Panzer’s begins pnzruro . The shared pnzr prefix is consistent with a relationship, but a naming pattern and a self-reported claim are not independent proof of operational control. An earlier CyberXTron profile described Panzer as a ransomware-as-a-service operation that recruits affiliates and uses double extortion, stealing data as well as encrypting files.
