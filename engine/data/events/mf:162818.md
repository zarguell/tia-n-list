Bimbo Bakeries USA (BBU) has confirmed that employee Social Security numbers were exposed after attackers exploited a zero-day vulnerability in Oracle E-Business Suite. The incident is linked to a third-party vendor using Oracle EBS and follows a broader global extortion campaign targeting Oracle customers. In a data-breach notification dated August 31, 2026, Bimbo Bakeries USA said it learned on December 6, 2025, that attackers had exploited a previously unknown Oracle E-Business Suite vulnerability to obtain files from the affected platform. Bimbo Bakeries USA Data Breach The company’s notification was filed with the California Attorney General’s Office on September 4, 2026. According to the filing , the compromise originated through a third-party vendor relationship, underscoring the supply-chain and third-party risks associated with enterprise resource planning platforms that process sensitive workforce and business information. After learning of the vulnerability, Bimbo Bakeries said it had implemented Oracle’s emergency security patches and had begun a forensic investigation. The review focused on determining the scope of unauthorized access, identifying affected files, and establishing whether the stolen data contained personal information. On August 19, 2026, Bimbo Bakeries determined that one of the files acquired during the incident contained individuals’ names and Social Security numbers.
