Arctic Wolf reported on September 6, 2024 that Akira affiliates were compromising local SSLVPN accounts on SonicWall devices as their initial access vector, with MFA disabled on every compromised account and vulnerable SonicOS firmware on every affected device. The bulletin repeats SonicWall's fixed-version table and recommends upgrading firmware, resetting locally managed SSLVPN passwords, and enabling MFA. It notes the advisory had expanded that same day to cover both management access and SSLVPN with exploitation language.
