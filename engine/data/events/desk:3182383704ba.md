Socket's earlier March 2026 report documented six malicious Composer packages under the ophimcms namespace that ship trojanized jQuery libraries. The payloads include URL exfiltration to userstat.net via Caesar cipher, FUNNULL-linked redirects through union.macoms.la, ad injection pointing to 23.225.52.67:4466, click hijacking via cre-ads.com, full-screen overlay ads tracked in localStorage under MPAd_ keys, and anti-debugging code that redirects to xemphimlau.com. The FUNNULL attribution is supported by DNS resolution routing through .nqsaaskw.com CNAMEs consistent with documented FUNNULL CDN naming, and by direct links to the Polyfill.io supply chain attack. The report notes approximately 2750 total installs and that the payloads were actively maintained as of March 10, 2026, nearly ten months after OFAC sanctions on FUNNULL (May 29, 2025).
