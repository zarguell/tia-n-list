Google promoted Chrome 157 to the stable channel on November 3, 2026 for Windows, Mac and Linux, rolling out over the coming days and weeks. The update ships 213 security fixes, including seven critical flaws: CVE-2026-121156 (buffer overflow in Dawn, $10,000 bounty, reported by Zhenpeng (Leo) Lin at depthfirst on September 30), CVE-2026-121229 (memory corruption in ANGLE, $1,000, reported by @mfx on September 17), plus five more criticals with no listed bounty reported by Google, Xinyang Ge of Anthropic, and Wenbin Liu. Google paid $31,000 across the release and left bounties undecided for 39 flaws, and states the update contains no in-the-wild exploitation. Desktop builds are 157.0.8103.38 on Linux and 157.0.8103.38 or .39 on Windows and Mac, with Chrome for Android at 157.0.8103.38.
