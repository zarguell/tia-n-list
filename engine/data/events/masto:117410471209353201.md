CrowdStrike reports that an AI pentest tool called ARTEX was used in attacks against South Korean banks, leveraging Claude and DeepSeek language models. The attackers gained access to the LLMs and used them to support intrusion activities. This shows direct weaponization of commercial AI for financial-sector targeting.
