The advisory says the admin order view echoes a guest customer email unescaped into the copy-to-clipboard attribute at `app/design/adminhtml/base/default/template/sales/order/view/info.phtml` line 111, so a crafted address can run script when an administrator opens the order or its invoice, shipment or credit memo pages. It lists affected ranges 20.7.0 through 20.18.2, 20.19.0 before 20.19.1 and 21.0.0-beta2 before 21.0.0-beta4, patched in 20.18.3, 20.19.1 and 21.0.0-beta4. It rates the issue Critical 9.3 with vector CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:C/C:H/I:H/A:N, credits @raylitebull through HackerOne and states that no CVE is known.
