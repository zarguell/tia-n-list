The holiday season brings a surge of invitations, promotional offers, and shared documents — all familiar ways for cybercriminals to disguise malicious activity. During the 2026 Thanksgiving and Black Friday season, ANY.RUN analysts investigated five cases involving fake corporate invitations, e-cards, healthcare surveys, promotional newsletters, and discount coupons. The campaigns aimed to steal credentials, collect personal data, deploy scripts, or gain remote access. Some relied on multi-stage redirects and browser fingerprinting; others abused legitimate services and software rather than traditional malware. These cases show why email inspection alone isn’t enough. SOC and MSSP analysts need to understand what happens after a user clicks a link or opens a file. Let’s look at how each attack worked, the indicators it left behind, and the lessons for defenders.
