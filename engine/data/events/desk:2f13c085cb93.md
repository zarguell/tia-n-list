The WordPress maintainers published GHSA-fpp7-x2x2-2mjf for CVE-2026-60137, rated Moderate: a facilitated SQL injection in the author__not_in parameter of WP_Query. Affected versions span 6.8.0-6.8.5, 6.9.0-6.9.4, and 7.0.0-7.0.1, patched in 6.8.6, 6.9.5, and 7.0.2. On WordPress 6.9 and higher it chains with GHSA-ff9f-jf42-662q to achieve Remote Code Execution. It was discovered and responsibly disclosed as a team by TF1T, dtro, and haongo.
