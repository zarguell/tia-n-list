As of 29 October 2026, five new fugitives, four men and one woman, have been added to the EU Most Wanted list. According to the Limburg Public Prosecutor’s Office (Openbaar Ministerie) and the Dutch Police (Politie), all five suspects are involved in the European Fine Art Fair (TEFAF) robbery that took place on 28 June 2022 in Maastricht, the Netherlands.
