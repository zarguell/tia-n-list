AlCybr reports Cisco Talos' finding that UAT-12643's Overlord RAT reads encrypted command-and-control URLs from Solana transaction memos, so server addresses can change without updating malware on victim machines. It argues that on-chain C2 removes registrar seizure and sinkholing options and pushes defenders toward monitoring RPC traffic and outbound connections. The article also identifies the Good Game infostealer and a clipboard hijacker that replaces copied cryptocurrency wallet addresses.
