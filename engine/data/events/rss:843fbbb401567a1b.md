Welcome to this week’s edition of the Threat Source newsletter. I’m just back from an interesting and demanding week of booth duty at it-sa Expo&Congress in Nuremberg, where Talos had a Point of Demonstration at the Cisco booth. I personally see it-sa as the German or European counterpart to the RSA Conference in San Francisco, with a slightly different center of gravity in at least two dimensions. First, the expo is the main event, not the congress. The congress is organized by associations, industry groups, and exhibitors, and plays more of a supporting role. Second, the focus is more local and European. Major topics were (of course) AI for defense tooling, securing AI systems themselves, and post-quantum cryptography. And here comes the local touch (in my opinion): digital sovereignty and regulatory compliance, with NIS2, CRA, and DORA. But local doesn’t translate to small, or to the city of Nuremberg. With more than 1,000 exhibitors and 33,000 visitors from 70 countries over three days of exhibition, preceded by a congress day, it was huge. Even as a trade show veteran, I was overwhelmed. My conversations at the booth had a different focus, though. It was a lot of chatter about vulnerabilities like php2shell or broccoli2shell and state-sponsored actors, but again with a local touch: education.
