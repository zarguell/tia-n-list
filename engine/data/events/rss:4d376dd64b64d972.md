Yet the increase did not extend across the techniques that had driven threat actor activity earlier in the year. QR code phishing remained far below its early-year peak, while CAPTCHA-gated phishing reversed several months of decline, driven in part by renewed use of SVG attachments. Monthly detected email threat volume (January – September 2026) The quarter also highlighted how sender identity and security exceptions can shape the outcome of detected threats. Microsoft detected approximately 129 million malicious messages spoofing the sender domains of tracked business services. Many were delivered because organization- or user-configured exceptions overrode security protections, even after the messages failed authentication checks and were identified as malicious, showing how overly broad exceptions intended to preserve legitimate workflows can also expose them to impersonation.
