Digital Security Lab Ukraine documented a spearphishing campaign active from June 10, 2026 that impersonated politician Pawel Kowal and the Ukraine Recovery Conference to target Ukrainian civil society. The chain used a reply-gated password-protected ZIP containing a Files.vhdx disk image, an LNK with its script hidden in the Description field, and four scheduled tasks including SecureDNSObserver, with WebDAV delivery from 89[.]125[.]66[.]183 and remote command sources 103[.]160[.]59[.]97 and secure-dns-hub[.]com. These techniques and IoCs overlap with what Microsoft later folded into its RedFlick reporting, making this the earliest public documentation of the chain.
