The joint FBI, CISA, and partner advisory was updated November 13, 2025 to state that Akira threat actors likely abused CVE-2024-40766 for initial access and to list it among the CVEs observed for entry alongside CVE-2023-27532 and CVE-2024-40711 in Veeam. It attributes approximately $244.17 million in claimed ransomware proceeds to Akira as of late September 2025 and documents TTPs including 1.5 to 2 hour access-to-exfiltration intervals, the itadm account, SharpDomainSpray password spraying, and Akira_v2 encryptor extensions .akiranew and .aki.
