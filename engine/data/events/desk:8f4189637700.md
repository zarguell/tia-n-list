Kudelski Security's advisory for CVE-2024-45519 describes an unauthenticated RCE in the postjournal email processing module, exploitable by sending a specially crafted email to a vulnerable server, affecting 8.8.15 up to Patch 45 and 9.0.0 up to Patch 40. It recommends updating to 8.8.15 Patch 46, 9.0.0 Patch 41, 10.0.9, or 10.1.1, restricting or disabling the postjournal service as a workaround, and monitoring system logs for suspicious postjournal activity.
