🚀 I just launched FireBreak, an open-source project I've been building! FireBreak helps you map the connections between your online accounts and understand how recovery and sign-in relationships could affect your security. The idea is simple: if one account gets compromised, which other accounts might be at risk through their recovery paths? 🔎 Explore account dependencies 🛡️ Review security recommendations 💥 Simulate a breach and inspect potential recovery paths I'm continuing to improve it and would love feedback from developers and security enthusiasts. ⭐ Check out the project, try it, and leave a star if you find it useful!
