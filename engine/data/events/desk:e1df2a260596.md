Security Affairs reported that the intrusion disrupted payslips, leave requests and medical reimbursements for more than ten days, with Sysper briefly failing again on 18 November, MyPMO restored and the JSIS health portal still down on 19 November; translation services were hit on 17 November. CERT-EU's 5 November technical note identified ARTEX as a possible tool, but the article stressed that no public information explained how the attackers entered and that ARTEX was a suspected tool rather than a confirmed method or attribution. It also noted that under Regulation 2018/1725 a confirmed personal data breach must be reported to the EDPS within 72 hours, with affected people informed without delay when high risk applies.
