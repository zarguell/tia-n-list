Meterpreter's 20 November 2026 roundup covered CISA's 19 November ICS advisories and placed the Elecbrakes BLE brake bugs beside OpenPLC Runtime and libumqtt flaws. It reports CVE-2026-96805 and CVE-2026-125273 at 9.6 for missing authentication and replay, and CVE-2026-124281 at 6.1 for firmware updates, quoting CISA's warning about brake control, firmware modification and disabled brake lights. The article says no known public attacks or live exploits were known at publication.
