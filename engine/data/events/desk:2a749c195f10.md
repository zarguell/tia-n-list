CERT/CC originally published VU#789985 on 6 March 2003 and last revised it in July 2017. The note states that an intruder with physical access can bypass software-based access controls, boot another operating system, copy or alter encrypted data, and install hardware or Trojan horses. Its recommended controls are restricting physical access to authorized personnel and encrypting data on mobile devices. The advisory frames FastUnlock's underlying condition as a long-recognized physical-access trust boundary rather than a new class of flaw.
