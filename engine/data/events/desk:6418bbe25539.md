Tribeau said monitoring detected suspected unauthorized logins on November 16 into the clinic management application supplied to Shonan Beauty Clinic and Ritz Beauty Surgery Tokyo, and it disabled the affected accounts that day. It reported reservation data for 12,641 people, message content plus date of birth and sex for three of them, and treatment name, date and fee for 6,804 people. The operator said it blocked attacker communications, had not confirmed external publication or misuse of the data, and confirmed the stolen records were not linked to case or review information including photographs.
