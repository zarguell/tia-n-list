The Zero Day Initiative published this advisory on July 15, 2026 as ZDI-CAN-30169, assigning CVE-2026-14266 a CVSS 3.0 score of 7.0 with the vector AV:L/AC:H/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H. It credits Lunbun LLC (Landon Peng) and records that the flaw was reported to the vendor on June 5, 2026, with coordinated public release on July 15, 2026. Crafted XZ-compressed data overflows a heap-based buffer in the processing of XZ chunked data, and the advisory notes the issue was fixed in 7-Zip 26.02.
