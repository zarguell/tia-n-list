The White House said on Monday it nominated Michael LoSapio to be director of the Department of Homeland Security's Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency. LoSapio will take over from acting director Nick Andersen, and the agency has been without a Senate-confirmed leader for almost 22 months. CISA's staffing has fallen by about a third since Trump took power in 2025, and the nomination follows three large-scale breaches at DHS, the FBI and the Pentagon disclosed in the past three months. LoSapio, Palantir's chief information security officer, did not immediately respond to requests for comment.
