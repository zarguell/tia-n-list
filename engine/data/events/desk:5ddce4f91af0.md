TRM Labs attributes the October 29, 2026 Binance theft of approximately USD 6.36 billion to North Korea's TraderTraitor unit. The attacker moved funds across ten blockchains in about 20 minutes starting at 20:05 UTC, using an EIP-7702 delegation to compromise a Binance hot wallet. About USD 2.7 billion was frozen by stablecoin issuers and the Arbitrum Security Council, leaving roughly USD 2.4 billion still under attacker control.
