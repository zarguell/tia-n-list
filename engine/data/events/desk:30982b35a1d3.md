The Hacker News published on November 6, 2026 that QiAnXin XLab researchers captured the cwpsrv_update.sh installer on June 29 and that it targets cwpsrv versions 1.15.10 and 1.24.0. Attackers trigger the backdoor with signed HTTP headers and receive encrypted command output in HTTP 200 responses. The report notes the access log exclusion that removes the forensic trail of the interaction.
