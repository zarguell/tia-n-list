A 49-year-old bricklayer sold his belongings before traveling 2,000 km by bus from his home in Brazil to meet “Jéssica,” an online influencer he had fallen for. Yet despite having a social media account with more than 300,000 followers, she never existed. Edimar Rocha Martins lives in Imperatriz, a city in northeastern Brazil. He says he struck up an online conversation with Jéssica Fazendeira, who supposedly lived near the southeastern city of Belo Horizonte. The AI persona promising love The online persona’s name, “Fazendeira,” loosely translates as “female farmer.” She described herself as living on a farm. Martins said that he had been talking with the persona online since 2020. He decided to visit her after she offered to pay for his trip and have him live on the farm with her, he said. The account’s operator disputes this, saying the character was created in 2025 and denying any private messages with him. A video posted on one of her online accounts (captured here by Brazilian news service Imirante) asked for a man to come and live with her and offered to fund the trip. The AI-generated farmer says, translated: I’m not joking, my intentions are serious. I want to meet someone who wants to build something real and beautiful with me. If you’re a simple man who likes peace and quiet and a calm life, then maybe we’d get along really well.
