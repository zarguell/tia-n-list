CISA has added CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Federal civilian agencies have been directed to remediate the vulnerabilities by October 29. There is an important correction to earlier reporting. Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 retracted its claim that PHP2Shell caused a European government agency compromise. Researchers confirmed that an attack occurred against a vulnerable PHP application, but could not establish that either PHP2Shell CVE provided initial access. This directly relates to our discussion yesterday about honeypots versus production compromises. CISA recognizes active exploitation, but the specific government intrusion should not be presented as confirmed PHP2Shell exploitation. submitted by /u/BTC-brother2018 [link] [comments]
