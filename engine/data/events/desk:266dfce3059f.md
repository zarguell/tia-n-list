This high-severity advisory, published 2026-08-12, reports that several model-artifact APIs treat caller-controlled paths as ordinary filenames: TransitionParser.train and TransitionParser.parse read and write outside the allowed roots, and AveragedPerceptron.save/load plus PerceptronTagger.save_to_json do the same, using built-in open() where pathsec-aware helpers reject the identical paths. The advisory names NLTK 3.10.3 as affected and 3.10.4 as patched, which is why 3.10.4 is the minimum safe version for the sandbox story as a whole.
