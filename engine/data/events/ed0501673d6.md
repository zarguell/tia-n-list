The White House issued a revised AI executive order that significantly weakens the federal government's role in vetting frontier AI models: companies will voluntarily provide access for "up to" 30 days (down from 90), the program explicitly states it is not mandatory or a licensing regime, and AI companies can help define which models qualify for testing. The Treasury Department will lead a new interagency cybersecurity clearinghouse for vulnerability coordination.

**Reaction:** Senator Mark Warner called it "a rehash of proposals" from the prior administration's 2023 EO, while AI czar David Sacks praised the shortened timeline as "a game changer" for industry. The order was released hours after Anthropic announced Project Glasswing's expansion — spotlighting the growing gap between rapid private-sector AI security capabilities and lagging federal policy frameworks.
