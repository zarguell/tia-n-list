The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security advisory AL26-020, dated September 10, 2026, warns of MikroTik RouterOS vulnerabilities, especially where SSH is exposed to the internet. It references a vendor advisory released September 3, 2026 and the centre's own AV26-887 from September 8, 2026. CVE-2026-67277 is a Missing Authentication for Critical Function flaw (CWE-306) that may allow a remote attacker to obtain potentially sensitive information.
