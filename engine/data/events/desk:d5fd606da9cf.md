Metro reported that the "Red Owl" signature and Mikhail Andropov name had also appeared on threatening emails sent to European television stations the previous week, and that a similar email reached 271 Italian schools on November 2 claiming to be from the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. Hoax bomb threats had already hit schools across Scotland and Northern Ireland in October, with as many as 300 schools receiving messages before 9am. The reporting establishes the November 11 wave as the latest in a multi-wave campaign rather than an isolated incident.
