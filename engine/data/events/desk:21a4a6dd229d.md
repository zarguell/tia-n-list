Cyber Kendra reported that the PHP security team would ship a security release on Wednesday, October 21, 2026 at 14:00 UTC to fix two critical vulnerabilities rated CVSS v4.0 9.2, after information about the embargoed flaws leaked online. The article notes that full details, affected versions, and fixed versions would be published with the release and that Wednesday was a day earlier than the date researchers under embargo had been working toward.
