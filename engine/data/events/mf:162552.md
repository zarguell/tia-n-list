A new QR code safety feature designed to reduce phishing and fraud risks in chats involving external users. As documented by Microsoft, the capability will, by default, obscure images containing QR codes when sent by external participants, requiring recipients to deliberately reveal the image before they can view or scan the code. Listed as in development on the Microsoft 365 roadmap, the feature is scheduled to begin rolling out in October 2026. Microsoft Teams Adds QR Code Protection Microsoft has assigned the update Roadmap ID 570439 and plans to make it available worldwide across Teams desktop, web-associated desktop experiences, Android, iOS, and Mac platforms. QR codes are increasingly used as a social-engineering delivery mechanism because they can conceal a destination URL from the user until it is scanned. An attacker can place a malicious QR code inside what appears to be an ordinary image, invoice, onboarding document, support request, or collaboration message. Microsoft’s planned Teams protection introduces friction before a QR code received from an external sender can be acted upon. Rather than displaying the QR image in the chat immediately, Teams will obscure it by default. Users who need to inspect the content must explicitly reveal the image before viewing or scanning it. This approach is intended to encourage a pause before users interact with QR-based content. The additional step may help employees notice that a message originated outside their organization
