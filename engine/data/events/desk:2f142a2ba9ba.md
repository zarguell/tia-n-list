Citrix published security advisory CTX697170 on October 13, 2026 for XenServer 8.4 and XenServer 9, rating the bulletin High severity. Three issues may allow a malicious privileged user in a guest VM to compromise or crash the host: CVE-2026-79610, CVE-2026-102700 and CVE-2026-102701, with CVE-2026-102700 affecting XenServer 8.4 only. A fourth issue, CVE-2026-79611, may let an unprivileged guest VM user crash that guest. Updates were pushed to the Early Access and Normal update channels.
