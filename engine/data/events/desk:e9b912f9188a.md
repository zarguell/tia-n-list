Advisory FG-IR-26-219, published November 10, 2026, covers CVE-2026-126170, an improper encoding or escaping of output [CWE-116] rated 9.8 in the FortiSASE-Sovereign, FortiAnalyzer, FortiManager, FortiAnalyzer Cloud, FortiManager Cloud, and FortiAnalyzer Big-Data web interfaces that lets an unauthenticated attacker execute code with a crafted HTTP request. FortiAnalyzer 8.0.0 through 8.0.2 and FortiAnalyzer Cloud 8.0.1 through 8.0.2 are fixed in the upcoming 8.0.3 or by applying a workaround, and FortiAnalyzer 7.6.0 through 7.6.7 moves to 7.6.8, with 7.4 and 7.2 listed as not affected. This is the highest-scoring unauthenticated web-path flaw in the November 10 batch.
