Hackers possibly aligned with the Pakistani government have targeted Indian defense contractors and military personnel in a cyberespionage campaign to install spyware on victim devices — but their tactics could also hit the defense sector elsewhere around the world, according to research out Tuesday from Lookout. The mobile security firm, in research first shared with CyberScoop, said it observed activity dating back 12 months that remains active using the GravityRAT malware commonly deployed by the Pakistani-aligned group known as SpaceCobra or Cosmic Leopard. What stands out is how far hackers went to make it believable, with fake job recruiters building realistic, detailed websites to trick victims into downloading the malware. One website featured an “our clients” section with logos of defense firms such as Airbus, BAE Systems, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, Rheinmetall, Rolls-Royce, Saab, Safran and Thales. Some of the websites’ social engineering was specific to India, some of it was not.
