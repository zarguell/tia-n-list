CISA’s latest red team advisory warns critical infrastructure operators: large security budgets and sophisticated tools cannot compensate for a security operations center that fails to recognize, escalate, and contain malicious activity. The report, “A Tale of Two SOCs,” compares parallel assessments of a Government Services and Facilities Sector organization and a Water and Wastewater Systems Sector entity. CISA operators used nearly identical tradecraft but observed radically different outcomes. In both engagements, the red team began with phishing to establish an initial foothold. The operators then abused common Active Directory weaknesses, including the default Machine Account Quota setting and misconfigured Active Directory Certificate Services templates. These conditions enabled privilege escalation, credential access, and lateral movement across enterprise environments, demonstrating how long-standing identity misconfigurations can turn a single compromised endpoint into a domain-wide incident.
