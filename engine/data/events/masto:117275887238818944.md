BambooToken uses MQTT, a lightweight IoT messaging protocol, as its C2 channel for controlling Windows and Linux systems. By abusing legitimate MQTT brokers, the malware avoids direct connections to attacker infrastructure, increasing evasion and resilience. Communications are asynchronous, ensuring operational continuity during temporary network disruptions.
