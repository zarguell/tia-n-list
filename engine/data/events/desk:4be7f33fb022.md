Suriq's Jack published a deep-dive on CVE-2026-93698, a 9.9-rated OS command-injection weakness (CWE-78) in the cPanel Multilang adminbin. The flaw lets a low-privileged hosting account run arbitrary commands on the server with root-level privileges. The article notes the fix was included in the cPanel and WHM update dated September 29, 2026, and advises that every account on an unpatched server should be considered able to reach the vulnerability.
