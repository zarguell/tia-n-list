SparroWocky: new malware attributed to Chinese actors targeting government entities. What stands out: the name evokes both SparrowDoor (the predecessor) and Wocky (likely a reference to communication protocols). ESET researchers documented SparroWocky replacing SparrowDoor in attacks against government organizations in Latin America, including Argentina, Ecuador, Guatemala, Honduras, Panama, Peru, Puerto Rico, and Venezuela.
