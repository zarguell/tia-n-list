Overview On November 24, 2026, Broadcom released VMware Security Advisory VMSA-2026-0011 to address several new vulnerabilities, including CVE-2026-123903 , a critical authentication bypass in the VMware Directory Service component of vCenter. Broadcom assigned it a CVSSv3.1 score of 9.8 Critical . An attacker who can reach an affected vCenter’s VMware Directory Service (vmdir) over the network can gain access without authenticating. Broadcom's supplemental FAQ distinguishes CVE-2026-123903 from an older vulnerability CVE-2026-123890 , which Rapid7 covered previously when CVE-2026-123890 was reported as being exploited in the wild. Both of these vulnerabilities affect the VMware Directory Service in vCenter and can give an attacker access to the vCenter without credentials. CVE-2026-123890 was reachable over RPC while CVE-2026-123903 is reachable over LDAP. As of this writing, there are no known public reports of exploitation of CVE-2026-123903 in the wild. A public proof-of-concept exploit is available and has been validated by Rapid7 to successfully achieve an authentication bypass. Attackers are known to be targeting vulnerabilities in the VMware Directory Service, therefore Rapid7 recommends remediating exposed systems on an urgent basis outside of normal patching cycles. Mitigation guidance Vendor updates are available for CVE-2026-123903. Broadcom lists no workaround , and these vCenter updates are not eligible for Quick Patch.
