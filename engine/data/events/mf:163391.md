The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added two vulnerabilities in MikroTik RouterOS to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, warning that these flaws are actively being exploited in the wild. On September 10, CISA listed CVE-2026-67277 and CVE-2026-86060, giving affected organizations until September 13 to implement vendor-recommended mitigations.
