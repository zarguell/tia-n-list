A Kazakh national has been extradited from Italy to face charges alleging he targeted US schools, hospitals, and healthcare entities in ransomware attacks. 43-year-old Roman Khlynovskiy was an alleged member of the "8-Digits Team" ransomware group, known for its affiliation with the INC and Lynx ransomware operations. Together with his accomplices, Khlynovskiy is believed to have extorted victims in at least six US states (including hospitals and schools) after breaching their networks and stealing sensitive data. After exfiltrating data from victims' compromised systems, they pressured them to pay the ransoms by threatening to release the stolen files online. "The conspirators boasted that they possessed over 50 terabytes of data consisting of more than 70 million confidential health records," the Department of Justice said . "The conspirators also threatened to publicize this highly sensitive medical information to increase pressure on victims to pay ransom demands. The coconspirators' actions resulted in over $500 million in ransom demands." Khlynovskiy faces up to 27 years in prison if convicted of conspiracy to commit computer fraud, identity theft, wire fraud, extortion, money laundering, and threats to disclose non-consensual intimate images. The INC Ransom cybercrime gang, whose leak site lists over 1,000 victims, emerged as a ransomware-as-a-service (RaaS) operation in July 2023.
