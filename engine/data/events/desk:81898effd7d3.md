CNN first reported that apparent hackers had intruded into the FBI National Crime Information Center and that two people briefed on the matter said the bureau was investigating. The database is used nationwide for warrants and background checks, and the article notes this as the second reported FBI breach in recent months after the September ShinyHunters intrusion of an FBI jobs site. A former FBI official quoted by CNN said the main concern was not only data theft but whether attackers could compromise the accuracy of records officers rely on. No actor, scope, or exfiltration count was disclosed.
