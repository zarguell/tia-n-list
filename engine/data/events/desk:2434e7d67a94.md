Reuters reported that a Chinese-speaking hacker used autonomous AI agents to attempt to breach more than 1,200 websites and gained access to sites belonging to more than 100 companies, including Deere, SharkNinja, and Cummins. The operator's own records showed roughly $5,000 in token spending, or about $50 per successful compromise, and instructions were sketched in fewer than 100 Chinese characters. Cairn, the open-source tool used, lets agents work toward a goal step by step and post findings to a shared board, and its author said it was not designed for hacking. Reuters reviewed the attacker's artifacts and named victims from its own review of the activity.
