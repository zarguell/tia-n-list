In France, one in five ransomware attacks is reported to have exploited AI systems at victim companies. AI can assist cybercriminals in reconnaissance and targeting, making ransomware campaigns faster and more selective. The claim comes from a French technology outlet and should be treated as indicative rather than confirmed statistics.
