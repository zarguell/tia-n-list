Attackers are exploiting CVE-2026-84869, a critical flaw in the ConnectWise ScreenConnect host client, to run files on Managed Service Provider (MSP) technicians’ computers by joining their support sessions as guests. Huntress confirmed one November 12, 2026 compromise in which a technician’s workstation was running an attacker-controlled remote access client about nine seconds after a guest pushed a script. What Is CVE-2026-84869? CVE-2026-84869 is a vulnerability in the ScreenConnect host client, the application MSP technicians use to join remote support sessions. It carries a CVSS score of 9.9 and is classed as an improper privilege management and missing authorization issue. ConnectWise fixed it in September 2026, but the patch gap sits on the technician side: researchers found host clients on 22.x, 24.2 and 25.8 builds still in use weeks after the fix.
