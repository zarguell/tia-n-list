Hospitals close portals preventively after critical Citrix flaw, care continues

An extremely critical zero-day vulnerability in Citrix software prompted hospitals in the Brabant and Friesland regions to take their patient portals offline as a precaution to prevent theft of medical records. Patient care continued. The hospitals stressed the shutdowns were a precautionary measure, not the result of a hack. The Dutch National Cyber Security Centrum (NCSC) reported the vulnerabilities affect Citrix NetScaler, leaving patients unable to view their records while the portals are down.
