CU*SOUTH told credit unions that CTRs batch filed through CBX were being rejected by FinCEN because of a service disruption affecting the BSA E-Filing System. An update on November 20 said CTRs transmitted from November 18 onward should be filed directly through the FinCEN portal, and a November 23 update said those reports were showing as Ready to Transmit in tool #991 Work with CTR Forms. The notice instructed filers who had already filed directly to change the filing type from Amended to Initial and save the form to avoid duplicate filings.
