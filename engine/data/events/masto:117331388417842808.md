The Hugging Face hack, more convincing phishing emails, and above all the speed: AI is turning cybersecurity upside down

German outlet t3n argues that AI and the agents built on it are upending cybersecurity practice. The piece points to the hack of Hugging Face, AI-generated phishing mails that read far more convincingly than older spam, and above all the sheer speed at which AI-equipped agents operate, as forces that break existing defense assumptions.
