A CISO who traded enterprise budgets for higher ed on what “creativity within constraints” really means, and the one habit worth stealing from how he leads. I have known Matt Martin for a few years, and the first thing I tell anyone about him has nothing to do with security. It is that he treats the work as a way to serve other people. I first knew Matt as the security leader who spends his own time flying to Ghana and Uganda to teach executives and students how to protect themselves. That instinct, more than any tool or certification, explains how he runs a security program. Matt spent two decades running security organizations in financial services, including at LPL Financial, with an enterprise budget and any tool he needed. A few years ago he made a deliberate choice to take on higher ed instead, because he wanted to prove he could build a resilient security program under real constraints. Today he is the Chief Information Security and Privacy Officer at Western Carolina University . I asked Matt to share his journey from a conference in Accra to a university in the mountains of North Carolina. His story is really about doing more with less, and right now, that is all of us.
