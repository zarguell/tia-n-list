Newsbytes reported that the National Privacy Commission is investigating an alleged theft of 68GB of database backups linked to Philippine Department of Migrant Workers systems. The investigation follows an Oct. 28 report by Hunt.io, which said it recovered transfer logs showing 27 Microsoft SQL Server backup files totaling 68.837 GiB were copied on Sept. 6, 2026, in less than 24 minutes. Hunt.io traced two servers involved in subsequent file retrievals to government portal domains under dmw.gov.ph, and said it notified the relevant national computer emergency response team before publishing.
