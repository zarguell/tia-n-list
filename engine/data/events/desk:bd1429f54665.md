CISA added CVE-2025-64328 to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on February 3, 2026 with a required-action due date of February 24, 2026, citing evidence of active exploitation. The entry describes the post-authentication command injection in Sangoma FreePBX Endpoint Manager via check_ssh_connect() and requires applying vendor mitigations or discontinuing use under BOD 22-01 guidance.
