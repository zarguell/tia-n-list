NVD published the CVE-2026-19868 entry on 6 November 2026 with SonicWall PSIRT as the source, describing an authentication enforcement flaw in the SonicOS SSL-VPN service. The entry notes that a user with valid credentials for an alternate authentication method may bypass SAML authentication controls, including SAML-enforced MFA, and CISA marks exploitation as active. SonicWall's advisory identifier is SNWLID-2026-0014.
