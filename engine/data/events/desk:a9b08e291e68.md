SentinelLabs researcher Dakota Cary mapped Justice Department indictments to Shanghai Firetech, a company the DOJ said works on behalf of the Shanghai State Security Bureau and took part in Silk Typhoon attacks while previously being identified in the 2021 Hafnium campaign. The review found patents for offensive tooling, including home-appliance analysis and evidence collection software that could support surveillance of individuals abroad. Cary concluded the company serves offensive missions beyond the Hafnium cluster.
