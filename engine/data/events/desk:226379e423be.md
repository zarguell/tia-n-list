The vendor advisory behind the story headline, posted September 22, 2026, promoted Chrome 154 to stable as 154.0.8037.57/.58 for Windows and Mac and 154.0.8037.57 for Linux with 108 security fixes, 11 of them critical. The criticals are all memory-safety defects: three ANGLE buffer overflows including CVE-2026-95350 (a $5,000 bounty, reported by STAR Labs researchers on 2026-08-24), CVE-2026-95281, and CVE-2026-95284, plus GPU out-of-bounds writes, WebGL bugs, and use-after-free defects in ServiceWorker, Fullscreen, WindowDialog, and AdFilter. It also credits OpenAI Codex Security researcher amyb with two V8 bugs, CVE-2026-95304 and CVE-2026-95306, reported on 2026-09-12, and notes bug details may stay restricted until a majority of users are updated.
