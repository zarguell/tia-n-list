DATA BREACH: Notion AI user conversations exposed after a double cyberattack at Fireworks AI.

Notion told its customers that they were affected through the third-party AI vendor, Fireworks AI. Fireworks disclosed two linked incidents: unauthorized use of internal credentials on October 6, 2026 that exposed environment variables holding API keys and tokens for a limited number of customer accounts, and a breach of a third-party cloud provider hosting Fireworks research and development machines on October 23, which an unauthorized party used on October 24 to reach Fireworks development machines and run an unauthorized workload.
