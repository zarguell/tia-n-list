Cyber Daily reports the company's 26 November ASX filing, naming Flight Centre, Travel Associates, Cruiseabout and some independent agents as the brands using the affected accommodation system while flight and corporate travel bookings are unaffected. It quotes the disclosure that a significant number of records may have been accessed but that the number extracted is unknown. The article records the split in data types, mostly names and basic travel information with a small percentage of passport details, email addresses, phone numbers and dates of birth, and states the company does not believe bank or credit card data was accessed.
