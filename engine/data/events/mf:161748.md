A malicious Chinese-language resume is targeting academic researchers with SNOWLIGHT shellcode and the VShell remote-access trojan (RAT) , using a believable graduate-school application as cover. The ZIP archive claims to contain a resume from a fresh Beijing Institute of Technology graduate named Zhang Yuguang. Instead, it contains a Windows executable disguised with a document-style filename. When opened, the malware launches a real Word document to avoid suspicion while silently deploying a multi-stage, fileless infection chain. The decoy resume presents the supposed applicant as a network-engineering graduate interested in AI-based power-grid diagnosis, intelligent electrical systems, renewable-energy control, and power-system automation. Its language suggests it was designed for a professor or research-group leader rather than a corporate recruiter. Several details indicate the document was tailored for a mainland Chinese academic setting, including Simplified Chinese, PRC education terms, WPS Office metadata, and language asking to join a supervisor’s research group. However, the lure does not prove the attacker’s identity, location, or connection to Beijing Institute of Technology.
