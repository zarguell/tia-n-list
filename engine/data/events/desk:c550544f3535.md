Google Cloud's GTIG and Mandiant, working with ManageEngine's METAL lab, disclosed that threat cluster UNC7017 conducted repeated short-burst (five to ten minute) configuration tampering on AOMEI origin servers between April and July 2026. During each window the attacker modified down_html.ini and Notifycfg.ini to redirect AOMEI Backupper clients to a malicious upgrade-now.zip carrying the CROSSROAD downloader and SHINYROAD backdoor, then reverted the files to authentic content. The report documents three confirmed windows (May 28, July 16, July 20) plus a WebDAV NTLM credential coercion vector and extensive indicators of compromise. Published October 16, 2026.
