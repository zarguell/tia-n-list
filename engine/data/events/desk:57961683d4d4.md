XLab, the research arm of QiAnXin Technology Group, disclosed CWPdoor on 2026-11-05: a passive backdoor built into a trojanized cwpsrv binary that replaces Control Web Panel versions 1.15.10 and 1.24.0, triggered by HTTP requests carrying X-Ngx-Integrity-Time, X-Ngx-Integrity-Data and X-Ngx-Integrity-Sig headers while suppressing logs of those requests. XLab said it first captured the installer script cwpsrv_update.sh on 2026-06-29, fetching the payload from 136.0.141.71, and that a cwpdoor sample named cwpsrv.suspeito with zero VirusTotal detections was uploaded from Brazil in the evidencia.tgz archive on 2026-09-30 alongside an Ebury backdoor, indicating real-world victims. More than 340,000 internet-exposed CWP instances were potential targets, according to the researchers.
