Security control validation fits in the validation stage of Continuous Threat Exposure Management (CTEM) , the fourth of its five stages. In this stage, it safely runs real attacker techniques against your controls, most often with breach and attack simulation (BAS) , and shows whether each one is prevented, detected, or missed. Its results then feed two other stages: prioritization uses them to re-rank exposures, and mobilization uses them to fix the controls that missed a technique.
