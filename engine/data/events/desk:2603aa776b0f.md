Cesanta and Trend Micro's Zero Day Initiative published GHSA-4mgp-8m9m-q872 for ZDI-CAN-29386, rating the MIP heap overflow CVSS 3.1 9.8 with no CVE identifier assigned. The advisory says a TCP FIN carrying payload makes read_conn() in net_builtin.c skip the receive-buffer resize check and reach a memcpy, giving an attacker control over the overflow size, content, and write offset. Version 7.23 was tested, versions up to 7.23 are listed as affected, and 7.24 is the patched version. The proof of concept sends 400 bytes of normal data into the default 512-byte MG_IO_SIZE receive buffer, then a FIN carrying 400 bytes of payload, overflowing by 288 bytes.
