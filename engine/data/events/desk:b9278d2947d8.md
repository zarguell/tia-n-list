ITmedia NEWS reported on November 6, 2026 that Iris Plaza announced on November 5 that about 7.7 million people's personal data stored in an external cloud service may have leaked. The breakdown is about 5.8 million members including former members and about 1.9 million non-members, with member fields such as name, address, phone, email, gender, encrypted password and date of birth, and non-member fields such as name, address, phone and email. Iris Plaza stated it would contact affected customers individually from November 5 and that no credit card data was stored in the affected cloud service.
