mSeven Software's support forum opened an Important Security Notice Nov 3, 2026 discussion on November 3, 2026 at 12:36 AM Pacific time, reproducing the vendor notice sent to current and former mSecure customers. The notice urges recipients to change all stored passwords, reset two-factor authentication, and remove passkeys after suspicious activity tied to the application. Customer replies in the thread describe the email as vague and say it contains no technical details or scope of impact.
