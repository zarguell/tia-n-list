Claroty Team82 published research on August 9, 2026 identifying three vulnerabilities in the Danfoss AK-SM 800A refrigeration controller (CVE-2025-41450, CVE-2025-41451, CVE-2025-41452), including an authentication bypass via a hidden code-of-the-day mechanism and command injection through the SMTP email configuration page. The researchers demonstrated that the hidden authentication algorithm could be reconstructed to generate valid daily passwords, granting administrative access to the embedded web interface.
