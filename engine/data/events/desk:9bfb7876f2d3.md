Fortinet's advisory, initially published January 13, 2026, describes CVE-2025-25249 as a heap-based buffer overflow in the FortiOS and FortiSwitchManager cw_acd daemon allowing remote unauthenticated code execution via crafted requests. Affected releases span FortiOS 7.6.0 through 7.6.3, 7.4.0 through 7.4.8, 7.2.0 through 7.2.11, 7.0.0 through 7.0.17, all FortiOS 6.4, and FortiSwitchManager 7.2.0 through 7.2.6 and 7.0.0 through 7.0.5, with fixes at 7.6.4, 7.4.9, 7.2.12, 7.0.18, 7.2.7 and 7.0.6. Suggested workarounds remove fabric access per interface or block CAPWAP-CONTROL on UDP 5246 to 5249 through a local-in policy. The page credits internal discovery by Gwendal Guégniaud of the Fortinet Product Security Team and still lists Known Exploited as No.
