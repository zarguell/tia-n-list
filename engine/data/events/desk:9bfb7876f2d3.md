Fortinet's advisory for CVE-2025-25249 was initially published on 2026-01-13 and last updated 2026-02-23, describing a CWE-122 heap-based buffer overflow in the FortiOS and FortiSwitchManager cw_acd daemon that may allow remote unauthenticated code execution. The vendor rated it High with a CVSSv3 score of 7.4, marked Known Exploited as No at publication, and noted that ASLR and PIE considerably raise the complexity and preparation effort required for exploitation. Workarounds are removing the fabric service from interface allowaccess settings or blocking CAPWAP-CONTROL on UDP ports 5246-5249 with local-in policies. A timeline entry dated 2026-02-23 removed FortiOS 6.4.17 from the fixed-version list, leaving all FortiOS 6.4 releases without an in-branch fix.
