The Fortinet PSIRT advisory behind the story's CVE, published January 13, 2026 (page date only) and last updated February 23, 2026. It documents CVE-2025-25249 as a heap-based buffer overflow (CWE-122) in the FortiOS and FortiSwitchManager cw_acd daemon allowing remote unauthenticated code execution via crafted requests, discovered internally by Gwendal Guegniaud, scored CVSSv3 7.4 with a temporal vector, and notes ASLR and PIE considerably raise exploitation effort. It lists affected branches and fixes: FortiOS 7.6.4, 7.4.9, 7.2.12, 7.0.18 or above, migration off 6.4, FortiSwitchManager 7.2.7 and 7.0.6 or above, and two workarounds: removing 'fabric' from interface allowaccess, or blocking CAPWAP-CONTROL on UDP ports 5246-5249 with local-in policies that admit only trusted devices.
