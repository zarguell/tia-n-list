Drupal published an installer-hardening guide on 18 November 2026 stating that the installer at /core/install.php is needed only during installation and should be blocked afterward. It recommends denying the path in the Apache or Nginx server configuration, warns that an exact-match Nginx rule can miss suffixed paths such as /core/install.php/foo, and gives .htaccess and file-removal alternatives for sites without server-config access. The guide says curl checks of /core/install.php, /install.php and /core/install.php/foo should all end in 403 or 404, and that blocking the installer does not affect update.php.
