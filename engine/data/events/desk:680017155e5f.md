Broadcom released VMSA-2024-0012 on June 17, 2024, fixing CVE-2024-37079, an out-of-bounds write vulnerability in the implementation of the DCERPC protocol in VMware vCenter Server, alongside CVE-2024-37080 and CVE-2024-37081. NVD cites this notification as the vendor advisory and patch reference. Broadcom's companion FAQ records that the advisory carried no workarounds and that at release VMware was not aware of exploitation in the wild.
