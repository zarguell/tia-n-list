Red Hat's CVE record went public September 23, 2026 at 12:00 UTC, rated Important at CVSS 3.1 7.8 (AV:L/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H), CWE-863. The mitigation is to append the Z suffix to all NOTBEFORE and NOTAFTER timestamps so they parse as UTC via timegm(), auditable with grep -rE 'NOT(BEFORE|AFTER)=' /etc/sudoers /etc/sudoers.d/. The statement notes sudo's existing unsetenv("TZ") and tzset() startup defense is insufficient on some platforms and that RHEL 7 through 10 and RHIVOS ship affected sudo, with per-stream analysis pending.
