SDV Sector reported on 20 November 2026 that three CISA-listed flaws in Elecbrakes EB2, VM1 and ELBC2000 controllers could let attackers control trailer brakes, change firmware or disable brake lights. It says fixes reach owners only if they install them through the Elecbrakes app, and that the discontinued ELBC2000 has no fix. CISA told the outlet that no known public exploitation of the flaws had been reported.
