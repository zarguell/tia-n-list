GHSA-8332-j7wj-wjw4 discloses a prototype-pollution class issue in Mongoose before 6.13.12, 7.8.13, 8.24.7 and 9.11.4, where three code paths copy data by property name without own-property checks during document loading, dotted-path sets and mergeClone. Attacker keys such as toString or $__schema can overwrite shared built-in functions or schema validators, causing denial of service or altered validation across unrelated queries until restart. The advisory rates it CVSS 8.2 high and states it is fixed in the listed versions, with no additional mitigation indicated.
