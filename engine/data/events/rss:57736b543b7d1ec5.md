Overview Today, Eclypsium released new research demonstrating the importance of addressing security risks in the infrastructure used for training and running AI models and agents, as well as the complex supply chains behind AI infrastructure components. If left unaddressed, infrastructure-level vulnerabilities can allow threat actors to steal and manipulate AI models during live inference or impact the availability of the AI infrastructure itself. This research underscores the importance of building specialized security capabilities and solutions focused on protecting AI infrastructure and its supply chains. Eclypsium is working with NVIDIA to advance infrastructure assurance through integration with the Open Agent Safety Platform . Why It Matters Global investment in AI infrastructure is projected to exceed $1 trillion annually , with AI expected to contribute $15.7 trillion to global GDP . Yet today’s assurance practices focus almost exclusively on model safety and governance, not the underlying hardware and firmware that determines whether a model executes faithfully.
