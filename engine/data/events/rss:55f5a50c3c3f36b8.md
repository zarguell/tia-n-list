Reference here Intro: Policy Purpose, Scope, and Threat Context As the enterprise rapidly integrates artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning models across critical business functions, administrative processes, and technology infrastructure, our governance and risk management paradigms MUST evolve in lockstep. The rapid deployment of probabilistic models and autonomous software agents offers unprecedented operational efficiency, but it simultaneously introduces novel structural vulnerabilities that can compromise enterprise integrity, business continuity, and organizational trust. Security controls cannot be treated as discretionary constraints secondary to deployment speed; digital trust requires that security engineering and oversight advance at the exact pace of technology adoption. This policy framework establishes a binding enterprise paradigm designed to harmonize AI innovation with deterministic risk controls, clear executive accountability, and resilient operational governance.
