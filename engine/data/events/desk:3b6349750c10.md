Yonhap's lead wire reports personal information of around 25,000 customers leaked from Shinhan Bank via its loan borrowing service, with loan records, annual income, names and phone numbers exposed on September 30. Sources told Yonhap the hackers, suspected to be based overseas, used advanced AI tools, and experts assessed Shinhan was caught in random AI-powered attacks on vulnerable online platforms rather than being specifically targeted. The financial watchdog conducted an on-site inspection.
