ESET researchers Peter Strycek and colleagues disclosed a previously unknown WinRAR zero-day, CVE-2025-8088, a path traversal vulnerability enabled through NTFS alternate data streams, exploited in the wild by the Russia-aligned RomCom group. ESET telemetry placed spearphishing campaigns between July 18 and July 21, 2025 against financial, manufacturing, defense, and logistics companies in Europe and Canada, delivering SnipBot, RustyClaw, and Mythic backdoors. WinRAR 7.12 was affected; a fixed beta shipped July 24, 2025 and the patched full release followed.
