The Progress Kemp LoadMaster vendor bulletin, which WatchTowr and eSentire date to a June 4, 2026 disclosure and which now carries a July 31, 2026 page date, describes critical CVE-2026-8037 (command injection RCE in the API) and high CVE-2026-33691 (whitespace padding in filenames bypasses OWASP CRS file upload extension checks via crafted multipart requests). It affects Kemp LoadMaster GA v7.2.63.1 and older and LTSF v7.2.54.17 and older, fixed in GA v7.2.63.2 and LTSF v7.2.54.18, with the same CVEs also covered for ECS Connection Manager and Connection Manager for ObjectScale at v7.2.63.2. At publication Progress stated it had no reports of exploitation and no known indicators of compromise.
