Red Hat rates CVE-2026-98432 as a Moderate-severity use-after-free in the Linux kernel IPv4 networking subsystem affecting RHEL 8, 9, and 10 (including Real Time kernel); RHEL 7 is not affected. The CVE page scores it 5.9 while Red Hat's machine-readable advisory later lists Important impact and 8.1, and the public date is October 15, 2026. Fixes were deferred to a future update as of October 29, 2026.
