Socket analyzed an October 8, 2026 burst in which two compromised maintainer accounts committed a workflow named security-audit.yml into 346 repositories, including uber/athenadriver, and extended collection from CI/CD secrets to cloud credentials. The report is the vendor-side telemetry behind the October wave, documenting the same stolen-token-into-workflow pattern that the November wfx-loot campaign later scaled to tens of thousands of probe pull requests.
