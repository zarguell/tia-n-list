GitGuardian reported that between October 13 and 21 the GhostAction operator pushed 72,026 commits across 22,964 repositories, and that from October 22 to 26 PolinRider loaders were added to 188 GhostAction commits that were then force-pushed. It identified the same Ethereum sender wallet later seen in the npm loaders, 0x1eef0849eda15d87f64e51c73c8442cf3c8bcef3, and listed 95.135.181.151 as fallback C2. The report describes five-stage PolinRider chains that resolve C2 addresses from Ethereum transactions and download XOR-encoded stages from /0x/cls and /0x/ls. GitGuardian said there were no clear indicators that the GhostAction and PolinRider operators were the same.
