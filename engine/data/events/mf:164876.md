Italy's CERT-AGID analyzed a malicious campaign that abuses the name and visual identity of the Servizio Sanitario Nazionale, Italy's national health service, to distribute malware against both Android devices and Windows systems. The healthcare theme is the centerpiece of the campaign and is used as a trust element to convince the user to launch the file they received.
