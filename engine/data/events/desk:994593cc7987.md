Hispion reported on 19 November 2026 that nearby attackers could manipulate braking, flash modified firmware and disable brake lights over BLE without pairing on Elecbrakes EB2, VM1 and ELBC2000 controllers. The article names CVE-2026-96805, CVE-2026-125273 and CVE-2026-124281, with maximum CVSS 3.1 score 9.6, and notes that fixes exist for EB2 and VM1 but not the discontinued ELBC2000. It says authorities confirmed no evidence of active in-the-wild exploitation at disclosure and places the devices in Australia and New Zealand transportation.
