DreamHost's web-based File Manager has been unavailable since October 23, 2026. During the outage the company expired passwords for some email and FTP/SFTP users as a precaution, and affected users cannot sign in until the account owner sets a replacement password. DreamHost asked account owners to check each mailbox for unfamiliar forwarding rules and warned that it will never email users asking for passwords. The company said it was still investigating the cause and had not confirmed that credentials were accessed.
