Yahoo reported that Trezor treated a single report of an implanted Safe 5 from CryptoBilis as credible before examining the device and credited SEAL 911 for help with the response. The company removed CryptoBilis as a reseller on October 9 following reports of tampered Ledger devices sold through it. An onchain analyst tallied more than $86 million drained from Ledger users across the related CryptoBilis incident.
