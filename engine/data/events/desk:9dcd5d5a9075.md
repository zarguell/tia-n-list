Philipp Esselbach reported on November 17, 2026 that Tails removed uBlock Origin rather than ship a partial fix, even though the upstream correction already existed in 1.75.4. The article identifies GHSA-cmqw-9r2w-6wv2, describes the picker hijack and the trusted filter smuggled through a newline in the `opt` field, and explains the UUID leak via a default uAssets filter rule. It notes the Tails statement that no exploitation in practice was known and that a restart wipes the planted configuration.
