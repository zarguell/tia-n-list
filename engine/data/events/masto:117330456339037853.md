Ransomware attack: Kreishandwerkerschaft Borken, the district trades guild in Borken, Germany. The incident listing records a data leak, ransomware deployment, data theft, and handling of stolen data (Datenhehlerei), attributes the attack to TeamInfoSec, and classifies it as a cyberattack. Details are published by the German incident tracker security-incidents.de.
