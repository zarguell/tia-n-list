The GeoServer advisory describes unauthenticated RCE on default installations through crafted OGC parameters that cause property names to be evaluated as XPath expressions. It says the flaw applies to all GeoServer instances and lists patched releases 2.22.6, 2.23.6, 2.24.4 and 2.25.2. The documented workaround removes `gt-complex-x.y.jar` or replaces the `gt-app-schema`, `gt-complex` and `gt-xsd-core` jars, with warnings that removal can break functionality.
