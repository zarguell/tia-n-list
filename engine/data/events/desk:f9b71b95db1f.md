ISC knowledge base article AA-01272 is the vendor advisory for CVE-2015-5477, stating that an error in handling TKEY queries can make named exit on a REQUIRE assertion failure in BIND 9.x before 9.9.7-P2 and 9.10.x before 9.10.2-P3. The original page now renders only through JavaScript, so its date rests on the Wayback Machine capture timestamped July 29, 2015 at 01:47 UTC, corroborated by the NVD record published the same day. CISA's KEV notes for this batch cite the archived advisory directly.
