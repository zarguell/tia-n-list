Palo Alto Networks (NASDAQ: PANW ) said Wednesday it has agreed to acquire AI infrastructure company Applied Compute in an all-stock transaction valued at $4.5 billion, subject to adjustments. Applied Compute’s platform lets companies adapt existing open-weight AI models to their own data and run them in production. Palo Alto Networks plans to offer the platform to its security customers, with deployment options in public clouds and sovereign environments. Applied Compute will continue serving its existing customers. The acquisition gives Palo Alto Networks a role in how enterprises build and deploy AI, expanding its reach beyond the security tools those companies use to protect their deployments. Palo Alto CEO Nikesh Arora said he expects businesses to use a mix of open-weight and proprietary models. Palo Alto Networks evaluated Applied Compute as a prospective customer, and its Unit 42 team assessed the platform’s security, Arora said. Applied Compute co-founder and CEO Yash Patil will become co-chief AI officer at Palo Alto Networks after the deal closes. The startup’s research team will also join the company and help train proprietary AI models for use across its products. The deal is expected to close in its fiscal third quarter of 2027, pending regulatory clearance and other customary closing conditions.
