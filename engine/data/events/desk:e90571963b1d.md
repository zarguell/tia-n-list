On June 26, 2026, The Hacker News reported CISA's KEV addition for CVE-2026-12569 (CVSS 9.3) and noted PTC confirmed heightened threat activity with attackers deploying persistent JSP web shells in /Windchill/login/ directories. The article lists additional attacker C2 IPs including 38.60.157.212 and 216.152.148.54, and describes hunting hints such as searching HTTP logs for POST to /Windchill/login/*.jsp, scanning for .jsp files matching hex patterns, checking for flst.txt in /tmp, blocking header X-windchill-req:, and restricting internet exposure of Windchill login endpoints.
