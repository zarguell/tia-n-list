Fortinet published FG-IR-24-535 on Jan 14, 2025 confirming active exploitation of an authentication-bypass vulnerability (CVE-2024-55591, CVSS 9.6) affecting FortiOS 7.0.0-7.0.16 and FortiProxy 7.2.0-7.2.12, expanded on Feb 11, 2025 to include CVE-2025-24472. The advisory details attacker-created admin accounts (random usernames, super-admin privileges), malicious SSL-VPN user groups, IoC log patterns, affected versions, upgrade paths (FortiOS 7.0.17+, FortiProxy 7.2.13+), and workarounds restricting administrative access.
