Attackers had been draining hot wallets on self-hosted BTCPay Server installations since October 30, 2026, and on November 1 the project told operators to switch servers off while it searched for the entry point. The project later traced the drains to version 2.4.1 or older, and Bitquery estimated about 1.63 million USD in bitcoin and litecoin left merchant wallets, with each merchant bearing its own loss.
