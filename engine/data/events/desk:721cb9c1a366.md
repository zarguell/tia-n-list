Reuters reported that the US Federal Election Commission's website was targeted by a hacker or hackers, disrupting the agency's electronic filings system a week before the November 3 elections, with the system down since around 10 p.m. ET on Monday. The FEC said its electronic filing system was offline while IT staff worked to restore service, users could still search past reports, and the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency and the FBI did not immediately respond to requests for comment.
