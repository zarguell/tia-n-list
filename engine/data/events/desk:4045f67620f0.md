Reuters reported, citing two people familiar with the matter, that CrowdStrike was in talks to buy Zscaler and that discussions might end without an agreement. It noted Zscaler's roughly 32 billion dollar market capitalization, a 17 percent after-hours share jump for Zscaler and an 8 percent drop for CrowdStrike. The report said a combination would fill CrowdStrike's network security gap, compete more directly with Palo Alto Networks, and likely need the backing of founder Jay Chaudhry, who remains Zscaler's largest shareholder. Reuters credited Bloomberg with first reporting the discussions.
