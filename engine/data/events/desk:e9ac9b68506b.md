GitHub advisory GHSA-rpvp-3pw9-3vcw, published 10 November 2026, covers two issues found in release 2026-09a. Failed ActiveSync, CalDAV/CardDAV and autodiscover logins were reported to netfilter with 127.0.0.1 as the client address and were never counted or banned, while passwordless autodiscover disclosed which mailboxes exist and their owners' display names. The advisory rates the combined issue 7.4 and says an unauthenticated attacker could run unlimited password guessing against every mailbox with EAS or DAV access, which is enabled by default. Fixed in 2026-11.
