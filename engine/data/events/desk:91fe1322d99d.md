Sentra says it uncovered a broad supply chain attack in which an attacker used credentials belonging to Qubole to reach dozens of Qubole customers through IAM roles those customers still trusted. The company detected the activity across several customer environments, traced stolen files to Qubole roles that had been dormant since the spring, and reported it to Wiz, which investigated with Sentra and Mandiant. Sentra counts more than a dozen organizations hacked in a single attack and about 100 passwords and keys read in seven minutes, with zero write actions.
