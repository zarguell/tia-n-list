Cyble scanned 1.3M+ newly registered domains and found AI-built sites leaking live secrets to the browser: OpenAI keys, Stripe keys, and database tokens. Spinning up a website no longer takes a developer. A prompt to an AI app-builder returns a working, styled, deployable site in minutes, and when one template works, the same operator clones it across a dozen domains before launch. That is also how secrets end up in plain sight. Over a month, Cyble Research and Intelligence Labs (CRIL) scanned more than 1.3 million newly registered domains, identified the ones built by AI app-builders, and read their public front-end code for exposed credentials. We built our own classifiers to spot AI-generated websites as they are registered, paired with a scanner that pulls exposed keys and credentials straight from their public code. On 35 of the 22,306 AI-built sites we identified, a genuinely private secret was sitting in the browser: live OpenAI keys, Stripe secret keys, backend database tokens, and, in several cases, a single credential cloned across a whole fleet of domains.
