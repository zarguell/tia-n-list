Russia is targeting Democratic candidates and supporters of Ukraine from both parties with disinformation ahead of the midterms, the top Democrats on Congress’s intelligence committees said Wednesday as they outlined foreign efforts to influence voters and undermine trust in U.S. elections. Sen. Mark Warner, D-Va., and Rep. Jim Himes, D-Conn., added that communication with the intelligence community has improved under Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton, including a report received Tuesday outlining foreign activity. But Himes said several legally required threat assessments remain outstanding. Himes and Warner’s descriptions of foreign adversaries’ operations ahead of next month’s elections echo patterns seen in previous cycles, with efforts to shape Americans’ views and undermine trust in institutions. There have been no indications of a foreign operation to manipulate voting machinery or alter ballots, they said. “Do we expect disinformation? Yes. Do we expect specific election interference with the machinery of our electoral system? We’ve not seen that,” Warner said at a roundtable with reporters.
