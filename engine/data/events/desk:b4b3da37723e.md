BankInfoSecurity reported that PJ&A's breach report, filed November 3 with the HHS Office for Civil Rights, said the hacking incident affected more than 8.95 million people. Northwell Health disclosed that nearly 3.9 million of its patients were affected, and Cook County Health reported 1.2 million patients impacted. The article notes Cook County Health had reported the incident to HHS OCR on September 24 with a placeholder estimate of only 500 individuals.
