Check Point Research published its findings on November 5, 2026, reporting that researcher Alexey Bukhteyev discovered a vulnerability bypassing ChatGPT Lockdown Mode. The flaw let attackers silently exfiltrate full chat histories, steal credentials through phishing, and deliver malware from inside the trusted ChatGPT interface. Check Point said it responsibly disclosed the issue to OpenAI and that OpenAI had already deployed a server-side fix on September 18, 2026; retesting confirmed the exploitation technique no longer works.
