LevelBlue SpiderLabs tracked a multi-stage kit it calls Wazza, active since at least early July 2026 and still running in October. Emails originated from compromised accounts and carried business-themed PDF lures; the infrastructure used Google Sites, Cloudflare Workers, .com and .eu domains. The final pages impersonated Adobe, DocuSign and OneDrive and walked victims through Microsoft's device authorization flow. The report notes campaign parameters, decoy websites and subdomain-based routines that let one kit serve multiple themes.
