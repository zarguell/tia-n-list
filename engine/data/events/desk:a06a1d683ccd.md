TechCrunch reported that an unknown group targeted systems already compromised by TeamPCP, kicking out TeamPCP and removing its tools before deploying worm-spreading code to steal credentials across cloud infrastructure. Alex Delamotte of SentinelOne, who found and named PCPJack, told TechCrunch it is unclear who is behind the campaign. TeamPCP had recently drawn attention through high-profile supply chain hacks, including a breach of the European Commission. The report describes PCPJack as replicating across cloud infrastructure and exfiltrating stolen credentials to attacker-controlled systems.
