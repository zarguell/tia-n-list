Google has restructured its Vulnerability Reward Programs in response to a flood of AI-assisted security research that has overwhelmed vulnerability triage — the Internet Bug Bounty program recently paused accepting new reports entirely due to the volume. Chrome bug payouts are dropping significantly: base rewards for memory safety issues are now $500 (with multipliers), and some researchers report rewards 10x smaller than before. Android rewards are increasing, with zero-click Pixel Titan M exploits now up to $1.5 million. Google is shifting both programs to prioritize concrete proof-of-exploitation over lengthy write-ups, explicitly noting that AI tools now make detailed reports effortless to generate while internal tooling can explain bugs automatically. Despite lower per-bug Chrome payouts, Google expects to increase total aggregate rewards for 2026 after paying a record $17.1 million in 2025.
