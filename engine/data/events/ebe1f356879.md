The RansomHouse extortion group has listed Trellix on its dark web leak site, claiming a successful breach of the cybersecurity firm's source code repository. Trellix confirmed unauthorized access to a specific portion of its internal source code repository but stated that its core operations and customer-facing software supply chains remain unaffected. Initial forensic findings show no evidence that the source code release or software distribution processes were compromised, and no indication of active exploitation of the accessed code. Third-party forensic investigators and law enforcement have been engaged. The attack against a major security vendor — formed from the McAfee Enterprise and FireEye merger — underscores that threat intelligence companies are not immune to targeting.
