The Hacker News reported DomainTools research describing more than 100 fake websites and malicious Chrome extensions from an actor active since approximately February 2024. The add-ons appeared to work as advertised while sending data to attacker servers, receiving commands, and executing arbitrary code, with manifest.json permissions spanning every visited site. DomainTools observed an onreset handler on a temporary document object model element used to bypass Content Security Policy, WebSocket connections for proxying traffic, and impersonation of services including DeepSeek, Manus, DeBank, FortiVPN, and Site Stats.
