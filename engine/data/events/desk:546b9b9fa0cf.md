Mitel issued MISA-2026-0012 on 16 November 2026 for five unauthenticated vulnerabilities in the Audio, Web and Video Conferencing component of MiCollab 10.3 (10.3.0.18) and earlier. The advisory tracks CVE-2026-133275, CVE-2026-133303, CVE-2026-133311, CVE-2026-133323 and CVE-2026-133324, with CVSS 3.1 base scores of 5.3, 9.0, 5.9, 10.0 and 5.3, and warns that exploiting them together can amplify impact. Mitel directs customers to upgrade to 10.3 (10.3.0.18) and apply the supplied patch or move to a later release, and suggests restricting external access to AWV when patching is delayed.
