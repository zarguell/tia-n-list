Zeabur's founder announced the platform will wind down, with shared cluster services pausing on December 1, 2026 and all dedicated servers and shared clusters stopping by December 31, 2026. The post attributed the decision to a second security incident on October 14, 2026 UTC with broader scope than the August incident, alongside scaling complexity after three and a half years of growth. Purchases of new dedicated servers and domains stopped immediately and renewal was disabled.
