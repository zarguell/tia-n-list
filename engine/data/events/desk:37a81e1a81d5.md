Citrix support article CTX697305 (schema.org publication timestamp 2026-11-12 20:27 UTC) documents 28 vulnerabilities across NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway, including CVE-2026-107412 at CVSS v4.0 9.5 (CWE-119). Preconditions for CVE-2026-107412 require the appliance to be configured as a Gateway (SSL VPN, ICA Proxy, CVPN, RDP Proxy) or an AAA virtual server, testable by searching the running configuration for `add vpn vserver` or `add authentication vserver`. Fixed versions are 14.1-73.57, 13.1-64.39, 14.1-73.58 FIPS, and 13.1-37.293, with credits listing Michael Tucker and Chew Keong Tan of the XOR team at JPMorgan Chase, Kyle Hannon of CapitalOne Offensive Security, Himanshu Anand and Sina Kheirkhah of watchTowr, and the Cloud Software Group Product Security Team.
