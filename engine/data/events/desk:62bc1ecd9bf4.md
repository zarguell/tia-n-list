Push Security published its analysis of a campaign that lured victims with fake OpenAI, SpaceX, and Anthropic event invitations. The registration pages combined two phishing-as-a-service platforms: Kali365 for Microsoft device-code phishing and VoidProxy as an adversary-in-the-middle reverse proxy that stole Google sign-in session cookies. Push Security said it detected and blocked visits to the malicious pages in late October 2026.
