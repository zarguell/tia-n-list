Security investigations often begin with a single suspicious IOC, but deciding what it means requires context. The more relevant intelligence analysts can connect to that finding, the better they can determine what deserves attention and what to do next. October’s release introduces a new powerful capability in ANY.RUN’s Interactive Sandbox – Related Threat Context. Now SOCs and MSSPs can save more time by uncovering relevant IOC connections and unlocking investigative insight with less effort. By bringing relevant intelligence directly into the workflow, analysts can easily assess their findings and decide how to respond without switching between multiple sources. The release also expands threat coverage and detection capabilities, improving visibility into emerging threats and malicious activity. Together, these updates help SOC and MSSP teams investigate more efficiently, reduce manual effort, and make better-informed decisions with greater confidence.
