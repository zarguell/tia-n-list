Hunt.io published the original disclosure of an active intrusion into Thai broadband provider 3BB on September 14, 2026. The researchers discovered an open directory at 92.63.180.133:8888 containing 298 files across 30 subdirectories, including exploitation scripts for CVE-2024-21762, MeshCentral agent configuration, and device inventories showing root-level access. The attacker used MeshCentral as a persistent backdoor with the group label TH-3BB, targeting RADIUS authentication databases and maintaining anti-forensic cleanup scripts.
