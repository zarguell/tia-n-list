Attackers are exploiting CVE-2026-76466, a critical Cisco Expressway flaw that gives unauthenticated attackers root command execution on clustered devices. In the first public case, they moved from two internet-facing nodes into the internal network, and CISA added the flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog on November 20, 2026. What Is CVE-2026-76466? CVE-2026-76466 is a remote code execution vulnerability in Cisco Expressway, the gateway that extends Cisco collaboration services such as video and voice calling beyond the corporate network. Cisco disclosed it on October 21, 2026, rates it 9.8 under CVSS v3.1 and maps it to CWE-798 (Use of Hard-coded Credentials). It affects Expressway-C and Expressway-E only when they are clustered. Release X15.5.20 fixes it, and Cisco offers no workaround, so upgrading is the only vendor fix.
