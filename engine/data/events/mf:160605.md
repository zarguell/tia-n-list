The ransomware-as-a-service group Medusa has adopted fresh tactics to gain access and added hundreds of victims in a little more than a year, according to an updated U.S. government advisory published Tuesday. The gang is relying on access brokers,compensating them anywhere from $100 to $1 million, with higher prices going to those who work exclusively with Medusa. However, most of the brokers work simultaneously for “multiple variants at the same time,” the advisory from the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency, FBI and Health and Human Services Department states in one of the updated portions of the advisory.
