TechCrunch reported that Sean Cairncross was stepping down, attributing the initial report to Axios and noting the split was described as amicable and timed to allow a Senate confirmation before Democrats take Congress in January. It said Cairncross took office in August 2025, was confirmed 59 to 35, and had no cybersecurity background before the job. The report noted the Office of the National Cyber Director was created in early 2021, that Cairncross was its third Senate-confirmed director, and that the role sat vacant for about 10 months after Chris Inglis left.
