Payy has confirmed that attackers exploited its Ethereum bridge contract , draining the bridge’s entire balance in an incident estimated at about $1.83 million. The company halted Payy Network activity and disabled wallet functionality while it investigates the breach and attempts to recover the stolen assets. The exploit occurred at 4:21 UTC on September 24; the affected component was Payy’s bridge contract on Ethereum, a smart contract designed to support transfers between Ethereum and the Payy Network. Hackers Drain $1.83 Million From Payy Ethereum Bridge Payy said the attacker emptied the contract’s full balance, turning the cross-chain bridge into the central point of failure for the attack. Payy later clarified that the stolen assets were users’ non-custodial deposits held in connection with Payy Network and Payy Wallet services. The company did not disclose the technical root cause, the vulnerable function, the attacker’s transaction method, or whether the exploit involved an access-control flaw, compromised signing authority, accounting error, or smart-contract logic vulnerability. As part of its immediate containment actions, Payy paused all Payy Network transactions. The suspension affects deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and card transactions, preventing users from moving funds through the affected ecosystem while the investigation remains active.
