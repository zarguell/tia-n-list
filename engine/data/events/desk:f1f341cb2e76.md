Synology advisory Synology-SA-24:23, published 5 November 2024, lists CVE-2024-10441 as a critical flaw in the system plugin daemon of BeeStation OS before 1.1-65374 and DiskStation Manager before 7.2-64570-4, 7.2.1-69057-6 and 7.2.2-72806-1. The vendor says remote attackers can execute arbitrary code through unspecified vectors and scores the issue 9.8 under CVSS 3.1. The advisory credits DEVCORE researchers Pumpkin Chang and Orange Tsai, reports the issue as resolved, and offers no mitigation other than upgrading.
