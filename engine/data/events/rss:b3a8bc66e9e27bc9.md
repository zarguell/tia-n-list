An enterprise environment emits events by the million every hour. During an incident, the responder rarely needs all of them. They need the few hundred that tell the story: the burst of 4625 failures followed by a single 4624 success, the encoded PowerShell spawned by winword.exe, the new service installed at 03:12 on a domain controller. Logs analysis is the discipline of getting from the first number to the second fast enough to matter. That job has become harder in two directions at once. Attackers using AI tooling generate evidence at machine speed and pad real actions with benign-looking activity. Meanwhile, the obvious shortcut of pushing logs into an LLM runs into guardrails, context-window limits and data-protection obligations. Hugging Face’s responders hit exactly that wall in July 2026, when roughly 17,000 events were refused by frontier models and had to be processed on a local model instead.
