CISA added CVE-2025-4008 to the KEV catalog on October 2, 2025 with a federal remediation due date of October 23, 2025, confirming active exploitation of Smartbedded Meteobridge. The entry describes remote unauthenticated command execution with elevated root privileges, lists CWE-306 and CWE-77, marks ransomware campaign use as Unknown, and requires agencies to apply vendor mitigations or discontinue use of the product.
