VulnCheck published the underlying research behind this story, describing 375,904 internet-facing FortiGate hosts with the FGFM management port exposed in Target Intelligence data from 14 October to 12 November 2026. The post explains that each host answers a connection with a TLS ClientHello, which the team fingerprinted with JA4 to place hosts in six FortiOS generations and map them to CVE sets, including CVE-2024-23113 on port 541. It reports that 6.8 to 36.1 percent of the cohort is on end-of-support branches and provides Suricata and Snort rules for defenders.
