Broadcom has released emergency security updates addressing CVE-2026-123890, a critical authentication bypass vulnerability in VMware vCenter Server that was exploited as a zero-day by a suspected China-nexus cyberespionage actor. The flaw resides in the VMware Directory Service and carries a CVSS 3.1 score of 9.8, Critical . Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) and Mandiant attributed observed exploitation to ARMORY CASTLE , formerly tracked as UNC5221 and also known as Warp Panda. Forensic evidence indicates that the group exploited the vulnerability in early September 2026, more than two months before Broadcom publicly disclosed and patched it on November 19. The attackers used a custom Golang exploitation utility named BONESAW to target VMware vCenter. Successful exploitation enabled the threat actor to establish an authenticated session as administrator@vsphere.local, extract the vCenter Lightning Memory-Mapped Database (LMDB), obtain SAML signing material, create rogue SSO administrator accounts, and execute commands on the affected appliance. Compromise can provide attackers with privileged access not only to the management appliance but potentially to managed ESXi hosts and virtual machines.
