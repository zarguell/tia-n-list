CERT-AGID's latest reconnaissance shows a sign of improvement: 168 of the 650 URLs initially identified as potentially vulnerable, equal to 25.8%, now declare a PHP version updated beyond the vulnerabilities under monitoring. The scan of 26 October 2026 examined the same list of 22,371 URLs as the first reconnaissance launched on 22 October. 20,117 addresses proved reachable, of which 467 potentially vulnerable, down from the initial 650: a 28.2% decrease in detections.
