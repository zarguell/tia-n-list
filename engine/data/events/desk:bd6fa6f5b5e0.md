Huntress documented two malicious Custom GPTs that pushed victims from sponsored ChatGPT results to Google Sites pages posing as Cloudflare checks. The ClickFix command downloaded ISOSimple.msi, which sideloaded code through a Canon-signed COTFileReadApp.exe and established Run key and scheduled-task persistence named Canon Configuration Reader. The SOC handled at least 40 incidents from the Google Sites domain, two confirmed as Custom GPT driven, and found a replacement GPT on 2026-09-27 after OpenAI took down the first on 2026-09-25. A 2026-10-14 update described a shift to gptverification[.]com and new Google Sites pages.
