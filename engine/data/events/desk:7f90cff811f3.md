Picus reports that OpenJDK published an advisory and Oracle shipped a patch for CVE-2026-114938 on November 17, 2026. The flaw is a heap out-of-bounds write in HotSpot's x86-64 Base64 decoder intrinsic, writing 62 bytes past the output buffer on CPUs with AVX-512 VBMI, with affected builds from OpenJDK 17.0.6 onward. Picus lists the temporary workarounds as disabling Base64 intrinsics or limiting AVX to level two until the JDK is patched.
