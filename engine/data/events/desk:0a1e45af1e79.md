Crédit Mutuel Arkéa's advisory states attackers entered through a service provider's platform and used the bank's own account to send transactions to other banking actors, with funds potentially converted into cryptocurrency. The bank explicitly denied any entry via Fortuneo and said clients face no financial consequences, with some funds frozen and a portion blocked. A prior press release was distributed on November 4 at 13:00 local time. The bank is working with judicial and police authorities to determine the attack's origin.
