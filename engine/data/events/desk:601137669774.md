Bay Area Labs listed Poper Blocker among seven Chrome extensions with a combined 7M+ installs that scrape AI chats or have the infrastructure to do so. The post, dated 11 May 2026, classifies Poper Blocker as Confirmed, noting it exfiltrates every URL visited and uses server-side gating that activates after about 24 hours. The extension is owned by Big Star Labs LP, carries Featured and Verified badges, and has 2M users.
