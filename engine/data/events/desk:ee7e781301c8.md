The Record reported that Amazon Threat Intelligence found a publicly accessible Cairn server on 10 September and reconstructed attacks on East Asian universities from its prompts and run history. It quotes the assessment that the path traversal had no assigned advisory and was unpatched at discovery, and that the operator used the same flaw to reach a second university. AWS declined to name the universities or their country. The article places Cairn alongside other autonomous offensive frameworks seen in recent intrusions and quotes Gambit Security's Eyal Sela on the tool's general-purpose design.
