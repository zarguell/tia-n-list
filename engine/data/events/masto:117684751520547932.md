DATA LEAK. Buffalo Grill, a French restaurant chain, is alerting all of its customers after the cyberattack at Brevo, the email and marketing platform that handles its mailing lists. The notification tells customers their contact data was exposed through the Brevo compromise rather than through a breach of the restaurant's own systems.
