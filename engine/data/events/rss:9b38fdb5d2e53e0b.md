tl;dr Prime Detect runs detection rules on your clients’ event streams before data reaches the SIEM. It applies Sigma rules to events in transit, tags each match with a severity and a MITRE ATT&CK technique, and links related matches into attack chains. In client testing, about 7% of telemetry was tagged. MSSPs use Prime Detect to give every client the same 20,000+ detections regardless of which SIEM that client runs, to send less data into SIEMs they pay to operate, and to keep each client’s data inside its own pipeline. It works alongside the SIEMs you already run. It does not replace them. Contact Sales Why MSSPs are moving detection ahead of the SIEM An MSSP’s detection coverage is usually set by whatever each client’s SIEM can hold. That creates three problems that grow with every client you add. Uneven coverage. Clients run different SIEMs with different rule limits. The same threat can be caught for one client and missed for another, depending on the platform underneath. Ingest cost. Where you operate the SIEM, every event ingested is a cost against your margin, whether it carries a threat signal or not. Engineering load. Your detection engineers spend their time porting and tuning the same rules across platforms instead of building the detections clients actually pay for. Prime Detect moves detection upstream.
