Original researcher write-up of CVE-2026-84388 by James Arnott of Bay Area Labs, covering the extension used by more than 1 million users: a webRequest listener on the path https://*/api/v2/monitor/web-ui/state added any requested hostname to the trusted-server list without checking the initiator or request success, externally_connectable was set to all URLs, non-JWT access tokens skipped validation instead of failing, and the page could click its own consent dialog (elements fortinet-sv-modal-overlay and .sv-btn-allow). Arnott states Bay Area Labs reported the issue to Fortinet on July 17 and the fix shipped August 1, and that there is no known real-world exploitation. The page displays date only (9 September 2026, no time of day); published_at is normalized to start of day UTC.
