CISA added CVE-2026-35273 to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on June 12, 2026, a missing authentication for critical function flaw (CWE-306) in Oracle PeopleSoft Enterprise PeopleTools that could allow an unauthenticated attacker to obtain takeover. The entry flags known ransomware campaign use, sets a remediation due date of 2026-06-15, and links Oracle's security alert advisory.
