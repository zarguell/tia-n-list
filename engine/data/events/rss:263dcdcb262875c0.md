The major cyber attacks of October 2026 were some of the year’s most consequential, and many of them started with access their victims already trusted. A cryptocurrency exchange lost billions through compromised engineer workstations in the Binance hot wallet heist, a backup provider’s cloud credential reached hundreds of Microsoft 365 tenants in the ConnectWise breach, and a supplier’s lookup account exposed records on 8.8 million people in Denmark’s CPR register breach. Other attacks disrupted day-to-day operations, from ransomware hitting Japan’s IDCF Cloud platform to Iran-linked remote sabotage of refrigeration systems in French supermarkets. Binance Loses About $6.3 Billion in Hot Wallet Theft Attackers began draining Binance hot wallets across several blockchains at 20:05 UTC on October 29, 2026, and moved most of the funds within about 20 minutes , according to blockchain analysts. Binance puts the loss at about $6.3 billion , the largest cryptocurrency theft publicly reported to date.
