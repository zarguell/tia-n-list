Cloudflare's own announcement, published the day before press pickup, states its intent to become a public certificate authority. It has applied to the Chrome, Apple, Microsoft and Mozilla root programs, signed a definitive agreement to acquire a GlobalSign root trusted since 2012, and plans first production Merkle Tree Certificates in Q1 2027. Issuance will be ACME-first and conditional on client support for ACME Renewal Information (RFC 9773), with reproducible builds, attested hardware security modules and a public issuance dashboard planned.
