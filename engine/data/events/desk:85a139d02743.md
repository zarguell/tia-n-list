TechCrunch reported on February 4, 2026 that ShinyHunters published what it claims are more than one million records from Harvard and UPenn on its leak site after the universities refused to pay a ransom. TechCrunch verified a portion of the dataset by matching records against alumni and public records such as student ID numbers, and reported the published data appears to match the types Harvard disclosed as stolen: email addresses, phone numbers, home and business addresses, event attendance, donation details, and biographical information. The hackers told TechCrunch the intrusions were part of a recently disclosed phishing campaign targeting Okta and single sign-on providers, and Harvard did not respond to a request for comment.
