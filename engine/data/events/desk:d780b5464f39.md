Apple sent threat notifications on August 13, 2026 to users targeted in 110 countries, and said it has notified customers in over 150 countries to date. The notifications warn of mercenary spyware attacks and now arrive as a push alert on the iPhone lock screen, with email and account-login copies as well. Citizen Lab senior researcher John Scott-Railton first highlighted the latest batch in a thread on X. Apple says it does not attribute attacks to specific governments and cannot confirm whether a device was actually compromised.
