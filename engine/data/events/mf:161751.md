TITAN, a ransomware-as-a-service (RaaS) operation active since May 2026, is promoting an on-premises artificial intelligence platform designed to analyze stolen corporate data and increase pressure on victims during double-extortion attacks. The group claims its AI engine can automatically classify sensitive files, identify valuable business information, calculate regulatory exposure, and generate notification packages for regulators and media organizations. These capabilities have not been independently verified, but the marketing reflects a growing effort by ransomware groups to automate data-driven extortion. TITAN was reportedly founded on April 4, 2026. Its leak site has listed 24 victims across 10 countries, with Italy accounting for 10 published victims. The Czech Republic has four listed victims, while the United States has three. Other affected countries include India, Sri Lanka, South Korea, Mexico, Tunisia, France, and Singapore. Manufacturing and professional services each account for roughly 29% of victims. This targeting pattern suggests the group is opportunistic and access-driven rather than focused on one industry. AI-Powered TITAN Automates Extortion TITAN operates a verification-gated affiliate program. Prospective partners reportedly undergo criminal-history checks, technical assessments, and reviews of prior intrusion experience. Affiliates must also pay a non-refundable registration fee.
