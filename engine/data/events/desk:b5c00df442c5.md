Volexity reported mass exploitation of CVE-2022-27925, an mboximport RCE in Zimbra Collaboration Suite, enabled as unauthenticated by the CVE-2022-37042 authentication bypass, with evidence of attacks as early as the end of June 2022. Internet-wide scanning identified over 1,000 backdoored ZCS instances worldwide, including government departments, ministries, and military branches. The post documents POST requests to /service/extension/backup/mboximport returning 401 as an exploitation signature, provides YARA rules for the deployed webshells, and lists patches 8.8.15P31/P33 and 9.0.0P24/P26.
