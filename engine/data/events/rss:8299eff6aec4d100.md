The FBI has confirmed that North Korean state-sponsored hackers known as TraderTraitor were behind the $6.3 billion crypto-theft attack on Binance, making it the largest cryptocurrency heist on record. In a security alert published Saturday, the agency attributed the attack to TraderTraitor, a North Korean hacking group also tracked as Lazarus Group, Jade Sleet, and APT38, linked to numerous cryptocurrency thefts. "The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) is releasing information advising the Democratic People's Republic of Korea (North Korea) was responsible for the theft of more than $6 billion USD in virtual assets from cryptocurrency exchange Binance, on or about October 29, 2026," the FBI said . This official attribution follows blockchain intelligence firms TRM Labs and Chainalysis, which had already linked the theft to TraderTraitor. As previously reported , Binance linked the breach to a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop, which allowed the attackers to access a service container and submit unauthorized withdrawal transactions. The attackers stole approximately $6.36 billion in cryptocurrency across ten blockchains, with most of the funds transferred in just 20 minutes. The FBI says it has observed TraderTraitor converting stolen assets primarily into Ethereum (ETH), which it expects will be further laundered through decentralized finance (DeFi) platforms and instant cryptocurrency swap services.
