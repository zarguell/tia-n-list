Keio Corporation has confirmed a ransomware attack against servers within its group, causing a system outage that has disrupted certain business systems at affiliated companies. The Japanese railway operator said it identified the incident during the early hours of September 26, 2026, and has launched an investigation with external cybersecurity specialists. The company stated that rail services remain operational and that it has not confirmed any data leakage so far. However, Keio is still assessing whether the intrusion accessed, stole, or otherwise affected confidential corporate data or customer information. Ransomware Attack Hits Keio Railway Group Servers In a public notice issued September 26, Keio said the attack targeted servers used by the wider Keio Group. The organization immediately implemented network-isolation measures intended to contain the incident and prevent further spread across its environment. Ransomware operations commonly combine encryption with data theft, allowing threat actors to pressure victims through double-extortion tactics. In this model, attackers may threaten to publish stolen files if the victim refuses to pay a ransom, even when systems can be restored from backups. Keio has not attributed the intrusion to a known ransomware group, disclosed the ransomware variant involved, or confirmed whether any files were encrypted.
