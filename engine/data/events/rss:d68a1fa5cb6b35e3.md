Artificial intelligence is creating new opportunities in medicine and biological research , but it may also make some biological risks harder to manage. The UK government has announced the AI Biosecurity Lab, a new organisation designed to use advanced AI to improve the country’s ability to identify, prevent and respond to biological threats. The programme will combine expertise from government agencies, life sciences organisations, universities and leading AI companies. Its early work is expected to focus on detecting emerging threats sooner and speeding up the development of vaccines, treatments and other medical countermeasures. What is the AI Biosecurity Lab? The AI Biosecurity Lab , or AIBL, is intended to become the world’s first organisation dedicated specifically to AI biosecurity . It will operate as an independent, non-profit organisation within the UK’s wider National AI Biosecurity Initiative. The British government has committed £100 million to establish the lab. OpenAI and Anthropic have pledged up to a further £200 million in model credits, computing access and technical support. The lab’s work will address both sides of the AI-biology relationship .
