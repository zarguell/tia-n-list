UNN reported the Prosecutor General's Office account of an international organization exposed with the FBI, with at least 27 members from Ukraine, Kazakhstan and Russia and a linked network of more than 60 people across eight jurisdictions that included former Conti members. At least 28 companies were identified as having paid more than $40.5 million, with final damages still being established. The report says the group impersonated IT support staff to obtain credentials and install remote-access software, used AI to automate calls, and demanded cryptocurrency while threatening to publish stolen data. It also reports 66 searches in Kyiv and 11 regions and the seizure of $370,000 and EUR 20,000 in cash.
