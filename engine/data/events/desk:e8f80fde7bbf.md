The BBC reported that TfL had notified the Information Commissioner's Office after suspicious activity on the photocard website, and quoted director of technology strategy and revenue Shashi Verma saying about 265,000 customers may have had account details compromised. TfL said the breach was detected on Monday morning when some customers received unexpected emails asking them to validate accounts, and that the system was shut down immediately. TfL said no photos, passwords, bank or payment card details, journey information, or names and dates of birth of children with Zip Oyster cards were compromised.
