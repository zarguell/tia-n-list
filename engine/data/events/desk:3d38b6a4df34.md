The Erlang Ecosystem Foundation CNA published CVE-2026-129514 on 24 November 2026 and rates it CVSS 4.0 10.0 for deserialization of untrusted data in the application/vnd.hex+erlang request body parser. It says an unauthenticated attacker could run operating system commands through a crafted Erlang external term format body and that the flaw was actively exploited against the hex.pm service on 2026-11-23. The record dates affected hexpm from 2016-02-23 to before 2026-11-23 and names commit 379b0a29 as the fix.
