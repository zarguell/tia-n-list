The site is the original disclosure for CVE-2026-58882, authored by researcher Mark B. (@sourone). It documents the malformed eight-byte WAP Push payload (`01 06 88 80 80 80 00 BE`) sent to port 2948, explains how the header-length value casts to -2147483648 and crashes `com.android.phone`, and notes the bug was reported to Google on 5 May 2026. The page states the public writeup was disclosed on 29 October 2026, one day before the first news cycle. It also confirms Google authored fix commit 735d967 on 15 June 2026, but the CVE was not listed in the October 2026 bulletin.
