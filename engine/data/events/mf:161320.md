Mythos readiness is a security program's proven ability to withstand attacks weaponized at the speed of frontier AI models such as Anthropic's Claude Mythos, where disclosure-to-exploit is measured in hours rather than weeks. A Mythos-ready organization can show which controls hold, which do not, and what evidence backs each decision to patch, mitigate, monitor, or accept.
