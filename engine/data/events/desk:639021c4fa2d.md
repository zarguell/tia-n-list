SecurityWeek reported on July 23, 2025 that Cisco updated the June advisory to confirm attempted in-the-wild exploitation of CVE-2025-20281, CVE-2025-20282 and the newly added CVE-2025-20337, all CVSS 10.0 and all yielding root command execution via crafted API requests. The flaws affect ISE and ISE-PIC 3.3 and 3.4, were fixed in 3.3 Patch 7 and 3.4 Patch 2, and all three arrived through the Trend Micro Zero Day Initiative.
