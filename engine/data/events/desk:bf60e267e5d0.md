Uptycs Threat Research describes CVE-2026-133968 as a CWE-24 path traversal in the image parameter of PUT /datastore/image, with a CVSS 3.1 score of 9.8 and active exploitation listed in CISA KEV since 23 November. It identifies MONAI Label versions before 0.8.6 as affected and 0.8.6 as fixed, with a CISA BOD 26-04 due date of 26 November. Recommended actions include upgrading, restricting network access, reviewing outbound connections from the MONAI Label process, isolating confirmed compromises and rotating credentials and SSH keys on affected hosts.
