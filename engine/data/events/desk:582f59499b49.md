Oracle's September Critical Security Patch Update lists CVE-2026-87268 in Oracle VM VirtualBox Core as a local, low-privilege flaw in version 7.2.16, with a CVSS 3.1 base score of 7.8 and confidentiality, integrity, and availability impact. The advisory credits James Forshaw for the report and says a successful attack can compromise Oracle VM VirtualBox. Its modification history records a 15 September 2026 initial release.
