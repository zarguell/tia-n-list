New York State ITS issued advisory 2026-059 on June 11, 2026, describing a critical remote code execution vulnerability (CVE-2026-35273) in the Updates Environment Management component of PeopleSoft Enterprise PeopleTools versions 8.61 and 8.62. The advisory states the flaw allows an unauthenticated attacker with network access via HTTP to completely take over the software, affecting government and business entities at high risk. It references Bleeping Computer reporting on ShinyHunters data theft attacks targeting Oracle PeopleSoft servers against over 100 organizations, and links to Oracle's security alert.
