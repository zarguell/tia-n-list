On August 25, Slovak authorities claimed to have disrupted a planned arson attack against Skyeton drone manufacturing facility near Prešov, a site that produces unmanned systems for the Ukrainian military. [1] The same day, a suspected sabotage against German energy infrastructure was reported. German authorities reported that improvised devices had been used in an attempt to deliberately short circuit a substation connected to the Jänschwalde power complex. [2] August 25, Germany also publicly accused Russia of being responsible for the recent drone attack at Leipzig/Halle Airport. German officials elevated the country’s threat assessment, announced diplomatic measures, and described Germany as a frequent target of hybrid attacks. [3][4][5] Assessment Recent reporting points to a sharp rise in physical attacks and sabotage attempts across Europe, primarily aimed at disrupting entities directly or indirectly involved in bringing aid to Ukraine. This includes attacks against defense companies involved in producing weapons for Ukraine, but also airports, logistics networks, and energy infrastructure.
