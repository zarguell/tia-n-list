ASEC published its Q2 2026 review of attacks on poorly managed IIS and Apache Tomcat servers running on Windows, finding that intrusions typically started with web shell uploads followed by privilege escalation, reconnaissance, lateral movement and remote control. One case was attributed to UAT-8099 and used LockBit 3.0 ransomware alongside Potato, AnyDesk, GotoHTTP, LCX, the NPS client and BadIIS for control and SEO manipulation. The report recommends patching, stronger firewall access controls and updated antivirus software.
