The Register reported Okta's assessment that O-UNC-053 had very likely changed brand affiliation from ShinyHunters to INCEPTION while keeping the same vishing and domain-registration tactics. The first five claimed victims, AMD, Cigna Healthcare, Paychex, PepsiCo and Henry Schein, were listed as leaked on November 17, followed by AutoZone on November 19 and Lucid Group on November 21. None of the seven confirmed a breach, and Okta said it could not validate the published data. The piece placed the cluster in the wider Com ecosystem and noted that infrastructure was registered six to 19 days before each company appeared on the new leak site.
