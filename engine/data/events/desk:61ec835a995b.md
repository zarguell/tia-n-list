BleepingComputer reported on March 30, 2026 that F5 reclassified CVE-2025-53521 from DoS to critical RCE and warned that attackers were using it to deploy webshells on unpatched devices, with indicators published in K000160486. The article cites Shadowserver tracking over 240,000 BIG-IP instances exposed online at the time, and notes F5 advised defenders to check disks, logs, and terminal history for signs of malicious activity.
