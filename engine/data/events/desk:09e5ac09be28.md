Unite.AI reported on November 9, 2026 that Fireworks expanded its October security incident disclosure, with attackers accessing account and billing information and, for a limited group of customers, secrets, datasets, and incomplete model weights. The article states the October 6 incident involved unauthorized use of internal credentials and that Fireworks had not initially detected access to customer models, datasets, training data, personal information, or inference traffic. It adds that billing information covered billing and shipping addresses, invoice and payment history, account balances, and credit usage, while payment card numbers were not stored and were not affected.
