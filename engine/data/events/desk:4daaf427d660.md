Maintainer Bram Matthys posted the full advisory text to oss-security, explaining that the labeled-response module fails to escape the IRCv3 label message tag, letting any user inject newlines into server-to-server traffic and issue commands with server authority, including KILL and GLINE. He called it the most critical issue found in UnrealIRCd in fifteen years and said the nine issues came out of an internal audit performed with AI assistance. The advisory documents a network-wide SPAMFILTER workaround for 6.0.4 and newer, the bundle-20261107 hot-patch, and a blacklist-module workaround for end-of-life UnrealIRCd 5.
