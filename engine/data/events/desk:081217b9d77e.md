In an update posted 12 November, Apify said an unauthorized third party used a compromised credential on 9 and 10 November to obtain a number of account credentials and access related Apify accounts. The access path is closed and no further attacker activity has been detected since 11 November. Apify revoked some OAuth connections to external integrations as a precaution, rotated critical internal secrets, and is notifying affected users individually; the company also notified the relevant authorities.
