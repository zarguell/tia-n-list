SAP has confirmed to BleepingComputer that it took urgent action to protect its customers after detecting suspicious activity in its IT environment. The German software giant said its evidence so far does not show downstream compromise of customer systems or data. Based in Walldorf, Germany, SAP is one of the world’s largest enterprise software vendors, with 110,000 employees in 157 countries, 300 million subscribers, and over $40 billion in annual revenue . Der Spiegel reported earlier today, based on an internal SAP memo it saw, that the software firm was hit by a cyberattack it tracks under the code name “Project Granite.” The software company reportedly asked all employees to keep the project details confidential. According to the German outlet, sources within the company said the attackers breached SAP’s GitHub environment, possibly with help from AI tools. Insider sources say that the attacker managed to steal authentication tokens from SAP employees and leveraged them to copy and also modify SAP source code on GitHub.
