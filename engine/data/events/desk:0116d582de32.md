Palo Alto Networks published the vendor advisory on May 13, 2026, disclosing an authentication bypass in the GlobalProtect portal and gateway (CWE-565), discovered internally, affecting PAN-OS 10.2.x through 12.1.x and Prisma Access, with Panorama and Cloud NGFW unaffected. It originally carried a CVSSv4 base score of 4.7 (Medium) and listed exposure as firewalls with authentication override cookies enabled plus a specific certificate configuration, with fixes across ten PAN-OS branches and two mitigations: use a dedicated certificate for authentication override, or disable the feature. The advisory timeline shows exploitation-status and fix-timeline updates on May 14 through May 29, when the exploitation status was changed and the score rose to CVSSv4 7.8 HIGH with Exploit Maturity: ATTACKED.
