IoT security is the practice of protecting the internet-connected devices in your home, like smart cameras, speakers, TVs and thermostats, from hackers. It covers strong passwords, regular updates and a secure home network so no single device becomes an entry point for an intruder. IoT security isn’t something on your radar until 2a.m., when you hear a stranger’s voice coming through your baby monitor. It sounds like a horror movie, but it’s happened to real families. In 2023, the FTC charged Ring with failing to adequately protect customers’ privacy and security, alleging that hackers took over Ring cameras and used them to harass and threaten people, including children and teens. Ring agreed to a $5.8 million settlement without admitting wrongdoing. The alleged attacks didn’t require any sophisticated tools. Many got in using stolen or reused passwords on accounts without second-factor authentication. Every camera, doorbell, lock and speaker you connect is another way into your home network.
