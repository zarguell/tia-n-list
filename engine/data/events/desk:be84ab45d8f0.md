Sansec published its BlockStrap analysis on October 15, identifying CVE-2026-103849 as a CVSS 10.0 unauthenticated remote-code-execution flaw in Magento and Adobe Commerce through version 2.4.9 (B2B through 1.5.3). The firm found four potential attack paths in under 24 hours and warned that Adobe's priority-2 rating underestimates urgency, comparing complexity to the StyleSmuggler zero-day that moved from hotfix to mass exploitation in three days. No in-the-wild exploitation had been observed at the time of publication.
