CERT-UA's alert, published February 1, 2026, reports malicious documents exploiting CVE-2026-21509 attributed to UAC-0001 (APT28) targeting Ukraine and EU countries. It is the first government exploitation telemetry for the bug and the samples were shared on MalwareBazaar tagged CVE-2026-21509. The affected documents are RTF files carrying Shell.Explorer.1 OLE objects.
