The CPR administration's own advisory, published 5 October 2026, states that misuse of a Danish company's lawful search access to the CPR system gave unauthorized parties names, addresses, and CPR numbers for about 8.8 million registered citizens. It confirms the company's access was stopped, the incident was reported to the Datatilsynet, and police are investigating with other authorities. People registered with name and address protection were not covered by the unauthorized access.
