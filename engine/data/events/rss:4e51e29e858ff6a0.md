US District Judge Jessica Clarke has acquitted two brothers accused of stealing $25 million in cryptocurrency in about 12 seconds through a scheme that exploited a bug in the Ethereum blockchain. Clarke's decision comes one year after a mistrial in the same case, after jurors failed to agree on whether to convict or acquit former Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) students Anton Peraire-Bueno and James Peraire-Bueno in this first-of-its-kind scheme, as Reuters first reported . "The question before the Court is not whether the Peraire-Buenos' conduct was right or moral, or even whether it violated uncharged crimes. It is simply whether the evidence at trial, construed in a light most favorable to the Government, could support wire fraud and related convictions in this case," the federal judge said . "Because the Government failed to prove that the Peraire-Buenos made any material misrepresentations in the execution of their scheme, the motion is granted, and a judgment of acquittal is entered for Anton Peraire-Bueno and James Peraire-Bueno." The Peraire-Bueno brothers were arrested in Boston and New York in May 2024 on wire fraud and money laundering charges and were facing 20 years in prison for each count if convicted. According to prosecutors, the brothers allegedly manipulated blockchain transaction validation processes by accessing pending private transactions, altering them, obtaining victims' cryptocurrency, and rejecting requests to return the stolen funds.
