The Hacker News reported that BeyondTrust disclosed CVE-2026-102764, an improper origin validation flaw in Remote Support rated CVSS 9.3, and that ARMORY CASTLE exploited it on a single self-hosted appliance on 29 August 2026. SaaS tenants were patched by 9 October and self-hosted versions 24.3.1 through 26.2.3 were told to apply BT26-05-RS. The report detailed SAWFLY, BARECLAW, SUID abuse of the system shell and access to the appliance credential vault, and noted CISA added the CVE to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with a 19 October federal deadline.
