SOCtember reports that GreyNoise's tracking page, updated at 10:48 UTC on November 19, recorded 187 Java4Shell-shaped requests from five IP addresses with no confirmed intrusions. GreyNoise defines an attempt as an HTTP Authorization Basic or Bearer value of 64 or 128 characters plus one to three more ending in an equals sign; the busiest source requested Confluence paths and a Spring Boot actuator endpoint. Cloudforce One saw a scanner return about 17 hours later posing as Chrome, and the article advises hunting Authorization values of 65 to 67 or 129 to 131 characters ending in an equals sign at reverse proxies, load balancers and WAFs.
