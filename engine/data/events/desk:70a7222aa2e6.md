BleepingComputer reported on November 19, 2026 that CVE-2026-123633 affects NGINX versions 1.19.7 through 1.31.8 when HTTP/2 is accepted and requests are proxied to a backend. The article notes Wiz demonstrated remote code execution but withheld a proof of concept, and that researcher Florian Roth found roughly 66 percent of a few hundred sampled NGINX HTTPS endpoints supported HTTP/2 while warning that version-only exposure claims miss configuration and backports. Citing the Censys tracker, it put approximately 8.8 million NGINX services as potentially exposed to the sibling CVE-2026-123007 as of November 19.
