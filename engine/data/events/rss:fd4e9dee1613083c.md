The Natto Team is honored to publish this ZRON leak special report, written by Ivan Kwiatkowski, CTO of VirLabs and cybersecurity researcher specializing in malware analysis and state-sponsored threat actors. Applying his expertise, Ivan provides a deep-dive technical analysis of the ZRON leak. The findings are the author’s own. In the previous installments of its ZRON leak series, the Natto Team examined ZRON’s product catalogue, its customers and the complex network of Chinese cybersecurity firms that the company relies on to conduct business. Though ZRON advertised a broad range of capabilities like computer surveillance and password cracking, the Natto Team found that much of ZRON’s commercial activity revolved around processing stolen information, translating documents and producing intelligence reports for government customers. But what do these capabilities actually look like under the hood? Was ZRON’s technology developed in-house or does it rely on existing software? And what can the leaked material tell us about the tools used to compromise victim networks in the first place? The leak provides a unique opportunity to investigate these questions. It contains product documentation, internal discussions, source code, software packages and malware implants, including components of a previously undocumented cross-platform Trojan, the Tx family .
