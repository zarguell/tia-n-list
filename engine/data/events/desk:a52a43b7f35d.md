GitLab's September 10, 2026 critical patch release shipped 19.3.2, 19.2.6, and 19.1.8, fixing 18 issues including CVE-2026-85706 (CVSS 10.0, CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:C/C:H/I:H/A:N, reported by s3ntago via HackerOne) and CVE-2026-87719 (CVSS 9.9 EE GraphQL subscription serializer deserialization, reported by kyyblin). The advisory lists wider affected ranges than most coverage repeated: CE/EE all versions from 18.7 before 18.11.12 and 19.0 before 19.0.9, in addition to 19.1 before 19.1.8, 19.2 before 19.2.6, and 19.3 before 19.3.2. It also credits the same release with CVE-2026-88765, a CVSS 8.5 authenticated RCE in the Advanced Search Unicode conversion wrapper, and links three GitLab threat detections for CVE-2026-85706 exploitation attempts.
