Synology published security advisory Synology_SA_26_13 on 2026-09-18 and rated it Critical. It addresses eight DSM vulnerabilities, including CVE-2026-13684 (improper output encoding in the SCGI component) and CVE-2026-13639 (insufficient entropy in login logic), both allowing remote unauthenticated attackers to read or write arbitrary files and cause denial of service. Fixed DSM releases are 7.2.1-69057-12, 7.2.2-72806-9, 7.3.2-86009-4 and 7.4-90075 or above, and Synology lists no mitigation other than updating.
