QR-code phishing, commonly known as quishing, is evolving beyond image-based payloads. Threat actors are now rendering scannable QR codes directly from HTML tables or text within email bodies, leaving no image attachment, embedded bitmap, or element for traditional email scanners to inspect. The technique targets a structural blind spot in Secure Email Gateways (SEGs).
