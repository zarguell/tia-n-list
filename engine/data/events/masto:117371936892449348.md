Data breach: a fourth one for LDLC. The French retailer is currently emailing customers to warn them of a new data breach. "We are informing you that a malicious act allowed unauthorized access to one of our information systems, which may have resulted in the viewing of some of your data," the company states in the letter. The affected data is nearly everything that can be held apart from banking and login information: name, first name, postal address, email address, fax, mobile phone, landline, customer language, salutation, technical customer codes, customer type (private or business), date of last login to the customer web account, and registration date on the site. "Your banking details (IBAN, card number), your login credentials or passwords were not affected," LDLC is quick to add. The company says it immediately isolated the affected part of its infrastructure, revised the corresponding access strategy, and reported the incident to the CNIL, in accordance with applicable regulations. As always in such cases, the stolen data "could be used for fraud and phishing attempts," LDLC warns, followed by standard advice not to click links received by SMS or email and never share personal information, credentials or bank details by email, SMS or phone.
