Clubic published the first account of the incident on 26 November 2026, reporting that Agence France-Presse closed AFP Forum, the client portal for downloading dispatches and images, after an intrusion during the night of Wednesday to Thursday. Citing an internal management message reported by Le Monde, it states the attackers struck from 01:30 Paris time and that AFP isolated the platform as soon as the intrusion was detected, with analyses continuing under external experts and no identification of the attackers. It also records the earlier September 2024 attack on part of AFP's content delivery systems and notes that French authorities have counted an average of three data thefts per day in the country since January.
