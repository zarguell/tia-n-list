The Alliance for Creativity and Entertainment (ACE) has announced the shutdown of Dizigom, one of Turkey's largest piracy sites, after a settlement agreement with the site's operators. Before being taken offline, the illegal TV and movie streaming service's websites had roughly 21 million visitors each month. The investigation into Dizigom's operations began three years ago, in 2023, with Turkish law enforcement authorities helping identify the platform's operators and their assets. As a result of this joint operation, Dizigom's operators agreed to cease operations, transfer control of 16 domains to ACE, and take the service offline. "After sustained investigative work, INTERPOL coordination, enforcement actions, and the advancement of criminal proceedings in Türkiye, ACE reached a settlement agreement with the operators to secure the permanent shutdown of the service and the transfer of 16 associated domains to the coalition," ACE said . Dizigom's domains now redirect visitors to ACE's "Watch Legally" portal, where they can find out more about legitimate streaming sources. ​ACE is backed by more than 50 major television networks and film studios, including Disney, Warner Bros., Netflix, Paramount, Sony Pictures, and Universal Pictures, and it focuses on taking down illegal streaming services through civil litigation, criminal referrals, and cease-and-desist operations.
