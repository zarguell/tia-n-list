Vendor advisory published 2026-11-04 (14:44 UTC) for improper certificate validation (CWE-295) in WatchGuard AuthPoint Gateway, allowing an on-path attacker to intercept and modify communications between the Gateway and the LDAP server. CVSS v4.0 score 9.1 (critical); affects versions 1.0 up to but not including 7.6.0, fixed in 7.6.0. WatchGuard states it is not aware of any exploitation in the wild.
