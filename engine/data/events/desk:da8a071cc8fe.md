CISA added CVE-2021-22681 to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on March 5, 2026 with a required-action due date of March 26, 2026 under BOD 22-01, citing an insufficient protected credentials vulnerability in multiple Rockwell products. The entry states the Logix Designer key can be discovered, letting an unauthorized application connect to Logix controllers from the network. It links the Rockwell support advisory (answer 1130301), ICSA-21-056-03, and NVD as sources.
