Transport for London (TfL) has announced that around 265,000 Oyster photocard customers may have had their account details exposed in a data breach. TfL is London's public transport authority, operating the Underground, Overground, buses, trams, and the Elizabeth line. It recorded 3.53 billion passenger journeys in 2025/26, including 1.78 billion bus journeys and 1.20 billion Tube journeys. Oyster photocards are travel smartcards with the holder's photo that give eligible passengers free or discounted travel. The organization discovered the security incident on November 16, 2026, after receiving customer reports about emails requesting them to create an account, which came directly from TfL's mail systems as a result of a compromise. The Oyster photocard website has now been taken offline and the organization is sending impacted individuals data breach notifications. Oyster photocards remain valid for travel, and customers who need to renew their cards or check their journey history can still do so by contacting TfL's Customer Services.
