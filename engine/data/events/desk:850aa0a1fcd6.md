Samsung's SMR Sep-2025 advisory entry for SVE-2025-1702, assigned CVE-2025-21043, describes a critical out-of-bounds write in libimagecodec.quram.so prior to SMR Sep-2025 Release 1 on Android 13, 14, 15 and 16 that allows remote attackers to execute arbitrary code. The page states the issue was reported to Samsung on August 13, 2025 by the Meta and WhatsApp Security Teams and privately disclosed, and carries the note that Samsung was notified an exploit for the issue has existed in the wild. The patch fixed the incorrect implementation.
