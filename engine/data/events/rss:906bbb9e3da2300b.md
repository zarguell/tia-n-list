Fraudulent reward portals and fake gift card giveaways are surging globally, tricking users into deceptive verification loops and paid subscriptions. These scams lure victims with a simple pitch: click an ad featuring a celebrity endorsement, claim a free gift card from a well-known brand, and complete a quick "human verification" step to unlock the reward. Instead, these campaigns operate as large-scale affiliate monetization schemes designed to collect personal information, generate application installs, and enroll unsuspecting users in recurring mobile subscriptions. Based on activity observed by CTM360, analysts identified more than 1,000 incidents linked to this recurring campaign. Threat actors exploit consumer familiarity with established brands, frequently impersonating major retailers and consumer platforms such as PlayStation, Sephora, SHEIN, Walmart, and Amazon.
