BleepingComputer reported on 5 August 2026 that CISA had added Langflow CVE-2026-9198, N-able N-central and Apache Tomcat flaws to the KEV catalog, giving federal agencies three days to mitigate. It describes the Langflow flaw as a CVSS 9.8 issue that chains two API endpoints on default deployments to bypass login and run code, with functional proof-of-concept exploits public since late July. The article says CISA confirmed active exploitation but did not attribute the activity or say whether ransomware was involved.
