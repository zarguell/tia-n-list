Modules render hidden video ads under the identities of legitimate CTV apps and enroll devices in eleven residential proxy networks, including PXYEDGE, which Satori assesses as the successor to IpMoYu. HUMAN links the catalog to TXCZ through C2 infrastructure, build-property checks, a preinstalled launcher and repository history, and publishes indicators covering drgdj[.]top, shzhtxcz[.]com and PXYEDGE servers.
