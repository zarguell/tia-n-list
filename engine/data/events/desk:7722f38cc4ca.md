Microsoft's support document lists the issue as fixed, stating classic Outlook for Windows builds 20026.20182 and higher could fail to show Copilot Chat or Copilot entry points for users with a Copilot Chat (Basic) license or paid M365 Copilot (Premium) account. The root cause was a bug that prevented Outlook from retrieving required account information from the user's Outlook profile. Workarounds included using new Outlook or OWA or creating a new Outlook profile, and Microsoft noted the button can also be hidden by licensing, app configuration, update channel, privacy settings, or other factors.
