Ernestas Naprys reports that an unsecured database assembled from multiple leaks exposed about 2 billion credential records across 8,471 plaintext folders, including session tokens, cookies, device details and saved application credentials. The largest domain-matched sets were 608 million Gmail records and 114.3 million other Google service records, with tens of millions tied to Facebook, Instagram, Amazon, Roblox, Yahoo, Microsoft and X, plus 61 million .gov and 46 million .edu addresses. The article notes that duplicates mean the count is not a victim count and that the repository owner could not be identified. Fowler's guidance centered on updated software, trusted applications and multi-factor authentication.
