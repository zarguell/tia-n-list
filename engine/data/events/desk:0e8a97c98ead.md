UpGuard Research (Greg Pollock) scanned roughly 300,000 domains showing signs of Supabase use and identified 16,326 databases exposing readable tables. Over half carried indicators of PII, with smaller subsets exposing passwords and authentication tokens and a very small set of plausible credit card data. The report attributes exposure to missing or ineffective row-level security and misuse of public keys, and notes tables created programmatically through the API do not enable RLS by default. UpGuard notified application owners where deeper analysis found significant exposure.
