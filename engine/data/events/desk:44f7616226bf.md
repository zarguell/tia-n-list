Ireland's NCSC warned that attackers had gained unauthorised access to refrigeration controllers directly reachable over the public internet and could view or modify configurations. It listed Danfoss AK-SC and AK-SM series and Copeland and Dixell XWEB devices as affected families. Recommended actions included removing direct internet exposure, using a secured VPN for remote access, and reviewing temperature setpoints, defrost schedules, alarm settings, remote access permissions and user accounts.
