The Red Hat bug record for CVE-2026-107360 was filed on 2026-10-07, documenting that the safemode parser allows op-assign AST nodes (op_asgn2, op_asgn_or, op_asgn_and, safe_op_asgn2 and op_asgn1) without inserting .to_jail on the receiver. The description credits Kaya Emre Arikan (GitHub kemrec), with confirmation from Foreman Security's Lukas Zapletal that a template-only user could call Host#destroy. The record was updated on 2026-11-12 when RHSA-2026:99949, 99950 and 99952 were linked as the fixes.
