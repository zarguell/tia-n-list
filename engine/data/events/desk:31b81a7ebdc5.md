Cloud Software Group published the NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway security bulletin covering the November 2026 CVE set, including CVE-2026-120869, CVE-2026-118268, and CVE-2026-118269, with fixes in builds 13.1-64.39 and 14.1-73.57 for ADC and Gateway. The bulletin also covers NetScaler ADC FIPS, NetScaler Console, and Secure Access Client for Windows and Linux, with fixed versions 14.1-73.58, 13.1-37.293, 14.1-74.34, 26.11.16, and 26.10.4 respectively. Administrators are urged to upgrade affected appliances and clients to the fixed builds.
