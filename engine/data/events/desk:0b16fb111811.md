DIVD's initial public disclosure of its own breach, published roughly three days before the story's first_seen. The organization confirms it was hacked after almost seven years, states the modus operandi indicates an agentic AI powered attack, and says it is handling the case as worst-case-scenario and assume breach until proven otherwise. It blocked access to its infrastructure, started forensics with a third-party incident response team, reported the incident to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens and the National Cyber Security Centre, and discussed options with the police.
