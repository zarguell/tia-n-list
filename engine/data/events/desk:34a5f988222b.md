OX Security disclosed three CVSS 9.2 Pillow vulnerabilities, including CVE-2026-112900 (disclosed 19 Oct 2026 according to their metadata). The post states a crafted EPS, TIFF, or JPEG file can trigger command execution or heap corruption in any Python service that processes user-supplied images. The article was published 22 Oct 2026 but references a disclosure date of 19 Oct 2026.
