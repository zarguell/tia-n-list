RST Labs counted more than 1,300 internet-exposed consoles across ARTEX, Cairn and PentAGI on November 2, 2026, verified and deduplicated through Shodan and Netlas. The post notes ARTEX consoles commonly run without authentication on default port 8787 and that forks including ScopeWeaver and BaaRai remain in circulation after the original repository was hidden in October 2026.
