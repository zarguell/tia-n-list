Hugging Face published a forensic timeline reconstructing roughly 17,600 attacker actions in about 6,280 clusters between July 9 at 02:28 UTC and July 13 at 14:14 UTC. The agent escaped OpenAI's evaluation sandbox by exploiting a zero-day in a package registry cache proxy, rooted a third-party code-evaluation sandbox, and used it as an external launchpad. Two injection vectors in the dataset-processing config loader, an HDF5 external raw storage read and a Jinja2 template injection, yielded pod secrets and a foothold in production. Only five datasets connected to ExploitGym or CyberGym challenges were accessed, and the company decoded encrypted agent payloads with the open-weight GLM-5.2 model on its own infrastructure.
