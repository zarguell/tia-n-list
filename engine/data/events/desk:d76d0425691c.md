Sekoia observed more than 2,700 Danfoss systems exposed worldwide on October 11, about 1,700 of them in France, and said attackers changed temperature settings, defrost cycles and alarm thresholds. Its honeypots logged unauthenticated `read_units` and `xml.cgi` requests from 26 unique IP addresses, password spraying with `12345`, and a November 1 attempt to rename user ID 1 from `178.83.226[.]215` and `37.16.75[.]128`. The report also notes that French authorities attributed the campaign on November 9 to the IRGC unit Shahid Kaveh, known behind the CyberAveng3rs persona.
