CISA added CVE-2024-21182 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on June 1, confirming active exploitation of this Oracle WebLogic Server flaw. The vulnerability allows **unauthenticated remote attackers to gain access via the T3 and IIOP protocols** — a classic attack surface for WebLogic that has historically been leveraged by ransomware operators. Federal agencies must remediate by **June 4, 2026** under BOD 22-01. While Oracle patched this in its July 2024 CPU, the KEV addition signals attackers are now scanning for and exploiting unpatched instances at scale.
