The upstream GitHub advisory GHSA-6j8m-r32v-4h38 was published on 17 November 2026 at 16:58 UTC and carries CVE-2026-110486 with a Critical rating. It states that brokers 2.1.0 through 2.1.2 using a websockets listener with the built-in websockets implementation are vulnerable to an out of bounds write and potential remote code execution, and credits orosec. The listed mitigations are disabling websockets support or compiling with libwebsockets; the advisory lists CWE-191, CWE-787 and CWE-908.
