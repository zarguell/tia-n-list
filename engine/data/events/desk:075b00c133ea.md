Okta Threat Intelligence reported that organizations targeted by O-UNC-053 began appearing on a new data leak site branded INCEPTION instead of the ShinyHunters site that published targets from March through September 2026. The cluster used vishing and a phishing kit to take SSO credentials and MFA factors, and Okta said the kit could not bypass phishing-resistant methods such as Okta FastPass and FIDO2/WebAuthn passkeys. Okta observed ShinyHunters listings 1 to 28 days after phishing domain registration, but said it could not validate the published data or confirm who operates INCEPTION.
