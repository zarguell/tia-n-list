Drupal published SA-CORE-2026-017 on 18 November 2026 for CVE-2026-132165, rating it critical at 18 out of 25 with no authentication required and exploitability listed as Proof. The advisory says an attacker can use the core installer at install.php to attempt a reinstall under specific conditions, leading to site takeover or remote code execution, and that some paths are mitigated when the web server cannot write to or change permissions on settings.php. Affected versions are Drupal below 10.6.22, 11.0.0 before 11.3.22 and 11.4.0 before 11.4.13; the flaw was discovered by Anthropic's OSS Scanner as ANT-2026-GMRPK0BU.
