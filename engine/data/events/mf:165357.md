In an incident observed in early June 2026, attackers used two compromised service principals application identities used to access cloud resources to map a victim’s environment, delete storage accounts and collect access keys. They also tried to remove protections tied to backup and recovery. Sysdig first reported JADEPUFFER in July 2026, describing a separate database-extortion attack that it assessed as the first documented agentic ransomware operation . Microsoft’s findings add the first detailed account of the actor’s Azure activity. However, Microsoft said the timing and parallel operations in this incident strongly indicate automation or scripting; its report does not establish that AI directed every Azure action. Storm-3168 Targets Azure Cloud Assets The first compromised service principal spent about 15 hours and 30 minutes discovering virtual machines, subscriptions, resource groups and other resources. It completed more than 300 successful read operations. A second service principal also surveyed the environment before carrying out the destructive activity. Both used Storm-3168-linked infrastructure, the same network fingerprint and the python-requests/2.34.2 user agent. The second identity then attempted more than 150 destructive or credential-related operations over 35 minutes.
