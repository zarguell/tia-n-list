CTM360 published its report "Gift Card Scams: Fake Rewards & Brand Impersonation" on 2026-11-04, three days before the story first surfaced. The report identifies more than 1,000 incidents tied to a gift-card scam campaign using fraudulent advertisements, celebrity-themed promotions, brand impersonation, and fake reward portals. It describes how the operations monetize through deceptive surveys, app-installation offers, and recurring subscriptions, impersonating brands including PlayStation, Sephora, SHEIN, Walmart, and Amazon.
