This strategic shift aims to protect users against future quantum computing attacks and mitigate data storage threats, following official approval by the National Institute of Standards and Technology . Because the technical specifications of ML-KEM make it incompatible with previous implementations, the browser must completely abandon the older framework to maintain optimal system performance. Retaining both security models would negatively impact network efficiency due to the substantial file sizes associated with post-quantum cryptography data exchanges. The upgraded protection will be natively integrated into Chrome 131 , though development channels are scheduled to receive testing support much earlier.
