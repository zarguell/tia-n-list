What Does the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) Mean for SOC Teams? Applicable since January 17, 2025, the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) establishes a common framework for managing ICT risks and strengthening operational resilience across the EU financial sector . The regulation covers five main areas: ICT risk management ICT-related incident management and reporting Digital operational resilience testing ICT third-party risk management Cyber threat intelligence and information sharing (voluntary sharing arrangements) Much of the work involved in supporting DORA compliance already falls within the scope of SOC operations: continuous monitoring, threat detection, incident investigation, and response.
