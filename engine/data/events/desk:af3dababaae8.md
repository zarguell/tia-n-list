Oracle's out-of-band Security Alert, initially released June 10, 2026, fixes CVE-2026-35273 in PeopleSoft Enterprise PeopleTools 8.61 and 8.62. The flaw is remotely exploitable without authentication over HTTP and leads to remote code execution, rated CVSS 9.8, in the Updates Environment Management component. Credit for the report goes to researchers at Trend Zero Day Initiative and Trend Research.
