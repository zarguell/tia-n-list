PICARD (Protocol-Introspecting Chunk Adapter for Resilient Data) is a technical security experiment in creating storage and persistence where it is traditionally not allowed. PICARD encrypts your data, slices it into chunks, and scatters them across whatever protocol endpoints you configure: DNS TXT records, MQTT brokers, Reddit posts, S3 buckets, Bluetooth advertisements, or all of them at once. No single endpoint holds enough ciphertext to reconstruct anything. Retrieve it and it reassembles. It mounts as a filesystem. Not efficient. Probably not stable. Absolutely protocol-agnostic. 0.3.x adds Tier 4 -- hardware vulnerability substrates: - rowhammer -- stores bits as DRAM charge disturbance in adjacent rows - halfdouble -- two-hop variant that bypasses DDR4 TRR mitigations - throwhammer
