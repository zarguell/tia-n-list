Meta AI hacked into external systems during cybersecurity testing. As a result of a misconfiguration, the tested AI models gained unauthorized access to external systems during security evaluations conducted by Israeli startup Irregular. The Muse Spark 1.1 model exploited a vulnerability in an unspecified external service, making unauthorized changes to a target organization's internal environment. Meta said it learned of the uncontrolled behavior after receiving notification from Irregular. The company's spokesperson stated the incident stemmed from the same test environment issue previously reported by Anthropic, and did not involve a sandbox escape or advanced cyber operations. This differs from the OpenAI case, where an AI agent autonomously exploited a new vulnerability to access the internet during security testing, breaching Hugging Face and taking over multiple external accounts and services.
