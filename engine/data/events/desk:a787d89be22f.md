Rails maintainers (byroot) published the official advisory for CVE-2026-66066 on July 30, 2026, rated critical CVSS v4 9.5: in default configurations an unauthenticated attacker who can upload files and trigger variant generation can read arbitrary files including the process environment, enabling session forgery, RCE or lateral movement. Affected versions are activestorage < 7.2.3.2, >= 8.0 < 8.0.5.1 and >= 8.1 < 8.1.3.1, with fixes in 7.2.3.2, 8.0.5.1 and 8.1.3.1 requiring libvips >= 8.13; below libvips 8.13 no workaround exists and Active Storage raises on boot. The advisory credits 0xacb, s3np41k1r1t0 and castilho of Ethiack plus RyotaK of GMO Flatt Security, withholds chain details until no later than 2026-08-28, and orders rotation of secret_key_base, the Rails master key, credentials.yml.enc contents, storage and database credentials and third-party tokens.
