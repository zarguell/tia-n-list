White House National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross has been fired, according to a White House official and two people familiar with the matter, creating another leadership opening in the Trump administration’s cybersecurity team. Axios https://www.axios.com/2026/11/19/trump-national-cyber-director-sean-cairncross-leaving ”>first reported his departure⁠. Pentagon Chief Information Officer Kirsten Davies will take his place, according to a defense official who spoke on the condition of anonymity to communicate the personnel change. A source close to the White House told Nextgov/FCW that President Trump fired Cairncross because he lacked the credentials to perform the job. Davies is taking the job in an acting capacity, this source added. Cairncross had been leading the Office of the National Cyber Director, which advises the president on cybersecurity and coordinates policy across federal agencies. Its responsibilities include overseeing implementation of the national cyber strategy and working with industry to protect the systems that government and businesses rely on.
