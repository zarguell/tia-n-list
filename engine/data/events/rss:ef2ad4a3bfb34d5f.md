Alongside the NCA, DOJ, FBI, Meta, and partners across 10 countries, Silent Push mapped adversary infrastructure behind West African scam centers during Joint Disruption Week in London. Every scam center requires digital infrastructure to operate: domains, hosting, and a steady stream of fresh accounts to scam new victims in various ways. Such operations can quickly go dark when a massive-scale, collaborative law enforcement effort disrupts those components. That principle drove Silent Push’s role in Joint Disruption Week, a coordinated international operation called Operation Blackout, aimed at criminal scam networks operating out of West Africa. Hosted by the UK’s National Crime Agency (NCA) in London, the effort brought law enforcement and private industry together to share intelligence in real time and act on it.
