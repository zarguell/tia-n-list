A coalition of Google, Plume, TrendAI, Lumen, HUMAN Security, Synthient, and Japan's National Police Agency announced a coordinated takedown of the X.CPE botnet hidden in more than 1.3 million set-top boxes across more than 200 countries. The backdoor was embedded in device firmware before sale, and the takedown of affiliate command-and-control domains was expected to cut access to at least 10 million additional compromised devices.
