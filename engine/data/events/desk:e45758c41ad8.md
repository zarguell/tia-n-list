N-able's status page says a disruption first reported on November 16 was caused by a security incident involving unauthorized access to one server in the Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud environment. The company says it disabled UI and API access proactively while mail flow and spam filtering continued, and that it had no evidence at that time of customer data or tenant mailbox compromise. N-able states that this incident is unrelated to the N-central incident.
