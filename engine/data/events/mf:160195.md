Hardware wallet maker Trezor has disclosed a third-party data breach affecting approximately 13,689 customers after unauthorized actors accessed systems operated by its shipping and fulfillment provider, ShipMonk. The incident exposed order-related personal data and poses a significant risk of targeted phishing , social engineering, and potential physical-world fraud against cryptocurrency holders. Trezor said ShipMonk notified the company of unauthorized access to systems holding customer information on August 10, 2026. ShipMonk Data Breach The investigation remains ongoing, but Trezor emphasized that the breach occurred within ShipMonk’s environment, not Trezor’s own infrastructure. The company stated that its devices, products, services, and customers’ wallet backups remain secure. The incident affected customers in the United States, United Kingdom, Sweden, Colombia, Brazil, Italy, and Portugal. Trezor initially identified orders received between May 10 and August 8, 2026, as being within the affected dataset. Of the 13,689 affected individuals, 11,742 had their names, email addresses, phone numbers, and shipping addresses fully exposed. A further 1,947 customers had a more limited set of information exposed: name, city, and email address. Trezor cautioned that this partially exposed group may include older orders and said it is verifying the precise timeframe with ShipMonk.
