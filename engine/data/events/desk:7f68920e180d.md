HarfangLab's report TRR260501 documents a dozen waves of spearphishing against Ukrainian state institutions since September 2025 abusing CVE-2025-8088, with the earliest exploit-bearing email dated September 26, 2025, delivering the GammaDrop and GammaLoad VBScript downloaders through Cloudflare Workers C2 with a kosoyed[.]ru fallback. Spoofed mail was relayed from the 194.58.66.0/24 subnet using stolen credentials, and delivery succeeded largely because impersonated government domains lacked enforced SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. HarfangLab attributes the activity to Gamaredon (UAC-0010) with high confidence and published IoCs and detection rules alongside the report.
