The Register reported Fireworks' second October incident and the wider customer data exposure involving billing records, datasets, and incomplete model weights. It states a third-party cloud provider hosting the company's research and development machines was compromised on October 23 and that attackers used that access the next day to reach Fireworks development systems and run an unauthorized workload. Fireworks suspects links to financially motivated criminals involved in LLMJacking, selling unauthorized access to AI models, and notes the first incident was disclosed on October 9 after detection on October 6.
