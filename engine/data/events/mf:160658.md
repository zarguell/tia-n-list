D-Link has disclosed and patched 15 Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVEs) affecting its DWR-M961 4G/LTE router, specifically hardware revision C1. This follows the discovery of multiple command injection and buffer overflow vulnerabilities in the device’s web management components. These vulnerabilities affect non-US DWR-M961 devices running firmware versions 1.1.2_C1_202602110044 and earlier revisions.
