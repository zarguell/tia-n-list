Insurance Journal reported on October 30, 2026 that Japan was facing an intense wave of AI-powered cyberattacks, with dozens of incidents affecting companies from brokerages to delivery firms. TrendAI vice president Tom Kellermann attributed many attacks to Russian-speaking groups such as LockBit and Qilin and said AI agents and open-source hacking tools lowered barriers against Japan's outdated infrastructure. Daiki Fukumori of GMO Cybersecurity by Ierae linked the Chinese-developed ARTEX tool to many recent attacks, and Yutaka Sejiyama of Macnica said at least 81 incidents had been reported since July, nearly a third of attacks in the last three years. The article also noted Japan's PHP exposure and emergency patching by firms such as Mizuho Financial Group.
