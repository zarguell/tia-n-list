Russian hosting provider ESTT said a cyberattack using file-encrypting malware disrupted services across its cloud infrastructure on Sunday, November 8, 2026. By Monday it had restored 90 percent of customers' virtual servers, though customers still could not access account dashboards, and the company said it found no evidence of a customer data leak. The article ties the hit to the Nov. 2 Beget outage, which RBC reported affected around 500,000 online resources including government service websites, news outlets, and online stores.
