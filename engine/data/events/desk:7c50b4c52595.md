Metro reported on 18 November that Transport for London had taken its Oyster photocard website offline after noticing suspicious activity on 16 November. TfL said about 265,000 photocard customers might have had account details exposed, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, dates of birth and security questions and answers. The article says all photocard customers were advised to change their passwords and security questions and answers, and that TfL had found no evidence at that point of the data being used.
