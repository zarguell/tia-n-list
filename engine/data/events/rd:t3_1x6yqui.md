Update to my post from a few days ago . That thread got a lot more attention than I expected, so thanks to everyone who jumped in. Full disclosure is out now. PWN.AI published their writeup today on CVE-2026-99999 , which they’re calling PwnEscape. It’s a guest-to-host escape. KVM itself isn’t the problem: it’s the kernel’s IPv4 fragmentation path (ip_do_fragment()). Possible effects of exploits include, host memory leaking out in packets, host crashes and in their lab setup, a working chain that got root on the host. Tested on 6.12 and 7.x. The full affected range isn’t listed, so assume you’re affected until your distro says otherwise. If you run multi-tenant VMs or untrusted guests, this goes to the top of your patch list.
