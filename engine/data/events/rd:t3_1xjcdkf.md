I run a small Spanish security site and for the last couple of months I've been measuring, every month, whether the corporate domains of Europe's biggest listed companies are protected against email spoofing. October's edition covers five indices: 266 index seats, 226 unique companies once you remove the overlap. 📊 The headline numbers (public DNS, measured 12 October 2026) 🔴 48 of 226 (21%) have no effective DMARC policy: either no record at all or p=none. Anyone can send mail "from" their corporate domain and the receiver is never asked to block it. 🟡 41 more only send forged mail to spam (quarantine) or only partly. 🟢 137 (61%) publish p=reject. 📉 By index, companies without an effective policy: Euro Stoxx 50: 3 of 50 DAX 40: 7 of 40 CAC 40: 7 of 40 IBEX 35: 11 of 35 FTSE 100: 24 of 100
