CNBC reported that JPMorgan Chase attributed delayed transactions on 16 November to a temporary software bug in on-premise hardware systems. A spokesperson told CNBC there was no evidence the issue was cyber-related and that it was not systemic, with other banks affected only where they were party to a Chase transaction. Users reported errors as early as 11:30 a.m. ET, and U.S. Bank said its own systems were not directly affected even though Zelle payments with other institutions could be delayed.
