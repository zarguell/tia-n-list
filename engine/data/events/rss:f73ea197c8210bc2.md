TOP ATTACKS AND BREACHES TSMC, a major Taiwanese semiconductor manufacturer, has confirmed a data breach at an unnamed cloud provider that exposed information about current and former employees. A hacker advertised 6.9 GB of allegedly stolen employee data on a cybercrime forum. According to the company, TSMC’s own systems and customer data were unaffected. Danish shipping and logistics giant Maersk confirmed unauthorized access and data theft from a legacy system inherited through an acquisition. The WhiteArmor extortion group claimed to have stolen approximately 100GB of information and demanded $5 million. Maersk stated that its other systems and customer operations remained unaffected. Japan’s Osaka city government disclosed a data breach exposing more than one million email addresses after attackers compromised a third-party email distribution service. The November 2 attack affected nearly 400 customers of the provider, exposing approximately 77 million addresses and enabling the distribution of fraudulent emails.
