This TrendAI Research write-up by Richard Chen and Lucas Miller, published April 23, 2026, details the double free in ikeext.dll that Microsoft's WARP & MORSE team discovered, caused by shallow-copied blob pointers during IKEv2 Encrypted Fragment reassembly, with code execution possible under the IKEEXT service as SYSTEM. Detection guidance is to monitor UDP 500 and 4500 for an IKE_SA_INIT carrying the Microsoft Security Realm Vendor ID byte sequence 68 6a 8c bd fe 63 4b 40 51 46 fb 2b af 33 e9 e8 followed by a fragmented IKE_AUTH with an SKF payload, noting the four-byte non-ESP marker shifts offsets on port 4500.
