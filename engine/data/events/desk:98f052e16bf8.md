ForkLog reported on November 14, 2026 that Kaspersky had seen attempts to infect computers with WebRAT when users visited certain Telegram channels. The article says the activity ran a hidden PowerShell script that downloaded the Rust trojan, with the first attempts recorded on the night of November 13. It also reports that Telegram disabled server-side support for HTML widgets, so bots could no longer send new widgets while previously received ones remained in chats.
