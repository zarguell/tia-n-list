Arm's Mali GPU bulletin, initially released September 8, 2026 (day precision), lists ten CVE ids across the Bifrost, Valhall, and Arm 5th Gen GPU Architecture kernel and userspace drivers, mostly use-after-free and kernel information disclosure classes. CVE-2026-9034 and CVE-2026-11891 are reachable from a non-privileged process including via WebGL or WebGPU. Fixes are in r56p0 and above for Valhall and 5th Gen Architecture, while the Bifrost kernel driver (affected from r12p0 onward) has no vendor-neutral fixed version listed.
