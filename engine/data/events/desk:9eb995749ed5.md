Release announcement for UnrealIRCd 6.2.7.1 and a no-restart hot-patch covering nine security issues, one of them CRITICAL as CVE-2026-114713, which lets ordinary users execute commands with server or IRC operator authority. The remaining issues are crashes, hangs, denial of service and two IP address disclosure bugs. The hot-patch applies to UnrealIRCd 6.0.0 through 6.2.7 and, on almost all 6.x networks, without downtime.
