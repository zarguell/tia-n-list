Key takeaways Managing identity risk should be an essential part of any exposure management program. However, in many organizations, exposure management programs often don’t include their identity-security teams. Relying on siloed identity-security teams and tools leaves organizations vulnerable to attackers who chain identity flaws with other infrastructure vulnerabilities. Tenable One now ingests findings from third-party identity security providers, so that you can prioritize such findings alongside all other types of exposures in your attack surface. If your exposure management program can’t see identity risk, it’s missing a key piece of puzzle. According to SANS Institute’s “ 2026 State of Identity Threats & Defenses Survey Insights Event ” report, 55% of organizations suffered at least one identity-related breach during the 12-month survey period, and among those organizations, 21% suffered more than one breach. Attackers often chain identity flaws with other security issues, such as vulnerabilities and misconfigurations, to create attack paths to your most critical systems and data.
