Brick Fanatics reported on 6 November 2026 that BrickLink would remain offline until Monday, with no further explanation from the LEGO Group beyond an earlier notice that the marketplace would be down for most of the day for necessary work. The planned return window had already been extended once, and the site stayed unavailable past the end of that window. The report describes the outage as still unexplained at that point, with the platform showing only a maintenance notice.
