Guardio Labs has uncovered a Vietnamese-linked phishing operation that has compromised more than 30,000 Facebook accounts, primarily targeting business account owners in the United States and Europe. The campaign's distinguishing feature is its abuse of Google AppSheet to send fully authenticated phishing emails — because messages originate from legitimate Google infrastructure, they pass SPF, DKIM, and DMARC checks and bypass secure email gateways entirely. The operators developed four distinct attack clusters: the primary cluster directed victims to Netlify-hosted pages cloning the Facebook Help Center, harvesting credentials plus identity documents including dates of birth and government ID photos. A secondary cluster offered fake blue badge verifications through Vercel-hosted pages that intercepted MFA codes in real time using invisible Unicode characters to evade NLP-based detection. A third cluster used Google Drive to host Canva-crafted fake Meta notification PDFs linking to a Socket.IO-based phishing panel with live WebSocket traffic, enabling human operators to request specific 2FA codes and capture browser screenshots. A fourth cluster impersonated corporate recruiters from major tech brands to build trust before moving targets to off-platform channels. All stolen data was streamed in real time via Telegram bots to private channels for rapid account takeover. Attribution to a Vietnam-based operator was confirmed through Google Drive PDF metadata and Vietnamese developer comments embedded in malicious JavaScript.
