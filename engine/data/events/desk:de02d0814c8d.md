Security Measures Lab published on October 30, 2026 that Iris Plaza found a suspicious file in an external cloud service on October 28, 2026 and immediately suspended the Iris Plaza EC site and related systems. The article records that unauthorized access was confirmed and reported to police and Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission on October 29. In its later update it reports the affected scope as about 7.7 million people, including about 5.8 million members and former members and about 1.9 million non-members, with credit card data not stored in the affected cloud service.
