IT-Connect reported that SonicWall PSIRT investigated a report indicating potential exploitation of CVE-2026-19868 and urged customers to upgrade, and that CISA added the flaw to KEV the same day, confirming active exploitation. The article notes the flaw affects only SSL-VPN where SAML is enabled and carries a CVSS score of 7.1. It describes the bypass as requiring valid credentials for another authentication method.
