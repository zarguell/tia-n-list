Pseudonymous researcher Nightmare Eclipse published the LegacyHive zero-day on July 14, 2026, shortly after that month's Patch Tuesday: a local privilege escalation in the Windows User Profile Service (profsvc) that abuses arbitrary registry hive loading so a standard user can mount another user's hive, including an administrator's. The public proof of concept is deliberately partial and requires additional user credentials, but experts warned capable attackers could weaponize the missing pieces quickly, noting the researcher's earlier drops saw rapid criminal adoption. A Microsoft spokesperson said on July 16 the company was aware of the reported vulnerability and actively investigating.
