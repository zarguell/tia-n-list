Here are this week’s highlights: ICS-Sniper attack picks off critical OT traffic University of British Columbia researchers have detailed ICS-Sniper , a proof-of-concept attack aimed at industrial facilities that rely on cloud-hosted SCADA systems. An attacker who controls a router on the network path could study the size, direction and timing of VPN-encrypted packets to predict when critical commands or data will be exchanged, then drop payload-carrying packets during those windows. In tests on emulated versions of the SWaT water treatment testbed, the attack delayed the start of clean water pumping by about 15 minutes and, in a separate test, kept a valve open more than nine minutes longer than intended. Intel pulls SGX from Xeon roadmap after Diamond Rapids Intel says Diamond Rapids, its next-generation Xeon platform, will be the last to include SGX, as it focuses its confidential computing roadmap on TDX.
