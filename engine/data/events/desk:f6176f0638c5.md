UC Berkeley's Information Security Office alerted service owners on November 18, 2026 that F5 had disclosed two critical HTTP/2 flaws in ngx_http_v2_module. It described CVE-2026-123633 as a use-after-free in reverse-proxy configurations under worker connection exhaustion and said public proof-of-concept code existed for sibling CVE-2026-123007, with no active in-the-wild exploitation observed at publication. The notice recommends 1.30.7 stable or 1.31.9 mainline, NGINX Plus 37.0.8 build 1 or 37.3.0 build 1, a full service restart, and http2 off as an interim block.
