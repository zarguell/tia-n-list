Horizon3 researcher Zach Hanley published the original disclosure on September 1, 2026, a day before mainstream coverage began. The post documents the vulnerable code path in PhoneAppsHandler.pm (POSTDATA body parsed with XML::Simple::XMLin(), PhoneIP concatenated into a query executed as PostgreSQL superuser), a single-curl exploit yielding a reverse shell, and honeypot telemetry from Defused Cyber showing exploitation attempts on August 30 from 176.65.148.184, including an initial payload of nc 176.65.148.184 39323 | sh. It notes Shodan shows roughly 4,000 internet-exposed devices, most in the United States, and that exploitation evidence lands in /var/log/switchvox/db-quirks.log.
