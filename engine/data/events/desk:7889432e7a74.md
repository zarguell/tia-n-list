AMD published bulletin AMD-SB-7079 on November 17, 2026, for CVE-2026-43613, scored CVSS 4.0 5.1 with physical access required. The bulletin says insufficient authentication of RPMC increment completion in the Secure Processor firmware can let a physically present attacker suppress counter updates through an interposer and bypass rollback protection on systems with RPMC-capable SPI flash. AMD recommends maintaining physical control and watching for tampering until firmware is available, and lists target releases including ComboAM5PI_1.2.0.3n for EPYC 4004 and ComboAM4v2PI_1.2.0.13 RC1 for EPYC 4005 in November 2026, with embedded lines targeted for February 2027.
