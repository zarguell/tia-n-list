Why the Risk Operations Center Is Becoming the System of Record for Proactive Enterprise Risk Reduction The cybersecurity industry has spent years improving how we discover vulnerabilities. The challenge now is no longer finding risk. It is reducing risk faster than adversaries can exploit it. The PHP2Shell vulnerabilities disclosed in October are a reminder of how quickly exposure can become exploitation. Within hours of disclosure, attack activity was already being observed. Across more than 10,000 customer environments, the Qualys Threat Research Unit identified more than 166 million PHP installations , approximately three-quarters of them internet-facing. Yet only a small percentage were patched during the critical first hours after disclosure. This trend is only accelerating. Frontier AI is reducing the time and effort required for reconnaissance, vulnerability analysis, attack planning, and exploitation. The gap between disclosure and weaponization continues to compress, placing increasing pressure on defenders to prioritize and remediate risk at machine speed. At the same time, the enterprise attack surface has fundamentally changed. Risk is no longer concentrated in servers, laptops, and cloud workloads. It now extends into mobile devices, medical technology, healthcare environments, manufacturing operations, operational technology, connected devices, and other cyber-physical systems.
