AI: When guardrails for AI models become obsolete

The Berlin administration, previously criticized as slow and outdated, has become a tech pioneer ahead of many other major cities, deploying AI extensively. But the recent Berlin data breach exposed the flip side: open AI models make it easier for attackers to circumvent safety mechanisms. An opinion column by Sascha Lobo in Der Spiegel examines what an unconstrained AI could do with data stolen in the Berlin hack, and argues that traditional regulatory guardrails for AI models are losing relevance as open-weight models proliferate.
