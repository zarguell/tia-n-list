nFlo reported on November 7, 2026 that IBM Aspera Faspex 5.0.0 through 5.0.19 contains an incorrect authorization flaw (CVE-2026-123218, CVSS 9.8) letting an authorized user craft a specific message to bypass authorization and gain full administrative privileges. IBM is the CNA and the vendor advisory is confirmed. Fixed in 5.0.19 PL1.
