SonicWall PSIRT published advisory SNWLID-2026-0014 covering CVE-2026-19868, an authentication enforcement flaw in the SonicOS SSLVPN service that lets a user with valid credentials for an alternate authentication method bypass SAML controls, including SAML-enforced MFA. The CVE record marks discovery as external and lists Gen7 and Gen8 SonicOS builds 7.3.3-7015 and older and 8.2.2-8015 and older as affected. The record carries CWE-308 with a CVSS 3.1 base score of 7.5.
