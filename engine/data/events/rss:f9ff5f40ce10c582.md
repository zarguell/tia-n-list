A suspected supply chain attack targeting Optimizely has led to malicious JavaScript being loaded on numerous popular websites, including Zoom, CNN, Fox News, BBC, Salesforce, and GitLab, exposing visitors to scripts designed to steal cryptocurrency and payment information and exploit vulnerabilities in iOS devices. Optimizely is a digital experience and experimentation platform that organizations use to test and personalize their websites, with its JavaScript files loaded into customers' webpages to deliver this functionality. The attack was first uncovered by security researcher Scott Helme, who reported Monday that his team had identified 188 infected Optimizely JavaScript files belonging to 178 organizations after analyzing 1,611 scripts used by the platform. According to Helme's analysis, attackers inserted malicious code into Optimizely customer projects, causing JavaScript files hosted on the company's content delivery network (CDN) to load malware on websites that used the service. BleepingComputer independently confirmed that the malicious script loaded, or attempted to load, on numerous affected websites using scans recorded by urlscan.io, including Zoom , KLM , GitLab , BBC , Scheels , CNN , American Girl , ManageBuilding , Smartsheet , Stamps.com , and Salesforce . Other affected websites include Fox News , Mutual of Omaha , American Eagle , Air France , Change.org , Optimizely , HSBC Insurance , Bitly , IG , and UNICEF USA .
