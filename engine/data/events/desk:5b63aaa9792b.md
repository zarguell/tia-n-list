On 13 May 2026 researcher William Bowling (vakzz@zellic.io) posted the kernel patch that fixes the skb coalescing regression (SKBFL_SHARED_FRAG propagation failure) that creates the Fragnesia vulnerability. The email confirms the bug is a separate regression from Dirty Frag (CVE-2026-43284) in the XFRM ESP-in-TCP path and that exploitation allows in-place decryption over page-cache-backed frags.
