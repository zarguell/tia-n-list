Claroty Team82 published its original research on August 9th, 2026, detailing a hidden code-of-the-day authentication path in xml.cgi with action=getauth, a post-auth command injection in the SMTP email configuration, and nginx header configuration injection through action=load_nginx_headers. The researchers reversed the check_code_of_day routine and reconstructed a date-derived password, then used the SMTP flaw for remote code execution and an injected route to crash sm_app repeatedly. They counted 2765 exposed AK-SM 800A management interfaces in Censys and advise upgrading to firmware R4.3.1 and removing direct internet exposure.
