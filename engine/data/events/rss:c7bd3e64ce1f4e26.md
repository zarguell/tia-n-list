Check Point Research discovered a campaign we track as Yoba where attackers abuse the popularity of AI tools such as ChatGPT and Claude, creating fake GitHub projects that pretend to be desktop applications like GPT-5.6 Sol , GPT-6 Astra , and Claude Opus . The malware is placed next to legitimate, signed executables from OpenAI and Anthropic so it can be loaded indirectly, a technique commonly known as DLL sideloading, which is often used to bypass security protections. This campaign demonstrates strong operational security (OPSEC) by avoiding dedicated command-and-control (C2) infrastructure and instead relying on Telegram, Tor, Catbox, and DNS over HTTPS (DoH). The actor behind the campaign shows indicators that suggest Russian origins and appears to operate primarily in the UTC-4 time zone.
