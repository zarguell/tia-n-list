The Guardian reported that schools in Scotland, Northern Ireland, the Republic of Ireland, and Italy received hoax bomb threats, with the Italian email containing the words "we have come to annihilate you" and signed by "Mikhail Andropov, leader of the Vilayat of Misoginistan." The PSNI considered the risk to the public low, while the Garda Siochana assessed the threat as "not credible" and did not call for evacuations. Italy's national association of headteachers called the threat "clearly fake" but warned of the disruption caused by such messages.
