The Japan Times reported on October 20, 2026 that GMO Cybersecurity by Ierae, a subsidiary of GMO Internet Group, announced it had detected use of a Chinese-developed AI tool called Artex in attacks targeting Japanese companies, based on forensic logs. GMO linked the same tool to a rash of breaches at South Korean financial organizations reported earlier in the month by CrowdStrike. A Teikoku Databank survey cited in the article said around one-sixth of Japanese companies had suffered breaches. The announcement preceded the broader October surge disclosures by several weeks.
