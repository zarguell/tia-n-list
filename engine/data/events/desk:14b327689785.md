ManageEngine's METAL lab published its analysis of the UNC7017 campaign against AOMEI update infrastructure, describing how attackers changed both the download URL and the expected version number and MD5 checksum in the remote configuration file so clients would accept a malicious ZIP archive. Researchers confirmed three brief tampering windows of ten, six, and five minutes but note more may have occurred, and the same infrastructure also serves AnyViewer customers. METAL first detected the activity in late May and notified AOMEI in early June. Published October 16, 2026.
