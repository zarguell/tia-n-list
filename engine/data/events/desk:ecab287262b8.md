Focus Taiwan reported that a Taiwan university's alumni email account was allegedly hacked and used to send bomb threat emails to more than 6,000 public schools across Italy on November 11, citing Italian media reports. Screenshots showed messages sent around 9:50 a.m. Wednesday from an account under Dayeh University's domain, displaying the name "Liu Fang-chun"; the private university in Changhua County said an initial investigation found an alumnus' account had sent a large number of emails and appeared hacked, and it suspended the account. Taiwan's representative office in Italy said Taiwanese agencies were willing to provide technical assistance to Italian investigators. This is the earliest public reporting naming the Taiwanese sender account that Hudson Rock later tied to infostealer data.
