Scareware is a type of scam that uses fake security warnings to frighten you into downloading malicious software. Bad actors may use it to claim your device has a virus and urge you to click a link, call a number or pay for help. A pop-up suddenly fills your screen: “Your PC Is Infected — Call Now!!” There may be a loud warning sound, a countdown or a message telling you to contact Microsoft right away. These are common scareware tricks designed to make you panic and act before you have time to think. Scareware has been used to trick people into paying for fake security services and unwanted charges. In one case, the Federal Trade Commission accused Jesta Digital of using fake virus alerts on Android phones that could lead to $9.99 monthly charges on users’ mobile bills. The alert may be fake, but knowing that isn’t always easy when it appears on your screen.
