Advisory FG-IR-26-276, published November 10, 2026, covers CVE-2026-126262, a SQL injection [CWE-89] rated 9.3 in FortiAnalyzer or FortiManager with FortiAnalyzer features that lets an unauthenticated attacker who can reach the oftp port execute code or commands with crafted OFTP packets. FortiAnalyzer 8.0.0 through 8.0.1 moves to 8.0.2, 7.6.0 through 7.6.7 moves to 7.6.8, 7.4.0 through 7.4.11 moves to 7.4.12, and 7.2.0 through 7.2.12 moves to 7.2.13, with FortiManager 8.0.0 and 7.6.0 through 7.6.7 also listed. A companion advisory, FG-IR-26-274, covers CVE-2026-126260, a server-side request forgery [CWE-918] rated 9.0 on the same oftp port.
