AMD published security bulletin AMD-SB-9031 on November 9, 2026 describing insufficient access controls in AMD Management Console services that may expose stored management credentials, plus hard-coded credentials in AMD Management Console, AMD DASH CLI, and the AMD Manageability API that could allow unauthorized KVM sessions under specific provisioning conditions. The bulletin covers CVE-2026-70518 and CVE-2026-70519. It is part of the same November 2026 AMD advisory wave.
