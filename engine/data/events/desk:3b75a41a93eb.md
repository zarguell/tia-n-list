This advisory, published 2026-10-26 with a CVSS 7.8 vector of AV:L/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N, reports that nltk.parse.dependencygraph.dot2img resolves the Graphviz dot executable by bare name and runs it with a raw subprocess.run, skipping the ownership check and loader-environment scrub that nltk.pathsec.spawn_trusted applies. A local unprivileged actor who can write to any group or world-writable directory on the victim PATH can plant a trojan dot that executes as the victim when a DependencyGraph is rendered, including the Jupyter _repr_svg_ path. The advisory lists NLTK 3.10.3 and earlier as affected and 3.10.4 as patched.
