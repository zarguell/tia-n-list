Greg Kroah-Hartman announced CVE-2026-99362 on the linux-cve-announce list, describing an out-of-bounds write reached when a client negotiates ca_maxresponsesize_cached down to NFSD_MIN_HDR_SEQ_SZ and nfsd4_alloc_slot() creates a zero-length sl_data[] buffer. The advisory says a COMPOUND tag can fill the narrowed buffer up to the SEQUENCE opcode but leave out the status word, so the operation is treated as successful and nfsd4_store_cache_entry() copies the full reply past the slot. It includes a KASAN report of an 80-byte write in read_bytes_from_xdr_buf and nfsd4_sequence_done, names fs/nfsd/nfs4state.c as the affected file, and lists fixes in 6.12.117, 6.18.61, 7.3.2 and 7.4-rc1.
