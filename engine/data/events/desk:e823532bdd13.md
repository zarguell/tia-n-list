President Donald Trump on Monday nominated Michael LoSapio, chief information security officer at Palantir, to lead CISA, which has been without a Senate-confirmed director for the entirety of Trump's second term. CISA's first pick, Sean Plankey, languished in the Senate before withdrawing in April. LoSapio could face obstacles because Palantir is unpopular with Democrats, who have scrutinized the company's work with the Department of Homeland Security even as Palantir denies aiding mass surveillance of immigrants. Sen. Ron Wyden, D-Ore., had placed one of several Senate holds on Plankey pending release of a public telecom cybersecurity report, and some observers expected acting director Nick Andersen to continue holding the role.
