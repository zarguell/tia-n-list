The Joomla Project announced the 6.2.1 security and bugfix release on 24 November 2026 at 03:00 UTC, with a companion 5.4.11 security release. The announcement says 58 security fixes were packed into the two releases and lists advisory 20261111, 20261112, 20261117 and 20261156 as the critical group. It recommends backing up and updating every site right away through System > Update > Joomla.
