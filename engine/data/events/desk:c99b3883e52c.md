Rapid7 reports that on 25 February 2026 Cisco published an advisory for CVE-2026-20127, a critical authentication bypass vulnerability (CVSS 10.0) in the vdaemon service of Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN (formerly Viptela) affecting Controller and Manager instances. The flaw allows an unauthenticated attacker to bypass authentication and gain administrative privileges, and Cisco confirmed in-the-wild exploitation.
