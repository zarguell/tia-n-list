A Hacker News thread linking to the DreamHost status page reached 164 points, with commenters speculating that the File Manager, described as a Monsta FTP installation, may have been compromised through a recently published PHP vulnerability and that FTP credentials could have been exfiltrated. DreamHost's support chatbot reportedly denied a link to the PHP vulnerabilities, though commenters remained skeptical. The company had not published a root cause as of the thread.
