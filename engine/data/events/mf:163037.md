Cybercriminals are increasingly using autonomous artificial intelligence agents to speed up cyberattacks and reduce the amount of manual work required from human operators. Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) and Mandiant observed a suspected financially motivated threat actor compromise an organization’s cloud infrastructure and deploy a multi-agent attack framework. The attackers were able to plan, build, and execute a large credential-harvesting campaign in less than six hours. The campaign shows how threat actors are moving beyond basic AI prompts and beginning to use agentic AI systems that can independently perform several parts of an attack. Google said these systems can manage scanning operations, solve technical problems, make decisions, and continue working with limited human involvement. AI Agents Mass-Steal Credentials According to the report, the attackers used an AI coding chatbot together with prompts and preconfigured instructions written in Markdown files. These instructions acted like operational playbooks that told the AI agents how to perform different stages of the campaign. The autonomous framework scanned internet-facing systems for weaknesses and automatically attempted to harvest credentials.
