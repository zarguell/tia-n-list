1Password posted a community announcement at 5:33 PM UTC on November 25 saying no customer vault content or protecting keys were accessed and that services continued to operate normally. It described a limited amount of customer account information, including names and email addresses, taken from a subset of customers, and said independent cybersecurity experts had been engaged to validate containment and remediation. The post told customers no account action was required and warned about phishing attempts that reference invitations or account recovery.
