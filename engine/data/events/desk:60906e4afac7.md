Unit 42 published research on November 12, 2026, attributing a Cairn campaign to Chinese-speaking attackers tracked with moderate confidence as CL-STA-1238. Between August and September 2026 the actors ran 25 operations against at least 19 organizations in semiconductor manufacturing, U.S. defense policy and civil society, pairing Cairn with Zhipu GLM and DeepSeek. Ten targets saw unauthorized access or data retrieval, including more than one million personal records at one organization, military and government identities, persistent administrator accounts and modification of a live industry standards ballot.
