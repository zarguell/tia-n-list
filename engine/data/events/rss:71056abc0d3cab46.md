In June 2026, attackers used a compromised legacy credential at Klue, a market intelligence platform, to steal the OAuth tokens its customers had granted it for Salesforce. With those tokens, they pulled Salesforce data from more than a dozen companies, LastPass among them ( GovInfoSecurity , BleepingComputer , FINRA ). Ten months earlier, attackers had done the same thing with tokens taken from Salesloft’s Drift chat agent ( Google Threat Intelligence ). In both the cases the attackers didn’t break into the victim, they instead used access that a trusted integration already had.
