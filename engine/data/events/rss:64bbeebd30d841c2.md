TOP ATTACKS AND BREACHES Binance, a global cryptocurrency exchange, has suffered a cyberattack resulting in the theft of approximately $6.3 billion in digital assets. Attackers reportedly compromised a DevOps engineer’s laptop and abused internal wallet infrastructure to authorize unauthorized withdrawals. The incident ranks among the largest cryptocurrency thefts ever recorded and is linked to North Korean group. Polymarket, a US-based prediction market platform, has disclosed a cyberattack involving a compromised third-party service that distributed malicious JavaScript to its website. The script hijacked authenticated trading sessions and attempted unauthorized transactions. Approximately $2.7 million in cryptocurrency was traced to a suspected attacker-controlled wallet. Japan Airlines, a major Japanese international airline, has disclosed a third-party data breach affecting approximately 92,000 passengers through the Amadeus reservation system.
