The Register reported on 19 November that Equinor was still dealing with an intrusion on its IT systems and had given no timeline for full restoration. Equinor said production continued, email was among the affected services, and there was no indication data had been stolen or altered. The article noted that NSM had raised Norway's cyber risk assessment to four on a five-point scale and had reported a tripling of successful cyber operations against Norwegian businesses in the prior six months.
