Google's Threat Analysis Group, writing through researcher Neel Mehta, reported OpenSUpdater as a known unwanted-software family that downloads and installs other suspicious programs, with most targets in the United States and prone to downloading game cracks and grey-area software. Since mid-August 2021 its samples carried deliberately invalid DER signatures in which an End of Content marker replaced the NULL tag of the SignatureAlgorithm parameters, an encoding Windows accepts but OpenSSL-based security products reject. The post includes two sample hashes on VirusTotal and notes Google Safe Browsing was enlisted against the family. G DATA's September 2026 post cites this report as prior OpenSUpdater research.
