The job interview that installs PolinRider: inside the AccuraSoftHire campaign. JFrog and StepSecurity expose AccuraSoftHire, a fake social-engineering operation that poses as a legitimate employer to trick job candidates.

The campaign uses a bogus recruitment process: victims are lured through a fake job interview and, under the guise of an assignment or pre-employment task, are pushed to run a trojanized installer that deploys the PolinRider backdoor. The lure leverages the trust candidates place in hiring workflows to get the malware onto their machines.
