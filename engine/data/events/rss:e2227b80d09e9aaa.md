Two senators say that revelations about online targeted advertising being used as a vector for spyware infections underscore the need for better security practices in the industry. Some members of the advertising industry that were subjects of the report, meanwhile, think their practices are up to the task. The Citizen Lab at the University of Toronto, in partnership with the cybersecurity and ad-tech firm Confiant, last week published a report on AdFection, a technique whereby targeted ads were delivering zero-click mercenary spyware. “Ad vendors must significantly level up their cybersecurity and threat detection capacity to effectively cut off malicious advertisers across the supply chain,” The Citizen Lab report states . “They must rigorously and regularly vet, screen, and audit their business partners and hold them accountable for their own supply chains.” In 2023, Democratic Sens. Ron Wyden of Oregon and Cory Booker of New Jersey introduced legislation to ban advertising networks and facilitators from using personal data to target, but the legislation never advanced past introduction.
