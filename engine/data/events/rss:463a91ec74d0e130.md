During a year-long investigation, Check Point Research identified three distinct operational clusters spanning Latin America, Asia, and Europe targeting telecommunications providers, government organizations, military entities, research institutions, and critical infrastructure. The clusters , SilentDelivery, OceanGate and DarkWire , are linked through shared infrastructure features and malware code similarities within the Sparrow malware framework. We track the entity behind the Sparrow framework as CopperDragon , a potential digital quartermaster supporting China-nexus threat actors, including FamousSparrow , Earth Estries , TropicTrooper , UAT-9244 , and others. Each cluster employed a different toolset and different initial access techniques, including compromising internet-facing infrastructure and highly selective watering-hole attacks. The compromised websites that were used for watering hole attacks were of government, shipping, and IT and research sectors. Our research uncovered several previously undocumented tools and new details about the Sparrow framework, including Windows and Linux variants, passive backdoor capabilities , and a connection to the Melofee backdoor.
