FortiGuard Labs documents EncystPHP, a persistent FreePBX webshell delivered via CVE-2025-64328 in incidents starting early December 2025, and attributes the activity to INJ3CTOR3, an actor targeting VoIP infrastructure since 2019 via CVE-2019-19006 and later CVE-2021-45461. The report details the newfpbx UID-0 backdoor account, crontab entries re-downloading k.php droppers at one to three minute intervals, webshell copies masquerading as ajax.php across /var/www/html subdirectories, and dropper infrastructure at 45.234.176.202 and 187.108.1.130.
