Google Threat Intelligence Group and Mandiant documented LLMJacking, the hijacking of enterprise access to large language models, based on multiple 2026 incident responses across North America, South America, and Asia Pacific in financial services, retail, technology, and transportation. The report records fraudulent charges above 100,000 USD in a single day in some incidents and cumulative charges approaching 1 million USD at some organizations. It describes financially motivated clusters and downstream operators running LLM Mixers, illicit routing gateways that proxy, aggregate, and resell unauthorized LLM access to underground subscribers. Dominant initial access was exposed or stolen cloud identity tokens, including IAM credentials, service account keys, GitHub personal access tokens, and active session tokens.
