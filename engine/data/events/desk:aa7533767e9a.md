Sophos Emergency Incident Response documents an Interlock ransomware intrusion against a Windows 10 host in which the operator used WinPmem to capture a full physical-memory image and then ran Volatility3's windows.hashdump.Hashdump module against it to recover NTLM and LM hashes plus cached domain credentials. Sophos tracks the group as GOLD EMBRACE, a small non-RaaS crew active since September 2024 that runs double extortion and threatens leaks via its Worldwide Secrets Blog, and links it to the CVE-2026-20131 zero-day in Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center. This is the earliest vendor reporting on the credential-theft technique, predating the coverage queued on 2026-08-10.
