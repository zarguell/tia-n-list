N-able says attackers who breached one of its servers have caused a major outage affecting Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud services worldwide. On Tuesday, the company initially blamed the outage on what it described as a "combination of platform configuration and service dependencies" affecting Mail Assure and SpamExperts services across the Americas, Asia-Pacific, and Europe. However, one day later, N-able disabled UI and API access and confirmed unauthorized access to a server in the Hosted Cloud environment. N-able also said the cyberattack didn't affect email flow or spam filtering and that it has yet to find evidence that the attackers gained access to customers' data and mailboxes. "Our earlier update said the disruption came from platform configuration and service dependencies and we believed we had remediated the issue. As we continued our investigation, we determined that the disruption was caused by a security incident affecting the Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud environment," the company said . "We are aware of unauthorized access to one of the servers in the Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud. We have taken steps to mitigate the threat and are continuing to harden our services to reduce the risk of recurrence. The investigation is ongoing, but at this time we do not have evidence that customer data was compromised, including access to tenants' mailboxes."
