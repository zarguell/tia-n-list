TokenPost reported that Ledger was investigating reported cryptocurrency losses linked to devices sold through Southeast Asian reseller CryptoBilis, with estimated transfers exceeding $86 million across Bitcoin, Ethereum and Tron. Ledger asked CryptoBilis to pause sales and shipments while the investigation continued, and customers who bought devices within the past 90 days were advised not to initialize unused devices. Ledger had not confirmed a compromise of its devices, firmware or broader infrastructure, and the investigation was focused on the reseller channel serving Indonesia, Malaysia and the Philippines.
