Summary On August 11, 2026 , the renowned Austrian luxury jewelry brand FREYWILLE ( freywille.com ) was targeted by the ransomware group Aurora . The cyberattack compromised sensitive information, including employee files, trade secrets, and legal documents. The attack highlights the ongoing vulnerabilities in the luxury industry’s cybersecurity measures.
