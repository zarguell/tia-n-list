Advisory FG-IR-26-275, published November 10, 2026, covers CVE-2026-126261, an improper authorization in the FortiManager FGFM service rated 9.0 that lets an unauthenticated attacker able to reach the FGFM port execute code or commands with crafted FGFM packets. FortiManager 8.0.0 moves to 8.0.1, 7.6.0 through 7.6.7 moves to 7.6.8, 7.4.0 through 7.4.5 moves to 7.4.6, and 7.2.0 through 7.2.8 moves to 7.2.9. The acknowledgement credits Theo Leleu of the Fortinet Product Security team with the use of the frontier AI model Claude Mythos.
