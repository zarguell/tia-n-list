The CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog entry for CVE-2025-20362, Cisco Secure Firewall ASA and FTD VPN Web Server Missing Authorization Vulnerability, carries a dateAdded of September 25, 2025 and a due date of September 26, 2025 for federal agencies. The entry notes the vulnerability could be chained with CVE-2025-20333, which was added to the catalog the same day.
