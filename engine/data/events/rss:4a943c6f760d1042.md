Coles Group Limited, Australia's second-largest retailer, is investigating what it described as a "cyber security incident" that has not impacted the company's stores. Founded in 1914, Coles now employs over 115,000 people, manages a network of more than 1,800 retail outlets, and operates the Flybuys loyalty program with over nine million active members. In a statement issued earlier today, the company said that it's now working with external experts to secure its systems and added that it has yet to find evidence that the attackers stole customer, employee, or supplier data from compromised systems. "Coles Group Limited is currently investigating a cyber security incident. Based on the findings of our investigation to date, we have not identified any evidence that customer, team or supplier data has been compromised or removed from Coles' systems," the retail giant said . "Coles is working with the Australian Cyber Security Centre and will continue to engage with appropriate agencies and regulators as investigations continue. Coles is also working with global cyber security experts to contain and secure the Coles IT environment." It also noted that customers can shop as normal, as Flybuys accounts, Coles Plus, Coles Plus Delivery or QuiteLike subscriptions, and Coles stores and online outlets have not been affected by the cyberattack.
