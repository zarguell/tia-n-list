How to Check Your Computer for Malware To check for malware, run a full scan with your operating system’s built-in security tool, then review the apps, startup items, and browser extensions you don’t recognize. If you find something, disconnect from the internet, remove it, and change your passwords from a different, clean device. The 5-step check that works on any computer The same five steps apply on a Mac, a Windows PC, a laptop, or a Linux machine; only the menus change. Update first. Install pending operating system and browser updates so your built-in scanner knows the latest threats. Run a full scan. Use Windows Security on a PC, or the automatic XProtect checks plus a reputable on-demand scanner on a Mac. Review what runs at startup. Look for apps, login items, and background processes you didn’t install. Check your browsers. Remove unfamiliar extensions and reset the homepage and search engine if they changed. Act on the results. Remove anything suspicious, then check whether your passwords were exposed. What Is Malware on a Computer?
