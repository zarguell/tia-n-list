A Scoop Extras user reported that SoftwareOK's unversioned Q-Dir 12.84 portable download served a trojanized, unsigned executable and asked maintainers to block the manifest rather than accept its new hash. The report describes a machine infected after a build accepted during November 13-15, with `dsv2_py39_agent.7z` installed under `C:\ProgramData\Microsoft\DeviceSync\b2`, persistence and TCP traffic to `8.210.69.51:9876`; it lists remote shell, file transfer, screenshots and tunneling. It also supplies SHA-256 values for the malicious archive and embedded executable.
