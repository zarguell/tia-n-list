Here are this week’s highlights: Raindrop raises $35 million for AI agent monitoring Raindrop, designed to detect unknown failures in autonomous agents, announced a Series A funding round of $35 million, adding to last year’s $15 million seed round. Raindrop continuously analyzes agent behavior to surface silent and emerging failure modes, and to help AI systems repair and learn from them.
