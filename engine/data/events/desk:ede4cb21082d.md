Alan Coopersmith reposted Ruby's advisory to oss-sec, quoting the seven memory-safety flaws and the maintainers' statement that proof-of-concept exploits can trigger remote code execution in some scenarios. The message lists all seven GHSA identifiers, the affected ranges of 3.3.13 and earlier, 3.4.0 through 3.4.12 and 4.0.0 through 4.0.9, and the fixed releases 4.0.10, 3.4.13 and 3.3.14. It adds no exploitation telemetry of its own.
