Chainalysis reports a 5.2x year-over-year surge in blockchain-assisted cyberattacks and says nation-state operators from North Korea and Iran make up the majority of blockchain dead drop activity. It describes dead drops that store payloads or command-and-control data in on-chain transactions and smart contracts so campaigns survive domain seizures, hosting takedowns and repository removals. The post traces the tactic from Namecoin and Bitcoin OP_RETURN data through EVM EtherHiding to more recent state-linked use.
