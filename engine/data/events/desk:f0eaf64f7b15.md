NIST NVD published the CVE-2026-104935 entry on 13 October 2026, describing an authentication-bypass-by-spoofing vulnerability in Ivanti Connect Secure before version 25.1.3.0 that allows remote unauthenticated attackers to execute arbitrary commands. The entry references the Ivanti vendor advisory and CISA KEV listing with an exploitation status of active and an automatable technical impact of total. It carries a CVSS 3.1 base score of 8.6 (HIGH) and lists affected version ranges including 25.1.0.0 through 25.1.1.2 and 22.8 R2 series.
