**Abstract (translated from French):** "Deconstructing Modern macOS Initial Access Vectors" - For years, a persistent myth suggested macOS was inherently immune to malware. Today, threat actors are aggressively shattering that illusion by deploying sophisticated initial access chains tailored to bypass macOS defenses. The talk provides a deep-dive analysis of how modern adversaries gain their first foothold on Apple hardware.

The presentation dissected the entire initial access pipeline, starting with infection vectors: deceptive Google Ads, malicious ClickFix campaigns, and sophisticated malvertising that trick users into lowering their guard. It explored the execution phase, analyzing how attackers weaponize scripting languages including Bash, Python, AppleScript, Compiled AppleScript, Perl, and JavaScript for Automation (JXA). Finally, it examined delivery mechanisms, contrasting abuse of native binaries (Mach-O, Platypus-packaged apps, Electron frameworks) with trojanization of storage and installer formats (DMGs and PKGs).

Speaker: Stephan Berger (dfir.ch). BSides Frankfurt, September 10, 2026.
