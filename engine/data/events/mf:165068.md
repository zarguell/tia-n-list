What’s in the Silent Push Threat Research Roundup? The Silent Push Threat Research Roundup for September 2026 collects six public investigations into adversary infrastructure, published by the Silent Push Preemptive Cyber Defense team between June and September. Each investigation includes exclusive recommendations defenders can act on. Access the September Edition below: This edition covers early infrastructure tied to The Gentlemen ransomware group, and attacker infrastructure targeting Amgen that the team spotted seven months before the company’s SEC filing. It also includes Danglegeddon, a simulation of dangling DNS subdomain takeover at scale. The remaining investigations examine residential proxy bandwidth-sharing networks, North Korean IT worker recruitment, and a fast-flux phishing operation.
