AI is fundamentally changing how organizations develop software, manage cloud environments and operate their businesses. AI coding assistants are accelerating development, autonomous agents are introducing new security risks, and SaaS applications are expanding the attack surface. At the same time, frontier AI is accelerating attacks, with Unit 42 research demonstrating that an AI-assisted attack could reach data exfiltration in just 25 minutes in a simulated scenario. Cortex Cloud 2.3 advances our vision of a platform for autonomous cloud defense. The release introduces new capabilities to continuously reduce risk, defend in real time and start secure across modern cloud environments. Here's a look at the key innovations. Align Security Goals with Business Outcomes New Security Initiatives help organizations drive measurable risk reduction by setting security goals, prioritizing remediation and tracking progress over time. Teams can create custom initiatives or use recommended goals based on posture and finding trends. Figure 1: Security initiative to eliminate critical vulnerabilities that are exploitable and fixable For example, a security team can set a goal to eliminate all critical and high-severity vulnerabilities from production applications by a specific date. With Security Initiatives, teams can coordinate remediation and track progress toward that goal, making it easier to measure and demonstrate risk reduction.
