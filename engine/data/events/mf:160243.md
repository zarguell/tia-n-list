The springs at Las Vegas were essential to western travel. The Old Spanish Trail connected Santa Fe to Southern California through more than a thousand miles of difficult desert terrain. Travelers and their animals depended on those springs to recover before pressing on into the Mojave. In a much larger network, Las Vegas became important because it was a trusted node. Conversations at BSides Las Vegas 2026 kept returning to trust: Our environments run on trusted authentication methods. Workloads are increasingly relying on trusted identities. And attackers are learning to follow those trust relationships rather than fight through the front door. This year marked the 17th installment of this annual event, overlapping Black Hat and dovetailing into DEF CON, rounding out the big three events that make up Hacker Summer Camp. This year, for the first time since the pandemic, BSidesLV completely sold out of attendee badges, making it the largest BSides in years.
