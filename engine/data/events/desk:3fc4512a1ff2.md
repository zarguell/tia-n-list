WIRED reported HUMAN's original BADBOX and PEACHPIT research on October 4, 2023, describing backdoors on seven Android TV boxes and one tablet and signs that about 200 device models could be affected. Researcher Daniel Milisic had found the T95 streaming box infected out of the box in January 2023, and HUMAN found affected devices in homes, businesses and schools in the United States. The related PEACHPIT ad fraud operation used at least 39 Android and iOS apps, which Google and Apple said they were reviewing or removing.
