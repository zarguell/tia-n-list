Updating the Data Security Paradigm for the AI-Enabled Enterprise AI transformation is still top of mind for the organizations we help protect. Autonomous agents are becoming further embedded into operational processes – from coding, through business automation, to customer-facing interfaces. And with this proliferation of agents, data flows are far less predictable and exponentially faster. As such, to prevent sensitive data exposure, security must adapt. In this new era, visibility is not enough. What’s needed is a unified operating model for protecting data across posture, prevention, and response. With a unified paradigm in place, policies, detection, automation, and AI-powered remediation all feed from a single source of truth – updated in real time and faithful to the organization’s reality. Teams can then proactively monitor and respond to data security incidents at agent speed and with end-to-end context.
