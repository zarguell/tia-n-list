Solutions numeriques reported on 30 September 2026 that Maileva, the Docaposte subsidiary of La Poste, had been offline from 23 September at 07:00 until 29 September after what Docaposte described as unauthorized access attempts aimed at sending illegitimate mail. The incident was first revealed on 24 September by Cyberattaque.org and FrenchBreaches, who obtained the message Maileva sent to its clients. Docaposte told the outlet it did not yet know whether fraudulent letters had actually been sent.
