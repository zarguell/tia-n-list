What Happened Four notable UK prosecutions illustrate the growing intersection of cybercrime, online exploitation subcultures, and digital terrorism involving teenage offenders: Scattered Spider Operators Sentenced for Transport for London (TfL) Hack: In July 2026, Thalha Jubair (20) and Owen Flowers (18) were sentenced at Woolwich Crown Court to five years and six months each in prison. Convicted under Section 3ZA of the Computer Misuse Act (CMA), reserved for attacks causing serious damage to critical national infrastructure, the pair, operating as part of the "Scattered Spider" collective, breached Transport for London (TfL) in late August 2024. Initiated when Flowers was 17, the attack bypassed two-factor authentication (2FA) by social engineering a TfL help-desk worker over the phone. The intrusion knocked 148 internal IT systems offline, forced in-person password resets for 27,000 staff, exposed up to 10 million customer records, and incurred £29 million to £39 million in operational and recovery costs.
