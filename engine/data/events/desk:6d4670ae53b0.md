NebuSec's technical write-up describes Muse's browser backends, including bundled Chromium observed at 152.0.7977.82 in early October and 155.0.8059.80 later, with the PDF-export process running without an IPC sandbox. The chain used V8 issue 554034656 for container code execution, then kernel exploits against a 7.0.0-39-generic Hatch build for container escape. Sentinel is mapped to a DNS and proxy service at 198.19.0.1 on ports 53 and 3128, with eBPF redirection, content screening and 60-minute egress budgets of 2 MiB per originator and 5 MiB per domain. After VM root, processes outside the container could bypass Sentinel and reach the unlocked 100 GiB LUKS2/Btrfs volume, secure credential storage and Sentinel itself.
