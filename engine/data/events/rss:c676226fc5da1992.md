The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency will review its services and concentrate resources on infrastructure whose disruption would cause the greatest harm, under a strategy released Monday that calls for a more targeted approach to defending essential systems. It also emphasizes removing hackers from networks before they can disrupt operations. “We will deprioritize threat-agnostic, all-hazards approaches and embrace risk-informed, consequence-based analysis,” the document reads. The agency would focus more closely on infrastructure whose loss would most affect public safety, the economy and national security. The strategy does not identify which services would receive fewer resources or which components would take priority. CISA will begin reviewing its core services and capabilities within 60 days to determine how they support the strategy’s objectives. That deadline applies to starting the review, not completing it.
