Hundreds of French supermarkets experienced refrigeration disruptions after hackers reportedly manipulated control systems overnight, destroying fresh and frozen food. Intermarché, Netto, Coopérative U, and Leclerc stores were among those affected, with potential nationwide losses reaching several million euros, according to BFMTV. The incident targeted refrigeration systems supplied by the Danish manufacturer Danfoss.
