The campaign we revealed back in September 2025 is back. In September 2025, GitGuardian alerted to an ongoing supply chain attack we named GhostAction. That campaign compromised 817 public repositories across 327 GitHub users and exfiltrated at least 3,325 secrets. The attack relied on injecting a malicious workflow to steal credentials from victims' CI/CD pipelines, likely using previously compromised credentials. This technique was later reused during the Shai-Hulud campaigns and remains at the core of the Mini Shai-Hulud malware family , which is still used today. This campaign saw a new spike in summer 2026. Researchers from Cynative independently observed the malicious commits and reached out to us about it. Same techniques, new victims The new wave of this campaign started around August 31st, at 2 pm GMT.
