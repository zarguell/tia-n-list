Mexico’s financial sector is facing a growing phishing threat from Balonx Sistema, a Phishing-as-a-Service (PhaaS) platform targeting more than 20 banks. The operation combines real-time phishing, Android malware, rotating domains, and AI-powered voice fraud to steal banking credentials and financial data. Group-IB researchers identified Balonx as a subscription-based criminal service operated by an individual in Mexico. The platform reportedly harvested credentials and financial information from more than 1,100 victims since at least October 2025. It is advertised through Facebook groups linked to telemarketing fraud and recruits affiliates through Telegram. Balonx offers weekly plans, allowing criminals with limited technical skills to launch banking scams. Its Individual Plan supports two devices, while its Office Plan enables up to eight subordinate accounts. Payments are automated using cryptocurrency exchange services, helping the operators run the platform like a commercial SaaS business. Balonx Targets Mexican Banks Unlike ordinary phishing kits that only collect credentials through a fake webpage, Balonx maintains a persistent WebSocket connection between the victim’s phishing page and the attacker’s control panel. This enables criminals to monitor victims in real time and alter the page during an active session.
