Microsoft documented a human-operated intrusion playbook in which attackers initiate cross-tenant Microsoft Teams communications while impersonating IT or help desk personnel to socially engineer users into granting remote access via Quick Assist. From that foothold, operators used trusted vendor-signed runtimes, native administrative protocols such as WinRM, and Rclone to move laterally and stage business data for exfiltration to external cloud storage. This earlier playbook established the same social-engineering and remote-access pattern later seen in the ESC1 and ESC8 certificate-abuse intrusions.
