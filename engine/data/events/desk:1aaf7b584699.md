Threadlinqs published a detection and IOC page for TL-2026-6463 covering ARMORY CASTLE, a suspected PRC-nexus actor formerly tracked as UNC5221, and its use of CVE-2026-123890 against vCenter 7.x and 8.x. The page describes an unauthenticated SRP-6a session key disclosure over DCE/RPC on port 2012 that yields an administrator@vsphere.local session, and lists post-exploitation files including ActiveSer.jsp, u.jsp, /tmp/123 and /usr/bin/vmtoolscs. It recommends restricting ports 2012, 5480 and 22, rotating the vCenter STS SAML signing key, and forwarding logs off-box because the actor deletes on-appliance logs.
