The Register reported on 19 November that hackers using an AI-powered security testing tool had disrupted Commission systems for more than ten days, taking Sysper, MyPMO and translation services offline. It credited Politico with the first report and cited sources saying defenders detected ARTEX, a publicly available framework whose developer pulled the repository in October while forks remained available. The article also highlighted CERT-EU's 5 November warning and an FBI alert finding more than 1,600 internet-exposed ARTEX consoles, with some attacks moving from targeting to privileged access in under three hours.
