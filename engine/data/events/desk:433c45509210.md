Europol and global partners dismantled infrastructure behind Gootloader, CastleRAT, and StealC malware, seizing 235 servers and 257 domains. Authorities identified over 6 million victim credentials in the criminal infrastructure, material intended for resale and further attacks including ransomware. CastleRAT is described as known since 2025, and the StealC action continues efforts from June 2026. Victim data will be shared via HaveIBeenPwned, DIVD, CheckjeHack, and other platforms.
