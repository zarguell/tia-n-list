CareCloud, a cloud-based healthcare solutions provider, disclosed a data breach that now affects 3,756,469 individuals, up from an initial estimate of roughly 350,000.

The company detected the intrusion in mid-March after a disruption to its electronic health record environment. Investigation showed threat actors accessed one of CareCloud's AWS environments between March 10 and March 16. The attackers claimed to have exfiltrated databases from the compromised environment.

Stolen data includes names, addresses, Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, dates of birth, health insurance information, and medical and healthcare information. A very limited subset of individuals also had full payment card data compromised.

No cybercrime group has publicly taken credit. It is unclear whether CareCloud paid a ransom. The HHS breach tracker confirmed the 3.7 million figure is accurate and reflects the most recent data provided to the agency, following initial suspicion of a clerical error after the ten-fold increase over earlier state-level reports.
