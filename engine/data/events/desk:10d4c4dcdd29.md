It traces delivery through Pillow, imageio, scikit-image, PHP Imagick, libvips, Qt KImageFormats and Matplotlib, and records 145 benign calls across 29 image APIs in two controlled runs. The post lists patch versions, still-open vendor trackers as of 12 November, and markers such as `/var/tmp/GS_CUPS_PIPE_RCE` and `PATH_CONTROL_BYTES_AFTER=0,0,0,0`.
