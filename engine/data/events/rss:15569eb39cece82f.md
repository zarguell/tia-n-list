For years, people have associated cyberattacks with stolen data, locked computers and disrupted digital services. However, the incident that affected numerous French supermarkets during the night of 11–12 October 2026 shows that a breach can have consequences beyond the digital world. This time, the attackers did not primarily target tills, payment systems or customer databases. Their target was the connected infrastructure used to regulate food refrigeration across different stores. According to published reports, the cybercriminals gained remote access to controllers manufactured by Danfoss and altered their operation. In some stores, prolonged defrosting cycles were reportedly activated and temperature settings were modified , reaching as high as 50°C in certain cases. The resulting break in the cold chain forced the affected supermarkets to remove large quantities of fresh and frozen products . Some stores had to temporarily close sales areas or suspend part of their operations while they inspected the equipment and disposed of the affected goods.
