SecurityAffairs reported on November 24, 2025 that Harvard revealed its Alumni Affairs and Development systems suffered a vishing breach exposing contact, donation, and biographical data of students, staff, and alumni, with no Social Security numbers, passwords, payment card data, or financial information stored in the breached systems. Harvard officials believe the breach exposed information belonging to alumni, their spouses, partners, and widows or widowers, donors, parents of current and former students, and some current students, faculty, and staff. The university launched an investigation with external cybersecurity experts, notified law enforcement, and sent data breach notifications to affected individuals on November 22, 2025.
