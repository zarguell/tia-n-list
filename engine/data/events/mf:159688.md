Phishing is a cyberattack where someone impersonates a trusted source a bank, a coworker, a well-known brand to trick you into handing over passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information, usually through a fake email, text message, or website. It’s less a piece of malicious code than a piece of social engineering: the attack works by manipulating trust, not by breaking through a firewall.
