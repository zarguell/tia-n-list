Overview The UK's National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) has put a date on a threat that security teams across Europe have been bracing for. In its 2026 Annual Review, The State of the Cyber Nation , the agency assesses it is highly likely that AI-powered malware will be used in a ransomware attack on UK Critical National Infrastructure by 2028. That is not a distant, speculative scenario. It is a two-year window, and it lands alongside a second judgement. By 2028, China, Russia and other nation-states will highly likely be using AI-enabled cyber capabilities routinely to pursue geopolitical goals, with the UK facing sophisticated attacks at greater scale and speed. The review also recorded that UK and EU member states jointly attributed a December 2025 attack on Poland's energy grid to Russia's FSB Centre 16. Had it succeeded, roughly 500,000 citizens could have lost power. Europe is not watching this threat from the sidelines; it is where the playbook is being tested. Key Takeaways 2028 is the marker.
