Broadcom's initial advisory, issue date July 29, 2026, describes CVE-2026-59310 as a directory traversal in the vCenter Syslog server with a maximum CVSSv3 base score of 9.8, exploitable by any malicious actor with network access to vCenter to execute arbitrary code, with no workarounds. The same advisory fixes CVE-2026-59309, a 9.8 authentication bypass in the VMware Directory Service, and credits Phil Brass and Matt South of Atredis Partners for reporting both. Fixed builds include vCenter 8.0 U3k, 9.0.2.0100 and 9.1.0.0300, with express 8.0 U2f patches added in the August 3 revision and 7.0 guidance in the August 19 revision.
