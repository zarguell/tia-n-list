Tor 0.4.9.17 includes a series of security fixes, one of which Goulet called especially important for relay DoS defenses because of how they deal with IPv6 handling. The release came as a rapid response to the ongoing DDoS attack that had affected most relay operators, with relays overwhelmed by a massive flood of client circuits. The project said it was actively evaluating additional defenses and expected further releases in the near future, and asked operators to upgrade as soon as possible with no action item required afterwards.
