Todoist’s advisory says an attacker used a stolen version-control credential between November 22 and 23, 2026 to modify workflows in three repositories and reach its cloud infrastructure. It reports an export of email addresses, account creation dates and salted SHA1 password hashes for approximately 6% of users, API and Slack tokens for approximately 0.003%, and credential-like content or Outlook mailbox data for approximately 0.001% in each smaller group. The company says it blocked access, rotated exposed internal credentials, retired SHA1 support and protected affected accounts; its update log records initial publication at 16:11 UTC on November 24.
