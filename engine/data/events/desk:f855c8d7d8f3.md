A public notice opened against the Apache Superset repository on April 12, 2026 warns maintainers that the project is one of 129 open-source repositories being targeted by the PolinRider supply-chain campaign, with malicious forks identified. Similar notices were filed the same day against other projects, including prisma-examples and neutralinojs, naming specific pull requests that carried the loader in their diffs. The notices document that the campaign reached mainstream open-source projects well before its November 2026 coverage.
