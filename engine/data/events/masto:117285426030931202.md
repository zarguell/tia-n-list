The hacker recruited hundreds of AI agents: 11 organizations breached in 26 seconds. An unknown attacker exploited hundreds of AI agents to target PaperCut NG and MF servers, compromising 440 instances across 395 organizations in 48 countries. Researchers from GreyNoise and Blackpoint Cyber discovered the campaign, which began on August 31, used AI models based on OpenAI Codex and DeepSeek to develop exploits and search for targets. Two zero-day vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-81578 and CVE-2026-82078, were urgently patched by PaperCut developers.

The attacker, reportedly Russian-speaking, used AI agents to study vulnerable PaperCut versions, set up an Active Directory lab, write working PoC exploits, and debug them. After successful testing, hundreds of agents simultaneously searched for internet-accessible servers and attacked them automatically. In under four hours, the attacker went from initial code execution on a real victim server to domain admin privileges in one of the compromised organizations.

At peak capacity, the campaign breached at least 11 organizations in just 26 seconds. At one US school, the path from initial access to domain admin took only seven minutes. The attacker stole credentials from 280 victims, extracted secrets from 147 others, and achieved domain admin in 12 organizations. Educational institutions were hardest hit with 204 victims. The attacker instructed agents not to touch organizations in 28 countries including Russia, China, and Iran, but the AI did not always respect these boundaries. Tools used included Mimikatz, Certipy, BloodHound, Rubeus, Impacket, NetExec, and Ligolo-ng.
