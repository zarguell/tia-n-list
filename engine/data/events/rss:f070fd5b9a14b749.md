Europol, in collaboration with law enforcement agencies from 14 countries across the globe, have successfully identified vendors and buyers from major dark marketplaces as part of Operation TorNado, a coordinated international effort targeting cybercrime, drug trafficking, and firearms offences. The operation, which spanned from May 2025 to October 2026, resulted in 354 arrests and hundreds of house searches worldwide.
