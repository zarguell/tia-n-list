Administrators can read and export every event log, but only from an elevated prompt. For everyone else, the built-in Event Log Readers group grants read access to most logs, locally and remotely, without admin rights. Add the account with net localgroup “Event Log Readers” /add, then sign out and back in. A handful of channels set their own permissions; wevtutil gl shows exactly who can read a given log. Analysts, log collectors and IR partners all need event logs, and none of them should need to be local admins for it. This guide covers the least-privilege setup and the reasons “Access is denied” shows up even when you think you have rights.
