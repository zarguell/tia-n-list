SBC Medical Group's English investor release described the November 16 database intrusion and a separate compromise of clinic administrator accounts on Tribeau, a booking service used by 56 clinics. It listed basic data for 1,624,000 people, reservation requests for 255,416, web inquiries for 217,769, treatment-monitor records for 4,513, reviews for 15,007 and expired or otherwise unprocessable card data for 14, plus about 20,000 employee account records and bank or pension details for 459 employees. SBC said it isolated the affected server, preserved system logs, tightened access restrictions, invalidated Tribeau clinic passwords and blocked communications from the attacking sources.
