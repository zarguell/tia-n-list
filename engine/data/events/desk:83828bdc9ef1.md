F5 Labs reported a 50 percent increase in GeoServer-related requests in January 2026 compared with December, split 93 percent GET and 7 percent POST. The most common operation was WFS 2.0 ListStoredQueries, alongside requests to /geoserver/web/ and WMS endpoints. The article reads the pattern as product discovery, service enumeration and validation of parsing paths, with scanners often bundling checks for several historical GeoServer issues.
