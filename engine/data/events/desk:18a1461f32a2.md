Newsdesk reported Octopus's 27 November disclosure that names and photographs for about 7,500 customers and contact details for about 22,000 merchants were affected. It says some records may include auto top-up bank codes and amounts, while credit-card and bank-account information was not involved and no customer financial loss had been found. The report says core services remained normal, with only a small number of functions potentially affected by preventive measures.
