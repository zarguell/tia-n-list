Docker CISO Mark Lechner confirmed that account-to-organization conversions reassigned the converted account's personal access tokens to the newly named owner without rotating the secret or asking consent, a behavior present since August 26, 2024. The report arrived September 30, 2026 and a production fix shipped the same day in under four hours; exposed users were notified October 2 and transferred PATs were force-deactivated October 8. Docker reviewed every conversion in the window and about 304,000 pushes made with transferred tokens, finding no evidence of exploitation.
