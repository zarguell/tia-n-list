The BADBOX malware botnet has returned following last year's disruptions, generating a record 40 billion fraudulent advertising bid requests per day through infected Android-based devices. According to HUMAN's Satori researchers, who uncovered and disrupted the latest variant, BADBOX 3.0 had grown into the largest documented ad-fraud operation by daily bid request volume. BADBOX was first discovered in early 2023 on a T95 Android TV box bought on Amazon. In December 2024, Germany's cybersecurity agencies warned that the botnet had infected tens of thousands of devices . In March 2025, a large-scale disruption operation took place where HUMAN and Shadowserver cut off more than 500,000 BADBOX 2.0 devices from their control servers, while Google removed 24 apps that spread the malware from Google Play. Google later sued the botnet's operators , saying the BADBOX 2.0 had infected over 10 million Android-based devices by April 2025. However, the previous disruptions didn't stop the BADBOX operators from rebuilding the botnet and setting new records for daily fraudulent advertising bid requests. "At its height, BADBOX 3.0 accounted for roughly 40 billion bid requests a day and more than 4% of all CTV traffic observed by the Human Defense Platform," reports HUMAN . "BADBOX 3.0's volume was more than three times the volume of the previous record holder, VASTFLUX, and represents a larger peak than the last five years' worth of published Satori investigations combined."
