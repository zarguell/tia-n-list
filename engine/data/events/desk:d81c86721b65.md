FBI/IC3 advisory I-051526-PSA, dated 15 May 2026, describes ShinyHunters as a criminal group behind a Learning Management System attack and large-scale data theft and extortion. It states SH actors use harassment of victims and their families, swatting, and false claims of possessing compromising material, and have posted stolen data to Tor-hosted leak sites. This is the advisory ShinyHunters' September leak-site post demands be removed.
