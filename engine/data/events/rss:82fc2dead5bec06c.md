1. Define the Use Case and Business Value – Purpose Before Technology Specify the intended purpose, users, expected value and measurable success criteria before selecting technology. Most of this is not new, it’s been a part of procurement processes for quite some time. But what does it mean? For example, without having a purpose or intended use for an AI application, resources will not be invested in what supposed to support business processes. Further, ensuring that the value can be measurable in different success criteria’s is a good method to evaluate if the choice fulfilled the intended use and value. A formula for this can be; Success criteria = Business outcome + AI quality + Adoption + Risk boundary, for example: The application is considered successful if it reduces manual processing time by >30%, produces correct outputs in >95% of evaluated cases, is actively used by >70% of the intended user group, and operates without exceeding defined security, privacy and compliance risk thresholds. 2.
