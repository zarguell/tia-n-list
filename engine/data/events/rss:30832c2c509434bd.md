October marks Cybersecurity Awareness Month, a timely opportunity to look at how American organizations can strengthen their security operations. Among the biggest challenges SOC teams face is making sure they not only detect and investigate threats, but doing so quickly enough to make the right security decisions without overwhelming analysts or increasing operational costs. In this article, ANY.RUN explores how better threat visibility and automation can help US-based SOCs achieve this, using a real phishing investigation and metrics to demonstrate how faster triage translates into greater ROI from existing SOC investments Key Takeaways Faster triage reduces investigation costs. A healthcare MSSP achieved 76% faster phishing triage with ANY.RUN, reducing investigation time from 30–40 to 4–7 minutes. Better visibility supports faster decisions. Behavioral analysis and automation help analysts identify genuine threats without lengthy manual checks. More cases resolved at Tier 1 improve SOC capacity. In the same customer case, the Tier 1 closure rate increased from 20% to 70%. Higher SOC ROI starts with existing resources.
