The ETH Zurich researchers' page and paper (ACM CCS 2026) detail Mior, reported to AMD on 2026-02-12 with an embargo ending 2026-11-10. The attack redirects PSP initialization writes to attacker-controlled device memory, leaving RMP entries uninitialized so a malicious hypervisor can read or modify confidential VM memory and forge attestation reports. Tested on AMD EPYC 9135 (Zen 5); Zen 3 and Zen 4 considered potentially affected. Requires hypervisor and BIOS/UEFI control plus a PCIe device. PDF last modified 2026-11-10.
