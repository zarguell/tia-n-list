Bitdefender Labs documented an ongoing SYS01 campaign that used Meta ads and Electron-based ZIP downloads to distribute an encoded PHP infostealer. The campaign impersonated brands including CapCut, Canva, Adobe Photoshop, Netflix and games, with thousands of ads and malicious infrastructure capable of reaching millions. The infostealer collected browser cookies and Facebook business information, while hijacked business accounts were used to launch additional malicious ads.
