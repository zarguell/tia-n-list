The Verge reported that 1Password said a hacker stole customer names and email addresses after gaining access to its backend systems, and that passwords and other vault contents were not exposed. It said the breach could raise phishing risk, that the number of affected users was unclear, and that tokens from emailed invitation or recovery links had to be paired with single sign-on or a six-digit code sent to the user's email. The article notes 1Password lengthened those codes to 10 alphanumeric characters and added new detection for similar attacks.
