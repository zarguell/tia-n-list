What is AI autonomy? Merriam-Webster defines autonomy as "the quality or state of being independent, free, and self-directing." Historically, this has applied primarily to people. But like all terms, the definition will continue to evolve with the culture that coined it. Today, that means dealing with agentic AI. When we talk about AI autonomy, we need to limit this definition a bit further. For AI agents, it means how much an AI system can pursue a goal without direct human involvement. At lower levels, a person still directs most of the work. The system recommends an action, drafts content, or performs a tightly bounded task.
