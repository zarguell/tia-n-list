Accenture, Dragos and the SANS Institute on Tuesday announced the Community Cyber Resilience Foundation, which aims to help under-resourced US water, electric and natural gas utilities protect their operational technology (OT) environments. They are putting up roughly $10 million to start, a mix of funding and services. The first phase will cover up to 500 utilities, which will get free security tools, equipment, deployment help and training. To qualify, a water, wastewater, electric or natural gas utility must be based in the US and bring in under $100 million a year. The companies estimate that this bar covers more than 50,000 organizations, or over 97% of US utilities. Each participant gets the Dragos Platform and related services, with Accenture handling setup, integration and continued support. SANS will train utility staff in ICS/OT security so they can run their defenses internally. The foundation will also fund the hardware needed to deploy and run the Dragos Platform. It will connect participants to a peer network of utilities that share operational experience. The companies say many small utilities struggle with limited funding, workforce shortages and aging technology, especially those serving rural or underserved communities.
