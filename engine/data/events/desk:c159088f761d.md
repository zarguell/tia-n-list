Yonhap reported that Samsung Securities said personal data of more than 8,000 clients had been breached in what it called the latest cyberattack on South Korean financial firms. The brokerage said unauthorized access was detected on its customer response website on Saturday and that customer names, physical addresses and other personal information were compromised. Yonhap says the breach did not extend beyond the affected system and that Samsung reported the incident to authorities and said it was strengthening its cybersecurity systems.
