Node.js 24.22.3 (LTS), dated 2026-11-11, is the security release for the 24.x line in the November 2026 batch. It carries HTTP/2 and TLS fixes and dependency updates to llhttp 9.4.4, undici, and sqlite 3.54.1. The 24.x line is affected by the remote HTTP/2 abort CVE-2026-123863, the HTTP/2 stream teardown response-memory leak CVE-2026-123866, and the mTLS renegotiation bypass CVE-2026-123872.
