GreyNoise Labs published honeypot findings on 18 June 2024 after SolarWinds disclosed the unauthenticated Serv-U path traversal CVE-2024-28995 on 5 June. Serv-U 15.4.2 HF 1 and earlier were affected and HF 2 and later were patched. The honeypots logged requests using the InternalDir and InternalFile parameters to fetch files including etc/passwd, windows/win.ini, Serv-U-StartupLog.txt, Serv-U.log and Users.ini, and GreyNoise observed at least one attacker adjusting payloads by hand.
