The developers of Goddess of Victory: NIKKE have confirmed that hackers tampered with the game's PC launcher scripts in a supply-chain attack, distributing malware capable of stealing credentials and cryptocurrency wallet data. According to a notice published on X, a client update was issued on November 4 at 02:30 (UTC +9) to address the issue. "We have identified an issue indicating that the PC Launcher's script files may have been tampered with by a third party," reads the statement on X . "Accordingly, client fixes and an update were implemented on November 4 at approximately 02:30 (UTC+9). We are currently investigating the scope of the impact caused by this issue, and we will provide further details regarding follow-up measures in a separate notice." Goddess of Victory: NIKKE is a popular sci-fi shooter RPG developed by the South Korean firm Shift Up for Windows, iOS , and Android . Its publisher, Level Infinite, reported more than 45 million downloads since its launch in 2022, while Google Play alone reports 10 million downloads. Although Level Infinite has not disclosed many details, a report on GitHub by independent security researcher MotherSphere alleges the incident involved four JavaScript files hosted on Level Infinite's official content delivery network (CDN). According to the researcher, the files were modified on September 27, 2026, to inject malicious JavaScript that downloaded additional code from attacker-controlled servers whenever players opened the game
