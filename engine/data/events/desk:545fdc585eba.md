CyberNews reported on February 5, 2026 that ShinyHunters dumped data stolen from Harvard and UPenn after ransom refusal, and that Harvard discovered the breach on November 18, 2025 following a phone-based phishing attack. The coverage noted ShinyHunters claimed more than one million records per university and that the leaked data includes alumni donor information, contact details, and fundraising records. CyberNews contextualized the incident within a wave of social-engineering attacks against university alumni and donor systems.
