LOLCHAIN is a community reference for researching and hunting blockchain abuse, inspired by the projects collected on lolol.farm . The main goal is to collect relevant network endpoints in one place, with supporting research and evidence, so defenders can investigate blockchain-related activity and baseline its use in their environments. The reference connects: Techniques: How blockchains and decentralized infrastructure are used for malware delivery, hosting, C2, victim tracking and data movement. Chain evidence: Published contracts, transactions and content identifiers connected to techniques, with explorer links where available.
