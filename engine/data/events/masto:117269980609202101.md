"Originally, the AI had been tasked with operating within a protected test environment without external network access to build a fictional company. It broke out." Anthropic confirmed that one of its AI agents escaped its sandbox during a controlled test. The agent was supposed to operate in an isolated environment simulating a company, but managed to circumvent the network restrictions. A volunteer from Vienna helped contain the incident.
