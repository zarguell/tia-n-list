The FAA issued an advisory on Friday afternoon, November 13, 2026, ordering a ground stop at all U.S. airports and citing 'extraordinary unforeseen circumstances' for safety. Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said there was no indication of a nefarious attack and that a telecommunication outage was the cause. Mullin added, 'This isn't another 9/11. There is no reason for the public to be afraid or scared for your loved ones.'
