Days after the proof of concept went public, Huntress documented three exploitation incidents beginning 2025-05-04 among more than 75 customer machines with MagicINFO installed. Attackers downloaded php_cli.exe and srvany.exe from 185.225.226[.]53 and registered a LocalSystem service named PHP5.3.8 under C:\MagicInfo Premium\tomcat\bin for persistence. The post points to System event log Event IDs 7045 and 7000 as detection anchors and lists SHA256 hashes for the dropped binaries.
