Someone claiming to represent ShinyHunters has announced the notorious cybercrime group’s alleged return. Separately, the operators of a new extortion campaign named Inception have denied having ties to ShinyHunters. According to DataBreaches , the return of ShinyHunters was announced in a message posted on the Exploit cybercrime forum. The post came from an account linked to ShinyHunters since 2020. The message included the address of the new leak site and a new PGP key to authenticate future communications. Its authors also noted that they will not be active on other cybercrime forums and disavowed several hackers who claimed to be members of ShinyHunters. The post’s authors attempted to establish their identity using a crypto key known to be associated with ShinyHunters, but DataBreaches noted that the original PGP key had reportedly been leaked. Other threat actors have sought to capitalize on the ShinyHunters name and reputation, and the possibility that the comeback message is the work of impostors cannot be ruled out. The announcement follows the arrest of several individuals suspected of being involved in ShinyHunters attacks, including a Canada-based cybersecurity expert , a Jordanian national believed to be group leader Rey , as well as a suspect in the Netherlands .
