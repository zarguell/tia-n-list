CNN reported, citing four sources familiar with the matter, that the CIA and ODNI suffered an apparent hack affecting unclassified information on a system used for open-source intelligence analysis. An ODNI official said there had been no disruptions to critical capabilities or classified systems. CNN said it was not clear when the hack occurred, who was responsible or whether it had been resolved, and that the incident did not appear related to the apparent hack of the FBI nationwide criminal information database. The report noted that ODNI oversees 17 US intelligence agencies.
