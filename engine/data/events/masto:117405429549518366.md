Poor IT security let hackers stay inside the Dutch Public Prosecution Service (Openbaar Ministerie) undetected for a month. Because active monitoring was lacking, a known Citrix vulnerability could only be closed after a month. The forced offline period is still causing delays in 9,000 court cases. NOS reports the OM had not been properly secured against hackers for years, and it remains unclear whether sensitive data was downloaded in last year's hack.
