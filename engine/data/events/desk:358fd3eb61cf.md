iTnews reported on 20 November 2026 that any Bluetooth device within 10 to 100 metres could apply full brakes on un-updated Elecbrakes EB2, VM1 and ELBC 2000 controllers, with a jackknife or loss of control as the stated risk. It names researchers Patrick Forck and Jeremy McCarthy and cites CVE-2026-96805 and CVE-2026-125273 at CVSS 9.6 and CVE-2026-124281 at 6.1. The article says Elecbrakes told it 60 to 70 percent of connecting controllers were then running the latest firmware, while the discontinued ELBC range still awaited remediation.
