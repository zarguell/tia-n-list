Huntress reported CVE-2026-86218 as an actively exploited pre-auth RCE zero-day in N-central with a 10.0 CVSS score, fixed in hotfix 2026.3 HF4, in its September 6, 7:30 AM ET update. The same post documents a Huntress proof of concept for a separate N-central access bypass chain, CVE-2026-86206 and CVE-2026-86207, and one confirmed customer compromise of a fully patched N-central server. N-able's own incident page described the vulnerability as exploited in the wild while its release notes did not.
