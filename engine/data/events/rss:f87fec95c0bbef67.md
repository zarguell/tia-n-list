AI is changing the threat landscape and this is shifting how organizations think about their existing security strategy. In response, we’ve invested in two areas over the past year: using AI to protect people and the agents that now read their email and making protection measurable so customers can verify that it works. AI is changing both sides of the inbox The first area of investment starts with a simple reality: AI now reads email alongside people, attackers are using AI to target both, and defense must use AI to stay ahead. Picture an ordinary vendor email: a meeting recap, a few action items, and a friendly sign-off. Hidden in white text on a white background is prompt injection: an instruction telling an AI assistant to gather financial documents and send them to an outside address. The employee isn’t the target; their AI agent is. People can be persuaded by messages that exploit fear, trust, or urgency, but agents don’t need persuading. Once an agent acts on an instruction, pulling the message back won’t undo it. This is why Microsoft Defender stops malicious messages before delivery and keeps reevaluating delivered mail as new intelligence comes in.
