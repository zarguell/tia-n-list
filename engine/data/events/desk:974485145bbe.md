Sekoia's TDR team published detailed research on the ErrTraffic ClickFix framework, which had grown from Hudson Rock's late-2025 disclosure into subscription malware-as-a-service with rentals rising from $300 to $380 per month in the first half of 2026. The report documents two operator clusters, Analytics and Beer, that inject the framework into compromised WordPress sites, resolve C2 through EtherHiding smart contracts on the Polygon blockchain, and deliver Vidar, Stealc, Remus, Salat, and SmokeLoader. It also publishes a detection pattern for ErrTraffic's XOR-decoding PowerShell commands based on ScriptBlockText logging.
