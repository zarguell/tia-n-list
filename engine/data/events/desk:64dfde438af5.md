SBS News reports that Flight Centre is investigating a data breach at one of its booking providers that may have exposed customer passport information, citing the statement to the ASX on Thursday. It quotes the company saying it is still working with the third-party vendor and its cyber security incident response partner to understand the nature and extent of the activity. Flight bookings and corporate travel bookings were not affected, and the company said a significant number of customer records were believed accessed while the number actually extracted was unknown.
