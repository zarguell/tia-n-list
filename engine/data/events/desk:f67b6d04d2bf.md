404 Media, in collaboration with Court Watch, reported that federal prosecutors said the FBI posed as both a ransomware victim and a threat actor during an undercover operation against a Florida data recovery firm run by Juan Ricardo Palacio Escobar and an unnamed associate. The company began in 2020 and claimed to offer data recovery services for ransomware victims. The article also describes legitimate victims listed in the indictment, including a Kansas business that Palacio allegedly charged $720,000.
