MFA to Passkeys in Microsoft Entra ID series · Part 6 of 7 If you can only do one thing from this series this quarter, focus on doing it for your administrators. A phished Global Administrator account gives the attacker the whole tenant. The attacker can: rewrite Conditional Access, register their own credentials on any account, grant themselves application permissions that no user MFA ever touches, turn off your logging. Microsoft's guidance requires phishing-resistant MFA for administrators. Its phishing-resistant deployment guide puts admins in their own persona, with FIDO2 security keys as the recommended portable credential. This part turns that guidance into a working program.
