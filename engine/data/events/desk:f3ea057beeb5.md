BleepingComputer reported on July 23, 2026 that Check Point had patched an actively exploited zero-day, CVE-2026-16232, which lets an unauthenticated attacker obtain an application login token and authenticate to Security Management or Multi-Domain Management as an administrator. Exploitation required an internet-reachable Management server IP and no Trusted Clients restrictions, and Check Point said all affected customers had been notified. The article gives the SmartConsole audit-log check for `Authentication method: application token`, five attacker IPs, and CISA's July 25, 2026 federal remediation deadline.
