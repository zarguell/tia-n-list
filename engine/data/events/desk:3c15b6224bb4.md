Google researchers presented this paper on UNC3569 and GRAYRABBIT at Virus Bulletin 2024, held in Dublin on October 2 to 4, 2024, two years before the Sogou exploitation surfaced. It documents GRAYRABBIT as UNC3569's first-stage implant, the RABBITFUR downloader delivery pattern, and the group's operations for Google since 2021 against government, education, technology, and finance targets concentrated in East and Southeast Asia, with ties to the leaked Chinese contractor i-SOON.
