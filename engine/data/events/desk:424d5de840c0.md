Corgea documents 13 malicious packages across PyPI, npm and RubyGems disclosed between November 2 and 4, 2026, including Overlord RAT wheel builds. It says the PyPI packages filipt, zyto and ln9qw38745g override the install command in setup.py to launch a bundled 33.1 MB Windows Overlord RAT executable during wheel building or installation. The research advises isolating hosts that built or installed the named packages and revoking sessions where companion stealers ran.
