Microsoft has expanded its bug bounty incentives for security researchers targeting vulnerabilities in Dynamics 365 and Power Platform services, with rewards ranging from $1,250 to $60,000 for qualifying submissions. The initiative targets security flaws with direct and demonstrable impact in Microsoft-supported cloud products. Microsoft is especially prioritizing cross-tenant vulnerabilities, which are among the most serious risks affecting multi-tenant enterprise platforms. A cross-tenant flaw could allow an attacker operating from one organization’s Microsoft environment to access data, resources, administrative functions, or business processes associated with another customer tenant. Microsoft Offers Up to $60,000 Such vulnerabilities can undermine the isolation controls that separate organizations using the same cloud platform. Microsoft has categorized critical cross-tenant vulnerabilities as high-impact scenarios that qualify for a 100% reward multiplier. Important cross-tenant findings are eligible for a 50% multiplier. This approach encourages researchers to focus on flaws that could affect multiple organizations or enable access beyond an attacker-controlled tenant. The expanded scope includes online Dynamics 365 applications, including Sales, Customer Service, Finance, Supply Chain Management, Business Central, Commerce, and Customer Insights.
