Ransomware remains the defining threat for organizations of every size, and no single control stops it effective defense layers prevention, detection, containment, and recovery. Some tools stop the initial execution; some detect and roll back encryption in progress; some contain the blast radius; and some ensure you can restore clean data when everything else fails. The right mix depends on your size, existing stack, and whether you’re buying a preventive platform, a containment layer, or resilient backup. This playbook examines eight leading ransomware protection solutions in depth features, best fit, pros, and cons and shows how to combine them alongside essential endpoint security strategies mapped to business size. Pricing is described by model only. Stage 1 — Understand the Ransomware Kill Chain Modern ransomware moves through initial access, privilege escalation, lateral movement, data theft (double extortion), and finally encryption. Defenders win by breaking the chain at multiple points: prevent execution with application control and allowlisting tools , detect and stop encryption early with behavioral EDR and rollback, contain spread via network or host containment, and guarantee recovery with immutable, tested backups.
