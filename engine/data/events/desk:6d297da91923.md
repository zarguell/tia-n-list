WPSec's WPSEC-2026-3301 advisory dates the fix to 16 November 2026 and lists affected versions from 5.4.7 before 5.6.2, with CVSS 3.1 8.1 and CWE-287. It says the OTP Spam Preventer add-on writes request-controlled email and phone values into the session keys user_email and phone_number_mo, and OTP resend then sends the code to those attacker-controlled destinations. Exploitation requires the add-on plus OTP verification on the WordPress login form; if the form also requires a password, only the OTP step is bypassed.
