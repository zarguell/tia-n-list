eSecurity Planet reported that CISA added CVE-2026-19868 to the KEV catalog on 6 November 2026 with a remediation deadline of 20 November 2026 for federal civilian agencies. The article lists affected Gen7 builds of SonicOS 7.3.3-7015 and earlier and Gen8 builds of 8.2.2-8015 and earlier, with fixes in 7.3.4-7013 and 8.2.3-8015. It stresses the flaw is an authentication enforcement issue requiring valid credentials for an alternate method, not an unauthenticated bypass.
