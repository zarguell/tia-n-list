A U.S. government order posted last week that jettisons Acronis products from federal civilian systems was years in the making and focused largely on the firm’s development ties in Russia, two sources familiar with the matter told Nextgov/FCW . The Swiss cybersecurity firm had been under the microscope since the first Trump administration, where officials had scrutinized it under Foreign Ownership, Control or Influence guidelines — or FOCI — according to a former U.S. official with knowledge of the matter. On top of this, key areas of scrutiny included whether Russian developers remained involved in Acronis products’ codebases, and whether the company had adequately mitigated related supply chain risks, according to a former DHS official aware of the efforts. Both former officials spoke on the condition of anonymity to communicate sensitive information about how Acronis has been examined across the Biden and Trump administrations. Neither said there was concrete evidence produced indicating Acronis offerings had backdoors communicating with Moscow.
