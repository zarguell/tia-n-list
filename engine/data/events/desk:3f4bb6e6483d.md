Il Sole 24 Ore reported that around 6,000 schools across Italy received emails containing bomb threats, though not all were evacuated. Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni called a meeting at Palazzo Chigi to coordinate the response, and Interior Minister Matteo Piantedosi said it was too early to speak of joint leadership. Defense Minister Guido Crosetto framed the threats as a strategy of terror, and similar threats were reported in Scotland and the UK.
