CrowdStrike publicly disclosed LIMINAL PANDA, a China-nexus actor targeting telecommunications providers since at least 2020, ahead of Adam Meyers' November 19, 2024 testimony to the U.S. Senate Judiciary Subcommittee. The group uses compromised telecom servers to reach further providers, emulates GSM protocols for command and control, and retrieves subscriber information, call metadata, and SMS. Enea later observed overlapping SilentAir and LIMINAL PANDA activity at some of the same operators, though it could not confirm a link.
