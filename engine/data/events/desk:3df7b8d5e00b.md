Published September 10, 2026, Martech Notes reports that Adobe's APSB26-142 bulletin covers eight CVSS 10.0 vulnerabilities affecting Campaign Classic v7. The Zero Day Initiative classified the bulletin as priority 1 with deployment urgency. The article notes CVE-2026-82004 (OS command injection, CVSS 10.0) and lists affected builds through v7.4.4 build 9401 on both Windows and Linux platforms.
