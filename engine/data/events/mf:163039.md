The One Question That Settles It Ask every provider this, and require the answer in the contract: “List every telemetry source you will ingest, actively monitor, and write custom detections against in my environment.” Three answers you’ll get: • A specific list — endpoints, identity provider, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, cloud accounts, firewalls, email. That’s genuine MXDR. • “We ingest anything” — ingestion is not monitoring. Data nobody writes detections for is storage. • Endpoint plus “integrations available” — that’s MDR with a wider acronym. The distinction matters commercially because MXDR typically costs meaningfully more than endpoint-only MDR from the same provider. That premium is worth paying when identity and cloud telemetry are genuinely monitored, since that’s where modern intrusions progress.
