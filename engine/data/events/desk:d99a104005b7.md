Cisco Talos original advisory disclosing VOLTA-AUTODOXER, a self-deleting Windows infostealer that sends a structured evidence bundle to an LLM for victim profiling. The malware harvests browser passwords, cookies, payment cards, cryptocurrency wallet seed phrases, Discord/Telegram/Steam/Roblox sessions, Microsoft OAuth tokens, SSH and cloud keys, Wi-Fi profiles, and screenshots. If the model refuses the assessment, VOLTA falls back to a three-model chain, showing direct engineering against guardrails. Published through the CAIRN research framework.
