Signal will now block Apple and Google operating systems by default from taking screenshots of user messages in its encrypted messaging app that feed into the companies’ artificial intelligence capabilities, the appmaker’s leadership said Friday. Signal already had taken similar steps with Microsoft 11 last year to stop Microsoft Recall from capturing and storing messages. It will extend those limitations to Siri AI’s Onscreen Awareness and Gemini Intelligence. Signal will “restrict agentic systems built into iOS 27 and on Android versions where we can detect an AI assistant attempting to read your Signal screen,” wrote the Signal Foundation’s president , Meredith Whitaker. “Right now, these agentic AI integrations are changing this environment in a way that’s making it increasingly hostile to privacy. This is an existential threat to Signal, and our ability to responsibly uphold our mission.” “In order to gain access to information about you, these OS AI agents ‘scan’ the information that’s on your screen,” she continued. “In doing so, your Signal data is captured, then made available to these agentic systems in order to allow them to answer your questions and do your bidding.” The announcement follows reports that Meta’s AI agent Muse was able to access the texts of Signal messages and read them.
