CISA's ICS advisory for CVE-2021-22681, originally published February 25, 2021 and revised March 18, 2021. It documents the verification-key authentication bypass in RSLogix 5000 and Studio 5000 Logix Designer affecting CompactLogix, ControlLogix, DriveLogix, GuardLogix and SoftLogix controllers, co-discovered by researchers at Soonchunhyang University, Kaspersky, and Claroty (Sharon Brizinov and Tal Keren). Rockwell determined the vulnerability cannot be mitigated with a patch and recommended network segmentation, blocking TCP 44818 from outside the ICS network zone, CIP Security deployment, and monitoring of controller change logs.
