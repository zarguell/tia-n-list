CrowdStrike Intelligence reported a targeted campaign against South Korean financial organizations, active from late September to early October 2026, in which an unattributed actor used ARTEX alongside large language models for intrusion activity. Analysis of attacker-controlled open directories exposed Claude Code session histories and ARTEX configuration files, and the campaign resulted in data exfiltration from several firms. CrowdStrike assessed with moderate confidence the operators were likely China-nexus, based in part on recovered operator artifacts.
