SolarWinds lists CVE-2026-82316 as a CVSS 9.1 critical TOCTOU flaw in Serv-U, credited to the Intigriti Bug Bounty Program. The advisory says a user with domain administrator or higher privileges can access or modify protected files outside the intended directory, potentially leading to code execution, with lower risk on Windows. Affected software is Serv-U 2026.3 and below and the fixed release is 2026.4. The page gives a first published date of 11/24/2026 without a time.
