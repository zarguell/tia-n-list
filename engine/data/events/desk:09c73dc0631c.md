Meta launched Muse on 8 September 2026 as an agent running in a dedicated Muse Secure VM, with a separate Sentinel process that approves network access and prompts for permission on sensitive actions. The company said users choose which services Muse connects to, can change or disconnect that access, can review a complete audit trail, and that conversations and VM data are not shared with its ad systems. Meta said an encrypted Muse Confidential VM would arrive later in the year.
