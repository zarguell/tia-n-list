Hackers used an autonomous AI agent platform called "Cairn" to discover a zero-day vulnerability in a medical imaging application and exploit it to take complete control of a server. According to Amazon Threat Intelligence, the suspected Chinese-linked attackers simply instructed the AI agents to "obtain shell access," leaving them to independently discover and exploit the flaw. Amazon Threat Intelligence uncovered the campaign after discovering that the attackers had left their Cairn instance publicly accessible without authentication, exposing their hacking operations. According to the researchers, the attacks were launched by a Chinese-nexus threat actor between September 8 and 10, 2026, targeting mainly East Asian educational institutions outside China. Cairn is a legitimate pentesting tool that can be used to coordinate multiple AI agents in cyber-operations, created for research and defense strengthening. Gambit Security documented cases of Cairn abuse as part of a large-scale credit card stealing operation in September. More recently, Google reported it was deployed in an attack against an aerospace organization in the U.S. During the campaign investigated by Amazon , the AI agents were not always successful. For example, a 16-hour and 148-action effort to take control of a Redis server failed.
