By Yair Kuznitsov, Co-Founder & CEO, Anecdotes The question in GRC is no longer whether your job is safe. It is whether your skillset is. AI agents are already doing the control testing, the evidence collection, and the framework mapping that used to fill a manager's week, and they get better at it every quarter. None of that is a knock on the people who built real careers doing that work well. The work is simply moving to the agents, and the value is moving to a different skillset, faster than anyone handed out a plan for it. The professionals who come out ahead will be the ones who retrain early, and the rest are left against the wall, not because they stood still, but because no one told them what to run toward. This is what GRC Agentification actually means. Not AI assisting a human who still does the work, but AI agents doing the work while the human moves up a level to direct and govern them. It is a far bigger shift than "adopt AI" makes it sound, and the guidance most professionals get stops right at the slogan. So here is the part nobody is spelling out: the three skills that separate the managers whose roles get automated away from the managers who start running the agents. All three are learnable, and the time to start is now: context, agent governance, and communication. An agent is only as good as the data you feed it.
