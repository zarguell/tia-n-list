By Hudson Rock · November 15, 2026 A new service advertised on cybercrime forums is adding a layer of interpretation to the sale of infostealer logs. The reviewed listings present each computer with a description of its apparent owner, workplace, applications, and potentially valuable data. Buyers can search those descriptions using natural language and combine the results with filters such as country, operating system, and price. The result is a catalog in which a compromised machine can be presented as a clinic’s reception desk, a defense contractor’s household computer, or a government employee’s family workstation. The service is marketing the context around the stolen data as part of the product.
