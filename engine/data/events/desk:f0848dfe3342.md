Beget, a Russian hosting, cloud, and domain provider serving more than 200,000 customers, is rebuilding its cloud infrastructure from scratch after a wiper attack on the night of November 1, 2026 reached the management layer of its entire architecture and ran data-zeroing scripts. The company disconnected all servers from the network shortly after detecting the malware, and said cloud data across VPS, S3, and Kubernetes was lost along with CDN settings and IP configurations. A November 6 assessment stated VPS file backups would be available for only about half of VPS users, and roughly 1,000 servers required manual checks for vulnerabilities and hidden scripts before being returned to service.
