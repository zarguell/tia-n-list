The Independent published the notification TfL sent to affected customers, which said suspicious activity on 16 November led to unauthorised access to personal information in web accounts despite action to limit access and take the photocard system offline. It reported that names, phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, dates of birth and security questions and answers were affected, while passwords, bank and payment card details, journey histories and Zip Oyster children's names and birth dates were not. The potentially affected account types listed were Zip Oyster cards for ages 5-10, 11-15 and 16+, 18+ Student, 18-25 Care Leaver, 60+ London, Apprentice and Veterans photocards; travel and ticket-machine top-ups continued while online access was disabled.
