ZDI published ZDI-21-1053 on 2 September 2021 for a Windows lock-screen authentication bypass credited to Abdelhamid Naceri, the researcher now using the FastUnlock handles. The advisory says a physically present attacker could bypass authentication without credentials and assigns CVE-2021-26431 with a CVSS score of 6.8. Its disclosure timeline records vendor notification on 30 June 2021 and coordinated release on 2 September 2021, and Microsoft issued an update. The case is a dated precedent for the same class of lock-screen access, though ZDI does not tie it to the new Narrator path.
