Reuters, syndicated by CNA on 17 November 2026, reported that Equinor's internal email system was down, citing a company source, and that NTB reported disruptions to IT applications and internal systems. Norway's National Security Authority said it was aware of the incident and noted that it had raised the cyberattack risk assessment to four on a five-level scale the previous month, with successful cyber operations against Norwegian companies having tripled over six months. A company spokesperson declined further detail.
