Danfoss published DSA-2025-08-01 on 01 August 2025 covering CVE-2025-41451, OS command injection in alarm-to-mail configuration fields on firmware before 4.3.1 with a CVSS score of 8.7, and CVE-2025-41452, external control of web interface settings leading to denial of service with a score of 6.8. The advisory says the flaws could lead to system compromise and instructs customers to install patch 4.3.1. It lists no mitigations and credits Tomer Goldschmidt of Claroty Team82.
