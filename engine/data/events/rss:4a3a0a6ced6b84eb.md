Malware is becoming increasingly sophisticated, making it difficult for organizations to detect emerging threats using traditional signature-based security tools alone. Attackers use techniques such as code obfuscation, polymorphism, and sandbox evasion to bypass conventional security measures. Artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) are transforming malware analysis by helping security teams identify suspicious patterns, analyze malicious behavior, and investigate previously unknown threats. AI-powered malware analysis platforms combine automated detection, behavioral analysis, and threat intelligence to help organizations strengthen their security posture. Understanding their capabilities can help security teams choose a solution that meets their investigation and threat detection requirements. How Is AI and ML Changing Malware Analysis? Traditional malware analysis relies on known signatures, predefined rules, and manual investigation. Although these methods remain important, they may not always detect new malware variants or sophisticated threats.
