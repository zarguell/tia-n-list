Boston Scientific is recovering from a cybersecurity incident that disrupted selected on-premises information technology systems, manufacturing operations, and the processing and shipment of customer orders. In an August 30 update, the medical-device maker said its investigation remained ongoing, but it had found no indication of unauthorized activity in its environment. The company said the incident did not affect its cloud-based systems or applications. Unauthorized activity was limited to certain on-premises systems, according to the update, indicating the outage and recovery effort are focused on internal operational technology-adjacent business infrastructure rather than the company’s cloud estate. Boston Scientific Cyberattack Boston Scientific has engaged CrowdStrike and other third-party cybersecurity specialists to support incident response , forensic investigation, containment, and recovery. The company has not identified the threat actor, disclosed a ransomware claim, or said whether data was accessed or exfiltrated. Its public statements focus on restoring affected operating systems and business applications while preserving continuity for healthcare customers and patients. The incident was identified on August 25 and triggered a network outage that disrupted access to systems used to manufacture products and fulfill customer orders.
