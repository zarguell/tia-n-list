Here are this week’s highlights: Heartbreaker macOS ransomware rebuilds itself from source Researcher Patrick Wardle has analyzed Heartbreaker , a Rust-based macOS ransomware sample that was uploaded to VirusTotal in May 2025 and, as of his writing, was still undetected by every antivirus engine on the service. After attempting to set up a LaunchAgent for persistence, it encrypts files in the user’s Downloads folder. It then tries to change its hash by taking its own embedded source code, randomizing identifiers and adding junk functions, and recompiling itself (even trying to install Rust if it’s missing). Wardle believes it’s unlikely to be a major threat in its current form, as it uses a single hardcoded AES key that makes free file recovery possible, and its rebuild process depends on a Rust framework that’s no longer publicly available.
