U.S. law enforcement has seized the primary domain of Virtualine, a bulletproof hosting service advertised on cybercrime forums for facilitating illegal activities. The service also operated under the names Secury, Massgod, Spamhouse, Lanedo, and Omegatech, and promised to shield customers from legal complaints and requests from law enforcement and other authorities. The U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) confirmed that Virtualine marketed services that permitted “illegal/black hat activities” ranging from phishing, spamming, and carding to port scanning. The FBI seized the primary domain of the service, virtualine[.net], following an investigation that involved Germany’s Polizei Hessen, the Netherlands’ Politie Midden-Nederland, the U.K.’s National Crime Agency, as well as private partners OverRIPE and Lumen. Although Virtualine drew an apparent line at child content, authorities say that the operator knowingly hosted an online forum promoting child abuse material and ignored repeated notices from law enforcement, the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC), and a victim’s attorney. Authorities allege that after legal pressure against the forum increased, Virtualine worked with the administrators to set up a new site. "Virtualine hosts this new website to this day, and it has already received at least one abuse complaint about sexually explicit images of children found on that website," the DoJ said .
