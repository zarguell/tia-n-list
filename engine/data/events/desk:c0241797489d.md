Layer3 Intel's Residential Proxy Observatory detected residential proxy exit activity on AS2650, the network reserved for the Executive Office of the President, from October 5 to November 3, 2026: 236 exits through 28 public proxy services, with one to three unique exits on most days. Cross-checks against Spur and Synthient independently identified the addresses as residential proxy exits. The firm said anyone could sign up to a public residential proxy service for a few dollars and route requests through the addresses, and that the evening-heavy pattern points to a single infected device rather than guest Wi-Fi. The disclosure was published on November 4, 2026.
