ReversingLabs tied the SSO vishing campaign to INCEPTION's Tor leak site, which indexed five claimed victims on November 17 and later added AutoZone and Lucid Motors for seven total. It confirmed 21 domains serving the SSO phishing kit, attributed 41 more at high confidence and four at medium confidence, and found the lure domains had no MX, DKIM or DMARC records, indicating phone-led delivery that bypasses email controls. The kit redirected unexpected visitors to legitimate Okta or NetIQ sign-in pages, and staging indicators included NICENIC registrations, Cloudflare nameservers and evening UTC registration windows. ReversingLabs advised hunting DNS, proxy and identity-provider logs from October 21, 2026 onward.
