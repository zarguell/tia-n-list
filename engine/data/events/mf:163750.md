Industrial-scale model distillation is better understood as an adaptive collection operation than as a single act of copying. The collector needs sustained access, coordinated tasks, useful outputs, and resilient routes; providers need to preserve legitimate access while protecting differentiated capability. AlphaHunt examines that incentive structure, the substitution problem behind account-by-account enforcement, and why the best strategic metric is the cost of campaign reconstitution rather than the number of accounts banned.
