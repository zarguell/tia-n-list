A critical vulnerability in the TranslatePress multilingual WordPress plugin could enable unauthenticated attackers to take control of administrator accounts and fully compromise affected websites. This vulnerability, tracked as CVE-2026-19632, has a CVSS score of 9.8 and affects all TranslatePress versions up to 3.3.1. The flaw affects a plugin installed on over 400,000 WordPress sites.
