Vendor advisory published 2025-09-17 and updated 2026-08-10 for an out-of-bounds write in the Fireware OS iked process, scored 9.3 on CVSS v4.0, reachable pre-authentication through Mobile User VPN or Branch Office VPN IKEv2 with a dynamic gateway peer, and still exposing devices where a branch office VPN to a static gateway peer remains configured. WatchGuard stated it had evidence of active exploitation. Fixes shipped in Fireware OS 2025.1.1, 12.11.4, 12.5.13 for T15/T35 models, and 12.3.1+722811 for FIPS, with an IoA of IKE_AUTH IDi payloads larger than 100 bytes.
