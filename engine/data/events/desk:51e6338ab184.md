Help Net Security reported that CVE-2026-19868 is an authentication enforcement flaw in the SonicOS SSL-VPN service exploitable only when SAML is enabled for SSL-VPN logins, and that the flaw was discovered and reported by Ryan Harris of IT services provider Electronic Office. The article notes no workarounds are listed and the same releases fix six other vulnerabilities. It states attackers need working credentials first, but SAML-enforced MFA is the control the flaw can bypass.
