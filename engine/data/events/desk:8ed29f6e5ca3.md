BleepingComputer reported that Shadowserver first observed exploitation of CVE-2024-36401 on July 9, 2024, shortly after public proof-of-concept exploits appeared. It cited ZoomEye's estimate of approximately 16,462 internet-exposed GeoServer servers and noted patched versions 2.23.6, 2.24.4 and 2.25.2. The article urged exposed installations to patch immediately and review systems and logs for compromise.
