Okta Threat Intelligence dissected multiple custom phishing kits offered as a service and built for voice-based social engineers. The kits let a caller change what a victim sees in the browser in sync with the call, intercept credentials and manipulate MFA prompts, and were used against Google, Microsoft, Okta and cryptocurrency providers. Okta said this synchronization defeats any MFA that is not phishing-resistant and published a customer-only advisory with details of two kits.
