The Register reported on September 1, 2026 that an attacker stole an API key belonging to AI testing organization METR and consumed roughly $600,000 worth of model credits over several weeks before anyone noticed. Okta's September 9 report cites this case, alongside a $25,000 bill at a software firm and Unit 42's near-million-dollar response cases, as evidence that stolen AI credentials monetize through the victim's own billing.
