Tenable counts a record 964 Microsoft CVEs in the September 2026 Patch Tuesday release, 104 critical and 860 important, including both Windows elevation of privilege bugs CVE-2026-81963 and CVE-2026-85880, each CVSSv3 7.8 and granting SYSTEM privileges. Per Microsoft, both were exploited in the wild as zero-days. Tenable notes CVE-2026-81963 is the first exploited-as-zero-day Windows Update Stack elevation of privilege flaw since 2022, and CVE-2026-85880 is the first ALPC flaw in Patch Tuesday in over three years.
