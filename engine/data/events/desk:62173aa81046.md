The Irish Times reported that Tánaiste and Minister for Finance Simon Harris described the suspected breach as deeply concerning and that Belfast-based Wellington IT had contacted affected credit unions earlier that week. The report said hackers exploited a vulnerability in the provider's software to extract some member records, including details of payments leaving accounts, while balances were not compromised. The Irish League of Credit Unions said the vulnerability had been closed, and the Data Protection Commission said it had received breach notifications from affected credit unions. The article put potential exposure at 1.8 million members, more than half of Irish credit union members, and said Wellington IT had been contacted for comment.
