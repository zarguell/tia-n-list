On February 20, 2026, CISA added CVE-2025-68461 and CVE-2025-49113 to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog based on evidence of active exploitation. CVE-2025-68461 is a Roundcube Webmail cross-site scripting vulnerability via the animate tag in an SVG document, with a due date of March 13, 2026 for federal agencies. CISA references the Roundcube security update and the fixing commit bfa032631c36b900e7444dfa278340b33cbf7cdb.
