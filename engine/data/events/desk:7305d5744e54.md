F5 published advisory K000156741 on October 15, 2025 (page shows Published Date Oct 15, 2025, Updated Date Apr 1, 2026) for a CWE-121 stack-based buffer overflow, internal ID 1977933, in the BIG-IP APM apmd process, reachable unauthenticated on the data plane when an access policy is configured on a virtual server. The bug was originally remediated as a DoS issue at CVSS 7.5 (v3.1) and re-categorized in March 2026 to critical RCE at 9.8 (v3.1) / 9.3 (v4.0) once F5 learned it had been exploited, with fixes in 17.5.1.3, 17.1.3, 16.1.6.1, and 15.1.10.8. F5 lists no mitigation, credits Schuberg Philis, Bart Vrancken, Fox-IT, and the Dutch NCSC, and warns that UCS backups taken from compromised systems can contain persistent malware.
