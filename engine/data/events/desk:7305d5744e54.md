F5 published advisory K000156741 on October 15, 2025 for CVE-2025-53521, a stack-based buffer overflow (CWE-121) in the BIG-IP APM apmd process that allows unauthenticated RCE when an access policy is configured on a virtual server. The bug was originally remediated as a denial-of-service issue with CVSS 7.5 (v3.1) and was re-categorized in March 2026 to a critical RCE at CVSS 9.8 (v3.1) / 9.3 (v4.0) after F5 learned it had been exploited, with fixes in 15.1.10.8, 16.1.6.1, 17.1.3, and 17.5.1.3. F5 lists no mitigation and credits Schuberg Philis, Bart Vrancken, Fox-IT, and the Dutch NCSC for coordinated disclosure, and warns that UCS backups from compromised systems may contain persistent malware.
