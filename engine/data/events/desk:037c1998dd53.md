Security Affairs reported on February 21, 2026 that CISA added CVE-2025-49113 and CVE-2025-68461 to the KEV catalog, noting Roundcube's history of targeting by APT28 and Winter Vivern. The article states CVE-2025-68461 is a cross-site scripting vulnerability in Roundcube Webmail before 1.5.12 and 1.6 before 1.6.12 via the animate tag in an SVG document. It reminds that Roundcube vulnerabilities have been used to steal login credentials and spy on sensitive communications.
