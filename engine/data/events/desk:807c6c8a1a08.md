The New York Post reported that Sean Cairncross was leaving after just over a year in the job and that a White House spokesperson had confirmed the departure. It said Kirsten Davies, the chief information officer at the Department of War, would replace him in an acting capacity, and relayed Axios reporting that Cairncross did not want to serve the final two years of the term and left early to allow confirmation before Democrats take the Senate in January. The report placed the exit on the same day Michael LoSapio, Trump's pick to lead CISA, testified at his Senate confirmation hearing.
