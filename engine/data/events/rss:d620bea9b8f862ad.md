Hackers are actively exploiting a newly disclosed N-able N-central zero-day vulnerability dubbed "4EverAble2Shell" to compromise servers and deploy cryptocurrency miners, with researchers reporting at least 21 affected systems, including 19 running the company's latest security update. The attacks follow security researcher Sean Bales' release of two new zero-day exploits today, dubbed 4EverAble2Shell and Agent2Shell, which he says allow attackers to gain root-level access to N-central appliances and SYSTEM-level access to Windows systems. N-central is a remote monitoring and management (RMM) platform used by managed service providers (MSPs) and IT teams to monitor, manage, and secure customer devices and networks remotely. The new vulnerabilities were disclosed on November 12, just two days after N-able released emergency security updates to address critical N-central zero-day vulnerabilities that Bales had publicly disclosed earlier this week. Bales published proof-of-concept exploits for both the 4EverAble2Shell and Agent2Shell vulnerabilities, saying they were tested against N-central Appliance 2026.4.1.26 HF2, the security update N-able released on November 10. The 4EverAble2Shell exploit allows unauthenticated attackers to execute commands as root on vulnerable N-central servers.
