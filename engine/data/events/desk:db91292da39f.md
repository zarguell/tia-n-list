Searchlight Cyber researcher Adam Kues disclosed the pre-authentication RCE on July 17, 2026, noting it needs no preconditions and works against a stock WordPress install with no plugins, in an ecosystem estimated at over 500 million WordPress sites. Affected branches are 6.9.0 through 6.9.4 and 7.0.0 through 7.0.1, with versions at or below 6.8.5 not affected; the recommended fix is 7.0.2 or 6.9.5, and the interim mitigation is blocking /wp-json/batch/v1 and ?rest_route=/batch/v1 at a WAF. The team also released wp2shell.com as a public exposure-check tool and withheld technical details to give defenders patch time.
