HIPAA Journal reported that Concentra confirmed on January 9, 2024 that 3,998,162 patients were affected, bringing the total to at least 14 million and making the PJ&A breach the largest healthcare data breach of 2023. At least 40 lawsuits had been filed against PJ&A and affected clients. North Kansas City Hospital and its subsidiary Meritas Health reported 502,438 individuals affected between March 27 and May 2, 2023.
