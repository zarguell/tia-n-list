Fortinet published FG-IR-25-934 on February 10, 2026, describing CVE-2025-68686, a CVSSv3 5.3 exposure of sensitive information in FortiOS SSL-VPN that bypasses the earlier patch for the symbolic link persistency mechanism via crafted HTTP requests. Exploitation requires a prior filesystem-level compromise through another vulnerability. FortiOS 7.6.0 through 7.6.1 and 7.4.0 through 7.4.6 are fixed in 7.6.2 and 7.4.7, and the FMWP virtual patch FG-VD-60389.0day shipped in db update 26.033.
