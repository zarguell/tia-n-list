Quick Answer: Registry security matures with your pipeline: start with what your platform includes ( GitLab’s built-in scanning, Red Hat Quay’s integrated Clair), wire Grype into CI free, then graduate to JFrog Xray or Aqua when gates not reports become policy, with Snyk carrying fix-culture and Prisma / Qualys joining at platform scale. The registry is where your software supply chain becomes enforceable but only at the maturity stage where scanning turns into gating. Most teams buy that enforcement years before their pipeline culture can honor it, then quietly disable the gates that break builds. This brief maps eight registry-security options to pipeline maturity stages: what ships free inside platforms you already run, the CI-scanning habits that cost nothing but discipline, and the gate-and-provenance stage where paid enforcement finally earns its keep.
