The FBI's IC3 hosts the joint cybersecurity advisory on WaterPlum/Contagious Interview, dated September 18, 2026 per its canonical 260918 CSA URL, issued by agencies from Japan, the United States, Australia, and Germany. As reported by The Hacker News, the advisory documents at least 30,000 compromised devices in over 100 countries, more than 7,000 cryptocurrency wallets drained, and at least $10.71 million stolen, and states some WaterPlum actors also operate as North Korean IT workers, with a laptop farm in Japan identified and dismantled.
