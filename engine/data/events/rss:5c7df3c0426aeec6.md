An administrator registers a shiny new FIDO2 security key. The security team marks the account as "phishing-resistant". Three months later the account is compromised through an AiTM phishing page. The attacker didn't touch the key. They sent a push notification, because push was still an acceptable way to satisfy every Conditional Access policy that applied to that account. A stronger credential doesn't remove weaker paths.
