Today is Microsoft's October 2026 Patch Tuesday, with security updates addressing a record-breaking 1,411 flaws, including one actively exploited and two publicly disclosed zero-day vulnerabilities. This Patch Tuesday addresses 70 "Critical" vulnerabilities, 57 of which are remote code execution, 11 are elevation of privileges, and 2 are spoofing.
