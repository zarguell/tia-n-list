NVD published the CVE-2026-53998 record on 16 November 2026 at 20:19 UTC. It states that from 13.0.0 until 15.7.0 the applyChatTemplate GraphQL query passed attacker-controlled F-string templates to FStringTemplateFormatter._format, while DotDict.__getattr__ failed to prevent traversal through private attributes. The record notes the endpoint is unauthenticated by default, that exposed PHOENIX_SECRET can enable administrative token forgery and decryption of stored credentials, and that version 15.7.0 fixes the issue.
