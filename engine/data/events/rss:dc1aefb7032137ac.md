Numerous Department of Agriculture applications have been taken offline over a multi-day period in response to a cybersecurity incident internally disclosed earlier this week, according to three people familiar with the matter and an email viewed by Nextgov/FCW and Government Executive . The outages have impacted many financial tools used to facilitate payments between the agency and farmers, said the people, who requested anonymity because details of the incident are sensitive. To help keep the nation’s food supply steady, USDA gives farmers a financial safety net for things like crop insurance or covering losses when factors like bad weather affect harvest yields. It’s not clear who carried out the intrusion or if any data was exfiltrated from systems. The department’s Cybersecurity and Privacy Operations Center “uncovered a security incident” that required its Information Services Division to “reset all database passwords in all environments, and this is requiring us to reset all applications causing outages,” per the email that was sent Monday by Chris Rottler, acting assistant chief information officer for USDA’s Farm Production and Conservation Business Center.
