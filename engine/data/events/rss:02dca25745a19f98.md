Exciting news for AI governance! Rapid7 has achieved ISO/IEC 42001:2023 certification for our Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS). The certificate was issued by Schellman Compliance, LLC, an ANAB accredited certification body, following an independent audit of how we govern the AI systems we develop in our role as an AI provider. What is ISO/IEC 42001? ISO/IEC 42001 is the first international management system standard for artificial intelligence. Where ISO/IEC 27001 focuses on information security, ISO/IEC 42001 focuses on how an organization governs AI. What exactly does “governing AI” mean? Essentially, it means how AI risks and impacts are assessed, how data is managed, how systems are monitored once they’re in production, and how humans stay in the loop across the AI lifecycle. This certification means an accredited third party has audited our AIMS and confirmed that it aligns to the standard. To note, this isn’t a one-time exercise, as the certification is maintained through annual surveillance audits. This means our AI governance program is held to the standard for as long as the certificate is active. Why is ISO/IEC 42001 certification important? AI is continually evolving how security teams work, and it’s also causing teams to reconsider the questions they ask their vendors. Where does the model come from? What data does it use?
