Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 reported active exploitation of CVE-2026-0257, an authentication bypass in PAN-OS GlobalProtect, by ransomware operators including Qilin and Inc, with public reporting also attributing exploitation to Settra. The most significant TTP evolution was a direct pivot from the compromised VPN gateway to the virtualization layer, where attackers disabled execInstalledOnly on ESXi hypervisors and used a custom tool to perform offline credential dumping against a domain controller's virtual disk (VMDK).
