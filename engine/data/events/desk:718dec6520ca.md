The Hacker News reported on February 21, 2026 that CISA added two Roundcube flaws, CVE-2025-49113 (CVSS 9.9) and CVE-2025-68461 (CVSS 7.2), to the KEV catalog citing active exploitation. The article notes CVE-2025-49113 was weaponized within 48 hours of disclosure by FearsOff, while CVE-2025-68461 was patched in December 2025. No details were provided on who is behind exploitation of the two flaws.
