Mandiant and Google Threat Intelligence Group reported that ARMORY CASTLE, a suspected PRC-nexus actor formerly tracked as UNC5221, exploited BeyondTrust Remote Support zero-day CVE-2026-102764 in late August 2026. The intrusion used the SAWFLY webshell and BARECLAW tunneler, with evidence in access, syslog, messages, thin-scc, brain and SSL logs on a LUKS-encrypted /home partition. The authors warned that the actor historically moves from selective zero-day use to broad opportunistic exploitation after public disclosure and urged defenders to prepare for that pattern.
