The Hacker News reported on September 17, 2026 that CVE-2026-91843 is a CVSS 9.8 stack overflow in the pre-authentication login of Check Point Security Management and Log Servers, fixable through LivePatch or Jumbo Hotfix updates. Check Point said there was no indication of exploitation at the time, and Censys reported 3,836 hosts carrying the default management role identity. The article counts CVE-2026-16232, CVE-2026-62144, CVE-2026-18574, CVE-2026-85103 and CVE-2026-91843 as five critical unauthenticated management flaws disclosed since July 22, and notes the July advisory tied CVE-2026-16232 exploitation to management exposed without IP restrictions.
