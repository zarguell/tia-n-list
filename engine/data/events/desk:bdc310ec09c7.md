GMO Internet's official advisory dated 2026-10-21 (4:00 JST) confirms unauthorized third-party access to Onamae.com Desktop Cloud and the installation of malicious programs in some customer server environments. The company began progressive shutdowns of customer servers starting at 04:00 local time on October 21, 2026. The notice states data stored on impacted systems may have been viewed, but no confirmed misuse or leakage of payment information (stored separately) has been reported.
