This critical advisory, rated CVSS 9.8 and published 2026-08-11, is the incomplete-fix predecessor to CVE-2026-128138: the CVE-2026-12841 patch added _validate_java_options() to block flags such as -agentlib, -agentpath, -javaagent, -Xrunjdwp and @argfile, but validation runs only in config_java() and not in the java() function's per-call options parameter introduced by PR #3683. All four Stanford wrappers (GenericStanfordParser, StanfordTagger, StanfordTokenizer, StanfordSegmenter) accept user-supplied java_options and route them through that unvalidated path in nltk/internals.py. Affected versions are 3.10.2 and earlier and the fix is 3.10.3.
