Octopus's 27 November notice says it detected abnormal activity from a cyberattack that morning and immediately took containment measures. It reports names and photographs for approximately 7,500 customers, with some records also involving Automatic Add Value Service bank codes and top-up amounts, plus contact details for approximately 22,000 merchants. The company says no credit-card or bank-account information was affected, core services remained normal, and affected customers would be contacted directly by email or SMS.
