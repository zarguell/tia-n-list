ESET documented an Operation Dream Job wave targeting European defense and UAV-sector companies with trojanized PDF readers and ScoringMathTea. The attackers used social engineering via fake job offers, DLL sideloading, and a new loader (BinMergeLoader) to deliver the payload. The campaign aligns with North Korea's drone program expansion and reverse-engineering efforts, making it a precursor to the 2026 zero-day exploitation.
