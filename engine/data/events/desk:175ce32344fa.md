This 20 November news item covered the GPGME 2.3.1 release and CVE-2026-129975, describing the long user ID trigger, possible data theft and the 20 November disclosure. It restated GnuPG's account of the 1,024-byte status and colon-line readers, the stale pointer left by an early read failure, and the affected products including Gpg4win, GnuPG Desktop, VS-Desktop, mail and XMPP clients, Samba and Flatpak. It also reported the 12 November notification from GitLab and the disclosure moved forward from 25 November because of the separate Ruby2Shell flaw.
