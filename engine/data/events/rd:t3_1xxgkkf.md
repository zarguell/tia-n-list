La Poste told AFP on Saturday that Maileva, its business-mail unit, was breached in mid-September. Names and postal addresses of 22 million people were taken. The specialist sites FrenchBreaches and cyberattaque.org reported it first. Maileva prints and mails payslips and invoices for about 70,000 business users (180 million documents in 2024). The 22 million are recipients of those clients' letters, so most of them never had an account anywhere near Maileva. Per cyberattaque.org, affected clients include the social landlords Altéal and Flandre Opale Habitat, plus the Foch hospital in Suresnes.
