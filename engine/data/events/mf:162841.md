Here it is! :) One of my main goals for 2026 has arrived a little later than expected, but it is finally here: the CYBER HUNTER PROJECT book. I have to say that writing a book from scratch has been quite an adventure. I have learned a lot about all the challenges and difficulties involved in taking an idea and turning it into a published book. I hope you enjoy it — and if you decide to grab a copy, you will be helping me turn all that effort into a well-earned beer or coffee! ☕ Why I Wrote This Book Over the years, I have created many hunts to investigate real situations. Some started with an alert, others with a suspicion, and quite a few with a simple thought: what if this is happening in my environment and nobody is looking for it? Many of those hunts later became articles, public projects, and queries. This book is where I bring that work together and explain what led me to each investigation. This is not a book built by following an existing framework or repeating ideas from other books. It comes from personal experience: investigations I have worked on, queries I have written, things that did not work as expected, and lessons I learned by doing the job. I do not want you to copy a query, run it, and stop there. I want you to understand why the hunt started, what evidence to look for, and how to turn the result into something useful.
