The Citrix bulletin that patched CVE-2026-8452 bundled fixes for six NetScaler CVEs in total, delivered in builds 14.1-72.61 and 13.1-63.18 (FIPS/NDcPP line 13.1-37.272). At publication Citrix described CVE-2026-8452 as a memory overflow leading to unpredictable behavior or denial of service and stated it had not observed any unmitigated exploitation. The Citrix support portal blocks direct crawling; the earliest Wayback Machine capture of the article is 2026-07-01T01:06:17Z, and reporting cited in this story places the advisory in June 2026.
