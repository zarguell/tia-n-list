Executive Summary Security teams have never had better visibility into their assets, vulnerabilities, and threats. What they lack is the speed to act on it. Frontier AI models are surfacing vulnerabilities faster than ever, attackers are using the same models to exploit them within hours, and most remediation still depends on scans, tickets, and change windows. Exposure management needs a new operating model that detects exposures as soon as they are disclosed, focuses only on what is proven exploitable, and remediates autonomously without breaking the business. That operating model is the AI-native Risk Operations Center (AI-ROC). On October 13, 2026, Microsoft released the largest Patch Tuesday in its history, fixing 1,414 vulnerabilities . That is more than seven times the 193 vulnerabilities Microsoft fixed in October 2025. One of the new vulnerabilities was already being exploited, and the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) set a deadline of just three days to patch it. At ROCon Americas 2026, we made the case that most organizations are focused on the wrong question. Knowing how many exposures you have is no longer useful on its own. What matters is how quickly you can turn those exposures into verified risk reduction.
