A malvertising operation dubbed MoldyRoost is buying online ads to compromise vulnerable iPhones and steal saved passwords, private messages, and cryptocurrency wallet recovery phrases. According to Confiant’s MoldyRoost investigation, loading the landing page after tapping an ad can trigger the attack. Visitors do not need to install an app, enter credentials, or run a command.
