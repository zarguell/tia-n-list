Mozilla has introduced an optional built-in Ad Blocker for Firefox on iOS, giving iPhone users a native way to reduce third-party advertising and related tracking activity while browsing. The feature, announced on September 1, 2026, is disabled by default and can be enabled through Firefox’s settings. The addition addresses a common mobile browsing problem: advertisements, pop-ups, overlays, and tracking scripts can consume limited screen space, slow page rendering, and interrupt access to content. Firefox for iPhone Adds Optional Built-In Ad Blocking Mozilla said the new option is designed to block many third-party ads and ad-related trackers before they load, reducing visual clutter and distractions on websites. Firefox’s iOS Ad Blocker uses Apple’s WebKit Content Blocker technology, the system-level framework that enables browsers and apps to apply content-filtering rules on iPhones and iPads. Mozilla has also integrated the widely used EasyList filter list to block advertising-related resources. Users do not need to download or configure a separate extension. The setting is available by opening Firefox, navigating to Settings> Browsing> Ad Blocker. Mozilla emphasized that the capability is optional, allowing users to decide whether ad blocking should be part of their mobile browsing experience.
