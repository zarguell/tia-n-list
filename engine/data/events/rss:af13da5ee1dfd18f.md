HUDSON ROCK Infostealers.com / Threat research Remus Stealer Uses a Fake Google Sheets Extension to Steal Gmail Messages Research from Invoke RE and Gen Threat Labs identifies a malicious Chrome extension that uses the victim’s existing Gmail session, clarifying the browser component behind Remus’s persistence claims. By Hudson RockNovember 24, 2026Research update Remus Stealer is distributing a Chrome extension disguised as Google Sheets to collect messages from Gmail, according to new findings published by Invoke RE . The extension reads mail through the browser’s existing login session and sends message contents to an attacker-controlled endpoint. The finding adds a concrete browser component to a question Hudson Rock raised earlier this month: how could a stealer claim continued mailbox access after its original executable had finished running? An extension left inside the compromised Chrome profile could perform later collection. Its presence would also mean that securing the Google account and cleaning the endpoint are both necessary parts of the response.
