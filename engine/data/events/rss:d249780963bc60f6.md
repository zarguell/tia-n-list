The SOCRadar Threat Research Unit (STRU) identified RedOps, a cyber espionage campaign conducted by a Vietnam-sponsored threat actor tracked as WeaveLotus . Between August and September 2026, the actor targeted military, government and legislative organizations in Southeast Asia to collect intelligence on South China Sea defense and military agreements. Across five confirmed intrusions in Thailand and the Philippines , WeaveLotus exfiltrated gigabytes of internal documents, project plans and credentials. Its activity against Indonesian government organizations did not progress beyond reconnaissance. The campaign name comes from the string “redops”, which appears repeatedly in the actor’s tooling. This post covers the full campaign, TTPs and IoCs. The complete technical analysis, including code excerpts and screenshots, is available in the whitepaper .
