Ukraine finds an Nvidia AI chip in a new Russian missile. According to the Polish outlet linuxiarze.pl, Ukrainian military intelligence (HUR) identified an Nvidia Jetson computer module inside the new Russian missile, a finding with sanctions-evasion implications since Nvidia components are subject to export restrictions.
