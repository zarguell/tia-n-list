Few security teams struggle to find exposures. They struggle with too many findings, too few people to work them, and no clear way to show that risk went down. SOCRadar’s Attack Surface Management (ASM) module has been rebuilt around that problem. It is now Autonomous Exposure Management (AEM) , live for all customers inside the SOCRadar Extended Threat Intelligence (XTI) platform. AEM replaces the ASM module outright, with nearly every screen rebuilt or replaced. ASM answered what an organization has and what is open. AEM answers what matters, who is likely to use it, and whether the fix held: it discovers the external attack surface, shows which threat actors have a way in, ranks what to fix first, and re-checks fixes on demand. From ASM to AEM SOCRadar’s Attack Surface Management started where every exposure program starts: continuous, automated discovery of an organization’s internet-facing assets, running inside Extended Threat Intelligence next to cyber threat intelligence and digital risk protection.
