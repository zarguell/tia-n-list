What Is Malware? (Definition and Meaning) Malware definition in one sentence Malware is any software designed to damage, disrupt, spy on, or gain unauthorized access to a device, network, or the data stored on it, without the owner’s informed consent. The term covers viruses, worms, trojans, ransomware, spyware, and infostealers, and it powers most cyberattacks, from stolen passwords to encrypted hospital systems. Malware meaning in plain English In plain English, malware means “bad software”: a program that does something its victim never agreed to and would not want. A legitimate app does what it claims to do. Malware hides its real purpose, whether that is copying your passwords, locking your files for ransom, or quietly using your computer to attack someone else. What separates malware from a buggy or annoying app is intent. Malware is written to harm or exploit, not to serve the person running it. What does “malware” stand for? Malware is a short form of “malicious software”, a blend of the words malicious and software . Strictly speaking, it is a portmanteau rather than an acronym.
