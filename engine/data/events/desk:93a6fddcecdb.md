The Register reported on 19 November 2026 that the attack caused more than 10 days of disruption, taking Sysper and MyPMO offline and affecting translation services; Sysper returned earlier that week but failed again on Wednesday. Citing Politico sources, it said Commission defenders detected ARTEX use and that CERT-EU had warned on 5 November about ARTEX-assisted attacks against organizations under its watch. The article also noted the FBI alert on more than 1,600 exposed ARTEX consoles and cases where attackers reached privileged access in under three hours.
