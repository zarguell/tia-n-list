An earlier WebSocket-for-Python issue reports that ws4py 0.5.1 and 0.6.0 disable TLS certificate and hostname verification by default for `wss://` clients unless `ssl_options` includes `cert_reqs`. The report includes an isolated self-signed-certificate interception test and proposes secure defaults, while noting that no fixed version was confirmed at reporting time. It concerns the ws4py dependency directly rather than assigning the pylxd CVE.
