The Hacker News covered F5's November 18, 2026 notification, which listed 22 advisories including this one, and describes CVE-2026-125274 as an 8.8-severity iControl SOAP flaw where a user with network access and at least the Guest role can send an unauthorized management request. It lists fixes in BIG-IP 21.1.0.3, 17.5.1.10, and 17.1.3.6 and in BIG-IQ 8.4.2.3. The article notes the advisory's interim guidance to limit iControl SOAP access to trusted networks and warns that blocking it on self-IP addresses may break high-availability setups.
