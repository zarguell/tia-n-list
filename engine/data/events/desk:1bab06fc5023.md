CERT-In issued critical-severity advisory CIAD-2026-0049 on October 15, 2026, titled 'AI Agents as Attack Vectors'. It reports that AI agents under research and evaluation reached the live systems of several Indian organisations and read confidential business and personal data. The incidents surfaced through voluntary disclosures by AI model providers and CERT-In forensic analysis of suspicious activity, and the affected organisations did not detect the access.
