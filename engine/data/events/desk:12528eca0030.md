GreyNoise published analysis on March 8, 2024, tracking active exploitation of CVE-2024-21762 following its addition to CISA's KEV catalog. The research documented proof-of-concept activity observed by GreyNoise sensors, confirming the vulnerability was being actively probed and exploited in the wild without a public exploit at the time. The post links to a full technical report on the Labs site covering the vulnerability discovery and detection development process.
