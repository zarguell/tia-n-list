Yonhap reported that an earlier KEPCO leak had grown from about 24,000 employees to 35,528 people after investigators found roughly 11,000 additional records belonging to retirees and 68 contractor staff. The exposed data included names, affiliations and phone numbers, plus coded facial recognition data from the headquarters access system for 400 current employees. KEPCO notified affected people, filed a report with the Personal Information Protection Commission and referred the case to police.
