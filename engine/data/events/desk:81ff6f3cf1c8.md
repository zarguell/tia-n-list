SpecterOps researchers Will Schroeder and Lee Christensen published the Certified Pre-Owned research identifying AD CS misconfiguration classes ESC1 through ESC8, including ESC1 template abuse that lets a low-privileged requester obtain a certificate for a privileged account and ESC8 NTLM relay to AD CS web enrollment endpoints. They reported the ESC8 relay issue to MSRC on May 19, 2021 and MSRC responded on June 8, 2021 that it was valid but did not meet the bar for a security update, directing customers to enable Extended Protection for Authentication. The research notes ESC1-ESC7 elevation issues were already proliferating in real environments since January 2021.
