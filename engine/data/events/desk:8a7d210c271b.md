Infosecurity Magazine reported Broadcom's advisory on Witchetty, aka LookingFrog, a group linked to TA410 and APT10. The advisory detailed Backdoor.Stegmap, which concealed malicious code in a Windows logo image hosted on GitHub, and attacks between February and September 2022 that exploited ProxyShell and ProxyLogon to place web shells on public-facing servers. The article noted the group's use of living-off-the-land techniques to maintain long-term access.
