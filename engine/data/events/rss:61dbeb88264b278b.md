Bell Canada has confirmed that an "agentic" threat actor breached its systems on November 17, 2026, and stole enterprise customer data, including information from a system used to order and provision telecommunications circuits. Bell is one of Canada's largest telecommunications providers, offering wireless, internet, television, and enterprise networking services. BleepingComputer first learned of the incident after receiving a tip from a customer who shared a breach notification sent by Bell's Information Security Team. According to the notification, an unauthorized party accessed a pre-production environment of Bell's Maximo system, which the company uses to order and provision telecommunications network connections. Bell warned that information belonging to the recipient's organization may have been stolen, including business contact information such as names, job titles, corporate email addresses, phone numbers, and business addresses. The potentially exposed data also includes information contained in ticket titles and some IP addresses associated with network elements or systems, along with limited contextual information. However, Bell said the affected dataset did not contain credentials, highly sensitive personal information, PDFs, file attachments, or other supplementary information stored in Maximo.
