S-EDV published an analysis of Citrix bulletin CTX697305 at 02:21:46 CET (01:21:46Z) on 2026-11-13, detailing the 28 NetScaler ADC and Gateway vulnerabilities, 9 of which carry a CVSS 4.0 score of 9.3 or higher. It highlights that CVE-2026-120868 (CVSS 9.3) affects every default NetScaler installation, while CVE-2026-107412 (CVSS 9.5) targets Gateway (SSL VPN, ICA Proxy, CVPN, RDP Proxy) and AAA virtual servers, and notes that multiple CVSS 4.0 vectors rate confidentiality and integrity as HIGH despite Citrix framing the impact as denial of service. It recommends inventorying all NetScaler ADC and Gateway instances including Secure Private Access nodes, upgrading internet-facing Gateway and AAA appliances first to 14.1-73.57, 13.1-64.39, 14.1-73.58 FIPS, or 13.1-37.293, keeping NSIP management addresses off the public internet, and monitoring for post-update crashes and restarts.
