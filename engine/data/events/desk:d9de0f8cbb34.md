Drupal's security team published a pre-announcement warning that all supported branches would receive a core security release on 18 November 2026 between 17:00 and 21:00 UTC, and urged operators to reserve update time because exploits might be developed within hours or days. It said the vulnerabilities were rated critical, at least one could be exploited by anonymous users, and more than one configuration was affected. The notice named Drupal 11.4.x, 11.3.x, 10.6.x, 12.0.0-beta3 and 11.5.0-beta1 as affected, and said Steward customers were already protected from known attack vectors at that point.
