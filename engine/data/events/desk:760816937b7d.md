Paulos Yibelo filed this critical, embargoed bug with Artifex on 1 September 2026 and attached a self-contained reproduction. The report describes a type error in `.putgstringcopy` that resumes execution before a bound `.forceput`, leaving a local string referenced from `systemdict` after `restore`; allocator reuse then yields native read/write and controlled dispatch. It reproduced against official Ghostscript 10.07.1 and 10.07.0 and against Google production 10.05.0, with `29 11 mul` returning 18 instead of 319 after the native pointer swap.
