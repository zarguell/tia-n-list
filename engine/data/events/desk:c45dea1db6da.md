BrandIconImage reported Kaspersky GERT's finding that BlackNevas ransomware activity in an ESXi environment in October 2025 and The Gentlemen ransomware in a Proxmox environment in August 2026 both relied on weak administrative controls rather than hypervisor flaws. In the ESXi case, PRTG Network Monitor had broad host access, the same root password was reused across nodes, and the attacker uploaded an `esx` binary, disabled execInstalledOnly, and deleted logs. The post recommends isolating virtualization management systems and eliminating shared privileged credentials.
