Dragos chief research officer HD Moore published the underlying study of nearly two million prompts sent from July through November 2026 with unique URLs, DNS names, and canary AWS credentials through AI routers and inference marketplaces. The report says three hosting providers were compromised, resellers read customer prompts through ordinary request logging, and some requests marked Zero Data Retention were still fetched or forwarded to Chinese labs. Seeded keys were used for tens of thousands of AWS API calls, including S3 listing, Secrets Manager and Parameter Store lookups, Bedrock model enablement, and IAM user creation.
