NVD published the CVE record on December 13, 2021, describing SSRF (CWE-918) in GitLab CE/EE affecting versions from 10.5 before 14.3.6, from 14.4 before 14.4.4 and from 14.5 before 14.5.2, where unauthorized external users could perform server-side requests via the CI Lint API. It carries two CVSS 3.1 scores, 6.8 and a 7.5 variant (AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:N/A:N) that drops the attack-complexity and privilege assumptions, both with high confidentiality impact. References include GitLab issue 346187, HackerOne report 1236965 and the CISA KEV catalog entry, and the record was last modified June 17, 2026.
