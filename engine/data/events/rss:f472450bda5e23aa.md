Hi everyone! I want to briefly share what it took to develop my Shipaton project. I won't load you with too much technical stuff; I'm sure there will be plenty of AI-written articles full of tech details. Let me say right away that I'm really glad I took part. Before this, I had never brought any of my projects to a release, and Shipaton gave me the motivation I was missing. Since I also have a full-time job, I worked on the project mostly in the evenings, at night, and on weekends. I got so worn out that I stopped remembering what day of the week it was, but it was worth it. In short, what the Rattitude Care app is for : keeping track of your rats, their weight and health, plus an AI assistant. Why people need it is probably not that interesting to you, but I have rats myself, so trust me, it's needed 😂 How it started I heard about Shipaton at the end of July while I was on vacation, on a road trip across Canada, sitting in a restaurant and scrolling my LinkedIn feed. From that moment I knew I want to take part, but first I had to get back home. When I got back at the beginning of August, I started working hard and thought it would be nice to also join BuildInPublic. Honestly, that was one of the most important decisions, because in the end I got my first users without any advertising.
