Der Spiegel broke the story on 26 November 2026 at 20:14 CET under the internal codename Project Granite, reporting that SAP was dealing with a hacking incident the company had acknowledged internally. The article, by Marcel Rosenbach, states that per company-internal sources the attackers targeted SAP's presence on GitHub and may have used agentic AI systems to help. A teaser segment behind the paywall adds that employee authentication tokens were stolen and used to copy and alter SAP source code.
