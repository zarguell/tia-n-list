born0monday published a technical writeup on 4 November 2026 demonstrating pre-authentication remote code execution in Mosquitto 2.1.x built with the built-in WebSocket listener, including a chain against the Home Assistant Mosquitto add-on exposed on tcp/1884. The post says the GitHub issue wrongly concluded there was no memory-safety impact, that the memory-safety report was sent to Eclipse on 20 August and closed as a duplicate on 28 October, and that no CVE or upstream fix existed at publication. Home Assistant had shipped add-on 7.1.2 on 19 October, while upstream advice was to firewall the websocket port or disable the built-in listener.
