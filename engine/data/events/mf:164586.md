We describe the technical details of the MikroTrick chain, which combines the CVE-2026-67279 and CVE-2026-86060 vulnerabilities and, when chained, allowed full takeover of a device without authentication. We explain the mechanics of the attacks observed in the wild, the coordinated disclosure of both vulnerabilities, and the practical role of LLM agents in the RouterOS research.
