Kaspersky ICS CERT published the original technical report on 5 November 2026, four months after the affected Russian software developer contacted the team in June 2025. The report documents a months-long BO Team (Black Owl) intrusion that began with password-authenticated SSH access to an internet-exposed Linux terminal server and moved to CVE-2021-44228 exploitation against VMware vCenter 7.0.3, followed by domain, virtualization, CI/CD and code-signing server compromise. It gives the persistence paths /etc/cron.hourly/pgcache and /etc/cron.hourly/1vmware-timesync, the nofrillsdns[.]com command-and-control names, malware MD5 hashes, and states the attack appears to have been detected and stopped before the attackers achieved their objective.
