Google Threat Intelligence Group attributed a campaign against North American academic, medical, and military research institutions to UNC6508, a PRC-nexus actor. GTIG says the actor exploited externally facing REDCap servers, deployed a webshell named help.php and the INFINITERED malware, then replayed harvested credentials more than a year later to reach internal networks and exfiltrate mail through a compliance rule named Patroit. The initial access method was not confirmed, and GTIG noted that UNC6508 probed legacy REDCap versions still present on target systems.
