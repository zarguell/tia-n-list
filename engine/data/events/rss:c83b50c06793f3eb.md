Agentic hacking platforms like ARTEX, where AI models autonomously drive vulnerability discovery and exploitation, have been observed to be used by various cyber criminals. Check Point Research’s analysis of the current landscape observed more than 1,800 publicly exposed instances for ARTEX alone , showing a massive scale of deployments. While previous threat reports on ARTEX focused on the observed telemetry on the victim side, we present artifacts from the operator consoles . This data shows which models were used as well as how the operators prompted their agents. Most attacks were fully autonomous besides the initial goal setup. Threat actors barely interacted with their agent fleets. In the observed activity, Chinese models are most prevalent , with Deepseek Flash taking the lead. Operators mostly only interacted with their agents to check the progress of the current operations – attacks were almost fully autonomous . The goals set by the attackers were exclusively to carry out opportunistic, financially motivated cybercrime , mostly on a broad scale.
