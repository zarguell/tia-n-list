Here are this week’s highlights: Ex-FBI agent’s $2.47M crypto theft compromised bureau investigations Patrick Steven Yaroch, a former FBI counterintelligence agent, faces up to 20 years in prison after pleading guilty to wire fraud and money laundering over the theft of roughly $2.47 million in cryptocurrency. Yaroch used wallet credentials stored on FBI systems to make about 100 fraudulent transfers tied to 35 investigations. He took funds from investigation subjects and adversarial nation-state actors, and the thefts compromised multiple investigations and FBI sources and methods. Baker Tilly client data taken from supplier’s platform US accounting giant Baker Tilly is informing clients that hackers stole personal information, including Social Security numbers, from a platform operated by one of its service providers.
