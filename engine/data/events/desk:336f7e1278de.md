Peter Ullrich posted the incident notice on the Elixir forum at 14:48 UTC on 24 November 2026, telling users to change their hex.pm password, reset 2FA with a new secret and sign in again with mix hex.user auth or rebar3 hex user auth. The post says the 23 November intrusion let an unauthorized third party run commands on web servers and that Hex blocked it and deployed a fix within 20 minutes. It reports no evidence of package modification or maintained access, while noting that database reads could not be ruled out. It also points affected library users to fixes tracked as CVE-2026-124283 in Ash, CVE-2026-133555 in db_connection and CVE-2026-130411 in flop.
