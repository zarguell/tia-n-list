Authorities arrested 354 suspects in a joint international operation targeting vendors and buyers on the Archetyp , Nemesis , Tor2Door, Bohemia , and Kingdom dark web marketplaces. Codenamed 'Operation TorNado,' this law enforcement action ran from May 2025 to October 2026 and involved agencies from 14 countries. "Additionally, law enforcement targeted vendors and buyers active on forums, single-vendor shops, and encrypted messaging services. National authorities conducted independent investigations with Europol's support, including cross-checks via SIENA and forensic analysis," Europol said . "The operation highlighted the evolving resilience of dark web marketplaces, which continue to adapt despite takedowns." They seized over €60 million ($66 million) in cash and cryptocurrency, 950 kilograms of illicit drugs (including amphetamines, cocaine, ketamine, psilocybin mushrooms, MDMA, and cannabis), and 57 firearms in Europe. Law enforcement authorities also seized more than 4.8 metric tonnes of illicit drugs, 152 firearms, and over €150 million ($166 million) in the United States. "Following the takedown of multiple darknet marketplaces and vendor targets in the previous JCODE operation, U.S. and international law enforcement agencies obtained intelligence to identify Dark Web drug traffickers, resulting in a series of complementary, but separate, law enforcement investigations," the US Department of Justice added .
