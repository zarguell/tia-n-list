OpenAI has introduced a “Defense Factory” model for continuous, agent-driven vulnerability discovery and remediation , warning that traditional cyber defenses may not keep pace as AI agents can chain exploits and sustain long-running operations. The company’s approach responds to a rapidly changing threat landscape in which increasingly capable open-weight models can help attackers automate reconnaissance, retain knowledge across sessions, identify relationships between weaknesses, and pursue complex attack paths at machine speed. OpenAI argues that defenders currently retain a structural advantage because they can provide authorized AI agents with direct access to proprietary source code, internal asset inventories, development environments, security findings, and engineering workflows. OpenAI Builds ‘Defense Factory’ as AI Agents Gain Ability Attackers generally lack this internal visibility and must rely on externally available tools and broadly distributed models. However, that advantage may not last. Long-running agent fleets can investigate systems continuously, test multiple attack paths simultaneously, and potentially connect vulnerabilities that would be difficult for a human operator to correlate. A weakness that once required hours or days of manual research could eventually be discovered, validated, chained with another flaw, and exploited before a conventional security team completes triage.
