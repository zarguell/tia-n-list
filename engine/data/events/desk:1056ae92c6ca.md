GL.iNet staff reposted the customer advisory in the official forum at 07:57 UTC on 21 November, restating that the cloud remote-management flaw was exploited to install software on a subset of routers and KVM devices. The thread became the live incident channel: staff added the router check for `crond -i /tmp/.log` on 21 November, warned that `netstat` can be replaced on an infected device, and later published model and firmware versions with reported indicators. Staff also said a clean result from the first check was not enough to rule out an earlier compromise.
