The ETH Zurich and Google team's page (IEEE S&P 2027) describes Skyfall, a microarchitectural data-sampling attack on AMD processors that builds on Transient Scheduler Attacks (first disclosed 2025-07-08). It recovers SEV-SNP guest register state and disk-encryption keys via aborting VMRUN, reads three fixed cache lines per targeted page, and leaks decrypted microcode and SMM state. Tested on EPYC 7313 (Zen 3) and EPYC 9124/9334 (Zen 4); not shown on Zen 5. AMD assigned CVE-2026-40671 and released microcode updates; EDK II assigned CVE-2026-18448. Page last modified 2026-11-10.
