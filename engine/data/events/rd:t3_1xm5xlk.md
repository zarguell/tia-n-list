Hi, I work at IPinfo (former journalist here), and today I ran a few numbers and updates on the supermarket fridge situation. Mostly because of the news that supermarkets in four other European countries reported attacks on their refrigeration. These attacks were in stores in Belgium, Sweden and Estonia, and also in some stores in Portugal. So I gave it a go. Our weekly Internet scans count Danfoss refrigeration dashboards that are open to anyone. France, hit on October 11–12, took most of its offline: 1,331 on October 6–7, 149 on October 27. The countries hit today didn’t move as much, given they hadn’t been attacked before. Between the same dates, Belgium went from 85 to 51 and Sweden from 73 to 64. Portugal dropped from 92 to 37.
