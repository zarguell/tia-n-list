Japan Cyber Watch reports that IDC Frontier (a SoftBank subsidiary) confirmed a ransomware attack on IDCF Cloud from about 03:40 JST on October 7, 2026. The article states that 495 companies and local governments were affected, four zones of East Japan Region 1 at the Shirakawa (Fukushima) data center were hit, and customer data in those zones is unlikely to be recoverable. It notes JR East and View Card disclosed that up to about 6.09 million records held by an email delivery service may have been viewed or taken. The report was updated through October 9.
