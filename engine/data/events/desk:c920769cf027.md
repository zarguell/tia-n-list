The kernel.org CNA record for the kTLS receive-path flaw was published September 5, 2025, almost a year before the KEV listing. It fixes the affected range as kernels 6.0 onward (introduced by commit 84c61fe1) and lists the stable fix releases 6.1.149, 6.6.103, 6.12.44, 6.16.4, and 6.17. The kernel.org CVSS v3.1 score is 9.8.
