CXO Digitalpulse reported that Todoist’s incident notice said a stolen development credential was used on November 22 and that production access followed on November 23. The account export covered approximately 6% of users and contained email addresses, account creation dates and salted SHA1 password hashes, not plaintext passwords. The report also described token exposure for approximately 0.003% of users and credential-like task content and Outlook mailbox excerpts for approximately 0.001% in each group.
