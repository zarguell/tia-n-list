REFORM.news reported overnight on November 10, 2026 that Belarusian hosting and cloud provider ActiveCloud was hit by a serious cyberattack. According to the company, its secure cloud hosting service in Belarus, its email service in Belarus and Russia, and the ActiveCloud website all suffered disruptions. Customer support channels, including chats, telephone support, and the ticketing system, were also unavailable while the company worked to mitigate the consequences.
