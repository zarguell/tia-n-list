Interfax-Ukraine reported that Ukraine's Prosecutor General's Office and the FBI exposed an international criminal organization that attacked foreign companies for multimillion-dollar ransoms. It cites at least 27 members from several countries and a linked network of more than 60 participants in eight jurisdictions, including former Conti members. Four key suspects were served with suspicion notices under Part 2 of Article 255 and Part 5 of Article 361 of Ukraine's Criminal Code and remanded in custody, facing up to 15 years. The report says 28 companies had paid over $40.5 million, and that 66 searches in Kyiv and 11 regions seized computers, phones, bank cards, almost $370,000 and EUR 20,000.
