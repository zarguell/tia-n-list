GitHub advisory GHSA-xvvm-8287-x8f9, published 10 November 2026, rates a TOTP 2FA brute-force flaw at 6.8. Failed second-factor checks had no rate limiting, attempt counting or lockout, so an attacker who knew a user's password could try TOTP codes indefinitely across admin, domain admin and mailbox roles. Fixed in 2026-11 with per-account counting, a lock after 10 failures within 15 minutes, and netfilter reporting for failed second factors.
