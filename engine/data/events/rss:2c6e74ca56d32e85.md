Hackers breached ASOS , a UK-based global online fashion retailer with many customers in the USA, earlier this month. The hackers stole the names, addresses, DOBs, and contact information of millions of ASOS customers. While the exact number of affected customers remains unknown, the fashion powerhouse has tens of millions of customers, with about 20% of its traffic coming from the USA. The ASOS leadership acknowledged the breach after many customers sounded the alarm on social media after receiving unauthorized push notifications on their smartphones. The cybercriminals used the notification to wreak havoc and to disclose that they had compromised customers’ details. The hackers threatened to leak the stolen information unless ASOS agreed to “engage with them,” likely demanding a ransom in return . Key takeaways ASOS suffered a data breach in which hackers stole the names, addresses, DOBs, and contact details of millions of customers. ASOS affected millions of Americans, as the company serves millions of US customers and operates a warehouse in Dallas, Texas.
