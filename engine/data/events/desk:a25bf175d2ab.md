ASUS published a bulletin titled Security Update for ASUS Control Center in its advisory feed at 13:46:40 +0800 on 18 November 2026, which is 05:46:40 UTC. The bulletin names Control Center Datacenter 4.0.0.2 and earlier and Control Center Enterprise 3.1.0.8 and earlier as affected, says a low-privileged authenticated user can escalate to administrator-level access, and directs all users to upgrade to the latest version immediately. It lists CVE-2026-126574 with CVSS 4.0 score 9.4 and the vector AV:N/AC:L/AT:N/PR:L/UI:N/VC:H/VI:H/VA:H/SC:H/SI:H/SA:H.
