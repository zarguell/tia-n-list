Google's Stable channel update to 157.0.8103.50/.51 for Windows and Mac and 157.0.8103.50 for Linux fixes nine vulnerabilities, including CVE-2026-125350, a high-severity use-after-free in Chrome's XML component reported by an anonymous researcher on November 6, 2026. Google states it is aware that an exploit for CVE-2026-125350 exists in the wild. The release also fixes seven other high-severity flaws and two medium-severity flaws, with vulnerability details restricted until a majority of users are updated.
