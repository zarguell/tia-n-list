RARLAB's vendor advisory announces WinRAR 7.13 (release date July 30, 2025), fixing CVE-2025-8088, a directory traversal flaw affecting the Windows versions of WinRAR, RAR/UnRAR, UnRAR.dll, and portable UnRAR that lets crafted archives bypass the user-specified extraction path. Linux/Unix builds and RAR for Android are not affected. RARLAB credits Anton Cherepanov, Peter Kosinar, and Peter Strycek of ESET for the report and calls for immediate action; the news item is dated 12.08.2025 15:00 site time (win.rar GmbH is registered in Germany).
