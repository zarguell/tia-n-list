Shubham Shah and Sam Curry describe a vulnerability chain giving remote code execution on the production ForgeRock servers handling customer logins for two unnamed automakers, discovered in early 2026 and reported the same day it was validated. Both automakers remediated within about two hours and rotated the affected credentials, and the affected systems served customers in multiple regions. The chain allowed creating a valid session for any customer account and, where a connected vehicle was present, remote access including location, door lock and unlock, and remote engine start and stop; only US accounts and vehicles were tested end to end.
