Doctor Web's primary advisory for the campaign behind this story, published a day before the first aggregated event. It states the first poisoned repositories appeared two years ago targeting crypto wallet users with blockchain-game, game-bot, and cheat-tool Visual Studio projects, then pivoted to scan-automation, security-testing, and log-analysis projects aimed at infosec professionals, with over 2,000 PCs infected at the time of research. It details the hiding places (malicious `PreBuildEvent` routines in `.csproj` files, PowerShell in `.suo` files, whitespace-concealed code in `.go` and `.py` files), the PowerShell staging paths under `C:\Users\Public\Pictures`, attacker domains such as `muckanthropic.com` and `muckdeveloper.com` plus IP `130.12.182.172`, and final payloads comprising a backdoor, a clipboard clipper, and a data-theft trojan delivered via the Electron loader `Microsoft.exe`.
