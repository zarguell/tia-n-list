On 10 November 2026 this report covered the fifth day of the BrickLink outage and quoted seller Simon Webster of DSLS Bricks, whose overall sales revenue was down 52% against the same four days of the previous November while his BrickOwl sales were up 79%. It cited Brick-Tracker's count of about 18,750 shops in BrickLink's last public store directory, including roughly 1,800 in Germany, and noted LEGO bought BrickLink in November 2019 with more than 10,000 sellers across over 70 countries. The piece also recalled the outage of 3 to 8 November 2023, which followed a threat and ransom demand and was later attributed to credential stuffing, with every user forced to reset a password.
