Quick Answer: ITDR buying follows your identity estate. Active Directory shops start with Microsoft Defender for Identity (often already licensed); EDR-consolidated teams extend CrowdStrike or SentinelOne into the identity layer; hybrid AD + legacy estates get unique protective (not just detective) coverage from Silverfort; cloud-identity and SaaS estates watch Permiso and Rezonate; AD recovery and hardening is Quest’s lane; analytics-heavy SOCs add Gurucul. Session-token theft and helpdesk social engineering including attacks taking advantage of vulnerabilities in identity and access management solutions are the primary risks these tools exist to mitigate. Who This Guide Is For SOC leads adding identity coverage after an EDR buildout to counter evasion tactics like EDR killers used in ransomware attacks , AD administrators defending domain controllers through proper Active Directory security strategies , and cloud security teams who discovered their IdP is the new perimeter.
