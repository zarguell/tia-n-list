Lauren Proehl ran a honeypot from July 7 to November 7, 2026, with a walkable git repository, a fake mail server, fake AI inference endpoints, and more than 500 canary AWS keys. Attackers used 155 of those keys from 12,353 distinct IPs; 72 percent of used keys went to SES or SNS send-quota checks and 36 percent touched Bedrock. The report measured a median of 37 minutes from theft to first use and documented a checker that graded stolen AI keys across five providers from 20 residential connections in 14 countries.
