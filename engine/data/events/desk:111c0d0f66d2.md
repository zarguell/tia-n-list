CISA added CVE-2026-46300 (Fragnesia) to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog on October 28, 2026, and ordered federal agencies to patch by Saturday October 10 under Binding Operational Directive 26-04. The advisory identifies Fragnesia as a high-severity Linux kernel privilege escalation bug in the XFRM ESP-in-TCP subsystem and notes that attackers can chain it with the PHP2Shell vulnerabilities CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 to deploy webshells and execute commands as root.
