Cloud entitlements are the quietest catastrophe in security: the typical cloud identity uses a tiny fraction of its granted permissions, service accounts outnumber humans many times over, and one over-privileged role chains a phished developer into a full-estate breach. Cloud Infrastructure Entitlement Management (CIEM) maps who-can-do-what across AWS, Azure, and GCP, right-sizes to least privilege, and increasingly enforces just-in-time access alongside broader zero trust security controls .
