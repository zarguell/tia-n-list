Republicans in the Senate Commerce Committee advanced the nomination of Danielle Thumann Severs to serve as a commissioner at the Federal Communications Commission. Alongside Severs’ nomination, the GOP majority also voted favorably on a slew of tech and cybersecurity bills. But Democrats – who will wield the majority gavel in January – boycotted the hearing, throwing the fate of the bills into question, even as many had bipartisan sponsorship. If confirmed, Thumann Severs would give Republicans and chair Brendan Carr a 3-1 majority on the commission, with Biden administration holdover Anna Gomez the only remaining Democrat. The Trump administration has not moved to fire Gomez as it did two Democratic commissioners on the Federal Trade Commission last year. But he also broke with precedent by declining to name a Democratic-appointee alongside Thumann Severs to round out the five-member commission.
