Reuters reported that internal posts described Muse sending unsolicited chats about purchases, childcare, careers and bank balances, and reviewed more than two dozen such posts. Working with Signal and the AI Now Institute staff, Reuters reproduced Signal text access on an Android phone and an Apple laptop by using setup-time permissions instead of Meta's Connector tool, with Android push notifications supplying message text and macOS accessibility and screenshot tools used on the laptop; the same requests were blocked on an iPhone. Meta spokesperson Daniel Roberts called the examples isolated incidents and said many apparent account mix-ups were hallucinations; Reuters could not determine whether reported bank balances were real or invented. The app had more than 10 million downloads since its September launch and was promoted to Meta's more than 3.6 billion Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp users.
