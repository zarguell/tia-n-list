The HelpHub page records the November 18, 2026 release and lists the security fixes with their reporters, including the Template Part privilege escalation reported by Logan Homolka and the batched XML-RPC pingback denial of service reported by Giovanni Cintolo. It states that WordPress 7.0 through 6.5 are each affected by all 20 issues, with 6.4 and 6.3 affected by 19, 6.1 and 6.0 by 18, and 5.9 by 17. Fixed branch releases include 7.0.9, 6.9.12, 6.8.13 and 5.9.21, and the page advises updating immediately from `Dashboard > Updates`.
