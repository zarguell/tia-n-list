Switzerland's National Cyber Security Centre confirmed its first AI-agent-assisted cyberattacks against Swiss organizations and critical-infrastructure operators across at least two sectors. It said the agents could search for vulnerabilities, select tools, evaluate results and plan subsequent attack steps, exploiting standard or easily guessed passwords and known software flaws. The bulletin named ARTEX and CyberStrikeAI as examples of publicly accessible agents and urged reduced internet exposure, critical patching within 24 hours, MFA or passkeys, least privilege and behavioral monitoring.
