TechCrunch reported that PJ&A told the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services more than 8.95 million individuals were affected by a breach that began as early as March 2023, and that the company began notifying patients on October 31. The stolen data included names, dates of birth, addresses, medical record and hospital account numbers, admission diagnoses, dates and times of service, and some Social Security numbers, insurance, and clinical information. Northwell Health confirmed 3.89 million patients affected and Cook County Health confirmed 1.2 million, including 2,600 records containing Social Security numbers.
