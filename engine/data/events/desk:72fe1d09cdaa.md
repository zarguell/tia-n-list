IBM first published this bulletin on 8 October 2026 and modified it on 23 October 2026. It records CVE-2026-84272, a CVSS 9.8 missing-authentication flaw in the Guardium edge-controller for 12.1 and 12.2.2 that lets an unauthenticated remote attacker execute arbitrary container images and take over managed edge clusters. IBM advises the 12.0p15004 Edge patch and the 12.0p147 Fix Pack, and lists no workarounds.
