Trend Micro reported on Jul 29, 2025 that Gunra's Linux variant uses up to 100 parallel encryption threads with partial-encryption control, targets Windows and Linux, and drops the .ENCRT extension. The group first appeared in April 2025, has victimized organizations across Brazil, Japan, Canada, Turkey, South Korea, Taiwan, and the U.S., and operates a Tor-based data-leak site. The blog notes 14 claimed victims and provides IOCs and hunting queries for Trend Vision One.
