Recent Breaches published a vulnerability profile on 12 November 2024 noting CVE-2024-8068 as actively exploited and added to the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on 25 August 2025 with a federal patch deadline of 15 September 2025. The profile confirms CVSS 5.1 (Medium), CWE-269, and lists affected Citrix Session Recording versions. It provides remediation guidance including applying the vendor update, segmenting Session Recording servers, restricting domain-user access, and deploying host-based monitoring focused on NetworkService process creation and session-recording storage paths.
