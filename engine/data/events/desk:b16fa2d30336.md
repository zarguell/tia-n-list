Joomla published advisory 20261111 on 24 November 2026 at 14:00 UTC for CVE-2026-123214, an authentication bypass in the web installer. It says the installer stays usable on an installed site while the installation folder exists, letting unauthenticated users rewrite the site configuration; affected versions are 4.0.0-5.4.10 and 6.0.0-6.2.0, fixed in 5.4.11 and 6.2.1. The advisory records a 30 September 2026 report date and credits multiple researchers.
