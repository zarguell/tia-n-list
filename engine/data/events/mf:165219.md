Lookalike websites are selling access to TypeSafe AI’s Jev API while routing requests through their own platforms. The sites offer a working service, but charge up to 11.5 times the official price and place a third party between customers and TypeSafe. Jev, launched on September 15, is a “System One” model designed to return structured decisions, such as yes/no answers, choices and scores, rather than generated text. TypeSafe lists input tokens at $0.042 per million, with no output-token charge. Three days after launch, jev-ai[.]pro and jevtypesafeai[.]com were registered. In searches conducted during the investigation, each appeared above TypeSafe’s official site for relevant Jev queries. Both presented playgrounds, documentation, pricing and checkout pages. Their lack of affiliation with TypeSafe was disclosed in less prominent areas, including footers or legal pages. Fake Jev Sites Proxy Official API The pricing difference is substantial. Monthly plans on jev-ai[.]pro and jev-agent[.]org cost about $0.247 to $0.483 per million input tokens, roughly six to 11.5 times TypeSafe’s listed rate. Annual plans on jev-ai[.]pro, paid upfront, bring that down to about $0.124 to $0.242 per million tokens. Starter packs on jevtypesafeai[.]com work out at up to $0.42 per million tokens. The sites do not appear to offer a separate Jev model. Their service forwards requests to an upstream API that mirrors TypeSafe’s request and response format.
