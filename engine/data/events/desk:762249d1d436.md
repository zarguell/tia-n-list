BleepingComputer reported on September 25, 2026 at 5:41 PM ET that Kiteworks had urged customers worldwide to temporarily shut down servers for a six-hour window on Saturday, September 26, after receiving credible threat intelligence from federal authorities. The shutdown recommendation was intended to protect against potential zero-day attacks, though neither the company statement nor customer notification confirmed a zero-day had been discovered or exploited. The article notes Kiteworks says all known vulnerabilities are fixed in version 9.5.1 and references the Clop extortion gang's history of targeting file-transfer platforms, including Accellion FTA, GoAnywhere MFT, SolarWinds Serv-U, Cleo, and MOVEit Transfer.
