One of the biggest questions I get whenever I’m teaching anything focused on core skills—whether it’s network security, SOC analysis, or ecurity essentials—is whether any of these classes are even necessary anymore because of AI. The assumption seems to be that AI is going to eliminate the need for foundational knowledge. People believe that AI will simply handle the technical details for us, allowing security professionals to skip over the basics and move directly to the advanced work. Unfortunately, that assumption creates a sense of hopelessness. Junior analysts who are trying to enter the field start wondering whether it’s worth studying at all. Companies looking to hire are hesitant because they’re not sure what the workforce will look like in a few years. Everyone is waiting to see where things land. Context Is Everything When it comes to core information security skills, I believe we need to recognize something important: The fundamentals matter more today than they ever have. The reason is context. The more context and understanding you have about a technical problem, the more effective AI becomes. Artificial intelligence is not a replacement for expertise. It amplifies expertise. If you put AI in the hands of someone who doesn’t understand networking, operating systems, or security fundamentals, the impact is often limited.
