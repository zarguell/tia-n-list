New York City Health + Hospitals, the largest public healthcare system in the US, disclosed a data breach affecting at least 1.8 million individuals. Attackers accessed internal systems between November 25, 2025 and February 11, 2026, through a compromised third-party vendor. The stolen data includes medical records, health insurance information, Social Security numbers, and — notably — biometric data including fingerprints and palm prints, distinguishing this breach from typical healthcare data incidents. NYC H+H detected the intrusion February 2 and immediately engaged cybersecurity professionals. The breach is among the largest healthcare data breaches of 2026.
