Objective-See Foundation researchers found a patched flaw in the macOS ChatGPT app that could let an attacker take over the app and reach its chat logs, other stored data, and interconnections such as browser sessions. Researcher Patrick Wardle said agents need broad access and become a high-value target when that access can be subverted. OpenAI acknowledged the flaw and fix in its system change log on September 25, according to WIRED.
