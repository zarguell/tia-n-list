Hackers breach and leak 300 GB of data from Uruguayan law and accounting firm Blanco & Etcheverry. The attack was carried out by the Gunra criminal group using ransomware. The leaked data includes judicial case files, tax declarations, and compliance folders. At least 35 individuals and 149 companies are affected. A countdown timer for ransom payment was posted on the dark web; when payment was not made, the data was published. The breach exposes the full client list, legal documentation organized by client, IRPF and capital gains tax returns, KYC files, beneficial ownership records, and due diligence folders. The investigation by Team Capybara assessed the confidentiality impact as CRITICAL.
