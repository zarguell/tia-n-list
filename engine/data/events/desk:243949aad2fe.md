Securifera published CVE-2026-90817 on September 20, 2026 as an unauthenticated remote code execution flaw in REDCap survey passthrough routing and Data Import processing, with CWE-94 and CWE-73 and a CVSS 3.1 base score of 9.8. The record says exploitation requires a valid public survey hash, and its prose states the flaw exists in REDCap 13.3.0 and higher. Its structured affected list names 13.3.0 alone and marks 16.0.49 LTS, 17.3.10 LTS, and 17.4.4 Standard as unaffected; Ryan Wincey is credited as finder.
