AMD's EDK II bulletin for Skyfall's SMM leak (CVE-2026-18448, CVSS 4.0 5.6, assigned by the EDK II project) traces the issue to missing TSA mitigations in EDK II firmware that let a ring-0 attacker infer residual microarchitectural state left by SMM. AMD recommends applying previously published TSA mitigations (AMD-SB-7029) plus an EDK II or platform firmware patch targeted for November 2026 on EPYC server lines; platform firmware is already available for EPYC 4004 and 4005. The EDK II project lists no patched version as of the advisory. Revision dated 2026-11-10.
