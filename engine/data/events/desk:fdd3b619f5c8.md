DOJ announced Wagenius pleaded guilty to conspiracy to commit wire fraud, extortion in relation to computer fraud, and aggravated identity theft. Court documents say that between April 2023 and Dec. 18, 2024 he used the kiberphant0m nickname to defraud at least 10 victim organizations, obtaining credentials with a tool called SSH Brute and moving them via Telegram, then extorting victims on BreachForums and XSS.is for at least $1 million while on active duty. DOJ credited Flashpoint and Unit 221B with assistance; sentencing was then scheduled for Oct. 6.
