TOP ATTACKS AND BREACHES IDScan.net, a US identity verification provider, has disclosed a data breach after detecting unauthorized access on September 1. Exposed data included names and government identification numbers, while a criminal marketplace advertised a collection containing millions of identity documents, including driver’s licenses, associated with the company’s verification services. Mathspace, an education platform used in Australia and New Zealand, has suffered a data breach affecting more than 1 million people. The attackers exploited CVE-2026-72898 in self-hosted tool Metabase to access an internal reporting database. Exposed information included names, email addresses, usernames, and locations, while passwords and academic records were not affected.
