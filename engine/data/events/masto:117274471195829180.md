Microsoft proposes integrating an emergency stop button into its AI systems and refusing them legal personhood. At the core: a kill switch mechanism that would allow human operators to immediately halt AI system operations in case of unsafe behavior. The proposal also addresses the question of whether AI systems should be granted legal personality, with Microsoft arguing against it. This comes amid broader industry discussions about AI safety controls and accountability frameworks.
