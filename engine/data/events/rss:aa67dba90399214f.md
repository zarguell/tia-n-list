Practical Use Cases for Data Pipelines in Detection Engineering Back in 2023, I wrote about why you need Data Engineering Pipelines before an enterprise SIEM . It opened with the classic “Data is the new oil” line and a simple question: why are so many detection engineers still dealing with log data in its crude form? Crude logs mean the dirty job of cleaning, parsing and enriching data lands on the desk of SIEM and content engineers. Over and over again, for every new detection use case. Three years later, most of the pipeline discussions out there still revolve around one thing: cost. Licensing, storage, GB/day. You know the drill. Money is always a valid driver. However, shortening detection delivery time and improving the overall quality of detection output are directly tied to costs too. This is a follow-up with a focus on practical use cases where refined data, delivered by a pipeline, speeds up the implementation of Threat Detection use cases. The pipeline is not where the alerting decision is made. It’s where the dirty job gets done, so detection engineers can focus on the detection logic and advanced use cases (alert frameworks, detection models, etc).
