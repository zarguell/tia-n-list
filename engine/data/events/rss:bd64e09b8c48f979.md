Outflank turned ten this year, and we’re celebrating by sharing our journey and giving away swag you can’t buy anywhere. Outflank started in 2016, when Cobalt Strike was still only a few years old, BOFs did not exist yet, EDRs were just AVs with a fancy name and the same easy bypasses, and generative AI was firmly science fiction. At the very beginning, we had no polished website, no elaborate launch campaign, and barely even a company name. What we did have was a four headed team eager to kick ass, an email address, enough red-team work to keep us very busy and a temporary ‘coming soon’ website with nothing more than just a red hoodie stock photo. That temporary page was intended to be there for ‘just a few weeks’. It stayed up for nearly a year as we were simply focused on client work. This created an unexpected amount of mystery and speculation. Looking back, that red hoodie picture was the accidental start of something that became and integral part of our identity. Go to an infosec conference today and you will likely spot red Outflank hoodies, worn by our team and by OST customers around the world. A decade later, a lot has changed. The work has evolved, the team has grown (but nobody has ever left!), and Outflank is now part of Fortra.
