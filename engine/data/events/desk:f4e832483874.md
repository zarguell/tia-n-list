The Record covered Symantec's report on Witchetty, also called LookingFrog, targeting two Middle Eastern governments and the stock exchange of an African nation. The reported incidents ran from February to September 2022 and involved ProxyShell and ProxyLogon exploitation, credential theft, lateral movement and a backdoor hidden in a Windows logo through steganography. Symantec researchers quoted in the article described the group's goal as classic espionage and data theft.
