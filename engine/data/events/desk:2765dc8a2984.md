TechCrunch reported on November 13, 2026 that Uber confirmed hackers stole driver's license information for thousands of US drivers during an October breach at Fireworks AI, a third-party AI cloud provider that held Uber records. In notices filed with several US attorneys general, including California, Uber said it was notified by the provider on October 9 that hackers had broken in and stolen internal credentials, and that it determined on October 13 that personal information had been accessed. Uber severed its data connections with the provider and began notifying affected drivers.
