It’s the most wonderful time of the year, especially for scammers. The holiday shopping season is packed with tempting one-day-only deals, gift guides, and retailers promising the perfect present at the perfect price. And wherever shoppers go, scammers aren’t far behind. Cybercriminals have always taken advantage of the holiday rush. Now AI makes it easier to create fake storefronts, products, reviews, and promotions that look and sound legitimate. For consumers, knowing what—and who—to trust is getting harder. Shoppers are worried. According to Malwarebytes research, 77% of consumers are concerned about being scammed by a retailer using a fake AI website, or using AI to misrepresent products or reviews. That concern is grounded in experience: More than one in four consumers (28%) have experienced an AI-related shopping scam in the past year Some were misled by AI-generated product photos or reviews. Others described ordering products that never arrived, sometimes from legitimate-looking websites that turned out to be fake.
