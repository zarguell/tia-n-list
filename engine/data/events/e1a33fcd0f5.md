President Trump signed **Executive Order 14409** on Monday, establishing binding deadlines for federal agencies to transition high-value assets and high-impact systems to post-quantum cryptography. Key requirements: agencies must designate a PQC migration lead, inventory cryptographic assets, and complete migration for key establishment by **December 31, 2030** and digital signatures by **December 31, 2031**.

The Department of Commerce will run a PQC pilot program through end of 2027. Federal contractors must comply with NIST PQC standards by end of 2030. The State Department is tasked with assisting allied governments in their transitions. The EO specifically cites the "harvest now, decrypt later" threat — adversaries already collecting encrypted data with intent to decrypt once quantum computers mature.
