In October 2026, Insikt Group® identified 82 high-impact vulnerabilities to prioritize for remediation , 52 of which had a Very Critical Recorded Future Risk Score. This represents a 13% decrease from the 94 vulnerabilities reported in September. 36 of October’s high-impact CVEs were surfaced through the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA)’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog, 33 were reported in open sources and validated by Insikt Group, and 13 were exclusively surfaced through honeypot data. The 82 vulnerabilities in this report affected products from 51 vendors, with Citrix and Microsoft together accounting for approximately 15% of the vulnerabilities. The remaining exposure spanned network edge and security management appliances; operating systems and endpoint software; collaboration, content management, and enterprise platforms; and artificial intelligence (AI), developer, and automation tools. Insikt Group created a Nuclei template to detect the two PHP2Shell vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619). Insikt Group also published templates for CVE-2026-87902 (WordPress) and CVE-2026-86124 (AutoAgent). These are available to customers in the Recorded Future Intelligence Operations Platform.
