Aflac Life Insurance Japan disclosed that attackers accessed its policyholder portal repeatedly between **June 15 and June 25**, exfiltrating personal information of **4.38 million customers** — including names, addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, and insurance account data. Premium transfer account information for approximately 230,000 people was also compromised.

The incident is confined to Aflac Japan systems and does not affect Aflac's US business. At least five services remain disrupted. No credit card data was exposed. Investigation is ongoing with third-party forensic support.
