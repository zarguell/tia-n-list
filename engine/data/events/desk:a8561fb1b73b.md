Dancho Danchev's original study of an Invision Power Board database dump covering Exploit.in from 18 February 2005 to 30 May 2008: 9,647 members, 13,925 threads and 80,891 posts, with the member table holding email addresses, IP addresses and password hashes. Matching the member list against private message archives from five later forums including XSS, RAMP and BreachForums found 205 distinctive handles present in both eras, 26 of them with twenty or more posts on Exploit.in. The article reports one handle match already published by law enforcement, AbdAllah, a documented alias of wanted bulletproof hosting provider Mykhaylo Rytikov on the US Secret Service most wanted listing. Ransomnews published only aggregate figures and withheld the member table.
