The Netherlands’ National Cyber Security Center (NCSC) has warned organizations to urgently patch two critical vulnerabilities in Check Point VPN products , saying widespread exploitation attempts are likely to begin soon. Tracked as CVE-2026-85102 and CVE-2026-85103, both flaws carry a CVSS severity score of 9.8 out of 10. The NCSC assesses both the probability of exploitation and the potential organizational impact as high. Check Point VPN products are widely deployed to establish encrypted connections between users, branch offices, and corporate networks across the internet. Critical Check Point VPN Flaws Because VPN gateways are often internet-facing and provide access to internal resources, critical flaws in these systems can create an attractive initial-access route for threat actors. CVE-2026-85102 affects the process that establishes VPN connections. According to the NCSC , an external attacker could exploit the issue without valid credentials, bypass security protections, and execute attacker-controlled code on the affected appliance or system. The second vulnerability, CVE-2026-85103, involves certificate-processing functionality. An unauthenticated remote attacker may be able to exploit the certificate-handling flaw to execute malicious code, again without a password or authorized VPN access.
