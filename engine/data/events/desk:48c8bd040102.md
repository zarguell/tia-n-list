Brandefense published a technical note on 7 November and added a 10 November update on CISA's KEV listing. It flags the gap between IBM's phrase authorized user and the CVSS vector setting privileges required to None, and says IBM credits no external researcher and offers no workaround. Its timeline records the CVE being reserved on 5 November, IBM publishing on 7 November and CISA adding the flaw on 9 November with a 12 November remediation deadline and forensic triage under BOD 26-04. The note recommends reviewing admin accounts and permission changes made since 5 November and restricting who can reach the message-handling interface.
