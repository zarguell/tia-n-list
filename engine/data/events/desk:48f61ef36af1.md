GitHub's advisory database records CVE-2026-111497 as affecting the PyPI brotli and brotlicffi packages from 1.0.3 up to but not including 1.2.1, with fixes in 1.2.1 and 1.2.1.0. The underlying buffer overflow is in the Brotli decoder's c/dec/decode.c and can corrupt adjacent decoder state, disclose memory, cause a denial of service or permit code execution from a crafted compressed stream. It lists upstream fixes in Brotli 1.0.10, 1.1.1 and 1.2.1.
