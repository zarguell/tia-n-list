AMD's vendor advisory, initial publication 2023-10-16 per its Revisions table, describes CVE-2023-20598 in the Radeon Software kernel driver pdfwkrnl.sys: improper privilege management lets an authenticated attacker craft an IOCTL to gain I/O control over arbitrary hardware ports or physical addresses, potentially yielding arbitrary code execution. It credits Takahiro Haruyama of VMware with the report and sets fixes at Adrenalin Edition 23.9.2 (23.20.11.01, 2023-09-19) and PRO Edition 23.Q4 (23.30.13.03), covering Radeon RX 5000/6000/7000 cards and Ryzen 6000/7000-series processors with Radeon graphics.
