Help Net Security reported that an unauthorized hardware implant in Ledger wallets sold through CryptoBilis was linked to roughly $93 million in thefts. The implant reads the 24-word recovery phrase from the device screen during setup, decodes it via an onboard microcontroller, and exfiltrates it over LTE. Ledger confirmed that all confirmed cases related to the disclosed draining incident involved devices sold through CryptoBilis.
