CISA added CVE-2025-21043 to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog on October 2, 2025, describing an out-of-bounds write in libimagecodec.quram.so in Samsung mobile devices that allows remote attackers to execute arbitrary code. The required action is to apply mitigations per vendor instructions or follow BOD 22-01 guidance, with a remediation due date of October 23, 2025. Known ransomware campaign use is listed as unknown.
