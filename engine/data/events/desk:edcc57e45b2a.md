CVE-2026-46300 (Fragnesia) is a local privilege escalation in the Linux kernel XFRM ESP-in-TCP subsystem disclosed on 2026-05-13 by William Bowling of V12 Security, with a public proof of concept that rewrites page-cache files such as /usr/bin/su. Tenable's FAQ, published 2026-05-14, notes the flaw is distinct from Dirty Frag, requires a separate kernel patch, and was not in the CISA KEV catalog as of that date.
