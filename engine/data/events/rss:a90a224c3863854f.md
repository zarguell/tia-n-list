Daxus Advertises Alleged Credential Database SOCRadar Dark Web Team identified a dark web marketplace advertisement associated with Daxus , a vendor offering infostealer logs and credential-related services. The operation promotes a collection of approximately 2.96 million URL-login-password (ULP) records through its service platform, Daxus.pro. The advertised services include searchable credential logs, session cookies, private logs, and email account access. The listing does not establish which malware families collected the credentials or identify specific affected organizations. If authentic, these records could facilitate credential stuffing, account takeover , and unauthorized access to corporate services, particularly where passwords have been reused.
