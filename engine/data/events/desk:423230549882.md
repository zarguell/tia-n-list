ConnectWise published a security advisory reporting unauthorized access to ScreenConnect partner accounts and deployment of unauthorized remote-access software in customer environments. As a precaution, it disabled five third-party ScreenConnect extensions across approximately 400 partner environments and told on-premises customers to disable them manually. The advisory recommended reviewing authentication logs, extension installation records, administrative activity, Run Command history, and Toolbox records, and warned that disabling extensions does not remove already-established malicious software or persistence.
