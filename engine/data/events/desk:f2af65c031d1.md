Trend Micro documented an August 2025 intrusion by The Gentlemen, then an undocumented group, that began with the compromise of an internet-exposed FortiGate appliance and its administrative account. The attackers killed security tooling with All.exe and ThrottleBlood.sys abusing the signed ThrottleStop.sys driver, then deployed password-protected ransomware domain-wide through the NETLOGON share. Victims spanned 17 countries, with manufacturing, construction, healthcare, and insurance most targeted; encrypted files received .7mtzhh extensions and README-GENTLEMEN.txt ransom notes.
