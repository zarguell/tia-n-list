NIST NVD published its entry for CVE-2026-19868 on November 6, 2026, sourcing SonicWall, with the description of an SSLVPN authentication enforcement flaw allowing bypass of SAML authentication controls and SAML-enforced MFA. The entry references the SonicWall PSIRT advisory and the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Both the NVD entry and the underlying CVE record were last modified later the same day.
