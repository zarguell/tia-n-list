Hyunwoo Kim posted the Dirty Frag report to oss-security on May 7, 2026 at 18:56 UTC, publishing at the linux-distros maintainers' request after the disclosure embargo was broken by a third party. The post states no patches or CVEs existed at publication, chains the xfrm-ESP and RxRPC page-cache write flaws, links the upstream ESP fix commit f4c50a4034e6, and gives a modprobe blacklist with rmmod for esp4, esp6 and rxrpc as the only mitigation. Full exploit source is embedded in the message.
