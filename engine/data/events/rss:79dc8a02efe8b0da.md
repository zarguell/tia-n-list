About the Briefing The European Repository of Cyber Incidents (EuRepoC) collects information and knowledge on cyber conflicts. Once every quarter, the researchers, together with DCSO, analyze the most recent cyber operations in Tagesspiegel Background Cybersecurity and present the key trends. The Cyber Conflict Briefing summarizes the key trends, dynamics, and findings related to the cyber operations documented by EuRepoC in each quarter. These operations did not necessarily occur in the respective quarter of 2026 but may have begun earlier. The focus is on technical, political, and legal aspects. Further information on the EuRepoC project can be found here . TL;DR Volume up sharply: 227 publicly disclosed cyber operations, 40% more than Q2 and above the long-term quarterly average of 191. The US was hit most (64). Among EU states, France led (25), followed by Germany (11) and Spain (8). Germany: The headline case was Rhysida’s attack on the Berlin Senate, with 5.8 TB of data leaked. Other attacks hit German organizations in telecoms, healthcare, energy, research and transport. Impact is getting more disruptive: 86% of incidents involved actual “damage” after intrusion, 60% involved data theft, and 42% caused system outages, the highest share since early 2025. DDoS fell to just 6% of incidents, down from 20% a year earlier. Attribution: 53% of incidents were unattributed. Russia overtook Iran as the most-attributed state.
