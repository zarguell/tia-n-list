Cooling Post reports that Glasgow-based Resource Data Management released new firmware after security firms found vulnerabilities in its supermarket controllers, which are more common in the UK than Danfoss units. It says thousands of RDM controllers were exposed, that significant operators were contacted and that smaller cold-chain operators may remain exposed. The trade outlet advises verifying that controllers are not reachable from the public internet and using a VPN or private APN where cellular modems are in use.
