Dassault Systèmes published this advisory on 16 November 2026 for a deserialization of untrusted data flaw in the Station Launcher App across 3DEXPERIENCE R2023x through R2026x. The vendor states the bug could lead to unauthenticated remote code execution and rates severity critical. The public page links to remediation information for customers but lists no fixed release or exploit telemetry.
