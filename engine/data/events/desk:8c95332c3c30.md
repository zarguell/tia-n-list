First news of legal fallout: multiple lawsuits filed in Louisiana against IDScan, with law firms including Markovits, Stock & DeMarco and Hall Attorneys investigating class actions, alleging IDScan failed to protect client data such as Hertz's. Reuters independently confirmed the FBI investigation, and BleepingComputer reported records belonging to Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth and an FBI assistant director appeared in the service. IDScan had made no public statement at that point, though the firm says business customers were notified around September 1.
