Sansec published its patch analysis on August 11, 2026, the same day as the Adobe advisory, and confirmed the fix changes how Magento handles customer identity in the account session to stop attackers switching a customer session onto another customer's account. It noted the fixes ship only as isolated patch files, no new Composer packages, and merchants must be on the latest -p release of their line before applying them. Sansec also stated its Shield WAF was already blocking CVE-2026-71362 exploitation attempts, the earliest exploitation telemetry, roughly two days before the story's first tracked news coverage.
