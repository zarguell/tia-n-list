N-able published the N-central 2026.4 HF1 release notes on November 2, 2026, shipping build 2026.4.0.32 with security fixes for CVE-2026-98432 (critical, CVSS 9.8 Linux kernel flaw), CVE-2026-111497, and CVE-2026-114406. The advisory notes N-central does not use the Brotli decoder affected by CVE-2026-111497 and is not vulnerable to it, and directs on-premises customers to upgrade to 2026.4 HF1 while hosted servers are upgraded by N-able.
