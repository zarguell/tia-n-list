Let's Encrypt announced on October 7, 2026 that starting February 10, 2027 all newly issued or renewed certificates will default to 64-day lifetimes, with staging tests beginning October 14, 2026. The final 90-day certificates are expected to expire May 11, 2027, and authorization reuse will shrink from 30 to 10 days ahead of a 2028 45-day default and a 2029 seven-hour reuse limit.
