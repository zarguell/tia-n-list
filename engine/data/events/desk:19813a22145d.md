ShinyHunters told BleepingComputer it used the alleged zero-day on a Monday night, then moved laterally into FBI-managed AWS GovCloud infrastructure and compromised FBI Criminal Justice, HR, and Medlink services. It claimed 2 TB to 3 TB of employee, applicant, and internal records, shared a defacement screenshot of apply.fbijobs.gov, and said it was exploiting the same flaw against other organizations including Fortune 500 companies. BleepingComputer could not independently verify the zero-day, lateral movement, or data volume, and the FBI confirmed only that it was investigating unauthorized activity affecting FBIjobs.gov.
