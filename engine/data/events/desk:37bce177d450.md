JPMorgan Chase CEO Jamie Dimon said the bank will not allow outside artificial intelligence agents to access customer accounts and operate on their behalf, citing risks to the company and its customers. A bank spokeswoman confirmed that external agents are blocked from operating on behalf of retail customers and that agents may only reach some account data in a read-only fashion through open banking providers. The bank set no timeline for safeguards that would let agents interact directly with accounts and warned that customers who authorize outside agents accept a transfer of liability.
