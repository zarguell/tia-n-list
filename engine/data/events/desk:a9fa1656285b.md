Citrix published bulletin CTX696939 on August 19, 2026, rating CVE-2026-19490 CVSS v4 9.3 as an authentication bypass using an alternate path that applies when the appliance is a Gateway (SSL VPN, ICA Proxy, CVPN, RDP Proxy) or AAA virtual server, with SAML action involvement required on builds 14.1-43.56 or later and 13.1-61.28 or later. Fixed builds are 14.1-73.32, 13.1-63.21, 14.1-73.32 FIPS and 13.1-37.277, with no workarounds. Customers are told to inspect configs for "add authentication samlAction", "add vpn vserver" and "add authentication vserver" to determine exposure. Cloud Software Group credited Samarth Vashisht of the pen-test team at JPMorgan Chase.
