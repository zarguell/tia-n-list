The Node.js project published a consolidated security release for November 11, 2026 covering the 22.x, 24.x, and 26.x lines and 16 vulnerabilities: 7 high, 6 medium, and 3 low severity, most reachable remotely by unauthenticated clients. The flaws concentrate in HTTP/2 and TLS handling, and the advisory lists patched builds v22.23.5, v24.22.3, and v26.13.1 alongside dependency updates to llhttp 9.4.4, undici, and sqlite 3.54.1. Named reporters include Chris Rohlf in collaboration with Claude and Anthropic Research for CVE-2026-123863, CVE-2026-123864, and CVE-2026-123865, and pimterry for the TLS renegotiation client-certificate bypass CVE-2026-123872. The advisory warns that end-of-life release lines are always affected when a security release occurs.
