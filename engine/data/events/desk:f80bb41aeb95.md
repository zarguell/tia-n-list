VYPR reported that a Microsoft Teams outage beginning around 9:00 a.m. local time in Japan prevented users from joining or creating meetings and hindered screen sharing, while one-to-one calls remained available. Microsoft's first official update was posted at 6:56 a.m. IST and a final advisory at 7:26 a.m. IST said the issue was largely mitigated; the root cause was attributed to maintenance activity.
