Key Takeaways The OWASP LLM Top 10 is a vendor neutral security framework from the Open Worldwide Application Security Project that catalogs the ten most critical risks in LLM integrated applications, serving as the security industry’s shared taxonomy for identifying, prioritizing, and addressing AI specific threats. The 2026 OWASP LLM Top 10 includes: Prompt Injection, Sensitive Information Disclosure, Excessive Agency, Supply Chain, Data and Model Poisoning, Unbounded Consumption, Misinformation, Hidden Context Exposure, Vector and Embedding Weaknesses, Improper Output Handling. Previous editions, including 2023/24 and 2025, ranked risks based on community vote and expert opinion alone. The 2026 edition is the first to incorporate real world incident data, with 6,639 cases analyzed. Notable changes include Excessive Agency rising from #6 in 2025 to #3 in 2026 and Misinformation showing the widest gap between community vote and incident data. Several OWASP LLM Top 10 risks mirror classic web application threats, including injection, supply chain compromise, and privilege escalation, now operating on a new surface.
