H.ua reported that Ukrainian police and the FBI uncovered a transnational group that used AI-assisted calls and ransomware to steal data and block large enterprises, with documented ransom payments exceeding $40.5 million, or about UAH 1.8 billion. Attackers posed as IT support staff, obtained credentials, persuaded employees to install remote-access software and automated fraudulent calls with AI before encrypting files and threatening to leak them. In one case they took 1.4 TB from a US company, demanded $10 million and received $5 million after negotiation. The article says four suspects were detained under Articles 255(2) and 361(5) of Ukraine's Criminal Code after 66 searches and face up to 15 years in prison.
