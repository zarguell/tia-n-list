The BBC reported on 11 November 2026 that LEGO had taken BrickLink offline after detecting what it called unusual activity on the platform, without confirming whether the site had been breached or whether customer data was affected. LEGO's homepage statement said it wanted to fully understand what happened by containing the issue, investigating how it happened and checking for related issues. The article noted the site had been down for three days and that a collector, Melanie Hoskin, had received multiple payment links for a limited edition release she had already paid for. A message promising a return by Wednesday evening had been deleted, leaving no timeframe.
