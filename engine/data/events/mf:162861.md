AI-powered customer service agents can be manipulated to bypass authentication controls, expose customer information, send phishing emails , and leak one-time passcodes. Documented by Intigriti security researchers Ayoub and Inti De Ceukelaire, the research illustrates how customer-experience agents create security risks when they can read support inboxes, access knowledge bases, invoke backend tools, or perform account actions. The researchers said the techniques produced more than $50,000 in bug bounties over several weekends, without relying on conventional vulnerability scanners. AI Customer Service Agents Can Be Hacked A major issue is the way some AI support systems process email identity. In one scenario, a chatbot accepted email-based conversations and used the visible From header to identify a customer. An attacker could spoof that header, issue a malicious instruction, and cause the bot to send a phishing message appearing to originate from a trusted support address. Other cases involved discrepancies between email authentication and the information an AI agent parsed.
