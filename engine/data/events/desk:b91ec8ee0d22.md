The OpenSSL vendor advisory dated 29 September 2026 details 14 fixed vulnerabilities including the high-severity CVE-2026-84782 (CWE-125 out-of-bounds read, CVSS 8.2) in DTLS handshake retransmission. It confirms affected versions (4.0 before 4.0.3, 3.6 before 3.6.5, 3.5 before 3.5.9, 3.4 before 3.4.8, 3.0 before 3.0.23, 1.1.1 before 1.1.1zj, 1.0.2 before 1.0.2zs) and provides exact upgrade targets. The issue was reported by Laurent Gaffie (secorizon.com) on 17 August 2026; fix developed by Ryan Hooper. The advisory also covers CVE-2026-84783 (moderate use-after-free in X.509 extension cache under concurrent use, reported 27 August 2026 by Tim Becker and independently 31 August 2026 by aydinmercan), plus low-severity issues in QUIC amplification, CPU DoS, timing side-channels, and SM2 scalar multiplication.
