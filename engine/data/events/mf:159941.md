Signal has introduced Automatic Key Verification, a new security feature that gives users a new way to ensure their encrypted chats haven&#39;t been intercepted. The new feature is part of a &#34;key transparency&#34; system that uses Cloudflare and Trail of Bits as trusted third-party independent auditors to verify the integrity of Signal conversations. &#34;It works through a system of verifications performed by you, your Signal connections, and third-party auditors that together provide the same assurance as manually verifying safety numbers. Unlike safety numbers, these verifications are done independently and do not require an in-person meeting or a secondary communication channel,&#34; Signal software engineer Katherine Yen said . &#34;This system of verifications ensures that the association between a phone number or username and its public encryption key is globally consistent and transparent to all participants in Signal&#39;s ecosystem. This protects against scenarios where a key is swapped out without the key owner&#39;s knowledge — for example, if a malicious party compromised Signal and associated a different key with your connection&#39;s phone number.&#34; Users can enable Automatic Key Verification in Signal by going to Settings &gt; Privacy &gt; Advanced and toggling on Automatic Key Verification.
