Infosecurity Magazine reported Enea's findings on SilentAir, including interception of 2FA codes for some targets and silent SMS activity directed largely at Taiwanese users around midnight. The article notes Enea has monitored the actor since early 2025 and that attacks came in waves ranging from a handful to several thousand subscribers in a month. It also records that CrowdStrike linked LIMINAL PANDA to China-nexus operations in November 2024.
