7NEWS reports the incident from the Thursday ASX statement, listing Travel Associates, Cruiseabout and some independent agents among the affected brands with flight and corporate travel bookings unaffected. It quotes the disclosure that most records hold only names and basic travel information and that a very small percentage may include limited passport details, email addresses, phone numbers, birth dates or in rare cases health or mobility information. The article states the company said no credit card, payment or bank account details were accessed and that leisure brands continue to operate.
