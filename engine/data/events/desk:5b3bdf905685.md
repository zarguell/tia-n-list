CNN published the original report on the outage of NSANet, the classified internal communications network used by the National Security Agency, at 15:31 UTC on 17 November 2026. The article says six people familiar with the matter confirmed the outage, that there was no evidence of a cyberattack at publication, and that the agency had enacted staggered arrivals for personnel while it assessed the scope. White House spokesperson Lauren Bis said on Newsmax that there was no crisis, and sources said backup networks preserved urgent intelligence delivery to the president traveling in Asia. CNN updated the article at 17:59 UTC.
