RÚV quotes 1984 managing director Mörður Áslaugarson saying the attack was extremely serious, clearly backed by well-funded actors and carried out at a speed he had not heard of before. The company, which hosts a large proportion of Icelandic websites, closed every service to protect customers. He said the complete shutdown would be brief, with email and DNS coming back first and other services restored gradually only once data and customer security were confirmed, but he gave no full restoration date.
