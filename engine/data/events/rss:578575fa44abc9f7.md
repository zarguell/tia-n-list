Agentic AI is no longer theoretical. It is happening now: Google has announced the beta of its on-device AppFunctions framework for Android, alongside Computer Control for UI-based automation. AI agents are becoming a native part of the mobile ecosystem. ThreatFabric has identified more than ten different AI agent apps on devices running our customers’ banking apps. Most were installed from outside Google Play and use Android Accessibility Services to read the screen and interact with apps on the user’s behalf, using techniques similar to those seen in banking malware. AI is also changing the economics of fraud. According to Interpol , AI-enhanced fraud is 4.5 times more profitable for criminals. That’s why ThreatFabric now supports Agentic AI Risk and Know Your Agent (KYA) : distinguishing humans from AI agents, legitimate agents from malicious ones, and a new AI model to differentiate good intent from bad, across mobile and web channels.
