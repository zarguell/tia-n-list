Wiz CIRT reports that between October 14 and October 26, 2026, a threat actor used IAM credentials from AWS account 805246085872, associated with Qubole, to assume customer integration roles that still trusted the vendor account. The actor enumerated resources, harvested secrets from Lambda environment variables, Secrets Manager and SSM Parameter Store, validated credentials with TruffleHog, and exfiltrated S3 data, sometimes downloading entire buckets in one session. Wiz states a source IP condition in the role trust policy would have blocked every role assumption it observed.
