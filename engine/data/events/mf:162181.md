Our approach: How we assess your maturity Learn more Cybersecurity incidents can unfold in hours, but response plans often fail at the point of execution: ownership is unclear, investigation findings is difficult to access, and critical decisions are delayed. That is why incident response cannot be something your organization figures out in real time. The Detection and Response Team (DART) – the Microsoft team that delivers Defender Experts Cybersecurity Incident Response – has supported organizations across 54 countries and regions through some of their most challenging security moments; and while we sincerely hope you never need to call us in the middle of a live incident, we do want to help you prepare for that possibility before it becomes real. That’s exactly what the Cybersecurity Incident Response Readiness Workshop is designed to do. What is the Cybersecurity Incident Response Readiness Workshop? The Cybersecurity Incident Response Workshop is a collaborative, scenario driven workshop designed to evaluate your organization’s incident response (IR) plan against realistic, real-world security events, guided by DART researchers. Rather than reviewing your plan in isolation, we work through simulated incidents together.
