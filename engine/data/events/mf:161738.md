[3] Assessment Truesec has no direct evidence that Server Killers is led by the Russian state. However, the group is linked to another Russian hacktivist group, Noname057(16), which has been exposed as the product of a Russian agency called CISM that appears to be involved in online information operations. Since Server Killers shares many characteristics with Noname057(16), including its messaging, it is likely that the group is led by the Russian state in a similar way.
