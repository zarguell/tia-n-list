What Is DPDPA Compliance? DPDPA compliance means meeting the requirements of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 , also called the DPDP Act , and its applicable Rules. These requirements govern how organizations collect, use, store, and share digital personal data, including lawful processing, consent, individual rights, and data security.
