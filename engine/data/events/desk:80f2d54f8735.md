Repository created 2025-07-04 by QiAnXin's RedDrip7 team, described as sharing threat intelligence and detection tools for APT NightEagle (APT-Q-95). It carries the report 'Exclusive disclosure of the attack activities of the APT group NightEagle.pdf' in English and Chinese plus apt-q-95_checktool detection executables and checksums. This is the original researcher disclosure that first publicly named NightEagle as APT-Q-95.
