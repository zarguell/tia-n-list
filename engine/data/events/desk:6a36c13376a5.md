NBC News reported that a White House official confirmed Sean Cairncross was leaving and that a source close to the White House said Pentagon CIO Kirsten A. Davies would replace him in an acting capacity, with the role requiring Senate confirmation. The report noted Cairncross had led the Office of the National Cyber Director since August 2025 and had been involved in White House AI policy deliberations. It said officials and Democratic members of Congress saw no indication the recent private and public sector outages were cyberattacks, though some suggested AI may have played a role.
