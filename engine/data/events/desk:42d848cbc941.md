Huntress independently verified that the then-latest MagicINFO 9 Server versions 21.1050.0 and 21.1040.2 were still exploitable with the public SSD Disclosure proof of concept, concluding the August 2024 patch for the earlier CVE-2024-7399 was incomplete or covered a separate flaw. The post reported exploitation already occurring in the wild, cited Arctic Wolf's observation of exploitation, and noted a Mirai botnet variant exploiting the flaw tracked by the SANS ISC. Huntress advised keeping MagicINFO 9 Server off the internet until a proper fix shipped.
