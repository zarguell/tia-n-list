Shengsheng He was sentenced to 51 months in federal prison for laundering more than $36.9 million from victims in an international digital asset investment scam carried out from scam centers in Cambodia. The court ordered $26,867,242.44 in restitution to victims. He was a former co-owner of Bahamas-based Axis Digital Limited and pleaded guilty to conspiracy to operate an unlicensed money transmitting business.
