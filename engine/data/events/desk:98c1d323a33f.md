Tor 0.4.9.14 addressed a series of high-severity security issues affecting clients, onion services, directory authorities and relays, with detailed ticket information withheld for at least another week per project policy. The announcement stated the project was on a rapid release cadence and that the next release would more likely arrive in the last week of October or first week of November. That forecast matches the 0.4.9.15 release on 28 October that introduced the new directory-request DoS defenses.
