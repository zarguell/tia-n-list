CISA, FBI, NSA, DC3, USSS, and South Korea's KNPA released a joint advisory on Aug 10, 2026 describing Gunra ransomware affiliates exploiting CVE-2024-55591 and CVE-2025-24472 in exposed FortiOS/FortiProxy appliances, along with SSH/credential weaknesses in public-facing VPN gateways. The advisory covers the group's double-extortion model, Linux variant weakness (predictable srand seed), IoCs, MITRE ATT&CK mappings, and mitigation steps including network segmentation and offline backups.
