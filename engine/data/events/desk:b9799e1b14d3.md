BleepingComputer reported on 29 October 2026 that Synology warned of active C3RB3r ransomware attacks exploiting a December 2024-patched flaw. The article notes the company linked to SA-24:20 for CVE-2024-10441, CVE-2024-50629, and CVE-2024-10445, and advised users to block the management web interface from the internet.
