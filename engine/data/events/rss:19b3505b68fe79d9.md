PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619) is fixed in PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, and 8.2.35. Node2Shell (CVE-2026-111497) is fixed in Node.js 22.23.4, 24.22.1, and 26.12.1. Both are on the CISA KEV list. PHP2Shell was added October 26. Node2Shell followed on October 27. A rescan confirms the fix worked and the application is no longer exposed. On October 21, fixes came out for PHP2Shell and Node2Shell, two critical flaws in the runtimes beneath your web apps and APIs. Five days later, PHP2Shell was on CISA’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities list , and Node2Shell followed the next day. Attackers had been probing PHP2Shell four days before a patch even existed . With a timeline that short, the standard response of finding hosts, upgrading, and closing the ticket is not enough. Patched is a claim about a server. However, attackers really connect to the applications and APIs running on them. The question for application security teams is not whether PHP and Node.js were upgraded.
