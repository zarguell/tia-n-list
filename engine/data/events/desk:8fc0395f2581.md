ESET researchers profiled TA410 as a cyberespionage umbrella group active since at least 2018 and loosely linked to APT10, split into the FlowingFrog, LookingFrog and JollyFrog teams. They found victims mainly in government and education sectors around the world and noted that TA410 had access to the ProxyLogon and ProxyShell Exchange vulnerabilities in 2021. This is the research that introduced the team later tracked as Witchetty.
