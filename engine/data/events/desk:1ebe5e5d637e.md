NVD published its CVE-2026-19707 entry at 10:16 UTC on 18 November 2026 with status Received and Airlock's CVSS 4.0 base score of 9.5. It states that attacker-controlled HTML in a backend response can trigger the Smart Form Protection flaw on Airlock Gateway 8.5.2 and earlier or 8.6.0, causing denial of service or remote code execution. Exploitation requires a mapping with Smart Form Protection enabled, and NVD lists 8.5.3 and 8.6.1 as the fixed releases.
