West Pharmaceutical Services, a $10B Pennsylvania-based manufacturer of injectable drug delivery systems, filed an SEC 8-K on May 7 disclosing a material ransomware attack. Intrusion was detected on May 4, with threat actors exfiltrating data and encrypting systems. The company proactively took all systems offline globally for containment, disrupting shipping, receiving, and manufacturing operations across all sites. Core enterprise systems have since been restored and critical processes restarted at some facilities, but full recovery timeline remains undetermined. Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42 has been engaged for incident response. No ransomware group has claimed responsibility as of May 13. West Pharma, which reported $3.5 billion in 2025 revenue, is a critical link in the pharmaceutical supply chain — injection-molded components from West are used in the majority of injectable drugs administered worldwide. The attack follows the broader pattern of healthcare/pharma sector targeting noted throughout 2026.
