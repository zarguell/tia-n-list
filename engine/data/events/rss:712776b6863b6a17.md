A reported cyberattack on Wellington IT has exposed sensitive information belonging to members of 50 credit unions, the majority of which are located in Ireland. Wellington IT is a Belfast-based supplier whose services are used by about 50 credit unions, including 43 in Ireland. While the company has not publicly disclosed a security incident, Minister of State Robert Troy confirmed to RTÉ that Wellington IT is the third-party service provider linked to the cybersecurity incident involving credit unions , resulting in the exposure of data from about 40 credit unions in Ireland. The company reportedly notified affected organizations of a data breach that posed a risk of financial fraud, impersonation, and targeted phishing to their members. According to the Irish Examiner , which saw the notices, Wellington IT informed customers that an automated script made five million requests to exploit an authorization flaw in the company's infrastructure. The attack unfolded between October 2 and 4, 2026, and the attacker accessed private records using valid credentials. Troy characterized the massive delay in Wellington IT notifying the credit unions as "not acceptable." At this time, the total number of impacted individuals is unknown.
