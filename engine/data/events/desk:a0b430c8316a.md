The ASX statement, board-authorised and dated 26 November 2026, says Flight Centre Travel Group is investigating unusual activity in a third-party accommodation booking system used by the Flight Centre brand, Travel Associates, Cruiseabout and some independent agents, and that flight bookings and corporate travel bookings are not impacted. The company says a significant number of customer records may have been accessed by unknown persons but that it is not clear how many were actually extracted from the third-party system. Most accessed records are customer names with basic travel information, while a very small percentage is believed to include limited passport details, email addresses, phone numbers, birth dates, or in rare cases health or mobility information, and no credit card, payment or bank account access has been identified. Containment described in the filing includes enhanced security and detection on affected systems, changed authentication keys and credentials, increased monitoring, notification of authorities and engagement of Mandiant.
