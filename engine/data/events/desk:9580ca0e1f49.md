CrowdStrike's own release announced a definitive all-stock agreement valuing Zscaler at approximately 43.9 billion dollars, with 1.135 CrowdStrike shares for each Zscaler share, an implied 260.41 dollars per share based on CrowdStrike's November 20 close and a 34 percent premium. It says Zscaler shareholders will hold about 15.9 percent of the combined company and that closing is expected in the second half of CrowdStrike's fiscal year 2028, subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals. The release describes a single platform with one data layer and one contract after closing, built on more than 2,000 shared customers and 16 integrations, and says Zscaler protects 50 percent of the Fortune 500 through more than 200 service locations. CrowdStrike projects about 1 billion dollars in annualized run-rate revenue synergies by 30 months after close.
