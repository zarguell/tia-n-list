The Wordfence Threat Intelligence team recently reviewed firewall data for websites on Asian country-code top-level domains and identified a sharp increase in attacks targeting .tw (Taiwan) websites, beginning in late October 2026. The number of .tw websites attacked per day approximately tripled, while .jp (Japan), .kr (South Korea), .hk (Hong Kong), .sg (Singapore) and .cn (China) showed no comparable change. We traced the increase to a single IP address, 45.38.60.254 . Our records indicate that it started scanning on October 26th, 2026, and the Wordfence Firewall has since blocked or logged over 246,000 requests from it. Of the requests blocked by our Malicious File Upload rule, 82.7% targeted websites on the .tw top-level domain. According to our logs, these requests were attempts to exploit known vulnerabilities, and they were blocked by the generic rules of the Wordfence Firewall. The data suggests that the attacker was most likely mapping Taiwanese websites, including WordPress sites, in search of vulnerable targets.
