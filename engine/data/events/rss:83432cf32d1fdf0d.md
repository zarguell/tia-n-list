President Donald Trump will nominate Kirsten Davies for permanent national cyber director, giving the acting role she just received last week for the post to Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton pending her confirmation. A White House official told CyberScoop Monday that “following the overwhelming support Kirsten received from industry and across government after Thursday’s announcement, President Trump has decided to nominate her to serve as national cyber director.” Davies, in the meantime, will serve as senior adviser for the Office of the National Cyber Director. The same person cannot serve in the acting role of a position while also being the nominee for it. The move is one of several in a recent federal cyber leadership shuffle, all following the November elections that saw Democrats take control of the House and Senate. The departure of Sean Cairncross as Trump’s national cyber director opened up his job. Davies was the chief information officer at the Department of Defense prior to receiving the acting national cyber director position, in addition to a background of holding leading cyber roles at big companies like Siemens and Estée Lauder.
