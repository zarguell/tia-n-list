Heise security published its English-language report on September 25, 2026 at 1:49 pm CEST, confirming Kiteworks had warned customers of an imminent cyberattack based on credible threat intelligence from law enforcement. The company recommended shutting down systems worldwide for six hours starting Saturday, September 26 at 4:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m. Central European time, even if systems are not directly internet-accessible. Kiteworks customer support told Heise the reason was to protect against potential zero-day attacks. The article notes large German companies including state banks, insurance firms, media groups, consulting firms, and automotive suppliers use Kiteworks products.
