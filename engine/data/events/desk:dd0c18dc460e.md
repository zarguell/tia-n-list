The Register reports on November 9, 2026 that Shah and Curry chained WAF path-traversal bypasses with default credentials on ForgeRock OpenIDM and the Apache Felix OSGi Web Console to reach production identity servers for two unnamed automakers. The US chain used path confusion and factory default credentials to schedule an inline Groovy task for shell access; the Australian chain used hardcoded staging credentials. The researchers reported the flaws in early 2026 and both automakers patched within two hours.
