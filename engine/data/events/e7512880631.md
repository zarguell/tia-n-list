The **Handala** group, an Iran-linked threat actor believed to be a front for Iranian government hacking operations, leaked **5 GB of files** allegedly stolen from California Water Service (Cal Water), one of the largest investor-owned water utilities in the US. The group claimed it could have disrupted the water supply but chose not to, framing the attack as retaliation for US actions against Iran. Dataminr analysis indicates the leaked data includes personal information from a customer billing database and internal RTKBase application. Cal Water confirmed it is investigating in collaboration with federal and state partners, stating preliminary findings show no operational disruptions to water/wastewater systems.
