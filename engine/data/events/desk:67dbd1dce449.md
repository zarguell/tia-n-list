WatchTowr Labs published the original researcher disclosure by Sina Kheirkhah on 12 November 2024. The post details how a misconfigured MSMQ instance combined with insecure .NET BinaryFormatter deserialization in Citrix Session Recording enables unauthenticated remote code execution via HTTP. It documents the full exploitation chain, from the Serializable EventMetadataWithTime class through the BinaryMessageFormatter to the HTTP-exposed MSMQ queue, and provides the disclosure timeline: initial report to Citrix on 14 July 2024, reproduction video on 11 August 2024, and mutual disclosure date of 12 November 2024.
