N-able released hotfixes to address zero-day bugs affecting its N-central remote monitoring and management (RMM) platform, which were publicly disclosed over the weekend and forced it to take vulnerable hosted environments offline. This follows N-able's decision to take all hosted N-central environments offline across the Americas, Europe, and Asia-Pacific regions until it could patch them against potential attacks targeting the zero-day flaws in the wild. "N-able is actively responding to non-responsible communication of critical vulnerabilities in N-central late Sunday night, November 8, found by an independent researcher," N-able told BleepingComputer on Monday. "As a precautionary measure, we took our Hosted N-central environment offline today, November 9, and provided guidance to customers operating on-premises N-central servers to temporarily take their servers offline until further notice." N-central lets IT departments and managed service providers (MSPs) monitor, manage, and maintain client networks and devices from a centralized web-based console.
