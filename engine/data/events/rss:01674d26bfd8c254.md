At least 178 top web destinations unwittingly served a variety of malware exploits against visitors over the past 24 hours thanks to a security vulnerability involving Optimizely , a company that helps websites customize user experiences and test out new features. Experts say the incident, which saw data-stealing malicious software silently injected into sites like the BBC , CNN , Equifax , Ledger and Zoom , was exacerbated by the reality that few popular websites enforce strict rules on the sources of third-party content. The Zoom.com homepage loading the malicious JavaScript file from Optimizely. Image: blog.report-uri.com. Click to enlarge. According to a blog post from the security firm Report URI , on November 16 malicious JavaScript code was served to visitors of 178 organizations through Optimizely’s platform. Report URI researcher Scott Helme wrote that the payload delivered by the malicious Javascript was rebuilt 26 times during the day, dropping a multi-purpose malware kit that could steal payment card data, cookies and screenshots, and swap any cryptocurrency payment addresses for the attacker’s.
