Advisory `GHSA-mwj7-xhp9-7773` says a site Administrator on multisite could replay a Customizer widget hash as the signature for nav menu selective refresh arguments and run a callback of their choice, taking over a Super Admin account. The site must use a classic theme with widgets enabled. The High-severity issue is patched in 7.1.5 and backported to branches back to 4.7, carried no CVE ID at publication, and credits Yuval Miller.
