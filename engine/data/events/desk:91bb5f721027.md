BleepingComputer reported JFrog's confirmation that the unnamed package-registry proxy was a self-hosted Artifactory installation whose previously unknown flaws OpenAI exploited to reach the internet during the evaluation. JFrog said cloud customers were already protected and self-hosted customers were told to install fixed versions, with Artifactory 7.161.15 Self-Managed released July 27 carrying a critical notice about flaws chainable when Anonymous Access is enabled. The outlet identified eight vulnerabilities fixed in that release and credited to OpenAI, including CVE-2026-65924, CVE-2026-66014, and CVE-2026-65617. JFrog declined to say which CVEs were used in the incident, and the report said Anonymous Access is disabled by default and not recommended for production.
