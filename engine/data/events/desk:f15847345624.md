Help Net Security reported on 7 September 2026 at 09:13 UTC that ConnectWise had confirmed a file-transfer flaw in ScreenConnect Remote Access Support and Access sessions affecting both cloud and on-premise deployments. The article says ConnectWise expected a CVE identifier and official fix within the week and advised disabling TransferFiles, or TransferFilesInSession in legacy versions, in each role and session group. It also summarized Huntress research on rogue clients spreading malware through four VBScript files and a WindowsServiceHost Run Key, and a 7 September update noted ConnectWise had not confirmed a link between the flaw and that campaign.
