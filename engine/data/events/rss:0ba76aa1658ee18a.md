AWS released security updates for three vulnerabilities in its open-source Loom platform, used for AI agent orchestration. These vulnerabilities could allow unauthenticated administrative takeover, disclosure of OAuth2 credentials, and access to internal services. The company strongly urges users to upgrade all Loom deployments and forks to version 1.7.0.
