Zyxel has released new firmware for 10 GS1900 switch models to fix CVE-2026-107441 , an out-of-bounds write that lets an unauthenticated attacker run OS commands on the switch. The flaw sits underneath an exploit that attackers had already used against nearly 1,000 GS1900 switches, and that exploit still works on the firmware Zyxel shipped in June to stop it. Binarly researchers, who found and reported the issue, say the June release (2.90 Patch 2) fixed only part of the bug behind CVE-2026-7273 , the earlier GS1900 flaw that CISA added to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog in September. Switches running Patch 2 need the new 2.90 Patch 3 build for their model. Why Are There Two CVEs for One Exploit? The vulnerability originates from CVE-2026-7273 (CVSS 8.8) , a stack-based buffer overflow in the GS1900 CGI program that Zyxel disclosed and patched on June 16, 2026.
