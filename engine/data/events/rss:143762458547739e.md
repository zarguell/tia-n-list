Ticket seller Fandango Media is notifying customers about a data breach that exposed payment card details entered on its checkout pages. The company says the details were exposed between October 8 and 11, and it impacts customers who made purchases through its official website. Card skimmer code injected into Fandango’s checkout page intercepted payment card details entered when placing orders, sending the data to hackers. Fandango Media is a major U.S. online movie ticketing platform. It sells tickets through its website and app for 31,000 U.S. cinema screens, including at AMC, Regal, and Cinemark. The company also operates the popular movie review website Rotten Tomatoes. “Our investigation identified evidence that an unauthorized party could access payment card information associated with certain transactions on our checkout page from October 8-11, 2026,” reads the notification to customers . “Fandango determined on October 30, 2026 that your information may have been impacted in this incident.” The company says the following data types may have been exposed: Fandango says the incident did not expose names, government-issued identity information, Social Security Numbers (SSNs), or any other account information. However, the information compromised in the incident could be used for unauthorized purchases on online stores.
