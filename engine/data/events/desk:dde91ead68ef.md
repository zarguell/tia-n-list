Kaspersky researcher Dmitry Kalinin documented KeenAndu, a firmware-level backdoor linked into libandroid_runtime.so during the build phase and injected into the Zygote process, sometimes delivered through official OTA updates. The research connects the Triada, BADBOX, Vo1d and KeenAndu botnets into one cluster and includes a Nova clicker module from the Phantom lineage that Bitdefender later associates with Midnight Mimosa payloads and the Android.Phantom family.
