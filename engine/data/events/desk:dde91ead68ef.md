Kaspersky researcher Dmitry Kalinin documented KeenAndu, a backdoor linked into libandroid_runtime.so during the firmware build phase and injected into the Zygote process, in some cases delivered through official OTA updates. The report ties the Triada, BADBOX, Vo1d and KeenAndu botnets into one cluster and includes a Nova clicker module from the Phantom lineage that Bitdefender later cites when connecting Midnight Mimosa payloads to Dr.Web's Android.Phantom family. It is the main prior public research on firmware-level malware in the same low-cost Android supply chain segment, predating this story by nearly eight months.
