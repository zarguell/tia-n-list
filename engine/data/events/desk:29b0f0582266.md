Sekoia's TDR team published part one of its FSB Matryoshka series on June 1, 2026, reconstructing a January 2026 Gamaredon infection chain that begins with weaponized XHTML smuggling a RAR exploiting CVE-2025-8088 to drop an HTA into the Windows Startup folder. GammaWorm, a VBScript worm exceeding 20,000 obfuscated lines, hides modules in NTFS Alternate Data Streams and resolves C2 through Telegram, Telegra.ph, Teletype.in, and Cloudflare Workers dead drops. The article also cites Google Threat Intelligence Group reporting exploitation of the same vulnerability by Sandworm, Turla, Gamaredon, and China-nexus intrusion sets.
