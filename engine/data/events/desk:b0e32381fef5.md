Tech Bytes reports that Eye Security neutralized the unencrypted updater flaw in Time4Popcorn, which exposed over three million desktop devices to remote code execution, by intercepting abandoned update infrastructure between November 2025 and March 2026. The updater polled popcorntimeupd.xyz over HTTP and accepted binaries without signature checks, executing them as SYSTEM on Windows or root on macOS. The article advises complete uninstallation and blocking the associated domains.
