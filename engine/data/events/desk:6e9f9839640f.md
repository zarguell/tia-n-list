ASCII reported on November 24, 2026 that McDonald's Japan had disclosed third-party access to part of the customer data in its official app database on November 20. It repeated the four affected fields and the exclusion of names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses and card data, and said the company found no unauthorized app or service use. The company was invalidating the authentication credentials used in the access, investigating related systems and reporting to Japan's data protection authority, with further updates promised.
