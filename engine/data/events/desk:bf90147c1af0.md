President Donald Trump nominated Michael LoSapio, CISO of Palantir, to lead CISA on Monday; the agency has been without a Senate-confirmed director since the end of the Biden administration and Trump's first nominee withdrew in April. LoSapio has no significant management background and appears never to have worked in government, spending two decades as an engineer including stints at three universities and a year as a site reliability engineer at SingleStore. He was a system engineer at Palantir in the mid-2010s, left, and rejoined in 2017, serving as head of network security from 2017 to 2024 and CISO since September 2024. If confirmed he would face rebuilding CISA's capacity after two years of layoffs, resignations, program freezes, service reductions and political attacks that eroded partner trust.
