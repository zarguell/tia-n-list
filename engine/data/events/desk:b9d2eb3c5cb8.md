Finwire reported that Binance called the ShinyHunters claim of stealing 335 million user records a likely scam, with no evidence of compromised systems or customer data. ThreatMon said the listing offered 335,900,000 lines of information tied to Binance users across roughly 170 countries for $900,000 in Bitcoin or Monero. Brandefense documented similar advertisements dating back to October without confirming the data was genuine.
