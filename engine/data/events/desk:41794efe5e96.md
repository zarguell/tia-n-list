Researcher XueDugu published evidence that the D-Link DIR-850L Rev.A runs a console login gate at boot: /etc/init.d/rcS chains into /etc/init0.d/S95password.sh, which executes /usr/sbin/consolepwd on the serial console and compares input against constants compiled into the ELF, accepting username ws3cd with password new_home!secu6-aar. The package includes positive and negative console transcripts, firmware and binary SHA-256 hashes, and poc/check-consolepwd.sh to re-extract the constants from a rootfs, and classifies the issue as CWE-798 and CWE-1188. It states D-Link SIRT was notified before publication and that no CVE had been assigned when the package was prepared, so this disclosure predates the CVE record by weeks.
