The Daily Mail reported Woolworths shoppers finding hundreds of dollars missing from WISH eGift cards through interstate transactions, with some card purchases made through third-party platforms such as Qantas Marketplace. One customer in Karratha said she learned of the loss at checkout and struggled to reach the gift card line; another in regional Queensland said she called 13 times. Woolworths told the outlet it was looking into the matter as a priority and said replacement cards were among the next steps for affected customers.
