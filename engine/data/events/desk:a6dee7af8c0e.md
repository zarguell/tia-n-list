DataBreaches.Net reported that WBEZ obtained records showing a federal criminal investigation into the PJ&A breach, including a grand jury subpoena sent to Cook County Health in November seeking all information related to the incident. The subpoena was signed by Acting U.S. Attorney Morris Pasqual and a prosecutor in the Justice Department's Fraud Section, and sought PJ&A's contract with Cook County, due-diligence records, and communications about the leak. WBEZ reported the breach potentially affected 1.2 million Cook County patients and 14 million people across the country.
