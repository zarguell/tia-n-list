Master of Malt has disclosed a customer-data breach after attackers compromised an application key associated with Ribon, a third-party BigCommerce application operated by Be A Part Of, a company describing itself as a Fastr brand. In an email sent to affected customers at 17:45 BST on September 18, founder Justin said BigCommerce notified the retailer earlier that day that Ribon had been hacked. The attackers allegedly obtained a BigCommerce application key held by Ribon and used it to access customer data stored within BigCommerce’s systems. Hackers Abuse Third-Party BigCommerce App The exposed information includes customer names, email addresses, telephone numbers, and postal addresses. Master of Malt said passwords, payment-card data, and other payment information were not exposed because those records were maintained in a separate system that was not compromised. The unauthorized activity reportedly occurred from 17:21 BST on September 13 until 21:12 BST on September 17, when the stolen application key was revoked. BigCommerce removed the affected application on September 17 and told Master of Malt that the compromise was no longer ongoing. Master of Malt later said the intrusion was not specifically targeted at the retailer. Instead, the attackers appear to have targeted Ribon, which had reportedly been installed across hundreds of BigCommerce stores.
