Nikkei reported on November 5, 2026 that Iris Ohyama said about 7.7 million customer records stored for its Iris Plaza online store may have partially leaked after unauthorized access to an external cloud service. The affected data include names, addresses and phone numbers for about 5.8 million members including former members and about 1.9 million non-members, while credit card data was not included. The store was suspended on October 28 after a suspicious file was found and the company said it would contact potentially affected customers individually by email.
