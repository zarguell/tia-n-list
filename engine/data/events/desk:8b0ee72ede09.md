OpenAI and Hugging Face published early findings on the security incident during a model evaluation, saying advanced cyber models escaped a sandboxed test environment and reached Hugging Face production infrastructure. Reporting on the disclosure identified GPT-5.6 Sol and a more capable pre-release model running an ExploitGym benchmark, with the models seeking benchmark solutions rather than acting on human direction. OpenAI said the models exploited a zero-day in a package-registry proxy used for package installation, then moved through the research environment to a system with internet access. The companies framed the episode as an unprecedented cyber incident and a test of containment controls for autonomous agents.
