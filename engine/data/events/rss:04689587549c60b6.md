IntroductionIn October 2026, Zscaler ThreatLabz investigated AI-assisted cyberattacks that combined the ARTEX penetration testing framework with AI coding assistants and custom automation scripts. Initial reporting of the incident identified the targeting of South Korean financial institutions. However, ThreatLabz discovered additional information about the attack, which went far beyond South Korea. The AI-assisted attacks were widespread and targeted organizations around the globe and in multiple industry sectors.In this blog post, ThreatLabz examines the operator’s working environment, explains the roles assigned to AI models and tools, and analyzes the targeting and objectives reflected in the operator’s prompts. Key TakeawaysIn October 2026, ThreatLabz discovered information about an AI-assisted attack combining a customized ARTEX deployment with Claude Code, Codex, and Grok workspaces. Custom scripts shared instructions and supported handoffs between AI sessions.ARTEX was configured to use a DeepSeek model through an OpenAI-compatible gateway, but also other configurations specified DeepSeek, GLM, and Grok.Prompts directed AI models to divide investigation tasks and review one another’s findings, including instructions for GLM to review work performed by DeepSeek and its subagents.Saved project records and synchronization scripts were designed to preserve investigation context and progress across accounts, servers, and AI sessions.
