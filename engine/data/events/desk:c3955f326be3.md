Adobe's bulletin, published August 11, 2026, fixes seven flaws in Adobe Commerce, Adobe Commerce B2B and Magento Open Source, led by CVE-2026-71362, a critical incorrect authorization (CWE-863) scored CVSS 9.1 that needs no authentication, no admin privileges and no user interaction. All release lines at the 2026-jul patch level or earlier are affected and the fixes are the 2026-aug releases, distributed as isolated patch files. Adobe credited researcher 0x0.eth and stated at publication it was not aware of exploits in the wild.
