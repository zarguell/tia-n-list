Adobe published bulletin APSB26-92 on August 11, 2026, fixing seven vulnerabilities in Adobe Commerce, Adobe Commerce B2B and Magento Open Source, five of them critical. CVE-2026-71362 is an incorrect authorization flaw (CWE-863) scored CVSS 9.1 with vector CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:N, exploitable without authentication, admin privileges or user interaction, reported by 0x0.eth; the batch also includes stored XSS CVE-2026-48413 (8.7) and CVE-2026-48414 (7.7). Affected versions are the 2026-jul and earlier builds of Adobe Commerce 2.4.4 through 2.4.9, B2B 1.3.3 through 1.5.3 and Open Source 2.4.6 through 2.4.9, with the 2026-aug releases listed as the fix.
