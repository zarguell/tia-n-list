The Hacker News covered CVE-2026-118383 on Nov 12, 2026 in a piece by Swati Khandelwal, the earliest news coverage this desk found. It repeats the key constraint from the vendor and Datadog material: the two unauthenticated requests only crash a vulnerable replica set member if that member is later elected primary while still behind, authentication does not block the requests, and MongoDB found the flaw internally with no known exploitation at announcement. The article also notes the same release train shipped CVE-2026-120828 (8.7, far-future read-concern waiter memory exhaustion) and other fixes.
