The CVE record was published on 18 November 2026 at 02:01:45 UTC with ASUS as the assigning CNA and 0x0dee credited as the finder. It describes a hard-coded cryptographic key in ASUS Control Center Express that lets a remote user bypass authentication by forging a session token for an account with a recent valid login, giving administrative control of the management console and the clients it manages. The record scores CVSS 4.0 at 9.0 with vector AV:N/AC:L/AT:P/PR:L/UI:N/VC:H/VI:H/VA:H/SC:H/SI:H/SA:H, lists affected versions as through v1.7.27, and cites the ASUS security advisory as its only reference. CISA ADP enrichment attached to the record rates exploitation as none and technical impact as total.
