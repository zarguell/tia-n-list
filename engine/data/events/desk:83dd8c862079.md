MikroTik's September 3, 2026 bulletin ships fixes for the RouterOS vulnerabilities CERT.pl codenamed MikroTrick, naming CVE-2026-67276, CVE-2026-86060 and CVE-2026-67277, in releases 7.25beta3, 7.24.2, 7.23.4 and 6.49.21. The vendor instructs administrators to keep SSH closed to untrusted networks, prefer WireGuard for management access, and check the new Flagged status mechanism that scans startup configuration for signs of compromise. RouterOS devices showing Flagged status should be treated as taken over.
