Some downloads of the Rank Math PRO WordPress plugin from the vendor’s official website carried malware that planted hidden backdoors in a supply-chain attack last week. According to the vendor, a limited number of users who downloaded version 3.0.123 of the plugin between October 15 at 20:02 UTC and October 16 at 07:12 UTC received compromised versions that could compromise their websites upon activation. “The affected plugin files contained malware that could compromise your website if the plugin was activated,” Rank Math warned . “We immediately removed the affected files and released a clean version, Rank Math PRO v3.0.124.” Users who installed and activated the trojanized version during this 11-hour window should treat their websites as compromised and follow the clean-up procedures in the advisory from the vendor. Rank Math PRO is a WordPress search engine optimization (SEO) plugin that helps websites improve visibility in search results. It is the paid version of the free variant that is active on more than 4 million websites . An advisory from the vendor highlights that only the PRO version was compromised and the free version of Rank Math SEO from WordPress.org remained unaffected. Rank Math says that the malware establishes persistent access, communicates with ‘rank1cdn[.]com,’ and recreates hidden backdoors when someone visits the infected website.
