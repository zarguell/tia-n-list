Joint research by Cisco Talos and Recorded Future’s Insikt Group examined sustained cyber-espionage activity by TAG-193 targeting government organizations across Central Asia and the wider Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS). Tracked by Cisco Talos as UAT-6275 and also known as Silent Lynx, TAG-193 combines diplomatic and economic targeting with an interest in environmental policy, water resources, and forestry. Exposed infrastructure provided new visibility into its malware, operational practices, and evolving delivery techniques. The findings point to possible links to Kazakhstan, but questions remain about the group’s origins. This post highlights how TAG-193’s operations reflect the intelligence value of regional diplomacy and resource management, and how its expanding toolkit supports that mission. Government Targeting across the Region TAG-193’s targeting extends across government institutions responsible for diplomacy, economic development, agriculture, and environmental management. These organizations hold information that can inform negotiations, economic planning, and regional security decisions.
