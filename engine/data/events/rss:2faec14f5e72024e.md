Your board doesn't ask how many alerts your team closed. It wants to know what your security investments are worth in terms it already uses: revenue protected, losses avoided, and costs reduced. That question is hard to answer when attacks are faster and cheaper to scale, the attack surface reaches well past the perimeter, and teams are expected to do more without more headcount. We wanted real numbers on the value Recorded Future customers have experienced, so we partnered with UserEvidence to publish our 2027 ROI Report. It was built from a survey that asked 142 Recorded Future customers about the returns they're seeing. The average result is a 468% annual ROI.* Here's a first look at how they get there. Visibility comes first: 75% found risks they didn't know they had Security teams can't act on what they can't see. Many of the biggest blind spots sit outside the corporate network, where typosquatted domains, leaked credentials, and vendor exposure never trigger an internal alert. Of the customers surveyed, 75% gained visibility into previously unknown risks, the most commonly reported outcome. Among those who gained new visibility, 72% identified lookalike domains before they were weaponized, and 67% caught compromised credentials circulating on dark web markets.
