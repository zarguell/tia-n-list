Huntress told The Hacker News it observed in-the-wild use of BlueHammer, RedSun and UnDefend, three Microsoft Defender flaws released as zero-days by a researcher known as Chaotic Eclipse. BlueHammer, tracked as CVE-2026-33825, is a local privilege escalation flaw patched in the April 2026 Patch Tuesday update, while RedSun and UnDefend had no fix at publication and enable SYSTEM access and disruption of definition updates. Huntress said BlueHammer was weaponized from April 10, 2026 and proof of concept use of the other two appeared on April 16, after commands such as whoami /priv, cmdkey /list and net group.
