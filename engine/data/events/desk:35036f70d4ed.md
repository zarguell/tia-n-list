The Hacker News covered CISA adding CVE-2026-132165, also called Fail2Install and scored 9.8, to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with a 27 November 2026 deadline for federal agencies. It relayed Drupal's report of live-site exploitation as early as 2026-11-19 19:00 UTC and Searchlight Cyber's finding that resource exhaustion makes the installer reopen on an installed site and end in arbitrary PHP execution. The article lists fixed versions 10.6.22, 11.3.22 and 11.4.13.
