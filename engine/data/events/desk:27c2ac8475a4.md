TechMeasured traced the intrusion from a stolen version-control credential through automated workflows in three repositories to cloud infrastructure, citing Todoist’s November 24 incident report. It reported the roughly 6% legacy salted SHA1 hash exposure and the smaller API, Slack, task-content and Outlook groups, and noted Todoist found no application-code changes or sign-ins to the SHA1 accounts. The article advised affected users to reset disabled passwords, rotate API tokens and revoke credentials stored in Todoist content.
