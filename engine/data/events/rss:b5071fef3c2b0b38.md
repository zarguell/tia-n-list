The federal cybersecurity agency is giving U.S. government departments until Thanksgiving to patch a vulnerability impacting a critical email security platform that blocks spam, malware and other threats. All federal agencies were urged to patch CVE-2026-134624 – a bug affecting N-able’s Mail Assure platform. The vulnerability carries a 9.3 severity score and was added to the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency’s (CISA) exploited vulnerabilities platform on Monday evening. N-able has dealt with a week of outages tied to the vulnerability after hackers breached one of the company’s servers through the bug, causing damage to Mail Assure and SpamExperts services. In a statement to Recorded Future News, N-able said it remediated the bug over the weekend and restored access to internal dashboards for customers. Inbound and outbound email flow and spam filtering continued to operate throughout the incident, they added. “Since completing these remediation actions, we have continued scanning and monitoring the Mail Assure environments and have found no evidence of exploitation beyond the single N-able-hosted server identified in our initial investigation,” a spokesperson said, adding that they hired incident responders to validate their findings. The company pledged to publish a root cause analysis within the next two weeks.
