Cybersecurity startup Cylake has raised $245 million to accelerate the development of its sovereign security platform, bringing the company’s total funding to $290 million only six months after its launch. Cylake said the investment will be used to develop its platform and expand its team ahead of a beta release expected by the end of 2026. General availability remains scheduled for 2027. The company emerged from stealth in March 2026 with a $45 million seed investment led by Greylock Partners. Cylake has since grown to more than 40 employees and plans to continue hiring across engineering, product, and other functions. Cylake was co-founded by CEO Nir Zuk, chief development officer Wilson Xu, and chief architect Ehud “Udi” Shamir. Zuk founded Palo Alto Networks and served as its chief technology officer for more than two decades. Xu was an engineering executive at Palo Alto Networks, while Shamir co-founded SentinelOne and later worked at Palo Alto Networks as a distinguished software developer and security researcher. They are also joined by René Bonvanie, who previously served as CMO at Palo Alto Networks. The startup is building what it describes as a complete, AI-native cybersecurity platform for government agencies, highly regulated enterprises, and other organizations that cannot depend on public cloud infrastructure.
