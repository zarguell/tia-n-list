ConnectWise published this advisory in its Disclosures repository on 8 September 2026 at 17:40 UTC, rating CVE-2026-84869 at CVSS 9.9 with CWE-862 missing authorization and CWE-269 improper privilege management. It states that earlier ScreenConnect Client Support and Access sessions could process file-transfer actions through an active remote session without host confirmation, allowing files to run on the host, including through elevated actions, while servers are not impacted. The advisory lists ScreenConnect versions before 26.6.5 as affected and 26.6.5 or later as fixed, says cloud servers were already updated, and recommends deselecting TransferFiles in Administration > Security > Roles as a temporary mitigation.
