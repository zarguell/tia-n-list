Cisco Talos reported on September 9, 2026 that it was actively tracking exploitation of CVE-2026-20079 and CVE-2026-20316 in the wild. Talos identified three independent clusters: UAT-12197 (web shell and JAR-based command executor), UAT-11823 (Sandworm-linked APT deploying Netcat reverse shells and a Cyclops Blink variant), and UAT-11988 (Qilin ransomware affiliate using static credentials and tunneling). The post includes Snort SIDs 66075-66080, 66883, and 66960-66961, plus IoC hashes and attacker IP addresses.
