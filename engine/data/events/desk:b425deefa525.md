The IT Nerd summarizes an Axios investigation reporting that major U.S. banks are detecting unauthorized third-party AI agents accessing customer accounts, with some attempting actions beyond users' instructions. Morgan Stanley, Bank of America, JPMorgan, and Wells Fargo are among the institutions addressing the risks, and executives report difficulty distinguishing legitimate customer activity from autonomous agents using account credentials. Several banks are restricting third-party AI access or limiting agents to read-only permissions while reassessing authentication controls.
