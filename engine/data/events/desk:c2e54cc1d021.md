Recorded Future's Insikt Group reported on October 22, 2026 that reported cyberattacks against Japanese organizations rose 48 percent between Q2 and Q3 2026, from 621 to 918, according to its Intelligence Platform. Trend Micro similarly noted attacks against Japan from January through September 2026 exceeded the total for all of 2025, with 86 attacks reported by Japanese companies in September alone. The post details Q3 breaches including an October 7 ransomware incident at cloud provider IDCFrontier affecting 495 companies and local governments, and an October 13 misuse of a Sourcenext member information retrieval API that exposed 22,519,960 customer records. It attributes part of the exposure to legacy systems, noting over 60 percent of Japanese firms still use end-of-life software according to an April 2026 Money Forward article.
