Cybersecurity budget planning for 2027 presents a growing challenge: security leaders must address emerging threats, expanding infrastructure, and increasing SOC workloads while keeping costs under control. According to IANS and Artico Search , 55% of CISOs reported flat or declining security budgets in 2026. Meanwhile, PwC’s 2027 Global Digital Trust Insights found that 84% of surveyed security and finance leaders expect cybersecurity budgets to increase. For decision-makers, the priority is to determine which investments can reduce operational costs, improve threat detection, and help existing security teams handle growing demands.
