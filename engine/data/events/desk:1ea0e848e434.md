Around 700 French supermarkets were affected, with no group claiming responsibility. CERT-FR first warned on October 12 of Danfoss AK-SM and AK-SC compromises and added Copeland XWEB and Dixell XWEB systems a day later. Copeland acknowledged a vulnerability, while most affected Danfoss devices were end-of-life AK-SM 800 and AK-SC 355 units no longer patched.
