This post is the first analysis in a trilogy that I will be producing over the coming weeks and months. In it, I will conduct an in-depth examination of the infection chains and the Brazilian banking malware ecosystem , specifically Eternidade Stealer , Astaroth , and Grandoreiro , that were observed in operation during the same period ( the first or second half of 2026 ). The goal of this trilogy is to provide the community with a comprehensive technical knowledge regarding these threats, which continue to run active campaigns. We will analyze and learn from their methods of delivery, downloading, decoding, injection, and implementation, as well as the internal workings of the banking malware itself. Brazilian banking malware operates within an ecosystem that is unique and remarkably rich compared to the rest of the world, notably in terms of target selection, delivery methods, and the programming languages ​​employed. These threats are not directed at specific individual targets, rather, they are financially motivated, leveraging massive phishing campaigns.
