Silent Push warned that more than 100 high-value enterprises were being targeted through Okta SSO and other SSO platforms using live phishing panels. It attributed the infrastructure to SLSH, an alliance of Scattered Spider, LAPSUS$ and ShinyHunters, and described a human-led vishing operation that intercepts credentials and MFA tokens in real time.
