A technician being able to reach a refrigeration controller tells you something about connectivity. It tells you much less about whether the site can preserve cooling, detect unsafe settings or recover from a compromise. Following French supermarket disruptions, CERT-FR confirmed numerous controller compromises. The initial intrusion route remains unconfirmed. Its advice also captures the operational tension: remove public exposure, but check insurance conditions before interrupting data feeds to the refrigeration provider. That makes renewal a service test, not just a network review. Can an authorized technician complete a constrained, attributable task? Can the owner verify the resulting settings? Can cooling and alarm response continue when remote access is deliberately withdrawn under a safe test procedure? Protected access can support a sustainable service.
