Prediction market platform Polymarket temporarily suspended trading on Thursday after hackers compromised a third-party service to distribute cryptocurrency-draining JavaScript, with approximately $2.5 million in assets traced to a suspected attacker wallet. The company confirmed the security incident, saying it had identified and fixed the issue affecting its international platform, Polymarket.com, and referred the breach to law enforcement. "One of our third party providers was compromised, and we have referred this to relevant law enforcement," Polymarket said . "Anyone who has been impacted will be made whole. Out of an abundance of caution, we have temporarily halted trading and are working to bring the platform back online." Polymarket initially placed its exchange into cancel-only mode at 2:05 p.m. ET and paused relayer actions while investigating the incident. At 2:37 p.m., the company announced that the issue had been fixed and that trading was temporarily halted out of an abundance of caution. Trading resumed approximately one hour after the initial suspension, with the incident marked resolved at 3:05 p.m. ET. "Trading is now fully back online," the company announced on X . "We'll make this right and are fully reimbursing all affected traders." Polymarket has not disclosed how many users were affected, how much cryptocurrency was stolen, or how the reimbursement process will work.
