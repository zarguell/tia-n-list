Marca reported that LaLiga had disclosed unauthorized access to personal data of users registered in digital services run by the league and some clubs. The article places the intrusion between 14 and 16 November and says security teams detected the access source and contained it on 16 November, stopping further extraction and blocking the attacker. It adds that LaLiga opened an internal investigation into scope and user count, notified the AEPD, filed a complaint with the National Police Cybercrime Unit, and was coordinating with affected clubs.
