Node.js 26.13.1, dated 2026-11-11, is the security release for the 26.x line in the November 2026 batch. It carries the same HTTP/2 and TLS fixes and dependency updates to llhttp 9.4.4, undici, and sqlite 3.54.1. The advisory states that on 26.x and 24.x the HTTP/2 stream teardown response-memory leak CVE-2026-123866 can be driven purely by a remote peer once the window-size change from 26.9.0 and 24.22.0 is present.
