The FBI is reportedly investigating a data breach involving the National Crime Information Center, a database used by U.S. law enforcement agencies that contains information on criminals, fugitives and missing people. The FBI did not respond to requests for comment but sources told CNN and later the Associated Press that the cyberattack took place on Thursday. The database, known as the NCIC, is used among local, state and federal agencies to share information on criminals and investigations. It offers law enforcement agencies data on outstanding warrants and contains sensitive information of value to criminals and others. President Donald Trump told Fox News this weekend that he had been briefed on the incident, adding: “I will say that it’s not good — I don’t like that.” He went on to say the breach may allow the public to have access to investigative files related to him. If confirmed, this would be the second cybersecurity incident affecting the FBI this year. In September, the ShinyHunters hacking group breached the FBI’s job site and allegedly stole troves of information on every FBI employee. Multiple people were arrested as part of the investigation into the ShinyHunters breach, including a cybersecurity expert from Canada . The U.S. government has suffered from several cybersecurity incidents this year in addition to the attacks on the FBI.
