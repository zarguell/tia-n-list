Fox News cited three sources familiar with the matter saying hackers breached NCIC and that the FBI was investigating how they entered and whether information was compromised. Retired FBI supervisory special agent Jason Pack said roughly 56 state terminals feed hundreds of thousands of patrol car computers, which officers use for warrants, criminal histories, missing children, gang members and suspected terrorists. Pack said NCIC does not hold classified intelligence or FBI investigative case files but does store personally identifiable information including names, dates of birth and Social Security numbers. He said investigators would examine IP logs and metadata and look at compromised passwords, a state terminal or a third-party vendor; he said there was no indication yet that records were altered or the system was down.
