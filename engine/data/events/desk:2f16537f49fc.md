Cloudflare's advisory names dm-thin pools configured with skip_block_zeroing and 64 KiB blocks as the root cause, with Firecracker presenting container root disks as /dev/vdc, and reports the researchers recovered residual material on 18 of 24 placements and 20 of 22 underlying nodes across four continents, including 2,700 distinct foreign directory inodes identified through ext4 directory block checksums. Remediation removed skip_block_zeroing fleet-wide, retired running container disks, and cleared pre-mitigation cached image snapshots, with rollout complete September 7, 2026 and snapshot cleanup complete September 19, 2026, and a review of retained disk-I/O telemetry found no exploitation. The issue was reported through HackerOne report 3997565 on September 4, 2026.
