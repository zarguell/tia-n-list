F5 assigned and published CVE-2026-125274 on November 18, 2026, describing an authenticated iControl SOAP user with at least Guest access who can inject an unauthorized management request. The vendor impact text says this may let an attacker with network access to the iControl SOAP utility execute arbitrary system commands, create or delete files, or disable services, with no data plane exposure.
