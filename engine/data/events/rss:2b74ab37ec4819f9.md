CISA Cybersecurity Awareness Month: Aligning Organizational Defenses with CISA Critical Infrastructure Guidance Policy Context, Operational Imperative, and Security Culture Strategic Mandate and Threat Environment Securing enterprise information technology (IT) and operational technology (OT) systems SHALL serve as a fundamental strategic mandate across the organization, particularly as enterprise assets directly or indirectly interface with national critical infrastructure. When networks connected to critical infrastructure are compromised, systemic consequences extend far beyond enterprise operational downtime; such intrusions directly threaten public safety, compromise municipal utilities, and degrade essential service continuity. Safeguard standards MUST reflect the reality that enterprise networks do not exist in isolation, and maintaining continuous operational resilience IS MANDATED to uphold public trust and national security. The contemporary threat environment is defined by adversary capabilities that are rapidly becoming faster and more sophisticated. Specifically, adversary integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) accelerates the speed at which malicious actors automatically scan networks, discover software vulnerabilities, and execute zero-day exploits.
