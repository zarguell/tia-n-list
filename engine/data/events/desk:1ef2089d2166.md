New York Attorney General Letitia James warned New Yorkers affected by the PJ&A breach to monitor their credit and consider freezes or fraud alerts. The AG said the breach affected nearly nine million patients, including approximately four million New Yorkers in New York City and Syracuse, and that Northwell Health and Crouse Health were affected. The AG said PJ&A became aware of the breach in May 2023 and that the data included some Social Security numbers, insurance, and clinical information.
