Brandefense reports that Dell published CVE records on 28 October 2026 for critical flaws in PowerProtect Data Domain, its backup appliance line. Four of the seven flaws score CVSS 9.8 and require no login; fixed DD OS builds are 8.9.0.0, 7.13.1.90, 8.3.1.50 and 8.6.1.30. The advisory DSA-2026-416 initially released on 1 October 2026 and describes no exploitation, with none of the CVEs listed in the CISA KEV catalog as of 27 October.
