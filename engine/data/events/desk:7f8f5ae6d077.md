Ravie Lakshmanan reported the KEV additions for CVE-2025-6204 (CVSS 8.0) and CVE-2025-6205 (CVSS 9.1), both affecting DELMIA Apriso Release 2020 through Release 2025 and patched in early August, alongside XWiki CVE-2025-24893. The article notes the same product took a third KEV entry, CVE-2025-5086 at CVSS 9.0, on September 11, 2025, a week after the SANS Internet Storm Center detected in-the-wild exploitation attempts, and that the chaining research came from ProjectDiscovery's Rahul Maini, Harsh Jaiswal and Parth Malhotra.
