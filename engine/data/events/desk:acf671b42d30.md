Crypto.news reported that Trezor warned customers who bought devices from reseller CryptoBilis to move their funds after a suspected hardware implant was reported in a Safe 5 wallet purchased in August 2026. The implant was described as an extra circuit board between the main board and screen with a cellular module and antenna. CryptoBilis was removed as an authorized Trezor reseller on October 9 after Ledger confirmed tampered hardware wallets sold through the same reseller.
