Matt Palmer's disclosure statement documents the original researcher finding of systemic Row Level Security misconfiguration in Lovable-built apps backed by Supabase, first discovered March 20, 2025. A scan completed March 21, 2025 found 303 exposed endpoints across 170 of 1,645 showcase projects, about 10.3 percent, and the CVE was published May 29, 2025 after a 45-day window with a CVSS v3.1 vector of AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N. The statement also records public exploitation of the same flaw on April 14, 2025.
