Summary On November 24, 2026 , the ransomware group Pixelleaks claimed responsibility for a cyberattack against NKN Delhi PoP (National Knowledge Network / NIC / MeitY, India) ( nkn.gov.in ), a critical infrastructure organization in India. The attackers have threatened to publish sensitive business records unless their demands are met. Incident Report Field Details Target NKN Delhi PoP (National Knowledge Network / NIC / MeitY, India) Domain nkn.gov.in Country India Attacking Group Pixelleaks Date Reported November 24, 2026 Threat Actor Statement “National research-network infrastructure · India This disclosure concerns a verified set of records from an organization in the National research-network infrastructure sector. The material scheduled for publication comprises a set of business records, including financial and operational material. Representative records have been examined and verified as genuine. Publication would make the records publicly available and would carry regulatory, competitive, and reputational consequences for the organization. Once published, the complete data set can be examined, downloaded, and redistributed without restriction.” Recommended Security Actions Ransomware attack
