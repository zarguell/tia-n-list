HIPAA Journal reported that Northwell Health, New York's largest health system, was notified by PJ&A on July 21, 2023 about a cyberattack that ran from March 27 to May 2, 2023. Northwell initially drafted a statement saying 3,891,565 individuals were affected, then recalled it and said it could not confirm the exact number. PJ&A completed its initial investigation on September 28, 2023, and the exposed data included names, addresses, dates of birth, diagnoses, test results, and some Social Security numbers.
