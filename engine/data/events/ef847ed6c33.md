The Windows version of Hola Browser was compromised in a supply chain attack that delivered a Monero cryptocurrency miner (`me.exe`, later renamed `HolaMonitorService.exe`) to ~0.1% of users. Discovered during AppEsteem certification testing by Sophos and Sygnia. The miner adds a Windows Defender exclusion rule and creates an auto-starting service activated when the system is idle.

Hola confirmed the compromise, stating the distribution pipeline has been rebuilt with code-signing verification and tighter access controls. No evidence of user data access was found.
