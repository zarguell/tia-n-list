Cyberwarzone reported on January 4, 2026 that CVE-2025-68461 is a CVSS 7.2 XSS in Roundcube Webmail allowing account takeover via malicious SVG animate tags, with patches available in 1.5.12 and 1.6.12. The article notes deployment lags among hosting providers and bundled installations, and cites warnings from Poland's CERT and the Dutch NCSC about immediate exploitation risk. It also recalls Roundcube's history of weaponized XSS vulnerabilities targeting diplomatic and intelligence personnel.
