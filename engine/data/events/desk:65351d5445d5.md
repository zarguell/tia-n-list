Microsoft published this advisory on 21 October 2026, attributing the ConnectWise supply chain compromise to Storm-0940. It confirms the actor used ARTEX agentic AI to accelerate reconnaissance, password spraying, and OAuth application abuse, hitting over 300 accounts across eight organizations at over 2,900 attempts per minute. The post also notes the threat actor attempted sign-ins to more than 2,250 tenants following the unauthorized access.
