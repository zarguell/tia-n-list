NVD published the record for CVE-2026-92369, a TOCTOU race condition in the Windows installer rollback mechanism of TeamViewer Full Client and Host prior to 15.82, on Sep 29, 2026. A local low-privileged attacker who wins the race can replace rollback backup files in a user-writable temporary directory before an elevated installer restores them, escalating to NT AUTHORITY/SYSTEM, scored CVSS 3.1 7.3.
