BreachNews reported that the kernel CVE team announced the fix on 19 November, six days before OpenSec's write-up, and that OpenSec credits ANSSI researcher Jérémy Jean with sending the fix upstream on 17 August. It says Antara flagged the same reply-cache path on 21 September and OpenSec reported it to the nfsd maintainers on 22 September. The article states that no vendor reported in-the-wild exploitation as of 25 November and that the CVE was not in CISA KEV. It lists exposure checks using uname -r, lsmod | grep -w nfsd, /proc/fs/nfsd/versions and ss -ltn 'sport = :2049'.
