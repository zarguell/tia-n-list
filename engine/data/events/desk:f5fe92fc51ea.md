F6 said its cyber safety center investigated the first in-the-wild exploitation of CVE-2026-76466 against a company in Russia after a public proof of concept appeared. Attackers gained root on Expressway X15.5.3 devices, compromised two internet-facing appliances and used one to reach two internal cluster nodes, then used custom Python scripts for network scanning, SMB discovery, SQL Server and WinRM access, and GitLab data theft. F6 published indicators including a nodeup entry in erlang.log, staged script paths under /mnt/harddisk, two IP addresses, and advised limiting TCP ports 4371 and 4372 to trusted cluster hosts.
