CISA alert with release date September 11, 2026 adding JFrog Artifactory CVE-2026-42016 (incorrect authorization) and CVE-2026-42018 (improper authentication) and ConnectWise ScreenConnect CVE-2026-84869 (improper privilege management and missing authorization) to the KEV catalog, under BOD 26-04 remediation requirements.
