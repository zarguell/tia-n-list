On November 23, 2026, a statement claiming to come from ShinyHunters appeared on a new Tor leak site, announcing the extortion group’s return about two months after arrests in the Netherlands, Jordan and the United States. As proof of authenticity, the authors relied on two ShinyHunters PGP keys from 2020 that had been revoked in March . Separately, Okta reported that a voice phishing (vishing) cluster it previously linked to ShinyHunters is now posting its victims on a new leak site branded INCEPTION. How Did ShinyHunters Resurface? The comeback was staged on two channels at once: a PGP-signed post titled “The Darkest Day” from the group’s Exploit forum account, and the same signed notice on its new Tor site. Naming that account, held since 2020, as the only official outlet is a credibility play. Account age carries a history that a fresh handle cannot fake, and Exploit’s paid registration sets a higher bar than the open forums where impostors have been trading on the ShinyHunters name. The logic cuts both ways, though: control of an old account, like possession of an old PGP key, proves access, not that the same people are behind it .
