Texas-based dental services provider Access Dental Management (ADM) has disclosed a data breach impacting more than 340,000 individuals. Providing dental care across Texas and in Illinois, Indiana, and New Mexico, ADM says it discovered the incident on May 5 and immediately began an investigation with help from third-party forensic specialists. The investigation determined that, between May 1 and May 4, hackers had accessed its network and viewed or copied certain data, including personal information. On October 21, ADM’s review of the compromised information determined that the attacker accessed names, addresses, dates of birth, Social Security numbers, medical information, and health insurance information. “Upon learning of this incident, ADM moved quickly to investigate and respond to the incident. As part of its ongoing commitment to the security of information, ADM is reviewing and enhancing its existing policies and procedures related to data privacy to reduce the likelihood of a similar future incident,” ADM said in a notice (PDF). Last week, the organization notified the Texas and Vermont Attorney General’s Offices that 341,049 individuals were affected by the data breach (341,039 in Texas and 10 in Vermont), but the total number could reportedly surpass 455,000.
