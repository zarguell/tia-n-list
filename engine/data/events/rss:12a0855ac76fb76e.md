Australia’s cyber security authority responded to 1,524 cyber security incidents in the 2025-26 financial year, a 20% increase from the previous year and a 36% rise over five years. The latest Annual Cyber Threat Report shows that phishing, compromised accounts, unpatched systems, ransomware and data theft remain the main operational pressures. Artificial intelligence is increasing the speed and scale of many established techniques, but the report’s central message is practical: organisations need to patch faster, protect identities, improve logging, manage supplier access and prepare to continue operating during an incident. The Australian Signals Directorate’s Australian Cyber Security Centre also expanded proactive warning activity, issuing more than 4,400 notifications about potential malicious activity during the year.
