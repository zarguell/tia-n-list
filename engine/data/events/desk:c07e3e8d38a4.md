FinCEN issued an order under section 9714(a) of the Combating Russian Money Laundering Act cutting MEXC Global off from the US financial system. The order finds the Seychelles-based exchange facilitated at least $16 billion in illicit transactions tied to Iranian, North Korean, and Russian sanctions evasion and cybercrime between May 2021 and May 2026. FinCEN announced the action as part of Operation Economic Outcast.
