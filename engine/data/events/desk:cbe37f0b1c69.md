tbreak summarized Kaspersky's finding that two ransomware attacks on ESXi in October 2025 and Proxmox in August 2026 needed no VM escape. In the BlackNevas case, a monitoring tool with broad access and a root password reused across several nodes let the attacker into ESXi over SSH, and ESXi's execInstalledOnly control was disabled after root access. The article notes patching alone is insufficient and recommends least-privilege administrative access.
