BleepingComputer reported on November 24, 2025 that Harvard disclosed a breach of Alumni Affairs and Development systems caused by a phone-based phishing (vishing) attack discovered November 18, 2025. The article echoed Harvard's statement that the accessed systems do not generally contain Social Security numbers, passwords, payment card information, or financial account numbers. Harvard engaged third-party cybersecurity experts and law enforcement and launched a webpage with updates and an FAQ.
