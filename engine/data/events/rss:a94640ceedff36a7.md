Anthropic on Monday released Claude Mythos 5.2, the latest version of its access-restricted AI model, and Mozilla says it roughly doubled the vulnerability discovery results of Mythos 5.1 in Firefox. Mythos 5.2 and Claude Fable 5.2, which was released alongside it, share the same underlying model. The difference lies in the guardrails: Fable, which anyone can use, carries protections against cybersecurity and biology misuse, while Mythos ships without some of them. Direct access to Mythos is reserved for vetted organizations, which can apply through Anthropic’s Cyber and Life Sciences Verification Programs. “We’ve worked closely with the Anthropic team all year to harden Firefox with successive generations of Claude models, and each round has left fewer, subtler bugs to find. Even so, Mythos 5.2 roughly doubled the vulnerability discovery metrics of Mythos 5.1 across respective scanning campaigns,” said Brian Grinstead, distinguished engineer at Mozilla. Internally, Anthropic measures progress with CVE Rediscovery, which gives a model the code of a project from before a known CVE was patched and checks whether the model spots the flaw without help. In an October run covering 1,829 CVEs, Mythos 5.2 achieved a success rate of 89.6%. Mythos 5.1 scored 78.3%, and Opus 5.5, tested without safeguards, scored 81.3%.
