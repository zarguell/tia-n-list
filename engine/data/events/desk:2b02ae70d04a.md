Kyunghyang Shinmun reported Samsung Securities' customer apology stating that an unauthorized external party accessed the VOC intake service on the afternoon of 21 November and that some customer-uploaded attachments were leaked. It puts the victim count at 8,241 and lists 13 personal-data categories including names, account numbers, addresses, contact details and driver's license numbers, with the precise set varying by customer. The report says the company blocked the IP, suspended the service, checked other systems and reported the incident to regulators.
