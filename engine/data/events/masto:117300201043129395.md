Attackers compromised the official HBO Max Reddit account and used it for 48 hours to run a ClickFix malvertising campaign. The ads led to a fake HBO Max landing page at hbomaxx[.]us, which prompted users to copy and paste a terminal command. Payloads included macOS stealers targeting browser credentials, Apple Notes, and saved passwords; fake Ledger, Trezor, and Exodus wallet apps designed to capture recovery phrases; and a Windows stealer loaded directly into memory. Hudson Rock and ADAMnetworks traced the distribution to a system called "PasteSwitch" that selects payloads based on the visitor's platform. The compromised account posted 108 ads before Reddit administrators locked it; fewer than half were HBO Max lures, with 47 promoting AI coding tools and 15 advertising a macOS disk cleaner.
