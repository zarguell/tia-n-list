Euronews reported on November 4, 2026 that Crédit Mutuel Arkéa confirmed a cyberattack enabling fraudulent transactions while insisting customers would suffer no financial loss. The bank said it temporarily suspended certain transactions to block further fraudulent movements. According to Le Télégramme, $133 million (€118 million) was reportedly stolen, though the bank told Le Parisien the amount was still being assessed and some funds were frozen. The fraud was first made public by ZachXBT, an independent cryptocurrency investigator.
