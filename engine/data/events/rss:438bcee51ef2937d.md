The vulnerability stemmed from the way the platform structured shareable web links, specifically by placing conversation titles directly into subdomains rather than the URL path. This architecture allowed monitoring systems to record the addresses in public passive DNS databases , enabling outsiders to easily uncover thousands of confidential chats with a single query. Exposed records included sensitive corporate revenue reviews, loan applications, internal schedules, and personal notes. Consequently, Meta decided to temporarily suspend the sharing functionality to investigate the issue and implement necessary fixes. The rapid proliferation of enterprise autonomous artificial intelligence agents introduces severe, non-obvious vectors for corporate data exfiltration and intellectual property loss.
