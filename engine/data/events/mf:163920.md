CenterPoint Energy has disclosed a cybersecurity incident after an unauthorized third party allegedly obtained a dataset containing personal information belonging to a portion of its customers. The Houston-based utility giant said it learned of an online post in September 2026 in which a third party claimed to have the customer data. In a Form 8-K filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on September 14, CenterPoint confirmed that an unauthorized party accessed personal information through one of its external-facing systems. CenterPoint Energy Data Breach The company has not identified the specific exposed data elements, the number of affected customers, the compromised system, the initial access vector, or the timeframe of unauthorized access. Its investigation remains ongoing. The disclosure indicates that the incident involved an internet-accessible CenterPoint system rather than an outage affecting energy-delivery technology. External-facing environments can include customer portals, web applications , remote-access services, application programming interfaces, or systems operated by third-party vendors. CenterPoint said it took steps to further protect its systems after discovering the online post.
