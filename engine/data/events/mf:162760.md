Bottom line up front: if you have fewer than 500 users and already run a firewall with web filtering, Cisco Umbrella or Sophos will cover you at a fraction of the cost of a full SSE platform. If you have a distributed workforce and are consolidating security, Zscaler or Netskope are the platforms to shortlist. Everyone else lands somewhere between, and this playbook walks the decision in five stages rather than handing you a ranked list to guess from. A secure web gateway sits between your users and the internet, inspecting web traffic to block malware, enforce acceptable-use policy, prevent data loss, and stop connections to malicious sites for every user, wherever they work.
