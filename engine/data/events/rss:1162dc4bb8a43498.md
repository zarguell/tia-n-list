WhatsApp has patched a low-impact vulnerability in its Android apps that it believes a scammer exploited to make scam messages seem more credible. Tracked as CVE-2026-23871, WhatsApp’s security team found the flaw. It’s tied to Click to WhatsApp ads and affects both WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business for Android. “Incomplete validation of external ad-reply URLs in WhatsApp for Android and WhatsApp Business for Android before 2.26.17.4 could have caused a target’s device to reveal its IP information to an attacker if an attacker sent media from an attacker-controlled URL when a crafted Click to WhatsApp ad-reply message was rendered and displayed in a message thread,” WhatsApp explained in its advisory . The advisory also says the vulnerability “may have been abused in conjunction with specific spam messages.” Asked by SecurityWeek about the exploitation, a WhatsApp spokesperson said, “Our team found this issue while disrupting a scam campaign, and we believe a scammer was using it to make their scam message seem more credible.” WhatsApp also published advisories for two medium-impact vulnerabilities, saying it has seen no evidence that either was exploited. One of them, CVE-2026-102769, is an SQL injection bug affecting WhatsApp for Android versions prior to 2.26.35.74.
