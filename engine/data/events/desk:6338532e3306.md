CISA alerted administrators that Microsoft released KB5005413 on July 23, 2021 to address the PetitPotam NTLM relay attack against Active Directory Certificate Services. The advisory instructs customers to review KB5005413 and apply the necessary mitigations such as disabling NTLM on AD CS web enrollment endpoints and enabling Extended Protection for Authentication. The mitigations target relay of coerced machine-account authentication to certificate enrollment services.
