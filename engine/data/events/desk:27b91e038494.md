CNBC reported that the FAA halted departures nationwide for more than four hours on Nov. 13 after telecommunications routers were overloaded with data, with Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy saying they went down. More than 10,000 flights were disrupted; Cirium counted 11,000 scheduled departures after 4 p.m. ET, about 1.5 million passengers and roughly 600 diversions. Homeland Security officials said there was no indication of a cyberattack and that the failure appeared mechanically driven.
