Cyberattaque.org reported that Ipsos confirmed a security breach affecting one internal information system and said it had filed a complaint with authorities on Wednesday 11 November, five days before its public statement. The company told the outlet that the investigation was still determining whether information about clients, projects, or business partners had been affected, and that it had not disclosed the intrusion date, discovery date, attack method, or data extraction. The report notes that Ipsos employs nearly 20,000 people in 90 markets and serves about 5,000 clients, but stresses that no theft of survey responses, personal data, or confidential study results has been confirmed.
