Helixar argues that a valid login does not prove an AI agent's authority, after Axios reported unvetted agents accessing U.S. bank accounts on October 22. The report describes banks restricting third-party agents and activity that appeared to depart from customers' instructions, while relying on anonymous sources and naming no agent products. Morgan Stanley told Axios it had not observed rogue AI agents accessing its platforms, and a Chase spokesperson warned customers not to share usernames and passwords.
