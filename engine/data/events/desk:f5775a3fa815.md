OpenClassActions reported on November 15, 2026 that card numbers, CVVs, expiration dates, billing ZIP codes and email addresses entered at Fandango's checkout between October 8 and 11 may have been accessed. It noted that Fandango filed the notice with the California Attorney General on November 13 and offered 24 months of IDX monitoring with a February 13, 2027 activation deadline. The site said it had identified no class action over the breach as of November 15.
