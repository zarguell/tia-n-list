ThreatAft published a consolidated analysis of 17 CVEs across 12 AI agent and LLM orchestration products disclosed on October 19-20, 2026. Six carry CVSS 9.8 unauthenticated remote code execution in Piflow, Langchain-Chatchat, DB-GPT, Letta, and Cognition, and five more at CVSS 8.8 require only an account. The article attributes the cluster to three recurring design failures: authentication defaulting to off, unsandboxed tool execution, and approval gates that fail open or are absent.
