TOP ATTACKS AND BREACHES The FBI has reportedly experienced a second cyber incident in recent months, this time targeting its National Crime Information Center (NCIC). The database shares records on fugitives, warrants, criminal histories, and missing persons among US law enforcement agencies. The attackers’ identities, entry method, and potential data exposure were not disclosed by the FBI. Japan’s RIKEN research institute, which operates one the world’s strongest supercomputers ‘Fugaku’, suspended operations after attackers breached its operations network and tampered with servers between November 10 and 13. The intrusion potentially exposed account information belonging to 4,597 users, including credentials that could provide access to external systems. The European Commission, the EU’s executive body, has suffered a cyberattack that disrupted internal systems for more than ten days.
