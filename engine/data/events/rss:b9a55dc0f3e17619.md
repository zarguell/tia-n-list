Killing the process does not clean a compromised switch or firewall when a scheduled job reinstalls the malware. Find and remove scheduled tasks before you call the device clean. On August 13, 2026, FortiGuard Labs, Fortinet’s threat research team, published an analysis of Evooo1Bot , a Linux botnet that targets internet-facing routers, firewalls, IP cameras, PBX phone systems, and NAS devices. A single bot command, !persist , installs several persistence mechanisms at once. One of them is a cron entry that runs every five minutes, downloads the malware again with wget or curl , and pipes it straight into a shell. If a responder kills the bot process and deletes the binary, the device reinfects itself on the next five-minute mark. That pattern is not unique to one botnet. In February 2026, Flare’s research team described SSHStalker , which installs a cron job that runs every minute and relaunches the kit if its process is gone.
