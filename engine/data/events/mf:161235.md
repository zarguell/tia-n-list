Arctic Wolf, a global leader in security operations, has announced the winners of the "2026 Arctic Wolf Partner of the Year Awards." The awards recognize top partners demonstrating the most notable achievements in strategic alignment, customer outcomes, and market leadership. Partners recognized for their contributions to expanding managed EDR and Incident360 Retainer adoption in the Japanese market include:

2026 Arctic Wolf Partner of the Year Awards - Japan Outstanding Partner: M-OTechs Co., Ltd.
2026 Arctic Wolf Partner of the Year Awards - Japan Excellence Support Partner: Hitachi Solutions, Ltd.
2026 Arctic Wolf Partner of the Year Awards - Japan Excellence Marketing Partner: Tecmatrix Co., Ltd.

"The recognized partners are vital members of the Arctic Wolf ecosystem," said Will Briggs, Senior Vice President of Global Channel at Arctic Wolf. "They share our commitment to delivering trusted security outcomes for customers. Together, we are helping organizations defend at machine speed while ensuring AI is verified in real-world conditions, governed, and effectively applied."

As organizations worldwide face accelerating threats and a chronic cybersecurity talent shortage, over 10,000 organizations globally rely on Arctic Wolf to reduce cyber risk. Arctic Wolf's solutions are powered by the Aurora Superintelligence Platform, built for the AI era. The platform combines agentic AI, security expertise, and rigorous validation to deliver trusted outcomes across security operations. Each week, the platform processes over 9 billion security events, transforming massive telemetry data into prioritized, actionable outcomes that help organizations respond faster and operate with greater confidence.
