Sina Kheirkhah at watchTowr Labs published a full pre-auth exploitation writeup on 14 Aug 2026, thirteen days before this story's first sighting. The research demonstrates unauthenticated remote code execution as root via a heap overflow in NetScaler's SAML ds:SignedInfo canonicalization, where an oversized InclusiveNamespaces PrefixList is copied into a fixed-size buffer in the nsppe packet engine, yielding a write-what-where primitive, RIP control by overwriting tx_pkt_complete_fptr, and a PHP webshell dropped at /var/vpn/theme/x.php on 13.1 before 13.1-63.18. The author notes Citrix did not confirm the CVE mapping but believes the bug is CVE-2026-8452 based on its memory-overflow description, and credits bulletin CTX696604 with the fix.
