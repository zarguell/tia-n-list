On 2 November 2026, the supermarket chains ICA and Coop confirmed that an incident affected refrigeration and freezer systems at a limited number of Swedish stores during the night. ICA Kvantum stores in Sjöbo and Tomelilla and ICA Supermarket Baronen in Stockholm were reported as affected, and Coop reporting that six of their stores were affected. The ICA Sjöbo store stated that its refrigeration systems had been hacked and that freezer temperatures were set to 50 degrees Celsius, requiring stock to be discarded and the store to close temporarily while systems were restored. Neither Coop or ICA have publicly confirmed the technical entry point, affected controller brands, or whether the activity was coordinated beyond the reported stores. [1] [2] The Swedish incident follows a destructive cyberattack against supermarket refrigeration systems in France on 11 and 12 October. Attackers remotely manipulated refrigeration controls, causing units at hundreds of stores to heat up and forcing retailers to discard food.
