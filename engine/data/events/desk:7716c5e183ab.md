Mandiant and Google Threat Intelligence Group reported renewed mass exploitation of CVE-2026-35273 by UNC6240 (ShinyHunters), after the June zero-day window of May 27 to June 9, 2026 that hit higher education hardest. The actor bypassed WAF rules blocking the Environment Management Hub endpoint by URL-encoding a single character, requesting /%50SEMHUB/ instead of /PSEMHUB/, and the renewed wave spans higher education, technology, IT services, healthcare, agriculture, transportation, and government.
