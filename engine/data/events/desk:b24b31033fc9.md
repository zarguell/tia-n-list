Fireworks published its first incident bulletin after detecting unauthorized use of internal Fireworks credentials on October 6, 2026 at 9:00 AM PDT. The unauthorized third party accessed environment variables used in evaluation jobs in a limited number of customer accounts, capturing API keys and tokens stored as secrets. The company revoked internal credentials, rotated employee API keys on October 9, notified affected customers, and revoked additional user API keys and rotated service-account tokens on October 10. At publication the company said it had found no evidence of access to customer models, datasets, training data, inference traffic, or personal information within its security perimeter.
