The Justice Department on Thursday announced coordinated actions against North Korean groups’ campaigns that have stolen billions of dollars in cryptocurrency, seizing cloud infrastructure behind a social engineering scheme known as FaceStealer and the court-authorized seizure of more than $1.2 billion stolen from cryptocurrency exchange Binance. The larger of the two operations is tied to the theft of more than $6 billion in virtual assets from Binance, which the FBI has attributed to North Korean military hackers it calls TraderTraitor. Investigators working with private virtual asset service providers have frozen more than $2.7 billion of the stolen funds, and courts have authorized the seizure of more than $1.2 billion of that amount.
