CERT Polska's technical analysis names the chain MikroTrick: CVE-2026-67279, an SSH state-machine flaw, chained with CVE-2026-86060, argument injection in /nova/bin/login, yields full unauthenticated administrative control. It dates attack telemetry to forum logs of Sep 2, one day before the Sep 3 patches, and lists indicators: failed SSH logins for user -2, an ops account in the full-privilege group, and IPs 82.192.72.4 and 103.102.31.18. It also separates CVE-2026-67276, a distinct RSA-key-forgery flaw, from the chain.
