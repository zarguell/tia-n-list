Malbear Labs published an analysis of Operation Snowball, an autonomous AI intrusion campaign against a European telecommunications provider and thirteen organizations hosted on its infrastructure. The report is based on more than seventy session transcripts recovered from an exposed open directory and describes a jailbroken DeepSeek Flash agent running for over two weeks under minimal operator direction. It details the harness architecture, CVE arsenal, command-and-control infrastructure, and adaptive techniques used against endpoint and network defenses.
