Brandefense published a summary on November 24, 2026 noting that Socket had reported 13 linked packages and that NuGet had removed 12 while WinUI.Button 1.0.0 remained listed at publication. It frames the risk as code executing during a build or when an application creates a UI control, and maps the behavior to MSBuild execution, malicious library execution and DLL side-loading techniques. It cautions that restoring a package alone is not proof of compromise, that the side-load was not tested end to end, and that no victim count was provided.
