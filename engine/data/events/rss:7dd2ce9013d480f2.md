IntroductionZscaler ThreatLabz analyzed a recently active exploitation campaign in which a threat actor abused ARTEX to target South Korean financial institutions. ARTEX is an open source agentic penetration testing framework developed in China that uses large language models (LLMs) to automate reconnaissance and exploitation tasks. First observed in late September 2026, the campaign affected multiple South Korean financial firms and resulted in the exfiltration of personally identifiable information (PII) belonging to approximately 67,000 individuals.ThreatLabz assesses with moderate confidence that the campaign is the work of a financially motivated, Chinese-speaking threat actor. The threat actor exploited peripheral internet-facing web applications, including customer-facing inquiry portals and employee-facing mobile application backends.
