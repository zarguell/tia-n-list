Google announced that Gemini 4 Carbon is now available to developers, enterprises, scientists, and consumers via the Gemini API and Gemini for business, with Google AI Ultra subscribers also receiving access. Introductory pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens applies until November 30, 2026, after which prices double to $4 and $20. Google disclosed that its Cloud Vulnerability Research team used Carbon to prove two bugs in a popular open-source project were exploitable, with severity comparable to PHP2Shell, and that Wiz found zero-day vulnerabilities at two major SaaS providers in under two hours using Carbon for cyber. Fairwind membership remains required for Carbon for cyber.
