The vendor advisory predates all press coverage and carries the authoritative record: REST cookie parsing turns `authenticationToken=j:%7B%7D` into an empty object, the XO token provider treats that object as an unfiltered lookup and returns the first token in the collection, and the caller is authenticated as that token's owner if it has not expired. Vates classifies the impact as Critical, states the flaw is actively exploited from 91.211.251.112:8001 and a Tor service, and lists Xen Orchestra before 6.9.2 (stable) and 6.10.1 (latest) plus the Xen Orchestra Appliance as affected. The advisory also publishes detection patterns, the campaign IP list, WAF rules for the empty-token cookie, and the fixed xo-server packages 5.213.1 (latest channel) and 5.211.2 (stable channel), and credits Filip Magic (@mad31k) and Christoffer Olsen of TDC NET Security.
