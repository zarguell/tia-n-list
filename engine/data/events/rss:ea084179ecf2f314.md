DreamHost has implemented forced password resets for certain email and FTP/SFTP accounts following an incident that affected its File Manager and webmail services. The company restored its browser-based File Manager on October 26, 2026. However, users affected by the incident must obtain new login credentials from their account owners before they can access their accounts.
