Why open-source patches often don't reach banks: many financial institutions struggle to detect which of their systems use affected open-source components and to prioritize vendor patches, so fixed vulnerabilities stay open in production long after upstream fixes ship. The piece describes the OSERA Risk Navigator, a tool that helps financial institutions find, prioritize, and close security vulnerabilities in their open-source dependencies.
