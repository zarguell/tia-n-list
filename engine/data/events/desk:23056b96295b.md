Wiz Research and other teams independently found the nginx HTTP/2 flaws and reported them to F5, with Wiz reporting the use-after-free on October 21, 2026 and receiving a patch for review on November 10. Wiz confirmed remote code execution with an ASLR bypass on official NGINX 1.31.7 and 1.31.8 builds and said it would not release its proof-of-concept exploits. The post notes that CVE-2026-123633 requires a reverse-proxy configuration and low connection slots, and that CDNs or WAFs terminating HTTP/2 may change reachability.
