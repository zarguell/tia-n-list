Park24's second report, published September 28, 2026 at 13:30 JST per the company RSS feed, roughly 16 hours before the BleepingComputer story. It confirms data stored on the breached system was obtained by a third party for about 6.6 million accounts, a figure that includes current and former Times Car members, Times Business Service corporate members, and people whose enrollment never completed. Passwords were stored in a form the company says cannot be restored, credit card data was not leaked, and the linked service IDs cover nine services including JR West's WESTER ID. Park24 reported to the Personal Information Protection Commission and police, is running forensics with an external specialist, and said individual notifications would follow.
