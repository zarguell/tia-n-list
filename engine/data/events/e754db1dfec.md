The coordinated international takedown of "First VPN" — a bulletproof VPN service deeply embedded in the ransomware ecosystem — involved French and Dutch authorities, Eurojust, and Europol seizing over 33 servers across 27 countries on May 19–20. Operation Saffron (Bitdefender's codename) also included a suspect interview in Ukraine. Critically, investigators obtained live traffic data from users who believed their activities were anonymous, yielding 83 intelligence packages covering 506 identified users — signaling a wave of follow-up prosecutions. The service appeared in nearly every major Europol cybercrime investigation.
