This CPU contains fixes for 1209 unique CVEs in 1295 security updates across 33 Oracle product families. Out of the 1295 security updates published this quarter, 8.6% of patches were assigned a critical severity. Medium severity patches accounted for the bulk of security patches at 47.3%, followed by high severity patches at 36.7%. This quarter's update includes 112 critical patches across 95 CVEs.
