The Hacker News covered Zscaler's March 2025 CoffeeLoader disclosure, describing a GPU-based Armoury packer, UAC bypass, elevated DLL execution and scheduled-task persistence every 10 minutes or at logon. The loader contacts C2 over HTTPS and can inject Rhadamanthys shellcode, with source-level similarities to SmokeLoader. The article dates CoffeeLoader's origin to around September 2024.
