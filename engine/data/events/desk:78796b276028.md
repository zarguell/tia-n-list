Wiz Research tracked an ARTEX cluster it calls ARTEX-Harvester, tying it at varying confidence to activity at more than a dozen organizations between late October and mid-November 2026, with more than 220 proxy IPs used against a single target. It observed self-labeling artifacts including pentest-recon user agents, pentest workspaces and pods, and incrementing pentest-iNNNNNN task counters. Wiz also reported that ARTEX consoles shipped exposed to the internet by default, often with default credentials, and that leaked operator logs can pair victim telemetry with the attacker planner intents and transcripts.
