ESET documents Webworm's 2025 activity and says the group began moving from traditional backdoors to legitimate or semi-legitimate tools such as SoftEther VPN in 2024. Decrypted Discord messages led ESET to an attacker-controlled GitHub repository that staged SoftEther, and a configuration file there contained an IP address matching known Webworm infrastructure. The research reports government victims in Belgium, Italy, Serbia and Poland, a South African university, over 400 decrypted Discord messages, reconnaissance against more than 50 targets, and a Serbian webmail target linked to a proof-of-concept exploit for CVE-2017-7692.
