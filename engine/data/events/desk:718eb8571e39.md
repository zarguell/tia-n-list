President Donald Trump nominated Palantir chief information security officer Michael LoSapio to lead CISA on Monday, selecting him to head an agency that has been without a Senate-confirmed leader since Trump returned to the Oval Office. CISA lost more than 1,000 personnel, one-third of its workforce, and saw its budget slashed by billions during the second Trump administration, and it remained largely behind the scenes ahead of last week's midterm elections. Nick Andersen has led the agency in an acting capacity since February after Madhu Gottumukkala was reassigned, and Sean Plankey withdrew earlier this year after months in limbo. It is unclear whether the Senate will take up the nomination during the lame-duck session, and if lawmakers adjourn without approving it Trump would have to renominate LoSapio for an incoming Democratic majority.
