First coverage of the AT&T disclosure: in an SEC filing the same day, AT&T said call and text metadata for roughly 110 million people was accessed in April 2024 from an AT&T workspace on a third-party cloud platform (Snowflake) protected by username and password only, with no MFA. The records covered May 1 to Oct. 31, 2022 and Jan. 2, 2023, and a subset included cell-tower identifiers that could approximate device location. AT&T learned of the breach April 19, 2024 and delayed disclosure at the FBI's request; TechCrunch tied the theft to a breach affecting more than 160 Snowflake customers.
