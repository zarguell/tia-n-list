A DEV Community analysis of OpenAI's November 4, 2026 research post summarized the funnel from 2,308 candidate findings to 948 working local privilege escalation exploits with candidate patches. It noted that 61 LPEs had been patched upstream, 43 had staging patches, and 299 were in discussion ten days after the scan, and compared the run's cost with historical bug bounties of $20,000 to $70,000 per Linux kernel LPE exploit.
