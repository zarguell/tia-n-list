TestingCatalog spotted a hidden "Additional sandbox options" setting under the computer use section of the Gemini desktop app, suggesting Gemini could operate outside selected folders, access the internet, and work across other apps without requesting approval at every step, while purchases and legal documents would still require human confirmation. The piece adds context that Google introduced Gemini 4 Argon on September 30, initially to selected cybersecurity experts through the Fairwind program, and is evaluating it for computer use on benchmarks including Agent's Last Exam and OSWorld 2.0. Google has not confirmed a connection between Argon and these settings.
