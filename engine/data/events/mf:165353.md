A Russian-language proxy network operating through dozens of Chrome extensions has exposed an estimated 356,000 users to browser-traffic interception. The campaign involves 31 linked extensions marketed as free “VPN for X” tools that can reroute web sessions through remotely controlled proxy infrastructure . Risky Plugins disclosed the activity on September 19, 2026, after analyzing the extensions’ code and remote configuration between September 3 and 4. The extension cluster remained active when researchers last scanned it on September 18. Fake Chrome VPN Extensions Hijack Browser Traffic The extensions were published through three reportedly linked Google accounts: sherechevskiy@gmail.com , andreysherechevskiy@gmail.com , and katia.noomova@gmail.com . Although promoted under different names, the tools share a common codebase and use a similar lure: promising access to a single blocked service. The catalog includes extensions presented as VPN services for YouTube, Telegram, Instagram, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Netflix, Discord, Spotify, LinkedIn, Facebook, Threads, X, and the Russian torrent tracker RuTracker. The largest sample, “РуТрекер VPN расширение для доступа к сайту,” accounts for roughly 200,000 of the group’s estimated 356,000 installs.
