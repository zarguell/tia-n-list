OffSeq Threat Radar records CVE-2026-115915 as a high-severity input-validation flaw in vLLM prior to 0.32.0. The advisory states that ImageMediaIO passes attacker-controlled image bytes through Pillow's Image.open without restricting decoder selection, allowing EPS payload dispatch. It also links the issue to Ghostscript CVE-2024-29510 for RCE and Pillow CVE-2026-59203 for DoS.
