Polymarket's status page posted an incident at 18:05 UTC on October 29, 2026, reporting the predictions trading API in major outage and the exchange in cancel-only mode with relayer actions paused. Subsequent updates stated the issue affecting Polymarket.com was identified and fixed, that a third-party provider was compromised and referred to law enforcement, that affected users would be made whole, and that trading resumed around 19:05 UTC.
