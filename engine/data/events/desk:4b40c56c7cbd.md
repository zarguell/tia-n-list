On October 27, 2026, the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Eastern District of Virginia and the FBI Washington Field Office announced the court-authorized seizure of virtualine.net. The site was used for prolific criminal activity, including advertising sexually explicit images of minors, ransomware, and cryptocurrency laundering. Authorities said Virtualine knowingly hosted a forum distributing CSAM and ignored repeated notices from NCMEC, law enforcement, and a victim's attorney.
