ExaTrack discovered Mélofée, a previously undocumented Linux implant and kernel rootkit family targeting Linux servers, linked with high confidence to Chinese state-sponsored APT groups. Check Point Research later connected Melofee to CopperDragon's SparrowShell through shared Reptile rootkit code and reused self-signed certificates. This establishes the Linux side of the framework years before the CopperDragon report.
