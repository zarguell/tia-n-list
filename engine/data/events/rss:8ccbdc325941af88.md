n0n is a data-exfiltration and destructive-extortion group that surfaced on the Dark Web in September 2026 and scaled to 93 listed victims within roughly five weeks. Its operators exfiltrate data, encrypt or destroy infrastructure, and then work victims through keyed negotiation rooms, per-victim Bitcoin wallets and a public leak site branded with the slogan “n0n | no js | no mercy”. In September 2026, the SOCRadar Threat Research Unit (STRU) found an exposed staging server holding the live workspace of an n0n operator. The directory exposed the operation end to end: credential stuffing against FortiGate SSL-VPN appliances, hijacked firewall management centers, mass S3 deletion, a custom C++ locker, and an AI coding agent backed by more than $7,000 in paid LLM API usage. The findings tie the workspace to at least 23 organizations across 14 countries. This profile is based on the findings of our prior research into n0n Ransomware.
