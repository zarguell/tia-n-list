Breakglass Intelligence described a Node.js stage-one dropper attributed with high confidence to Lazarus Group's TraderTraitor cluster that resolved C2 addresses from memos posted to an attacker-controlled Solana wallet. The report counted 51 memo transactions over roughly four months and seven Vultr VPS nodes in France, with activity dating back to November 2025. It noted that the dead drop leaves no domain to sinkhole and that the C2 IP addresses had no detections in ThreatFox, MalwareBazaar or VirusTotal at the time of analysis. This established the Solana memo dead-drop technique months before the Overlord RAT variant using it was reported.
