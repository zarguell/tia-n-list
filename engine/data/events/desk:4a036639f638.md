Apple began sending a new round of threat notifications on Friday, October 30, 2026 to targeted iPhone users in 99 countries, the second wave in eleven weeks. The 99-country figure and the over-150 total come from the notification text posted by Costin Raiu, not from a company announcement. Apple has never disclosed how many people receive the alerts and does not attribute them to specific attackers or regions. Genuine Apple threat notifications never ask recipients to click a link, open a file, install an app or profile, or provide a password or verification code.
