Austria's Foreign Ministry said late last week that hackers breached one of its websites and accessed the names and official contact information of employees at its Vienna headquarters and at Austria’s diplomatic missions abroad. The breach occurred between November 12 and 13 and affected a website operated by an external service provider, according to a ministry statement reported by public broadcaster ORF on Friday. The ministry said the breach had been contained and that no personal information belonging to members of the public was compromised. The hackers have not been identified, and it remains unclear how they gained access to the website or whether they stole any information. The ministry said it was taking the incident "very seriously" and had notified Austria's cybersecurity and data protection authorities. A spokesperson told Recorded Future News: “For security reasons, we do not disclose details of specific technical vulnerabilities or security measures. The incident has not been attributed to any particular actor.” The attack coincided with a global Microsoft service outage that disrupted several of the company's services for approximately 24 hours. Microsoft attributed the disruption to a software bug and infrastructure failures. Austrian authorities have not established whether the two incidents were connected.
