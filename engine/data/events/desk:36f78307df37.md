The Hacker News reports on November 9, 2026 that ForgeRock is now part of Ping Identity and that the failure was deployment hygiene rather than a product bug, so there is no CVE and no vendor advisory. The Felix console at `/system/console` still used the documented `admin:admin` default, and the built-in scheduler ran scripts for code execution on the identity servers. The researchers tested only on cars they owned or had consent to use and said they could open a session for any customer account.
