Hackers have disrupted refrigeration systems at several ICA and Coop supermarkets across Sweden, destroying food and prompting temporary closures. One store manager reported finding a freezer at 55°C, which highlights the physical consequences of attacks on connected retail equipment. The disruption follows attacks against French supermarket refrigeration systems, which similarly damaged chilled and frozen stock.
