Dan Goodin's first-person account: a high-resolution scan of his license appeared on Nexus within hours of an employee scanning it at a car rental counter, implying near real-time access to the scanning vendor's data. Each record carried six image files including infrared and ultraviolet captures, which could let cloned counterfeit IDs pass hologram tests. Nexus went dark within hours of the KrebsOnSecurity scoop, removing the only way for people to check whether their IDs were included.
