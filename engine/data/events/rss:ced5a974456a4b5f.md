A portion of ConnectWise’s customers have been adrift for days, and hundreds are compromised, as the company’s efforts to restore its Cloud Backup service remain underway with no confirmed timeline for when customers can regain access. The company temporarily shut down multiple services Saturday after a threat actor gained access to a highly sensitive credential that allowed its customers to connect the vendor’s application with Microsoft 365 environments. The threat actor, which hasn’t been identified, used the application programming interface (API) secret to compromise a series of ConnectWise Cloud Backup customers’ Microsoft 365 applications, including Exchange Online, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint Online, Cloud Backup, MS Graph and Partner Center, according to Huntress. Researchers observed an initial wave of attacks Friday, and by late Saturday Huntress “had seen sign-in activity from the malicious infrastructure across almost 1,000 Entra tenants, with more than 300 of those sign-ins appearing to succeed,” Jonathan Semon, principal security operations analyst at Huntress, told CyberScoop. The cybersecurity company, which primarily supports small- to mid-sized businesses, confirmed unauthorized sign-ins in 162 tenants and said additional compromises beyond its telemetry are likely.
