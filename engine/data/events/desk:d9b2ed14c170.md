Arista published Security Advisory 0199 disclosing a SQL injection issue in VeloCloud Orchestrator (VCO) on-prem tracked as CVE-2026-114625, rated CVSSv3.1 9.9 critical. The flaw may allow a remote attacker to access privileged internal functionality and impact the VCO host, with successful exploitation compromising confidentiality, integrity, and availability. The advisory is the vendor's own disclosure and predates the story's first observation.
