The FBI issued FLASH-20261113-001, titled Threat Overview of ARTEX Autonomous Penetration Testing Framework, on 13 November 2026 and marked it TLP:CLEAR. The advisory highlights tactics, techniques and procedures and indicators of compromise associated with ARTEX, the autonomous penetration testing framework the FBI says multiple actors have abused since at least August 2026. It predates the first report of Barracuda as a victim by three days.
