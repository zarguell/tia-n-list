The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) offers two free assessment tools, ScubaGear and ScubaGoggles, to help organizations identify insecure configurations in Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace. Developed under the Secure Cloud Business Applications (SCuBA) project, these tools compare cloud tenant settings against established security baselines and generate reports that highlight configuration gaps.
