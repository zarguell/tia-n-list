Bottom line up front: SDP is how you retire your VPN. Instead of putting a device on your network and hoping nothing goes wrong, it authenticates the user and device first, then builds a one-to-one encrypted connection to a single application leaving everything else invisible. Small teams should start with Twingate or GoodAccess , mid-market with Check Point Harmony SASE , and large enterprises with Zscaler using zero-trust access . This playbook covers how to choose and, more importantly, how to migrate without breaking your business. A software-defined perimeter hides infrastructure behind an authentication layer. An unauthorized user scanning your address space finds nothing to attack, because nothing responds until identity is proven. Stage 1 — Confirm SDP Is What You Need SDP, ZTNA, and zero-trust access are used almost interchangeably by vendors. What matters is whether the model fits your problem.
