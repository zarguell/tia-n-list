North Korea-linked hackers are targeting people and organizations focused on Ukraine with fake PDF documents that install a PowerShell malware tool called VelvetCake. SOCRadar’s Threat Research Unit named the campaign Operation Conflict Compass and assessed with moderate confidence that Konni, also tracked as TA406 and Opal Sleet, is responsible. Activity was observed from early August 2026. Researchers believe the attackers want intelligence on the direction of Russia’s war in Ukraine. Konni Targets Ukraine With VelvetCake The likely entry point is a spear-phishing email with a ZIP attachment. Inside is a Windows shortcut, or LNK file, made to look like a PDF. The document themes include Russia-Ukraine peace plans, a Ukrainian social researcher’s résumé, and the possible effect of the Strait of Hormuz situation on global food prices. Those subjects suggest the attackers may be seeking access to diplomatic groups, think tanks, researchers, and nongovernmental organizations; they do not establish a confirmed victim list. Konni Profile on SOCRadar Platform, Threat Actor Intelligence (Source: socradar) When a target opens the shortcut, it runs a command that retrieves malicious files staged on GitHub . One is a VBScript that creates a scheduled task named OneDriveUpdateScheduler . The task launches a PowerShell payload every minute, allowing the attackers to keep checking for new work.
