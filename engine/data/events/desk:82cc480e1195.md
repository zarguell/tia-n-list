MetaMask's public statement carries a September 30, 2026 update disclosing an ongoing security incident affecting part of its infrastructure, addressed internally with external partners and security advisors, and an October 1 update confirming it worked with partners to take the precautionary step of exiting affected validators. The company states there is no indication that MetaMask wallets or customer funds have been affected, that containment and verification continue, and warns users never to share their Secret Recovery Phrase or private keys.
