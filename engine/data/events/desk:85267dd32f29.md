Mitel issued MISA-2026-0013 on 2026-11-18, one day before the queued story appeared. The advisory lists three Platform component flaws in OpenScape 4000 and OpenScape 4000 Manager: MTLVULN-1785, unauthenticated access at CVSS 3.1 10.0; MTLVULN-1786, unauthenticated command injection at 10.0; and MTLVULN-1793, adjacent-network command injection at 9.6. Affected builds include V11 R0.22 through V11 R2.11 and listed V10 R1 releases, with V11 R2.14, PLT HF5 for V11 R1.26, and patches for older releases as fixes. Mitel recommends Security Mode restricted access as a workaround and states that CVE identifiers were requested but not yet assigned.
