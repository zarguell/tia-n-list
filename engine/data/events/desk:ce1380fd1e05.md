Censys published a rapid response advisory listing CVE-2026-123633 among five nginx2root flaws, with affected versions 1.19.7 through 1.30.6 and 1.31.0 through 1.31.8 and a configuration requirement of HTTP/2 proxying to a backend with a worker low on worker_connections. It states that F5 rates the flaws 8.1 under CVSS 3.1 and 9.2 under CVSS 4.0, while the nginx advisory labels CVE-2026-123633 medium. Censys says Open Source fixes are 1.30.7 and 1.31.9, Plus fixes are 37.0.8.1 and 37.3.0.1, and that its researchers knew of no in-the-wild exploitation as of November 18, 2026.
