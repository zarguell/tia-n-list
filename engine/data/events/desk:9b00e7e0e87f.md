NICT's CYNEX threat intelligence team LETTICE warned on October 13, 2026 that attackers were reverse-engineering publicly available Android applications to discover backend APIs and attack the servers behind them, a technique it calls App-to-Backend (A2B) attacks. Even when the app itself has no vulnerability, weaknesses in backend authentication, authorization, or input validation can lead to unauthorized access and personal data leaks. NICT noted that AI can make app analysis and vulnerability discovery more efficient, potentially extending these attacks to more systems. The alert links to a PDF containing attack details, targeted weaknesses, countermeasures, and IoCs.
