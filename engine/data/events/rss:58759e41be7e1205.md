Executive Summary On November 2 and 3, supermarket chains in Belgium, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Sweden, Ireland and Estonia reported overnight disruption to store refrigeration systems. The incidents came three weeks after attacks on refrigeration systems at hundreds of French supermarkets. Attacks on refrigeration systems predate the 2026 incidents, including attacks on Russian supermarkets and Israeli stores documented by Danfoss in 2022, and a 2024 attack on a Los Angeles meat processor that the DOJ attributed to CARR. Reporting and official statements suggest attackers changed temperature and defrost settings through the systems’ own management interfaces. ANSSI’s assessment of the French cases at the time pointed to internet-exposed administration interfaces and weak passwords. Russian-aligned groups including CARR, NoName057(16) and Z-Pentest have a documented record of disrupting operational technology and have claimed refrigeration intrusions, making them a credible line of investigation. However, available evidence does not yet establish who is responsible for the European incidents. Freezer failures at U.S. military commissaries in August predate the French attacks. U.S. officials reportedly suspect Iran-linked actors in those cases, an assessment that is provisional and does not concern Europe.
