PrestaShield published its own advisory on 30 October for all litespeedcache versions before 1.6.2, scoring it 9.8 in CVSS 3.1. It says the ESI controller handles public requests without login and, through 1.6.1, did not verify that fragment parameters were generated by the store, letting a caller choose the Smarty template and module method. Version 1.6.2 signs those parameters with an installation-specific key, ignores POST values and filters invalid templates and methods. The entry cites the public exploitation reports and notes that LiteSpeed had not confirmed them; PrestaShield says its score is an estimate based on the patch.
