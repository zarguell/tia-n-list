CISA issued Emergency Directive 25-03 on September 25, 2025, deeming CVE-2025-20333 and CVE-2025-20362 an unacceptable risk to federal systems and connecting the campaign to ArcaneDoor activity from early 2024, including zero-day exploitation and ROM manipulation persisting through reboot and upgrade. It ordered core dump collection by September 26, 2025, permanent disconnection of end-of-support ASA 5500-X models by September 30, 2025, and patching of everything else. A November 12, 2025 implementation note warns agencies had marked devices patched while still on vulnerable versions, and the April 23, 2026 V1 update adds persistence-eviction requirements, a physical power pull by April 30, 2026, and references CISA's FIRESTARTER backdoor malware analysis report AR26-113A.
