Brickset reported on 9 November 2026 that BrickLink had been down since late the previous week and would remain offline until at least Wednesday evening. The post stated that no announcement or communication about the cause had been made, and it noted that Brickset hotlinks minifigure images from BrickLink servers and uses the BrickLink API for prices and availability, so its own listings were missing images and price data. Brickset pointed readers to BrickOwl, the independent marketplace, as the working alternative.
