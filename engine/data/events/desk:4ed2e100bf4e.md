A Japanese-language analysis published on September 28, 2026 at 19:58 JST details the confirmed impact across Keio Group businesses following the September 26 ransomware attack. It reports credit card and electronic payment disruptions at some retail stores, suspension of new reservations at Keio Presso Inn, delays at Keio Plaza Hotel, and unavailable loyalty point services. The article emphasizes that no recovery timeline has been announced and cautions users to verify official sources rather than responding to potential phishing messages referencing the incident.
