Use LogPoint’s Search API, which works in two calls to the same endpoint, /getsearchlogs. The first call sends your query, time range and repos, and returns a search_id. The second call sends that search_id and returns the rows once the search is final. Both calls are form-encoded POSTs authenticated with your username and secret key. LogPoint is a popular SIEM in Europe, and the Search API is the cleanest way to get a defined slice of its data out. The same endpoint family lists them for you (getalloweddata with type logpoint_repos). Time range. time_range takes either a preset like “Last 24 hours” or a pair of epoch seconds.
