Arista has disclosed CVE-2026-114625 , a critical SQL injection vulnerability in on-premises VeloCloud Orchestrator deployments. The flaw is being exploited in the wild and allows any authenticated portal user, including an Enterprise Read Only account, to gain local superuser operator privileges. Organizations should upgrade immediately to the fixed release for their branch, restrict the VCO web interface to trusted networks, and examine logs for activity identified in Arista’s advisory . What Is CVE-2026-114625? CVE-2026-114625 is an SQL injection vulnerability in VeloCloud Orchestrator On-Prem, the management platform for VeloCloud SD-WAN environments. Arista assigned a CVSS v3.1 score of 9.9 and a CVSS v4.0 score of 9.4 and tracks the issue internally as BUG2043622. SOCRadar Vulnerability Intelligence card for CVE-2026-114625 A successful attack can elevate a valid portal session to the local superuser operator role and expose functionality intended only for internal use. Arista warns that exploitation may affect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the orchestrator and the data it manages. Which Systems Are Affected?
