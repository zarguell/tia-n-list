Yonhap reported KEPCO's confirmation that 650,487 KEPCO ON users and 869,761 SRM users were affected, with 426,827 corporate representatives and 442,934 KEPCO and company employees in the latter group. It said the company treated the entire SRM user base as affected because individual exposure could not be determined, notified the Personal Information Protection Commission, and opened an investigation with the National Intelligence Service and the climate ministry's cyber safety center. KEPCO attributed the intrusion to an external attacker and cited increasingly sophisticated attacks using technologies such as AI without giving an access method.
