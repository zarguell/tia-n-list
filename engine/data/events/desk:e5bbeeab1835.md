First-day coverage of the US takedown announcement: Ardit Kutleshi, 26, and Jetmir Kutleshi, 28, were arrested in Kosovo and awaiting extradition, while Shpend Sokoli was arrested in Albania to be prosecuted there. Per the indictment, Rydox was active since at least February 2016, with more than 7,600 sales generating at least $230,000, at least 321,372 products listed, and over 18,000 users. The US seized the www.Rydox.cc domain, seized servers in Malaysia with local police, and obtained authorization to seize approximately $225,000 in cryptocurrency.
