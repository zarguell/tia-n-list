Microsoft's advisory for CVE-2020-17103, an elevation-of-privilege flaw in the Windows Cloud Files Mini Filter Driver (cldflt.sys), was first published on December 8, 2020 as part of the December 2020 security updates, rated Important with a CVSS 3.1 base score of 7.0. Microsoft marked the issue as not publicly disclosed and not exploited at release, and re-released the entry on June 9, 2026 to direct customers to the June 2026 updates after the MiniPlasma proof-of-concept showed the original fix had not held.
