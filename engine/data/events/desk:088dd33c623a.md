The United States sought extradition of Ryan James Roach, a Saskatoon man accused of hacking into American university systems to illegally use their supercomputers for cryptocurrency mining. Roach faced conspiracy to commit unauthorized use of a computer, punishable by up to ten years in prison in Canada, and maintained his innocence through his lawyer. One undisclosed U.S. educational institution suffered about 337000 US dollars in damages from system shutdowns, forensic analysis, and rebuilding compromised infrastructure. A Saskatchewan judge had to decide whether the U.S. evidence met Canada's extradition test.
