CBS News reported that multiple sources said Sean Cairncross was fired and that chief of staff Stephen Miller had long wanted him out, questioning his expertise for the national cyber director role. The report said Miller selected Pentagon CIO Kirsten Davies as acting replacement and that a permanent appointment would need Senate confirmation before Democrats take control in January. It also quoted Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin saying investigators had seen nothing linking the recent Microsoft, aviation, banking and NSA outages and that he was 98 percent confident they were not malicious foreign activity.
