TL;DR PHP disclosed three additional critical vulnerabilities, separate from the PHP2Shell issues covered in our October analysis. Upgrade to PHP 8.5.13, 8.4.28, 8.3.37, or 8.2.36, as appropriate for your branch; check with your vendor for package availability or backported fixes. Inventory PHP deployments and confirm each has an owner and a patch path. For background on the earlier PHP2Shell vulnerabilities, read our October analysis . PHP2Shell reminded us that PHP powers a vast range of websites, applications, and products, often running behind the scenes in places organizations may not immediately think to look. With three new advisories now drawing attention to PHP’s internals, security teams face a familiar challenge: understanding where the runtime is deployed, how it is configured, and who can move quickly when a fix is available.
