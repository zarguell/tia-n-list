## Kimsuky Targets Individual Business Owners with Malicious "August Tax Filing Deadline Notice" PDF.lnk

The Kimsuky group (North Korean APT) is targeting individual business owners with a malicious file disguised as an August tax filing deadline notice. The lure file is named "8월 신고 납부기한 통지서.pdf.lnk" (August Tax Filing Deadline Notice.pdf.lnk), but the actual payload is "암호.txt.lnk" (password.txt.lnk).

**File Details:**
- Size: 1 MB
- MD5: ab7c07d41578a1cd0d0ff15c0841bf93
- SHA-1: 8356f319f00caccd9a6200ef380d809b12d7dc18

The attack leverages tax season urgency to trick Korean business owners into executing the malicious shortcut file.
