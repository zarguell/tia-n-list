North Korea-linked threat actor TraderTraitor has expanded its macOS malware campaign beyond cryptocurrency firms, targeting an Indian IT services provider through fake job interview projects hosted on GitHub. SentinelOne found that the attackers used two Rust-based macOS backdoors, named FLATROOF and ROOFDECK, previously linked to the April 2026 LayerZero compromise. In that incident, attackers allegedly stole $292 million from KelpDAO after compromising infrastructure connected to LayerZero. The newly identified victim was not involved in cryptocurrency. Instead, it was an IT services organization where a DevOps engineer used an Apple Silicon MacBook for Terraform, Ansible, AWS, OVH, and OpenStack administration. This highlights how attackers may target developers for the cloud credentials, source-code access, and infrastructure permissions available on their systems. ROOFDECK Uses Nostr C2 TraderTraitor used a known North Korean social-engineering method called Contagious Interview. Attackers contacted job seekers, mainly DevOps and FinTech engineers, and sent them GitHub repositories disguised as coding assignments. The repositories included names such as Northwind-IAC, novacart-interview, and terraform-candidate-repo. They contained a malicious .terraform.lock.hcl file pointing to typosquatted Terraform provider domains controlled by the attackers.
