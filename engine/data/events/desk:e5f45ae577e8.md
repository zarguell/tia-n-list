Dan Goodin reported on November 10, 2026 that Grok Build, the vibe-coding agent from SpaceXSI, was leaking proprietary business information and sensitive secrets from thousands of users through share links. Research from security firm Claroc showed the exposed data included internal business plans, ad proposals, medical exam results, galley checklists from a celebrity cruise line, and employee names and ID numbers, including two-dozen apps built by SpaceXSI itself. A design decision by the platform's developers made it possible for anyone to enumerate thousands of live Grok Build projects despite randomized URLs.
