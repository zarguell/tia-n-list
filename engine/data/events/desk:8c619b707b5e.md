Google's Chrome team shipped 95.0.4638.69 with a fix for CVE-2021-38003, an inappropriate implementation in V8 reported by Clément Lecigne of Google TAG and Samuel Groß of Google Project Zero on 2021-10-26. The advisory states Google was aware that exploits for CVE-2021-38003 existed in the wild at the time of release. This is the vendor advisory for the V8 bug Gen Digital later observed being reused against Sogou Input Method's frozen Chromium 80 engine, a build that predates the fix.
