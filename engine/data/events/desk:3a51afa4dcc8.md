CERT Polska's September 5, 2026 disclosure of six RouterOS vulnerabilities, including the two-flaw chain it named MikroTrick, which gives unauthenticated full control of devices with SSH exposed to the internet. Fixes shipped in 7.25beta3, 7.24.2, 7.23.4, and 6.49.21. Confirmed attacks date to at least September 2 from 82.192.72.4, with 103.102.31.18 used in exploitation attempts; indicators include the log lines 'login failure for user -2' and 'user <name> added by ssh:-2@<ip>', a highly privileged ops account, and the Flagged marker via /system/device-mode/print.
