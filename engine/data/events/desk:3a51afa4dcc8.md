CERT Polska disclosed six RouterOS vulnerabilities on September 5, 2026 and confirmed active exploitation of a two-bug chain, named MikroTrick, that takes full control of devices with SSH exposed to the internet. Observed attacks left log lines reading 'login failure for user -2 from <ip> via ssh' and 'user <name> added by ssh:-2@<ip>', plus a highly privileged account named 'ops', attributed to source IP 82.192.72.4 since at least September 2 with 103.102.31.18 used in attempts. MikroTik accompanied the fix with its first-ever push notification to phones of users with the MikroTik app installed.
