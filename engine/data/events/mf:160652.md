A newly uncovered cybercrime operation, dubbed StopAndProtect, has transformed thousands of compromised WordPress websites into a distributed infrastructure for malware delivery, command-and-control communications, and the storage of stolen data. Checkpoint first observed the campaign in mid-May 2026 and found that it blends ransomware activity with targeted data theft, enabling operators to decide whether to encrypt victims’ files, silently collect documents, or do both. The campaign begins with a ClickFix social-engineering lure hosted on hacked websites. Visitors are shown a fake CAPTCHA prompt that instructs them to copy and run a PowerShell command. Hackers Turn Thousands of WordPress Sites Once executed, the command launches a hidden PowerShell process, downloads additional payloads, and starts a multi-stage infection chain. The sequence progresses from PowerShell scripts to .NET loaders, then deploys functional malware modules onto the victim’s system. StopAndProtect’s malware toolkit includes a ransomware component known as SilentEncryptor, a credential and data stealer called SilentDataCollector, a lock-screen payload, a custom chat tool, and propagation components capable of moving through SMB shares, USB devices, WMI, and removable media.
