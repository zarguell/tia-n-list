Nik Tsytsarkin of Meta Red Team X published the technical disclosure on November 9, 2026, one week before the news coverage in this story. He shows that a sandboxed app could set AudioUnit property 2009 to a forged 32-byte client audit token naming audiomxd, triggering the daemon's internal-client shortcut and allowing microphone capture without a TCC permission check; declining the prompt did not stop recording. On the earlier build he analyzed, the orange indicator stayed off and logs attributed capture to the daemon rather than the app. The post records fixes in iOS 27 beta 5 on August 10, iOS and iPadOS 26.6.1 and macOS Tahoe 26.6.2 on August 17, macOS Sequoia 15.8, tvOS 27, watchOS 27 and visionOS 27 on September 14, and iOS and iPadOS 18.7.11 on October 26.
