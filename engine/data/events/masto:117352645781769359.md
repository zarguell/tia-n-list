Poper Blocker spyware in the Chrome Web Store downloaded by millions

The Chrome Web Store still hosts the Poper Blocker ad blocker with more than 2 million users, a Featured badge, and a 4.8-star rating, even though researchers found the extension exfiltrates users' full browsing histories. Reporting by Bay Area Labs, covered by Dark Reading, further describes Poper Blocker harvesting page content, screenshots, and AI chat conversations from browsers where it is installed.
