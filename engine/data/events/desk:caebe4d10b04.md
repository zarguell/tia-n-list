The Xen Project publicly released XSA-523 version 2 on 2026-11-03 at 12:00 UTC, disclosing two grant-copy validation flaws that let a malicious guest read and write arbitrary memory of other domains, including Dom0. All Xen versions from 4.6 onward are vulnerable and 4.5 and earlier are not; the advisory states there is no known mitigation. The issue was discovered by Anthropic using Claude, validated by Stephan Lipp of Ada Logics, and independently reported by the CodeAnt AI Security Research Team. Attached patches cover xen-unstable through 4.19.x and 4.18.x through 4.17.x.
