Escudo Digital reported on 21 November 2026 that Handsome Boy Hacking Club had added FIFA to its dark-web leaks page and was demanding a ransom within a few days. At publication, the group had shared no screenshots, data categories or theft volume, and neither FIFA nor independent sources had confirmed a breach. The report noted prior false FIFA-related extortion claims and a June streaming-platform access-control patch without linking either to this group. It also said HBHC had recently targeted Frontier Communications, UCHealth and Smith & Wesson.
