Wordfence published the CVE record as CNA on 2026-09-22 after notifying vendor wpclever on 2026-09-18 and disclosing publicly on 2026-09-21, crediting researcher lhking. The record describes stored XSS via the qty parameter in versions through 8.6.6: a float cast during quantity validation lets numeric-prefixed payloads such as `1<img src=x onerror=...>` pass, and the markup is stored verbatim in order item metadata under the _woosb_ids key. CVSS 3.1 base score is 7.2 with vector AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:C/C:L/I:L/A:N, and the CISA vulnrichment entry at publication marked exploitation as none.
