China-aligned advanced persistent threat (APT) groups are using low-quality Chinese-language casino and adult websites to conceal command-and-control (C2) infrastructure linked to the PeckBirdy malware framework. The tactic helps attackers blend malicious network activity into a vast ecosystem of suspicious gambling sites that many security teams may dismiss as unwanted browsing or low-priority web traffic. Researchers found that PeckBirdy operators have used this approach since at least 2023 to target corporate and government organizations across Asia. PeckBirdy is a flexible JScript-based framework that can support multi-stage intrusions and malware delivery. Earlier reporting connected the framework to campaigns using living-off-the-land binaries, while the latest analysis shows its operators expanding the use of casino-themed decoy sites and Chinese-language adult portals. PeckBirdy Hides in Casinos One example identified by researchers is vip311.cc , a Chinese-language casino site that embedded the PeckBirdy C2 domain cache-mcp.com . The malicious JavaScript established a service worker and created WebSocket-based communications with another domain, mcp-source.online .
