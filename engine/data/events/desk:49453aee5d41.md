Trezor posted on X that it learned through responsible disclosure of a single hardware implant in a Trezor Safe 5 bought from reseller CryptoBilis in August 2026. The device was purchased via Tokopedia and the implant appears to operate similarly to those seen in Ledger devices. Trezor said no funds have been reported stolen and urged CryptoBilis buyers from March 2026 onward to move funds to a new wallet with a new seed.
