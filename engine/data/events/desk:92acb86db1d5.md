Nelson Aguilar reports that a database of about 2 billion login records for sites across the internet was accessible without a password, according to a report published by ExpressVPN. The files included usernames and passwords tied to email, social media, shopping and financial services, and an advertisement offering email accounts sorted by geography. CNET notes that the figure counts records, not people, and that the hosting provider restricted access several days after being told. The article recommends checking Have I Been Pwned, changing passwords from a trusted device, signing out other sessions and enabling two-factor authentication.
