TechCrunch reported that Microsoft's daylong outage began as an Outlook and Teams problem and spread to Copilot, Defender and Intune, with Kentik measuring about 18 hours of downtime. Microsoft said there was no evidence of malicious activity and that the incident was not related to cyber or AI agents, while engineers initially could not identify the cause. The company did not answer questions about whether it had the logs needed to detect malicious activity.
