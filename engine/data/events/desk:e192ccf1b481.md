SecurityWeek reports on December 18, 2025 that CISA warned of active exploitation of CVE-2025-59374 (CVSS 9.3). The backdoor was introduced in a supply chain compromise linked to Operation ShadowHammer, attributed to APT41 (Brass Typhoon, Wicked Panda, Barium). Over 1 million ASUS users may have downloaded the backdoored utility, but the hackers targeted only around 600 specific devices based on hardcoded MAC addresses. ASUS released a patch by March 2019 and discontinued the Live Update client.
