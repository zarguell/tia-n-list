Malware protection combines software, settings, and habits to detect, block, and remove malicious programs before they steal data, lock files, or take control of a device. No single tool covers everything, so effective protection works in layers: real-time scanning, regular updates, safe browsing, strong authentication, and backups. Quick checklist: keep your operating system and apps updated, keep real-time protection on, treat unexpected links and attachments with suspicion, use unique passwords with multi-factor authentication, and keep an offline or cloud backup. What Is Malware Protection? Malware protection is any security control that prevents malicious software from running on a device or network, or finds and removes it if it does. The term covers antivirus engines, built-in operating system defenses, firewalls, web and email filtering, and enterprise endpoint tools. What does malware protection actually do? It inspects files, processes, downloads, and network traffic for signs of malicious behavior, then blocks, quarantines, or removes what it finds.
