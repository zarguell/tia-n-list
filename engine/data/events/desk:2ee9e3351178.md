BleepingComputer reported that PJ&A said a March 2023 cyberattack exposed the personal information of almost nine million patients, with threat actor access between March 27 and May 2, 2023. The HHS Office for Civil Rights breach portal confirms the number as 8,952,212 patients. PJ&A began sending breach notices on October 31, 2023, and Northwell Health said its data was stolen between April 7 and April 19.
