The Harvard Crimson reported on November 22, 2025 that Harvard's Alumni Affairs and Development Office systems were accessed by an unauthorized party earlier that week after a phone-based phishing attack, discovered November 18, 2025. CIO Klara Jelinkova and Alumni Affairs and Development chief James J. Husson wrote that accessed systems include donation details and event attendance records plus email addresses, telephone numbers, and home addresses, and do not generally contain Social Security numbers, passwords, or financial account numbers. Harvard had not yet decided whether to send specific notifications to affected affiliates and was working with third-party cybersecurity experts and law enforcement. The article noted similar donor and alumni record breaches at Princeton University earlier that month and at the University of Pennsylvania in October.
