The kernel.org CNA record for the ebtables SNAT out-of-bounds write was published June 25, 2026, roughly three months before the KEV listing. The flaw lets the ARP sender-hardware-address rewrite write through skb_store_bits() into a nonlinear fragment backed by a splice-imported file page without the range first being made writable. It was introduced in 5.10 by commit 63137bc5882a and fixed across 5.10.259 through 7.1, scored CVSS v3.1 8.8 by kernel.org.
