BambooToken, a new malware, uses the MQTT protocol to infect Windows and Linux systems, according to Lumen researchers who identified it. The malware leverages MQTT, a lightweight messaging protocol designed for IoT devices, as its command-and-control channel. By abusing legitimate MQTT brokers, the malware avoids direct connections to attacker infrastructure, improving evasion.
