Google published the November 2026 Android Security Bulletin covering patch levels 2026-11-01 and 2026-11-05. The 2026-11-01 level fixes 119 defects including 65 in Framework and 53 in System; the 2026-11-05 level fixes 97 bugs in Kernel and vendor components. The bulletin is the authoritative source for CVE-2026-58868 and CVE-2026-58882.
