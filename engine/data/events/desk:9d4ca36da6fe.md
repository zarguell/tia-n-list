CISA added CVE-2026-12569 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog on June 25, 2026, citing evidence of active exploitation. The bulletin confirms the vulnerability affects PTC Windchill and FlexPLM and requires federal civilian agencies to apply patches or mitigations on an emergency timeline per BOD 26-04. This was the first-ever PTC product vulnerability added to CISA KEV, signaling widespread in-the-wild exploitation rather than theoretical risk.
