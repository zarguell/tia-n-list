CISA's 18 November 2026 alert adds CVE-2024-10441 in Synology DSM, CVE-2026-40217 in BerriAI LiteLLM, CVE-2026-90436 in Apache Axis and CVE-2026-90817 in REDCap to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog based on evidence of active exploitation. The alert describes the flaw classes as improper encoding or escaping of output, unprotected alternate channel, unsafe reflection and code injection, and points federal agencies to BOD 26-04 risk-based remediation. It says these vulnerability types are frequent attack vectors for malicious cyber actors and pose significant risks to the federal enterprise, and encourages all organizations to adopt risk-based remediation.
