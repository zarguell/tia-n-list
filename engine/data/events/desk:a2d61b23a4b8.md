The Cloud Security Alliance daily brief records Project Zero's replacement of the 2021 90 + 30 policy with fixed disclosure deadlines on November 13, 2026, and notes the removal of soak time, grace periods and variant handling. It quotes the claim that zero patch-to-exploitation time is a reasonable model and Project Zero's statement that it now finds several vulnerabilities a week in targets where it once found a few a year. The brief maps the affected patch surface to browsers, mobile and desktop operating systems, hypervisors and embedded libraries, and flags that a 30-day critical patch SLA will be as long as the whole disclosure window by 2028.
