The primary research behind this story, by Ellis Stannard and Nick Smart, published three days before the first news rewrites. It documents the _Z module, 69,470 characters of base-91 and control-flow-flattened JavaScript delivered in the C2 /init response, which scans Ethereum mainnet via public RPC providers (publicnode.com, drpc.org, blastapi.io) for transfers from signal wallet 0x33ff3edaf55a8e03dcbc7cb40d498a49cd499891 and decodes the C2 endpoint from the first six bytes of the recipient address.
