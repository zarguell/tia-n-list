Broadcom's official companion FAQ to VMSA-2024-0012, initially published at 2130 PDT on June 17, 2024, confirms the advisory release date and lists CVE-2024-37079, CVE-2024-37080 and CVE-2024-37081. It states there are no workarounds in the advisory, that ESXi hosts are not affected, and that Cloud Foundation receives an asynchronous patch. It also documents a 7.0 U3q/U3r patching failure with custom TLS cipher configurations that aborts the vpxd-svcs service, tracked in KB 369485.
