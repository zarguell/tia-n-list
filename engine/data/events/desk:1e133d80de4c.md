Securifera researcher b0yd published a technical disclosure on 27 September 2026 for CVE-2026-90817 in REDCap survey hosting. The write-up traces a double URL decoding mismatch in the survey passthru check, rights assigned before the survey identity exists, an attacker-controlled fname stream passed to PHP file functions, and overwrite of a predictable BladeOne template cache that the application includes as PHP. It scores the chain 9.8 and says a valid public survey hash is required, while advising temporary containment by disabling public surveys or blocking survey access to the Data Import and Parcel controllers.
