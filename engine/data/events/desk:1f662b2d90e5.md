BleepingComputer reported that a Hacker News post by user petecooper leaked details of two critical PHP remote code execution flaws, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, ahead of the planned disclosure. watchTowr confirmed the flaws were real, believed to affect PHP 5 through 8, and reported reproducing CVE-2026-109618, while stating it had not yet seen in-the-wild exploitation. PHP's official advisory rated both flaws CVSS v4.0 9.2 and scheduled emergency fixes for 21 October 2026 at 14:00 UTC.
