Accenture APEX Labs reported pre-disclosure exploitation of the five MiCollab AWV flaws in MISA-2026-0012. At one victim, web-shell activity preceded Mitel's November 16 advisory; the actor reached root within 30 minutes and used MiCollab's LDAP connector credentials against a domain controller at T+32 minutes. A second victim had communicated with the same actor infrastructure since November 9, but its initial access vector was not established.
