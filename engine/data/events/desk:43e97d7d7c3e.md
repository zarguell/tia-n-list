NIST NVD published the CVE-2026-106173 record on 13 October 2026, identifying an improper path-traversal vulnerability in Fortinet FortiSIEM 7.5.0 (CPE 7.5.0, plus multiple 7.4.x, 7.3.x, 7.2.x, 7.1.x and 7.0.x ranges) that can allow attackers to execute unauthorized code or commands via specially crafted packets. The entry cites the Fortinet PSIRT advisory FG-IR-26-180 and assigns a CVSS 3.1 base score of 9.8 (CRITICAL) with network attack vector, no privileges required, and high impact on confidentiality, integrity and availability.
