J.P. Morgan Chase warned it suffered a data breach after a cyberattack at a third-party service provider exposed customers' personal information and marketing data. The bank said the incident didn't disrupt its services and is unrelated to a service disruption that occurred earlier this week. "We have learned that third parties who provide services to the firm were the target of a cyber attack," reads the firm's announcement on X. "Based on our investigation so far, we believe the attackers accessed certain marketing data held by those vendors, which may include some customer names and email addresses." J.P. Morgan Chase Bank is the largest U.S. bank by assets, with about $4.1 trillion as of June 2026. Its consumer banking division reports about 78 million active digital customers.
