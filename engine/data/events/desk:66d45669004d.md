The Hacker News reported on SentinelOne researcher Alex Delamotte's analysis of PCPJack, a credential theft framework that targets exposed cloud infrastructure and removes TeamPCP artifacts. PCPJack targets Docker, Kubernetes, Redis, MongoDB, RayML, and vulnerable web applications, spreading worm-like and moving laterally inside compromised networks. Its assessed end goal is illicit revenue through credential theft, fraud, spam, extortion, or resale of stolen access, and it notably lacks a cryptomining component. The report notes targeting overlaps with TeamPCP, which rose to prominence by exploiting vulnerabilities such as React2Shell and cloud misconfigurations.
