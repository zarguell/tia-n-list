Enterprise software giant SAP has confirmed it is investigating a cybersecurity incident following reports that threat actors targeted its development infrastructure. German publication Der Spiegel [paywalled] broke the news on Thursday, citing SAP insiders and an internal notification sent to employees. SAP told SecurityWeek that it had detected suspicious activity and implemented measures to protect customers and secure its services. The German company did not disclose details of the attack. “Based on our investigation to date, we have found no evidence of unauthorized access to customer business data,” SAP told SecurityWeek . German news agency DPA reviewed internal SAP communications that revealed the suspicious activity had been identified within GitHub. According to an insider cited by Der Spiegel, the hackers obtained SAP employees’ authentication tokens, enabling them to copy and alter source code stored on GitHub. SAP publishes developer tools, sample applications, and open source projects on GitHub. It’s unclear whether the threat actor accessed any proprietary code. Separately, Handelsblatt reported that SAP’s emergency security measures have affected numerous teams. The software giant has reportedly replaced signing keys, limited access to GitHub, and suspended automated development operations.
