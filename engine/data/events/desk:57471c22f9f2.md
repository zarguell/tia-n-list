The vendor advisory, first published August 22, 2024, discloses an improper access control flaw (CWE-284, CVSS 9.3) in SonicOS management access and SSLVPN affecting Gen 5, Gen 6, and Gen 7 devices on 7.0.1-5035 and older. A September 6, 2024 revision added that the vulnerability is potentially being exploited in the wild, and November 2024 revisions added threat actor IPs including 88.119.175.104, 45.149.172.51, 172.86.116.8, 216.146.25.208, 194.48.154.67, 162.120.71.224, and 45.61.157.15. Fixed builds are 5.9.2.14-13o for Gen 5, 6.5.4.15.116n (6.5.2.8-2n for SM9800, NSsp 12400, NSsp 12800) for Gen 6, and anything above 7.0.1-5035 for Gen 7; EoL units such as NSA 2600 get no patch.
