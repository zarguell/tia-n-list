Thieves stole two Nvidia trucks believing they contained valuable GPUs, but ended up with 10 tons of sand. The criminals targeted what they assumed was a shipment of graphics cards, only to discover the load was worthless filler. The incident is a hardware theft rather than a cyberattack, but it underlines how high GPU prices have made semiconductor shipments a target for physical cargo crime.
