President Donald Trump nominated Palantir chief information security officer Michael LoSapio to lead CISA on Monday, seeking to fill a leadership vacancy that has stretched through nearly two years of staffing cuts. LoSapio would oversee the federal government's main civilian cyberdefense unit, which helps protect agency networks and works with operators of water, power, telecom and other critical infrastructure. CISA has been without a permanent Senate-confirmed director since Jen Easterly's departure in January 2025, with Nick Andersen leading the agency in an acting capacity since February. LoSapio has served as Palantir's CISO since September 2024, after leading the company's infrastructure and network security group from December 2017.
