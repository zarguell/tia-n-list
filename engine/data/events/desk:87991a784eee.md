Google Threat Intelligence Group and Mandiant reported on November 19, 2026 that ARMORY CASTLE, formerly tracked as UNC5221 and described as a suspected PRC-nexus actor, exploited CVE-2026-123890 in early September 2026 using a Go utility named BONESAW. The flaw is an unauthenticated SRP-6a session key disclosure over DCE/RPC that produces an administrator@vsphere.local session and access to the vmdird database. The report names five new malware families and utilities, including SLIMSAVAGE, CAGEMATCH, BEARBONES, BONESAW and SAWSNAKE, and records actor scans of ports 9080, 5480 and 2012.
