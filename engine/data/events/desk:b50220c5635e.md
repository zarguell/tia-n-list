BleepingComputer reported on September 18, 2025 that Google released emergency updates for the sixth actively exploited Chrome zero-day of 2025, a high-severity type confusion (CWE-843) in V8 reported by Google TAG. The piece notes Google TAG frequently flags zero-days used by government-sponsored actors in spyware campaigns against high-risk individuals, and recounts the five earlier 2025 zero-days (CVE-2025-2783, CVE-2025-4664, CVE-2025-5419, CVE-2025-6554, CVE-2025-6558).
