The short answer: run the search, click Export in the job toolbar under the search bar, and choose Raw Events, CSV or JSON . For a few thousand events, that’s all you need. For bigger pulls, skip the browser and use the splunk search CLI or the REST export endpoint. Both stream results instead of making Splunk Web build one huge file for you. Why export from Splunk at all, if the data is already indexed? Usually because someone outside your Splunk needs to look at it: an IR partner, a colleague on another team, or you, on a laptop, without writing SPL for an hour.
