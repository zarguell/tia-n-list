JPCERT/CC published on October 21, 2026 the results of its investigation into infrastructure used in a series of unauthorized access incidents reported by multiple Japanese organizations. It confirmed attackers used AI to scan for known vulnerabilities and carry out attacks via APIs, and found they analyzed Android application packages to identify API endpoints and test them for server-side request forgery. On another server, investigators found APK files plus Blutter and jadx, tools for reverse-engineering Flutter and Android applications, along with apk_extract directories and extracted string files. JPCERT/CC also found evidence of EDR evasion, web shells, and attacks against MySQL, Jenkins, and email gateways, and noted some targeted services outside Japan.
