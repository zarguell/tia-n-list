Albania's Special Anti-Corruption and Organized Crime Structure (SPAK) announced operation RYDOX on 12 December 2024, arresting three administrators including Kosovo citizen Sh.S. (Shpend Sokoli), who resided in Albania. It states Rydox made over 7,600 sales since February 2016 generating at least $230,000, offered more than 321,372 cybercrime products to an 18,000-user base, and sold names, addresses, Social Security numbers, card data, and login credentials of thousands of people residing in the United States. Seizures listed: one computer, six laptops, five mobile phones, other storage devices, documents, and cryptocurrency assets, while US authorities seized the www.Rydox.cc domain. Partners named include the FBI's Pittsburgh office, Albania's BKH, Kosovo's Special Prosecution and Police, and the Royal Malaysia Police.
