Hitachi's advisory hitachi-sec-2026-165, released November 10, 2026, confirms a partial string comparison vulnerability (CVE-2026-115646, CWE-187) in HiRDB with a CVSS 3.1 base score of 9.8 (AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H). It lists affected HiRDB Server Version 10 (10-00 before 10-12-01), Version 9 (09-50-06 through 09-66-22), and the Additional Function and 32-bit variants across AIX, HP-UX, Linux, and Windows. Fixed Version 10 builds are 10-12-01, 10-11-02, and 10-08-05; the advisory states no workarounds and directs customers to contact Hitachi support.
