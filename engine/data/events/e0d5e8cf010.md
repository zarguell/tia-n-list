INTERPOL's first large-scale cybercrime operation coordinated across the Middle East and North Africa concluded with 201 arrests, 53 servers seized, and 382 suspects identified. Running October 2025–February 2026, Operation Ramz targeted phishing infrastructure, malware networks, and cyber-enabled financial fraud. Participating nations included Algeria, Bahrain, Egypt, Iraq, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Morocco, Oman, Palestine, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and Tunisia.
