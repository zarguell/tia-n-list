Security Risk Advisors published the first public technical advisory on July 17, 2026, three days after the vendor fix, covering CVE-2026-9586 (unauthenticated SQL injection to RCE via the /pa endpoint's unsanitized PhoneIP value in PostgreSQL queries, CVSS 9.3) plus CVE-2026-9585 (reflected XSS, 8.6), CVE-2026-9587 (authenticated local file inclusion, 7.1), and CVE-2026-9588 (stored XSS, 7.0). Researcher Cameron Lischke submitted the findings to the vendor between May 11 and 26, 2026. Affected versions are Switchvox SMB 8.3 (104997) before 8.4.0.2, with upgrade to 8.4.0.2 or later as remediation.
