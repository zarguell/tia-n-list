Signal has announced new protections that block screenshots on iOS 27 and prevent AI assistants from capturing conversations on supported Android devices. The protections are designed to stop Siri and Gemini, the AI assistants built into iOS and Android, from accessing private conversations while performing tasks for users. The change comes after Signal introduced a feature in May 2025 that blocks Microsoft's controversial Recall screenshot-capturing feature on Windows 11. "We will now be taking similar measures to restrict agentic systems built into iOS 27 and on Android versions where we can detect an AI assistant attempting to read your Signal screen," Signal's president, Meredith Whittaker, said in a blog post . Whittaker says OS-level features such as Siri's Onscreen Awareness and Google's Gemini Intelligence can capture chat content as it appears on the app's screen, putting potentially sensitive private data outside Signal's control and exposing all conversation participants. Signal says its engineers spent months figuring out how AI systems access app data, designing targeted protections to block all extraction pathways verified through reverse engineering. However, Signal warns that AI agents could still access conversations through other methods, which Whittaker describes as "an existential threat to Signal."
