AI agents are transforming how organizations develop and deploy software. From writing code and configuring pipelines to deploying applications and testing security, autonomous agents are taking on greater responsibility across cloud environments. While AI is accelerating development, security teams are struggling to keep pace. Traditional security controls, fragmented visibility, and manual response processes were not designed for environments where autonomous agents can make changes at machine speed. At the same time, advances in frontier AI are introducing new security challenges. Adversaries are gaining the ability to discover vulnerabilities, develop exploits, and execute increasingly sophisticated attacks faster than ever before. Today, we're releasing our sixth annual State of Cloud Security Report, based on a survey of 2,800 security leaders and practitioners across 10 countries and 13 industries. The research reveals a growing disconnect between the speed of AI-driven development, the increasing sophistication of cyberattacks, and organizations' ability to prevent, detect, and respond to security risks.
