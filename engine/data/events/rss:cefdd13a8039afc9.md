Overview Law enforcement agencies from 11 countries have turned their attention to the financial engine powering Europe's illegal streaming industry. Rather than simply shutting down illegal IPTV services one by one, investigators are now tracing the cryptocurrency payments that keep these operations profitable and the people who ultimately pocket the proceeds. The effort, coordinated by Europol, brought police together with major cryptocurrency exchanges and a blockchain analytics firm. The goal was to map the money flowing from subscribers to the criminal networks behind digital piracy, and to identify who controls the wallets at the end of that chain. Five Days in Seville Disrupt 57 Illegal IPTV Services The operation ran for five days, from 16 to 20 November 2026, in Seville, Spain. During that window, investigators worked alongside the cryptocurrency industry to uncover the financial networks supporting illegal IPTV services. The results were significant. Authorities disrupted 57 illegal IPTV services and identified 321 websites offering copyrighted content without authorization. Together, those websites drew more than 7.3 million visits a year.
