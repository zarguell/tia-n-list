Application control flips the endpoint security model from “block known bad” to “allow only known good” a deny-by-default posture that stops ransomware, living-off-the-land attacks, and unapproved software before they run. It is one of the most effective controls available, but also one of the most operationally demanding, because a poorly tuned allowlist blocks legitimate work. The right tool depends on your size and appetite for management: small teams need simple, well-supported allowlisting; mid-market wants automation and ringfencing; enterprises need policy at scale integrated with EDR. Below, eight tools are scored on five weighted criteria and then examined in depth features, best-fit use case, pros, and cons. This is an editorial assessment to aid shortlisting, not a lab benchmark ; validate with a proof of concept. Pricing is by model only. Pairing endpoint restriction with dedicated managed cybersecurity services can help manage the operational overhead of rule tuning. Scoring Methodology Five criteria, weighted: Control model & effectiveness (25%) allowlisting/ringfencing depth; Automation & manageability (25%) ; Coverage & integration (20%) OS, EDR, cloud; Usability & support (15%) ; Value & fit (15%) . Scores reflect fit for a shortlist, not a benchmark.
