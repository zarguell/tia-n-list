Summary On September 13, 2026 , the ransomware group Barracuda claimed responsibility for a significant cyberattack on i2i-systems ( i2i-systems.com ), a major telecommunications company based in Turkey. The attack led to the exfiltration of 693 gigabytes of sensitive data, including source code for key projects, with threats of making the data publicly available if their demands are unmet.
