watchTowr confirmed on 20 October 2026 that details of two embargoed PHP remote code execution flaws, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, had leaked ahead of the planned 22 October disclosure. The vulnerabilities affect PHP 5 through 8, including end-of-life branches, and PHP released fixes on 21 October 2026 as 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35. watchTowr observed exploitation in the wild from 21 October 2026 via its Attacker Eye honeypot network.
