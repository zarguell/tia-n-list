Military Times reported on Sept. 24, 2026, that a vulnerability in a Defense Manpower Data Center file-sharing system allowed unauthorized users to access unencrypted files between October 2025 and July 16, 2026. A notification letter confirmed exposed data included Social Security numbers, names, dates of birth, contact information, sex, race, and military occupational specialty. The article notes DMDC discovered the issue July 16 and patched it, and that approximately four million DoD personnel may be affected according to two people familiar with the incident.
