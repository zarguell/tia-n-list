Amirul Cyber documented 15 page loads on household-name sites whose Optimizely bundles fetched 1688360.cc/track.js, including cdn.optimizely.com/js/5113954737848320.js on about.gitlab.com. The write-up breaks the payload into a checksum-validating crypto clipper covering eight chains, a screenshot and cookie exfiltration module posting to /hit.php, a card skimmer using Luhn checks and sendBeacon to /card.php, and an iOS second stage from two .icu hosts. It records that two sites blocked the payload through CSP script-src while the poisoned snippet still loaded, and lists IOCs including the SHA-256 of track.js and attacker wallet addresses.
