The vendor blog (dated June 26, 2025 on the page) states the Citrix security bulletin CTX693420 for CVE-2025-5777 was published on June 17, 2025, rates the flaw CVSS 9.3, and says there are no mitigations and no WAF signatures, only fixed builds. It instructs admins to run the kill sessions commands after upgrading for CVE-2025-5777, notes 12.0/13.0 builds are EOL with no fixes, and points to recovery runbook CTX694799 and NetScaler Console file integrity monitoring. It also records that CISA added the CVE to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog on July 10, 2025, after Citrix initially saw no exploitation evidence at disclosure.
