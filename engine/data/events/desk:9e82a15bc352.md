Robin Dost's analysis, February 3, 2026, establishes the root cause as an allowlist gap: Office still instantiates Shell.Explorer.1 because only Shell.Explorer and Shell.Explorer.2 were ever blocked, with WebDAV used purely for delivery and the .lnk as the execution vector. Proxy testing of the campaign servers showed strict geofencing: of 114 countries tested, only the Czech Republic, Poland, and Romania received payloads, with other origins getting HTTP 403. Hosting on Romanian and Moldovan providers matched the intended target regions.
