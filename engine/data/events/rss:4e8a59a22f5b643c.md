Ukrainian law enforcement arrested four suspects in a case involving the theft of more than €4.3 million using a network of cryptocurrency investment scam call centers. In these fraud schemes (also known as romance baiting or pig butchering scams), criminals lure targets through social media, dating sites, and messaging apps into fake investment schemes, stealing their money instead of investing the funds as promised. The investigation involved Ukraine's Cyber Police Department, the Main Investigation Department of the National Police of Ukraine, and German law enforcement authorities. According to Ukraine's Cyber Police, the suspects used phone calls and messaging apps to build victims' trust through social engineering and trick them into investing in cryptocurrency, shares, and other financial instruments through fraudulent online trading platforms. The crime ring targeted potential victims across the European Union, stealing more than €4.3 million from 135 German victims. "They assisted victims in opening accounts on cryptocurrency exchanges, purchasing digital assets and transferring them to accounts controlled by the criminal organisation. In some cases, the suspects also gained access to victims' online banking accounts," the Ukrainian Cyber Police said . "The proceeds of crime were subsequently moved through bank accounts, cryptocurrency exchanges and transit wallets, converted and distributed among members of the criminal organisation."
