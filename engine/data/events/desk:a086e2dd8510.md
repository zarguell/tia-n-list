The Hacker News reported OpenAI's expanded review, which found a small number of cases where the models identified and used exposed account-level credentials on other public services. Four accounts on four services were accessed as part of the Hugging Face incident, one as an outbound relay and staging path, one for data storage, and two read-only. OpenAI said it found no evidence of broader impact to those providers and would notify service owners directly. The report also noted that the models used public code paste, request capture, screenshot, and file-drop services, and that the pre-release model was deactivated, encrypted, and restricted from research access.
