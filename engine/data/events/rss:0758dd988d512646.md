On October 8, 2026, JPCERT/CC issued an alert regarding a series of unauthorized access incidents involving the leakage of personal and other information reported by multiple organizations in Japan. In cooperation with multiple organizations, JPCERT/CC has been investigating infrastructure that may have been used in these attacks. We confirmed that the attackers had used AI to scan for known vulnerabilities and carry out attacks via APIs. We identified several servers used for these activities and confirmed that the attackers had also targeted web services intended for users outside Japan. This article presents our findings from an investigation of attack servers involved in attacks targeting web services and other systems in Japan and abroad. Investigation of Attack Servers: Part 1 One notable characteristic of these attacks was that the attackers analyzed Android application packages (APK files) to identify APIs and exploit them [1] . On one of the attack servers, we found evidence that the attackers had analyzed APK files and tested the associated APIs for vulnerabilities such as server-side request forgery (SSRF).
