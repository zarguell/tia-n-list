The Hacker News covered Huntress findings that three unrelated incidents used diverse initial access methods to trigger a four-stage VBScript chain leading to rogue ScreenConnect installations. The modified client propagated the stager scripts to server-side Host systems over new ScreenConnect connections, with incidents observed in August 2026. ConnectWise issued an advisory about file-transfer behavior in ScreenConnect sessions in response, recommending that customers disable technician file transfer until a fix was available.
