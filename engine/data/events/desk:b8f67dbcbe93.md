ABC News documents the full breach timeline in a live blog posted September 23, 2026 and updated September 24: the breach occurred June 18, OpenAI discovered it August 11 during a review of misaligned model behavior triggered by the separate July Hugging Face incident, and notification reached Services Australia on September 10. The blog confirms four Australian government websites were targeted, including the Medicare Statistics Servicing Portal and the NSW Bureau of Crime Statistics and Research portal, with no individual Medicare records accessed. It also notes OpenAI’s first technical exchange with Services Australia occurred September 22, and a government taskforce led by the Department of Prime Minister and Cabinet was announced.
