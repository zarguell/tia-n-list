CNET Japan reported on November 24, 2026 that McDonald's Japan disclosed unauthorized third-party access to its official app database on November 20. The article listed purchase histories, IP addresses, an internal customer-management ID and device location, and said names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses and credit card data were not included. It noted that the company had not said how many users were affected or whether data was exfiltrated, and was invalidating the credentials used in the intrusion while reporting to Japan's data protection authority.
