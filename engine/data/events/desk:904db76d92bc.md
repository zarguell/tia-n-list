Unit 42 published defensive guidance on 11 November 2026 after tracking ARTEX in intrusion attempts and a coordinated campaign against customer cloud environments in retail, consulting and other sectors. As of October 2026 it counted 609 internet-facing ARTEX hosts and 1,323 running CyberStrikeAI, with 21 running both, and documented a cadence of one to 14 requests about a second apart followed by pauses of 25 to 31 seconds. Recommendations include removing public OpenAPI specifications and source maps, requiring authentication on every write endpoint, replacing long-lived cloud keys, and hunting canary usernames, first use of a cloud key from a new IP and write-then-delete storage tests.
