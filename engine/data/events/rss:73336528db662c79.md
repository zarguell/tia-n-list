French Supermarket Cyberattack: Exposed OT Behind France’s Food Supply Supermarkets across France opened on Monday, October 12, to warm freezers and spoiled stock. Overnight, attackers had taken control of refrigeration controllers in hundreds of stores and forced them into defrost, heating equipment to as high as 50°C. Prosecutors confirmed fires, retailers estimated losses in the tens of millions of euros, and Coopérative U’s chief executive called the attack unprecedented. The incident brings a long-discussed Operational Technology (OT) risk into the food supply chain. The affected controllers are mostly end-of-life Danfoss units that no longer receive updates, and passive analysis of internet scan data shows thousands of the same management interfaces still reachable from the internet in France. The attack also lands in a week of active hacktivist pressure on French organizations, though no group has claimed it.
