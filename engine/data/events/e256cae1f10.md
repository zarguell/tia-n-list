Peter Stokes, a dual U.S.-Estonian national known online as "Bouquet," has been charged for his alleged role in Scattered Spider (Octo Tempest). Stokes was arrested April 10 in Finland while boarding a flight to Japan. Court documents describe social engineering campaigns targeting corporate help desks to bypass MFA, including a 2023 incident where attackers impersonated employees to request MFA resets and gained access to systems containing sensitive employee data. Encrypted chat logs show real-time coordination with accomplices during intrusions. Scattered Spider's young, geographically distributed membership and social engineering tradecraft have made it one of the most impactful cybercriminal groups of the past two years.
