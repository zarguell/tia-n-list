Hunt.io published its original research blog on 2026-08-18 (held until then under TLP:AMBER after CERT notifications on 10 August 2026). The report documents a 35-day campaign (June 17 to July 22, 2026) that compromised 14,530+ Dahua IP cameras using brute-force, authentication bypass (CVE-2021-33044/CVE-2021-33045), and P2P relay abuse. It details the persistent p2pwn backdoor account, recovered 407 MB of operator files, and notes that the campaign concentrated in Ukraine and Russia.
