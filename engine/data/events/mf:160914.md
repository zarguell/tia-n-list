Security researchers Tom Stacey from PortSwigger and Tobia Righi from TurtleSec have introduced a new category of HTTP request smuggling attacks known as “CRLF-Powered Desync Attacks.” This method exploits a frequently overlooked HTTP header injection vulnerability, which can lead to full account takeovers, theft of HTTPOnly cookies, and even the creation of self-propagating desync worms.
