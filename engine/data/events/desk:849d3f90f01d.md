The Canadian Cyber Centre advisory AV25-449, published 2025-07-23, relays Mitel's advisories for critical flaws in MiVoice MX-ONE and MiCollab. It lists MiVoice MX-ONE versions 3 (7.3.0.0.50) to 7.8 SP1 (7.8.1.0.14) as affected and links the vendor bulletin MISA-2025-0009. It notes the critical authentication bypass carried a CVSSv3 score of 9.4 and that no CVE identifier was assigned at the time of publication.
