CERT-In's critical advisory, last week, gives a name to the campaign draining Indian fintechs: CRAFTY FALCON, an "AI-assisted financial threat actor" that moves money by calling its victims' own payment and disbursement APIs. Organizations that fixed their systems after CERT-In's first warning in October have been hit again. The AI label will get the headlines. The attack chain tells a plainer story. Hardcoded API tokens, staging servers that share credentials with production, IP allow-lists that trust shared third-party fetchers: none of these weaknesses is new. What the AI tooling changes is the time between an attacker finding one and cashing it out, and that time is now shorter than most remediation cycles. What CERT-In has confirmed The latest advisory updates the previous Oct. 9 advisory. CERT-In's finance team, CSIRT-Fin, named the actor and called the campaign active and ongoing, with victims across NBFCs, digital lenders, fintechs, payment aggregators, payment service providers and crypto exchanges.
