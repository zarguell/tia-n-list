Federal News Network reported on Sept. 28, 2026, that a DMDC information system breach exposed unencrypted records of nearly 2.8 million living and 294,000 deceased individuals. The unauthorized access spanned October 2025 to July 2026, stemming from a file-sharing vulnerability discovered July 16. The Pentagon said there is no indication of misuse and is offering 12 months of credit monitoring through IDX.
