Istio 1.31 migrates its release artifacts away from Google Cloud. Worth noting: infrastructure dependencies on a single cloud provider for container images and release artifacts are a supply chain consideration for anyone running Kubernetes service meshes. The move is covered as part of the Istio 1.31 release, which also introduces agentgateway support.
