heise online published Dennis Schirrmacher's initial German-language report at 15:24 CET (14:24:00Z) on 2026-11-12 covering Citrix bulletin CTX697305, which patches 28 memory flaws across NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway (9 rated critical and 15 rated high). The article notes that CVE-2026-120868 (CVSS 9.3) affects default packet-processing configurations on all appliances, while CVE-2026-107412 (CVSS 9.5) is rated the most severe and requires the appliance to be configured as a Gateway (SSL VPN, ICA Proxy, CVPN, RDP Proxy) or an AAA virtual server. It records that Citrix has received no reports of active exploitation, that Secure Private Access Hybrid deployments using customer-managed NetScaler instances are affected, and that remediating builds are 14.1-73.57, 13.1-64.39, 14.1-73.58 FIPS, and 13.1-37.293.
