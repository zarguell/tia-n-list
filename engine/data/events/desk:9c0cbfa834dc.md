Aftenposten reported on 17 November 2026 that Equinor had detected an ongoing cyberattack against some IT systems, with no data theft indicated and no effect on production. The article said disruptions involved applications and internal IT systems, and gave Equinor's scale: about 25,000 employees, offices in more than 20 countries, 67 percent Norwegian state ownership and a market value of 980 billion kroner. It also reported that NSM, the petroleum safety regulator and several ministries had been notified.
