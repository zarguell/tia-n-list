Yahoo Finance reported reports of problems across JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, U.S. Bank, Wells Fargo, PNC, Citigroup, Capital One and Zelle on 16 November, citing Downdetector. JPMorgan told the outlet that a software bug in on-premise hardware caused transaction delays, that there was no evidence of a cyberattack, and that the issue was resolved Monday evening. The article noted that Downdetector reports are user submitted and do not necessarily indicate outages at the institutions themselves.
