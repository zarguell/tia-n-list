ShinyHunters attacks ransomware Clop's leak site and threatens to extort rival gang

via Mastodon https://tugatech.com.pt/t91374-shinyhunters-ataca-site-do-ransomware-clop-e-ameaca-extorquir-grupo-rival

---

The ShinyHunters extortion group infiltrated and defaced the Clop (also known as Cl0p) ransomware group's data leak site on the Tor network. According to BleepingComputer, the attackers claim to have stolen server data and the group's private onion service keys.

The intrusion began on Friday night. ShinyHunters claims to have exploited an alleged unauthenticated file upload vulnerability in Grav CMS. Initially, the group uploaded a small text document with a warning message for Clop and a link to their own leak portal. Hours later, the main page was fully replaced by ASCII art of an Umbreon (the logo adopted by ShinyHunters), accompanied by a message stating they had been accessing systems since 2019.

## Tor key theft and ultimatum

Beyond the visual defacement, the attackers claim to have obtained full access to the server. Among the stolen information is reportedly the source code, Grav CMS plugins, and system logs stored in "/var/log", which could expose the IP addresses of anyone who connected to the infrastructure.

Additionally, ShinyHunters claims to possess Clop's private Tor service keys. If the claim is true, this would allow the attackers to host an identical site with the same onion address, taking control of the original portal. The objective of the action is now to extort the rival gang, with a 72-hour deadline imposed for Clop to make contact.

## Revenge for death threats and exploits

The motivation for the attack stems from a dispute over Clop's 2025 Oracle E-Business Suite data theft campaign. At the time, ShinyHunters accused Clop of using a vulnerability (CVE-2025-61882) in Oracle's platform whose exploit belonged to them and had allegedly been obtained without authorization.

The tension escalated when, after the incident, a Clop representative allegedly contacted a ShinyHunters member directly. According to the group now responsible for the attack, the Clop representative sent explicit death threats and promised to expose the identities of ShinyHunters members, triggering this direct retaliation against the rival group's infrastructure.
