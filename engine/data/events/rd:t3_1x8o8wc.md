Online Robust Mahalanobis scores each sample by how far it is from the centre of the healthy joint residual, measured in units of the residual's own spread. What it is The healthy residuals of a robot joint form a cloud around zero. Its shape is not a sphere: the joints are correlated, and a fault along one direction costs more than a fault along another. The classical way to measure distance in this cloud is the Mahalanobis distance: subtract the mean, divide by the covariance, take the Euclidean norm of the result. A sample that is far in units of its own distribution is anomalous. The classical estimator uses the sample mean and the sample covariance. Both are sensitive to outliers.
