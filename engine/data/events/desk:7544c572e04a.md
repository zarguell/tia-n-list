Mark Thomas posted the Apache advisory to the announce list, rating the flaw critical and describing the TLS 1.3 TLS-PSK peer validation failure. The message lists the three affected release families, gives TLS 1.2 with TLS-PSK as the immediate mitigation and names 11.0.30, 10.1.63 and 9.0.125 as pending upgrades. It credits Mike Read and records 19 November 2026 as the original advisory date.
