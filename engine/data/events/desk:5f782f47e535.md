Spring's advisory for CVE-2026-59285, published August 20, 2026, describes unsafe deserialization when a Spring for GraphQL application processes paginated GraphQL queries. Spring says exploitation requires Spring GraphQL, Jackson 2.x for JSON deserialization, an exposed paginated Connection field, and specific classes on the classpath that can execute unintended logic during instantiation or deserialization. Affected versions are Spring for GraphQL 2.0.0 through 2.0.4; the listed fixes are 2.0.5 for OSS and 2.0.4.1 for Enterprise Support. The advisory rates the issue HIGH and lists no further mitigation steps beyond upgrading.
