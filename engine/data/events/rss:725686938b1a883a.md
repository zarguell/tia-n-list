This guide covers iPhone, iPad, and Android, from everyday stalkerware to government-grade attacks like Pegasus. What Is Spyware on a Phone? Spyware on a phone is software installed without your informed consent that monitors your activity and reports it to a third party. It differs from normal apps because it hides, and it takes access that no legitimate function requires. Spyware on Phone: Definition in Simple Words Phone spyware is a secret program that records activity on your device and sends it to someone else. That person may be a criminal after your bank login, a partner trying to control you, or a customer of a surveillance vendor. What Spyware Can See and Do on Your Phone Depending on its access, spyware can read texts and chat apps, log calls, track GPS location, copy photos and files, capture passwords and screens, and switch on the camera or microphone.
