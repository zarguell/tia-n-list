watchTowr published a technical analysis of CVE-2024-23113 on 14 October 2024, reverse engineering the format string flaw in FortiGate's fgfmd service and the FGFM protocol over TCP port 541. The post notes the vulnerability affected maintained FortiOS branches before patching and had been highlighted by CISA as exploited in the wild. It credits Fortinet's Gwendal Guégniaud with the discovery and describes reverse engineering the FGFM protocol to reproduce exploitation and build a detection artifact.
