watchTowr Labs researchers Sonny and Aliz Hammond published root-cause analysis and a proof of concept for CVE-2024-55591: a pre-authentication WebSocket to /ws/cli/ passes the local_access_token check, then races the Telnet CLI handshake on localhost port 8023 to inject a login context with an attacker-chosen super_admin profile before the server sends its Connected greeting, demonstrated against FortiGate-VM64-AWS v7.0.16. The post cites Shadowserver counts of nearly 50,000 vulnerable instances and releases a detection script and PoC. It also notes Arctic Wolf's campaign reporting emerged before Fortinet's advisory.
