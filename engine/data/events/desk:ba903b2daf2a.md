Ethiack researchers André Baptista (0xacb), Bruno Mendes (s3np41k1r1t0) and Rafael Castilho published the original disclosure overview of KindaRails2Shell on July 29, 2026, an arbitrary file read and RCE chain in Rails Active Storage with the default vips processor, found independently of RyotaK at GMO Flatt Security who reported it days later. The post lists affected ranges (Rails 6.0.0 to 6.1.7.10 in non-default Active Storage setups, 7.0.0 to 7.2.3.1, 8.0.0 to 8.0.5, 8.1.0 to 8.1.3), notes ImageMagick users are not affected by this vector, and states technical details and PoC were withheld to allow patching. It directs readers to the official Rails advisory and recommends upgrading to 7.2.3.2, 8.0.5.1 or 8.1.3.1, libvips 8.13 or newer, VIPS_BLOCK_UNTRUSTED as a stopgap, and treating WAF rules as time-buying rather than a fix.
