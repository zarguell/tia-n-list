Previdian Research reports lab verification of the guest-to-command chain against Cacti 1.2.31, described as insufficient validation of a guest-writable setting producing a stored second-order SQL injection. The post states no official patch had been identified and Cacti's advisories and the 1.2.31 release notes do not name the `preview_graphs_per_page` setting. It tracks the issue as VULN-2026-0010 and names disabling the guest user as the interim mitigation.
