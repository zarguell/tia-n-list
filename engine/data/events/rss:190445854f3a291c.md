Recorded Future’s 2027 State of Threat Intelligence report surveyed 768 security professionals worldwide on how they use threat intelligence today, how they apply AI to it, and where they plan to invest. The report presents the global findings. The regional executive summaries in this blog cover insights from the same survey, breaking data down by global region into dedicated briefs covering the specific conclusions for cybersecurity leaders in North America , EMEA , APJ , and LATAM . Start with your region to see how your peers are approaching threat intelligence, and read the others for a wider view. North America (United States and Canada) In North America, threat intelligence now informs decisions well beyond the security organization. Five teams consume it on average, and 85% of organizations surveyed have teams outside core security using it , including risk management (54%), fraud prevention (44%), and risk and compliance (44%). With intelligence supporting this many teams, 81% plan to invest more in the next 12 months. The challenge is getting the right intelligence to each of those teams in a form they can act on. Doing that depends on how well intelligence comes together, and this is an ongoing effort for many programs. Most surveyed organizations (63%) draw on four or more intelligence vendors.
