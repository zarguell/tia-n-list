Bill Toulas reports StrikeReady research showing CVE-2025-27915 was exploited as a zero-day starting at the beginning of January 2025, before Zimbra shipped 9.0.0 Patch 44, 10.0.13, and 10.1.5 on January 27, 2025 without mentioning exploitation. The attack spoofed the Libyan Navy's Office of Protocol to hit a Brazilian military organization with a Base64-obfuscated JavaScript payload in an oversized .ICS attachment that stole credentials, email, and contacts, added a mail filter named Correo forwarding to a Proton address, and exfiltrated through the Zimbra SOAP API every four hours. TTP overlap with UNC1151 is noted, and Zimbra told the outlet the activity did not appear widespread.
