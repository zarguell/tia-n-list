SANS ISC recorded live scanning for the EncystPHP webshell on FreePBX systems on April 13, 2026: GET requests to /admin/modules/phones/ajax.php carrying the webshell's hardcoded md5 credential, and /restapps/applications.php exploit requests that download k.php from 45.95.147.178 and reset passwords on ten accounts including root, asterisk, and freepbxuser.
