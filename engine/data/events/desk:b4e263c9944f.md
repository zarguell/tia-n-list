The Hacker News reports on December 18, 2025 that CISA added CVE-2025-59374 (CVSS 9.3) to the KEV catalog, describing it as an embedded malicious code vulnerability from a supply chain attack that occurred between June and November 2018. The article notes the trojanized ASUS Live Update builds were embedded with a hard-coded list of over 600 unique MAC addresses, and ASUS fixed the issue in version 3.6.8. ASUS formally announced end-of-support for the Live Update client as of December 4, 2025, with the final release being version 3.6.15.
