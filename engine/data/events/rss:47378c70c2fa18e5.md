Meet the New Talon: An AI SOC Analyst That Investigates Every Alert, Explains Its Work, and Looks After Your SIEM Rebuilt from the ground up and running inside SOCFortress CoPilot Every SOC knows the arithmetic. Alerts come in faster than analysts can open them, and most of the work on each one is the same: pull the event, check the host, look up the hash, see what happened before, write it up. The judgment calls are where analysts earn their keep. The legwork is what burns them out. Talon is SOCFortress’s AI SOC analyst, and it was built to take on that legwork. The first version showed what was possible. The new Talon is a full rebuild. It is designed to be trusted with real client data, to show its reasoning, and to fit the way a managed SOC actually runs. It lives inside CoPilot, the open-source SOC platform you already use to manage Wazuh, OpenCTI and Velociraptor. Here’s what’s new. Every alert gets a real investigation When CoPilot hands Talon an alert, Talon works it the way an analyst would: Gathers the evidence. It reads the raw event, searches the customer’s own Wazuh indices, checks the host through the Wazuh manager and Velociraptor, and looks up indicators in threat intel. Remembers. It pulls in prior reports on the same host or indicator, and the lessons your analysts have taught it when they reviewed earlier reports. Reasons.
