Vulnerability exploitation is still the leading way in, and the window to fix it is closing. Executive Summary Mandiant and Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) published AI Risk and Resilience 2026 in September. Attackers now use agentic AI for reconnaissance, vulnerability validation, and, in at least one case, a zero-day, yet vulnerability exploitation is still the leading way in for the sixth year running. AI has changed how fast attackers exploit far more than what they exploit. Exploitation is still the front door. Exploits caused 32% of intrusions in M-Trends 2026 , and the mean time to exploit is now an estimated minus seven days. Mandiant concludes that defense has to move beyond manual triage to machine speed. Most AI incidents started as exposures. Poisoned packages, long-lived CI/CD credentials, unsigned plugins and MCP servers, and over-permissioned agents were all in place before the attack. AI infrastructure is now a target in its own right. An autonomous AI agent reached cluster-admin in Hugging Face’s Kubernetes environment with no CVE to match, and exploits for AI components are appearing before disclosure. The Risk Operations Center is the missing layer. The report answers with runtime controls and an agentic SOC, which act after the attacker is inside.
