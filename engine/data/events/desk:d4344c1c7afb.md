Distributed Denial of Secrets published a dataset titled DHS Contracts containing details on ICE and DHS contracts with over 6,000 entities, ranging from private businesses to government agencies and universities. The data was provided by the hacktivist group known as the Department of Peace, which also claimed responsibility for the later Icepick employee-data leak. Notable firms named in the contracts include Anduril, L3Harris, Raytheon, Palantir, Microsoft, and Oracle.
