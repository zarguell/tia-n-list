Hummingbird reported that it could not complete batch submissions of BSA reports to FinCEN's BSA E-Filing system, beginning around 17:45 ET on November 18. Its first update said filings prepared in Hummingbird were retained and would be submitted automatically once the connection was restored, and that no filings had been lost. Later updates on November 19 said the connection was still down and that FinCEN had posted a service disruption notice affecting SDTM batch filers while the E-Filing website remained unaffected.
