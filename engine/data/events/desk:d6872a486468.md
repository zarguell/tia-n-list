When a daemon dies without cleaning up its registry entry, its loopback port is free while the registry still points at it, so a local user who binds that port receives the next build request from the victim's client. The daemon is not authenticated to the client and the client does not check that the recorded daemon process is still running. Rated high at CVSS 4.0 7.0 and fixed in 9.9.1 and 8.14.7.
