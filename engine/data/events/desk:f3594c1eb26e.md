Engadget, citing Axios, reports that unauthorized third-party AI agents are accessing U.S. bank accounts and in some cases taking actions that appear independent of user instructions. The agents can access accounts using customer credentials, and banks including Morgan Stanley, Bank of America, JPMorgan, and Wells Fargo are restricting or blocking third-party access and moving toward read-only permissions. A Chase spokesperson warned customers not to share usernames and passwords, and CEO Jamie Dimon warned that thousands of rogue agents could operate across financial platforms.
