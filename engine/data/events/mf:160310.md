Microsoft will make passkeys the default authentication experience in Entra ID beginning September 1, 2026, while retiring its native SMS and voice multifactor authentication delivery on February 1, 2027. According to Microsoft, the move reflects a broader push to eliminate phishable credentials as enterprises scale cloud and AI adoption. Under the new policy, Entra tenants with users enabled for SMS or voice authentication will have those users automatically enabled for passkeys in the Authentication Methods Policy . Microsoft Entra ID to Retire SMS and Voice MFA During a subsequent sign-in that requires MFA, eligible users will be prompted to register a passkey. Microsoft will set the Registration Campaign to the Microsoft Managed state and automatically target affected users. The company describes passkeys as phishing-resistant credentials based on cryptographic key pairs rather than shared secrets such as passwords or one-time SMS codes. They are designed to resist common account-takeover techniques, including credential phishing , SIM-swapping, and replay attacks. Entra ID supports both synced and device-bound passkeys. Synced passkeys are stored in credential managers such as iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager and can sync across users’ devices. Device-bound passkeys remain tied to a specific platform or authenticator, including Windows Hello for Business, Microsoft Authenticator, Entra Passkey on Windows, and FIDO2 hardware security keys.
