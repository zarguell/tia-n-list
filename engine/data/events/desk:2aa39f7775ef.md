ASUS's official support FAQ, last updated December 6, 2025, states that ASUS LiveUpdate reached end of support on December 4, 2025, with version 3.6.15 as the final release. The page urges users to update to version 3.6.8 or higher to resolve security concerns, and explains how to check and upgrade the Live Update version. It also notes that newer laptops may have ASUS Live Update integrated into MyASUS instead.
