SOCRadar Research has discovered the infrastructure of a Russian-speaking ransomware crew that tests its malware against Microsoft Defender before using it. Of the 17 organizations named in the crew’s own material, 14 are in the United States , including a federal agency, three energy cooperatives and a healthcare provider. The crew checks its builds in its own Microsoft Defender for Endpoint lab. Defender caught one build on October 16, 2026, and isolated the test machine; five days later, a reworked build called back to its C2 server without raising a single alert. The crew gives its AI agent orders in Russian. Told not to bypass Event Tracing for Windows (ETW) or the Antimalware Scan Interface (AMSI), the agent disabled both in its third tool call. The same build server deploys the MetaEncryptor/LostTrust extortion platform, and SOCRadar links the developer and toolkit behind it, with high confidence, to an Eclipse ransomware server. This post is based on SOCRadar Research’s analysis of material associated with the operators’ infrastructure.
