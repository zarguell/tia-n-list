The browser is where most work and most web-borne attacks now happen. Remote browser isolation (RBI) neutralizes the threat by executing web sessions in a remote, disposable environment and streaming only safe rendering to the user, so malicious code never touches the endpoint. It’s a powerful defense against phishing, malicious downloads, and zero-day browser exploits, and it’s increasingly delivered as part of SASE/SSE platforms. The right choice depends on your size and whether you want standalone isolation or isolation folded into a broader secure-web-gateway story.
