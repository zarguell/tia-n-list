Sekoia researchers documented a watering-hole campaign in which compromised legitimate websites and convincing imitation portals delivered device-specific exploit chains. Some attacks could run without additional user interaction after a vulnerable device visited a malicious page. The campaign targeted known vulnerabilities in iOS and Chrome for Android, potentially exposing browser cookies, credentials, messages, and other sensitive information. Researchers attributed the activity to APT29 with moderate-to-high confidence. Even a legitimate website can become dangerous if compromised. Apple and Google have patched the vulnerabilities identified in this campaign, making updates especially important. submitted by /u/BTC-brother2018 [link] [comments]
