Strategic Objectives and Submission Foundations The modernization of the National Vulnerability Database (NVD) marks a critical transition from the legacy era of reactive, manual entry toward an AI-augmented, continuous, and contextual ecosystem. As technology cycles accelerate and AI-enabled threats emerge, the NVD must evolve from a static repository into a dynamic intelligence hub. This submission provides a strategic blueprint for aligning NIST’s modernization efforts with the operational realities of high-scale enterprise environments, ensuring that vulnerability management moves at the speed of the modern software supply chain. The following five pillars form the foundation of this strategic response: Scalability: Expanding processing throughput to accommodate the exponential growth in CVE volume without increasing latency. Automation: Reducing mean-time-to-enrichment (MTTE) by transitioning from manual analyst workflows to machine-driven data ingestion. Interoperability: Architecting data schemas that ensure seamless integration with modern DevOps toolchains, SBOM repositories, and threat intelligence feeds. Transparency: Establishing rigorous audit trails and cryptographic provenance for AI-assisted scoring and prioritization. Utility: Maximizing the actionable value of NVD data for diverse stakeholders, from policy strategists to automated remediation agents.
