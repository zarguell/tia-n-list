On October 16, 2026, at 1:47 p.m. ET (17:47 UTC), ConnectWise published an advisory investigating attempts at unauthorized access associated with the ConnectWise Cloud Backup environment (formerly SkyKick). The post reports MSP observations of rapid tenant connections, data exfiltration of email, Teams, and SharePoint, and a third-party vendor confirming compromise and recommending disabling the application.
