Threat actors are advertising a new “uncensored” artificial intelligence service, dubbed Luciferus, as a subscription-based assistant that can handle malware-development requests mainstream AI platforms typically reject. Sophos Counter Threat Unit (CTU) researchers identified the offering on August 24, 2026, in a post on the Exploit underground forum. The advertisement, published by a user operating under the persona “Optimus_Prime,” targets criminal customers seeking AI-generated malicious code, attack guidance, and fewer operational restrictions than commercial AI services provide. Luciferus Uncensored AI Advertised The forum account was created on April 18 and was marked with a “coding / coder” activity label. By September 4, the account had published 21 posts. The listing describes Luciferus as an AI service with “no moral or ethical restrictions” and claims the platform uses a proprietary 120-billion-parameter model. Sophos said it could not independently confirm the claimed model architecture, parameter count, privacy protections, or the service’s overall performance.
