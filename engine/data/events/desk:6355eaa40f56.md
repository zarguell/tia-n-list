CyberScoop reported that Mandiant traced Armory Castle exploitation of BeyondTrust CVE-2026-102764 to late August and quoted GTIG saying only a small number of organizations were known to be impacted at that time. BeyondTrust said the first known exploitation occurred on 29 August and was detected and contained on 28 September, with patches released to self-hosted and cloud customers in early October. The article notes the group's prior zero-day use against Ivanti and Citrix products and an average dwell time of 400 days in earlier Brickstorm campaigns.
