A series of major technology outages have hit the United States, disrupting email, air travel, banking and government operations. The incidents included a nearly day-long Microsoft 365 outage , a nationwide FAA ground stop , a technical issue at JPMorgan Chase that slowed transactions at other banks, and an outage affecting the NSA’s classified internal network . United Airlines and Apple also experienced disruptions. Officials said there was no evidence of a cyberattack or a link between the incidents. Microsoft traced its outage to a months-old software defect, the FAA blamed a newly installed router, and the NSA cited routine maintenance. Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton, however, linked the NSA disruption to patching for a PHP vulnerability found with the help of AI. Sen. Mark Warner said AI-driven vulnerability discovery is pushing organizations to patch many systems at once, putting strain on infrastructure that wasn’t built for that pace of change. Industry professionals have commented on whether AI-accelerated patching played a role, the need for tested rollback plans, and the fragility of interconnected systems.
