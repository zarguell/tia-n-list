Ubiquiti's advisory, published May 21, 2026 and updated to version 1.1 on May 22, 2026, covers five CVEs across the UniFi OS family: CVE-2026-33000 (CVSS 9.1), CVE-2026-34908 and CVE-2026-34909 (both CVSS 10.0), CVE-2026-34910 (CVSS 10.0), and CVE-2026-34911 (CVSS 7.7). It lists UniFi OS Server 5.0.6 and earlier as affected, fixes in 5.0.8, and gives per-product fixed versions across the hardware lineup (5.1.12 for most gateways and recorders, 5.1.10 for UNAS, 5.1.11 for UDM-Beast, 4.0.14 for Express). Credited researchers include Duc Anh Nguyen, Abdulaziz Almadhi of Catchify Security, John Carroll, V3rlust, and Hakai Security.
