Removable media remains a stubbornly effective route for both malware ingress and data exfiltration a single unmanaged USB stick can carry ransomware in or sensitive data out. Device control (often bundled with data loss prevention) governs which peripherals can connect, what they can do, and whether transferred data is logged, encrypted, or blocked. The right tool depends on your size and data-protection ambitions: small teams need simple USB allow/block; mid-market wants granular policy plus content-aware DLP; enterprises need cross-platform control integrated with a broader data-protection program. This guide reviews eight leading device control and USB security tools in depth features, best fit, pros, and cons mapped to business size. Pricing is described by model only.
