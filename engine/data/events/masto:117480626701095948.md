Cybersecurity alert in Europe: several government sites in France, the Netherlands and Finland taken offline.

Several government websites in France, the Netherlands and Finland were temporarily taken offline on Wednesday after the discovery of two critical PHP vulnerabilities that could allow cyberattacks.

PHP developers announced on Tuesday that they had identified "two critical vulnerabilities" and recommended that users "update as soon as it becomes available". Publication of a patch fixing the two security issues was scheduled for Wednesday at 14:00 GMT, 17:00 in Romania, according to news.ro.

According to Dane Knecht, chief technology officer of Cloudflare, hackers have already tried to exploit the vulnerabilities. "We are seeing the first attacks appear and we are adapting our protective measures as new vectors emerge", he wrote on X. He stressed that the protective measures implemented are only a provisional solution.

PHP is used by many website management systems, including WordPress, Drupal, Joomla and Magento. At this stage all PHP versions from version 5 onward could be affected, though developers have not specified which versions are actually vulnerable.

In France many sites were inaccessible on Wednesday, including all the French prefecture websites, according to Le Figaro. The French Interior Ministry confirmed the measure in a statement published on Wednesday. "As a precautionary measure, the prefecture websites were temporarily taken offline, pending publication of a security patch by the publisher", the agency said, according to Reuters. Similar measures were adopted in the Netherlands and Finland. The number of affected sites and the duration of the interruption to the French prefecture services were not specified.
