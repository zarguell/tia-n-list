N-able Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud services experienced a days-long disruption across all regions after hackers compromised one of the company’s servers. The disruption began on November 16, and N-able initially blamed it on “a combination of platform configuration and service dependencies”. Mail flow and filtering were not affected, but related services, such as logging, mailbox synchronization, the management portal, and quarantine, were taken offline. On November 18, the company confirmed that a cyberattack was behind the disruption, noting that it found no evidence that customer data was compromised. “Our investigation determined that the incident was isolated to a single web server, where an unauthorized party may have been able to access certain credentials stored on the platform,” the company told SecurityWeek . N-able says its restoration and remediation efforts continue. While inbound and outbound filtering are operating, access to its portal and API remains disabled. “We took immediate action to contain the incident, including taking the affected server offline, rotating internal platform credentials, revoking existing customer API tokens, invalidating OAuth tokens, and proactively resetting passwords for local users,” N-able said.
