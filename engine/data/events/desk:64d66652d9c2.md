Eye Security disclosed Operation IXIM, in which between November 2025 and March 2026 it registered lapsed Time4Popcorn update domains and pushed a harmless update that disabled the updater on more than two million systems. The research traces the flaw to an HTTP updater with no signature verification that executes supplied binaries as SYSTEM or root, and estimates roughly 2.5-3.0 million Windows and 250,000-350,000 macOS hosts. The 15 domains Eye controlled were later transferred to The Shadowserver Foundation for sinkholing.
