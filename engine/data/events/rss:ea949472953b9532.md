TL;DR 4,064 high-impact leaks affected 1,588 companies: GitGuardian identified these exposures over five months, with 45.6% of the leaks rated critical. 62% of previously valid credentials still worked after notification: Of the credentials confirmed working when first discovered, nearly two-thirds still worked when retested after notification. 77.8% of public leaks remained online: Among leaks that were public when we emailed affected companies, nearly four in five were still publicly accessible as of late November. Only 35% of companies replied: Most organizations we contacted did not respond to our warnings. High-impact leaks had been public for a median of 320 days: Nearly half (46.2%) had already been exposed for more than a year before we flagged them. 5% of leaks originated with suppliers or contractors: A third party exposed credentials belonging to another organization, meaning the person who committed the secret wasn't necessarily part of the company whose systems were at risk. Since June 2026, we've identified 4,064 high-impact leaks tied to 1,588 companies and these are the findings from the first five months of GitGuardian's Enterprise Good Samaritan Program . We warned companies that their credentials were exposed in public code. When we checked back, 62% of the valid credentials still worked. The exposed code often remained online, too.
