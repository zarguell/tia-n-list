The lead advisory for the chain rates the flaw CVSS 3.1 9.8 with vector AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H, CWE-862. ConditionalModifier and ConditionalTalesModifier in Modifiers.py, plus the size-guard modifiers AbortVersioningOfLargeFilesAndImages and SkipVersioningOfLargeFilesAndImages in StandardModifiers.py, declare no security, so methods with docstrings including edit are publishable without a permission check and an anonymous visitor can change a modifier's TALES condition and then run it. The fix protects edit with Manage portal and the accessors with View management screens, and makes the size-guard hooks private.
