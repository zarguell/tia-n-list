Mathspace, an online mathematics learning platform used by schools in Australia and New Zealand, has reported a data breach affecting 1,079,819 students, parents or guardians, teachers, and staff members. The company stated that attackers exploited a critical vulnerability in its self-hosted Metabase reporting environment, allowing them to gain administrator-level access without legitimate credentials.
