The Defense Department’s top zero trust official is leaving government service at the end of this week, according to an email obtained by Nextgov/FCW . Randy Resnick, who led development of the department’s framework for adopting the cybersecurity approach across its networks, will retire Friday, he told colleagues in the email sent late Tuesday night. Resnick oversaw the Pentagon’s push to overhaul how it protects military networks and meet zero trust requirements by the end of fiscal 2027. The approach follows a “never trust, always verify” principle, assuming hackers may already be inside a network and limiting their access to sensitive data through continual verification and tighter controls.
