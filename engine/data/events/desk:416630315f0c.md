The joint FBI/DHS technical alert, released April 16, 2018, assessed that Russian state-sponsored actors exploited large numbers of enterprise and SOHO routers and switches worldwide, abusing GRE, SNMP (161/162), HTTP (80) and Cisco Smart Install (port 4786), and used compromised routers for man-in-the-middle attacks and persistent access. It advised hunting for TCP traffic to port 4786 from anything other than trusted management hosts and noted attackers harvest password hashes and SNMP community strings from stolen configuration files.
