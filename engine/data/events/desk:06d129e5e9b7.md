Kuan-Ting Chen's fix, dated May 4, 2026 in the netdev net tree, documents the ESP-half root cause: MSG_SPLICE_PAGES can attach pipe pages directly to an skb, the IPv4/IPv6 datagram append paths did not set SKBFL_SHARED_FRAG, and ESP input then took the no-COW fast path and decrypted in place over pages the skb did not own. The patch marks spliced frags with SKBFL_SHARED_FRAG and forces ESP input back to skb_cow_data() when the flag is present. No distribution kernel shipped this change at the time of the Dirty Frag disclosure.
