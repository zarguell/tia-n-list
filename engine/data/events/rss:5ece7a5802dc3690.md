Norwegian energy companies Equinor and Skagerak Energi are among the organizations affected by a Microsoft cloud security vulnerability that hackers have exploited to target sensitive credentials, Norway's cybersecurity authorities said. The vulnerability affected Azure API Management (APIM), a Microsoft service that allows organizations to manage and secure connections between applications and other systems. According to Norway's National Security Authority (NSM), an unidentified threat actor has been actively targeting access tokens and other sensitive information stored in the platform. Equinor, one of Europe's largest natural gas suppliers, and electricity producer Skagerak Energi both reported cyberattacks last week. The vulnerability was first discovered by Norway's energy sector, with Equinor playing a key role in identifying the issue and alerting authorities, the agency said on Friday. Equinor said it detected the attack last Tuesday but did not fully contain it until Friday. The incident disrupted some internal services, although the company's oil and gas production continued normally. "There is no indication that data has been stolen or altered," Equinor said in its latest statement , adding that the attack did not appear to have specifically targeted the company. Skagerak Energi, which produces and distributes electricity and is majority-owned by Norway's state-owned renewable energy company Statkraft, disclosed a separate cyberattack on Wednesday.
