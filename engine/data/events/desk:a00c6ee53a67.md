SecOps Signal reported VulnCheck's research on 16 November 2026, describing roughly 375,904 exposed FGFM management ports on TCP 541 that leak firmware information through TLS handshakes. The article says the 30-day measurement ran from 14 October to 12 November 2026 and was queried on 13 November. It frames the risk as vulnerability mapping against internet-facing FortiGate devices and notes Fortinet's guidance that the port should not be public.
