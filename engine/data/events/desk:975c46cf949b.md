Arian Eidizadeh published this write-up on August 15, 2026, reproducing the public authentication-bypass chain on Cisco Secure FMC 10.0.1-1 and confirming uid=0(root) execution. It documents reuse of the boot-created csm_processes session, the report:snortrules machine credential, the /var/tmp/license.tmp write via validateLicense, and a post-exploitation path that read /etc/vault/token on the appliance to recover a working LDAP service credential from a local Vault.
