Original researcher disclosure by Group-IB reverse engineer Bruno Bijelic, published per the Group-IB blog feed on September 23, 2026, about five hours before this story's first event. Group-IB documents RemControl as a previously undocumented Android banking trojan run as a Malware-as-a-Service platform, with first samples on VirusTotal dated July 19, 2026 and the C2 domain bnbnhura[.]top registered May 12, 2026. The dropper requests BIND_VPN_SERVICE to tunnel com.android.vending into a null tunnel, cutting off Play Protect real-time checks, and generates a unique signing certificate per installation with subject CN=Android Debug, O=Android, C=US. The report confirms more than 30 phishing overlay targets across Italy, France, Spain, Poland, Portugal, Canada, and GCC member states, tracks the operator as UNKK, and notes a possible link to the Medusa UNKN affiliate botnet.
