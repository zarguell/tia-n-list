Original researcher disclosure by Group-IB reverse engineer Bruno Bijelic, timestamped Wed, 23 Sep 2026 07:26:04 GMT in the Group-IB blog feed, about five hours before this story's first event. It documents RemControl as a previously undocumented Android banking trojan run as a Malware-as-a-Service platform, tracked to operator UNKK, with first VirusTotal samples on July 19, 2026 and C2 domain bnbnhura[.]top registered May 12, 2026. The dropper requests BIND_VPN_SERVICE and routes com.android.vending into a null tunnel to suppress Play Protect real-time checks, then signs each payload with a fresh certificate under subject CN=Android Debug, O=Android, C=US. It confirms more than 30 phishing overlay targets across Italy, France, Spain, Poland, Portugal, Canada and GCC member states, and assesses a possible link to the Medusa UNKN affiliate botnet.
