An internal network the National Security Agency uses to communicate classified information was offline Tuesday after what the agency described as “routine maintenance.” The problem disrupted the operations of NSANet, a classified internal network used by employees of the agency, according to a defense official who was not authorized to discuss the matter by name and spoke to The Associated Press on the condition of anonymity. “The National Security Agency experienced temporary degradation during scheduled routine maintenance,” the NSA said in a statement. “While some internal systems were affected, mission-critical capabilities remain fully operational and our ability to defend national security continues. NSA’s infrastructure is designed with multiple layers of redundancy to maintain operations during planned maintenance or unexpected events.” The intelligence agency did not provide any additional details on the cause of the outage or the scope of the disruption. The outage was first reported by CNN. The NSA episode is among a series of cyber-related disruptions to critical government and private sector functions in the last week, including a system overload Friday that resulted in a ground stop of air traffic in the United States, a technical issue at JPMorgan Chase on Monday that caused ripple effects in the financial system and an internal systems outage at United Airlines that delayed departing flights.
