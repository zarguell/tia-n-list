AI has changed everything and turned the landscape upside down. In my day-to-day role as Global Consumer Operations Manager at Panda Security, I see firsthand how this technology has redefined cybersecurity for SMEs. On one hand, it optimizes cybercriminals’ processes by allowing them to automate attacks against small and medium-sized businesses , identify vulnerabilities at lightning speed, and customize entry points. On the other hand, it equips SMEs with advanced detection and mitigation capabilities that were previously out of reach due to cost. A lack of budget, specialized personnel, and proper tools has traditionally left SMEs in a vulnerable position. Today, I observe attackers using AI to analyze networks, exploit vulnerabilities, and execute mass credential theft . Understanding how these techniques work is the essential first step to stopping them. How AI Is Transforming Cyberattacks In recent months, I have verified that artificial intelligence has not only increased the volume of cyberattacks. But also drastically reduced their execution time.
