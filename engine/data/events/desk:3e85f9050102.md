Nicolas Dorier released BTCPay Server 2.4.7 on October 29, 2026 as an emergency security update. It fixes 85 security findings from Anthropic's Frontier OSS scanner, rated 1 critical, 13 high, 52 medium and 19 low, spanning merchant isolation, payouts, authentication and payment accounting. The release notes say updating is highly recommended, especially for servers shared by multiple merchants.
