Check Point Research analyzed operator-side artifacts from ARTEX deployments and found 2,557 systems hosting or having hosted ARTEX panels, with 1,608 live public instances at the time of research; ten instances exposed the full ARTEX database. The exposed databases showed operators mostly using Chinese LLM families, led by DeepSeek Flash, for campaigns aimed at cryptocurrency theft, code-signing certificate theft, gaming credentials and denial of service. Check Point noted guardrails appearing only with Anthropic's Opus 5.5, after which an operator switched to more permissive models.
