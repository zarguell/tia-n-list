A customer copy of LiteSpeed's urgent advisory was posted on 4 November, with a separate section for the PrestaShop cache module. It says all versions before v1.6.2 are affected, an unauthenticated attacker could send crafted requests that cause the server to process attacker-supplied content, and v1.6.2 or later is the fix. For stores that may have been exposed, the advisory directs operators to review administrator accounts and installed modules for unfamiliar entries and to rotate the database and other credentials stored in the site configuration. The notice said a CVE was forthcoming.
