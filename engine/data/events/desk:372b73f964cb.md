Forbes reported on November 9, 2026 that Fireworks, a popular infrastructure provider helping enterprises and startups build and run AI models, said some customers' datasets and models were accessed by hackers, marking its second known hack in weeks. The article notes two customers told Forbes they had already moved their business off Fireworks systems, with one describing a series of very bad hacks and poor communication. Forbes ties the incident to LLMJacking, where attackers steal access to AI models and resell it without permission.
