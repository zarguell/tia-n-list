Researcher ek0ms savi0r, publishing under Church of Malware, uploaded the original writeup and proof-of-concept code for 4HTTP2SHELL (also called 4HTTP2ROOT). The chain moves from a fresh anonymous session to a shell in four requests: a guest write of `preview_graphs_per_page`, a stacked `UPDATE` of `poller_item.arg1` through the `graph_view.php` preview LIMIT clause, then `graph_realtime.php?action=init` reaching `popen()`. It was demonstrated against Cacti 1.2.31 in a lab where the web tier ran as root.
