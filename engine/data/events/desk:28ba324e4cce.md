From 0.7.1 through 2.2.1, the /auth/saml/callback endpoint decoded SAML XML, used its NameID and attributes to resolve or provision a user, and minted a gpustack_session JWT without verifying signature, issuer, audience, destination, or time conditions. Any network client could submit an identity for an existing user including an administrator, or provision a new account, even when SAML was not configured; captured signed responses could be replayed. Fixed in 2.2.2; reported by Austin Chu, Sohee Kim, and Corban Villa (UC Berkeley).
