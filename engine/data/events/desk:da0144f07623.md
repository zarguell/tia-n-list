Mitel's MISA-2026-0012 disclosed five unauthenticated AWV flaws in MiCollab, including CVE-2026-133324, and rates the authentication bypass Medium at CVSS 5.3. Supported MiCollab 10.3 (10.3.0.18) and earlier are affected; the remedy is to upgrade to 10.3.0.18 and apply the KB000129195 patch, or move to a later release. Mitel advises restricting external access to AWV until the fix is implemented.
