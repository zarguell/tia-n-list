Sploitus indexed a public Python PoC for CVE-2026-118383 by researcher googlesky, first published to the index on 2026-11-11 at 05:59:43 UTC, the day after MongoDB's advisory. The write-up pins the root cause to src/mongo/db/repl/replication_coordinator_impl.cpp: a CatchupState completion callback frees the object from inside abort_inlock() and the outer frame keeps using it, while two readConcern.afterOpTime waiters with adversarial opTime terms keep the real catch-up waiter from ever being satisfied. It records the two fixes in 8.0.37, commit 23dfc542199ab3b88d6a9420b6cd3f125a897620 for SERVER-138175 (shared_ptr ownership of CatchupState) and commit 6111165c32bfc74f197e2dfeb9480d0b52154d68 for SERVER-138119 (reject client-supplied afterOpTime without the internal ActionType), tested on 8.0.34, 8.2.12, and 8.0.37.
