Apple has issued a new wave of high-confidence Threat Notifications to selected iPhone users in 110 countries, warning that their devices may have been individually targeted by mercenary spyware. The notifications signal suspected activity associated with exceptionally capable, well-funded surveillance operations, not routine phishing, commodity malware, or broad criminal campaigns. Apple says threat notifications are intended for people believed to be targeted because of “who they are or what they do.” Apple Sends Spyware Attack Alerts The company characterizes mercenary spyware operations as vastly more complex than typical cybercrime: attackers dedicate exceptional resources to a very small pool of victims, exploits can be expensive and short-lived, and the activity evolves quickly to evade detection. Public research has historically linked such attacks to state actors and private surveillance vendors. Pegasus, developed by NSO Group , is one prominent example cited by Apple, although the company does not attribute individual notifications to a particular spyware family, customer, government, or country. Apple Sends Spyware Attack Alerts (Source: CSN) Apple has issued these alerts multiple times each year since 2021 and says it has notified users in more than 150 countries overall.
