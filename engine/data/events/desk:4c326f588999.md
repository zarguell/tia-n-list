Original disclosure: on August 31 a new identity theft service called Nexus advertised on the Exploit forum access to scans of more than 153 million US and Canadian driver's licenses, plus 10 million ID cards, 3 million travel documents and 579,000 medical cards. Krebs verified records via consenting victims, matched scan timestamps (in GMT) to Hertz rentals and Planet13 dispensary visits, and observed roughly 400,000 records added in 24 hours, with sellers claiming over a year of continuous exfiltration. The FBI's New Orleans field office opened an official investigation into idscan.net the same day; Nexus went dark hours after publication.
