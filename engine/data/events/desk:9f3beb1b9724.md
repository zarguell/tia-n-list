CISA added CVE-2025-53521, F5 BIG-IP Stack-Based Buffer Overflow Vulnerability, to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on March 27, 2026 with a required-action due date of March 30, 2026, based on evidence of active exploitation. The entry directs agencies to F5's K000156741 and K000160486 guidance and to check internet-accessible F5 products for signs of compromise. Federal Civilian Executive Branch agencies were required to apply mitigations or discontinue use by the due date.
