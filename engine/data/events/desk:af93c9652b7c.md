RescuePHP covered CVE-2026-124251 on 11 November 2026, describing a CWE-352 flaw with CVSS 3.1 score 8.8 and vector AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H that needs no attacker account but a privileged user action. Citing the WordPress.org changelog, it says 3.6.4 on 8 November enforced request verification on all WPIDE admin pages, while 3.6.5 on the same day also rejected plain-text and form-encoded bodies for data-changing requests at Patchstack's suggestion. It recommends updating to 3.6.5 or later, removing the plugin when the editor is unnecessary, setting DISALLOW_FILE_EDIT in wp-config.php and reviewing recently modified PHP files after suspected exposure.
