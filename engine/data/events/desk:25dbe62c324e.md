Halcyon updated its KillSec threat profile on 13 November 2025, documenting the group as a Ransomware-as-a-Service operation that formalized its platform in June 2024. The profile records over 250 documented compromises, a $250 entry fee with 88% affiliate retention, and primary infection vectors exploiting misconfigured AWS S3 buckets and CVE-2025-31161. It notes the group reached over 250 victims with geographic concentration in India, USA, and Latin America, and had active operations through November 2025.
