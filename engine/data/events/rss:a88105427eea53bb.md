What is the Health Care Cybersecurity and Resiliency Act? The Health Care Cybersecurity Act, formally the Health Care Cybersecurity and Resiliency Act of 2026 (S. 3315) , is a U.S. Senate bill that directs the Secretary of Health and Human Services (HHS) to update the HIPAA Security Rule around minimum, risk-based cybersecurity practices, and directs HHS and the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) to coordinate on defending the Healthcare and Public Health Sector [1].
