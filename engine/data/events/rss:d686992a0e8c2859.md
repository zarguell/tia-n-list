The Tor Project has released a security update for its network relay software to increase defenses against an ongoing distributed denial-of-service attack that it says has overwhelmed relays and affected most operators. Tor version 0.4.9.17 includes a series of security fixes, including one the project described as especially important for relay denial-of-service defenses involving IPv6 handling. “This release comes in rapid response to the ongoing DDoS attack on the network, which has affected most relay operators,” the Tor Project said in its release announcement . The project said a massive ongoing flood of client circuits has overwhelmed relays across the privacy network, causing a problem that has been difficult to address. The project says this update includes a security fix to improve relay defenses against denial-of-service attacks. "One fix in particular is especially important for relay DoS defenses related to IPv6 handling," explains the project. Tor is working on additional defenses and expects to issue further releases in the near future. Relay operators are urged to upgrade to version 0.4.9.17 as soon as possible. The project said no additional action is required after installing the update. The announcement did not provide any other details as to who is behind the DDoS attacks or when they began.
