French market research company Ipsos is investigating a breach that hit one of its internal information systems. The company made the incident public on Monday, November 16, five days after filing a complaint with authorities. According to Ipsos, the breach has not disrupted its business. “This incident had no impact on our operations or our ability to deliver for our clients,” the company said. After detecting the incident, Ipsos launched its response process and worked to contain it, bringing in outside security experts to assist with the probe. “The investigation is ongoing to determine the nature and scope of the incident,” Ipsos said . The company has not said whether any data was accessed or stolen. “We take protection of the information entrusted to us very seriously. Should the investigation establish that information relating to clients, projects or business partners has been affected, we will contact those concerned directly,” Ipsos said. The market research giant has not revealed when it detected the breach, which system was affected, or how the breach occurred. Ipsos said it could not provide further comment while the probe is underway. Established in France in 1975, Ipsos ranks among the biggest market research firms globally.
