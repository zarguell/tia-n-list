First news coverage of the breach: the attack began Friday night when ShinyHunters exploited what it claims is an unauthenticated file upload vulnerability in Grav CMS to plant a text file on Clop's Tor leak site, then replaced the site with an Umbreon defacement. ShinyHunters claims full server access, theft of source code, Grav plugins, all files under /var/log, and the private keys of Clop's onion service, and demanded an eight-figure payment rising every 24 hours. Page shows 'September 19, 2026 09:48 AM' US Eastern; the site's RSS feed confirms the -0400 offset, giving 13:48 UTC.
