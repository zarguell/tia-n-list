The Dutch National Cyber Security Centre published advisory NCSC-2026-0365, initial version 1.0.0 on September 10, 2026 at 09:12 Europe/Amsterdam, covering CVE-2026-85102 and CVE-2026-85103 in Quantum Security Gateway and Security Management, both CVSS 9.8. The NCSC stated it expected large-scale exploitation attempts in the short term and urged organizations to follow Check Point's advisory urgently; a September 23 revision recorded Check Point's confirmation that abuse of CVE-2026-85102 had been observed.
