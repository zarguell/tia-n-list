The refrigeration cyberattack campaign that hit about 700 French supermarkets on October 11, 2026, is now a multi-country pattern of attacks on critical infrastructure. A second wave struck food businesses in 13 countries on November 2, cold rooms at a contractor for France’s national blood service were set to 110°C (230°F), and three UK meat suppliers were hit through Copeland XWEB controllers. On November 5, five US agencies warned of ongoing attacks on refrigeration and cold chain systems. SOCRadar has followed each stage as it broke, from the French supermarket cyberattack to the freezer attacks in Belgium, Sweden and Luxembourg and the FBI and CISA refrigeration warning. This analysis links these events, tracing the campaign from the 2025 Danfoss disclosures through its malware-free attack chain and attribution claims to the US – covering military commissary failures, federal warnings, past intrusions by these groups, SOCRadar exposure data, and impacts in Canada and the UK. The US angle matters more than it first appeared.
