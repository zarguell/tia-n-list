Google Threat Intelligence Group attributed the NOROBOT downloader (disclosed by Zscaler as BAITSWITCH) and the YESROBOT and MAYBEROBOT backdoors to COLDRIVER, noting the actor began re-arming five days after the May 2025 LOSTKEYS disclosure.
