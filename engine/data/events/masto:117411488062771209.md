A double-extortion ransomware attack targeted Costa Rican government entities, affecting more than 27 vital government institutions including the Ministry of Finance. The attackers demanded payment under threat of leaking stolen data. This is part of a broader trend of ransomware groups targeting government infrastructure in Central America.
