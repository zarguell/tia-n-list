CyberInsider reports that Eye Research delivered more than two million update downloads to disable an abandoned Popcorn Time updater that could have allowed attackers to hijack Windows and macOS systems with full privileges. The vulnerability CVE-2026-30612 affected the Time4Popcorn fork, whose update mechanism trusted unsigned executables delivered over unencrypted HTTP, including via expired domains. Android installations received no intervention because updates there require user interaction.
