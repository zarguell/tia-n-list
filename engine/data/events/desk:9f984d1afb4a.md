Check Point disclosed on July 22, 2026 that CVE-2026-16232, an authentication bypass in SmartConsole login using an application token, had been exploited in the wild against a handful of customers whose Management server was exposed directly to the internet without IP restrictions. The advisory says Smart-1 Cloud customers were already protected and lists fixes in the July 22 jumbo hotfix: R82.10 Take 36, R82 Take 118 and R81.20 Take 158. It also lists six IP addresses observed in exploitation and recommends limiting Trusted Clients and restricting management access with a firewall.
