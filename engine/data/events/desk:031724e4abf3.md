Kaspersky's Cyber Threat Intelligence team identified a malicious campaign in which C2 agents controlled by an LLM-driven CyberStrikeAI framework targeted educational organizations across Africa and Latin America. The framework's implant is a conventional C2 beacon with no AI logic, while an LLM orchestrator on the control server plans actions, selects tools, and generates commands. Kaspersky published the analysis on November 2, 2026, roughly three hours before the story's first_seen timestamp, detailing reconnaissance, persistence, credential dumping, lateral movement, and data exfiltration completed within 3 to 4 days.
