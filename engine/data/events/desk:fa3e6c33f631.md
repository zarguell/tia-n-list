Samsung's rewards program annual report, published March 16, 2026, states that in 2025 Meta Security alerted Samsung that vulnerabilities in a specific media library used in its devices were being exploited in the wild, that Samsung addressed the zero-day immediately and raised the severity of a one-day, and that Google Project Zero and Google's Threat Analysis Group then reported further vulnerabilities in the same component. The report also notes the program protects hundreds of millions of Samsung Galaxy devices worldwide.
