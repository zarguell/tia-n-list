Unit 42 profiled The Gentlemen, also tracked as Storm-2697, as a ransomware-as-a-service program active since at least July 2025 that grew out of an affiliate relationship with Qilin. It counted 580 claimed victims across 77 countries through July 7, 2026, including 103 in manufacturing, with 117 claims in June 2026 alone. The report lists exploited bugs CVE-2024-55591, CVE-2025-32433, CVE-2025-33073 and CVE-2025-55182, plus CVE-2025-7771 in ThrottleStop.sys, and recommends alerts on scheduled tasks matching the string gentlemen*, EDR tamper protection, and monitoring for wevtutil, vssadmin and wmic abuse.
