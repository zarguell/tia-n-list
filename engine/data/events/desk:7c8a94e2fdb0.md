OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber, a cybersecurity-tuned model rated Critical under its Preparedness Framework, available initially through the API and Codex to approved Daybreak Red customers. Its researchers used it to find 81 previously unknown high-severity vulnerabilities in Firefox and to develop working exploits for 948 Linux kernel privilege-escalation bugs. The model scored 48.5 percent on ExploitGym versus 32.1 percent for GPT-5.6 Cyber and avoided out-of-scope exploitation in 99.93 percent of cases.
