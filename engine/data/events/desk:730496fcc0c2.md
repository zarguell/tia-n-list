Cleafy Labs report published June 20, 2024 documenting the Medusa UNKN botnet, which targeted European users with campaigns focused on Italy and France, was installed via droppers downloaded from untrusted sources, and resolved its C2 address through social media dead-drops including Telegram. Group-IB's RemControl report cites this work: droppers sharing the instal*tvtap*.apk naming convention carried Medusa UNKN payloads until July 2026, then switched to RemControl, and the UNKN tag differs by one letter from RemControl's UNKK affiliate tag. This is the earliest reporting on the distribution channel RemControl now rides on.
