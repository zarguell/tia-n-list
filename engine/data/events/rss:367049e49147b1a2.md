The FBI and the U.S. Department of Justice have disrupted FaceStealer , a North Korean social engineering campaign that used hijacked accounts, replayed webcam footage and fake video calls to steal more than $400 million in cryptocurrency over the past 18 months. The DOJ announced on November 12, 2026, that it had seized cloud-based infrastructure behind the operation, and the FBI seized more than 900 webcam videos of victims under a federal warrant. The same announcement covered a separate action: court authorization to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion from the October 29 Binance theft , which the FBI attributes to the North Korean cluster it calls TraderTraitor . A Meeting Invite From a Familiar Contact FaceStealer does not start with a stranger. The lure arrives from a real account belonging to an earlier victim, which makes the invitation look routine.
