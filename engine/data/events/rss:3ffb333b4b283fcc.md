Ralph Losey discusses the evolving relationship between information, knowledge, and wisdom, emphasizing that wisdom is an ongoing process involving learning from actions and consequences. He advocates for a collaborative human-machine approach to enhance understanding, warning against the dangers of misinformation and the need for accountability in AI systems. A focus on reversibility and contestability is vital to developing a wisdom-based infrastructure that improves decision-making and fosters cooperation.
