Zscaler ThreatLabz analyzed an exploitation campaign in which a threat actor abused ARTEX, an open source agentic penetration testing framework developed in China that uses LLMs to automate reconnaissance and exploitation, to target South Korean financial institutions. First observed in late September 2026, the campaign affected multiple South Korean financial firms and resulted in exfiltration of personally identifiable information belonging to approximately 67,000 individuals. ThreatLabz assesses with moderate confidence the campaign is the work of a financially motivated, Chinese-speaking threat actor.
