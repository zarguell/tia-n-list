Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 details the CL-STA-1114 campaign using a zero-click Zimbra XSS (CVE-2025-66376) that started in July 2025. The payload uses an invisible SVG with Base64-encoded and XOR-encrypted JavaScript to collect CSRF tokens, email archives, 2FA scratch codes, environment details, and organization directories. Nine C2 IP addresses and nine domains (e.g., zmailanalytics.com, zimbra-metadata.com) were observed, with servers active for an average of 35.4 days.
