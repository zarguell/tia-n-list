Joint advisory from CISA, FBI, NSA, and 13+ international partners confirms LAUNDRY BEAR (also Void Blizzard / CL-STA-1114) has exploited CVE-2025-66376 in Zimbra Collaboration Suite since July 2025. The zero-click XSS exploit requires only viewing a malicious email and exfiltrates 90 days of email, passwords, 2FA tokens, GAL, and newly created application passcodes. The vulnerability was patched in November 2025 (ZCS 10.1.13 and 10.0.18) but exploitation continues against unpatched instances.
