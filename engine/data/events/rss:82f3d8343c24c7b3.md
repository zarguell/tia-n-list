What are Amazon scams? Amazon scams are attempts to trick you by using Amazon’s name or brand. Scammers may contact you through a text, email or phone call, or use a fake listing to steal your money, login details or personal information. Amazon is woven into the everyday shopping of millions of people, so its name carries a lot of weight. That makes it an easy disguise for scammers. Amazon scams can show up in emails, texts, phone calls and fake product listings, all designed to look like something connected to your account or a purchase. In 2025, 28% of Amazon impersonation scams reported by customers globally involved attempts to gain access to their accounts. The goal is to get your money, your account password, or your personal information . Some scams create a sense of urgency, while others rely on a deal that looks convincing. We’ll break down 14 common Amazon scams and the signs that can help you spot them. Why Amazon Is One of the Most Impersonated Brands Amazon gives scammers a useful disguise because so many people shop on the platform and expect regular deliveries.
