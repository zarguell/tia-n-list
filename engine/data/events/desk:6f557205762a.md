Canonical's grouped LXD advisory describes CVE-2026-127163 as omission of TLS server verification in pylxd WebSockets for `Instance.execute()` and `Client.events()`, enabling an active man-in-the-middle to intercept stdin and spoof output or event streams. It rates the issue High 7.4/10 and says the release fixes all listed critical and high LXD vulnerabilities in 4.0.16, 5.0.12, 5.21.10 and 6.10.2, initially through candidate channels.
