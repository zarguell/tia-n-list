The Justice Department announced that Roman Khlynovskiy, 43, a Kazak national, made his initial appearance in Knoxville federal court after extradition from Italy. The indictment alleges he was a member of the 8-Digits Team, affiliated with INC and Lynx ransomware, and that co-conspirators targeted victims in at least six states, exfiltrated data, then demanded payment in exchange for promises not to publish it. DOJ quotes the conspirators claiming more than 50 terabytes of data and over 70 million confidential health records, with over $500 million in ransom demands. He faces five charges with a maximum penalty of 27 years; the release credits Italian, Ukrainian and Lithuanian authorities with assisting the arrest and extradition.
