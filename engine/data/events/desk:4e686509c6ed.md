Proofpoint attributed a March 26, 2026 email campaign using Atlantic Council lures to Star Blizzard, marking the first time the group was observed targeting iCloud accounts and Apple devices. Messages sent from compromised sender addresses redirected iPhone browsers to the DarkSword exploit kit, which had leaked on GitHub, for credential harvesting and intelligence collection. Proofpoint also noted a significant increase in malicious email volume over the preceding two weeks.
