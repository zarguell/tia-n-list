CISA added CVE-2025-3248, listed as Langflow Missing Authentication Vulnerability, to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog on May 5, 2025 with a federal remediation due date of May 26, 2025. KEV listing requires evidence of in-the-wild exploitation, placing confirmed abuse of the flaw more than a year before the JADEPUFFER report. The addition date is confirmed in the KEV table on the NVD detail page.
