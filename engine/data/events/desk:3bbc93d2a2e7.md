Onchain investigator ZachXBT first identified Crédit Mutuel Arkéa as the alleged victim of a cryptocurrency theft estimated at more than $133 million, naming the bank on November 3, 2026. He traced withdrawals from several centralized exchange accounts and published Ethereum addresses he attributes to the theft, with funds allegedly swapped into ether and Monero. CoinDesk reported ZachXBT first put losses above $106 million before adding another $27 million in exchange withdrawals. The disclosure predates the bank's public confirmation by roughly two days.
