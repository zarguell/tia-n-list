ARTEX is the open source AI agent behind the first agentic attack on South Korean banks. Seven South Korean financial institutions, including Shinhan Bank, KB Kookmin and Hana Bank, were breached by intrusions that police and the Financial Security Institute link to ARTEX, a Chinese-developed open source AI-agent penetration testing tool. Between 65,000 and 68,000 people are estimated affected, and the key forensic artifact was a Chinese control-console string left on compromised web servers.
