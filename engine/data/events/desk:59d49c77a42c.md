Nine reported that Coles was investigating a cybersecurity incident while stores, the website and app, Flybuys and gift cards remained usable. Coles acknowledged issues with Liquorland click-and-collect for a small number of customers and said it had found no evidence that customer, team or supplier data was compromised. The company said it was working with the Australian Cyber Security Centre and global cybersecurity experts, and advised customers to watch for suspicious links and SMS messages.
