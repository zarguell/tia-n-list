The Eastern District of Texas bankruptcy court posted a service-disruption notice on October 13, 2026. It states that late on Friday, October 9, 2026, BAE Systems notified the Administrative Office of a disruption to the Bankruptcy Noticing Center systems and related websites, including bnc.uscourts.gov, bankruptcynotices.uscourts.gov, notices.uscourts.gov, and the National Creditor Registration Service.
