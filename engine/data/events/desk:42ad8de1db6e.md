BleepingComputer reported on August 30, 2026 that Anthropic began emailing Claude users whose active sessions were stolen by infostealers and drained by attackers, naming Vidar, LummaC2, StealC, RedLine and Acreed on Windows plus Atomic Stealer (AMOS) on a small number of Macs. Anthropic revoked compromised sessions, removed saved payment methods and refunded charges it identified as unauthorized, and warned that signing users out does not remove the malware. One affected user traced the infection to a pirated game download.
