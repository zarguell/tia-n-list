The White House said Monday it sent Michael LoSapio's nomination to the Senate to serve as director of DHS's Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency. CISA's top post has had no Senate-confirmed occupant since Jen Easterly stepped down in January 2025; Sean Plankey withdrew in April after more than a year awaiting confirmation, Madhu Gottumukkala led in an acting capacity until February, and Nick Andersen has served as acting director since then. LoSapio has served as Palantir's CISO since 2024, joined the company in 2013 as a systems engineer, and led its infrastructure and network security group from 2017 to 2024 after earlier work as a Linux systems engineer at three universities. The nomination comes as CISA works through a broad reshaping of its leadership and strategy while operating without a Senate-confirmed director.
