Most cloud breaches trace back to misconfiguration, not exotic exploits a public storage bucket, an over-permissive role, an exposed database. Cloud security posture management (CSPM) continuously inventories cloud resources, checks them against benchmarks and best practices, and flags (or fixes) risky configurations across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. The right CSPM depends on your size and cloud footprint: a startup can start with a free open-source scanner or its cloud’s native tool; a mid-market team wants multicloud coverage and prioritization; an enterprise needs attack-path correlation and integration into a broader cloud-native application protection platform (CNAPP). Evaluating these systems alongside overall cloud security tools helps establish a unified defensive baseline.This guide reviews eight leading CSPM options in depth features, best fit, pros, and cons mapped to business size.
