Do Google, Microsoft, Apple and GitHub Scan for Malware? (Quick Answer) Yes. Each one runs its own built-in defense: a Google malware scanner in Play Protect and Safe Browsing, Microsoft Defender on Windows, XProtect and Gatekeeper on Apple devices, and Dependabot malware alerts on GitHub. Each covers only its own ecosystem, and mostly threats it already knows about. Google Play Protect alone scans 200 billion Android apps every day, yet none of these tools stops an attack that depends on a person cooperating, such as a fake support call, a cracked installer, or a stolen password.
