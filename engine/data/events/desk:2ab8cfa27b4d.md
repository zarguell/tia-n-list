First same-day English coverage of the incident, six days before CrowdStrike framed the campaign as AI-driven. Shinhan Bank apologized after a breach exposed data on about 25,000 customers, including names, phone numbers, annual income, loan eligibility limits, 66 cases involving resident registration numbers and 97 cases of connected information. The bank said an unauthorized external party bypassed authentication, and the Financial Supervisory Service conducted an on-site inspection.
