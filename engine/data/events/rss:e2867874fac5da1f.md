Palo Alto Networks has disclosed two critical NGINX vulnerabilities that could let unauthenticated attackers remotely execute arbitrary code or crash worker processes on affected PAN-OS and Panorama deployments. The flaws, tracked as CVE-2026-123633 and CVE-2026-123007, affect specific releases in the PAN-OS 12.2, 12.1, and 11.2 branches. Exposure requires HTTP/2 to be enabled.
