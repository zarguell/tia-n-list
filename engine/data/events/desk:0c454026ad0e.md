The Asia Business Daily reported that Samsung Securities posted a press release and customer apology on 23 November after detecting an external intrusion into its Voice of Customer system on 21 November. The report says 8,241 customers were affected and that 13 categories of data, including names, account numbers, addresses, contact details and driver's license numbers, were involved, varying by customer. It says the company blocked the attack with its firewall, notified the Financial Supervisory Service and KISA, and told customers that only VOC submitters were in scope.
