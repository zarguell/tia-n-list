Hopital Foch notified patients on 6 November 2026 that payment reminder letters it sends through Kiwee and prints via Maileva may have been read by an attacker who entered Maileva's systems in September 2026. The hospital learned on 28 October that the contents could have been viewed, and the exposed fields cover name, address, invoice number, care date and amount for reminders issued between 9 and 23 September 2026. The notice says Maileva corrected the flaw and that no data was modified or deleted.
