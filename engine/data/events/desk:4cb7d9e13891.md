Zyxel's advisory, dated 17 November 2026 as its initial release, pairs CVE-2026-79614, an out-of-bounds write in libclinkc.so that lets an unauthenticated attacker elevate privileges and potentially execute arbitrary commands through a crafted HTTP request, with CVE-2026-86676, a post-authentication command injection in the Description parameter of a CGI program. The tables list 48 models affected by the first flaw and 29 by the second, with DX3301-T0 and EX3301-T0 fixed in 5.50(ABVY.8.4)C0. The vendor says WAN access is disabled by default, credits Adam Simuntis and Joni Gadd, and directs customers to Zyxel sales, support, or their ISP for patch files.
