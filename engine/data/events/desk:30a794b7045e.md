Apache Jena developer Andy Seaborne posted the CVE-2026-86155 advisory to oss-security at 22:03 UTC on 23 November 2026. The advisory says SHACL validation in Apache Jena Fuseki through 6.2.0 can cause server-side request forgery through SHACL SPARQL targets, and rates it CVSS 4.0 10.0 with network attack vector, no privileges required and no user interaction. Users are told to upgrade to Fuseki 6.3.0. The finding credit names MopMonk-AI and Immanuel Tikhonov.
