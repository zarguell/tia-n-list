VeroSecurity , founded in 2025 by IDF 8200 alumni Elad Tessler (CEO) and Daniel Meirov (CTO), has raised $11 million in a seed funding round led by TLV Partners. The firm is building a platform for AI Security Engineering (ASE) – the process of securing AI tools and agents – and will use the funding to advance the platform and grow its go-to-market presence in the United States. AI Security Engineering is a new, and as yet ill-defined, security discipline. Its purpose is to secure the AI tools and agents that are increasingly being adopted, used, and modified in-house by company employees. This is happening at pace and volume – so the object of ASE is a moving target; and is a security requirement that is different to and separate from cybersecurity. ASE policy is defined by security teams. Vero turns these policy definitions into enforced controls, automatically updates them as the AI tools evolve, and refers only those enforcement decisions that require a human judgement to the security team. Vero’s idea is to bring a new approach to securing AI. “CISOs have seen enough dashboards and tables that tell their teams what’s wrong and leave the work to them.
