SecurityWeek reported Accenture's finding that attackers exploited several MiCollab AWV flaws before Mitel's November 16 advisory. At the confirmed victim, commands ran 12 minutes after the first request, root was reached within 30 minutes, and internal backup and database servers were accessed roughly 20 hours later. The actor was not attributed, and evidence of commercial AI API use was assessed as possible assistance rather than proof of fully autonomous exploitation.
