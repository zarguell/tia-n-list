Socket published on November 12, 2026 a detailed account of Japan's October surge, reporting 233 publicly disclosed security incidents, roughly 2.4 times the previous monthly record, and an MM Research Institute estimate of about 185 million personal information records exposed or potentially exposed. It documented the Arara breach of approximately 81.3 million email addresses affecting up to 380 corporate customers and the Ymir Link Cuenote FC intrusion exposing about 47.9 million records from 49 systems via software exploitation, stolen session information, and CSV export abuse. It also reported Nitori's disclosure of up to 37.62 million customer records on November 9 and Japan's announcement of a 10 billion yen emergency cybersecurity package. Socket noted PHP runs on about 92 percent of Japan-hosted sites and that exploitation of recent critical PHP flaws began before their October 21 public disclosure.
