Check Point Research identified Volant Manticore , a previously undocumented Iranian threat group linked to Iran’s Ministry of Intelligence and Security (MOIS). Based on our observations, we believe this is the first Iranian group to designate Cyprus as a primary espionage target, focusing on telecommunications companies, airlines, and critical infrastructure. The group uses KuboShell , a new backdoor that utilizes IPFS for command-and-control (C2) functions, to deploy the Homa Framework , a post-compromise framework built on a reimplementation of MuddyWater’s MuddyC2Go client. Alongside custom malware, Volant Manticore delivers RMM tools and runs credential-harvesting campaigns. Further analysis shed light on the operators’ internal setup, including development, testing, and operational environments, both on-premise and in the cloud. We observed the group testing their tooling against antivirus products before deployment, as well as using Fortinet SSL VPN clients which suggests access to the victims’ networks via VPN.
