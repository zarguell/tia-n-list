RÚV reports that the managers of Icelandic hosting company 1984 decided on 20 November to temporarily stop all services after a large and serious cyberattack on the company's IT infrastructure. A joint notice from 1984 and CERT-IS says the scope is still under investigation and that initial findings point to a well-organised, technically advanced attack. DNS and email are expected to return quickly and will operate under special monitoring, while the company gave no date for full restoration. The shutdown is described as a safety step to limit further impact, preserve the investigation and prevent services being restored before their security is verified.
