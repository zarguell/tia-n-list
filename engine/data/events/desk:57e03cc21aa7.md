BleepingComputer reported on February 23, 2026 that CISA flagged two Roundcube Webmail vulnerabilities as actively exploited and ordered U.S. federal agencies to patch within three weeks. Roundcube is the default mail interface for cPanel since 2008, and Shodan tracks over 46,000 Roundcube instances accessible on the internet. The article recalls CVE-2023-5631, a stored XSS exploited by Winter Vivern and APT28 against European and Ukrainian government email systems.
