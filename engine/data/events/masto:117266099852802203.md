**Summary:** On July 17, 2026, the INC Ransom leak site posted an entry for "Kyokuto Kaihatsu Kogyo" (revenue listed as $790.3M). The listing showed the company name and overview but no data sample, exfiltrated volume, or ransom demand. Approximately seven weeks later, on September 8, Nippon T-Rex - a 100% subsidiary of Kyokuto Kaihatsu Kogyo - reported a system outage. Kyokuto initially described it as a system failure. On September 11, a second disclosure confirmed the cause was "unauthorized access by a third party." Nippon T-Rex suspended internet ordering, dealer parts ordering, and email communication with customers and business partners, continuing operations via alternative methods (fax). As of September 11, no personal information or customer data exfiltration had been confirmed. No restoration timeline was announced. Kyokuto has not publicly linked the September incident to ransomware or to the July INC Ransom claim. Nippon T-Rex manufactures special-purpose vehicles (dump trucks, tank cars, garbage trucks) as a subsidiary of Kyokuto Kaihatsu Kogyo.

**Timeline:**
- July 17, 2026: INC Ransom leak site lists "Kyokuto Kaihatsu Kogyo"
- September 8, 2026: Nippon T-Rex system outage reported
- September 11, 2026: Cause confirmed as unauthorized third-party access; ordering and email systems suspended

**Key detail:** The company has not confirmed whether the September access was ransomware-related or connected to the July INC Ransom listing. Forensic investigation across the group (parent and subsidiary) is recommended given shared identity infrastructure, VPN, and admin accounts.
