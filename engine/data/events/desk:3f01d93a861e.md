Google Threat Intelligence Group disclosed DarkSword on March 18, 2026 as a full-chain iOS exploit used by multiple actors since at least November 2025, targeting iOS 18.4 to 18.7 via compromised legitimate websites. The chain uses six vulnerabilities, several originally exploited as zero-days, and has been adopted by commercial surveillance vendors and suspected state actors including UNC6353, a suspected Russian espionage cluster that added it to watering-hole attacks on Ukrainian sites. Apple patched all of the flaws by iOS 26.3.
