Italian police arrested Xu Zewei, a 33-year-old from Shanghai, at Milan's airport on July 3, 2025, on a US warrant charging wire fraud, aggravated identity theft and unauthorized access to protected computers. The Justice Department unsealed a nine-count indictment accusing Xu and co-defendant Zhang Yu of computer intrusions from February 2020 to June 2021, including the Hafnium campaign that compromised thousands of computers worldwide. Prosecutors said Xu acted at the behest of China's Ministry of State Security and the Shanghai State Security Bureau, and that the team targeted a Texas university in 2020 to obtain COVID-19 vaccine research.
