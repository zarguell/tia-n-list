The unofficial community advisory CSA-2026-01 was published on 21 November and rates the incident critical, actively exploited, and without an assigned CVE. Beyond GL.iNet's `crond -i /tmp/.log` process, it lists checks for the shell pipe `/tmp/f`, an empty `/etc/mp_ip_mapping_info` dated 20 November or later, and a `netstat` binary replaced by a hiding script. It records KVM beta fixes 1.10.2 for RM1-family models and 1.8.6 for RMQ1, with stable router and KVM firmware scheduled for 22 November.
