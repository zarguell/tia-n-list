US movie ticketing company Fandango is warning customers that payment card information from some purchases made through its checkout page between October 8 and 11 may have been accessed by an unauthorized party. Fandango says it launched an investigation, assisted by external cybersecurity experts, after learning of a problem affecting the page. “Our investigation identified evidence that an unauthorized party could access payment card information associated with certain transactions on our checkout page from October 8-11, 2026,” the company says in its notice to customers. Fandango recently determined which customers’ data was potentially at risk. It has not disclosed how the incident occurred, but says the problem has been fixed and its security processes have since been strengthened. Potentially exposed data includes the card number, CVV code, expiration date, and billing ZIP code, as well as the customer’s email address. According to Fandango, customer names, Social Security numbers, government-issued identification details, and other account data were not affected. “Fandango is not currently aware of any fraudulent use of your information as a result of this incident, but nonetheless is informing customers to take appropriate steps to protect their information,” the notice reads.
