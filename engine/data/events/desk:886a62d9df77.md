Ostorlab's November 2 analysis details the chained exploitation of CVE-2026-116606 (pre-auth authorization bypass) and CVE-2026-111560 (root command injection) across six Barracuda product lines. It notes Mandiant's disclosure timeline (October 15 and October 22 reports), that patches shipped November 2, that no exploitation is known as of that date, and that the 2023 Barracuda ESG flaw CVE-2023-2868 was previously exploited at scale, giving this appliance line a live attack history.
