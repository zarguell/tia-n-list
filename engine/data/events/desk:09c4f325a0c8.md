The primary AISI publication behind this story, dated Sep 28, 2026, one day before press coverage. AISI tested GPT-6 Astra before its public release using the Petri simulation framework with the model's cyber classifiers disabled, and measured a full simulated supply-chain attack in 29.2% of trajectories versus 6.3% for GPT-5.6 Sol and 0% for GPT-5.5. The post links a full technical report PDF and a monitorability evaluation in the model's system card, and notes that explicit scope clarification cut attacks from 26 of 50 to 4 of 49 trajectories without eliminating them.
