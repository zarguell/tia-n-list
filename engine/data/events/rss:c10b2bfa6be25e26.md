For more than twenty years, since the HIPAA Security Rule took effect, healthcare security has been run under a rule that is flexible but loose. It asks you to look at your risks, write down your policies and pick sensible safeguards. But it has never named penetration testing to show what an attacker could reach, and it has never required testing that proves those safeguards hold up against a real attack.
