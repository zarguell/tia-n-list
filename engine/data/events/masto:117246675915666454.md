OpenAI: 130 billion tokens to solve Navier-Stokes. But mathematicians are not convinced. OpenAI claims its AI models have made progress on the Navier-Stokes equations, one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems in mathematics, using 130 billion tokens of training data. Mathematicians have expressed skepticism about the significance of the results, questioning whether the AI's approach constitutes genuine mathematical progress or pattern matching on existing literature. The Navier-Stokes equations describe fluid dynamics and have profound implications for physics and engineering. A valid solution or proof of their properties would earn a $1 million Millennium Prize.
