ManageEngine's advisory page for CVE-2026-12507 lists the flaw as fixed in AssetExplorer 7860 and ServiceDesk Plus 15290 on June 19, 2026, and in ServiceDesk Plus MSP 15150 and SupportCenter Plus 15150 on June 22, 2026. Affected builds are AssetExplorer 7850 and below, ServiceDesk Plus 15280 and below, ServiceDesk Plus MSP 15140 and below, and SupportCenter Plus 15140 and below. The advisory states an authenticated user holding the Create Query Reports permission could run specially crafted database queries with Jasper expressions and execute arbitrary commands. The report came in through ManageEngine's bug bounty portal.
