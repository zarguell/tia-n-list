Natalie Silvanovich announced that Project Zero was replacing its 90 + 30 policy, in place since 2021, with a fixed-deadline model called Full X: technical details are published a set number of days after the vendor report whether or not a fix exists. Full 90 starts on November 13, 2026, followed by Full 60 on February 2, 2027 and Full 30 on January 3, 2028. The post removes the 30-day soak period, the grace period, most special cases and the variant policy, while keeping exceptions for in-the-wild exploitation, already-public details and vendor requests. It argues that LLM-assisted reverse engineering of patches makes zero patch-to-exploitation time a reasonable model of attacker behavior.
