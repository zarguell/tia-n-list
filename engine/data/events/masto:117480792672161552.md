Major data breach at India's largest bank. What does it mean for customers?

The State Bank of India, the country's largest financial institution measured by balance sheet, has been the victim of a serious cyber incident. A suspected digital attack led to sensitive personal data from thousands of customers ending up on the dark web. The bank immediately started a large-scale investigation into the scope and the precise cause of the breach.

The incident again underscores how vulnerable even the largest banks in the world are to advanced cybercrime, and highlights the acute importance of robust digital protection. For Dutch and European organisations the incident highlights the risk of cross-border network connections. International banks exchange data on a daily basis, which means a leak outside Europe can have direct consequences for the supply chain security. Companies should therefore tighten their cybersecurity and supplier controls. Proactive dark web threat monitoring is becoming a necessary standard to limit the damage from data theft in good time.
