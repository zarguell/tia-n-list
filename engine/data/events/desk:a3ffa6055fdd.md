Yorick Koster's original researcher disclosure, published August 1, 2018, showing that Office documents embedding a Shell.Explorer.1 OLE object (CLSID {EAB22AC3-30C1-11CF-A7EB-0000C05BAE0B}) can open the legacy Internet Explorer engine and trigger file download and execution with two clicks. MSRC declined to fix at the time, stating the technique requires significant social engineering and that it would not issue a fix. The post also flagged the sibling Forms.HTML objects and gave defenders the object list to watch for.
