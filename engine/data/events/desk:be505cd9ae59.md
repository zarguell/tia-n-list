VulnCheck researcher Adam Powis published the findings on LinkedIn, stating that only 269 of 838,100 FortiOS hosts seen in Target Intelligence advertise a version. He said about 376,000 FortiGates leave a management port open and that 375,904 were placed into software version ranges. His post reports that 160,171 hosts, or 42.6 percent, are in generations where every version measured has a vulnerability known to be exploited in the wild.
