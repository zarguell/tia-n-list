Censys tracked php2shell exposure worldwide and reported that PHP 8.2.0 through 8.2.34, 8.3.0 through 8.3.35, 8.4.0 through 8.4.26 and 8.5.0 through 8.5.11 are vulnerable, with source-only backports for 7.4, 8.0 and 8.1 and no fix for PHP 5.4 through 7.3. PHP gave advance notice on 20 October 2026 and published fixes on 21 October 2026. Censys also noted that VulnCheck holds a non-public working exploit for CVE-2026-109618 and that at least one public repository claims an ASLR-bypassing remote exploit.
