Ensuring that there are "humans in the loop" is the gold safety standard for making sure artificial intelligence (AI) agentic systems act safely, ethically, and legally. However, as automated processes accelerate and scale, humans lose the ability to provide meaningful oversight over machine-speed workflows. Rather than simply inserting a human into a machine process, leaders must engineer systems that enable AI agents to operate at machine speed while ensuring they act within human-authorized intent, appropriate controls, and safety and legal boundaries. This means thinking strategically about where acceleration is possible and where slowing down to human speed is necessary to avoid catastrophic risk. Machine-assisted decision-making has long been used in the hiring process to manage volume and improve fairness and efficiency.
