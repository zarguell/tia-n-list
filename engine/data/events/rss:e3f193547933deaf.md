Users trust a cloud provider to configure and maintain their IT services, including data storage. In turn, the provider guarantees that hosted services will stay up and that data will remain strictly confidential. To demonstrate that their platforms are secure, providers choose different strategies: some run bug bounty programs, some rely on regular internal audits, and some bring in external research teams. However, cloud pentest methodology still has plenty of blind spots: there’s no single standard, and the outcome depends on the experience and skill of the team. Most pentests in this area have historically focused on the external layer: the management console, customer interfaces, APIs, and the basic network perimeter. This layer is important, but it’s only the surface. In this introductory article, we share our approach to penetration testing of cloud environments, which we’ve developed over several years and dozens of projects. We don’t claim to cover every possible scenario. Instead, we want to show the reasoning behind our pentests and highlight less obvious points that are easy to miss.
