Cybernews reported on November 18, 2026, that Nvidia faced ransom demands from The Gentlemen after the group added the chipmaker to its dark web leak site. The article said a countdown timer showed 234 hours, less than 10 days, until the alleged leak would activate. The reporting treated the entry as a claim rather than a confirmed breach, and cited researcher Aras Nazarovas urging caution because the group had previously named the wrong victims and reused data from earlier incidents.
