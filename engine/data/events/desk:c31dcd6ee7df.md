CISA, FBI, USSS and international partners released the joint AA26-293A advisory for SafePay ransomware on October 20, 2026. The advisory states the group has affected at least 600 organizations since August 2024 across North America, South America, Europe, and Australia, using data theft, encryption, and deliberate backup-system destruction. It includes IOCs, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, and detection recommendations.
