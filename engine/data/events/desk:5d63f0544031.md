Sergiu Gatlan reports that MetaMask disclosed an ongoing infrastructure security incident, saying there is no immediate threat to MetaMask wallets and that affected validators in its non-custodial staking operations are being proactively exited in coordination with clients and partners. Lido Finance separately said MetaMask Staking (ex Consensys Staking) is exiting its ETH validators from the Lido protocol, with the final validators expected to be exited, though not fully withdrawn, by the end of October 7, 2026. Lido warned the move will likely incur foregone rewards as well as possible downtime penalties.
