CISA released advisory AA26-281A on October 8, 2026, describing Integrity Technology Group-enabled threat actors combining automated scanning (MicroScan), botnets, and hands-on exploitation. The advisory lists exploited CVEs including CVE-2014-6278 (Shellshock) and CVE-2019-11510 (Pulse Secure VPN), recommends MFA, input sanitization, and timely patching. Joint publication with UK, Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand, and Spain. The release predates the diesec coverage by four days.
