Enea disclosed SilentAir, a previously unknown state-level actor that runs SS7 and Diameter signaling attacks from hijacked network nodes inside mobile operators in at least six Asian countries, with Taiwan as the primary target. The report also tracks LIMINAL PANDA activity through 2026 and notes SilentAir has been active since before 2025, with a dozen other countries and territories affected. Enea assessed with low confidence that SilentAir aligns with China-nexus operations based on the Taiwan focus, absence of Chinese targets, and UTC+8 working-day timing.
