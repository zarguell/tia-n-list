Summary On October 18, 2026 , the notorious ransomware group N0n claimed responsibility for a cyberattack on All Business Technologies ( allbusinesstechnologies.com ), a key player in the accounting and tax preparation sector in the USA. The attack resulted in the exposure of sensitive financial and legal documents belonging to over 60 client companies.
