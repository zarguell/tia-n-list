NBC News reported, citing two people briefed on the matter, that the FBI was investigating an apparent breach of its National Crime Information Center. The bureau had no comment as of Friday night, and NBC said CNN had reported the matter earlier in the evening. The report noted that NCIC connects federal, state and local authorities so law enforcement information is available nationwide. It also pointed to a September FBI investigation into a hacking group that claimed to have stolen data on agents through the bureau's job portal, and to an apparent attack disclosed Thursday on a US Department of Agriculture system used to pay farmers.
