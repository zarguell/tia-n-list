N-able on Monday took hosted N-central environments offline in response to a security researcher’s public disclosure of more than 30 alleged zero-day vulnerabilities. “As a precautionary measure, we are temporarily taking our Hosted N-central environment offline while we take the necessary actions to secure the environment and implement appropriate remediation,” the company said . While N-central and the related services remain inaccessible, N-able says it has made progress validating the flaws and creating a fix, which should roll out around midday on November 10. On-premises N-central deployments should also be kept offline until patches are released, the company says. “N-able Engineering continues to make progress on the N-central hotfix and associated validation activities. The work remains on track with no setbacks, and our teams are progressing as quickly as possible while maintaining the validation required for a safe remediation,” the company said. N-able also pointed out that it has no evidence that the vulnerabilities have been exploited against its N-central environments. The zero-day bugs were published on GitHub on Sunday by security researcher Sean Bales and have yet to be assigned CVE identifiers. Bales dubbed the flaws Able2Shell.
