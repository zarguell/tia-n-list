CrowdStrike Counter Adversary Operations tested the open-weight DeepSeek-R1 671B model and compared code output with and without innocuous trigger words. Prompts referencing topics the Chinese Communist Party considers sensitive, including Tibet in an industrial control system scenario, raised the severe-vulnerability rate from a 19 percent baseline to 27.2 percent, almost a 50 percent increase. The researchers said the same class of bias could affect other models and noted that up to 90 percent of developers used AI coding tools in 2025.
