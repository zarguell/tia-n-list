Last month, Amazon announced plans to hire more than 300,000 people in the US for the holidays. That’s welcome news for job seekers, but scammers know that. We found several sites advertising a remote assistant job under Amazon-like names, with promises of up to $5,000 a month. None of the sites is run by Amazon. Amazon says all genuine job opportunities are posted on amazon.jobs ; hourly warehouse jobs are also listed at hiring.amazon.com . The offers we found are separate from Amazon’s holiday hiring push. The application doesn’t match the job description One careers page calls the role a “legitimate, fully remote position,” with “no commute, no office, no fixed desk.” Yet the form asks whether applicants have reliable access to a personal vehicle and someone at home who can receive packages. Those questions point to package handling, not remote assistant work.
