Cesanta published Mongoose 7.24 with a security-fix list that includes built-in TCP/IP FIN packet payload handling, IPv4 and IPv6 fragment handling, and IPv6 extension-header checksum validation. The same release lists WebSocket, HTTP, TLS, PPP, and mbedTLS hardening plus a fix tracked as CVE-2026-106024. This is the upstream release that carries the MIP FIN-payload repair later detailed in GHSA-4mgp-8m9m-q872.
