The release notes, dated November 24, 2026, list 18 SolarWinds CVEs and 11 third-party CVEs fixed in Serv-U 2026.4. Nine SolarWinds issues are rated 9.1 critical, including TOCTOU flaws CVE-2026-82315, CVE-2026-82316 and CVE-2026-82319, IDOR escalation CVE-2026-82322, type confusion CVE-2026-82320 and CVE-2026-82321, and Linux root or privilege escalation issues CVE-2026-123167, CVE-2026-123172 and CVE-2026-123168. Most of the 18 are credited to the Intigriti Bug Bounty Program. The notes also record the move to OpenSSL 3.5 LTS and a change that prevents MFA one-time passcodes from being written to log files.
