ACDS documented a second wave on November 1 and 2 in which more than a hundred stores across Belgium, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Sweden, Estonia, Ireland and Portugal had controllers forced into defrost, with some units reaching 50C. Its mapping showed that three of the five largest exposed AK-SM 800A estates outside France were hit, while half of the world's visible controllers were still online three weeks after the first wave. By November 4 the exploitable remainder in attacked countries was down to about 200 addresses, almost all reachable only through their carrier.
