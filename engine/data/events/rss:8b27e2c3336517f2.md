Securing Privileged Access in Industrial Control Systems Industrial control systems are built for reliability, not for the identity and access controls that information technology (IT) teams take for granted. These are the systems that keep production lines running and water treatment plants operating. They keep power flowing steadily to the grid. But in recent years, industrial control systems (ICS) and operational technology (OT) networks have become more connected to corporate IT, and the privileged accounts that operate them are often the least defended parts of critical infrastructure. Industrial PAM solutions close that gap without disrupting the operations they’re built to protect. Why Does Privileged Access Look Different in Industrial Environments? In a typical IT environment, privileged access management (PAM) assumes a few things: Accounts can be rotated Sessions can be interrupted Multi-factor authentication (MFA) can be layered onto nearly any login ICS and the broader OT environment it belongs to rarely offer that kind of flexibility. Programmable logic controllers and supervisory control and data acquisition systems often run on decades-old firmware that was never designed to support modern authentication. Many of these assets can’t just accept a software agent or be patched without a planned outage because they were engineered around uptime and safety rather than governing identities.
