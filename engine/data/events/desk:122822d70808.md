OpenAI announced a cybersecurity-focused model citing internal benchmark improvements in vulnerability discovery, patch development, and adherence to safety guardrails. Preview results included at least 81 novel Firefox vulnerabilities, exploits for 948 Linux kernel bugs, and a validated critical remote-code-execution flaw in Brotli. The model rolls out first to a limited set of Daybreak Red customers, with safeguards such as identity checks and frequent high-risk behavior check-ins.
