An independent researcher published a Shodan and Censys point-in-time analysis of ARTEX exposure on October 4, 2026, following AI-agent attacks against South Korea's financial sector. The post documents exposed ARTEX infrastructure, disposable VPS and proxy egress, and incident indicators of compromise, and notes the Korea Financial Security Institute briefed about 160 financial-sector security practitioners on AI-agent attacks on September 17, 2026. It argues AI agents reduce the cost of reconnaissance while disposable infrastructure reduces the cost of rotating egress.
