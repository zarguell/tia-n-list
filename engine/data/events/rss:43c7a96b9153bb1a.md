Alleged Canadian Infrastructure Webmail Access Is Offered for Sale SOCRadar Dark Web Team detected a dark web post advertising alleged webmail access to a Canadian infrastructure company . The threat actor, operating under the alias Horsefucker, claimed that the targeted organization generates more than $10 million in annual revenue . The listing identified Canada as the target country and webmail as the access type but did not disclose the organization’s name, email domain, or supporting evidence. If legitimate, compromised webmail access could facilitate Business Email Compromise (BEC) , credential theft, phishing, and further unauthorized access to corporate systems.
