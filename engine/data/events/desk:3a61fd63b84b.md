Nine reported that dozens of Woolworths customers had WISH eGift card balances drained, sometimes discovered only when they tried to pay at checkout. Customers quoted by Nine described cards shown as redeemed, purchases at Dan Murphy's and Big W often in another state, and long waits on the gift card support line. One shopper bought a $500 card, spent about $130 in Melbourne and found the remaining $367 spent in New South Wales three days later.
