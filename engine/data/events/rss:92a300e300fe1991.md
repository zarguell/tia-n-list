American trucking and logistics companies rely on digital platforms to verify carriers, onboard brokers, exchange documents, and coordinate freight. Cybercriminals increasingly exploit these routine business processes. ANY.RUN researchers investigated three campaigns targeting the US logistics sector: HelloFriends, NodeRemote, and Fake Freight Portals. Each uses familiar business interactions to steal sensitive data, deploy malware, or gain remote access. For SOC and MSSP teams, these campaigns highlight a key challenge: effective defense requires more than blocking suspicious files.
