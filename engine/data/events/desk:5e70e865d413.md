Adobe's security bulletin APSB26-146 assigns CVE-2026-75650 (CVSS 10.0) and confirms active exploitation in the wild. The bulletin lists affected versions: Adobe Commerce 2.4.4-2.4.9 (including August 2026 releases), Adobe Commerce B2B 1.3.3-1.5.3, and Magento Open Source 2.4.6-2.4.9, and directs users to the VULN-39341 hotfix. The advisory carries Priority 1, Adobe's highest urgency rating, and explicitly recommends applying the hotfix immediately.
