The Hacker News reported that Varonis Threat Labs chained three bugs in Microsoft 365 Copilot Enterprise Search into a one-click exfiltration path named SearchLeak, tracked by Microsoft as CVE-2026-42824. The crafted link pointed at a real microsoft.com domain, so conventional URL filtering was unlikely to flag it, and the chain used a streaming render race plus a Bing image endpoint as an exfiltration proxy. Microsoft rated the issue critical and mitigated it on the backend, with CVSS scores of 6.5 from Microsoft and 7.5 from NVD; Varonis published a proof of concept, not observed exploitation.
