President Donald Trump acknowledged a hack of the FBI’s national crime database Saturday, calling it “not good” but suggesting the intrusion could reveal information about bureau investigations he has long criticized. CNN https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/20/politics/fbi-hacked-again-target-crime-database ”>first reported the breach Friday, citing two people with knowledge of the matter who said the FBI was investigating. It’s unclear who the hackers are or what may have been accessed. Nationwide law enforcement agencies use the National Crime Information Center, or NCIC, to share information about wanted and missing people, protection orders and stolen property. State, local, tribal and territorial law enforcement personnel — the users and contributors of information to NCIC — have not received formal communication from the FBI about the intrusion, according to a law enforcement representative who was granted anonymity to discuss the matter.
