Kaspersky's Threat Response team reported on May 5, 2026 (updated May 8) that DAEMON Tools installers distributed from the vendor's own website were trojanized starting April 8, 2026, with compromised builds of DAEMON Tools 12.5.0.2421 through 12.5.0.2434 backdooring signed binaries including DTHelper.exe, DiscSoftBusServiceLite.exe and DTShellHlp.exe. The implant contacted env-check.daemontools[.]cc, a domain registered March 27 that typosquats the legitimate daemon-tools[.]cc, and staged follow-on payloads from 38.180.107[.]76: a .NET information collector, a minimalistic RC4-encrypted shellcode backdoor, and a QUIC RAT aimed at a single Russian educational institution. Kaspersky observed several thousand infection attempts across more than 100 countries, but further-stage payloads landed on only about a dozen machines at retail, scientific, government and manufacturing organizations in Russia, Belarus and Thailand, indicating selective targeting. Following disclosure, the vendor acknowledged the issue and release 12.6.0.2445 no longer includes the malicious behavior.
