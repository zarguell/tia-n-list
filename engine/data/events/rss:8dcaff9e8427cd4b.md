A cyberattack disrupted operations at B2B top-up solutions provider GlobeTopper, causing an estimated $2.5 million in lost revenue, while the attacker redeemed digital gift cards worth $70,000. Parent company iQSTEL said that the intrusion was detected on October 22 and GlobeTopper responded by suspending its platform and starting an investigation. GlobeTopper is a business-to-business digital gift card distributor. Various retailers, fintech apps, and rewards platforms use its API. According to the platform’s website , it is used by more than 4,000 brands across 65 countries, including IKEA, Adidas, Spotify, Target, and Roblox. In a filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), iQSTEL said that GlobeTopper detected more unauthorized activity the day after it identified the attack. In response, the company "took the platform and all affected servers offline" in the early hours of October 23 to investigate and fix the problem. The company deployed a completely rebuilt platform on AWS on October 31, while commercial operations were restored in stages, starting November 1.
