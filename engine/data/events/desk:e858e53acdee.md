Sophos Counter Threat Unit research describes BRONZE SANDCASTLE, a suspected China-based group, abusing Sandboxie DLL sideloading in legitimate Windows applications to run encrypted backdoors and steal Active Directory databases. Intrusions spanned August 2025 through August 2026 across victims in the Philippines, Taiwan, Afghanistan, and Africa. The group used modified Meterpreter shells with two observed backdoor versions, 1.1.5 and 1.1.7, and elements associated with TrustedSec COFFLoader.
