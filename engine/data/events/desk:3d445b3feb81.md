The PHP security team gave advance notice of an upcoming security release scheduled for 21 October 2026 at 14:00 UTC addressing two critical vulnerabilities rated CVSS v4.0 9.2, tracked as CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. The notice urged all PHP users to prepare to upgrade and reserved the right to release earlier if details leaked or active exploitation appeared. Technical details, affected versions and fixed releases were withheld until the update shipped.
