VulnCheck's Initial Access team documented in-the-wild exploitation of CVE-2026-58115 beginning October 30, 2026, when a canary recorded an attacker deploying a Node-RED flow that downloads and runs a Linux bot. The exploit came from 4.201.105.116 in Microsoft's ASN (AS8075), with 16 events seen from that IP between October 30 and November 1, 2026. The post decodes a three-layer dropper pulling bot-linux-amd64 from 85.215.73.250:8080 into /dev/shm/.b and lists IoCs including a second payload server at 45.194.90.57:38472; VulnCheck moved the CVE into its KEV catalog.
