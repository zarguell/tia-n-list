What’s in the Silent Push report on North Korean remote IT workers? This report follows a North Korean recruiting scheme that Silent Push found on a Discord server, where a recruiter offered U.S., E.U., and Latin American citizens 35% of a salary to act as the face and the legal identity on job applications. The remaining 65% goes back to the North Korean worker through cryptocurrency, wire transfers, or third-party payment processors. The report explains why the scheme passes document-based KYC and AML checks, shows how connection origin data exposes it, and sets out the indicators HR, fraud, and cybersecurity teams can use to spot a North Korean IT worker before onboarding.
