Kaspersky's Russian-language Telegram channel reported on November 13, 2026 that it had detected suspected exploitation of the new Telegram widgets, with the first attempts seen that night. The post says visits to certain channels silently launched a PowerShell script that downloaded and ran WebRAT, also called Salat Stealer, a Rust trojan capable of system control and theft of crypto wallet and Telegram account data. Its stated advice was not to launch widgets until a fix was released.
