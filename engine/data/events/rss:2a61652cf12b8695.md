Cyber headlines often focus on the flashy attacks, but the ones that actually cause OH $#!T moments are the tactics that abuse the tools and workflows you already trust. If you run a small or mid-sized organization, whether you handle IT yourself or outsource it, it’s a constant race to keep up with attackers and their evolving tactics. The bottom line is that you need to know which attacks are landing in environments like yours, and how to prioritize your defenses against them. That’s why Huntress created the Tragic Quadrant , a ranked list of the cyber tactics putting businesses at risk, built to show you where to focus regardless of what’s trending week to week. Each of these tactics hides inside something your team uses every day, like a remote access tool or a Microsoft 365 login. Remote monitoring and management (RMM) tools are a staple for IT teams, which is exactly why attackers abuse them. One rogue RMM install gives an attacker persistent access that looks like ordinary admin work. In Q1 2026, 45% of endpoint-related incidents Huntress investigated involved RMM abuse. Attackers are also using AI to build convincing fake document shares and service agreement lures that trick users into installing unwanted RMMs . So far in 2026, 24.6% of Identity Threat Detection and Response (ITDR) signals point to mailbox manipulation and persistence.
