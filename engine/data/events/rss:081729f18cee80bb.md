Security teams have never lacked vulnerability data. The harder problem is deciding which issues create a credible path to business impact, then acting before that path can be used. As frontier AI makes it faster and less expensive to connect weaknesses, operational assumptions, and legitimate system behavior, that decision window is becoming even tighter. The Gartner® report, Mythos Reprioritizes Cybersecurity Outcomes and Investments , examines what this shift means for cybersecurity priorities and investment. Rapid7 is offering complimentary access to the research, which we believe can help security leaders reassess how they prioritize exposure and infrastructure teams reassess how they plan response and recovery. Why does AI change the vulnerability management equation? Gartner states, “Mythos collapses the distance between theoretical weaknesses and executable attack scenarios, forcing cybersecurity to optimize for outcomes, not activity.” For leaders already managing more vulnerabilities than their teams can address, the pressure comes from the growing speed at which separate weaknesses can become a credible attack path. The change also extends beyond one model. Gartner states, “By 2027, Mythos will be overtaken by new frontier-AI-enabled models, signaling that this moment is not about Mythos, but a new era of AI-assisted vulnerabilities and functional exploits.” At Rapid7, we believe this requires a more attacker-aware view of exposure.
