The State Department's Rewards for Justice program offered up to $10 million for information on the whereabouts of Zhang Yu, identified as director of Shanghai Firetech Information Science and Technology and accused of acting for the Chinese government in breaches of thousands of computers. The announcement says Zhang violated the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act in attacks on at least one university and a law firm, alongside Xu Zewei, who was extradited to the US in April. Zhang remains at large.
