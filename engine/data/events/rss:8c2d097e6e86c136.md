Tel Aviv-based Bay has emerged from stealth with $50 million funding (comprising a $37 million Series A round led by Battery Ventures and a previously undisclosed $13 million seed funding led by YL Ventures and Striker Venture Partners). Bay was founded in February 2026 by Yuval Zacharia (CEO), Boris Danilovich (VP of R&D), and Aner Morag (CPO). All three are alumni of the Israeli Military Intelligence (IDF Unit 8200). In business, companies cannot ignore the economic advantage offered by AI agents, but nor do they always know what any individual agent might do. Agentic AI behavior is decided at runtime by each agent acting on its user’s behalf and with its user’s permissions. Bay is designed to allow companies to install and employ AI agents confident that they will not run amok with unintended actions. It provides a single control plane that discovers, understands and controls agents wherever they run. When installed, Bay automatically builds a full inventory of the AI agents, coding assistants, MCP servers, skills, extensions and local models in use, and maps how they connect, including risky combinations of tools. It follows each agent session from the first prompt to the final action. Its Enforcer component evaluates every proposed action in the context of the full session and decides in milliseconds whether to allow the action, refer it to the user, redirect it based on policy, or simply deny it.
