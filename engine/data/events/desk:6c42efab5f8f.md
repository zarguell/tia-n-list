Seoul Economic Daily reported that KEPCO confirmed on November 20 the exposure of 12 data categories for 650,487 KEPCO ON customers, including resident and foreign registration numbers, bank and card details, and billing records, representing 7.1% of the platform's 9.2 million users. It reported 426,827 procurement representatives and 442,934 KEPCO and corporate employees affected through SRM, for 1,520,248 people in total. The article raised concern that SRM holds procurement records for power grid construction projects and that facility locations or equipment details could be exposed beyond the personal data.
