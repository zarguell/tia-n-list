Decrypt reported that Trezor was made aware through responsible disclosure of a single hardware implant in a Trezor Safe 5 bought from CryptoBilis in August 2026. The implant sits between the main board and screen and appears capable of reading the recovery phrase from the screen and transmitting it over a mobile network when plugged in. Trezor advised affected buyers to move funds to a new wallet with a new seed and offered replacement devices or vouchers for purchases since March 2026.
