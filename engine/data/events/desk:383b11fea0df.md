Travel Associates, a Flight Centre Travel Group leisure brand, published a customer FAQ it states is updated as of 15:30 AEST on 26 November 2026. It says the parent company is investigating unusual activity in a third-party booking system used by some leisure brands including Travel Associates, confirms Mandiant is engaged, and repeats that a significant number of customer records may have been accessed while the number extracted is unknown. The FAQ says the company does not believe credit card, bank or other payment details were accessed but that this position may change, tells customers not to click links or share personal information in unexpected messages, and directs inquiries to databreach@travelassociates.com.
