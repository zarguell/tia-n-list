Here are this week’s highlights: ChatGPT Sites exposure Pluto researchers combed web archives, public code repositories, search engines and other sources and found 16,666 sites created with ChatGPT Sites (which builds and hosts web apps for ChatGPT and Codex users), 12,830 of which were accessible to anyone. Manual checks confirmed sensitive data exposure at 27 organizations, including a Fortune 500 company’s sales dashboard, a freight forwarder’s customer quotes, and a vendor’s sales pitch. Sites are visible only to their owners and admins unless shared, but owners can open them to the entire internet.
