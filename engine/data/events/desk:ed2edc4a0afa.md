BleepingComputer's first report on the incident, published September 25, 2026 at 04:33 AM (site displays US Eastern time, 08:33 UTC), says Bitget discovered the breach Thursday evening after security systems flagged multiple unauthorized transfers and engaged Mandiant and SlowMist plus law enforcement. CEO Gracy Chen linked the theft to North Korean hackers based on IP behavior patterns and on-chain analysis, said attackers compromised a critical backend system in the wallet infrastructure to spoof transaction data and trigger the authorization process, and listed the affected chains as Ethereum, XRP Ledger, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Optimism, BSC and Base. The article predates any mention of the zero-day exploits, which were first revealed on September 30.
