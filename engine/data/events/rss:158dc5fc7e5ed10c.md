Since late August 2026, threat actors have used ARTEX, an open-source framework in which LLM-driven agents carry out a penetration test from reconnaissance to data exfiltration, in real intrusions. Two of them are VAPOR PANDA, a China-nexus espionage group that used ARTEX to run parallel intrusions against more than 200 organizations [1], and Onyx Sleet (Andariel), a North Korean group that added it to operations already accelerated by AI [2].
