Kirsten Davies, currently Chief Information Officer at the Department of War, has been appointed as the new National Cyber Director. Sworn in as DOW’s CIO in December 2025, Davies will replace Sean Cairncross, who was confirmed as the top cybersecurity official in August 2025, more than five months after being nominated for the position. Before her DOW tenure, Davies led cybersecurity at multiple organizations. She served as CISO at Unilever, The Estée Lauder Companies Inc., Hewlett-Packard, and Siemens, and as CSO at Barclays, and founded and was the CEO of the Institute for Cyber. “Our nation stands in the crossroad of analog, digital, and quantum technologies, our American way of living increasingly interconnected. Our opportunity is to dominate the cyber landscape for generations to come …but only if we modernize and drive resilience into the foundations our great nation runs on, as well as build cyber talent for our future,” Davies said on X. “The Department congratulates Kirsten Davies on her new role as the National Cyber Director. Throughout her tenure at DOW, she has proven herself to be a dedicated patriot and professional, and we are confident she will advance President Trump’s America First agenda across the cybersecurity landscape,” Secretary of War Pete Hegseth said in an X post .
