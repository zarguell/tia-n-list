A threat actor breached the internal network of Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO) and accessed two systems containing customer, employee, and contractor data. The attack exposed details of more than 1.5 million individuals before the company blocked access from external IP addresses and suspended the affected services. KEPCO is South Korea’s main electric power company and a state-controlled, major operator of the country’s critical infrastructure. The organization detected the attack on November 20, when it confirmed unauthorized access to its KEPCO ON electricity billing platform and its electronic procurement system (SRM). “On Friday, November 20, Korea Electric Power Corporation (hereafter KEPCO), led by President Kim Dong-cheol, confirmed that some customer information held in its IT systems had been exposed,” the power company states . “The company is making every effort to prevent harm.” ON is an integrated customer platform that allows customers to view and pay electricity bills. SRM is the company’s procurement system for contracting services.
