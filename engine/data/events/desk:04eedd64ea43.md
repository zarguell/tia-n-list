ThreatLabz named CoffeeLoader in March 2025 and described a loader originating around September 2024 that downloads second-stage payloads while evading endpoint controls through call-stack spoofing, sleep obfuscation and Windows fibers. Its Armoury packer impersonates ASUS Armoury Crate, hijacks library exports and decrypts code on the GPU with an OpenCL XOR kernel, and the researchers saw it protecting both SmokeLoader and CoffeeLoader. The report also documents a DGA fallback, certificate pinning and observed delivery of Rhadamanthys shellcode.
