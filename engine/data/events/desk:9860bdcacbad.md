CISA and partner agencies issued joint advisory AA23-341A attributing Star Blizzard to Russia's FSB Centre 18 and warning of spear-phishing against academia, defense, government, NGOs, and think tanks in the US, UK, NATO countries, and states neighboring Russia. The advisory described methodical research and rapport-building before credential theft, often to victims' personal email addresses to bypass corporate controls. It also noted 2022 expansion into defense-industrial targets and US Department of Energy facilities.
