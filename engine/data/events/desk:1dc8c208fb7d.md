Greg Pollock of UpGuard describes an unauthenticated single-node Elasticsearch 8.13.0 cluster in Docker that exposed 99 indices and 1.64 billion documents, about 259 GB, organized around Chinese national ID numbers and phone numbers. The index families included `eryaosu_*`, `sanyaosu_*`, `alipay_v1`, `person_v1`, `xueji_*` and `enterprise_v1`; UpGuard estimated at least 840 million distinct national ID numbers, nearly 300 million phone records, 11 million student enrollment records and 7.8 million business registrations. The sample included children and people likely deceased, and UpGuard assessed the trove as an illicit identity-lookup aggregation rather than a legitimate government or company database. Pollock identified the exposure in October 2026 and said the data was still being updated in the week before discovery.
