On September 1, 2026, EDRM released EDRM 2.0, a significant update to the Electronic Discovery Reference Model. The new version reflects advances in e-discovery over the past two decades, emphasizing continuous analysis, iterative workflows, and the integration of AI. This redesign improves the model’s structure and relevance, addressing contemporary challenges in information governance and data management.
