The Baker Tilly Advisory Group is notifying clients that a data breach has compromised certain personal information about them and their dependents. The company said the exposed data was stored on a platform operated by a third-party service provider Baker Tilly uses to serve clients. Baker Tilly learned of the incident on September 6 and completed its review of the affected data on October 28, when it identified whose information was involved. “On September 6, 2026, Baker Tilly became aware of a potential cybersecurity incident involving certain information stored on a platform operated by a third-party service provider used by Baker Tilly in connection with services we provide to clients,” reads the notification to customers. “The unauthorized actor was able to obtain data relating to certain Baker Tilly clients,” the company added. The breach notification does not provide a count of impacted clients, does not name the service provider, and leaves the specific data types exposed as a placeholder. Data from the Office of the Texas Attorney General reports that Baker Tilly impacted 10,456 individuals in the state of Texas alone. The portal lists Social Security numbers (SSNs), full names, addresses, and other information as exposed data types. Baker Tilly is a major accounting firm in the U.S., providing auditing, tax planning, compliance, advisory, and business operations services. Globally, the company operates in 147 countries and reports an annual revenue of $6.8 billion
