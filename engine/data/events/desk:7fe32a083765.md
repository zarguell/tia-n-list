Airlock published its CNA record for CVE-2026-19707 at 09:34 UTC on 18 November 2026, ahead of the story's first sighting. It describes a CWE-122 overflow in the Smart Form Protection HTML entity decoder affecting Airlock Gateway 8.5.0 through 8.5.2 and 8.6.0, with fixes in 8.5.3 and 8.6.1. The record says exploitation requires Smart Form Protection or equivalent expert settings on a mapping whose backend returns certain multi-byte HTML character references, can cause denial of service or remote code execution, and credits Manuel Feifel and Marc Bollhalder of InfoGuard Labs.
