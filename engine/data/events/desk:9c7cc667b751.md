A Cloud Security Alliance Labs note described CVE-2026-102764 as a CVSS 9.3 origin-validation flaw in BeyondTrust Remote Support and summarized ARMORY CASTLE exploitation beginning 29 August 2026. It argued that compromise of the support broker should be treated as potential compromise of every system the appliance can reach, and recommended patching, hunting and credential rotation in that order. The note calculated a 41-day interval between first exploitation and completion of SaaS patching and advised rebuilding suspected appliances rather than cleaning them in place.
