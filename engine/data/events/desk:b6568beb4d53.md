CERT-EU published its own warning that several threat actors worldwide had used ARTEX to automate campaigns, and stated that it had observed similar AI-driven activities against its constituency, assessed as highly likely involving ARTEX. The advisory maps the activity to T1595 active scanning, T1588.002 tool acquisition and T1190 exploitation of public-facing applications, and notes there is no hard technical enforcement of scope inside the framework. It recommends limiting internet exposure, isolating management panels, sanitising error responses, deploying WAF and MFA coverage, and keeping even low severity findings inside the remediation cycle so agents cannot chain them. This is the same CERT-EU later named in the Commission incident, and it was published fourteen days before the Commission confirmed anything.
