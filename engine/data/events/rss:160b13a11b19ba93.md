German defense technology company Quantum Systems confirmed that it detected an incident in late October that was part of an active cyberattack by "hostile actors." The company responded quickly, working with relevant authorities to resolve the issue swiftly and ensure its unmanned systems could safely resume operations. Quantum Systems is a German defense technology company that builds reconnaissance and interceptor drones, along with other unmanned systems and the software that controls them. Its drones are in service with Germany’s military, and in October Germany announced funding to build tens of thousands of Quantum Systems interceptor drones in Ukraine for Ukrainian forces. Quantum Systems believes it was targeted because of its recent announcement of support for Ukraine and says it is working closely with state authorities to investigate the attack. “The European defense industry has been facing persistent cyberattacks for years,” Quantum Systems stated without making a specific attribution. “Companies at the forefront of supporting Ukraine are being targeted precisely because our work is critically relevant to customers’ security.” According to reports from Der Spiegel and the Financial Times , citing internal sources, the compromise occurred via a malicious QBase update file.
