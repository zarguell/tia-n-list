IBM's vendor bulletin, initial publication 17 Sep 2026, covers 37 CVEs in Guardium Data Protection 12.2 including the story's lead flaw CVE-2026-84076 (improper authorization, CWE-285, CVSS 7.6) plus CVE-2026-84036, CVE-2026-84077, CVE-2026-84081 and CVE-2026-84241. It states the vulnerabilities are addressed in the SqlGuard_12.0p233_FixPack on Fix Central and lists no workarounds. It also discloses severer entries the story never saw, among them CVE-2026-82967 (9.8, unauthenticated bypass of IP-based access controls on the management interface) and CVE-2026-84275 (9.8, unauthenticated GIM file-upload path traversal).
