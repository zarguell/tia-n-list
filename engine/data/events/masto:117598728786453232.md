CISA has added the Cisco BroadWorks (CVE-2026-20320, CVSS 7.5, XML XXE injection, fixed in August 2026) and Openwave Webtop Media Transcoder (CVE-2026-111793, CVSS 4.3, patched since 2021) vulnerabilities to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Both flaws are being actively exploited even though fixes have been available for a long time, which leaves a large unpatched base of messaging and telephony infrastructure exposed.
