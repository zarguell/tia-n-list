The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has urged service providers to prioritize clear, timely, and transparent communication during major IT or operational technology (OT) outages. In newly published joint guidance, Communicating Under Pressure: Best Practices for Service Providers, CISA, the FBI, and international partners outlined how organizations should prepare for and manage outage communications affecting customers, critical-infrastructure operators, government entities, and the public. Published September 2, 2026, the guidance applies to disruptions caused by cyberattacks, human error, equipment failures, natural hazards, or defensive actions such as isolating systems during an incident. CISA Urges Service Providers to Provide Transparent Updates A significant outage at a single provider can affect a broad range of interconnected services, creating cascading operational consequences across sectors. Organizations dependent on telecommunications, cloud platforms , managed service providers , industrial systems, and other third-party infrastructure may lose visibility into the incident while facing immediate business and safety impacts. CISA warned that uncertainty can rapidly fuel speculation, misinformation, and public alarm. Without reliable provider updates, affected organizations and end users may rely on incomplete information, social-media claims, or unverified reporting to determine the scope and expected duration of an outage.
