Cofense documents a surge in campaigns using Vercel's v0[.]dev GenAI tool to mass-produce highly convincing phishing pages. Attackers generate spoofed Microsoft, Spotify, Facebook, and corporate career login pages via simple text prompts, deploy them instantly on Vercel's hosting, and integrate with Telegram bots for real-time credential exfiltration. The AI generates slightly different outputs per prompt, aiding evasion. Campaigns span fake job recruitment (Adidas, Nike, Ferrari), Microsoft credential harvesting, and Spotify payment capture. Vercel's free tier eliminates infrastructure costs. The broader trend: generative AI is transforming phishing from a technically demanding activity into an accessible, scalable operation requiring only basic knowledge and free tools.
