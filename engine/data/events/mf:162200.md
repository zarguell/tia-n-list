Anthropic has launched two frontier artificial intelligence models, Claude Fable 5.1 and Claude Mythos 5.1, targeting software engineering, enterprise knowledge work, scientific research, and cybersecurity defense. The models share the same core architecture but differ in the level of safeguards and access controls applied to potentially dual-use capabilities. Claude Fable 5.1 is generally available through Anthropic’s platforms and API, while Claude Mythos 5.1 is restricted to vetted users in Anthropic’s trusted-access programs, including cybersecurity defenders and life sciences researchers. Anthropic Launches Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 The rollout is designed to address enterprise concerns around AI operating costs, data privacy, and false-positive safety interventions that can limit legitimate defensive security work. Anthropic said Claude Fable 5.1 is approximately 25% less expensive than Fable 5 for typical workloads and can be up to 45% cheaper in heavily agentic deployments. The savings are primarily driven by a 75% reduction in cached-input read pricing, which now costs $0.25 per million tokens. Standard input and output token prices remain $10 and $50 per million tokens, respectively. For enterprises with strict data-residency requirements, Anthropic is introducing its Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS) framework .
