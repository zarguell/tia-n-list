SolarWinds published this advisory on 3 November 2026 for an authenticated command injection in Dameware that can compromise the remotely controlled host. Dameware 2026.3 and all previous versions are affected, and 2026.3.1 is the fixed release. The advisory rates the flaw 9.9 Critical with vector AV:N/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:C/C:H/I:H/A:H and credits Greg Bernatowicz of the Boeing Enterprise Security Red Team.
