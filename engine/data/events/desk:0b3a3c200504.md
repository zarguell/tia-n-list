Symantec's original Webworm disclosure describes customized versions of Trochilus, Gh0st RAT and 9002 RAT, with at least one indicator used against an IT service provider operating in multiple Asian countries and others in pre-deployment or testing stages. It links Webworm to the group tracked as Space Pirates, says the actor was active since at least 2017, and names government agencies plus IT services, aerospace and electric power enterprises in Russia, Georgia, Mongolia and other Asian countries as targets. The post details a loader chain using Logger.exe, logexts.dll and logexts.dat that unpacks Trochilus with configuration paths under C:\ProgramData\Logger.
