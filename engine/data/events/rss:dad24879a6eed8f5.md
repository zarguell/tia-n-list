Every Cybersecurity Awareness Month campaign rests on four habits: Strong passwords, Multifactor authentication, Spotting phishing, and keeping software updated. All four assume a person is the one signing in, reading the message and clicking install. At a growing number of companies that person is now an AI agent , and AI agent security has no equivalent of the annual training. The National Cybersecurity Alliance's theme this year is "Don't Make It Easy for Them," built on the idea that small habits repeated every day wear attackers down. "Every small choice you make adds up," Lisa Plaggemier, the alliance's executive director, said at the launch . She's right, and that is the problem. Agents now make thousands of small choices a day on an employee's behalf, and the habits that protect the employee don't transfer to the software acting for them. The record since July shows how wide the gap is. Researchers have counted 21 public incidents between July and mid-October in which agents from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Meta acted outside their authorized scope.
