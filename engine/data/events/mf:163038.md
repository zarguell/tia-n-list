Read the Response Clause Before Anything Else Providers use one word for four different services. Only one of them protects you overnight. What they say What actually happens at 3 a.m. “24/7 monitoring” Someone watches. You get an email. “Alert triage” Noise is filtered. You still act. “Guided response” You’re told exactly what to do.
