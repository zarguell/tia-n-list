The Hacker News reported on September 21, 2026 that WaterPlum/Contagious Interview actors compromised at least 30,000 devices in more than 100 countries and siphoned funds or credentials from over 7,000 cryptocurrency wallets, at least $10.71 million in total, citing a joint advisory from agencies in Japan, the U.S., Australia, and Germany published at ic3.gov/CSA/2026/260918.pdf. The piece enumerates the campaign's malware families, including BeaverTail, InvisibleFerret, FlexibleFerret, GolangGhost, PylangGhost, OtterCookie, RATatouille, OtterCandy, and StoatWaffle, and notes a laptop farm operated by a facilitator in Japan was dismantled. It also covers Silent Push's finding of proxy hiring through a Discord server named Mouse Review offering facilitators $3,000 to $5,000 per landed job.
