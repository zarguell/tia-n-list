The BBC reported that the top US cybersecurity official was leaving after just over a year, citing US media reports that Cairncross had been fired after losing the confidence of Stephen Miller. It said there had been no official confirmation of the removal, while Secretary of State Marco Rubio posted that Pentagon CIO Kirsten Davies was the new national cyber director. The report noted Rubio's earlier statement that there was zero evidence the outages were connected and that the US was not under systemic attack, alongside congressional scrutiny of the blackouts.
