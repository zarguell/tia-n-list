In early April 2018, a group signing itself JHT mass-exploited CVE-2018-0171 (Smart Install) and overwrote device configurations on network infrastructure including Russian and Iranian networks. Iran's ICT ministry reported over 200,000 routers affected worldwide, with 3,500 in Iran. This is the earliest large-scale weaponization of the exact flaw the 2026 advisory says Center 16 still uses.
