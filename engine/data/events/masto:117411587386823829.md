CERT-UA discovered UAC-0277, a campaign in which over 100 Ukrainian websites were turned into ClickFix traps to distribute Lunex Stealer via a smart contract mechanism. The campaign uses compromised sites to deliver malware through deceptive user interaction. Organizations should monitor for ClickFix-style delivery patterns.
