Sorcerer's apprentices among themselves. Anthropic and OpenAI co-call for pause on AI models.

via Mastodon https://www.heise.de/news/Anthropic-OpenAI-Co-fordern-Pause-bei-KI-Modellen-11451543.html

Anthropic and OpenAI have jointly called for a pause in the development of the most powerful AI models, arguing that the current pace of capability advancement outstrips the industry's ability to ensure safety. The two companies, normally fierce competitors, issued a rare joint statement warning that without deliberate slowdowns and stronger governance frameworks, the risk of deploying unsafe systems grows with each generation. The call targets frontier model development specifically and does not propose halting open-source or smaller-scale AI work.
