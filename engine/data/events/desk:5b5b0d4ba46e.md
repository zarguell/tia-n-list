AMD published security bulletin AMD-SB-6028 on November 10, 2026 covering 44 issues affecting Radeon and Instinct graphics products, including ten with a high severity score, with mitigation release dates ranging from January 2026 through August 2026 and some target releases planned for November 2026. The bulletin names external reporters including researchers from Meta Platforms, Seoul National University COMSEC Lab, and bug bounty researchers. It is one of the bulletins rolled up into AMD's November 2026 patch wave.
