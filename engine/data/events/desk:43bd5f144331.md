HUMAN's March 2025 report detailed BADBOX 2.0 as an expansion of the 2023 BADBOX operation, with backdoored low-cost, uncertified Android Open Source Project devices seen in 222 countries and territories. The BB2DOOR backdoor gave operators persistent privileged access for click fraud, two forms of advertising fraud and residential proxy services including IpMoYu, with ATO, fake account, DDoS, malware distribution and OTP theft among the downstream uses. HUMAN and partners including Google, Trend Micro, Shadowserver and the FBI partially disrupted the infrastructure, and Google removed publisher accounts and warned Play Protect users about associated apps.
