Rapid7 has uncovered a sophisticated intrusion in which Iranian state-sponsored actors linked to MuddyWater (Seedworm) masqueraded as the Chaos ransomware group to conduct targeted espionage. The campaign began with social engineering via Microsoft Teams — the attackers initiated external chat requests, conducted screen-sharing sessions, and instructed victims to enter credentials into local text files. Once inside, the group deployed a custom "Game.exe" RAT (trojanized from the legitimate Microsoft WebView2APISample project) using the ms_upd.exe downloader, both signed with the "Donald Gay" certificate — a known MuddyWater MOIS-linked code-signing identity. C2 infrastructure (moonzonet[.]com and uploadfiler[.]com) overlaps with MuddyWater's "Operation Olalampo" targeting U.S. and MENA organizations. The attackers never encrypted files — the ransomware component served purely as a false flag to complicate attribution and delay defensive response. Persistence was established via DWAgent and AnyDesk remote management tools. The incident underscores the convergence of state-sponsored intrusion and cybercriminal tradecraft, where ransomware branding is leveraged for concealment rather than financial gain.
