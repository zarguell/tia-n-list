Dan Goodin's article is the first mainstream coverage, published roughly a day before the cyberpress.org story in this event list. It quotes co-author Nadia Heninger of UC San Diego and Allurity's Karsten Nohl, and frames the cost: forging a 1024-bit RSA signature took about 2^65 operations and 1,380 core-years, versus an estimated 2^80 operations and 500,000 to 1 million core-years to factor the same key. It also identifies Privacy Pass, used by Apple and Cloudflare, as the best-known blind-RSA deployment, where an attacker would need roughly 2^43 token requests.
