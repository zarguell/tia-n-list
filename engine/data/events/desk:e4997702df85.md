Info-Gov.uk reported that TfL had notified the ICO and was asking photocard customers to change passwords, security questions and answers, and to change passwords on other services using the same information. It said the photocard website remained offline, renewals and journey-history queries would go through Customer Services on 0343 222 1234, and Freedom Pass applications were unaffected because London Councils handles them. The article noted TfL's 2024 breach prompted more than 7.1 million customer notifications, about 5,000 heightened-risk contacts and a February 2025 ICO decision that formal regulatory action was not proportionate.
