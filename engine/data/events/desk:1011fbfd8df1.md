HKCERT published this bulletin on November 11, 2026 under reference 20261111, cataloguing the Fortinet advisory wave by linking the individual PSIRT entries FG-IR-26-190 through the later numbers in the batch and rating the aggregate risk medium. The bulletin states a remote attacker could exploit some of the flaws for denial of service, elevation of privilege, security restriction bypass, sensitive information disclosure, remote code execution, data manipulation and spoofing. It is a regional CERT acknowledgement of the batch and carries no exploitation telemetry.
