A newly formed data extortion group named ‘Inception’ has been linked to a large-scale campaign that targets single sign-on (SSO) credentials of multiple global brands and firms. The gang surfaced on November 17, claiming seven victims on its extortion site, including chip maker AMD, PepsiCo, Cigna Healthcare, AutoZone, and Lucid Motors, but none of them have publicly confirmed a data breach. However, cybersecurity company ReversingLabs has been tracking Inception gang's activity and says that it has been operating since late October. The security firm has confirmed the existence of 21 domains used for SSO phishing, 41 domains believed to be used in campaign infrastructure, and 4 other domains with suspected use. The researchers identified an extensive phishing infrastructure impersonating dozens of major corporations, including Dropbox, Duolingo, Western Union, Indeed, Activision Blizzard, Riot Games, Twitch, Reddit, Nestlé, Anthropic, Nintendo, Starbucks, McDonald's, Paramount, and NBCUniversal. The existence of these domains is not proof that those firms have been breached, or even targeted yet, and none have recently disclosed cyberattacks or other related incidents. The campaign appears to rely on phone-based social engineering rather than email phishing, strongly resembling the tactics used by the infamous ShinyHunters extortion group.
