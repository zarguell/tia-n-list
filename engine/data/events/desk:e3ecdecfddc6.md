CIS and MS-ISAC issued advisory 2026-135 on 17 November 2026 after Oracle's Critical Security Patch Update. The advisory lists CVE-2026-114924 and CVE-2026-114925 among the affected E-Business Suite components and warns that the most severe Oracle flaws can allow remote code execution depending on account privileges. Its threat-intelligence section discusses the separate Java4Shell issue and reports no active exploitation for that issue as of 17 November; it makes no exploitation claim for the two EBS CVEs.
