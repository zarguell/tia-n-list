Acronis Threat Research Unit disclosed that a single operator in Hualien City, Taiwan, ran a mesh of 12 AI agents called BOSSAI that targeted hundreds of organizations across 11 countries. Confirmed breaches included a Taipei fire-department patient-care platform exposing medical records dating to 2018 (ECG readings, ambulance video, patient vital signs) without authentication, plus case records pulled from Taipei's 119 emergency-dispatch API across seven districts. The operator was attributed with high confidence through OPSEC failures including a home IP shared with a Korean skincare storefront and a GitHub account tied to a gas store in Hualien County.
