BleepingComputer reported on November 15, 2026 that the FBI had warned about ARTEX, an AI-powered framework used since at least August 2026 to automate intrusions worldwide. The article notes that more than 1,600 ARTEX consoles were publicly exposed by early November and that the original GitHub repository was removed on October 8, 2026, while forks, archived copies and ARTEX PRO kept the tool available. It also describes the October ConnectWise Cloud Backup supply-chain attack, where compromised application identities allowed access to Microsoft 365 customer environments, and a detection signature pack released by researcher MalwareMustDie.
