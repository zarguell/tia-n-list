Euronews reported on 19 November 2026 that the Commission had suffered a major cyberattack with no attribution and no confirmation that staff data was stolen. Quoting spokesperson Thomas Regnier, the article said the attack used a publicly available AI tool and identified it as ARTEX, with detection and confirmation on 22 October and no ransomware request. It noted that HR platforms hold names, pay grades, home addresses and contact details that could feed more targeted campaigns, while internal documents sit on a separate system; Sysper was working again but the Joint Sickness Insurance Scheme remained unavailable.
