Vendor advisory, published 2025-12-19 (page shows a later update on 2026-08-10), for the out-of-bounds write in the Fireware OS iked process scored 9.3 on CVSS v4.0, exploitable pre-authentication via IKEv2. It ships fixes in Fireware OS 2025.1.4, 12.11.6, 12.5.15 for T15/T35, and 12.3.1-b728352 for FIPS, plus Indicators of Attack: IKE_AUTH CERT payloads above 2000 bytes, peer certificate chains longer than 8, iked hang and crash, and six threat-actor IP addresses. Discovery is credited internally to WatchGuard, so there is no separate third-party researcher disclosure.
