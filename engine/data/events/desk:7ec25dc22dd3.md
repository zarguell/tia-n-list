The original researcher disclosure, received by the IACR ePrint archive on 2026-09-20 and approved 2026-09-22, by Laura Shea, Miro Haller, Adam Suhl, Nadia Heninger and Emmanuel Thome. The authors implemented the 2007 Joux-Naccache-Thome algorithm, spending 1,380 CPU core-years over five calendar months and 2^32 oracle queries so any signature on a 1024-bit key can later be forged offline in about 180 core-years. They conclude RSA with a signing oracle loses 15 to 30 bits of concrete security across the 1024-bit to 4096-bit parameters, leaving even 4096-bit RSA below a 128-bit level in that model.
