GitHub's October 12 Changelog release adds a durable, authenticated pusher identity to every commit, separate from the author and committer fields that the pushing client can supply. It also adds a new ruleset condition called "Committer must match pusher", which can block pushes when the committer metadata does not match a verified email address of the authenticated pusher. The practical value is better auditability for compromised accounts and suspicious pushes. GitHub says the preview is limited to Enterprise customers, who can request access through their account team. The release adds the control, but does not say it is enabled by default.
