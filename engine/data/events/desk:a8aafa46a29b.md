Harvard Magazine reported on November 25, 2025 that Harvard officials said the breach was discovered November 18, 2025 and that the university acted immediately to remove the attacker's access, with no evidence of further unauthorized access. The accessed systems do not generally contain Social Security numbers, passwords, or financial account numbers, but include personal information such as email addresses, telephone numbers, home and business addresses, event attendance, details of donations, and biographical information pertaining to fundraising and alumni engagement. Harvard notified affected individuals and launched an investigation with third-party cybersecurity experts and law enforcement.
