Digital Times reported that Samsung Securities found external intrusion traces in its VOC system on 21 November and confirmed that some attachments uploaded by customers had been exported. It says 8,241 customers were affected, with 13 possible data fields including names, account numbers, addresses, contact details and driver's license numbers; the exact fields differ by person. The article says the company blocked the attacking IP, suspended the service, reported to the Financial Supervisory Service and KISA, and warned of voice phishing, spam and malware follow-on risk.
