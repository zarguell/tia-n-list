Palo Alto Networks has acquired Console, an AI-native platform designed to deliver agentic capabilities across enterprise operations, as the cybersecurity giant advances plans to automate security investigation, prioritization, and response workflows. The acquisition, announced September 1, 2026, will deepen agentic AI capabilities within Palo Alto Networks’ Cortex security operations platform. The company said Console’s technology is intended to help security teams analyze alerts, resolve operational issues, and execute requests at machine speed through AI-driven workflows.
