Brandefense published early coverage at 21:08 UTC on 19 November 2026 noting that both CVEs affect EBS 12.2.3 through 12.2.15 and that Oracle publishes no single fixed version number. It advises selecting installation-specific patches from Oracle's EBS patch availability document, checking Database and Fusion Middleware components, and reviewing web-tier access logs for unexpected unauthenticated requests. The article says a newer release number alone does not prove the November fixes are installed and that the earlier October EBS flaw is a separate issue.
