Forescout Vedere Labs named ROMULUS a reusable multi-payload ClickFix delivery platform discovered while investigating infrastructure tied to the Custom GPT campaign. The research described separate infection chains, one delivering a RAT through a Stardock installer and another delivering LegionLoader through a PowerShell script, and identified six IP addresses hosting ROMULUS panels through VirusTotal and Shodan pivots. One server overlapped with infrastructure already reported by Huntress.
