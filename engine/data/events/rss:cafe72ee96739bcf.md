Then learn how to skip the rotation entirely. Long-lived API keys and passwords end up scattered across the credential layer, often in plaintext and in places they were never meant to be. They tend to stay there, valid and usable by anyone or anything that finds them. They remain this way for one simple reason: Credential rotation is one of the hardest problems in modern security. The stakes are fairly straightforward: every exposed credential is an open door to stolen data, destroyed systems, or deeper access. This is exactly what attackers have always gone after. Agentic AI raises the stakes, as it acts with whatever credentials are within reach, even when its operator has good intent. This is a growing problem.
