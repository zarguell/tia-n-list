AuditTeam is an active threat group that uses the OpsAgent framework to compromise organizations across at least seven countries and territories. The campaign began in January 2026 and remained active through October 2026, at a minimum. In its ransom note, AuditTeam demands an “Audit & Consulting fee” in exchange for vulnerability reports. During this period, the group has infected dozens of organizations in Russia, Italy, the Philippines, Hong Kong, Senegal, Finland and China. The group’s leak site also claims victims in seven additional countries. OpsAgent provides file system management, process injection, privilege escalation, keylogging, screenshot capture, and network pivoting. During the campaign OpsAgent was transitioned from C++ to Rust while adding new capabilities including interactive shells and WebSocket upgrades. Its command-and-control domains use Amazon CloudFront subdomains, which OpsAgent discovers via a public GitHub repository. Two specialized modules extend its functionality. An antivirus/endpoint detection and response (AV/EDR) killer module installs a digitally signed Beijing Jiangmin Antivirus driver to terminate security products.
