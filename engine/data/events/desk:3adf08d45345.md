SC Media reported that Concentra Health Services notified HHS that 3,998,162 of its patients were affected, bringing the total number of patients impacted by the PJ&A breach to over 14 million and making it the largest U.S. health sector breach of 2023. The attackers had access between March 27 and May 2, 2023 and exfiltrated data from April 7 to April 9. Cook County Health reported 1.2 million patients affected and Northwell Health initially said 3.9 million.
