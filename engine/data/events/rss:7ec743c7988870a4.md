The Binance hack drained an estimated $6.3 billion from the exchange’s hot wallets across roughly 10 blockchains on the evening of October 29, 2026. The Binance hack is a hot wallet compromise in which an attacker made Binance’s own withdrawal infrastructure sign and send funds to attacker-controlled addresses, mostly within about 20 minutes. Binance has confirmed the incident, and the evidence points to a serious, well-resourced operation: staged in advance, executed across chains at once, and built to get unauthorized transactions through the controls meant to stop them. About $3 billion has been blocked through on-chain freezes, and Binance has committed to paying for the rest.
