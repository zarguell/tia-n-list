Cisco Talos published an advisory on 13 November 2026 on growing in-the-wild use of AI-enabled penetration testing frameworks, including ARTEX. It describes ARTEX as developed under the pseudonym Autumn-27 and released on GitHub in July 2026, with planner and worker agents and a shared graph of assets, findings and progress. Talos found ARTEX deployed alongside VShell, GoPhish and AdaptixC2 through its Model Context Protocol extensibility, and cited CrowdStrike attribution of an espionage campaign beginning 17 October 2026 to VAPOR PANDA using ARTEX against more than 200 organizations.
