Jay's Brick Blog reported on 11 November 2026 that BrickLink had been down for almost a week, having gone offline on 5 November, and that the latest promised return date of 11 November had passed with no new date given. The post noted that BrickLink had by then said it detected unusual activity on the platform, after an earlier period with no communication at all. It compared the timing to the early November 2023 outage and criticised the lack of notice to sellers during one of the busiest shopping periods of the year.
