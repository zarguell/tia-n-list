Google's GHSA-7g3h-59cw-27vf is the original technical disclosure for CVE-2026-74127, a variant of Masterkey (CVE-2018-8930) in the Zen 1 and Zen+ PSP boot ROM, with Zen 2 and later listed as unaffected. It explains that the unsigned module directory and a donor module with size_signed equal to zero let modified main-bootloader code survive in SRAM at load address 0x100 and execute with full PSP privilege, without physical access. The advisory reports verification on a Ryzen 5 1600X and a Ryzen 7 2700X, supplies a PoC targeting MSI X370 Krait Gaming BIOS E7A33AMS.160, and says the bug was reported to AMD on August 19, 2026 with no mitigation planned.
