Rackspace is shutting down its Rackspace Spot cloud platform after a security breach allowed hackers to access customer Kubernetes clusters and secrets, giving customers until December 8 to migrate their services and download their data. Rackspace Spot is a cloud platform that allows organizations to deploy and manage containerized applications using Kubernetes. The company announced the shutdown in a new security notification sent to customers and obtained by BleepingComputer, saying it had decided to discontinue the service after reviewing the incident. "Following a review of the situation, Rackspace has made the decision to wind down the Rackspace Spot service. This decision is being made to provide a clear path forward for customers," reads the notification. Rackspace says the service will be permanently discontinued on December 8, 2026, at 5 PM Central Time, when all remaining workloads will be terminated, and customer data will no longer be accessible. Customers have been instructed to retrieve their data and migrate their workloads to different platforms before the deadline. The decision comes after hackers breached Rackspace Spot on November 17 using compromised credentials, allowing them to create unauthorized pods in customer Kubernetes clusters, read Kubernetes Secrets, and list nodes and volumes.
