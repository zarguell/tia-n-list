In our first look at the ZRON leak, we followed the company’s salespeople through cold calls, free trials, gifts of Xinyang Maojian tea and stalled budgets, and argued that the ecosystem exposed by the 2024 i-SOON leak remains recognizable, if evolving. This piece looks at what ZRON sells, and what its customers actually bought. The company, with 18 employees according to its own procurement filings, advertises tools spanning most of the intelligence cycle, from remote-control implants and covert email collection to translation, relationship mapping and AI-drafted reports. The AI angle drew wide attention when the Wall Street Journal reported on September 16, 2026 that ZRON used AI to make stolen foreign government data easier for police to digest, with Russia and Pakistan among the targets. The sales records help establish which parts of this broad catalogue found buyers. Of roughly 300 unique client codes logged by five company salespeople between August 2025 and June 2026, including separate contracts, at least 26 show recorded contracts, payments or deliveries. Most of those that name a product involve data processing, translation and written reports, while intrusion tools appear far less often.
