Magic number used in Sauron Loader This blog post provides a technical analysis of a novel malware we have encountered in multiple recent customer engagements. Sold in the underground as “Sauron Loader” , and undocumented as of now, it offers loader-type functionality exclusively to Russian cybercriminals. We detail its inner workings and offer tools and detection content to the cybersecurity community. Key Takeaways Multiple real-world incidents in Germany with novel malware family Sold as Malware-as-a-Service (MaaS) in the Russian underground Exclusively sold to Russian-speaking cybercriminals You can find indicators and tooling on our GitHub . Background During several recent campaigns targeting multiple organizations in Germany, we encountered a previously unknown malware family. In the cases we supported, Sauron Loader was the final stage of these attacks. It was delivered in various attack chains that all started with social engineering techniques: The attackers used both, ClickFix-style attacks as well as the recently popular voice phishing (vishing) technique. In the vishing attacks users were flooded with spam emails and then contacted by attackers posing as IT support. As part of the investigation, DCSO observed a post in an underground forum by a user operating under the alias “S4ur0n” (“leetspeak” for “Sauron”).
