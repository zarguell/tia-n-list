The Hacker News reported on October 8, 2026 that researchers identified a targeted campaign against South Korean financial organizations using the open-source agentic penetration-testing suite ARTEX. The article notes CrowdStrike analysis of open directories exposing Claude session histories and ARTEX configs at a Hong Kong IP, and references overlapping targets including Shinhan Bank and Yegaram Savings Bank. A Telegram message from the suspected actor (@YY520CN) posted the same day denied responsibility.
