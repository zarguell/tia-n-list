INTERNET Watch reported at 18:23 JST on November 9, 2026 that Nitori Holdings had announced unauthorized access to a system it manages, with up to 37.62 million records on its servers potentially leaked. The affected users are people who ordered through Nitori Net and the official Nitori shops on e-commerce mall sites, with member numbers, names including kana readings, email addresses, addresses, telephone numbers, birth dates and gender in scope for individuals, and company name, department, representative name, company address and telephone, and contact person details in scope for corporate members. The report states that credit card information and login passwords are not included, that the intrusion was detected on November 6 and that access may have continued from mid to late October, and that affected users will be contacted by email or post.
