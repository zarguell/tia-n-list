GangExposed, the anonymous leaker who spent 2025 exposing Conti and Trickbot leadership, has moved on to Qilin . A new post on the channel shares an investigation naming a Russian national as “Haise”, the persona that has fronted Qilin’s affiliate program on underground forums since 2022. The timing is hard to ignore. Only weeks ago, Germany arrested a Russian national suspected of being a leading Qilin member after extradition from Japan. Nothing public connects that suspect to Haise, and no authority has confirmed GangExposed’s identification. What we have is a single investigator’s chain of forum records, Telegram history and leaked Russian databases, and it deserves a careful read before anyone treats the name as settled.
