Beginning in May 2026, Oracle introduced CSPUs as a monthly release cycle that sits between the larger quarterly Critical Patch Updates (CPUs), addressing a focused set of high-severity issues on a faster cadence. This CSPU contains fixes for 640 unique CVEs in 655 security updates across 21 Oracle product families. Out of the 655 security updates published, 9.5% of patches were assigned a critical severity. Medium severity patches accounted for the bulk of security patches at 41.8%, followed by high severity patches at 38%. This month's update includes 62 critical patches across 57 CVEs.
