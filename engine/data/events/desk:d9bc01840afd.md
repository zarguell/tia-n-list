The Associated Press reported that the FBI was investigating an apparent hack of NCIC, citing a person familiar with the matter who spoke anonymously. NCIC holds records on fugitives, missing persons, criminal history, stolen property and other topics for local, state and federal law enforcement. The FBI declined to comment, the scope was not immediately clear, and AP said CNN first reported the hack. President Donald Trump told Fox News he had just heard about it and called it not good.
