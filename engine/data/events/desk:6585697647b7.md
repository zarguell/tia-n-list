DreamHost disclosed that an unauthorized third party accessed the server running its web-based File Manager at files.dreamhost.com, discovered on the afternoon of October 23, 2026. The intruder exploited a vulnerability in the File Manager software and could access FTP/SFTP usernames and passwords of users who logged in between approximately August 2026 and October 23, 2026. DreamHost said the attacker may also have accessed webmail systems, exposing session information including credentials for a limited number of users. The company said it found no evidence of control panel login or billing data exposure, and that the affected group was well under approximately 10 percent of hosted email and FTP/SFTP accounts.
