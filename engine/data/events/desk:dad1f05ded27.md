Sansec's original research article published 2026-10-24 describes the long-running loader campaign affecting 953 online stores, mostly Magento. It details the rotation of loader domains (manulfie.click, neswnsssn.click, lanhd6549tdhse.top, additionalver.com, and the October 2026 switch to deepveindrilling.com behind Cloudflare). The report notes that 471 stores were injected in March 2026 via the PolyShell vulnerability (CVE-2026-48356) and that Sansec Watch recorded about 52,000 browser loads of the additionalver.com loader. The loader uses CMS block injection and visitor fingerprinting.
