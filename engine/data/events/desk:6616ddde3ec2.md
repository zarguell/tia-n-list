Coverage of DSA-2026-479 published three days after Dell's advisory, describing 44 Dell-specific PowerStore vulnerabilities with seven at CVSS 9.8 and 11 at 9.0 or above, most of them in the SDNAS file services layer. The write-up states no exploitation has been reported and that the only listed fix is the upgrade to PowerStore OS 5.1.0.0-2798105 or later. It separates unauthenticated bugs reachable without credentials, such as the NAS TFTP stack overflow CVE-2026-116443 and the path traversal CVE-2026-116439, from flaws that require an internal attacker or an adjacent peer on the storage-processor interconnect.
