Arctic Wolf has identified an ongoing Microsoft 365 phishing campaign targeting organizations in healthcare, education, manufacturing, government, professional services, and other sectors across the United States, Canada, and Europe. The campaign, linked through technical and behavioral overlaps to the Payroll Pirates activity cluster known as Storm-2755, uses adversary-in-the-middle (AiTM) phishing pages to steal authenticated Microsoft 365 sessions. The attackers are primarily interested in employees who handle payroll, HR, finance, invoices, payments, and banking information. Unlike basic credential phishing, this operation can work even when multi-factor authentication is enabled. The AiTM proxy relays the real Microsoft login process in real time, allowing victims to complete MFA on what appears to be a legitimate sign-in page. The attackers then intercept the resulting session material and use it to access the account. Payroll Pirates Hijack M365 Sessions The campaign begins with voicemail-themed phishing emails impersonating an automated call-notification service. Messages use Microsoft branding and include fabricated caller IDs, dates, durations, and reference numbers to create urgency. Observed subjects follow a pattern similar to: [Organization] :ATTN: Review messages. Ref id: [random string] Victims are asked to click an “OPEN [Organization] VOICEMAIL PORTAL” button.
