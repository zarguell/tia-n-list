Travis Today’s frontier large language models mostly think out loud. Ask one to solve a hard problem and it writes out its reasoning as it works through it, in English, before answering: what it’s considering, what it’s ruling out, why. That readable scratchpad is called chain-of-thought, or CoT, and it’s one of the only windows researchers have into the process a model goes through to arrive at an answer. That makes it an important safety feature. Researchers can use those traces to detect dangerous behavior, understand why safeguards failed, and catch emerging problems that would be much harder to identify from outputs alone.
