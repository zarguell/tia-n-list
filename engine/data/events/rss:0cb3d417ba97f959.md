Medical technology company Boston Scientific is sending data breach notices to individuals affected by a cyberattack on the firm. According to a notification sample Boston Scientific shared with U.S. authorities, the hacker used social engineering in a voice phishing (vishing) attack to gain access to the company's cloud infrastructure. The attack exposed cloud-based applications and cloud-hosted data between May 7 and 1. The company became aware of the unauthorized access on May 12, and the investigation determined that a data breach occurred on November 5. Individuals in multiple U.S. states have been impacted, exposing personal details and medical information. Boston Scientific is a Massachusetts-based medical device maker whose products include stents, catheters, pacemakers, defibrillators, and endoscopes. It is one of the world’s largest medical device manufacturers, with more than 59,000 employees, 13 manufacturing facilities, and a presence in 127 countries.
