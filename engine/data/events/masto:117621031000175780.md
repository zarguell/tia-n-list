Multiple websites spread iPhone malware via ads. Several websites this month unknowingly distributed malicious content through advertising, targeting iPhone users.

The malicious ads were served through online advertising networks, meaning visitors to otherwise legitimate sites could be exposed to the threat without seeking it out. The report covers how the campaign was delivered via ad placements and what users can do to reduce their risk.
