An organization that tracks election threats for state and local officials said Tuesday afternoon that it had seen limited threat activity during the midterms, though a handful of incidents that briefly disrupted voting and equipment problems in some states have fueled unfounded claims of fraud. Most cases have been resolved quickly, the Elections Infrastructure Information Sharing and Analysis Center said in a 3 p.m. EST update. “The midterm elections are a massive undertaking. Each cycle, some jurisdictions experience challenges and rely on layered safeguards to protect the accuracy of results,” said Geoff Hale, an election security visiting fellow at the Center for Democracy and Technology. “This election cycle, the incidents we’re hearing reports of aren& #39 ;t significantly different than those in previous years. So far, we have not seen evidence of significant cyber incidents,” added Hale, who was also former associate director for election security and resilience at the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency.
