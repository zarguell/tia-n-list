Malwarebytes reviewed the Remus claims on November 11 and stressed that the promotional material showed a mailbox interface but did not prove retrieval of new messages after the account was comprehensively secured. It noted that Google documents password changes as invalidating access and refresh tokens, so token theft alone does not establish continued access. Its response guidance called for using a separate trusted device, disconnecting and cleaning the infected machine, reviewing signed-in devices and app access, and checking Gmail forwarding, filters, delegation, and recovery settings.
