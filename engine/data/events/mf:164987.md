Crypto casino Duelbits has confirmed a cybersecurity incident that drained roughly $7 million from its hot wallets, prompting the platform to take its website offline while its team investigates the breach and rebuilds core transaction infrastructure. Duelbits co-founder Joe disclosed the incident in a post on X, stating that customer funds remain protected and that the platform will resume operations after investigators complete their review and the company replenishes affected wallets. The outage affects deposits and withdrawals as the company works to secure its environment. The breach came to light after blockchain security firm Scam Sniffer reported suspicious withdrawals worth approximately $4.2 million from Duelbits-linked hot wallets across Ethereum , BNB Chain, and Tron. Duelbits Confirms $7 Million Hot Wallet Hack The assets moved to newly created wallet addresses, a pattern often associated with unauthorized cryptocurrency transfers and rapid laundering activity. Scam Sniffer assessed the initial activity as a suspected private-key compromise. Later analysis identified an additional 8.1 BTC withdrawn from the company’s Bitcoin hot wallet, bringing the total reported loss to around $7 million. On Ethereum, the compromised wallet transferred 836 ETH, around 593,000 USDT, 97,000 USDC, 31,500 DAI, and 12.4 billion SHIB within minutes.
