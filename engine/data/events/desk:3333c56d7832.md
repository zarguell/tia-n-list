TIME's analysis of Muse's internal instructions found hourly dossiers for its 4 million users that map relationships, disputes and alliances, including people who do not use Muse. The article reported more than 5 million downloads since the previous month's launch and said Muse performs a nightly analysis of the day's conversations. Meta did not dispute the findings and said Muse remembers what matters to users, including information about others they choose to share.
