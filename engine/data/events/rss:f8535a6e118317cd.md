ATT&CK v20: Critical Infrastructure Campaigns, Adversary Use and Abuse of AI, & Iranian Cyber Espionage ATT&CK v20 brings more critical infrastructure and cross-domain activity into the framework, with Campaigns for attacks on U.S. water utilities and an AI-assisted breach of Mexican government infrastructure that reached both IT and OT, along with new and updated Groups and Software tied to Iranian cyber espionage, Chinese and North Korean operations, and ransomware. Enterprise gains AI-focused and AI-enabled Techniques, ICS added Techniques for command and script execution and command and control over protocols industrial networks already use, and Mobile un-deprecated a Technique for delivering malicious apps outside official stores. On the defensive side, ICS Detection Strategies (DETs) now carry platform and Asset context, tying behaviors to the operational role of each system, and Mobile DETs reflect the visibility limits of Android and iOS. Here’s what’s in v20: ICS: Two Campaigns, the 2026 U.S. water utility attacks and the IT/OT breach of Mexican government infrastructure; two new pieces of Software, CyberAv3ngers’ IOControl and the AI-generated toolkit from the Mexico breach; and Command and Scripting Interpreter with six Sub-Techniques plus two new command and control Sub-Techniques, so ICS mappings can align to the specific interpreter or protocol an adversary used. Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI): Iran-linked NIMBUS MANTICORE and 13 pieces of its espionage toolkit
