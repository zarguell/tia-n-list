Security researcher Bashis (mcw@noemail.eu) published full disclosure on 2021-10-06 after limited disclosure on 2021-09-06. The disclosure documents two authentication bypass techniques: CVE-2021-33044 (NetKeyboard clientType trust bypass) and CVE-2021-33045 (loopback source-address spoof). Both return full administrator sessions without valid credentials. A proof-of-concept repository (DahuaConsole) and a timeline of vendor interaction from June 13 through October 6, 2021 are included.
