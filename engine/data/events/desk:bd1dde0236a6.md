Researcher Sean Bales released two zero-day exploits, 4EverAble2Shell and Agent2Shell, tested against N-central Appliance 2026.4.1.26 HF2. BleepingComputer reported at least 21 affected systems, including 19 running the latest update, with cryptocurrency miners deployed, and said exploitation requires the platform's Legacy API to be enabled. The disclosures followed N-able taking hosted N-central offline and urging on-premises customers to shut down servers after Bales published more than 30 alleged zero-day vulnerabilities.
