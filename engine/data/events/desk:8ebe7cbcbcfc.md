Google Threat Intelligence Group disclosed DarkSword, an iOS full-chain exploit kit observed since at least November 2025 and used by multiple commercial surveillance vendors and suspected state-sponsored actors against targets in Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Malaysia, and Ukraine. The kit supports iOS 18.4 through 18.7 and chains six vulnerabilities to deploy GHOSTBLADE, GHOSTKNIFE, and GHOSTSABER payloads. GTIG published the analysis on March 18, 2026, months before the same DarkSword chain appeared in the APT29 watering hole campaign.
