Italy's CERT-AGID today identified and disrupted a new MintsLoader campaign that abuses compromised PEC (certified email) mailboxes to send malicious messages to other certified email addresses. Important note: a PEC only certifies the transmission and delivery of a message. It does not guarantee that the sender's mailbox was not compromised, nor that the received attachment is safe.
