Threadlinqs Intelligence published TL-2026-1570 on July 20, 2026, linking the ChainVeil and ViteVenom malware families to PolinRider at medium confidence and to a North Korean nexus. The report describes a four-tier Tron, Aptos and BNB Smart Chain dead-drop resolver used to hide command-and-control addresses, alongside obfuscated JavaScript loaders in nearly 2,000 GitHub repositories and more than 108 malicious packages or extensions across npm, Packagist, Go modules and the Chrome Web Store. It lists two operator wallet addresses, 9 detection rules and 47 tracked indicators of compromise.
