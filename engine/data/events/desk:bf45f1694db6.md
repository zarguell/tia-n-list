Offseq Threat Radar published an entry for the SANSAR report on November 12, 2026, describing a Rust orchestrator that manages CPU and GPU miners over Tor after abuse of an exposed Ray job agent. The entry notes the ftrace kernel rootkit, the NVML wrapper that falsifies telemetry to 0% utilization, and the netns backdoor that retrieves command-and-control addresses from Ethereum smart contracts. It states that no specific patch or fix is indicated and advises restricting the Ray job agent, hunting for indicators, and monitoring GPU telemetry anomalies.
