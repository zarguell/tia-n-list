Reporting on DFIR firm QUIRSO's findings published August 13, 2026: CVE-2026-59310 was being exploited in an active campaign deploying a reverse SSH tool for persistence and remote access, with compromised systems first connecting to attacker-controlled infrastructure on August 3, five days after the patch. QUIRSO identified compromises at 361 IP addresses across 47 countries, more than half in Germany, the U.S., Turkey, Iran and France, attributed to a suspected APT actor.
