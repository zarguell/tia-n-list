Greg Kroah-Hartman posted the Linux kernel CVE team's disclosure of CVE-2026-99231 on 2026-11-02, describing a use-after-free in io_uring/waitid where io_waitid_wait() clears ->head before owning the request, allowing a concurrent owner to complete and free it while the wake-up handler still touches it. The flaw was introduced in 6.12.113 and fixed in 6.12.115, 6.18.60, and 7.2.14, rated CVSS 3.1 7.8. The fix keeps ->head set until ownership is acquired.
