The Hacker News reported on November 10, 2026 that Fireworks AI's investigation into a security incident first reported in early October uncovered further malicious activity affecting account and billing information, alongside secrets, datasets, and incomplete models for a limited set of accounts. The attackers reached Fireworks' development environment via an unnamed third-party cloud provider that hosted its R&D machines on October 23, 2026, then ran an unauthorized workload on October 24 that led to additional customer data impact. Notion disclosed that the incident impacted fewer than 1% of Notion workspaces, and Fireworks stated payment card numbers are not stored by the company and were not impacted.
