President Donald Trump nominated Palantir security chief Michael LoSapio to lead CISA, a move that could end the agency's nearly two-year wait for a Senate-confirmed director after Jen Easterly's January 2025 departure. CISA has shed more than 1,000 staff, roughly a third of its workforce, since Trump returned to the White House, and acting director Nick Andersen said in September that about 250 recruits were still waiting for security clearance. LoSapio is Trump's second nominee for the role; Sean Plankey withdrew in April after more than a year awaiting confirmation amid a dispute involving Senator Rick Scott over Coast Guard contracting. Palantir, co-founded by Peter Thiel, is a major supplier to DHS and ICE, and its immigration work has drawn criticism from civil liberties groups and Democratic lawmakers.
