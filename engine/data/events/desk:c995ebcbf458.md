Advisory `GHSA-cr2m-2j4r-3v3x` rates as High a stored cross-site scripting flaw where an unauthenticated visitor posts a comment containing a shortcode whose escaped attribute value decodes into markup after the comment was sanitized. Where a Comments or Latest Comments block was placed in post content, that markup rendered under the post allowlist. Affected versions span 4.7.0 through 7.1.4, the fix is in 7.1.5 and backported to branches back to 4.7, no CVE ID was assigned at publication, and rafiem is credited.
