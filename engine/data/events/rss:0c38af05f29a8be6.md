On-premises servers can be essential to business operations, but they are increasingly attractive targets to attackers. AI can help less-skilled actors analyze software, develop malicious tools, and repeat attack workflows at greater speed and scale, shortening the window between exposure and exploitation. Organizations need to strengthen security faster without disrupting the critical workloads they rely on. Today, we’re announcing a new on-premises security initiative, initially focused on server workloads. The initiative brings together guidance, migration , and product investments to help customers understand and prioritize risk, reduce their on-premises footprint wherever possible, and strengthen critical workloads that need to remain. Guidance for the initiative will soon be available through Secure Now , which also includes our AI readiness checklist in Microsoft Security Exposure Management and guidance for strengthening foundational security. Understand Identify the most important on-premises risks and prioritize actions customers can take now. Reduce Move workloads to Microsoft 365 where possible to take advantage of cloud-based security, resilience, and continuous innovation. Secure Strengthen critical workloads that need to remain on-premises and reduce the manual work required to keep them secure.
