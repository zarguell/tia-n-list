Swiss and Italian cybersecurity agencies are warning about automated cyberattacks powered by AI agents targeting organizations in the critical infrastructure sector. The two agencies listed four AI-powered penetration testing and red-teaming frameworks that have been involved in recent breaches and hacking incidents : ARTEX AI, CyberStrikeAI, DeepSeek Harness - Red Team, and PentAGI. Both agencies have urged organizations to reduce the number of systems exposed on the internet, prioritize patching, and monitor for unusual activity. CSIRT Italia says it detected large-scale malicious campaigns that used autonomous AI agents to identify and exploit vulnerabilities and misconfigurations on exposed endpoints. In some cases, the agency observed multiple AI agents working in parallel to compromise a large number of systems within a few hours. In one instance involving a DeepSeek Harness setup, a coordinator assigned tasks to five AI agents that undertook a broad spectrum of operational stages, from information gathering to exploitation and persistence.
