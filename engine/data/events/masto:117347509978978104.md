ENISA has published the 2026 edition of its Threat Landscape report, based on incidents and events observed between January 1 and December 31, 2025. The report concludes that threat groups continued to adapt their methods across the reporting period, with cybercriminals, hacktivists, and state-linked groups all affected by the same underlying shifts.

Key findings: groups increasingly relied on scalable techniques, shared tooling patterns, and trusted digital environments rather than bespoke operations. The report also documents progressive integration of AI into malicious cyber activity during 2025, observed among cybercriminals, state-linked attack groups, and information manipulation (IMS) groups. The framing for the EU is a threat landscape shaped by geopolitical developments and the growing interconnection of digital ecosystems, with actor behaviors converging across groups.
