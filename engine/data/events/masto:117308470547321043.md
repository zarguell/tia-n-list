Google's Mandiant team infiltrated an analyst within TeamPCP, a threat actor group responsible for a supply chain attack targeting open source projects. The attack propagated malware and stole credentials from more than 1,000 companies. While operating inside the group's infrastructure, Mandiant notified victims of the compromise.

The operation represents a significant law enforcement-style action by a private threat intelligence firm. TeamPCP compromised open source projects to distribute malware, leveraging the trust inherent in open source ecosystems to reach downstream users. The credential theft affected a broad range of organizations across multiple sectors.

The infiltration allowed Mandiant to observe the group's operations from inside and provide early warning to affected parties. The attack on open source supply chains continues to be a major concern for the software industry, as compromised packages or repositories can propagate malicious code to thousands of downstream users before detection.
