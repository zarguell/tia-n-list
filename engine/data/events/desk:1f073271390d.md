Defused Cyber reported active exploitation of CVE-2026-46817 observed on June 27, 2026, with six unauthenticated file-read attempts against Oracle EBS decoys from a single EU source IP. The attacks happened roughly six weeks after Oracle's May 2026 patch and before any public proof-of-concept existed. The attacker used a POST to /OA_HTML/ with a crafted XML DeliveryRequest payload targeting the Payments File Transmission component. No broad scanning was observed; the activity was characterized as targeted proof-of-concept testing.
