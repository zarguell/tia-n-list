France's cybersecurity agency ANSSI announced it will stop certifying security products that lack quantum-resistant encryption, effective from 2027. Businesses should be buying only quantum-safe products by 2030. Because ANSSI certification is required for use in French government agencies and critical infrastructure, the policy represents a de facto phase-out of traditional encryption across France's most sensitive sectors. The move, reported at the France Quantum conference by ANSSI Chief of Staff Samih Souissi, signals accelerating momentum in post-quantum cryptography adoption at the national regulatory level.
