Research published on 5 November by Apiiro's Shmulik Cohen and Matan Giladi showed that Claude Code can retain a complete approved shell command, including credentials, in .claude/settings.local.json. The firm found 1,122 public copies of the file holding at least one secret, and broader scans surfaced 2,539 secrets across 1,994 public repositories. The exposure comes from the permanent approval option saving credential-bearing commands in plain text.
