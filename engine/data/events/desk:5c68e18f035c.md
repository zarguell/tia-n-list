WIRED reported that researcher Karan Joshi extracted Muse's internal instructions through the normal chat interface and shared them with the publication. One instruction tells Muse to maintain a page for every person in the user's life, refreshed hourly, covering family, partners, friends, colleagues, collaborators and followed accounts. The pages may record facts, shared history, relationship details and open threads; Meta said the files were intentionally accessible and that Muse gathers context from public information and what users choose to share.
