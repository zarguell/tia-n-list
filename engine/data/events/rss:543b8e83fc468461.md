Every SIEM can hand its data back in one of two shapes: the original raw events, or its own parsed JSON. For analysis outside the SIEM, raw events are almost always the better choice. Export them for a narrow time window through the API or CLI, not the browser. The table below shows the best route for each major platform, with a link to the full guide. Why bother taking data out of a SIEM at all? For a second opinion, to share with an IR partner who has no access, or to run detection logic your SIEM doesn’t have. Prime Core has 1M detection rules across these platforms, mapped to 676 MITRE ATT&CK techniques, and LogTotal runs them against a file for free.
