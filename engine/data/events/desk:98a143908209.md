SecQuill reported, from Google Threat Intelligence research, that ARMORY CASTLE exploited CVE-2026-123890 as a zero-day in early September 2026, more than two months before VMware disclosed it on November 19. The report says the actor used port 2012 for the exploit, moved through an internal Linux appliance with the SLIMSAVAGE and CAGEMATCH web shells, used the BONESAW Go utility to copy the vCenter directory database, and used SAWSNAKE to forge SAML login tokens. It also notes a recovered BONESAW build date of July 13 and that blocking port 2012 from unapproved addresses would have stopped the exploit from reaching the vulnerable service.
