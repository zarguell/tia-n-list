Ukraine's Cyberpolice and National Police published the account of a two-year joint investigation with the FBI. Acting police chief Maksym Tsutskiridze said the group built a platform with AI-generated voice messages, called employees posing as IT support and persuaded them to hand over credentials or install remote-access software before stealing data and encrypting files. He said at least 28 companies had paid more than $40.5 million, about UAH 1.8 billion, and described one US victim that lost 1.4 TB of data, was asked for $10 million and paid $5 million after negotiation. The release says four key suspects were charged and detained after 66 searches, with almost $370,000 and EUR 20,000 in cash seized.
