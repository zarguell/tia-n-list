Advisory FG-IR-26-265, published November 10, 2026, covers CVE-2026-126251, an incorrect authorization [CWE-863] in FortiAuthenticator rated 9.1 that allows an attacker to bypass authentication and gain unauthorized access. FortiAuthenticator 8.0.0 through 8.0.3 moves to 8.0.4 and 6.6.0 through 6.6.10 moves to 6.6.11. The acknowledgement credits Gwendal Guegniaud of the Fortinet Product Security team for an internal discovery made with the use of the frontier AI model Claude Mythos, one of many advisories in this batch with the same credit.
