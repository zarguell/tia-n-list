Drupal administrators are facing an actively exploited vulnerability tracked as CVE-2026-132165, also known as Fail2Install , that can allow an unauthenticated attacker to force a live Drupal website back into its installation workflow. Under specific conditions, this can lead to complete site takeover or pre-authentication remote code execution. Drupal originally disclosed the vulnerability on November 18, 2026 and rated it Highly Critical, 20/25 , after exploitation was later confirmed on live sites as early as November 19 at 19:00 UTC. CISA added the flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 24, giving U.S. federal agencies until November 27 to remediate affected systems. Fail2Install targets Drupal’s built-in installer, located at: /core/install.php The installer remains present after a Drupal site has been deployed. Researchers demonstrated that temporary resource-exhaustion conditions can make the installer incorrectly conclude that an existing site is not installed, reopening setup functionality to an anonymous remote attacker. Contact Sales Searchlight Cyber demonstrated the attack against Drupal 8.9, 9, 10, and 11 using Apache or PHP-FPM with both MySQL and PostgreSQL. The researchers also showed a path from installer takeover to arbitrary PHP execution by abusing Drupal’s SQLite installation option.
