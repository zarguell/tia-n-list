Check Point has issued an urgent security advisory warning that attackers are actively exploiting two critical vulnerabilities affecting its VPN gateway and security-management products. The flaws, tracked as CVE-2026-85102 and CVE-2026-93616, both carry a CVSS severity score of 9.8 and enable unauthenticated attackers to gain code-execution capabilities in exposed environments. CVE-2026-85102 affects Check Point Security Gateway and Spark Firewall deployments using Remote Access VPN or certificate-based Site-to-Site VPN. Critical Check Point VPN and Management Vulnerabilities The vulnerability stems from improper validation of certificate data during VPN negotiation , potentially allowing a remote unauthenticated attacker to execute arbitrary code on a vulnerable gateway. Check Point released patches for the issue on September 9, 2026, initially reporting no evidence of exploitation. However, the company later detected exploitation attempts beginning September 12 against Spark Firewall customers worldwide. The activity originated through anonymization infrastructure, including VPN services and proxy networks, making source attribution and network-based blocking more difficult. Observed attacks used suspicious certificate subjects including CN=vpn,OU=users,O=global , CN=vpn-user,OU=users,O=global , and CN=vpnuser,OU=users,O=global .
