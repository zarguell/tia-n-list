Beazley Security's October 21, 2026 advisory covered Expressway-C and Expressway-E versions below X15.5.20 and reported no public exploitation or proof of concept at that time. It relayed Cisco's position that there are no workarounds and recommended restricting the database service and cluster communication to trusted cluster peers, including blocking internet access to the service on Expressway-E. The firm said it was running exposure checks and threat hunts for exploitation attempts.
