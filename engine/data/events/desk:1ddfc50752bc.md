Harvard University Information Technology published an FAQ on November 22, 2025, stating that on November 18, 2025 an unauthorized party accessed Alumni Affairs and Development information systems via a phone-based phishing attack, and access was removed immediately. The breached systems do not generally contain Social Security numbers, passwords, payment card information, or financial account numbers, but do include email addresses, telephone numbers, home and business addresses, event attendance, donation details, and biographical information tied to fundraising and alumni engagement. Affected groups include alumni, alumni spouses, partners, and widows or widowers, donors, parents of current and former students, and some current students, faculty, and staff. Harvard engaged third-party cybersecurity experts and law enforcement to investigate.
