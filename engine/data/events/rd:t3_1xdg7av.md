New technical findings make the PHP2Shell situation more serious than the original patch announcement alone suggested. Amazon Threat Intelligence identified attempted exploitation dating to October 17, four days before the vulnerabilities were publicly disclosed. The vulnerabilities are: CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 Both carry critical CVSS v4.0 scores of 9.2. Amazon observed additional attempts before the public release, followed by widespread exploitation attempts after the October 21 disclosure. Researchers have not established that every observed attempt successfully compromised a real-world server. Amazon Web Services, Inc.
