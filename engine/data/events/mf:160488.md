Pokémon Center has begun notifying customers in the United Kingdom and Germany that their personal information was exposed after a cyberattack on CEVA Logistics, the third-party responsible for fulfilling Pokémon Center orders in both markets. The incident has also led to the cancellation of a batch of pending orders, extending the impact of a data-security event into customer service and supply chain disruptions . The retailer said its own website was not the source of the compromise. Instead, the exposure was traced to CEVA Logistics, which processes delivery information for retail clients. Pokémon Center Data Breach In breach-notification emails, Pokémon Center apologized for canceling recent orders due to an “unforeseen fulfillment issue” and then explained that CEVA had been the victim of a cyberattack. CEVA told Pokémon Center the activity began on July 30, 2026, affecting systems used in the fulfillment process. Pokémon Center said unauthorized parties may have accessed customers’ full names, mailing addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and details about the contents of their PokémonCenter.com orders. The company said payment-card details were not involved because CEVA does not have access to that information. It also stated that other Pokémon Center account information was not affected.
