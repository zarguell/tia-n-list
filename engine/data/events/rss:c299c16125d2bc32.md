President Donald Trump intends to nominate Pentagon official Kirsten Davies to be the country’s next cyber czar, according to a White House official. Davies, the military’s chief information officer, was named the acting national cyber director last week after the departure of Sean Cairncross, a former Republican National Committee official. However, following the “overwhelming” support Davies received from industry and government leaders after the announcement, Trump decided to fill the position permanently, the White House official said. Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton, who already chairs the administration’s senior-level commission on artificial intelligence, will step into the role while Davies awaits Senate confirmation. “I’m excited to work more with Kirsten, who has the experience and capabilities needed to help America navigate our increasingly complex and powerful digital infrastructure as our next National Cyber Director,” Clayton said in a statement. “I look forward to working with the ONCD team to ensure Kirsten has a seamless transition into the role upon her confirmation by the Senate.” Prior to the Pentagon, Davies was the chief information security officer at Unilever, a role she previously held at Estée Lauder Companies. She was also the managing director and group chief security officer for Barclays Africa, now known as ABSA.
