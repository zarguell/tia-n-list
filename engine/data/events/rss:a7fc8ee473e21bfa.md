GoDaddy has revoked 404,607 SSL/TLS certificates after discovering that certain subscriber certificates were issued outside the defined profiles in its Certification Practice Statement (CPS) and Certificate Policy (CP). The revocation took effect on October 21, 2026, requiring affected customers to obtain and install replacement certificates to avoid browser security warnings and potential website outages.
