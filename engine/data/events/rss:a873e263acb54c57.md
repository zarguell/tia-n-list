The Federal Trade Commission is asking a court to force the owner of multiple digital security and privacy companies from further operations, saying the businesses are serially defrauding their customers out of millions of dollars. Earlier this month, the FTC filed a federal lawsuit in Ohio against Dmitry Belkin and 12 of his businesses, alleging they engaged in unfair and deceptive trade practices under the Federal Trade Act and violated the Telemarketing Act’s prohibition against deceptive and abusive telemarketing acts since at least 2020. The majority of Belkin’s companies, with names like Digital Forensics Corp., 1 st All Data Recovery and Remote Backup Corp., advertised services to help people deal with ongoing online romance or “sextortion” scams, where a scammer threatens to publish a nude or sexual photo of the victim unless they pay a ransom. They operated websites like BlackmailHelpline.com, DigitalForensics.com and other sites that would routinely be advertised on Google, YouTube, X and other sites when users are searching for help with sextortion attacks.
