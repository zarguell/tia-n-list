Patrick Forck and Jeremy McCarthy of Element Digital published a technical write-up on 19 November 2026 describing four-byte unauthenticated writes to an Elecbrakes brake-control BLE characteristic, with no pairing, PIN or replay protection and notification feedback on brake state. They report testing on their own hardware, including a closed-road drive test where higher force values produced stronger deceleration, and an OTA channel reachable after Just Works pairing with firmware integrity checked only by CRC32. The post says the vendor shipped fixes on 6 July 2026 for EB2 3.0.4.4.3 and VM1 1.3.4.2.1, left the older ELBC 2000 without a fix, and found no evidence of exploitation.
