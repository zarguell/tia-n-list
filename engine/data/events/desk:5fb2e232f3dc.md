The Guardian reports that an operation led by Whitehall and the NCSC followed the discovery of weaknesses in RDM devices by Omega Solutions employee Neil McCourt and validation by Pen Test Partners. Garry Turner-Smith of Omega said an attacker with full control of vulnerable RDM data managers could interfere with refrigeration across multiple sites. Ken Munro said the discovery method was not published, that many thousands of controllers at small stores were still exposed, and that operators should take controllers off the public internet or apply updated firmware.
