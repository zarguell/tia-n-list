Politico broke the story in Brussels Playbook on 19 November 2026, reporting that the cyberattack potentially compromised swaths of personal data, blocked staff from entering salary and leave information, and prompted the Commission's Corporate Management Board to discuss the crisis. An EU official said most of the attack had been contained but the extent of damage was unclear; CERT-EU had detected earlier that month that international hackers used ARTEX, and the official said some actors misuse such tools and had targeted the Commission. Sysper and MyPMO had been restored earlier that week, though Sysper briefly failed again, and translation services were hit on Tuesday.
