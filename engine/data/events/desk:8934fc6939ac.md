Ostorlab published a deep dive on 2026-11-04 at 10:00 UTC tracing the root cause to commit d28f42f2703e, which introduced the deferred-release grant-copy cache in Xen 4.6.0 and matches the advisory's vulnerable range. The post notes that no Xen release tarball carried the XSA-523 patches as of 2026-11-04 and that branches 4.6 through 4.16 receive no upstream patch because they are end of life. It also records that no exploitation was known and neither CVE was in the CISA KEV catalog as of that date.
