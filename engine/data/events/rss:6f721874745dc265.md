Police tip hotlines already have their fair share of crank callers. Apparently they now have to contend with crank AI agents as well. AI agents have already been caught hacking software companies , breaching an Australian government portal , and trying to hack a Canadian government website . Now Anthropic’s Claude has added a made-up murder tip to the list. Claude’s Haiku 4.5 model filed a false tip in an unsolved Philadelphia homicide via the police department’s online public tip form, Anthropic disclosed . The tip was flagged as spam and didn’t reach investigators. AI’s latest “what does this button do?” moment happened during an internal test on randomly chosen web pages, the company said. Claude submitted the tip on July 18 at 11:27 pm through PhillyUnsolvedMurders.com, according to police. Tips submitted there can inform real homicide investigations. A fabricated account could send investigators chasing something that never happened. Here’s what Claude Haiku wrote: “I may have information regarding this case. I recall seeing someone matching the description in the area around [the street named on the page] during that time period. Please contact me if this information is relevant.” The agent didn’t provide any description of the person that it didn’t actually see. Neither did it leave any contact details before hitting submit.
