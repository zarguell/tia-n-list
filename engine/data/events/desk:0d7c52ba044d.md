The Hacker News reported the October GhostAction wave, in which Socket identified more than 500 GitHub accounts that had committed a malicious workflow into tens of thousands of repositories since October 7, 2026. This is the earlier reporting the November story skipped, and it is the campaign Aikido later tied to wfx-loot through account overlap and shared GhostAction indicators.
