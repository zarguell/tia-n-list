I published the full write-up for four vulnerabilities I reported in the ZTE SmartLife ecosystem. The most serious one was a password reset flaw rated CVSS 8.8 High. The SmartLife backend accepted an account ID and a new password without requiring the reset code. The account verification flow could also disclose whether an email was registered and return its backend account ID. Put together, I could take one of my test accounts from an email address to a changed password and a valid authenticated session. The four assigned CVEs are CVE-2026-86552, CVE-2026-86553, CVE-2026-86554 and CVE-2026-86555. I tested everything against researcher-controlled accounts and disclosed the findings to ZTE PSIRT. ZTE later confirmed that the vulnerabilities had been patched. The scale was also worth noting.
