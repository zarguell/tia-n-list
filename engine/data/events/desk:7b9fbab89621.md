Amazon Threat Intelligence reported that its MadPot honeypot captured an attempt to exploit the PHP2Shell vulnerabilities on October 17, 2026, four days before public disclosure. The payloads and anonymization choices varied, suggesting multiple actors, and AWS CISO CJ Moses said PHP2Shell is now seeing broad exploitation attempts in the wild. Dataminr's TRACE team separately observed only scanning, fingerprinting and non-working exploits with no known compromises, while noting working proof-of-concept code or successful exploitation could follow. These observations concern CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 rather than CVE-2026-115560.
