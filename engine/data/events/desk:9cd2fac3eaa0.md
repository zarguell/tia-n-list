Jamf researchers documented a MacSync variant delivered as a code-signed and notarized Swift application inside a disk image named zk-call-messenger-installer-3.9.2-lts.dmg, distributed from zkcall.net under Developer Team ID GNJLS3UYZ4. The certificate was revoked after a report to Apple. The same report traces the family to its April 2025 debut as Mac.C by the actor Mentalpositive and notes anti-sandbox connectivity checks and decoy PDF padding inflating the DMG to 25.5 MB.
