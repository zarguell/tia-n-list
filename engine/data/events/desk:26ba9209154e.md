Resecurity published analysis on 10 September 2025 describing a KillSec ransomware attack on MedicSolution, a Brazilian healthcare software vendor. The breach exposed over 34 GB of data containing 94,818 files, including unredacted patient pictures, lab results, and records related to minors. The attackers exploited exposed AWS cloud buckets rather than penetrating networks, affecting downstream healthcare facilities including Vita Exame and Centro Diagnostico Toledo.
