NCC Group's Q3 review is the report the Qilin unmasking article cites for its victim count. It records Qilin as the most active threat group of the quarter with 365 attacks, edging out The Gentlemen at 359, and notes ransomware rose 23% in Q3 over Q2 while September attacks fell 26% month-on-month to 821. Industrials was the most targeted sector with over 838 attacks in Q3.
