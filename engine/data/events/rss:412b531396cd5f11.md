Seqrite Labs recently identified a CLoader bot malware campaign targeting organizations and individuals in Thailand through spear-phishing emails. The attacker employs a multi-stage infection chain to deliver the CLoader bot malware. Malware leverages several anti-analysis and defense-evasion techniques to evade endpoint security solutions and hinder malware analysis. Notably, it uses a LuaJIT executable bundled with an obfuscated Lua script to conceal its malicious activities and reduce the likelihood of detection.
