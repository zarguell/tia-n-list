The Hacker News reported on July 6, 2026, that researcher Hyunwoo Kim disclosed "Januscape" (CVE-2026-53359), a 16-year-old use-after-free in Linux KVM's shadow MMU affecting both Intel and AMD hosts. It also references Kim's earlier disclosures (Dirty Frag in May, ITScape/CVE-2026-46316 in June) and states the vulnerability was used as a zero-day in Google's kvmCTF program.
