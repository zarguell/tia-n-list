The Hacker News reported that PHP warned of a new unpatched RCE flaw, CVE-2026-115560, two days after emergency patches for the critical PHP2Shell vulnerabilities CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. The issue affects only web applications configured in an unusual way, and PHP considers it an unlikely risk. The recommended mitigation is setting session.upload_progress.enabled=0 until the next routine bugfix release, the same setting already advised for CVE-2026-109619. PHP2Shell is not fixed by those emergency updates and CVE-2026-115560 is separate from them.
