Reporting on SOCRadar Threat Research Unit findings published September 9, 2026, hours before the KEV listing, says exploitation of CVE-2025-25249 began no later than July 2026 and remains active. The campaign targeted more than 30,000 FortiGate IP addresses and produced 178 confirmed PivotC2 infections, with the United States the most affected. The attack delivers unauthenticated RCE through crafted CAPWAP traffic to UDP port 5246 using a self-extracting Linux binary named fortirun.bin that abuses CAPWAP discovery responses to defeat ASLR and corrupts linked-list pointers for an arbitrary write primitive.
