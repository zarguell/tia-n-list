Microsoft released the MSRC advisory for CVE-2026-42897 on May 14, 2026, rating it Critical for spoofing (CWE-79) with a CVSS 3.1 base score of 8.1 and marking Exploited: Yes with an Exploitation Detected assessment. The FAQ describes exploitation via a specially crafted email opened in OWA and notes Internet Explorer and Edge in IE Mode are not protected because they do not support CSP. The page lists security updates for Exchange Server Subscription Edition RTM (KB5094139), Exchange 2019 CU15 and CU14 (KB5094140, KB5094142), and Exchange 2016 CU23 (KB5094144), with EEMS applying the mitigation automatically.
