On most distributions, two group memberships cover nearly everything an analyst needs: adm for the text logs in /var/log on Debian and Ubuntu, and systemd-journal for the full system journal. Add the account with sudo usermod -aG adm,systemd-journal and start a new login session. A few logs stay root-only by design, notably /var/log/secure and /var/log/messages on Red Hat-family systems and everything under /var/log/audit. For those, use narrowly scoped sudo, not a root shell. Giving someone root just so they can read logs is the kind of shortcut that shows up in the next audit, or the next incident. This guide covers the least-privilege way, plus the traps that make “just add them to a group” less simple than it sounds.
