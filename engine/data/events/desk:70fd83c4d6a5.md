watchTowr published a GeoServer FAQ at 12:45 UTC on November 25, calling the five-CVE chain exploited in the wild with no attribution made public. It describes CVE-2026-129244 leaking the data directory path, CVE-2026-129243 bypassing admin rules with x/WFS, CVE-2026-120705 pulling files and password digests, CVE-2026-129245 forging the remember-me cookie and CVE-2026-129242 executing code through JDBC deserialization. The FAQ says CISA had not listed CVE-2026-129245, recommends 3.0.2, 2.28.6 or 2.27.7, and points responders to wfs/query and workspaces/*/wfs/query folders.
