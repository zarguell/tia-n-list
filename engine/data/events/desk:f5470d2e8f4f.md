Kaspersky identified Tropic Trooper delivering CrowDoor, a variant of the SparrowDoor backdoor first documented by ESET in September 2021, through DLL search-order hijacking against a government entity in the Middle East. The finding places TropicTrooper among the consumers of the Sparrow toolset well before the CopperDragon framework was named. CrowDoor traces its lineage back to SparrowDoor, which Check Point later folds into the CopperDragon ecosystem.
