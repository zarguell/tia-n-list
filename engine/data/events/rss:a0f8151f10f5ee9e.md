Threat Actor Profile and Strategic Targeting A China-linked threat group known as Longlegs has been actively targeting critical infrastructure, government bodies, and educational institutions in Spanish- and Portuguese-speaking countries using Warlock ransomware . The attackers consistently gain initial network access by exploiting vulnerabilities within on-premises Microsoft SharePoint deployments. Once inside, they employ advanced evasion tactics such as DLL sideloading, bringing their own vulnerable drivers to disable security software, and abusing Visual Studio Code tunnels for covert remote access. To distribute the ransomware widely, the threat actors stage their malicious payloads inside the domain’s SYSVOL share , allowing automated replication to spread the infection across numerous machines simultaneously. Cybersecurity platforms utilize unified threat tracking and behavioral analysis to intercept these complex multi-stage attacks before encryption occurs.
