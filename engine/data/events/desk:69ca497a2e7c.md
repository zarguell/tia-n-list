Carlo Meijer of Midnight Blue found six pre-authentication stack-based buffer overflows in AK-SM800A firmware up to and including 5.3.2 in under an hour of manual review. Five sat in `sm_app` web request handlers and one in the `sm_fcgi_app` session-control path, with root code execution demonstrated on 3.3.0 while the bugs remained present in 5.3.2. The authors warned that OS-level access could persist through factory reset and firmware update because the i.MX6 secure boot fuses were unused, and argued for cutting exposure instead of prioritising patches.
