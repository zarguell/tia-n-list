But potentially by reading what a swap service knows before coins ever enter the privacy network — and what it records when those coins leave. That is the central finding of Hudson Rock’s review of purported Wagyu.xyz transaction data supplied alongside a WhiteArmor-associated claim. The files contain order histories, cryptocurrency destinations, transaction identifiers, connection metadata and an IP-level activity index.
