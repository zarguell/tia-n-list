ThreatDefence published an advisory on November 20, 2026 covering CVE-2026-123890 and the other entries in Broadcom's VMSA-2026-0009 release. It lists fixes in vCenter 9.1.1.0200, 9.0.2.0200 and 8.0 U3m released November 19, notes that vCenter 7.0 has no public fix and that there is no workaround, and prioritizes servers with TCP port 2012 reachable from user or partner networks. Its hunting guidance calls for forwarding /var/log/audit/sso-events/audit_events.log and reviewing accounts and permissions added since early September 2026, including administrator accounts with the sps- prefix.
