BleepingComputer reported on September 29, 2026 at 5:04 AM ET that Kiteworks had lifted its precautionary advisory after patching a critical vulnerability in an unnamed feature used by less than 1% of all customers. The company brought hosted systems back online on Monday, September 28, and noted no abnormal activity or indication of compromise. Self-hosted customers running Kiteworks Advanced Forms were advised to contact support. Shadowserver had spotted nearly 400 internet-accessible Kiteworks instances, mostly from the United States (234), but the dashboard provides no information on how many are honeypots or already patched. The company has not assigned a CVE ID for the fixed vulnerability.
