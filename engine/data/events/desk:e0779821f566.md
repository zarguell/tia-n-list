The developer advisory, published 15 June 2026, announces iCagenda 4.0.8 fixing a guest event-creation entry point present on all Joomla versions and a critical file attachment upload limited to Joomla 6.0.0 through 6.1.1. It states the vulnerability has been actively exploited since 15 June at 8 a.m. UTC by automated attacks targeting sites with iCagenda installed, and notes Joomla 6.1.2 reinstated core-level blocking of unsafe uploads. A version matrix scopes the affected range to 3.2.1 through 4.0.7, with 3.9.15 following on 16 June for Joomla 3, and the update warns that patching does not clean up an already compromised site.
