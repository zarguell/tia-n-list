CERT-AGID has identified a new phishing campaign that exploits the graphics and institutional logos of the Fascicolo Sanitario Elettronico (FSE), the Ministry of Health, the Department for Digital Transformation, and the Ministry of Economy and Finance, with the goal of stealing personal data and payment card details from victims. The psychological lever is the promise of a supposed 19% reimbursement for pharmacy medicine purchases (for an exact amount of €20.73).
