Norway's National Security Authority published this notice on 20 November, saying an unknown actor had actively targeted access tokens, secrets and named values in Azure API Management and that all APIM operators should assume their secrets and named values were exposed and rotate all tokens and secrets. NSM named Equinor and Skagerak Energi among affected organizations, credited the Norwegian energy sector with finding the flaw, and said impact reached other sectors and countries outside Norway. The notice includes a gateway RuntimeVersion check with patch thresholds and says the extent of exploitation remains uncertain.
