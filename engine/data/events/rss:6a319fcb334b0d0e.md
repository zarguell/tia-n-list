Mozilla has released Firefox 160 with fixes for 99 security vulnerabilities. Mozilla announced the update on November 10, 2026, as part of Mozilla Foundation Security Advisory 2026-125, which rates its overall impact as high. The advisory lists 57 high-severity flaws, 24 moderate and 18 low. Most of the high-severity bugs are memory-safety problems.
