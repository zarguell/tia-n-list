Computer spyware is malicious software that secretly runs on a PC, laptop, or Mac, records your activity, and sends that data to someone else. To detect spyware, check running processes, startup items, browser extensions, and permissions, then run a full security scan; to remove it, boot into Safe Mode, delete the unknown software, scan again, and change your passwords from a clean device. This guide covers Windows and Mac, including the common question of whether Windows itself is spyware. What Is Computer Spyware? Computer spyware is software that gathers information about a user or their activity without informed consent and reports it to a third party. Its defining trait is secrecy, which separates it from ordinary programs that collect usage data and report it to you. Computer Spyware Definition in Simple Words Computer spyware is a hidden program that watches what you type, visit, and store, and sends the details to its operator. In computer science terms, it is a category of malware built for covert surveillance and data theft rather than damage.
