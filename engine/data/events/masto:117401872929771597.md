Hackers reportedly compromised country-code top-level domain (ccTLD) registries to hijack Google domains. This was not a flaw in Google's own services: the attackers obtained fraudulent TLS certificates for Google domains after breaching the registry operators of .GH, .SL, and .AS and tampering with authoritative DNS records. Google says its systems were not compromised and has blocked the certificates in Chrome while working with the issuing certificate authorities to revoke them.
