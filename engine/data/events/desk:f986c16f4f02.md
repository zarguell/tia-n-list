BleepingComputer reports a watering-hole campaign that uses n-day exploits, including CVE-2024-27834 as a Pointer Authentication bypass in an iOS 16.7 exploit chain. The campaign is attributed to APT29 and is delivered through compromised websites, adversary-in-the-middle style fake domains, and a supply-chain compromise of a Ukrainian web analytics service. The activity targets both iOS and Android users and aims to steal cookies from mobile devices.
