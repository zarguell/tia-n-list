Key Takeaways FedRAMP authorization isn’t the finish line. Providers are expected to keep monitoring their security posture and give federal agencies the information they need for ongoing, risk-based decisions. CISA’s BOD 26-04, issued on June 10, 2026, moves federal vulnerability management toward risk-based prioritization instead of a single fixed deadline for every finding. FedRAMP has said the legacy monthly scan is no longer sufficient. Its new Vulnerability Detection and Response (VDR) and Vulnerability Evaluation and Reporting (VER) rules become mandatory for certification on December 7, 2026. In PwC’s 2025 Global Compliance Survey, 85% of executives said compliance requirements have grown more complex over the last three years.
