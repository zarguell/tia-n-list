SBC Medical Group's first Japanese notice reported unauthorized access to databases managed by its Japanese subsidiary, discovered on November 16, with basic data for 1,624,000 customers from 2010 through 2023 plus separate reservation, inquiry, treatment-monitor and review categories. It said the system was hit by 770,000 consecutive attacks from overseas overnight, that passwords from that period were stored unencrypted, and that the database incident was reported to Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission on November 16. The company said the intrusion method and route remained under investigation and that it had not confirmed acquisition of electronic medical records or data from the mySBC reservation site.
