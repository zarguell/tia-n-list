Joomla's Security Centre published advisory 20261156 on 24 November 2026 at 14:00 UTC. It says inadequate escaping of media field parameters in generated form XML allowed manipulation of the form definition and remote code execution, affecting Joomla CMS 4.0.0-5.4.10 and 6.0.0-6.2.0, fixed in 5.4.11 and 6.2.1. The advisory rates impact critical but severity and probability low, gives a report date of 26 October 2026, and credits VulnCheck.
