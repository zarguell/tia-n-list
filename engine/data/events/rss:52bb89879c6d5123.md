SAP has confirmed that it identified suspicious activity and took immediate measures, after media reports on November 26 said attackers had stolen authentication tokens belonging to SAP employees and used them to access and alter source code on GitHub. The company says its investigation has so far found no evidence of unauthorized access to customers’ business data . An internal SAP letter to employees placed the suspicious activity on GitHub. SAP has not commented on the token theft reports, named the repositories involved or said whether any modified code reached software that customers run. No threat actor has been named, and there is no public claim of responsibility. What Happened?
