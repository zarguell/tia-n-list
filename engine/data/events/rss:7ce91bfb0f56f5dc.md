Five U.S. federal agencies have issued a joint warning about refrigeration cyberattacks, saying threat actors are targeting refrigeration controllers and electronic temperature controllers in retail grocery and cold storage facilities. The fact sheet, released on November 5, 2026, by CISA, the FBI, HHS, USDA and the Department of Transportation, says attackers can remotely alter settings and push units into continuous heating and defrost cycles. The warning follows weeks of overnight freezer incidents at supermarkets across Europe, an attack that hit blood storage sites in France and reported hacks of freezers on U.S. military bases. The fact sheet itself names no products, victims or attackers. What the FBI and CISA Refrigeration Warning Says The fact sheet , titled “US Government Partners Urge Hardening Industrial Refrigeration and Cold Chain Monitoring Systems,” is marked TLP:CLEAR and carries no advisory number. It is addressed to the Healthcare and Public Health, Chemical, Food and Agriculture, and Transportation Systems sectors, including refrigerated transport services.
