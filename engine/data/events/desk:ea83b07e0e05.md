mySites.guru published the original disclosure on 8 July 2026 after a customer received a Hetzner abuse report showing a live POST to the Balbooa Forms upload handler. The post documents that the frontend task index.php?option=com_baforms&task=form.uploadAttachmentFile required no login, no CSRF token, and no extension allow-list, writing attacker-named files under images/baforms/uploads/form-<id>/ inside the web root. Balbooa shipped the 2.4.1 fix the next day and credited Phil Taylor as reporter. The post's later update notes record CVE-2026-65880 fixed in 2.4.3 on 28 July and CVE-2026-67364 with CVE-2026-67363 fixed in 2.4.3.2 on 18 August, making 2.4.3.2 the minimum safe version.
