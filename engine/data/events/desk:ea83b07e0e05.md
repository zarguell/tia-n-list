Disclosure of an unauthenticated file upload in the Balbooa Forms (com_baforms) upload handler at index.php?option=com_baforms&task=form.uploadAttachmentFile, which required no login, no CSRF token, and no extension allow-list, writing attacker-controlled .php files into images/baforms/uploads/ inside the web root. Found through a Hetzner abuse report on a customer site, reported privately, and fixed by the vendor in 2.4.1 released July 9, 2026, crediting Phil Taylor as reporter. Exploited in the wild before a patch existed; no public proof of concept has been released.
