New campaigns specifically target **US domestic airlines** through fraudulent hiring portals — a notable geographic expansion beyond the group's traditional Middle East and European focus. Lures include trojanized Zoom installers and OnlyOffice-hosted ZIP archives deploying the updated MiniJunk backdoor.
