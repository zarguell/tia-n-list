Checkmarx published the original MALFEX disclosure on October 5, 2026. The report documents a three-year npm supply chain campaign running since August 2023, with 12 published packages (eight malicious), distributing Overlord RAT and infostealers via independent delivery paths. Three malicious packages remained installable as of October 1, including function-flag with more than 37,000 downloads and no advisory flagging it malicious.
