This early news write-up reports that Ruby patched seven memory-safety flaws in its regular expression engine and string case conversion and released 4.0.10, 3.4.13 and 3.3.14. It describes a one-byte heap overflow and an integer overflow in case conversion, a double free during regular expression compilation and an out-of-bounds read in case-insensitive matching. The article notes that CVE identifiers were requested but not assigned and repeats the maintainers' proof-of-concept warning.
