GPUStack before 2.2.0 treated a loopback request source as the built-in administrator when force_auth_localhost was false (the default), via client_ip_getter accepting a same-host reverse proxy peer or a client-supplied X-GPUStack-Real-IP loopback value, granting unauthenticated admin access. The flaw is fixed in 2.2.0, and active in-the-wild exploitation was reported against a 2.1.1 deployment. Credit to Nikko Enggaliano, Wei Yunzhou, and Rui Yang and Haoyu Wang (Johns Hopkins University).
