Mitigate First Against AI Attackers | SANS AI Track Patching remains the fix, but a patch can take days or weeks to roll out. In this session, our Co-founder and CTO, Volkan explains a mitigation-first approach: use the security controls you already run to block or detect a new threat within hours, while IT patches at its own pace. He walks through a three-step model and shows it working in the Picus Platform. You'll learn how to: Why the window from CVE disclosure to working exploit has shrunk from weeks to hours How to harden your controls against new techniques and CVEs: map, validate, tune Where pentesting fits: as checks and balances after hardening How change-driven, agentic validation keeps your defenses current, with your team on the loop Article Link: Mitigate First Against AI Attackers: SANS AI Track
