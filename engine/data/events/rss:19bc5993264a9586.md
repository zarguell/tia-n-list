Cybercrime , Threat Intelligence Fake court ADR phishing campaign distributes LodaRAT A phishing campaign impersonating a U.S. federal court targets more than 100,000 companies and delivers LodaRAT, a commodity remote access trojan. October 21, 2026 Andrea Bianchini Executive summary Attackers impersonating the United States District Court, Southern District of Florida, are running a phishing campaign that targets companies with personalized emails, each containing a unique Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) ID. The emails redirect targets to a fake court ADR portal ( florida[.]courtadr[.]com ) that serves a password-protected ZIP archive containing an ISO with three differently disguised files. All three files deliver the same LodaRAT payload, rebuilt under three names ( SystemSettings.exe , Taskmgr.exe , and msedge.exe ) served from us[.]wind0ws[.]net and beaconing to my[.]thispc[.]net:4000 . According to our research, more than 100,000 companies are targeted. ThreatDown Endpoint Protection blocks the remote access trojan (RAT) samples and the campaign’s network communications.
