A new congressional report focused on how some American companies are embedding China-developed open weight artificial intelligence models into their tech stacks calls the trend alarming and cautions U.S. firms against the practice. Pointing to how Huawei and ZTE technology quickly found their place in the marketplace and threatened American networks, the new report warns that if China-developed open-weight models are allowed to take root, removing them down the road will take several years and cost taxpayers billions. Open weight models’ numerical weights and biases are made public for download. The joint investigative report — produced by the House Homeland Security Committee and the Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party — focuses on Airbnb, DoorDash and Cursor coding platform owner Anysphere as examples of companies deploying Chinese open weight models. The companies were using them for customer assistance, coding projects and taking inventory. None of the firms immediately responded to a request for comment. Report co-authors Reps. Andrew Garbarino (R-NY) and John Moolenaar (R-NY) drew the 50-page-long report’s findings from hearings, classified briefings, and agency advisories.
