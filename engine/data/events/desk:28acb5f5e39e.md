DailySecu reported a DarkForums post dated November 19 and attributed to the account Noiem, claiming 592,585 external user records, 95,054 supplier records, 401,951 bid price records and 26,859 sole-contract records from KEPCO. The listed supplier, bid and contract material overlaps the work handled by KEPCO's electronic procurement system at srm.kepco.net, but the post named no source system or intrusion path. The report said the data's authenticity, overlap with prior leaks and actual transfer outside KEPCO were unverified.
