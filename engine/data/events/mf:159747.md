In recent days CERT-AGID has observed a significant increase in smishing campaigns that abuse the name, logo, and graphics of INPS, Italy's national social security institute. The new element in this wave is the introduction of an additional control layer over documents uploaded by the victim, based on an artificial intelligence model. The AI-based check sits between the victim and the fraud backend, so the campaign now validates what the target submits before proceeding.

The shift matters because it automates a step that previously required manual review, letting the operators scale the campaign while filtering for plausible submissions. The abuse of a trusted state institution's branding means users are more likely to hand over identity documents and credentials. Organizations and individuals in Italy should treat unsolicited INPS-themed messages, especially ones requesting document uploads, as fraudulent and verify through official INPS channels.
