Alleged Finaxis.cloud Data Breach is Advertised SOCRadar Dark Web Team detected a post advertising an alleged 400GB data breach involving Finaxis.cloud , a payment processing platform serving high-risk industries, including iGaming. The threat actor claimed to have obtained sensitive internal information after the company’s management allegedly refused to meet ransom demands. The advertised data reportedly includes full source code, merchant and partner lists, P2P agent and trader contact information, financial statements, and banking details . The seller offered individual components for purchase or the complete dataset for $15,000 .
