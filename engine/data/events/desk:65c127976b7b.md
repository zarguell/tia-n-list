Companion commit landed in mainline on 3 November 2026 alongside the NoisyTCP fix. It switches the IPv4 and IPv6 established lookups, inet_twsk_purge() and bpf_sk_assign() to refcount_inc_not_zero_acquire(), because commit fb041bb7c0a9 removed the full memory ordering that atomic_add_unless() used to provide on weakly ordered architectures. Without acquire ordering, accesses made after a successful reference acquisition can be reordered ahead of it, so kernels on arm64 and other weakly ordered CPUs need this change in addition to 4e2b3ef19e0e.
