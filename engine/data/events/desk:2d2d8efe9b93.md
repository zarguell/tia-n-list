Infosecurity Magazine reported that QUIRSO observed exploitation of the July 29 vCenter advisory five days after disclosure, counting 361 victim IP addresses across 47 countries. The exploited bug was the Syslog server directory traversal CVE-2026-59310, rated CVSS 9.8, with attackers deploying the reverse_ssh tool for persistence. QUIRSO saw the first compromised systems contacting attacker infrastructure on August 3, and roughly 95 percent of the 361 IPs had appeared by August 5. Broadcom listed no workaround and fixed releases 9.1.0.0300, 9.0.2.0100, 8.0 U3k and 8.0 U2f.
