Philippe Lagadec's February 3, 2026 writeup confirms the in-the-wild samples are plain RTFs with Shell.Explorer.1 objdata objects, detectable with oletools rtfobj and his olecheck gist. He published YARA rules (CVE-2026-21509_RTF.yar and a faster no-whitespace variant) matching the RTF header, \objdata, and the hex CLSID string C32AB2EAC130CF11A7EB0000C05BAE0B, uploaded to MalwareBazaar as SUSP_RTF_with_potential_CVE_2026_21509_exploit_nows so new samples auto-trigger. The post also notes Microsoft's kill-bit list blocked Shell.Explorer and Shell.Explorer.2 but overlooked the .1 ProgID.
