CyberXero is a Russian-speaking, financially motivated Initial Access Broker (IAB) active on Dark Web forums, including Dread and HackForums. The actor runs two parallel operations: an opportunistic global campaign that mass-exploits WordPress, Magento and other e-commerce platforms for access and payment data, and a directed campaign that conducts curated reconnaissance against Ukrainian energy and utilities organizations. What sets CyberXero apart is its AI layer, with up to 51 specialized Claude Code agents on its primary workstation and a PentAGI deployment wired into a Cobalt Strike Team Server through the Anthropic API. The SOCRadar Threat Research Unit has tracked the actor’s infrastructure since July 2026, and CyberXero remained active at the time of publication. This profile is based on the findings of our prior research into CyberXero . Who Is CyberXero? CyberXero came to light after SOCRadar identified an exposed open directory on an attacker-controlled HTTP server.
