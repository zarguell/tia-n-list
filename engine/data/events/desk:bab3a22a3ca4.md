Censys published an advisory on September 16, 2026 for CVE-2026-91843, a stack-based buffer overflow in the unauthenticated login of Check Point Quantum Security Management and Log Servers that can execute code as root through an excessively long username. Censys observed 3,836 hosts worldwide presenting the default cp_mgmt SIC identity, while stressing this is total role presence and not a confirmed-vulnerable count. The advisory says Smart-1 Cloud is unaffected, that the flaw was not in CISA KEV or VulnCheck KEV at the time, and that passive scans cannot reveal build or Jumbo Hotfix level.
