Illustration by Lauren Bolger for Bellingcat A pilot who died when his plane crashed during a failed drug run in South America last year previously flew a multimillion dollar cocaine shipment to Australia in the same kit aircraft, authorities have said. Bellingcat revealed last September that the deceased pilot, Timothy James Clark, had links to alleged Kinahan cartel associate Oliver Andreas Herrmann , a German national who is facing charges over the importation of 200kg of cocaine to Australia in December 2024. Insert: Deceased Australian pilot Timothy Clark. Main: The wreckage of his plane. Source: Facebook, TV Pajuçara / YouTube The Australian Federal Police (AFP) said Herrmann allegedly met a “small aircraft” at the Overlander Airstrip in Western Australia the day before he and another man were arrested. The AFP did not disclose the make and model of the aircraft, but said it had not seized the plane as part of its investigation. Bellingcat first reported the links between Clark and Herrmann after Clark’s small plane, a single-engine Sling 4 TSi, crashed in Coruripe on the southern coast of the Brazilian state of Alagoas in September 2025. The body of the former Melbourne stockbroker was found at the wreckage along with about 200kg of cocaine. Insert: Former marathon runner Oliver Herrmann.
