Every day, thousands of SOC and IR teams worldwide analyze suspicious files, emails, and links with ANY.RUN . Beyond individual investigations, this activity generates valuable data on emerging threats, industry trends, and SOC operational performance. Drawing on real-world investigations across thousands of organizations, ANY.RUN provides visibility into threats affecting different sectors , from banking to retail. Security teams can see not only what they’re encountering, but also how those threats compare with the broader industry landscape. Detecting a single threat gives you a verdict. Understanding it in the context of your industry’s threat landscape helps you make a decision. This visibility helps SOC leaders benchmark performance, identify emerging risks, and make informed decisions about detection and response priorities. To show what this looks like in practice, we examined 74,086 Sandbox analyses from one financial organization over a quarter and compared its findings with banking -sector benchmarks.
