Spring's August 20, 2026 advisory for CVE-2026-59270 says the embedded UnboundID LDAP server in Spring Security registers an administrative credential unconditionally and binds its listener to all available network interfaces. An attacker who can reach the LDAP port can authenticate with a well-known administrative bind DN and then read or modify entries in the in-memory directory. The advisory lists affected Spring Security lines from 5.7.0 through 7.1.0 and fixes including 7.1.1 OSS, 7.0.7 OSS, and enterprise-only releases for older branches. It rates the flaw CRITICAL.
