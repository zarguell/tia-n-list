Fortune reported the PHP Foundation's emergency disclosure of CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, collectively called php2shell, which allow unauthenticated remote code execution on affected PHP web runtimes. The article says GPT-5.6 Sol and other AI tools helped find the flaws, and that multiple research groups independently found them in August before coordinating fixes with the PHP Foundation. Cloudflare saw tens of thousands of exploit attempts within 45 minutes of reports breaking on Oct. 20, hundreds of thousands within two hours, and millions by the evening of Oct. 21. AWS said attempts had also appeared before disclosure and that exploitation was broad in the wild.
