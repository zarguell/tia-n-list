The Crown Prosecution Service announced that Kurtaj pleaded guilty to hacking Rockstar Games, BT/EE, Revolut and Uber, with sentencing set for 23 November 2026. It said the Rockstar intrusion involved 90 gameplay videos and a threat to release source code, while the Uber attack caused costs above $2.8 million and the Revolut intrusion compromised thousands of customers' information.
