OpenSSH released version 10.6p2 on November 5, 2026 to address a password authentication bypass affecting Linux systems that use musl libc without PAM, including Alpine Linux. OX Security's review of the research at sshhole.com found the flaw was located by building sshd across glibc, musl, PAM and no-PAM combinations and running roughly 17,600 deliberate tests. Alpine released fixes the same day.
