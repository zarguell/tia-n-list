Sergiu Gatlan reported on October 28, 2025 that CISA flagged active exploitation of two DELMIA Apriso flaws, a manufacturing operations management and MES product: CVE-2025-6205, a critical missing authorization bug allowing unauthenticated actors to remotely gain privileged access, and CVE-2025-6204, a high-severity code injection bug requiring high privileges. Dassault patched both in early August 2025 for Release 2020 through Release 2025, and federal agencies face a November 18 deadline under BOD 22-01.
