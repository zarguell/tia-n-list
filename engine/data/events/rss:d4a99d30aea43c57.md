The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency circulated an internal framework last week intended to help senior officials assess major cybersecurity threats and act before they could potentially escalate into a crisis, according to an internal email viewed by Nextgov/FCW . The Nov. 5 email, which is unclassified but marked “For Official Use Only,” was sent to staff by acting Director Nick Andersen. It outlines what agency leaders need to know to assess risks and make timely decisions, aiming to help officials “cut through the noise” and distinguish useful information from what is mission-critical. The framework sets out six “operational focus areas” that include priorities assessing national risk, defending against major cyber threats, preparing for serious incidents, helping critical infrastructure withstand disruptions and coordinating with partners. Its “critical information requirements” spell out what senior officials need to know to make decisions. Those include major changes in risk, serious threats and incidents and impacts on nationally significant infrastructure.
