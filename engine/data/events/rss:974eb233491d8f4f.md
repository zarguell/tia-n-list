The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) has released finalized guidance to assist organizations in replacing cryptography that is vulnerable to quantum computing before future quantum computers pose a threat to sensitive information. Published on October 27, 2026, the report establishes a framework for migration that includes cryptographic standards, federal deadlines, and implementation priorities.
