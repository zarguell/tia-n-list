Federal civilian agencies have until Thursday to address two critical PHP vulnerabilities that CISA has now warned are being exploited by hackers, escalating the government’s response to flaws in software used to operate websites worldwide. The Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog | CISA ”> added both flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog Monday, setting an Oct. 29 remediation deadline. The catalog entries do not identify victims or describe the evidence behind the decision, leaving the scale of successful attacks unclear. Known collectively as PHP2Shell, the vulnerabilities can allow hackers to run commands on a web server without an account or password, potentially enabling them to steal data or alter a website.
