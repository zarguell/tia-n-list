Rapid7's Emergent Threat Response post, initially published May 29, 2026, is the original researcher disclosure: MDR identified successful exploitation across numerous customers with the earliest observed exploitation on May 17, 2026, four days before the May 29 PoC release and KEV listing. Reverse engineering of /usr/local/bin/gpsvc showed main_DecryptAppAuthCookie decrypts portal-userauthcookie and portal-prelogonuserauthcookie values submitted to /ssl-vpn/login.esp and trusts the plaintext with no signature verification, so anyone holding the public key of a certificate shared with the HTTPS service can forge cookies. Rapid7 Labs published a working proof-of-concept at github.com/sfewer-r7/CVE-2026-0257 (forge_cookie.py) and documented two exploitation waves from Vultr and Dromatics Systems sharing the spoofed MAC aa:bb:cc:dd:ee:ff, with 8 of 10 impacted MDR customers seeing authentication probes only and no full VPN session.
