BleepingComputer reported on Google Threat Intelligence Group research into a China-linked espionage campaign that used exposed REDCap servers and the INFINITERED payload against a North American medical research institution. The report describes a compromise beginning in September 2023 with activity through November 2025, three modular INFINITERED components, and a compliance rule named Patroit that BCC-forwarded matching mail to a Gmail account. It advises REDCap administrators to upgrade, remove legacy deployments, enforce 2-step verification, and apply the published YARA rules and indicators.
