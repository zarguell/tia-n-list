Sysdig Threat Research Team reported on April 9, 2026, that exploitation of CVE-2026-39987 began 9 hours and 41 minutes after the advisory was published. The attacker connected to the /terminal/ws endpoint without authentication, validated execution with a scripted PoC, then manually explored the filesystem, read .env files, and attempted to retrieve SSH private keys. No public proof-of-concept existed at the time of the first observed exploitation attempts. The blog details the attacker timeline from 07:31 to 09:04 UTC on April 9 and confirms the complete credential-theft chain occurred in under three minutes.
