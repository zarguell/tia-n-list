Bitdefender Labs reported on November 26 that it had identified the first malicious SYS010 advertisement on October 16 and observed requests across Asia, Europe, North America, Africa and South America. The campaign distributes a working wallpaper app that enrolls browsers in attacker-controlled management and installs extensions capable of stealing cookies and Meta business data. Its Chrome Docs Helper 1.0.25 extension can read up to 50 recent Gmail or Outlook messages per task and permanently delete attacker-selected mail.
