CBS News reported that Jordanian authorities detained Saif al-Din Khader and that ShinyHunters had claimed, without public verification, to hold data on every FBI employee. The group claimed it stole 2 to 3 terabytes of data related to FBI workers using a new Oracle PeopleSoft vulnerability. The report also said a 24-year-old Amsterdam man, Pepijn van der Stap, was arrested on September 15, days before ShinyHunters announced the FBI jobs website breach.
