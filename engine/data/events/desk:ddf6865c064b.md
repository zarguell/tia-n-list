Dream Research Labs disclosed an active DarkBlinders espionage campaign running August to October 2026 against Israel and the Kurdistan Region of Iraq. Operators used government webmail, cloud-drive, and meeting-service lures including the fake StarkMeet video-meeting app, plus GitHub repositories under the PeakyBlindersTeam account for command and control. The investigation confirmed compromise of a high-profile Israeli individual and a government cloud environment in Kurdistan, with roughly ten distinct hosts in available check-in data. Researchers accessed GitHub read-only and did not issue commands to any malware instance.
