Here are some of the most important cybersecurity M&A deals announced in October 2026: Absolute Security acquires FirmGuard Endpoint security and cyber resilience firm Absolute Security has acquired FirmGuard, the UEFI BIOS firmware security and management business of Phoenix Technologies. Absolute will integrate FirmGuard with its firmware-embedded Absolute Persistence technology, adding below-the-OS management, continuous firmware integrity monitoring and patching, and compliance enforcement. Aikido Security acquires OutKept and Cyberly Belgian application security unicorn Aikido Security has acquired OutKept and Cyberly and used the two startups to launch Noon, a workforce security business. OutKept is based in Belgium and provides phishing simulations and security awareness training. Poland’s Cyberly develops AI-powered attack simulations and tools for tracking employee AI usage. Both deals closed in September but weren’t made public until October. Anthropic to acquire Opaque Systems AI company Anthropic has agreed to acquire Opaque Systems, a confidential AI company that grew out of research at UC Berkeley.
