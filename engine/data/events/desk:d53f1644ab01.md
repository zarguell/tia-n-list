eSentire's Threat Response Unit reported that beginning June 29, 2026, one day after the WatchTowr PoC release, it identified exploitation attempts targeting CVE-2026-8037; in observed cases exploitation was not successful and no post-compromise activity was seen. The advisory lists attacker IPs 192.42.116.58, 192.42.116.105 and 146.70.139.154, and assesses exploitation attempts will increase given the availability of PoC code. Affected and fixed versions match the Progress bulletin (GA 7.2.63.1 to 7.2.63.2, LTSF 7.2.54.17 to 7.2.54.18).
