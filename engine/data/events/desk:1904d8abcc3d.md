Help Net Security reported active exploitation of CVE-2026-132165 since at least 19 November, citing the advisory update that gave the start time as 2026-11-19 19:00 UTC. It lists fixes in 10.6.22, 11.3.22 and 11.4.13, notes that 11.2.x, 10.5.x, Drupal 8 and Drupal 9 are end of life, and recommends blocking access to /core/install.php in web server configuration or .htaccess as an interim measure. The article says Drupal Steward covers known attack vectors but that sites still need to update.
