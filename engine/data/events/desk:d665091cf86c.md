ESET researchers Anton Cherepanov, Peter Strycek, and Damien Schaeffer disclosed that CVE-2025-8088, a zero-day path traversal in WinRAR that abuses alternate data streams, was exploited in the wild starting July 18, 2025. WinRAR 7.13 beta 1 shipped July 24, 2025 and final 7.13 on July 30, 2025. Russia-aligned RomCom delivered SnipBot, RustyClaw, and Mythic agent backdoors through CV-themed RAR lures aimed at financial, manufacturing, defense, and logistics organizations in Europe and Canada between July 18 and 21, 2025, and ESET noted a second exploiting actor whose research was independently published by BI.ZONE.
