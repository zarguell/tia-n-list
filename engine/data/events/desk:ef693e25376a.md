Independent diff of RouterOS 7.23.3 against 7.23.4 packages, published on 4 September 2026 (CERT Polska records the article at 03:22 UTC), a day before CERT Polska's advisory. It identified the new validLoginParamInput validator guarding /nova/bin/login against the "-2" file-descriptor argument injection, demonstrated an end-to-end SSH authentication bypass by forging rsa-sha2-256 signatures for an account bound to a known RSA e=3 key, and reproduced controlled code execution in nova/bin/mtget through an oversized TFTP fetch pathname with a working ROP call. It also listed campaign indicators beyond the vendor text: scheduler entries pairing fetch with import, the domains mythtime.xyz, leappoach.info, and eeongous.com, unexpected /ip socks enablement, and an ops user in the full group.
