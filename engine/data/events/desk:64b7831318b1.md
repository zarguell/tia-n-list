CSA's 18 August 2026 research note describes CVE-2026-9198 as a chain of CVE-2026-9103 and CVE-2026-8481 against Langflow OSS 1.0.0 through 1.10.0. An unauthenticated call to /api/v1/auto_login mints a SUPERUSER token, and a second request to /api/v1/validate/code passes attacker Python to exec() for full code execution; IBM fixed the chain in 1.10.1 on 17 July 2026. The note reports exploitation attempts from early July, hundreds of attacker IPs across dozens of countries, and recommends log review for those two endpoints plus rotation of model, database and cloud credentials.
