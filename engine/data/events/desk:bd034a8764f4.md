Unit 42 observed a campaign combining email phishing with a fake IT support Microsoft Teams call to deliver EtherRAT. The attacker used an external account (helpdesk@Progressive936.onmicrosoft.com), obtained remote screen control, guided the victim to install HopToDesk and AnyDesk, and delivered a malicious MSI (v7.msi) from camorreado.click that drops a blockchain-anchored C2 bot.
