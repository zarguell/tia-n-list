GMO Cybersecurity by Ierae confirmed on October 19, 2026 that ARTEX was present on attacker infrastructure it investigated, corroborating its earlier assessment that AI was used in the Japanese breach wave. The firm found about 20 Japanese companies targeted, nine of them in the same industry, and six additional AI attack servers focused on separate sectors. Eight of the targeted companies had already published breach notices in September and October 2026, and the firm shared the findings with law enforcement.
