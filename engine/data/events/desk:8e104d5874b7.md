The Mirror reported that the email sent to Scottish schools was titled "DEATH IS NEAR" and read "This is Mikhail Andropov, leader of the 'Red Owl' project. We've come to destroy you. We're going to carry out a genocide against your people." Scottish papers led their front pages with the school bomb threats, and a parent described being called by her school after news sites were already reporting the threat. The message matched threats sent to schools across the UK and Europe.
