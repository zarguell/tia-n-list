Cisco's event response, first published September 25, 2025, reports that in May 2025 Cisco was engaged by government incident response agencies to investigate attacks implanting malware on ASA 5500-X devices with VPN web services enabled, exploiting CVE-2025-20333 (CVSS 9.9) and CVE-2025-20362 (CVSS 6.5) together for unauthenticated full device control. The actor, assessed with high confidence as the one behind the 2024 ArcaneDoor campaign, disabled logging, intercepted CLI commands, crashed devices to defeat forensics, and modified ROMMON for persistence across reboots and upgrades. The page lists first fixed releases per train, the Snort rules 65340 and 46897, and an April 24, 2026 update stating the actor developed FXOS-level persistence that survives the September 2025 fixes and broadened its radius to any ASA or FTD device.
