This item covers Cisco Talos research describing operators that store encrypted command-and-control endpoints in Bitcoin transactions, EVM smart contracts, TRON wallets and Solana memo programs, then retrieve them with read-only JSON-RPC calls. It says blockchain-hosted configuration removes the single point of failure that domain seizure and sinkholing rely on. The piece recommends monitoring outbound JSON-RPC traffic and inspecting endpoint memory, and maps the behavior to dead drop resolver technique T1102.001.
