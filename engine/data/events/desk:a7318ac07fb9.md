The companion advisory for CVE-2026-28325 was first published September 22, 2026 and describes an unauthenticated RCE from deserialization of untrusted data when the application is configured to use a specific communication mode. It affects Observability Self-Hosted 2026.2.2 and below, is fixed in 2026.2.3, and carries CVSS 3.1 8.8 with an adjacent attack vector (AV:A/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H).
