Bitdefender Labs disclosed GhostMap on Nov. 5, 2026, an Android denial-of-service flaw found after a banking dropper from the wild crashed its analysis emulators on Aug. 12, 2026. Two Android libraries interpret ZIP compression differently, letting a malformed APK bypass size checks and be mapped past its contents; reading the entry triggers SIGBUS. The flaw was reproduced on a Pixel 10 with Android 17 and the Aug. 5, 2026 patch and affects Android 10 and above; Google closed the August report as not reproducible with no patch.
