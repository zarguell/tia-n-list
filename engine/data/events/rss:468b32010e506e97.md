SOCRadar Threat Research Unit (STRU) has uncovered an AI-driven intrusion ecosystem run by affiliates of the INC Ransomware and The Gentlemen Ransomware groups. A single publicly accessible command-and-control server exposed the operators’ AI session databases, their central knowledge base and the source code of Phantom , a new “Autonomous Red Team Kill Chain Framework”. Configuration files, logs and credentials inside that server led STRU to six more exposed adversary-controlled servers. The operators exploited edge and remote-access systems at scale, including FortiClient EMS, SimpleHelp, cPanel and VPN gateways. They used AI-managed knowledge bases to track victims, stolen credentials and campaign progress. The servers also held 46 custom INC encryptor builds staged for an October 1, 2026 deployment, evidence of a live victim encryption, and a branded extortion package for a case listed by The Gentlemen . The campaign came to light in late September 2026 after OPSEC failures left the operators’ source code, credentials and AI session logs exposed. Their recorded AI spending exceeds $23,000.
