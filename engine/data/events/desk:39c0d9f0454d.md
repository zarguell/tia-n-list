SOCRadar's Threat Research Unit (STRU) published the original disclosure of the PivotC2 campaign on 2026-09-08, the day before the GBHackers and Cyberpress articles carried in this story. The report ties active exploitation of CVE-2025-25249 to PivotC2, a Node.js-based FortiGate post-exploitation RAT, and is the primary source both outlets cite for the 30,000-plus targeted IP addresses and 178 confirmed infections.
