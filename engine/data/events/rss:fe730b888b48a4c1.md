Ransomware reached record levels in Q3 2026, but the scale of activity is only part of what changed. The ecosystem producing those attacks is becoming increasingly fragmented: more ransomware brands are claiming victims, the top groups account for a smaller share of overall activity, and several major organizations are pushing back against high-value ransom demands. At the same time, established operators such as Qilin and The Gentlemen remain firmly at the top, creating a landscape where strong leaders coexist with an increasingly crowded field of ransomware groups. Key observed findings Q3 2026 was the highest-volume quarter on record, with 2,830 victims published on data leak sites. July recorded 964 victims, August reached 1,042, and September recorded 824. The ransomware ecosystem became more fragmented. The top 10 groups’ share of victims fell for the third consecutive quarter, from 71% in Q1 to 58% in Q2 and 49% in Q3. For the first time, groups outside the top ten collectively accounted for the majority of observed victims.
