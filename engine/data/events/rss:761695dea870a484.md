Although Recorded Future was founded by Swedish scientists and innovators, our HQ has always been a key part of the Somerville and Boston tech startup scene. Recognized for our award-winning platform and amazing culture, the team and our HQ have changed quite a bit over the years. Initially established in Somerville, MA, north of Boston, the Futurists' headquarters was once a laundromat. Twelve years after we set up camp there, it is time for the next chapter. Our HQ has moved to downtown Boston in Post Office Square — and we’re ready to make new memories there. Somerville Office laundromat sign | Boston Office (Current) on Franklin Street Key functions operate out of our Boston-based office, including customer success, security, data engineering, product, and marketing. Nearly 200 Futurists call the Boston office their home base, and many others travel for team meetings and strategic onsites. “In 2019, when I started, we were about 300 people globally — now we’re almost 200 just in the Boston office!” Lacey Maddalena Global Office Operations & Facilities Director “We’ve grown to more than 1,000 employees, and let’s just say the days of one-person teams are behind us!” Lisa Cefalo Senior Vice President, Controller
