This November 2023 article covers VMware Carbon Black researcher Takahiro Haruyama's study of 34 Windows drivers with firmware access through port I/O and memory-mapped I/O, explicitly naming PDFWKRNL.sys (CVE-2023-20598) among them. It notes six of the drivers allow kernel memory access that can be abused to elevate privilege and defeat security solutions, foreshadowing the exact BYOVD use Lunex applies to the driver.
