Apache has released version 11.0.25 of Apache Tomcat to address ten security vulnerabilities, including multiple flaws that could lead to authentication bypasses, access-control evasion, and denial-of-service (DoS) conditions. The most serious issues affect Tomcat’s processing of security constraints, authentication mechanisms, HTTP/2 implementation, and behavior of the RewriteValve.
