On AMD-based PCs and some Intel PCs, the TPM lockout for Windows Hello and BitLocker PINs can be reset by someone who opens the PC's case, researchers at Technische Universität Berlin say. That leaves short PINs open to guessing. In lab tests, the researchers found a four-digit Hello PIN on an AMD laptop and a 6-digit BitLocker PIN on an Intel laptop this way.
