Advisory FG-IR-26-199, published November 10, 2026, covers CVE-2026-126147, a path traversal [CWE-22] in FortiMail rated 9.8 that lets an unauthenticated attacker execute code or commands with crafted requests. FortiMail 8.0.0 through 8.0.1 moves to 8.0.2, 7.6.0 through 7.6.6 moves to 7.6.7, 7.4.0 through 7.4.8 moves to 7.4.9, and the whole 7.2 branch has no fix and is directed to migrate. The advisory states Fortinet had already remediated the issue in FortiMail Cloud on October 5, 2026 by moving cloud firmwares to v7.6.7 GA or v8.0.2 GA.
