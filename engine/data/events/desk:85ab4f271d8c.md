IONIX published a technical analysis on March 26, 2025 confirming active exploitation of CVE-2025-2746 and CVE-2025-2747 in Kentico Xperience 13 through version 13.0.178. The post details both digest-auth bypass via empty SHA1 usernames and the WSE 3.0 'SendNone' missing-password bypass, with mitigation steps including upgrading to 13.0.179, disabling the Staging service, and switching to X.509 certificate authentication.
