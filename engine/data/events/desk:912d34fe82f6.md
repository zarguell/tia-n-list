OpenAI published a security research post describing an autonomous pipeline that found, validated, and generated patches for 948 local privilege-escalation vulnerabilities in the Linux kernel. The run started from 2,308 candidate findings produced in 12 hours for $316,549, reproduced 1,724 vulnerabilities in 30 hours for $73,330, and screened them to 313 information-leak and 1,238 LPE candidates, at a total API cost of $1,213,430. Ten days after the scan, 61 LPEs had been patched upstream, 43 had staging patches, and 299 were being reported and discussed publicly.
