MLQ.ai reported that TSMC said on November 12 that personal data on some current and former employees was posted on an external website without authorization, in an SEC statement signed by CFO Wendell Huang. The data matches information stored in a cloud service provider's systems, and TSMC said it found no evidence its own systems were compromised and that operations and customer data were not involved. TSMC did not give a record count; Wccftech, citing Taiwanese press reports, said the files cover about 46,000 employee records and that the hackers demanded 3 bitcoin.
