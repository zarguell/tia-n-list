The Zoho/ManageEngine advisory lists ADSelfService Plus builds 7000 and below as affected and build 7001 as the fix, with a stated fixed-on date of August 24, 2026, one month before the story's first_seen. It describes an unauthenticated attacker with access to the Windows logon screen executing code in the NT AUTHORITY\SYSTEM context through the GINA client's embedded kiosk browser, and credits researchers Marouane Belabbassi and Amjad E Alhejaili through the Zoho BugBounty program. The page carries no publication timestamp of its own, so the date used here is the fixed-on date stated on the page.
