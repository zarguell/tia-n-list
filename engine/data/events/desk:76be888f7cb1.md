TeamViewer's trust center bulletin TV-2026-1010, issue date Sep 29, 2026, Priority Important with CVSS up to 8.8, covers five CVEs in TeamViewer Full Client and Host on Windows, Linux, and macOS. It details CVE-2026-92370, an improper access control flaw letting an authenticated remote attacker bypass user-configured session permissions, alongside CVE-2026-19743 (IPC path traversal), CVE-2026-92368 (heap overflow in .tvs session recording playback), CVE-2026-92369 (installer rollback TOCTOU), and CVE-2026-92371 (Cloud Session Recording link resolution). Fixes ship in 15.82 plus legacy builds 15.64.8, 14.7.48855, 13.2.36230, 13.2.153995, and 13.2.153994. The bulletin states TeamViewer is not aware of any public disclosure or active exploitation in the wild.
