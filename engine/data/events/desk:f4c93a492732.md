LaLiga's press release says a cybercriminal accessed one of its fan-services technology platforms between 14 and 16 November 2026 and extracted personal data from users registered with LaLiga and some clubs. The league says its security teams identified the access source, contained the attack on 16 November, stopped the extraction and blocked the attacker, with no effect on other LaLiga systems or competition operations. It reported the incident to the Spanish Data Protection Agency and filed a complaint with the National Police Central Cybercrime Unit, and says affected clubs were informed.
