Japan McDonald's said a third party accessed part of the customer-related data in the database for its official mobile app. The listed fields are product purchase histories, an internal customer-management ID, IP addresses and location from mobile devices used when the service was provided; names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses and card details were not included. The company said it had not confirmed any unauthorized use of the app or services, was invalidating the authentication credentials used in the access, investigating related systems and reporting to Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission. It said it would update the notice as new information became available.
