Summary On August 26, 2026 , the notorious ransomware group Akira claimed responsibility for a cyberattack on Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery ( omscare.com ), a healthcare provider specializing in oral surgery in Brockton, MA. The threat actors announced their intention to leak 14GB of data, which includes sensitive employee and patient information, unless their demands are met.
