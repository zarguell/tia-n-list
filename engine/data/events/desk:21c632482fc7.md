Oracle's May 2026 Critical Security Patch Update contains 35 new security patches, with 12 targeting Oracle E-Business Suite. CVE-2026-46817 is listed among the EBS patches for the File Transmission component in Oracle Payments, affecting versions 12.2.3 through 12.2.15. The advisory notes that attackers have previously succeeded against unpatched systems and urges immediate application of available fixes. Published May 28, 2026.
