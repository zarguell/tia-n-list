Beazley Security published an advisory dated September 9, 2026 reporting that Check Point had released emergency updates that day for two critical VPN certificate-handling vulnerabilities. It describes CVE-2026-85103 as a heap overflow in VPN certificate ASN.1 decoding affecting Security Gateways and Security Management systems, and CVE-2026-85102 as improper certificate validation during VPN negotiation, both CVSS 9.8, with fixes delivered through LivePatch and the Jumbo Hotfix Accumulator.
