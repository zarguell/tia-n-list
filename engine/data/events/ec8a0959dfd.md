The campaign mirrors broader trends where stolen cookies and authenticated sessions — which bypass MFA entirely — have become more valuable than passwords on underground markets. The operator's development cadence, versioned updates, and customer support structure increasingly resemble legitimate software businesses. This marks a significant professionalization of infostealer operations from smash-and-grab credential collection toward persistent access monetization.
