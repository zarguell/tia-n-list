EvilTokens is a criminal of online service which tricks people into thinking they are logging into a real Microsoft website. In reality, this scam allows attackers to take over victims’ Microsoft 365 accounts. This service started in 2026 and makes it easier for scammers to target Microsoft 365 users. It does this by sending customized messages and generating fake login codes while also automating their actions once they gain access to accounts.
