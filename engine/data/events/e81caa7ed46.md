Foxconn, the world's largest contract electronics manufacturer ($258.3B 2025 revenue), confirmed a cyberattack affecting multiple North American factories including its Wisconsin AI hub and Houston facility. The Nitrogen ransomware gang claimed responsibility, asserting theft of 8TB / 11M+ files including schematics for Apple, NVIDIA, Dell, Google, and Intel products. Production halted around May 1, with workers sent home due to network outages. Factories are resuming normal operations. This is Foxconn's fourth known ransomware incident (following LockBit attacks in 2022 and 2024, and a 2020 incident), underscoring persistent targeting of the contract manufacturing giant. Nitrogen, built on Conti's builder, has a reportedly broken decryptor per Coveware (Feb 2026), meaning paying ransom offers no recovery guarantee.
