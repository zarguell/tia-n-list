Notion published an advisory stating Fireworks notified it on November 2 that a small set of data had been accessed, which Notion initially assessed as Notion AI synthetic testing data with no customer data involved. By the morning of November 3 Notion disconnected from Fireworks, deleted its data in the Fireworks system, and stopped communication; Fireworks notified Notion on November 6 that customer data may have been accessed and confirmed the final impacted dataset on November 8. The dataset contained Notion AI chat transcripts used for the Insights feature, and Notion reached out to impacted customers on November 9. Fewer than 1 percent of Notion workspaces were affected, most transcripts were unattributable to a workspace or user, the Insights feature was disabled, and Notion revoked exposed Notion API keys found in affected user messages on November 11.
