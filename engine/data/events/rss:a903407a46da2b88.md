Doppelgänger (German for “double”) describes the campaign’s hallmark: fake copies of well-known news sites, with a design, logo and layout meant to make readers think they are reading an article from a legitimate media outlet. In recent weeks, ClearSky identified renewed activity by the Russian influence campaign in Israel. The campaign, known as “The Double” – Doppelgänger, spreads false and misleading messages built on parts of real publications, while impersonating well-known media outlets. The messages are spread on social networks, mainly on X, through a network of fake accounts. The goal is not only to spread one-off false information. The campaign aims to push, over time, messages that serve Russian interests, weaken trust in the government and its institutions, deepen divisions in Israeli society and pollute AI engines. Between May and October 2026, ClearSky’s analyst team documented at least 76 fake articles published on walla[.]ist, a site impersonating the Walla news site. The campaign’s resurgence since August 2026 may stem from a Russian intent to influence Israeli public opinion ahead of the elections scheduled for October 27, as Russia has done in election campaigns in other countries.
