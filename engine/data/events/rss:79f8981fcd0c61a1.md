If your browser is showing pop-ups, redirecting searches, or loading extensions you never installed, you may be dealing with Chrome malware. The good news is that you can usually remove malware from Chrome in under 30 minutes without reinstalling Windows or paying for a tool. The harder part is making sure the Chrome malware infection is really gone and that nothing it stole is still being used against you. This guide covers both. Quick answer: the 5-step fix Remove suspicious extensions. Open chrome://extensions and delete anything you don’t recognize. Revoke notifications and site permissions. Settings > Privacy and security > Site settings. Reset Chrome. Settings > Reset settings > Restore settings to their original defaults. Scan the whole computer. Run a full scan with Microsoft Defender or a trusted antivirus, because malware often lives outside the browser. Change your passwords from a clean device, then check whether your logins have already leaked to the dark web. What Is Chrome Malware? Chrome malware is any malicious software that targets, abuses, or hides inside Google Chrome. It isn’t one specific virus.
