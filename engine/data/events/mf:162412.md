CERT-AGID has identified malicious sites using PagoPA's name, logo, and graphics to steal personal data and payment card information from victims. The fraudulent pages present an alleged refund for an overpayment of TARI (waste tax), inducing users to follow an online procedure to confirm their identity and provide bank account details.
