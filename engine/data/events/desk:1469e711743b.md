The Hacker News reports that APT flaw CVE-2026-109518 lets attackers with HTTP traffic control replace verified packages and execute code as root when downloads retry. Debian issued DSA-6607-1 and Ubuntu issued USN-8996-1 on November 4, 2026; Max Justicz reported the flaw. None of the advisory reports describe attacks exploiting the flaw or offer a workaround for systems that cannot update yet.
