ClickFix campaign abuses trusted websites to deploy Psychedelic Stealer

SecurityAffairs covers a ClickFix-style campaign that abuses trusted, legitimate websites to deliver the Psychedelic Stealer malware to Windows users. The lure pattern walks victims into running attacker commands under a fix-like pretext, with the stealer as the payload.
