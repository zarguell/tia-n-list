Roundcube published security updates 1.6.12 and 1.5.12 on December 13, 2025, fixing two vulnerabilities. One fix addresses a cross-site scripting flaw via the SVG animate tag reported by Valentin T. of CrowdStrike; the other fixes an information disclosure issue in the HTML style sanitizer reported by somerandomdev. The Roundcube team strongly recommended updating all productive 1.6.x and 1.5.x installations.
