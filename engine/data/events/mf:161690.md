Key Takeaways Since March 31, 2025, all 51 former “best practice” requirements in PCI DSS 4.0 have been fully scored. Every 2026 assessment covers them. APIs fall inside the definition of bespoke and custom software, so inventory, pre-release review, and protection against business-logic abuse all apply.
