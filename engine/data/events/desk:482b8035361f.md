1340 WJOL reported that Wilmington School District 209U was investigating a possible ransomware attack and data breach. The article reproduced a message from Superintendent Kevin Feeney saying the district was working with its cyber insurance team and cybersecurity specialists to determine scope and protect district systems and information. Feeney said district-issued laptops and other Windows devices were temporarily suspended while internet access, Chromebooks and cell phones remained usable, and that the district was still determining whether personal information was accessed.
