A federal grand jury in Rochester, New York indicted Ryan James Roach of Saskatchewan and Mathiew James Stubbings of Ontario on conspiracy and computer damage charges tied to a campaign running from June 2016 through December 2019. The defendants allegedly used virtual private servers registered under fraudulent identities to deliver malware and mining software to victim computers and store stolen login credentials. Universities in New York, Massachusetts, North Carolina, and Tennessee were compromised, with a focus on campuses running supercomputers. Roach was extradited from Canada on October 28, 2026 and arraigned on November 3, while extradition proceedings against Stubbings continue.
