Apple released iOS 27.2.1 and iPadOS 27.2.1 on November 9, 2026, fixing nine vulnerabilities across AirPlay, App Intents, Bluetooth, brotli, CoreUtils, Messages, and rapportd. The Messages fixes include CVE-2026-113961, which could let a remote attacker join an iMessage group chat and receive its messages, and CVE-2026-113960, which could let a user added to a group access messages sent before they joined. The update is available for iPhone 11 and later and corresponding iPad models.
