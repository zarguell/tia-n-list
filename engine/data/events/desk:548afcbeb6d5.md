Google promoted Chrome 158 to the Stable channel for Windows, Mac and Linux, with 158.0.8127.36 on Linux and 158.0.8127.36/.37 on Windows and Mac rolling out over the coming days and weeks. The advisory lists 204 security fixes and six Critical CVEs: CVE-2026-130963 (use after free in WebView), CVE-2026-131031 and CVE-2026-131054 (out of bounds writes in GPU and ANGLE), CVE-2026-131060 (buffer overflow in Media), CVE-2026-131008 (use after free in GarbageCollection) and CVE-2026-130975 (use after free in WebAudio). Google notes that bug details and links may stay restricted until a majority of users are updated.
