2 News Nevada reported on November 16 that Roman Khlynovskiy appeared in federal court in Knoxville after extradition from Italy, citing the Justice Department. The article says prosecutors tied him to the 8-Digits Team and its INC and Lynx affiliations, with alleged victims among hospitals, schools and other healthcare entities. It states that the states involved were not identified. The report repeats court document claims of more than 50 terabytes of stolen data, over 70 million confidential health records and more than $500 million in ransom demands.
