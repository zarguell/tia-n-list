ConnectWise's bulletin dated 08 September 2026 details CVE-2026-84869, a CWE-862 missing authorization flaw scored CVSS 9.9 that may allow files to be transferred and executed through an active remote session without authorization or host confirmation; ScreenConnect servers are not impacted, the client is. ScreenConnect versions prior to 26.6.5 are affected and the stated interim mitigation is deselecting the TransferFiles permission for every role under Administration > Security > Roles. The bulletin is rated Priority 1 High and carries post-patch steps to reinstall host clients, update access agents, review users, and enable MFA.
