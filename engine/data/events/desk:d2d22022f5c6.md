Bitdefender's original disclosure, by Adrian Mihai Gozob and Alex Baciu, published about 25 minutes before the story's first-seen timestamp. It documents a platform-signed system application preinstalled in the firmware of low-cost MediaTek Android phones, shipping as com.android.system.lite and rotating names including com.android.sys.prot, com.android.sys.gmsprot and com.android.sys.bcprot. The report details at least 32 unique disguised payload apps, ad and click fraud, residential-proxy enrollment, 13 related apps on Google Play under two developer accounts, a command-and-control channel disguised as a weather API at api.weatherlive[.]world, and firmware signed with certificates attributed to Shenzhen Zediel. Bitdefender observed the campaign on thousands of unique devices across more than 150 countries over roughly two years and published 120 sample MD5 hashes.
