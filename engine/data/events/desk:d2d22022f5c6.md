Bitdefender's original disclosure by Adrian Mihai Gozob and Alex Baciu documents a platform-signed system application preinstalled in low-cost MediaTek Android firmware, shipping as com.android.system.lite and rotating names including com.android.sys.prot, com.android.sys.gmsprot and com.android.sys.bcprot. The report details at least 32 disguised payload apps, ad and click fraud, residential-proxy enrollment, a C2 disguised as a weather API at api.weatherlive[.]world, certificates linked to Shenzhen Zediel, and thousands of affected devices across over 150 countries over roughly two years.
