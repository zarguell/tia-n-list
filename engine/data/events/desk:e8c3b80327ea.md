Google reported that Star Blizzard used fake CAPTCHA pages and the ClickFix technique to deliver a new malware family named LostKeys in campaigns observed in January, March, and April 2025 against advisors to Western governments, journalists, think tanks, and NGOs. LostKeys steals files from a hard-coded list of extensions and directories and was deployed only in highly selective cases. This documents the multi-step malware delivery tradecraft that RedFlick later replaced.
