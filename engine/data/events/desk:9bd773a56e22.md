Zeabur's preliminary report states that on October 14-15, 2026 an attacker used leaked plaintext internal credentials to access shared clusters across nine regions, some Dedicated Servers, and Zeabur internal systems, then read the platform database. The incident affected 321,810 accounts and roughly 3.2 million environment-variable records, with root sign-ins on 101 GCP servers. Admin tokens had no expiry, were created in January 2024, and were not included in the post-August rotation.
