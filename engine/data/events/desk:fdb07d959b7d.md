CISA added CVE-2026-67277 and CVE-2026-86060 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on September 10, 2026, citing evidence of active exploitation. CVE-2026-67277 is a MikroTik RouterOS Missing Authentication for Critical Function flaw in the bandwidth-test service. The advisory references MikroTik's September 2026 vulnerability notice and directs federal agencies to patch.
