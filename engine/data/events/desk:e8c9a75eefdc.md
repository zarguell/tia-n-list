A data leak at Amadeus exposed passenger data, with access credentials visible in plain text via an unprotected heap dump. Amadeus said it was made aware on September 21, 2026 of a potential vulnerability affecting a display environment for travel bookings, that certain booking data may have been accessible while payment or financial data could not be accessed, and that the vulnerability has since been fixed. heise online reported that readers alerted it to the issue and that current booking data, including full names, dates of birth, PNRs, and contact, ID, and passport data in plain text, was retrievable.
