A cybersecurity development worth paying attention to today. According to a Reuters report published on November 5, India's CERT-In has identified an active hacking campaign called Crafty Falcon, targeting non-bank lenders, fintech companies, and cryptocurrency exchanges. What's interesting is how the attackers are reportedly using publicly available AI tools to automate parts of their operations. What do we know? CERT-In identified four tools associated with the campaign: - ARTEX - PentAGI - Cairn - CyberStrikeAI Researchers have linked at least two of these tools to China, although CERT-In has not publicly attributed the campaign to a specific country or state-sponsored group.
