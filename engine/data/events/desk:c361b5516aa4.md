Aikido identified Go malware in two Terraform providers (gocommunity-io/dockerd, kreuzwenker/docker) and two Go modules (gocommunity.io/orderedbtree, gogets.dev/btreex), the first malware distribution through Terraform providers it has observed. The providers arm only when the SHA-256 hash of containerName and networkID matches a hardcoded value, and the second-stage RAT uses dual C2 over Slack and an Arbitrum Sepolia smart contract; Aikido's telemetry showed 18 unique victim hostnames across 725 plaintext check-ins.
