Max Milbers announced VirtueMart 4.8.10 as an important security fix, saying the earlier 4.8.8 release addressed an RCE caused by a fallback that could be misused when the server had the right programs installed. The new release fixed a separate flaw that let a shopper fake a paid order. The notice provided two hotfixes, one for VirtueMart 4.6 and above and one for versions below 4.6, and said the fixes apply to VirtueMart 4.0 and above.
