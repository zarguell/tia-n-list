A 23-year-old college student has been arrested for a radio signal spoofing attack that forced three Taiwan High Speed Rail (THSR) trains into emergency stops during the Qingming Festival holiday, stranding passengers for 48 minutes. The attacker exploited a vulnerability in THSR's computer systems to gain network access, then used electromagnetic interference tools and wireless broadcasting hardware to impersonate a restricted TETRA mobile communication device. The spoofed General Alarm signal — indistinguishable from a legitimate distress signal — triggered standard safety protocols that initiated immediate emergency stops. The attack blends cyber intrusion with physical-layer radio frequency manipulation, highlighting a growing vulnerability in transit systems that rely on radio-based OT networks.
