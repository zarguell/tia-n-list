Joint CISA and MS-ISAC advisory, initial version August 16, 2022, responding to active exploitation of multiple ZCS vulnerabilities: CVE-2022-24682, CVE-2022-27924, CVE-2022-27925 chained with CVE-2022-37042, and CVE-2022-30333. It warns that unpatched or internet-exposed ZCS instances in government and private sector networks should be assumed compromised and provides Snort signatures, IOCs, and detection guidance for hunting.
