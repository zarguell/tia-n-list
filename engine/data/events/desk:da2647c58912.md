The Apache CNA record published CVE-2026-133325 as a CWE-322 key exchange without entity authentication issue in Apache Tomcat. It says an unauthenticated node could join a cluster secured with TLS 1.3 and TLS-PSK, lists 11.0.28 through 11.0.29, 10.1.62 and 9.0.124 as affected, and recommends switching cluster transport to TLS 1.2 with TLS-PSK or upgrading to 11.0.30, 10.1.63 or 9.0.125 when released. Mike Read is credited as the finder.
