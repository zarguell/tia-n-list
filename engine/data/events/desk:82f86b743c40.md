Mallory published an early brief describing CVE-2026-99362 as a heap-based buffer overflow in NFSv4.1 session reply-cache handling that is also reachable through NFSv4.2. It says a client can negotiate ca_maxresponsesize_cached down to NFSD_MIN_HDR_SEQ_SZ, that nfsd4_alloc_slot() then allocates a zero-length sl_data[], and that a KASAN reproduction showed an 80-byte out-of-bounds write. The brief lists fixed upstream releases 5.10.275, 5.15.226, 6.1.193, 6.6.163, 6.12.117, 6.18.61, 7.3.2 and 7.4-rc1, recommends restricting TCP port 2049 and disabling NFSv4.1 or NFSv4.2 as interim steps, and records no reported in-the-wild exploitation as of 25 November.
