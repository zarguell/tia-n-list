Wiz Research disclosed a misconfigured Supabase database behind Moltbook, a social network for AI agents, that granted unauthenticated read and write access to all platform data. The exposure included 1.5 million API authentication tokens, 35,000 email addresses, private messages, and roughly 4.75 million records; the maintainer applied fixes within hours of contact starting January 31, 2026. The post describes the recurring pattern of Supabase keys in client-side JavaScript without RLS in vibe-coded applications.
