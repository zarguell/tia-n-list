ConnectWise is scrambling to restore its Cloud Backup, SaaS Security, and Cloud Management services after hackers abused a compromised credential to access customers’ M365 tenants. The company suspended the Cloud Backup service (formerly known as SkyKick) on October 17, roughly 12 hours after it started investigating the suspicious activity. “We recently identified unauthorized access to application identities within the ConnectWise Cloud Backup environment. As a protective measure for our Cloud Backup partners and their clients, we are shutting off Cloud Backup application access,” the company said . The next day, it decided to shut down SaaS Security and Cloud Management as well, to implement mitigations, noting that customers’ backups remain encrypted and protected and that it has no evidence of unauthorized access to them. The containment measure continues to impact scheduled backups, archive access, and restores, as well as SaaS Security monitoring and alerting, and Cloud Management user and license management, and ConnectWise has not provided an estimated restoration timeline. However, it has shared information on the IP addresses used in the intrusion and has published guidance for reviewing MSPs’ Microsoft 365 environments. According to cybersecurity firm Huntress , the compromised Cloud Backup credential was used to access Microsoft 365 tenants on October 15.
