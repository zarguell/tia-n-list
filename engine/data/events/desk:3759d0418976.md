The Record reported on 30 September 2024 that AFP said hackers attacked its IT systems the preceding Friday, affecting part of its delivery service to clients. AFP said the perpetrators were unknown and that its technical teams were working with the French national agency ANSSI, with the relevant authorities notified, while its newsroom continued normal coverage. No group claimed the incident and AFP did not answer questions on whether it faced ransomware, the report adding that AFP was founded in 1835 and operates in 150 countries.
