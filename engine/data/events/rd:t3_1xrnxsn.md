I built a behavioral detection engine focused on Living-off-the-Land binaries on Windows and wanted to share it with the community for feedback. Problem it tries to solve Traditional signature/hash-based detection struggles with LOLBins because they are legitimate, signed Microsoft binaries. Attackers also rename them, obfuscate command lines, and chain multiple utilities across time. Single-event rules often miss the broader operational context.
