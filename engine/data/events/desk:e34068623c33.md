Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter, that CrowdStrike was in talks to acquire Zscaler in a deal that would rank among the largest in cybersecurity and create a combined market value of about 265 billion dollars. The report said deliberations were ongoing with no certainty of a transaction, and that neither company immediately commented. It put Zscaler's market value at 32 billion dollars and CrowdStrike's at about 233 billion dollars, noting CrowdStrike's Falcon focus on endpoint security.
