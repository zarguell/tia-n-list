Pen Test Partners describes how Neil McCourt of Omega Solutions found a significant flaw in Resource Data Management refrigeration controllers and how its hardware team validated it and found additional issues. The post says RDM's initial customer advisory RDM-SA-2026-001, dated 2 November and revised 3 November, lacked impact detail and independent validation, and that Omega alerted the NCSC before a wider industry notification effort. The researchers used Shodan to locate exposed controllers and contact large operators, say smaller cold-chain operators may still be exposed, and withhold the exploit method while advising removal from the public internet and firmware updates.
