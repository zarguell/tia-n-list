Executive Summary Since the spring of 2026, Insikt Group has observed multiple Russian-language traffer teams that have quickly adapted affiliate-fraud models to capitalize on growing public interest in prediction markets such as PolyMarket and Kalshi. The platforms offered by three such teams — AtlasCore, PolyFucked, and Magnetic Team — provide websites, administrative panels, cryptocurrency payment infrastructure, victim-management capabilities, technical support, and promotional guidance. This model lowers barriers to entry for affiliates and enables campaigns to scale rapidly as new trends attract potential victims. The services combine convincing prediction-market interfaces with established fraud mechanisms designed to manipulate victims and prevent them from recovering deposited funds. These mechanisms include controlled trading outcomes, fabricated market activity, artificial withdrawal restrictions and fees, and the collection of sensitive identity and payment information. Artificial intelligence (AI) further accelerates this opportunism by reducing the time, cost, and expertise required to adapt campaigns to emerging events.
