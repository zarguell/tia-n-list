Splunk PSIRT published an advance notice on November 11, 2026 stating that advisories would be released on November 18 for vulnerabilities fixed in Splunk Enterprise and Universal Forwarder 10.6.1, 10.4.5, 10.2.9, 10.0.12 and 9.4.17, Enterprise Security 8.7.0, SOAR 8.8.0, Stream 8.1.7 and AI Assistant 2.0.0. Customers were told to apply the listed or latest versions to remediate the pending disclosures. A November 12 changelog entry corrected the 10.0.12 version listed for Enterprise and Universal Forwarder.
