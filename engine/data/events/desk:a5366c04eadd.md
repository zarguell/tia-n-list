TechCrunch reported on September 25, 2026 at 8:52 AM PDT that Kiteworks (formerly Accellion) had notified customers of a credible threat from law enforcement indicating a potential imminent attack. The company recommended shutting systems before the weekend as a precaution, stressing no confirmed compromise. The article notes Kiteworks has thousands of customers across healthcare, technology, education, automotive, and government, and references at least a thousand internet-facing Kiteworks instances spotted by security researcher Kevin Beaumont. Kiteworks CISO Frank Balonis confirmed the advisory and said all known vulnerabilities are addressed in release 9.5.1.
