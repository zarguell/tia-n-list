Without adequate governance, agentic AI threatens to autonomously, invisibly and rapidly morph from a business boon into a business disaster. Sysdig has turned its runtime visibility skills into providing governance to agentic AI. The need is clear: “AI is moving from answering to acting. It used to be chat machines. Now agentic AI does stuff for us,” explains Loris Degioanni, Sysdig founder and CTO. The problem is it can do more than we tell it, faster than we can control it, and has no sense of morality. Sysdig has stepped into this space with the announcement of Sysdig AI Defense offering runtime security and governance for AI agents. “AI has moved from answering to acting, and agents now ship code, change infrastructure, and reach into production systems at a speed no team can review by hand,” warns Sysdig. Unattended, the problem of AI agents going rogue is likely to increase over time: agents are designed with increasing autonomy, complexity and ‘reasoning’ to maximize their undoubted economic benefit. Increasing complexity increases the potential to drift into error. Sysdig AI Defense sees and monitors every action an agent takes, from its origin to its completion. This is no simple task. Agents can assume new powers very rapidly as they seek to complete their assumed tasks.
