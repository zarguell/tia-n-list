The FBI warns that multiple threat actors have abused ARTEX, an AI-powered penetration-testing framework, since at least August 2026. The agency says it has been used against organizations in finance, government, technology, healthcare and other sectors. ARTEX can automate reconnaissance, vulnerability exploitation and privilege escalation. The FBI connects it to attacks involving ConnectWise cloud backup integrations and Microsoft 365 environments, with some intrusions progressing to privileged access in under three hours. Attackers with limited expertise may be able to launch complex intrusions faster and against more targets.
