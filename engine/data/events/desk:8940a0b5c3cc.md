Arctic Wolf Labs documented a campaign against FortiGate firewalls with exposed management interfaces, with anomalous jsconsole activity observed as early as November 16, 2024 on firmware 7.0.14 through 7.0.16. Attackers created random-named super_admin accounts, added local users to SSL VPN groups, and reached DCSync lateral movement; loopback traffic hit TCP ports 8023 and 9980, and jsconsole logins used spoofed source and destination IPs such as 127.0.0.1 and the 8.8.8.8/8.8.4.4 and 1.1.1.1/2.2.2.2 pairs. Arctic Wolf assessed mass exploitation of a zero-day as highly likely, four days before Fortinet published its advisory.
