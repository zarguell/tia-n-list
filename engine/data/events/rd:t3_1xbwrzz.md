Your browser and OS already dropped the eight compromised eMudhra intermediates via revocation. Your command-line stack mostly didn't — curl, node, go, python's certifi/pip, and anything with a pinned CA bundle keep trusting them until the bundle actually updates. So I (mostly Claude) wrote a small bash script that asks each client one question: do you still trust the compromised chain? It live-tests against the disclosure site's probe hosts (inverted logic — a successful HTTPS response means you're vulnerable), covers curl/node/go/python + venvs + env/config CA overrides, and only reports — it never touches your trust store.
