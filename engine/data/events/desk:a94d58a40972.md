Keio Corporation published an official notice dated September 26, 2026, confirming that a ransomware attack on group servers was detected in the early hours of the same day. The company stated it immediately implemented network isolation measures to prevent further damage, reported the incident to police, and began investigating the attack route and damage scope with external experts. Train operations remained unaffected, though some group business systems were disrupted. No information leakage had been confirmed at the time of publication.
