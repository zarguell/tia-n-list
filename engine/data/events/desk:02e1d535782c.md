The Hacker News reported research by CloudSEK and Checkmarx on a campaign they call MALFEX, in which a lone actor published 12 npm packages since August 2023, eight of them malicious. The packages were downloaded 40,767 times, with 37,419 downloads attributed to function-flag, and three packages acted as Overlord RAT loaders that used lifecycle hooks to fetch and run a Windows executable. Other packages delivered the movinlike Node.js stealer aimed at Discord, browsers, Telegram and cryptocurrency wallets. This was earlier evidence that Overlord RAT, which uses Solana transactions to retrieve its C2 address, was being distributed through npm supply-chain lures.
