Switzerland explores sovereign alternatives to Microsoft 365 for its government administrations. The move is not just about cost: it is about digital sovereignty and reducing dependence on a single foreign vendor for critical government IT infrastructure. Swiss authorities are evaluating domestic and European alternatives to replace Microsoft 365 across federal and cantonal administration, driven by concerns about data residency, vendor lock-in, and geopolitical risk.
