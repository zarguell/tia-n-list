The FBI and Dutch National Police have announced the arrest of an alleged leader of the ShinyHunters cybercrime group , an extortion-focused threat actor network accused of compromising more than 140 organizations and collecting at least $70 million in payments since last year. The Dutch National Police’s High Tech Crime Unit carried out the operation in coordination with FBI investigators. FBI Director Kash Patel said the suspect was arrested under Dutch law and described the individual as one of ShinyHunters’ alleged leaders. FBI and Dutch Police Arrest Alleged ShinyHunters Leader Patel said FBI teams are continuing to work with international and private-sector partners to develop and execute further investigative leads arising from the arrest. Dutch authorities said the suspect is a 24-year-old man from Amsterdam, arrested on September 15 on suspicion of participating in a criminal organization connected to ShinyHunters. Authorities have not publicly named the man. Reporting by Reuters and other outlets identified him as Pepijn van der Stap, although Dutch police have not formally confirmed that identity. The suspect was remanded in custody for at least 90 days while the investigation continues. The case highlights the international reach of ShinyHunters, a cyber-extortion operation associated with large-scale data theft, victim harassment, and threats to publicly release stolen information.
