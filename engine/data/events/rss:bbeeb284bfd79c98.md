Ukrainian authorities, working with the FBI, have arrested four suspected members of an international ransomware organization linked to more than $40.5 million in ransom payments from at least 28 companies worldwide. The arrests come after a two-year investigation into a cybercrime organization comprising at least 27 individuals from Ukraine, Kazakhstan, Russia, and other countries. Investigators say the organization was connected to a larger cybercrime network of more than 60 members operating across eight jurisdictions, including former members of the notorious Conti ransomware gang. According to Ukraine's National Police , the group targeted organizations in the oil and gas, pharmaceutical, manufacturing, logistics, and financial sectors, as well as critical infrastructure. The attackers researched their victims' financial standing, assets, employees, and IT vulnerabilities before launching attacks designed to steal corporate data, encrypt systems, and extort cryptocurrency payments. Speaking in an exclusive interview with Ukrainian news outlet Censor.NET , acting National Police chief Maksym Tsutskiridze said investigators had identified at least 28 companies that paid more than $40.5 million in ransoms.
