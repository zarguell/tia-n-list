A new Android malware family called WindRelay is being used with the SpyNote remote access trojan (RAT) to steal money through live NFC card-relay fraud. The operation combines phone-based social engineering, remote device control, and contactless payment abuse in one fast-moving attack. The campaign shows how fraudsters no longer need a victim’s physical card. Instead, they convince targets to tap their bank card against an infected Android phone. WindRelay then captures and relays the live NFC exchange to an attacker-controlled device, allowing fraudulent in-store payments or ATM withdrawals . NFC relay fraud is growing quickly. Kaspersky reported that NFC-based Android attacks rose 188% in the first four months of 2026 compared with the same period in 2025. The technique works by placing malicious devices between a real payment card and a legitimate terminal, relaying the transaction data in real time.me-en. WindRelay and SpyNote Attack In the observed case, the fraud began with a phone call from an attacker posing as a bank employee. The caller claimed there was a problem with the victim’s payment card and instructed them to install an Android application. That first app was a personalized SpyNote RAT . Rather than using a generic label, the malware reportedly displayed the victim’s own name.
