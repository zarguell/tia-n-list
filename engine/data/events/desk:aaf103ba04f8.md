Searchlight Cyber published technical research on Fail2Install, describing pre-authentication remote code execution across Drupal 8.9, 9, 10 and 11 on Apache or PHP-FPM with MySQL or PostgreSQL. The researchers showed that EMFILE, database connection and TCP source-port exhaustion could make failed checks read as an uninstalled site, letting the installer reopen, create an administrator-controlled account and write a SQLite database with a .php extension under the document root. They state their finding duplicated ANT-2026-GMRPK0BU reported by Anthropic's OSS Scanner, but argue that making it work on stock configurations was novel.
