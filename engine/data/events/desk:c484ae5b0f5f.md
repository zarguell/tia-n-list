Nokia's Deepfield Emergency Response Team first published this report on July 11, 2026 after a DNS hunt in protected networks found subscriber devices beaconing to a BADBOX 2.0 and MoYu control rotation that matched no published indicator list. The team traced sideloaded, clean-looking IPTV apps through signed bootstrap requests and plaintext module descriptors on a1 and t1 client-pull subdomains to a server-gated zhima residential-proxy module named com.miyc.transfer.Client. It identifies that client-pull host as the discriminating DNS indicator and notes that cover domains on the same registrable name can serve an ordinary IPTV portal on the apex and C2 on a subdomain.
