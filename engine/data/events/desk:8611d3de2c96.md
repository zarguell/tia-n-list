The NVD entry for CVE-2021-22681, published March 3, 2021 and last modified June 17, 2026, describes the flaw in Rockwell Studio 5000 Logix Designer and RSLogix 5000 where a key used to verify Logix controller communications can be bypassed. It maps the issue to CWE-522 (Insufficiently Protected Credentials) and cites CISA advisory ICSA-21-056-03 and the KEV catalog as references. The June 2026 modification shows the record was still being maintained weeks before the water-sector campaign began.
