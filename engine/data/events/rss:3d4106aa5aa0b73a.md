A recently patched Drupal core vulnerability is being exploited in the wild, according to the cybersecurity agency CISA and Drupal developers. The vulnerability, CVE-2026-132165 , can be exploited for remote code execution. It was patched on November 18, and Drupal’s advisory says in-the-wild exploitation began shortly after. “The Security Team has received a report this was exploited on live sites as early as 2026-11-19 19:00 UTC,” Drupal noted. The discovery of the flaw is credited to Anthropic’s OSS Scanner, a free opt-in service that regularly scans enrolled open source projects for vulnerabilities. Anthropic has not said how many vulnerabilities OSS Scanner has found since its October launch, but as of early November, it reported disclosing more than 5,000 vulnerabilities overall, across 680 open source projects. CISA added CVE-2026-132165 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog on November 24, instructing federal agencies to address it by November 27. No public information appears to be available on attacks exploiting CVE-2026-132165. It’s unclear how many websites are affected. The security hole affects the installer that ships with Drupal core, which enables users to set up a new site.
