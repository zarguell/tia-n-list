Dutch police and the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) took down a massive botnet comprising at least **17 million infected devices**, seizing over 200 servers at a Dutch hosting provider. The botnet is linked to **Asocks**, a proxy service advertising 7 million IPs across 150 locations and 100,000 clients, with subscriptions ranging from $5–$15/month. The infrastructure enabled DDoS attacks, traffic proxying, and cryptocurrency mining. While Asocks markets itself as a legitimate proxy service, the NCSC confirmed the device owners were not knowing participants.
