Cybersecurity plans are only effective when people understand their roles and know how to respond under pressure. Tabletop exercises provide organizations with a practical way to test their preparedness without disrupting normal operations. Our Tabletop Exercises & Security Education services help employees, executives and boards understand cybersecurity risks and practice responding to realistic scenarios. Test Your Incident Response Plan A tabletop exercise brings key stakeholders together to work through a simulated cyber incident. Participants discuss how they would identify, escalate and respond to the situation. Exercises can explore scenarios such as: Ransomware attacks Data breaches Business email compromise Executive account compromise Insider threats Third-party security incidents Cloud or technology disruptions Cyber-related business crises Engage Executives and Boards Cybersecurity incidents often require rapid business decisions. Tabletop exercises give executives and boards an opportunity to understand how cyber incidents can affect operations, reputation, legal obligations and business continuity.
