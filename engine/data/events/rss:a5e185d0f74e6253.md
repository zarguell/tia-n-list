The encrypted messaging platform Signal has announced it will by default stop users from taking screenshots of messages if they are using Google or Apple operating systems, a move fueled by concerns about how the tech giants deploy artificial intelligence agents. Last year, the widely used messaging app restricted Microsoft 11 users from screenshotting and keeping messages. Similar restrictions will now apply to Google’s Gemini Intelligence and Apple's Siri AI, Signal Foundation president Meredith Whittaker said in a Friday blog post. “These agentic AI integrations are changing this environment in a way that’s making it increasingly hostile to privacy,” the blog post said. “This is an existential threat to Signal, and our ability to responsibly uphold our mission.” Whittaker said Signal plans to specifically restrict the agentic systems integrated into iOS 27 and versions of Android where Signal detects AI assistants trying to read text. The operating systems’ AI agents attempt to glean information from users by scanning what’s on their screens, Whittaker said, and in doing so hoover up the data in order to “answer your questions and do your bidding,” the blog post said. Whittaker warned the agents are making the captured data accessible to random local databases and remote servers at random. “All of this is outside of Signal’s control once the data has been captured, and imperils the privacy of all the participants in a conversation,” the blog post said.
