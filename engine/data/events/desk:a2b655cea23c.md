Fortinet's PSIRT advisory FG-IR-24-029, published 8 February 2024, describes CVE-2024-23113 as a use of an externally controlled format string in the FortiOS fgfmd daemon that allows unauthenticated remote code execution. It lists FortiOS 7.4.0 through 7.4.2, 7.2.0 through 7.2.6 and 7.0.0 through 7.0.13 as affected, with fixes in 7.4.3, 7.2.7 and 7.0.14. The advisory timeline records potential exploitation in the wild on 11 October 2024 and recommends removing fgfm access from interfaces as a workaround.
