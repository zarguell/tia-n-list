Key Findings Antidetect browsers obscure the digital identity of their users, enabling a single operator to silently manage thousands of isolated accounts across multiple platforms (including Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube), while evading detection systems that look for scams and coordinated inauthentic behavior. Threat actors abuse these platforms, taking over accounts and using them for financial fraud, phishing and malware distribution. These accounts can also be used to coordinate influence operations, which becomes an even greater concern ahead of elections. Social platforms primarily detect coordinated activity at the account and behavioral layer. Antidetect browsers run below the surface enabling them to remain undetected. The three main antidetect browsers we discuss in this report are Kameleo, AdsPower, and MultiLogin. These tools already use AI integration, from built-in automation templates to offering a dedicated MCP server that pipes LLM-generated content directly into pre-warmed account profiles at machine speed.
