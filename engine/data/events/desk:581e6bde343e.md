AMD bulletin AMD-SN-7075 describes improper signature verification in the PSP boot ROM of Zen 1 and Zen+ processors and scores CVE-2026-74127 at CVSS 9.4 with a local SPI-flash access prerequisite. An attacker able to write platform firmware could bypass secure boot authentication and execute arbitrary unsigned code at full PSP privilege, with loss of confidentiality, boot-chain integrity and availability. AMD says it does not plan mitigations because the PSP boot ROM is immutable, credits Matteo Rizzo of Google for the report, and lists November 17, 2026 as the initial revision.
