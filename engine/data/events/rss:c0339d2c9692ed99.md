As AI agents move from experimental toys to tools that make autonomous decisions, interact with databases, and call external APIs, organizations need to be clear on who — or what — is executing these actions in their cloud environments. Why non-human identities (NHIs) aren’t secure enough for AI agents Security teams categorize identities into two buckets: human identities and non-human identities (NHIs), such as service accounts, secrets, and automated scripts. Treating AI agents as standard NHIs is a recipe for disaster. AI agents combine their non-deterministic nature with their ability to execute flexible, outcome-oriented actions, rather than following strict, prescribed instructions.
