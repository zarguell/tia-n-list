Advisory FG-IR-26-200, published November 10, 2026, covers CVE-2026-126148, a command injection [CWE-77] in FortiMail rated 9.8 that an unauthenticated attacker reaches through crafted high-availability join-request packets. FortiMail 8.0 and 7.6 are affected, with 8.0.0 through 8.0.1 fixed in 8.0.2 and 7.6.0 through 7.6.6 fixed in 7.6.7, while the 7.4 and 7.2 branches are listed as not affected. As with the sibling FortiMail advisories, FortiMail Cloud was already remediated on October 5, 2026.
