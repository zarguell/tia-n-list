Shattered.io published a detailed analysis on August 31, 2026 documenting the Clop group's exploitation of CVE-2026-12569 in PTC Windchill and FlexPLM, confirming 43 listed victims including GE, Philips, and Shell. The report states researchers at ReliaQuest confirmed active exploitation using a custom Java-based webshell placed inside /Windchill/login/ with hexadecimal filenames. It also notes Clop began exploiting the flaw as a zero-day by early June before the June 17 patch, and that SecurityWeek put the victim count at 43 with claimed data volumes from roughly 1 GB to multiple terabytes per organization.
