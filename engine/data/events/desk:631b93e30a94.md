SecureBlink reported on 17 November that the n0n extortion gang listed Visa and JPMorgan Chase on its leak site with a 48-hour countdown to publication. Visa said the activity it was investigating began at an unnamed third-party provider in Asia Pacific, that its own systems were not involved, and that it had found no sign payment card data was affected; JPMorgan said its ongoing outage was a software bug and did not link it to the listing. The report describes n0n as financially motivated, with financial services making up 23 percent of claimed victims, and says documented intrusions used weak or reused credentials rather than vulnerability exploitation, including credential stuffing against more than 300 FortiGate SSL-VPN appliances.
