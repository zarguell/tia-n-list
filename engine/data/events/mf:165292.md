The Call for Presentations is now open for SecurityWeek’s 2026 CISO Forum Virtual Summit, taking place online November 11-12, 2026. With thousands of registered attendees, the virtual summit brings together CISOs, senior cybersecurity executives, practitioners, industry experts, and other security leaders to share practical experience and examine the issues shaping enterprise cybersecurity strategy. SecurityWeek is seeking original presentations that move beyond high-level observations and offer actionable guidance, real-world lessons, and candid perspectives. Strong representation from end-user cybersecurity leaders is a priority. Suggested topic areas include, but are not limited to: Security leadership, governance, and enterprise risk Communicating cybersecurity value to boards and executives AI-driven attacks, defenses, governance, and automation Nation-state threats, ransomware, and geopolitical risk Supply-chain and third-party risk management Zero Trust, identity security, and cloud-native security Resilience engineering, business continuity, and crisis management Regulatory, legal, disclosure, and compliance challenges Cybersecurity workforce development and leadership succession Incident-response lessons and real-world breach case studies Industry-specific security challenges Speakers must be available to present virtually and should have access to a reliable internet connection, a high-quality camera, and a microphone.
