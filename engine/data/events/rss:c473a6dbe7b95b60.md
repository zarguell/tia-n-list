Authorities seized the domain of Virtualine , a bulletproof hosting provider that cybercriminals used to commit a large-scale sextortion scheme, ransomware, data theft, malware hosting and money laundering, the Justice Department said Tuesday. Virtualine facilitated prolific criminal activity, including the distribution of child sexual abuse material (CSAM), the Justice Department said. The Russian-speaking bulletproof hosting provider was among the most active this year in crypto-related crime in particular, researchers said. The site and its associated infrastructure stands out among the dozens of bulletproof hosting providers currently tracked by TRM Labs. “It was not among the largest by total inflows, but it received the most illicit-attributed value in 2026 to date,” the blockchain and cryptocrime research firm wrote in a blog post . Virtualine received almost one-fifth of all illicit-attributed inflow among bulletproof hosting providers tracked by TRM this year to date. A Russian drug marketplace accounted for nearly 44% of the direct payments Virtualine received from customers and attributed counterparties so far this year, according to TRM.
