OSTIF published expanded details on CVE-2026-48710, a host-header input-sanitization flaw in Starlette that bypasses authentication with a single character. OSTIF warned that slow uptake of patched Starlette versions and exposed live services were a serious concern, and that the bug hits a wide swath of Python LLM infrastructure including FastAPI, LiteLLM, vLLM, most OpenAI shim proxies, MCP servers, agent harnesses, and model-management UIs.
