First news coverage of the WorkOS breach reported that founder Michael Grinich attributed the intrusion to the ARTEX AI penetration testing suite, an open-source project by Chinese developers that later went private after use against South Korean banks. The report notes investigators found user-agent strings `artex-enrich/1.0` and `norma/0.4` in attacker web requests, and cites prior ARTEX-linked activity including a CrowdStrike-reported campaign against 200 organizations and an attack on the DIVD non-profit.
