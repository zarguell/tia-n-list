Sonatype's August 21, 2026 post says Broadcom published a large batch of Spring security advisories on August 20 and that Sonatype tracked 91 CVEs across Spring Framework and related projects. At publication, Sonatype Guide reported 148,608 affected software components, many of them non-Spring components bundling vulnerable Spring code. The post singles out CVE-2026-59285 in Spring for GraphQL as 9.2 Critical and CVE-2026-59318 in Spring AI as a Medium-severity tool-calling boundary issue. It recommends mapping affected versions across applications and repositories and moving them to fixed releases.
