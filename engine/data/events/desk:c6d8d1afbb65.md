Beget, one of Russia's largest domain registrars and hosting companies, said attackers accessed the systems used to manage its infrastructure and ran scripts designed to erase data after an overnight attack on November 2, 2026. The damage was severe in its cloud infrastructure, where data tied to virtual private servers, cloud storage, and Kubernetes services was wiped, and the company said it lost all binary backups of virtual servers while file-level backups stayed intact. Beget said restoring all services would take considerable time because more than 1,000 servers had to be manually inspected.
