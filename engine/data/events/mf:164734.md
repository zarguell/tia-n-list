Every year, tens of thousands of Chinese organizations spend weeks bracing for an attack that could come at any hour, from any direction, with no advance warning of when or how – standing watch around the clock to defend their networks. The exercise is known as HuWang Operation (护网行动, hù wǎng , literally “protect the network”), or simply HW. Organized by the Ministry of Public Security (MPS), HW has evolved from a limited pilot covering “some important industries” into an annual nationwide stress test. 1 The 2026 edition, which ran through July and August, marked its tenth anniversary. Its scope and priorities have shifted alongside changes in China’s cybersecurity policies and legal framework, with AI assuming an increasingly prominent role in recent editions. 2 HW Operation logo. Most importantly, HW has become a significant driver of demand for China’s domestic cybersecurity industry, helping push the sector from compliance-driven spending toward attack-defense capabilities, and an increasingly central mechanism through which China cultivates and evaluates offensive and defensive cyber talent at scale.
