SecurityOnline reports that Dell SONiC 4.6.1 fixes 13 proprietary-code vulnerabilities in Enterprise SONiC Distribution. It identifies CVE-2026-103782 as a CVSS 9.9 argument injection flaw and says two critical issues in the set allow arbitrary command execution on affected switches. The article was published at 01:00:41 UTC on 25 November 2026, about six hours before the story was first seen.
