Early news coverage of the iter.ca report and the vendor advisory. It describes the four-stage attack, from internal extension identifier leakage through a hidden picker frame, message channel control, newline filter injection and a captured user click, and lists uBlock Origin and uBO Lite on Manifest V2 Firefox and Manifest V3 as affected. The article reports the fix as a secret password passed from the content script to the picker that rejects messages from outside pages.
