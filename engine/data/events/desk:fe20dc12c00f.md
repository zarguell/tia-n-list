Cisco's advisory for CVE-2026-20079 (CVSS 10.0, CWE-288, no workarounds) was first published March 4, 2026 as part of the March 2026 Secure Firewall bundled publication and credits Brandon Sakai of Cisco internal security testing. A July 31, 2026 revision added bug ID CSCwt95974, indicators of compromise, and hotfixes, and revision 2.5 states the Cisco PSIRT became aware of active exploitation in August 2026. First fixed releases are 7.0.10, 7.2.12, 7.4.8, 7.6.6, 7.7.13, 10.0.2, and 10.1.0, with Snort rules 66075-66080 referenced for detection.
