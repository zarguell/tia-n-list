CIM da Regiao de Leiria suffers ransomware attack but recovers data after isolating server. The Comunidade Intermunicipal (CIM) da Regiao de Leiria was the target of a cyberattack during the early hours of Tuesday. According to Paulo Santos, executive secretary of the entity, speaking to Lusa news agency, the situation involved a ransomware virus targeting the email server, but the rapid response prevented greater damage to the region's technology infrastructure.

Timely identification of the problem allowed the technical team to immediately isolate the threat. According to Santos, the exact moment of infection was detected overnight, which ensured the opportunity to recover all data up to that exact time, without loss of essential information. Currently, only one server remains disconnected from the network as a precaution. Porto de Mos is the only one of the 10 municipalities covered that continues without email access as a safety measure. The CIM used the incident to accelerate migration of the service to the Office 365 platform, a process already planned for Friday that was brought forward.

This positive outcome is attributed to the strengthening of the region's cybersecurity defenses. The executive secretary emphasized that the CIM's cybersecurity system and triple-redundancy backups functioned fully. This infrastructure resulted from an investment of over three million euros over the last two years, serving the municipalities of Alvaiazere, Ansiao, Batalha, Castanheira de Pera, Figueirao dos Vinhos, Leiria, Marinha Grande, Pedrogao Grande, Pombal, and Porto de Mos. The attack, which appears criminal in nature, has been reported to the competent authorities.
