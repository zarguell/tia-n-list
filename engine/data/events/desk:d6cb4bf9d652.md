Cisco disclosed CVE-2026-76418, a CVSS 8.6 high-severity flaw in the Remote Access SSL VPN service of ASA and FTD software caused by logic errors when secondary authentication is configured on a tunnel group. An unauthenticated remote attacker can send a crafted request when connecting to the SSL remote access VPN to trigger an unexpected device reload and denial of service. Cisco PSIRT became aware of active exploitation in October 2026 and released fixed software; no workarounds exist, though disabling secondary authentication on affected tunnel groups mitigates the issue. Fixed releases include ASA 9.16.4.104, 9.18.4.96, 9.20.4.50, 9.22.3.200, 9.23.1.48, 9.24.1.27 and 9.24.10.1, and FTD 7.0.11, 7.2.13, 7.4.9, 7.6.7, 7.7.14, 10.0.3 and 10.1.1.
