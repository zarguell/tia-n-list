Italy’s data protection authority has fined Meta Platforms Ireland Limited €805 million after finding that Facebook accessed photos, videos, and associated metadata on Android smartphones without specific, informed consent. The decision, announced on November 14, 2026, concerns approximately 20 million Android users in Italy and requires changes to Facebook’s gallery suggestion feature.
