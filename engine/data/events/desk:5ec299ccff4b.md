Kaspersky reported in August 2026 a multistage Android downloader distributed through built-in updaters in car head unit firmware, attributing it with high confidence to the MoYu Group linked to BADBOX. The chain delivers a JarService dropper, a loader and stage three modules for click fraud and a reverse proxy named zhima; Kaspersky found eight zhima variants, the earliest numbered 57. The attribution rests on the AdmoyuService name and infrastructure overlap independently identified by Nokia Deepfield, including the zhima C2 address 128.14.210[.]58. Detection names included HEUR:Trojan-Proxy.AndroidOS.Zhima.* and HEUR:Trojan.AndroidOS.Vo1d.*.
