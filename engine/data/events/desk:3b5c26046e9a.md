Mandiant and Google Threat Intelligence Group attribute a PeopleSoft compromise and extortion campaign to UNC6240 (ShinyHunters), observed May 27 through June 9, 2026 and consistent with zero-day exploitation of CVE-2026-35273 before Oracle's June 10 advisory. The actor staged MeshCentral agents masquerading as Azure services with C2 at wss://azurenetfiles.net:443/agent.ashx, propagated a fanout.sh lateral movement script over SSH, and published stolen data on the ShinyHunters leak site on June 9. Mandiant notified over 100 organizations whose IP addresses correlated with potentially vulnerable endpoints, 68 percent of them higher education institutions.
