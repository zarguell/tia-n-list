The Hacker News reported on 10 November that CISA added four flaws to KEV citing active exploitation, including CVE-2026-123218 in IBM Aspera Faspex. It describes the 9.8 flaw as incorrect authorization that lets an authenticated user craft a message to escalate privileges and notes it was fixed in November 2026. The other additions were OpenSSH CVE-2026-123158, SignalWire FreeSwitch CVE-2026-116779 and GPUStack CVE-2026-102895.
