The UK’s Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) took a wide range of its online services offline on November 27, 2026, after identifying what it calls a cyber incident affecting its systems . Claimants have since been unable to sign in to Universal Credit accounts, apply for Personal Independence Payment (PIP) or the State Pension online, or use the Tell Us Once service to report a death. The DWP cyber incident has not touched scheduled payments. The department says regular benefit and pension payments are not affected and will be paid as normal . The DWP is investigating with the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) and the Government Cyber Coordination Centre (GC3), and it has not said what kind of incident it is dealing with, whether personal data was accessed or when services will return. DWP Online Services Hit by the Outage The department has not published a full list of affected DWP online services. Based on its statement and live reporting, these are the services claimants could not use: Service What People Use It For Status (November 27, 2026) Universal Credit online account Reporting changes in circumstances, checking payment dates, messaging caseworkers, requesting an advance on a first pay
