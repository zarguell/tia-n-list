GitGuardian summarized Microsoft's ARTEX reporting and argued the exposed-secret problem predates the tool: its State of Secrets Sprawl 2026 counted 28.65 million new hardcoded secrets in 2025 and found 64% of valid secrets leaked in 2022 still active in 2026. The post described two unauthenticated checks, GET /api/health and GET /api/auth/status, that identify an ARTEX console and whether first-run setup is complete. It cited roughly 1,400 exposed instances from Censys as of 4 November and more than 1,800 from Check Point on 5 November, noting the two counts use different methods.
