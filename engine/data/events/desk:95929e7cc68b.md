DZONERZY published MASKerade on 2 August 2026, describing a pre-authentication heap overflow in the built-in WebSocket backend added in Mosquitto 2.1 and enabled by default in CMake builds. The write-up traces a masked zero-length WebSocket frame to net__read_ws() returning four masking-key bytes it never copied into the caller's buffer, underflowing the MQTT packet reader's to_process counter, and chains that primitive to an interactive shell on the broker. The research predates the public GitHub issue and the CVE assignment by more than a week.
