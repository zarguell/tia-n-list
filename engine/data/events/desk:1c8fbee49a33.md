CISA added CVE-2026-20079, a Cisco Firewall Management Center authentication bypass using an alternate path or channel, to the KEV Catalog based on evidence of active exploitation. The vulnerability had originally been patched in Cisco's March 2026 bundled advisory for Secure Firewall ASA, FMC, and FTD software. Federal agencies were required to remediate by the BOD 26-04 deadline.
