A previously unknown threat actor tracked as UTA0576 chained three Ivanti Connect Secure zero-day vulnerabilities to run code as root on VPN appliances without logging in. The attacks began in early October 2026 and hit at least one appliance that was fully patched at the time. Ivanti says exploitation has affected a limited number of customers. The fix arrived in two stages. Ivanti patched the authentication bypass, CVE-2026-104935 , on October 13, then shipped fixes for the remaining two flaws, CVE-2026-111357 and CVE-2026-118527 , on October 29. CISA has added all three vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, and the federal deadline for the privilege escalation bug falls today, November 2 . How the Three Flaws Combine Into Unauthenticated Root Researchers uncovered the chain while investigating suspicious activity at a customer network on October 5 and traced it to an Ivanti Connect Secure (ICS) appliance running 22.8R2.4 .
