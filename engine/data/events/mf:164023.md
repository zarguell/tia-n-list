Multi-cloud stopped being a strategy debate and became a fact on the ground acquisitions, data-gravity, and best-of-breed services put most organizations in two clouds and many in three. Security’s multicloud problem is parity: each provider’s native tools excel at home and stop at the border, leaving teams to normalize three consoles, three IAM models, and three detection stacks. Normalizing these heterogeneous controls requires adopting a unified Cloud Security Posture Management framework to automatically validate policies and reduce cross-cloud misconfigurations. Multi-cloud security platforms exist to be the one policy plane above them.
