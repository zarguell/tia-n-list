Check Point Research published the original disclosure of the Yoba campaign on 27 October 2026. Attackers created fake GitHub repositories impersonating ChatGPT and Claude desktop apps and used legitimate signed OpenAI and Anthropic executables to sideload a malicious DLL. The final payload is XMRig 6.26.0, which connects to a TLS-enabled Tor .onion mining endpoint and pauses mining when user activity is detected.
