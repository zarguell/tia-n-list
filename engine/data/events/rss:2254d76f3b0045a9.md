CrowdStrike on Tuesday announced agreements to acquire AI agent observability firm Fiddler AI and AI security testing company Dreadnode, in deals reportedly worth a combined $495 million. CrowdStrike has not disclosed the financial terms of either acquisition. According to The Wall Street Journal , which reported the deals ahead of the official announcements, the cybersecurity giant is paying roughly $400 million for Fiddler and about $95 million for Dreadnode . CrowdStrike said both purchases will be paid mostly in cash, with a portion in stock subject to vesting conditions. The deals are expected to close in the fourth quarter of CrowdStrike’s fiscal year 2027, which ends on January 31, 2027. Fiddler was founded in 2018 and is based in Palo Alto, California. In January, it announced a $30 million Series C round that brought its total funding to $100 million. The company’s technology monitors AI agents and explains the decisions they make. CrowdStrike says Fiddler will add pre-deployment evaluation of agents, tracing of their reasoning and tool calls, and detection of behavioral drift and hallucinations in production. It will also log agent activity for compliance audits and track token usage and spending. CrowdStrike plans to sell the technology as Falcon Observe.
