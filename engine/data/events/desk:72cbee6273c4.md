The oss-security mailing list carried the signed Xen advisory text on 2026-11-03 at 12:00:20 UTC, mirroring XSA-523 v2 and its CVE assignments. It restates the impact as arbitrary memory read and write across domains including Dom0, the affected range of Xen 4.6 and later, and the absence of any mitigation. The message also lists the patch filenames and SHA-256 checksums for the two patch sets.
