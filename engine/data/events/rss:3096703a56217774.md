OWASP has issued a warning that AI agents and chatbots are reducing the skills required for certain aspects of cyberattacks, particularly in areas like target discovery and vulnerability research. Their latest research indicates that beginners can advance by utilizing AI guidance; however, human expertise remains essential when tools fail or when dealing with complex attacks.
