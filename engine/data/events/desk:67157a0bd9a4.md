Published 12 August 2026 and updated 13 August, this high-severity CWE-287 advisory describes Artifactory returning an internal anonymous-user token to an unauthenticated caller when anonymous access is disabled. Affected branches are below 7.111.20 plus 7.117.0 to 7.117.27, 7.125.0 to 7.125.19, 7.133.0 to 7.133.28, and 7.146.0 to 7.146.8, with those latter versions as the fixes. The CVE reached the KEV catalog one month later.
