Threat actors are actively exploiting CVE-2025-25249, a critical heap-based buffer overflow in FortiOS and FortiSwitchManager’ s cw_acd daemon, to compromise exposed FortiGate firewalls and deploy the PivotC2 remote access trojan (RAT). SOCRadar Threat Research Unit (STRU) assessed with high confidence that exploitation began no later than July 2026 and remains active. The campaign reportedly targeted more than 30,000 FortiGate IP addresses and resulted in 178 confirmed PivotC2 infections, with the United States accounting for the largest concentration of affected systems. Critical FortiGate Flaw CVE-2025-25249 carries a CVSS v3 score of 9.8 and enables unauthenticated remote code execution through specially crafted CAPWAP traffic sent to UDP port 5246. Fortinet appliances use CAPWAP to centrally manage wireless access points. The vulnerable cw_acd process is present in affected FortiOS releases, including versions 7.6.0 through 7.6.3, 7.4.0 through 7.4.8, 7.2.0 through 7.2.11, 7.0.0 through 7.0.17, and all FortiOS 6.4 versions. FortiSwitchManager versions 7.2.0 through 7.2.6 and 7.0.0 through 7.0.5 are also affected. Attackers use a self-extracting Linux exploit binary, fortirun.bin , with Bash and Python automation scripts. The tool fingerprints a target’s firmware and hardware configuration, abuses CAPWAP discovery responses to defeat ASLR protections , grooms the daemon heap, and corrupts linked-list pointers to gain an arbitrary-write primitive.
