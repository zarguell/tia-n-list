Newsweek reported that n0n published around 16,000 customer email addresses, phone numbers and JPMorgan Chase account IDs, apparently tied to the invitation-only Reserve card program for clients with at least $10 million in assets under management. The bank said vendors were targeted and that attackers reached marketing data that may include customer names and email addresses, with no service disruption and no connection to the earlier outage. The article also cited Cybernews on more than 6,000 affected Visa premium cardholders and a 502-kilobyte leaked file whose completeness researchers could not establish.
