Bimbo Bakeries USA (BBU) has revealed that attackers gained access to files containing names and Social Security numbers by exploiting a zero-day vulnerability in Oracle E-Business Suite (EBS), which a third-party vendor uses. The company confirmed the unauthorized access in December 2025 but did not identify the existence of Social Security numbers until August 2026.
