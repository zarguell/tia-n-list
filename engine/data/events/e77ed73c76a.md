Cyble Research & Intelligence Labs (CRIL) documented a campaign from INJ3CTOR3 deploying the previously unknown JOMANGY PHP webshell family against FreePBX VoIP infrastructure. The campaign exhibits a six-layer persistence architecture designed to self-heal within minutes from any single surviving channel — including recurring cron polling (every 1–3 minutes), shell profile stagers, and aggressive competitor eviction (scrubbing 50+ rival webshell signatures, blocking 11 rival C2 IPs). An inventory of 3,080 C2 IP addresses was observed, with ~39% on Alibaba Cloud infrastructure. The primary candidates for initial access are CVE-2025-64328 (post-auth command injection) and CVE-2025-57819 (pre-auth SQL injection).
