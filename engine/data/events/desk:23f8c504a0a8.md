DreamHost's status page shows operations staff first reported connectivity issues affecting the File Manager interface at 2:20 p.m. PDT on October 23, 2026, with File Manager offline and some webmail users signed out by 5:20 p.m. PDT. As a precaution the company reset passwords for some email accounts and, by 10:13 p.m. PDT, extended the reset to FTP and SFTP users. The password reset for affected users was reported complete on October 24 at 10:37 a.m. PDT, with instruction emails sent in batches through the weekend.
