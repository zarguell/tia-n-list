SolarWinds' own advisory for CVE-2026-28324 was first published September 22, 2026, two days before the press coverage captured in the story. It describes an unauthenticated RCE from insufficient integrity checks affecting Observability Self-Hosted 2026.2.2 and below, fixed in 2026.2.3, with CVSS 3.1 score 9.8 and a network attack vector (AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H). Kai Huang from Armadin is credited with the report.
