Handelsblatt reported SAP's own confirmation on the evening of 26 November 2026, with the company stating it had identified suspicious activity and taken the necessary measures to protect customers and secure services. Per Handelsblatt information, SAP restricted access to GitHub, halted automated workflows in software development and replaced signing keys. The article documents that the incident was aimed at software development, following Der Spiegel's earlier report of a hacking attack, and that SAP said its investigation had so far found no evidence of unauthorised access to customer business data.
