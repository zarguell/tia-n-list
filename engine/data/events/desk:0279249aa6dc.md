Original researcher disclosure posted by Hyunwoo Kim to oss-security once the embargo agreed with the linux-distros list expired. It documents two trigger flows, a request_sock reuse ABA race that produces a remote panic and a full child socket ABA race that Kim says can trigger a remote use-after-free but is generally hard to trigger without special techniques. It records the affected range as introduced 079096f103fa (2015-10-03) and fixed 4e2b3ef19e0e (2026-11-03), timings of about 5 seconds to 2 minutes against default nginx, Apache and SSH on QEMU/KVM guests versus about 14 hours against a quiet 8-queue bare-metal nginx host, and mitigations of net.ipv4.tcp_syncookies=0 per network namespace or serialization to a single RX queue with RPS and RFS disabled.
