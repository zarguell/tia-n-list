Advisory FG-IR-26-202, published November 10, 2026, covers CVE-2026-126150, a missing authentication for critical function [CWE-306] in FortiMail rated 9.1 that lets an unauthenticated attacker reset the password of an arbitrary IBE webmail user and take over the account with crafted HTTP requests. FortiMail 8.0.0 moves to 8.0.1, 7.6.0 through 7.6.5 moves to 7.6.6, 7.4.0 through 7.4.7 moves to 7.4.8, and 7.2.0 through 7.2.9 moves to the then-upcoming 7.2.10. The advisory notes FortiMail Cloud was remediated on October 5, 2026.
