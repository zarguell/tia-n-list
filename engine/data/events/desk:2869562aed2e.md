Vendor bulletin with a changelog entry of October 8, 2026 for initial publication, covering CVE-2026-107406: a memory overflow (CWE-119) in NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway that can lead to remote code execution or denial of service when the appliance is configured as a SAML service provider or SAML identity provider, CVSS v4.0 base score 9.5 with vector AV:N/AC:H/AT:N/PR:N/UI:N. Appliances already fixed for the earlier SAML bulletin (14.1-73.37 through 14.1-73.41 inclusive, 14.1-FIPS 73.37 through 73.41 FIPS, 13.1-64.23 through 13.1-64.28, 13.1-NDcPP 13.1-37.279 through 37.282) are affected only when configured as a SAML IdP, while older builds are affected as SP or IdP. Remediation is 14.1-73.46, 13.1-64.29, 14.1-73.46 FIPS, and 13.1-FIPS or 13.1-NDcPP 13.1.37.283 and later; the bulletin lists no workaround, notes Secure Private Access Hybrid deployments are also affected, and credits the XOR Team at JPMorgan Chase.
