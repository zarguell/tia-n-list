The original Dirty Frag disclosure, first made public on 2026-05-07 per the document itself, was written by Hyunwoo Kim (@v4bel) and chains the xfrm-ESP Page-Cache Write (CVE-2026-43284) with the RxRPC Page-Cache Write (CVE-2026-43500) to get root on major distributions without any race condition. CVE-2026-43284 affects kernels from commit cac2661c53f3 (2017-01-17) up to fix f4c50a4034e6 (2026-05-05), roughly nine years of vulnerable code, and was tested on Ubuntu 24.04, RHEL 10.1, openSUSE Tumbleweed, CentOS Stream 10, AlmaLinux 10, and Fedora 44. The stated mitigation is rmmod esp4 esp6 rxrpc plus modprobe blacklist entries.
