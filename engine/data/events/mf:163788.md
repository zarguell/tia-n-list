A SOC team of just five professionals protected their company’s infrastructure from a multi-stage email threat using in-depth browser visibility and full-scale attack detonation. The company is a leading German manufacturer operating a large computer network with 10,000 endpoints. Security Lead Philipp Z. shared insights from a real-world case his team unraveled with ANY.RUN . What the Initial Alert Looked Like The security team’s XDR system flagged a suspicious shortcut (.lnk) file being opened. As per protocol, the analyst who detected it isolated the computer on which it was executed. This swift action helped prevent the threat from spreading across the network. That’s how the incident started. To investigate it, the analysts used the computer’s history logs. They traced the file back to a web download and then to its source an incoming email. The Entire Attack Chain Revealed in One Sandbox Analysis To investigate the threat further, the analyst detonated the suspicious email in ANY.RUN’s Interactive Sandbox . The analysis provided immediate visibility into its hidden malicious attributes, redirects, and decryption process. The following attack chain was revealed: The Vector: The spear-phishing email contained a benign-looking PDF attachment. The Redirect: Inside the PDF was a malicious link directing the user to download an external archive. The Stealth Layer: The external archive was an encrypted, password-protected ZIP file.
