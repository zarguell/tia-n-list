nFlo's 16 November 2026 alert covers three Station Launcher App advisories: the CVSS 10.0 deserialization bug CVE-2026-93664 and two CVSS 9.9 OS command injection bugs, CVE-2026-19732 and CVE-2026-77033, across R2023x through R2026x. It records CISA KEV as No and ransomware as not confirmed, and says public advisories omit fix numbers while Dassault support document QA00000522688 holds remediation details. The alert recommends restricting network access to trusted networks and users, reviewing 3DEXPERIENCE server logs for unusual processes and commands started by the platform's service accounts, and confirming SaaS patching with the vendor or partner.
