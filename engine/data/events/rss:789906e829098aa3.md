Attackers are exploiting CVE-2026-132165, a critical Drupal core flaw that lets an unauthenticated visitor push a live site back into its installer, take it over and, under the right conditions, run arbitrary PHP code. The Drupal Security Team has received a report that the flaw was exploited on live sites as early as November 19, 2026, one day after the patch shipped . CISA added the flaw, now widely known as Fail2Install, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 24 . Detailed public research showing how to reach the bug on stock deployments appeared the same day, so any unpatched Drupal site should be treated as exposed. What Is CVE-2026-132165? CVE-2026-132165 is a remote code execution (RCE) vulnerability in the Drupal core installer, the install.php script used to set up a new site. Drupal’s security advisory rates it “ Highly critical ,” with a risk score of 20 out of 25, and the flaw carries a CVSS score of 9.8 . No account is needed. Drupal lists the required access as none, meaning an anonymous visitor can attempt the attack, although it rates the attack itself as complex.
