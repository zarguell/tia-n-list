Dream Research reports discovering an attack campaign that successfully compromised 25 firewall servers, including three in a U.S. government-affiliated environment. Attackers chained PHP2Shell with a Linux kernel privilege-escalation vulnerability. Palo Alto Networks separately confirmed active PHP2Shell exploitation targeting Internet-exposed management interfaces. This is the production-system evidence we've been looking for. It goes beyond earlier successful exploitation of deliberately vulnerable honeypots. Evidence distinction: Dream's 25-compromise finding is based on recovered attacker artifacts. Palo Alto independently confirms exploitation activity against its management interfaces, but the two companies have not publicly established that their findings concern the same campaign.
