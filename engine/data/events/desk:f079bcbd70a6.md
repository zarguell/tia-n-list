Cisco's June 25, 2025 advisory covers CVE-2025-20281 and CVE-2025-20282 in ISE and ISE-PIC, both rated CVSS 10.0. CVE-2025-20281 stems from insufficient validation of user-supplied input in a specific API and allows OS-level command execution; CVE-2025-20282 lets crafted files be uploaded into privileged directories through another API. Both were fixed in ISE 3.3 Patch 7 and 3.4 Patch 2 and were reported through Trend Micro's Zero Day Initiative.
