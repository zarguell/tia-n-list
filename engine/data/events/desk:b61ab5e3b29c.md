Advisory FG-IR-26-273, published November 10, 2026, covers CVE-2026-126259, a missing authentication for critical function [CWE-306] in FortiOS rated 9.0 that lets an unauthenticated attacker running against a cluster configured with HA FGSP and standalone-config-sync, or with autoscale, execute commands as super admin through crafted HA packets. The flaw sits in the internal cluster protocol rather than the management or SSL-VPN surface.
