Spyware is malicious software that secretly installs itself on a phone or computer, collects information about you, such as passwords, messages, browsing activity, and location, and sends it to someone else without your consent. It can log keystrokes, capture screens, and switch on a camera or microphone, and it is designed to stay invisible. This guide covers what spyware is, how it spreads, the main types, real examples, how to detect and remove it, whether it is legal, and where the stolen data ends up. What Is Spyware? Spyware is software that monitors a person’s activity, collects their data without informed consent, and reports it to a third party. Secrecy is what separates it from ordinary software that gathers analytics and tells you. Spyware Definition in Simple Words Spyware is a hidden program that watches what you do on a device and passes the details to someone else. That someone might be a cybercriminal after your bank login, an advertiser building a profile, a jealous partner, or a government buyer of surveillance tools.
