Iris Plaza, the Iris Ohyama group's official online store, published its first notice on October 29, 2026 after confirming unauthorized access to an external cloud service it used. The notice stated the EC site and related systems were suspended on October 28 when a suspicious file was found, and that the incident was reported to police and Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission the same day. At that point the possibility of personal information leakage was still under investigation.
