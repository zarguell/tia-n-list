The GitHub Advisory Database published CVE-2025-2746 on March 24, 2025, describing an authentication bypass in Kentico Xperience through version 13.0.172 via the Staging Sync Server's handling of empty SHA1 usernames in digest authentication. The entry references watchTowr Labs research, NVD, and CISA's KEV catalog, and notes a CVSS 3.1 score of 9.8 with CWE-288 and CWE-287 classifications.
