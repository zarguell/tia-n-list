The Verge covers Eye Security's finding that the updater for the Time4Popcorn fork of Popcorn Time remained installed on millions of Windows, macOS, and Android devices, phoning home to abandoned domains over unencrypted HTTP. Researchers registered one domain and pushed an update that disabled the updater, sending over two million update downloads by March before transferring domains to the Shadowserver Foundation. The app also runs an outdated Chrome engine with the sandbox disabled.
