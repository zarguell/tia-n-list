Peter Molnar and Krisztian Hozak of mptrdev.com describe how a throwaway Docker Hub account converted to an organization with a target named as owner yields a token that authenticates as that target, which they used to push to the target's namespace and pull the target's private repositories with no notification to the victim. First reproduction on 2026-09-28, a second independent reproduction on 2026-09-29, report to security@docker.com on 2026-09-30. They also flag, unconfirmed, a worst case in which the official library images sit in the same conversion path. The page carries no publish time of day; its footer states updated 2026-10-13.
