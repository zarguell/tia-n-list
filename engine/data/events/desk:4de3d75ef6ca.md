Cyber Security News reported on 13 November 2024 that PoC exploitation attempts for CVE-2024-8068 and CVE-2024-8069 were observed by Shadowserver at around 16:00 UTC on 12 November 2024, shortly after the WatchTowr disclosure. The article confirms the vulnerabilities impact the Session Recording component of Citrix Virtual Apps and Desktops, notes the BinaryFormatter risk cited by Microsoft, references the GitHub PoC repository, and urges organizations to apply Citrix hotfixes immediately.
