A critical security flaw in the WooCommerce Wholesale Lead Capture plugin is being actively exploited, allowing remote attackers to upload malicious files and potentially take full control of vulnerable WordPress sites. The vulnerability, tracked as CVE-2026-27540, affects WooCommerce Wholesale Lead Capture versions 2.0.3.1 and earlier. The premium plugin, used by an estimated 6,000 websites, helps store owners build wholesale-registration forms and supports file uploads as part of the registration process. Security researchers publicly disclosed the issue on February 20, 2026. It received a CVSS severity score of 9.8 out of 10, placing it in the critical category. The vendor has addressed the flaw in version 2.0.3.2, and users are strongly advised to update immediately. According to Wordfence, its firewall has already blocked more than 100,000 exploitation attempts. Attackers have reportedly targeted the weakness for months, with heightened activity observed between June 4 and June 17, as well as on July 1 and August 30. WooCommerce Flaw Enables Site Takeover The issue is an unauthenticated arbitrary file-upload vulnerability. This means an attacker does not need a WordPress account or administrator access to attempt exploitation. WooCommerce Wholesale Lead Capture includes an AJAX upload handler called wwlc_file_upload_handler .
