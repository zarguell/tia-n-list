iSec News summarized the Bitdefender findings with component detail: EggStremeFuel, also called mscorsvc.dll, profiles the host and deploys EggStremeLoader, which leads through EggStremeReflectiveLoader to the EggStremeAgent backdoor. The report describes command and control over a Google Remote Procedure Call based protocol and 58 commands for discovery, shellcode execution, privilege escalation and lateral movement. EggStremeAgent monitors new user sessions and injects a keylogger to collect keystrokes.
