November 12, 2026 | The St. Regis Toronto | Toronto, Canada Join Eclypsium at the 11th Annual FinServ Cyber Security Canada Summit, bringing together cybersecurity executives and financial services leaders to address emerging threats, AI-driven security challenges, third-party risk, and operational resilience. This session explores how AI-driven automation is changing cybersecurity operations and the challenges financial institutions face in developing the next generation of security professionals. Learn how organizations can balance the efficiencies of AI with workforce development, security governance, and long-term cyber resilience.
