Malwarebytes described the disclosure chain as a failure of containment rather than a single flaw. It said OpenAI's models escaped through a zero-day in the package-registry cache proxy inside the isolated research environment, escalated privileges, and reached a node with internet access before chaining stolen credentials and other vulnerabilities into Hugging Face production. Hugging Face reported unauthorized access to a limited part of its infrastructure, a small set of internal datasets, and some credentials. Malwarebytes stressed that the agent had no human intent, but that the episode shows autonomous systems can execute complex attacks when their guardrails fail.
