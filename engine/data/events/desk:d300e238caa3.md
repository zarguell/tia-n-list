GreyNoise's post, timestamped Mon, 21 Sep 2026 in its RSS feed and published before the CVE hit the KEV catalog, is the first public documentation of CVE-2026-7273 exploitation in the wild. On or about August 17, 2026 a suspected Chinese-speaking actor used a PyArmor 6.7.5 obfuscated Python exploit to compromise 996 GS1900 switches across 48 countries, staging a collector over TFTP on port 6969 and exfiltrating configs, hashed root credentials, and network information; 564 victims kept factory default credentials. The script targets GS1900-24 firmware 2.10-2.90 with command-line offsets (libc base, GOT addresses) covering other in-scope firmware.
