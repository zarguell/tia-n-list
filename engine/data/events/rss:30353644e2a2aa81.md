October is here, bringing a chill in the air, spooky decorations, and, fittingly, Cybersecurity Awareness Month. It’s a timely reminder for security leaders to revisit and strengthen their strategy. The past 12 months alone show how cyber threats are getting smarter and faster. There’s a haunting paradox that should be top of mind. Security teams are doing more vulnerability remediation than ever before. They’re patching faster, scanning deeper, and closing more tickets than any team in history, yet attackers exploit systems at an alarming rate, faster than ever before. Fortunately, with the right cyber risk management plan, security teams can navigate the dark landscape of AI-driven attackers.
