Zero Day Initiative published the coordinated advisory for CVE-2026-8037 on June 9, 2026, crediting Syed Ibrahim Ahmed of TrendAI Research. Its disclosure timeline records the vulnerability was reported to the vendor on April 15, 2026, roughly seven weeks before the coordinated public release. The advisory frames the flaw as an uninitialized memory condition in the LoadMaster API's apiuser handling that leads to remote code execution.
