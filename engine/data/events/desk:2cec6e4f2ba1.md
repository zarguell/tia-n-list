CNN reported that the FBI was investigating an apparent breach of the National Crime Information Center, citing two people briefed on the investigation. The intrusion, discovered in recent days, was described as the second such breach in recent months. NCIC is used nationwide to share warrant and background-check information. A former FBI official quoted in the report said the main concern was not only access to sensitive information but whether hackers could compromise the accuracy of the information officers rely on. The report also recalled the September ShinyHunters attack on an FBI jobs website and a 2023 breach of a New York field office system.
