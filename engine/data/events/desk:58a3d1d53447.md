TechRadar, syndicated by Yahoo, reported that n0n had added Visa and JPMorgan Chase to its leak site and threatened to publish data unless a ransom was paid within 48 hours. At the time of writing the group had not posted a sample or described the files or how it got in. Visa confirmed an incident at a third-party provider and said its own systems were unaffected and no payment card data was involved, while JPMorgan attributed the week's service disruptions to a software bug and denied a cyber-related cause.
