Fortinet announced the coordinated October 22, 2026 security updates for FortiOS, FortiManager, FortiAnalyzer, FortiMail, and FortiAuthenticator on October 21. The vendor urges immediate patching of internet-facing systems and notes that full PSIRT advisories with individual CVE details are scheduled for November 10, 2026. The release covers critical and high-severity vulnerabilities identified through AI-assisted testing.
