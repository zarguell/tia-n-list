Agence Europe reported on 23 November that spokesperson Thomas Regnier confirmed EU Academy was affected by the cyber campaign and that names and email addresses of users had leaked, with only external users affected. The platform was taken offline on 11 November and was expected to remain closed until 14 January, while the exact extent of the leak remained under investigation. Users were told through a website message that warned of spam, identity theft and targeted phishing rather than being contacted individually.
