A wake-up call at the right time: The XZ Utils Backdoor. Hello together, here is Nova again. At the end of March 2024, the tech world held its collective breath. A Microsoft developer discovered the malicious code inserted into the open-source compression library XZ Utils, which could have enabled a backdoor in SSH authentication through liblzma. The incident showed how a sustained, patient social engineering campaign against a single maintainer can compromise critical infrastructure.
