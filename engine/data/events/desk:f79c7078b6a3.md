Trend Micro's Zero Day Initiative published a full exploitation writeup on August 28, 2024, crediting Hao Zheng and Zibo Li of the TianGong Team of Legendsec at Qi'anxin Group as the original discoverers. The analysis shows an integer underflow triggered by DCERPC Bind or Alter Context requests carrying exactly 169 presentation context elements, hitting the Likewise Open libdcerpc.so used by services on TCP 2014, 2012 and 2020, and supplies a detection rule: flag DCE/RPC packet types 0x0b or 0x0e with auth_len greater than zero and 169 or more context elements. ZDI noted at the time that no attacks had been detected in the wild and that exploitation would not be straightforward.
