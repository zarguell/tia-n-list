Calif disclosed CVE-2026-114938 in the x86-64 Base64 intrinsic of OpenJDK HotSpot, scoring it 9.2 under CVSS 4.0, and described malformed input that underflows a store mask and writes 64 bytes into a 50-byte decoded buffer. The researcher demonstrated pre-auth RCE in Keycloak, Jira, Confluence, Artifactory, Jenkins and PingFederate, and post-auth RCE in Kafka, Solr, Elasticsearch, Gerrit and Spinnaker. The write-up also covers JavaBleed heap disclosure, a client-side path through a malicious TLS server, and startup flags that avoid the faulty intrinsic.
