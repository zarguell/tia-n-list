The Daily Byte reported on 9 November 2026 that a bug in Mosquitto's built-in WebSocket listener allowed unauthenticated remote code execution, citing born0monday's research as its source. It noted the fault was first reported publicly as GitHub issue 3704 on 10 August and judged to have no memory-safety impact, and that Eclipse closed the researcher's report as a duplicate on 28 October. The article said no CVE had been assigned at that point, fixed upstream releases were expected before 18 November, and Home Assistant add-on 7.1.2, released 19 October, already contained a fix.
