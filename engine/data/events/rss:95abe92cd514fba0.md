What Is NIS2? NIS2 is Directive (EU) 2022/2555, the EU’s baseline cybersecurity law for organizations that keep energy grids, water systems, hospitals, logistics chains, food supply and digital infrastructure running. It replaced the original NIS Directive (EU) 2016/1148 and turned a loosely applied, sector-by-sector regime into a single framework with common obligations, common reporting timelines and real enforcement powers. So, what is NIS2 in practical terms for a security team? Three things. First, it defines who is in scope through size thresholds and two sector annexes, splitting covered organizations into essential and important entities. Second, it prescribes a minimum set of risk-management controls in Article 21, from incident handling and logging to supply chain security and cryptography. Third, it imposes a staged incident notification process in Article 23, with the first signal due to the national CSIRT or competent authority within 24 hours of becoming aware of a significant incident. Because NIS2 is a directive rather than a regulation, every Member State writes its own national law. The core obligations are shared, but registration portals, supervisory authorities and reporting forms differ from Rome to Warsaw, which is exactly why a country-level view matters.
