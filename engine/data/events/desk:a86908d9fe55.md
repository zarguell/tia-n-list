Vendor advisory rated high with no CVE assigned. It says Firefox MV2 uBlock Origin 1.75.2 and lower let a page drive the element picker and add arbitrary trusted filters after one user click when a prevent-xhr or prevent-fetch scriptlet fulfills a response with a WAR. The report reached the vendor on 2026-11-01; fixes appear in 1.75.2, 1.75.4 and 1.76.0 through commits bf69cf7, 9859477, b1f7d27 and 150df12. Credit goes to syvb, and the advisory also notes extraction of the WAR secret on MV2 Chromium.
