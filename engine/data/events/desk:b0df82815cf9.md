eSecurityPlanet summarized the Acronis TRU BOSSAI investigation, reporting that a single operator used 12 AI agents to target hundreds of organizations across 11 countries and exposed Taiwan emergency records. The accessible medical records dated to 2018 and included ECG readings, ambulance video footage, and patient vital signs, while agents also accessed a city's 119 emergency-dispatch system. The campaign also hit universities, financial firms, and government systems.
