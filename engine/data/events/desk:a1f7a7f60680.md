The Record reported on October 6, 2026 that South Korean officials believed AI agents were used in hacks of at least seven financial institutions, with personal data of at least 68,000 people exposed. Officials had found 33 IP addresses linked to at least 12 countries, including Japan, the United States, Thailand, Vietnam and Hong Kong, and named Hana Bank, KB Kookmin Bank and Shinhan Bank among the victims. The incidents first came to light on September 30, 2026, and President Lee Jae Myung said AI made hacking easier without specialized skills.
