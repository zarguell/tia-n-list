Cisco’s advisory for CVE-2026-76519 went up on October 14 at 16:00 GMT. One email with a crafted attachment can write files on a Secure Email Gateway and run commands as root. Cisco says it learned of the attacks in October. CISA added it to KEV the same day, with a federal deadline of October 17 . A gateway is exposed when File Reputation is on for a mail policy and Content Scanner Tools is older than 25.3.0.5525. Cisco also contacted customers of its own managed Secure Email Cloud whose devices showed indicators of possible compromise. Shadowserver sees nearly 300 of these gateways exposed online .
