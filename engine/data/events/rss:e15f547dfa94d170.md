Summary On November 11, 2026 , the ransomware group Interlock claimed responsibility for a cyberattack on Garden Valley School Division ( gvsd.ca ), a public school district in Manitoba, Canada. The attack compromised sensitive data, including student and staff records, potentially leading to severe privacy breaches. Incident Report Field Details Target Garden Valley School Division Domain gvsd.ca Country Canada Attacking Group Interlock Date Reported November 11, 2026 Threat Actor Statement “Garden Valley School District is a Manitoba public school district with a student population of approximately 4,300. Their negligent attitude toward their own and their students’ safety has resulted in the compromise of confidential student, staff, and health information, including student information system databases (names, dates of birth, grades, attendance, disciplinary and special education records), employee payroll records and bank account information, employee personnel files, accounting spreadsheets, student and staff data files, and district administrative records.
