Sean Cairncross is leaving his post as national cyber director, CyberScoop has learned. Cairncross led the Office of the National Cyber Director, which coordinates cybersecurity policy and strategy across the federal government. Though he had experience in Republican politics and national security, he lacked a technical or cyber background. His office nevertheless became involved in key administration decisions on AI security and the protection of critical infrastructure. CyberScoop has reached out to the White House for further information. Reports state that Pentagon Chief Information Officer Kirsten Davies will move into an acting role in the White House office, a transition that comes amid staff departures and debate over how the government should manage artificial intelligence risks. CyberScoop has reached out to the Department of Defense for comment. Cairncross met with technology company leaders and helped shape a June executive order that asked leading AI firms to submit new models for voluntary government testing before public release. He also was assigned to co-lead a working group tracking a nonbinding AI security commitment signed by major labs. Cairncross advocated for collaboration between government and industry, arguing that security measures should not slow the development of AI.
