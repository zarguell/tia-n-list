Supermarket freezer attacks have moved beyond France. Overnight into Monday, November 2, 2026, refrigeration systems at dozens of stores in Belgium, Luxembourg and Sweden showed sudden temperature rises, with some freezers set as high as 50°C. Retailers threw out spoiled stock, and several stores opened late or stayed closed for the morning. The incidents are suspected cyberattacks. Sweden’s national cybersecurity center says it is responding to multiple incidents involving store cooling systems, and the refrigeration contractor that runs Delhaize’s installations in Belgium has called the Belgian events a cyberattack. As of November 2, no group has claimed responsibility, and no authority has named a suspect or an entry point. Which Retailers Reported Problems?
