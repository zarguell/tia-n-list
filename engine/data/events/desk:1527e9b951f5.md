Webhosting.today reported on 5 November that the same LiteSpeed customer notice covered a separate critical flaw in the LiteSpeed Cache module for PrestaShop, affecting versions before 1.6.2 and fixed in the 30 October emergency release. The article says an unauthenticated attacker could send crafted requests with potential full control of the store and access to customer and payment data, and that a CVE was still forthcoming. It repeats the advice to review administrator accounts and installed modules and to rotate database and other credentials stored in the site configuration.
