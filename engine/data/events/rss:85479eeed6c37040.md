This research was conducted jointly with Julian-Ferdinand Vögele of Recorded Future’s Insikt Group. Details of the joint research and presentation are available here . Cisco Talos identified UAT-6275 ( TAG-193 by Recorded Future) activity targeting government organizations across the Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS). Recovered artifacts show additional interest in water resources and energy. This focus is consistent with intelligence collection and may reflect the regional effects of the Russia–Ukraine war. In activity observed from June 2025 through August 2026, Talos identified four infection-chain types that use macro-enabled documents, PS2EXE downloaders, GitHub-hosted PowerShell, and a crafted WinRAR archive exploiting CVE-2025-8088. The recovered payloads include several custom reverse shells that provide interactive remote access. Three publicly accessible staging directories and GitHub repository history exposed payload builds, tool inventories, and changes to command-and-control (C2) infrastructure. These operational security lapses gave us direct visibility into the group’s recent activity. The files recovered from the exposed infrastructure suggest preparation for post-compromise operations and potential activity against Linux hosts and internet-facing appliances.
