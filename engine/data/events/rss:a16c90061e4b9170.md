Polymarket halted trading on its international platform on October 29 after users reported unauthorized withdrawals. According to the prediction market’s status page , the exchange was first switched to cancel-only mode, with relayer actions suspended. “We’ve identified & fixed an issue impacting our international platform, Polymarket.com. One of our 3rd party providers was compromised, and we have referred this to relevant law enforcement. Anyone who has been impacted will be made whole,” the company said . Trading resumed about an hour later, and Polymarket pledged to reimburse all affected traders. It has not named the vendor or disclosed losses. Researchers have linked the theft to malicious code served from DebugBear, a UK-based website performance monitoring firm. Polymarket’s US platform was reportedly unaffected. DebugBear confirmed being “impacted by an ongoing security incident” and urged users of its real user monitoring (RUM) product to pull its snippet from their websites. It also said it brought in specialist incident responders. Hudson Rock obtained a copy of the malicious JavaScript from a DebugBear address used by Polymarket. The code hunted for a logged-in user’s trading tools and portfolio.
