CISA added CVE-2025-59374 (ASUS Live Update Embedded Malicious Code Vulnerability) to its KEV Catalog on December 17, 2025, citing evidence of active exploitation. The advisory notes the vulnerability is an embedded malicious code issue introduced through a supply chain compromise, and urges federal agencies to prioritize remediation. The entry links to CVE.org and binds remediation under BOD 22-01.
