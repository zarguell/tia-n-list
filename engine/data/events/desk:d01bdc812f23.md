Claroc Research, authored by Asaf Aprozper, published the primary research on November 10, 2026, three hours and twenty-seven minutes before this story's first_seen. The team resolved 43,601 names under grok.me by subdomain discovery, opened the first page of 43,551 addresses from the public internet while signed in to nothing, found 37,571 still online and 35,578 of those (94.7 percent) opening for anyone; only 1,993 answered HTTP 401. The post dates the start of the work to November 8, 2026, the SpaceXSI security.txt notification to that same afternoon, the report of the remaining 22 staff apps to November 9, and the vendor reply to November 10. It also documents the Grok Build publish panel offering Anyone with the link preselected, a team app whose generated sign-in logic let an uninvited Google account read, edit and delete records, and an estimate of roughly 650 to 800 apps holding private data.
