This Cloud Security Alliance research note dates the policy change to November 13, 2026 and lays out the 90, 60 and 30 day schedule. It argues that enterprise consumers of vendor software lose the post-patch adoption buffer, and that a vendor's severity rating no longer buys time because severity does not affect Project Zero's disclosure timing. The note recommends non-patch mitigations, including feature flags, filtering and hotpatching, as a first-class governance requirement for organizations dependent on vendor patch cycles.
