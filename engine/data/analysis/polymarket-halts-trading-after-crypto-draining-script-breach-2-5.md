Polymarket halted trading on October 29 after a third-party provider, DebugBear, was compromised. A malicious JavaScript script served through the performance-monitoring integration targeted logged-in traders’ sessions, attempting to sell positions or redirect assets. Hudson Rock identified wallet-draining code in the DebugBear URL used by Polymarket. The company switched its international platform to cancel-only mode at 2:05 p.m. ET, fixed the issue, and resumed trading around 3:05 p.m. ET, promising full reimbursement.

The incident is a supply-chain breach, not a blockchain exploit. The attacker accessed a trusted frontend script rather than the smart-contract layer. About $2.5 million in assets was traced to a suspected attacker-controlled wallet, though aggregate losses remain unverified. The key watch is whether other sites using the same DebugBear snippet were affected, since the vendor confirmed its own security incident and urged customers to remove the snippet.
