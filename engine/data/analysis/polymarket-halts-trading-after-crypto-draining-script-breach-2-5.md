On October 29, 2026, Polymarket's international platform Polymarket.com served malicious JavaScript through a third party performance monitoring integration that drained user wallets. Hudson Rock retrieved a 15,187 byte script from a DebugBear URL and found code that searched for a logged in user's trading client and signer, inspected the portfolio and attempted to sell or transfer assets to an attacker controlled wallet. Polymarket placed the exchange in cancel only mode, identified and fixed the issue, and resumed trading, while production build metadata recorded removal of the DebugBear integration.

Loss figures stayed inconsistent across reports. Hudson Rock cited an 800,000 dollar reported loss and a separate recipient address screenshot showing over 2 million in holdings, while BleepingComputer traced roughly 2.5 million to a suspected attacker wallet and an early social post cited about 2.68 million. Polymarket said affected users would be made whole and referred the incident to law enforcement, but did not disclose how many users were affected or how the reimbursement process would work.
