Polymarket suspended trading after attackers compromised a third-party performance-monitoring script (DebugBear RUM) to distribute cryptocurrency-draining JavaScript on its international platform. The breach triggered cancel-only mode at 2:05 p.m. ET, a fix by 2:37 p.m., and full resumption at 3:05 p.m. ET. The company referred the incident to law enforcement, said affected users would be made whole, and has not disclosed the number of victims or reimbursement mechanics.

Hudson Rock identified the payload embedded in the vendor script: it inspected portfolios and attempted to sell or redirect assets for logged-in clients. Approximately $2.5 million in assets was traced to a suspected attacker-controlled wallet, with one screenshot showing over $2 million in holdings. A production build explicitly records removal of the integration, corroborating remediation.

The core risk is supply-chain compromise rather than a direct platform vulnerability. Watch for confirmation of reimbursement, the law-enforcement outcome, and whether similar third-party script compromises surface at other trading platforms.
