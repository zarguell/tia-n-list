MetaMask disclosed an ongoing security incident affecting part of its infrastructure, specifically the validators behind its Ethereum staking operations, after unauthorized access to infrastructure. The company is proactively exiting all affected validators from its non-custodial staking product in coordination with clients and partners, including Lido Finance, with the final validators expected to be exited (though not fully withdrawn) by October 7, 2026. MetaMask states there is no indication that MetaMask wallets or customer funds have been affected, and that containment and verification continue.

The incident matters because it touches validators backing staked ETH rather than end-user wallets, and Lido warned that the mass exits will likely incur foregone rewards plus possible downtime penalties for stakers. The scope of the unauthorized access, how many validators were affected, and the value at stake have not been disclosed. MetaMask also warned users never to share their Secret Recovery Phrase or private keys, reiterating that those are not at risk from this incident.

What to watch is whether the investigation confirms any validator key compromise or slashing, how Lido stakers are compensated for missed rewards, and whether MetaMask publishes a fuller post-incident report on the access vector and containment.
