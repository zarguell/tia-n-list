MetaMask issued a statement saying a recent attack did not affect its wallet infrastructure, according to BleepingComputer. The company sought to reassure users that the breach reported elsewhere was limited in scope and did not touch the systems that hold user funds or keys.

The story rests on a single source so far, with no CVE, actor name, or timeline disclosed in the available reporting. Watch for MetaMask or security researchers to publish details of what the attack actually was, and for any confirmation on whether user credentials or funds were exposed.
