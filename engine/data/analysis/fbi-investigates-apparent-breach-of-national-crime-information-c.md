CNN cited two people briefed on the investigation that the FBI is looking into an apparent breach of the National Crime Information Center, discovered in recent days and described as the second such incident in recent months. NCIC is the nationwide system police use for warrants, background checks, wanted and missing persons, stolen vehicles and firearms, and protection orders. Reuters, NBC, the BBC, AP, the Guardian and ABC carried the report. The FBI had no public comment as of Friday night, and CNN did not say when the intrusion occurred or how the attackers got in.

Timing, entry path and whether data was taken remain unknown. One report following the story put NCIC at more than 19 million records. A former FBI official quoted by CNN said the larger concern is not only exposure of sensitive records but whether an attacker could alter the information officers rely on during stops and investigations. The Guardian grouped the FBI report with an apparent attack disclosed Thursday on a US Department of Agriculture system used to pay farmers, amid a wider wave of tech outages.

The report follows a September investigation into ShinyHunters claims of stolen agent data through the FBIJobs.gov recruitment platform, tied to an unpatched Oracle PeopleSoft vulnerability, and a 2023 breach of a New York field office system.
