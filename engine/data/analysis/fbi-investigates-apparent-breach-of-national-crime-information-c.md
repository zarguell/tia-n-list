The FBI is reportedly investigating a possible cyber intrusion involving the National Crime Information Center, a sensitive US law enforcement database that holds records such as arrest warrants and criminal history. Reports from Mathrubhumi and thecybersecguru describe the incident as suspected rather than confirmed, and the Mathrubhumi link frames it as the second breach of this kind in months.

Any question about NCIC data integrity would reach beyond ordinary credential theft, because police rely on those records for day-to-day decisions. Watch for an FBI statement on whether records were accessed or changed and for clarification on any link to other recent federal database incidents. The available posts give no actor, timeline or intrusion detail, so the scope remains unknown.
