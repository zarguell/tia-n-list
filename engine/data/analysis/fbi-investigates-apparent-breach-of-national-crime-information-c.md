CNN reported, citing two people briefed on the investigation, that the FBI is looking into an apparent breach of the National Crime Information Center, the nationwide system police use for warrants, background checks, wanted and missing persons, stolen vehicles and protection orders. The intrusion was discovered in recent days. CNN described it as the second such breach in recent months, and Reuters, NBC, the BBC and others picked up the report. The FBI had no public comment as of Friday night.

Timing, entry path and whether data was taken are all unknown. A former FBI official quoted by CNN said the larger concern is not just exposure of sensitive records but whether an attacker could alter the information officers rely on. NCIC holds more than 19 million records, according to one report following the story. The system connects federal, state and local agencies, which makes any integrity problem operational rather than just a privacy issue.

The report follows a September investigation into ShinyHunters claims of stolen agent data via the FBIJobs.gov recruitment platform, which was tied to an unpatched Oracle PeopleSoft vulnerability, and a 2023 breach of a New York field office system. The Guardian also grouped the FBI report with an apparent attack on a US Department of Agriculture payments system amid a wave of tech outages. Watch for formal FBI confirmation, an attribution, and any notice about affected records or data integrity.
