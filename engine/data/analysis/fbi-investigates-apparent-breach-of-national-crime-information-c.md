CNN reported, citing two people briefed on the investigation, that the FBI is looking into an apparent breach of the National Crime Information Center discovered in recent days. NCIC is the nationwide system for warrants, background checks, wanted and missing persons, stolen vehicles and firearms, and protection orders, shared across local, state and federal agencies. Reuters, NBC, the BBC, AP, the Guardian, ABC and SecurityWeek carried the report. AP obtained confirmation from a person familiar with the matter who spoke anonymously.

Timing, entry path and whether data was taken remain unknown. The FBI had no public comment as of Saturday, and AP said the scope was not immediately clear. One report following the story put NCIC at more than 19 million records. A former FBI official quoted by CNN said the larger concern is not only exposure of sensitive records but whether an attacker could alter the information officers rely on during stops and investigations. AP said there was no immediate indication the NCIC incident was related to the recent run of government and private-sector outages that officials have described as upgrade and vulnerability-remediation work.

The report follows a September investigation into ShinyHunters claims of stolen agent data through the FBIJobs.gov recruitment platform, tied to an unpatched Oracle PeopleSoft vulnerability, and a 2023 breach of a New York field office system. CNN also described the NCIC incident as the second such breach in recent months. Watch for formal FBI confirmation, an attribution, notice of affected records, and any indication that record integrity was changed rather than only read.
