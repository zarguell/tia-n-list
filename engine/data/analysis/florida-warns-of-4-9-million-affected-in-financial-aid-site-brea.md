Florida's Department of Education warned that a financial aid site breach exposed Social Security numbers and personal data of approximately 4.9 million individuals. The breach was reported by The Record and confirmed by state officials. Affected individuals include students and families who used the state's financial aid portal. Florida advised affected residents to monitor for identity theft and use official state channels for updates.

The exposure of nearly 5 million Social Security numbers represents a significant state-level data breach with long-term identity-theft risk. The state's response includes notifications and recommendations for credit monitoring, though no details about the attack vector or attacker attribution have been released.

What to watch: whether the breach triggers federal review or additional state-level disclosures, and if the attacker attempts to monetize the data through identity fraud rather than ransomware-style extortion. The scale makes this one of the larger state education-system breaches reported recently.
