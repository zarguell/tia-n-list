Florida authorities warn that roughly 4.9 to 5 million people had Social Security numbers exposed in a breach of a state financial aid website, as reported by The Record and confirmed on the Florida Department of Education's data breach status page. The disclosure is limited to the scale of affected individuals and the exposure of Social Security numbers; the event content does not name the specific site compromise mechanism, attacker, or timeline beyond the public notice.

Given the volume of exposed identifiers, affected individuals face elevated identity-theft and fraud risk. Watch for official guidance from the Florida Department of Education on credit monitoring, freeze options, and any follow-up on the scope of records accessed, and treat unsolicited contact referencing the breach as potential fraud.
