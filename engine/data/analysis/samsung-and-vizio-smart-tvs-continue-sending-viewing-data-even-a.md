Researchers testing smart TV privacy controls found that Samsung and Vizio sets continued sending traffic consistent with automatic content recognition after users opted out of ACR. The transmissions were encrypted, so researchers could not confirm whether the payloads contained actual viewing data or whether the manufacturers retained the data. The finding raises questions about how reliably privacy settings limit telemetry on smart TVs.

Of the eight smart TVs examined, only four fully honored the ACR opt-out, meaning half of the tested devices kept sending tracking-related traffic after users disabled it. Users should treat opt-out toggles as unverified and consider network-level controls, such as blocking TV internet access or isolating sets on a guest VLAN, where viewing-data collection is a concern.
