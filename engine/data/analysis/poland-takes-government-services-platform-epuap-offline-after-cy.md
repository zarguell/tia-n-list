Poland temporarily disconnected its ePUAP government services platform following a cyberattack, taking the platform offline to contain the incident. The action was reported via SecurityAffairs and The Record, with beyondmachines.net tracking the disruption.

No technical details of the intrusion have been published beyond the takedown itself. Watch for restoration timelines, any disclosure of affected data or services, and whether Polish authorities attribute the attack to a specific actor.
