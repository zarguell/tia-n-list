The Qilin ransomware group claimed Alaska Electrical Apprenticeship as a victim on its leak site, adding to a steady stream of recent postings. Qilin is a Russia-linked ransomware operation that has been active since 2022 and uses a double-extortion model combining data theft with encryption. The group operates a RaaS program with affiliates using both Windows and Linux encryptors, including variants targeting VMware ESXi.

In the same reporting window, the Play ransomware group posted new victims including GT Distributors, and IncRansom claimed cullottalaw.com. The volume of ransom group blog posts indicates continued high activity across multiple operations. Qilin in particular has maintained a consistent posting cadence throughout 2026.

For defenders, this is a reminder that ransomware operations continue to diversify their targeting. Apprenticeship and workforce development organizations hold personally identifiable information and government funding data that can be valuable for extortion or secondary fraud. Organizations in this sector should ensure they have immutable backups, network segmentation, and incident response plans that account for double-extortion scenarios.
