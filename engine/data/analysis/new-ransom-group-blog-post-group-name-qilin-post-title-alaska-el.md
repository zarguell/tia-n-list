The Qilin ransomware group posted a new victim: Alicotrans, a French transportation and logistics company. The listing appeared on the group's leak site tracked by CTI.FYI. Qilin has been active throughout 2026, targeting mid-market companies across Europe and North America with a double-extortion model.

Alicotrans operates trucking and logistics services, fitting Qilin's pattern of targeting companies where operational disruption creates pressure to pay. The group typically encrypts systems, exfiltrates data, and threatens public release on their leak site if ransom demands go unmet. This victim joins a growing list that includes Alaska Electrical Apprenticeship and others tracked in this story.

Qilin's leak site activity indicates ongoing operations rather than a burst campaign. The group maintains a consistent posting cadence, suggesting organized affiliate operations. Organizations in transportation, logistics, and manufacturing should monitor Qilin TTPs and ensure backup isolation and lateral movement controls are in place.
