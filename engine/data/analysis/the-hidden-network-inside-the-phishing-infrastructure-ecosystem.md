Microsoft uncovered a Chinese-language cybercriminal ecosystem over the past year, centered on a marketplace called YAM Store (tracked as Storm-3119) and a phishing-kit developer called Storm-2990 (the Iron Man System). Threat actors bought phishing kits and access to compromised or fraudulent Microsoft and Google cloud environments to target healthcare, financial services, wholesale distribution, and IT sectors across Japan, the United States, Australia, and India. Microsoft's Digital Crimes Unit disrupted the primary developers and infrastructure in collaboration with global partners.
