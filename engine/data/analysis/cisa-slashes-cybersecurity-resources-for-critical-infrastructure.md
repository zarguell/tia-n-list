CISA is cutting back on free cybersecurity resources for critical infrastructure operators. The agency is reducing capacity to coordinate vulnerability disclosure and incident response across sectors that depend on federal support for threat intelligence sharing. This matters because CISA has been the primary coordinator for ICS/SCADA vulnerability disclosure and the main federal entity providing no-cost security tools to water, energy, healthcare, and transportation operators.

The resource reduction comes as nation-state actors continue targeting US critical infrastructure. With less capacity for coordination, vulnerability disclosure timelines may slip and smaller operators who rely on CISA advisories and free tools will have fewer resources to defend themselves. Watch for increased fragmentation in how critical infrastructure vulnerabilities get disclosed and patched across sectors.
