Ukraine's CERT-UA attributes a recruitment-themed campaign to UAC-0145, a cluster that includes subcluster UAC-0002, also tracked as Sandworm, APT44, and Seashell Blizzard. The operation has been running since at least May 2026 and targets system administrators and IT professionals. Operators scrape candidates' resumes from job-search platforms, contact them while posing as IT companies such as ATLAS Business Group, move conversations to Telegram, and run video interviews over Zoom in English with mock technical assignments.

The payload is a trojanized WireGuard VPN client, a trusted tool for exactly the audience being targeted. In one variant the lure is a fake corporate VPN test that creates a scheduled task and downloads malware on Windows. The multi-step social engineering is designed to survive scrutiny, so defenses should focus on the interview process itself: job seekers in tech should verify recruiter identities out of band and treat unsolicited VPN or corporate-test downloads as suspicious.
