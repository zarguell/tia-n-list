Chinese state-sponsored group Fire Ant has moved beyond VMware hypervisors into Cisco networking infrastructure. Investigated by Sygnia, the actor was found persisting on Cisco IOS XR routers via GRE tunnel interfaces that had no corresponding configuration or commit history. Custom malware runs as a fake system service, activating only during alternating hours to avoid detection. The implant suppresses syslog messages that would expose tunnel activity, opens outbound Telnet connections to Fire Ant command-and-control, and provides interactive shell access without logging.

The scope extends beyond routers. Fire Ant also compromised TACACS authentication servers and Linux management hosts, establishing a foothold across the trusted infrastructure layer. The attackers captured router traffic and exfiltrated PCAP files to external FTP servers, potentially exposing internal topology, admin connections, authentication flows, and routing relationships. This transforms a router from a transit device into an espionage platform. First reported in 2025 targeting VMware environments, Fire Ant's shift to network infrastructure represents a significant escalation in China-nexus espionage tradecraft against critical infrastructure.
