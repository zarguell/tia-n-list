A suspected customer data breach affects Japanese sushi delivery operator Sushi and food delivery service operator Ride On Express Holdings, reported Tuesday. Customer data appears exposed, though the full scope and entry vector remain unclear. The dual-operator impact suggests either a shared service provider or coordinated intrusion. Affected customers should expect notification; businesses using these operators should verify whether their data resides in impacted systems and review vendor-access controls. Monitor Japanese data-protection authority announcements for breach-registration updates.
