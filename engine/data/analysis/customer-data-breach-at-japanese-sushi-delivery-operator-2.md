Ride On Express Holdings, operator of sushi and food delivery services in Japan, confirmed a breach affecting about 6.5 million registered customers. The Japan Times reported the exposure on October 14, 2026, making it a significant consumer data incident. The event adds to a growing pattern of delivery-service data breaches targeting large user bases in Asia.

The scale demands monitoring for fraud or identity abuse reports linked to the dataset. Watch for regulatory response under Japan's data protection rules and any clarification of the entry vector or data classes exposed.
