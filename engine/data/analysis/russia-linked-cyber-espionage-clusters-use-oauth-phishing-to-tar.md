Google Threat Intelligence Group detailed three Russia-linked clusters (UNC6293, UNC7005, UNC5976) conducting OAuth phishing, device-code phishing, and credential harvesting against academics, think tanks, and organizations in the US and Europe. GTIG assesses with moderate confidence that UNC6293 is an initial-access subcluster of ICE RELIC (formerly APT29/Cozy Bear/Midnight Blizzard). The campaigns do not exploit software flaws but abuse legitimate OAuth consent flows to trick targets into granting access to email, cloud accounts, or messaging platforms.

UNC6293 used domains including foreignrelations[.]us and dosportal[.]app for OAuth phishing, with the first domain mimicking content from the Council on Foreign Relations. Historical registration data linked these domains to internationalaffairsportal[.]us and stateaffairs[.]us, all registered through Dynadot around the same period. The infrastructure overlap and the use of Evilginx reverse-proxy setups suggest a maturing operation that combines multiple credential-theater techniques into coordinated campaigns. The scope of targets across US and European academia and policy institutions points to intelligence collection priorities.
