Cyble Research and Intelligence Labs documented a campaign by threat actor INJ3CTOR3 deploying the previously unknown JOMANGY PHP webshell family against FreePBX VoIP infrastructure. The campaign uses a six-layer persistence architecture designed to self-heal within minutes from any single surviving channel. Persistence mechanisms include recurring cron polling every 1 to 3 minutes, shell profile stagers, and aggressive competitor eviction that scrubs over 50 rival webshell signatures and blocks 11 rival C2 IPs. Researchers observed an inventory of 3,080 C2 IP addresses, with approximately 39 percent hosted on Alibaba Cloud infrastructure.

Initial access candidates are CVE-2025-64328 (post-auth command injection) and CVE-2025-57819 (pre-auth SQL injection), both in Sangoma FreePBX. The self-healing design is notable: killing one persistence layer does not kill the campaign, because surviving layers regenerate the eliminated one. This makes incident response significantly harder and requires simultaneous remediation of all six layers. FreePBX operators should treat any compromise as persistent by default and consider full system rebuilds rather than in-place cleanup. The scale of C2 infrastructure (3,080 IPs) and the deliberate eviction of competing webshells indicate an organized operation focused on monopolizing compromised VoIP systems.
