TA4922, a Chinese-speaking threat actor, is deploying PackClient RAT through tax-themed phishing campaigns targeting organizations in mainland China and India. Proofpoint observed the activity in May and July 2026. The first campaign impersonated the Shandong Provincial Tax Bureau, warning recipients they had been selected for a 2026 tax inspection and allegedly failed to pay stamp duties. Emails directed victims to download a ZIP archive from gov12366[.]com, which contained an executable that initiated the PackClient infection chain.

PackClient is a modular RAT framework marketed through Chinese-language Telegram channels. It provides data-theft, surveillance, remote control, payload delivery, and persistence capabilities. The first-stage downloader checks for elevated privileges, drops a DLL such as xMain.dll, and invokes it through rundll32.exe. This modular design makes the malware adaptable to espionage, financial crime, reconnaissance, or follow-on ransomware operations.

The campaign highlights the growing availability of sophisticated malware in Chinese-speaking cybercrime marketplaces. TA4922's use of tax-themed lures is tailored to the target region's bureaucratic context, making the phishing more convincing. Organizations in China and India should be especially wary of unsolicited tax-related communications requiring software downloads.
