Southern Company notified roughly 400,000 customers that an unauthorized third party accessed limited account information through its online customer portal. About 300,000 accounts belong to Georgia Power, 100,000 to Alabama Power; Mississippi Power is affected but no figure released. Exposed data includes name, mailing address, phone, email, last 4 digits of Social Security Number, and basic account details. No bank or payment card numbers accessed. The company engaged law enforcement and stopped the activity upon detection.
