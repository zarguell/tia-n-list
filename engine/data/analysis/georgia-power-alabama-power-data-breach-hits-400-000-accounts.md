Southern Company notified roughly 400,000 customers of unauthorized access through its online customer portal. The Atlanta-based holding company serves more than 9 million customers through Georgia Power, Alabama Power, and Mississippi Power. Approximately 300,000 affected accounts belong to Georgia Power and roughly 100,000 to Alabama Power; no figure was released for Mississippi Power. The company stated the attacker did not access bank account numbers or payment card numbers.

The exposed information includes name, mailing address, phone number, email, the last 4 digits of Social Security numbers, and other basic account details. Southern Company engaged law enforcement and took steps to stop the activity upon detection. Watch for regulatory notifications, state attorney-general filings, or follow-up reports clarifying whether the attacker moved beyond the customer portal into operational or billing systems.
