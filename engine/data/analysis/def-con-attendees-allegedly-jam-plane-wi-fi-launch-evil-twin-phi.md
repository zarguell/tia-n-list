A Delta Air Lines flight returning from Las Vegas became the site of an in-flight Wi-Fi incident involving DEF CON attendees. On Delta Flight 591, a Boeing 757 flying from Las Vegas, someone on board allegedly disrupted the aircraft's official internet service and broadcast a fraudulent wireless network, then used it to run an evil twin phishing attack against other passengers. Federal authorities are reportedly investigating.

The incident is notable less for the technique and more for the environment: a closed, captive-audience network at altitude where the operator has few alternatives. Evil twin phishing on commercial flights is a new venue for a well-known attack, and it puts the aviation industry on notice that onboard connectivity needs the same rogue-AP defenses as any enterprise Wi-Fi. Passengers should treat in-flight networks like public Wi-Fi and avoid entering credentials until the incident is fully understood.
