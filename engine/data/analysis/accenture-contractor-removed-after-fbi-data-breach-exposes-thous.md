The FBI terminated an Accenture contractor responsible for managing an Oracle PeopleSoft HR platform after a breach exposed personal details of thousands of bureau employees. Reuters reported October 5 that sources identified Accenture as the platform manager and confirmed the contractor failed to apply a security patch explicitly issued to secure the system. FBI cyber chief Brett Leatherman stated the incident occurred because of a security failure of the third-party managed platform, and the bureau removed the contractor and mitigated further risk. The stolen data included addresses, phone numbers, spouse information, intelligence and surveillance roles, and private medical records.

ShinyHunters claimed responsibility for exploiting the unpatched PeopleSoft instance and previously claimed 300 compromised instances across over 100 organizations. Google recently warned the group targets vulnerable PeopleSoft servers. The breach underscores the risk of delegated patch management and the exposure of sensitive workforce data when vendors miss critical updates. Watch for further FBI confirmation of scope, any CISA KEV addition for the underlying PeopleSoft vulnerability, and whether ShinyHunters publishes or sells the stolen employee data.
