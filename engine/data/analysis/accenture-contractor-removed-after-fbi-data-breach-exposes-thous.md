The FBI removed an Accenture contractor after a breach exposed personal data of thousands of FBI employees. Reuters reported the incident stemmed from an unpatched Oracle PeopleSoft HR platform managed by Accenture; FBI cyber chief Brett Leatherman confirmed the contractor failed to apply an explicitly issued security patch. ShinyHunters previously claimed exploitation of PeopleSoft, and Google warned the actor targets vulnerable instances. The breach reinforces supply-chain risk: one missed patch on a third-party HR system exposed thousands of employee records.
