Unit 42 reports a Southeast Asian cyberespionage campaign deploying PerfectPioneer RAT and the modular KUKURAT family through HTTP/3 command-and-control channels. The use of QUIC-based C2 is the distinctive technical feature: it puts attacker traffic in a protocol that many enterprise network controls inspect less deeply than traditional HTTPS.

A repost from cyber.netsecops.io attributes the campaign to TGR-STA-0049 and says the targets are Southeast Asian governments. The available event content is limited to those two summaries, so no CVE, victim count or detailed infection chain is available here. Watch for the full Unit 42 report's indicators, especially HTTP/3 endpoints and loader hashes, and for any overlap with other regional espionage clusters using QUIC transport.
