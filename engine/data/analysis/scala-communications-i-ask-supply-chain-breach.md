Scala Communications' FAQ system i-ask suffered a supply chain breach when attackers accessed its admin site and planted a program. A new disclosure confirms Sompo Japan is the third affected client, with approximately 60,000 inquiries about SMILING ROA exposed. The original breach involved unauthorized login to the admin site and program deployment on a shared environment. The attacker access path and full scope across clients remain partially unclear, but the repeated client disclosures show the breach has tangible downstream impact. Organizations using i-ask or similar vendor-hosted FAQ platforms should verify their own exposure and review vendor access controls.
