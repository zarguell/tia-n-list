Palo Alto Networks released patches for 13 vulnerabilities, with the most significant being CVE-2026-0322, an authenticated RCE in the PAN-OS management interface with a CVSS of 8.6. The company confirmed very limited exploitation in the wild targeting PA-Series and VM-Series firewalls, Panorama, and M-Series appliances. Fixes were released for PAN-OS versions 10.2, 11.1, 11.2, 12.1, and 12.2; 11.2.16 remains pending until October 15. The flaw requires existing credentials and network access to the management interface, so restricting that interface to trusted internal IPs is the primary mitigation until patching completes.

No critical CVEs were included in the broader advisory, but the active exploitation of CVE-2026-0322 makes this a priority patch cycle for firewall administrators. Threat Prevention subscribers can apply signature-based coverage that offers limited protection against known attack patterns, though Palo Alto notes no complete workarounds exist. The event set includes a Canadian advisory (AV26-1041) and the original patch notice, giving the story moderate authority despite only five sources.

Watch for updates if Palo Alto expands the exploitation footprint or if the pending 11.2.16 fix is delayed. The story currently carries an analysis score matching its 5.9 store score, with recency and authority providing the bulk of the weight.
