Germany arrested a Russian national suspected of being a core Qilin ransomware group member after extradition from Japan. The National Police Agency confirmed the suspect arrived in Japan as a tourist and was detained under Japan's Extradition Law for Fugitives at Germany's request. Qilin, which emerged in August 2022 as Agenda, operates a ransomware-as-a-service model using double-extortion tactics. Statistics indicate over 2,350 victims across 62 countries, including Nissan, Asahi, Lee Enterprises, and Australia's Court Services Victoria. The arrest represents a significant international enforcement action against one of the most active RaaS operations.
