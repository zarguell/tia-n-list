Japan detained a core Qilin ransomware member and extradited him to Germany, according to Asahi Shimbun reports cited by Japan Cyber Watch. The suspect, described as an alleged Russian national, was detained in Osaka. The extradition is part of an ongoing international RaaS investigation. Japan Cyber Watch notes unnamed sources and no official government statement, so details remain unverified by official channels. The event reinforces that Qilin remains under active law enforcement pressure across jurisdictions. Monitor for official confirmations from German or Japanese authorities and any follow-on indictments.
