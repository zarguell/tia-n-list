Kaspersky's August 2026 sample is a new CoffeeLoader variant, not a new Retadup worm. An NSIS installer drops a patched 7z.dll into a temporary directory and executes it with rundll32.exe; part of the legitimate 7-Zip library is replaced by self-decrypting shellcode for an updated Armoury packer. The prior Armoury build decrypted with an OpenCL kernel, while this one performs the XOR work through OpenGL shaders, and the unpacked downloader injects into processes such as dllhost.exe after fetching a signed, MD5-checked payload from /payload?md5=. No CVE or product patch attaches to this campaign; it is live malware delivery through a worm whose command servers were disrupted in 2019.

The important shift predates the August sample. Kaspersky traces re-registration of old Retadup C2 domains to 2024, with SmokeLoader served first and CoffeeLoader from early 2025, so this is a two-year-old distribution channel being upgraded rather than a fresh outbreak. A dormant USB stick carrying the 2017 worm can still check in and receive a modern, signed loader, which puts old media and Windows hosts outside normal patching in scope, not just currently managed endpoints. Kaspersky blocked attempts on about 15,000 systems in the first three quarters of 2026, concentrated in Peru, Angola, Russia and Bolivia, far below the 850,000 systems disinfected after the 2019 takedown but enough to keep a loader in circulation that can fetch infostealers or ransomware.

Block the named Retadup C2 domains alphanoob[.]com, newblackage[.]com, newminersage[.]com, radnewage[.]com and internet-speed-check[.]com, plus requests to /payload?md5=, and inspect removable-drive autorun. Hunt for 7z.dll written to temp by an NSIS installer, rundll32.exe loading a DLL from C:\ProgramData\b79faace\ or C:\Users\[username]\AppData\Local\b79faace\, Run values named b79faace.dll, the StartupApproved\Run entry that hides autorun from Task Manager, and injection into dllhost.exe, WerFault.exe, svchost.exe, OpenWith.exe or AtBroker.exe. There is no software version to patch here, so the controls are egress filtering, execution policy on USB media and those host-level indicators.

A move beyond the current Peru, Angola, Russia and Bolivia concentration, or a new set of re-registered 2019 C2 domains, would turn this from a contained legacy-worm revival into a broader loader network. The other trigger is another packer revision that drops OpenGL or the X-Signature and X-Content-MD5 verification, because those checks are why a simple payload swap against the existing servers will not hold.
