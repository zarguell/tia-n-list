Boston Scientific, one of the world's largest medical device manufacturers, disclosed a cyberattack that caused global operational disruptions. The company detected the incident on August 25, 2026, reporting that it caused a network outage impacting access to operating systems and business applications, including the ability to process and ship customer orders. Boston Scientific has 59,000 employees and 13 manufacturing facilities worldwide, making this a significant disruption to medical device supply chains.

The company activated incident response procedures and contracted external cybersecurity experts. As of the disclosure, Boston Scientific said it does not yet know when all affected systems will be restored. The attack's scope and method have not been publicly detailed, but the operational impact is confirmed across the company's global operations.

This incident follows a pattern of cyberattacks targeting medical device manufacturers and healthcare supply chains. The lack of attribution or technical details leaves open questions about whether data was exfiltrated or if the disruption was purely operational. Healthcare organizations relying on Boston Scientific products should monitor for potential supply delays and watch for further disclosures about the incident's root cause.
