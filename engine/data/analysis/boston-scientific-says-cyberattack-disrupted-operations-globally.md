Boston Scientific detected a cybersecurity incident on August 25, 2026, causing a network outage that disrupted access to on-premises operating systems and business applications. The company could not process or ship customer orders. By August 30, investigators found no signs of unauthorized activity in the environment since the initial detection date. The incident did not affect cloud-based systems.

The medical device manufacturer, which makes stents, catheters, pacemakers, and defibrillators, has 59,000 employees and 13 manufacturing facilities globally. It engaged CrowdStrike and other third-party cybersecurity specialists for incident response, forensic investigation, containment, and recovery. The company has not identified the threat actor, disclosed a ransomware claim, or stated whether data was accessed or exfiltrated.

As of the latest update, Boston Scientific continues restoring affected systems. Customers could still submit orders electronically, but the company said a timeline for full restoration remained unknown. The focus is on internal operational technology-adjacent business infrastructure rather than the company's cloud estate.
