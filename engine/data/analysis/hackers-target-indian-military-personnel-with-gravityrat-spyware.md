Lookout reported a cyberespionage campaign delivering GravityRAT through recruitment themed lures to Indian military and defense industry personnel. The spyware was disguised as a recruitment app, and Lookout detected it on 14 devices in India over 12 months. Telemetry pointed mainly to people seeking jobs with foreign defense contractors. The researchers said defense personnel in other countries may also be targets.

SecurityOnline's 20 November write-up describes fake job portals as the delivery route and calls the campaign active. The captured reporting does not list specific data stolen by the implant, so the confirmed artifacts are the 14 affected devices, the recruitment app lure and the GravityRAT designation. Watch for Lookout's full indicator set and for reports of the same job portal theme outside India.
