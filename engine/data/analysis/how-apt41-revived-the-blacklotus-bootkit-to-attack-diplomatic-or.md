APT41 revived the BlackLotus bootkit as a new firmware implant called BlackX, exploiting the Baton Drop vulnerability (CVE-2022-21894) to bypass UEFI Secure Boot. Kaspersky GReAT discovered the sample in early 2026 during an APT campaign in Central Asia targeting diplomatic organizations in Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, and Turkmenistan. BlackLotus was advertised on hacking forums in October 2022 and confirmed by ESET in March 2023; Microsoft released updates to address CVE-2022-21894, after which BlackLotus went quiet until this resurgence.

Defenders should ensure systems are patched against CVE-2022-21894 and verify Secure Boot and firmware integrity, since BlackX operates below the operating system and persists across reinstalls. Coverage from Kaspersky and The Hacker News attributes the campaign to APT41.
