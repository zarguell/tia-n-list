Cisco's October 7 advisory covers 35 vulnerabilities, with over a dozen rated critical. The most severe are in NX-OS NGOAM (CVE-2026-76485, CVE-2026-76486, CVE-2026-76501, all CVSS 9.8) allowing unauthenticated remote code execution as root or denial of service via crafted packets. Additional critical issues affect License On-Prem (CVE-2026-20328, unauthenticated access; CVE-2026-76454, DoS; CVE-2026-76480, CVE-2026-76482, CVE-2026-76483 covering missing authentication, improper cryptographic verification, and unprotected credentials) and Nexus 3000/9000 switches (CVE-2026-76455, CVE-2026-76459, CVE-2026-76471, CVE-2026-76465). No workarounds exist; patches have been released and immediate application is advised, particularly for devices with NGOAM or NX-API enabled.
