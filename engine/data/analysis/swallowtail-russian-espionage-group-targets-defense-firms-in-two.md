Symantec attributes an espionage campaign against European defense and law-enforcement targets to APT28 (Fancy Bear, Forest Blizzard), tracked as Swallowtail and linked to Russia's GRU Unit 26165. The targets included a NATO naval shipbuilder, a manufacturer of military aircraft engines, and a law enforcement agency in two NATO countries. In the shipbuilder intrusion, which lasted two months, the attackers stole credentials and tunneled into the network, with Symantec observing hands-on-keyboard activity as operators repeatedly returned to a compromised host over a week to steal credentialed material, indicating deliberate targeting rather than accidental infection.

The campaign used the HOOKEDGE backdoor, a webhook-based tool linked to BlueDelta (APT28) with code overlap with the modular Windows backdoor HEADLACE, per a Recorded Future Insikt Group report dated 2026-08-27 covering activity from September 2025 through early April 2026. Initial access in at least one case came from an employee opening a malicious Microsoft Word document received as an email attachment in June, and Recorded Future linked the HOOKEDGE campaigns to APT28 with moderate confidence, targeting government and diplomatic organizations in Romania, Spain, and Turkey.

What to watch: whether the espionage expands beyond the named defense and law-enforcement targets, and whether the HOOKEDGE/HEADLACE tooling overlap yields further attribution or additional victims.
