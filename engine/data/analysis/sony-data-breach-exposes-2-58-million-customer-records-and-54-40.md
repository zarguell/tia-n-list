Sony Network Communications disclosed a data breach exposing 2,585,481 customer records tied to its So-net and Plala (S Course) internet services, announced on October 27, 2026. The breach compromised passwords for 54,401 email addresses but did not affect primary membership passwords or credit card information. The compromised database included 2,141,986 records from So-net. Sony did not confirm how the unauthorized access occurred or the full scope of affected users.

This breach adds to a history of Sony data exposures, including a 2016 incident involving about 34,000 people and a 2019 breach at a billing partner affecting roughly 11.9 million patients in a separate case. The current disclosure provides limited detail on discovery timeline, victim notification status, or root cause. Organizations with So-net or Plala accounts should assume exposure of associated email credentials and rotate related passwords.
