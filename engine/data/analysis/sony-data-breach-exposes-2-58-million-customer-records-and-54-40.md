Sony Network Communications, an ISP subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, confirmed a breach detected on October 23, 2026 following an October 19 intrusion, exposing 2,585,481 customer records across its So-net (2,141,986) and Plala S Course / NTT Docomo Hikari (443,495) internet services. No banking or payment card details were exposed, but 54,401 email addresses and their account passwords were compromised. The company shut down the affected servers and blocked external communication, and advised users to change passwords.

NTT Docomo, which contracts Sony Network Communications for Docomo Hikari service, also notified customers and warned of phishing emails and fake websites impersonating its d POINT Club and a Universal Studios Japan campaign. Affected customers should expect credential-stuffing and targeted phishing attempts against the exposed email credentials.
