Canonical confirmed a sustained DDoS attack disrupted Ubuntu websites in early October, limited to web properties such as ubuntu.com, canonical.com, and security advisory pages. Developer services including Launchpad and the Snap Store were unaffected. Most services were restored within roughly four hours on October 9. The incident follows a larger cross-border attack in April that also targeted Canonical infrastructure; a group called The Islamic Cyber Resistance in Iraq claimed that earlier incident. No attribution has been made for the October DDoS.

The short downtime suggests limited operational impact but shows persistent targeting of open-source infrastructure providers. Monitor for follow-up claims or expanded campaigns targeting package repositories rather than just marketing sites. The event references an Ubuntu Discourse post.
