mSeven Software, developer of the mSecure password manager, emailed current and former customers on November 3, 2026 about suspicious activity tied to the application and urged them to change all stored passwords, reset two-factor authentication and remove passkeys. The notice gives no technical details, no timeline, and no indication of whether customer data was accessed; one report says an encrypted customer database was exposed.

The vague warning leaves users to judge the risk themselves, with customers reporting hundreds of stored credentials and asking whether resets apply to every 2FA method and passkey. Independent analysis of the shipped macOS client and coverage from multiple outlets have not clarified the scope, so the incident's impact remains unconfirmed.
