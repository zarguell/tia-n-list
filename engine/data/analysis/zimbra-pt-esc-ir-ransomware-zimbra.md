Positive Technologies' ESC IR team encountered a new ransomware campaign targeting Zimbra mail servers, exploiting CVE-2026-73570. The attack campaign specifically targets Zimbra Collaboration Suite email servers, which remain a popular target due to their widespread deployment in enterprise environments. Ransomware groups are increasingly targeting email infrastructure because it provides access to sensitive communications and can serve as a lateral movement pivot point. Organizations running Zimbra should verify they have applied patches for CVE-2026-73570, review access logs for suspicious activity, and ensure email servers are not exposed to the internet without proper access controls. This campaign adds to the growing list of ransomware operations targeting collaboration and email platforms.
