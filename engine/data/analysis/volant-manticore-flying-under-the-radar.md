Check Point Research disclosed Volant Manticore on 24 November 2026 as an Iran-nexus group it links with medium confidence to the Ministry of Intelligence and Security and assesses is likely a spin-off of former MuddyWater operators. Activity goes back to September 2025; a July 2026 fake CV for Athena Marinou led a Cyprus-based airline to a Catbox archive dropping KuboShell, a 64-bit Windows backdoor that uses Pinata-pinned IPFS files as a command mailbox. KuboShell polls every 60 seconds, runs returned text through hidden PowerShell, and hands off to the Homa Framework, whose third stage reimplements the retired MuddyC2Go client and posts to 185.205.187[.]109 on port 8000.

The important part is the detection gap, not another Iran attribution. Homa kept MuddyC2Go's URI and POST format while KuboShell moved C2 to a public file-pinning service, so detections built around MuddyWater's current frameworks or fixed C2 servers miss both stages. Check Point documents one Cyprus airline lure, one likely government target via the January 2026 MilitaryHeritageProposal.doc, and one Saudi private hospital mailbox likely compromised through leaked credentials, with at least two operators sharing the Pinata account. For telecom, aviation, government, and healthcare organizations in Cyprus or Saudi Arabia, that means a low-signature foothold that can queue commands through legitimate infrastructure before any remote-management tool appears.

No CVE or vendor patch applies, so the response is hunt and containment.
