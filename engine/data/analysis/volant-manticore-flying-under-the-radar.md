Check Point Research describes Volant Manticore as a previously undocumented Iranian group linked to Iran's Ministry of Intelligence and Security, with activity traced back to September 2025. The group made Cyprus a primary espionage target, focusing on telecommunications companies, airlines and critical infrastructure, then shifted attention to Saudi healthcare in September 2026. Check Point assesses with medium confidence that the operators are former MuddyWater personnel who split from that group. Reporting from The Hacker News and malware.news carried the findings on November 24.

The toolset is the main reason to track this group. KuboShell is a new backdoor that uses IPFS and Pinata-pinned files as a command mailbox, registering hosts with MAC-derived files under C:\Users\Public and polling every 60 seconds. It deploys the Homa Framework, a reimplementation of MuddyWater's MuddyC2Go client, with stages tied to 185.205.187[.]109 on port 8000 and an earlier Word lure to 185.205.187[.]108:25498. Check Point also saw NetLock RMM, N-able RMM and MeshCentral, an AI-generated Athena Marinou CV lure, and operator testing against antivirus products before deployment.

For defenders, the combination of a public decentralized C2 channel and legitimate remote-management tools reduces the amount of custom malware available for detection. Watch for Pinata or IPFS polling from servers that have no business using it, unexpected RMM installations, and the published hashes and domains. The Cyprus and Saudi targeting also suggests the group follows regional diplomatic priorities, so similar lures may appear in other government-adjacent sectors.
