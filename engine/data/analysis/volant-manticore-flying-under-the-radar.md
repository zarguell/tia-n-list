Check Point Research identifies Volant Manticore as a previously undocumented Iranian threat group linked to Iran's Ministry of Intelligence and Security. Check Point assesses it as the first Iranian group to make Cyprus a primary espionage target, with telecom companies, airlines and critical infrastructure in its sights, and records an expansion to Saudi Arabia in September 2026. The group uses KuboShell, a backdoor that takes command and control from IPFS files pinned through Pinata, and deploys the Homa Framework, a reimplementation of MuddyWater's MuddyC2Go client.

The reporting reaches back to September 2025 and describes an AI-generated Athena Marinou CV lure. KuboShell registers hosts with MAC-derived files in C:\\Users\\Public and polls every 60 seconds; the three Homa stages map to 185.205.187[.]109 on port 8000, while a January 2026 Word lure points to 185.205.187[.]108:25498. The operators also use NetLock RMM, N-able RMM and MeshCentral, run credential-harvesting campaigns, test tools against antivirus products, and use Fortinet SSL VPN clients, suggesting existing network access. Check Point assesses with medium confidence that the group is a spin-off of former MuddyWater operators.

Watch for further use of IPFS or Pinata as a command mailbox, new Homa infrastructure, and RMM-based intrusions in Saudi healthcare, where Check Point observed a September 2026 phishing spike. The report publishes file hashes and domains that are useful for hunting.
