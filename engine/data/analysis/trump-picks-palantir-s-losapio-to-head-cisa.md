President Donald Trump on Monday nominated Michael LoSapio, chief information security officer at Palantir, to direct the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency. CISA has had no Senate-confirmed director since Jen Easterly's departure in January 2025. Trump's first pick, Sean Plankey, languished in the Senate and withdrew in April. Nick Andersen has led the agency in an acting capacity since February, following incidents tied to prior acting director Madhu Gottumukkala.

LoSapio has served as Palantir's CISO since September 2024 and previously led the company's infrastructure and network security group from December 2017. The nomination lands in a crowded Senate calendar with funding and defense priorities, and Palantir's Department of Homeland Security work has drawn scrutiny from Democrats. Sen. Ron Wyden (D-Ore.) had placed holds on Plankey pending release of a public telecom cybersecurity report. Republicans hold the Senate until January, and the nomination itself pauses the statutory clock on Andersen's acting tenure.
