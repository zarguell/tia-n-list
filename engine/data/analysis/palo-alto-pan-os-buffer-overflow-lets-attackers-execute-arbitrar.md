Palo Alto PAN-OS carries two tracked buffer overflows: CVE-2026-0310, a CWE-787 out-of-bounds write in XML processing disclosed on 9 September 2026, and CVE-2026-111497, a Brotli decoder overflow with a CVSS of 9.2 affecting PAN-OS 12.2, 12.1 and 11.2, confirmed by Palo Alto on 26 October 2026. CVE-2026-0310 exposes both the management web interface and the dataplane interface; an unauthenticated attacker with network access can trigger root-level arbitrary code execution on PA-Series hardware firewalls, while VM-Series devices suffer only denial of service. The vendor advisory for CVE-2026-0310 assigns a high severity rating and notes no special device configuration is required for vulnerability, meaning exposure is purely a reachability question.

The exposure profile is worse than a typical firewall advisory because root execution on a perimeter PA-Series device gives an attacker the ability to alter security policies, intercept traffic, establish persistence, and pivot into protected networks; the event content explicitly notes these downstream risks. The score breakdown shows authority at 1.2, severity at 4.5, and breadth at 2.0, reflecting broad vendor acknowledgment rather than niche research, and the first_seen of 10 September 2026 arrived one day after Palo Alto's 9 September advisory, meaning public awareness was immediate but not instantaneous. This story demands priority not because exploitation telemetry is confirmed - the vendor states no malicious exploitation was known at disclosure - but because the attack surface is unauthenticated, the privilege gain is total, and the affected versions (PAN-OS 12.2, 12.1, 11.2) are actively maintained production releases.

Defenders should upgrade affected PAN-OS instances to the named fixed releases cited in the vendor advisory, restrict management interface access to trusted internal IP addresses as Palo Alto recommends, and treat both management and dataplane interfaces as exposed until patches are verified. For hunting, defenders should inspect XML-processing logs on PA-Series devices and monitor for abnormal Brotli-compressed traffic patterns; the event mentions no specific IoC names, so telemetry should focus on unexpected root-level process spawn from the firewall management plane and anomalous connections to management interfaces. Watch for any CISA KEV addition for CVE-2026-0310 or updated vendor hotfixes for CVE-2026-111497 that expand the affected version range beyond 12.2, 12.1 and 11.2.
