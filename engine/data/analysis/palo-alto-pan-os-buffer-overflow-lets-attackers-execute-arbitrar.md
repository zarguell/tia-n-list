Palo Alto Networks disclosed CVE-2026-0310, a high-severity buffer overflow in PAN-OS XML processing that allows unauthenticated, network-based attackers to execute arbitrary code with root privileges on PA-Series hardware firewalls through management or dataplane interfaces. VM-Series deployments are limited to a denial-of-service condition. The advisory was published on 9 September 2026, and the vendor stated at that time that no malicious exploitation was known. Exposure hinges on management interface reachability, since no special configuration is required for the flaw to be reachable.

A separate but related issue, CVE-2026-111497, is a CVSS 9.2 buffer overflow in the Brotli decoder affecting PAN-OS 12.2, 12.1, and 11.2. Palo Alto confirmed the flaw on 26 October 2026, published hotfixes, and shipped workarounds ahead of full patches, noting affected Brotli library versions 1.0.3 through 1.2.0 must be updated to 1.0.10, 1.1.1, or 1.2.1. The company was still investigating full product impact at publication.

Defenders should prioritize PA-Series firewall patching for CVE-2026-0310 and apply the PAN-OS hotfixes for CVE-2026-111497, while restricting management interface access and verifying no unauthorized configuration or root-level changes occurred in the interim.
