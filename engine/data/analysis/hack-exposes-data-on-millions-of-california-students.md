Hackers stole records for millions of California public school students through a breach at testing contractor ETS, which runs an SFTP file-transfer site for the state Department of Education. The exposed data covers names, addresses, demographic details and disability status for students enrolled over the past three school years; the incident involved a compromised login rather than a direct CDE system breach.

The California Department of Education and ETS both notified families on October 16. Multiple outlets (Politico, LA Times, GalaxyWarden) confirm the scope spans roughly 7 million student records across the state. Watch for follow-up identity-protection offers, any confirmation of observed misuse of the disability-status fields, and whether ETS discloses the exact access window and whether additional districts outside the initial notification are affected.
