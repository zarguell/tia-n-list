Paradigm Healthcare Services disclosed that unauthorized access to its local network affected more than 970,000 Medi-Cal members. The intrusion occurred between October 8, 2025 and October 15, 2025; Paradigm became aware on October 13, 2025. Compromised files contained names, dates of birth, gender, and Medi-Cal member identification numbers. The company processes billing for California's Medicaid program under a data use agreement with DHCS.

Notification letters are dated September 14, 2026, roughly 11 months after detection. Paradigm states there is no evidence the information has been used for fraud or identity theft, and its billing platform Student Health Network was not impacted. The notice does not name a threat actor, explain the initial access vector, or confirm whether data was exfiltrated. The company took systems offline, notified law enforcement, and engaged external incident response experts.
