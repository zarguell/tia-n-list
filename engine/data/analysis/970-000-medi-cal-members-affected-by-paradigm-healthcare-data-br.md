Paradigm Healthcare Services notified the US Department of Health and Human Services that a breach affecting 970,000 Medi-Cal members occurred between October 8 and October 15, 2025. The company became aware of unauthorized network access on October 13, 2025, but did not finalize the determination of data exposure until August 18, 2026, with notification letters dated September 14, 2026.

Compromised files contained names, dates of birth, gender, and Medi-Cal member identification numbers. Paradigm stated it has no evidence the information was used for fraud or identity theft. The online billing platform, Student Health Network (SHN), was not impacted. The 11-month delay between detection and notification raises concerns about disclosure timelines, though Paradigm engaged outside incident response experts and notified law enforcement.
