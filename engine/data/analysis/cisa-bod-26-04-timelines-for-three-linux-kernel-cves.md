CISA BOD 26-04 adds CVE-2025-39682, CVE-2025-39964, and CVE-2026-53266 to KEV with a 3-day remediation deadline that passed September 21. CVE-2025-39682 is the highest severity; CVE-2026-53266 affects Netfilter ebtables SNAT; CVE-2025-39964 is also actively exploited. Red Hat updated advisories confirming public exploits exist. Organizations with exposed Linux assets should treat the deadline as expired and patch immediately, using Qualys context to identify affected systems.
