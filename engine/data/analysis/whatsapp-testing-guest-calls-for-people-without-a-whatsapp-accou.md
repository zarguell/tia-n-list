WhatsApp is developing a guest-calling feature that would let people without WhatsApp accounts join end-to-end encrypted calls through a browser-based link. The capability, first reported by WABetaInfo and still under development, extends WhatsApp's existing call-link functionality to unregistered users who can join via a web interface without installing the app.

Under the proposed model, a host generates a call link from the Calls tab and shares it externally. The recipient opens the link in a browser, entering a WhatsApp Web-based call interface. WhatsApp reportedly generates a unique identifier for that browser session and derives encryption material from it, maintaining end-to-end encryption even though one participant has no persistent account.

This mirrors WhatsApp's previously reported guest-chat function for text conversations. From a security perspective, the feature expands WhatsApp's encrypted communication surface to unauthenticated browser sessions, which introduces new trust and session-management questions. The design must handle ephemeral session identity, browser-based key derivation, and potential abuse for anonymous communication without weakening the encryption model.
