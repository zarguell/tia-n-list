Oracle Health's breach tally for the legacy Cerner systems has climbed to nearly 20 million people, according to a Bloomberg report citing the Texas attorney general. Earlier filings and patient notifications had listed much lower counts. The breach traces to February 2025, when attackers used stolen customer credentials to access a legacy server not yet migrated to Oracle Cloud. Oracle has not publicly confirmed the new total and declined to comment to Bloomberg. The figure reinforces the scale of healthcare data exposure when legacy EHR infrastructure is not promptly decommissioned. Monitor for an updated Oracle advisory or state-level disclosure that confirms the exact count and affected entities.
