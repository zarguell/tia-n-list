Oracle Health's breach tally climbed to nearly 20 million patients, according to a Texas attorney general report cited by Bloomberg. The figure exceeds earlier filings and patient notifications. The incident involved Oracle's legacy Cerner systems, which Oracle acquired in June 2022 for roughly $28.3 billion. Attackers used stolen customer credentials to access an old legacy server after January 22, 2025, then copied data to a remote server. Oracle began alerting healthcare customers in March 2025. A threat actor identified only as "Andrew" made extortion attempts but has not been linked to any established ransomware gang. The updated count means the breach is among the largest health-sector data exposures reported this year.

What to watch: whether the Texas attorney general releases the full report, whether additional states update their breach notices with the revised count, and whether Oracle clarifies why the 20 million figure differs from earlier disclosures. No CVE applies, but the long dwell time between the January 2025 intrusion and the March 2025 notification remains a compliance concern.
