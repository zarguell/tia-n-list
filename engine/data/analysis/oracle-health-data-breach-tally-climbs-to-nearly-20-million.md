A 2025 cyberattack on Oracle Health's legacy Cerner systems compromised the personal and medical information of nearly 20 million people, far exceeding earlier breach disclosures, according to Bloomberg citing a Texas attorney general filing. About 3 million Texas residents were affected. Unauthorized access began on or around January 22, 2025 and was identified around February 20, 2025, after an attacker used stolen customer credentials to access an old legacy server not yet migrated to Oracle Cloud. Oracle began alerting healthcare customers in March 2025 and declined to comment on the total number of affected individuals.

Extortion attempts against affected hospitals came from an individual threat actor known as "Andrew," who had not claimed links to any established ransomware gang. Notification was delayed at federal law enforcement request. Healthcare organizations using Oracle Health or legacy Cerner systems should verify their notification status and review access controls on unmigrated legacy infrastructure.
