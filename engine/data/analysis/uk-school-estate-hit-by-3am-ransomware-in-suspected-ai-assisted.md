A 3AM ransomware affiliate encrypted about 37 systems at a UK school estate. Bitdefender researchers observed signs of AI-assisted command retries in a sequence of slightly modified commands issued at regular intervals of roughly 40 to 100 seconds. The attackers used a modified public PowerShell tool to extract and decrypt credentials from three Veeam backup servers and copied a Chrome database. Bitdefender could not confirm the exact model or whether a person copied instructions, but described the behavior as an agentic loop.

This is a notable development because it documents hybrid human-plus-automated ransomware operations rather than fully autonomous attacks. The concrete artifacts include the 37 encrypted systems, the three Veeam backup servers targeted for credential extraction, and the 40-to-100-second retry intervals observed by Bitdefender. The malicious activity spanned September 10 to 19, 2025, and the initial access method remains unknown.
