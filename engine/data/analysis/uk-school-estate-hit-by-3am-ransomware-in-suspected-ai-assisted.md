A 3AM ransomware affiliate hit a UK school estate between September 10 and 19, 2026, encrypting about 37 of nearly 50 machines reached from an unmanaged host. The attacker used a modified public PowerShell tool to extract and decrypt credentials from three Veeam backup servers, copied a Chrome credential database, and deployed Mimikatz and LSASS memory-dumping tools, then created two backdoor domain administrator accounts named library and svc_bkpmon. Bitdefender researchers could not establish the initial access method.

Bitdefender suspects AI assistance based on a sequence of repeated commands slightly modified at each attempt and issued at roughly regular 40 to 100 second intervals, consistent with an agentic loop, though they could not identify the model, whether it ran locally or remotely, or whether a human relayed between the implant and the model. The hands-on phase ran at human pace, suggesting a hybrid human-AI operation rather than machine-speed automation. Financial data was stolen before encryption.

Watch for whether the initial access vector is later identified and for more hybrid AI-assisted intrusions of this kind in 2027, since the technique requires no first-class infrastructure and is executable with a local model.
