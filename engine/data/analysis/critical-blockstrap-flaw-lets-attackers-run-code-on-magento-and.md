The BlockStrap vulnerability (CVE-2026-103849) is a CVSS 10.0 unauthenticated remote-code-execution flaw in Magento and Adobe Commerce through version 2.4.9 (B2B through 1.5.3). Adobe's APSB26-153 bulletin from October 13 assigns priority 2 (patch within 30 days) and describes improper neutralization of special elements in the template engine leading to arbitrary code execution. Sansec's October 15 analysis found four potential attack paths in under 24 hours and warns Adobe's priority-2 rating underestimates urgency, comparing complexity to the StyleSmuggler zero-day that moved from hotfix to mass exploitation in three days. BleepingComputer's October 16 report confirms no in-the-wild exploitation had been observed at publication but expects threat actors to move quickly, and notes a plain Composer install is insufficient without Adobe's isolated BlockStrap patch plus the earlier StyleSmuggler hotfix.

The store covers versions up to 2.4.9, and the canonical event (desk:be84ab45d8f0) names Sansec, the CVSS 10.0 score, the affected version range, and the four attack paths found in under 24 hours. The new Mastodon event (masto:117458024548809868) links the same BleepingComputer article, so it reinforces the same narrative rather than introducing a separate claim. What to watch: whether exploitation appears in the wild given Sansec's comparison to StyleSmuggler's rapid escalation, whether Adobe raises the priority beyond priority 2, and whether merchants apply both the BlockStrap patch and the earlier hotfix rather than relying on Composer alone. No CISA KEV or observed victim counts are reported yet.
