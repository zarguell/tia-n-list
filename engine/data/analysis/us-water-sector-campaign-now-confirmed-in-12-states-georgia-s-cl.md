A coordinated campaign against US water and wastewater utilities has now been confirmed in at least 12 states, starting with 30+ Minnesota community systems hit July 26-27 and expanding through new confirmations in Georgia (Clayton County Water Authority), Michigan, South Dakota, New Jersey (Cape May, Woodbine) and unnamed Alabama utilities. Clayton County reported a temporary disruption with reduced water pressure, and Michigan and Rapid City, South Dakota confirmed incidents involving wastewater lift stations. The FBI's operational details describe attackers targeting internet-exposed Rockwell MicroLogix 1100/1400 PLCs, changing IP addresses and setting passwords to cause loss of view or function, with at least one organization finding modified ladder-logic project files.

CISA issued a sector alert documenting the tradecraft: attackers lock out operators by modifying PLC passwords, disconnect devices by changing IPs, and often reach them through undocumented cellular modems installed by operators, vendors or integrators. Censys counted roughly 10,000 internet-exposed Rockwell, Siemens and Schneider PLCs, with 4,100+ Rockwell hosts and 4,100 Siemens hosts reachable over the public internet and nearly half of exposed Rockwell devices reachable over cellular networks, matching the Minnesota vector. CISA added CVE-2021-22681, a critical (CVSS 9.8) authentication bypass in Rockwell Logix controllers with no vendor patch, to KEV in March 2026 after confirmed exploitation.

Attribution to Iran remains preliminary and is not officially confirmed by the US government, but the activity aligns with the July 22 update to CISA advisory AA26-097A, which links this profile to Iranian-affiliated actors including CyberAv3ngers and Handala targeting Rockwell, Schneider Electric and Siemens PLCs. The pattern matches Iranian targeting of Israeli water facilities via cellular routers in 2020. No significant drinking-water safety impact has been reported, but loss of pressure can allow untreated groundwater to seep into pipes, which is why several utilities issued boil-water notices and operated manually.

Watch for: new state confirmations and whether any utility reports drinking-water contamination, the release of the WaterISAC TLP:Amber alignment report into public reporting, KEV additions tied to this campaign, and whether the cellular-modem initial-access vector shows up in non-water sectors. Water operators should audit for internet-exposed and cellular-connected PLCs, enforce VPN-gated remote access with changed credentials, keep clean PLC image backups for password-lockout recovery, and monitor MicroLogix 1100/1400 devices for IP, password and ladder-logic drift.
