Recorded Future published "Mines, Minds, and Machines: The Journey of AI," a report tracing the full supply chain of the fourth industrial revolution from raw minerals to deployed AI systems. The chain runs minerals to chips, chips to data centers, data centers to model training, and models to physical systems. The report argues that geopolitical rivalry and cyber operations now run along every link of that chain, making the AI economy a contested infrastructure story rather than a software-only one.

The relevance to threat intelligence is the attack surface spread across the chain. Each link is an industrial dependency: mining and refining, semiconductor fabrication, data center construction and power, model development, and edge deployment into physical systems. Disruption at any link cascades, and the report positions that as a strategic targeting consideration for both state actors and cybercriminals.

This is analytical framing rather than an incident report, useful for understanding where AI-related cyber risk sits and how supply chain dependencies shape exposure. Organizations building on AI infrastructure should read it as a map of where their dependencies concentrate and where a single compromised supplier or disrupted facility has outsize impact.
