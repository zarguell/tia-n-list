CISA advisory AA26-293a covers SafePay ransomware targeting businesses across North and South America and Europe. The campaign affects over 600 organizations, including critical infrastructure operators in multiple regions. SafePay operates as file-locking malware demanding payment after encrypting business data. Organizations should reference the CISA advisory for indicators and mitigation steps. The scope and cross-border targeting make this a high-priority incident response case for affected sectors, with emphasis on backup verification, network segmentation, and rapid isolation of compromised endpoints.
