CISA, in a joint report with the FBI, USSS, German and UK agencies, says the SafePay ransomware gang has breached over 600 critical infrastructure organizations worldwide since August 2024. Initial access includes credential exposure, brute force, and exploitation of VPNs, particularly Mullvad VPN endpoints. The advisory urges MFA, identity and access management, and patching VPN flaws. Paying ransoms does not guarantee file recovery.

SafePay is described as one of the most active ransomware groups, with victims across North and South America, Europe, and Australia. The event set includes the joint report, earlier CISA advisory, and an update confirming the 600-organization count. There are no CVEs assigned to the ransomware itself, but the advisory highlights VPN vulnerabilities as entry points.

Watch for any CISA follow-up naming specific exploited VPN CVEs or providing IOCs. Organizations should review Mullvad VPN usage and enforce MFA on all VPN access points.
