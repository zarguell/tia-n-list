Storm-0940, a China-state aligned actor, used the agentic AI penetration testing framework ARTEX to automate attacks against hundreds of organizations globally beginning as early as September 2026. Several of those attacks resulted in privileged access to cloud infrastructure, including cases where the time from initial targeting to privileged access was under three hours. Microsoft assesses Storm-0940 was responsible for the recently disclosed ConnectWise supply chain compromise of a multi-tenant application, and that ARTEX accelerated abuse of the compromised application to authenticate into Microsoft 365 customer environments through already-granted application access.

The story matters because it shows threat actors integrating agentic AI into existing offensive workflows to scale operations exponentially, working continuously and pursuing multiple breach paths in parallel. That compresses the window defenders have to detect and respond to complex supply chain compromises.

Watch for follow-up advisories from Microsoft and ConnectWise on affected customers and indicators, and whether additional organizations report ARTEX-driven intrusions. Defenders should review application consent grants and privileged access to Microsoft 365 tenants.
