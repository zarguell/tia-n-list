Mandiant has been investigating BREEZE COMET (previously UNC5669), a financially motivated threat actor breaching Brazilian financial services, retail, and eCommerce organizations since 2024. The group overlaps with activity publicly reported as Plump Spider and SHADOW-AETHER-064. Their target set is specific: organizations with direct access to banking software, payment APIs, and Brazil's payment infrastructure including Pix, STR, and Boleto platforms.

BREEZE COMET uses password spraying, voice phishing, malicious remote-management tools, and rogue hardware devices to gain initial access. Once inside, they compromise Active Directory and cloud accounts to obtain persistent access to payment-processing systems. The group then submits hundreds of fraudulent transfers in tightly timed waves, leveraging stolen credentials and privileged accounts against Brazil's National Financial System Network. Completing these fraud operations requires valid mTLS certificates and knowledge of the victim's payment processes, making the attacks more complex than typical phishing but potentially far more damaging.

The AI-assisted malware component of their toolkit is notable but secondary to the core tradecraft: social engineering combined with deep understanding of Brazilian payment rails. Organizations in the Brazilian financial ecosystem should review access to payment platforms, monitor for anomalous transfer patterns, and harden voice-phishing resilience among staff with payment-system access.
