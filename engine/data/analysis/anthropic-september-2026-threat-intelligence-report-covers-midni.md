Anthropic's September 2026 Threat Intelligence Report covers three active campaigns: Midnight Blizzard (also tracked as GTG-20006), JackPoterz ClickFix with DNS hijacking, and DarkSword. The report represents Anthropic's expanding role in threat intelligence, moving beyond AI safety disclosures to cover the broader threat landscape affecting AI infrastructure and users.

Midnight Blizzard is a well-documented Russia-linked threat actor known for targeting government and technology organizations. The JackPoterz campaign combines ClickFix social engineering with DNS hijacking, a technique that tricks users into executing commands under the guise of troubleshooting browser or connection issues. DarkSword rounds out the report as another active campaign under tracking.

This report signals that AI companies are becoming standard contributors to the threat intelligence ecosystem. Anthropic is publishing actor profiles and campaign analysis alongside its AI-specific safety disclosures. For threat intelligence teams, this adds another source to the mix. The intersection of AI platform targeting and traditional threat actor campaigns is the emerging space to watch, as attackers increasingly target the AI tools that organizations are integrating into their workflows.
