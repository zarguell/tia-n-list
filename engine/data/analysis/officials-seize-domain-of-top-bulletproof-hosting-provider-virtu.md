U.S. authorities seized virtualine.net, the domain of Virtualine, a Russian-speaking bulletproof hosting provider active in crypto-related crime. The Justice Department said the service knowingly hosted a forum distributing child sexual abuse material and ignored repeated notices from NCMEC, law enforcement, and a victim's attorney. TRM Labs reported Virtualine received the highest illicit-attributed value in 2026 among tracked bulletproof hosts, with a Russian drug marketplace accounting for roughly 44% of direct payments, a sextortion scheme for nearly 17%, a cryptocurrency mixer for almost 13%, ransomware for nearly 5%, and malware for 2%.

The seizure is significant because it removes a top-tier bulletproof host linked to ransomware, CSAM, and cryptocurrency laundering, not just low-level spam. Watch whether the operator relocates to new aliases (Secury, Massgod, Spamhouse, Lanedo, Omegatech) or whether the forum content moves to replacement infrastructure. The case also highlights the value of blockchain tracing combined with international law enforcement cooperation (Germany, Netherlands, UK, private partners OverRIPE and Lumen).
