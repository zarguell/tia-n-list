Authorities seized the domain of Virtualine, a Russian-speaking bulletproof hosting provider, according to a Justice Department announcement reported by Cyberscoop. Virtualine hosted infrastructure for large-scale sextortion schemes, ransomware operations, data theft, malware hosting, and money laundering, and it also facilitated the distribution of child sexual abuse material. Blockchain researcher TRM Labs noted that Virtualine received almost one-fifth of all illicit-attributed inflow among tracked bulletproof hosts in 2026 to date, with a Russian drug marketplace accounting for roughly 44% of its direct payments.

The seizure is part of broader enforcement against the hosting layer supporting cybercrime. The event confirms the domain seizure and links Virtualine to cryptocurrency-related crime, ransomware at nearly 5% of payments, and a cryptocurrency mixer at almost 13%. The provider's infrastructure is now under Justice Department control, disrupting operations tied to a significant share of bulletproof-hosted criminal traffic this year.
