US authorities seized the domain of Virtualine, a Russian-speaking bulletproof hosting provider that facilitated large-scale criminal activity including ransomware, malware hosting, data theft, and cryptocurrency-related crime. The Justice Department said Virtualine also distributed child sexual abuse material and processed significant illicit cryptocurrency inflows in 2026.

TRM Labs tracked Virtualine as the most active bulletproof provider for crypto-related crime this year. Seizures of hosting infrastructure disrupt ongoing campaigns but rarely eliminate them; operators typically migrate to new domains and resumes. Monitoring for successor infrastructure should be a priority for threat-intel teams tracking ransomware and dark-market operations.
