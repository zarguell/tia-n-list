Authorities seized the domain of Virtualine, a Russian-speaking bulletproof hosting provider linked to ransomware, malware hosting, money laundering, and child sexual abuse material distribution. The Justice Department stated Virtualine received almost one-fifth of all illicit-attributed inflow among tracked bulletproof hosting providers in 2026. A Russian drug marketplace accounted for nearly 44% of direct payments, a sextortion scheme nearly 17%, and a cryptocurrency mixer almost 13%.

The seizure disrupts a significant node in the cybercrime supply chain, but operators will likely migrate to alternate hosting. Law enforcement and threat-intelligence teams should track infrastructure migration patterns and update blocklists based on new Virtualine-associated domains or IP ranges.
