Officials and reporters are converging on rushed patching of AI-discovered bugs as one factor in a week of service failures. The Washington Sun reported that Wall Street executives and the NSA told members of Congress that the outages were caused by AI-driven patches. Testimony coverage from implicator.ai named the NSA network failure, the FAA ground stop and Microsoft in the same discussion, and CBS News tied the NSA network outage to a software vulnerability. The claim has political weight, but it is not a single technical root cause established across every incident.

The underlying events differ. TechCrunch reported a daylong Microsoft outage that began in Outlook and Teams before spreading to Copilot, Defender and Intune, with Kentik measuring about 18 hours of downtime. Microsoft's preliminary report named a Teams gateway failure, roughly 14 percent of that gateway already out of service and a latent bug from July, and said a rollback was not possible. Microsoft also said there was no evidence of malicious activity and no link to cyber or AI agents. CNBC reported that the FAA stopped departures for more than four hours on 13 November after telecom routers were overloaded with data, disrupting more than 10,000 flights, about 1.5 million passengers and roughly 600 diversions, with Homeland Security saying the failure appeared mechanically driven.

The patch-pressure example is PHP2Shell, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. Fortune reported that GPT-5.6 Sol and other AI tools helped find the pair, which carries CVSS v4.0 scores of 9.2 each. Cloudflare saw tens of thousands of exploit attempts within 45 minutes of reports breaking on 20 October, hundreds of thousands within two hours and millions by the evening of 21 October, while AWS reported attempts before disclosure. Watch the final Microsoft report, the Congressional record and any agency finding that connects a specific patch to a specific outage. Vendor statements so far support patch urgency, not a blanket claim that AI-assisted fixes caused every outage.
