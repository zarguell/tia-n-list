Reporting on this week's widespread technology outages has converged on a claim that rapid patching of AI-discovered vulnerabilities caused at least part of the disruption. The Washington Sun reports that Wall Street executives and the National Security Agency told members of Congress that this was the cause. Cyberscoop frames the explanation as a growing consensus rather than a settled finding.

The available text of the report cuts off before describing the specific failures, affected services or evidence the officials provided. That matters because outage attribution has been contested: the same period produced unrelated service failures and speculation about cyberattacks. Until the congressional testimony or a full report is available, the causal claim is best treated as an assertion by named officials, not a confirmed technical root cause.
