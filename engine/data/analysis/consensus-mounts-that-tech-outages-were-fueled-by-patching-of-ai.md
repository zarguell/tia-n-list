A cluster of recent outages is being discussed in Congress as a possible consequence of emergency patching for AI-assisted vulnerability discoveries. A Washington Sun item relayed through Mastodon says bank executives and the NSA told members of Congress that this week's widespread outages were caused by patches. The underlying patch push is documented: Fortune reported the PHP Foundation's emergency disclosure of CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, together called php2shell, with GPT-5.6 Sol and other AI tools credited in discovery. Cloudflare saw tens of thousands of exploit attempts within 45 minutes of the 20 October reports, hundreds of thousands within two hours and millions by the evening of 21 October.

The outages themselves have separate documented causes in the reporting. TechCrunch reported that Microsoft's daylong Outlook and Teams outage spread to Copilot, Defender and Intune, with Kentik measuring about 18 hours of downtime; Microsoft said there was no evidence of malicious activity and that the incident was not related to cyber or AI agents. Its preliminary post-incident report cited a Teams gateway failure, roughly 14 percent of that gateway's capacity already out of service, and a latent bug from July. CNBC reported the 13 November FAA ground stop after telecommunications routers were overloaded with data, disrupting more than 10,000 flights, with Cirium counting 11,000 scheduled departures after 4 p.m. ET and roughly 600 diversions. Homeland Security said there was no indication of a cyberattack.

PBS reported officials in Britain, Finland and Japan describing limited disruption, precautionary downtime or patch instructions, with the FBI advising sites to go offline if they could not patch the PHP flaws immediately. Newisty and Mother Jones posts picked up the same claim on 19 November, framing the NSA and banking outages as fallout from rushed AI-assisted fixes, but neither adds a technical link between a patch and a specific service failure. The gap between the congressional claim and the vendor post-incident findings is what to watch: none of the event content ties a specific patch to a specific outage failure.
