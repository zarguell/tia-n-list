UAT-11985 runs an adversary-in-the-middle phishing campaign against Taiwan research affiliates since mid-2026. The actor uses fake event invitations to relay victims through cloned Google Forms, harvesting Google credentials. Cisco Talos documented the activity; the latest social reference confirms the campaign remains active. There are no CVEs tied to this campaign. Watch for expanded targeting beyond Taiwan research organizations and for any credential-reuse follow-on intrusions.
