Mitel MiCollab's AWV component is affected by an unauthenticated authentication bypass tracked as CVE-2026-133324. The advisory summary says versions up to 10.3 are vulnerable and urges prompt patching. A follow-up report says CISA added the MiCollab flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, moving the issue beyond a theoretical access-control weakness to a vulnerability listed as exploited.

The reports do not supply a fixed release, affected configuration, CVSS score, victim count or attack timeline. That limits claims about the depth of access, but not the priority of the signal: an unauthenticated bypass on collaboration infrastructure can undermine the boundary between an exposed service and trusted users. CISA's exploited-vulnerability listing is the concrete confirmation in the available material. It should not be inflated into a claim about a named actor or a specific intrusion.

Operators should identify MiCollab AWV deployments at or below 10.3, check whether they are externally reachable and apply the vendor patch when the fixed version is confirmed. Until then, reducing exposure is the practical interim step. Watch for Mitel's patched-version details, CISA's remediation deadline and primary-source incident reporting that establishes exploitation prerequisites or observed access beyond authentication bypass.
