Mitel MiCollab's Audio, Web and Video Conferencing (AWV) component has an unauthenticated authentication bypass, CVE-2026-133324 (MTLVULN-1877), caused by improper authentication handling. Mitel rates it Medium at CVSS 3.1 5.3 and lists supported MiCollab 10.3 (10.3.0.18) and earlier as affected; it permits unauthorized actions inside the application, not unrestricted file access. CISA added it to KEV on November 25 with a November 28 federal remediation deadline, alongside four sibling AWV flaws. In Accenture's confirmed intrusion, the bypass occurred at T+1 minute, two uploads were accepted at T+9, a web-shell command ran at T+12, root was reached by T+30, and MiCollab's LDAP connector credentials were used against a domain controller at T+32.

The Medium score understates the enterprise consequence because the observed chain crosses the application boundary: MiCollab stores directory credentials, and the actor used them to enter Active Directory, Kerberoast service accounts, and reach backup and database servers roughly 20 hours after initial access. Accenture observed the same actor infrastructure at a second organization from November 9, establishing multiple victims but not prevalence; no source supplies an installed-base count. Any internet-facing MiCollab 10.3.0.18 or earlier instance with AWV published through a reverse proxy or MiVoice Border Gateway belongs in the exposed set, because a reverse proxy did not stop this intrusion. The directory pivot and pre-patch exploitation make this more urgent than a stand-alone login flaw.

Upgrade to MiCollab 10.3 (10.3.0.18) and apply the KB000129195 patch, or upgrade to a later release; the base 10.3.0.18 build without the patch is not the fix. Until then, restrict external access to AWV and inventory every gateway, proxy, and WAF path that publishes it. Image MiCollab and gateway servers before patching or rebuilding, because the actor reset system logs and filtered web-shell requests from local web, error, and admin logs. Hunt upstream logs from November 6 for AWV CGI POSTs under `/awcuser/cgi-bin/`, follow-on `/pictures/` requests, `shell.asp`, HTTPS PUTs to `/bkup/` on `204.76.203[.]204`, and contacts with `204.76.203[.]216` or `204-76-203-204.sslip[.]io`. Also alert on MiCollab's LDAP connector account authenticating across many domain controllers, bursts of RC4 Kerberos service tickets, and `/tmp` artifacts such as `roast*.py`, `testcreds*.py`, and `*_batch_*.tgz`.

Watch for actor attribution, additional victims, and Mitel guidance on whether all five CVEs share the same prerequisites. Confirmed exploitation of the second victim through MISA-2026-0012, rather than an older flaw, would move the observed pre-disclosure window back to at least November 9.
