eMudhra is exiting the TLS business after it was caught using a certificate private key improperly. The Ars Technica report frames this as a disgraced CA stepping away from issuance after the private-key misuse undermined trust.

No new CVEs accompany the exit, but the underlying certificate compromise has direct implications for trust stores and browser vendors. Watch whether Chrome or Mozilla impose additional restrictions on remaining eMudhra-rooted certificates.
