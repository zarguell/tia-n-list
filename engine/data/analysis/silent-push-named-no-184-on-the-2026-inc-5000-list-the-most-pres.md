Silent Push announced it ranked No. 184 on the 2026 Inc. 5000, citing 1,744% three-year revenue growth. The company sells preemptive cybersecurity intelligence, tracking attacker infrastructure to identify campaigns before they launch, and lists U.S. government organizations, Fortune 100 companies, and global security vendors as customers.

This is a company announcement, so treat the growth figure as marketing until verified, but the underlying signal is real: demand for preemptive, infrastructure-level threat intelligence is expanding fast enough to sustain triple-digit growth. The market is voting for detection that is earlier and more automated than reactive IOC feeds.

The operational takeaway for defenders is to lean into attacker-infrastructure intelligence: new-domain telemetry, fast-flux and parking patterns, and adversary infrastructure correlation. That is where detection capability is heading, and it is where vendors are consolidating.
