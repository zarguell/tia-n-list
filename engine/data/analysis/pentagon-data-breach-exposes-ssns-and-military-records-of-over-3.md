Pentagon breach exposes records of over 3 million people. A Defense Manpower Data Center file-sharing vulnerability let unauthorized users access unencrypted files from October 2025 through July 2026. Compromised data includes Social Security numbers, names, dates of birth, contact details, and military occupational information. The Pentagon patched the flaw and began notifications, but the mix of SSNs with biographical data creates lasting identity-theft risk. Watch for follow-on phishing or fraud campaigns targeting affected service members and families.
