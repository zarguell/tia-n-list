A cyberattack against government contractor BAE Systems has disabled the notice system supporting US federal bankruptcy courts. The Administrative Office of the US Courts confirmed that all BNC systems and related bankruptcy noticing processes are unavailable, including bnc.uscourts.gov, bankruptcynotices.uscourts.gov, notices.uscourts.gov, and the National Creditor Registration Service. Production and distribution of bankruptcy notices halted. BAE is actively investigating and working to restore operations. BAE Systems is Europe's largest defense contractor and a major IT and cybersecurity provider to military, intelligence, and civilian government agencies.

The incident affects judicial infrastructure rather than a private enterprise, making it a government service disruption. The Administrative Office is coordinating with impacted courts. No attribution or ransomware claim was confirmed in the initial press release. The full scope beyond bankruptcy notices remains unclear, so watch for updates on restoration timelines and any data-access claims from BAE or the judiciary.
