Canada's Cyber Centre advisory AV26-1035, dated October 13, 2026, bundles Ivanti's October fixes: Ivanti Connect Secure versions 25.1.1.1 and prior, 25.2.2 and prior, and 22.8R2.4 and prior; Ivanti Neurons Patch for Intune builds older than September 16, 2026; and Xtraction 2026.2.1 and prior. The advisory links Ivanti PSIRT notices for Connect Secure (CVE-2026-104935), Xtraction (multiple CVEs), and the Neurons Patch for Intune October update (CVE-2026-109412).

The headline item is CVE-2026-104935: CISA added it to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on October 13, 2026, the same day the advisory published. A KEV listing means confirmed in-the-wild exploitation and a federal remediation deadline, and Connect Secure is an internet-facing VPN appliance, so exposure is direct rather than hypothetical. This advisory is the Canadian Centre's rollup of the same Ivanti patch wave tracked in the separate active-exploitation story.

Organizations running Connect Secure should treat the patched builds as urgent, since unpatched 25.x and 22.8R2.4-and-prior instances are the KEV population CISA is pointing at. Worth watching: whether exploitation spreads beyond the "limited number of customers" Ivanti described, and whether the Neurons Patch for Intune and Xtraction issues draw their own exploitation as admins work through the bundle.
