An international law enforcement operation dismantled a network used by the KillSec ransomware group. The Swiss Federal Police announced the action on 30 September 2026, with coordination across Bern and other partners. KillSec operates as Ransomware-as-a-Service, with over 250 documented compromises, a $250 affiliate fee, and infection vectors through misconfigured AWS S3 buckets and CVE-2025-31161.
