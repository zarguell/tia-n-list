KillSec, a Ransomware-as-a-Service operation active since mid-2024, has been disrupted in a coordinated international law enforcement operation. Authorities in Spain, the UK, and Romania arrested three alleged affiliates, and reports describe a dismantled network. Prior analysis documents over 250 compromises, a $250 entry fee with 88% affiliate retention, and primary infection through misconfigured AWS S3 buckets and CVE-2025-31161. A separate September 2025 incident affected Brazilian healthcare vendor MedicSolution: over 34 GB of data with 94,818 files including unredacted patient pictures and lab results were exposed via exposed AWS cloud buckets, impacting downstream facilities. The group reached over 250 victims, with geographic concentration in India, the USA, and Latin America.
