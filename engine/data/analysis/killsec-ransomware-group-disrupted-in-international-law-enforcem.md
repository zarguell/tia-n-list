A coordinated international operation on 30 September 2026 dismantled the computer network of the KillSec ransomware group. Swiss Federal Police and Europol partners announced the disruption from Bern. KillSec operates as Ransomware-as-a-Service, formalized in June 2024, with over 250 documented compromises, a $250 affiliate entry fee, 88% retention, and infection vectors exploiting misconfigured AWS S3 buckets and CVE-2025-31161. Prior analysis from Resecurity (September 2025) documented a KillSec attack on MedicSolution, a Brazilian healthcare vendor, exposing over 34 GB of data (94,818 files) including unredacted patient pictures and lab results affecting downstream clinics. Halcyon updated its KillSec profile in November 2025 recording over 250 victims, geographic concentration in India, USA, and Latin America. The disruption removes active infrastructure but does not recover previously stolen healthcare or enterprise data.
