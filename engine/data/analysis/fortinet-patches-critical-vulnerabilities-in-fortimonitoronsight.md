Fortinet disclosed and patched a critical vulnerability in its FortiPAM Chrome extension, tracked as CVE-2026-84388 with a CVSS score of 9.1. The flaw, discovered by security researcher James Arnott, allowed a malicious website to manipulate browser proxy settings, open attacker-controlled tabs, and exfiltrate screen recordings from the extension used by over one million users. The attack chain exploited a webRequest listener that automatically added any host requesting a specific API path to a trusted-server list without validating the initiator, combined with the extension's external messaging configuration that could be abused to inject commands. Fortinet also patched FortiMonitorOnSight versions 7.2.0 through 7.2.7 and FortiSandbox versions 4.4.0 through 4.4.8 and 5.0.0 through 5.0.5. The Cyber Centre advisory AV26-898 covered the full batch of affected products including FortiOS 7.6.1 through 7.6.6 and FortiProxy 7.6.2 through 7.6.6. Organizations should update all affected Fortinet products immediately and audit for signs that the Chrome extension's trusted-server list was tampered with.
