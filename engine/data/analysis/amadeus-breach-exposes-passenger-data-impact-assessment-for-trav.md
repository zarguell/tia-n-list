Amadeus, the shared passenger name record and ticketing provider used by many airlines, confirmed a data breach that exposed passenger booking data across multiple carriers, including Cathay Pacific, Korean Air, Asiana and All Nippon Airways. Access reportedly came from valid credentials on an Amadeus system rather than a software vulnerability, and the exposed information includes passenger booking records and related travel details.

Affected travelers should watch for targeted phishing or fraud using booking references and personal details, and monitor airline notices for the scope of their exposure. The breach highlights concentration risk in shared travel infrastructure: one compromised provider can expose passenger data across several airlines at once.
