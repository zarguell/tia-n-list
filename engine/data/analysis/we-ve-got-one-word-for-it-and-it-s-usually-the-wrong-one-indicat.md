Trezor disclosed a security incident at Brevo, its third-party email marketing platform, which affected approximately 347,000 newsletter subscribers. On September 9, 2026, an unauthorized actor gained access to Brevo's system and used Trezor's account to send phishing emails to the full opt-in subscriber list. The emails used the subject line "Critical Security Alert: STM32 Entropy Vulnerability" and contained a malicious link prompting users to download an app that requested their wallet backup.

Trezor took down the malicious domain at the DNS level within 20 minutes of discovery, limiting exposure to roughly 2,500 people who had clicked the link before takedown. The email-sending function was disabled immediately. No Trezor product, wallet, or account system was compromised. Brevo holds no passwords, wallet data, or other personal information beyond email addresses.

The incident highlights supply chain risk in third-party communication platforms. While Trezor's core systems were untouched, the 347,000 exposed email addresses remain at risk for future phishing campaigns. Users who entered their wallet backup through the phishing link were urged to immediately move funds to a new wallet. This is not the first time a marketing platform breach has been weaponized against crypto users, and it underscores the need for crypto companies to evaluate the blast radius of their third-party integrations.
