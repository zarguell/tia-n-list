Sekoia's TDR team published part one of its FSB Matryoshka series on June 1, 2026, reconstructing a January 2026 Gamaredon intrusion chain aimed at Ukrainian government, military, and critical infrastructure. The chain opens with a weaponized XHTML file that pings a Supabase tracking pixel and HTML-smuggles a RAR archive exploiting CVE-2025-8088, the WinRAR path traversal patched in 7.13 on July 30, 2025. Extraction silently writes an HTA into the Startup folder; on next logon mshta.exe, disguised with a fake www.bbc.com@ credential prefix, pulls the next stage from Supabase. GammaWorm, a VBScript worm of over 20,000 obfuscated lines, hides its modules in NTFS Alternate Data Streams, persists through the RunOnce value ExplorerGuard and three scheduled tasks, and resolves C2 through Telegram, Telegra.ph, Teletype.in, and Cloudflare Workers dead drops. GammaSteel stages 71 DPAPI-encrypted modules in the Windows registry and pushes documents to S3-compatible storage. Sekoia assessed the campaign as still active at publication.

The notable fact is not the bug but its age. CVE-2025-8088 shipped with a patch on July 30, 2025, was exploited that same July by RomCom against European and Canadian targets per ESET, by a second actor documented by BI.ZONE, and was later tied by Google Threat Intelligence Group to Sandworm, Turla, Gamaredon, and China-nexus sets, yet it still opened a state espionage chain in January 2026. The binding constraint for defenders is therefore unpatched archive tooling, including third-party products that embed UnRAR.dll or its portable source, a dependency WinRAR's own release notes flag. A successful hit does not produce encryption or ransom demands; it produces permanent document espionage where every stage doubles as a backdoor, the worm spreads over USB and network shares to jump air gaps, and Sekoia judges a full system wipe the only safe remediation, an operationally expensive outcome for any government network.

Start with inventory: find WinRAR below 7.13 and any product shipping UnRAR.dll or portable UnRAR, then upgrade or isolate. Hunt for the stream names GTR, save, URL, LNK, and SERVER under %USERPROFILE% using dir /R or Get-Item -Stream, and alert on wscript.exe command lines carrying a colon path such as C:\\Users\\[USER]:GTR. Check for the RunOnce value ExplorerGuard, the HKCU\\Console values WindowsUpdates, WindowsResponby, WindowsDetect, URLTeletype, WindowsTelegra, URLTelegra, and IpURL, and the scheduled tasks DiskDiagnosticDataCollector, SilentCleanup, and SmartRetry. On the network, flag non-browser processes fetching supabase.co, graph.org, workers.dev, teletype.in, telegra.ph, or t.me, and curl.exe saving telegram.me/s/oberfarir to Temp\\cancelH0S.mpg. Two file hashes to sweep for: 1794369214b7f62e70a0485e61335c61 (xHTML lure) and 8e1624d110c090ff57d4b493a9107c66 (the ~.gif worm). Restrict USB storage where policy allows, and treat a confirmed host as a wipe, not a clean.

Two developments would move this assessment. Fresh exploitation of CVE-2025-8088 with new infrastructure would show the unpatched-archive-tooling problem persists beyond Ukraine and push a re-scan of any fleet still below 7.13. And any appearance of the GamaWiper payload outside the researcher sandboxes where ClearSky saw it would signal that Gamaredon's destructive option has moved from anti-analysis trick to operational tool.
