The FBI called on remaining ShinyHunters members to come forward after the arrest of alleged leader Pepijn van der Stap, 24, of Amsterdam. Dutch police arrested him on September 15 while he was on probation after serving part of a 2023 sentence for hacking and extortion. Authorities say he is suspected of playing a role in the group and of planning two murders abroad, with evidence found on his laptop.

According to the FBI, van der Stap has been involved in hacking over 140 organizations and collecting at least $70 million in extortion payments since 2025. The group targets third-party vendors in cloud-based platforms, steals sensitive data, and threatens publication. The arrest removes a key operator, but the FBI's public appeal signals that other members remain active and that law enforcement expects additional cooperation from insiders.
