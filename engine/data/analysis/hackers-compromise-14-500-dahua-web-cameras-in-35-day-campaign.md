Researchers at Hunt.io uncovered a 35-day campaign dubbed CameraSwarm that compromised 14,530 Dahua IP cameras, mostly in Ukraine and Russia. Attackers exploited CVE-2021-33044 and CVE-2021-33045 (authentication bypass vulnerabilities), brute-forced TCP port 37777, and used offline recovery codes from serial numbers for cloud-registered devices. The operation captured camera snapshots, exported credentials, and delivered results to Telegram and Dahua's SMART PSS platform. Hunt.io recovered 407 MB of operational data including source code, logs, and exploitation results.

The campaign ran from June 17 to July 22. Dahua's 2021 advisory confirms identity authentication bypass in multiple product series with fixed firmware versions listed for each family. The story carries 8 sources, a score of 5.8, and KEV confirmation. The scale and geographic focus suggest targeted espionage rather than opportunistic crime.

Monitor for additional victim disclosure, vendor patches applied to the affected Dahua series, and any evidence that the recovered data has been reused in follow-on campaigns. Network defenders should audit exposed camera infrastructure and restrict management interfaces.
