Rakuten Securities said data held in a test environment may have been taken out in an unauthorized-access incident. Japan Cyber Watch first described the exposure as login IDs for up to 11.25 million accounts. Japan Times reported that login IDs and branch codes for up to 11.25 million accounts may have been exposed, and a later report said the company was moving quickly to address a breach caused by unauthorized access.

The captured reports do not identify the intruder, the method, whether the test environment held production data, or whether passwords and account credentials were touched. The scale estimate and the data categories are the substantive facts so far. Watch for a company notice that confirms the final account count, separates login IDs from branch codes, and states whether any customer funds or trading actions were affected.
