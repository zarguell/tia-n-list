Rakuten Securities, one of Japan's largest online brokers, said data held in a test environment may have been taken out after unauthorized access this month. Japan Times reported on November 18 that login IDs and branch codes for 11.25 million accounts may have been exposed. Japan CyberWatch carried the initial disclosure, and a November 19 Japan Times follow-up said the company was working quickly to address the breach.

A November 24 report from The Perimeter Site describes the incident as a confirmed data breach affecting millions of customer accounts. Published coverage names login IDs and branch codes as the data at issue and does not describe exposure of account balances or trading data. Watch for Rakuten to move from "may have been taken" to a confirmed exfiltration count, for additional data fields in the disclosure, and for any revision to the 11.25 million figure. The accounts so far come from Japan CyberWatch, Japan Times and The Perimeter Site; confirmation in Rakuten's own notice should precede treating the larger number as final.
