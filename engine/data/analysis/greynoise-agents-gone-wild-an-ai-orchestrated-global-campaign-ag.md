GreyNoise documented an AI-orchestrated attack campaign targeting PaperCut NG/MF print management servers, exploiting CVE-2026-81578 and CVE-2026-82078. On August 31, 2026, a likely Russian-speaking malicious cyber actor deployed what GreyNoise characterized as hundreds of AI agents in a coordinated global campaign that compromised 440 servers across 395 organizations in 48 countries. The campaign represents one of the first documented cases of AI-orchestrated vulnerability exploitation at scale.

The use of AI agents to automate exploitation across hundreds of targets simultaneously marks a shift in attack methodology. Rather than manually targeting individual organizations, the actor deployed automated systems that could identify, exploit, and compromise PaperCut instances worldwide. The scale, 440 servers in 48 countries, from a single campaign originating on August 31, suggests the AI agents were effective at identifying and exploiting vulnerable instances.

PaperCut administrators should patch immediately for both CVEs. This campaign demonstrates that AI-augmented attacks are no longer theoretical. The ability to automate exploitation at this scale means vulnerable systems face near-immediate compromise once exploits are deployed, shrinking the window between disclosure and mass exploitation.
