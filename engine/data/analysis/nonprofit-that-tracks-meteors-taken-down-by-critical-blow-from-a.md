The American Meteor Society, a nonprofit organization that tracks and reports meteor sightings across North America, has been taken offline following a cyberattack described as a "critical blow." The organization's systems remain compromised, preventing normal operations including public reporting and data collection services. No specific attack vector or threat actor attribution has been disclosed.

The attack demonstrates that nonprofit organizations and scientific research entities are not immune to cyber threats despite lacking the financial profile of typical ransomware targets. The timing during meteor season increases operational impact, as real-time sighting reports from citizen scientists cannot be processed or validated.

This incident follows a broader pattern of attacks against scientific and research organizations. Nonprofits should ensure incident response plans account for extended downtime scenarios and that backup systems can sustain operations during recovery.
