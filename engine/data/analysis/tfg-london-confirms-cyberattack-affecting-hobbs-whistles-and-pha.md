TFG London, parent of Hobbs, Whistles, and Phase Eight, confirmed a cybersecurity incident affecting the IT systems supporting its UK retail brands. The company disclosed the disruption in a stock exchange announcement on October 13, 2026, identifying the security event on October 11. Several systems remain unavailable as of the latest update. No customer data exposure claim has been confirmed yet, and no CVEs are linked.

The disruption affects operational systems rather than point-of-sale networks directly, but the extended downtime suggests significant infrastructure impact. Retail cyber incidents of this scale often involve ransomware or supply-chain compromise; monitoring for follow-up disclosure is warranted. The event has picked up two independent sources (GBHackers and BleepingComputer) and a Mastodon update.
