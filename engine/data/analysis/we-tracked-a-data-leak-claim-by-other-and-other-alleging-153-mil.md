Other And Other, a threat actor tracked by Dark Web Sonar, has posted two separate data breach claims in the past 24 hours. The first alleges 153 million verified driver's license records from Canada and the United States. The second alleges exposure of records from Daryn.online, a Kazakhstan educational platform, with claims of over 4 million records. Both claims were posted via the Dark Web Sonar tracking account on Mastodon, citing the actor's listings.

Neither claim has been independently verified. The actor's name, "Other And Other," does not match any well-known threat group in existing intelligence databases. The claims lack sample data dumps or proof-of-access posts that would allow validation. Data breach claims on dark web forums are routinely exaggerated or fabricated for attention, extortion leverage, or resale marketing. However, the specificity of the targets (driver's license databases, educational platform) and the large claimed volumes warrant monitoring.

If either claim proves accurate, the impact would be significant. 153 million driver's license records from two countries would represent a major PII breach with identity theft implications. The Daryn.online breach, while smaller, would expose student and educational records in Kazakhstan. Organizations that operate or contract with these platforms should investigate. Dark Web Sonar is tracking the actor, and further verification should emerge in the coming days if the data is offered for sale or posted as samples.
