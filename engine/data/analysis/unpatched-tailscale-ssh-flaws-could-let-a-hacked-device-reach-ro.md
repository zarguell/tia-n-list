Erica Windisch of Cyberdione Labs published the Tailworm report assessing Tailscale v1.104.2 and headscale v0.29.5, identifying 11 unpatched vulnerabilities. The testing window ran from October 22 to October 24, 2026. Among the findings are SSH Unix-socket forwarding issues that allow a compromised device on a tailnet to reach root on reachable servers, bypassing expected zero-trust isolation.

The vulnerabilities remain unpatched at the time of reporting, meaning any organization running affected Tailscale or Headscale builds should treat this as an active exposure. The disclosure was made with zero advance notice to the vendor, increasing urgency. Security teams should review ACLs that allow one device to reach others and restrict Unix-socket forwarding paths until patches are released.
