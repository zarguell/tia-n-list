Railway disclosed that attackers exploited a previously unknown vulnerability in its build infrastructure on October 20 to escape filesystem isolation and obtain an internal credential, then used Railway's internal source code to find a second flaw involving an improperly configured trust relationship. The combined access let them decrypt customer environment variables for at most 1.85% of workspaces, roughly 100,000, and generate about $740,000 in fraudulent AI usage. The incident lasted roughly 26 hours.

The exposure centers on secrets stored in environment variables, including API keys, database passwords, and registry credentials, rather than confirmed theft from every affected workspace. Railway has urged all customers to rotate stored secrets even where impact is unconfirmed.

Customers should rotate any credentials that were stored in Railway environment variables and review usage logs for unexpected AI or cloud spend. Watch for Railway's final confirmed impact count and any follow-on abuse of the leaked secrets.
