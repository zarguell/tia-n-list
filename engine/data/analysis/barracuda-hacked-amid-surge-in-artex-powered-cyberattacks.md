SecurityWeek reports that Barracuda is among the victims of intrusions built on ARTEX, an agentic AI penetration-testing framework published on GitHub in July 2026. ARTEX uses a planner to set objectives and create tasks, agents to carry them out, and a platform that records progress, findings and assets. Reporting on the framework names DeepSeek v4.1-flash as the initial model, with GLM-5.3 and Grok 4.6 seen in some instances, though users mostly rely on Chinese models. The FBI says abuse in real intrusions has been underway since at least August and has increased sharply in recent weeks.

The FBI alert cited in the coverage points to attacks on South Korean financial firms and on ConnectWise, with compromised cloud backup integrations leading into Microsoft 365 environments and some intrusions reaching privileged access in under three hours. The related FBI warning says ARTEX automates reconnaissance, exploitation and privilege escalation, giving attackers with limited expertise a way to run several complex operations at once.

The developer removed the GitHub repository in October, but forks, archived copies and ARTEX PRO remain available, and other reporting counted more than 1,600 exposed ARTEX consoles by early November. Watch for new exposed consoles, detection signatures for ARTEX activity, and whether attacks continue to concentrate on peripheral devices and cloud backup supply-chain paths.
