Federal investigators pursuing ShinyHunters have identified executives at two ransomware negotiation firms who maintained close relations with the extortion group. One U.S. executive allegedly helped ShinyHunters operate an extortion website. ShinyHunters has claimed dozens of major breaches since 2019 and, according to the FBI, has extorted more than $70 million from victims this year. The group recently hacked the FBI recruitment site fbijobs.gov, stealing files that included medical and psychological records, Social Security numbers, home addresses, and unit assignments.

The key development is the suspected collaboration between a professional ransom negotiator and the extortion operation itself. If confirmed, this undermines the assumption that negotiation firms are neutral intermediaries. Watch for indictments or public identification of the executives involved, and for any impact on how victims engage negotiation services.
