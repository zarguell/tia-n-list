Federal investigators linked ransomware negotiators to ShinyHunters operations. KrebsOnSecurity reports that U.S. and Dutch suspects helped run the extortion site, with chat records showing negotiators knew victim details before victims did. The FBI says ShinyHunters has extorted over $70 million this year. One arrest involved a Canadian security executive; sources describe close ties to the teenage operator "Rey" in Amman, Jordan. The group previously breached fbijobs.gov, exposing employee records.

This matters because it shows how the ransomware ecosystem mixes legitimate consulting with criminal extortion. Negotiators with insider access to breach details could steer payments or conceal leaks. The FBI recruitment-site intrusion demonstrates the group's capability against federal targets. Watch for additional arrests or indictments, and any disclosure of which consulting firms handled ShinyHunters victims.
