Federal investigators tracking the ShinyHunters group have identified executives at two ransomware negotiation firms who maintained unusually close relations with the extortion group. KrebsOnSecurity reports one U.S. executive helped ShinyHunters operate a victim-pressure website. ShinyHunters has claimed dozens of major breaches since 2019 and extorted over $70 million this year, including an FBI recruitment site breach in late September.

The investigation highlights collusion between negotiators and criminals. The group, originally operated from France, has transitioned control to "Rey," a teenage hacker from Amman, Jordan. The FBI breach involved medical and psychological records, Social Security numbers, and home addresses. Watch for arrests, sanctions, or further disclosure of the negotiation firm connections.
