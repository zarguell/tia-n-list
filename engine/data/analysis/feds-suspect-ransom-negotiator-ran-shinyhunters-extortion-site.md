U.S. federal investigators pursuing the ShinyHunters hacking group have identified executives at ransomware negotiation firms who maintained close relations with the extortion group. According to the event content from KrebsOnSecurity, one U.S.-based executive helped ShinyHunters operate a website pressuring victims into paying ransoms. The FBI reports the group has extorted more than $70 million this year, and in late September ShinyHunters hacked the FBI recruitment site fbijobs.gov.

The investigation points to collusion between negotiators and criminals, which undermines the trust model of ransomware response services. Watch for further federal actions, including potential indictments of negotiation firm executives, and for any disruption to ShinyHunters affiliates as the group loses protective intermediaries.
