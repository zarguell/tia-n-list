Icelandic hosting provider 1984 ehf shut down all systems on Friday, November 20, 2026 after what it called a large-scale cyberattack. RUV reported that the company saw no other way than to turn everything off. A later VSX report says every service was stopped and that email and DNS were given priority for restoration, with the company treating those two services as the immediate goal for customers. No full restoration date is given.

A Visir update adds a targeting detail the earlier reports lack: the company's CEO said attackers first damaged websites whose domain names indicated support for Palestine. That is the only stated motive or selection clue in the reporting. No actor, ransomware claim, entry vector, data-theft figure or IOC is reported.

Taking a hosting provider fully offline is itself an operational outcome, because email and DNS outages spread to every customer domain. Watch for service-by-service restoration, forensic findings on how the attack entered the environment and whether the Palestine-related domain targeting was the initial access point or only the first visible damage. Until the company publishes detail, the confirmed facts are the shutdown, the email and DNS priority and the CEO's statement about the first sites damaged.
