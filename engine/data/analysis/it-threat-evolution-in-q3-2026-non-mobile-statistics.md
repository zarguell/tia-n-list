Kaspersky's Q3 2026 threat report, drawn from consenting Kaspersky Security Network users, recorded more than 311 million attacks from online resources blocked, 47 million unique malicious links flagged by Web Anti-Virus, and more than 23 million malicious or unwanted objects stopped by File Anti-Virus. The quarter saw 4,470 new ransomware variants, more than 81,000 users hit by ransomware (with Qilin accounting for 15 percent of victims whose data appeared on leak sites), and more than 217,000 users targeted by miners. The report also noted the August sentencing of the Ransom Cartel RaaS creator and lead administrator to 16 years in prison.

The companion mobile section recorded more than 1.66 million attacks involving mobile malware, adware or unwanted software, with Trojan-Banker as the leading category at 31.63 percent of detected malicious apps, over 378,000 malicious installation packages including 119,688 mobile banking Trojan packages, and a July malicious app disguised as Google Play Protect that may have used AI to automate fraud against Brazilian users.
