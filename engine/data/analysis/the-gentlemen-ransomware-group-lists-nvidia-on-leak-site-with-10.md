The Gentlemen ransomware group added Nvidia to its extortion site on November 18, 2026, with a countdown of roughly 234 hours to November 28. Ransomware.live indexed the entry at 09:22 UTC that day with the attack date listed as November 18 and status pending. The listing has Nvidia's logo and a general company description, and the stolen-data field says only DATA INFO - SOON. No files, samples or technical account of an intrusion accompanied the claim.

Nvidia told BleepingComputer and SecurityWeek that it had found no indication its own systems or networks were affected and that it was investigating an incident that may involve a third-party partner. The company did not name the partner or say what data might be involved. Ransomware.live also records background exposure figures from Hudson Rock, including 353 compromised employee credentials and 327 third-party employee credentials, but those numbers describe unrelated infostealer collections and are not evidence of this intrusion. Researcher Aras Nazarovas urged caution because the group has previously named the wrong victims and reused older data.

Microsoft tracks The Gentlemen as Storm-2697. Unit 42 describes a ransomware-as-a-service operation active since at least July 2025 that grew out of a Qilin affiliate relationship, with 580 claimed victims across 77 countries through July 7, 2026, including 103 in manufacturing. Watch whether the countdown produces sample data and whether Nvidia or the unnamed partner confirms an incident; until then this is an unverified extortion claim, not a confirmed breach.
