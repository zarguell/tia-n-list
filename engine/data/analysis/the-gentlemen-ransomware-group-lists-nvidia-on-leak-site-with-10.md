The Gentlemen added NVIDIA to its dark web extortion site on November 18, 2026, with a timer of about 234 hours running to November 28. Ransomware.live indexed the entry at 09:22 UTC and marked it pending; the data field says only `DATA INFO - SOON`, with no samples, screenshots or document list, and NVIDIA had not confirmed an intrusion in the reporting carried by three outlets between 13:51 and 21:40 UTC. No CVE, affected version or intrusion detail is attached to this claim. What is verified so far is extortion pressure, not a compromise.

A confirmed hit would be more than another leak-site name, because NVIDIA sits upstream of GPU drivers, AI tooling and data-center systems, and The Gentlemen runs double extortion at volume. Unit 42 counted 580 claimed victims across 77 countries through July 7, including 103 in manufacturing and 117 in June alone; later reporting put the group at 1,123 claimed organizations in 95 countries. The group has form for naming the wrong victim and recycling old data, which is why Cybernews researcher Aras Nazarovas urged caution. What makes this claim worth a defender's attention is the combination of a supply-chain target, a fixed November 28 deadline and Hudson Rock's background count of 353 compromised NVIDIA employees and 1,115,967 compromised users, a credential pool an affiliate can work through even if the page was posted without a breach.

Hunt as if access were possible without declaring an incident. Unit 42's guidance for this actor is to alert on scheduled tasks matching `gentlemen*`, unexpected loading of unsigned or known-vulnerable drivers such as ThrottleStop.sys, `wevtutil` clearing of Security and System logs, and `vssadmin` or `wmic` shadow-copy deletion. Patch the initial-access bugs the actor exploits, CVE-2024-55591 in FortiOS and FortiProxy, CVE-2025-32433 in Erlang/OTP SSH, CVE-2025-33073 in the Windows SMB client and CVE-2025-55182 in React2Shell, plus CVE-2025-7771 for the ThrottleStop.sys driver; disable SMBv1, enforce SMB signing, isolate ESXi management interfaces and keep host SSH off outside maintenance windows. Watch for Advanced IP Scanner traffic and SystemBC-pattern outbound connections on non-standard ports, and watch the ransomware.live entry for a change from `DATA INFO - SOON` to actual files.

The assessment changes to a confirmed incident if samples appear before November 28 or NVIDIA acknowledges an intrusion, and toward a fabricated claim if the listing is quietly removed or the timer resets without publication. Either outcome will show whether the 234-hour clock was negotiation leverage or a real exfiltration schedule.
