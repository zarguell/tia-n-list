A federal judge issued a second injunction blocking the USPS from implementing Section 3 of the executive order on mail-in voting. Judge Indira Talwani ruled that the states are likely to succeed on the merits of their separation-of-powers challenge, and the order preserves the current electoral status quo for elections on or before November 3, 2026, with less than 90 days before the midterms. The administration has petitioned the Supreme Court to review the case.

The story matters to election security because mail-in voting infrastructure is a contested target: the ruling keeps the existing state-run process in place rather than shifting ballot handling to federal control. The practical watch item is the Supreme Court's response to the petition, since a stay or reversal before November would force rapid operational changes in how ballots are distributed in the affected states.
