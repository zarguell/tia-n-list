Quantum Systems confirmed that it detected an intrusion in late October 2026 in which attackers placed malware on an internal update server. The malicious file was disguised as an update for QBase, the company's ground-control and mission-planning software used with Vector reconnaissance drones. Der Spiegel and the Financial Times reported the compromise on November 12, and the company confirmed the attack that evening.

The malicious QBase update reached servers at the Bundeswehr and other customers, but reporting says it was caught in a check before it reached the drones themselves. The Bundeswehr nonetheless grounded its entire Quantum Systems fleet as a precaution. It was the only customer to do so, and a company spokesperson later said the drones were flying again and the worldwide fleet was operating normally. Multiple German federal authorities are involved in the investigation.

Quantum Systems told outlets that it believes it was targeted because of its recent announcement of support for Ukraine, where Germany had pledged funding for tens of thousands of its interceptor drones. The company did not make a specific actor attribution. What to watch is whether investigators tie the planted QBase update to a named group and whether other customers found the same file on their servers, since the compromised update server gave one vendor breach reach across its customer base.
