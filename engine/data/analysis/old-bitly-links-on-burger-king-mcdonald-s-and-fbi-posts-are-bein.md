Bitly links that were published years ago by Burger King, McDonald's, the FBI and Trump accounts and left dormant have been reclaimed and repointed at unrelated Wikipedia pages. The original short URLs still resolve, so anyone following an old post or an archived campaign asset lands on content the brand never chose. Security Online reported the hijack.

The mechanism matters more than the specific brands. A shortener account that changes hands, lapses or is compromised keeps every link ever issued under it, and those links sit inside press releases, email footers and news articles with no expiry date and no owner watching them. Inventory dormant short links tied to owned accounts, check where the high-traffic ones currently resolve, and prefer links that can be re-pointed only by an authenticated owner.
