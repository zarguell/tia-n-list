ASOS confirmed a data breach caused by a social engineering attack that compromised an employee's credentials. The attacker, calling themselves Xuanye Group, used the stolen credentials to access third-party platforms used by ASOS. ASOS stated that basic personal information and contact details may have been exposed, but payment card data was not accessed. The company locked affected platforms and launched an investigation with external experts, law enforcement, and regulators. Customers received push notifications through the ASOS app and follow-up emails confirming the exposure scope.
