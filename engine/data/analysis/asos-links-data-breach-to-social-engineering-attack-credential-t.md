ASOS confirmed a data breach caused by a social engineering attack in which hackers stole an employee's login credentials and accessed third-party platforms used by the company. The exposed data includes basic personal information and contact details; the company states payment card information was not accessed. A threat actor calling itself Xuanye Group claimed customer data theft and demanded staff engagement via Telegram, but ASOS eventually confirmed the breach through an official website statement and customer notification.

This is a confirmed credential-based breach at a major UK retailer with exposure limited to basic personal and contact data rather than payment details. The attack path was social engineering followed by third-party platform access, not direct database intrusion. Monitor for follow-on phishing using the exposed contact details and verify that affected third-party integrations have had credentials rotated.
