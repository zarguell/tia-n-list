Google released Chrome 158 to the stable channel with fixes for 204 vulnerabilities, six of them critical. The critical set includes three use-after-free bugs in WebView, GarbageCollection and WebAudio, out-of-bounds writes in GPU and ANGLE, and a buffer overflow in Media. Severity breakdown reported by SecurityWeek is 62 high, 82 medium and 54 low. Use-after-free is the most common class with 51 occurrences, followed by incorrect authorization at 34, missing authorization at 16 and out-of-bounds write or read at 15.

The rollout is version 158.0.8127.36/.37 for Windows and macOS and 158.0.8127.36 for Linux. Google discovered 145 of the flaws and researcher Xinyang Ge reported 15. Of the 204 issues, 163 drew no bug bounty, $11,000 was paid for five externally reported flaws, and reward amounts for 36 reports were still undecided. Google did not report in-the-wild exploitation for any of the patched bugs, but the six critical memory corruption issues are the ones to prioritize when updating fleets.
