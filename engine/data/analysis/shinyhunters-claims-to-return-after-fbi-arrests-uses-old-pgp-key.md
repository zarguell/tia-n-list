The ShinyHunters extortion gang claims to have returned in posts on a new Tor site launched November 23. The messages, in Russian and English, announce the group's return, distance it from alleged impostors and denounce a former member reportedly arrested and cooperating with law enforcement. Two verification messages were signed with historical ShinyHunters PGP keys dating to 2020, including one used to authenticate communications with BleepingComputer in August 2025. BleepingComputer confirmed that both signatures match known group keys.

Key access does not establish that the original operators are back, because those keys have circulated among many people. Databreaches notes that someone using the 2020 ShinyHunters account on Exploit posted a similar explanation signed with the original key, and that the key was known to have leaked months ago. The claim follows arrests including a 24-year-old Amsterdam man identified as Pepijn van der Stap, also known as Umbreon, and the group's September assertion that it breached FBI systems through an Oracle PeopleSoft vulnerability. Watch for new leak-site postings and independently confirmed victims before treating the return as more than a claim.
