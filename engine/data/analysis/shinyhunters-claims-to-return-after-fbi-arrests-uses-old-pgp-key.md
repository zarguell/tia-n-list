A statement titled The Darkest Day appeared on a new Tor leak site on November 23, 2026, announcing the return of ShinyHunters about two months after arrests in the Netherlands, Jordan and the United States. The same notice was posted from the group's Exploit forum account, held since 2020 and named as the only official outlet. The operators signed both posts with two ShinyHunters PGP keys from 2020 that were revoked in March. BleepingComputer confirmed the signatures against known historic keys, including one used to authenticate its August 2025 communications, but the keys had circulated widely, so control of them proves access rather than continuity of the original operators.

The comeback follows arrests including a 24-year-old Amsterdam man identified as Pepijn van der Stap, known online as Umbreon, and comes after the group claimed an FBI breach in September through an Oracle PeopleSoft vulnerability. One post denounces a former member who is reportedly cooperating with law enforcement. Okta separately reported that a vishing cluster it had linked to ShinyHunters is now posting victims on a new leak site branded INCEPTION.

For defenders the useful signal is the overlap between the old keys, the long-held forum account and the new INCEPTION branding, not the claim of identity itself. Watch whether the new site posts real victim data, whether the INCEPTION cluster keeps using the same vishing tradecraft, and whether law enforcement statements clarify who controls the historical keys.
