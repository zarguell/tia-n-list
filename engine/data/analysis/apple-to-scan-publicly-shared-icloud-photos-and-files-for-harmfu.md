Apple says it will begin automatically scanning files shared through public iCloud links starting with iOS 27.3, inspecting shared photos and files for harmful content. The reporting states that the scanning disables end-to-end encryption on protected items, so Apple can view the content of files shared by public link. The change covers publicly shared links rather than private iCloud libraries.

The move puts Apple in the position of scanning customer files it previously treated as private. Users who share files by public link should expect automated inspection, and organizations that rely on iCloud link sharing for sensitive documents should review what they place in public shares. Whether scanning extends beyond publicly shared links into broader account content is not established by the reporting.
