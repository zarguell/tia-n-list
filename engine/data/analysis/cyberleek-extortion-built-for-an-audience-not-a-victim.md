CyberLeek presents itself as a financially motivated extortion group that steals data and intellectual property partly to attract an audience and grow revenue from public leaks. The available reporting describes publicity as part of the group's model, with leaked material used to intrigue an audience rather than only to pressure one victim. No CVE, named victim, or malware family is attached in that profile.

A later report attributes a malware distribution campaign to the CyberLeek name, using more than 355 linked domains and subdomains and fake Grand Theft Auto VI pages to deliver dangerous files. The domain count is the concrete indicator here. Watch for victim postings or infrastructure lists that show whether the extortion profile and the game-download lure operation share operators; the events link them by name only.
