FortiBleed remains an active global campaign targeting internet-facing Fortinet FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN gateways. The October 6 joint FBI and U.S. Secret Service advisory adds operational detail: actors steal credentials, create new admin accounts for persistence, and in some cases delete or change passwords of legitimate accounts, locking defenders out of their own devices. The campaign facilitates internal pivoting and potential ransomware deployment. Both the original GBHackers report and the updated malware.news analysis describe the same threat. No CVEs are tied to FortiBleed; the issue is exploitation of unpatched firewall configurations and stolen credentials. Defenders should review Fortinet advisory indicators, audit admin accounts, and review access logs for lockout events.
