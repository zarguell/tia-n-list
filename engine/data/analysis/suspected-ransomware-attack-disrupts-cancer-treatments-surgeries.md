ANSSI said a suspected ransomware attack impaired systems at the University Hospital of Guadeloupe and forced staff to postpone surgeries, suspend some cancer treatments and return to pen and paper. The hospital canceled some surgical procedures, consultations and imaging tests through at least Friday. Radiation therapy was paused over safety concerns and Monday chemotherapy sessions were canceled. Emergency care, dialysis and urgent cardiac procedures stayed available.

Investigators are examining whether the attackers entered through an unnamed IT supplier compromised several months earlier, and French prosecutors opened a criminal investigation. Hospital management said no ransom demand had been received and ruled out paying one. The attacker is not identified.
