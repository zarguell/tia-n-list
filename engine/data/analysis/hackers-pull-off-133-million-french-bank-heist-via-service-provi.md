Crédit Mutuel Arkéa, a French banking group with more than 5.8 million members and 211.5 billion euros in assets, disclosed on 2026-11-04 that fraudulent transactions of up to $133 million were initiated from its own account to other banking organizations. The bank said customer assets were unaffected and that the incident was closed and under control, with some transfer transactions delayed.

Blockchain fraud investigator ZachXBT flagged the activity on 2026-11-02, warning of large-scale unauthorized withdrawals from multiple centralized exchange accounts that began on 2026-11-01, and estimated up to 118 million euros stolen as USDC and USDT. ZachXBT said $30 million had been laundered by 2026-11-03 before EU law enforcement could freeze it. Bitquery's on-chain investigation traced $108.8 million of the movement, with $61.0 million still on Ethereum.

Stablecoin outflows from exchange accounts remain the visible trail here, and the speed of laundering against law-enforcement freezing windows is the operational lesson. No arrests or attribution were reported in the events observed for this story.
