Crédit Mutuel Arkéa was hit by a cyberattack that enabled fraudulent transactions of up to $133 million (about 118 million euros), discovered by blockchain investigator ZachXBT on November 1 and publicly named on November 3. The bank disclosed on November 4 and said customer assets are unaffected.

Attackers entered through a service provider's platform, not through Fortuneo, and used the bank's own account to send transactions to other banking organizations. Funds were converted to USDC and USDT, then ETH and Monero, with $30 million laundered before disclosure.

The bank suspended certain transactions, froze some funds, and is working with judicial and police authorities. The service provider's identity and the exploited platform were not disclosed.
