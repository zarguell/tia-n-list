A Chinese national, 44-year-old Zhen Zhao, was arraigned in California after extradition from the Netherlands for his alleged role in a cryptocurrency investment scam that stole nearly $37 million from 174 U.S. victims. The scheme, described as pig butchering or romance baiting, involved unsolicited contact via social media, dating sites, messaging apps, and phone calls to build trust and lure targets into fake digital asset investments.

Prosecutors say Zhao and accomplices in the United States and Cambodia arranged for victim funds to be converted to the stablecoin Tether (USDT) through Deltec Bank and transferred to attacker-controlled wallets. The group created fake trading websites resembling legitimate platforms to convince victims their investments were growing. This case highlights the persistent scale of romance-based investment fraud targeting U.S. residents.
