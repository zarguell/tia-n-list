Two unrelated databases left more than 3.5 billion usernames, passwords, national identity numbers and documents accessible to anyone online, according to findings shared with WIRED and relayed by DataBreaches.net on 24 November 2026. The reporting describes two separate exposures, so the combined count should not be read as one incident or one victim population. No database owner, intrusion path or notification count is named in the available event text.

A separate ExpressVPN research link reports nearly 2 billion credentials in a publicly accessible database found by a different researcher. The available text gives no owner, no field-level breakdown and no confirmation of overlap with the WIRED total. Watch for owner identification, for a statement on whether the credentials were stored in plaintext, and for service-level notices that would let users map the exposure to accounts they actually hold.
