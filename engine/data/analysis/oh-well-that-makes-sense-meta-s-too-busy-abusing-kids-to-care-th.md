BBC reports scammers are demanding ransoms from Instagram users whose accounts have been compromised. The campaign targets Instagram accounts and uses the threat of exposing personal content or information to extract payment from victims. Meta's platform has been a persistent target for social engineering campaigns, and this represents a continuation of account takeover and extortion tactics targeting the platform's user base.

This is significant because it demonstrates the ongoing monetization of compromised social media accounts beyond credential resale. Ransomware-style extortion applied to personal social media accounts creates direct financial harm to individual users. The scale of Instagram's user base means even a small success rate generates substantial revenue for attackers. Organizations should remind employees that personal account compromises can have spill-over effects on corporate security through password reuse and social engineering vectors.
