N-able disclosed on Wednesday that an unknown actor gained unauthorized access to one server in the cloud environment hosting Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud. The company disabled management interfaces and APIs after detecting the intrusion. GBHackers reports that administrative services were disrupted across the Americas, Asia-Pacific and Europe, while inbound and outbound email delivery and filtering kept running.

N-able says it has taken mitigation steps and is hardening services against recurrence, with the investigation still ongoing. The company told GBHackers it had found no evidence that customer data was compromised, although the GBHackers headline raises the possibility that credentials were exposed. Those two statements sit uneasily together, so the exposure question should be treated as open until N-able publishes investigation findings.

Watch for an updated incident notice that names the affected region, the access path and whether any customer or administrative credentials were read. MSPs using Mail Assure or SpamExperts Hosted Cloud should also check for unexpected changes in management-plane activity even though email filtering itself was not interrupted.
