Java4Shell is CVE-2026-114938, a heap out-of-bounds write in the HotSpot JVM's x86-64 Base64 decoder intrinsic. A malformed padded Base64 tail makes the decoder write 62 bytes past the end of its output buffer. Calif rated the result 9.2 under CVSS 4.0 and reported heap-secret reads plus unauthenticated remote code execution; Oracle's CVSS 3.1 score is 8.1. The vulnerable path needs AVX-512 VBMI hardware, so OpenJDK 17.0.6 and later on affected Intel Xeon and AMD EPYC CPUs are in scope, while ARM64 and older x86 hosts are not. Oracle and OpenJDK shipped fixes on 17 November 2026, including 17.0.21.1, 21.0.13.1, 25.0.5.1 and 27.0.1.1.

Reachability comes from ordinary protocol data. Reporting on the flaw names HTTP Basic headers, JWTs, SAML artifacts and file uploads as inputs that can reach the decoder before login. Calif demonstrated pre-authentication RCE on default installs of Keycloak, Jira, Confluence, Artifactory, Jenkins and PingFederate, plus authenticated RCE against Kafka Broker, Solr, Elasticsearch, Gerrit and Spinnaker. Each target needs its own chain, and a separate rebuild of the upstream diff reproduced the out-of-bounds write and array-length corruption without producing a full execution chain. CISA, Canada's AL26-029 and Cisco Talos all urged immediate patching; Talos said it had not seen in-the-wild exploitation at disclosure.

By early 19 November the probing had started. SecurityWeek reported more than 150 GreyNoise requests matching a Java4Shell attack shape from five IP addresses, with no successful intrusions on GreyNoise sensors. WatchTowr told the outlet its honeypots saw 'java4shell-recon' User-Agent strings and blind exploitation attempts with correctly shaped requests. That is reconnaissance and attempted exploitation, not a confirmed compromise, and it fits the earlier Talos assessment. Watch for a shift from shaped requests to successful callbacks, for downstream Java vendors that have not yet shipped their builds, and for exposure in container images that inherit vulnerable runtimes. Until patching is possible, reporting names -XX:-UseBASE64Intrinsics and -XX:UseAVX=2 as interim flags.
