PaperCut issued patches replacing earlier emergency hotfixes for two vulnerabilities that were confirmed actively exploited in the wild. The flaws affect PaperCut NG and MF, print management software widely deployed in enterprise and education environments. Prior to these permanent fixes, PaperCut had released emergency mitigations while working on full patches, indicating the severity and urgency of the issues.

The active exploitation confirmation means threat actors had working exploit code before patches were available. Print management systems are attractive targets because they often have elevated network privileges, access to document repositories, and connections to authentication directories. Previous PaperCut vulnerabilities were exploited by ransomware groups, including a documented case where AI-powered attack tools were used to scale exploitation across hundreds of organizations.

Organizations running PaperCut NG or MF should verify they have applied the latest patches immediately. The transition from emergency hotfixes to permanent fixes suggests the initial mitigations may not have been fully effective or introduced operational issues. Monitor for indicators of compromise associated with PaperCut exploitation, including unexpected print jobs, credential harvesting, and lateral movement from print servers.
