IBM Verify Access carries seven CVEs in a single disclosure: CVE-2026-16823, CVE-2026-16916, CVE-2026-18740, CVE-2026-19482, CVE-2026-19491, CVE-2026-78401, and CVE-2026-78406. The remaining five reach CVSS 9.0 or above, and the full set covers unauthentized RCE, deserialization, and related access failures in the identity infrastructure. The sources (threataft.com, securityonline.info, all-about-security.de) published on 2026-10-09, with the original Mastodon post at 04:19Z and updates through 05:06Z.

A successful attack against an exposed Verify Access instance yields unauthenticated remote code execution inside the identity and access management layer, which is worse than application-layer compromise because it grants adversaries session and credential visibility across the enterprise. Five of the seven CVEs score 9.0 or higher, and two are unauth RCE at 9.8, meaning no credentials or user interaction are required. The disclosure clusters seven flaws in one product in a single wave, which distinguishes it from the usual scattered patch-Tuesday noise and demands prioritized patching rather than routine queuing. Organizations that expose Verify Access management interfaces to broader networks face direct, unmediated exploitation risk.
