Canadian Centre for Cyber Security advisory AV26-888 covers two vulnerabilities: CVE-2026-7565 and CVE-2026-75650, both affecting Adobe Commerce, Adobe Commerce B2B, and Magento Open Source. Adobe has confirmed CVE-2026-75650 is exploited in the wild. The affected versions span all releases up through and including the August 2026 security patches (2.4.9-2026-aug, 2.4.8-2026-aug, 2.4.7-2026-aug, 2.4.6-2026-aug for Magento Open Source; corresponding versions for Commerce and Commerce B2B). Adobe's hotfix for CVE-2026-7565 is explicitly excluded from the affected list, meaning the hotfix addresses one issue but not both.

CVE-2026-75650 maps directly to the StyleSmuggler unauthenticated RCE zero-day disclosed by Sansec, which is tracked in the companion story on this feed. The advisory is notable because it confirms that August 2026 patches do not protect against CVE-2026-75650, and that Adobe's own hotfix for CVE-2026-7565 is a separate fix. Organizations running any Magento or Adobe Commerce version need to apply the hotfix for CVE-2026-7565 immediately and treat CVE-2026-75650 as an open, actively exploited gap until Adobe releases a dedicated patch.
