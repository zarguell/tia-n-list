Dell patched two maximum-severity flaws in its Container Storage Modules (CSM), tracking them as CVE-2026-63688 and CVE-2026-63692, both CVSS 10.0. The authorization-module bugs let unauthenticated remote attackers grab storage-backend administrator credentials and gain full control over PowerStore, PowerScale, PowerFlex, PowerMax, and Unity XT arrays linked to Kubernetes clusters. Dell also fixed four additional critical CSM issues the same day. Organizations running Dell CSM should apply updates immediately; the missing-authentication weakness is straightforward to exploit and grants complete administrative access.
