The FBI's Operation Lonestar Roundup searched more than 40 Texas residences and seized 187 laptops used to help North Korean operatives obtain remote jobs at U.S. companies under false identities. Facilitators configured employer-issued devices and transferred earnings to regime-controlled accounts; over 70 companies were targeted.

The scheme gave operatives access to corporate systems while generating revenue for sanctioned weapons programs. CrowdStrike provided intelligence support and noted two arrests in Ohio.

Watch for additional DOJ releases, naming of affected companies, and defensive guidance on vetting remote hires and monitoring contractor device access.
