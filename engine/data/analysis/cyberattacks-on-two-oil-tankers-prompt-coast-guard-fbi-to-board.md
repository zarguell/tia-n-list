Two oil tankers bound for Texas were boarded by US Coast Guard and FBI personnel after cyberattacks disrupted the vessels during their voyage. The VL Prosperity, a Liberian-flagged crude tanker, was attacked on August 7 while transiting the Strait of Gibraltar. Iran's Mehr News Agency reported that hackers slowed coolant flow, raised engine speed, and interfered with fuel delivery in the engine room. Navigation and cargo systems were also accessed, and communications were cut for approximately 30 hours.

A Coast Guard cyber team, FBI Cyber Action Team members, and law enforcement boarded the VL Prosperity on August 8 and spent four days aboard. A second vessel was boarded on August 24 after arriving in the Gulf of Mexico. The Coast Guard has not publicly linked the incident to Iran, but the Mehr News Agency report and the timing during a Strait of Gibraltar transit suggest Iranian involvement.

This is a significant escalation in maritime cyberattacks. The ability to manipulate engine room controls while a vessel is underway demonstrates capability beyond data theft: this is cyber-physical interference with potential safety-of-life implications. The federal response, including FBI boarding, signals that this is being treated as a national security matter. Watch for attribution updates and potential sanctions or military responses.
