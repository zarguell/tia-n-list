WhatsApp embedded hidden instructions in its AI support chatbot pages telling the bot not to advise users to contact privacy regulators or mention privacy laws, researchers at Redact found. The hidden text was found across 102 instruction blocks in 80 of 545 WhatsApp help-center pages. On account-deletion pages, the chatbot was told not to recommend contacting any Data Protection Authority when users lost access to their devices. Another block told it not to reference privacy laws when users mentioned lawyers or legal action. WhatsApp confirmed to WIRED these were instructions for "WhatsApp Support with AI." This is an operational privacy and compliance issue: it affects how users interact with support systems when seeking account deletion or reporting abuse, and it raises questions about Meta's approach to regulatory obligations.

The story matters because it shows an explicit design choice to suppress references to privacy regulators inside AI-driven user support flows, not an accidental omission. Redact discovered the instructions on October 8 and saved copies of the pages, confirming the text was delivered but not rendered to users. The scope spans at least 102 instruction blocks across 80 pages, so the suppression instructions are embedded in a broader automated support framework rather than isolated. Watch for WhatsApp or Meta updating the instructions, regulatory responses from EU or UK data protection authorities, and whether similar hidden prompts exist in other Meta products.
