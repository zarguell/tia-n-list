ShinyHunters claims it breached apply.fbijobs.gov, the FBI's jobs portal, through an Oracle PeopleSoft pre-authentication zero-day giving remote code execution. The group defaced the site with a "This site has been seized by ShinyHunters" banner and claims a 2 to 3 TB haul covering current, former, and prospective FBI employees. It also claims lateral movement onto FBI-managed AWS GovCloud servers with access to HR, MedLink, and CJIS services. The FBI is investigating, per NPR.

The extortion demand is unusual: ShinyHunters wants the FBI to retract its 15 May 2026 IC3 bulletin (Alert I-051526-PSA), which described the group's attacks on online learning management systems and its pressure tactics, including threatening texts, calls to victims' relatives, and swatting. Reporting collected in the events notes this escalation to non-financial demands.

The most sensitive angle comes from Joseph Cox's reporting: the stolen personnel data includes members of the FBI's Remote Operations Unit, the team that builds exploits and covert-access tools. Exposure of that roster would be valuable to criminals and foreign intelligence services.

Watch for FBI confirmation of what was actually taken, whether the claimed PeopleSoft zero-day matches an Oracle advisory, and any confirmed GovCloud or CJIS access beyond the group's claims.
