ShinyHunters claims to have stolen sensitive data from an FBI jobs site, and the FBI is investigating. NPR reported the investigation on September 24, 2026, after the group began advertising the haul. The FBI jobs portal reportedly runs on Oracle PeopleSoft, and follow-on reporting on September 25 claims the intrusion used a PeopleSoft zero-day. If the claims hold, the exposure could extend past applicant data to identifiable staff of the FBI's Remote Operations Unit, the team that conducts offensive hacking operations, which would turn a routine job-board breach into a counterintelligence problem.

Both key claims, the zero-day and the personnel exposure, currently rest on the attackers' assertions and secondary reporting rather than confirmed FBI or Oracle statements. ShinyHunters has a track record of inflated or partially false breach claims, so the data volume and sensitivity remain open questions.

Watch for three things: an FBI or Oracle confirmation naming PeopleSoft and any assigned CVE, release of sample records that would substantiate the Remote Operations Unit angle, and whether Oracle issues an emergency patch for an unpatched PeopleSoft flaw. If a PeopleSoft zero-day is confirmed, expect rapid scanning of internet-exposed PeopleSoft deployments, which are common in government and education.
