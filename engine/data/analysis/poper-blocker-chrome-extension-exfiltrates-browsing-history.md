Poper Blocker, a Chrome Web Store extension with more than two million users, Featured badge, and Verified status, has been found to exfiltrate full browsing histories, page content, screenshots, and AI chat data. Bay Area Labs classified the extension as Confirmed in its reporting, noting it exfiltrates every URL visited and activates server-side gating after roughly 24 hours. The extension is owned by Big Star Labs LP.

The campaign fits a broader pattern of malicious Chrome extensions using trusted storefront badges to reach large user bases before activating data collection. Events reference a Dark Reading article and Mastodon discussions linking the extension to the same research. The combined event count in the story shows sustained attention rather than a single disclosure.

What to watch: Chrome Web Store removal actions, any response from Big Star Labs, and whether other extensions in the same cluster are identified and blocked.
