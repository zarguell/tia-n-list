Kreiskrankenhaus Greiz-Ronneburg in Germany was hit by a ransomware attack that disrupted hospital operations and patient intake. Security-incidents.de described the incident as a ransomware break-in causing an outage, and a later report from cyberworldops.eu says the emergency department reopened after the extortion attack.

No actor, ransom amount, entry vector or data-theft claim is given in the available material. Watch for the hospital's own incident notice, details on whether patient data was taken, and confirmation that other departments have returned to normal operation.
