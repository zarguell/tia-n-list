Google's Chrome 155 update patches 247 vulnerabilities, including four critical use-after-free bugs (CVE-2026-106382, CVE-2026-106197, CVE-2026-106358, CVE-2026-106347) affecting Chromecast, Browser, Navigation, and Track. Three were reported by researcher Xinyang Ge, who used AI to find two; Google paid roughly $33,000 in bounties. The release includes 53 high-severity fixes and 190 medium/low defects, with incorrect authorization (41) and missing authorization (34) the most common categories. Exposure is broad because Chrome is the dominant desktop browser; defenders should upgrade to 155.0.8059.39 (Linux/Android) or 154.0.8037.40 (Windows/Mac) immediately. Watch for disclosure of remaining bounties and any in-the-wild exploitation of the four critical use-after-free flaws, since no active exploitation is confirmed but AI-accelerated discovery suggests faster timelines.
