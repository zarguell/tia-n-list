Google released Chrome 155 with 247 security fixes, including four critical use-after-free flaws tracked as CVE-2026-106382, CVE-2026-106197, CVE-2026-106358, and CVE-2026-106347. The bugs affect Chrome's Chromecast, Browser, Navigation, and Track components. One was discovered by Google; the other three were reported by Xinyang Ge, who used AI to identify two defects. The update also resolves 53 high-severity vulnerabilities, with 34 from external researchers, and 190 medium- and low-severity issues. Google paid about $33,000 in bounties but has not disclosed amounts for nearly 50 reports. The most common vulnerability types included incorrect authorization (41), use-after-free (34), missing authorization (34), and UI misrepresentation (20). This is a standard large-scale browser patch cycle; the critical use-after-free bugs warrant prompt updates.
