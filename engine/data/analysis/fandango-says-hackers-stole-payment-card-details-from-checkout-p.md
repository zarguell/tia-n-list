Fandango is notifying customers after card skimmer code was injected into its checkout page and intercepted payment details entered during purchases from October 8 to 11, 2026. The company determined on October 30 that the transactions may have been affected. The notification says names, government IDs, Social Security numbers and other account information were not exposed, but the captured card data could be used for unauthorized purchases. Fandango sells tickets for 31,000 US cinema screens and also owns Rotten Tomatoes.

This is a client-side skimming incident rather than a credential dump, so the useful signals are the four-day injection window and the checkout-page location. Fandango is offering two years of IDX credit and dark web monitoring and identity theft recovery services. Watch for further disclosure on how the code reached the checkout page and for similar skimmer activity on other high-traffic ticketing and payment pages during the same period.
