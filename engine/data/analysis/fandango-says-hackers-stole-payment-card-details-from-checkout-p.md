Fandango is notifying customers after card skimmer code was injected into its checkout page and captured payment details entered between 8 and 11 October 2026. The company determined on 30 October that the transactions may have been affected. Exposed fields include card number, CVV, expiration date, billing ZIP code and email address; names, government IDs, Social Security numbers and other account information were not affected. Fandango sells tickets for 31,000 US cinema screens and also owns Rotten Tomatoes.

Securityweek's follow-up says the problem has been fixed, security processes were strengthened, and Fandango is not aware of fraudulent use of the information. Affected customers can sign up for two years of IDX credit monitoring, dark web scanning and identity theft insurance. Watch for disclosure of how the code reached the checkout page and for similar skimmer activity on other high-traffic ticketing pages during the same four-day window.
