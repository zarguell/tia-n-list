The Storm ransomware group has claimed a data breach at Penfold Motors and separately at Insight Credit Union, both announced on September 15, 2026. The Penfold Motors claim threatens to release exfiltrated data if the company refuses to negotiate. The Insight Credit Union claim follows the same pattern: Storm claims network infiltration and threatens full data release without engagement. Both victims are mid-sized organizations in sectors (automotive and financial services) that increasingly appear in ransomware leak site postings.

Storm is a ransomware-as-a-service operation that has been active since mid-2025, specializing in double-extortion campaigns where data theft precedes encryption. The group typically gains initial access through compromised VPN credentials or exploitation of unpatched public-facing applications. The simultaneous claims against a US credit union and a motor dealership suggest the group is running parallel campaigns against multiple victims rather than a single coordinated attack.

Organizations in the financial services and automotive sectors should verify that VPN multi-factor authentication is enforced, review backup isolation procedures, and confirm incident response plans account for data exfiltration scenarios. The Storm group's leak site typically publishes data within 7-14 days of a failed negotiation, creating urgency for affected organizations to respond quickly.
