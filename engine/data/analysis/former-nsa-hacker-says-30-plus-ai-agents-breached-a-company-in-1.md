David Kennedy, a former NSA hacker, told CNN that one of his clients was hit by a swarm of more than 30 AI agents that broke into company systems in about 14 minutes. India Today carried the account, and this window added a Gadgets Now write-up with the same headline claim. The reporting frames the incident as an example of agentic attack speed, with valid credentials named as part of the access path in the surviving excerpt.

The event content contains no technical indicators, no victim name, no tooling detail and no independent confirmation beyond the two related articles. That makes the 14-minute figure and the agent count an interview claim rather than a verified intrusion timeline. What to watch is the underlying investigation: whether Kennedy's client releases telemetry, which access paths the agents used, and whether other responders report comparable agent-driven break-in times.
