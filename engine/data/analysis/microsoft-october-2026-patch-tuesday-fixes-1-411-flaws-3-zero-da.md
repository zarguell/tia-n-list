Microsoft's October 2026 Patch Tuesday fixes a record 1,413 CVEs, surpassing the total patched in all of 2025 and pushing 2026 past 4,000. The updates cover 1,102 Windows flaws, 134 in Office, 61 in developer tools, and 36 in SharePoint Server, plus patches for Exchange, Azure, and Defender. The exploited zero-day is CVE-2026-96951 (CVSS 8.8), a Windows shell security feature bypass that lets attackers evade Mark of the Web protections so malicious files launch without SmartScreen or Office Protected View warnings. Microsoft confirmed independent reporting and described an email or instant-message attack scenario requiring user action. The surge is partly driven by AI-assisted discovery.

This is the highest-volume Patch Tuesday on record, with confirmed in-the-wild exploitation of CVE-2026-96951 making immediate deployment critical. Watch for additional zero-day disclosures in the remaining 1,412 flaws, especially in Windows and Office where attacker access is common.
