Microsoft's October 2026 Patch Tuesday addresses a record 1,411 flaws, including one actively exploited and two publicly disclosed zero-days, plus 70 critical vulnerabilities. The event notes end-of-support dates for Windows 11 24H2 Home/Pro and Windows 10 Enterprise LTSB 2016, making this both a security and lifecycle milestone.

Organizations should prioritize the actively exploited and zero-day fixes first, then work through the critical remote-code-execution bugs. Plan migrations off the now-unsupported editions promptly, since no further updates will be released.
