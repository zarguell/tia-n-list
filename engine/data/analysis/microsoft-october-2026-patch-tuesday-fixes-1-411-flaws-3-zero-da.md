Microsoft's October 2026 Patch Tuesday is the largest on record, fixing 1,411 flaws including one actively exploited and two publicly disclosed zero-days. Seventy of the bugs are rated Critical, 57 of them remote code execution. The headline total counts only what shipped on Patch Tuesday itself and excludes 348 flaws fixed earlier in the month across Azure services, Exchange, and other products. Microsoft credits AI-assisted discovery for the swelling totals.

Two things deserve attention beyond the count. Windows 11 24H2 Home and Pro plus Windows 10 Enterprise LTSB 2016 just reached end of support, so unpatched holdouts lose coverage now. And the sheer volume strains enterprise testing cycles, which is exactly when the exploited zero-day matters most. Watch for CISA KEV additions tying specific CVEs to in-the-wild use and for any reports of exploitation racing the patch window.
