Microsoft's October 2026 Patch Tuesday fixes 1,411 flaws, including three zero-days, across the monthly cumulative updates. Coverage also highlights an extended servicing cutoff for Microsoft Exchange 2016 and 2019, where security updates end in October, and separate patches for two dozen remote code execution flaws in Office. Self-hosted and legacy estates face the largest exposure window.

Organizations should treat the zero-days and any KEV-listed items as the first patching wave, followed by internet-facing and identity-facing components. Exchange 2016 and 2019 shops in particular need an upgrade or extended support plan because routine security updates stop after this cycle. Unpatched systems beyond the service cutoff will carry unaddressed risk with no further official fixes.
