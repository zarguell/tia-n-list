Microsoft's October 2026 Patch Tuesday fixes 1,411 flaws, including 70 critical vulnerabilities (57 remote code execution, 11 elevation of privilege, 2 spoofing). The release covers one actively exploited bug and two publicly disclosed zero-days. Notable CVEs listed include CVE-2026-102214, CVE-2026-105733, CVE-2026-108398, CVE-2026-108399, CVE-2026-56155, CVE-2026-70229, CVE-2026-70343, CVE-2026-70571, CVE-2026-88156, CVE-2026-92707, CVE-2026-96207, and CVE-2026-96951. Windows 11 24H2 Home and Pro, plus Windows 10 Enterprise LTSB 2016, reach end of support with this update. The scale is record-breaking and reflects AI-assisted remediation efforts.

This is a must-patch cycle: active exploitation plus public disclosure of zero-days means attackers already have working paths. The end-of-support milestone for 24H2 Home and Pro adds urgency for consumer and small-business environments that may delay updates.
