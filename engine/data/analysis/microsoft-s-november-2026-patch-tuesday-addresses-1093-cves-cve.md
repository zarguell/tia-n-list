Microsoft's November 2026 Patch Tuesday fixed 1093 CVEs across 287 products and components, with 63 rated critical, 1029 important, and one moderate. Elevation of privilege flaws made up 48.9% of the total and remote code execution 27.1%. The heaviest-hit products were Office Word (65 CVEs), Office Excel (64), SQL Server (53), the Windows Advanced Rasterization Platform (37), and Windows Installer (36). This is the eleventh and largest Patch Tuesday of 2026 by raw count.

The one actively exploited issue is CVE-2026-124194, an Outlook spoofing vulnerability rated moderate at CVSS 3.1 6.5. The product UI fails to warn the user of an unsafe action, letting an unauthenticated network attacker spoof content. Microsoft's own advisory frames it as achievable by convincing a target to open a crafted Office file, after which the attacker obtains the target's NTLMv2 hash for relay or offline cracking. Microsoft added CVE-2026-124194 to the CISA KEV catalog, and it is the flaw every downstream roundup flagged as the one to patch first this cycle.

Despite the low CVSS score, the NTLMv2 credential theft angle and the confirmed in-the-wild use make CVE-2026-124194 the priority among a very large batch. Prioritize Outlook and Office patching, then move to the critical RCE and EoP items in Word, Excel, SQL Server, and the Windows graphics and installer components. Watch for follow-on advisories on any of the 63 criticals that later show exploitation, and for a possible expansion of the KEV listing.
