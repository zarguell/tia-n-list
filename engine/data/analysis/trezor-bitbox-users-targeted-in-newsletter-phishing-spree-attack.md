Attackers are targeting Trezor and BitBox hardware wallet users through compromised or spoofed newsletter mailing channels. The phishing emails impersonate legitimate wallet vendor communications and urge recipients to create wallet backups, which would expose private keys or seed phrases.

This is a supply-chain-adjacent phishing technique: rather than sending emails from obvious fake domains, the attackers are exploiting the trust users place in legitimate mailing list infrastructure. Crypto wallet users are high-value targets because a successful phish yields direct access to funds with no intermediary bank to reverse transactions.

The attack underscores that newsletter and mailing list platforms are a viable phishing vector for financial targeting. Users of hardware wallets should verify any backup or recovery request directly through the device manufacturer's official channels or app, never through email links.
