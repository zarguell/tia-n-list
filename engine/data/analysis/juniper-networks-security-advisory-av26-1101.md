AV26-1101, dated 28 October 2026, covers Juniper Session Smart Conductor and Session Smart Router releases before 7.0.5, 7.1.7 and 7.2.3. The companion Juniper bulletin JSA112183 lists 18 CVEs in those products, two rated 9.8. ThreatAft flagged CVE-2026-103401 at 8.8, an arbitrary file overwrite as root that affects mist-managed deployments but not conductor-managed ones. SecurityWeek carried the Juniper fixes in a broader patch roundup with Adobe and Veeam; the exploitation discussion in the material here concerns the Juniper command injection, not the other vendor products.

Attention moved to CVE-2026-103406, an operating system command injection a remote unauthenticated attacker can reach on Session Smart Conductor and Router. CVE.org's record labels it exploited. Posts from Infonewscyber, AX News, ErrorBudget and others call it actively exploited, and a CISA advisory notice, CISA-2026:1125, reports the addition of one exploited vulnerability to the catalog. A 26 November Secquill article repeats that CISA says attackers are exploiting the critical Session Smart Conductor flaw. The items agree on the flaw and the exploitation claim but do not publish victim counts, indicators or a detailed attack chain.

Operationally, this splits into a broad patch exercise and a single urgent bug. The 18-CVE bulletin applies to the upgrade boundary of 7.0.5, 7.1.7 and 7.2.3, while CVE-2026-103406 should be treated as an active threat on any reachable conductor or router. Watch for the KEV remediation date, for a vendor advisory with exploitation details, and for confirmation of whether the mist-managed path behaves differently from conductor-managed deployments. No patch status or detection guidance beyond the version boundary appears in this story's material.
