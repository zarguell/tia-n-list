Juniper's October 28, 2026 Session Smart Router and Conductor bulletin covers 18 vulnerabilities. The Canadian Cyber Centre lists affected releases prior to 7.0.5, 7.1.7 and 7.2.3, so administrators should move to the appropriate fixed train. Two flaws were rated 9.8, including the actively exploited command-injection issue in Conductor.

The most urgent flaw is CVE-2026-103406, a CVSS 9.8 OS command injection in Session Smart Conductor's AutomatedProvisioner service. A specially crafted Salt minion ID can execute arbitrary commands with root privileges. Juniper updated its advisory after at least one malicious exploitation was reported to its SIRT, and CISA added the bug to KEV on Wednesday with a three-day federal remediation deadline.

Both Conductor and Router deployments should be checked against the bulletin rather than relying on product name alone. Watch for additional victim disclosures and detection guidance around crafted Salt registrations. Upgrade to a fixed release, and investigate unexpected AutomatedProvisioner activity on systems that remained exposed.
