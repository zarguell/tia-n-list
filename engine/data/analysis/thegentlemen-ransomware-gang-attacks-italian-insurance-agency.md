TheGentlemen ransomware group has claimed an attack against an insurance agency in Lodi, Lombardy, Italy. The victim operates in the regional insurance market serving the Milan metropolitan area. Separately, LockBit 5 affiliates have claimed an attack against the municipality of Robecco sul Naviglio, also in the Milan province. Both incidents fit the pattern of Italian regional organizations being targeted by ransomware operators seeking payouts from entities with limited cybersecurity budgets and recovery capabilities. TheGentlemen is a relatively newer entrant to the ransomware-as-a-service ecosystem, having emerged in early 2026 with a focus on European targets. Insurance agencies are high-value targets due to the combination of policyholder PII, financial records, and potential access to health data. Organizations in this sector should verify backup integrity and ensure incident response plans account for double-extortion scenarios.
