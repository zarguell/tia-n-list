Cisco Talos tracks a financially motivated cluster it calls UAT-12643, with a Go-based Overlord remote access trojan, a Python infostealer named Good Game and a multi-cryptocurrency clipboard hijacker. Lures and payload names point at cryptocurrency holders, gaming account owners, finance staff and VoIP users. A financial statement decoy PDF opens when Good Game runs, while other variants hide in a trojanized Viber installer and files named microsip.

Overlord hides its command-and-control by embedding encrypted server URLs in Solana transaction memos. Breakglass Intelligence described a related Node.js stage-one dropper with high confidence links to TraderTraitor that resolved C2 from memos on an attacker-controlled Solana wallet, counting 51 memo transactions over about four months and seven Vultr VPS nodes in France, with activity back to November 2025. Those IPs had no detections in ThreatFox, MalwareBazaar or VirusTotal at the time, and blockchain memos leave no domain to sinkhole. Earlier npm research on the MALFEX campaign found 12 packages published since August 2023, eight malicious, with 40,767 downloads and three Overlord RAT loaders using lifecycle hooks.

File harvesting keywords in English, French, Spanish and Portuguese suggest European victims. Watch for further use of Solana memos as a dead drop, npm packages that fetch executables through install hooks, and hunting for Overlord RAT and Good Game artifacts on endpoints in finance, call-center and crypto-related environments.
