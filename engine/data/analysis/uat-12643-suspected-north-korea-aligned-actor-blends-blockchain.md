Cisco Talos tracks UAT-12643 as a low-confidence North Korea-aligned cluster running three payloads: the Go-based Overlord RAT, the Python Good Game stealer version 1.1.2 packaged from Python 3.12.0 with PyInstaller, and a C++ clipboard hijacker. Overlord discovers C2 by reading encrypted WebSocket URLs from Solana Memo Program transactions on an actor-controlled wallet, selecting 43-character instruction data after getSignatures and getTransaction calls. Talos counted 35 RAT commands, including reverse SOCKS proxying and reflective DLL plugin loading. The implant marks itself critical through ProcessBreakOnTermination so forced termination triggers a CRITICAL_PROCESS_DIED crash, and it survives a reset by writing a random 20-character payload and a batch stub under the Recovery OEM path while patching ResetConfig.xml.

That mix turns malware removal into asset recovery. One execution can expose 21 cryptocurrency wallets, 23 browser families, Discord and ROBLOSECURITY tokens, and documents matching a four-language keyword list in English, French, Spanish and Portuguese, while the clipboard hijacker rewrites copied wallet addresses every 500 milliseconds. Archives are uploaded to gofile.io and a Discord webhook carries the victim profile, so funds can move before a ticket is triaged. The Solana memo channel makes single-host blocks age badly: posting a new memo redirects every deployed implant on its next discovery cycle. Recovery-path persistence also means a routine reset can bring the RAT back, so a clean rebuild has to include the recovery partition. Overlord distribution is not exclusive to this cluster; October reporting on the MALFEX npm campaign counted 40,767 downloads of packages delivering the same RAT.

No CVE or vendor hotfix applies here, since the campaign abuses installers, shortcuts and living-off-the-land binaries. Block egress to 41.216.188.11 and the observed ports: 8000 for the Flask dropper, 5173 for Overlord WebSocket C2, and 5656 and 5959 for Python stealer C2. Hunt for MpCmdRun.exe invoked with -DownloadFile, certutil -urlcache -f, mshta.exe fetching raw binaries through MSXML2.ServerXMLHTTP and ADODB.Stream into %TEMP%\s.exe, and msbuild.exe compiling inline C# with a spoofed BITS/7.8 user agent. Check Run keys for Zoipers and MsgEdgeUpdate, the hidden %APPDATA%\MsgEdgeUpdate.exe, SK_ prefixed archives in temp, url_opened flag files, and ResetConfig.xml changes under the Recovery OEM directory. ClamAV signatures such as Win.Trojan.OverlordRAT-10060615-0 and Win.Infostealer.GoodGame-10060617-0, plus Snort SIDs 1:67491 and 7:75, give detection coverage.

Two developments would change the assessment. Publication of the actor-controlled Solana wallet and its memo history, or a confirmed victim list in Europe, would turn low-confidence attribution and inferred victimology into a scoped incident with measurable losses. Evidence that the same operators sit behind the Lazarus Solana memo activity reported in March 2026 and the UNK_DeadDrop Overlord campaign Proofpoint documented in June 2026, rather than several actors sharing tooling, would raise confidence in state alignment and widen targeting beyond crypto holders, gamers and VoIP users.
