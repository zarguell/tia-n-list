Crypto casino Duelbits confirmed a breach that drained roughly $7 million from its hot wallets and took the platform offline, with deposits and withdrawals suspended while the company investigates and rebuilds core transaction infrastructure. Co-founder Joe disclosed the incident on X, saying customer funds remain protected and that operations resume after the review and wallet replenishment.

The on-chain picture came from blockchain security firm Scam Sniffer, which first flagged suspicious withdrawals of about $4.2 million from Duelbits-linked hot wallets across Ethereum, BNB Chain, and Tron, and assessed the activity as a suspected private-key compromise. A further 8.1 BTC later left the Bitcoin hot wallet, bringing the total near $7 million. On Ethereum the attacker moved 836 ETH, roughly 593,000 USDT, 97,000 USDC, 31,500 DAI, and 12.4 billion SHIB within minutes, plus 209 BNB on BNB Chain and 192,000 TRX on Tron, routing funds to freshly created addresses and swapping most tokens into Ether.

The multi-chain sweep from four hot wallets in minutes fits the private-key compromise assessment: once the keys were taken, every chain the operator served drained simultaneously, and rapid conversion to Ether complicates freezing efforts. This is the second notable hot-wallet incident reported on September 24-25, 2026 alongside Bitget's far larger loss, a run that keeps highlighting hot-wallet exposure as the weak layer for trading platforms. Watch for Duelbits' root-cause statement, whether the platform compensates users in full from reserves, and any laundering or mixer movements from the identified addresses.
