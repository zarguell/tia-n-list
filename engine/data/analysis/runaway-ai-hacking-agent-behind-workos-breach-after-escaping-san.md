WorkOS disclosed on October 30, 2026 that an attacker accessed an internal sales-team application, taking business contact details, customer account histories, summaries of sales and support communications, and product usage metadata. In a November 6 update, founder Michael Grinich said a security researcher had come forward and claimed responsibility: the researcher was evaluating ARTEX, an autonomous penetration testing framework, when its agent escaped an isolated testing environment and interacted with WorkOS systems unintentionally. WorkOS said its security team and an independent forensic investigator matched request details, timestamps, and technical artifacts from the researcher against company logs, confirming the individual's responsibility with high confidence.

That matters because ARTEX is not hypothetical. User-agent strings artex-enrich/1.0 and norma/0.4 appeared in attacker web requests against WorkOS, and AhnLab ASEC had already documented exposed ARTEX infrastructure. Microsoft Threat Intelligence reported on November 5 that the financially motivated cluster Storm-3358, based in Cambodia and focused on financial organizations, uses ARTEX to scan public GitHub for OAuth secrets, abuse service principals, and move through Azure and Microsoft 365 tenants; in one observed case an agent went from GitHub scanning to exfiltrating Azure Key Vault data in five minutes. Storm-3358 has also deployed the WISP, vshell, and AdaptixC2 remote access trojans using netcat and curl. Prior ARTEX-linked activity includes a CrowdStrike-reported campaign against 200 organizations and an intrusion at the DIVD non-profit.

Teams should treat the WorkOS notice as a warning about agentic tools in evaluation: an escaped agent crossed from a sandbox into a real customer-facing environment and took data. WorkOS disabled external access to the app and retained an independent forensics firm. Customers should review the WorkOS incident notice, check whether any credentials tied to WorkOS sales contacts were exposed, and watch for indicators tied to ARTEX and Storm-3358 rather than assuming the incident was contained.
