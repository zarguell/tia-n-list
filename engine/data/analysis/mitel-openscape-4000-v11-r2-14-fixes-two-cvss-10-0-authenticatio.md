Mitel advisory MISA-2026-0013, dated November 18, 2026, lists three Platform component flaws in OpenScape 4000 and OpenScape 4000 Manager. MTLVULN-1785 is unauthenticated access rated CVSS 10.0, MTLVULN-1786 is unauthenticated command injection rated 10.0, and MTLVULN-1793 is adjacent-network command injection rated 9.6. The two unauthenticated flaws make exposed management interfaces a priority, particularly because command injection can threaten the telephony systems these products administer.

Affected builds include V11 R0.22 through V11 R2.11 and listed V10 R1 releases. Mitel identifies V11 R2.14, PLT HF5 for V11 R1.26, and patches for older branches as fixes, and recommends Security Mode restricted access as a workaround. The advisory said CVE identifiers had been requested but were not yet assigned when it was published.

Subsequent reporting attaches CVE-2026-137724, a CVSS 10.0 missing-authentication flaw for a critical function, to the unauthenticated access issue and names V11 R2.14 as the fixed release. That gives scanners and asset inventories an identifier for MTLVULN-1785, but the available material does not map the CVE to either command-injection flaw.

No event in this story reports observed exploitation or a public proof of concept. Watch for internet-exposed OpenScape 4000 Manager interfaces, patch levels against the listed V11 and V10 builds, additional CVE mappings for MTLVULN-1786 and MTLVULN-1793, and evidence of active exploitation. Restricted access is a stopgap rather than a replacement for the fixed releases.
