Check Point Research's State of Ransomware Q2 2026 report shows the ecosystem still concentrated but with a widening tail. The top 10 groups accounted for 57.6% of all victims, down from 71% in Q1, while the number of active groups climbed from 71 to 93, a new high for the tracked period. Victim volume held at an elevated baseline: data leak sites recorded 2,139 victims in Q2, essentially flat quarter over quarter (up 0.8%) but up 33% year over year.

Qilin remained the most prolific operator for a fourth straight quarter with 279 victims, though its count fell 17%, while The Gentlemen surged 62% to 269 victims and actually outpaced Qilin during June. The top of the field is still winning, but the gap between leaders and challengers narrowed sharply, and the pool of active groups is the largest on record.

The takeaway for defenders is that the RaaS market is becoming more fragmented, not less. More active groups means more distinct TTPs, leak-site cadence and extortion behavior to track, and the flat victim volume at record levels suggests pressure on organizations is not easing. Watch whether the Q3 count keeps the tail growing and whether challengers like The Gentlemen convert close quarters into sustained leadership, plus any shifts in average ransom demands that usually accompany a more competitive extortion market.
