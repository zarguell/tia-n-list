IDC Frontier disclosed a ransomware attack on its IDCF Cloud service that caused an outage at an East Japan data center cluster starting October 7 at 3:40 AM local time. The company shut down the network to prevent spread and said 495 companies and government clients were impacted. The infrastructure-as-a-service platform rents virtual servers, storage, and networking in Japanese data centers; management consoles were disabled across all regions while security verification proceeds.

This is a significant disruption to Japanese digital infrastructure tied to a SoftBank Group subsidiary. Watch for confirmation of the intrusion vector, whether customer data was exfiltrated beyond encryption, and restoration timelines for the affected East Japan Region 1 services.
