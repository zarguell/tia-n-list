IDC Frontier disclosed a ransomware attack on its IDCF Cloud that started October 7 at 3:40 AM local time in East Japan Region 1, forcing a shutdown of network and systems. The company says 495 companies and local governments using the cloud service were impacted. IDCF isolated the region and disabled customer management-console access across all regions while verifying security.

No CVEs or actor attribution have been named. Watch for updates on restoration timelines, whether the intrusion route is identified, and if other SoftBank Group subsidiaries report related activity.
