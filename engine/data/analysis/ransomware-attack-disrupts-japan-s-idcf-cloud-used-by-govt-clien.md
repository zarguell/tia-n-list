Japan's IDCF Cloud, a SoftBank subsidiary serving government clients, was hit by a ransomware attack affecting 495 organizations, according to thecybersecguru.com reporting. The threat actor claims 3.6 PB of encrypted data. The disruption affects government and enterprise clients, prompting emergency response considerations. Watch for confirmation from Japan's Cybersecurity Strategic Headquarters, any CISA-style advisory, and updates on data exfiltration versus pure encryption.
