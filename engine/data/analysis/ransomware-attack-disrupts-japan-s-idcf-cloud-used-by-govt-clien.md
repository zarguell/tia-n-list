IDC Frontier confirmed a ransomware attack on its IDCF Cloud service starting October 7 at 03:40 local time, forcing a shutdown of East Japan Region 1. The firm reports 495 companies and local government clients affected. Network isolation and system shutdown were completed; other regions had management consoles shut off pending verification. The platform is operated by IDC Frontier, a SoftBank Group subsidiary, and provides virtual servers, storage, and networking in Japanese data centers.

This is a significant supply-chain incident for Japanese government and enterprise customers relying on the region. The company states the intrusion route is still under investigation and that customer notifications are ongoing. Watch for updates on the scope of data access (not just service disruption), whether customer virtual instances were encrypted or exfiltrated, and any cross-region spread.
