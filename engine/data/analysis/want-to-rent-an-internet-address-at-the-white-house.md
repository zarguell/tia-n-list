Layer3 Intel detected residential proxy nodes beaconing from the internet address block reserved for the Executive Office of the President (AS2650) between October 5 and November 3, with 236 exits across 28 providers. Synthient tied the activity to multiple Windows 11 installations of Infatica, a proxy program frequently bundled into free mobile and desktop applications.

Infatica's founder, Vladimir Fomenko, is a Russian national whose King Servers hosting firm was linked to 2016 intrusions against election systems in Arizona and Illinois. The finding raises the possibility that staff endpoints in the White House network are running proxy software that rents out government IP space. Watch for confirmation of whether devices were compromised or misconfigured, and for any official remediation or takedown actions.
