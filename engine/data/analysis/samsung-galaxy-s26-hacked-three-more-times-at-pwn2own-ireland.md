Samsung Galaxy S26 was hacked three times during the second day of Pwn2Own Ireland 2026, according to the event report. Researchers from KAIST Hacking Lab, PetoWorks, and Mobile Hacking Lab exploited the flagship. The contest also saw exploits of a Sonos Era 300 speaker, an Oracle Autonomous AI Database, and a Home Assistant Green hub, with $232,500 awarded for 45 unique zero-day vulnerabilities. Trend Micro's Zero Day Initiative organizes the event; vendors have 90 days to patch after disclosure. The S26 results show mobile security remains a live target at high-skill competitions.
