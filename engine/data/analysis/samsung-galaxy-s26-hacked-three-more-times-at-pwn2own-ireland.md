Samsung Galaxy S26 was hacked three times during Pwn2Own Ireland 2026 by KAIST Hacking Lab, PetoWorks, and Mobile Hacking Lab researchers. The exploits were demonstrated against fully patched devices running latest firmware, meeting Pwn2Own rules that require arbitrary code execution. Trend Micro Zero Day Initiative organizes the contest and gives vendors 90 days to patch before public disclosure. The Samsung exploits were part of 45 unique zero-days shown on day two, with $232,500 in awards distributed. No CVEs are listed yet for these mobile exploits. Watch for vendor patches and ZDI disclosure timelines.
