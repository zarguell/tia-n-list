Operation TorNado, a joint international action running from May 2025 to October 2026, produced 354 arrests targeting vendors and buyers on the Archetyp, Nemesis, Tor2Door, Bohemia and Kingdom dark web marketplaces. Agencies from 14 countries took part, with Europol coordinating cross-checks through SIENA and forensic analysis, and national authorities running separate investigations against vendors and buyers on forums, single-vendor shops and encrypted messaging services. Europol's framing is that the markets keep adapting despite repeated takedowns, which is the durable lesson from a decade of these operations.

The seizure numbers are the concrete part. In Europe, authorities took more than 60 million euro in cash and cryptocurrency, 950 kilograms of drugs including amphetamines, cocaine, ketamine, psilocybin mushrooms, MDMA and cannabis, and 57 firearms. In the United States, the tally was more than 4.8 metric tonnes of drugs, 152 firearms and over 150 million euro. The Department of Justice describes the US cases as complementary but separate investigations that followed the intelligence generated by earlier JCODE takedowns of darknet markets and vendor shops.

For threat intelligence teams the operational value is in the market churn rather than the arrest count: Archetyp, Nemesis, Tor2Door, Bohemia and Kingdom were all live targets in the same window, so any monitoring that keys on a single market name will lose coverage when one folds. What to watch is which of the five re-emerges under new administration, and whether the drug-centric enforcement focus leaves the credential and data shops that share the same infrastructure untouched.
