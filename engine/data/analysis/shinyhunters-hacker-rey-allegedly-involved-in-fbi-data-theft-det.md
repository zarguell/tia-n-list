A key member of the ShinyHunters hacking group, known as "Rey," was detained in Jordan and is allegedly cooperating with the FBI. The group claims to have stolen data on every FBI employee. Reuters reported that two sources said he was helping identify fellow members. The detention follows previous ShinyHunters operations, including exploitation of Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day CVE-2026-35273 before Oracle's June 10 advisory.

The group has targeted government and enterprise environments through supply chain and application vulnerabilities. Law enforcement cooperation from a detained leader could expose infrastructure, affiliates, and victim lists. Organizations should review exposure from any ShinyHunters-related breaches, rotate credentials, and monitor for leaked data from FBI-related sources. The case demonstrates international law enforcement coordination against high-profile extortion groups.
