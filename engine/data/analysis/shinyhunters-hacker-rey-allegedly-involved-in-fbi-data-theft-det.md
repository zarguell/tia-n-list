Reuters reports that a key ShinyHunters member known as Rey was detained this week in Jordan, citing three people familiar with the matter. Two of the sources say he is cooperating with the FBI to identify fellow members. The group had claimed it stole data on every FBI employee.

The FBI claim traces to September 2026. ShinyHunters told BleepingComputer it exploited the Oracle PeopleTools zero-day CVE-2026-35273 (CVSS 9.8, unauthenticated RCE, disclosed by Oracle on June 10) on the night of September 21, reached FBI-managed AWS GovCloud infrastructure, and stole a claimed 2 to 3 TB covering current and former employees, job applicants, and internal records. The FBI confirmed it was investigating unauthorized activity affecting FBIjobs.gov. Mandiant and Google Threat Intelligence separately tracked renewed mass exploitation of the same PeopleSoft flaw by UNC6240, including WAF bypasses that requested /%50SEMHUB/ instead of /PSEMHUB/.

This detention is the first visible law-enforcement action tied to the FBI breach claim, and the cooperation reporting is sourced but not officially confirmed.
