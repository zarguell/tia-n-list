The ShinyHunters extortion group is linked to a detained member identified as Saif al-Din Khader, known online as "Rey" and "Hikki-Chan," who was detained in Jordan. Reuters reports he is cooperating with the FBI to identify fellow members. The group previously exploited Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day CVE-2026-35273 before Oracle's June 10 advisory, then moved to a claimed FBI breach via AWS GovCloud infrastructure in September, with assertions of 2TB to 3TB of employee, applicant, and internal records stolen.

No new technical IOCs are confirmed beyond the existing PeopleSoft exploitation wave tracked under CVE-2026-35273. The detention and cooperation could reduce group activity, but the PeopleSoft vulnerability continues to be exploited across higher education, technology, healthcare, and government targets.
