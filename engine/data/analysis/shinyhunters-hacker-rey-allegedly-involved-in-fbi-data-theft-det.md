A core ShinyHunters operator known as Rey, allegedly part of the crew that claims to have stolen data on every FBI employee, was detained in Jordan this week, and two of three Reuters sources say he is cooperating with the FBI to identify his fellow members. The theft claim behind that case dates to September 21, when the group told BleepingComputer it used CVE-2026-35273, the unauthenticated PeopleTools remote code execution flaw Oracle patched in an out-of-band Security Alert on June 10, 2026 and rated CVSS 9.8, to reach FBI systems and then move laterally into FBI-managed AWS GovCloud infrastructure. The group claims 2TB to 3TB taken, covering current and former employees, job applicants, and services it names as FBI Criminal Justice, HR, and Medlink. The FBI has confirmed only an investigation into activity affecting its recruitment portal, FBIjobs.gov.

The detention is the story, not the breach claim itself, because it converts months of extortion theater into a counterintelligence matter with a cooperating insider at its center. A crew that developed this exploit against universities in the May 27 to June 9 zero-day window, then retooled it in September to defeat the mitigations defenders deployed after June, demonstrated a repeatable industrial pipeline, and Mandiant's September 26 report already shows the renewed wave spreading from higher education into technology, IT services, healthcare, agriculture, transportation, and government. The critical lesson sits in the bypass: the actor defeated string-based WAF rules by requesting /%50SEMHUB/ instead of /PSEMHUB/, a single URL-encoded character, which means every organization that considered a literal-path WAF rule sufficient after June 10 has been re-exposed since September. An FBI breach claim would normally be noise from a group with a history of exaggeration, but a detained operator who can name names is the kind of development extortion claims do not produce.

Defenders running PeopleSoft should first verify the June 10, 2026 Security Alert build is actually deployed, then stop trusting undecoded path matching and either normalize URLs before rule evaluation or block the Environment Management Hub servlet outright. Hunt for Mandiant's named tooling: a Ple64.exe binary on PeopleSoft hosts, tunnel.jsp or tunnel.jspx servlets staged from the Neo-reGeorg toolkit in web directories, unexpected MeshCentral MeshAgent services, and outbound traffic from PeopleSoft hosts to winmanage-me.network at 104.219.234.138. Rotate Integration Broker and web-tier credentials plus any cloud credentials reachable from the web tier, and treat root-level activity with particular suspicion since a quarter of the actor's observed post-exploitation commands ran as root or NT Authority\SYSTEM.

Two developments would move this assessment. Substantiation or collapse of the 2TB to 3TB FBI claim would settle whether the bureau suffered a real personnel-data loss or absorbed a bluff, and either outcome will shape how aggressively the group is pursued. Extradition timelines out of Jordan matter just as much, because Rey's value to the FBI decays as the group rotates infrastructure and members, and any named associates who surface in charging documents would confirm how much of ShinyHunters the cooperation actually reaches.
