The United Kingdom sanctioned Cryptomus, a cryptocurrency platform also known as Certa Payments, Xeltox Enterprises, and now Heleket, for processing payments tied to ransomware, narcotics, darknet markets, and Russian government financial institutions. The sanctions freeze U.K.-held assets and bar British firms from doing business with the platform; VexPay, TokenSpot, and Tsunami Payments were also targeted. Chainalysis noted the sanctions strike broader infrastructure, and the platform has changed names repeatedly since 2022.
