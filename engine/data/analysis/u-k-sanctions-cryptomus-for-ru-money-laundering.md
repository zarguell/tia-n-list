On October 8 the UK sanctioned Cryptomus, a crypto payment platform accused of processing payments that benefit the Russian government and help it dodge financial restrictions. The sanctions freeze its UK-held assets and bar British firms from dealing with it. Also named were VexPay, TokenSpot, and Tsunami Payments. Cryptomus emerged in 2022, has cycled through names including Certa Payments and Xeltox Enterprises, and reportedly rebranded to Heleket after Canada hit it with a record 176 million Canadian dollar fine for anti-money laundering failures.

Investigators describe the platform as a key cashier for Russian exchanges and cybercrime services spanning ransomware, narcotics, darknet markets, and fraud. Chainalysis notes the action targets the wider infrastructure, not just one brand. Watch for allied designations, measurable drops in the named services' volumes, and which successor platforms absorb the displaced flow.
