The Philippines Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) is investigating a potential data breach involving 410 files tied to 48 accredited technology firms. A hacker claims to have leaked the files. The event references beyondmachines.net. The claim is unverified by official DICT confirmation beyond the investigation announcement. The scale (48 firms, 410 files) suggests a supply-chain or accreditation database exposure if confirmed. Watch for DICT findings, any named affected firms, and whether leaked data appears on public forums or dark web markets.
