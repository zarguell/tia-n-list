DHS is banning DeepSeek and its affiliates from use by federal civilian agencies through a Federal Register notice. The action, ordered by Secretary Markwayne Mullin and effective since June 26, cites the Federal Acquisition Supply Chain Act and a review by the Federal Acquisition Security Council. The notice names Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence as the parent company and bars all related IT products and services.

Mullin claims DeepSeek models store data in China, where national security laws allow broad government access. The notice also points to potential privacy, mass data collection, and prompt-response transfer risks. The policy mirrors broader U.S. efforts to restrict Chinese AI infrastructure in government systems and should be watched for expansion to defense or contractor networks.
