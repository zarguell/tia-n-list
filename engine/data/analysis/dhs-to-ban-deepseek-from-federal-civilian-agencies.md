The Department of Homeland Security has ordered federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek AI products and any third-party technology built on DeepSeek models. The exclusion order, numbered 2026-01 and signed by Secretary Markwayne Mullin on July 24, 2026, was kept out of public view until an October Federal Register notice. The notice cites supply-chain risks, privacy concerns, and the possibility that user data could be accessed by the Chinese government.

The event content references a Federal Register publication set for Tuesday, a June 26 exclusion letter, and the broader removal mandate. Sources include CyberScoop, the BBC, and the Federal Register notice itself. The ban covers both direct DeepSeek products and downstream integrations, making it broader than a simple vendor blocklist entry.

No CVEs or exploitation details apply; the risk is strategic and supply-chain oriented. Agencies that have adopted DeepSeek-based tools should audit their environments, identify dependent products, and plan removal in line with the DHS order.
