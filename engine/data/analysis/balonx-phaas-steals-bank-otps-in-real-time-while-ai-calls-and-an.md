Group-IB researchers have identified Balonx Sistema, a subscription-based phishing-as-a-service platform targeting over 20 Mexican banks. The operation combines real-time phishing pages, MFA interception via an Android RAT, and AI-generated vishing calls to harvest credentials and OTPs. Balonx has harvested data from more than 1,100 victims since at least October 2025 and is operated from Mexico, promoted through Facebook groups associated with data trading and telemarketing fraud.

The platform lowers the barrier for banking fraud. Affiliates with limited technical skills can run campaigns through a centralized web panel. The Android RAT captures SMS messages and intercepts one-time passwords in real time, enabling MFA bypass. The AI vishing component adds a social engineering layer where victims receive phone calls from synthetic voices directing them to phishing pages.

Balonx represents the industrialization of banking fraud in Latin America. The combination of real-time OTP theft, mobile malware, and AI voice scams in a single subscription package is a step up from traditional credential kits. Financial institutions in the region should monitor for Balonx infrastructure and update fraud detection rules to account for the multi-vector approach. The campaign's reliance on Facebook for recruitment also highlights the platform's role in distributing criminal tooling.
