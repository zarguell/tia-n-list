The GSA Office of Inspector General found 116 improperly shared files in the agency's Google Drive containing controlled unclassified information, including Social Security numbers, phone numbers, proprietary research, vendor proposals, and site plans for a Washington, D.C. building. The audit said GSA did not effectively manage its third-party data loss prevention tool and that oversight deficiencies led to improper sharing with employees and contractors lacking legitimate need.

GSA IT responded that the documents were not exposed externally and found no indication of malicious intent. Despite that, the exposure of CUI and personally identifiable data to unnecessary internal access is a real data-protection failure. Monitor for any follow-up from GSA on remediation of the oversight gaps.
