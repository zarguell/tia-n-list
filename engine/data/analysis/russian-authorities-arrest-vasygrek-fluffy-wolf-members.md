Russia's Interior Ministry says police have dismantled VasyGrek, also known as Fluffy Wolf, a hacking group that mainly hit Russian companies. Earlier reporting said three suspected members of the group were arrested.

So far the reporting rests on two sources, en.hacks.gr and mccyber.tech. Watch for follow-up detail on the suspects, the group's tooling and victimology, and any related prosecutions. Treat the dismantlement claim as law-enforcement attribution until more reporting confirms the scope.
