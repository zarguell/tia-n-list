Japanese manufacturer Iris Ohyama, operator of the Iris Plaza online store, disclosed that personal information belonging to roughly 7.7 million people may have been exposed after unauthorized access to a third-party cloud service the company used. The company discovered the incident late last month and first notified the public on October 29, temporarily suspending Iris Plaza and related systems. The impact extends to up to 5.8 million registered and former members plus 1.9 million non-members, with exposed data including customer personal details.

The root cause is a compromise of an external cloud service used by the online store; investigators found a suspicious file in that cloud environment and confirmed unauthorized third-party access. Iris Ohyama is still determining the exact number of records and affected individuals. Watch items: whether the third-party vendor is identified, whether the scope changes as the investigation continues, and whether regulators or affected customers are notified given the scale of exposed personal data.
