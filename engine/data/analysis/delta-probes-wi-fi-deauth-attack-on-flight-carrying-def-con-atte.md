Delta is investigating an unauthorized Wi-Fi network that appeared on Flight 591 from Las Vegas to Atlanta, carrying passengers returning from DEF CON. The airline says the network was present for a short time, was not operated by Delta, and did not affect aircraft systems or safety.

The pattern matches the known Wi-Fi deauth prank: a rogue access point or deauthentication tool aimed at passengers' devices. DEF CON attendees are a deliberate target because of the sensitive material they carry. Delta says it will work with federal law enforcement and aviation regulators.

The practical lesson is device hygiene on aircraft: connect only to the airline's verified SSID, treat anything unexpected as hostile, and avoid touching work credentials or sensitive files over in-flight networks.
