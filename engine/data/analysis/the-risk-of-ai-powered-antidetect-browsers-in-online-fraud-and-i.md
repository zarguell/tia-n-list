Checkpoint research details how antidetect browsers like Kameleo, AdsPower, and MultiLogin enable large-scale fraud and influence operations. These tools hide digital identity, letting one operator manage thousands of isolated accounts across Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube while evading behavioral detection. The report notes AI integration: built-in automation templates and MCP servers that feed LLM content into pre-warmed profiles at machine speed. Threat actors use them for account takeover, financial fraud, phishing, malware distribution, and coordinated influence ahead of elections. It is a structural enabler rather than a single CVE, but it directly explains why platform-level detection keeps failing.
