Pokemon Center is notifying customers in the United Kingdom and Germany that personal information was exposed after a cyberattack on CEVA Logistics, the third-party fulfillment partner that processes deliveries for Pokemon Center in both markets. The company confirmed that Pokemon Center's own website and systems were not compromised. The breach activity began on July 30, 2026, according to CEVA's notification to Pokemon Center. Exposed data includes full names, mailing addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and details of PokemonCenter.com orders. Payment-card numbers were not affected because CEVA does not have access to that data. Pokemon Center also cancelled a batch of pending orders as a result of the incident, extending the operational impact beyond data exposure into supply-chain disruption.

The breach touches the UK and Germany specifically, suggesting CEVA's Pokemon-related fulfillment infrastructure in those two markets was targeted. While the absence of payment-card data limits one category of immediate financial fraud, the exposed combination of names, physical addresses, and order contents presents a material phishing and social-engineering risk. Pokemon Center customers should watch for targeted scams referencing recent orders.

Third-party logistics breaches are a recurring pattern. CEVA Logistics is a major global freight and fulfillment provider serving many retail clients. The Pokemon Center incident raises the question of whether CEVA's broader customer base was affected, or whether this was specific to Pokemon-related systems. Watch for CEVA's own disclosure and any notification from other retailers that use CEVA for fulfillment.
