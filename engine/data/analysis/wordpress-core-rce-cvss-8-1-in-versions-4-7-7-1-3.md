CVE-2026-119977 is a high-severity (CVSS 8.1) unauthenticated remote code execution flaw in WordPress Core versions 4.7 through 7.1.3, discovered by Picus Security researcher Umut Bayram. It is a time-of-check to time-of-use race condition in full-package core upgrades: WordPress stages installer files in predictable, web-accessible locations during an upgrade, and an attacker can probe those paths and reach installer components, potentially replacing wp-config.php, within the brief upgrade window.

WordPress released version 7.1.4 on October 28, 2026, addressing 22 vulnerabilities across 17 advisories, with same-day backports to supported branches down to 4.7.39. CISA added CVE-2026-119977 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 3, 2026, requiring federal civilian agencies to remediate by November 6 under Binding Operational Directive 26-04. Wordfence telemetry recorded more than 18,500 requests targeting staged setup-config.php paths in the first 21 hours after disclosure and over 240,000 exploit attempts overall; Wordfence Premium received a firewall rule on October 28, with free users receiving it after a 30-day delay on November 27.

The flaw is notable because the act of updating can itself be the exposure: sites are vulnerable only while an upgrade is in progress, but successful exploitation means complete site compromise. Administrators should ensure upgrades complete promptly, verify sites are on 7.1.4 or the corresponding backported release, and review for signs of compromise if an upgrade was in progress during the exposure window.
