Group-IB researchers identified Balonx Sistema as a subscription-based Phishing-as-a-Service platform operated by an individual in Mexico, targeting over 20 Mexican financial institutions. The platform has harvested credentials and financial data from more than 1,100 victims since at least October 2025. It is advertised through Facebook groups linked to telemarketing fraud and recruits affiliates via Telegram, offering weekly subscription plans with Individual (two devices) and Office (eight subordinate accounts) tiers.

Unlike standard phishing kits, Balonx maintains a persistent WebSocket connection between the victim's page and the attacker's control panel, enabling real-time monitoring and live page manipulation during active sessions. Operators can push up to 14 fraudulent screens. The platform also deploys Android malware for mobile credential theft and uses AI-powered voice fraud (vishing) to supplement the phishing chain.

Payments are automated through cryptocurrency exchange services, structuring the operation like a commercial SaaS business. The combination of real-time phishing, Android RAT, rotating domains, and AI vishing in a single subscription package represents an escalation in the industrialization of financial fraud tooling. Financial institutions operating in Mexico should monitor for the infrastructure indicators and Android malware associated with Balonx.
