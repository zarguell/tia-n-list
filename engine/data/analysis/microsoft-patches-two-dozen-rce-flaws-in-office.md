Microsoft released a second batch of patches after its October Patch Tuesday, fixing over 30 vulnerabilities with the Office suite taking the bulk. Two dozen of those are high-severity remote code execution flaws, including half a dozen reachable via the Preview Pane. Most RCE bugs are buffer overflows, off-by-one errors, use-after-free, path traversal, or numeric truncation defects in Word, with a few in Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint, and SharePoint. Microsoft states none were exploited as zero-days. Additional fixes cover information disclosure, a security bypass in Office, a denial-of-service in ASP.NET Core, an elevation of privilege in Identity Web, and information disclosure in MSAL for Node.js. Mac versions of Office LTSC and Office 365 also received updated advisories.

The scope is significant for enterprise document workflows because Preview Pane exploitation lowers the interaction bar. Attackers still need a victim to open a malicious file in most cases, which keeps the risk tied to social engineering rather than unauthenticated remote access. Organizations running Office on Mac should apply the updated advisories alongside the main patches. With no in-the-wild exploitation reported, the priority is routine deployment rather than emergency response.
