The ICO reprimanded the UK Home Office over failures in the Helios border watchlist system, where records could not be corrected and a phone call served as the official workaround. The Register reported the system was waved through without a working fix for bad data.

The case highlights operational risk in border and identity systems where data quality failures translate directly into harm for individuals wrongly flagged. Regulators treating the process failure as a reprimand rather than a fine leaves enforcement weight in question.

Watch for whether the Home Office publishes a remediation plan and whether affected individuals gain a formal correction route.
