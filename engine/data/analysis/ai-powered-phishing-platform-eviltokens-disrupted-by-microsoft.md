Microsoft disrupted EvilTokens, an AI-powered phishing platform active since February 2026 that compromised over 12,000 email accounts at more than 10,000 organizations across the US, Canada, UK, Australia, India, and France. The platform used device code phishing and AI-generated customized lures with 44 themes. Attackers gained persistent access tokens without passwords.

Watch for residual EvilTokens infrastructure and follow-on campaigns using similar device-code-abuse patterns.
