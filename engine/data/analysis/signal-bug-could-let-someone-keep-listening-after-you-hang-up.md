Signal calling library RingRTC has a shutdown-flaw tracked as CVE-2026-113380 (CVSS 9.3). A malicious participant can flood the call event queue and prevent disconnection, leaving the microphone (and possibly video) open after the victim hangs up. Affected versions are before 2.72.0. The bug does not break Signal encryption or expose messages; it only interferes with call teardown. There are no reports of in-the-wild exploitation yet. Users should update to 2.72.0 and treat any unexpected audio continuation as a possible trigger.
