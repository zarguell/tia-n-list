A flaw in Signal's RingRTC calling library, tracked as CVE-2026-113380 with a CVSS score of 9.3, affects versions earlier than 2.72.0. A call participant can overwhelm the call's internal event queue and interfere with the hangup teardown, potentially continuing to hear audio after the other party believes the call has ended. Video may also remain active, though that behavior is not confirmed. The issue does not involve encryption, stolen passwords, or compromised accounts, and there are no known reports of in-the-wild exploitation. The fix is to upgrade RingRTC to 2.72.0.

The risk is privacy-focused rather than system compromise: unwanted listening after a hangup could capture offhand remarks or nearby conversations. Users should update Signal clients to pull in the fixed RingRTC version.
