Signal fixed a critical flaw in its calling library, CVE-2026-113380 (CVSS 9.3), affecting RingRTC versions before 2.72.0. The vulnerability allows another call participant to overwhelm the call's internal event queue and interfere with disconnection steps, potentially leaving audio active after the user hangs up. The published description notes video may also remain active, though that behavior is unconfirmed.

No exploitation in the wild has been reported. The flaw relates specifically to how RingRTC shuts down a call rather than encryption, and it does not involve stolen credentials or account compromise. Signal users should upgrade to RingRTC 2.72.0 or later to eliminate the risk of unwanted post-hangup listening.
