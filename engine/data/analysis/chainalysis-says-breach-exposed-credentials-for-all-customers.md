Blockchain intelligence firm Chainalysis disclosed that an intrusion into its systems and internal tools exposed credentials and live API tokens belonging to all customers. The attackers used AI-assisted offensive agentic techniques to access customer names, email addresses, user IDs and organizational role labels. Chainalysis's customer base includes law enforcement agencies, government bodies, financial institutions, insurance companies and cryptocurrency businesses worldwide.

The company initially described the impact as limited to non-sensitive identity metadata, but a later update revealed that usernames and password hashes for all users were exposed in an on-path attack, also known as a man-in-the-middle attack, along with live tokens. Chainalysis stated it has no evidence that investigators' graph data was accessed or that its blockchain attribution repositories were altered. The company locked affected accounts and forced password resets.

Because live tokens were exposed, all Chainalysis customers should rotate API tokens and credentials immediately and review account access logs. The incident shows that tracing firms themselves are high-value targets, and customer organizations should assume their Chainalysis account data may be in adversary hands.
