Zscaler research examines how generative AI compresses the timeline from initial access to ransomware deployment. The piece argues that attackers use AI to accelerate reconnaissance, payload customization, and lateral movement, reducing the defender's reaction window.

For security teams, the key takeaway is that traditional detection and response timelines are too slow. The article emphasizes runtime monitoring and faster containment policies as the practical countermeasures.

Watch for follow-on reports that quantify actual time-to-ransom compression in observed incidents, since this piece is conceptual rather than incident-based.
