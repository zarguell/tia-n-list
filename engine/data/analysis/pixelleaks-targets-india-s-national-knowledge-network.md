Pixelleaks claimed responsibility on 24 November 2026 for an attack on NKN Delhi PoP, the National Knowledge Network node run under NIC and MeitY in India (nkn.gov.in). The group describes the target as national research-network infrastructure and says it holds a verified set of records, including financial and operational business material. It threatened to publish the full set unless its demands are met, noting that published files could be downloaded and redistributed without restriction.

The claim comes from the group's own statement carried by malware.news. The available material gives no independent confirmation of the intrusion, no data volume, and no ransom amount. The target is described as critical infrastructure, and the group argues that disclosure would carry regulatory, competitive and reputational consequences for the organization, which raises the stakes if any of the access is real.

Watch for a Pixelleaks leak-site posting with representative files, a response from NKN or NIC confirming or denying the intrusion, and any notice to Indian government agencies. Until one of those appears, the claim is unverified. Defenders with visibility into nkn.gov.in systems should look for signs of access rather than wait for the group's publication deadline.
