The Russian influence network CopyCop (Storm-1516) ran a three-wave disinformation campaign against the Firebird AI data center in Hrazdan, Armenia, between June 24 and July 13, 2026. The facility is a joint US-Armenian investment. Insikt Group documented three separate media impersonations timed to the data center's July opening. The first fabricated an imminent earthquake risk to the site. The second questioned the facility's economic viability. The third impersonated Iranian military communications, framing the data center as a legitimate military target.

The campaign's reach escalated across the three waves, growing from limited initial engagement to over 1.6 million combined views by the third impersonation. CopyCop reused the same social media amplifier network it has deployed against other Armenia-linked Western investment projects, suggesting a standing operational playbook rather than one-off activity.

The targeting of an AI-specific infrastructure project is notable. CopyCop appears to be capitalizing on heightened media attention around AI megaprojects to amplify pro-Russian narratives and undermine Armenia's westward economic realignment. The Iranian military impersonation in the third wave is a escalation, converting financial skepticism into a security threat narrative. Expect CopyCop to continue targeting Western technology investment in the South Caucasus, with AI projects drawing disproportionate attention due to their public profile.
