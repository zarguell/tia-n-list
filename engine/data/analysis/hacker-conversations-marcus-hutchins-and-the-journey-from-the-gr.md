SecurityWeek's Hacker Conversations series profiles Marcus Hutchins, best known for the WannaCry kill switch, tracing his path from the gray market to his current defensive security work. The interview covers his early activity, the WannaCry sinkhole, the federal charges he faced, and his subsequent reinvention in the industry.

Hutchins is one of the more visible case studies of the researcher gray zone, where skills that produce both malware and world-class defense live in the same person. The profile matters less as threat intelligence and more as context on how talent flows between the two sides and how the legal system treats that trajectory.

Watch for the specific lessons he draws about disclosure and working with law enforcement, which are the parts that inform how the industry handles former gray-market actors who move into legitimate research.
