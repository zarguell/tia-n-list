Recorded Future published a detailed report on Tajin Group, a third-party vendor operating on two Telegram-based Chinese-language guarantee marketplaces: Dabai Guarantee and Xinbi Guarantee. These marketplaces serve as underground escrow and brokerage platforms where Chinese-speaking criminals buy and sell stolen data, phishing kits, payment card details, and money laundering services. Tajin Group functions as a middleman, advertising services and facilitating transactions between buyers and sellers across these platforms.

The report documents how marketplace users are adopting stronger operational security. Third-party vendors increasingly use services that sell Telegram usernames and anonymous virtual numbers, letting operators link multiple Telegram accounts to a single number without needing physical SIM cards. This makes attribution and takedown harder. Tajin Group itself faces competitive pressure from other threat groups and adapts its tactics accordingly, including adjusting pricing and service offerings in response to market changes.

The phishing, payment card theft, and money laundering activities flowing through these guarantee marketplaces directly impact banks, fund transfer services, cryptocurrency exchanges, and individuals. The report frames Tajin Group as one node in a larger ecosystem rather than an isolated actor, highlighting the challenge of disrupting these distributed underground economies where vendors can rebrand or relocate across platforms.
