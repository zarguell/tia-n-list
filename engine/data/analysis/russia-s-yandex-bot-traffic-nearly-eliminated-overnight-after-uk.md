Ukraine drone strikes hit multiple Yandex cloud data centers, nearly eliminating Yandex bot traffic overnight. The strikes are described as retaliation for Moscow's strikes on Kyiv data centers. A Reddit repost links to a Tom's Hardware report; an Italian-language Mastodon post links to redhotcyber.com confirming strategic targeting of Yandex cloud infrastructure.

Why it matters: Yandex is a major Russian tech and cloud provider; disruptions to its data centers affect bot networks, search, and cloud services. The incident links cyber-physical targeting (drone strikes) directly to digital infrastructure impact.

What to watch: whether the strikes cause sustained service degradation for Yandex, whether Russian authorities confirm the damage, and whether the disruption measurably reduces disinformation bot activity tied to Yandex-hosted infrastructure.
