TheMailer is a self-hosted phishing kit that uses commercial AI models to write and rewrite lures. A November 17 research report from senior security researcher Daniel Kelley, covered by GBHackers, says the kit supports ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini, Mistral and DeepSeek. Its Inbox Loop feature feeds delivery results back into message generation, revising text that was flagged as spam. Dark Reading describes the same kit as using attacker-supplied models to personalize lures, evade spam filters and scale campaigns to tens of thousands of targets.

Filter evasion here is iterative rather than a fixed template. A kit that can regenerate a message after a spam verdict gives an operator a way to probe mailbox defenses and tune wording without rebuilding the campaign, which puts more weight on authentication, reputation and behavioral signals than on static content rules.

What to watch is whether Inbox Loop and the named model backends appear in live campaign reporting, along with any detail on the delivery-feedback signal the kit consumes. The two writeups describe capability and scale, not a specific victim set or a fixed set of lure text, so those remain open questions.
