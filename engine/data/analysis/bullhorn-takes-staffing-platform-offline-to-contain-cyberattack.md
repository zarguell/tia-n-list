Bullhorn, a cloud-based staffing and recruitment platform serving over 10,000 customers, disabled access to its platform to contain a cyberattack that caused a system-wide outage. The company isolated the issue, eradicated the immediate threat, and is working with CrowdStrike to restore services after security reviews. Customers began experiencing job-posting issues on Friday, with a system-wide outage confirmed the next day.

Bullhorn has not yet determined whether customer data was exfiltrated and promised direct notification if a breach is confirmed. Because the platform handles candidate and payroll data across thousands of staffing firms, a confirmed data breach would have broad downstream impact.

Customers should monitor Bullhorn's status page and FAQ, prepare for potential data-exposure notifications, and review their own access logs and credentials tied to the platform.
