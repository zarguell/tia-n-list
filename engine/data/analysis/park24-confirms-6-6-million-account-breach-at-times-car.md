Park24 has confirmed that attackers took data on about 6.6 million accounts from Times Car, Japan's largest car-sharing service. The first disclosure landed on September 28, and the company has already issued a third report, an unusually fast revision cycle that signals the scope is still moving.

The third report, covered September 29, narrows one of the worst aspects: identity document images were taken for about 1.6 million of the 6.6 million affected accounts. Images of licenses or ID cards are materially more dangerous than contact details, since they feed identity fraud and account takeover at other services rather than just spam.

Japan Cyber Watch is the single source in this story so far, and the truncated disclosures leave the full stolen-data list unclear. Watch for Park24's next report to confirm exactly which ID document types were exposed, whether the 1.6 million figure grows, and how the company handles notification obligations under Japan's revised APPI breach regime.
