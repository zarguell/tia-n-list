SOCRadar reports that a Russian-speaking ransomware crew tests its builds against Microsoft Defender before deployment. Of 17 organizations named in the operators' own material, 14 are in the United States and include a federal agency, three energy cooperatives and a healthcare provider. Those names come from crew material and should not be treated as a confirmed victim list.

In the researchers' Defender for Endpoint lab, a build was detected and isolated on October 16, 2026. Five days later, a reworked build contacted its command-and-control server without raising an alert. The crew's AI agent, instructed in Russian, disabled Event Tracing for Windows and the Antimalware Scan Interface on its third tool call. SOCRadar links the same build server and the MetaEncryptor/LostTrust extortion platform to an Eclipse ransomware server with high confidence.

The combination of critical-sector targeting, automated EDR evasion and pre-deployment testing is the operational concern. Watch for victim or authority notifications that validate the named organizations, plus endpoint detections for ETW or AMSI being disabled and network activity associated with the crew's callback infrastructure. The reported lab result shows a detection gap, not by itself a successful intrusion against every organization on the list.
