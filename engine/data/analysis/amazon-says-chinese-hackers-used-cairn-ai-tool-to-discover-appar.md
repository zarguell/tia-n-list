Amazon Threat Intelligence found an unauthenticated, publicly accessible instance of the Cairn AI penetration-testing framework on September 10, exposing an operator's prompts and the agents' output. The prompts were in Simplified Chinese and timestamps used China Standard Time. The campaign ran from September 8 to 10 against universities in East Asia outside mainland China. Operators supplied target IP addresses and told the agents to obtain shell access, and the agents planned, executed and adjusted without step-by-step direction. Many attempts failed: one run took 148 actions over 16 hours against an open Redis service, including a failed Log4Shell exploit, and another tried more than 30,000 credential combinations over six hours against a university GPU server.

On one target the agents identified MONAI Label, mapped its API and found a path traversal in the datastore upload endpoints in about 11 minutes, then wrote files arbitrarily. GHSA-qc22-rmgc-vg94 lists monailabel through 0.8.5 as affected, with no patched version at publication and authentication disabled by default through MONAI_LABEL_AUTH_ENABLE=False. In a later session the agents planted a Python reverse shell in a file executed at interpreter startup and got a root shell, then used a proxy supplied by the operator against a second university. Amazon assessed with high confidence that the flaw was a zero-day because no fix, issue, advisory or database entry existed. It is now tracked as CVE-2026-133968 and has been added to CISA's KEV catalog.

Related reporting widens the picture. Unit 42 attributes a separate Cairn campaign to Chinese-speaking actors tracked with moderate confidence as CL-STA-1238, describing 25 operations against at least 19 organizations in semiconductors, US defense policy and civil society between August and September 2026, with ten cases of confirmed access or data retrieval, more than one million personal records at one victim, persistent administrator accounts and modification of a live industry standards ballot. Google reported a Cairn intrusion in US aerospace. What to watch is patch availability for MONAI Label, the KEV deadline, and whether other exposed Cairn instances reveal more agent-discovered zero-days.
