Frontline Education is notifying school districts of a data breach after attackers exploited a vulnerability in third-party software to access systems and steal employee information, including Social Security numbers, email addresses, and physical addresses. The company, which provides administration and workforce management software used by school districts, identified the vulnerability on August 14, 2026, engaged an independent cybersecurity firm, remediated the issue, and contacted law enforcement. It has not disclosed which third-party application was involved or when access first occurred. A notification seen by BleepingComputer indicated all employees at one district were impacted. Frontline did not respond to a press inquiry.
