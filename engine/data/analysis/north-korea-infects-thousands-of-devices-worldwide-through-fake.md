This queue slot holds two unrelated campaigns that landed under one "thousands of devices" headline, and telling them apart is the first job. The original event, The Independent's September 22 piece, covers the North Korean fake-recruiter operation tracked as Contagious Interview and WaterPlum, which a joint advisory from Japan, the United States, Australia, and Germany published September 18 at ic3.gov/CSA/2026/260918.pdf puts at more than 30,000 infected devices in over 100 countries, over 7,000 cryptocurrency wallets drained, and at least $10.71 million taken from web designers, engineers, and crypto and Web3 specialists approached with job offers on LinkedIn and similar platforms. The update events describe a second, separate intrusion set: GreyNoise's Global Observation Grid watched a Chinese-speaking actor, scanning and attacking from a single IP address since early June 2026, exploit the wp2shell WordPress chain (CVE-2026-63030 and CVE-2026-60137) alongside ZyXEL GS1900 switches and known flaws in PAN-OS GlobalProtect, FlowiseAI, Nuclio, Proxmox, and Ubiquiti devices, stealing data from 996 devices and more than 18,500 backend database records across at least 49 organizations in 29 countries. GreyNoise attributes the activity to an actor related to Red Heron, the group behind a critical Gitea RCE (CVE-2026-60004), and one intrusion at an unnamed Western government organization involved a custom wp2shell exploit plus Windows reconnaissance checking Microsoft Defender and AMSI state.

The timing is the least current part of both stories, and that is the argument. The wp2shell chain was disclosed July 17 by Searchlight Cyber's Adam Kues (GHSA-ff9f-jf42-662q and GHSA-fpp7-x2x2-2mjf), patched the same day in WordPress 7.0.2 and 6.9.5, added to CISA's KEV catalog July 21, and scripted in public proof-of-concept exploits within days; Searchlight separately reported exploit brokers paying $500,000 for a WordPress RCE of this class, against an install base it estimates at over 500 million sites. September coverage of "Chinese hackers exploiting WordPress" therefore describes a two-month-old, KEV-listed, publicly scripted pre-auth RCE, and the operative question is not who is attacking but which internet-facing installs still run 6.9.0 through 6.9.4 or 7.0.0 through 7.0.1, with 6.8.x exposed to the CVE-2026-60137 SQL injection alone. The DPRK half is the same pattern in slower motion: the September 22 Independent story added nothing to the September 18 advisory it follows. A defender skimming the event list would read two fresh outbreaks; in fact both are late echoes, and neither shares infrastructure, victims, or remediation with the other.

Remediation splits along the same line. For WordPress, the floor is 7.0.2, 6.9.5, or 6.8.6 by branch (7.1 beta2 also carries the fix); WordPress.org is forcing auto-updates, so stragglers are hosts with updates disabled and need manual verification. Until patched, block /wp-json/batch/v1 and ?rest_route=/batch/v1 at the WAF or disable anonymous REST API access, noting Cloudflare's finding that the vulnerable path is reachable when no persistent object cache is in use. Hunt web server access logs for requests to the batch endpoint, for newly created administrator accounts, and for unfamiliar plugin directories or PHP files; Rapid7 has shipped unauthenticated detection checks since its July 20 content release, and Searchlight's wp2shell.com checker confirms exposure directly. Update ZyXEL GS1900 firmware and hunt for the GreyNoise actor's pattern, repeated exploit attempts against WordPress, GlobalProtect, Proxmox, and Ubiquity endpoints from one source IP, pulling the 18,500-record database exfiltration angle into any breach review for the 49 affected organizations. For the recruiter campaign, treat job-assessment coding tests as hostile payloads on developer endpoints and hunt for the BeaverTail, InvisibleFerret, OtterCookie, OtterCandy, and StoatWaffle families the advisory and NTT Security Japan's September 18 research index enumerate.

Two developments would change this assessment. Mass wp2shell scanning beyond the 49 confirmed organizations, visible in GreyNoise sensor volume or hosting-provider cleanup reports, would convert targeted exploitation into background noise, at which point edge blocking of the batch endpoint is the only control that scales ahead of patch verification. And a follow-up to the four-country advisory, or NTT's continued tracking of OtterCookie capability additions, that ties WaterPlum more tightly to the IT-worker proxy scheme now recruiting through Discord's "Mouse Review" server would confirm the campaign's shift from draining individual wallets toward insider placement and corporate infiltration through employed developers.
