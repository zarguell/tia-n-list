ShinyHunters claims it stole psychiatric and medical records of FBI staff and later said it will not publish the data. A Reuters report (Sept 27) and a Nextgov/FCW update (Sept 29) describe the group's intrusion into an FBI jobs portal and its subsequent demands. The group circulated limited samples containing agents' contact details, family information, and duty assignments. CyberScoop sources called the exposure a serious counterintelligence risk, describing it as a roadmap for locating FBI personnel. The FBI has not confirmed the full scope or directly attributed the breach to ShinyHunters, stating only that it is actively investigating. The group described its actions as a marketing campaign after the FBI warned about its tactics in a May PSA. The jobs site remains offline. Watch for official FBI attribution, confirmation of data categories, and any notification to affected staff.
