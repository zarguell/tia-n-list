ShinyHunters claims it breached the FBI jobs portal (fbijobs.gov) using an Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day (CVE-2026-35273, added to CISA KEV June 12), stealing psychiatric, medical, and staff records. The group demands the FBI retract a May 15 PSA. Multiple outlets (Reuters, NPR, The New York Times, 404 Media) confirm sample data includes names, addresses, Social Security numbers, and assignment details. The breach affects current, former, and prospective FBI employees. Watch for the FBI's response, arrest updates (Dutch police detained Pepijn van der Stap on September 15), and any evidence the group publishes the data.
