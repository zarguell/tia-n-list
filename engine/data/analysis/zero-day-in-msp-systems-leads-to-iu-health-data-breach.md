The story covers a zero-day vulnerability in managed service provider systems that led to an IU Health data breach; the AME Group suffered a ransomware attack exploiting this path. A newer update reports a separate healthcare AI breach at Xsolis that affected at least nine health systems and 1.4 million patients through a targeted phishing attack detected June 5, with unauthorized access occurring on January 20.

Both incidents highlight how healthcare and MSP supply chains remain vulnerable to ransomware and phishing-driven intrusion. The Xsolis breach specifically exposes utilization management and care coordination technology used by large patient populations, creating downstream risk for health systems relying on that platform. Watch for forensics conclusions from either breach, updated victim counts, and any disclosure of IOCs or attacker infrastructure.
