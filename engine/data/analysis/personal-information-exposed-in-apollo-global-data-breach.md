Apollo Global Management, one of the world's largest private equity firms, disclosed a data breach that exposed sensitive personal information to threat actors. The company notified affected individuals that social engineering enabled unauthorized access to some of its cloud platforms between July 6 and 10, 2026. The investigation remains ongoing.

This breach follows the familiar pattern of social engineering targeting cloud infrastructure at major financial institutions. The attack vector (social engineering) and the target (cloud platforms) suggest the threat actors likely compromised employee credentials or manipulated support staff into granting access. The four-day window of exposure (July 6-10) indicates a relatively brief but potentially impactful intrusion.

The breach matters because Apollo manages hundreds of billions in assets and holds sensitive financial data for institutional investors, portfolio companies, and employees. The scale of potential exposure depends on which cloud platforms were accessed and what data they contained. Watch for updates on the investigation's findings regarding the specific data types compromised, the number of affected individuals, and whether any follow-on incidents emerge from the exposed information.
