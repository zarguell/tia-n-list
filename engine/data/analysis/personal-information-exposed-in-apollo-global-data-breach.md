Advantest Corporation confirmed in an October 6, 2026 notification that a February ransomware attack exposed personally identifiable information. The attacker breached the Japanese semiconductor test equipment manufacturer's network on February 15, accessed some systems, deployed ransomware, and extracted data. Advantest previously could not confirm whether customer or employee data was affected; the new notification confirms PII was stolen, though the company says it has no evidence of misuse. The affected group (customers, employees, partners, or mixed) and total number of individuals remain unclear. This is a standard post-ransomware disclosure with limited operational detail; the key watch is whether the data appears in leak markets or triggers downstream identity fraud.
