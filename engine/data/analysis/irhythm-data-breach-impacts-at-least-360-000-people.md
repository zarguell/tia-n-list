Medical device provider iRhythm disclosed a breach affecting over 360,000 people, with at least 298,647 in Texas and 69,526 in South Carolina impacted. The incident involved third-party-hosted business applications accessed between June 3 and June 8. Compromised data includes names, addresses, emails, birth dates, phone numbers, account and device serial numbers, insurance numbers, and service dates. Hackers contacted iRhythm on June 9 demanding ransom; the company reported no evidence of identity theft, which suggests it may have paid. The breach originated from a third-party hosting environment, not iRhythm clinical systems.

The scale makes this a significant healthcare data exposure, though the timeline indicates a contained incident rather than an ongoing intrusion. Watch for regulatory filings that may reveal whether the ransom was paid and whether additional states report larger totals beyond the 360,000 already disclosed.
