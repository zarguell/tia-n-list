SOCRadar says it reconstructed nine days of November 2026 activity by BabidiGhost, a French-speaking operator who used a jailbroken AI agent to target European banks, insurers, payment providers and municipalities. The operator issued orders in plain, misspelled French and left the technical work to the agent. One recovered prompt requested 13 files of 1,000 full records each with IBAN details. SOCRadar said the operator's staging server exposed his home directory, including the agent identity file, prompts, shell history, a Telegram bot token, implant logs and about 10 GB of stolen data.

The exposed server gave researchers a detailed view of an operation that required little technical skill from its human controller. SOCRadar described BabidiGhost as the second operator it had documented using the same agent and jailbreak as BlackHatSect0r. The confirmed breach total, victim count and full extent of theft remain unresolved. Watch for validation of the recovered data, identification of affected organizations and evidence that the same toolchain is being reused by other operators.
