Microsoft expanded its bug bounty program for Dynamics 365 and Power Platform, raising maximum payouts to $60,000 for critical cross-tenant vulnerabilities. The program covers online Dynamics 365 applications (Sales, Customer Service, Finance, Supply Chain Management, Business Central, Commerce, Customer Insights), Power Apps, Power Automate, Power Pages, Dataverse, AI Builder, Power Admin, and Copilot Studio.

Cross-tenant bugs are the priority. A critical cross-tenant flaw lets an attacker in one customer's environment access data, resources, or administrative functions belonging to a different tenant, breaking the isolation that multi-tenant platforms depend on. Microsoft assigns a 100% reward multiplier to critical cross-tenant findings and 50% to important ones. The tiered structure ($1,250 to $60,000) targets flaws with direct, demonstrable security impact in supported cloud services.

The timing matters because Microsoft's multi-tenant platforms handle sensitive enterprise data across thousands of organizations. Cross-tenant vulnerabilities in Dataverse or Dynamics 365 could expose financial records, customer data, or supply chain information at scale. Security researchers who find these bugs now have stronger financial incentive to report through official channels rather than sell on secondary markets. The expanded scope also signals Microsoft's awareness that the attack surface in its cloud ecosystem continues to grow as more services integrate with Copilot and AI Builder.
