MDR reported that the University of Leipzig will likely stay offline for weeks after a cyberattack. The university's own disclosure adds that information may have left the UBL: names, postal addresses, phone numbers and email addresses. That is the first concrete scope statement in this material. The available text gives no actor, intrusion date, record count or technical entry point.

The combination of a prolonged outage and a personal-data notice points to an incident that touched both availability and stored records, though the two reports do not say whether the same cause produced both. Watch for the university to confirm which systems were restored, whether the UBL data set was actually exfiltrated rather than merely accessible, and when individual notifications go out. Until then, avoid treating the data list as a confirmed leak.
