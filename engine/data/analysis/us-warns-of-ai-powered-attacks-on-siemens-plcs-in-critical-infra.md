Five U.S. agencies (NSA, CISA, FBI, DOE, EPA) issued a joint advisory on an active campaign targeting Siemens S7 Series PLCs across critical infrastructure sectors: water, food, energy, chemical, manufacturing, and commercial facilities. The advisory explicitly states the threat extends beyond Siemens to all PLC owners. Attackers are using internet scanning services (Censys, ZoomEye) to find exposed PLCs, then targeting critical and high-severity vulnerabilities, outdated firmware, and weak authentication.

The standout detail: attackers are using AI to generate Python exploitation scripts built on snap7.dll and python-snap7 libraries. The agencies call this an evolution in threat actor capabilities, noting AI dramatically reduces the technical expertise and time needed to develop ICS exploitation tools. This lowers the barrier for adversaries to adapt to defenses and pivot to additional attack vectors quickly.

The advisory does not attribute the campaign to a specific nation-state. It frames the activity as a broad, ongoing threat to all PLC operators with internet-exposed devices. Mitigations center on network segmentation, authentication hardening, and firmware updates. Watch for follow-on advisories or sector-specific alerts as the agencies narrow attribution or identify additional targeted platforms.
