SecurityWeek reports that Turnitin disclosed unauthorized activity involving certain customer-facing systems in a 23 November letter from CEO Chris Caren. The company says it started its incident response plan, notified relevant authorities and brought in outside cyber experts. Caren said the systems are fully operational and that these customer-facing systems do not hold government identification credentials or institutional financial information. The affected surface includes the student submission portal, the instructor grading interface, institutional integration APIs and reporting dashboards.

Turnitin did not say which of those systems saw activity, whether data was accessed, or how many customers are affected. The University of Colorado Boulder said it was in contact with the company to learn whether CU Boulder data was involved. Watch for a scope statement from Turnitin and direct notices to institutions that rely on the submission and grading services.
