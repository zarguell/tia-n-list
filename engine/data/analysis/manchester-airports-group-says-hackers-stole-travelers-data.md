Manchester Airports Group (MAG), the UK's largest airport operator, disclosed a breach affecting customer data across Manchester, London Stansted, and East Midlands airports. The compromised information includes email addresses, phone numbers, vehicle registration numbers, and postcodes collected through Wi-Fi sign-ups, car park bookings, lounge access, and Fast Track registrations. MAG says payment details were not accessed and airport operations were unaffected, but the company suspended its online "Manage My Booking" service as a precaution and directed customers to phone support.

MAG operates three airports handling over 66 million passengers annually, employs 40,000 people, and generates 1.5 billion pounds in annual revenue. The breach was attributed to an unauthorized third party. MAG says it contained the intrusion, engaged external cybersecurity experts, and notified law enforcement. Customers are advised to watch for phishing attempts referencing their travel details.

The combination of travel patterns, vehicle registrations, and contact information creates a high-value dataset for targeted phishing and social engineering. The timing and scope suggest the attacker had sustained access to booking and loyalty systems rather than a single-point exfiltration.
