Double Counter, a Discord security bot, suffered a targeted infrastructure breach. Operator Tellter SAS reported that an attacker accessed cloud credentials, hijacked the bot, copied about 12 GB of database contents, and misused a separate payment account. The GBHackers update confirms the incident exposed personal information linked to millions of accounts. The original Reddit post mentioned roughly 275,000 email addresses and Discord usernames. Watch for further disclosure from Tellter SAS on exact victim scope and whether the attacker retained persistent access.
