Europol coordinated Operation TorNado with law enforcement from 14 countries over a span running from May 2025 to October 2026, targeting vendors and buyers on major dark web marketplaces involved in cybercrime, drug trafficking, and firearms offences. The operation resulted in 354 arrests and hundreds of house searches worldwide, with authorities seizing 5 tonnes of drugs and more than EUR 210 million in criminal assets.

The takedown is a law-enforcement action rather than a vulnerability, but it matters because dark web markets are a distribution channel for stolen data, malware, and initial-access services. The scale of the asset seizures and the 18-month operational window show a sustained, coordinated effort rather than a single-day bust. What to watch is whether successor markets absorb the displaced vendors and whether the seized assets include cryptocurrency or infrastructure that could disrupt adjacent cybercrime services.
