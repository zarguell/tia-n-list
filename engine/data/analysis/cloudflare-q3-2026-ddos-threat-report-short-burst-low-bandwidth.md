Cloudflare's Q3 2026 DDoS threat report records 18.2 million network-layer attacks across its network. The dominant trend is a 208 percent surge in short-burst, low-bandwidth floods lasting under 10 minutes and below 1 Gbps, while hyper-volumetric attacks declined. Roughly 96 percent of the mitigated attacks lasted less than 10 minutes, showing attackers favor frequent, quick disruptions over sustained high-volume floods.

The shift has defensive implications: brief attacks can still disrupt services and exhaust automated defenses without triggering long-duration mitigation thresholds. Organizations relying on rate-based protections may need to tighten time windows to catch sub-10-minute bursts, and monitoring should focus on frequency metrics rather than peak bandwidth alone.
