IDCF Cloud (IDC Frontier, SoftBank subsidiary) suffered unauthorized access around 03:40 JST on October 7, taking East Japan Region 1 offline. The attacker disrupted infrastructure needed for restoration. IDCF told UD Talk that restoring data is difficult, meaning backups or systems were compromised or unavailable. This is a live backup-destruction lesson: ransomware actors target recovery paths first. No specific CVE assigned; the incident highlights operational resilience gaps rather than a new vulnerability.
