The IDCF Cloud ransomware incident remains active with an October 9 update. The event notes that unauthorized access began around 03:40 JST on October 7, impacting East Japan Region 1 and affecting JR East and View Card through an external email delivery service. The update suggests potential data exposure for customers tied to the breached service. This is a continuing breach with government-level impact, since IDCF serves government clients, and the timeline spans from the initial October 7 access through the October 9 confirmation of affected partners.
