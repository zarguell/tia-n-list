IDC Frontier, a SoftBank subsidiary providing cloud services, confirmed a ransomware attack on October 7 caused a system outage affecting 495 corporate and local government customers in Japan's East Japan Region 1. The company is investigating damage and whether personal information was compromised. Updates note data restoration is difficult and external email delivery services may have exposed data for JR East and View Card. No CVE is linked; the incident is operational and under investigation.
