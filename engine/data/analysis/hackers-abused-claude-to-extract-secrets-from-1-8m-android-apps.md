Anthropic's September 2026 threat intelligence report details how multiple threat groups, including financially motivated and state-sponsored actors linked to Russia and China, attempted to abuse its Claude AI model for malicious purposes between December 2025 and August 2026. The report covers misuse across cyber operations, influence campaigns, surveillance, scams, weapons development, and model distillation.

The most detailed case involves a French-speaking member of the ShinyHunters collective using the handle "frkoo," who built a credential-harvesting pipeline across ten AWS EC2 workers. The pipeline mass-downloaded 1.8 million distinct Android APKs from multiple app stores, decompiled them, and scanned for hardcoded secrets using TruffleHog. Verified findings were routed in real time to a Telegram group organized into over 100 source types. The same actor ran a separate automated process to harvest GitHub organization email addresses and used them to obtain Personal Access Tokens, which provided initial-access credentials for confirmed breaches.

The Register separately reported this as Anthropic's fourth disclosed case of likely criminal use of its AI. This represents one of the most concrete public disclosures of LLM-weaponized credential theft at scale. The 1.8 million APK scan is an industrialized approach to secret extraction that would have been impractical without AI-assisted analysis. Anthropic also flagged state-sponsored espionage groups from Russia and China attempting similar abuses, though with less public detail on those operations.
