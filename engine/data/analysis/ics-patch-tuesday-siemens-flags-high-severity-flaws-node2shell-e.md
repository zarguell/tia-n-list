Siemens, Schneider Electric, and other industrial vendors published November ICS advisories. Siemens released nine advisories covering 10 vulnerabilities, including high-severity flaws in ROS#, Simcenter Femap, Mendix Studio Pro MCP Server, Simatic SCADA and PCS 7, Meta-iot2050, and Simatic WinCC Graphics Designer, plus two advisories on Node2Shell impact. Node2Shell exploitation attempts have been observed, but there is no indication of attacks specifically targeting Siemens products. Patches are unavailable for several affected products and some versions will not receive fixes; mitigations are recommended.

Schneider Electric published one medium-severity advisory for EcoStruxure Power Build Rapsody, covering arbitrary code execution via processing malicious files. Germany's VDE CERT published eight new advisories covering vulnerabilities in Beckhoff and MB Connect products.

Operators should inventory affected Siemens and Schneider products, apply available patches or mitigations, and monitor Node2Shell exploitation. For products that will not be fixed, plan compensating controls.
