Hacking attempts against the US Federal Election Commission's electronic filing system triggered an outage that left the system offline roughly a week before the November 3 elections, according to an email seen by Reuters. The FEC confirmed that its electronic filing system is currently offline while it responds to the activity.

The timing raises the stakes because the disruption lands during the final stretch of the election cycle, when campaigns and committees are filing reports. The available reporting does not specify who is behind the attempts, whether any data was accessed or altered, or when full service will be restored.

What to watch is whether the outage extends past filing deadlines, whether the FEC discloses a breach of filer or donor data, and whether investigators attribute the activity. A prolonged outage or confirmed data compromise would move this from an attempted intrusion to an election-infrastructure incident.
