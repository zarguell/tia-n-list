The US Federal Election Commission shut down its electronic filing system after an attempted cyber intrusion, blocking campaign-finance disclosures. Reuters reported the disruption a week before the November 3 elections, based on an internal email. The FEC stated the filing system was offline at the time of the statement, though the agency did not attribute the incident to a specific actor.

The timing makes this more than a routine outage: it prevents mandatory pre-election disclosure filing. Whether the intrusion achieved access or was limited to service disruption remains unclear. Watch for a post-incident attribution, confirmation of data access versus mere denial of service, and whether any filings submitted before shutdown were altered or exfiltrated.
