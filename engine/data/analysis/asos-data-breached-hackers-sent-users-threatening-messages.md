ASOS confirmed a breach after users received push notifications titled "Asos hacked" that claimed full data compromise and included a threatening link. BBC reported the notification reached dozens of users and ASOS shares fell almost 12 percent on the London Stock Exchange following the reports. The incident involved in-app messages rather than a direct infrastructure disclosure, so the scope of accessed data remains unconfirmed beyond the notification campaign.

The breach notification targeted mobile app users with a false claim of total compromise and a malicious link, which is a social-engineering escalation rather than a standard data-exfiltration event. ASOS confirmed the breach but did not release customer counts or categories of exposed data comparable to other retail breaches. Watch for follow-up regulatory filings or customer notices that clarify whether account credentials, payment details, or identity documents were accessed.
