ASOS confirmed unauthorized access to a third-party customer-communication platform after attackers delivered push notifications titled "Asos hacked" through the retailer's mobile app. The notification claimed a full Snowflake environment compromise and threatened data release, but ASOS has not verified that claim. The company says basic customer information, including names and contact details, may have been exposed; it does not believe payment-card data or account passwords were impacted. Shares dropped almost 12 percent after the alerts began around October 6. Multiple sources including BBC, The Guardian, BleepingComputer, and Standard note that the attackers used the notification infrastructure itself, which suggests unauthorized access to marketing or messaging systems rather than confirmation of a database breach. Watch for any verified data-leak publication and clarification of how many customers were affected.
