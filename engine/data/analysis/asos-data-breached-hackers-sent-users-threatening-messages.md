Asos mobile app users received a push notification titled "Asos hacked" that claimed full compromise of the retailer's data and included a threatening link. BBC News reported the notification reached dozens of users on October 6, 2026, and that Asos shares on the London Stock Exchange fell almost 12 percent following the reports. The event is covered by technology reporter Joe Tidy for BBC. The situation matters because it combines an apparent account compromise that enabled direct user-facing disinformation with measurable market impact on a major retailer, but the incident appears limited to the notification vector rather than a confirmed full data breach.
