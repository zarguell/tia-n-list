ASOS users received a push notification titled "Asos hacked" claiming full data compromise and containing a threatening link. The message reached dozens of users and caused ASOS shares to fall almost 12 percent on the London Stock Exchange according to the BBC report. The breach notification appears to have come through the retailer's customer notification platform rather than an authenticated account, and ASOS confirmed the incident to users. The story has been covered by BBC, Sky News, the Guardian, and other outlets since October 6.

No CVEs are directly tied to this incident; it is an operational breach and notification abuse rather than a vulnerability disclosure. The event updates include a BBC consumer guidance article asking what users should do after the message, which extends the story's public impact. Security teams should treat the notification abuse as a sign that the retailer's messaging pipeline or vendor integration was compromised.
