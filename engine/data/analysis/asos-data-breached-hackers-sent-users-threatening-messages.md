ASOS confirmed hackers compromised a third-party communication platform and sent unauthorized push notifications to mobile app users titled "ASOS hacked" claiming full Snowflake instance compromise. The notification threatened data leakage and included a link. ASOS told the London Stock Exchange that basic user information like names and contact details may have been accessed, but said payment card data and account passwords were not impacted. The retailer restricted access to notification platforms and engaged authorities. The company did not confirm a Snowflake compromise despite the claim in the notification. The breach caused ASOS shares to fall almost 12 percent. Monitor for any data leak publication by the attackers and confirmation of the third-party platform involved.
