Boston Scientific is notifying people affected by a cyberattack in which a hacker used voice phishing to gain access to its cloud infrastructure. The company learned of unauthorized access on May 12 and determined on November 5 that a breach had occurred. The Texas attorney general's breach portal puts the affected population at 117,789 people across multiple U.S. states, with personal details and medical information exposed.

The company says patient medical records and data from remote monitoring systems, connected medical devices, and clinical case management or workflow applications were not exposed. It also reports no disruption to manufacturing, ordering, distribution or service to patients. The boundary between the exposed cloud data and the excluded medical records is the key detail for impact assessment. Watch for the complete exposure dates, the final affected count and any revision to the data categories as notifications and regulatory filings develop.
