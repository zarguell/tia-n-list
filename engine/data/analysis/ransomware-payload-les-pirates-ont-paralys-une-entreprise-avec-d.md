Galago is a newly observed ransomware operation first flagged on 9 September 2026. Researchers began monitoring its dark leak site (DLS) on 15 September and found it inactive with no listed victims. Galago claims an operational partnership with Panzer and uses a Tor leak-site address with the pnzr4del prefix, sharing the pnzr prefix with Panzer's pnzruro site. That naming pattern suggests a possible link but does not prove shared operators, malware, or access.

Panzer has a more visible record: 32 victims published between 5 August and 23 September 2026, with advertised builds for Windows, Linux, ESXi, and FreeBSD. The event explicitly states those capabilities should not be attributed to Galago without evidence. No confirmed intrusion through Galago's own leak site has been substantiated.

What to watch: whether Galago's DLS becomes active with listed victims and whether analysis of any future incident links its malware or infrastructure directly to Panzer's known builds.
