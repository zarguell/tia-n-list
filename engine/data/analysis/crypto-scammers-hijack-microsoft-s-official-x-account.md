Unknown attackers hijacked Microsoft’s official X account, which has over 13 million followers, for what appeared to be a crypto pump-and-dump scheme. The event references a BleepingComputer report describing the compromise on Thursday. No long-term data breach was reported, but the account was used to post malicious content to a massive audience before recovery.

The incident is a reminder that high-profile brand accounts remain prime targets for crypto scams. Organizations should enforce stronger authentication and monitoring on official social media accounts, not only internal infrastructure.
