On Thursday, attackers hijacked Microsoft's official X account, which has over 13 million followers, and used it to promote a $Clippy crypto token in an apparent pump-and-dump. The account followed and reposted content from @clippymsftcto, a now-suspended account impersonating Microsoft's Clippy assistant, while a companion account @ClippyMSFT kept promoting the token with a claim of a liquidity pool paired directly with $MSFT.

Microsoft confirmed unauthorized access, removed the posts, and secured the account. It stated it does not support any cryptocurrency or crypto-related token and that it will take legal action, and it apologized for the posts. The Verge first reported the incident. Microsoft has not explained how the account was accessed.

Watch for the forensic outcome, since the attack path is undisclosed for now, and for further Clippy-brand impersonation accounts and similar brand-account hijacks used for token promotion.
