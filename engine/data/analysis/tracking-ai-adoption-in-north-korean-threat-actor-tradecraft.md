Microsoft Threat Intelligence tracked how North Korean threat actors operationalize artificial intelligence across their operations. The firm said the actors use AI as a force multiplier to accelerate and scale established workflows, with recent activity showing Jade Sleet running campaigns through three front companies in a single week and Onyx Sleet generating dozens of tailored lures in minutes.

The adoption extended to code development and offensive tooling, including Coral Sleet building a capability to trojanize private source code repositories and Diamond Sleet using AI for tailored targeting, including persona development and front company creation. Onyx Sleet and Pearl Sleet used the open source agentic AI penetration testing framework ARTEX in operations, showing quick adoption of new AI technologies. The actors operate under extensive international sanctions.
