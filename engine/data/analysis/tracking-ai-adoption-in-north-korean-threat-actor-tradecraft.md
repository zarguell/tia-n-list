Microsoft Threat Intelligence reports that North Korean threat actors are expanding their use of AI across operations, using it as a force multiplier to accelerate and scale established workflows. Named groups include Jade Sleet, Onyx Sleet, Coral Sleet, Diamond Sleet, and Pearl Sleet. Recent activity cited includes Jade Sleet launching campaigns through three front companies in a single week, Onyx Sleet generating dozens of tailored lures in minutes, Coral Sleet rapidly building a capability to trojanize private source code repositories, and Diamond Sleet using AI for tailored targeting workflows including persona development and front company creation.

Onyx Sleet and Pearl Sleet are also leveraging ARTEX, an open-source agentic AI penetration testing framework, indicating willingness to adopt new AI tooling for operations. Microsoft frames this as AI-assisted targeting and reconnaissance, social engineering and identity deception, software and tooling development, and operational scaling. The Democratic People's Republic of Korea operates under extensive international sanctions, which contextualizes the tradecraft, including the use of front companies and stolen identities to generate revenue. Defenders should watch for AI-generated lures and synthetic personas in recruitment and contracting channels.
