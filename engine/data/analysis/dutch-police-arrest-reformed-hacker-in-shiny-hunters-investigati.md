Dutch police arrested Pepijn van der Stap, a 23-year-old convicted cybercriminal previously linked to the ShinyHunters group. Sources say van der Stap operated under the handle Umbreon, was convicted in 2023 for data thefts earning between €1.5 million and €2.7 million, and worked by day at cybersecurity startup Hadrian while extorting victims. Following his arrest, remaining ShinyHunters members escalated attacks, stealing sensitive data from the FBI and extorting the Russian ransomware group Cl0p. The case highlights how convicted actors can maintain dual identities and how group dynamics shift rapidly after a key arrest.
