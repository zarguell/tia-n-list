Dutch police confirmed arresting a suspect in the ShinyHunters investigation, identified through KrebsOnSecurity reporting as Pepijn van der Stap. The police statement describes a 24-year-old man from Amsterdam arrested on September 16 while on supervised release after serving three years of a four-year sentence for the 2023 conviction covering multiple intrusions with data theft and extortion that Dutch prosecutors valued between 1.5 and 2.7 million euros. He now works as offensive security lead at Neo Security. Earlier reporting placed him in Almere and Lelystad, previously employed as a software engineer at the security startup Hadrian and volunteering at the Dutch Institute for Vulnerability Disclosure while operating as Umbreon, the handle under which he sold a database on 2.3 million Dutch residents on RaidForums in September 2021.

Two details make this more than a routine arrest. First, ShinyHunters' recent breach of the FBI jobs site appears to have been pinned on van der Stap by the group's current leader, a teenage criminal from Amman, Jordan known as Ray and part of Scattered LAPSUS$ Hunters; the FBI site was hit with what looks like a modified exploit for CVE-2026-35273, the Oracle PeopleSoft flaw ShinyHunters exploited as a zero-day in June, and the defacement included an oversized Umbreon image. Second, in early September Dutch police publicly sought help identifying a voice on an audio recording, indicating the investigation had been running before the arrest. Days after the arrest, remaining ShinyHunters members moved to extort the ransomware group Cl0p with stolen data.

The correct reading is that leadership removal has never disrupted this collective: French arrests of BreachForums administrators in June 2025 and the October 2025 BreachForums seizures preceded its richest run, including the Salesloft Drift token campaign touching roughly 1.5 billion records at 760 companies and a PeopleSoft campaign Google tied to CVE-2026-35273 across more than 100 organizations. The value here is intelligence and precedent, not disruption. Defenders should verify PeopleSoft estates carry the CVE-2026-35273 fix, audit OAuth grants and integration tokens on SaaS platforms, and enforce phishing-resistant MFA since the group's waves relied on vishing rather than product bugs. Watch the Rotterdam District Court proceedings expected to yield further information, and whether charges tie van der Stap to specific ShinyHunters intrusions or to the FBI breach.
