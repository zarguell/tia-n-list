MI5 issued an unprecedented Security Service Espionage Alert on September 30, 2026, stating that the primary purpose of the China General Technology Research Institute (CGTRI, also known as the China Academy of General Technology) is to fund research that directly improves the technical capability of China's Ministry of State Security for espionage. MI5 assesses CGTRI as an MSS front company. The Hacker News reported the alert on October 3.

More than 100 UK-linked academics have contributed to research projects funded by the MSS through CGTRI, in some cases without knowing who was behind the money. Funded topics include AI, cybersecurity, covert communications systems, and steganography. MI5 urged UK academic institutions to review ongoing and planned collaboration with CGTRI and to trace funding sources behind research partnerships with Chinese institutions, warning that continued collaboration could lead to prosecution under the National Security Act 2023 for providing material assistance to a foreign intelligence service. The Chinese embassy in London dismissed the accusations.

Watch for named institutions disclosing CGTRI ties, similar alerts extending the funding-front model beyond academia, and whether the first National Security Act enforcement cases emerge from this alert.
