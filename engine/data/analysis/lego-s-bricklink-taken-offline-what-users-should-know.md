LEGO took its BrickLink marketplace offline after detecting unusual activity. The company has not confirmed a hack or whether customer data was affected, and the outage lasted about a week before the marketplace returned online.

Users should watch for suspicious payment requests and avoid reusing passwords across accounts. The incident was reported by Infosecurity Magazine and Slashdot, and collectors unable to access the marketplace during the outage were advised to monitor official LEGO communications for updates.
