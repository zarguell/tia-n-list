The Register's 16 November report says customer and staff data at Infoblox was exposed through an internal SharePoint instance. The r/cybersecurity relay attributes the exposure to a third-party migration vendor that had access to that instance, and a Mastodon post links the same article. The available event content gives no record count, data categories, discovery date or window of vendor access.

The control to examine is the access granted for migration work on an internal document store. Watch for an Infoblox notice naming which customer and employee records were in the affected SharePoint site, how long the vendor account could read them, and whether it was scoped to migration tasks or held broader permissions.
