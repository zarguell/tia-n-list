The Register reports that customer and staff data at Infoblox was exposed through an internal SharePoint instance. The account relayed in the r/cybersecurity submission attributes the exposure to a third-party migration vendor that had access to that SharePoint instance. No record count, data categories or discovery timeline are given in the available event content.

The incident fits a recurring pattern in which a contractor's legitimate access becomes the path to internal document stores, so the access granted for migration work is the control to examine. Watch for Infoblox's own notice on which customer and employee records were in the affected SharePoint site, the window of vendor access, and whether the vendor account was scoped to migration tasks or had broader read permissions.
