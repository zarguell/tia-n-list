SBC Medical Group disclosed two incidents in a Form 8-K filed with the SEC. The first involved unauthorized access to databases in a website system run by its Japanese subsidiary, discovered on November 16. The company said an unauthorized third party accessed and extracted customer information, while the intrusion method and full scope remained under investigation. The second was unauthorized access to clinic administrator accounts on Tribeau, a third-party booking platform; SBC was notified of that compromise on the same day. Both affected Japan-based businesses.

The filing breaks the database exposure into categories. Basic personal information for 1,624,000 people included names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth, gender, patient card numbers, member IDs and survey responses. Reservation requests for 255,416 people and web inquiry content from 217,769 people were also taken. Treatment descriptions and stored image file names for 4,513 people, basic data plus treatment names and review text for 15,007 people, and credit card information for 14 people were compromised. SBC said all those cards were already expired or otherwise unable to process additional payments. SecurityWeek framed the total as over two million people. SBC manages clinics with 7 million patient visits a year and trades on Nasdaq as SBC.

Watch for an amended filing that separates the two incident populations and identifies whether Tribeau account abuse contributed to the database extraction. The current filing gives a discovery date and category counts, but no intrusion method, actor, or remediation timeline beyond the ongoing investigation.
