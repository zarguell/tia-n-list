SBC Medical Group disclosed two separate cybersecurity incidents in a Form 8-K filed with the SEC. The first involved unauthorized access to databases in a website system run by its Japanese subsidiary, discovered on November 16. The company said an unauthorized third party accessed and extracted customer information, while the intrusion method and full scope remained under investigation. The second incident was unauthorized access to clinic administrator accounts on Tribeau, a third-party booking platform; SBC was notified of that compromise on the same day. Both affected Japan-based businesses.

The database incident exposed basic personal information for 1,624,000 people, described in reporting as about 1.6 million customers and employees. Not every affected person had every data type exposed. SBC manages clinics that receive 7 million patient visits a year and is Nasdaq-listed as SBC, with a limited US presence and a minority stake in OrangeTwist.

Watch for an amended filing that narrows the data categories, separates the two incident populations and identifies whether the Tribeau account abuse contributed to the database extraction. The current filing gives a discovery date and a count, but no intrusion method, actor or remediation timeline beyond the ongoing investigation.
