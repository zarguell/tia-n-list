A CVSS 10 zero-day vulnerability in Cisco ISE (Identity Services Engine) allows attackers to gain root-level access without authentication. This is the same CVE-2026-76460 that CISA added to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Cisco ISE is widely deployed as a network access control and policy enforcement platform in enterprise environments. With 12 independent sources covering this story and a perfect 10.0 severity score, this is one of the highest-priority vulnerabilities in the current cycle.

The flaw lets an unauthenticated attacker compromise the ISE server, which controls who and what can access the network. A successful attack gives the adversary root on the appliance, visibility into authentication policies, and the ability to pivot to other network infrastructure. Public reporting indicates active exploitation with at least 9 detections and 9 IOCs cataloged. The Perimeter Site's coverage notes that attackers are already inside affected environments.

Organizations running Cisco ISE should patch immediately, check for the IOCs circulating in public advisories, and assume compromise if the appliance has been internet-facing without the fix. The combination of no authentication requirement, root access, confirmed exploitation, and KEV listing makes this a drop-everything patch.
