The DOJ and FBI seized two hacking tools linked to the Chinese government-backed group Flax Typhoon: Microscan, a vulnerability scanner, and FishHub, a spearphishing tool. Both were created by Integrity Technology Group, a China-based firm sanctioned last year and targeted in a 2024 botnet takedown. The seizures were court-authorized in the Western District of Pennsylvania and paired with a multi-agency advisory warning that the actors combine automated scanning, botnets, and hands-on exploitation against critical infrastructure.

The advisory highlights cross-site scripting, password spraying on Microsoft Exchange, and persistence through VPN software. This action removes infrastructure rather than arresting operators, so the underlying threat group remains active.
