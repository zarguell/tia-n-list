The Justice Department and FBI seized domain names for Microscan and FishHub, tools tied to the Chinese government-linked group Flax Typhoon and the Integrity Technology Group. The seizures, authorized in the Western District of Pennsylvania, deny hackers access to vulnerability scanning and spearphishing infrastructure. A joint FBI, CISA, and NSA advisory notes these actors combine automated scanning, botnets, and hands-on exploitation against critical infrastructure, using password spraying and VPN software for persistence. Watch for follow-on advisories and whether the seizures disrupt ongoing campaigns.
