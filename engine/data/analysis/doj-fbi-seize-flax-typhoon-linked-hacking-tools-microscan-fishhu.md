The Justice Department and FBI seized seven domains linked to the Chinese government-backed group Flax Typhoon and the firm Integrity Technology Group, which was sanctioned by the U.S. in 2024. The seizures target Microscan, a vulnerability scanning tool, and FishHub, a spearphishing tool. Authorities paired the domain takedowns with a joint advisory from the FBI, CISA, and NSA describing automated scanning, large-scale botnets, and hands-on exploitation aimed at critical infrastructure sectors. The advisory notes cross-site scripting attacks and password spraying on Microsoft Exchange servers, plus persistence via VPN software. This is a continuation of U.S. action against Integrity Technology Group, which was previously linked to a massive botnet operation.
