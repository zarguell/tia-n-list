CISA added four vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on September 9, 2026: CVE-2025-25249 (FortiGate CAPWAP buffer overflow, CVSS 7.3/9.8), CVE-2026-19490 (new, not previously tracked in this window), CVE-2026-20079 (Cisco Secure FMC authentication bypass, CVSS 10.0), and CVE-2026-87491 (Chromium V8 RCE, part of the BlueMoon exploit chain). Three of these four are already covered by dedicated stories: the FortiGate flaw is tied to active PivotC2 RAT exploitation, the Cisco FMC bug is under active exploitation confirmed by Cisco and Talos, and the Chromium zero-day is part of a multi-APT exploit chain.

CVE-2026-19490 is the novel addition not covered elsewhere in this batch. Its inclusion in KEV means CISA has evidence of active exploitation and expects federal agencies to remediate by the specified deadline. The batch spans firewall infrastructure (Fortinet, Cisco) and browser clients (Chromium), reflecting the breadth of currently targeted attack surfaces.

Organizations subject to BOD 22-01 should verify remediation of all four CVEs. The overlapping coverage with other active campaigns makes this a high-priority update: any unpatched FortiGate or Cisco FMC appliance is not just theoretically vulnerable but is actively being targeted by distinct threat operations.
