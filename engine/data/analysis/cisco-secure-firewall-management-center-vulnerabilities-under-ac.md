Cisco Talos has warned of active exploitation targeting two vulnerabilities in Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center: CVE-2026-20079 and CVE-2026-20316. Both are rated with severity scores indicating critical impact, and the advisory confirms observed exploitation in the wild. This is a fresh advisory from September 10, 2026.

The Firewall Management Center is the centralized management platform for Cisco's Secure Firewall product line. Compromise of this component gives attackers visibility into firewall configurations, the ability to modify access rules, and potential pivoting opportunities across managed devices. The two CVEs likely target different attack surfaces within the same management interface.

Organizations running Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center should apply any available patches immediately and audit for unauthorized access. Cisco typically provides mitigation through software updates and, where patches are not immediately available, configuration hardening guidelines. Given that management infrastructure is a high-value target, defenders should assume that exploitation may have occurred prior to patch application and check logs for suspicious administrative activity.
