The story covers eight compromised intermediate TLS certificates (eMudhra) that were widely trusted and could allow attackers to forge browser-trusted credentials for arbitrary domains. A researcher credited as Wayne demonstrated possession by signing messages with each weak key; Apple blocked the certificates on October 19, Microsoft on October 20, and Go released emergency updates blocking them on October 19.

The compromise was disclosed by Cloudflare on October 19, 2026 at approximately 17:00 UTC. Independent verification is available through a disclosure site published by Dennis Jackson that provides certificates, public keys, and signatures for the eight compromised intermediate CA keys. The scope spans root programs that had to push emergency blocks, making this a significant infrastructure trust event.
