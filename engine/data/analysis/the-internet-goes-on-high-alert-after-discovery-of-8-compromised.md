Eight intermediate CA certificates from eMudhra were compromised after researchers found their P-384 private keys were generated with values smaller than 256, allowing recovery by testing just 256 possible numbers. A researcher named Wayne discovered the weakness on October 17 and published signatures for verification. Mozilla blocked the certificates, Google and Apple followed, and Go released emergency updates blocking them. The event content notes the private key for the "EM OV TLS CA - G3C" certificate had a value of just 4, and BleepingComputer independently verified the match.

The core risk is certificate forgery: anyone holding one of the recovered private keys can mint browser-trusted credentials for arbitrary domains. Organizations should verify certificate trust stores and apply vendor updates promptly, especially since the certificates were cross-signed with Sectigo. This is a supply-chain-level trust failure with immediate TLS interception potential.
