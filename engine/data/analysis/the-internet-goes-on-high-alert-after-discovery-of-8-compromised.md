Researchers discovered eight compromised TLS intermediate certificates from eMudhra that used trivially brute-forceable P-384 private keys. Major browsers revoked the certificates and Mozilla blocked eight keys via OneCRL within three hours, then shipped Firefox 158.0.1 and NSS 3.131. The compromise shows how weak key generation in certificate issuance can undermine the entire PKI chain.

The event notes no exploitation has been confirmed yet, but the revoked certificates could have been used for man-in-the-middle attacks. Watch for any additional browser updates or vendor advisories confirming the full scope of affected certificates.
