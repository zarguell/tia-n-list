Eight eMudhra intermediate TLS certificates were compromised, with weak private keys allowing arbitrary domain forgery. Cloudflare reported awareness on October 19 at approximately 17:00 UTC, Apple blocked the certs on October 19, Microsoft on October 20, and Go released emergency updates on October 19. A researcher demonstrated possession by signing messages with each key.

Chrome and Firefox issued emergency blocks but left a gap: roughly 30,000 unexpired certificates remain unblocked. Monitor browser vendor updates and whether additional root programs revoke the remaining unblocked certs.
