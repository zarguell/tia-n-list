Eight compromised TLS certificates belonging to eMudhra intermediate CAs were discovered, with weak private keys making impersonation trivial. Six certificates trusted by Chrome, Mozilla, and Apple have been blocked; two Microsoft code-signing certificates were also blocked. The misconfiguration reduced entropy from 384 bits to 3 to 8 bits during an April 2025 key generation ceremony. Cisco Talos reported the issue and vendors have issued patches or trust-store updates. The incident affects a broad range of technologies relying on the compromised intermediates.
