Researchers discovered that private keys could be extracted from public certificate data, compromising eight intermediate certificates from eMudhra's Certificate Authority. The incident, disclosed October 20, 2026, prompted emergency blocks across browser platforms and revealed failures in the authority's cryptographic key generation process.

The affected certificates include six TLS intermediates. The key setup script bug allowed extraction with tiny integer values, meaning the compromise was structural rather than targeted. Organizations relying on eMudhra certificates should verify revocation status and review trust store updates.
