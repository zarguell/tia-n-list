Seqrite APT Team researchers tracked a China-nexus threat actor conducting Operation QUICSILVER, a targeted malware campaign against Myanmar government and diplomatic personnel. The operation used Burmese-language decoy documents impersonating Myanmar's Information Technology and Cyber Security Department under the Ministry of Transport and Communications. One lure posed as a graduation ceremony invitation for government training programs, complete with an official-looking ministry seal. The campaign targets government and IT-sector personnel in Myanmar, with recovered deleted files from the Recycle Bin suggesting attackers also maintain interest in diplomatic matters involving ASEAN, BIMSTEC, UN meetings, Malaysia, China, and Myanmar's foreign affairs.

The attack chain delivers malware through a Virtual Hard Disk (VHD) file disguised as a JPG image. When mounted, the VHD presents victims with what appears to be a PDF named TrainingAnnouncement.pdf, but the file is actually a malicious Windows shortcut (TrainingAnnouncement.pdf.lnk). Windows hides known file extensions by default, so victims see only a PDF icon. When opened, the LNK file launches a legitimate Microsoft-signed binary to execute a three-stage infection: initial access via the LNK, split payload reconstruction through an ftp script, and deployment of QUICAgent, a custom Golang-based backdoor implant. The campaign timeline spans from initial findings through technical analysis, with Seqrite publishing IOC indicators and MITRE ATT&CK mappings.

The campaign's focus on Myanmar diplomatic targets and its China-linked attribution make it significant for Southeast Asian geopolitical threat monitoring. The attackers operationalized recycled Bin artifacts from their own development environment, providing researchers with attribution context. Organizations in Myanmar's government and IT sectors should treat these IOCs as priority detection items, and diplomatic entities across ASEAN should monitor for similar VHD-based lures targeting foreign affairs personnel.
