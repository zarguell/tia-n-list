Justin Swaddle, a 20-year-old from Leeds tied to the loosely organized online criminal network The Com, was sentenced to two years in prison after pleading guilty to child sexual abuse offenses and blackmail affecting 117 victims across multiple countries. The National Crime Agency investigated him from January 2024 after his October 2023 arrest on charges of possessing, making, and distributing indecent images. He must register as a sex offender.

Swaddle was a minor when some of the crimes were committed, but the case lands in the middle of an ongoing law-enforcement crackdown on The Com's pattern of targeting children and teenagers for sextortion and blackmail. The network has seen a string of prosecutions in the US and UK, and each sentence adds to the pressure on its participants.

The takeaway for defenders is operational, not legal: sextortion campaigns tied to The Com and similar networks continue to target minors through social platforms, and organizations that operate those platforms should expect continued scrutiny of their reporting and takedown processes. Watch for further sentencings and for disclosures of the network's infrastructure as cases progress.
