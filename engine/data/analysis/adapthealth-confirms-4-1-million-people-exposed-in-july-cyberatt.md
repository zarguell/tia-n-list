AdaptHealth has confirmed that 4.1 million people had data exposed in a cyberattack discovered in July 2026, attributed to the ShinyHunters threat group. The company, which provides home medical devices and supplies including sleep-apnea equipment, oxygen therapy, and mobility products, said the breach occurred on June 5, 2026, when an unnamed threat actor compromised a third-party contractor's privileged account through social engineering.

The attackers accessed cloud-based business applications including internal patient management systems, document storage platforms, and electronic health record system portals. On June 15, the threat actor contacted AdaptHealth with a ransom demand. The exposed data includes full names, contact information, demographic data, health insurance information, and health records. AdaptHealth first disclosed the incident in an SEC filing on July 2, 2026, and issued breach notifications with instructions for 12-month credit monitoring enrollment.

ShinyHunters is a well-established threat group known for data extortion campaigns across healthcare, retail, and technology sectors. The social engineering vector targeting third-party contractor accounts is a recurring pattern in healthcare breaches, where vendor access to clinical systems creates broad exposure from a single compromised credential.
