SolarWinds patched two pre-authentication RCE flaws in Observability Self-Hosted, its on-premises and hybrid IT monitoring platform, both reported by Kai Huang of Armadin and fixed in version 2026.2.3. CVE-2026-28324 (CVSS 9.8, network vector) stems from insufficient integrity checks and affects versions up to 2026.2.2 running non-default, non-secure configurations. CVE-2026-28325 (CVSS 8.8, adjacent vector) is a deserialization of untrusted data flaw that requires a specific communication mode. Both advisories were published September 22, 2026, and neither is flagged as exploited in the wild.

Days earlier, on September 17, the vendor fixed CVE-2026-28326 (CVSS 8.8) in Access Rights Manager 2026.2.1: an unauthenticated RCE caused by a hardcoded static key, again credited to Huang. Three unauthenticated RCEs across two infrastructure-monitoring products in one week, with no exploitation evidence so far, is a pattern that recalls SolarWinds' history as an APT target, even though these are patch-response bugs rather than supply chain compromise.

The configuration caveats blunt but do not erase the risk, since monitoring platforms often run for years with settings nobody revisits. ARM's hardcoded key especially invites reverse engineering into reliable exploit code. Watch for PoC releases following the Armadin research, any KEV listings, and exploitation attempts against internet-exposed SolarWinds interfaces.
