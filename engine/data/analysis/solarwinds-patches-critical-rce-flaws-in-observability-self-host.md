SolarWinds patched three unauthenticated remote code execution vulnerabilities across two products in September 2026. In Observability Self-Hosted, CVE-2026-28324 (CVSS 9.8) is an insufficient integrity check leading to RCE on non-default, non-secure configurations, and CVE-2026-28325 (CVSS 8.8) is deserialization of untrusted data in a specific communication mode. Both affect all versions up to 2026.2.2 and are fixed in 2026.2.3, released September 22, 2026.

The third bug, CVE-2026-28326 (CVSS 8.8), affects Access Rights Manager up to 2026.2 and stems from a hardcoded static key; it was fixed in ARM 2026.2.1 with the advisory published September 17, 2026, five days before the Observability advisories. Canada's Cyber Centre flagged the Observability pair in advisory AV26-950 on September 23. All three were reported by Kai Huang of Armadin, and SolarWinds makes no claim of in-the-wild exploitation.

Configuration scope is the practical catch: the 9.8 and one 8.8 require non-default or specific communication settings, so teams should verify their actual configuration as well as patching. SolarWinds' monitoring and access-management products sit deep in enterprise networks, where an unauthenticated RCE is a jump-off point. What to watch: exploit releases for the integrity-check flaw, KEV listing, and whether hardened defaults land in future releases.
