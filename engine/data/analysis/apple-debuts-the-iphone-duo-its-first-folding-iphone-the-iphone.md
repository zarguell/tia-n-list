Apple announced the iPhone Duo, its first foldable iPhone, at the "Surprise and Shine" event on September 9, 2026. This is a product launch announcement, not a security event. The device represents Apple's first entry into the foldable phone category with a clamshell design.

From a security standpoint, new device categories introduce fresh attack surfaces: folding mechanisms may require new display drivers and hinge-related firmware, the larger unfolded display could change how apps handle screen transitions and data exposure, and Apple's first foldable will need to defend against an entire class of vulnerabilities that existing iPhone models have never faced. No specific security advisories or vulnerabilities have been disclosed for the iPhone Duo at launch.

This story is tracked for product awareness but does not require security action. Monitor Apple's security advisory feed for AV26-series advisories targeting the iPhone Duo once it ships.
