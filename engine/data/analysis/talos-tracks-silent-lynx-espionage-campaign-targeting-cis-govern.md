Cisco Talos published new research on a Kazakhstan-linked espionage group tracked as UAT-6275 (also TAG-193 by Recorded Future). The campaign targets government organizations across CIS countries, with infection chains using macro-enabled documents, PS2EXE downloaders, GitHub-hosted PowerShell, and a crafted WinRAR archive exploiting CVE-2025-8088. Recovered payloads include custom reverse shells, and exposed staging infrastructure revealed C2 builds and operational security lapses.

The group shows sustained activity from June 2025 through August 2026, with medium-confidence attribution to a Kazakhstan nexus based on infrastructure access, language artifacts, sample submissions, working hours, and regional targeting. Three publicly accessible staging directories and GitHub repository history provided direct visibility into payload builds and C2 changes. The focus extends to water resources and energy, consistent with regional intelligence collection. Watch for new indicators from the exposed infrastructure and any expansion beyond CIS targets.
