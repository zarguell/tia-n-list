Reco has documented a campaign it calls City-Forum that targets data exposed to anonymous users on Salesforce Experience Cloud and ServiceNow Service Portals. The operator uses a single machine, IP 158.220.87.79 hosted by German provider Contabo and resolving to city-forum.com, which has carried the same domain since March 2025 with no rotation. The toolset is custom and multi-platform: one Go binary hits Salesforce over both Aura and the newer LWR implementations and ServiceNow from the same box. The campaign is the first observed in-the-wild exploitation of Salesforce's UI-API guest surface, and it also abuses a native ServiceNow search endpoint with little public documentation.

Both platforms keep a Guest User that cannot be deleted, so anything readable by the guest profile is reachable by anyone on the internet. The busiest target logged over 560,000 events, essentially all guest Aura enumeration, and data is also pulled from LWR sites via GraphQL. Exfiltration is high volume but protocol-legitimate, which makes it hard to spot. Reco notes the activity is ongoing and increasing, and does not rule out ShinyHunters as the operator, though City-Forum's custom tooling differs from ShinyHunters' modified AuraInspector.

The fix is configuration, not patching: disable self-registration so guests cannot upgrade to authenticated users, and audit what guest profiles and sharing rules expose. Organizations running Experience Cloud or ServiceNow portals should check their guest-visible data against the IOCs and remediation guidance in Reco's report.
