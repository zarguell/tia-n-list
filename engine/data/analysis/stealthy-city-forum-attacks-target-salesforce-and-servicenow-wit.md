SecurityWeek reports stealthy campaigns designated 'City-Forum' targeting Salesforce and ServiceNow deployments with a custom toolset. The reporting was published August 12, 2026; technical details, including specific techniques and indicators, had not been released at that point.

Salesforce and ServiceNow are widely deployed enterprise platforms, and both manage sensitive tenant data, which makes them high-value targets for credential theft and tenant compromise. The campaign designation and the use of custom tooling suggest an organized, resourced operator.

Watch for the technical writeup with indicators of compromise, confirmation of affected tenants, and any overlap between the toolset and known malware families. The absence of public detail means the first technical report will define the response window.
