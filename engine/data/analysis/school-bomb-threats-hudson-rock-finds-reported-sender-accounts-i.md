On November 11, 2026, bomb-threat emails reached roughly 6,000 Italian schools, with more than 300 evacuated, and similar messages hit schools in Scotland, Northern Ireland, and Ireland. The mail was signed by "Mikhail Andropov, leader of the Vilayat of Misoginistan" and used the "Red Owl" label. Hudson Rock traced the two reported senders, Mosaad_Khalifa@den.kfs.edu.eg and f0523219@cloud.dyu.edu.tw, to stolen-credential holdings. The Egyptian address maps to a Vidar infection dated October 1, 2021, whose logs show 112 credentials pulled from Chrome, including Microsoft authentication entries. The Taiwanese address appears in AlienTXTBASE, dated more than a year before. Dayeh University suspended the alumnus account after finding it had sent bulk mail and appeared hacked.

The sending path was a legitimate university mailbox, not a rented server, so the attacker borrowed reputation and time instead of building either. A multi-year gap between the 2021 Vidar theft and the 2026 use shows unrotated credentials keep value long after the infection that produced them, and the Egyptian record's Microsoft authentication entries mean the same data could support inbox access, not just sending. Every organization with staff addresses in infostealer corpora faces the same exposure. The school disruption was the payload; the aged mailbox was the delivery mechanism.

Query mail gateway and Microsoft sign-in logs for Mosaad_Khalifa@den.kfs.edu.eg and f0523219@cloud.dyu.edu.tw, and for outbound mail with the subject "DEATH IS NEAR" or display name "Mikhail Andropov." Check abnormal outbound volume, new device sign-ins, and inbox rules that forward or delete mail. Reset passwords and revoke active sessions rather than only rotating, since the credentials were saved in Chrome and may sit in other breaches. Two developments would change the assessment. First, confirmation of what happened to the Egyptian account: exposure is established, but not whether that address sent any November 11 mail, and if it did the campaign has a second live origin. Second, attribution: Italian investigators hold the original headers and can say whether the accounts were operated directly or sold, and a named actor would move this from credential hygiene to active intrusion. The October 19 and November 2 waves, the latter to 271 Italian schools under an IRGC claim, show the signature is reused, so another wave is the likely near-term event.
