The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security published advisory AV26-798 covering the August 2026 SAP security patch rollup. The affected product list spans SAP Commerce Cloud, including the Data Hub Adapter in COM_CLOUD 2211 and 2211-JDK21, SAP Manufacturing Integration and Intelligence in XMII and MII_ADMIN versions 15.4 and 15.5, and a broad swath of SAP NetWeaver and ABAP Platform kernel versions from 7.22 through 9.19. The Change and Transport System Attach Tool, ABAP Developer Tools across SAP_BASIS 750 through 920, and the BusinessObjects Business Intelligence Platform Central Management Server are also in scope.

SAP monthly rollups are routine, but they consistently carry code-injection and memory-corruption fixes in components that sit deep in enterprise ERP estates. The kernel coverage alone spans dozens of supported versions, which means most large SAP shops will have at least one affected component. The advisory contains no evidence of active exploitation, and the exposure depends on which modules an organization actually runs.

Priority action is inventory-driven: confirm which of the listed products and versions are present, then apply the August patches through the standard SAP maintenance window. Organizations running older kernel versions outside mainstream support should treat the rollup as a reason to accelerate a supported maintenance strategy, since patch coverage is the only control that matters for these products.
