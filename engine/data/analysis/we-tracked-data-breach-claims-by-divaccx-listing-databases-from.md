DarkWebSonar tracked data-breach claims by a threat actor using the handle "divaccx" on Mastodon, listing databases from multiple Indonesian government agencies and financial institutions. The claims name BPJS Ketenagakerjaan (Indonesia's social security employment agency) among the targeted organizations. The original disclosure came September 9, with an update on September 10 expanding the list of claimed victims. The claims have not been independently verified, and the actor's track record and the authenticity of the data remain unconfirmed. Indonesia's government agencies and state-owned financial institutions are frequent targets of opportunistic and financially motivated threat actors. If the claims prove valid, the breach could expose personal data of millions of Indonesian workers and pension contributors. The single-source nature of this intelligence (DarkWebSonar) and the lack of corroboration from the targeted organizations mean this should be treated as an unconfirmed claim pending verification.
