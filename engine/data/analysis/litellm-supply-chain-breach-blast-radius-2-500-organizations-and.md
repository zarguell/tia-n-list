CloudSEK's reconstruction of the LiteLLM AI supply-chain compromise, attributed to Team PCP, puts the blast radius at more than 2,500 organizations and roughly 434,000 CI/CD pipelines worldwide. The malicious package was pushed to the popular LLM gateway in March 2026 and existed only briefly, but exposure downstream of build and deploy chains is what matters now: cloud credentials, source-code repositories, Kubernetes environments, package registries, and AI services all sit in the blast radius.

The practical consequence is that the dependency chain should be treated as compromised even for organizations that cannot point to a specific impacted build. Rotate cloud credentials, audit pipeline secrets, and review Kubernetes access. Watch for follow-on research that maps which organizations and which specific packages were actually pulled, since the short-lived package means remediation urgency will vary by exposure.
