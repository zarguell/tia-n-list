Bimbo Bakeries USA (BBU) disclosed that attackers exploited a zero-day vulnerability in Oracle E-Business Suite (EBS) to steal files containing employee names and Social Security numbers. The intrusion was identified on December 6, 2025, but the company did not discover that the compromised files included SSNs until August 19, 2026. The breach notification was filed with the California Attorney General on September 4, 2026.

The compromise originated through a third-party vendor relationship. BBU said it applied Oracle's emergency patches after learning of the zero-day and launched a forensic investigation to determine the scope of unauthorized access. The eight-month gap between initial detection and identification of SSN exposure highlights the difficulty of classifying stolen data from ERP platforms, where file inventories may not be immediately apparent.

This incident is part of a broader extortion campaign targeting Oracle EBS customers that has been reported in recent months. The use of a zero-day in enterprise resource planning software is notable because EBS environments process sensitive workforce and financial data, and the third-party vendor vector underscores that supply-chain risk extends beyond software dependencies to managed service providers with direct platform access.
