Stale links on UK public-sector websites are redirecting visitors to gambling sites after partner organizations shut down and their domains lapsed. The Register reported that defunct partner domains, still referenced by live government pages, now serve gambling content instead of the intended resources.

The risk is reputational and practical rather than a direct vulnerability: citizens following official links land on gambling pages, and the affected agencies appear not to monitor outbound references. The fix is link hygiene and domain ownership checks rather than patching.

Watch for whether departments audit their partner link inventories and whether similar lapsed-domain takeovers appear across other public-sector estates.
