Mathspace, an educational technology platform, confirmed a data breach affecting 1,079,819 individuals across Australia and New Zealand. Unauthorized parties accessed an internal reporting system and exfiltrated user information. The company disclosed the incident on September 3, 2026. Affected records span students, parents or guardians, teachers, and Mathspace staff members. The exposed data includes names and additional personal information, though the full scope of compromised fields has not been detailed in the initial disclosure.

The breach is notable for its scale in the education sector, where platforms handle data on minors. An internal reporting system access suggests the attacker may have had persistent access or exploited a misconfigured administrative interface rather than a direct vulnerability in the platform's core functionality. The involvement of both student and staff data increases the identity theft and social engineering risk for affected individuals.

Watch for Mathspace's detailed breach notification, any regulatory actions from Australian or New Zealand data protection authorities, and clarification on how the internal reporting system was compromised. Education technology platforms are increasingly targeted because they aggregate large volumes of personal data across age groups with varying levels of security awareness.
