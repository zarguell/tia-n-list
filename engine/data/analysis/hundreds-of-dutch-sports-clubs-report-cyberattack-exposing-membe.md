More than 350 Dutch sports clubs reported a data breach to the Dutch Data Protection Authority after a cyberattack on the AllUnited membership administration system on Monday evening, with the count expected to rise. The breach may have exposed members' personal information. A separate report attributes the mass exposure to unencrypted passwords stored by the platform, which would let an intruder read member credentials directly.

The incident is a shared-provider compromise affecting hundreds of small organizations at once. Clubs and members should assume credentials and personal data are at risk until AllUnited clarifies what was stored and whether passwords were hashed. Watch for follow-up from the Dutch DPA, any expansion of the affected club count, and guidance on mandatory password resets.
