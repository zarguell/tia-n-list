Check Point Research reported a record 2,830 ransomware victims in Q3 2026, claimed by 117 distinct ransomware brands. The figure marks a new high for a single quarter and reflects continued fragmentation across the ransomware ecosystem rather than dominance by a handful of groups.

The volume of claimed victims suggests the barrier to running ransomware operations remains low and the pool of targets is still expanding. Defenders should expect continued pressure on mid-market organizations and weakly segmented environments.

Watch for whether Q4 figures track the same trajectory and whether law-enforcement disruptions of major brands continue to drive fragmentation rather than reduction in total victims.
