Unit 42's July 10, 2026 report charts The Gentlemen, tracked by Microsoft as Storm-2697, as the second most active RaaS program of 2026 with 580 claimed victims across 77 countries through July 7, 103 of them in manufacturing. The program is the rebrand of ArmCorp, a Qilin affiliate that launched its own operation in September 2025 with a 90% affiliate payout, a core team of roughly 20 operators, and encryptors written in both C and Go, including a dedicated ESXi locker. Its signature is operator-supplied offense: the GentleKiller EDR-killer framework with at least eight driver-abusing variants, a custom Go backdoor, and a suspected zero-day exploit used to blind endpoint defenses.

The growth is a business-model result, not a technical breakthrough, and that is what makes this story rank above most of the rest of the day's queue. Halcyon's May 2026 assessment documents an inventory of roughly 14,700 already-compromised FortiGate devices plus nearly a thousand brute-forced FortiGate VPN credentials, built while the operators paid affiliates 90% where the market standard is 70 to 80%. That stockpile means an organization patching CVE-2024-55591 today can still be holding a pre-positioned access path, because the credentials and sessions were harvested before the fix; remediation without an exploitation audit leaves the door open. Exposure also does not follow the usual geography. Only 7% of claimed victims are US-based, Thailand leads with 27, and the group's leaked internal chats show affiliates sorting candidates by FortiGate misconfiguration rather than country, so edge-device hygiene, not location or company size, is the risk variable.

Scope and patch the five CVEs the events carry: CVE-2024-55591 on FortiOS and FortiProxy, CVE-2025-32433 on Erlang/OTP SSH, CVE-2025-33073 in the Windows SMB client, CVE-2025-55182 (React2Shell), and CVE-2025-7771 for the ThrottleStop.sys driver. For every FortiGate that was internet-facing during the CVE-2024-55591 window, audit for prior compromise rather than assuming the patch closed it. Hunt for scheduled tasks matching gentlemen*, directories named GentlemenCollection, and unsigned binaries whose version information impersonates security vendors, the naming scheme ESET documented across the killers. Alert on wevtutil clearing the Security or System logs, vssadmin or wmic shadow copy deletion, and AnyDesk, WinSCP, or Advanced IP Scanner appearing on servers. Enforce SMB signing, disable SMBv1, keep SSH off ESXi hosts outside maintenance windows, and move ESXi management interfaces to a dedicated VLAN. A README-GENTLEMEN.txt note or a .7mtzhh file extension, first documented by Trend Micro in September 2025, confirms impact.

Two developments would change this assessment. A CVE identifier and patch for the zero-day Expel analyzed on June 30 would convert the group's most disruptive defense-evasion tool into a routine patch cycle, so track Expel and ESET for that disclosure. And the May 2026 insider leak, which exposed nine internal accounts including zeta88, the handle tied to hastalamuerte, should have slowed the group; if claimed victims hold near June's 117 through July and the HasanBroker BreachForums recruiting partnership keeps supplying affiliates, the leak was a speed bump and the 90% model remains the story.
