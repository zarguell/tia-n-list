Blackpoint reported that the ROMULUS ClickFix delivery platform is pushing a previously undocumented remote access trojan that resembles CNCMachineRMS. The RAT gives an operator surveillance and control of an infected PC. Blackpoint assesses that ROMULUS works as a delivery service rather than a single campaign, distributing CNCMachineRMS and Legion Loader through separate chains while keeping ClickFix prompts and signed-binary sideloading as its core techniques.

ClickFix depends on the victim running a copied command or fake verification step, so the initial access is user-driven instead of an exploit. Signed binary sideloading then lets the loader run under a legitimate executable. Separating the two payload chains matters for defence: blocking one family does not necessarily cut off the other, and both can arrive through the same delivery platform with the same social engineering pattern.

Watch for the two loaders appearing in the same environment, for unsigned DLLs loaded by signed binaries and for help-desk or verification lures that tell users to paste and run commands. The public reporting does not give hashes, C2 domains or a victim count, so the useful near-term signal is behaviour at the delivery layer rather than a fixed IOC list.
