A BleepingComputer checklist article outlines eight controls for securing remote monitoring and management software, referencing recent incidents. It cites the N-able N-central pre-auth RCE (CVE-2026-86218) patched in September 2026 and the earlier Microsoft SharePoint ToolShell zero-days (CVE-2025-53770 and CVE-2025-53771) exploited in July 2025. The article notes roughly 1,500 N-central servers were exposed online during the emergency hotfix cycle. The core point: RMM management planes are high-value targets because one compromised privileged account extends blast radius across thousands of customer endpoints.
