A hacker stole approximately 4,000 BTC worth around $340 million from the Liquid Network, a Bitcoin sidechain operated by Blockstream. The attacker exploited a bug in the network's wallet infrastructure. Blockstream paused operations and the self-described "white hat" hacker agreed to return the funds once the bug was fixed. As of Monday, approximately 3,400 BTC (roughly $293 million) had been returned, with the remaining 600 BTC (about $47 million) still under the attacker's control.

Former Blockstream executive Samson Mow confirmed the bug fix on X and said operations would remain suspended until additional security improvements are made. The Liquid Network, launched in 2018, functions as a settlement layer used by several cryptocurrency exchanges. The signing key was not compromised; the exploit targeted a different vulnerability in the withdrawal mechanism.

This ranks among the largest cryptocurrency thefts tracked by the Rekt leaderboard. The partial return suggests either a genuine white-hat motivation or an attempt to avoid legal consequences. Organizations using Liquid Network for settlement should monitor for resumed operations and any post-incident security disclosures from Blockstream.
