F5 released out-of-band hotfixes on November 18 for five critical stack-based buffer overflows in BIG-IP: CVE-2026-121805, CVE-2026-120292, CVE-2026-120285, CVE-2026-116953 and CVE-2026-123637. The flaws sit in the data plane, in BIG-IP APM policies and iRules, and unauthenticated requests can terminate the Traffic Management Microkernel, with remote code execution described as possible. Rapid7 scores CVE-2026-123637 at CVSS 4.0 9.3 and CVSS 3.1 9.8. A separate high-severity APM SAML issue, CVE-2026-126822, is an integer underflow that can cause denial of service. BleepingComputer reports that F5 has no mitigation measures and advises installing the hotfixes.

The triggers are configuration-specific. CVE-2026-123637 needs a per-request policy with the Client Information and Endpoint Security agents applied to a virtual server, and a related entry for CVE-2026-120722 names an iRule using the DNS::scrape command. F5 advisory K000163937 covers the APM flaw, K000163945 covers the iRule issue and K000164058 is the notification index. Fixed builds listed by the Canadian Cyber Centre's AV26-1178 include BIGIP-17.1.3.7.0.21.4-ENG, BIGIP-17.5.1.11.0.61.12-ENG and BIGIP-21.1.0.4.0.19.3-ENG, plus CNF-2.2.5-EHF1, CNF-2.3.4-EHF1, CNF-2.4.2-EHF1 and matching BNK builds for BIG-IP Next for Kubernetes, and BIG-IQ 8.4.2.3.

Exposure is broad even without confirmed exploitation. Shadowserver tracks more than 177,000 BIG-IP instances and over 14,600 IP addresses with BIG-IP APM fingerprints online, though the count includes unknown numbers of honeypots and already patched systems. Rapid7 and StackFlag list no CISA KEV entry and no known exploitation for the APM and iRule bugs, and StackFlag records no EPSS score. The data-plane placement is the concern: ordinary client traffic to a configured virtual server can reach the vulnerable parser, so an outage can arrive without any compromise of the management interface.

Watch for public PoCs or exploitation reports that change the current no-known-exploitation status, and for hotfix coverage of every affected branch, especially 17.1, 17.5, 21.1, CNF and BNK. APM policies using both named agents and iRules using DNS::scrape are the first configurations to inventory. The SAML integer underflow CVE-2026-126822 is a separate denial-of-service path on identity gateways and should not be lost in the attention on the five critical overflows.
