Apify, a Czech web-scraping and automation platform with 91,000 customers worldwide, confirmed that an attacker accessed customer data after compromising internal systems and customer accounts on November 9 and 10, 2026. The intrusion was discovered on November 11, when Apify blocked the attacker's access. The attacker used a compromised credential to obtain further credentials and reach related Apify accounts. On November 12 the company confirmed that some customers had personal information or stored data accessed and began notifying those affected, while stating that customers who received no notification have not been identified as affected so far.

According to a breach notification shared by an Apify customer on Reddit, exposed data included names, email addresses, billing details, account activity, and IP addresses, along with salted password hashes, 2FA recovery codes, phone numbers, and authentication tokens tied to Google and GitHub sign-ins. Apify has not disclosed how many customer accounts were compromised, and the investigation is continuing. Affected users should rotate passwords and revoke linked Google and GitHub tokens, and treat any stored scraping data or API keys held in Apify as potentially exposed.
