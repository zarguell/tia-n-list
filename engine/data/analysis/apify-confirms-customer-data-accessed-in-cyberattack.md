Web scraping and automation platform Apify confirmed that customer data was accessed during a cyberattack that compromised internal systems and customer accounts. The intrusion took place on November 9-10 and was discovered on November 11, when Apify blocked the attacker's access and opened an investigation. The attacker used a compromised credential to obtain additional credentials and reach related Apify accounts. On November 12 the company confirmed that some customers had personal information or stored data accessed, and contacted those affected; customers not notified have not been identified as affected so far.

According to a breach notification shared by an Apify customer on Reddit, the exposed data included names, email addresses, billing details, account activity, and IP addresses, plus salted password hashes, 2FA recovery codes and phone numbers, and Google/GitHub sign-in authentication tokens. Apify has not disclosed how many customer accounts were compromised; the company says it serves 91,000 customers worldwide. The investigation is continuing, and the possibility of payment card data exposure was indicated in the notification.
