The US Department of Homeland Security has ordered DeepSeek out of US civilian government systems, extending the ban beyond the Chinese company's models to any technology built with their aid. The order targets federal civilian use of DeepSeek-derived technology, reflecting concern about data exposure and supply-chain risk from Chinese AI tooling in government environments.

The directive is a policy action rather than a technical vulnerability, but it has operational consequences for agencies and contractors that integrated DeepSeek-based tooling into workflows. Organizations should inventory AI dependencies for DeepSeek-derived components, assess data residency and handling, and prepare to migrate to approved alternatives. The scope phrase "any technology built with the aid" suggests downstream products and fine-tunes are also in scope, which widens the compliance surface beyond direct API use.
