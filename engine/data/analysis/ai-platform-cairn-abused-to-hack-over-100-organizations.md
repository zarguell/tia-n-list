Threat actors have been abusing the open-source AI platform Cairn to automate malicious operations against hundreds of websites worldwide, AI security firm Norforge reports. Cairn acts as a general-purpose problem-solving engine that can coordinate agents step by step from origin to predefined goals. Norforge identified three malicious clusters focused on persistent access and financial theft; the largest included over 1,100 attack projects across 14 dashboards targeting e-commerce and payment infrastructure in the US, Japan, and Australia. The threat actors ranked targets by traffic, tested them with Microsoft Playwright, and used automated validation. The goal appears to be persistent access via webshells, likely monetized later. Defenders should treat autonomous agent platforms as an emerging attack surface and review web application and payment-system monitoring.
