Access Dental Management, a Texas-based dental services provider with care locations in Texas, Illinois, Indiana and New Mexico, disclosed a breach affecting more than 340,000 people. The company discovered the incident on May 5 and, working with third-party forensic specialists, determined that attackers had accessed its network between May 1 and May 4 and viewed or copied personal information.

A review completed on October 21 found that the exposed records included names, addresses, dates of birth, Social Security numbers, medical information and health insurance information. ADM told the Texas and Vermont attorneys general that 341,049 people were affected, split between 341,039 in Texas and 10 in Vermont, although the reported total could exceed 455,000. The combination of Social Security numbers and health data raises the identity theft and insurance fraud risk for those notified.

ADM is sending written notification letters and urging affected people to stay alert for identity theft and fraud. The gap between the May discovery and the attorney general filings, and the difference between the 341,049 notified figure and a possible total above 455,000, are the open questions. Additional state filings or a revised count would show whether the current notification scope is complete.
