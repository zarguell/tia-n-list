A security posture analysis from valtersit.com reports Grafana carries 54 CVEs, including 13 rated high and 5 rated critical, with an estimated 87% of installations still unpatched. The report assigns Grafana a trust score of C, citing observability dashboards as a wide attack surface due to their broad data access and common deployment as internet-facing services.

Grafana's CVE backlog spans authentication bypasses, path traversal flaws, and plugin sandbox escapes. The 87% unpatched figure, if accurate, reflects the challenge of updating a component that sits at the intersection of monitoring, logging, and business intelligence stacks. Many organizations run Grafana as a long-lived sidecar or shared service where updates require coordinated maintenance windows.

The trust score is a vendor-specific metric and should be evaluated alongside your own risk tolerance and deployment context. Organizations running Grafana should inventory their instances, prioritize the five critical CVEs, and establish a regular patching cadence. Pay particular attention to Grafana instances with public-facing endpoints or those configured with anonymous access or weak authentication.
