NSA, CISA, and FBI issued joint advisory AA26-251A accusing six Chinese AI companies of industrial-scale distillation attacks against U.S. frontier models. DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Alibaba, MiniMax, StepFun, and Z.AI extracted billions of tokens through millions of API requests from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and xAI models since at least late 2024. The agencies assess the scale and sophistication indicate Chinese government awareness.

The attackers distributed requests across fraudulent and shared accounts, cloud services, aggregators, and "transfer station" proxies to bypass geographic restrictions, usage limits, and detection systems. Some prompts specifically targeted restricted chain-of-thought reasoning. Automated systems rotated between providers and probed whether defenders had degraded response quality, suggesting an organized operational tempo beyond individual researcher curiosity.

This is the first joint advisory explicitly naming Chinese companies engaged in systematic AI IP theft through API abuse. It reframes model distillation from a gray-area research technique to a state-adjacent intelligence collection activity. For the AI industry, the practical impact is accelerated API abuse defenses, rate-limiting, and account verification. For policy, it adds ammunition to export control and AI competition debates already simmering in Congress.
