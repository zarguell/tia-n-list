ShinyHunters breached and defaced the Clop ransomware gang's Tor leak site, replacing its content with ShinyHunters branding and a message threatening to extort the extortionists. The attack began Friday night when ShinyHunters exploited an unauthenticated file upload vulnerability in Grav CMS running on Clop's leak site. BleepingComputer confirmed the uploaded file could be downloaded directly from the Tor site.

ShinyHunters claims to have stolen server data and the private keys for Clop's onion service. The defaced site displayed ASCII art of Umbreon (ShinyHunters' Pokémon logo) and a link to their own leak site. As of the latest update, ShinyHunters has escalated demands every 24 hours Clop fails to engage, and has added "Mackenzie" (reportedly Clop's operator) to their site.

This is significant because it represents rare inter-gang retaliation rather than typical victim targeting. ShinyHunters, active since 2019 and known for mass data theft and extortion (Instructure, Medtronic, Charter Communications), has turned its playbook against a fellow criminal operation. Clop, known for mass exploitation of file-transfer platforms (MOVEit, GoAnywhere, PTC Windchill), now faces the same pressure it applies to its own victims. If the onion key theft is verified, Clop would need to rebuild its leak site infrastructure entirely.
