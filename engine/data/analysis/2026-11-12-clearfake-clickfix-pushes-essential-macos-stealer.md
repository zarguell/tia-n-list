Two Malware-Traffic-Analysis reports are grouped here. The November 12 entry covers ClearFake ClickFix pushing an Essential stealer against a macOS host, and the November 13 entry covers ClearFake ClickFix pushing ACR, also known as Amatera stealer, against a Windows host. The feed items are link posts and carry no hashes, domains or infection details beyond those payload names and dates.

The pair is still useful as a split by operating system: the same ClickFix delivery theme is being used to push different stealers for macOS and Windows within a day. Watch the linked lab reports for the actual fake update pages, downloaded file names and network indicators, then separate the two chains rather than treating them as one payload. Indicator overlap between the Essential and ACR/Amatera samples would be the strongest sign of shared infrastructure.
