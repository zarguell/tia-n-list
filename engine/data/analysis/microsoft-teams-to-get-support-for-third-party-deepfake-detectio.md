Microsoft Teams is adding third-party deepfake detection for synthetic audio and video in meetings, with rollout targeted for November 2026 under Roadmap ID 573451. The feature integrates certified third-party detection signals rather than an in-house model, letting organizations flag AI-generated media during calls. This matters because meeting-based social engineering and impersonation are rising; automated detection at the collaboration layer reduces reliance on user awareness alone. Watch the provider certification list and whether Microsoft exposes detection confidence scores to admins, since false-positive rates will determine operational value.
