Shift Up confirmed that the PC launcher for Goddess of Victory: NIKKE was tampered with in a supply-chain attack, and issued a client fix on November 4 at about 02:30 UTC+9. The game has over 45 million downloads since 2022, with 10 million on Google Play alone.

Researcher MotherSphere alleges four JavaScript files on Level Infinite's CDN were modified on September 27, 2026 to inject a loader for insightjs.com, which from October 23 downloaded a remote-access agent called K2F on some launches. Indicators include the string k2f_chan in Launcher/intl_service/debug.log and a persistent Windows DLL named platform_experience_helper.dll. The attacker's fallback domain insightjs.org was registered on November 3 and later replaced with a security warning in four languages.

Players who ran the PC launcher during the affected window should treat their systems as potentially compromised, rotate credentials and check wallets, since the malware targeted credentials and cryptocurrency wallet data. Shift Up is still investigating the scope of impact.
