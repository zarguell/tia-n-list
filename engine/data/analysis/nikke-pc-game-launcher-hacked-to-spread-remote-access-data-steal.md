Attackers tampered with the PC launcher for Goddess of Victory: NIKKE in a supply-chain attack, modifying four JavaScript files hosted on Level Infinite's official CDN on September 27, 2026 to inject malicious code that downloaded additional payloads whenever players opened the game. The injected malware steals credentials and cryptocurrency wallet data. The publisher confirmed the tampering and issued a client fix on November 4 at 02:30 UTC+9, while an independent researcher published the technical details on GitHub.

The blast radius is large: NIKKE has more than 45 million downloads since its 2022 launch and 10 million on Google Play alone, and the malicious code ran through the official launcher path, so signature-based trust in the publisher's CDN did not protect users. The investigation into the full scope of impact is still ongoing.

Players who launched the PC client before the November 4 update should treat their credentials and wallets as potentially compromised and rotate passwords and keys. Watch for the publisher's follow-up notice on scope and remediation, and for any secondary abuse of stolen wallet data.
