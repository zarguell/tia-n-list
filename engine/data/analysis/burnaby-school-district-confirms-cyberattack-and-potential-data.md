Burnaby School District confirmed a ransomware attack that exposed sensitive student and staff data. The district says the incident may have leaked personal information of students and parents, though specific records affected have not been fully quantified. The event is corroborated by feed and social references linking to Global News reporting.

This is a standard education-sector ransomware case with data exposure risk rather than a targeted campaign or novel technique. No CVEs or specific actors are named, and the incident appears operational rather than strategic. Schools remain high-value, low-security targets.

Watch for follow-on disclosure of breached records, notification timelines, and whether ransomware actors claim the attack. No exploitation claims or IOCs accompany the disclosure.
