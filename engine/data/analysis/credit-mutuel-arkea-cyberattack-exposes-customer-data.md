Le Monde reported on November 5, 2026 that hackers stole part of Credit Mutuel Arkea's customer data after a cyberattack on the French bank, in a wave that put European banks on notice about the scale of their vulnerability.

A separate report from beyondmachines.net said Arkea confirmed a cyberattack involving fraudulent bank transfers. Watch for Arkea's disclosure of how many customers were affected and whether stolen data surfaced in fraud or resale.
