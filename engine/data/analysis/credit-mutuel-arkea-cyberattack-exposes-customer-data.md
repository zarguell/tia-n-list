Credit Mutuel Arkea confirmed a cyberattack in which attackers gained access through a service provider and used the bank's account to carry out fraudulent transfers. The bank disclosed the incident on its own information page, and Le Monde framed the attack as a warning to European banks about the scale of vulnerability after a cyberattack on a French bank.

The incident matters because a compromise of the service-provider access chain let attackers reach the bank's account and initiate fraudulent transfers. That is a supply-chain style attack against a financial institution: the attackers moved through a trusted third-party connection instead of attacking the bank directly. Customer funds and data may be affected, and the disclosure follows other recent attacks on financial institutions' third-party relationships.

Arkea's customer communications will indicate which accounts and transfers were affected, and other European banks are likely to review service-provider access after the disclosure. Watch for further details on how the provider was compromised and whether customer data was exfiltrated alongside the fraudulent transfers.
