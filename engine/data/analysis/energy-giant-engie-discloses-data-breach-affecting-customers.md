ENGIE, a French energy giant with 90,000 employees and €71.9 billion in revenue, confirmed attackers stole customer contact details and energy contract information through a partner marketing platform. The company said its internal IT systems were not compromised and the exposure is limited to customers in France. ENGIE notified authorities and contacted affected customers individually, emphasizing that no banking data was involved. The incident highlights risks from third-party marketing tools used in customer prospecting. Watch for updates on the number of affected customers and whether phishing campaigns follow.
