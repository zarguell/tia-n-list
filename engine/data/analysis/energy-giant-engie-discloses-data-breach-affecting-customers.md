ENGIE, the French energy group operating in 30 countries with over 90,000 employees and about €71.9 billion in annual revenue, disclosed that attackers stole customer data by breaching a marketing platform used by business partners for customer prospecting and marketing of energy offers. ENGIE's internal IT systems were not compromised; the company cut off the attackers' access. The incident is limited to customers in France.

The stolen data is limited to customers' contact details and certain information relating to their energy contracts; no banking data was affected. ENGIE notified relevant authorities and individually informed affected customers, urging precautions against fraud and phishing. The number of affected customers was not disclosed.

The breach highlights third-party risk in partner marketing tools: attackers reached customer data without penetrating ENGIE's own systems. Customers should watch for targeted phishing referencing energy contracts.
