Norway's data protection authority Datatilsynet fined Microsoft-owned advertising firm Xandr $9.6 million (NOK 92 million) for unlawfully sharing sensitive Grindr user data. Between July 2018 and April 2020, Xandr ran real-time ad auctions inside Grindr that exposed users' profiling information, including sexual orientation, to thousands of bidding companies. Xandr acted as joint controller but did not verify Grindr's consent. This is the largest fine the authority has issued. Thousands of Norwegian users were affected.

The breach highlights failure of ad-tech intermediaries to validate consent for sensitive data categories. The fine targets Xandr rather than Grindr directly. The event text references BleepingComputer. No exploitation claims are made; the issue is regulatory enforcement. Watch for similar actions in other EU jurisdictions.
