Socket's October 7 report identified 16 malicious Firefox extensions that impersonate cryptocurrency wallet portals, desktop utilities, and browser tools. By October 5, Mozilla had unpublished the extensions. The packages attempt to intercept recovery phrases and private keys during wallet imports and transmit secrets to attacker-controlled Cloudflare Workers.

Fifteen of the packages contain malicious payloads directed at crypto wallet users. This is a browser-extension supply-chain and social-engineering threat rather than a vulnerability with a CVE. The primary sourcing comes from GBHackers citing Socket's analysis, with updates from The Hacker News.

Users should verify extension publishers, avoid importing seed phrases into unverified browser tools, and remove any extension matching the reported impersonator names.
