Finnair disclosed a third-party data breach affecting approximately 116,000 passenger reservations, stemming from an Amadeus third-party compromise. A related disclosure by Thai Airways also appears in the same cluster, suggesting the same upstream compromise may have touched multiple airlines' booking systems.

Exact exposed fields are not detailed in the available sources; Beyondmachines.net carried both disclosures. The scope appears limited to reservation data rather than payment credentials, but that should be confirmed against the vendor's own notice.

Airlines and their travel-IT vendors should verify scope, notify affected passengers, and review third-party access to booking data. Watch for additional airline disclosures tied to the same Amadeus incident.
