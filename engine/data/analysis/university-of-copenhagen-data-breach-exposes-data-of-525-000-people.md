The University of Copenhagen confirmed a breach of a test environment that exposed personal data for about 525,000 people. Intrusion detected October 20 and disclosed October 23; investigators are still assessing scope. Compromised data includes full names and Danish national identification details. The university reports it blocked the attacker, contained access, and saw no disruption to daily operations. Risk focus is phishing and social engineering using the exposed records.

This is a significant institutional breach at Denmark's oldest university, with 36,800 students and 10,700 staff. The additional exposure of PIN codes for about 20,000 people led to temporary physical-access restrictions on campus. The incident has been reported to the Danish Data Protection Authority. Watch for updates on final scope and any follow-on attacks against the university community.
