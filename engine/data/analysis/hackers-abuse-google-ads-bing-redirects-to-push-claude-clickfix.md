Attackers are running a malvertising campaign that uses legitimate Bing redirect URLs as the visible destination for sponsored Google Ads. Push Security calls the technique "Adception" and reports it was discovered when researchers spotted a malicious Google ad for the query "claude mac" that displayed bing.com as its destination. The chain passes through Bing's click-tracking endpoint (bing.com/ck/a), then a compromised South American retailer's WordPress site, and finally lands on a fake Claude download page at claude-desk-code[.]com. The embedded timestamp in the Bing link decodes to October 5, 2026, indicating staging days before disclosure. The toolkit links to additional domains including cli-desktop[.]com and lake-90[.]com. The campaign targets macOS users and uses multiple layers of cloaking to evade scanners. No patch is available; users and defenders should block the listed IoCs and scrutinize sponsored results that redirect through tracking URLs.
