Attackers abuse legitimate Bing redirect URLs as click targets inside Google Ads to deliver fake Claude installers. Push Security named the technique Adception. A sponsored result for the search term "claude mac" showed bing.com as the destination, then routed clicks through Bing's /ck/a endpoint, a compromised South American retailer WordPress site, and finally to claude-desk-code[.]com. The redirect chain carries an embedded timestamp from October 5, 2026, staged days before disclosure. The campaign links to the ClickFix toolkit tracked as AcSig, with additional domains cli-desktop[.]com and lake-90[.]com. The technique evades advertising security checks by using a trusted domain in the ad, then cloaks the payload from direct scanner access. Watch for additional AcSig-linked domains and macOS-targeting ClickFix installers using similar redirect cloaking.
