Gorbag is a Russia-aligned threat actor that has been running a targeted cyberespionage campaign against Ukrainian law enforcement, local administrations, justice institutions, and defense organizations since at least February 2025. The group exploits the WinRAR vulnerability CVE-2025-8088 to deliver BeautyStealer, a compact infostealer built for rapid intelligence collection rather than long-term remote control of compromised systems.

The use of a WinRAR flaw as the initial access vector points to document-based delivery, likely malicious archives sent to targets in the Ukrainian public sector. BeautyStealer's small footprint suggests the operators prioritize quick exfiltration of credentials and documents over persistent access, which fits a collection-first espionage model aimed at frontline and administrative targets.

Defenders in the affected sectors should treat unpatched WinRAR installations as an active risk and monitor for BeautyStealer indicators. Patching WinRAR and restricting archive handling on sensitive systems remain the immediate mitigations, while hunting for the infostealer's collection artifacts can help identify prior compromise.
