Gorbag, a Russia-aligned threat actor, has targeted Ukrainian law enforcement, justice institutions, frontline local administrations and defense bodies since at least February 2025. Greek site en.hacks.gr and GBHackers both reported the campaign. The intrusions are narrowly focused on government and military targets rather than broad consumer or corporate victims.

Reporting from ESET, carried by secquill and GBHackers, ties Gorbag to the WinRAR vulnerability CVE-2025-8088, which it uses to deploy BeautyStealer, a compact infostealer built for rapid intelligence collection instead of prolonged remote control. GBHackers carried the findings under ESET's research. The latest coverage describes intrusions against Ukrainian justice and city targets.

Watch for continued WinRAR-based delivery against Ukrainian government networks and for additional victim counts or IOCs from ESET. Concrete artifacts: CVE-2025-8088, BeautyStealer.
