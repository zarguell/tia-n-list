A Danish company misused its lawful access to the national CPR register, exposing names, addresses, and CPR numbers for about 8.8 million registered citizens (around 5 October 2026). The CPR administration's advisory confirms the unauthorized access used a legitimate search account, not a technical vulnerability in the register itself. The firm's access was stopped; the incident was reported to the Datatilsynet and police are investigating. People registered with protected address details were not covered by the unauthorized access.

The breach is notable for scale (most of Denmark's registered population) and mechanism (insider/misused legitimate access rather than an exploit). Residents should treat exposed CPR numbers as long-lived identifiers and watch for fraud targeting the exposed address data.
