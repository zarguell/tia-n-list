A breach of Denmark's Central Person Register (CPR) exposed records protected by weak credentials: at least three accounts at the Funen-based IT company Pays used the password "123456", including an administrator account. The incident adds to earlier reporting on the CPR breach linked to Pays. No CVEs or specific threat actor attribution are mentioned in the available reports. Watch for official Danish data-protection authority updates, confirmation of total affected records, and whether Pays discloses remediation steps.
