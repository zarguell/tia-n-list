The Danish Central Person Register confirmed unauthorized access exposing names, residential addresses, and CPR numbers for about 8.8 million registered citizens. The breach occurred via misuse of a legitimate Danish firm's access to the CPR system; the firm's access was stopped and police are investigating. The advisory notes people with protected name/address records were not covered. This is a state-level identity registry breach with massive population exposure. Watch for regulatory response from Datatilsynet and any claims of data misuse.
