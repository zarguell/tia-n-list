The IDScan data breach exposes 153 million records, according to disclosures linked to the company. IDScan handles identity verification and document checks. The breach was referenced in updates tied to existing reporting, and the event was redirected to this story during triage because the new event linked to an already-carried article URL. The scale of exposed records makes it a significant data exposure, though specific technical details of the intrusion mechanism remain limited in the available event content.

What to watch: whether additional breach details emerge, including how the records were accessed and whether identity verification data is actively misused. Organizations that rely on IDScan for document verification should assess exposure risk based on the 153 million figure.
