Veradigm disclosed in an SEC filing that a third-party vendor compromise allowed an attacker to obtain credentials for a Veradigm customer service API. The threat actor used the API to copy patient data including personal details and Social Security numbers. Clinical and medical information was not accessed. The company says the incident did not cause operational disruptions and affected a small number of customers. Veradigm notified law enforcement and is investigating.

BleepingComputer confirmed that a ransomware gang claimed credit for the attack. OSINT Sights provided additional coverage. The stolen data includes SSNs, which elevates this beyond typical PHI exposure and into the category of directly exploitable identity theft material. Veradigm, formerly Allscripts Healthcare Solutions, supplies EHR, e-prescribing, and practice management software to thousands of U.S. hospitals, clinics, and biopharmaceutical firms.

The third-party vendor vector is the key detail: Veradigm's own network was not breached, but an API exposed through a vendor's compromised environment was sufficient to exfiltrate patient data. This is a pattern seen repeatedly in healthcare breaches, where API access paths bypass the perimeter security organizations invest in. Affected patients face long-term SSN exposure risk regardless of how limited the initial breach scope appears.
