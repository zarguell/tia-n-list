Midnight Blue researcher Carlo Meijer found six pre-authentication remote code execution flaws in the Danfoss AK-SM800A refrigeration controller, each giving remote root access without a login. He found them in under an hour of manual firmware review. All firmware versions through 5.3.2 are affected.

No patch is available, and the researchers say the newest firmware, version 5.4.0, does not fix the flaws. Attacks against internet-exposed Danfoss and Copeland controllers began in France on October 11. Cybersecurity agencies in Belgium, Ireland, and Germany told operators this week to take action.

The combination of no authentication requirement, no fixed firmware, and prior attacks on exposed controllers makes this an operational risk for refrigeration systems rather than a theoretical bug set. Watch for a vendor fix that actually covers 5.4.0, further agency guidance, and any expansion of the attacks beyond the initially reported French targets.
