AnonyMousKIT is an AI-powered Phishing-as-a-Service platform designed to unlock stolen iPhones by social-engineering owners into surrendering their device passcode, Apple ID credentials, and live two-factor authentication codes. The platform runs as a credit-based criminal service: subscribers enter stolen device details (model, owner info, Find My status), and the system generates customized phishing lures claiming the device was found or is being held by Apple Support.

The most notable feature is its use of conversational AI voice agents across five personas, including English, Spanish, and Brazilian Portuguese versions of an "Apple Support representative" named Alice. The AI agent uses victim-specific details, asks the target to confirm ownership, requests the iPhone passcode, and directs the victim to a phishing page via SMS. The platform operates across five channels: email, SMS, WhatsApp, recorded calls, and live AI voice calls. The campaign logged 200 AI voice interactions during the research period. This represents the industrialization of social engineering through AI, lowering the skill barrier for large-scale device theft operations.
