The ransomware landscape continues to fragment with new groups posting victim data on dedicated leak sites. CTI.fyi tracks ongoing blog posts from ShadowByt3$ and Chaos, both posting victim information including company names and data samples. ShadowByt3$ has posted about "John Engel Team" and other victims, while Chaos targeted "steelhausinc.com" and additional organizations.

This is a monitoring story rather than a single incident. The proliferation of ransom groups operating leak sites reflects the low barrier to entry for ransomware-as-a-service operations. Both groups appear to be mid-tier operations using standard double-extortion tactics: encrypting data and threatening to publish stolen files unless ransoms are paid.

Defenders should monitor these leak sites for mentions of their organization or supply chain partners. The volume of new group appearances suggests the ransomware ecosystem remains highly active despite law enforcement disruptions. Organizations should maintain offline backups and incident response playbooks for ransomware scenarios.
