The CTI.fyi tracker has flagged new blog posts from multiple ransomware groups over the past 48 hours. ShadowByt3$ listed victims including John Engel Team, while other groups such as pear, Panzer, and krybit also posted new victims. The krybit group listed www.kashkha.com as a target. These blog posts typically appear when groups publish stolen data after victims refuse to pay.

Ransomware groups use leak sites to pressure victims into paying by threatening to publish sensitive data. The volume of new postings suggests active operations across multiple groups simultaneously. Security teams should monitor these leak sites for exposure of their organization or supply chain partners.

The groups tracked here represent both established and emerging ransomware operations. The rapid succession of posts within a 48-hour window indicates coordinated campaigns or opportunistic exploitation of vulnerable organizations. Defensive measures should include monitoring for unauthorized data exfiltration indicators and ensuring backup systems are isolated from the production network.
