Germany's far-right AfD party was hit by a cyberattack that exposed roughly 421,000 internal emails, according to Mastodon references linking to winfuture.de and heistberlin.com. The incident was reported on October 20. The scope of leaked material includes internal communications and may involve documents tied to the party. Watch for confirmation of the attacker, the access method used against the IT service provider, and whether the leaked data appears on public leak sites or is being used for targeted influence or fraud.
