Zohar Pinhasi, owner of Florida-based MonsterCloud, was indicted on two wire-fraud counts and one conspiracy count. The Justice Department alleges Pinhasi charged hundreds of clients more than $19 million over five years ending in 2023, using no proprietary decryption tool. Instead, he allegedly paid over $8 million in ransoms without telling clients, while billing inflated fees. The case shows the ransomware response market operates with little transparency.
