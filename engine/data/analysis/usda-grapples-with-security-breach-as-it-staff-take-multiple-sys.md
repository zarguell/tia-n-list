USDA took numerous applications offline over several days after its Cybersecurity and Privacy Operations Center uncovered a security incident. An internal email from Chris Rottler, acting assistant chief information officer for the Farm Production and Conservation Business Center, said the Information Services Division had to reset all database passwords in all environments, which in turn required resetting all applications and caused outages.

People familiar with the matter told Nextgov/FCW and Government Executive that the disruptions hit financial tools used for payments between the agency and farmers, and that staff lost access to loan and aid information during a period when producers rely on crop insurance and loss coverage. A DysruptionHub post on November 19 reported the same disruption to payment tools. Syracuse.com reported on November 20 that the USDA website was down Thursday with a message saying it was temporarily down.

No attacker has been identified and there is no public confirmation of data exfiltration. The all-environment password reset is the clearest concrete detail and indicates the agency treated credentials as part of containment rather than a single application fault. Watch for a USDA statement on scope, attribution and data access, plus confirmation that payment tools, loan systems and the public website are fully restored.
