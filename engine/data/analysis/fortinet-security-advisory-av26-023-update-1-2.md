Fortinet's security advisory AV26-023, originally published January 13, 2026, was updated on September 9, 2026, to reflect CISA's addition of CVE-2025-25249 to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. The advisory covers a heap-based buffer overflow in the cw_acd daemon affecting FortiOS (6.4 through 7.6.3), FortiSwitchManager (7.0.x and 7.2.x), FortiSASE (25.1.a.2 and 25.2.b), FortiSIEM (6.7 through 7.4), FortiFone (3.0 and 7.0), and FortiSwitchManager (7.0 and 7.2).

The CISA KEV addition formalizes what SOCRadar researchers have confirmed: active exploitation of this vulnerability since at least July 2026, with a custom exploit toolchain delivering the PivotC2 RAT. The attack targets UDP port 5246 (CAPWAP) and requires no authentication or user interaction. Fortinet's original advisory shipped patches alongside disclosure in January, but the eight-month gap between patch availability and active exploitation campaigns is a reminder that unpatched edge devices remain attractive targets.

Organizations should apply the fixed releases listed in the advisory: FortiOS 7.6.4, 7.4.9, 7.2.12, 7.0.18, or 6.4.17; FortiSwitchManager 7.2.7 or 7.0.6; FortiSASE 25.2.c or later. Until patching is confirmed, restrict inbound CAPWAP traffic (UDP 5246-5249) from untrusted networks and disable the fabric service on internet-facing interfaces where not required.
