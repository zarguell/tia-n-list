Japanese car-sharing service Times Car (Park24 Group) confirmed that approximately 6.6 million current and former user accounts were compromised. A third party accessed systems at the beginning of September; unauthorized access was blocked on September 26. The company disclosed the breach on September 25 and confirmed data theft in a subsequent update.

Exposed information includes full names, department names for corporate members, physical addresses, dates of birth, telephone numbers, email addresses, driver's license information (including images), account passwords stored in a non-reversible form, linked service IDs, and identity verification document details. Times Car states that credit card information was not affected and there is currently no evidence the stolen data has been distributed online.

The breach affects both the consumer Times Car membership and the Times Business Service corporate program. With 6.6 million accounts involved and identity document exposure included, the incident carries significant fraud and impersonation risk for affected users. Monitor for any reports of data appearing on leak sites or being sold, and watch for follow-up regulatory action in Japan given the scale.
