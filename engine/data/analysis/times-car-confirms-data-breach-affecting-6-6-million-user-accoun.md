Times Car, the Japanese car-sharing service operated by Times Mobility under the Park24 Group, confirmed that roughly 6.6 million current and former member accounts were compromised. A third party accessed its systems at the beginning of September; the company blocked the unauthorized access on September 26 and announced the incident on September 25, confirming the data theft in a September 30 update. The exposure also covers current and former members of the Times Business Service corporate program.

The stolen data is unusually complete: full name, physical address, date of birth, phone, email, department for corporate members, driver's license information including images of identity documents, account passwords, and linked service IDs. Passwords were stored in what the company called "a form that cannot be restored," suggesting hashing or encryption, and credit card data was unaffected. No evidence yet shows the data circulating online.

Identity-document images plus passwords make this a strong kit for follow-on account takeover and fraud in Japan, so watch for phishing that references Times Car membership and for the dataset surfacing on leak forums. The gap between initial access in early September and containment on September 26 is also a reminder of how long attackers can sit inside consumer platforms before detection.
