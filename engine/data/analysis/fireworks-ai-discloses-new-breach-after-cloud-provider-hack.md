Fireworks AI disclosed a second security incident in which attackers reached its development machines through a compromised third-party cloud provider, discovered October 23 after the company had already revoked credentials from an earlier breach. The earlier October incident involved a financially motivated group using LLMJacking to sell unauthorized access to large language models; the new breach exposed customer resource metadata, billing and shipping details, invoice and payment history, account balances, credit usage, and incomplete model weights and datasets for some customers.

The company says the attackers could not access full model weights or complete customer datasets, and it has notified law enforcement and affected customers. Defenders running inference platforms or relying on third-party cloud providers should review credential rotation, secret storage, and provider-side access controls, since both incidents trace to compromised cloud-side access rather than a Fireworks product flaw.
