Insikt Group reports that Russian-language traffer teams have adapted affiliate-fraud models to target prediction markets like PolyMarket and Kalshi since spring 2026. Three platforms are named: AtlasCore, PolyFucked, and Magnetic Team. Each offers administrative panels, cryptocurrency payment infrastructure, and promotional support. Fraud mechanisms include controlled trading outcomes, fabricated market activity, artificial withdrawal restrictions, and collection of identity and payment data. The event text notes AI is used to accelerate platform development and social engineering.

This is a fraud and abuse report, not a vulnerability or intrusion disclosure. No CVE numbers, victim counts, or specific timelines for individual campaigns are given. Security teams should treat prediction-market fraud as an emerging social-engineering vector rather than an infrastructure threat, monitoring for affiliate campaigns that combine cryptocurrency payments with fabricated trading interfaces.
