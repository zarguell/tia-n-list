Barracuda Email Security Gateway carries critical flaws that researchers demonstrated can be chained from an unauthenticated position to full root access on the device. The cluster tracks CVE-2023-7102 alongside CVE-2026-111560 and CVE-2026-116606, with reporting from Barracuda's trust site, BleepingComputer, SecurityWeek, WatchTowr Labs and The Perimeter Site among 14 sources.

An unauthenticated exploit chain to root on an edge email gateway lets an attacker control mail flow, harvest credentials, and pivot into the network without any user interaction. The public demonstration of the chain means exploitation tooling is likely to follow quickly, and email gateways sit inside the network perimeter where they can read and modify mail for every user.

Watch for Barracuda patches and firmware guidance, confirmation of exploitation in the wild, and any IOCs or network signatures for the exploit chain traffic.
