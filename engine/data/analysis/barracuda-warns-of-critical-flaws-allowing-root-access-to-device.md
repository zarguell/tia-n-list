Barracuda's Email Security Gateway chain is the rare appliance bug with a public end-to-end exploit. CVE-2026-116606 is a pre-authentication authorization bypass in ESG versions earlier than 9.4.0.030: an unauthenticated remote attacker can invoke a configuration-import function and restore a crafted backup archive, rewriting the appliance's global configuration, and it is rated CVSS 9.3. CVE-2026-111560 is an OS command injection in the timezone configuration field of shared code, reachable by an authenticated administrator and executing as root, rated CVSS 8.6, and it affects ESG 9.4.0.27 and earlier plus ADC 6.4.0, WAF 11.0.1, WSG 16.1.100.100, BMA 5.5.4.0 and WCC 2.2.1.

Chained, the two give an unauthenticated attacker root. watchTowr published a technical analysis by Sina Kheirkhah on November 5, 2026, Barracuda then updated its advisory to confirm exploitation against a small number of organizations, and CISA added both CVEs to the KEV catalog on November 10 with a three-day patch window. The Friday deadline circulating now is that KEV date, not a new vulnerability.

Patch first, then cut exposure: restrict the administrative interface to trusted internal networks and require source-IP allowlists. Check ESG firmware against 9.4.0.030 and the other five products against their fixed versions, and review configuration backups and logs for unexpected restores. Because the entry point is a crafted backup archive, a successful intrusion may show up as an unexplained global configuration change rather than as malware.
