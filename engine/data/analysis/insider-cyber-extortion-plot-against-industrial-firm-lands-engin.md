Daniel Rhyne, a 59-year-old core infrastructure engineer at an industrial firm in New Jersey, was sentenced to 32 months in prison for an insider ransom attack in November 2023. He pleaded guilty to extortion and intentional damage to a protected computer. Rhyne scheduled tasks that deleted 13 domain admin accounts, changed passwords for 301 domain users, and altered local admin passwords affecting 254 servers and 3,284 workstations to “TheFr0zenCrew!”, denying the firm access to systems and data. Within an hour, employees received an external email titled “Your Network Has Been Penetrated” claiming backups were deleted and administrators locked out. The DOJ announced the sentencing; the firm served multiple industries including healthcare, food, manufacturing, and energy. Watch for similar insider-extortion patterns where infrastructure engineers abuse privileged access.
