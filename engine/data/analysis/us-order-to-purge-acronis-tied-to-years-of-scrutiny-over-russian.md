DHS published a FASCSA exclusion and removal order that bars Acronis products from federal civilian executive-branch systems, with agencies required to remove them. The order, posted October 30, 2026, follows years of scrutiny under Foreign Ownership, Control or Influence (FOCI) guidelines dating to the first Trump administration. Officials examined whether Russian developers remained involved in Acronis codebases and whether supply-chain risks were adequately mitigated, though neither former official cited concrete evidence of backdoors communicating with Moscow.

The case spans both the Biden and Trump administrations and illustrates how long bipartisan foreign-ownership reviews can take. Acronis is a Swiss cybersecurity firm; the move affects its footprint in U.S. federal civilian environments rather than alleging a specific compromise.
