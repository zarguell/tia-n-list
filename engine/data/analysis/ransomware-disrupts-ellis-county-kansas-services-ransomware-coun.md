Ransomware has disrupted government services in Ellis County, Kansas. DysruptionHub reported the incident affecting public services, though specific details on the ransomware variant, entry vector, and scope of data compromise have not been disclosed. Ellis County joins a steady stream of local government ransomware incidents in 2026.

The attack highlights the continued targeting of county-level government entities, which often operate with limited IT security budgets and small teams. County governments typically manage critical public services including tax records, court systems, and public safety coordination, making them attractive targets for extortion.

No group has claimed responsibility and no ransom details have been published. Local governments should ensure offline backups of critical systems, maintain incident response plans that account for limited internal security staff, and consider shared services or state-level cybersecurity assistance programs.
