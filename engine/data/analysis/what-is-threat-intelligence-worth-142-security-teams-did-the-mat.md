This item is a vendor-sponsored report, not a security incident. Recorded Future and UserEvidence surveyed 142 Recorded Future customers for a 2027 ROI report and report an average 468% annual return. The stated outcomes are visibility based: 75% of respondents found risks they did not know they had, 72% of that group found lookalike domains before weaponization, and 67% found compromised credentials on dark web markets. Citizens Financial Group is offered as a customer example of a smaller attack surface from outside-in credential visibility.

Nothing here carries a CVE, actor, victim, timeline or indicator, and the figures are self-reported by customers of the seller. Treat the numbers as marketing evidence rather than a measurement of threat intelligence effectiveness, and do not read the 468% figure as a validated loss-avoidance calculation. The only defensive detail worth keeping is the claimed value of external discovery of typosquatted domains, leaked credentials and vendor exposure, which internal alerting may not surface. There is no development to watch beyond publication of the full report and any methodology disclosure that would support the survey claims.
