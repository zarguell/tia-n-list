A new ransomware-as-a-service operation called Panzer has listed 16 alleged victims across 11 countries on its dedicated leak site, which CyberXtron first observed active on August 5, 2026. The group follows the standard double-extortion model: stealing sensitive corporate data before encrypting files, then threatening to publish stolen information on their leak portal.

The victim list spans Thailand (3 victims), Italy (2), Indonesia (2), Serbia (2), and single organizations in Curacao, South Korea, Spain, Czech Republic, Germany, Nigeria, and Switzerland. Sectors hit include technology (4 victims), manufacturing (3), government and defense, agriculture, energy, education, and retail. The breadth of targeting across countries and verticals indicates affiliate-driven opportunistic operations rather than a focused campaign by a single group.

Panzer's rapid accumulation of 16 victims within its first month of activity suggests an aggressive affiliate recruitment model. The mix of targets from critical infrastructure sectors (energy, government) alongside commercial enterprises means defenders across multiple industries should be aware of this new threat. The RaaS model means Panzer tooling will likely proliferate through the criminal ecosystem, increasing the volume of attacks over the coming months. Organizations should ensure backup integrity and monitor for indicators associated with this emerging operation.
