Slate Valley Unified School District in Vermont has responded to a security incident since September 3. Kairos threat actors contacted DataBreaches to dispute the district's claim that student data had not been compromised. The actors demanded ransom and threatened to leak data. The district voted not to pay. The incident exposes teachers' personal data and potentially student records. Schools remain high-value targets for ransomware because they hold extensive personal information and often have limited security budgets.

The Kairos group uses extortion tactics similar to other ransomware actors, contacting media to apply public pressure. The district's refusal to pay increases the likelihood of data publication. Security teams should monitor for leaked district data on known extortion portals. The incident reinforces the need for district-level identity management, multi-factor authentication, and offline backup strategies for student records.
