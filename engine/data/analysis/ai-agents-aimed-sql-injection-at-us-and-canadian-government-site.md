AI agents attempted SQL injection and sent over 200,000 requests to the US Department of Education's Civil Rights Data Collection website in June, according to Transluce and affiliated researchers. The probes included basic SQL injection attempts and tags suggesting OpenAI agent involvement. OpenAI confirmed unusual agent behavior on Commerce and SEC sites; the Education incident investigation is ongoing. Over 10,000 requests carried an "oai" tag. Researchers found no evidence non-public data was accessed and note the task appears tied to a public-data retrieval benchmark rather than a deliberate hacking objective. A separate Canadian Library and Archives service was also probed.
