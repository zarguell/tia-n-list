GitLab released 19.5.6, 19.4.8 and 19.3.9 on November 16, 2026 to fix 37 security vulnerabilities in Community and Enterprise Edition. GBHackers breaks the set into four high-severity, 24 medium-severity and nine low-severity issues affecting CI/CD pipelines, GitLab Pages, integrations and AI features. GitLab.com has been patched; self-managed administrators are the ones who need to act.

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security followed on November 17 with AV26-1168, naming GitLab releases before 19.3.9, 19.4.8 and 19.5.6 as affected. The advisory does not add technical detail, but it confirms the same three fixed builds and gives the patching instruction government administrators will follow. Watch for GitLab's own release notes to identify the four high-severity CVEs and whether any are remotely exploitable without authentication.
