Samsung Securities disclosed on 23 November 2026 that a cyberattack exposed personal information belonging to 8,241 customers. The South Korean brokerage detected unauthorized access on Saturday 21 November in its Voice of Customer system, the part of its website where customers submit inquiries and feedback. An unauthorized party obtained some files that customers had attached.

A later report on the same breach says the leaked details include names and account numbers. The VOC system is a customer service channel rather than a trading platform, which fits the attached-file access described in the disclosure, but the exact set of exposed fields is still only partly reported.

Watch for Samsung Securities to publish the complete data categories, its regulator filing, and any indication that the attached files contained identity documents or financial records. The two reports agree on the 8,241 customer count and the mid-November discovery, and neither states who was behind the attack.
