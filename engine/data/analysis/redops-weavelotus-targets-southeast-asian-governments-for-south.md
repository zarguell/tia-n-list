According to SOCRadar's Threat Research Unit, the Vietnam-sponsored actor WeaveLotus ran the RedOps espionage campaign between August and September 2026. It targeted military, government and legislative organizations in Southeast Asia to collect intelligence on South China Sea defense and military agreements. SOCRadar confirmed five intrusions in Thailand and the Philippines, where the actor exfiltrated gigabytes of internal documents, project plans and credentials. Activity against Indonesian government organizations remained at reconnaissance and covered more than 30 organizations.

The reported initial-access paths were exposed open-source web applications: GeoServer tracked as CVE-2024-36401, JasperReports using default credentials, and osTicket tracked as CVE-2021-42235. The campaign name comes from the repeated "redops" string in the actor's tooling. SOCRadar says its full technical analysis contains the campaign TTPs and IoCs, along with code excerpts and screenshots.

Confirmed document and credential theft across five organizations makes this a sustained espionage operation rather than an isolated compromise. Organizations running exposed GeoServer or osTicket instances, or JasperReports with default credentials, should treat those assets as priority exposure checks for this actor. Watch for publication of the detailed IoCs and any expansion of the confirmed target set. The current evidence establishes reconnaissance in Indonesia, not successful compromise there.
