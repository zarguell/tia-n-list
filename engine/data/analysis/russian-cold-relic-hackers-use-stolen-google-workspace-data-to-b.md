Google's Mandiant and Threat Intelligence Group published a case study on Cold Relic, the Russia-linked espionage group also tracked as COLDRIVER and UNC4057. In a 2026 investigation at a US organization, the attackers took control of an administrator account, downloaded the entire cloud drive and employee directories, and searched email, passwords and personnel records. The Record dates the initial theft to late 2025. The intruders stayed inside for several months, kept reading mail through third-party applications, and found ways back in after password changes and session resets. US authorities have tied the group to Russia's Federal Security Service, and earlier reporting places government officials, defense contractors and nonprofit organizations in Europe and North America among its targets.

The stolen material became the input for a second intrusion. Cold Relic used contact details and correspondence from the first victim to impersonate an employee and approach a second US organization, converting one cloud compromise into a trusted third-party approach. That sequence matters more than any single malware artifact: containment at the first victim did not remove the risk because staff directories and mail history were already in attacker hands. The case also shows why session management and third-party application grants belong in the incident scope, since the attackers continued reading mail and regained access after credentials were rotated.

Watch for administrator accounts exporting whole drives or directories, new third-party applications with mail access, and logins that survive password and session resets. When a Cold Relic incident is declared, partner and contact notification deserves the same priority as endpoint cleanup, because the second breach relied on identity and correspondence taken from the first. The published material gives no victim names or counts for the second organization, and no CVE or exploit is involved, so any scope judgment should wait for further Mandiant detail or a disclosure from the affected organizations.
