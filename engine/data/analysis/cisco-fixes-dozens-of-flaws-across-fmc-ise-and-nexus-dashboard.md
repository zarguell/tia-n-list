Cisco has released patches for dozens of critical-severity CVEs across Secure Firewall Management Center (FMC), Identity Services Engine (ISE), and Nexus Dashboard. The ISE updates are the most urgent, covering 20 CVEs including 12 critical-severity flaws. Three of these, CVE-2026-20282, CVE-2026-20283, and CVE-2026-20284, have already been publicly disclosed. CVE-2026-20282 and CVE-2026-20283 are SQL injection and data tampering bugs rated medium severity but considered high risk by Cisco because they provide privilege levels that can easily escalate to root. CVE-2026-20284 is a critical input validation flaw enabling data viewing, modification, and denial of service.

Additional critical ISE vulnerabilities include three remote code execution issues, two command injection flaws leading to root-level command execution, and an authentication bypass in the REST API. The FMC patches address flaws being actively exploited in ransomware and APT campaigns, including activity attributed to state-sponsored groups. Cisco's advisories note that the PSIRT is aware of public exploit announcements for some of the disclosed vulnerabilities.

Organizations running Cisco ISE, FMC, or Nexus Dashboard should treat these patches as emergency-level updates. The combination of publicly disclosed exploits, active exploitation, and critical-severity RCE flaws in widely deployed network infrastructure makes delayed patching a high-risk decision. Cisco recommends using iACLs to restrict traffic to affected devices as a temporary measure where immediate patching is not feasible.
