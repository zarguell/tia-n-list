Cisco patched dozens of vulnerabilities across three major products on September 17. The ISE updates are the most urgent: 20 CVEs including 12 critical-severity flaws. Three were publicly disclosed before patching (CVE-2026-20282, CVE-2026-20283, CVE-2026-20284), covering SQL injection, data tampering, and arbitrary command execution. While all three require administrative access, Cisco flags them as high risk because the privilege level they grant can escalate to root. Six additional critical ISE flaws include three RCE issues, two command injection bugs leading to root execution, and an authentication bypass in the REST API.

CVE-2026-76460, an ISE authentication bypass, was added to CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on September 16. The Canadian advisory (AV26-932) also covers Cisco Secure Firewall Threat Defense (FTD) and Adaptive Security Appliance (ASA) software, with patches for versions ranging from 7.0.10 through 10.1.0 for FTD and 9.16.4.103 through 9.24.1.26 for ASA. Nexus Dashboard is patched prior to version 4.3.1.175.

Separately, Cisco disclosed three critical vulnerabilities on September 16 in an internal hardening release: CVE-2026-20234, CVE-2026-20332, and CVE-2026-20331, all affecting ISE. These are now merged into this story. The sheer volume (over 20 ISE CVEs, multiple critical) and the KEV listing make this a patch-now situation for any organization running Cisco ISE, FMC, or Nexus Dashboard.

Watch for: Exploitation of CVE-2026-76460 given its KEV status, and whether the publicly disclosed CVE-2026-20282/20283/20284 see weaponization before organizations can patch. ISE is a central authentication and network access control platform, so compromise has outsized impact.
