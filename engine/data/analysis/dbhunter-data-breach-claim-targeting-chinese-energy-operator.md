DBHunter, a threat actor tracked by Dark Web Sonar, has expanded its data breach claims to target government and military entities across Saint Lucia, Brazil, and the Philippines. The actor previously claimed breaches against a Chinese coal and energy operator and an Indonesian university (Universitas Singaperbangsa Karawang). The latest claims, reported September 21, 2026, allege SQL database exfiltration from government and military targets in three countries. DBHunter's pattern shows a focus on critical infrastructure and government entities across Asia and the Pacific, with claims escalating from energy companies to military targets. The claims remain unverified, and the actor's credibility is not yet established, but the breadth of targets (now spanning China, Indonesia, Saint Lucia, Brazil, and the Philippines) and the specificity of the SQL database claims suggest either genuine access or a sophisticated social engineering campaign. Organizations in the named countries and sectors should monitor for corroborating evidence and check their databases for unauthorized access.
