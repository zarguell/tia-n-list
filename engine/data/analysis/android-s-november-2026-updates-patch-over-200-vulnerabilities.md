Google's November 2026 Android security update resolves more than 200 vulnerabilities, including roughly two dozen critical-severity issues, delivered in two patch levels. The 2026-11-01 level fixes 119 defects across Android runtime, Framework, and System, with Framework receiving the most (65), followed by System (53), plus one high-severity information disclosure bug in runtime. The most severe issue is CVE-2026-58868, a critical Framework vulnerability enabling remote code execution with no additional privileges and no user interaction. The 2026-11-05 level fixes 97 bugs in Kernel, Arm, Imagination Technologies, MediaTek, Unisoc, and Qualcomm components. Seventeen flaws were pushed ahead of the monthly rollout via Google Play system updates.

The breadth of the update matters because the critical RCE in Framework and additional RCE, elevation-of-privilege, and denial-of-service flaws in System are remotely reachable, and the vendor-component patches in the second level cover the kernel and chipset layers that OEMs must integrate.

What to watch: devices will only be protected once they receive both patch levels, so OEM and carrier rollout lag leaves a window for CVE-2026-58868 exploitation even though no in-the-wild use is confirmed. Administrators should verify devices report the 2026-11-05 level, not just the first.
