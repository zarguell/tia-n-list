Binance is investigating a potential security breach involving up to 5.8 billion dollars in digital assets, in what could become the largest cryptocurrency hack in history. If confirmed stolen, the amount would far exceed the 1.5 billion stolen from Bybit by North Korean hackers in February 2025, the largest confirmed crypto heist on record.

The suspected breach has already prompted stablecoin issuers Tether and Circle to freeze more than 2.3 billion dollars in assets associated with the incident, including more than 1.567 billion in USDT frozen by Tether. Binance told BleepingComputer that its teams were aware of reports of a potential security issue and were actively investigating, while reassuring customers that user funds were completely safe. Founder Changpeng Zhao said on X that the incident appeared to be a hack, while noting he no longer manages the exchange and expects all user funds to be fully covered. The key uncertainty is whether the funds are actually stolen or merely moved or frozen as a precaution, since Binance has not confirmed a loss and is still investigating.
