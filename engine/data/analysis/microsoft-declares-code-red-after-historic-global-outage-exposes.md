Reuters reports that Microsoft declared a "Code Red" after a historic global outage, citing internal memos. The reporting does not identify affected services, outage dates, a root cause or a vulnerability, so this is an internal engineering escalation rather than a confirmed cyber incident.

The declaration signals senior-level attention to reliability and engineering controls, but claims of attacker involvement would outrun the available material. Watch for Microsoft's incident review, service-specific impact and any evidence connecting the outage to a security event.
