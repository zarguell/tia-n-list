SonicWall patched seven vulnerabilities in SonicOS, including CVE-2026-19868, an SSL-VPN authentication bypass flaw with a CVSS score of 7.1 that allows attackers with valid credentials for an alternate authentication method to bypass SAML authentication controls, potentially including SAML-enforced multi-factor authentication. The flaw only affects SonicOS SSL-VPN services configured to use SAML authentication and impacts SonicOS prior to 7.3.4-7013 and prior to 8.2.2-8015.

SonicWall's PSIRT investigated a report indicating potential exploitation, and CISA added CVE-2026-19868 to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, confirming in-the-wild exploitation and ordering federal civilian executive branch agencies to secure affected systems by November 20, 2026. The advisory also fixes six additional vulnerabilities, including two more serious flaws. Researcher Ryan Harris of The Electronic Office discovered and reported CVE-2026-19868.

The concrete artifacts are CVE-2026-19868 and the fixed releases SonicOS 7.3.4-7013 and 8.2.2-8015. Organizations running SonicWall SSL-VPN with SAML authentication should upgrade immediately, as the SAML bypass can defeat MFA and the vulnerability is already being exploited.
