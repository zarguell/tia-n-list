SonicWall advisory AV26-1133, dated November 6, 2026, discloses multiple SonicOS vulnerabilities. CVE-2026-19868 is an SSL-VPN authentication enforcement flaw with a CVSS score of 7.1, tracked as SNWLID-2026-0014. It lets a user with valid credentials for an alternate authentication method bypass SAML authentication, and successful exploitation may also bypass SAML-enforced multi-factor authentication. The flaw only exposes devices where SAML-based login is enabled. Affected versions are SonicOS prior to 7.3.4-7013 and prior to 8.2.2-8015. The flaw was reported by Ryan Harris of The Electronic Office.

Why it matters: CISA added CVE-2026-19868 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 6, and under Binding Operational Directive 26-04 federal agencies must address it by November 20. SonicWall PSIRT investigated a report indicating potential exploitation, but neither the vendor nor CISA has shared details of suspected attacks, and it is unclear whether exploitation occurred before a patch was available. The November 6 advisory describes seven vulnerabilities in total.

What to watch: confirmed exploitation evidence, patches for remaining flaws in advisory AV26-1133, and whether additional CVEs from the advisory enter the KEV catalog.
