SonicWall patched CVE-2026-19868, a high-severity (CVSS 7.1) authentication enforcement flaw in the SonicOS SSL-VPN service that can let a user with valid credentials for an alternate authentication method bypass SAML authentication controls, including SAML-enforced MFA. The flaw only affects SonicOS SSL-VPN deployments with SAML-based login enabled. SonicWall's product security team investigated a report of potential exploitation and urged customers to upgrade; CISA added the CVE to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 6, 2026 with a federal remediation deadline of November 20, 2026. Fixes are in SonicOS 7.3.4-7013 and 8.2.2-8015, and the advisory also resolves six additional vulnerabilities.

The vulnerability was reported by Ryan Harris of The Electronic Office. Neither SonicWall nor CISA has shared details of the suspected attacks, and it is unclear whether the flaw was exploited as a zero-day before a patch was available. Impacted devices include Gen7 TZ and related platforms.

Administrators should upgrade affected firewalls promptly, verify SSL-VPN SAML configurations and review logs for suspicious authentication activity. The combination of MFA bypass and KEV listing makes this a priority patch even without confirmed widespread exploitation details.
