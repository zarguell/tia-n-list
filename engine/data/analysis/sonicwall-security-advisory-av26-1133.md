SonicWall SonicOS is affected by an SSLVPN MFA bypass flaw, CVE-2026-19868 (CVSS 7.5), that CISA added to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with active in-the-wild exploitation confirmed; SonicWall also flags CVE-2026-107155 and CVE-2025-41450 in the same advisory. The vulnerability affects Gen7 and Gen8 firewalls, and fixed releases are SonicOS 7.3.4-7013 and 8.2.2-8015. Attackers are exploiting the flaw against SSLVPN, and the KEV listing carries a federal remediation due date.

The risk is concentrated because SSLVPN gateways are internet-facing and the flaw bypasses MFA, turning a credential or session weakness into direct network access. With exploitation already observed and a federal due date set, unpatched appliances are likely to be mass-scanned and compromised.

Watch items include completing patches to 7.3.4-7013 or 8.2.2-8015 on all Gen7 and Gen8 units, reviewing SSLVPN access logs for compromise indicators, confirming the KEV due date is met, and monitoring for post-exploitation activity tied to CVE-2026-19868.
