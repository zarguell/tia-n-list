BigCommerce suffered a supply chain breach through Ribon, a third-party storefront app. Attackers used a compromised Ribon application key from September 13 to September 17 to access merchant customer data, including names, emails, phones, and addresses. The key was revoked on September 17 and affected applications uninstalled. Over 1,200 third-party apps are supported by BigCommerce; this incident shows how a single vendor key can expose customer records across hundreds of stores. Monitor for further notifications from BigCommerce and Ribon.
