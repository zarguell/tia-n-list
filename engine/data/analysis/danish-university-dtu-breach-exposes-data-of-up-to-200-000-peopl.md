DTU's breach exposed data for up to 200,000 people after attackers used compromised credentials to access DTUBasen, the university's IAM system. Potentially exposed details include Danish CPR numbers, names, home addresses, work information, and next-of-kin data. DTU cannot confirm exactly what was downloaded, but reports access spanning over two decades of user records for roughly 40,000 active and 160,000 former users.
