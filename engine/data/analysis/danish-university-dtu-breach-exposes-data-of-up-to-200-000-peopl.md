Danish university DTU disclosed that attackers compromised its identity and access management system (DTUBasen) using stolen credentials, downloading data covering up to 200,000 current and former users. The dataset spans more than two decades of user records; exposed fields for active users include Danish civil registration numbers (CPR), full names, home addresses, work emails, job titles, and next-of-kin contact details. DTU states it cannot determine precisely what was downloaded or how many people are affected. Watch for further disclosure of attacker attribution or regulatory notifications under Danish data-protection rules.
