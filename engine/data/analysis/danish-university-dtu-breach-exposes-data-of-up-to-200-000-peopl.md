DTU confirmed hackers used compromised credentials to access DTUBasen, its IAM system, exposing data for up to 200,000 people. The dataset spans nearly 40,000 active and 160,000 former users, including Danish CPR numbers, names, addresses, job details, and next-of-kin information. DTU says precise download scope and affected counts remain undetermined. Active users' next-of-kin and address data is retained; former users' sensitive fields are deleted after six months. This is a serious identity-breach incident requiring notification monitoring.
