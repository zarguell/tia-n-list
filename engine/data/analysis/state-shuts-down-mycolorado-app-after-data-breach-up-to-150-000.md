The Governor's Office of Information Technology found on 18 November 2026 that an unauthorized user had accessed data on the myColorado app platform. The state kept the app offline while investigating, disrupting digital ID and mobile access to state services. Reporting from Kiowa County Press puts the possible exposure at up to 150,000 people.

A later update says the app remained offline after the cybersecurity incident. The reviewed material does not list the exposed data fields, the intrusion method, or the identity of the actor. Because the app carries state digital ID functions, an exposure of this scale raises questions about impersonation risk and about how quickly residents are told which credentials need replacement.

Watch for the state's incident notice, a list of affected fields, a restoration timeline for myColorado, and any credit monitoring or ID replacement offer. The 150,000 figure is an upper bound from early reporting, not a confirmed victim count.
