Another ShinyHunters suspect arrested. The group, active since 2019, focuses on data theft and extortion via social engineering and software exploits. A teenager from Amman, Jordan, suspected of leading the group under the handle "Rey," was detained and is reportedly cooperating with the FBI. This follows the September arrest of 24-year-old Pepijn van der Stap in the Netherlands. The suspect was linked to extorting a Boeing divested unit. KrebsOnSecurity identified Rey as Saif Al-din Khader in a November 2025 profile; Reuters reported the detention on October 3. The FBI has warned remaining members and indicated detainees are talking. Victims include Kodak, McKesson, and a rival gang site defaced. This tightens the net on a prolific crime group.
