Synology warned customers of active C3RB3r ransomware attacks exploiting a vulnerability patched in December 2024 on NAS devices running outdated DiskStation Manager. The advisory, SA-24:20 from November 2024, covers CVE-2024-10441, CVE-2024-10445, and CVE-2024-50629 in DSM, flaws that allow arbitrary code execution, arbitrary file read, and arbitrary file write on unpatched appliances. Synology urged users to update DSM immediately, enable automatic updates, and block the management web interface from the internet.

The gap between patch and exploitation is the story: the flaws were public and fixed in December 2024, yet devices left unpatched for nearly two years are now being encrypted for ransom. C3RB3r targets NAS appliances that are often internet-exposed and rarely monitored, and Synology rarely issues security advisories, which signals elevated concern. Administrators running DSM should verify they are on a patched release, restrict management access, and treat any internet-facing NAS as a likely target until updated.
