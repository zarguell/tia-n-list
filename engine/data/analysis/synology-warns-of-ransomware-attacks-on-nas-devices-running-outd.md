Synology issued an October 29 alert warning that attackers are exploiting a flaw patched in December 2024 on NAS devices running outdated DiskStation Manager versions, with ransomware attacks reported against those systems. The alert urges users to update DSM to current versions. This is a distinct issue from Synology's separate advisory covering eight DSM vulnerabilities including two critical unauthenticated flaws.

Technical details such as the specific CVE, affected DSM versions, and the ransomware family involved were not included in the available alert text. Administrators running older DSM builds should treat the alert as a prompt to patch and verify their devices are on supported, current firmware. The risk is concentrated on internet-facing or unpatched NAS devices that have not received the December 2024 fix.
