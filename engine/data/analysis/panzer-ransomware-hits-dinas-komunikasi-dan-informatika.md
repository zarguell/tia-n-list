Panzer ransomware group claimed a September 3 attack on Dinas Komunikasi dan Informatika, the Central Java Province Communication and Informatics Service in Indonesia. The target operates under diskominfo.jatengprov.go.id and handles government communications, informatics, coding, and statistics for the province. Panzer posted organizational details about the agency, suggesting they obtained internal documents during the intrusion.

Government IT agencies in Southeast Asia remain frequent ransomware targets because they hold citizen data and operate under tight procurement cycles that delay patching. Central Java's communication office would likely hold records related to provincial digital services, making data exfiltration a credible threat. Panzer has been active against government and critical infrastructure targets in the region.

No CVEs were disclosed in the claim, and the group has not specified a ransom deadline or data leak timeline. Monitor for follow-up data dumps on Panzer's leak site and any Indonesian government incident response statements. The lack of technical detail in the claim suggests this may be early-stage extortion rather than a completed data sale.
