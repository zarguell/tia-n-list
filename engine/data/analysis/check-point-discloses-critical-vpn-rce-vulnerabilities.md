Check Point disclosed two critical vulnerabilities in its VPN technology, CVE-2026-85102 and CVE-2026-85103, both rated CVSS 9.8. The flaws affect Remote Access VPN and Site-to-Site VPN functionality, allowing unauthenticated remote attackers to execute arbitrary code on affected security gateways under certain conditions. Check Point's internal research team discovered and patched both issues.

The Dutch National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) issued a warning that exploitation of these flaws is imminent. This escalation from vendor disclosure to national-level advisory signals real-world threat actor interest. Organizations running Check Point VPN appliances should prioritize patching immediately, particularly those with internet-facing VPN concentrators.

What to watch: Monitor for proof-of-concept exploits or observed exploitation in the wild. The CVSS 9.8 rating and Dutch NCSC advisory suggest threat actors are actively developing or deploying exploits. Check Point's 13-source coverage across security outlets indicates broad industry awareness.
