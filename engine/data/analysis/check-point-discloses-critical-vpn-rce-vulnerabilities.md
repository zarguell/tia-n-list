Check Point disclosed two critical vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-85102 and CVE-2026-85103, in its VPN technology that allow unauthenticated remote attackers to execute arbitrary code on affected security gateways. Both Remote Access VPN and Site-to-Site VPN are impacted. A third CVE, CVE-2026-91843, was also addressed in the same advisory cycle.

The company said its internal research team discovered and resolved the issues. The vulnerabilities are pre-authentication, meaning no credentials are required to exploit them under certain conditions. Check Point has published patches and urges immediate application. This story has been covered by 20 sources including BleepingComputer, SecurityWeek, The Hacker News, and Check Point's own support portal, reflecting the breadth of organizations running Check Point VPN appliances.

The 20-source coverage and pre-auth RCE nature make this one of the higher-severity VPN disclosures this quarter. Organizations should verify their Check Point gateway versions against the advisory and apply patches before attackers develop reliable exploit code.
