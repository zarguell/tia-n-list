CISA reported that over 100 internet-exposed water systems were targeted in cyberattacks during July 2026. The advisory highlighted the continued focus on US water infrastructure by threat actors. Separately, Governor Jared Polis confirmed that foreign actors breached two small Colorado water utilities in late August, each serving fewer than 200 people. The attackers manipulated equipment settings, disabled remote access and alarms, and altered drinking water control systems according to Axios reporting via DataBreaches.net.

The Colorado breaches are notable because the attackers changed equipment configurations rather than deploying ransomware, suggesting either espionage or pre-positioning for disruptive capability. Small utilities with limited IT staff and budget are particularly vulnerable targets. The two Colorado utilities were privately owned and serve rural communities, where operational technology security is often minimal.

What to watch: whether the Colorado incidents are attributed to a specific nation-state actor, the number of confirmed compromises among the 100+ targeted systems, and whether CISA issues specific IOCs or hardening guidance for water sector SCADA and ICS systems.
