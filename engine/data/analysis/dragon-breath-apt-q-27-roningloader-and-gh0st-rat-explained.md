Dragon Breath, also known as APT-Q-27 and Golden Eye Dog, is a Chinese cybercrime group active since 2020 that targets Chinese-speaking users and organizations across the Asia-Pacific region. Its campaigns use trojanized installers for Telegram, Chrome, and Teams, with loaders delivered through double-clean-app DLL side-loading chains. The RONINGLOADER chain deploys a modified Gh0st RAT that talks to command and control servers over WebSocket.

The group's 2026 operations include an intrusion at a certificate provider that yielded signing material used to sign Dragon Breath malware, which materially improves the quality of its payloads. Gh0st RAT variants are a common workhorse for this ecosystem, and WebSocket C2 over standard ports makes the traffic harder to distinguish from normal web activity. Defenders in the region should watch for signed-but-untrusted binaries and side-loading patterns around legitimate applications, and treat the certificate compromise as a reason to review trust decisions.
