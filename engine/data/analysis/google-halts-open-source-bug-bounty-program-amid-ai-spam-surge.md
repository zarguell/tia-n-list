Google suspended submissions to its Open Source Software Vulnerability Rewards Program after a surge of AI-generated reports. The OSS VRP, launched in August 2022 with rewards up to $31,337, covers critical open-source dependencies including Golang, Angular, Bazel, and Protocol Buffers. The pause does not affect supply chain reports or the Patch Rewards Program. Google is working to readjust the program. This is a policy response to automation abuse rather than a vulnerability event.
