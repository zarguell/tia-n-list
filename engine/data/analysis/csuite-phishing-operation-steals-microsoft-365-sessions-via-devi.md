ANY.RUN's research, now echoed by broader coverage, documents CSuite, a financially motivated phishing and remote-access operation hitting US and EU organizations. The campaign runs two paths from the same infrastructure: device-code OAuth phishing that steals Microsoft 365 sessions without capturing a password, and deployment of legitimate RMM agents (ScreenConnect, Action1, Atera, Syncro, PDQ Connect) renamed as Adobe, DocuSign or Dotloop installers.

Delivery leans on trusted business lures: Adobe Document Cloud share invitations sent from hijacked Adobe tenants, DocuSign envelopes, Zoom and Google Meet invites, Dropbox files and Microsoft 365 voicemail pages. Victims who take the identity branch are sent to Microsoft's real device-login page with an attacker-initiated code, handing over access and refresh tokens. On the endpoint branch, a 324-byte batch file self-elevates through PowerShell and pulls a ScreenConnect MSI from a public GitHub account (Ivan3900); the installed client registers a credential provider, appends an LSA authentication package that loads at boot, and survives safe mode with networking.

Scale and craftsmanship are what make this notable. The operator's CSuite v1.1 panel exposed 29 captured M365 sessions, 1,593 documents sent through hijacked Adobe tenants and 15,955 harvested email addresses, and 51 percent of observed sandbox submissions came from the United States. A single 66 KB anti-analysis gate at the path /m/js/utils.js has served the same kit across roughly 170 hosts for about seven months, filtering security vendors, sandboxes and automation before showing a lure. Activity ran from at least March 2026 into September 2026.

Watch for the /m/js/utils.js path in proxy and DNS telemetry, Entra ID sign-in logs showing the device-code flow, and unsanctioned RMM agents registering to unknown tenants. Since victims' mailboxes were worked by hand from a remote-desktop host, invoice fraud and payment redirection are the likely follow-on, so any user who entered a device code needs immediate session and token revocation.
