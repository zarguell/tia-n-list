OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Cyber, an advanced security model rated Critical for cybersecurity under the company's Preparedness Framework, with stricter alignment controls than its predecessor and superior performance in exploit testing. Researchers have used the model to uncover vulnerabilities in major systems such as the Linux kernel and Firefox, prompting timely patches from maintainers. Access is currently restricted to approved enterprise customers participating in specialised testing programmes, with safeguards refined through feedback from industrial security experts.

The announcement frames a generational step in autonomous vulnerability research: frontier models are crossing capability tiers that let them identify and address software flaws at scale, while alignment training aims to keep them within authorized scope and away from unauthorized targets. The rapid evolution of such models represents a shift in both offensive and defensive cybersecurity operations, with restricted access and enterprise-only deployment reflecting the dual-use nature of the tooling.
