A cyberattack on refrigeration systems forced hundreds of French supermarkets to discard refrigerated and frozen food overnight from October 11 to 12. Stores across Intermarche, Netto, Carrefour, E.Leclerc, and Monoprix were affected; equipment supplier Danfoss confirmed legacy AK-SM800 and AK-SC355 systems with no updates were targeted. Attackers extended defrost cycles from one hour to eight hours, delaying alarms. Les Mousquetaires estimated losses at several million euros and the Paris prosecutor opened an investigation. The incident is tied to the broader European supermarket fridge disruption.
