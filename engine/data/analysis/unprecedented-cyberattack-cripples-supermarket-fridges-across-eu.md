Attackers remotely defrosted refrigeration systems at major French supermarket chains by exploiting older Danfoss refrigeration controller software. Victims include Intermarché, Coopérative U, Carrefour Market, Monoprix, Leclerc, and Super U. Reports cite between 250 and 350 affected stores. The attackers triggered built-in automatic defrost cycles, raising temperatures to around 50°C and forcing food waste and store closures.

The Paris prosecutor confirmed the attack. EU agriculture commissioner Christophe Hansen condemned it as unprecedented. A Reddit update linked the vulnerability to a previously exploited flaw used in an earlier US DoD commissary refrigeration disruption. The event carries CVE-2025-41450 in its story record, though the direct attribution to that CVE in the event text is partial.

This is a confirmed operational-technology attack with real economic impact across multiple retailers. Watch for further attribution and whether the same controller vulnerability is exploited elsewhere.
