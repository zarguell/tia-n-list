A cyberattack disabled refrigeration at hundreds of supermarkets across France overnight, pushing temperatures to 50 degrees Celsius and forcing automatic defrosting. The incident disrupted delivery schedules and affected store pricing. The campaign ties to broader European retail infrastructure targeting, with 21 sources tracking the event across outlets including Reuters, BBC, and AFP. A CVE-2025-41450 reference appears in tracking data but is not the primary driver here; the focus is operational disruption of cold-chain logistics at scale.

Watch whether the same actors expand from refrigeration control to point-of-sale or supply-chain systems. Retail cooling infrastructure is rarely hardened like IT networks, making it an easy target for physical-impact attacks.
