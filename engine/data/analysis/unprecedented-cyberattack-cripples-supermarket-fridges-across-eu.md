French supermarket refrigeration systems were disrupted by a cyberattack targeting Danfoss control systems, forcing some fridge units into automatic defrosting. Reports cite Intermarche and Super U chains affected, with temperatures rising to 50 degrees and fresh products destroyed across hundreds of stores. Delivery and pricing were also impacted. The incident is linked to a breach of industrial refrigeration controllers rather than standard IT networks. No CVE is assigned. Confirmed via Mastodon references to bfmtv.com and infosecurity-magazine.com reporting.
