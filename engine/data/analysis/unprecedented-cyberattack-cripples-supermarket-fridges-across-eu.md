A cyberattack disrupted supermarket refrigeration systems across Europe, forcing automatic defrosting that raised fridge temperatures to around 50C overnight. The incident affected delivery schedules and store prices. Reporting from The Guardian, BBC, and multiple European outlets describes it as unprecedented in scale for retail refrigeration infrastructure. The attack appears to target operational-technology networks that manage cooling systems rather than traditional IT endpoints.

Retail and logistics operators should segment refrigeration OT networks from corporate IT and review remote-access controls for HVAC and cooling systems. Monitor for similar disruptions in grocery and cold-chain supply networks.
