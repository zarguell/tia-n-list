Reports describe an unprecedented cyberattack that activated automatic defrosting in supermarket refrigeration across Europe, impacting delivery schedules and store pricing. The attack targeted cooling infrastructure rather than point-of-sale or customer data systems. Multiple outlets covered the incident.
Why it matters: operational-technology attacks on cold-chain infrastructure show a shift from data theft to physical-disruption objectives. Watch for attribution, vendor patch timelines for affected refrigeration controllers, and whether the same actors target other logistics sectors.
