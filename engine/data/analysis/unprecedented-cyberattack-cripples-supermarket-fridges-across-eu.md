Attackers remotely defrosted refrigerators at hundreds of French supermarkets overnight by hijacking Danfoss refrigeration controllers. Victims include Intermarche, Cooperative U, Carrefour Market, Monoprix, Leclerc, and Super U, affecting roughly 250-350 stores. Attackers triggered built-in automatic defrost cycles, raising temperatures to 50C and spoiling fresh and frozen food. The Paris prosecutor confirmed the attack. Danfoss confirmed exploitation of a previously known vulnerability; none of the controllers had been patched. A linked CVE-2025-41450 is noted in the story metadata. The attack is described as unprecedented for targeting food-preservation infrastructure at scale.
