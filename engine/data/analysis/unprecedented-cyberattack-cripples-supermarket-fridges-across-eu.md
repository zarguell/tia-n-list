French supermarket refrigeration controllers were forced into remote defrost by attackers who exploited a previously used vulnerability (CVE-2025-41450, linked to the same controller family). Vendor DanFoss confirmed the controllers had not been patched. The incident hit chains including Intermarché and Super U, raising storage temperatures to 50°C in some cases and destroying fresh products. A Reuters report on October 12 frames it as economic sabotage. The original Reddit post and follow-up sources (BBC, BFM TV, cyberworldops.eu, Reuters, diningandcooking.com, Seattle Times) all point to the same campaign rather than separate events. The key risk: unpatched DanFoss controllers remain exposed across the sector.
