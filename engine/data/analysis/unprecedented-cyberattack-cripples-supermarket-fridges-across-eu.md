Hackers activated automatic defrosting across supermarket refrigeration systems across Europe, disrupting delivery schedules and impacting store pricing in what authorities describe as an unprecedented cyberattack. The incident demonstrates operational-technology compromise at scale, affecting cold-chain logistics and retail pricing systems rather than traditional IT assets. Retailers should audit industrial-control access to HVAC/refrigeration controllers, segment OT networks from corporate IT, and review physical-defrost event logs for unauthorized activation patterns.
