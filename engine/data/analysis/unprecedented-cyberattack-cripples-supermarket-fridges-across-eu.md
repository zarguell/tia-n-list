Attackers remotely defrosted refrigerators at over 250 French supermarket chains, primarily Intermarche and Cooperative U, by compromising Danfoss refrigeration controllers. Reports cite temperatures rising to 50 degrees Celsius and triggering automatic defrost cycles, forcing stores to discard fresh and frozen products. The incident began Sunday night and also affected Carrefour Market, Monoprix, Leclerc, and Super U outlets, with some reports extending to Dutch retailer Albert Heijn. Paris prosecutors confirmed the cyberattack. The scale spans hundreds of stores and represents an operational attack on physical infrastructure rather than data exfiltration. Watch for attribution and whether the same threat actor targets additional EU retailers.
