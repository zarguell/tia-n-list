Attackers remotely defrosted refrigerators at multiple French supermarket chains overnight, targeting older Danfoss refrigeration controllers to trigger automatic defrost. Victims include Intermarche, Cooperative U, Carrefour Market, Monoprix, Leclerc, and Super U; reports cite 250 to 350 stores affected with significant food waste and financial losses. EU agriculture commissioner Christophe Hansen condemned the attack, confirming Leclerc among victims. This is an operational-technology intrusion with physical impact on food supply chains. Watch for attribution and whether similar refrigeration controllers elsewhere are being targeted.
