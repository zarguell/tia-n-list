Overnight 11-12 October 2026, attackers remotely manipulated Danfoss refrigeration controllers at roughly 700 French supermarkets, including Intermarche, Netto, Cooperative U, Carrefour, E.Leclerc and Monoprix, forcing defrost cycles and pushing temperatures to about 50 C. The attackers extended defrost cycles from one hour to eight hours, delaying alarms, and caused fires and food losses estimated in the millions of euros. Affected hardware included legacy Danfoss AK-SM800 and AK-SC355 units plus Copeland and Dixell XWEB systems, most of which no longer receive software or security updates.

A second wave followed on 2 November 2026 across Belgium, Luxembourg, Sweden, the Netherlands, Estonia, Ireland, Finland, Italy and Portugal, hitting more than 100 stores with the same defrost-to-50 C technique. No group has claimed responsibility and investigators have not confirmed whether the two waves are connected. The underlying flaws trace to Claroty Team82 research from August 2026 identifying three vulnerabilities in the Danfoss AK-SM 800A controller: CVE-2025-41450, an improper authentication flaw via a hidden code-of-the-day mechanism, plus CVE-2025-41451 and CVE-2025-41452, which allow command injection through the SMTP email configuration page. Danfoss had published an advisory for CVE-2025-41450 on 24 March 2025. IPinfo scans found 2,528 exposed Danfoss StoreView dashboards across 73 countries five days before the first attack, with 85 percent of French units running software carrying known flaws and only 195 fully patched. France held more exposed dashboards than the rest of the world combined.

The same known vulnerability had already been used in August 2026 against US military commissary refrigeration, and Danfoss has advised customers to retire obsolete models and keep supported equipment updated. Even after France took most units offline, dashboards in Belgium, Sweden and Portugal remained reachable. Watch whether further waves spread to additional chains or countries, and whether investigators confirm the entry point or a single coordinated actor, since neither has been established.
