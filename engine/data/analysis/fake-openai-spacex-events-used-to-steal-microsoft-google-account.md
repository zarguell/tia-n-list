Push Security documents a phishing campaign that uses convincing fake OpenAI, SpaceX, and Anthropic event registration pages offering free premium AI services to lure employees into signing in with corporate Microsoft or Google accounts. The pages integrate two phishing-as-a-service platforms: Kali365 for Microsoft device-code phishing and VoidProxy as an adversary-in-the-middle reverse proxy that intercepts Google sign-ins and steals session cookies, with VoidProxy also acting as a second stage to capture Microsoft credentials and cookies after Kali365 obtains authentication tokens.

Push Security detected and blocked visits to the malicious registration pages in late October 2026. The campaign follows a similar July 2026 operation reported by ANY.RUN that abused legitimate Zoom event pages to promote fake Meta, OpenAI, and Anthropic events leading to device-code and adversary-in-the-middle phishing. Defenders should treat unsolicited AI-themed event invitations as credential-harvesting attempts and enforce phishing-resistant multifactor authentication on Microsoft and Google accounts.
