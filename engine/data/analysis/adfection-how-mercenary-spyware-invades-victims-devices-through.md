Citizen Lab at the University of Toronto disclosed AdFection, the first documented case of zero-click mercenary spyware delivered through targeted advertising. The campaign is attributed to Israeli firm Discover-Tech, which presents itself as a legitimate ad network but acts as a facilitator for spyware partners including Candiru. The ads directed millions of devices to contact Candiru servers, with likely goals of large-scale tracking and surveillance.

The infections included devices at media outlet Al Jazeera, and the ads appeared on networks associated with the U.S. Department of Defense, NATO, the International Atomic Energy Agency, and cybersecurity agencies, according to partner firm Confiant. The researchers call for regulatory investigation and sanctions against Discover-Tech. This raises direct questions for the advertising industry about how adversaries exploit legitimate adtech infrastructure for surveillance campaigns.
