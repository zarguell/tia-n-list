PHP2Shell remains the defining event in this story: CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, both CVSS v4.0 9.2, were fixed on October 21 in PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35 after details leaked ahead of the coordinated release. The bugs are unauthenticated pre-application RCE paths reached through chunked HTTP/1.1 or length-less HTTP/2 multipart bodies that skip post_max_size, plus empty file parts with filename="" that still create $_FILES entries. CERT-EU warned that versions back to PHP 5 could be affected.

Exploitation started before disclosure. Amazon saw PHP2Shell attempts on October 17, Cloudflare logged tens of thousands of attempts within 45 minutes of the first reports and millions by the evening of October 21, and a Perplexity honeypot captured confirmed RCE against mod_php on PHP 8.4.26. CISA added both CVEs to KEV. Qualys reported only 199,000 of 156 million PHP packages with a known version patched in the first 12 hours, and organizations in Japan and Europe took public sites offline while patching.

The next release window arrived on November 19, 14:00 to 14:30 UTC, covering all supported PHP branches with the highest severity listed as Critical. PHP's maintainers said the critical issues need non-default configuration or uncommon setups. CVE-2026-115560, the session upload progress RCE disclosed two days after PHP2Shell, is expected in this batch: it needs session.serialize_handler set to php_binary or php_serialize, a runtime switch to php with ini_set() before session_start(), and session upload progress still enabled. A November 19 post reports the flaw patched in the November 19 release; disabling session upload progress is the interim mitigation.

Watch patch level across every supported branch, since this update follows the four-week cycle rather than a new emergency, and keep session upload progress disabled where it is not needed. The older PHP2Shell scanning wave has not gone away, so log review for multipart abuse and PHP worker child processes still matters.
