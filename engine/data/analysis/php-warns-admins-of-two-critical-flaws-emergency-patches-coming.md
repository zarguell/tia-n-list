A new update reports that attackers are exploiting leaked zero-day flaws in PHP. The event, sourced from bankinfosecurity.com and shared via Mastodon, references widespread PHP exploitation. This aligns with the existing story tracking PHP2Shell, the paired critical vulnerabilities CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, both rated CVSS 4.0 9.2 and affecting PHP 8.2 through 8.5 across multiple deployment modes including Apache mod_php, PHP-FPM, and embedded appliance runtimes.

CISA added the PHP flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on October 26, and reporting confirms scanning and exploitation attempts continue. Administrators should apply the out-of-cycle fixes released October 21: PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, and 8.2.35. The event reinforces that PHP2Shell remains actively targeted, with exposure extending to firewall and appliance management interfaces that embed vulnerable PHP runtimes.
