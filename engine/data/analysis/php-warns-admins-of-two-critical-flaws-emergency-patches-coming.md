The flaws are being tracked by CISA and multiple vendors, with exploitation on disclosure day leaving little time for defenders. Ivanti Connect Secure CVE-2026-104935 also appears in the same October vulnerability reporting, indicating overlapping exploitation activity across enterprise edge devices.
