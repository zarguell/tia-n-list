PHP's security team warned administrators to prepare for emergency updates addressing two critical remote code execution flaws, CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, each rated 9.2 under CVSS v4.0 and believed to affect PHP 5 through 8. Details leaked ahead of the scheduled October 21, 2026 disclosure, and the team reserved the right to release earlier if details went public or active exploitation appeared. watchTowr reported the flaws to BleepingComputer. PHP powers many content management systems, e-commerce platforms, and custom web services, so exposure is broad.

Attacks were subsequently confirmed and CISA sounded the alarm, with a third flaw, CVE-2026-115560, emerging in the same cluster. Administrators should verify patched PHP releases are deployed across all web-facing and backend systems and watch for follow-on advisories, as the cluster of PHP vulnerabilities continues to attract exploitation.
