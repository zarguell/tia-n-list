PHP is preparing emergency patches for two critical remote-code-execution vulnerabilities tracked as CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, both rated 9.2 under CVSS v4.0. The security team warned administrators to prepare for updates on October 21 at 14:00 UTC. Details leaked ahead of disclosure; watchTowr told reporters the flaws likely affect PHP versions 5, 6, 7, and 8. The team reserved the right to release earlier if exploitation appears.

This is a high-severity, wide-impact advisory. PHP underpins a large share of web applications, so rapid patching is critical. No technical details or confirmed exploitation counts have been released, but the pre-disclosure leak raises the risk of weaponization. Monitor the PHP security announcements and any CISA or vendor follow-on advisories confirming active use.
