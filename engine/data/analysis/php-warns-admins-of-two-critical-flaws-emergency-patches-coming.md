CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 are two remote code execution flaws in PHP, each rated CVSS v4.0 9.2, reportedly affecting PHP versions 5 through 8. Details leaked ahead of the scheduled disclosure, so the PHP security team is shipping emergency updates on Wednesday, October 21, at 14:00 UTC, and warns it may release earlier if details spread further or evidence of active exploitation appears. The PHP team has not disclosed technical details, specific affected versions, or which releases contain fixes; watchTowr told BleepingComputer the flaws are RCEs believed to span PHP 5, 6, 7, and 8.

The leak of embargoed vulnerability details before patches are available raises the risk of opportunistic exploitation against the large population of PHP-powered sites, including content management systems and e-commerce platforms. Administrators should prepare to patch immediately at release and monitor for early exploitation.

What to watch: whether the PHP team accelerates the release, any public proof-of-concept or in-the-wild exploitation reports, and confirmation of affected version ranges and fixed releases. Until patches ship, treating PHP deployments as at risk and reducing exposure of PHP endpoints is prudent.
