A hardware implant inside Ledger signer devices sold through Southeast Asia reseller CryptoBilis intercepted the 24-word recovery phrase displayed during setup and transmitted it over LTE. Confirmed losses exceed $86 million across multiple users. Ledger confirmed on October 13 that all confirmed fund losses involved signers sold through CryptoBilis, and told customers to stop using devices bought from that reseller in the last 90 days. The implant was manually soldered to the circuit board, sitting inside the enclosure to read screen data. It is a supply-chain hardware compromise, not a remote exploit, which makes it harder to detect with conventional endpoint controls. Watch for additional reseller channels and for Ledger's confirmation of total affected devices and compensation scope.
