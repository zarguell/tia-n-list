A hardware implant inside Ledger signers sold through Southeast Asia reseller CryptoBilis is blamed for at least $86 million in cryptocurrency theft. The implant reads the 24-word recovery phrase shown on screen during setup and transmits it over LTE. Ledger confirmed all confirmed losses involved devices from that reseller and told affected customers to stop using the devices and move assets to a new signer with a new seed.
Why it matters: it is a supply-chain attack against offline hardware wallets, undermining the assumption that cold storage guarantees isolation. Watch for Ledger's follow-up advisory and any identification of additional compromised resellers.
