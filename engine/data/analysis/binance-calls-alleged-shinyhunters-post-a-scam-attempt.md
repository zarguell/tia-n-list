A threat actor using the ShinyHunters handle listed what it claimed were 335.9 million Binance user records on a newly emerged underground forum, asking $900,000 in Bitcoin or Monero and telling Binance it had "two options left: pay or leak." The listing allegedly included KYC documents, internal infrastructure details, account passwords, crypto wallet private keys, transaction history, emails, and phone numbers.

Binance denied the claim on November 8, saying its initial review found no evidence that its systems, customer KYC data, account credentials, wallet private keys, or internal infrastructure were compromised. The exchange told BleepingComputer that it does not hold private keys for users' external wallets, does not store passwords in plaintext, and no longer operates in some regions named in the listing. It also said similar past listings often involved infostealer malware data collected from users' own devices rather than a breach of exchange systems.

Brandefense said on November 3 that it had examined four versions of related Telegram advertisements without opening a sample, and ThreatMon reported the seller kept a sample behind a forum paywall. The claim is unverified and should be treated as an extortion attempt until a sample is analyzed. The separate October 29 wallet attack that moved about $6.34 billion in digital assets is unrelated.
