Silent Push published a report on North Korean remote IT worker schemes targeting financial institutions. The scheme uses a Western front person who passes identity checks, while the salary splits 35% to the face and 65% back to the North Korean operator via cryptocurrency, wire transfers, or third-party processors. The report lists indicators for HR and fraud teams to detect the setup before onboarding. The Mastodon post links directly to the Silent Push white paper.

This story fits fraud and insider-threat categories rather than malware or vulnerability reports. The primary evidence is the Silent Push research paper, with malware.news as a secondary source. No CVEs are assigned; the risk is organizational and process-level. Watch for DOJ or Treasury follow-up actions that confirm the scale of financial-sector infiltration.
