Sygnia published a disclosure on Fire Ant, a China-nexus threat actor first identified in 2025, describing how the group has expanded its targeting beyond hypervisors into trusted infrastructure. The actor compromises routers, authentication systems, and Linux management hosts to maintain covert access, harvest credentials and network traffic, and pivot toward high-value connected environments.

The activity is described as long-running espionage rather than a single campaign. Fire Ant's TTPs center on abusing trust relationships in infrastructure: if you control the router or the auth system, you inherit the trust of everything behind it. The shift from hypervisor-level compromise to the surrounding management plane suggests a maturing operational playbook.

No specific CVEs, victim organizations, or tooling details were included in the available reporting. The source is malware.news syndicating Sygnia blog posts, and the full Sygnia writeup was not included in the ingested content.
