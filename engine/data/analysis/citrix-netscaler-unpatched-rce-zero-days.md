Citrix NetScaler devices face unpatched remote code execution flaws that have been confirmed in exploitation. WatchTowr verified reports of RCE vulnerabilities, and administrators reported supplier warnings to shut down appliances before public disclosure. The incident predates public confirmation by roughly ten hours and involves a pre-notification from NCSC-NL distributed under TLP:AMBER+STRICT.

The story tracks multiple CVEs in the CVE-2026-88771 to 88778 range and reflects sustained attacker interest in network appliances as initial access points. NetScaler owners should treat any unpatched appliance as high risk, apply vendor patches when available, and review logs for suspicious access patterns dating back to late September.
