Citrix NetScaler zero-days CVE-2026-88771 through CVE-2026-88778 were patched after confirmed in-the-wild exploitation, with Zscaler publishing an exploitation analysis and attack chain breakdown for CVE-2026-88771. The cluster spans many corroborating posts tracking the same campaign.

The risk is high: unpatched NetScaler appliances exposed to the internet are being actively exploited, and the CVE range covers multiple distinct flaws. Operators should treat any internet-facing NetScaler as potentially compromised until patched and reviewed.

Watch for vendor updates on which builds are affected, for indicators of compromise tied to the exploitation chain, and for any CISA KEV addition. Until patches are applied, restrict management interfaces and monitor for unauthorized access.
