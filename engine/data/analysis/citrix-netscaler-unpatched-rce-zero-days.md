Threat actors are actively exploiting two critical zero-day vulnerabilities in Citrix NetScaler (CVE-2026-88771 through CVE-2026-88778) to deploy a modified Platypus remote administration framework on compromised ADC and Gateway appliances. Binary Defense investigated intrusions showing evidence of exploitation. The flaws are patched but were exploited before disclosure. The modified Platypus backdoor provides remote administration; organizations running unpatched NetScaler appliances face confirmed active exploitation. Watch for CISA KEV updates and further reports of Platypus variants tied to these CVEs.
