Citrix released emergency updates for CVE-2026-88779 (CVSS 8.7), a memory-overflow vulnerability in NetScaler ADC and Gateway appliances configured with SAML authentication. The company confirmed targeted attacks leading to denial-of-service; affected versions include 14.1 and 13.1 branches. Patches 14.1-73.41 and 13.1-64.28 (plus FIPS variants) are available. Monitor whether researchers confirm remote-code-execution potential beyond DoS, since the vulnerability affects authentication paths on exposed appliances.
