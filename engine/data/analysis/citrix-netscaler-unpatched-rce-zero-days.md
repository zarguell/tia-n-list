The Citrix NetScaler saga escalated from rumor to confirmed emergency patching and now includes primary attacker analysis. Unit 42's September 30 update provides attacker TTPs, malware analysis, and IOCs for the NetScaler zero-day exploitation tracked as CVE-2026-88771 through CVE-2026-88778. watchTowr confirmed multiple unpatched RCE flaws circulating in the wild, ruling out the older CVE-2026-19489 and CVE-2026-19490 pair. Citrix patched the set after confirmed exploitation; Canada's Cyber Centre described CVE-2026-88771 as an improper input validation flaw allowing remote unauthenticated code execution (CVSS 9.8). September 30 trade reporting described government and finance offices hit hard by CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772, while CERT-EU published exploitation guidance and Sygnia urged immediate patching plus pre-patch compromise investigation.

The operating assumption remains that any NetScaler exposed before patching is compromised. Unit 42's new analysis adds concrete attacker behavior to hunt for, and the listed IOCs should feed threat-intel feeds immediately. Patch all customer-managed ADC and Gateway appliances, restrict appliance reach to internal systems, rotate session material and credentials visible to the appliance, and review authentication logs. Expect further victim disclosures and watch for attribution to either a specific actor or an initial-access broker. Any unpatched appliance still facing the internet should be treated as breached.
