Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway appliances spent late September under active exploitation with no patches available. On September 25, 2026, a reddit thread on r/Citrix carried shutdown advice attributed to the Dutch national cyber security center, and by September 26 researchers at watchTowr and Kevin Beaumont had confirmed reports of two unpatched zero-day RCE flaws being exploited in the wild. Tenable's Research Special Operations team set up an FAQ while details were still thin, and some organizations simply took NetScalers offline rather than run them exposed.

Patches and identifiers followed, including CVE-2026-88771, a critical pre-authentication command injection flaw that Picus and others have since documented. Citrix and CISA have urged immediate patching of NetScaler ADC and Gateway. The exploitation is not hypothetical: Binary Defense's incident response team investigated an intrusion involving two NetScaler devices where the zero-days were used to deploy a modified version of the open-source Platypus remote administration framework on compromised appliances.

This mirrors the 2023 CitrixBleed pattern, where slow patching of edge appliances turned into mass compromise. Any internet-facing NetScaler still unpatched should be treated as potentially backdoored, since a Platypus implant persists through later patching. Watch for additional post-exploitation reports from IR firms, CISA KEV movement on the CVEs, and whether the modified Platypus backdoor shows up in other victims.
