Citrix confirmed active exploitation of two NetScaler zero-days and shipped emergency patches after roughly two days of unofficial warnings. CVE-2026-88771 is an improper input validation flaw giving unauthenticated attackers remote command execution on NetScaler ADC and Gateway deployments in their default configuration. CVE-2026-88772 is a memory overflow allowing remote code execution or denial of service where DTLS is enabled, and Citrix notes DTLS is on by default on VPN virtual servers. Both carry a CVSS v4.0 score of 9.5. The Sunday advisory CTX697096 fixed the two exploited flaws plus six additional defects.

The exploitation timeline is what makes this severe. GreyNoise recorded CVE-2026-88771 exploitation attempts on September 24, more than three days before public disclosure. The Dutch NCSC warned organizations about two zero-days allowing shellcode injection into memory before CVEs were assigned, hospitals in the Netherlands closed portals preventively, and enterprises pulled appliances offline before Citrix spoke. CISA added both CVEs to the KEV catalog on September 27 and ordered federal agencies to patch by Wednesday. watchTowr published a PoC repository, and Truesec listed potential C2 IPs including 104.248.244[.]66 and a host under 139.180.x.x. watchTowr founder Ben Harris criticized the information vacuum, noting customers got warnings through unofficial channels while the vendor stayed publicly silent, and the Citrix blog carried a noindex meta tag.

NetScaler appliances sit at the enterprise edge providing VPN, authentication, and application delivery, so compromise yields a foothold between external users and internal infrastructure. Third parties including Rapid7, Truesec, Sophos, Unit 42, Kudelski, Horizon3, and Nextron have shipped detection content, including THOR rules, which defenders should run against appliances they cannot patch immediately. Watch for post-patch incident reports, additional exploited defects beyond the two named CVEs, and whether exploitation shifts to the remaining unpatched NetScaler install base.
