The Citrix NetScaler saga has run through three distinct flaws. WatchTowr's Sina Kheirkhah published a pre-auth RCE writeup on 14 August 2026 for CVE-2026-8452: a heap overflow in the SAML ds:SignedInfo canonicalization path where an oversized InclusiveNamespaces PrefixList is copied into a fixed-size buffer in the nsppe packet engine, giving a write-what-where primitive, RIP control, and a PHP webshell at /var/vpn/theme/x.php on 13.1 before 13.1-63.18. Exploitation requires the appliance to run as a Gateway (SSL VPN, ICA Proxy, CVPN, RDP Proxy) or AAA virtual server. CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772 followed, patched after confirmed exploitation.

The newest flaw, CVE-2026-88779, hit customer-managed NetScaler ADC and Gateway appliances configured for SAML authentication. Citrix rates it CVSS v4.0 8.7 and describes a remotely triggerable memory overflow causing denial of service without authentication. It is nonetheless exploited in targeted attacks: CISA added it to the KEV catalog on October 4, 2026, with a federal remediation deadline of October 7. Patched builds per the Canadian Centre's October 5 advisory AV26-996 are 14.1-73.41, 13.1-64.28, and 13.1-37.282 (FIPS and NDcPP included).

The story's opening act matters for process watchers: a TLP:AMBER NCSC-NL pre-notification leaked into a sysadmin thread roughly ten hours before watchTowr confirmed the rumors publicly, and organizations were already pulling NetScalers offline before Citrix said anything.

Watch for further KEV additions, whether CVE-2026-88779 turns out to be exploitable beyond DoS given it sits in the same SAML attack surface as CVE-2026-8452, and patch uptake, since NetScaler appliances are historically slow to update and heavily targeted by ransomware crews.
