The Citrix NetScaler story tracks multiple critical remote code execution vulnerabilities in NetScaler ADC and Gateway appliances. CVEs cited include CVE-2026-88771 through CVE-2026-88778 and CVE-2026-107406 (CVSS up to 9.5). Confirmed in-the-wild exploitation was reported by watchTowr and others before patches were available; CISA added related flaws to the KEV catalog. Citrix issued bulletin CTX697191 on October 8, 2026, urging immediate upgrades for builds configured as SAML service providers or identity providers. The story spans 79 sources, includes backfill and update events, and remains active because the appliances sit at network perimeter and provide remote-access functionality.
