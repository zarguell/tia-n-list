Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway zero-day remote code execution vulnerabilities CVE-2026-88771 through CVE-2026-88778 were patched after confirmed global exploitation. CISA's September 27 alert confirms active exploitation of CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772, both added to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. Affected versions include NetScaler 14.1 before 14.1-73.37 and 13.1 before 13.1-64.23. watchTowr's FAQ confirms in-the-wild exploitation, lists affected versions, and notes they warned clients of exposure on September 26, before CVE IDs were assigned. The story has broad source coverage and a high score. What to watch: patch deployment timelines, additional CVEs added to KEV, and whether exploitation continues against unpatched appliances.
