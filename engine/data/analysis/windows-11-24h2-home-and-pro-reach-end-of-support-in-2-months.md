Microsoft has warned that Windows 11 24H2 Home and Pro editions will reach end of updates on October 13, 2026. After that date, devices running these editions will no longer receive monthly security patches or non-security preview updates. Windows 11 24H2 Enterprise and Education editions remain under mainstream support until October 2027. Windows 10 Enterprise LTSB 2016 also reaches end of updates on the same date.

For unmanaged Home and Pro devices, Microsoft will automatically upgrade to Windows 11 25H2, though users can postpone the restart. Managed devices require IT administrators to plan the transition. The 25H2 release, also called the Windows 11 2025 Update, became generally available in September 2024 as a minor update installed through an enablement package. Organizations should verify application compatibility before deploying 25H2 at scale.

The security implications are straightforward: after October 13, any newly discovered vulnerability in Windows 11 24H2 Home and Pro will remain unpatched. This creates a growing window of exposure as threat actors accumulate knowledge of unpatched flaws. Organizations running these editions should treat the upgrade to 25H2 or Enterprise/Education editions as a priority remediation task, not a routine lifecycle event.
