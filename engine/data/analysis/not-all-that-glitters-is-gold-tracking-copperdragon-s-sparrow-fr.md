Check Point Research says a year-long investigation linked three operational clusters, SilentDelivery, OceanGate and DarkWire, to a shared Sparrow malware framework it tracks as CopperDragon. The clusters span Latin America, Asia and Europe and target telecommunications providers, government organizations, military entities, research institutions and critical infrastructure. Check Point describes CopperDragon as a potential digital quartermaster supporting China-nexus threat actors including FamousSparrow, Earth Estries, TropicTrooper and UAT-9244.

Each cluster used a different toolset and initial access method. The reported techniques include compromising internet-facing infrastructure and highly selective watering-hole attacks; the compromised sites belonged to government, shipping, IT and research sectors. The research also uncovered previously undocumented tools and new details about Sparrow, including Windows and Linux variants, passive backdoor capabilities and a connection to the Melofee backdoor.

The framework's breadth and the watering-hole targeting of government and research sites make this a notable China-nexus espionage operation. Organizations in the named sectors should review exposure to the Sparrow indicators and treat the three clusters as linked rather than separate campaigns.
