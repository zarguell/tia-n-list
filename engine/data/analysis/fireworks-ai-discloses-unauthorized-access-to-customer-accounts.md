Fireworks AI, a cloud platform for running and fine-tuning open-source AI models, disclosed unauthorized access to some customer accounts, and one of its customers, the productivity vendor Notion, has begun notifying affected users. Fireworks says it detected unauthorized access on October 6, 2026 and again across October 24 to 25. The incidents hit in two different ways: the first through compromised internal credentials, and the second through a third-party cloud provider that hosted Fireworks research and development machines. In some accounts the attackers reached customer billing data and unfinished model weights. Fireworks contained the second incident the same day.

The second intrusion, on October 24, is tied by the reporting to a recent rise in LLMJacking, the abuse of stolen inference access to run unauthorized AI workloads on a victim's account. The attackers executed an unauthorized workload that led to the compromise of customer data. Fireworks notified Notion on November 2, 2026 that Notion AI data had been accessed. Notion had used Fireworks as a general inference provider to deliver open-weight models before the incident.

Notion's own investigation found that the exposed material was Notion AI chat transcripts used by its Insights feature, which helps customers understand their AI usage. Fewer than 1% of Notion workspaces were affected, and most of the transcripts could not be tied back to a specific customer or workspace. Notion says that beyond the contents of a transcript there is no information linking a chat to a workspace, user, or company, and it has contacted affected customers with instructions for retrieving and reviewing the data.

What to watch is scope beyond Notion: Fireworks hosts many AI builders, so the compromised billing data and unfinished model weights in other accounts matter as much as the Notion transcripts. Track whether Fireworks attributes the credential compromise and the LLMJacking-linked workload abuse to a single actor, whether the third-party cloud provider is named, and whether other Fireworks customers disclose downstream exposure of model weights or billing records.
