TITAN is a ransomware-as-a-service operation founded on April 4, 2026, that has been active since May and lists 24 alleged victims across 10 countries. Italy accounts for 10 of those victims, followed by the Czech Republic with four and the United States with three. Manufacturing and professional services each represent roughly 29% of victims, suggesting opportunistic, access-driven targeting rather than a specific industry focus.

The group is promoting an on-premises AI platform it claims can automatically classify stolen files, identify valuable business information, calculate regulatory exposure, and generate notification packages for regulators and media organizations. These capabilities have not been independently verified, but the marketing reflects a broader trend of ransomware groups automating data-driven extortion to increase pressure on victims during double-extortion campaigns.

TITAN operates a verification-gated affiliate program requiring criminal-history checks, technical assessments, and reviews of prior intrusion experience. Prospective affiliates must also pay a non-refundable registration fee. The group offers affiliates 90% of ransom payments while retaining 10%. The combination of AI-powered data classification and a structured affiliate program indicates a well-organized operation despite its recent founding. Organizations should monitor for TITAN indicators and ensure backups are isolated from the network.
