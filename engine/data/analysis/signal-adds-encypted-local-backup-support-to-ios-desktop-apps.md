Signal released version 8.30, completing its encrypted backup rollout across Android, iOS, Linux, macOS, and Windows. The feature, first launched on Android about a year ago, supports both Signal-hosted backups (45 days of media and 128 KB per message for free users; 100 GB in paid plans) and local on-device backups with no size limits. Both options encrypt backups with a recovery key, and hosted backups rotate an additional key daily inside a Trusted Execution Environment for forward secrecy. The update brings on-device backups to iOS and desktop, unifies Android backups to the same cross-platform format, stores media separately from the archive, and removes duplicate files. Signal notes threat actors have already incorporated the backup recovery key into targeting scopes shortly after the feature's introduction. The update also lets linked devices retrieve older attachments missing from chat history.
