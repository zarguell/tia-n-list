Cisco Talos has documented Antino, a previously undocumented backdoor used by a China-nexus actor against government and policy organizations in Taiwan. The write-up circulated via The Hacker News on October 2, 2026, and the coverage indicates the infection chain relies on Outlook as part of its delivery or command traffic.

Targeting of government and policy bodies in Taiwan is consistent with long-running Beijing-aligned espionage priorities, and a previously undocumented backdoor in that ecosystem is worth tracking. Talos naming it means defenders get indicators and detection hooks they did not have last week.

Worth watching: whether additional victims outside Taiwan surface, whether Talos or others publish IOCs and YARA rules, and how the Outlook component of the chain is abused, since mail-adjacent tradecraft often evades controls that trust the mail client.
