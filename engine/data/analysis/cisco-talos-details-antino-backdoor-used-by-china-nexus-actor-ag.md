Cisco Talos documented Antino, a previously undocumented backdoor used by a China-nexus actor (UAT-11587) against government and policy organizations in Taiwan, India, and the Philippines. The malware hides C2 traffic inside Outlook mail and OneDrive files, making it blend with normal Microsoft Graph activity. This is primary reporting with no CVE yet assigned. Watch for IOCs shared by Talos and any vendor advisories that name related infrastructure.
