Cisco Talos documented Antino, an undocumented backdoor used by a China-linked actor against government and policy organizations in Taiwan, Indonesia, and other regions. The malware hides command-and-control traffic inside Outlook mail and OneDrive files, making it resemble normal Microsoft Graph activity. A secondary aggregator repost noted 9 detections and 27 IOCs, but that count is unverified because the aggregator is low-trust. The primary source is the Talos report linked by the event. Watch for updates on detection rules and any attribution refinement linking Antino to a named group.
