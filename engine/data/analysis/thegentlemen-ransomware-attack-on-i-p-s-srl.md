The ransomware group TheGentlemen listed seven new victims on its leak site between August 13 and 14, 2026, across five countries. The targets are I.P.S. Srl (recycling and environmental services, Italy, ipssrl.com), ACLI (Italian Catholic workers association, acli.it), Avanta Maroc Ex Adecco (HR and recruitment, Morocco, avanta.ma), Vector Two Technology (Brazil, vtt.com.br), Plaza Auto Mall (Brooklyn car dealership, USA), KFC Kosova (fast food, kfckosova.com), and Safeware Inc. (U.S. safety and security solutions provider, safewareinc.com). Each entry follows the same template: a claim of intrusion plus a threat to publish the full leak unless a company representative opens negotiations through the group's channels.

The breadth matters more than any single victim. These are mostly mid-sized companies in non-IT verticals, which suggests opportunistic access rather than a targeted campaign, and the group is posting new victims roughly every few minutes in the same leak-site batch. Safeware Inc. stands out because of its customer base: first responders, schools, and government agencies, which raises the stakes if the claimed data includes their records. KFC Kosova is the highest-profile brand name in the set.

Watch whether any victim confirms an intrusion or discloses what data was taken, and whether the leak site actually publishes files for any of the seven. None of the claims are independently verified yet, so treat each as an allegation. TheGentlemen has been adding victims steadily; a larger pattern of these batch listings across this week would point to a sustained operation rather than a one-off burst.
