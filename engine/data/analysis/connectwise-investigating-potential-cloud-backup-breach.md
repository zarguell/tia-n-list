ConnectWise is investigating unauthorized access attempts tied to its Cloud Backup environment, formerly SkyKick. A compromised application identity allowed threat actors to sign into 173 Microsoft 365 tenants. Observers reported rapid tenant connections and exfiltration of email, Teams and SharePoint data.

A third-party vendor confirmed the compromise and recommended disabling the application. The incident highlights supply-chain exposure through cloud backup credentials rather than endpoint exploitation. Watch for the final incident report and any confirmed victim counts.
