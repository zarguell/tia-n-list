ConnectWise is investigating unauthorized access to its Cloud Backup platform (formerly SkyKick). Third-party sources claim a threat actor pivoted from the backup environment into live Microsoft 365 environments of MSP customers, which could expose EntraID data. ConnectWise has not confirmed a successful breach. Because Cloud Backup serves MSP-managed O365 tenants, a confirmed intrusion would cascade widely. Track the vendor status page and MSP incident reports for confirmation.
