ConnectWise is investigating unauthorized access attempts against its Cloud Backup platform (formerly SkyKick), which MSPs use to back up Microsoft 365 environments including EntraID. A third-party vendor confirmed compromise and recommended disabling the application, while MSP observations report rapid tenant connections, data exfiltration of email, Teams and SharePoint, and pivoting into live O365 tenants.

The original event notes ConnectWise has not confirmed successful intrusion, but third-party intelligence contradicts that. The updated social posts describe attackers abusing a compromised ConnectWise Cloud Backup application to expose M365 tenants. The score is 7.4. Watch for ConnectWise status updates, MSP customer advisories, and confirmation of whether data files or tenant environments were actually accessed.
