ConnectWise is investigating unauthorized access to its Cloud Backup platform, previously SkyKick. A status advisory from October 16, 2026, notes MSP observations of rapid tenant connections, data exfiltration from email, Teams, and SharePoint, and a third-party vendor confirming compromise. The advisory recommends disabling the application. No confirmed breach of customer data has been declared by ConnectWise itself, but third-party reports describe pivoting into live Microsoft 365 tenant environments.

Because Cloud Backup serves as a backup bridge for MSP-managed Office 365 environments including EntraID, a confirmed compromise could expose broad tenant data. Watch for a final incident report from ConnectWise, any CISA advisory, and confirmation of the vendor's findings regarding data exfiltration scope.
