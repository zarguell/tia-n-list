ConnectWise is investigating unauthorized access to its Cloud Backup platform, formerly SkyKick. The status page confirms an investigation but has not confirmed successful intrusion. Third-party reports claim a stolen vendor credential opened 173 Microsoft 365 tenant environments last week. The platform is used by MSPs to back up full Office 365 environments including Entra ID, so any confirmed access would have broad downstream impact. The event adds to growing concern about supply-chain exposure through cloud-backup vendors. Watch for an official confirmation from ConnectWise and any CISA or vendor advisory that validates the reported tenant exposure.
