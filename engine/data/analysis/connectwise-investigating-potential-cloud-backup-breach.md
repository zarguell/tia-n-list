ConnectWise is investigating unauthorized access to its Cloud Backup environment (formerly SkyKick), used by MSPs to back up Microsoft 365 environments including Entra ID. Third-party reports describe abuse of a compromised application identity that allowed sign-ins across 162 to 173 customer tenants, with observations of rapid tenant connections and data exfiltration from email, Teams, and SharePoint. ConnectWise published an advisory on October 16 and shut off application access to interrupt backups. The incident highlights supply-chain risk where a single vendor credential collapses multi-tenant trust boundaries.
