ConnectWise suspended its Cloud Backup service after attackers compromised Microsoft 365 tenants. The update links to The Hacker News coverage of the breach. The story carries 11 sources and a score of 7.2, with multiple updates tracking incident response.

This breach is significant because cloud backup services are trusted infrastructure, and compromise here can cascade into customer environments. The suspension indicates active containment rather than routine maintenance. Watch for ConnectWise status updates and any attribution or victim disclosure.
