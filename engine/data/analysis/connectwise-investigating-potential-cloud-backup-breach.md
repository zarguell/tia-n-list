ConnectWise is investigating unauthorized access to its Cloud Backup environment (formerly SkyKick). The advisory, published October 16 at 1:47 p.m. ET, reports rapid tenant connections and data exfiltration of email, Teams, and SharePoint content. A third-party vendor confirmed compromise and recommended disabling the application. Since then, updates note ConnectWise disabled 7 application identities and suspended Cloud Backup. The breach affects Microsoft 365 tenants through compromised MSP connections. No CVE is assigned, but the incident carries supply-chain risk because attackers moved through application identities rather than tenant credentials directly. The score is 7.4 with 12 sources, reflecting sustained coverage.

What to watch: whether ConnectWise releases a full incident timeline, whether any affected MSP clients confirm data loss, and whether Microsoft issues guidance for tenants that connected through the compromised backup service. The absence of a CVE or KEV entry does not diminish the operational impact for managed service providers relying on the backup pipeline.
