ConnectWise is investigating unauthorized access to its Cloud Backup platform, formerly SkyKick. The company posted to its status page that it has not confirmed a successful intrusion, but third-party reports say the actor pivoted into customers' live Microsoft 365 environments. Cloud Backup is used by MSPs for O365 and Entra ID, so a confirmed breach would expose backup and identity data broadly. Watch for a confirmed impact statement from ConnectWise and any CISA notification.
