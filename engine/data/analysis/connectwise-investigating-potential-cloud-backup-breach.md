ConnectWise is investigating unauthorized access to its Cloud Backup environment, formerly SkyKick, after attackers abused compromised application credentials to reach Microsoft 365 customer tenants. The incident began on 16 October 2026, with ConnectWise posting its advisory at 17:47 UTC, and third-party observers reported rapid tenant connections and data exfiltration of email, Teams, and SharePoint content. Huntress and Guardz published analyses of the credential abuse in MSP environments, and reporting cited access to between 162 and 173 customer tenants.

ConnectWise shut off application access and interrupted backup services in response, halting cloud backups for affected customers. The exposure is significant because Cloud Backup holds privileged access to entire Microsoft 365 tenants, including Entra ID, email, Teams, and SharePoint data for managed service provider clients. The root cause points to compromised vendor credentials rather than a customer-side misconfiguration.

MSPs and their customers should assume backup data and tenant contents may have been accessed, rotate any credentials or tokens tied to the Cloud Backup application, review audit logs for unauthorized sign-ins, and confirm restoration capability before re-enabling the service. Monitoring for downstream phishing or business email compromise using exfiltrated data is prudent.
