ConnectWise is investigating unauthorized access to its Cloud Backup platform (formerly SkyKick), used by MSPs to back up Microsoft 365 and Entra ID environments. The company posted on its status page that it has not confirmed a successful breach, but third-party sources report that a threat actor compromised vendor credentials and pivoted to live M365 tenants of MSP customers. A Mastodon update links to a ConnectWise advisory. No CVE has been assigned.

The risk is structural: backup platforms with broad M365 permissions are high-value pivot points. Even unconfirmed access warrants attention because a compromise could expose EntraID and mailbox data across multiple downstream customers. Watch for a definitive ConnectWise post-incident report, victim disclosures, or CISA guidance.
