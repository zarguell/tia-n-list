ConnectWise is investigating a potential cloud backup breach that has led to Microsoft 365 tenant compromises via supply-chain attack. BleepingComputer reports hackers breaching M365 tenants through ConnectWise. The story has 9 sources including the vendor status page and coverage from Huntress, Blumira, and Guardz. No CVEs are assigned; the issue is operational, not a product vulnerability.

What matters: supply-chain attacks through managed service platforms remain a high-impact vector. Organizations using ConnectWise cloud backup services should review access logs and rotate credentials. Watch for official vendor disclosure and any confirmed data exfiltration details.
