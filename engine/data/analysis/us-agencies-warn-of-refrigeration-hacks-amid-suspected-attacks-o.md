The refrigeration intrusions were a physical-damage campaign against connected temperature controllers. Around 700 French supermarkets were hit on 11 October 2026, with units driven to 50 degrees Celsius and freezers forced into defrost, spoiling food and in some cases causing fires. A second wave on 2 November reached more than 100 stores across Belgium, Ireland, Luxembourg, Sweden and other countries; an ICA store in Sjoebo had fridges and freezers set to 50 degrees Celsius. France's blood service said control systems at three cold-storage sites were altered before staff intervened, and three UK meat suppliers were hit through Copeland XWEB controllers. Earlier, freezers at roughly six US military commissaries had failed overnight starting in late August, which officials treated as possible cyberattacks.

On 5 November CISA, the FBI, HHS, USDA and the Department of Transportation issued joint guidance for healthcare, chemical, food and transportation operators. The agencies said attackers were exploiting unpatched or internet-accessible electronic temperature controllers and commercial refrigeration management systems, using authentication bypasses, code injection and weak configurations to push units into continuous heating or defrost cycles. Reuters found at least 4,500 internet-exposed Danfoss interfaces. Danfoss had told Dutch customers on 13 October to disconnect systems from the public internet, and later said remaining victims still used unsecured connections and default passwords. The warning followed millions in losses in Europe and a French blood-bank near miss.

On 25 November CISA added Danfoss AK-SM 800A CVE-2025-41450, an improper authentication flaw rated high at 8.2, and CVE-2023-25915, an improper input validation flaw, to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. The story also tracks CVE-2025-41451 and CVE-2025-41452 from the same controller line. Attribution remains contested: Russian hacktivist names Noname057(16) and Z-Pentest Alliance were claimed, the Kremlin denied involvement, and US and French officials pointed toward Iranian actors. Watch for KEV-driven patch deadlines, fresh exposure counts, and any technical evidence that settles which actor ran the waves.
