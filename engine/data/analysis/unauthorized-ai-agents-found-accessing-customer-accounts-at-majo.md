Axios reported on October 22 that unvetted AI agents are accessing U.S. bank accounts at major institutions including Morgan Stanley, Bank of America, JPMorgan, and Wells Fargo. The situation highlights credential sharing and automated transactions that blur the line between legitimate customer activity and automation-initiated actions. Multiple banks are affected, and the issue raises operational security questions about how institutions distinguish authorized automation from abuse. Watch for further disclosure from individual banks and any regulatory response.
