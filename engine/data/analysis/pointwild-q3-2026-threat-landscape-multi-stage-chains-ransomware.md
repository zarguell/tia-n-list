PointWild's Q3 2026 threat-intelligence report highlights multi-stage attack chains, a ransomware surge, and abuse of legitimate system functionality. It references CVE-2026-20079 and CVE-2026-58644, linking the observations to known vulnerability clusters used in active campaigns.

The abuse of legitimate functionality reduces detection opportunity for signature-based controls.
