Suprema BioStar 2, a physical access control system used in buildings worldwide, leaks Active Directory service-account passwords in cleartext through its API. CVE-2026-31278 affects the /api/v2/setting/adserversetting endpoint in BioStar 2 versions before 2.9.12 and BioStar X. Any authenticated operator can retrieve the AD credentials stored in the system, and those credentials often belong to service accounts with broad directory permissions.

The vulnerability scored 7.7 (High) because exploitation requires only a low-privilege BioStar operator login, but the blast radius extends into the customer's Active Directory environment. Researchers at Suriq.io published a proof of concept, and multiple advisory outlets (TheHackerWire, Yazoul) confirmed the issue. Patches are available in BioStar 2 2.9.12 and BioStar X updates.

This matters because physical access control systems sit at the intersection of IT and OT networks. A compromised BioStar deployment can expose AD credentials that grant lateral movement into corporate infrastructure. Organizations running BioStar 2 should patch immediately and audit whether their AD service accounts follow least-privilege principles.
