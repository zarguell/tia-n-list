Norway's development aid agency Norad was hit by a cyberattack on November 3. The perpetrators may have accessed the names and email addresses of 20,614 registered users of Norad's Grant Portal, and they extracted information tied to grant applications from at least three organizations. Norad said the intrusion came through compromised accounts and disclosed the incident on November 7, noting it was still assessing the full scope.

The story matters because a grant portal holds contact details and application data that can feed targeted phishing against grantees and staff. Aid agencies are also a sensitive constituency, so any application-related data taken beyond names and emails could be used for convincing social engineering. Watch for Norad's full disclosure, for what application data was actually extracted, and for whether the compromised accounts link to a wider campaign against aid organizations.
