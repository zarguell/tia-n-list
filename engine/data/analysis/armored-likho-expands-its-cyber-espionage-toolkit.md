Armored Likho, also tracked as Eagle Werewolf, is running a cyber-espionage campaign active since May 2026 against private individuals and organizations in Russia across major corporations, the public sector, IT, and education. The initial infection uses a fake app that mimics a donation service as bait. The group's implant arsenal has expanded significantly compared with earlier activity, and the current campaign shares more characteristics with the group's February operations than with its recent attacks.

The expansion of the toolset is the headline: a dedicated espionage group with a working lure is growing its capability set. Organizations with Russian exposure, or that track APT groups targeting that region, should monitor the new implants and the donation-app lure pattern.
