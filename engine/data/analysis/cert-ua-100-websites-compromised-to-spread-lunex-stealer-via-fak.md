CERT-UA tracks UAC-0277, a campaign in which attackers compromised over 100 legitimate Ukrainian websites to distribute Lunex Stealer through ClickFix social-engineering traps. Victims are shown a fake Cloudflare verification page and tricked into running a PowerShell command that installs the stealer. The malware harvests passwords, tokens, and cryptocurrency wallets, and also deploys the LunarAxe browser extension to steal cookies, browse directories, and execute remote commands. CERT-UA links the operation to a relatively new malware-as-a-service platform; Ontinue previously documented similar activity targeting Ukrainian users in early September.
