Gyazo, the widely used screenshot and image sharing tool, suffered a breach exposing 23.62 million user records. The Hacker News reported the incident, which saw the company's user database exfiltrated. The breach was disclosed after the data appeared on underground forums.

The exposed records include user account details. Gyazo has not publicly detailed the attack vector or provided a timeline for notification of affected users. The scale, 23.62 million records, is significant given Gyazo's popularity among developers, designers, and gamers who use it for quick screenshot sharing.

This is a straightforward credential and PII exposure event. Users who created Gyazo accounts should assume their associated email addresses and any reused passwords are compromised. The incident adds to the growing list of SaaS platform breaches where relatively simple tools with large user bases become high-value targets for credential harvesting.
