Roman Khlynovskiy, a 43-year-old Kazakh national, made his initial appearance in federal court in Knoxville after extradition from Italy. Prosecutors allege he was a member of the 8-Digits Team, a group affiliated with the INC and Lynx ransomware operations. He faces charges of conspiracy to commit computer fraud, identity theft, wire fraud, extortion, money laundering and threats to disclose non-consensual intimate images, with a maximum sentence of 27 years if convicted. The Justice Department credits Italian, Ukrainian and Lithuanian authorities with assisting the arrest and extradition.

The indictment describes victims in at least six US states, including schools, hospitals and other healthcare entities. Prosecutors say the conspirators exfiltrated data and threatened to publish it to force payment. DOJ quotes them claiming more than 50 terabytes of data and over 70 million confidential health records, with more than $500 million in ransom demands. INC Ransom began operating as a ransomware-as-a-service in July 2023 and its leak site lists over 1,000 victims across education, healthcare, government and industrial sectors.

The scale of the claimed medical data theft is the substantive part of this case, not the extradition itself. Healthcare and school networks remain the pressure targets for INC and Lynx affiliates, and the leak-site count shows how broad the affiliate footprint is. Watch the court timeline for guilty pleas or trial dates, plus any disruption to INC or Lynx infrastructure that follows the arrest.
