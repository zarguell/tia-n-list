Bulgaria disclosed on November 18 a five-year cyberespionage operation against state institutions, with at least 130 GB of government documents exfiltrated, more than 62 million pages. Innovation and Digital Transformation Minister Ivan Vasilev described it as the longest-running known espionage operation against Bulgarian state institutions, and reporting attributes the activity to international hacking groups. A later Hungarian report puts the start of the operation in 2021.

The available accounts agree on the scale and the discovery date but give limited detail on the actors or the exact access path. What matters for assessment is the combination of long dwell time and bulk document access: a five-year window means a large share of the material may already be stale, but it also implies sustained access to systems that held sensitive government records.

Watch for follow-up from Bulgarian authorities on entry vector, affected agencies and any attribution, and for the publication of indicators or a formal incident report. Repeats of the 130 GB and 62 million page figures across outlets trace back to the same ministerial disclosure rather than independent measurements.
