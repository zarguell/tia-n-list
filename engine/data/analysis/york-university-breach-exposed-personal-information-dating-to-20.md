York University in Toronto confirmed that a threat actor breached its systems and accessed personal information submitted through online forms. The unauthorized access was discovered on October 25 and affected forms on 14 university websites spanning 2007 to 2026. The university, which counts more than 55,700 students and 7,000 administrative staff, said it contained the incident quickly after discovery but confirmed theft of personal information from some departmental forms.

Most stolen records contained basic contact information and York ID numbers, though some individuals had government ID or financial account information exposed. York said it will directly notify those individuals and offer two years of credit monitoring and identity-theft protection. The university stated the affected website forms are now secure, and warned that exposed contact data raises phishing and social-engineering risk for affected people.
