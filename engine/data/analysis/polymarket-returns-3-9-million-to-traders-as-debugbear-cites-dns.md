Polymarket is returning $3.9 million to traders after a supply-chain attack that injected malicious JavaScript via a compromised third-party provider, DebugBear. The script targeted logged-in users on the international platform, checking portfolios and either dumping positions or transferring assets to an attacker-controlled wallet. Hudson Rock linked the activity to code served from a DebugBear address loaded by Polymarket's site.

The breach affected 726 wallets directly; Polymarket reported that all but 37 had been made whole by October 30, with the remainder being paid after verification. In addition to roughly $2.7 million stolen, the firm is covering $1.19 million in losses across about 5,500 wallets affected by trading halts or forced transfers, bringing total payouts to $3.9 million. The attack relied on open session permissions rather than extracting private keys or seed phrases.
