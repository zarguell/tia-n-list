Hackers compromised emergency alert applications in both Ukraine and Russia, pushing fabricated nuclear and radiation warnings to users through trusted civilian warning channels. Ukraine reported interference late on November 1, 2026, and Russia followed hours later by announcing a "massive cyberattack" against its emergency ministry's mobile application. Officials in both countries sought to reassure residents that no corresponding emergency existed.

The incidents targeted air-raid alert apps and the Russian Emergencies Ministry app, distributing fake nuclear attack warnings via push notifications. Coverage came from GBHackers, LoopWire, and Reddit discussions in r/cybersecurity and r/netsec, with no named threat actor and no technical entry point disclosed in the available reporting.

The concrete artifact to track is the pair of compromised alert apps themselves. Watch for any official statement naming the vulnerability or the responsible group, and for follow-up on whether the same actor hit both countries' systems or whether the incidents were separate.
