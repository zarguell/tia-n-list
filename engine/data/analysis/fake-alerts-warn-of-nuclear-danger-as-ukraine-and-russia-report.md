Hackers compromised emergency alert mobile applications in Ukraine and Russia, pushing fabricated nuclear and radiation warnings to civilians. Ukraine reported interference late on November 1, 2026, and Russia's emergency ministry announced a "massive cyberattack" against its mobile app hours later. Residents in Ukraine received false warnings of an imminent nuclear threat through the air-raid alert app they rely on for safety guidance. Authorities in both countries sought to reassure residents that no corresponding emergency existed.

It remains unclear whether the two incidents are related, and neither side has publicly identified the perpetrator or the technical entry point. The timing coincided with the end of a limited ceasefire. The attacks illustrate how compromise of trusted civilian warning systems can amplify fear and disruption during an active conflict.
