Microsoft Threat Intelligence attributes the new StormEncryptor ransomware family to Storm-1175, a financially motivated actor believed to be China-based and previously linked to the Medusa ransomware operation. The recent intrusions were likely preceded by exploitation of CVE-2026-18577, the N-able N-central authentication bypass that CISA added to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog this month.

StormEncryptor is the actor's third ransomware strain in recent months, and Storm-1175 has a documented history of weaponizing zero-days and n-days across GoAnywhere MFT, SmarterTools SmarterMail, Microsoft Exchange, Ivanti Connect Secure, and JetBrains TeamCity. RMM appliances are the weak link here: an unpatched N-central instance should be treated as fully compromised and hunted for StormEncryptor artifacts, because the appliance itself grants broad remote access to every managed endpoint.
