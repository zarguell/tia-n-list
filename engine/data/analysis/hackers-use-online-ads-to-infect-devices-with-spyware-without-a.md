Citizen Lab has confirmed AdFection, the first public case of zero-click mercenary spyware delivered through targeted online ads. The campaign used ads bought by Israeli firm Discover-Tech to deliver Candiru spyware (rebranded Integrity Labs) to devices at Al Jazeera. Multiple outlets including The Guardian, Ars Technica, and GBHackers have covered the finding. The attack did not require user interaction, relying on real-time ad auctions to profile targets and deliver exploits.

The story matters because it turns the advertising supply chain into a direct spyware delivery vector, removing the phishing step that usually protects targets. Defenders should treat targeted ad networks as high-risk surfaces for high-value users, not just nuisance channels. Watch for vendor responses from Google regarding Discover-Tech's access and any additional targets beyond Al Jazeera.
