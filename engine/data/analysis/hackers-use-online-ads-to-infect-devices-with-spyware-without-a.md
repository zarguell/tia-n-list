Citizen Lab confirmed the first public case of zero-click mercenary spyware delivered through targeted online ads, calling the technique AdFection. The attack hit Al Jazeera devices with Candiru spyware; ads were bought through an Israeli ad-tech firm named Discover-Tech. The zero-click delivery removes the user-interaction barrier that traditionally limits mercenary spyware campaigns.

This is a significant development in commercial surveillance: targeted advertising infrastructure is now a direct infection vector. Watch for additional victim reporting from Citizen Lab and whether ad networks implement new verification controls. The new event attaches correctly to the existing AdFection cluster.
