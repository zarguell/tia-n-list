Citizen Lab documented AdFection, the first public case of zero-click mercenary spyware delivered through targeted online ads. The operation hit Al Jazeera devices with Candiru spyware, using ads bought by the Israeli ad-tech firm Discover-Tech. The ads exploited real-time auction mechanics, requiring no user click to deliver payloads.

This demonstrates a new delivery path for commercial spyware, moving beyond traditional phishing or drive-by downloads. Monitor for additional targets and vendor responses that could confirm broader campaigns.
