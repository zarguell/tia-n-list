Citizen Lab confirmed the first public case of zero-click mercenary spyware delivered through targeted online advertisements, naming the technique AdFection. The attack targeted Al Jazeera with Candiru spyware; the malicious ads were purchased by Israeli ad-tech firm Discover-Tech through its Controlium platform. Researchers say Discover-Tech acts as a bridge between mercenary spyware vendors and the advertising ecosystem, using mainstream ad networks to deliver payloads that require no user interaction beyond rendering.

This matters because it shows the ad auction pipeline being weaponized for surveillance, not just tracking. Previous similar attacks included Amnesty International's 2025 Aladdin case linked to Intellexa and Predator spyware; AdFection extends that model with a confirmed operational campaign against journalists. Watch for whether Discover-Tech's customers include other spyware vendors, and whether ad exchanges take action against the platform accounts used.
