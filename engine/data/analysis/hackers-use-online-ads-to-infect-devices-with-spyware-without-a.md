Citizen Lab confirmed "AdFection," the first public zero-click mercenary spyware delivered via targeted online ads. The campaign hit Al Jazeera devices with Candiru spyware, and the ads were purchased through an Israeli ad-tech firm called Discover-Tech. The real-time auction model makes attribution and prevention harder.

This is a technique confirmation, not a broad campaign count. The substance is the proof of concept: ads as a zero-click delivery vector for mercenary spyware. Watch for additional researchers naming other victims or vendors patching related ad-auction exposure.
