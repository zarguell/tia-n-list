Citizen Lab confirmed the first public zero-click mercenary spyware campaign delivered through targeted online ads, calling the technique AdFection. The attack used Candiru spyware against devices at Al Jazeera, with ads purchased through an Israeli ad-tech firm named Discover-Tech. It is a concrete espionage incident, not a theoretical vulnerability, but it carries no CVE or CVSS score. The story has eight sources spanning GBHackers, Citizen Lab, The Guardian, Ars Technica, and others. Watch for follow-up attribution of the ad-tech intermediary and whether any targets file legal action.
