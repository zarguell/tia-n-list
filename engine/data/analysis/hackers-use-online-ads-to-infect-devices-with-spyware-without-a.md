Citizen Lab confirmed the first public case of zero-click mercenary spyware delivered through targeted online ads, naming the technique AdFection. The attack hit devices at Al Jazeera with Candiru spyware; the ads were bought by an Israeli ad-tech firm called Discover-Tech. The campaign exploits real-time ad auctions to deliver spyware with no user interaction, expanding the mercenary spyware delivery surface beyond traditional phishing and watering-hole vectors. The story has 15 sources, no assigned CVEs, a score of 6.9, and no CISA KEV flag. Watch for additional victim reports beyond Al Jazeera and any vendor or platform-level ad-auction mitigations.
