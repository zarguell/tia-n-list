The FortiBleed campaign continues to target internet-accessible Fortinet FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN appliances. SOCRadar confirmed approximately 86,644 compromised devices in 194 countries. A joint FBI/USSS advisory warns attackers lock organizations out by changing passwords and deleting accounts. The campaign started in June using previously compromised credentials and brute-force. The event includes a Daily Briefing reference and confirms no patch alone stops the access-broker activity; credential hygiene and account monitoring are critical.
