FortiBleed remains an active credential-theft campaign against Fortinet devices. A joint FBI and Secret Service advisory this week warns that attackers are locking organizations out of their FortiGate appliances by changing passwords and deleting accounts. SOCRadar reports approximately 86,644 confirmed-compromised devices across 194 countries. The campaign started in June, uses previously compromised credentials and brute-force techniques, and targets internet-accessible SSL VPN appliances. Victims should audit device accounts and assume persistent access until full remediation is completed.
