FortiBleed remains active. Attackers are locking organizations out of Fortinet FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN appliances by changing passwords and deleting accounts. A joint FBI and US Secret Service advisory notes confirmed compromises of approximately 86,644 devices across 194 countries, with some victims locked out for months. The campaign uses previously compromised credentials and brute-force techniques.

Why it matters: This is not only a credential-harvesting operation but now an active lockout threat. Organizations with exposed Fortinet appliances should audit account integrity immediately. Watch for updated FBI indicators and whether Fortinet releases new defensive guidance.
