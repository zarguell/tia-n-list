FortiBleed remains active. SOCRadar confirmed about 86,644 compromised Fortinet devices in 194 countries. Attackers use previously stolen credentials and brute force against internet-accessible FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN appliances. A joint FBI and US Secret Service advisory warns attackers lock victims out by changing passwords and deleting accounts. Some victims may permanently lose device access if accounts are removed. Watch for expanded lockout tactics and further device counts.
