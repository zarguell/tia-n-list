The FortiBleed campaign is still active, with attackers locking organizations out of Fortinet FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN appliances. The campaign started in June and has hit over 86,000 devices across 190 countries, with SOCRadar confirming approximately 86,644 compromised devices in 194 countries. A joint FBI and US Secret Service advisory warns that hackers are deleting accounts and changing passwords to block victim access. The attackers rely on previously compromised credentials and brute-force techniques rather than new zero-days, which means strong credential hygiene and patching remain the primary defenses. Watch for additional government advisories and victim notifications as the confirmed inventory grows.
