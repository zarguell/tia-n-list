The FortiBleed campaign remains active, with attackers now locking organizations out of Fortinet devices by changing passwords and deleting accounts. A joint FBI and US Secret Service advisory confirms the behavior. SOCRadar counts approximately 86,644 confirmed-compromised devices across 194 countries, emphasizing that breached devices stay in the actors' inventory for months. The campaign uses previously compromised credentials and brute-force against internet-accessible FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN appliances. Watch for new victim reports and check whether affected organizations can recover access without full appliance rebuilds.
