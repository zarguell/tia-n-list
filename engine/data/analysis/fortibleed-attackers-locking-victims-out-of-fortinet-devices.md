The FortiBleed campaign remains active and has escalated to locking victims out of their Fortinet appliances. SOCRadar confirmed approximately 86,644 compromised devices across 194 countries, emphasizing that these are confirmed breaches rather than exposure estimates, with devices breached months ago still in the attackers' inventory. A joint FBI and US Secret Service advisory released this week warns that attackers are changing passwords and deleting accounts to block organizational access. The campaign started in June, targeting internet-accessible FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN appliances, and relies on previously compromised credentials and brute-force against poorly protected devices. CVE-2025-24472 is tied to the advisory. Over 86,000 devices in 190 countries were hit within the first week. The attackers have been linked to a Russian initial access broker. Remediation requires patching, credential rotation, and device auditing beyond simple patching, since attackers who have established accounts can maintain persistence.
