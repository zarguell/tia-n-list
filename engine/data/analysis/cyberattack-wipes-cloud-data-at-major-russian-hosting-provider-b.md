A cyberattack destroyed data across the cloud infrastructure of Beget, a major Russian web-hosting provider and domain registrar serving more than 200,000 customers including online stores, media, and educational services. On the night of November 1, an attacker gained access to the management layer of Beget's entire architecture and deployed malware that erased server data. Beget detected the attack shortly after the malware activated and disconnected all servers from the network to stop it from spreading.

More than a week later Beget said 60 percent of shared hosting and 40 percent of dedicated servers had been restored, with recovery still in progress. The company said all data in its cloud infrastructure was lost, including VPS, S3, and Kubernetes environments along with CDN settings and IP configurations. VPS binary backups were completely lost and VPS file backups only partially survived, available for roughly half of VPS users according to a November 6 assessment. Beget stated that it is impossible to recover all data.
