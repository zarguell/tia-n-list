Lithuanian Customs confirmed a cyberattack on November 10 that forced its main user management portal, BAP, offline. The Record reported restoration work over the weekend and a later update said services had been brought back while a possible data breach remained under assessment. No actor, malware or CVE is named in the available reporting.

A 19 November Hungarian report added that BAP was left accessible without authorization and that data of users who logged in on November 9 and 10 may be affected. That widens the initial timeline from the November 10 attack date and points at authentication records as the exposed category. Watch for a formal statement on whether data was accessed, which records were exposed and whether the portal outage touched other Lithuanian government systems.
