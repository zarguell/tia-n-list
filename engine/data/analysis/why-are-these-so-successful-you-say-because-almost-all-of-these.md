Arctic Wolf disclosed a data theft and extortion campaign tracked as PREY-0058 targeting Microsoft 365 and other SaaS platforms. The attackers impersonate internal IT help desk staff through phone calls, directing targets to authentication-themed URLs controlled by the operators. These sites run adversary-in-the-middle login flows that harvest credentials, MFA tokens, and session cookies.

The campaign primarily targets directors, vice presidents, and other executives. After stealing session tokens, attackers replay them from residential proxy infrastructure to blend in with legitimate traffic. They perform SharePoint discovery queries, then bulk-exfiltrate data from SharePoint, OneDrive, Exchange, and Box before sending extortion demands. The attack chain involves no endpoint malware or lateral movement, making it harder to detect with traditional EDR.

Lure domains include passkey-mfa[.]com, assignpasskey[.]com, and registermymfa[.]com, among others. Hundreds of subdomains impersonate real companies. The activity shares infrastructure overlaps with the group Google calls UNC6671 and the Cinder/Pink extortion operation. Targets are concentrated in U.S. construction, healthcare, real estate, and financial services. Organizations should implement phishing-resistant MFA, restrict SharePoint data access scope, and train help desk staff on vishing recognition.
