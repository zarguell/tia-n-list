Microsoft's August 2026 Patch Tuesday addressed at least 398 vulnerabilities, including 42 rated critical. One of them, CVE-2026-68820, is a privilege escalation bug in afd.sys, the driver behind Windows socket connections on effectively every endpoint, and it is already being actively exploited. Two other flaws were publicly detailed before the release. The volume follows a record 570-plus updates last month, and Microsoft attributes the surge to vulnerability discovery aided by artificial intelligence.

The afd.sys issue is the one to prioritize. It is not a front-door vulnerability; it is the second step in a chain that starts with phishing or another initial access method, then uses the flaw to reach system privileges on nearly any Windows machine. The exploitation in the wild means defenders should not wait for a full patch window. Apply the update to endpoints first, and treat the actively exploited bug as the critical item in this release regardless of how the rest of the bundle is scheduled.
