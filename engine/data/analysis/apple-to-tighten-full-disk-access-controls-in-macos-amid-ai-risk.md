Apple is changing a consent model, not patching a vulnerability, and no CVE is attached to this story. On October 2, 2026 the company said Full Disk Access largely sidesteps macOS privacy controls so backup apps can work, and that some developers use it to expose files, mail, messages, and browsing history. The concrete design landed in macOS 27.3 beta 2 on November 16: Device Control and Data Access splits into System & App Protection Override and Accessibility, with app-by-app selection, folder-scoped Full Disk Access guidance, an ask every time option, and a transparency indicator when one app controls another. No exploitation is claimed here.

The real risk is standing consent, not a remote exploit. The Muse messages dispute of September 30 and the October 2 WIRED report on Patrick Wardle's patched ChatGPT Mac app flaw both turned on broad local access, and OpenAI acknowledged that flaw and its fix on September 25. The detail that changes the fleet picture is Apple's statement that all app permissions previously granted remain in effect, and that known apps keep their existing experience without an entitlement. Beta 2 does not retroactively shrink access on a Mac that already granted Full Disk Access, so machines can look current on macOS 27.3 while retaining grants that reach messages and browsing history.

Treat this as a permission audit with a beta test attached. Check Full Disk Access under Apple menu, System Settings, Privacy & Security, remove agents that do not need whole-disk reads, and narrow remaining grants to specific folders or specific apps under System & App Protection Override. Enable ask every time for high-risk agents and watch the new controlling-app indicator in beta 2 pilots. Backup and assistive-technology vendors need the entitlements Apple says will preserve pre-27.3 behavior for new installs. There is no patch version or IoC set to deploy, so the control is reducing existing grants before general availability.

The assessment changes if general-availability notes narrow the known-app exemption or make pre-existing grants expire, or if the entitlement list turns out to cover common AI agents. A documented case of data read through an unchanged grant would move this from platform hygiene into incident response.
