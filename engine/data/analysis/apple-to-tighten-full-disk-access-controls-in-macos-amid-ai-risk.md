Apple is previewing more granular data access controls in macOS 27.3 beta 2. The change gives Mac users more options and transparency around Device Control, Data Access and Full Disk Access, including for apps that run AI agents. Earlier reporting described the direction as tighter Full Disk Access controls that require manual app authorization before sensitive personal files can be read. The stated driver is AI agent data access, and the beta status means the exact prompting and default behavior can still shift before release.

This is a platform consent change rather than a patch for a named vulnerability, and the tracked advisories list no CVEs. The practical question for defenders is whether an agent or helper app can reach documents outside its own sandbox without a separate user grant, and how those grants are managed on fleet devices.

Watch for the final macOS 27.3 release notes, for changes to the Device Control and Data Access prompts between beta 2 and general availability, and for Mac management guidance on approving Full Disk Access for AI tooling.
