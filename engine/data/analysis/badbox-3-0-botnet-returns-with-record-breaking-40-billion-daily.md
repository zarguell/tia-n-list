HUMAN's November 18 advisory says BADBOX 3.0 peaked at roughly 40 billion fraudulent bid requests a day, more than 4% of CTV traffic observed by the Human Defense Platform and over three times the VASTFLUX record. The hosts are low-cost, uncertified AOSP devices, not Android TV OS or Play Protect certified hardware. A backdoor pulls a versioned module list from public code repositories with backup downloads on a second repository host, an operator file server, a public CDN and gh.drgdj[.]top, a self-hosted GitHub proxy. The module manifest had reached the mid-500s by early 2026. Modules draw invisible video ads under the names of real CTV apps and enroll devices in eleven residential proxy networks, including PXYEDGE, which HUMAN ties to the earlier IpMoYu operation.

Scale alone is not the argument; continuity is. The first BADBOX held more than 190,000 devices, BADBOX 2.0 grew past 10 million, HUMAN and Shadowserver cut 500,000 devices off in March 2025, and Google's XCPE disruption covered more than 1.3 million set-top boxes, yet the rebuilt operation set a higher record. Sinkholing C2 domains and deleting Play apps did not touch the device supply chain or the public repository catalog, so the same hardware can be re-armed under new domains. One infected box now produces two distinct losses: ad inventory billed as legitimate household CTV viewing, and a residential exit node that HUMAN says is rented for account takeover, fake-account creation and carding. The advertiser whose campaign is spoofed and the household whose uplink is sold are victims of the same device.

No firmware patch is available for a backdoor in a non-writable partition; quarantine or replace suspect uncertified AOSP boxes, and use Play Protect certification as an intake check rather than a cleanup step. In DNS and proxy logs, alert on api.drgdj[.]top, file.drgdj[.]top, drgdj[.]top paths containing /dd/cc2, the ad-config route api.shzhtxcz[.]com/videoadmin/api/advertisement/getByModel, and PXYEDGE infrastructure pxyedge[.]com, 129.227.67[.]234, 128.14.141[.]107, 128.14.201[.]106, 128.14.207[.]34 and 107.151.239[.]17. On endpoints, hunt for com.google.system.* module class names, the com.android.systemsafe package loading com.speed.adv, the com.google.AdActivity showHideWindowAd code path, the TXCZ launcher com.shzhtxcz.txbox, and sideloaded IPTV apps requesting zhima descriptors from a1./t1. hosts on ports 2052 or 8080. Ad platforms should segment bid requests from a few cheap device models that call one app family and from proxy-flagged addresses; about 40% of one abused app's requests and 80% of its US requests came from residential proxy exits.

Watch for a new module repository or a manifest that advances past the mid-500s version counter after the latest domain disruption, and for PXYEDGE changing names again as its about 200,000 advertised nodes or roughly 340,000 peak unique connections move to new hosts. Either would confirm that the catalog and the proxy business, not any single C2 domain, are what takedowns have failed to remove. A shift toward SSAI spoofing outside the supply paths HUMAN named would widen the victim set beyond devices it can attribute.
