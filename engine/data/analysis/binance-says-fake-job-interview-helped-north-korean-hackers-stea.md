On October 29, 2026, attackers drained roughly $6.3 billion from Binance hot wallets across about ten blockchains in roughly twenty minutes, the largest cryptocurrency theft on record. Blockchain intelligence firms TRM Labs and Chainalysis attribute the theft to North Korea's TraderTraitor group, also tracked as Lazarus Group, Jade Sleet, and APT38, with high confidence; the FBI formally attributed the attack to North Korea on November 1 and asked cryptocurrency firms to block transactions involving the stolen funds. The DPRK denied involvement through state media. Approximately $2.8 to $3 billion has been frozen on-chain through Tether and Circle, and Binance has pledged to cover the remainder from its own funds and the Secure Asset Fund for Users (SAFU).

Binance's incident report traces the intrusion to a months-long social engineering and supply-chain campaign rather than a single breach. In June, a Wallet DevOps engineer downloaded a tampered Homebrew installer while searching for an AI coding tool; Binance detected it, replaced the device, and reset credentials, but the attackers had already learned the team's structure. In September, a second engineer was approached on a legitimate job platform by an impersonated project director, interviewed by fake HR and a fake CEO over video and chat, and given a malicious coding assignment. The compromised endpoints provided access to a service container that let attackers submit unauthorized withdrawal transactions through Binance's own signing path, with no evidence of leaked wallet private keys. TRM's on-chain analysis shows the operation was staged for at least a day and a half, with the wallet funded through Tornado Cash, malicious code trialed on other wallets, and Ethereum outflows leveraging EIP-7702.

The case is a reminder that endpoint and recruitment-channel hygiene matter as much as key custody. Organizations should treat developer laptops as high-value targets, verify coding assignments and interview tooling out-of-band, and monitor for service-container abuse and rapid multi-chain withdrawals. Binance has suspended deposits and withdrawals during remediation, revoked authorizations for the affected address, and is working with law enforcement and blockchain partners to trace and recover the remaining funds.
