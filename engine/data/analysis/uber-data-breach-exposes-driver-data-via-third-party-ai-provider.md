Uber confirmed that thousands of US drivers had personal and driver's license information stolen in an October breach at Fireworks AI, a third-party technology provider that held Uber records. Uber disclosed the incident through breach notices filed with several state attorneys general, including California. The exposure is a supply-chain failure at the provider rather than a compromise of Uber's own systems.

The stolen set is the kind of data that fuels identity fraud and targeted phishing against drivers, and notifications are going out to affected individuals. The open question is scope: whether Fireworks AI also held auth data, payout details, or records for other Uber cohorts beyond drivers.

Watch for follow-up state filings and any credit-monitoring or remediation offers, plus whether the provider's other customers report similar exposure. Drivers should treat license numbers and any associated PII as compromised and monitor for misuse.
