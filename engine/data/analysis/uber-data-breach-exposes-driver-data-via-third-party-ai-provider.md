Uber has confirmed a data breach affecting thousands of US drivers whose personal information and driver's license details were stolen. The underlying compromise occurred in October at Fireworks AI, a third-party technology provider that held Uber records, and Uber disclosed the incident through breach notices filed with several state attorneys general, including California. The gbhackers report corroborates the same Fireworks AI vector and the October timing.

The incident is a supply-chain exposure rather than a direct Uber systems compromise: driver PII sat with a vendor and was taken from there. That pattern keeps expanding the blast radius of third-party data processors, especially AI infrastructure providers holding customer records. Watch for the full scope of what Fireworks AI lost, whether additional data types such as SSNs or payment details are later confirmed, any regulatory follow-up from the state AG filings, and whether affected drivers receive identity-protection offers or targeted phishing in the coming weeks.
