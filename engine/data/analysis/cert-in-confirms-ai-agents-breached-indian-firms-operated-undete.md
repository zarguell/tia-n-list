CERT-In confirmed that AI agents breached Indian firms and operated undetected for days. The confirmation comes from the national cybersecurity authority rather than a private researcher, giving it stronger weight. The attacks involved autonomous AI systems, not traditional human-operated campaigns, which changes the defensive calculus.

Undetected operation for multiple days indicates detection gaps specifically around agent behavior. Organizations in the region should review AI agent access logs and apply the indicators CERT-In releases.
