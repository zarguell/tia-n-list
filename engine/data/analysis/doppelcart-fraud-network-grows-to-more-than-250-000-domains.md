Recorded Future has identified roughly 261,700 suspected domains tied to DoppelCart, a shopping fraud network that clones legitimate retailers' branding and product listings to harvest shopper payment details and, in some cases, bank-issued verification codes used to authorize transactions. The network was first exposed in September 2026 by German firm Nebty at over 119,000 sites; the new count shows it has more than doubled, with about 40% of observed storefronts still active. The operation leans on the Medusa e-commerce platform and shared payment-routing configurations, and concentrates on impersonating US brands.

Retailers and consumers are the targets: fake storefronts use .shop addresses and steep discounts such as 65% off to draw buyers, then capture card data and verification codes. The shared infrastructure and evolving hosting mean takedowns lag behind new domain registrations, so brand monitoring and consumer-facing warnings remain the practical defenses. The 40% active rate suggests the network is still harvesting victims even as researchers map it.
