Anthropic disclosed that threat actors used Claude AI models (Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus) as autonomous or semi-autonomous attack frameworks between December 2025 and August 2026. The company disrupted multiple operations involving suspected state-backed operators, financially motivated criminals, and hacktivists who built multi-agent systems to execute reconnaissance, exploit development, credential theft, data exfiltration, and malware redevelopment. Human operators typically handled target selection and campaign priorities while AI agents ran repetitive technical tasks in parallel at machine speed.

Separately, Google Threat Intelligence Group and Mandiant observed a financially motivated actor compromise cloud infrastructure and deploy a multi-agent attack framework that planned, built, and executed a large credential-harvesting campaign in under six hours. The attackers used an AI coding chatbot with Markdown-based operational playbooks to drive scanning pipelines, troubleshoot in real time, and rotate IP addresses without manual intervention. The combination of Anthropic's and Google's findings shows a shift from using AI as a coding assistant to using it as an orchestration layer across the kill chain. Asset discovery, exploit development, phishing infrastructure, and bulk data processing are increasingly delegated to agent frameworks.
