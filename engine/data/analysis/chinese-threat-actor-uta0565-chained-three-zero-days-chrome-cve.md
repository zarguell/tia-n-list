Chinese threat actor UTA0565 chained three zero-day vulnerabilities in Chrome and Windows ALPC to compromise targets. The CVEs involved are CVE-2026-85046 and CVE-2026-87491 (both Chrome) and CVE-2026-85880 (Windows ALPC). The Hacker News reported the chained exploitation, which used fake job recruitment documents as the initial access vector. The campaign demonstrates advanced capability to chain browser and operating-system flaws for remote code execution and privilege escalation.

The story includes 11 events with a score of 8.6, spanning threat intelligence reporting and technical analysis. The chained zero-days allowed the attacker to move from browser exploitation to deeper system access via the Windows ALPC vulnerability. Organizations should prioritize patching Chrome and Windows systems, review suspicious recruitment-related attachments, and monitor for signs of UTA0565 activity in their environments.
