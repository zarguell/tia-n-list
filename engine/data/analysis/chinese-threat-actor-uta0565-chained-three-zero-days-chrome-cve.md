On September 3 and 4, 2026, the Chinese actor UTA0565 ran the BlueMoon exploit kit against visitors to fake media and NGO websites, exploiting Google Chrome CVE-2026-85046 (V8 type confusion), CVE-2026-87491 (V8 sandbox escape via WebAssembly), and Windows ALPC CVE-2026-85880 (CVSS 7.8) in one unbroken chain from a single click to code execution inside the Chrome browser process, outside the renderer sandbox. Volexity's September 21 report shows the kit hidden in a hidden config.html iframe on americanprgoress[.]top, a typosquat of the Center for American Progress, delivering a previously unseen backdoor, CLEANGULP, disguised as chrome_cleanup.exe. The Windows stage only elevates on older builds: Windows 10 1809 through 22H2, Server 2022, and Windows 11 21H2, which is most of the legacy enterprise desktop estate.

What makes this story more than one more APT write-up is commoditization with a manufactured clock. Proofpoint had already counted four state-aligned users of BlueMoon by September 9, starting with TA412 on August 28, and Volexity's two reports bring the distinct clusters to six; the kit spread across the Chinese espionage ecosystem in roughly one week. The window existed because the chain was a patch-gap weapon: researcher Serotav reported CVE-2026-85046 on August 4, the fix landed in Chromium source on August 7, and Chrome stable did not ship it until 152.0.7977.82/.83 on September 3, so UTA0565 attacked on the very days the patch began rolling out. Any Windows host still on an old build with an unupdated Chromium browser was one convincing typosquat click away from SYSTEM, and the delivery was generic enough that mail rules tuned to government-themed lures would not flag it.

Defenders should close both halves of the chain and then hunt for the payload. Chrome fleets need the September 2026 stable releases (CVE-2026-85046 is fixed in 152.0.7977.82/.83, published September 3) and Windows needs the September 9 cumulative for CVE-2026-85880; the CISA KEV deadline of September 22, 2026 has already passed, so unpatched hosts are policy failures, not open tickets. Hunt CLEANGULP by its install path %LOCALAPPDATA%\Microsoft\IME\MicrosoftIME.exe, its scheduled task named MicrosoftIME, its plain-HTTP beacon POST /beacon/pre-register to thecovnresation[.]com, and the sample hash 8858ea412dc306b3558885af18006c5ca24689e8875733b5e13b3c2692e603cb. In EDR, alert on chrome.exe spawning cmd.exe that runs curl.exe to drop an executable into %TEMP%; in DNS and proxy logs, sweep for americanprgoress[.]top, chinadigitaltimes[.]top, thecovnresation[.]com and .net, and the hosting IP 96.9.125[.]52; Suricata ET rules 2071919 through 2071924 and Proofpoint's YARA rule MAL_BlueMoon_ExploitKit catch the loader itself.

Two developments would change the assessment. First, kit proliferation beyond espionage: Proofpoint expects BlueMoon to reach financially motivated actors, and every Chromium derivative that inherits V8 on a slower update cadence, Edge and Brave included, extends the gap the kit was built to exploit. Second, UTA0565 infrastructure churn: all seven of its typosquat domains were registered September 2 through 4, 2026, so a fresh cluster of lookalike media, halal restaurant, or corporate training domains is the leading indicator of the next campaign, and a KEV addition covering the two Chrome CVEs would widen mandatory remediation from Windows patch compliance to browser fleet compliance as well.
