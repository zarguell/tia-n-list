RAVEN (Reconnaissance & Attack on Vulnerable Elasticsearch Nodes) is an open-source offensive framework that targets exposed or poorly secured Elasticsearch and Kibana clusters. The tool chains together reconnaissance, exploitation, full database exfiltration, and persistence mechanisms. Its post-exploitation workflow can steal entire Elasticsearch databases and rebuild backdoors even after defenders delete them, by reinstalling persistent access through Kibana plugin abuse or index-level manipulation. The research demonstrates why exposed Elasticsearch instances remain high-value targets: a single misconfigured cluster can hand an attacker complete data access and durable persistence. Organizations running Elasticsearch or Kibana should verify authentication is enforced, management interfaces are not internet-facing, and plugin installation is restricted.
