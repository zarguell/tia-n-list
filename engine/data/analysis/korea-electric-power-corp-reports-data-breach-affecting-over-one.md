Korea Electric Power Corporation confirmed on November 20 that an external attacker reached its internal network and accessed two systems: the KEPCO ON electricity billing platform and the supplier relationship management system known as SRM. KEPCO put the total at 1,520,248 affected people, made up of 650,487 KEPCO ON customers and 869,761 SRM users. The SRM group includes 426,827 procurement representatives and 442,934 KEPCO or corporate employees. The company blocked external IP access, suspended both services, notified the Personal Information Protection Commission and opened an investigation with the National Intelligence Service and the climate ministry's cyber safety center.

Exposed fields for KEPCO ON users include names, resident and foreign registration numbers, addresses, phone numbers, customer numbers, bank and card details and billing records. SRM records include names, employee IDs, email addresses and company addresses. Seoul Economic Daily noted that SRM holds procurement material for power grid construction, so facility locations or equipment details could be exposed beyond the personal data. KEPCO attributed the intrusion to an external attacker and cited increasingly sophisticated attacks using AI without describing an access method, and the reporting does not confirm that AI was used here.

A separate claim on DarkForums from an account named Noiem, posted November 19, advertised 592,585 external user records, 95,054 supplier records, 401,951 bid price records and 26,859 sole-contract records, with some reports describing a 560M CSV that also mentioned military and government suppliers. DailySecu and ZDNet Korea said the provenance and overlap with KEPCO's disclosure were unverified. This follows an earlier KEPCO leak that grew from about 24,000 employees to 35,528 people and included coded facial recognition data for 400 staff. Watch for confirmation of whether the DarkForums material came from SRM and whether procurement documents entered the leak set.
