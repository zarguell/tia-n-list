Korea Electric Power Corporation confirmed on November 20 that an external attacker reached its internal network and accessed two systems: the KEPCO ON electricity billing platform and its supplier relationship management system, SRM. KEPCO put the total at 1,520,248 affected people, comprising 650,487 KEPCO ON customers and 869,761 SRM users. The SRM group includes 426,827 procurement representatives and 442,934 KEPCO or corporate employees. The company treated the entire SRM user base as affected because it could not determine individual exposure. It blocked external IP access, suspended both services, notified the Personal Information Protection Commission and opened an investigation with the National Intelligence Service and the climate ministry's cyber safety center.

KEPCO ON data included names, resident and foreign registration numbers, addresses, phone numbers, customer numbers, bank and card details, and billing records. SRM records included names, employee IDs, email addresses and company addresses. Seoul Economic Daily noted that SRM holds procurement material for power-grid construction, raising concern that facility locations or equipment details could also be exposed. KEPCO cited increasingly sophisticated attacks using technologies such as AI, but did not describe an access method and the reporting does not confirm that AI was used in this intrusion.

A November 19 DarkForums post by an account named Noiem advertised 592,585 external user records, 95,054 supplier records, 401,951 bid-price records and 26,859 sole-contract records. DailySecu and ZDNet Korea said the provenance, overlap with prior leaks and actual transfer outside KEPCO were unverified. This incident also follows a separate KEPCO leak that grew to 35,528 people and included coded facial-recognition data for 400 employees. Watch for the initial-access finding, confirmation of whether procurement documents were exposed and evidence tying the DarkForums material to SRM.
