Korea Electric Power Corp confirmed a data breach reported on 23 November 2026, and a later report put the loss at 1.52 million records. KEPCO is South Korea's electric utility, so the affected population extends well beyond a typical retail customer list. The available accounts agree on the scale but give no technical detail on the intrusion path, the type of records involved or the identity of the party behind it.

The main thing to watch is whether KEPCO publishes the categories of data exposed and a timeline. With 1.52 million records in play, the practical risk is misuse of personal or account information held by a national utility, but that risk cannot be scoped until the record contents are known. Additional reporting from Korean regulators would also help separate a confirmed breach from early estimates.
