Recorded Future's Insikt Group tracks TAG-217, a threat actor behind recent AI-assisted attacks on South Korean financial institutions. Research shows the group was testing infrastructure and assembling attack tools as early as August 2026, weeks before the banking campaign went public. Subsequent reporting tied the breaches to the exposure of personal data belonging to at least 68,000 people across at least seven financial institutions.

The investigation expands the actor's known timeline and capabilities: tools for stealing cryptocurrency wallet files, gaining access to Linux systems, and probing mobile devices. Despite operational security mistakes that let researchers uncover the preparations, the campaign shows how AI enables attackers to scale across more targets and environments, though it does not prevent the errors that created defensive opportunities.

Defenders should hunt for the ARTEX tooling and TAG-217 infrastructure, watch South Korea's Financial Services Commission disclosures for affected institutions, and expect follow-on waves given the months-long preparation cycle. The mixed picture of gains and slip-ups suggests the group is adapting rather than stopping.
