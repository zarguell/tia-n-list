AhnLab ASEC's July 2026 dark web trend report covers activity across the major forums. RaidForums moved to a new domain, shut its previous one, and launched the RaidBin service with published rules for its ransomware-as-a-service section. On BreachForums, operator HasanBroker announced retirement and a handover of operations. Separate claims include a LAPSUS$ front declaring activity and various hacktivist operations against ISPs and public institution sites.

The forum churn matters because these marketplaces set the operating norms for the extortion economy. A RaidForums domain migration and a BreachForums leadership change alter where victims are posted, how leaks are monetized, and which groups get access to RaaS infrastructure. Much of the underlying reporting is unverifiable from open sources, so the signal is directional rather than confirmed.

Watch for confirmation of the BreachForums handover and who takes over the operation, since successor administrations often change data-posting and extortion practices. Also watch whether RaidBin's RaaS rules attract new affiliate groups. Treat forum claims of infrastructure compromises as unconfirmed until a victim or independent researcher verifies them.
