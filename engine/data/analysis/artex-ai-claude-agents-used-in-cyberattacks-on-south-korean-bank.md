A Chinese-speaking attacker used the open-source ARTEX agentic penetration-testing suite paired with Claude agents to breach South Korean banks, including Shinhan Bank, KB Kookmin Bank, and Hana Bank. CrowdStrike linked the campaign through attacker infrastructure that exposed Claude Code session histories, ARTEX configs, and Claude memory files. The ARTEX instance ran DeepSeek v4.1-flash as its primary LLM backend, with GLM-5.3 (Zhipu AI) and Grok 4.6 backing additional Claude Code sessions; CrowdStrike noted probable access through the LLM proxy/reseller xcai[.]pro. The breach exposed clients' personal data and credit card information and caused system outages, prompting an emergency government meeting in South Korea.

The incident is significant because it shows agentic AI tools being weaponized at scale in a real financial-sector breach, not just in lab demonstrations. The overlap with prior financial-sector breach reporting gives high-confidence attribution. Watch for follow-on reporting on victim counts, regulatory actions by South Korean authorities, and whether the exposed attacker identity (from a resume created with the same AI tools) leads to arrests.
