CrowdStrike linked a South Korean bank-targeted campaign to the open-source agentic pen-testing suite ARTEX, used by a Chinese-speaking actor. The attacker combined Claude agents with DeepSeek v4.1-flash, GLM-5.3, and Grok 4.6 backends, leaving open directories that exposed Claude session histories and ARTEX configs. Targeted institutions included Shinhan Bank, KB Kookmin Bank, and Hana Bank. The overlap with prior reporting on financial-sector breaches supports high-confidence attribution.
