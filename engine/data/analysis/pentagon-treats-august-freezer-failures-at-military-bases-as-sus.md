The Pentagon is investigating suspected cyberattacks behind freezer malfunctions that spoiled food at military bases across the United States in August. The outages affected about six commissaries, the on base grocery stores serving military personnel, and at Fort Huachuca in Arizona a freezer entered defrost mode and heated its contents. U.S. officials familiar with the matter said Iran linked hackers were behind a series of cyberattacks targeting freezers at military bases, with intrusions beginning around late August.

Attribution is unconfirmed. Investigators have not concluded what caused the failures, and officials said it was unclear how the cyberintruders may have accessed the freezer systems, though digital vulnerabilities tied to freezer controls can expose equipment to hacking. Cyber Command and Defense Department law enforcement units are probing, with French authorities also examining the matter. The incident should be treated as suspected rather than confirmed.
