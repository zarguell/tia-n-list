Apple released iOS 27.2.1 and iPadOS 27.2.1 on November 9, 2026, fixing nine security vulnerabilities affecting iMessage, Bluetooth pairing, AirPlay, App Intents, and the brotli compression library. The flaws range from memory corruption to weak authorization and broken rate limits, and Apple's security releases page lists the update. The SANS Internet Storm Center's November 2026 roundup covers the same release across iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, and visionOS.

The SANS diary attributes the fixes to nine CVEs published on November 10, including CVE-2026-113960 and CVE-2026-113961 (unauthorized access to iMessage group chat messages), CVE-2026-113963 (an app accessing sensitive user data), five Bluetooth flaws (CVE-2026-113964, CVE-2026-113965, CVE-2026-113968, CVE-2026-113969, CVE-2026-113970) that bypass pairing rate limits or pair devices without user interaction, and CVE-2026-113971 (lock-screen AirPlay pairing). Neither event reports in-the-wild exploitation.

Users should install iOS and iPadOS 27.2.1 and the corresponding macOS, watchOS, and visionOS updates. The Bluetooth and AirPlay flaws are the most exposed to nearby attackers, so devices that accept pairing requests or sit on shared networks are the priority.
