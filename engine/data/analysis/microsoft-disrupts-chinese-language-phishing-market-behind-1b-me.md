Microsoft and Health-ISAC disrupted a Chinese-language phishing market comprising YAM Store, CoGUI, and Iron Man. The action followed a US District Court order; over one billion malicious messages targeted 75,000 organizations since January 2026, with 750 million aimed at Microsoft customers.

This is a law-enforcement disruption rather than a vulnerability advisory. Organizations should remain alert to follow-on phishing campaigns using residual infrastructure, monitor for blocked domains, and report any YAM Store or CoGUI-related messages to security teams.
