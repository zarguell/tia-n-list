DataBreaches.net reported that approximately 94,000 unclassified but sensitive tips submitted through P3 Global Intel apps and websites used by the US military were exposed due to insecure configurations. The tips were part of the Navigate360 breach investigation. DDoSecrets.org previously described the Navigate360 breach as "BlueLeaks 2.0" due to its scale and the nature of the leaked law enforcement data. The P3 Global Intel platform collects suspicious activity reports and tips from military personnel and cleared contractors.

The exposed data includes tip submissions that, while unclassified, contain operational details about suspicious activities reported by military and intelligence community personnel. The breach raises questions about Homeland Security's response to the Navigate360 incident and whether affected individuals have been notified. The reporter notes they had previously reported on the school safety platform's vulnerabilities, suggesting systemic neglect of security in government-adjacent platforms.

What to watch: whether DHS has notified the individuals whose tips were exposed, the full scope of data included in the 93,000+ records, and whether any of the tip data could be leveraged to identify confidential sources or ongoing investigations.
