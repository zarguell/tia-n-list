DarkBlinders, an Iran-aligned threat actor, ran an espionage campaign from August through October 2026 that compromised a high-profile Israeli target and a government cloud environment in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq. Operators used tailored social engineering, including lures posing as Dubai Airports recruiters, to deliver a trojanized coding challenge, and separately deployed a fake video meeting application while abusing GitHub repositories for command and control or payload delivery.

The campaign shows a multi-stage intrusion playbook: a recruitment-themed lure to reach a high-value individual, followed by backdoor deployment and data theft from government cloud infrastructure. Reporting attributes the activity to Dream Research Labs and notes the operation spanned at least two months, with no public attribution beyond the Iran-nexus assessment.

Defenders should scrutinize unsolicited recruiter outreach and coding assessments, monitor GitHub-hosted payloads and fake meeting applications, and review cloud access for unusual persistence or data exfiltration. The targeting of both an individual and a government cloud suggests the actor is pursuing both personal and institutional intelligence.
