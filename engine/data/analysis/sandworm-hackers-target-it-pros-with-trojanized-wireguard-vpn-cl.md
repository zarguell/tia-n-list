CERT-UA documented a social engineering campaign attributed to UAC-0145, a cluster believed to be part of the Russian threat group Sandworm (APT44), that has run since at least May against system administrators and IT professionals. The operators pose as IT companies and recruiters, study targets' resumes on job sites, then move contact to Telegram and hold English-language technical interviews over Zoom. One observed case impersonated the IT firm Sopra Steria using lookalike email addresses.

The interview includes mock technical assignments that require connecting to a "corporate" VPN. The file the candidate downloads produces a fake error and prompts installation of a modified WireGuard-based client, which is the payload. The campaign deliberately targets people with privileged access to corporate networks, and the recruitment pretext gives the attacker a legitimate-looking channel for delivery. Administrators should treat unsolicited interview offers with VPN setup requirements as high-risk and verify the hiring company through independent channels before running any supplied installer.
