The ZRON leak is a nearly 10 GB trove from Chinese cybersecurity firm Zhengzhou Zhirong Network Technology, circulating since June 2026 and highlighted by the Wall Street Journal on September 16, 2026. Documents describe AI-driven systems organizing stolen foreign government data for police use, targeting Russia, Pakistan, and others. Internal chats, marketing materials, and government data samples suggest authentic commercial hacking ecosystem activity. Researchers note many documents align with public events, but full authenticity is tentative.
