Group-IB and NyxLab have tied an active campaign against exposed FortiGate and Palo Alto GlobalProtect edge devices to the actor k0t (CryptoFeel, Member19), according to a report referenced in the events. The original coverage states hackers breached enterprise firewalls and VPNs, exposing over 70,000 credentials. The campaign targets exposed edge infrastructure rather than end-user endpoints, which makes the exposure particularly severe for network defenders since compromised VPN and firewall access provides direct lateral movement paths.

The story score is 5.0 and includes updates from The Hacker News and Mastodon reposts linking to the same report. There is no specific CVE assigned to this campaign, and no vendor patch is mentioned. Watch for Fortinet and Palo Alto advisories confirming the attack pattern and for any IOCs released by Group-IB or NyxLab.
