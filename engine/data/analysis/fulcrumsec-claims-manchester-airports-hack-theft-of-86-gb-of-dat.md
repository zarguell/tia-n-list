Extortion group FulcrumSec claims to have stolen approximately 86 GB of data from Manchester Airports Group, the UK's largest airport operator. BleepingComputer validated one record by matching it against a traveller's known Manchester Airport purchase history, confirming the sample contained accurate Fast Track purchase details, booking times, terminal information, spending amounts, and trip purposes. The breach covers Manchester, London Stansted, and East Midlands airports.

MAG disclosed on August 27 that an unauthorized third party accessed data from car park, lounge, Fast Track bookings, and in-airport Wi-Fi registrations. FulcrumSec's samples indicate the breach exposed considerably more detailed customer, booking, and travel information than MAG initially revealed. The group claims it obtained access through airport-specific entry points.

The validated samples include a roughly 21.5 GB Manchester customer export with consolidated profiles combining customer identifiers, historical booking activity, and marketing classifications. This is a significant infrastructure breach affecting millions of travellers across three major UK airports. Organizations should assess whether their employees or customers had data in MAG systems and monitor for follow-on phishing or identity-fraud campaigns using the exposed booking and travel details.
