LNbits has told all operators to stop their servers immediately after an active attack wave, superseding earlier advice to disable only the Paywall extension. Security patches are being prepared, with an estimate of 24 to 48 hours.

One operator self-reported 2,285,545 sats withdrawn from his instance by newly created accounts between 11:22 and 16:00 Asia/Gaza. No CVE, fixed build or global victim count has been published.

Operators should keep instances offline until a patched release is announced, then verify account and transaction logs before resuming, as the attack surface is the wallet server software rather than Bitcoin or Lightning protocol.
