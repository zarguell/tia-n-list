Ledger has asked resellers and distributors in Southeast Asia and Hong Kong to stop selling its wallets and pull stock from shelves while it investigates tampered devices. The confirmed cases trace to reseller CryptoBilis: devices were modified with a hardware implant that recovers the 24-word seed phrase and exfiltrates it over LTE through a provisioned eSIM, following user reports of drained funds that prompted an investigation starting October 9. Ledger's Donjon team confirmed the implant design earlier reported by Mark Karpeles and Tibane Labs.

The implant is a separate component that leaves the Secure Element, firmware, and transaction signing untouched, so Ledger's integrity self-checks cannot detect it. That makes point-of-sale trust the whole problem: a genuine-looking sealed box from a third-party seller is no longer assurance enough. Watch for Ledger to name additional affected sellers beyond CryptoBilis, for the scope of confirmed fund losses, and for any recall or verification program for devices already in the channel.
