Bell Canada confirmed that an "agentic" threat actor breached its systems on November 17, 2026 and stole enterprise customer data. BleepingComputer first learned of the incident from a customer who shared a breach notification from Bell's Information Security Team. The unauthorized party reached a pre-production environment of Bell's Maximo system, which the company uses to order and provision telecommunications network connections.

Bell said the affected dataset may contain business contact names, job titles, corporate email addresses, phone numbers and business addresses, plus ticket titles, some IP addresses tied to network elements or systems and limited contextual information. The company said the set does not contain credentials, highly sensitive personal information, PDFs, file attachments or other supplementary material stored in Maximo. A malware.news repost on November 19 relayed the same confirmation.

Bell told BleepingComputer that the actor accessed multiple systems, so the Maximo pre-production environment is only the confirmed portion. Watch for the scope of the other systems, notification of affected organizations and any sign that circuit provisioning data or network element addresses were used for follow-on access.
