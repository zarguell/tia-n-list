FinCEN disclosed a cybersecurity incident involving unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems and said it had taken some systems offline as a precaution. The BSA E-Filing website remained operational, although some services were unavailable, and the Financial Institutions Hotline was unaffected. The agency did not identify the affected systems, the intrusion method, the actor or whether data was accessed or stolen.

Batch-filing problems began around 17:45 ET on November 18, with Hummingbird reporting failed BSA submissions and CU*SOUTH reporting rejected currency transaction reports through Secure Direct Transfer Mode. FinCEN's November 24 order, FIN-2026-R002, moved deadlines for certain BSA reports and Beneficial Ownership Information reports or FinCEN ID updates covering November 18 through December 29 to December 30. Other BSA obligations, including suspicious-transaction monitoring, remained in force.

The disruption affects a channel used for sensitive financial-intelligence reporting, but the public notice does not establish a breach of filings. Watch for restoration of batch services, clarification of the systems and data involved, and whether the exceptive relief is extended. Financial institutions should track direct filings and amended-versus-initial status to avoid duplicates while the batch route is impaired.
