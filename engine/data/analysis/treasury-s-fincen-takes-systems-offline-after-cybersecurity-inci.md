FinCEN disclosed on 25 November that it is investigating a cybersecurity incident that resulted in unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems, and that some systems were taken offline as a precaution. The notice sits on the Bank Secrecy Act E-Filing System website. The E-Filing website remained operational with some services temporarily unavailable, and the Financial Institutions Hotline was unaffected. The agency did not say which systems were touched, how the intruder got in, how long access lasted, or whether data was taken. The Verge first reported the disclosure, and later posts on 26 November restate the confirmation without adding technical detail.

The visible impact is in filing operations. Hummingbird reported failed BSA batch submissions from about 17:45 ET on 18 November and said prepared filings were retained rather than lost. CU*SOUTH told credit unions that CTRs sent through CBX were rejected, that filings from 18 November onward should go directly through the FinCEN portal, and that filers who had done so should change the filing type from Amended to Initial to avoid duplicates. JBizNews said only Secure Data Transfer Mode batch filers were affected while the website remained up, and noted that at that point FinCEN had given no cause or restoration time. Order FIN-2026-R002, issued 24 November, moved deadlines for certain SDTM reports and for Beneficial Ownership Information Reports or FinCEN ID updates from the 18 November to 29 December window to 30 December.

The incident lands in a busy period for US government systems. SecurityWeek placed it alongside the breach of an unclassified CIA system and disruption at other agencies, and earlier reporting recalls the 2024 Treasury breach for which twelve Chinese nationals, including two Ministry of Public Security officers, were charged. No actor has claimed this FinCEN intrusion and no link to those cases is stated in the available material. Watch for a scope statement on accessed data, restoration of SDTM batch filing, and any attribution or KEV-style remediation detail from Treasury.
