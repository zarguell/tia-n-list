FinCEN disclosed on November 25 that it was investigating a cybersecurity incident resulting in unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems. It took some systems offline as a precaution. The notice was posted on the Bank Secrecy Act E-Filing System website, which remained operational although some services were temporarily unavailable. The Financial Institutions Hotline was unaffected. FinCEN did not identify the systems touched, the intrusion method, the duration of access, the actor or whether data was stolen.

The visible operational impact began with batch filing failures. Hummingbird reported failed BSA submissions from about 17:45 ET on November 18 and said prepared filings were retained rather than lost. CU*SOUTH told credit unions that CTRs sent through CBX were rejected, advised direct filing through the FinCEN portal for reports from November 18 onward, and instructed filers who had already filed directly to change the type from Amended to Initial to avoid duplicates. Other reporting said Secure Data Transfer Mode batch filers were affected while the website remained available.

Order FIN-2026-R002, issued November 24, moved deadlines for certain SDTM reports and for Beneficial Ownership Information Reports or FinCEN ID updates from the November 18 through December 29 window to December 30. The order said other BSA obligations, including ongoing monitoring for suspicious transactions, still applied. The incident temporarily strained financial-crime reporting, but the available material establishes no link to earlier Treasury breaches. Watch for restoration of SDTM batch filing, a scope statement on accessed data and any attribution or remediation detail from Treasury.
