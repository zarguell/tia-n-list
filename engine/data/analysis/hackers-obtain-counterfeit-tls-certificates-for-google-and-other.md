Hackers obtained counterfeit TLS certificates for Google and other large services, according to an Ars Technica report. The certificates were issued through a misconfigured certificate authority that allowed unauthorized issuance. Google revoked the fraudulent certificates after discovery. No evidence of active exploitation of the counterfeit certs for traffic interception was reported at the time of disclosure. Organizations relying on the affected CA should audit their certificate chains and rotate any potentially compromised certificates. Watch for further CA misconfiguration reports and any confirmed interception campaigns.
