Eight intermediate certificates from the intermediate CA eMudhra were created with weak P-384 keys containing only 3 to 8 bits of entropy instead of 384 bits. The flaw originated in an April 30, 2025 key generation ceremony under the emSign Root TLS CA G3. An attacker with any compromised intermediate CA private key can impersonate any website or service. Six TLS certificates trusted by Chrome, Mozilla, and Apple have been blocked; two Microsoft-trusted code-signing certificates were also blocked. eMudhra revoked the compromised certificates, stopped new issuance under G3, and vendors are patching across technologies.
