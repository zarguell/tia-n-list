Eight intermediate certificates belonging to the eMudhra intermediate CA were found with weak private keys that evaluated to single-digit and double-digit integers rather than the required 384 bits of entropy. The flaw originated during an April 30, 2025 key generation ceremony for 24 subordinate CAs, where a wrap/unwrap step created a second set of weak P-384 keys that were mistakenly used for eight intermediate CA certificates. The impacted certificates include six TLS certificates trusted by Chrome, Mozilla, and Apple, and two code-signing certificates trusted by Microsoft.

An attacker with any of the compromised private keys could impersonate any website or service. Browser vendors and Microsoft have blocked the affected certificates, and eMudhra revoked them and halted new issuance under its G3 root. Organizations relying on these certificates should verify their certificate stores and prepare for additional patches across technologies.
