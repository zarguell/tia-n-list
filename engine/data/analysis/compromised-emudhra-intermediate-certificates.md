Cisco Talos disclosed eight compromised intermediate certificates from eMudhra, where private keys were reduced from 384 bits of pseudo-random security to single-digit and double-digit integers due to a key-generation flaw. Six TLS certificates trusted by Chrome, Mozilla, and Apple have been blocked; two Microsoft code-signing certificates were also blocked. The event details how the flaw occurred during an April 2025 wrap/unwrap step that created weak P-384 keys, making private key recovery trivial.

This is a significant CA compromise with broad ecosystem impact. Because the compromised intermediates can impersonate any website or service, organizations should verify that browsers and operating systems have applied the vendor blocks. The event notes eMudhra revoked the certificates and stopped issuing under the G3 root, but software vendors may need additional patches to fully remediate exposure.
