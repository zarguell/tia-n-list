Cisco Talos found that eight intermediate certificate authority certificates issued by eMudhra were generated with near-zero entropy. During an April 30, 2025 key-generation ceremony for 24 subordinate CAs, a flaw in eMudhra's key generation process created a second set of weak P-384 keys during a wrap/unwrap step, dropping entropy from 384 bits to 3-8 bits and making the private keys trivially recoverable. An attacker holding any compromised intermediate's private key could impersonate any website or service. Six of the TLS certificates, trusted by Chrome, Mozilla, and Apple, have been blocked by those vendors, and two code-signing certificates used by Microsoft have also been blocked.

eMudhra has revoked the compromised certificates and stopped issuing new certificates under its G3 root. Additional patches are expected across a variety of technologies as applications remediate exposure. Organizations should verify their trust stores and certificate chains reflect the revocations and vendor blocks, and reissue any certificates chained to the affected intermediates.
