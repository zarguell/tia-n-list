Cisco Talos reported eight compromised eMudhra intermediate CA certificates with weak 3-8 bit P-384 private keys, down from the required 384 bits of entropy. The certificates, trusted by Chrome, Mozilla, Apple, and Microsoft, have been blocked by vendors. eMudhra revoked them and stopped issuing under the G3 root. Researcher Wayne signed messages with each key; Dennis Jackson published a verification site. This is a supply-chain-level certificate compromise with broad browser and code-signing impact. Monitor for follow-on patches across technologies that depended on these intermediates.
