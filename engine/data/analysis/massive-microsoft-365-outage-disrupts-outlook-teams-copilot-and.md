Microsoft suffered a major Microsoft 365 outage that began November 12 and continued into November 13, 2026, disrupting cloud connectivity across services including Outlook, Teams, Copilot, and Defender XDR. Microsoft confirmed that users in any region could experience connectivity problems as the investigation widened from an initial focus on Exchange Online mailbox access in the Americas, and said it was working to restore the affected infrastructure.

The security angle is secondary but real. When Outlook or Teams is down, users reach for ad hoc workarounds, and security commentary warns that some quick fixes, such as forwarding through personal accounts or unsanctioned recovery tools, can hand corporate data to third parties. Organizations riding out the disruption should avoid unsanctioned forwarding or shadow-IT recovery paths, confirm mail flow through approved channels once service returns, and stay alert for attackers using the outage as cover for credential phishing and MFA-fatigue attempts.
