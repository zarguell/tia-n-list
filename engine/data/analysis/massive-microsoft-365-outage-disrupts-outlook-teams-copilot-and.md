A major Microsoft 365 outage beginning Thursday, November 12, 2026 disrupted cloud connectivity and affected Outlook, Teams, Copilot 365, Defender for Office 365, Intune and the Microsoft 365 Apps Admin Center. Microsoft confirmed users in any region could experience connectivity problems, expanding an investigation initially scoped to Exchange Online mailbox access in the Americas. CNBC reported nearly 50,000 Downdetector reports for Microsoft 365 alongside problems for Teams.

Microsoft said the elevated load was not driven by an increase in traffic but by a number of servers becoming unexpectedly unavailable, straining the remaining infrastructure. A core process on some servers became overwhelmed, causing them to fail automated health checks and go offline. Microsoft had not identified any recent change correlated with the start of the impact; its investigation indicated the failed health checks may originate from a specific component responsible for processing server health. The incident was tracked under EX1550543 and MO1550821.

Microsoft tested a configuration change and an alternate health check method to return affected servers to service, but actions taken had not provided the anticipated relief. The outage ran past 12 hours. Administrators should monitor the Microsoft 365 admin center and status.cloud.microsoft for updates and review dependencies on Exchange Online and Teams for critical operations.
