A Microsoft 365 outage that began on November 12, 2026 as an Exchange Online mailbox access problem in the Americas widened into a global service degradation. Outlook, Teams, Copilot, Defender XDR, Purview, Intune, and the Microsoft 365 Apps Admin Center were affected. Downdetector recorded close to 50,000 reports at the peak, and Microsoft confirmed that users in any region could be affected.

Microsoft said the cause was technical. Several servers became unexpectedly unavailable, failed automated health checks, and left the remaining infrastructure overloaded. The company found no recent change correlated with the start of the impact, tested a configuration change, and worked on an alternate health check method to return servers to service. Recovery ran past 12 hours before Microsoft reported broad stabilization and apologized, saying the incident was not related to cyber activity or AI agents.

The outage hit hospitals, courts, state agencies, and other organizations that depend on Microsoft 365 for email and collaboration. Phishers moved quickly to exploit the extended disruption, and IT News reported that attackers were already using the outage as cover. Watch for follow-on phishing that references outage-related delays, shadow IT workarounds that expose tenant data, and Microsoft's post-incident review for the failed health-check component.
