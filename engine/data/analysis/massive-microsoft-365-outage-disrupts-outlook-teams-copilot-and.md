A widespread Microsoft 365 outage began on the afternoon of November 12, 2026 and continued into November 13, disrupting Outlook, Teams, Copilot, Defender XDR, Intune, and the Microsoft 365 Apps Admin Center for users across multiple regions. Microsoft 365 drew nearly 50,000 reports on Downdetector, with problems reported for Teams alongside Exchange Online. The incident is tracked under EX1550543 and MO1550821 in the Microsoft 365 admin center and status.cloud.microsoft, and Microsoft confirmed users in any region could experience connectivity problems after initially scoping the issue to Exchange Online mailbox access in the Americas.

Microsoft attributed the elevated load not to a traffic increase but to servers becoming unexpectedly unavailable and straining the remaining infrastructure. A core process on some servers became overwhelmed, causing failures in automated service health checks that took servers offline. Microsoft found no recent change correlated with the start of the impact and identified the failed health checks as possibly originating from a specific component responsible for processing server health, and was testing a configuration change plus an alternate health check method to return affected servers to service.

As of the latest updates, Microsoft's actions had not provided the anticipated relief and the incident ran past 12 hours. Administrators should monitor the Microsoft 365 status pages for EX1550543 and MO1550821, expect degraded productivity services until recovery completes, and note that no security compromise is indicated in the available reporting.
