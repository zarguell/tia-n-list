Huntress tracked a ten-week adversary-in-the-middle token-replay campaign against Microsoft 365 from July 14 onward: 497 successful sign-ins across 97 accounts at 79 organizations. The attackers did not break MFA; they stole the session token that MFA had already established, then replayed it. The events cite Huntress reporting the numbers and method. Watch for Microsoft session-hardening updates and organizational token-replay detection.
