Threat actor DBHunter has posted a data breach claim naming Petroecuador, Ecuador's state-owned oil company, alleging theft of 385 GB of sensitive data. The claim was tracked by DarkWebSonar and posted to their public Mastodon feed on September 10. A second claim from DBHunter on September 11 named Universidad Autonoma Tomas Flias, a public university in Bolivia, suggesting the actor is actively listing multiple Latin American targets.

DBHunter is a known threat actor in the dark web ecosystem that claims data theft from organizations and publishes samples to validate their claims. The 385 GB figure, if accurate, would represent a significant breach of Ecuador's largest petroleum company. State oil companies are high-value targets because they hold operational data, financial records, and sometimes classified national security information. Ecuadorian authorities and Petroecuador have not publicly confirmed the breach. Organizations in the Latin American energy and education sectors should be aware that DBHunter appears to be in an active listing campaign.
