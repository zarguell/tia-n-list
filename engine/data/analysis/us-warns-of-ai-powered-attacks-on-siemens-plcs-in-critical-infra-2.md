Five U.S. agencies — NSA, CISA, FBI, Department of Energy, and EPA — jointly warned that threat actors are using AI-generated scripts to exploit Siemens S7 Series PLCs in critical infrastructure. The advisory, issued August 13, 2026, describes the activity as ongoing and characterizes it as an active threat. Siemens S7 PLCs are among the most widely deployed industrial controllers globally, used to manage physical processes in power plants, water treatment facilities, and manufacturing lines.

The AI angle is the key development here. Previous PLC exploitation required specialized ICS/OT knowledge — understanding Modbus protocols, Siemens TIA Portal, and the specific behavior of industrial processes. AI-generated attack scripts lower that barrier, potentially enabling a broader set of adversaries to target operational technology. The advisory explicitly notes that PLC targeting extends beyond Siemens, suggesting the technique is vendor-agnostic.

The joint-agency composition is unusual and signals cross-sector concern: NSA handles national security, CISA covers civilian infrastructure, FBI handles criminal/nation-state investigations, DOE covers energy, and EPA covers water. This breadth indicates the threat is not confined to one sector. Operators should apply Siemens's recommended mitigations immediately and review network segmentation between IT and OT environments. The absence of attribution in the advisory is notable — if this were clearly Iranian, the agencies would likely say so given the current geopolitical context.
