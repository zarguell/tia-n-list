A cyberattack on Arizona's court system stole personal information for 1.3 million people with unpaid fees and fines, plus 30,000 active or inactive protection orders and 150,000 foster care reports. The attack started when an employee clicked a malicious link; staff shut it down on a backup server about two hours after detection on September 24. No jurors, witnesses, or court employees were affected, and no records were altered. Investigators say there is no evidence the data has been used.
