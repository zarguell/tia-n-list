Arizona's court system suffered a breach that copied personal information for 1.3 million people with unpaid traffic and criminal fees, plus nearly 30,000 active and inactive orders of protection and 150,000 foster care board reports dating to 2010. The attack began when a court employee clicked a malicious email link; staff stopped it on a backup server about two hours after detection on Sept. 24. The Arizona Supreme Court says no records were altered or deleted and there is no evidence the stolen data was used or shared. Jurors, witnesses, and court employees were not affected, and no court cases have been delayed. The incident remains under investigation.
