Microsoft researchers attributed a ConnectWise supply-chain attack targeting customer Microsoft 365 environments to Storm-0940, a China-state aligned threat group active since 2021. The attackers used the agentic AI penetration-testing framework ARTEX to automate attacks against hundreds of organizations across aerospace, defense, automotive, chemicals, energy, government, manufacturing, technology, and other sectors.

Before the ConnectWise compromise, Storm-0940 ran a widespread but unsuccessful password-spraying campaign ending October 14, targeting 327 Microsoft 365 and Exchange Online accounts across eight organizations with over 151,000 failed logon attempts (peaking at 2,900 per minute). The next day, the group used AI agents and the same malicious infrastructure to compromise ConnectWise Cloud Backup customers and access their Microsoft 365 environments.
