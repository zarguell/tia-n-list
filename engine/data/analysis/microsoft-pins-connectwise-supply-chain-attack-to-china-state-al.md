Microsoft attributes a supply-chain attack targeting ConnectWise customers' Microsoft 365 environments to the China-state aligned group Storm-0940. The group used the AI penetration framework ARTEX to automate attacks across hundreds of organizations globally, targeting aerospace, energy, government, manufacturing, technology, and non-profits. Between October 10 and 14, Storm-0940 conducted over 151,000 failed logon attempts against 327 M365 accounts across eight organizations, peaking at 2,900 failures per minute. It then used AI agents and malicious infrastructure to compromise ConnectWise Cloud Backup customers' M365 environments.
