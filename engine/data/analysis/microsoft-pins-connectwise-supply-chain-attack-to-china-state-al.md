Microsoft researchers attributed a recent ConnectWise supply-chain attack to Storm-0940, a China-state aligned threat group active since 2021. The attackers used the ARTEX agentic AI penetration-testing framework to automate scanning and compromise ConnectWise Cloud Backup customers' Microsoft 365 environments. A four-day password-spraying campaign ending October 14 targeted 327 accounts across eight organizations with over 151,000 failed logon attempts, followed by the supply-chain compromise.

This incident demonstrates state-aligned actors using AI automation to accelerate both reconnaissance and exploitation. The scale of the password-spraying campaign and the rapid transition to supply-chain access highlight the efficiency gains AI provides to attackers. Organizations using ConnectWise cloud services should audit M365 sign-in logs for anomalous authentication and rotate any credentials that may have been exposed through the backup service.
