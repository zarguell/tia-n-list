Anthropic published a threat intelligence report documenting misuse of its Claude models across seven categories: cyber operations, influence operations, surveillance, scams and fraud, biological misuse, conventional weapons development, and model distillation. The report covers activity observed between December 2025 and August 2026, with Anthropic stating it disrupted each operation and shared intelligence with authorities and industry partners.

The cyber operations detailed are notable in scale and sophistication. A Russian-aligned espionage campaign targeted more than 20 government and defense organizations across Ukraine and Europe. Two Chinese undergraduates ran an automated exploit factory that produced over a dozen potential zero-days in a single month. Affiliates of the ShinyHunters crime collective dumped 2,100 cloud access tokens across 40 corporate tenants in 34 hours. A lone hacktivist also operated using Claude-powered capabilities.

The report's central finding is that AI has removed the skill barrier that once distinguished state-sponsored hackers from individual criminals. Anthropic frames this as a responsibility disclosure, arguing that model capabilities will continue to increase the risk landscape unless AI developers and defenders act to mitigate threats. The surveillance category specifically addresses campaigns using Claude to monitor dissidents, which was flagged in separate reporting.
