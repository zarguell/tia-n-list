N0n is a data-exfiltration and destructive-extortion group that surfaced on the dark web in September 2026 and reached 93 listed victims in roughly five weeks, according to a SOCRadar Threat Research Unit profile. The operators steal data, then encrypt or destroy the victim's infrastructure, and negotiate in keyed rooms with a per-victim Bitcoin wallet and a leak site branded "n0n | no js | no mercy". The locker appends the .n0n extension to encrypted files. The group is particular about its name, described as two lowercase n letters with a zero between them.

Most of the tradecraft detail comes from a staging server SOCRadar found exposed in September 2026 holding an operator's live workspace. That directory showed credential stuffing against FortiGate SSL-VPN appliances, hijacked firewall management centers, mass deletion of S3 objects, a custom C++ locker, and an AI coding agent with more than $7,000 of paid LLM API usage. SOCRadar ties the workspace to at least 23 organizations across 14 countries.

The defensive priority follows from that chain: edge VPN appliances are the entry point and object storage is the destruction target, so the checklist is FortiGate SSL-VPN hardening with MFA and credential rotation, S3 versioning plus object lock and delete alarms, and alerting on bulk delete API calls from unusual principals. The reason to track this group specifically is that its post-breach stage is destructive rather than purely encryptive, which turns backups and restore drills into the primary control instead of a recovery convenience. Watch for attribution, an affiliate name, and any victim-side confirmation of whether the destructive stage is wiper-style or ransomware with a working decryptor.
