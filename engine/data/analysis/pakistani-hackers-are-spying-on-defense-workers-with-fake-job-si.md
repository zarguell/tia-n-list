Lookout research first shared with CyberScoop describes a cyberespionage campaign against Indian defense contractors and military personnel that installs GravityRAT on victim devices. The activity is attributed to a group possibly aligned with the Pakistani government and tracked as SpaceCobra or Cosmic Leopard. Lookout says it has observed the operation for 12 months and that it remains active.

The lure is a set of fake job recruitment sites built to look like legitimate defense-industry portals. One site included an "our clients" section with logos for Airbus, BAE Systems, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, Rheinmetall, Rolls-Royce, Saab, Safran and Thales. Lookout researcher Parth Bhatt noted that some sites were India-specific while at least one made no reference to India, and a Google Play download button only produced an "app is under review" message. A Greek-language report on the same research was posted to Mastodon as a social link, adding no new finding.

The campaign matters because the social engineering is aimed at people with defense-sector access and uses recognizable contractor branding to make a fake recruitment flow credible. What to watch is whether the same site template appears outside India, whether Google Play-style download chains are copied by other actors, and whether Lookout publishes indicators tied to the GravityRAT samples from the active campaign.
