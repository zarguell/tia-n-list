Netskope reports XWorm expanded from 35 plugins in July 2025 to 46 in October 2026, evolving from a Windows RAT into a modular surveillance platform. The new plugins include GPS location tracking (Maps), live audio streaming (Microphone), webcam capture, and HBrowser which hijacks authenticated browser sessions without touching credentials, bypassing MFA entirely. Netskope observed over 1,200 incidents across 100 organizations during Q3. Activity surged after a cracked builder was released publicly in June 2025. The malware remains highly active; the plugin growth indicates a shift toward persistent surveillance rather than simple remote access. Watch for HBrowser use against MFA-protected accounts and for new plugin releases.
