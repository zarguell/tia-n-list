XWorm developers expanded the RAT with new plugins that turn infected systems into a surveillance platform, Netskope reports. The malware, active since 2022 and observed in over 1,200 incidents across 100 organizations in Q3 2026, grew from 35 plugins in July 2025 to 46 in October 2026. New capabilities include GPS tracking (Maps plugin), live audio streaming (Microphone plugin), webcam capture, and interactive browser session hijacking via HBrowser that bypasses MFA without stealing credentials. A cracked builder released in June 2025 increased threat activity. Watch for continued plugin expansion and increased incident volume.
