XWorm RAT, first observed in 2022, has expanded from a standard Windows remote access trojan into a full surveillance platform, according to Netskope. The developers added plugins for GPS location tracking, live microphone streaming, webcam capture on demand, and interactive browser session hijacking that bypasses MFA without touching credentials. Plugin count grew from 35 in July 2025 to 46 in October 2026. Netskope observed over 1,200 incidents across 100 organizations in Q3 2026, with activity rising after a cracked builder was released publicly in June 2025. The HBrowser plugin specifically targets authenticated sessions, making it a direct threat to multi-factor authentication implementations. The growth in both capability and incident volume makes XWorm a sustained enterprise risk.
