Cisco ISE (Identity Services Engine) is facing active exploitation of CVE-2026-76460, a critical authentication bypass vulnerability with a CVSS score of 10.0. The flaw allows an unauthenticated remote attacker to gain root-level access to affected devices without any credentials. CISA has added CVE-2026-76460 to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, requiring federal agencies to remediate by a specified deadline. The vulnerability also carries a secondary CVE designation, CVE-2026-20192, in some advisory contexts.

The attack surface is significant because ISE is widely deployed as a network access control and policy enforcement platform in enterprise environments. A compromised ISE instance gives an attacker the ability to manipulate network authentication policies, enroll rogue devices, and potentially pivot to other network segments. Researchers have identified 9 unique detections and 9 indicators of compromise associated with active exploitation campaigns.

Organizations running Cisco ISE should immediately apply the vendor patch and review network logs for signs of unauthorized access. If patching cannot be completed immediately, isolating ISE management interfaces from untrusted networks is a critical interim measure. The CVSS 10.0 rating and KEV listing confirm this is not a theoretical risk but an actively exploited vulnerability with real-world impact.
