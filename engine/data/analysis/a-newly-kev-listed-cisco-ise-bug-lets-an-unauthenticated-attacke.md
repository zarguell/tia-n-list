Cisco disclosed CVE-2026-76460 on September 16, 2026 in advisory cisco-sa-ISE-ABP-VNSW7Tn5: an API endpoint in Identity Services Engine and ISE Passive Identity Connector applies authentication controls so weakly that a single crafted request bypasses the web-based management interface, rated CVSS 10.0 and exploitable regardless of device configuration. Cisco PSIRT confirmed the flaw was exploited in the wild as a zero-day, CISA added it to the KEV catalog the same day with a September 19 remediation deadline for federal civilian agencies, and successful exploitation can end in commands run as root. Cisco found the bug through a technical support case, named no attacker, and reported the endpoint also supports arbitrary file upload and download. The same release batch carried 20 ISE CVEs, 12 of them critical, including the previously public CVE-2026-20282, CVE-2026-20283 and CVE-2026-20284, an improper access control bypass tracked as CVE-2026-20192, and critical hardening items CVE-2026-20234, CVE-2026-20331 and CVE-2026-20332.

Root on ISE is not one more server takeover: the appliance decides which users and devices get onto the network. An attacker holding it can rewrite access policy to admit their own hardware, extract the credentials ISE stores, erase or conceal the very logs Cisco points defenders at, and then move through every segment ISE polices, as VulnCheck exploit developer Landon Rice put it. No exposed-asset count exists for ISE this cycle, and that is structural rather than an oversight, because most ISE management planes sit inside the data center where external scanners never see them; the population at risk is every appliance reachable from any compromised interior host, which no Shodan query will enumerate. What makes this story the one to work from the week's pile is repetition. June 2025 brought CVE-2025-20281, CVE-2025-20282 and CVE-2025-20337, all CVSS 10.0 in the same API surface, with attempted exploitation confirmed within a month and fixes in 3.3 Patch 7 and 3.4 Patch 2. Fifteen months later Cisco ships 41 ISE fixes at once while Check Point, its closest architectural rival in the policy and gateway plane, took two pre-auth root flaws and confirmed exploitation of CVE-2026-85102 starting September 12. Identity policy engines are a standing target category now, and treating each advisory as an isolated event is how fleets of unpatched policy servers accumulate.

Upgrade ISE and ISE-PIC to 3.5 Patch 4, 3.4 Patch 7, 3.3 Patch 12, 3.2 Patch 11 or 3.1 Patch 12. No workaround exists, so until every node is patched, restrict traffic to the appliance with infrastructure ACLs and hold management access to admin jump hosts only. Cisco's hunt is specific: review access.log for suspicious usernames, and in distributed deployments collect it from every node. Treat those logs as perishable, since root access permits their deletion, so ship them off-box first and reconcile recent administrative sessions against configuration change history before trusting the appliance's own view of what happened. Organizations also running the Check Point products in this cluster should confirm LivePatch Take 26 or Jumbo Hotfix Accumulator Takes 44 (R82.10), 126 (R82), 166 (R81.20) and 190 (R81.10) for CVE-2026-85102 and CVE-2026-85103, search Logs and Events for certificate-based Mobile Access logins with subjects CN=vpn, CN=vpn-user or CN=vpnuser, and check the CVE-2026-91843 marker "Administrator failed to log in: Username too long" in Audit and Admin login logs.

Two developments would change this assessment. A public proof of concept for CVE-2026-76460 would move exploitation from whoever triggered Cisco's support case to every commodity scanning crew within days, the pattern the 2025 ISE bugs followed once ZDI disclosures circulated. Confirmation that victims extend beyond the initial support case, or an attribution, would turn a patch sprint into an incident-response exercise, because root on the policy engine means intruders had the keys to delete their trail and to let themselves back in through policy they wrote.
