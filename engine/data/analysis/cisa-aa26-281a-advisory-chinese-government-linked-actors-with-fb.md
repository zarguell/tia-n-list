CISA advisory AA26-281A, published October 8 with FBI, NSA and international partners, links Chinese government-linked actors tracked as Flax Typhoon (Integrity Tech / Ethereal Panda / Red Juliett) to campaigns against US critical infrastructure and organizations in Southeast Asia, Africa, and North America. Initial access uses XSS credential-harvesting pages that deliver DiagTrack.exe malware combined with EBurst password spraying against Exchange. Actors maintain persistence with SoftEther VPN clients disguised as conhost.exe or dllhost.exe for stealthy remote access. Post-access activity includes Curlc4.txt and office-cli email collection paired with DCSync credential harvesting via DC.exe.

The advisory matters because it documents hands-on intrusion combined with automated reconnaissance rather than generic malware distribution. The multi-agency attribution (FBI, NSA, UK, Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand, Spain) and named infrastructure targets elevate it above typical threat-intel marketing. Watch for any CISA KEV additions or vendor detection updates tied to DiagTrack.exe and the disguised SoftEther clients, and for confirmation of which US critical-infrastructure sectors have been impacted.
