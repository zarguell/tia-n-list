Eclypsium launched InfraTrust and the monthly InfraTrust Pulse report to prioritize infrastructure, firmware, networking and edge device vulnerabilities by exploitability, exposure and real-world risk rather than CVSS alone. The July 2026 inaugural report counted 61 advisories from 14 vendors, 6 critical advisories and 26 remotely exploitable unauthenticated flaws, with attention to routers, VPNs and firewalls targeted by Russian and Chinese state actors.

The November report covers October 15 through November 19 and counts 373 new advisories from 23 infrastructure vendors, spanning 3,880 vulnerabilities. Ninety-eight advisories were critical and 38 included flaws in CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. In 33 of those 38, the exploited flaw was in bundled third-party software rather than vendor-written code, most often PHP, Node.js or OpenSSH. The report's point is that an upstream patch does not end exposure until the infrastructure vendor rebuilds and ships its product.

PHP2Shell is the given example: CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 are now exploited against Palo Alto Networks and Check Point firewalls, where attackers chained them with other flaws for root access and Check Point reported destructive attacks. Watch for vendor rebuild timelines for bundled components and follow-on exploitation tied to the remaining KEV-bearing advisories.
