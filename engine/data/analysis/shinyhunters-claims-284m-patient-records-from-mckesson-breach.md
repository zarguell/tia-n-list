McKesson, one of the largest healthcare and pharmaceutical distributors in the United States, disclosed a cybersecurity incident on August 25, 2026. The company confirmed unauthorized access to third-party applications and exfiltration of data in a Form 8-K filing with the SEC. The ShinyHunters extortion group has claimed responsibility and says it stole 284 million patient data records. McKesson has activated incident response protocols and launched an investigation, but says it has not yet determined whether the incident is material or will have financial impact.

The scale of the claimed breach, if accurate, would make this one of the largest healthcare data breaches on record. McKesson supplies medicines, medical supplies, technology, and services to healthcare providers and pharmacies nationwide, meaning the compromised data could span patients across numerous provider networks. The involvement of ShinyHunters, an extortion group known for targeting major organizations and demanding ransom, suggests the stolen data will likely be used for extortion or sold on dark web markets.

McKesson says the investigation is in early stages and has directed customers to its cybersecurity incident page for updates. The company's careful language about materiality suggests legal and regulatory scrutiny will follow. Healthcare organizations remain prime targets for data theft due to the high value of patient records on underground markets, and a breach of this magnitude would be among the most significant healthcare security incidents in recent years.
