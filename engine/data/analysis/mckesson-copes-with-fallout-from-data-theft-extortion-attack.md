McKesson, which distributes roughly one-third of all pharmaceuticals used in North America, disclosed a cyberattack on August 29 via regulatory filing after ShinyHunters added the company to its data-leak site. The intrusion ran from August 21 to 25 before detection, with attackers hitting third-party applications and exfiltrating data from customers in the oncology, multispecialty, and medical-surgical business units. McKesson reported $403.4 billion in revenue for the year ending March 2026.

McKesson says distribution centers and operations remain functional, describing the impact as temporary service interruptions rather than operational shutdown. The company did not confirm ShinyHunters' claims or answer questions about the group's involvement. ShinyHunters is a well-established cybercrime operation known for large-scale data theft followed by extortion, not encrypting systems but threatening to publish stolen data unless paid.

This is a significant healthcare supply chain event. McKesson's role as a pharmaceutical distributor means a breach affecting its customer data ripples across hospital networks and patient populations. The four-day dwell time before detection on August 25 is notable for a company of this size. No CVEs are involved since the attack targeted third-party applications rather than a specific vulnerability. Watch for ShinyHunters' typical pattern: a countdown timer followed by data publication if McKesson refuses to pay.
