Ardit Kutleshi, a Kosovar national who created and ran the Rydox cybercrime marketplace, pleaded guilty in a US court to identity theft and money laundering conspiracy charges. He faces a mandatory two years for aggravated identity theft plus up to 20 years for money laundering, with sentencing set for February 9, 2027.

Rydox operated from February 2016 until its December 2024 takedown and served roughly 18,000 users. Sellers listed more than 321,372 products: stolen names, addresses, Social Security numbers, credit card data, and login credentials, along with phishing kits, stealer logs, and spamming tools. The marketplace logged over 7,600 sales and generated at least $230,000 in revenue.

The takedown was international. Kosovo police arrested Kutleshi and Jetmir Kutleshi in December 2024, Albania's SPAK arrested Shpend Sokoli, the US seized the Rydox.cc domain, and servers in Kuala Lumpur were taken with the Royal Malaysia Police, along with roughly $225,000 in cryptocurrency. The Kutleshi brothers were extradited to the US in early April 2025.

The plea closes the marketplace case but the parallel BlackDB.cc prosecution, whose admin Liridon Masurica was extradited in May 2025, continues from the same FBI-led Kosovo operation. Watch Kutleshi's sentencing and whether buyers and sellers on the marketplaces face follow-on charges.
