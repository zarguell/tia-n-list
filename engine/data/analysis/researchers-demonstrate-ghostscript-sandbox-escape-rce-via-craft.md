Upload2Shell is CVE-2026-125396, a Ghostscript -dSAFER sandbox escape rooted in the .putgstringcopy procedure in gs_fonts.ps. CIRCL scores it 8.5 under CVSS 4.0 and lists Ghostscript releases before 10.08.0 as affected. Paulos Yibelo reported the bug to Artifex on 1 September 2026 with a self-contained reproduction; Artifex shipped 10.08.0 on 8 September, the CVE record appeared on 9 November, and pwn.ai published the research on 21 November. Upstream release notes said only that the release addressed potential security issues.

The technical report describes a Type 1 font-loader use-after-free that resumes execution before a bound .forceput, leaving a local string referenced from systemdict after restore. Allocator reuse gives native read and write plus controlled dispatch while -dSAFER remains enabled. A published check returned 18 where 29 11 mul should have returned 319. The flaw reproduced on official 10.07.1 and 10.07.0 and on Google production 10.05.0. Testing across Pillow, imageio, scikit-image, PHP Imagick, libvips, Qt KImageFormats and Matplotlib recorded 145 benign calls across 29 image APIs in two controlled runs.

pwn.ai documents command execution through Debian 13 CUPS print jobs, Roundcube attachments, public WordPress upload forms including Forminator and Elementor, MediaWiki, DokuWiki, Mayan EDMS, ResourceSpace and Plesk, plus a Google document-renderer case. GBHackers reported the same finding on 23 November as a risk to WordPress sites that generate PDF previews through Imagick with a vulnerable Ghostscript backend. Hunt targets named in the research include /var/tmp/GS_CUPS_PIPE_RCE and PATH_CONTROL_BYTES_AFTER=0,0,0,0.

Canonical fixed the bug in USN-8949-1 on 21 October across seven Ubuntu releases, including 10.02.1~dfsg1-0ubuntu7.10 for 24.04 LTS and 9.55.0~dfsg1-0ubuntu5.15 for 22.04 LTS. As of 22 November, Amazon Linux still listed a pending fix, Debian 12 remained vulnerable and the Red Hat tracking bug was open. Any service that passes user-controlled files to Ghostscript through print, mail or upload pipelines deserves version checks and a review of the published markers.
