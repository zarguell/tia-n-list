Upload2Shell is a Ghostscript sandbox escape tracked as CVE-2026-125396, a -dSAFER bypass in the .putgstringcopy procedure in gs_fonts.ps that calls the privileged .forceput. Paulos Yibelo reported the Type 1 font-loader use-after-free to Artifex on 1 September 2026 with a self-contained reproduction. It recovered native read and write after restore, reproduced on official Ghostscript 10.07.1 and 10.07.0 and on Google production 10.05.0, and changed the result of `29 11 mul` from 319 to 18 after the native pointer swap. CIRCL lists CVSS 4.0 8.5 with a local attack vector; Ghostscript 10.08.0 shipped on 8 September and the CVE was published on 9 November.

pwn.ai's evidence index records command execution through Debian 13 CUPS, Roundcube, Forminator, Elementor, WordPress Core, MediaWiki, DokuWiki, Mayan EDMS, ResourceSpace and Plesk, with a live HTTP-to-Ghostscript handoff in Gradio. The technical post traces delivery through Pillow, imageio, scikit-image, PHP Imagick, libvips, Qt KImageFormats and Matplotlib, and records 145 benign calls across 29 image APIs in two controlled runs. Markers named in that post include /var/tmp/GS_CUPS_PIPE_RCE and PATH_CONTROL_BYTES_AFTER=0,0,0,0.

The practical risk is that ordinary PDF, PostScript and image uploads reach Ghostscript indirectly through preview and conversion paths, so the rendering process's permissions become the ceiling for code execution. Canonical published USN-8949-1 on 21 October for seven Ubuntu releases, listing fixed packages 10.02.1~dfsg1-0ubuntu7.10 for 24.04 LTS and 9.55.0~dfsg1-0ubuntu5.15 for 22.04 LTS.

Watch distribution patch state rather than the upstream release. As of 22 November, reporting said Amazon Linux still listed a pending fix, Debian 12 remained vulnerable and Red Hat's tracking bug was open, and a 23 November post said the 10.08.0 fix was still moving through distributions.
