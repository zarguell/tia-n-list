Upload2Shell is CVE-2026-125396, a Ghostscript -dSAFER sandbox bypass in the .putgstringcopy procedure in gs_fonts.ps. CIRCL records a CVSS 4.0 score of 8.5 and notes that Ghostscript before 10.08.0 is affected. Paulos Yibelo reported the bug to Artifex on 1 September 2026 with a self-contained reproduction; Artifex released 10.08.0 on 8 September, the CVE appeared on 9 November, and pwn.ai published the research on 21 November. Upstream release notes said only that the release addressed potential security issues.

The technical report describes a Type 1 font-loader use-after-free that resumes execution before a bound .forceput, leaving a local string referenced from systemdict after restore. Allocator reuse then gives native read and write and controlled dispatch, with commands running while -dSAFER is still enabled. A published check returned 18 where 29 11 mul should have returned 319. The finding reproduced against official 10.07.1 and 10.07.0 and against Google production 10.05.0. The researcher recorded 145 benign calls across 29 image APIs in two controlled runs through Pillow, imageio, scikit-image, PHP Imagick, libvips, Qt KImageFormats and Matplotlib.

pwn.ai documents command execution through Debian 13 CUPS print jobs, Roundcube attachments, public WordPress upload forms including Forminator and Elementor, MediaWiki, DokuWiki, Mayan EDMS, ResourceSpace and Plesk, plus a Google document-renderer case. Published markers include /var/tmp/GS_CUPS_PIPE_RCE and PATH_CONTROL_BYTES_AFTER=0,0,0,0, which give defenders concrete hunt targets.

Canonical fixed Ubuntu in USN-8949-1 on 21 October across seven releases, including 10.02.1~dfsg1-0ubuntu7.10 for 24.04 LTS and 9.55.0~dfsg1-0ubuntu5.15 for 22.04 LTS. As of 22 November, Amazon Linux still listed a pending fix, Debian 12 remained vulnerable and the Red Hat tracking bug was open. The practical risk is any service that passes user uploads to Ghostscript, even when the application itself never invokes it directly.
