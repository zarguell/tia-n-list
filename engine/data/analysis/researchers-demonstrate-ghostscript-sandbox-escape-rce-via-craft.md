Upload2Shell is CVE-2026-125396, a Ghostscript -dSAFER sandbox bypass in the .putgstringcopy procedure in gs_fonts.ps, which calls the privileged .forceput. CIRCL records a CVSS 4.0 score of 8.5 and affects Ghostscript before 10.08.0. Paulos Yibelo filed the bug with Artifex on 1 September 2026 with a self-contained reproduction; Artifex released 10.08.0 on 8 September, the CVE was published on 9 November and pwn.ai published its research on 21 November. Upstream release notes only said the release addressed potential security issues.

The technical report describes a Type 1 font-loader use-after-free that resumes execution before a bound .forceput, leaving a local string referenced from systemdict after restore. Allocator reuse then yields native read and write and controlled dispatch, with commands running while -dSAFER is still enabled; a reproduced check returned 18 where `29 11 mul` should have returned 319. Delivery paths include Pillow, imageio, scikit-image, PHP Imagick, libvips, Qt KImageFormats and Matplotlib, and the researcher records 145 benign calls across 29 image APIs in two controlled runs.

pwn.ai lists command execution through Debian 13 CUPS print jobs, Roundcube attachments, public WordPress upload forms including Forminator and Elementor, MediaWiki, DokuWiki, Mayan EDMS, ResourceSpace and Plesk, plus a Google document-renderer case. Published markers include /var/tmp/GS_CUPS_PIPE_RCE and PATH_CONTROL_BYTES_AFTER=0,0,0,0. Canonical patched Ubuntu in USN-8949-1 on 21 October across seven releases, with 10.02.1~dfsg1-0ubuntu7.10 for 24.04 LTS and 9.55.0~dfsg1-0ubuntu5.15 for 22.04 LTS. As of 22 November, Amazon Linux still had a pending fix, Debian 12 remained vulnerable and the Red Hat tracking bug was open; downstream patch state is the thing to watch.
