A Chinese-speaking actor used the open-source ARTEX AI penetration-testing suite paired with Claude agents to hit South Korean banks in late September through early October 2026. CrowdStrike linked the activity through open directories at a Hong Kong IP that contained Claude Code session histories, Claude memory files, and ARTEX configuration files. Targets included Shinhan Bank, KB Kookmin Bank, Hana Bank, and Yegaram Savings Bank; exposed data covered personal and credit-card details, with some outages. The actor used DeepSeek v4.1-flash as the primary LLM backend and supplemented it with GLM-5.3 and Grok 4.6. Overlapping targets and shared AI-tool artifacts — including a resume draft — gave researchers high-confidence attribution. The South Korean government convened an emergency meeting in response. No CVE is assigned; this is an operational intelligence report, not a vulnerability disclosure. Watch for additional victim disclosures and whether the identified infrastructure (@YY520CN, Telegram presence) leads to attribution actions.
