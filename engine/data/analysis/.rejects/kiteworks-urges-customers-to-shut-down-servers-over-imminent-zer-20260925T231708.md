Kiteworks has written to customers warning of concrete indications of an imminent zero-day attack against its servers and urging them to shut the servers down. BleepingComputer reports the guidance as a 6-hour shutdown window over potential zero-day attacks. No CVE, no patch, and no exploited-vulnerability detail are public at this point.

A vendor telling customers to power off rather than wait for a patch is a strong signal that it believes exploitation is imminent and that mitigation in place is insufficient. Kiteworks deployments typically sit in sensitive document-transfer paths, so exposed instances are high-value targets for theft and extortion.

What to watch: the advisory with affected versions and indicators, a CVE and fixed release, confirmation of in-the-wild exploitation, and whether attackers are the same crew that recently hit other managed file transfer products.
