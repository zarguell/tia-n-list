The US Treasury's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) has cut the Seychelles-based MEXC Global crypto exchange off from the US financial system, citing sanctions evasion and links to cybercrime networks. MEXC, founded in 2018 and serving over 40 million users across more than 170 countries, allegedly allowed at least $16 billion in illicit transactions tied to Iranian, North Korean and Russian sanctions evasion between May 2021 and May 2026.

FinCEN says MEXC continued processing transactions with sanctioned Russian entities Garantex and Grinex as recently as June 2026, and facilitated at least $226 million for the Russia-based, OFAC-sanctioned A7 sanctions-evasion network. The order also cites roughly $1 billion in direct transactions with more than 40 identified Iranian cryptocurrency exchangers, some under US and international sanctions, plus ransomware, scam and darknet-market activity. The action rests on deficient anti-money-laundering controls rather than a single incident.
