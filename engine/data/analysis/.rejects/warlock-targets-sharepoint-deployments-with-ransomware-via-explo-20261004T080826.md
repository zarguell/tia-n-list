Warlock, a suspected China-linked actor, targets SharePoint deployments with ransomware by weaponizing both known and new SharePoint flaws, disabling security tooling before deployment. The campaign exploits unpatched or recently patched vulnerabilities to deploy ransomware in enterprise environments.

The link to a China-nexus actor and the use of SharePoint as an entry point raises the risk for organizations running unpatched SharePoint instances. Monitor for new CVEs tied to this activity, patching timelines, and any ransomware group attribution updates.
