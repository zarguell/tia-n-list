The Hacker News reports a newly discovered, unpatched flaw in OnePlus devices that lets already installed Android apps silently gain root without permissions or user interaction. Once rooted, a malicious app takes complete control of the phone with no visible cues to the user.

The practical problem is that the flaw requires no special privileges and no consent, so an ordinary-looking app can escalate itself and then persist at full privilege. Because the flaw is unpatched and no CVE is named in the coverage, OnePlus owners have no vendor fix to apply yet and defenders have no signature or identifier to hunt for.

What to watch: an OnePlus acknowledgment and advisory, CVE assignment, the list of affected models and OS versions, and any evidence the bug is being used in the wild rather than only demonstrated by researchers.
