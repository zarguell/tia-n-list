ASEC identified phishing campaigns disguised as requests to confirm purchase orders or submit project quotes. The emails ask recipients to review attachments and reply with inventory or pricing feedback, then prompt them to download compressed or DOCX files. The campaign targets business communication workflows rather than broad consumer email, which increases the likelihood that recipients will open attachments in professional contexts.

These are standard business-targeted phishing operations using familiar document lures. The defense is user awareness combined with email filtering for compressed attachments and macro-enabled documents from external senders. No specific CVEs or exploits are mentioned; the risk is credential theft or initial access through malware delivery.
