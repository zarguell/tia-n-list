French cold-storage units maintained by Coolworld for the national blood service were targeted overnight 1-2 November. Attackers reprogrammed temperatures from -30°C to 110°C, affecting blood samples and frozen plasma. An on-duty technician detected the rise before operational impact; the Ministry of Health stated zero impact. The incident was reported by Le Canard enchaîné and DataBreaches.Net, with Anadolu Agency confirming.

This is a targeted physical-environment attack on healthcare supply infrastructure, not a data breach. The attacker path appears to be through the maintenance provider Coolworld rather than the blood service directly. Watch for attribution, any ransomware or extortion link, and whether other Coolworld facilities were hit. The zero-impact claim should be verified against inventory audits of affected samples.
