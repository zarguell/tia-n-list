AhnLab says some Q-Dir and DesktopOK installers distributed from SoftwareOK's official website were tampered with. Its investigation found malicious files were produced both for direct downloads and during the update process, turning a trusted distribution channel into the delivery mechanism. A Greek report describes the payload as a Python-based remote access trojan.

The incident matters because victims had no obvious reason to distrust files obtained from the vendor's own site. Users and administrators should verify the origin and integrity of existing Q-Dir or DesktopOK installers rather than assuming an official download page guarantees a clean build. Watch for ASEC or SoftwareOK to identify affected files, hashes, the compromise window and the point at which official downloads were restored.
