US prosecutors charged Zohar Pinhasi, owner of MonsterCloud, with wire fraud for operating a fake ransomware recovery service. The indictment says Pinhasi claimed proprietary decryption capabilities but instead paid ransoms to affiliates and billed clients inflated fees — over $8 million in ransom payments and over $19 million in client charges. A single transaction involved an $8,200 ransom that was resold to a victim for about $150,000. Pinhasi is a US and Israeli national; if convicted he faces decades in prison.
