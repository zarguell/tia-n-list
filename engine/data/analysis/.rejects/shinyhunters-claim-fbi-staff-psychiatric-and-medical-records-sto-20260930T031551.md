ShinyHunters claimed to have stolen psychiatric and medical records of FBI staff, circulating limited samples that include personal contact information, family details, office assignments, and agency specialties. The FBI confirmed it is investigating but did not confirm the full scope or directly attribute the breach. The FBI jobs site was temporarily defaced and remains offline as of the report date.

A new update (FBI dropping public threat videos after Dutch police arrested alleged ringleader Pepijn van der Stap, a convicted hacker previously working at Hadrian and volunteering at DIVD) adds enforcement context. The Dutch arrest occurred days before the data theft escalation, and remaining ShinyHunters members escalated attacks against the FBI and Russian ransomware group Cl0p afterward. A separate update notes ShinyHunters described the confrontation as a "marketing campaign" and claimed it never intended to publish the stolen data.

Watch for FBI confirmation of data types compromised and any Dutch law enforcement updates on van der Stap's role. The combination of enforcement action and escalating data-theft claims increases exposure risk for FBI personnel.
