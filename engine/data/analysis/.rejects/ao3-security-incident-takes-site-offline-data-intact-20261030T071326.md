AO3 (Archive of Our Own) went offline after a security incident. The site posted an incident notice stating expected downtime of two to four weeks, that all data remains intact, and that staff do not believe user information was stolen though they have not ruled it out. No CVEs or attacker attribution are given. The event is a confirmed service disruption at a major fanfiction archive, relevant to users who rely on the platform and to observers tracking attacks on community infrastructure. Watch for updates from otwstatus.org on restoration timelines and any confirmation of data access.
