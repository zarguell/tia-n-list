Two legitimate MemTensor packages on npm and PyPI were compromised to push sckit, a Go-based credential stealer targeting developer environments. The supply chain compromise was reported by The Hacker News and corroborated through Mastodon references. The attacker used the legitimate package names to distribute the malicious payload, which aims to steal credentials from systems where the packages are installed. This aligns with a broader trend of supply chain attacks where trusted package repositories are used to distribute malware without altering well-known high-download packages.

Because the event content is brief, specific IOCs, version numbers, and the exact mechanism of compromise remain unclear. Organizations using MemTensor packages should audit installations, rotate any credentials that may have been exposed during the window of compromise, and monitor for Go-based credential stealer behavior. Watch for detailed technical reports with IOCs and removal timelines from npm and PyPI.
