BleepingComputer reports that former US Air Force members have been sentenced to prison for their role in business email compromise attacks. The case is notable because the defendants wore the uniform before turning to BEC, the fraud class that the FBI consistently ranks among the costliest cybercrime categories by financial loss.

The report adds to a run of BEC enforcement outcomes where insiders or former government-affiliated individuals used familiarity with institutional processes to make invoice and payment redirection schemes more convincing. Watch the sentencing details and charges in the full report, and treat it as a data point in the wider trend of BEC prosecutions reaching conviction, which the FBI's IC3 warnings have been pushing organizations to take seriously.
