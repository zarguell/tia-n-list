Oracle Health’s legacy Cerner breach now affects nearly 20 million people, according to a Texas attorney general filing cited by Bloomberg — well above earlier notifications. Attackers used stolen credentials to access an old server around January 22, 2025, and data was copied to a remote server. Notification started in March 2025 and was delayed at federal law enforcement request. Oracle declined to confirm the tally. The revised figure makes this one of the largest health-sector breaches reported this year. Watch for regulatory filings and additional state disclosures.
