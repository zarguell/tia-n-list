Federal agencies warned Thursday that hackers are targeting refrigeration systems worldwide, urging operators to secure equipment whose recent failures have spoiled food and created fire hazards. CISA, the FBI, and the departments of Health and Human Services, Agriculture, and Transportation issued joint guidance warning that attackers are targeting electronic temperature controls at grocery stores and cold storage facilities. The warning follows Nextgov/FCW reporting that U.S. officials suspect Iran-linked hackers were behind freezer malfunctions at military bases across the country, beginning around late August and affecting roughly six commissaries.

The Kremlin has denied involvement in the supermarket refrigerator cyberattacks, according to Romanian media outlet stirileprotv.ro. Reuters initially reported that the Pentagon was treating the freezer incidents as possible cyberattacks. Separately, French blood banks were hit by a cyberattack around the same period. The convergence of suspected state-sponsored activity against cold-chain infrastructure raises concerns about food safety, public health, and critical civilian systems.
