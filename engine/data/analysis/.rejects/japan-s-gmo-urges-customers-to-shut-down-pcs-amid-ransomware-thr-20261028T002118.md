The GMO Group urged customers to disconnect systems after a threat actor breached its Onamae Desktop Cloud and threatened a ransomware attack. The company advised shutting down computers to prevent the attack from spreading. No CVEs are tied to this incident.

This is a live operational threat rather than a disclosed vulnerability, which limits predictive value but confirms active attacker presence. Watch for follow-up reports confirming whether ransomware was deployed, which systems were affected, and whether GMO releases a post-incident analysis with attribution or technical indicators.
