A Kubernetes YAML misconfiguration can grant full GCP organization ownership because Google treats certain service-account bindings as working as designed. Intel Threadlinqs reports 9 detections and 7 IOCs. The failure mode is structural, not a product vulnerability, but attackers can exploit it by injecting malicious YAML that inherits high-privilege roles. Audit GCP Kubernetes engine bindings and restrict service-account scopes at the organization level.
