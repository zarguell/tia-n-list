Trezor warned of a phishing campaign targeting crypto owners after a data breach at an email marketing provider exposed customer contact information. The breach is the second for Trezor involving an email provider. Attackers are sending messages that reference the breach to establish credibility and trick users into revealing credentials. The original reporting from Killbait and TechCrunch describes hundreds of thousands of affected users. Users should verify communications through official Trezor channels, avoid clicking links in unsolicited emails, and confirm any wallet-related requests through independent verification rather than email links.
