The City of Vicksburg, Mississippi took its computer systems offline after a ransomware attack. Mayor Willis Thompson told The Vicksburg Post that the city disconnected its internet operations in response. In-person utility payments may be delayed while systems are down, but emergency response and utility services continue.

Municipal ransomware of this pattern usually means disrupted revenue collection and records access for weeks, even when core services limp along. For a city the size of Vicksburg, manual processes around utility billing are the immediate pain, and restoration timelines depend on backup hygiene the city has not described publicly.

Worth watching: whether a ransomware group claims the attack and leaks data, how long systems stay offline, and whether the city discloses whether data was exfiltrated before encryption.
