FIFA confirmed a cyberattack in which hackers gained unauthorised access to a limited set of personal and business information, working with law enforcement. The Handsome Boy Hacking Club (HBHC) group claimed to have stolen more than 12 GB of data from FIFA's Microsoft environment and attempted extortion before publishing the data on a cybercriminal forum; the group emerged recently and little is known about its members.

The incident shows even well-resourced international organizations with small staff footprints remain targets for extortion groups, and the published dataset allegedly includes identity and immigration documents, financial and legal records, and governance information.

Watch for confirmation of the full data scope and any follow-up action by the HBHC group against other sports bodies.
