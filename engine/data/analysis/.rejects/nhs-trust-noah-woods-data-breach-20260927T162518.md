NHS Trust Noah Woods data breach. Ten staff at East Suffolk and North Essex NHS Foundation Trust have been suspended or removed from duty after unauthorized access to digital medical records of three-year-old Noah Woods. The trust launched an urgent investigation led by deputy chief medical officer Dr Martin Mansfield. The breach involves access to the child's digital health records; the exact exposure scope and whether data was exfiltrated remain under review. NHS data breaches involving pediatric records are high-sensitivity incidents given regulatory obligations under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act. The staff removals suggest internal access controls failed or insider involvement is suspected. Watch for the investigation outcome, regulatory notification to the ICO, and whether the trust confirms any exfiltration beyond unauthorized viewing.
