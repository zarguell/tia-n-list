mSecure (mSeven Software) emailed current and former customers on November 3 instructing them to change passwords, reset two-factor authentication, and remove passkeys following a security disclosure. The scope of exposed data, whether the vault or only account metadata was affected, and the timeline of discovery have not been fully detailed in the available event content. Because the advisory reached both current and ex-customers, the potential footprint extends beyond active subscribers.

Users should treat this as a mandatory rotation event for any account tied to mSecure and for any other accounts that shared credentials with the manager. Watch for a more detailed post-incident report clarifying whether encrypted vaults were accessed, whether master passwords or passkeys were involved, and what specific vulnerability or intrusion triggered the disclosure. Until then, assume the worst-case exposure window includes any data present at the time of compromise.
