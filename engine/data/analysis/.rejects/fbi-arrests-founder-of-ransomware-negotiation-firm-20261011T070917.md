The FBI arrested Edward Dubrovsky, co-founder of Canadian ransomware-negotiation firm CYPFER, in Pennsylvania. KrebsOnSecurity links him to assistance for the ShinyHunters group, which recently stole sensitive data on thousands of FBI agents. Dubrovsky was visiting for the Cyber Risk Summit in Philadelphia. The New York Times reported the arrest; FBI Director Kash Patel confirmed it via statement. A source says the ShinyHunters investigation is centralized at an FBI field office in Texas.

This is significant because it connects a ransomware-negotiation business co-founder to a group that targeted federal agents. It also raises questions about insider access within the negotiation industry. Watch for formal charges, court filings, and whether additional CYPFER staff are implicated. The Times and Krebs reports agree on the core facts but do not fully name the suspect — that attribution should firm up in the docket.
