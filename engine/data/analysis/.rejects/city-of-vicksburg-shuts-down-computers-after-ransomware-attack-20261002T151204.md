The City of Vicksburg, Mississippi, disconnected its computer systems after a ransomware attack. Mayor Willis Thompson told The Vicksburg Post the city shut down internet operations as a precaution. Emergency response and utility service continue, but in-person utility payments may be delayed. Joseph Topping reported the shutdown through DataBreaches.Net. The incident is a standard municipal ransomware response: isolate systems to contain spread, accept service disruption. No specific actor or ransomware family is named in the available reports. Watch for confirmation of the strain, whether data was exfiltrated, and restoration timelines from city officials.
