The GMO Group urged customers to disconnect systems and shut down PCs after a threat actor who breached Onamae Desktop Cloud threatened a ransomware attack. The company’s cloud desktop environment appears to be the initial access point, with the attacker setting a specific timeline for the ransomware deployment. Customers were told to physically disconnect from the internet rather than rely solely on endpoint protections.

This is a rare public warning that treats an active intrusion as an imminent ransomware event rather than a past breach. The key question is whether the attacker’s timeline holds and whether other Onamae customers are affected. Watch for confirmation of encryption or data theft, and whether GMO discloses the entry vector beyond the desktop cloud compromise.
