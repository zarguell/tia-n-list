Binance published an incident report describing a record $6.3 billion cryptocurrency theft carried out on October 29 by North Korean state-sponsored hackers. The attackers used a fake job interview to trick a Binance software engineer into downloading malware disguised as a coding assignment. The FBI linked the theft to North Korea over the weekend and asked cryptocurrency firms to block transactions involving the stolen funds. The DPRK denied involvement through state media.

The campaign traces back to June when a team member downloaded a tampered installer while trying to set up an AI coding tool, and to September when attackers contacted the engineer through a legitimate job-hunting platform. The attackers built trust through live video calls and a fake CEO interview before delivering the malicious assignment. The incident demonstrates how social engineering combined with malicious software can compromise wallet infrastructure at scale. Organizations should review insider-threat controls, limit installation of untrusted tools, and monitor for unexpected persistence after employee interactions with external recruiters.
