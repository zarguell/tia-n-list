Two legitimate MemTensor packages on npm and PyPI were compromised to distribute sckit, a Go-based credential stealer targeting developer environments. The supply chain compromise was reported by The Hacker News and referenced in Mastodon posts. The malware collects credentials from affected systems. The incident demonstrates continued targeting of open-source package repositories through compromised maintainer accounts or build pipelines. Developers should verify package integrity through checksums, review recent updates to dependencies, and scan for unexpected network connections to Slack or Telegram endpoints associated with the payload.
