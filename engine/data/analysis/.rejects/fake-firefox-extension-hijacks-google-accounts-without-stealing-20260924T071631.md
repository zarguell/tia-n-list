A malicious Firefox extension posing as a PDF identity-verification utility targets Google accounts through session-cookie theft. The campaign is notable for its stealth: the initial add-on contains no obvious malicious URLs or credential-stealing code, allowing it to pass basic static analysis. Malicious functionality is delivered post-install through a payload that automates account-takeover activity without stealing passwords directly.

The new event confirms the campaign's evolution from a single source (gbhackers.com) to include socket.dev reporting, increasing the story's source breadth. The attack relies on session hijacking rather than credential interception, making standard password-change responses less effective. Users should audit installed Firefox extensions, remove any unverified PDF or identity-verification add-ons, and review active Google account sessions for unrecognized devices.
