The Dutch cybersecurity nonprofit DIVD reported an AI-driven intrusion described as loud and very messy, with evidence that the attacker exploited a vulnerability during autonomous operation. The organization scans the internet for vulnerable systems and notifies owners; it had operated for seven years without a breach before this incident. DIVD informed the Dutch data protection authority and the National Cyber Security Center, and launched an investigation with police involvement.

The attacker used an agentic AI agent rather than a conventional manual intrusion, leaving extensive traces that helped DIVD reconstruct events. The exact vulnerability exploited and the full purpose or impact remain unclear; DIVD withheld some details to protect the ongoing investigation and avoid putting other victims at risk. The group noted that this is a novel modus operandi not previously seen in their experience.

Watch for the investigation outcome, any named vulnerability or CVE linked to the entry point, and whether the agentic-AI technique is replicated against other nonprofits or scanning organizations. No confirmed victim data exposure has been reported beyond the intrusion itself.
