The City of Vicksburg, Mississippi shut down computer systems after a ransomware attack. Mayor Willis Thompson told The Vicksburg Post the city disconnected internet operations. The disruption may delay in-person utility payments, while emergency response and utility service continue. Source reporting from Joseph Topping at DataBreaches.Net links the incident to ransomware. No CVE or actor attribution is named in available content. Watch for confirmation of the ransomware strain and restoration timeline.
