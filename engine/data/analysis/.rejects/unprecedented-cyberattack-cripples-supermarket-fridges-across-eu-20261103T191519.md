A coordinated cyberattack on October 11-12, 2026 forced refrigeration controllers at hundreds of European supermarkets into forced defrost, raising temperatures to 50°C and spoiling food. Affected chains included Intermarché, Coopérative U, Carrefour, Monoprix, E.Leclerc, and others across France, with a second wave on November 2 hitting stores in Belgium, Sweden, Luxembourg, Estonia, Ireland, and the Netherlands. The controllers were mostly legacy Danfoss AK-SM 800 and AK-SC 355 units that no longer receive security updates; CERT-FR also expanded alerts to Copeland and Dixell XWEB systems.

Attackers exploited known vulnerabilities, including CVE-2025-41450, CVE-2025-41451, and CVE-2025-41452 disclosed by Claroty in August 2026, which cover authentication bypass and command injection in Danfoss controllers. The incident shows how unpatched industrial refrigeration controllers, exposed to remote management interfaces, can be turned into physical-disruption weapons. Retailers should inventory refrigeration OT assets, replace end-of-life controllers, and segment management interfaces from public exposure.
