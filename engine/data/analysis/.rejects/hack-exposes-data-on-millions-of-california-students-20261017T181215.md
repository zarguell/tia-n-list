Hackers accessed student records through a breach at Educational Testing Service (ETS), a contractor for the California Department of Education. The stolen data includes names, addresses, demographic information and disability status for millions of current and former students over the past three school years, with reports citing roughly 7 million affected. The LA Times and CDE notice describe the intrusion as unauthorized access to an ETS SFTP site via a compromised login, not a direct state system breach. The exposure of disability status raises heightened privacy concern beyond standard contact data. CDE distributed a notification to assist families, and the vendor (not the state) bears direct responsibility for the compromised file-transfer endpoint. No CVE is tied to this breach; the risk is data exposure rather than active exploitation of a software flaw. Schools and districts should expect follow-on identity-targeting attempts given the demographic depth of the records taken.
