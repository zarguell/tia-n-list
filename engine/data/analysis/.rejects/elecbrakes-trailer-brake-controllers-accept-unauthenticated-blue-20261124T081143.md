Researchers found that Elecbrakes EB2 and VM1 trailer brake controllers accept brake commands over Bluetooth Low Energy with no pairing, no PIN and no authentication. The payload is four plain bytes, and any Bluetooth device in range could apply the trailer brakes at any force and receive a confirmation notification. A Swedish report on 24 November repeated the core finding: the trailer brakes could be controlled over Bluetooth because of a vulnerability.

This is a physical safety issue rather than a data theft case. The events give no CVE, affected firmware range or vendor fix, so the confirmed scope is the two controller models and the unauthenticated command path. Watch for an Elecbrakes firmware response, for disclosure of the affected version range, and for any indication that the command can be replayed or extended beyond direct radio range.
