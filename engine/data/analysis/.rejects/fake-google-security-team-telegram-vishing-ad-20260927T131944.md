The Register reports a Telegram recruitment ad for a suspected Google Security Team voice-phishing operation. The ad instructs callers not to read scripts while providing the exact words. This confirms organized vishing recruitment through Telegram. The event is a direct primary-source reference with no aggregator exaggeration. The risk is operational: organized caller recruitment increases the scale and consistency of voice-phishing attacks. Organizations should reinforce employee awareness of unsolicited security calls and verify any security contact through official channels only.
