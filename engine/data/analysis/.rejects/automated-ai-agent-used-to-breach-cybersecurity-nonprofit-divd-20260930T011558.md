Dutch nonprofit DIVD, a volunteer vulnerability disclosure organization, reported it was breached by an autonomous AI agent after seven years without intrusion. DIVD described the attack as "loud and very, very messy" and said the agent exploited a vulnerability before running automated post-exploit actions. Evidence indicates agentic AI-powered behavior rather than a manual attacker. DIVD notified police, the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens), and the National Cyber Security Center. Full details are withheld to avoid influencing the investigation or endangering victims. The organization emphasized this is not its first intrusion, but the first where the modus operandi points to agentic automation. Watch for the investigation outcome and any disclosed vulnerability used for initial access.
