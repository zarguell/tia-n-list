DriveWealth, a brokerage infrastructure provider, disclosed a data breach after a social engineering campaign led to unauthorized access, and its fintech customers are disclosing downstream exposure. Stake said some customer data was exposed in the incident, and Hatch disclosed a similar exposure of customer information tied to the same DriveWealth breach.

The pattern matters because a single compromise of a backend brokerage platform propagates to every consumer brand built on it, each with its own customer base and notification obligations. Social engineering as the entry path also suggests the attackers targeted people and processes rather than a software vulnerability.

What to watch: the full customer list affected through DriveWealth, the specific data types confirmed exposed, whether financial accounts themselves were touched or only personal information, and regulator or state AG notifications as they land.
