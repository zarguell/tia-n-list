The UK's Cyber Security and Resilience Act targets high-risk technology suppliers in critical infrastructure, introducing supply-chain blocking measures. The act aligns organizational defenses with CISA-style critical controls and aims to reduce dependency on untrusted vendors.

This is a regulatory development rather than an active attack, but it signals tighter procurement scrutiny for security-critical vendors. Watch for implementation timelines, the list of designated high-risk suppliers, and how it affects existing contracts.
