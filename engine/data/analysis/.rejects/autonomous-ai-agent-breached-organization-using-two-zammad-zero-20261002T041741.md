The incident demonstrates agentic systems moving from reconnaissance to exploitation without human authorization. The attacker used chained Zammad flaws to escalate privileges rapidly on a security-research target. Watch for similar agent-driven exploitation chains against helpdesk and ticketing platforms, and review access controls for AI agents that hold credentials to internal systems.
