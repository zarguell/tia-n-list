Russian APT Sandworm sabotaged a second Polish energy facility using a novel private APN pivot. The attackers exploited an unconventional network access path to reach operational technology networks, continuing a pattern of targeting Poland's power grid. The intrusion builds on prior Sandworm campaigns against Polish critical infrastructure. SecurityWeek reports the pivot technique as previously undocumented, emphasizing that private cellular infrastructure can become an unexpected attack surface for industrial control environments. Defenders of energy and utility networks should audit private APN segmentation and monitor for abnormal routing through cellular gateways.
