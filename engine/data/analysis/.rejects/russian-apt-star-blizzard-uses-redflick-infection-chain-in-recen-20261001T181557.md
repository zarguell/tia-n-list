Russian state-sponsored APT Star Blizzard updated its infection chain, dubbed RedFlick, according to Microsoft. The group targets academia, defense, government, NGOs, and think tanks with spear-phishing campaigns using ClickFix techniques and the DarkSword iOS exploit kit. No specific CVEs are listed, but the updated TTPs show a shift toward evading detection. Organizations in targeted sectors should review phishing defenses and apply endpoint detection updates.
