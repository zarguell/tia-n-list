Unit 42 reports an AI-assisted espionage campaign (CL-UNK-1211) targeting government Linux infrastructure between October 2025 and April 2026. Attackers used AI-generated scripts, custom malware including SysGoRAT, and web shells to breach Linux servers, harvest administrator credentials, and establish persistent backdoors. The cluster remains unattributed under Unit 42's framework; researchers have not confirmed state sponsorship or financial motivation. The same campaign links to exploitation of BeyondTrust Remote Support zero-day CVE-2026-102764, which is being actively exploited in targeted espionage operations.

The campaign's use of AI-assisted scripting and Linux-focused malware represents a notable evolution in espionage tradecraft. Government infrastructure operators should treat the combined indicators—SysGoRAT deployment, credential theft patterns, and the BeyondTrust zero-day exploitation pathway—as an active, high-priority threat. Watch for additional attribution updates and CISA advisory revisions.
