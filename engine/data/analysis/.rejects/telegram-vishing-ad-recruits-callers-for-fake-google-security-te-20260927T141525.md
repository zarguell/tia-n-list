A Telegram recruitment post targets English-speaking callers for an apparent Google Security Team voice-phishing operation. The Register confirms the post tells recruits not to read scripts, then publishes the exact script. The event carries verified provenance to The Register. The operation uses Telegram for recruitment and distributes a script to callers. This indicates organized vishing infrastructure rather than isolated scams. Watch for additional recruitment channels, victim reports, and whether Google or law enforcement take action against the campaign.
