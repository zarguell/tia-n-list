Gigabud is a banking trojan that pairs with Vwork to clone a victim's banking app into an Android work profile, evading standard fraud checks. Group-IB's September 9 research confirmed the malware uses lures posing as airlines, tax offices, or government services to trick users into installing the cloned profile. Once inside the work profile, the cloned app operates with the same permissions as the legitimate version, allowing attackers to intercept transactions and steal credentials without triggering standard app-integrity alerts.

The new event from group-ib.com reinforces the technical mechanism: app cloning inside Android work profiles plus social-engineering lures. What to watch: whether financial institutions deploy work-profile detection or enhanced app-attestation to block cloned banking apps, and whether the malware expands beyond Android work profiles into other containerized environments.
