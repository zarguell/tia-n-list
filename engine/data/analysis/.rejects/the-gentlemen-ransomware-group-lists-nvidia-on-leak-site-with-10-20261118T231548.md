The Gentlemen ransomware group listed NVIDIA on its leak site on November 18, 2026, with a roughly 10-day countdown timer. The listing is a claim, not proof of a breach: reporting on the post says no supporting data was published and NVIDIA had not confirmed the incident as of the posts in this story.

Watch for a data sample or ransomware note from the group, a confirmation or denial from NVIDIA, and whether the countdown produces an actual leak. Until then, treat the claim as unverified extortion pressure rather than evidence of a confirmed compromise.
