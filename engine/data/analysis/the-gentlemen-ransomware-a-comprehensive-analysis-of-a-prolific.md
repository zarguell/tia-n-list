Acronis Threat Research Unit published an analysis of "The Gentlemen," a Russian-speaking ransomware-as-a-service group that emerged in mid-2025, reportedly after affiliates split from LockBit and Qilin. The group offers affiliates up to 90% of ransom revenue, claims over 800 victims across the US and Europe, and operates cross-platform variants targeting Windows, Linux, NAS, and BSD.

The group uses EDR-killing tooling, including a custom dropper abusing vulnerable Safetica ProcessMonitor and Lenovo BootRepair.sys drivers (BYOVD). A breach of the group's own infrastructure gave researchers visibility into affiliates. Claimed victims include LA Metro (with a nine-day deadline) and Sharp Office; additional victims appear on leak sites and ransomware tracking feeds.

Defenders should watch for BYOVD-based EDR evasion, monitor for the named vulnerable drivers, and treat the group's high affiliate payout as an incentive for rapid affiliate recruitment. The 800-victim claim is self-reported and unverified.
