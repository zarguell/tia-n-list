The Gentlemen ransomware-as-a-service operation is a Russian-speaking group that emerged in mid-2025, reportedly after affiliates split from LockBit and Qilin. Researchers documented a rapid attack timeline, with a median of about two days from initial access to encryption, and some incidents completing in under 24 hours. The group operates a double-extortion model: affiliates steal data, threaten publication, then deploy ransomware. By the end of July 2026, their leak site listed 683 victims, including 169 added during July alone. Affiliates gain access by exploiting exposed firewall vulnerabilities or using stolen VPN credentials, often moving laterally through RDP and storing tools in C:\PerfLogs.

The operation claims over 800 victims across the US and Europe and offers affiliates up to 90% of ransom revenue. Researchers also found cross-platform variants targeting Windows, Linux, NAS, and BSD, plus custom EDR-killing tooling using BYOVD techniques. A breach of the group's own infrastructure gave researchers rare visibility. The key defensive priority is patching exposed VPN and firewall devices, enforcing multi-factor authentication, and monitoring for RDP and PerfLogs abuse.
