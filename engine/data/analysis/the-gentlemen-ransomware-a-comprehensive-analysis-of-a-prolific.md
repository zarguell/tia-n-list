The Gentlemen operates as GOLD SHERWOOD, a Russian-speaking ransomware-as-a-service group active since mid-2025 that offers affiliates up to 90% of ransom revenue. The events reference cross-platform variants targeting Windows, Linux, NAS, and BSD, with attackers moving from initial access to encryption in under 24 hours and a median of about two days to deployment. A recent incident used a custom dropper abusing vulnerable Safetica ProcessMonitor and Lenovo BootRepair.sys drivers in a BYOVD chain, and the group had listed 683 victims by the end of July 2026. CVE-2025-70795 is tagged in the story record but the events do not describe a specific vulnerability; the group instead gains access through exposed firewall vulnerabilities and stolen VPN credentials, including an incident where multi-factor authentication was not enabled on a Fortinet SSL VPN.

This group matters because it combines extreme affiliate incentives with rapid operational tempo and explicit double-extortion. The events state 800+ claimed victims across the US and Europe and 169 added in July 2026 alone, placing The Gentlemen near the top of the RaaS victim-count rankings behind only Qilin. Their leak site had listed 683 victims by July's end, and they actively disable defenses after entry by abusing legitimate tools, storing payloads in C:\PerfLogs and moving laterally through RDP to file servers and domain controllers. The exposure profile is broad: organizations using Fortinet VPNs without MFA, NAS or BSD infrastructure, and environments lacking EDR resilience against BYOVD driver abuse face disproportionate risk because the playbook is optimized for speed rather than stealth.

Defenders should prioritize patching exposed firewall and VPN surfaces, enforcing MFA on all remote access, and hunting for tools staged in C:\PerfLogs alongside anomalous RDP sessions targeting domain controllers. The events mention custom droppers abusing Safetica ProcessMonitor and Lenovo BootRepair.sys drivers, so endpoint telemetry should flag unexpected driver loads from those vendors and any rapid backup-service termination followed by encryption. Network restrictions should segment NAS and BSD systems from standard RDP-accessible file-server segments, given the cross-platform ransomware variants described.

A verified breach claim from LA Metro (listed September 7, 2026, with a nine-day deadline) or confirmation of data release from Sharp Office would change the assessment, as would publication of a new CVE directly tied to the group's initial-access chain. If the group's own infrastructure breach yields additional affiliate tooling or victim lists, that visibility should be incorporated immediately.
