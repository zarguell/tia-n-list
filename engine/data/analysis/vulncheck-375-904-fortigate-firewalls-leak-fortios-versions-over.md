VulnCheck counted 375,904 internet-facing FortiGate hosts with the FGFM management port on TCP 541 exposed between 14 October and 12 November 2026. The FortiGate answers a connection with a TLS ClientHello rather than waiting as a server, so JA4 fingerprints place each host in one of six firmware generations spanning FortiOS 5.4 through 8.0.1. Generation 3 holds 110,078 hosts and includes anchors such as 7.0.13 and 7.2.4, branches where CVE-2024-23113 applies to 7.0.0 through 7.0.13 and 7.2.0 through 7.2.6. Fortinet rated that fgfmd format string RCE at CVSS 9.8 in FG-IR-24-029 on 8 February 2024 and recorded potential in-the-wild exploitation on 11 October 2024; this census is exposure, not compromise.

The operational gain is target selection without banner probing. Only 269 of 838,100 FortiOS hosts in the same data set advertise a version, and VulnCheck's researcher post put 160,171 hosts, 42.6 percent, in generations where every measured version has a known-exploited CVE. A passive handshake now substitutes for active version scanning against edge devices that enforce the perimeter for everything behind them. The 375,904 figure is also a floor: VulnCheck scanned port 541 over IPv4 only while its mitigation covers TCP 541 and 542 on both address families, so the real FGFM exposure surface is at least as large as the count.

Remove fgfm from WAN interfaces under config system interface and set allowaccess, then add a local-in policy that accepts FGFM only from the designated FortiManager peer. Block inbound TCP 541 and 542 from the internet on IPv4 and IPv6, and hunt in both directions because a live 7.6.2 image sends the same ClientHello outbound to FortiManager. Patch CVE-2024-23113 to FortiOS 7.4.3 or later, 7.2.7 or later, or 7.0.14 or later; the 25,640 hosts in Gen 1 and Gen 2 run FortiOS 5.4, 5.6 or early 6.0 and need replacement, not a patch. Stock Suricata JA4 logic and Snort ssl_state:client_hello do not fire when the FortiGate is the listener sending the handshake, so use VulnCheck byte-marker rules sid 4100001 through 4100007 and port-only fallback sid 4100006 keyed to source ports 541 and 542 and JA4_c markers such as ML-DSA 0904-0906 or the 7.4.12 pinned list starting 0203,0403,0503,0603,081a.

Two developments would change the assessment: a repeat scan showing the count holding or rising after the advisory, and telemetry tying CVE-2024-23113 exploitation attempts to this port 541 cohort rather than to the CVE in general. Those together would move the story from exposed attack surface to incident response. If FortiOS 7.6.8 proves that its runtime-populated TLS settings are administrator-configurable, the JA4 generations stop being a clean firmware proxy and the version estimate needs a new basis.
