Microsoft Defender Experts have mapped over 30 rotating domains tied to MacSync Stealer, a macOS-focused information stealer. The infrastructure supports the full attack chain: payload delivery, command-and-control, credential theft, data staging, and chunked exfiltration. The campaign begins with ClickFix social engineering lures that trick victims into pasting malicious commands into Terminal, launching an interactive zsh session that uses curl to fetch attacker payloads. Operators rapidly replace C2 domains after public exposure, which is why Microsoft pivoted to tracking recurring endpoint and network behaviors rather than relying on static domain IOCs. Earlier research from RST Cloud identified a smaller subset of the same infrastructure. The breadth of the domain rotation and the sophistication of the exfiltration pipeline suggest an operation with dedicated resources targeting macOS users specifically.
