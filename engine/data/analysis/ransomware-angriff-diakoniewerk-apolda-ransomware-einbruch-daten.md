Ransomware hit Diakoniewerk Apolda, a social services organization in Thuringia, Germany, with the attack reported by security-incidents.de on September 21, 2026. The incident involved intrusion and data theft. A separate but related ransomware attack on Vigatec, a technology company, was reported the same day by the same source, both attributed to the TeamInfoSec tracking label. These are part of a steady stream of German-language ransomware incidents tracked by the security-incidents.de platform, which catalogues attacks across German-speaking organizations. No specific ransomware family has been named in the available reporting, and neither incident has been claimed by a major ransomware group yet. The data theft component suggests double extortion is in play. German organizations, particularly in the social services and technology sectors, should be aware that these sectors are actively targeted.
