Trezor warned customers that attackers breached a third-party email provider and are sending phishing emails claiming a fake STM32 microcontroller vulnerability that could expose seed phrases. The company confirmed the domain used in the campaign has been taken down. The same story includes an earlier August breach of shipping provider ShipMonk that exposed names, addresses, emails, and phone numbers for 81,000 U.S. customers (revised upward from an initial 14,000). Trezor told affected users not to click links in emails referencing a "Critical Security Alert: STM32 Entropy Vulnerability." Monitor for additional breach disclosures and confirmation that the phishing infrastructure is fully dismantled.
