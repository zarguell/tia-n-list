InfoHawk reported a WebRTC-based payment-card skimmer on the checkouts of Lascana, Samsonite, American Tourister, Revolve and FWRD before Black Friday. The skimmer was configured for phone-sized screens, and Heise subsequently reported that cybercriminals used WebRTC to capture card data at several large online retailers. The five named stores are the concrete scope in the available reporting.

The reports describe malicious checkout code, not a WebRTC protocol vulnerability. Code running on a retailer's payment page can read card details as customers enter them. Watch for retailer breach notices, confirmation that the skimmer has been removed, and whether additional checkout domains or affected users are identified. Defenders should compare checkout scripts and network calls with known-good deployments, paying particular attention to unexpected WebRTC use.
