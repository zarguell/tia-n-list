Canadian cybersecurity executive Edward Dubrovsky, 54, was arrested Thursday in Pennsylvania on federal extortion and computer fraud conspiracy charges. He is a former chief operating officer and founder of CYPFER, a ransomware-negotiation firm. The case appears linked to the ShinyHunters attack on FBI IT systems. FBI Director Kash Patel referenced “another suspected co-conspirator” without naming Dubrovsky. The New York Times reported the arrest connection; other case details remain sealed. Watch for unsealed indictments and whether the FBI confirms Dubrovsky’s role in the breach response chain.
