Vanderbilt University in Nashville, Tennessee is undergoing a campus-wide ransomware attack, according to the Vanderbilt Hustler student newspaper's breaking report on November 9, 2026. The university asked its community to shut down university devices and disconnect from campus Wi-Fi while it responded, and VUIT had not yet confirmed the incident at the time of reporting.
