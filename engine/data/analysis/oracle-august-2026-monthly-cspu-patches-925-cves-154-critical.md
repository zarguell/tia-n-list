Oracle released its August 2026 Critical Security Patch Update on August 18, addressing 925 unique CVEs across 943 patches spanning 23 product families. Of the total, 154 patches carry a critical severity rating, roughly 16% of the release. Fusion Middleware alone accounts for 262 patches, or 28% of the entire update, making it the single most heavily patched product family.

This CSPU is significantly larger than previous between-cycle releases. The June 2026 CSPU covered 243 CVEs across 11 product families. The August release nearly quadruples that patch volume and expands to 23 product families, blurring the line between a focused interim release and a full quarterly CPU. For context, the April 2026 quarterly CPU contained 481 patches across 241 CVEs, and the July 2026 quarterly CPU, the largest so far in 2026, shipped 1,449 patches across 1,235 CVEs. The August CSPU at 943 patches exceeds the entire April quarterly CPU.

The scale of this release warrants attention from Oracle estate operators. Organizations running Fusion Middleware, Database, or other Oracle products should prioritize patching given the volume of critical fixes. No specific CVEs were cited in the available event content, but the breadth across 23 product families suggests wide-ranging vulnerability classes.
