Oracle released its October 2026 Critical Patch Update, addressing 1209 unique CVEs in 1295 security updates across 33 product families. The event content notes 112 critical patches, with Fusion Middleware receiving the highest count at 206 (15.9%) and E-Business Suite at 194 (15%). Several WebLogic Server flaws carry a CVSS 9.8 score with no authentication required, affecting versions 12.2.1.4.0, 14.1.1.0.0, 14.1.1.2.0.0, and 15.1.1.0.0. The release demands immediate attention because Oracle explicitly warns that attackers have exploited previously patched vulnerabilities when customers delayed updates.

What to watch: the 9.8-scored WebLogic entries (CVE-2026-60672, CVE-2026-60696, CVE-2026-60698, CVE-2026-60977) are remotely exploitable and should be treated as high-priority. The October CPU is the final quarterly release of 2026, making any missed patches a risk through the holiday window.
