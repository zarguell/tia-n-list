Cisco Firepower Management Center (FMC) is under active exploitation by at least three threat clusters, including Sandworm (Russian military intelligence) and Qilin (ransomware). The campaign targets CVE-2026-20079 and related vulnerabilities in the FMC management interface, which sits on the network perimeter and controls firewall policies for entire organizations. Compromising FMC grants an attacker the ability to modify security rules, exfiltrate traffic, and establish persistent access across the managed network.

The vulnerability chain includes CVE-2026-20316, CVE-2026-48710, CVE-2026-49869, CVE-2026-59822, CVE-2026-82329, CVE-2026-83548, CVE-2026-83549, and CVE-2026-9586. Multiple sources confirm that exploitation has been observed in the wild since at least mid-August. Sandworm's involvement suggests nation-state interest in network infrastructure, while Qilin's participation indicates criminal groups are also exploiting the same access path for ransomware deployment.

The attack surface is particularly dangerous because FMC appliances typically have full administrative access to managed firewalls. An attacker who compromises FMC can disable intrusion detection, open firewall rules for C2 traffic, and pivot to any system behind the managed perimeter. Organizations running Cisco Firepower should prioritize patching and verify that FMC management interfaces are not exposed to untrusted networks.
