Cisco Talos confirmed that three independent threat clusters exploited two vulnerabilities in Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center (FMC), the centralized console that manages an organization's entire firewall fleet. CVE-2026-20079 carries a CVSS score of 10.0 and allows an unauthenticated remote attacker to bypass authentication and execute scripts as root. CVE-2026-20316 (CVSS 5.3) provides hardcoded static credentials for a low-privilege account. Both were patched in March 2026, but exploitation was observed in the wild starting in August, and CVE-2026-20079 was added to CISA's KEV catalog on September 9.

The three clusters had distinct objectives. UAT-12197 dropped a JSP web shell into the CSM Tomcat web root and used OmniQuery.pl to extract authentication data. UAT-11823, assessed with high confidence as sharing tooling with Russian state-linked Sandworm, chained both CVEs, deployed a Cyclops Blink variant (Linux ELF implant), and collected managed firewall configurations. UAT-11988, assessed as a Qilin ransomware affiliate, used the static credentials from CVE-2026-20316 for initial access, performed environment mapping, harvested credentials, deployed an AV killer, and delivered Qilin ransomware.

VulnCheck counted roughly 300 to 700 FMC instances exposed to the internet as of March 2026. The six-month gap between patch availability and observed exploitation means organizations that only track application servers in their exposure inventory would miss this entirely. FMC holds firewall policies, configurations, and often credentials; compromising it allows an attacker to rewrite firewall rules, suppress logging, and pivot into the managed network. Cisco's forensic guidance includes checking whether license.tmp was replaced and whether the CSM Tomcat web root contains unexpected JAR files. The September 2 KEV batch also added SonicWall SMA1000 (CVE-2026-83548, CVE-2026-83549), Kestra OSS (CVE-2026-49869), JFrog Artifactory (CVE-2026-82329), Sangoma Switchvox (CVE-2026-9586), Kludex Starlette (CVE-2026-48710), and BerriAI LiteLLM (CVE-2026-59822) -- several are management or orchestration components whose value to attackers comes from the trust they hold.
