GMO Internet faced a ransomware threat after a breach of its Onamae Desktop Cloud service. The threat actor threatened to launch a ransomware attack, prompting GMO to urge customers to disconnect systems and shut down computers. The incident affects 26,739 customer servers according to the latest update. This is a significant operational disruption in Japan's hosting sector. Watch for confirmation of encryption or data exfiltration, and whether the threat actor follows through.
