GMO Internet's Onamae Desktop Cloud was breached by an attacker who installed malicious programs in customer server environments and then threatened a ransomware attack dated October 21, 2026. The company confirmed unauthorized access on October 20 and began progressively shutting down all 26,739 customer servers starting at 04:00 local time. A follow-up advisory on October 22 confirmed malicious programs were present in some server environments and that an investigation into unauthorized access and potential data compromise was ongoing.

The breach combines malware installation with a direct ransomware threat against a major Japanese cloud desktop provider, forcing a full service shutdown rather than a targeted encryption event. Watch for disclosure of which customer data was accessed, whether the attacker exfiltrated information before the shutdown, and whether other Onamae services share the same infrastructure and require similar containment.
