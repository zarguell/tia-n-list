GMO Internet faced a ransomware threat after a threat actor breached the company's Onamae Desktop Cloud service and installed malicious programs in some customer server environments. The company began progressively shutting down customer servers at 04:00 local time on October 21, 2026, and later told customers to disconnect from the internet and shut down PCs. A second advisory dated October 22 confirmed malicious program installation by a third party and stated that 26,739 customer servers were at risk. The actor threatened to launch a ransomware attack and also threatened to leak customer data.

This is a real operational disruption with confirmed intrusion into server environments, not a marketing announcement. The company is investigating unauthorized access and potential data compromise. Organizations using GMO's cloud services should follow the vendor's shutdown and disconnection guidance and await further updates from the company. No CVE is associated; the incident centers on unauthorized server access rather than a specific software vulnerability.
