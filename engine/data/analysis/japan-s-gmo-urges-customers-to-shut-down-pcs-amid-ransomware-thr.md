Japan's GMO Internet Group urged customers of its Onamae.com Desktop Cloud service to disconnect systems, back up data, and shut down after suspected attackers threatened ransomware and data publication. The emergency instructions followed unauthorized access to the cloud desktop environment and a ransom ultimatum. The incident is part of a broader GMO ransomware event; no confirmed data publication is cited in the current event, but the urgency of the vendor directive signals serious operational disruption.
