TrendAI researchers analyzed a coordinated extortion campaign attributed to BYOD and report at least nine claimed organizations from 5 October onward. The reporting describes the group as targeting hospitals and universities in the United States through identity, which fits the pattern of extortion claims rather than a confirmed breach count. Only the claims attributed to BYOD are documented here; the covered writeups do not confirm compromise at each named organization.

A follow-up French cyber-watch post, citing the TrendAI Security Blog publication, describes BYOD as a new ransomware group working through identity against US healthcare and higher education. That adds no independent victim details beyond the TrendAI finding, so the nine-organization figure remains a claim count from one research team. What to watch: further victim postings, any hospital or university confirmation, and whether TrendAI publishes the specific identity mechanism used for entry.
