A major cyber breach was declared at NSW Health on an isolated server, with officials stating patient data was not affected. The breach was reported around 1pm on Friday, October 30, 2026, and Cyber Security NSW confirmed investigations were underway and declared it a major incident. Police are investigating.

The incident is notable for the gap between a declared major incident and the reassurance that patient data was untouched. Health systems should treat isolated servers as in-scope for incident response and verify containment rather than rely on initial assessments.

Operators of public health infrastructure should ensure segmentation and logging on auxiliary servers, since the root cause and any data exfiltration have not been confirmed. The case also shows how quickly an isolated server incident can escalate to a criminal investigation, warranting full forensic review even when patient data appears unaffected.
