A major cyber breach was declared at NSW Health after an isolated server was compromised, with officials stating patient data was not affected. The incident occurred around 1pm on Friday, October 30, 2026. Cyber Security NSW confirmed investigations were under way and declared the breach a major incident; police are investigating.

The affected system was an isolated server within NSW Health, not the broader patient-data estate, according to officials. Cyber Security NSW is responding and the matter has been referred to police for criminal investigation. No threat actor or initial access vector has been publicly identified.

The incident adds to a run of healthcare-sector breaches in Australia and underscores the need to segment administrative and clinical systems. Organisations should monitor for follow-up disclosures on scope, data types, and remediation as the investigation proceeds.
