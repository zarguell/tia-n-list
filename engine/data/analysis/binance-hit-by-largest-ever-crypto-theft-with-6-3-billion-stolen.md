On October 29, 2026, attackers moved roughly $6.36 billion out of Binance wallets across ten blockchains in about twenty minutes, the largest cryptocurrency theft on record and roughly four times the $1.5 billion Bybit heist of February 2025. TRM Labs attributes the theft with high confidence to North Korea's TraderTraitor group, matching signatures from earlier state-linked operations. The attack is reported to have begun around 4:20 p.m. ET, with initial entry possibly through a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop; Binance has not named an actor.

Binance says user funds are safe and has promised to cover losses from its emergency fund and company funds, with about $3 billion frozen with help from blockchain partners. The FBI is engaged with public and private partners. Because the amount dwarfs prior exchange heists, expect follow-on phishing and fake recovery or support scams targeting affected users. Watch for official Binance channels only and treat any unsolicited recovery offer as fraudulent.
