A ransomware gang has claimed a data breach affecting Nutex Health, a health insurance and healthcare services company. The claim appeared on the gang's leak site, but the event's source article content was not available for full extraction. The story has a single source (SecurityWeek) with limited detail so far.

Nutex Health operates health plans and provider networks primarily in Texas and other US states. A confirmed ransomware breach in the healthcare sector typically indicates potential exposure of protected health information, personally identifiable information, and operational disruption. The absence of a named threat actor or detailed scope at this stage suggests the claim is recent and may develop further.

Watch for follow-up reporting on the claimed data volume, whether Nutex Health issues a public disclosure or breach notification, and whether the ransomware group releases samples to pressure payment.
