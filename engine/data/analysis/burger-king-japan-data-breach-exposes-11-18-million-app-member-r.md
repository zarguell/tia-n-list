Burger King Japan confirmed a data breach affecting 11.18 million app member records. Unauthorized access occurred between October 2 and October 7, 2026, through the server managing official smartphone app member information. The exposed data includes names, dates of birth, gender, email addresses, phone numbers, and order history. The company completed a follow-up investigation on October 13, blocked access, and reported the breach to Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission. No credit card numbers or user credentials were stored on the compromised server.

This is a substantial customer data exposure with regulatory notification completed. Organizations operating loyalty apps should verify server isolation and confirm no payment data co-mingled with PII in the breached environment.
