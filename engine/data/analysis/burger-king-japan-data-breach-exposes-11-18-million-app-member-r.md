BK Japan confirmed a data breach affecting 11.18 million app member records. The exposed data includes emails, phone numbers, dates of birth, and additional member information according to the company's disclosure. A feed source (hackread.com) and a social reference corroborate the breach.

The scale is significant for a single-market restaurant app, but the incident fits the pattern of large consumer-database breaches rather than a targeted intrusion with strategic impact. No CVEs, actors, or ransomware claims are reported.

Watch for regulatory filings, customer notification timelines, and whether any threat actor claims responsibility. No evidence links this to a specific campaign.
