Russia's Sandworm APT (tracked as APT44/Seashell Blizzard) is chaining two Cisco vulnerabilities to deploy an upgraded variant of the Cyclops Blink botnet, the same malware the FBI disrupted in 2022. Dark Reading reports the group is exploiting CVE-2025-20333 and CVE-2025-20338 in Cisco IOS XE and IOS XR respectively, targeting edge networking equipment to establish persistence and scan for additional targets. The upgraded Cyclops Blink now includes enhanced reconnaissance capabilities and network scanning modules not present in earlier versions.

This marks a significant return of a state-sponsored botnet infrastructure after a two-year quiet period. Sandworm's focus on network infrastructure means compromised devices become both espionage platforms and lateral movement staging grounds. The chain targets devices that are rarely patched and often lack endpoint detection, making the initial foothold difficult to spot. Organizations running Cisco IOS XE or XR should verify they have applied the relevant patches and audit for indicators of Cyclops Blink, particularly unexpected outbound connections on non-standard ports and unauthorized configuration changes to device interfaces.
