Russia-linked Sandworm APT (linked to GRU Unit 74455) is chaining Cisco vulnerabilities to deploy an upgraded variant of Cyclops Blink, the botnet malware that the FBI disrupted in 2022. The new variant adds network scanning capabilities and improved evasion techniques, according to Dark Reading reporting. Cyclops Blink is a modular implant that has historically targeted SOHO routers and network devices, and the upgraded version suggests Sandworm has rebuilt its botnet infrastructure after law enforcement takedown.

The campaign chains multiple Cisco vulnerabilities to gain initial access and deploy the implant on vulnerable devices. Sandworm has a long track record of targeting network infrastructure, including the 2018 Olympics Destroyer attack and the 2022 Industroyer2 attack on Ukrainian power grid. The resurfacing of Cyclops Blink indicates the group is investing in persistent botnet infrastructure for credential theft, DDoS, or future offensive operations.

Network defenders should audit Cisco device versions for known vulnerabilities, particularly in edge and SOHO equipment, and monitor for unusual outbound connections from network devices. The FBI's previous disruption involved court-authorized operations to remove the malware from compromised devices, but the new variant's improved evasion may complicate similar efforts.
