Star Blizzard, the FSB Centre 18 unit Microsoft also tracks as SEABORGIUM, replaced its multi-step ClickFix chains with a delivery technique Microsoft named RedFlick in its September 29, 2026 report. The chain now needs exactly one user interaction: after a target replies to a first, attachment-free email, a follow-up carries a password-protected RAR or ZIP holding a VHDX disk image or an LNK disguised as a PDF. Opening it runs a script that fetches an MSI, which creates scheduled tasks that pull down and install CosmicPulse, the group's Python backdoor, storing its AES key under HKCU\Software\Classes\.mollis. The chain kept mutating through 2026: by July the PowerShell stage hid inside a downloaded PDF behind a cAB magic-header marker, and by mid-August the group used steganography and sent a unique ZIP file to each target.

The scale numbers are the story. Microsoft counted at least 13 large-scale campaigns from January to August 2026 affecting over 100 organizations, primarily in the United States and United Kingdom, sent in waves of tens to hundreds of emails, from an actor that previously built bespoke trust one diplomat or academic at a time. Two properties make this worse than ordinary mass phishing. The reply-gated second stage means the payload only ever reaches users engaged enough to answer, so mail-gateway telemetry and the first wave of delivered mail contain nothing detonatable, and a clean mail log proves nothing. Files delivered inside a mounted VHDX also carry no Mark-of-the-Web flag, so SmartScreen never prompts. Microsoft adds that the group now sends from accounts created on compromised cPanel and WordPress sites and places the impersonated organization in the local part of the sender address rather than the domain, which defeats sender-reputation filtering. The practical consequence: a junior analyst at a Ukraine-policy NGO or financial institution is now squarely in the target pool, not just the director.

There is no patch because no CVE exists; this is tradecraft, so the defense is detection and policy. Hunt for scheduled tasks named Internet Quality Test Connection, Network Configuration Manager, System Health Monitor, or SecureDNSObserver, for the .mollis registry key, for conhost.exe spawning curl, and for ssh.exe invoked with PermitLocalCommand; Defender signatures cover Trojan:Script/RedFlick and Backdoor:Python/CosmicPulse. Digital Security Lab Ukraine's June telemetry adds the curl user-agents WindowsUpdatesMonitor and DNSObserver plus the C2s 103[.]160[.]59[.]97, 89[.]125[.]66[.]183, and secure-dns-hub[.]com. Restrict outbound SSH and WebDAV, enable Microsoft's attack surface reduction rules against obfuscated scripts and prevalence-unverified executables, keep Safe Attachments and Safe Links on, and move Ukraine-facing staff to phishing-resistant MFA. Train for the actual trigger point: a reply to an unsolicited message that produces an archive whose password arrives as an image.

Two developments would change this assessment. If another state actor copies the reply-gated mass-mail model or the single-click archive chain, detections tuned to Star Blizzard's lures and infrastructure will miss it. And because August's per-target ZIPs and steganography defeat hash and identifier blocking, the published IoC list will age fast, so a material Microsoft update, or evidence that targeting has spread beyond the observed 100 organizations and the US-UK focus, is the signal to re-raise exposure.
