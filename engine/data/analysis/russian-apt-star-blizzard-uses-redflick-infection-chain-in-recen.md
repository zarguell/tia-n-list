Russian APT Star Blizzard, linked to FSB Centre 18, has adopted a new malware delivery technique called RedFlick that requires only a single user interaction. Microsoft reports the group sends password-protected RAR or ZIP archives in follow-up emails after initial phishing. Between January and August 2026, Star Blizzard launched over a dozen campaigns using RedFlick lures posing as Ukrainian authorities or reputable think tanks, targeting Ukrainian individuals, international NGOs, governments, and financial institutions supporting Ukraine. The group also creates accounts on compromised websites to send tens to hundreds of phishing emails per campaign.
