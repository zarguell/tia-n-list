Citrix NetScaler appliances face sustained exploitation pressure. The core story covers CVE-2026-19490, an authentication bypass rated CVSS 9.3 that affects Gateway and AAA virtual-server configurations (SSL VPN, ICA Proxy, Clientless VPN, RDP Proxy). The second bulletin flaw, CVE-2026-19489, is an 8.8-rated memory overflow that can trigger denial of service when SIP ALG is enabled in large-scale NAT groups. The September zero-day emergency (CVE-2026-88771 through CVE-2026-88778, bulletin CTX697096) is a separate story and has since been patched in 14.1-73.37 and the 13.1 FIPS lines after confirmed exploitation. Previdian observed exploitation attempts against the auth bypass starting September 3, with requests from three distinct source IPs in Australia, the United States, and Germany. Horizon3 now maintains a dedicated research page for CVE-2026-19490 covering exploitation risks and remediation, keeping the older bypass on the active-research list even as the RCE emergency dominates attention. The combination of a high-value network-edge target, confirmed in-the-wild scanning, and repeated emergency cycles makes this a priority incident.
