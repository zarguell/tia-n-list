Critical Citrix NetScaler authentication bypass CVE-2026-19490 carries CVSS v4.0 score 9.3 and affects NetScaler ADC and Gateway 14.1 versions prior to 14.1-73.32 and 13.1 versions prior to 13.1-63.21, plus FIPS builds. It allows unauthenticated remote attackers to bypass access controls on appliances configured as AAA virtual servers or as Gateway services (SSL VPN, ICA Proxy, Clientless VPN, RDP Proxy) when SAML Action is configured. A second high-severity flaw, CVE-2026-19489 (CVSS 8.8), can cause denial of service when SIP ALG is enabled within a Large Scale NAT configuration. Citrix published the bulletin on 19 August 2026; Rapid7 noted no observed exploitation at that time, but Previdian researcher Ryan Dewhurst reported exploitation attempts starting 3 September from IPs in Australia, the US, and Germany, matching a published proof-of-concept. By 27 September, watchTowr reported two unpatched NetScaler RCE zero-days are being exploited in the wild with 9 detections and 14 IOCs, prompting advisories for organizations to shut down appliances. The story has 11 events spanning from the August bulletin through the September shutdown warnings.

The key development this window is the shift from theoretical exploitation attempts to verified active exploitation and emergency shutdown recommendations. The events do not name the new zero-days with CVEs, and no specific victim counts are given. The existing CVEs (19489, 19490) remain the canonical identifiers for the original bulletin, while the new reports describe additional unpatched zero-days beyond those.

What to watch: whether Citrix releases patches for the new zero-days, whether more IOCs or victim reports emerge from watchTowr and security vendors, and whether organizations with internet-facing NetScaler appliances complete emergency shutdowns or upgrades.
