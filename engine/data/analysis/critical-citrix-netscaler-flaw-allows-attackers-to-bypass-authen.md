Citrix disclosed CVE-2026-19490 in mid-August, a CVSS 9.3 authentication bypass affecting NetScaler ADC and Gateway appliances configured as AAA virtual servers or Gateways (SSL VPN, ICA Proxy, CVPN, RDP Proxy). An unauthenticated attacker can bypass access controls without valid credentials, gaining direct entry to internal services. Exploitability depends on firmware version and whether SAML Action is configured. The flaw also affects CVE-2026-19489, a denial-of-service vulnerability disclosed alongside it.

As of September 3, exploitation attempts are confirmed in the wild. Previdian's NetScaler sensors detected requests matching a published proof-of-concept from three distinct source IPs geolocated to Australia, the United States, and Germany. A September 19 dev.to analysis highlighted that approximately 239,000 NetScaler instances remain exposed on the internet, underscoring the scale of the attack surface. Citrix's August 19 advisory urged immediate patching but did not flag active exploitation at that time.

NetScaler appliances typically sit at the network edge enforcing authentication for remote access. Compromise bypasses the entire authentication layer, providing attackers with an internal foothold without any credentials. Organizations running affected versions (14.1-43.56+, 13.1-61.28+ depending on configuration) should patch immediately and review logs for connections matching the PoC pattern. The combination of confirmed exploitation, 239,000 exposed instances, and CVSS 9.3 makes this a persistent high-priority risk.
