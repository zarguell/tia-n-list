Citrix NetScaler appliances face sustained exploitation pressure. The core story covers CVE-2026-19490, an authentication bypass rated CVSS 9.3 that affects Gateway and AAA virtual-server configurations (SSL VPN, ICA Proxy, Clientless VPN, RDP Proxy). The second bulletin flaw, CVE-2026-19489, is an 8.8-rated memory overflow that can trigger denial of service when SIP ALG is enabled in large-scale NAT groups. Previdian observed exploitation attempts against the auth bypass starting September 3, with requests from three distinct source IPs in Australia, the United States, and Germany. No patch exists for the newer RCE pair, while the August bulletin fixes are available in 14.1-73.32 and 13.1-63.21 or later. The combination of a high-value network-edge target, confirmed in-the-wild scanning, and unpatched RCE makes this a priority incident.
