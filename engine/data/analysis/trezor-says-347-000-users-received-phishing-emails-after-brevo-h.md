Trezor disclosed that 347,000 users received phishing emails after attackers compromised Brevo, Trezor's third-party email marketing platform. The breach occurred on September 9, 2026, when threat actors exploited Brevo's SAML Single Sign-On implementation to gain unauthorized access to multiple customer accounts, including Trezor's. Approximately 2,500 users clicked a malicious link before Trezor took down the phishing domain within 20 minutes.

The phishing emails posed as critical security alerts from help@trezor.io, claiming a hardware microcontroller vulnerability in Trezor wallets' STM32 chips could expose seed phrases to brute-force attacks. Recipients were directed to download an app that prompted them to enter their wallet backup. The campaign targeted Trezor's opt-in newsletter subscriber database. Other affected Brevo customers include BitBox and CoinTracking.

This incident compounds risk for Trezor users, coming shortly after a separate data leak involving Trezor's third-party shipping provider. The combination of newsletter subscriber emails and shipping data gives attackers both contact information and physical addresses for targeted phishing or social engineering. The 347,000 exposed email addresses remain at risk for future phishing campaigns.
