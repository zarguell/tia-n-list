Ipsos has confirmed an intrusion into one of its internal information systems. The French market research company made the incident public on Monday 16 November, five days after filing a complaint with authorities. It says the breach had no impact on operations or on its ability to deliver for clients, that outside security experts were brought in to contain and investigate it, and that the nature and scope are still being determined. No claim about data access or theft has been made.

The company has not said when it detected the breach, which system was affected or how the attacker got in. That matters because Ipsos has nearly 20,000 employees across 90 markets, works with 5,000 clients, and relies on survey and social media data. Watch for a follow-up notice naming the affected system, the detection date and whether client, project or business partner information was involved.
