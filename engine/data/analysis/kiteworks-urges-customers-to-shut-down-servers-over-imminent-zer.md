KiteWorks has urged customers to shut down their file-transfer servers due to credible indications of an imminent zero-day attack. The manufacturer wrote to customers citing concrete threat intelligence received from law enforcement, requesting a six-hour worldwide shutdown window on Saturday, September 26, from 04:00 to 10:00 Central Europe. Heise's German-language report quotes CISO Frank Balonis's customer email, noting Germany's BKA declined to comment for tactical investigative reasons and BSI had not responded by late Friday afternoon.

The shutdown advisory is preventive rather than reactive, indicating that attackers may have discovered an unpatched vulnerability in KiteWorks products and prepared exploitation campaigns. The story has 7 events with a score of 6.6. Customers who rely on KiteWorks for secure file transfer should follow the shutdown instructions, apply any available patches immediately upon restoration, and review server logs for unauthorized access attempts before and after the maintenance window.
