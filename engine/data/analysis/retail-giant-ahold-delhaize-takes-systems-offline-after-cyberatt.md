Ahold Delhaize, a multinational retailer operating over 9,400 stores across Europe, the US, and Indonesia, confirmed it took systems offline after a cybersecurity incident. The company reported net sales exceeding $104 billion in 2024 and employs nearly 400,000 people. Its press release states stores remain open, but employees at its Stop & Shop chain reported disruptions accessing schedules and pay stubs. The incident affects a wholesale and retail network serving roughly 60 million customers weekly. No CVE or threat actor attribution has been disclosed, and the company says it is assessing and mitigating the issue.

This matters because Ahold Delhaize is one of the world's largest food retail chains; any sustained network compromise could ripple through supply chains, point-of-sale systems, and employee payroll across multiple countries. The partial system shutdown indicates active containment rather than a routine outage. Watch for updates on whether customer or supplier data was accessed, the scope of impacted brands (Delhaize, Food Lion, Stop & Shop, Giant Food, Hannaford), and any ransomware or data-theft claims from threat actors.
