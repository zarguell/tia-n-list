A Saudi national and OpenAI engineer, Abdulrahman Alfozan (35), was arrested October 9 and charged by the DOJ with illegally intercepting oral communications. Security discovered a concealed audio recording device inserted into a USB port behind a television in an executive conference room on September 30; the device contained encrypted data and had been hidden around September 28. The room hosted confidential discussions about proprietary research, product plans, and government engagements. Camera footage placed Alfozan near the room repeatedly despite no authorization to attend. This is an insider-threat case, not a vulnerability advisory, but the exposure of executive-level strategic discussions carries significant corporate and national-security relevance.
