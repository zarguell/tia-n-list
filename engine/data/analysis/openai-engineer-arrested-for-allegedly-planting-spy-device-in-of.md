An OpenAI engineer, 35-year-old Saudi national Abdulrahman Alfozan, was charged with planting a hidden audio recording device in a company conference room to capture sensitive technology conversations. The device was discovered by OpenAI, which used its own models to trace it to the employee, fired him, referred the case to the FBI, and made an arrest within a week. The company states there is no indication of model weights, source code, or customer data being accessed. The complaint does not specify what Alfozan intended to do with captured audio.
