An update to the ASOS breach reports that hackers accessed customer details and shopping searches through a third-party platform, according to Standard. Snowflake has stated its platform was not the source of the vulnerability, pushing back on the attackers' earlier claim that the Snowflake instance was fully compromised. The update reinforces that the incident involved unauthorized access to a communication or marketing platform used by ASOS rather than a confirmed database intrusion. No payment-card or password exposure has been confirmed, and the total number of affected customers remains undisclosed.
