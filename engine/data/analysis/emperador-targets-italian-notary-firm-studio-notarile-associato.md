The Emperador ransomware group claimed an attack on Studio Notarile Associato Salvatore Costantino E Anna Favarato, an Italian notary firm operating under costantinofavarato.it. The group announced the breach on September 20, 2026, stating it had infiltrated the firm's systems and exfiltrated numerous customer and employee documents. Emperador threatened to leak the stolen data if demands were not met.

Notary firms are high-value targets for data-theft extortion because they handle legally sensitive documents: property transactions, wills, power of attorney filings, and identity records. The compromised data likely includes personally identifiable information and financial details of the firm's clients, making it attractive for both direct extortion and secondary fraud schemes.

Emperador is a relatively new ransomware operation that has been steadily building a victim list, primarily targeting small and mid-sized organizations in Europe and Latin America. The group operates a standard double-extortion model: encrypting systems while simultaneously threatening to publish stolen data. Organizations in the legal and notary sector should treat this as confirmation that they remain in active targeting scope.
