Google promoted Chrome 158 to Stable on November 17, 2026, with 204 security fixes. The advisory lists six Critical CVEs, not three: CVE-2026-130963 (use after free in WebView), CVE-2026-130975 (use after free in WebAudio), CVE-2026-131008 (use after free in GarbageCollection), CVE-2026-131031 (out of bounds write in GPU), CVE-2026-131054 (out of bounds write in ANGLE) and CVE-2026-131060 (buffer overflow in Media). NVD records describe crafted HTML pages for most of them, with WebView reachable through crafted network traffic and WebView and ANGLE giving out-of-sandbox code execution. Fixed builds are 158.0.8127.36 on Linux, 158.0.8127.36/.37 on Windows and macOS, and 158.0.8127.42 on Android.

The volume is the story: 62 High, 82 Medium and 54 Low severity fixes, with use after free the largest class at 51 occurrences. Google reported that 163 of the issues carry no bounty, paid 11,000 dollars for five externally reported flaws and had amounts pending for 36 more. Anthropic researcher Xinyang Ge is credited with two of the critical findings in the coverage. Canada's Cyber Centre issued AV26-1171 on November 17 for desktop versions before 158.0.8127.36/.37.

Google made no claim of in-the-wild exploitation for the Chrome 158 issues in the material reviewed here, and bug details may stay restricted until most users update. Watch for the six Critical CVEs moving into NVD with analysis details, for Android rollout gaps, and for any exploitation report that would change this from a large scheduled patch into an incident.
