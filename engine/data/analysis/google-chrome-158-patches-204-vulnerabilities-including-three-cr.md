Google released Chrome 158 to the desktop Stable channel on November 17, 2026, with 204 security fixes. SecurityOnline flags three critical CVEs: CVE-2026-130963, CVE-2026-131031 and CVE-2026-131060. Google's own stable-channel announcement, as reported by SecurityWeek and GBHackers, counts six critical bugs: use-after-free flaws in WebView, GarbageCollection and WebAudio, out-of-bounds writes in GPU and ANGLE, and a buffer overflow in Media. The count makes this a substantial browser update even though no exploit claims are attached to it.

Patched builds are 158.0.8127.36 on Linux and 158.0.8127.36 or 158.0.8127.37 on Windows and macOS, rolling out over coming days and weeks. SecurityWeek reports 62 high, 82 medium and 54 low severity fixes, with use-after-free the largest class at 51 occurrences, followed by incorrect authorization at 34. Google and external researcher Xinyang Ge found 145 and 15 flaws respectively; $11,000 went to five externally reported bugs and 163 issues drew no reward.

No source reviewed here reports in-the-wild exploitation of the Chrome 158 flaws, so urgency rests on the critical ratings and browser reach rather than a confirmed campaign. Watch for Google's detailed release notes on the three named CVEs and for any later KEV or exploit report that changes that assessment. Enterprises should push the fixed builds rather than wait for the staged consumer rollout.
