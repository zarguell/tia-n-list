SecurityOnline reports that Google Chrome 158 fixes 204 security flaws. Three are called out as critical: CVE-2026-130963, CVE-2026-131031, and CVE-2026-131060. The count alone makes this a substantial browser update, and the critical ratings place those three CVEs above the rest of the batch for attention.

Chrome is deployed across desktops at scale, and browser flaws are often reached simply by visiting a page. The report does not give affected build numbers, technical descriptions of the three critical bugs, or any indication that they have been exploited, so those points cannot be asserted from this source.

Watch for Google's Chrome 158 release notes on the exact fixed builds and for details of the three critical CVEs. Confirming whether any of them is under active attack will determine how urgently the update needs to be pushed out beyond a normal browser patch cycle.
