Google promoted Chrome 158 to the Stable channel on 17 November 2026 with 204 security fixes. The six Critical CVEs are CVE-2026-130963, a use-after-free in WebView; CVE-2026-131031, an out-of-bounds write in GPU; CVE-2026-131054, an out-of-bounds write in ANGLE; CVE-2026-131060, a buffer overflow in Media; CVE-2026-131008, a use-after-free in GarbageCollection; and CVE-2026-130975, a use-after-free in WebAudio. NVD records tie CVE-2026-130963 to crafted network traffic and code execution outside the sandbox on Android before 158.0.8127.36, and CVE-2026-131031 to a crafted HTML page with code execution outside the sandbox. The GPU, ANGLE, Media, GarbageCollection and WebAudio records describe code execution inside the sandbox via crafted HTML pages.

The patched builds are 158.0.8127.36 on Linux and 158.0.8127.36 or 158.0.8127.37 on Windows and macOS, rolling out over days and weeks. Android gets 158.0.8127.42 on Google Play. The Cyber Centre advisory AV26-1171 identifies desktop versions before 158.0.8127.36/.37 as affected. Of the 204 fixes, 62 are High, 82 Medium and 54 Low. Use-after-free is the largest class at 51 occurrences, followed by incorrect authorization at 34 and missing authorization at 16. Google paid 11,000 dollars in rewards for five externally reported issues and has yet to decide amounts for 36 more; no reward will be paid for 163 weaknesses, including all six Critical ones.

Google's advisory notes that bug details and links may stay restricted until most users are updated. The reporting reviewed here makes no claim of in-the-wild exploitation for the Chrome 158 set. The practical watch items are the outside-the-sandbox WebView and GPU bugs, which deserve the fastest rollout in a managed fleet, and the lag between Stable channel availability and the version actually installed on endpoints. Extend the same check to Android devices, where the WebView record applies, and revisit the Chromium issue references once Google lifts the restrictions.
