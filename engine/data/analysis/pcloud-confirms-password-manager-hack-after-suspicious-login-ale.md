pCloud confirmed a cyberattack against pCloud Pass after customers reported new-device alerts for an unfamiliar device named "pd" at 45.131.244.7 in Luxembourg. Several users said the alerts arrived despite two-factor authentication, while some saw no matching device in their account. Other recipients said they had never used pCloud Pass or had asked for the linked storage account to be deleted almost a year earlier. GBHackers separately reported that a small number of email addresses were exposed and automated new-device alerts were generated.

pCloud said the incident was quickly contained and limited to pCloud Pass, with Cloud Storage unaffected. The reports establish exposed email addresses and login attempts, but do not establish theft of passwords or vault contents. Alerts without visible device entries leave the actual account access and intrusion path unclear.

Watch for pCloud to explain the "pd" device and Luxembourg IP address, identify the initial access vector, and state whether sessions or credentials were revoked. Users should review active sessions and change reused passwords. The concrete artifacts to monitor are 45.131.244.7, the "pd" device label, and any company confirmation of broader data exposure.
