Solar's monitoring, as relayed by aggregators, indicates the Belarusian hacktivist group Cyber Partisans maintained access to a Russian healthcare organization from early 2024 until the access was detected in December 2025. The access was held through a backdoor tracked as Vasily. Follow-on summaries describe roughly two years of presence inside the health network, with the operators taking tasking over Telegram, and about 35 associated indicators published.

Three things make this worth tracking. It shows an opposition-aligned hacktivist group running quiet, long-dwell access against Russian critical infrastructure rather than the flashy defacements and leaks the group is known for. It shows healthcare networks on the receiving end of politically motivated operations, where intrusion and disruption carry patient-safety risk. And it shows Telegram used as a command channel, which most monitoring treats as low-priority traffic.

Watch for Solar's full reporting: the complete IOC set, the backdoor's persistence and communication mechanics, and whether the target is named. Also watch whether the Telegram C2 pattern shows up in other Vasily-family detections, and whether Russian authorities acknowledge the intrusion.
