Iran's Prince of Persia APT, also tracked as Infy, resumed operations in May 2026 after a three-month pause, the longest confirmed downtime in its nearly 20-year history. Semperis reports the group ran a C&C server from the US this year, the first such placement since 2016. The halt overlapped with the Iran-US-Israel conflict that began February 28. After restarting on May 27, the malware family Tonnerre siphoned over 1,800 files from a single victim within seven days. Targets include Iranian dissidents and entities across the US, Europe, and the Middle East. The US-hosted infrastructure changes the group's typical geographic footprint and deserves monitoring.
