The NSA, CISA, and FBI issued a joint advisory warning that six China-based firms, including DeepSeek, are systematically stealing US AI models at scale. The advisory identifies a coordinated campaign by Chinese companies to exfiltrate proprietary AI intellectual property through a combination of cyber operations, supply chain compromises, and insider access. This represents a significant escalation in state-sponsored IP theft targeting the US AI sector.

The agencies name DeepSeek among the implicated firms, a detail that will draw attention given the company's prominent position in the Chinese AI ecosystem. The advisory frames the activity as part of China's broader strategy to achieve parity in AI capabilities by acquiring foreign models rather than developing them independently. The scope extends beyond simple data theft to include model weights, training data, and architecture details that would allow adversaries to replicate or fine-tune stolen models.

This advisory matters because it signals that US intelligence agencies have sufficient confidence to publicly name specific Chinese companies, a step that typically precedes sanctions or indictments. Organizations developing frontier AI models should review their security postures, particularly around model serving infrastructure, API access controls, and supply chain integrity. The advisory also suggests that foreign governments view AI model theft as a national security priority comparable to traditional espionage targets.
