The Qilin ransomware group added at least two new victims to its leak site in the past 24 hours: Geieg and Bravo Group. The posts appeared on Qilin's public blog, tracked by cti.fyi. This brings the group's visible caseload to a steady trickle of mid-market victims across multiple sectors.

Qilin (also tracked as Aggregate Boot) operates a ransomware-as-a-service model with affiliates using both ESXi and Windows encryptors. The group has been active since mid-2024 and typically exfiltrates data before encryption, threatening double extortion. No CVEs or specific initial access vectors are associated with these particular claims.

The low source count (cti.fyi only) and single-platform reporting suggest these are fresh postings that haven't yet drawn broader attention. Worth monitoring for follow-on data dumps or extortion negotiations, but individually these are routine Qilin operations rather than indicative of a new campaign or capability shift.
