Attackers hijacked Microsoft's official X account (13 million followers) Thursday in a crypto pump-and-dump scheme. The attackers had the account follow and repost a tweet from a suspended account impersonating Microsoft's Clippy assistant, then promoted a $Clippy token claiming liquidity paired with $MSFT. Microsoft removed the posts, secured the account, and confirmed unauthorized access; a spokesperson said the company does not support cryptocurrency and will take legal action. No CVEs or technical intrusion details are reported; the incident is a social-engineering and brand-abuse breach rather than a product vulnerability.
