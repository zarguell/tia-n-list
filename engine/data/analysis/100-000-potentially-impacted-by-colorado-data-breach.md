Colorado OIT disclosed unauthorized access to documents uploaded to the myColorado application, which serves about 2.1 million users for digital identification, state services, and document storage. The state took the app offline after the intrusion and said AWS threat detection triggered the discovery. Attackers reached the myCards and Documents area, which can hold PDFs, government ID photos, vehicle registrations, and insurance cards. About 100,000 people may be affected, no payment information was accessed, and the estimate remains provisional while OIT matches documents to users.

The exposed records are sensitive identity documents rather than payment data, creating a risk of identity misuse for affected residents. The app remained offline with no restoration date announced. Watch for a revised victim count, confirmation of which document types were involved for each person, and the state's notification and recovery timeline.
