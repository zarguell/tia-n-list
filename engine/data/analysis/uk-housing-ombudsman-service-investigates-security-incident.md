The UK Housing Ombudsman is investigating a third-party IT incident that hit its case management system, forcing it to suspend new online complaints while the impact is assessed. Residents are being directed to phone or email instead. The watchdog, which settles disputes between social housing residents and landlords in England, says the incident is contained, has notified the Information Commissioner's Office, and is still determining whether personal information was affected.

The practical risk is follow-on fraud against complainants whose cases sit in the affected system, and the service has already warned tenants to treat unsolicited messages with suspicion. Watch for confirmation of the supplier involved, any finding of personal data exposure, and restoration of the complaints portal.
