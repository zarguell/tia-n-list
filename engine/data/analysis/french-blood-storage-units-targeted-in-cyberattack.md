France's national blood service, the Etablissement francais du sang, was hit by a cyberattack overnight November 1-2, 2026 against cold-storage facilities maintained by Coolworld. Attackers reprogrammed storage units from about minus 30 degrees Celsius to 110 degrees Celsius, endangering blood samples and frozen plasma.

The intrusion was detected by an on-duty technician who noticed the temperature rise. Le Canard en chaine, Anadolu Agency and OMM reported the incident on November 3, and the French Ministry of Health said there was zero operational impact. BleepingComputer carried the story on November 5.

Watch for attribution of the Coolworld intrusion and any disclosure of how many storage units were affected.
