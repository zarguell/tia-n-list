Hackers targeted refrigeration equipment operated by Coolworld, a service provider for France's Etablissement Francais du Sang, and allegedly reprogrammed plasma storage freezers from about -30 C to 110 C. The attack was detected overnight between November 1 and 2, 2026, and affected cold-storage facilities used by France's national blood service. A similar attack on hundreds of French supermarkets occurred in October, making this the second wave within a month.

The incident raises the prospect of destroyed blood products and disrupted supply to hospitals, though the full scope of damage has not been confirmed. No actor has publicly claimed responsibility and the attack vector has not been confirmed.

Healthcare and cold-chain operators should review remote access to refrigeration controllers and ensure temperature excursions trigger independent alarms. The vector remains unknown, so patching alone may not address the root cause.
