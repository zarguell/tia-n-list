Cold-storage facilities used by France's national blood service were targeted overnight from Sunday to Monday, with blood sample units reportedly programmed to reach 110 degrees Celsius (230 degrees Fahrenheit). French weekly Le Canard enchaîné reported the incident, and Anadolu Agency also covered it. The cyberattack targeted facilities storing blood samples and altered temperature settings in a way that could destroy biological material.

The attack is a direct operational-technology incident against healthcare infrastructure, with physical consequences for blood storage. It underscores the risk of network-connected cold-chain equipment in national health services.

Watch for official attribution or confirmation from the French blood service, and whether similar temperature-manipulation attacks appear against other cold-storage facilities in Europe. The 110-degree target indicates deliberate intent to destroy samples rather than simple data theft.
