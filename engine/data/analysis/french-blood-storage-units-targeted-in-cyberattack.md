France's national blood service, the Etablissement francais du sang (EFS), had its cold-storage chain compromised overnight November 1-2, 2026, through its refrigeration equipment provider Coolworld Rentals. EFS confirmed on November 3 that it was not directly targeted; the intruders hit its refrigeration supplier and used remote monitoring software to change temperature and defrost settings at three facilities.

Cold-storage chambers were reprogrammed from about minus 30 degrees Celsius to 110 degrees Celsius (230 degrees Fahrenheit), threatening stored blood samples and frozen plasma. Coolworld said the attackers exploited a vulnerability in a third-party component outside its direct control, and that only a limited number of units were affected. An on-call technician detected the temperature rise.

France's Ministry of Health reported zero operational impact. The incident was reported by Le Canard Enchaine, Anadolu Agency, DataBreaches.Net, BleepingComputer and others. Watch for attribution of the vulnerable third-party component, confirmation of whether any blood products were destroyed, and any guidance from EFS, Coolworld or ANSSI.
