Silent Ransom Group, active since 2022 with core members likely from the defunct Conti syndicate, practices data-leak-only extortion rather than ransomware deployment. A recent analysis of leaked chats reveals internal discussions of recruiting disposable agents via Telegram for physical infiltration, inserting USB drives with malicious code, making physical threats against victim leadership, and soliciting intelligence from US military personnel for sale to the Russian government. The group targets law firms and organizations that would suffer severe reputational harm from confidential leaks. The Luna Moth files claim was denied by the group, but analysis suggests the data is genuine.
