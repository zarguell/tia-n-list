AdaptHealth, a network of over 680 medical equipment facilities across the US, confirmed a data breach impacting 4.1 million individuals. The breach occurred in early June when a threat actor gained access to the company's cloud-based applications, including internal systems for patient management and document storage. The stolen data includes electronic protected health information (ePHI).

Baylor Genetics reported to HHS that 2,810,878 individuals had their ePHI compromised. The remaining affected individuals likely had other types of personal information stolen. AdaptHealth describes itself as a provider of healthcare solutions and medical equipment to patients, making the breach significant given the volume of sensitive medical data involved.

The breach adds to the growing list of healthcare sector incidents in 2026. Organizations in the medical equipment and device space should review their cloud access controls and ensure proper segmentation between patient management systems and document storage. The delay between the early June breach and September disclosure suggests a lengthy investigation period, which is common in healthcare breaches due to the complexity of ePHI data classification.
