Adobe's September 2026 security update patches vulnerabilities across eight products, headlined by CVE-2026-75650, a critical unauthenticated RCE in Adobe Commerce and Magento Open Source dubbed "StyleSmuggler." Sansec Forensics disclosed this zero-day on September 5 after observing active exploitation since September 4, three days before Adobe released Hotfix VULN-39341 on September 7. Multiple victim stores were confirmed across different attack campaigns. The vulnerability allows unauthenticated remote code execution on Commerce and Magento instances, enabling attackers to backdoor online stores.

Beyond the Magento zero-day, the broader release covers critical flaws in Adobe Acrobat, Reader, ColdFusion, Creative Cloud, and other products. The total patch count exceeds 170 vulnerabilities. Organizations running Adobe Commerce or Magento should treat CVE-2026-75650 as an emergency patch given the confirmed active exploitation window and the availability of attack details from Sansec's public disclosure.

The StyleSmuggler campaign follows a familiar pattern of Magento exploitation: unauthenticated RCE leading to web shell deployment and payment card skimming. Retailers running Adobe Commerce should verify the hotfix is applied and audit for indicators of compromise including unauthorized PHP files in the webroot and suspicious database modifications. Tenable detection plugins are forthcoming.
