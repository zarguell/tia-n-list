WorkOS disclosed a breach of an internal application used by its sales team, with unauthorized access identified on October 30, 2026. The attacker accessed business contact records and summaries of customer communications and used an AI offensive security framework, identified as ARTEX, to automate reconnaissance and exploitation attempts. WorkOS states that no application data or production services were accessed or affected.

The company disabled external access to the affected application, reviewed access controls on internal tools, and contracted an independent forensics and incident response firm. WorkOS will notify affected customers directly and warned users to stay alert for phishing or social engineering built on the exposed information. WorkOS provides enterprise authentication and identity tooling to at least 3,000 companies, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, Perplexity, Snowflake, Plaid, Webglow, and Netlify.

Watch for customer notifications, follow-on phishing campaigns using the stolen contact records, and any further detail the forensics firm releases about how the ARTEX framework was used in the intrusion.
