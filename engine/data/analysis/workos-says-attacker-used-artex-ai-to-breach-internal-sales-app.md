WorkOS, a developer infrastructure provider used by at least 3,000 companies including OpenAI, Anthropic, and Snowflake, disclosed unauthorized access to an internal sales application on October 30, 2026. The intruder accessed business contact records and summaries of customer communications; WorkOS says no app data or production services were affected. The company disabled external access to the affected app, engaged an independent forensics firm, and warned customers to watch for phishing or social engineering leveraging the exposed information.

WorkOS attributes the intrusion to an AI-powered offensive security framework, naming ARTEX AI, used to automate reconnaissance and exploitation against the internal application. The incident highlights how AI-assisted tooling lowers the cost of targeting vendor internal tools that hold customer contact data.

Watch for WorkOS's customer notifications and whether the ARTEX framework details are published, plus any follow-on phishing campaigns against WorkOS customers using the stolen business contact records.
