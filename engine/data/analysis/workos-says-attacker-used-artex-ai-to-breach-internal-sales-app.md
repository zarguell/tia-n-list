WorkOS disclosed a breach of an internal sales application after a threat actor accessed business contact records and customer communication summaries. The attacker used an AI-powered offensive security framework to automate reconnaissance and exploitation attempts. According to the company, no production services or app data were accessed. WorkOS provides authentication and identity APIs to over 3,000 companies including OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor, Perplexity, Snowflake, Plaid, Webglow, and Netlify. The firm disabled external access to the affected app, contracted an independent forensics team, and warned affected customers to stay alert for phishing or social engineering attempts.

The incident matters because it demonstrates AI-assisted intrusion tooling being used against developer-platform supply chains, even when production systems remain untouched. The exposed contact records and conversation summaries create a targeted phishing risk against a high-value customer base. Watch for whether the independent forensics investigation reveals additional access, and whether WorkOS publishes IOCs or a more detailed timeline.
