The Gentlemen ransomware-as-a-service operation, tracked as GOLD SHERWOOD by researchers, has listed 683 victims on its leak site as of the end of July 2026, with 169 added in July alone. The group emerged in mid-2025 and operates a double-extortion model. Attackers gain access through exposed firewall vulnerabilities or stolen VPN credentials, including at least one case involving a Fortinet SSL VPN with compromised credentials and no MFA enabled.

Once inside a network, affiliates disable security tools and backup services before encrypting systems. Researchers found a median time of about two days between first post-compromise activity and ransomware deployment. Lateral movement happens via RDP, with tools stored in C:\PerfLogs. The group targets organizations opportunistically across industries with no clear sector focus.

The speed from initial access to encryption is notable. Two days leaves a narrow window for detection and response. Organizations should ensure MFA is enforced on all VPN concentrators, monitor for unusual RDP activity, and verify that backup systems are isolated from the main network. The 683-victim count on the leak site likely represents only confirmed victims who refused to pay.
