The ransomware group qilin has posted a claim against Touring Club Suisse, one of Switzerland's largest automobile clubs with over 1.5 million members. The claim was posted on September 20, 2026 and is currently marked as unverified by threat intelligence tracker Yazoul. No data leak has been published yet and the specific data allegedly stolen has not been disclosed.

Qilin (also styled "Qilin" or "Agenda") is a Ransomware-as-a-Service operation that has been active since 2022. The group targets organizations across healthcare, education, government, and manufacturing sectors, primarily in Europe and Asia. They use both Windows and Linux encryptors and have been observed demanding ransoms in the range of $50,000 to $800,000 in previous incidents.

The claim is unverified at this stage, meaning there is no confirmation that Touring Club Suisse has actually been breached or that any data was exfiltrated. Ransomware groups routinely post claims before or instead of publishing data, and some claims are fabricated or exaggerated. If verified, the impact could be significant given the club's membership base and the personal data it likely holds, including vehicle registration details, insurance information, and contact records. Watch for data publication on qilin's leak site within the next 72 hours as an indicator of claim authenticity.
