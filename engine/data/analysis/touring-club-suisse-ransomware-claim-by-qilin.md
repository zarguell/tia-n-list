Qilin's claimed attack on Touring Club Suisse now has secondary corroboration: a German incident listing records a ransomware attack on Touring Club Switzerland (TCS) with data theft and leak, where the original attribution to Qilin still rests on an unverified claim listing from yazoul.net. TCS itself has not confirmed the incident in any captured material, and no leak publication has been observed.

The story also carries the MedImpact breach, where the pharmacy benefit manager took 11 months to notify members of a breach linked to Qilin and now faces a potential class action. That is a confirmed breach disclosure, unlike the TCS claim, and the two should not be conflated: one is an unverified actor claim, the other a documented incident with legal consequences.

When reading this story, keep claim and confirmation separate. Watch for TCS confirmation or a Qilin leak-site listing that would upgrade the claim, the MedImpact litigation outcome and its notification-timeline implications, and Qilin's overall claim cadence as a gauge of the group's current operational tempo.
