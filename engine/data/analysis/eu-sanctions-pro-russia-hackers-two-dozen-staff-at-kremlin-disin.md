On Monday 16 November 2026 the Council of the EU sanctioned 30 individuals and two entities for Russian information manipulation and malicious cyber activity. The influence side adds the Social Design Agency, 24 of its staff and six pro-Kremlin commentators under the Russia destabilizing activities regime created on 8 October 2024, bringing that regime to 111 individuals and 21 entities. The cyber side lists NoName057(16), a pro-Russian group that has run DDoS attacks against European websites for four years. The EU's diplomatic service calls this its largest sanctions package targeting foreign information manipulation and interference.

A sanctions listing will not take down the botnet, but it changes the legal exposure for European companies that might pay, host, employ or otherwise do business with listed parties. Putting a disinformation contractor and a DDoS crew in the same announcement shows the EU treating them as two arms of one hybrid campaign.
