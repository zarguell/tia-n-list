Polish healthcare was breached twice within a month, and in both cases the entry point was a software vendor rather than a hospital. The first incident hit MyDr, with The Record reporting the personal information of potentially 19 million people exposed. The second hit Qbusoft, the company behind the Medyc software used by Polish healthcare providers: per Help Net Security, a SQL injection flaw combined with an export function that had no time limit let attackers steal about five million patient records. DataBreaches.net covered the Medyc attack on September 26, 2026.

The pairing matters because it shows a repeatable path into a national health sector: compromise one widely deployed vendor and reach many clinics at once. The Qbusoft detail that an export routine lacked any time bound suggests basic application hygiene failures, not a sophisticated intrusion. The incidents drew a reaction from Poland's Deputy Prime Minister, so the political pressure on vendor security requirements is likely to grow.

Watch for confirmation of the final MyDr exposure count, whether the stolen Medyc records surface on leak forums, whether additional clinics running Medyc report compromises, and any Polish regulatory action against healthcare software vendors.
