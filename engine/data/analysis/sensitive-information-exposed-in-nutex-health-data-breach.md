Nutex Health, a for-profit healthcare operator running 28 facilities across 12 states, disclosed a data breach in an SEC filing. The company said an unauthorized third party accessed and exfiltrated information from its servers, including data that "may be private and/or confidential." Nutex has $875 million in annual revenue and trades as NUTX on the Nasdaq Capital Market with a $1.28 billion market cap. The company has not yet determined what specific data was compromised or whether patients, employees, or business partners were affected.

After detecting the intrusion, Nutex hired external incident-response and forensic specialists, activated its cybersecurity response plan, implemented containment measures, and notified law enforcement. The breach investigation is ongoing. Two outlets (BleepingComputer and SecurityWeek) have now covered the incident, confirming the SEC disclosure and the company's description of exfiltrated data.

This story matters because Nutex is a significant healthcare operator with a large physical footprint and publicly traded valuation. The ambiguity around affected populations is a red flag: healthcare breaches involving patient data carry regulatory consequences under HIPAA and often result in class-action exposure. Watch for the company's follow-up disclosures on data scope, any HHS breach notification filings, and potential downstream effects on NUTX stock.
