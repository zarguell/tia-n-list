Riken confirmed unauthorized access to the operating system of the Fugaku supercomputer, with personal information and research data belonging to 4,597 users potentially exposed. The intrusion is reported to have happened last week, and the operator announced it on Monday while suspending Fugaku operations. Investigators are still working to establish when the access occurred and what the intruder reached.

The public disclosure gives no CVE, entry vector, actor, or evidence that data was actually taken, so 4,597 is a population at risk rather than a confirmed loss count. What to watch is Riken's timeline for the start of the access, whether the exposed material includes user identifiers or research outputs, and whether other Riken systems share the affected credentials or management plane. Japan Times and malware.news cover the same operator statement, with no independent technical detail yet.
