Foreign hackers targeted operational technology systems at two private water utilities in Colorado in late August 2026, according to the Colorado governor's office. The attackers changed equipment settings, disabled remote access and alarms, and altered pumping cycles at facilities serving fewer than 200 people. Disruptions were brief and did not affect water service or public safety.

The governor's office described the attackers only as "foreign actors" and noted awareness of "ongoing efforts across the nation by an Iranian-backed group to access drinking water and wastewater systems, as per CISA." However, it has not been confirmed whether the Colorado attacks are part of the broader campaign that hit water systems in at least a dozen states beginning in July, which included coordinated incidents at 30 Minnesota water utilities.

Technical details remain sparse. The incident follows CISA's July alert that over 100 internet-exposed water systems were being targeted, and a Senate bill to fund water sector cybersecurity hardening. The Colorado case is notable because it confirms actual OT manipulation at US water infrastructure, not just scanning or credential access.
