Foreign hackers targeted Colorado water systems in August 2026, according to the state governor's office, as reported by Reuters. The attack on critical water infrastructure is the latest in a pattern of foreign state-sponsored campaigns targeting U.S. water and wastewater systems. CISA has previously warned that over 100 U.S. water systems were targeted in a July cyber campaign.

Colorado's governor disclosed the incident publicly, though specific details about the attacker's identity, techniques used, and whether the intrusion succeeded in disrupting operations remain limited. The timing places it within an ongoing surge of nation-state activity against American critical infrastructure, particularly water systems that often lack dedicated cybersecurity staff and run legacy industrial control systems.

Water utilities remain high-value targets because disruption can have immediate public safety consequences. Organizations in this sector should verify network segmentation between IT and OT environments, ensure remote access is properly authenticated, and review CISA's cross-sector critical infrastructure performance goals. The lack of public detail about this incident suggests either an ongoing investigation or an intrusion that was contained before operational impact.
