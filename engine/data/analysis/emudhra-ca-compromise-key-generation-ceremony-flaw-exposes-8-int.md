The eMudhra CA compromise involved eight intermediate certificate private keys exposed through a Key Generation Ceremony (KGC) flaw at the Indian certificate authority. The breach enables attackers to issue TLS certificates for arbitrary domains, undermining trust for any site relying on eMudhra-issued chains. CERT-In, The Register, and SecurityWeek documented the incident, with browser vendors and programming languages initiating emergency revocation measures.

Affected intermediate certificates include keys tied to high-trust issuance paths; the incident triggered an emergency cleanup across the PKI ecosystem. No specific CVE is tied to the ceremony failure itself, but CVE-2026-111943 appears in the story metadata. The exposure window and full revocation status remain active concerns, and organizations should audit certificate pinning and revocation policies until the affected intermediates are fully revoked.
