Eight intermediate CA certificates from Indian authority eMudhra were compromised due to insufficient entropy during key generation. Mozilla and Google blocked them on October 17; Apple and Microsoft followed on October 19 and 20. The weak keys could let anyone forge trusted HTTPS certificates and impersonate any site against clients that have not removed trust. eMudhra revoked the certificates and downstream stores were notified. Mozilla’s Dennis Jackson published a client-check tool; some clients may still need updates. No exploitation campaign is confirmed, but the exposure window makes verification of site certificates important.
