eMudhra CA compromise: a Key Generation Ceremony flaw exposed eight intermediate CA private keys, six for TLS (DV, OV, EV) and two for code signing. Go assigned CVE-2026-111943 and released Go 1.26.10 / 1.27.3 with denylist updates; Apple blocked the certificates on October 19 and Microsoft on October 20. CERT-In also flagged the incident. The primary risk is unauthorized certificate issuance under a trusted chain rather than ordinary endpoint compromise. Defenders should monitor TLS chains and certificate stores for unexpected intermediates and apply browser and language-runtime updates that block the affected keys.
