Unit 42 reports an AI-powered espionage campaign that targeted government Linux infrastructure between October 2025 and April 2026. Attackers used AI-assisted scripts to compromise servers, harvest administrator credentials, and deploy persistent backdoors and web shells. The activity remains unattributed under the researchers' framework, with no state or criminal sponsor identified. The behavior points to intelligence-gathering rather than financial gain.

A related advisory notes CVE-2026-102764 is under active exploitation in this context, tied to BeyondTrust Remote Support prior to version 26.2.3. The campaign highlights how AI-assisted tooling accelerates both intrusion and credential theft in government-facing Linux environments. Watch for attribution updates and any expansion of the CVE footprint.
