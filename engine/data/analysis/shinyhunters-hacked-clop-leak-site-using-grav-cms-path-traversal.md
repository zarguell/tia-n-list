ShinyHunters breached the Clop ransomware gang's Tor leak site by exploiting an unauthenticated Grav CMS path-traversal vulnerability. The group defaced the site, claimed theft of source code, server logs, onion service private keys, and Grav plugins, and demanded an eight-figure ransom. Clop has moved to a new onion address and denies any relationship with ShinyHunters. Separately, law enforcement actions continue against ShinyHunters members, including a new FBI arrest of a Canadian suspect in Pennsylvania linked to the recent FBIjobs.gov breach.
