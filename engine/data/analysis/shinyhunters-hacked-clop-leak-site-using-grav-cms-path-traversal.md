ShinyHunters breached the Clop ransomware gang's data leak site earlier in September by exploiting an unauthenticated path traversal vulnerability in an unpatched Grav CMS instance, planted a text file, then replaced the site with a full-page defacement bearing its Umbreon Pokemon logo and a link to its own leak site. ShinyHunters claims it stole source code, Grav CMS plugins, server logs, and the private keys for Clop's Tor onion service, and issued a ransom demand against Clop, reportedly an eight-figure amount rising every 24 hours, threatening to publish which companies paid Clop, how much, and to what Bitcoin address.

Clop has since moved its leak site to a new onion address and says the old one will stay up briefly before retirement. It denied any relationship with or negotiations with ShinyHunters and confirmed its Grav installation had not been fully updated, while disputing ShinyHunters' theft claims. The flaw class matches CVE-2024-27921, a Grav file-upload path traversal fixed in Grav 1.7.45, which TantoSec documented in October 2024 chained with CVE-2024-34082 SSTI account takeover for unauthenticated RCE; the specific CVE used against Clop is not publicly confirmed.

The episode matters twice over. First, the stolen server logs could reveal the IP addresses of everyone who connected to Clop's leak site, which unmask victims researching their own data and possibly gang infrastructure. Second, disclosure of Clop's payment records would expose organizations that secretly paid a ransomware gang, a secondary extortion wave aimed at victims rather than at Clop itself. The feud reportedly traces to competing claims over the Oracle E-Business Suite zero-day CVE-2025-61882 that Clop exploited in 2025.

Watch for ShinyHunters publishing stolen logs or payment records, for confirmation of which Grav CVE was used, and for any Clop victims appearing in ShinyHunters' leak site as a direct result of this breach. Anyone running Grav CMS should verify they are on 1.7.46 or later, since the chain to full RCE is public and years old.
