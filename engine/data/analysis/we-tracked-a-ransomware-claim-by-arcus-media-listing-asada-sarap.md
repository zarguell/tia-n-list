ARCUS MEDIA, a ransomware group, has claimed a cyberattack against Asada Sarapiqui, a water supply utility serving the Sarapiqui region of Costa Rica. The claim appeared on the group's dark web leak site, listing the organization as a victim. Asada Sarapiqui provides water services to communities in the northern lowlands of Costa Rica, an area with significant agricultural activity.

Ransomware targeting water utilities has escalated in frequency since the Oldsmar, Florida incident in 2021. Costa Rica's water infrastructure has faced repeated ransomware pressure, including the Conti attacks against government ministries in 2022. ARCUS MEDIA is a lesser-known group, but targeting critical water infrastructure in Central America aligns with the broader trend of ransomware operators seeking high-impact targets in regions with limited incident response capacity.

If confirmed, this would represent disruption to essential services for thousands of residents. Water utilities typically run legacy SCADA/ICS systems with limited segmentation, making them attractive targets. Organizations in the water sector across Latin America should review network segmentation, backup integrity, and incident response plans.
