Distribution and supply chain giant Wesco has confirmed it is investigating a cybersecurity incident after the extortion group ExfilSquad claimed to have stolen data and posted it on its leak site. Wesco says the incident involves its cloud CRM environment and that it has worked with the CRM vendor; the company states it does not believe sensitive data is at risk and that there has been no business interruption.

CRM environments are a high-value target because they hold customer records, contract terms, and sales pipeline data, so an exfiltration claim in that area deserves skepticism until the review is complete. ExfilSquad is an active data-theft operation, and supply chain companies are attractive targets because one compromise can expose thousands of downstream customers. Watch for a formal breach notification, a scope statement from the CRM vendor, and any leak-site postings that name data types or volumes.
