Three chained vulnerabilities in LG smart TV webOS allow pre-authentication root access from the local network with no PIN and no user interaction. The chain was tested on real TV models and yields full root without authentication. Because LG smart TVs are common in both consumer and enterprise environments (conference rooms, digital signage), the exposure is significant. There is no mention of a patch timeline in the report. Watch for LG advisory response and for network-level exploits targeting webOS TV endpoints.
