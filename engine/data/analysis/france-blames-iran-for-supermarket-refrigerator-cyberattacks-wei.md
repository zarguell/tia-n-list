France has blamed Iran for a cyberattack that disrupted refrigeration at around 700 French supermarkets. The incident affected supermarket operations at scale and prompted France to weigh sanctions against Tehran.

Iran denied involvement. France summoned Tehran's ambassador over the attack, which raises the incident from a criminal or technical intrusion to a state-level dispute with diplomatic consequences.

The event is a reminder that connected refrigeration and other building systems can become a public safety issue when they are attacked. Operators should review remote access to refrigeration controllers, monitor for unauthorized configuration changes, and keep manual fallback procedures ready.
