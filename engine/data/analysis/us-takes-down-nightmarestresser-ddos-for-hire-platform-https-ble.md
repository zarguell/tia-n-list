The FBI seized the nightmare-stresser[.]com and nightmare-stresser[.]org domains on September 16, 2026, taking down one of the longest-running DDoS-for-hire platforms. The service claimed over 566,000 registered users and 52 dedicated servers capable of launching DDoS attacks up to 200 Gbps targeting Layer 4 and Layer 7 protocols. The FBI Cyber Division confirmed that since 2022, NightmareStresser was used to launch hundreds of thousands of actual or attempted DDoS attacks targeting victims worldwide.

The takedown is part of Operation PowerOFF, a coordinated international law enforcement effort that has been running since December 2018. Previous actions under this operation include the seizure of 15 DDoS service domains in 2018, the DigitalStress takedown in the UK, the Dstat.cc seizure in Germany, multiple rounds of domain seizures (13 domains, then 48 domains), and arrests of stresser operators in Poland. The DOJ previously took down nightmare-stresser[.]com in December 2022 and arrested six suspects, but the service returned under new domains.

This enforcement action removes a significant booter infrastructure but is unlikely to meaningfully reduce DDoS-for-hire availability. The barriers to launching replacement services remain low, and the demand from script-kiddies and extortionists persists. Organizations should continue to plan for volumetric DDoS rather than counting on law enforcement to eliminate the threat.
