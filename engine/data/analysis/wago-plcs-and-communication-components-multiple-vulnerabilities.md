cert.vde.com published CSAF advisory version 1.1.0, VDE-2025-081, covering multiple vulnerabilities in WAGO PLCs and communication components. The advisory tracks CVE-2025-41700, CVE-2025-41738 and CVE-2025-41739.

Watch the advisory's affected-product list and fixed firmware versions, and whether any of the three CVEs is added to CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog or paired with public exploit code. ICS and PLC flaws are sensitive because reachable devices often run without the option of quick downtime for patching.
