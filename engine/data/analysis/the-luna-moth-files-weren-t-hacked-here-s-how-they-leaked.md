The Luna Moth Files appeared online without any claim of responsibility. Silent Ransom Group called them "fake" and denied a breach, but DataBreaches.net reports they were real leaks. The files' origin was not a direct hack of Silent Ransom's infrastructure; the disclosure describes how they actually leaked. This is primary reporting by the site author, not a secondary aggregator repost.

Watch for confirmation of which data categories are in the files and whether Silent Ransom updates its denial. The story turns on verifying the leak mechanism rather than accepting the group's public claim.
