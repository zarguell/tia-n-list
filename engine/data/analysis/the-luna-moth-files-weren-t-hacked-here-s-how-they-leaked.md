The Luna Moth ransomware group's internal files appeared on an onion site without a direct claim by the group. Silent Ransom Group called the leak fake and denied any breach. DataBreaches.net investigation confirmed the files are genuine, revealing operational tactics of the group. The incident highlights the difficulty of verifying ransomware leak site claims and the risk of misattribution when actors dispute ownership. Monitor for further releases or law enforcement action tied to the exposed material.
