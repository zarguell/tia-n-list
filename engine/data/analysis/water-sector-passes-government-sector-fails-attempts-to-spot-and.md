CISA published a rare public breakdown of its red team assessments, covering two targets tested on a voluntary, by-request basis: one government-sector organization and one water-sector organization. Red teamers gained initial access in both cases, but only the water organization caught the simulated attack and responded.

At the government organization, labeled Organization A, CISA used phishing from an internal email address to compromise multiple workstations, gained elevated privileges over the domain, and moved laterally into sensitive business systems and cloud resources undetected; CISA's analysis notes the organization did not respond effectively to the red team activity. At the water organization, Organization B, network defenders quickly detected the initial compromise and quarantined the affected systems.

The publication lands amid a month of elevated targeting of water facilities across the United States and repeated federal warnings to the sector. Watch whether utilities and government networks act on the core finding that detection and response, not perimeter strength, decided both outcomes, and for further CISA red team reports published in this format.
