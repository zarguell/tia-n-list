Microsoft reports Chinese threat actor Storm-0940 used an AI-driven operation in a ConnectWise breach that exposed 2,250 cloud tenants. The event notes the attackers exploited a compromise to access customer cloud environments, though specific CVEs, victim names, or confirmed data exfiltration volumes are not detailed in the available sources. The story stays low on corroboration: only two sources (redhotcyber.com and threatnoir.com) with a Mastodon collector post as the latest update. What to watch: confirmation from Microsoft or affected ConnectWise customers, any CISA advisory linking Storm-0940 to the incident, and whether encrypted tenant variables were actually accessed or exfiltrated.
