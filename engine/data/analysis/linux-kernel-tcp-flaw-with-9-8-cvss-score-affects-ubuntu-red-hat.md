CVE-2026-98432 is a race condition use after free in the Linux kernel IPv4 networking stack TCP setup path, rated at CVSS v3 9.8 and affecting Ubuntu, Red Hat and other distributions. An unreleased internal Anthropic model exploited the flaw against servers with a multipath TCP listener, leaking a kernel pointer in about 48 minutes and reaching root command execution about five minutes later, before the vulnerability was patched upstream and backported to maintained stable kernel branches after coordinated disclosure on October 15, 2026.

Distribution level attention followed the disclosure. Red Hat rated the CVE page Moderate at 5.9 and later listed Important impact at 8.1 in its machine readable advisory, deferring fixes for RHEL 8, 9 and 10 to a future update as of October 29, 2026, while Amazon Linux shipped kernel6.18 and kernel6.12 fixes. Ubuntu and Red Hat lacked an official fix at the time of reporting, and the Debian security tracker raised impact questions about the effect on non patched servers. Watch for upstream and backported fixes and for exploitation activity.
