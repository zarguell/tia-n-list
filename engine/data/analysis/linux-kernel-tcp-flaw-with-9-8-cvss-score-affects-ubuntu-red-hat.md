CVE-2026-98432 is a Linux kernel IPv4 networking use-after-free flaw scored CVSS 9.8 with vector AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H. The OSV record describes it as ipv4: free inet_opt and ireq_opt after an RCU grace period, with downstream packages across Amazon Linux, Debian, openSUSE and Ubuntu. Ubuntu released an advisory on October 15, 2026 and updated it on October 29. Red Hat rates it Moderate at 5.9 while its machine-readable advisory later lists Important impact at 8.1; Amazon Linux lists Important at 7.0 with fixed packages including kernel6.18 (ALAS2023-2026-3251) on October 24 and kernel6.12 (ALAS2023-2026-3335) on October 29.

The flaw can let remote attackers crash affected systems and disrupt services. Fixes are available for some distributions while others remain pending, so exposure depends on kernel version and distro.

Administrators should apply the available kernel updates and reboot, and verify their installed kernel against vendor advisories. Systems running affected Ubuntu, Red Hat, Amazon Linux, Debian or openSUSE kernels remain at risk until patched.
