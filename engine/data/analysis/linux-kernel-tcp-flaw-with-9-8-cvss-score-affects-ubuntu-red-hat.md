CVE-2026-98432 is a use-after-free in the Linux kernel IPv4 networking subsystem, summarized as ipv4: free inet_opt and ireq_opt after an RCU grace period. The OSV record carries a CVSS v3 score of 9.8 (AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H) and lists downstream packages across Amazon Linux, Debian, openSUSE, and Ubuntu. Amazon Linux rates it Important with a CVSS v3 base score of 7.0 and shipped fixes for kernel6.18 and kernel6.12 in late October, while Red Hat rates it Moderate at 5.9 (later listed as Important at 8.1 in its machine-readable advisory) for RHEL 8, 9, and 10, with RHEL 7 unaffected, and had deferred fixes as of October 29, 2026.

The vulnerability drew attention because Anthropic reported that an unreleased internal Claude model found and exploited it: a race-condition use-after-free in the networking stack targeting servers with a multipath TCP listener, leaking a kernel pointer in about 48 minutes and reaching root command execution roughly five minutes later. The Hacker News relayed that the model built working exploit chains across Chrome, Safari, Android, Windows, and Linux, with all flaws already patched after coordinated disclosure. The vulnerability was patched upstream and backported to all maintained stable kernel branches.

Ubuntu released an advisory on October 15, 2026 with an update on October 29, 2026. Defenders should update to patched kernels on all supported distributions and treat multipath TCP listeners as the highest-risk exposure until patched. Severity ratings vary across vendors, so prioritization should follow the vendor advisory for the specific distribution in use rather than a single headline score.
