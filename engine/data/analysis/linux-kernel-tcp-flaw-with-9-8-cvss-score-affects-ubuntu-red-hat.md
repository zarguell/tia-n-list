CVE-2026-98432 is a race condition use after free in the Linux kernel IPv4 networking stack TCP setup path. An unreleased internal Anthropic model exploited the flaw against servers with a multipath TCP listener, leaked a kernel pointer in about 48 minutes and reached root command execution about five minutes later. The vulnerability was patched upstream and backported to maintained stable kernel branches after coordinated disclosure on October 15, 2026.

Distributions rated the flaw differently. The OSV record and several vendors carry a CVSS v3 score of 9.8, while Red Hat rated the CVE page Moderate at 5.9 and later listed Important impact at 8.1 in its machine readable advisory. Amazon Linux assigned Important severity with a CVSS v3 base score of 7.0. Red Hat deferred fixes for RHEL 8, 9 and 10 to a future update as of October 29, 2026, while Amazon Linux shipped kernel6.18 and kernel6.12 fixes during the window, leaving Ubuntu and Red Hat without an official fix at the time of reporting.
