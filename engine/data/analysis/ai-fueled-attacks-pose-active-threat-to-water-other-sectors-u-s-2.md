U.S. government agencies — NSA, CISA, FBI, DOE, and EPA — issued a joint advisory warning of ongoing attacks targeting Siemens S7 Series programmable logic controllers (PLCs) across water, food, energy, chemical, manufacturing, and commercial facilities. The attackers are using AI-generated scripts to exploit these industrial control systems, which manage physical processes in critical infrastructure. The advisory does not attribute the activity to a specific nation-state but characterizes it as an active threat requiring immediate mitigations.

This is notable because PLCs sit at the intersection of IT and OT — compromising them can cause physical damage, service disruptions, or environmental harm. AI-lowered barriers for attackers mean less skilled actors can now target specialized industrial systems that previously required deep OT expertise. The joint-agency framing (five agencies) signals the U.S. government considers this a priority across multiple critical infrastructure sectors simultaneously.

Watch for follow-on advisories from Siemens with specific firmware patches or configuration hardening guidance. The advisory explicitly notes that PLC targeting extends beyond Siemens, so operators of other PLC vendors should review their exposure. If attribution emerges — particularly if linked to Iranian state actors given ongoing U.S.-Iran tensions — the policy and sanctions implications escalate significantly.
