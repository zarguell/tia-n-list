Aesto Health, a SaaS provider that helps healthcare organizations migrate and archive patient data during EHR transitions, disclosed a breach affecting 9,540,683 individuals. The intrusion occurred between December 2 and December 18, 2025, when an unauthorized actor accessed a portion of Aesto's Amazon Web Services infrastructure. The breach was confirmed internally on May 26, 2026, following a forensic investigation by external specialists, and disclosed publicly on June 24.

The exposed data includes full names, dates of birth, medical information, driver's license numbers, financial account numbers, health insurance information, individual taxpayer identification numbers, other government IDs, and Social Security numbers. This is a near-complete identity kit, making the affected population highly vulnerable to medical identity theft, insurance fraud, and financial account takeover.

What makes this breach significant beyond its scale is the downstream exposure. Aesto Health acts as an intermediary, holding patient data from multiple covered entity clients. The 9.5 million affected individuals are patients of various healthcare organizations that used Aesto's migration and archival services, meaning the breach radius extends across an unknown number of hospitals, clinics, and health systems. Organizations that used Aesto Health for data migration should determine whether their patient populations are impacted and prepare breach notifications accordingly. The 6-month gap between compromise confirmation and public disclosure is also notable, though not unusual for complex healthcare breach investigations.
