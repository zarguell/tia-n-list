The Akira ransomware group claimed two new victims in mid-September 2026: Stransky Heiz-Mess-Regeltechnik GmbH, a German industrial manufacturer specializing in heating, measurement, and control technology, and Manders Companies, a family-owned contractor in the Washington Metropolitan Area providing maintenance, renovation, and painting services for multi-family, commercial, and residential properties.

The Manders Companies claim is more detailed. Akira threatened to leak 70GB of corporate data including employee personal information (SSN numbers, passports, driver's licenses), financial records, confidential client files, contracts, agreements, and NDAs. The attack was reported on September 16, 2026. The Stransky Heiz-Mess-Regeltechnik claim appeared on the group's blog via cti.fyi, though specific data exfiltration details were not provided in the available reporting.

Akira continues to target mid-sized organizations across diverse sectors, from European manufacturing to US-based service contractors. The group's pattern of naming victims and threatening data leaks with specific volume claims (70GB in this case) is consistent with their double-extortion playbook. Organizations in similar sectors should monitor for indicators of compromise and ensure backup systems are isolated and tested.
