South Korea's Bithumb crypto exchange disclosed a data leak following unauthorized access to its internal mobile system used by employees for internal communications. The breach was discovered on Tuesday and is being investigated. CoinTelegraph and Yonhap reported that Bithumb notified authorities and is assessing the extent of exposed employee data.

The incident is one of three financial firm breaches reported in South Korea that day. Because the compromise targeted an internal employee communication platform rather than customer wallets, the direct customer impact may be limited, but employee data exposure can fuel follow-on phishing and credential-targeted attacks against exchange staff.
