Bithumb discovered a breach of its internal employee mobile communication system, notifying authorities. CoinTelegraph and Yonhap News report the crypto exchange is assessing the leak extent; no crypto funds were lost. The incident is one of three South Korean financial firm breaches reported the same day.

Because the breach targeted an internal employee platform rather than customer-facing wallets, the primary risk is employee data exposure and potential follow-on social engineering. Monitor for Bithumb's final scope disclosure.
