Bitget has confirmed the mechanics of last week's $387.5 million theft: attackers exploited zero-day vulnerabilities in two third-party security appliances to reach the exchange's wallet environment. Parallel investigations by SlowMist and Google Cloud's Mandiant found a web shell dropped on one appliance, a C2 channel, lateral movement to the production wallet job server, and a custom withdrawal tool used to drain hot and warm wallets.

The timeline is long for an intrusion of this size. SlowMist dates the earliest malicious activity to August 31, when a hidden script under a service on one appliance node read an environment variable holding a database password and connected to the database, with similar activity on two more nodes on September 23 and 25. Mandiant places privileged access to both appliances on September 24, followed by the web shell and lateral movement. The theft itself ran roughly three hours after midnight on September 25 (02:31 to 05:23 UTC+8) across Ethereum, XRP Ledger, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Optimism, BSC and Base, hitting ETH, XRP, BNB, AVAX, USDT, USDC and other tokens. CEO Gracy Chen attributes the attack to North Korean hackers based on IP behavior and on-chain analysis, consistent with the country's record of exchange heists such as the $1.5 billion Bybit theft.

The operational lesson is that perimeter security appliances are becoming the preferred pivot into high-value targets, the same pattern seen in recent edge-device campaigns. A zero-day in a monitoring or security product silently turns the defender's own tooling into the attacker's foothold, and from there the path to wallet-signing infrastructure ran through one database credential and one job server.

Watch for disclosure of the appliance vendors and the specific CVEs, which Bitget has not yet named. Bitget has resumed partial withdrawals and launched a Recovery Bounty Program paying 5 percent of recovered or frozen funds, so on-chain tracking of the stolen assets and any bounty-driven freezes will show how much of the $387.5 million can be clawed back.
