Arion Kurtaj, now 21, pleaded guilty at Woolwich Crown Court on 16 November to breaching Rockstar Games and attempting to extort the developer. The plea also covers other cybercrimes from his teenage involvement with Lapsus$. The intrusion occurred in 2022 and included leaking Grand Theft Auto VI clips years before the game's release.
