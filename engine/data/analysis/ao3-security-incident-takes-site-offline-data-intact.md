Archive of Our Own (AO3) remains offline after a security incident detected around October 30, 2026. The site's official status page (otwstatus.org) states the expected restore time is two to four weeks, that all data is intact, and that user information does not appear stolen, though administrators say they are not ruling that out. A Mastodon update confirms the site was taken down to mitigate the attack. No CVEs are linked to the incident and no active exploitation details or attacker attribution have been published.

The story is limited to a single authoritative source (otwstatus.org) with secondary Mastodon confirmation. There are no reports of data exfiltration, no CVSS scores, and no third-party forensics released. Watch for updates from otwstatus.org on restoration timelines and any revised breach assessment, particularly if user data exposure is later confirmed.
