Archive of Our Own (AO3), the fanfiction archive operated by the Organization for Transformative Works, went offline on October 29, 2026 after a security incident was detected. The site was initially labeled maintenance before the operator acknowledged the incident roughly 18 hours later. AO3 houses about 18.8 million works; the organization says no data was deleted and all works, bookmarks, and kudos are safe, but it cannot rule out theft of the user database, which holds usernames, email addresses, IP addresses, and hashed passwords.

The outage is expected to last two to four weeks while the OTW investigates and remediates. Because the database may have been accessed, users should treat their AO3 passwords as compromised and change any reused credentials elsewhere, even though passwords were stored in scrambled form. The incident drew mainstream coverage and community scrutiny of the organization's communications.
