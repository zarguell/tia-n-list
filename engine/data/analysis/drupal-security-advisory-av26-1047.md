Drupal patched critical remote code execution flaws in core, tracked as CVE-2026-108097, affecting Drupal core before versions 11.4.10, 11.3.19 and 10.6.19. The advisory (SA-CORE-2026-014, AV26-1047) states the Field UI flaw is not exploitable with core alone but contributed modules create paths, including at least one that requires no authentication. The Canadian Cyber Centre and Drupal project both rate it critical and warn that exploits could develop within hours or days of disclosure.

Over one million Drupal sites are in scope, many in government, university and non-profit environments. The security team urged administrators to reserve update time before the October 14 window. No confirmed in-the-wild exploitation was reported in the event content, but the combination of critical severity, unauthenticated paths through contributed modules and rapid exploitation timelines makes immediate patching the priority. Watch for follow-up reports of active scanning or PoC releases.
