ThreatFabric disclosed Hirudo, an advanced Android banking trojan delivered through dropper apps disguised as shopping, adult video, or short drama applications. Unlike standard overlay trojans, Hirudo replaces the target banking app with a patched copy that disables security checks from inside the app. The malware includes remote control via WebRTC screen viewing, remote camera access, and Accessibility-driven interaction, making it a full remote access trojan combined with banking fraud.

The current campaign targets eight Indonesian banking apps, with internal code suggesting potential expansion to China, India, Mexico, Morocco, and Thailand. ThreatFabric identified the operators using fake update prompts to uninstall genuine apps and install the modified replacements. The payload hooks signature, Accessibility, screen-capture and Play Integrity checks internally. Watch for broader geographic expansion and distribution via social media-advertised apps.
