Hirudo malware delivers fake updates to patched banking applications, then establishes persistence through modified installers. The campaign targets mobile and desktop banking clients that have not verified update sources. Security sources including BleepingComputer and SecurityAffairs track the distribution mechanism.

No specific CVE is assigned to Hirudo itself, since it relies on social engineering rather than a software vulnerability. Watch for updated IOCs or banking regulator alerts that link the malware to specific financial institutions. Users should verify updates through official app stores and disable sideloading.
