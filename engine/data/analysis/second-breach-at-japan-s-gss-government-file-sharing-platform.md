Japan's Digital Agency disclosed a second potential data breach involving the Government Solution Service (GSS), the shared IT platform used by government agencies to exchange files. Japan Cyber Watch describes it as another breach of GSS involving access by someone other than the intended recipients. The Cyber Security Guru post carries the same disclosure and calls it another potential leak from the platform.

A second disclosure on the same shared platform points to a recurring exposure rather than a single file-sharing mistake. The available reports do not name the affected agencies, a record count, or the access window, so the scale is still open. Watch for the Digital Agency's scope statement, whether this incident and the earlier GSS case share a cause, and which ministries' documents were in the affected exchanges.
