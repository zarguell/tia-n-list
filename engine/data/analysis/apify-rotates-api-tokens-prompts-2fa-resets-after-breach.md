Apify detected unauthorized access to parts of its internal systems on November 9 and 10, 2026, discovering the intrusion the following day at 12:13 UTC. The company cut off the attacker, invalidated internal credentials, rotated API tokens and Apify Proxy passwords, reset all active sessions, and revoked third-party OAuth connections including GitHub out of caution. Apify reports 80,000 customers, including Microsoft, Samsung, T-Mobile, Accenture, Siemens, the European Commission, and Roche, and says it found no indication that account data stored on the platform was affected.

The incident matters because Apify sits in the data-collection layer for many downstream systems, so a compromise of its internal tooling could expose customer secrets and proxy credentials even if stored account data was untouched. Watch for Apify's follow-up notifications to affected customers, confirmation of which OAuth integrations were actually abused, and whether any rotated secrets are later seen in credential-stuffing or scraping abuse.
