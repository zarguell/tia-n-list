Atlassian published its November 2026 Security Bulletin on 17 November, documenting 318 vulnerability entries across Jira Software, Jira Service Management, Confluence, Bitbucket, Bamboo, Crowd, Fisheye and Crucible. The bulletin counts 266 high-severity entries and 52 critical entries involving third-party components, and some CVEs appear under more than one product. The reported fixes shipped in product releases over the preceding month rather than in a single November build. Reporting highlights CVE-2026-4800, CVE-2026-47890 and CVE-2026-21600 among the entries.

The large count reflects the breadth of Atlassian's product set and its bundled dependencies, not 318 separate newly found flaws in Atlassian code. For defenders, the useful detail is that third-party components account for the critical entries, so patching the Atlassian application alone may not be enough where a bundled dependency carries the fix. The sources reviewed here make no claim of active exploitation for these CVEs. The next check is mapping each of the three named CVEs to the exact product and fixed release in the bulletin, then confirming which internet-facing Jira, Confluence and Bitbucket instances still run a pre-fix build.
