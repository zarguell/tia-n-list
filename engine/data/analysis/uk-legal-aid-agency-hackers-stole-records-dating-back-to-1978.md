The UK Legal Aid Agency confirmed that a breach first disclosed in May 2025 exposed records dating back to 1978. Forensic review expanded the scope: approximately 250,000 records predate 2007, with the earliest verified entry from 1978. Compromised data includes names, National Insurance Numbers, case references, payment details, practitioner contracts, and account credentials.

This is a long-tail breach with multi-decade data retention risk. The update event (rss:bad4176f2fda5b85) attaches to the canonical story, which already holds four events. Watch for regulatory action under UK GDPR and any notifications to affected applicants whose records extend back decades.
