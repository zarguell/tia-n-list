SOCRadar Threat Research Unit describes a multi-stage campaign from a Russian-speaking actor that chains XaocMail phishing, Phantom53 command and control, and the Havoc post-exploitation framework. Preparation started in October 2025, with testing and framework development in early 2026 and the campaign launching in August 2026. XaocMail is self-hosted at 185[.]224[.]132[.]148:8585 and generates personalized document-sharing lures. Its ClickFix pages are served through Cloudflare serverless infrastructure and deliver Phantom53 MSI installers.

Phantom53 uses DNS-over-HTTPS for command and control, drops PowerShell payloads and deploys Havoc on demand. GBHackers, citing STRU, reports that the actor used Anthropic's Claude Code to build and harden parts of the toolkit. That detail fits the wider pattern of attackers using coding agents for infrastructure work, but the observable campaign artifacts here are the phishing platform address, the MSI delivery path and the DoH-based C2 design.

Watch for copies of the XaocMail panel, new ClickFix landing pages on Cloudflare infrastructure and Phantom53 samples with DoH configuration changes. Because the chain relies on user execution of an MSI, endpoint telemetry around installer launches and subsequent PowerShell activity is a more reliable detection point than the lure content alone.
