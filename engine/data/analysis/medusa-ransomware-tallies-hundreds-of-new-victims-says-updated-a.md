CISA, FBI, and HHS published an updated joint advisory on Medusa ransomware, revealing the gang has impacted over 500 critical infrastructure victims since June 2021, with hundreds added in the past year alone. The advisory details Medusa's reliance on access brokers who receive $100 to $1 million per entry, with premium pricing for exclusive partnerships. Most brokers, however, work simultaneously for multiple ransomware variants. The gang operates a ransomware-as-a-service model and targets sectors including healthcare, defense industrial base, critical manufacturing, government services, IT, and financial services. Medusa's revenue model has evolved: the advisory notes fresh tactics for gaining access and a significant expansion in victim count. The 500-victim milestone across critical infrastructure makes this one of the most active ransomware operations currently tracked by U.S. agencies.
