CISA, FBI, and HHS published an updated advisory on Medusa ransomware, revealing the group has added hundreds of new victims in just over a year. The RaaS operation now relies heavily on access brokers who receive between $100 and $1 million per initial foothold, with premium pricing for brokers who work exclusively with Medusa. In practice, most brokers sell access to multiple ransomware variants simultaneously.

The advisory expands on a March 2025 version, drawing from ongoing FBI investigations. Medusa's tactic shift includes fresh initial access methods and updated post-exploitation procedures. The group's scale and speed of victim acquisition make it one of the more active RaaS operations currently tracked by federal agencies.

The advisory signals sustained federal focus on Medusa as a top-tier ransomware threat. Watch for additional technical indicators and defensive guidance as FBI investigations continue.
