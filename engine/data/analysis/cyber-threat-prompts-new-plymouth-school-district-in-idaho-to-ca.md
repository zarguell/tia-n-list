New Plymouth School District in Idaho canceled classes at all three of its schools on Tuesday, November 11, 2026, after detecting a cyber threat in the district network. Superintendent Wade Wilson told teachers, families, and students that the district is not using computers or its network and asked recipients to delete suspicious messages, per DysruptionHub reporting relayed by Databreaches.net.

The incident is a small-district operational disruption rather than a confirmed data breach: no data theft or ransomware claim is reported, and the response centers on taking systems offline and warning staff and students. Watch for whether the district confirms unauthorized access, restores network services, or identifies the intrusion vector, since school networks are frequent targets for credential phishing and ransomware staging.
