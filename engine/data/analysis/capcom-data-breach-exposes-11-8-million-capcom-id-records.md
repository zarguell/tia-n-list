Capcom confirmed a data breach exposing 11,774,247 CAPCOM ID records worldwide. The company detected suspicious activity on October 19, 2026 and blocked access while investigating. Compromised data includes names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth, and gameplay data; 196 account login passwords were changed by the attacker. Capcom states no credit card information was compromised and urges users to watch for phishing.

This is a confirmed breach with a precise victim count (11.8 million). The event adds a new Mastodon reference to an existing story with 20 events and 4 sources. No additional exploitation claims are made beyond the original disclosure.
