Capcom confirmed a data breach exposing 11.8 million CAPCOM ID records worldwide. The event notes 196 accounts received special attention and highlights the breach involved centralized game identity systems used for web services and online games. Capcom disclosed the intrusion and the scale of affected accounts in an official statement.

This is a large-scale data breach affecting gaming and web service accounts rather than a targeted infrastructure vulnerability. The exposure of centralized identity records increases the risk of account takeover and phishing campaigns using stolen profile data. Affected users should change passwords, enable multi-factor authentication, and monitor for unusual login activity tied to their CAPCOM IDs.
