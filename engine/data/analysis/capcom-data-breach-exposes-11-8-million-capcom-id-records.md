Capcom confirmed unauthorized access to its CAPCOM ID online membership service, with a third party gaining access after suspicious activity was detected on October 19, 2026. The breach covers 11,774,247 CAPCOM ID records worldwide, including names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth, and gameplay data; login passwords were changed on 196 accounts. Capcom states no credit card information was compromised and urges users to watch for phishing attempts.

The scale of the exposed dataset, which includes identity and gameplay data, creates a phishing and account-takeover risk even without payment data. Users should change passwords on any service where the same credentials were reused and be alert to targeted phishing leveraging the leaked personal details.

Watch for confirmation of how the attacker gained access and whether additional Capcom services or partner data were affected.
