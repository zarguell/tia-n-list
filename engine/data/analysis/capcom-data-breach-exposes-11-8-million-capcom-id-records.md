Capcom confirmed a data breach affecting approximately 11.7 million CAPCOM ID records worldwide. Exposed fields include names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth, and gameplay data. For 196 accounts, login passwords were changed by attackers. A smaller subset of 43 accounts had additional personal data exposed. The company detected suspicious activity on October 19, 2026, blocked access, and is investigating with authorities.

The event content states the breach scope is 11,774,247 records and that 196 accounts had passwords changed. Capcom's official notice (in Japanese) and English-language coverage both confirm the unauthorized access came from a third party. There is no CVE assignment; this is an incident report rather than a vulnerability advisory.

Watch for any follow-up on attacker attribution, regulatory notifications, or evidence of data misuse. Users should rotate Capcom ID passwords and enable two-factor authentication.
