Delhi Police have apprehended a 14-year-old boy after an alleged intrusion into a test website used by the Indian home ministry to let former Agniveers register and look for employment in other government departments. The DataBreaches.net item says the juvenile accessed the site, extracted information and posted an advertisement on the dark web claiming to hold personal data on about 4,000 Agniveers. Mathrubhumi reported the arrest and put the listing above 2,500 records.

The two counts do not match, and the available text does not show whether the advertisement contained real records, a sample or only a claim. No field list is given for the data, so the exposure cannot be scoped beyond the site's purpose. A test portal for a recruitment workflow is still a government system, but nothing here says how it was configured or whether it held live applicant data.

Watch for the formal charge, a forensic inventory of what was actually taken, and any notice to people whose details were on the portal. The case also turns on whether the site was internet reachable with weak access controls, a detail neither report supplies. Until that is established, treat the record counts as claims rather than confirmed breach totals.
