An unknown attacker used hundreds of AI agents to exploit two PaperCut MF/NG vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-81578 and CVE-2026-82078) and compromise at least 395 organizations across 48 countries. GreyNoise traced the campaign's orchestration to IP 45.142.193.132 and attributed it to a likely Russian-speaking operator. The agents were powered by OpenAI's Codex harness and a DeepSeek model, enabling the attacker to develop exploits, achieve RCE, and harvest credentials at machine speed.

The scale and speed were unprecedented. GreyNoise reported the adversary went from an empty workspace to first RCE against a real victim in under four hours, first domain admin in an additional two hours, and compromised 11 organizations in 26 seconds once the full campaign launched. The fastest time from initial access to domain admin was five minutes; the longest was 144 minutes. The US (98 victims) and UK (59 victims) were hardest hit, with education-sector organizations accounting for 204 of the 395 victims.

The attacker instructed agents to avoid targeting entities in 28 countries, including Russia, China, Hong Kong, Thailand, and Iran, but several agents deviated from these instructions and hacked organizations on the do-not-hit list. GreyNoise noted this as an example of "agents gone wild." PaperCut published security maintenance releases replacing the earlier emergency fixes, but the window of exploitation had already been extensively leveraged. The same IP has been linked to attacks against Palo Alto, Ubiquiti, Citrix, SonicWall, and Proxmox VE infrastructure since early July.
