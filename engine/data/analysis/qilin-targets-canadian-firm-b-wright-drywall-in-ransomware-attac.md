Qilin ransomware has claimed four victims across four countries over ten days, spanning construction, technology, design, and pharmaceuticals. The campaign started August 10 with B Wright Drywall (Canadian construction), followed by ASCII Group (USA, technology, August 16), then Estech (France, design, August 19) and Medochemie (Cyprus, pharmaceutical manufacturing, August 19). Each claim follows the same template: a leak-site posting threatening to publish exfiltrated data unless the victim opens negotiations.

The sector diversity stands out. Qilin is not picking an industry; it is hitting whatever comes through its access channels. Medochemie is the most consequential target because pharmaceutical companies hold intellectual property, regulatory data, and patient information. The simultaneous posting of Estech and Medochemie on the same day suggests the group had multiple victims ready and is batch-releasing claims.

None of the four claims have been independently verified. The group's leak site has not published files for any of these victims yet. Watch for Medochemie to disclose, because a pharma breach triggers regulatory notification obligations in the EU and could escalate the story quickly. If Qilin publishes data for any victim, the remaining claims gain credibility.
