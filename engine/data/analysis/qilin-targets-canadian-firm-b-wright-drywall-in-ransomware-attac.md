The Qilin ransomware group has been on a sustained campaign, with the story accumulating six events over the past two weeks. The original claim targeted B Wright Drywall, a Canadian construction firm, on August 10. Subsequent claims hit ASCII Group (US tech firm, August 16), Medochemie (Cyprus pharmaceutical company, August 19), Air International Thermal Systems (US thermal management provider, August 26), and most notably the US Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (August 26).

The ATF breach is the highest-profile event in this sequence. ATF confirmed a "major incident" involving a standalone system that operates separately from the ATF enterprise network, the eForms system, and other ATF systems. ATF terminated connections to the affected environment and initiated incident response with the Department of Justice. Qilin posted ATF to its dark web data leak portal without specifying whether files were stolen or a ransom demanded. The breadth of targets, spanning construction, technology, pharmaceuticals, manufacturing, and a US federal law enforcement agency, indicates Qilin is operating at scale with no sector discrimination.
