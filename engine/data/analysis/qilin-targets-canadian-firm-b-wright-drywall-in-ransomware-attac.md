Qilin has been running a sustained campaign since at least August 10, 2026, hitting at least seven organizations across Canada, the US, the UK, and Cyprus. The victims span construction (B Wright Drywall, CCQ), technology (ASCII Group), pharmaceuticals (Medochemie), thermal manufacturing (Air International), financial services (AP Capital Partners), and notably the US Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives. The ATF breach is the most consequential: ATF confirmed a "major incident" involving a standalone system, launched DOJ-coordinated forensics, and terminated connections to the affected environment. ATF emphasized the compromised system was separate from its enterprise network, eForms system, and other agency systems, and said operations were unaffected.

The campaign shows Qilin operating with broad geographic and sectoral ambition. Two Canadian hits (B Wright Drywall on Aug 10, CCQ on Sep 4) suggest sustained focus on that market. The UK financial firm and Cyprus pharmaceutical target further extend the footprint. Most claims follow a standard template: data exfiltration threatened, negotiations demanded, no specific CVEs cited. The ATF entry appeared on Qilin's dark web leak portal without an initial ransom demand or exfil volume claim, which is unusual for the group.

What to watch: whether ATF discloses more about the compromised system's scope and whether any sensitive firearms/explosives data was exposed. The DOJ investigation may produce additional details. Qilin's pace (seven claimed victims in under four weeks) suggests either a very active affiliate base or a coordinated push. Monitor for additional claims and whether any of the smaller victims confirm actual data loss.
