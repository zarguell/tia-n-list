Okta's Threat Intelligence team assesses that a vishing cluster it tracks as O-UNC-053, previously thought to be affiliated with the ShinyHunters extortion crew, may have rebranded as INCEPTION. The group surfaced on November 17 with seven claimed victims on its extortion site, including AMD, PepsiCo, Cigna Healthcare, AutoZone and Lucid Motors. None of those companies has publicly confirmed a breach, so the victim list is still an attacker claim.

ReversingLabs has tracked INCEPTION activity since late October and confirmed 21 domains used for SSO phishing, 41 domains believed to be campaign infrastructure and four more with suspected use. The phishing sites imitate corporate authentication services, mainly Okta but also NetIQ and OpenText Access Manager, and impersonate brands including Dropbox, Duolingo, Western Union, Indeed, Activision Blizzard, Riot Games, Twitch, Reddit, Nestlé, Anthropic, Nintendo, Starbucks, McDonald's, Paramount and NBCUniversal. Domain registration alone is not evidence that those firms were breached or even targeted, and the campaign leans on phone-based social engineering to capture SSO credentials and approvals.

The attribution is contested. INCEPTION rejects the ShinyHunters link and says it has no affiliation, while reporting from Infosecurity Magazine cites Okta's view that the same crew is still calling staff after its leak site went offline following arrests. Watch for victim confirmations, new phishing domains, and whether the extortion site returns; the phone-based lure and SSO focus are the durable tradecraft here, not the group name.
