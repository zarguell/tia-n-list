Okta Threat Intelligence assesses that O-UNC-053, a cluster active since March 2026 and previously tied to the ShinyHunters leak site, very likely shifted its extortion brand to INCEPTION. The mechanism is live voice phishing: callers impersonate support staff, demand urgent password resets, and steer victims to legal-themed domains that capture usernames, passwords, one-time codes and push approvals while the caller controls the page in real time. Okta says the kit cannot defeat Okta FastPass or FIDO2/WebAuthn passkeys. The INCEPTION site appeared on November 16, seven victims were listed by November 21, and none confirmed a breach. Okta saw the cluster's infrastructure registered six to 19 days before companies were named.

The seven leak-site names understate the exposure and overstate the evidentiary value of the claims. ReversingLabs confirmed the kit on 21 domains, attributed 41 more at high confidence and four at medium confidence, including lures for brands that were never listed, while Silent Push tracked the same human-led SSO vishing model against more than 100 enterprises in January. A successful call hands over an SSO identity, putting mail, cloud consoles, source code and financial applications behind one login rather than one compromised machine. The September arrests of alleged ShinyHunters members coincided with a drop in O-UNC-053 registrations, not an end to the cluster, and INCEPTION's public denial of affiliation does not erase the shared infrastructure and targeting sequence.

Enforce Okta FastPass, passkeys, smart cards or other FIDO2/WebAuthn factors in policy and keep sensitive applications off unmanaged devices; Okta states that its kit fails against phishing-resistant authentication. Require independent verification before help desks reset passwords or MFA, and deny or step up logins from rarely used networks and the three ASNs Okta named, AS19318, AS210558 and AS399486. Block the `legal-TARGET.com`, `legaldept-TARGET.com`, `TARGET.legal` and `TARGET-legal.com` patterns at DNS and web proxies, including legal-amd[.]com, legal-pepsico[.]com and legal-lucidmotors[.]com, and monitor new registrations and certificate logs for the same naming. Hunt DNS, proxy, Okta and identity-provider logs back to October 21, 2026 for those lookups, cross-origin SSO referrers and anomalous post-authentication SaaS activity; revoke sessions and reset credentials where a match is confirmed.

The assessment changes if a named victim confirms exfiltration or Okta raises its judgment to high confidence that O-UNC-053 operates the INCEPTION site rather than partnering with another extortion actor. A fresh leak site or a new domain theme using the same evening registration window and Cloudflare nameserver pairs would confirm the brand is disposable and leave the tradecraft assessment intact.
