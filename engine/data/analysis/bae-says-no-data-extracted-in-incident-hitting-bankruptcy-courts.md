BAE Systems restored the federal judiciary's Bankruptcy Noticing Center (BNC) and stated that no data was extracted during the cybersecurity incident that knocked the system offline for more than two weeks starting around October 9. The company said no other systems were affected and no personal information was breached. The Administrative Office of the US Courts confirmed the BNC came back online on October 26 and began clearing delayed notices. BAE did not specify the incident cause or confirm whether it was a cyberattack, and no ransomware group has claimed responsibility.

The Judicial Conference declared a Bankruptcy Rules emergency on October 22, allowing judges to extend deadlines and issue orders to manage fallout from the outage. While the restoration and the statement that no PII was taken reduce immediate risk, the prolonged downtime of a central federal court notification channel demonstrates how a single operational system failure can disrupt nationwide legal processes. Agencies should evaluate contingency plans for critical service providers and require vendors to disclose root causes promptly.
