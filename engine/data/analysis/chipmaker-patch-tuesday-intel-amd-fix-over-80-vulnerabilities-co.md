Intel and AMD shipped patches for more than 80 vulnerabilities combined in their August 2026 Patch Tuesday updates. Intel published 42 advisories covering 72 vulnerabilities, with high-severity fixes for privilege escalation in Xeon processors, TDX, and CSME and SPS, denial of service in Active Management Technology, information disclosure in Data Center Attestation Primitives, and privilege escalation, local code execution, and DoS in PROSet/Wireless WiFi software. A large set of medium-severity issues was resolved across Intel's AI and ML toolchain, including Transfer Learning Tool, Extension for PyTorch, LLM-on-Ray, Gaudi Container Runtime, and NPU Drivers.

AMD published five advisories covering a dozen vulnerabilities, including five high-severity issues in the Vitis development environment that could lead to private key disclosure, privilege escalation, and arbitrary code execution, plus code execution fixes in Ryzen Master Utility, SEV-SNP, and Power Design Manager. Earlier this month AMD also disclosed a Safe RET interrupt vulnerability and the PowerHooK SEV attack method. There are no known in-the-wild exploits tied to this round, but the Xeon, TDX, and CSME items are the ones to prioritize for datacenter fleets, and the Vitis private key disclosure is relevant to hardware development pipelines.
