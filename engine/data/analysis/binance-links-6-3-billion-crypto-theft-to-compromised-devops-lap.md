Binance linked the theft of about $6.3 billion in cryptocurrency to a compromised DevOps engineer laptop. The access allowed attackers to submit unauthorized withdrawal transactions after reaching a service container; Binance stated it has not found evidence of a leaked wallet private key and emphasized the attack was not a single action but involved a more complex process. The company reassured customers that funds were safe, suspended deposits and withdrawals during checks, and revoked authorizations for the affected address.

Binance said it will cover losses from the SAFU fund and company funds, and will publish an updated Proof of Reserves after remediation. Blockchain intelligence firm TRM attributed the attack to TraderTraitor, though Binance itself has not named a threat actor. Additional breach points were uncovered, but facts remain under investigation.

Watch for Binance's updated Proof of Reserves, confirmation of recovered or traced funds through blockchain partners and law enforcement, and whether additional breach points reveal broader access. The $6.3 billion figure and attribution to a compromised developer laptop make this a significant supply-chain and insider-access incident.
