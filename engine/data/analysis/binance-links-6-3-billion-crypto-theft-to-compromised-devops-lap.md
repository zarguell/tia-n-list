Binance linked the theft of approximately $6.3 billion in cryptocurrency to a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop, confirming unauthorized withdrawal transactions were submitted after attackers accessed a service container. Co-CEO Richard Teng stated the breach involved a more complex attack process, not a simple one-click action or leaked wallet private key. Binance suspended deposits and withdrawals, revoked authorizations, and committed to covering losses from the SAFU fund and company reserves.

Blockchain analysis firm TRM Labs attributed the attack to TraderT, though Binance has not named a specific threat actor. The company uncovered additional breach points but has not fully disclosed them, meaning the incident scope may expand. User funds are reported safe, but the operational disruption and scale of the theft make this a major exchange security event.

Monitor Binance's updated Proof of Reserves, additional breach disclosures, and any attribution updates linking TraderT or other actors to the incident. The combination of insider-access compromise, service container abuse, and multi-billion-dollar loss demands scrutiny of supply chain and privileged access controls across crypto infrastructure.
