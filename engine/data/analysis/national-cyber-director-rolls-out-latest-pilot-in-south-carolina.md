The Office of the National Cyber Director launched Project Lifeline 250, a six-month pilot in South Carolina focused on rural hospitals. It connects industry partners to strengthen hospital networks, deploy backup devices after an incident, and replace high-risk legacy equipment. National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross debuted the program in South Carolina; the health sector reported 804 large breaches affecting over 140 million people last year.

The sector is the FBI's top ransomware target for 2025, and rural hospitals lack the resources of larger infrastructure operators. The pilot mirrors an earlier Texas water-sector program and draws in state cybersecurity, law enforcement, and local government partners. It is policy-level defense, not a vulnerability or breach event, but signals sustained federal focus on under-resourced critical infrastructure.
