ProPublica reported that political appointees at the Department of Homeland Security asked CISA, the agency charged with protecting election systems, to secretly acquire a voting machine and hack it. The request, made a few months into the administration, raised concerns among officials about the integrity of the agency's election security mission.

The episode underscores tension between DHS leadership and the cybersecurity agency it oversees. Election officials and researchers will be watching whether CISA's independence and its role in safeguarding voting infrastructure are affected.
