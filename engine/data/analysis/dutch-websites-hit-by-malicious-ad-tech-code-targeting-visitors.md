Dutch websites were hit in early November 2026 by malicious code served through an external advertising supplier. Scientias.nl warned visitors that the code, delivered via its ad technology, collected data about visits and attempted to exploit vulnerabilities on visitors' devices. NOS, NRC, Tweakers, and others reported the same campaign across VI.nl, GeenStijl, Bright.nl, and De Dagelijkse Standaard, with iPhones singled out as targets; GeenStijl named the compromised supplier as Next Day Media.

Desnips reported on November 15 that the campaign abused Dutch advertising networks for zero-click iPhone compromise, which matches the earlier reporting that no click was needed. The significance is that the malicious code rode legitimate ad slots on high-traffic sites, so visitors were exposed just by loading a page. Watch for the named supplier's network, for further Dutch outlets confirming scope, and for the iPhone vulnerability said to be exploited.
