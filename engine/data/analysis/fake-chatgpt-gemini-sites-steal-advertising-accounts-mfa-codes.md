A phishing campaign uses fake ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity sites to steal advertising account credentials and MFA codes through browser-in-browser attacks. Researchers found the malicious pages target agency staff and media buyers whose accounts extend to multiple downstream clients, letting attackers spend balances on fraudulent campaigns or resell accounts. The operation leveraged the Muse AI agent launch and relied on a fake Google login iframe displaying accounts.google.com to harvest credentials.

The attack targets high-value ad-management accounts rather than individual consumers, and the browser-in-browser technique (devised by mr. dox in March 2022) remains effective because the fake window shows the correct URL and interface. There are no CVEs linked to this campaign; the threat is purely social-engineering and credential theft. Watch for reports of actual financial losses from compromised agency accounts or additional AI-product impersonation pages.
