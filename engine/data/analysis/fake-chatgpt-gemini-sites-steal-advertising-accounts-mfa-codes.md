A phishing campaign impersonates ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and Perplexity advertising tools to steal ad-manager credentials and MFA codes via browser-in-the-browser frames. The operation targets agency staff and media buyers whose accounts give access to downstream client campaigns and available balances. Researchers observed hundreds of victim submissions and active exploitation during the investigation. The technique, first shown by mr. dox in March 2022, uses a fake Google login window inside the page with accounts.google.com displayed in the address bar. The attackers monetize by running fraudulent ad spend or reselling the stolen accounts. No CVEs are associated; the defense is user awareness and MFA hardening rather than a patch.
