SOCRadar's Threat Research Unit found an exposed staging server in September 2026 that held the live workspace of an operator linked to the n0n ransomware group. The open directory at 185.157.161[.]148 held 1,719 files, including breach and exfiltration tooling, locker source code, extortion infrastructure, and an AI coding agent environment with more than $7,000 in paid LLM API usage. The research ties the operation to at least 23 organizations in 14 countries, described by sector and geography rather than by name, including a US sports retailer, a cloud provider, a teachers' union, a Venezuelan ISP, an Argentine ministry, and a pharmaceutical subsidiary.

Every documented compromise relied on weak or stolen credentials; no vulnerability exploitation was identified. Where n0n does not encrypt, the operator destroys exfiltrated data and backups. A later Italian-language post frames the case as an extortion campaign against Visa, Mastercard and JPMorgan, but the source research quoted here lists victims by category and does not confirm those named companies. Watch for named victim disclosures and for whether the exposed tooling changes n0n's targeting or extortion tactics.
