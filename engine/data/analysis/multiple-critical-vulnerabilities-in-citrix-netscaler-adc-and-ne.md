Citrix confirmed eight vulnerabilities in NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway under bulletin CTX697096 on September 27, 2026. The two critical zero-days, CVE-2026-88771 (CVSS 9.5, unauthenticated improper input validation RCE affecting all deployments by default) and CVE-2026-88772 (CVSS 9.5, memory overflow leading to RCE or denial of service when DTLS is enabled, which is the default on VPN virtual servers), were exploited in the wild before patches existed. The remaining six CVEs range from CVE-2026-88773 (HTTP request smuggling, 9.3) through CVE-2026-88778 (TCP ISN prediction, 8.8, fixed only by enabling Enhanced ISN Generation, not by the upgrade alone). Affected customer-managed builds include 14.1 before 14.1-73.37 and 13.1 before 13.1-64.23, plus FIPS branches.

The story matters because NetScaler appliances sit at the edge of enterprise networks as VPN gateways, load balancers, and authentication proxies. A single unauthenticated RCE on that boundary does not just compromise one server; it gives an attacker a persistent foothold to pivot into internal applications, harvest credentials, and intercept user traffic. The event list notes exploitation prior to patch release, and both CISA (which added the pair to its KEV catalog on September 27, 2026) and CERT-EU advisory 2026-014 confirm active global exploitation. What the event list does not state is that watchTowr publicly warned of unpatched NetScaler RCE exploitation on September 26, 2026 - before Citrix assigned CVE IDs - and that Secure Private Access hybrid deployments using NetScaler instances are equally exposed and must be upgraded.

Defenders must treat every internet-facing NetScaler as potentially compromised. Preserve forensic evidence before patching: capture logs, a snapshot, a support bundle, and a core dump, then check the NetScaler Console Security Advisory page (version 14.1-73.36 or later with telemetry enabled) for Citrix-provided indicators of compromise. Update to the fixed builds (14.1-73.37, 13.1-64.23 or 13.1-64.24 to avoid a known reboot loop on 13.1, and the corresponding FIPS releases), rotate all secrets and certificates stored on or used through the appliance, keep management interfaces off the public internet, and enable Enhanced ISN Generation for CVE-2026-88778. Forward NetScaler logs to your SIEM and inspect for abnormal administrative sessions and unexpected outbound connections.

Watch for public attribution of the exploitation campaign - none has been made - and for confirmation that threat actors move from the KEV pair to the six configuration-dependent CVEs, especially the memory-overflow and request-smuggling issues that could expand the attack surface beyond the default settings. A change in exploitation scope or a vendor confirmation of in-the-wild abuse of CVE-2026-88773 through CVE-2026-88778 would shift the assessment from targeted critical patching to broader perimeter hardening.
