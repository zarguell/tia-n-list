Denmark confirms a major breach of its Central Person Register (CPR) affecting about 8.8 million registered individuals, exceeding the national population due to historical records. Exposed data includes names, residential addresses, CPR numbers, and potentially other civil-registration information. The incident involved unauthorized access to the national identity database. The breach has been reported by GBHackers and security sources, with additional coverage noting the scale exceeds the current population because the register retains records for deceased and emigrated citizens.
