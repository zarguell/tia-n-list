Denmark's Central Population Register (CPR) confirmed an unauthorized access incident affecting approximately 8.8 million registered individuals, roughly 80 percent of the 11 million records in the national civil registry. The breach occurred in September 2026; CPR administration became aware on October 2. Attackers abused a private Danish company's legitimate search access to extract names, residential addresses, CPR numbers, and related data. The Danish Data Protection Agency noted a brute-force enumeration of valid CPR numbers followed by data extraction. The private firm's access has been blocked, police are investigating, and authorities are warning citizens of follow-on phishing. The scale and the misuse of authorized third-party access make this a significant supply-chain-style exposure.
