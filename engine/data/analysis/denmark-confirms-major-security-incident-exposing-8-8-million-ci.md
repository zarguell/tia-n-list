Denmark confirmed a serious breach of its Central Person Register (CPR). About 8.8 million registered individuals had CPR numbers, names, and residential addresses exposed through abuse of a Danish firm's legitimate access. The Ministry press release links to the official disclosure published 05 October 2026. The impact exceeds Denmark's current population because records include past registrations. No CVEs apply; this is an access-control failure, not a vulnerability. Watch for follow-on identity fraud using the exposed CPR data.
