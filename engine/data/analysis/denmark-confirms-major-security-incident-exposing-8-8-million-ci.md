Denmark has confirmed a serious cybersecurity incident involving unauthorized access to the Central Person Register (CPR), affecting about 8.8 million registered individuals. Exposed records include names, residential addresses, CPR numbers, and potentially other personal data. The impact exceeds Denmark's current population because the registry includes historical entries.

Why it matters: A national civil registry breach of this scale exposes citizens to identity fraud and targeted social engineering. The exposure of government identification numbers makes remediation harder than a typical commercial breach. Watch for official Danish updates, regulatory notifications, and any evidence of exploitation.
