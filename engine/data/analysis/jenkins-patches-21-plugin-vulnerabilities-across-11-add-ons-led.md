Jenkins released updates on November 25 for 11 plugins covering 21 vulnerabilities. The highest-risk item is sandbox bypass CVE-2026-136834, with RCE issues including CVE-2026-136835 and CVE-2026-136837 also named in the reporting. The German advisory gives the same 21 flaws across 11 add-ons and does not add a different count.

Watch for plugin-specific fixed versions and whether any of the RCE paths are reachable without authentication. The available text does not report exploitation, victims or public proof of concept code, so the bulk patch matters more as CI/CD exposure than as an observed campaign.
