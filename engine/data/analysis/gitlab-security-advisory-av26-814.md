The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security issued advisory AV26-814 on August 13, 2026, covering vulnerabilities in GitLab prior to 19.0.6, 19.1.4, and 19.2.2, effective as of August 12. The advisory directs administrators to the GitLab patch release (19.2.2, 19.1.4, 19.0.6) but names no CVE identifiers and makes no exploitation claim. The affected-versions framing suggests issues fixed across the current minor release train rather than a single-point zero-day.

GitLab is a common on-prem and self-managed CI/CD target, and self-hosted instances frequently lag on patching, so even unglamorous advisories here tend to get exploited once details are public. The lack of CVE numbers in the advisory is the gap to watch: the patch release notes will carry the identifiers, and exploit research typically follows disclosure. Priority action is upgrading self-managed instances to 19.2.2 or later and confirming no exposure of unpatched versions to the internet.
