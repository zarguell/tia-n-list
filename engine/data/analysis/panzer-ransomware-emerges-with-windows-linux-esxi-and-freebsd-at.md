Panzer is a new ransomware-as-a-service operation that launched on August 5, 2026 with a leak site and affiliate platform. Within its first month, the group claimed between 16 and 19 victims across 11 countries. Two confirmed Italian victims: kitchen manufacturer Doimo Cucine (30 GB allegedly stolen, listed August 17) and telecommunications engineering firm NTE Italia (16 GB allegedly stolen, listed August 21). Neither company has publicly confirmed the incidents. Leak-site posts remain attacker claims.

The operation stands out for its cross-platform capability, advertising payloads for Windows, Linux, VMware ESXi, and FreeBSD. Panzer uses a semi-open RaaS model where affiliates apply through Tox messaging, undergo screening, and receive an 80 percent share of ransom payments. The platform includes automated Bitcoin invoice generation and revenue tracking. The group has not yet had a sample publicly analyzed, but its rapid victim posting cadence and mature affiliate infrastructure suggest an experienced operator behind it.

Italian ransomware activity is accelerating in parallel. Ransomfeed reported 212 claimed attacks against Italian organizations by early September 2026, already exceeding the 169 claims for all of 2025. Panzer is part of this broader surge and should be tracked by security teams, particularly those with ESXi or FreeBSD infrastructure, as cross-platform ransomware targeting virtualization layers remains a high-impact threat vector.
