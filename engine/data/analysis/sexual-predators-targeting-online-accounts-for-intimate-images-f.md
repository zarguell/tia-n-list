The FBI issued a public service announcement warning that criminals are breaking into social media and personal accounts to steal and distribute non-consensual intimate images, which the bureau calls NCII. The stolen material is being posted or sold on criminal marketplaces alongside victims' names, phone numbers, email addresses, and social media handles, creating a ready-made path for harassment, stalking, and sextortion.

Account takeover is the enabling step, which makes this as much a credential and MFA story as a content crime one. Users with intimate material on their devices or accounts should enable strong authentication, review connected apps and session activity, and treat any unsolicited contact referencing private images as an extortion attempt. Organizations should expect this to surface in their user base: compromise indicators include credential stuffing patterns and accounts with new login locations or device enrollments.
