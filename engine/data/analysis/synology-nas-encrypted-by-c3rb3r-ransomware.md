C3RB3R, a Cerber-family ransomware strain that appends the .L0CK3D extension, has been encrypting Synology NAS devices running outdated DSM builds. Qihoo 360 identified it in an October 29 investigation and linked it to Synology-SA-24:20, the advisory patched in December 2024 for CVE-2024-10441, CVE-2024-10445, and CVE-2024-50629. Victim accounts describe uneven recovery: one NAS had a video partially restored to roughly nine seconds of image without a decryption key, while photos were lost entirely, and a second device that had not been reinstalled showed the intrusion in its logs with no identified vulnerability, only a reset.

The story is less about a new zero-day than about unpatched, internet-facing NAS appliances being swept up by an older flaw. Operators still running outdated DSM should treat the December 2024 patches as mandatory, block the management interface from the internet, and assume that any device left unpatched is at active risk of encryption.
