Siemens' October 2026 advisories address six critical fixes across PLCs, edge devices, and CROSSBOW products. The updates cover vulnerabilities in industrial control and edge computing stacks, which are high-consequence targets given their role in manufacturing and critical infrastructure. A separate report notes active exploitation of CVE-2026-110936, where attackers used digitally signed Siemens Software Center components to load malicious DLLs, indicating a trusted-software supply chain compromise rather than simple remote exploitation.

The active exploitation detail is the critical development: attackers leveraged original, signed Siemens binaries to deploy malicious DLLs, which suggests the campaign targeted engineering workstations or update mechanisms rather than direct PLC exploitation. Organizations running Siemens industrial software should prioritize patching the October advisory set and reviewing endpoint telemetry for unauthorized DLL loads tied to legitimate Siemens executables. No CVSS scores or full CVE lists were included in the brief advisory reference, so verification against the vendor's full bulletin is required.
