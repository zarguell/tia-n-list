N0n ransomware claims responsibility for an attack on PG Group (Pure Options), a Michigan cannabis operator. The actor reports exposing a directory database covering 577 accounts, staff and administrator passwords, and financial backups including payroll and contracts. The group states the data is exposed and unpaid. No CVE applies; this is a pure extortion claim. Organizations in retail and cannabis verticals should treat this as a confirmed breach indicator and rotate any credentials tied to the exposed directory.
