MALFEX delivered the Overlord RAT and an infostealer through eight malicious npm packages published since August 2023, accumulating 40,767 total downloads. The packages had 12 releases and targeted developers with a supply-chain infection that runs both remote-access and credential-stealing payloads. This is a sustained campaign, not a one-off package drop, because the download count spans more than a year of releases.

The threat is supply-chain compromise at scale: 40,767 downloads across 12 releases means widespread potential infection of developer machines and downstream build environments. No CVE is assigned to the malicious packages themselves; the harm is operational malware delivery rather than a vulnerability in a legitimate package. Watch for additional releases from the same publisher patterns and for Overlord RAT detections in build pipelines.
