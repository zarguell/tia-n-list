The FBI, UK NCSC, and Netherlands AIVD published a joint advisory on 15 September 2026 formally naming a Windows spyware family used by Iran's Ministry of Intelligence and Security (MOIS) since at least autumn 2023. The malware is tracked as HEAVYGRAM by the FBI and CHOSEN BRICK by the NCSC. It targets dissidents, journalists, and activists opposed to the Iranian government in the UK, US, and Netherlands.

Initial access comes through social engineering on WhatsApp, Telegram, and other platforms. Operators impersonate trusted contacts or tech support and convince targets to download files disguised as legitimate software. Observed lures include Pictory, KeePass, Telegram itself, RunwayML, Norton Antivirus, and even fabricated MRI scan results. A convincing decoy installation screen displays while the malware installs silently in the background. The attack chain uses chained loaders: base64-encoded PowerShell one-liners fetch archives from an abused Vultr Object Storage bucket, and a heavily padded VBScript (183,897 bytes across 63,791 lines) decodes and runs only when the host disk exceeds 50GB as a sandbox evasion check.

The stage-2 payload (Smqdservice.exe, a 23.1MB ZIP bundling a Python 3.11 runtime) establishes a dedicated per-victim Telegram bot for bidirectional C2, isolating each victim's traffic and blending with legitimate Telegram usage. Newer variants proxy this Telegram traffic through commercial residential proxy services (IPRoyal, Lightning Proxies). Capabilities include screenshot capture, microphone activation, theft of Telegram and WhatsApp data from browsers, saved-password and email theft, additional-malware downloads, file deletion, and in at least one observed version, full disk wiping. The malware persists via Registry Run keys under SMQDService or winappx and evades detection by instructing Microsoft Defender to exclude its own working directories. Stolen data from victims has appeared on pro-Iranian leak sites; the DOJ seized four such sites in March 2026. This update adds a Western intelligence advisory angle to the existing MOIS Telegram C2 story, confirming multi-government attribution.
