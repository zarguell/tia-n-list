Japan's Onamae Desktop Cloud, operated by GMO Internet, suffered unauthorized access in which an attacker installed malware on up to 26,739 customer servers. GMO confirmed the intrusion on October 20 and began progressively shutting down customer servers at 04:00 local time on October 21. The Desktop Cloud service is marketed for stable automated FX trading including MetaTrader 4. GMO states payment information is stored on separate infrastructure and is not affected, and has not confirmed data misuse or leakage, though the breach may have exposed data on impacted servers.

Customers running services on Onamae Desktop Cloud should check for security notifications from GMO and follow containment guidance. The provider suspended services across all plans and continues to investigate; further updates are expected as the investigation proceeds.
