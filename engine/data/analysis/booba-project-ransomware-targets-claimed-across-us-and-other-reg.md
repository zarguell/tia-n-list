Booba Project is a ransomware group posting victim claims on ransomware.live and related leak sites. In this window the group claimed the Research Foundation of the City University of New York among US targets, and separately claimed Drouin SAS, a French firm, per a report on infonewscyber.fr. The available reporting is claim-level only: no encryption details, ransom demands, or confirmed data exfiltration have been published.

Treat these as unverified assertions from the group's own leak site until affected organizations or regulators confirm an incident. Defenders should watch for follow-up disclosures from the named victims and for any sample data releases that would substantiate the claims; absent that, the entries function as threat-intelligence leads rather than confirmed breaches.
