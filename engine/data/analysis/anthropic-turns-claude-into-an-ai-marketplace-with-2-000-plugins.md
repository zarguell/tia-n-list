Anthropic launched Claude Marketplace, a public storefront for Claude-connected tools. The marketplace offers over 2,000 connectors and plugins from vendors including Atlassian, Google, Microsoft, Notion, Salesforce, CrowdStrike, Cursor, and Snowflake. Anthropic is also opening sales of Claude-powered agents and consulting integrations from Accenture, Boston Consulting Group, and Deloitte. The launch mirrors OpenAI's earlier apps marketplace attempt, which Anthropic explicitly wants to avoid repeating by allowing broader third-party publishing via Model Context Protocol (MCP) and Agent Skills. For defenders, this expands the Claude supply chain surface: third-party plugins, consulting integrations, and agent deployments create new dependency risks similar to browser extensions or IDE plugins. Monitor for security reviews of high-privilege connectors (e.g., Salesforce, Microsoft integrations) and any reports of malicious or overreaching agent behaviors published through the marketplace.
