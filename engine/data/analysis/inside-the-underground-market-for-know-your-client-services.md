Flare's research tracks an underground market for Know Your Client services, with data spanning March through September 2026. The report connects fabricated identity documents (passports, driver's licenses, Social Security cards from dozens of countries) to verification bypasses at banks and cryptocurrency exchanges. The analysis notes that the OnlyFake operator pleaded guilty in February 2026 after generating more than 10,000 fraudulent digital identity documents. Underground actors advertise not only fake documents but also manipulated photos and videos, live identity verification assistance, and accounts that claim to have passed checks. The study captures how these packages are promoted and sold in monitored communities, linking identity fraud directly to money laundering and financial crime infrastructure.

The coverage matters because identity verification is a foundational control for financial institutions, yet the market demonstrates consistent, scalable ways to defeat it. The findings are based on Flare's platform monitoring of hundreds of thousands of search results related to KYC services and verified account trading. No specific CVEs or software vulnerabilities are involved; the threat is operational and economic rather than a patchable code flaw. Watch for regulatory responses and whether exchanges tighten live-image comparison requirements, since the report shows that attackers already advertise help with passing those exact checks.
