Warlock ransomware, linked to the China-aligned threat actor tracked as Longlegs by Symantec, has breached a water utility, telecom provider, regional government, and university by exploiting SharePoint vulnerabilities including CVE-2025-49704 and related ToolShell flaws. The attacker deploys a web shell across multiple SharePoint versions, disables AV/EDR on over 40 hosts within two hours, and launches ransomware on at least 33 hosts. The group has focused on Portuguese and Spanish-speaking countries since emerging in June 2025. Recent reporting links the actor to Symantec's tracking and confirms ongoing use of on-prem SharePoint as an entry point. Organizations with on-prem SharePoint must patch ToolShell-related CVEs, audit for web shells, and review EDR telemetry for rapid protection disablement.
