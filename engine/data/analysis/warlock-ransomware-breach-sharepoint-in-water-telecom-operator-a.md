The China-linked ransomware group Warlock, also tracked as Longlegs by Symantec, targeted a water utility, a telecom provider, a regional government, and a university by exploiting SharePoint ToolShell vulnerabilities (CVE-2025-49704, CVE-2025-49706, CVE-2025-53770, CVE-2025-53771). Over the past two months the actor focused on Portuguese- and Spanish-speaking countries across Europe, Africa, and Latin America. In one intrusion starting July 22, the attacker disabled protection on at least 40 hosts within about two hours, then deployed Warlock ransomware on at least 33 hosts. After initial access through on-premises SharePoint, the attacker drops a multi-version web shell. Symantec and Carbon Black researchers note AV/EDR-killing tools were deployed in some Longlegs attacks. The group emerged in June 2025 and gained notoriety a month later after exploiting the zero-day chain.

Monitor for new victim disclosures in the targeted regions, updates on ToolShell patch adoption, and any attribution linking the actor more directly to state sponsors. The rapid host compromise and ransomware deployment show short dwell-time tactics that limit defender response windows.
