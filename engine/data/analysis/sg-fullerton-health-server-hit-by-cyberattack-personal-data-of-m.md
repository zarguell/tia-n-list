Fullerton Health, a healthcare service provider in Singapore, confirmed on October 31, 2026 that a server supporting its Migrant Worker Primary Care Plan (PCP) programme was hit by a cybersecurity incident involving unauthorized access, which appears to have affected migrant workers' personal data. The company issued a statement on its website on November 9, 2026. Because the PCP programme serves migrant workers, the affected population is a vulnerable group with limited recourse and elevated risk from identity theft or targeted scams.

Details on the scope of affected data, the number of individuals involved, and whether the data was exfiltrated or merely accessed have not been disclosed. What to watch is Fullerton Health's follow-up notification to affected workers, any regulatory reporting under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act, and whether the incident connects to broader targeting of healthcare providers serving migrant populations.
