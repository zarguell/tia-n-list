Criminal IP (by AI SPERA) is introducing AITEM, an AI-powered Threat Exposure Management platform positioned as the next evolution of Attack Surface Management. Rather than pure asset discovery, AITEM aims to filter noise, enrich exposure context, and guide prioritization so security teams can respond in real time. The announcement is tied to GovWare 2026 and reflects industry pressure to move from visibility to actionable response, especially as automated scanning lowers attacker barriers.
