The DDosia botnet, operated by the pro-Russian NoName057(16) hacktivist group, released a new target configuration on September 13 targeting Polish infrastructure. The target list includes Grupa Azoty (major chemical manufacturer), PZU and IN PZU (Poland's largest insurance group), Netia (major ISP), BondSpot (stock exchange infrastructure), and Powiat Zary (local government). DDosia is a crowdsourced DDoS platform where participants earn cryptocurrency for conducting denial-of-service attacks against designated targets aligned with the group's geopolitical objectives.

This configuration continues NoName057(16)'s pattern of targeting Polish critical infrastructure and financial services, consistent with their broader campaign against NATO-aligned countries supporting Ukraine. The inclusion of BondSpot and PZU suggests an intent to disrupt financial market operations and insurance services. Polish organizations in the crosshairs should verify DDoS mitigation capacity, ensure upstream provider filtering is in place, and monitor for HTTP flood and DNS amplification patterns typical of DDosia campaigns. Previous DDosia waves against Poland have generated traffic volumes in the hundreds of gigabits per second range, making CDN-level or ISP-level scrubbing essential for resilience.
