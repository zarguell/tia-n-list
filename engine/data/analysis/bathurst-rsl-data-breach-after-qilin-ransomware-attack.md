Bathurst RSL reported a data breach after a Qilin ransomware attack, adding to a cluster of Australian victims claimed by the same group within four days. The club stated the incident was contained, but the timing aligns with a broader Qilin campaign that has targeted multiple sectors in rapid succession. Qilin remains the most active ransomware group by claim volume, and manufacturing is the hottest sector according to recent tracking.

The key point for defenders is the speed of Qilin's multi-victim campaigns rather than a single breach. Australian organizations should review Qilin indicators, audit remote access paths, and prepare for rapid disclosure timelines given the group's tendency to publicize attacks quickly. The Bathurst RSL event reinforces that regional clubs and community organizations are within the group's targeting scope.
