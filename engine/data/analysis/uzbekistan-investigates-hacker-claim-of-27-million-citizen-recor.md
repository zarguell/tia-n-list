Uzbekistan is investigating reports of exposed personal data after hackers claimed access to records on 27 million citizens. The reports surfaced through coverage at timesca.com and ow.ly, and Uzbek authorities have not confirmed the breach; the claim remains under investigation.

A claim of 27 million citizen records would rank among the largest personal-data exposures in the region if substantiated. Watch for official confirmation from Uzbek authorities, any walkback of the hacker claim, and any signs of misuse of the exposed data.
