Oleksii Oleksiyovych Lytvynenko, a 44-year-old Ukrainian national, was sentenced to four years in US federal prison for conspiracy to commit wire fraud connected to the Conti ransomware operation. According to the Department of Justice, Lytvynenko participated in a conspiracy to deploy Conti, which infected more than 1,000 victim computers. Conti was one of the most prolific ransomware operations before it formally disbanded in 2022 following a leak of its internal communications. The group was linked to attacks on critical infrastructure including Ireland's health service and multiple US organizations. This sentencing represents one of the continuing law enforcement outcomes from the Conti ecosystem, showing that prosecutors are still working through cases tied to the group's peak activity period.
