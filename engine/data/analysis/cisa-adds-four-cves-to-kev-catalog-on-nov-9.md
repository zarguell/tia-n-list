CISA added four CVEs to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on November 9, 2026, citing exploitation evidence. The list includes GPUStack CVE-2026-102895, FreeSWITCH CVE-2026-116779, CVE-2026-123158, and CVE-2026-123218, the IBM Aspera Faspex incorrect authorization flaw. The announcement is CISA's own post on cisa.gov, so these are primary-source confirmations of in-the-wild exploitation rather than second-hand reports.

A KEV listing binds federal agencies to CISA's patch timeline, so all four CVEs carry a federal patch deadline. GPUStack users should move to the patched release, FreeSWITCH administrators should apply the vendor fixes, and Aspera Faspex operators should patch immediately given the CVSS 9.8 incorrect authorization flaw. What to watch: confirmed exploitation campaigns for each of the four CVEs, vendor fix availability for GPUStack and FreeSWITCH, and any federal agency patch status reports tied to the November 9 KEV batch.
