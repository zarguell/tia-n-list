RemControl is an Android banking trojan first observed in July 2026 that targets customers of more than 30 financial institutions across Western Europe, the Middle East, and Canada. The malware is distributed through fake Google Play Store pages advertising a TV streaming app (TVTap) and uses malvertising campaigns with geo-targeted mobile delivery. After installation, a dropper launches a local VPN service that blocks Play Protect checks, creates a new signing certificate for the payload, and requests Accessibility Service permissions. Once granted, RemControl displays full-screen phishing overlays from its C2 server to capture PINs, mobile banking codes, and card expiry dates. It also supports real-time screen streaming and remote control functions, making it a high-capability mobile banking threat.
