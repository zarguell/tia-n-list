Group-IB disclosed RemControl on September 23, 2026: a previously undocumented Android banking trojan operated as a malware-as-a-service platform by an affiliate tracked as UNKK, with first VirusTotal samples on July 19, 2026 and C2 domain bnbnhura[.]top registered May 12, 2026. Delivery runs through malvertising and fake Google Play pages impersonating the TVTap IPTV app, with at least one Italian campaign using geofencing and mobile User-Agent checks to serve the APK only to targets, and Meta Pixel tracking IDs on the download pages pointing to abuse of Meta's ad ecosystem.

The dropper's anti-analysis is the distinctive part. It requests BIND_VPN_SERVICE and routes com.android.vending traffic into a null tunnel so Play Protect cannot perform real-time checks, a trick also seen in a recent ToxicPanda version, then signs each payload with a fresh certificate under CN=Android Debug to defeat certificate-based detection. Once installed, it abuses the Accessibility Service to detect the foreground app and display full-screen phishing overlays served from C2 for more than 30 financial institutions across Italy, France, Spain, Poland, Portugal, Canada, and GCC states, requesting PINs, mobile banking codes, and card expiry details before revealing the real app. It also supports real-time screen streaming and remote control. Group-IB notes one overlay displaying an AI assistant response, a strong indication the overlays were built with AI models, and assesses a possible link to the Medusa UNKN botnet: droppers sharing the instal*tvtap*.apk naming convention carried Medusa UNKN payloads until July 2026, then switched to RemControl, whose UNKK tag differs by one letter.

The practical exposure is mobile users of banks in Western Europe, the Middle East, and Canada arriving through ad channels rather than the official store. Watch for expansion of the overlay target list beyond the current 30-plus institutions, additional operators renting the MaaS platform beyond UNKK, and whether the Medusa UNKN connection is confirmed, which would fold RemControl into an established distribution pipeline.
