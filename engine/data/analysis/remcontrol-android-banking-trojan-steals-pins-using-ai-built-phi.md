Group-IB disclosed RemControl on September 23, 2026 as a previously undocumented Android banking trojan run as a malware-as-a-service platform by an actor they track as UNKK. There is no CVE because nothing in Android is broken; every stage of the chain abuses legitimate mechanisms. First samples reached VirusTotal on July 19, 2026, but the C2 domain bnbnhura[.]top was registered May 12, 2026 and the two distribution domains, tvtap-hd[.]app and tvtap-liveapp[.]com, on July 10, so the operation was staged for weeks before the first build shipped. Victims arrive through malvertising on fake Google Play pages impersonating the TVTap IPTV app, with the observed Italian campaign serving the APK only to mobile User-Agents on Italian IP addresses. The dropper requests BIND_VPN_SERVICE and routes com.android.vending into a null tunnel, cutting Play Protect off from the network during installation, then signs the payload with a fresh per-install certificate under the generic subject CN=Android Debug, O=Android, C=US. The payload rides the Accessibility Service to inject full-screen overlays fetched from C2 at /overlay/{result_id}, harvesting PINs, mobile banking codes and card expiry dates, while streaming screenshots and the UI tree over a WebSocket and reconstructing unlock patterns across ten OEM lock-screen implementations.

The headline number, more than 30 targeted financial institutions across Italy, France, Spain, Poland, Portugal, Canada and GCC states, is a floor for what an infected device faces, not a ceiling on the operator's targeting: Group-IB found the target list is provisioned per device at registration, so any phone already compromised can be repointed at banks absent from the observed list without reinfection. Lineage matters as much as capability. The UNKK tag differs by one letter from the UNKN botnet Cleafy documented in June 2024, and Group-IB assesses that droppers sharing the instal*tvtap*.apk naming convention carried Medusa UNKN payloads until July 2026, then switched to RemControl. On that reading this is not a new operation but a payload swap inside an affiliate distribution channel that has been harvesting Italian and French banking customers for over two years, and the exposed panel API, which generates per-affiliate builds with configurable lure HTML, exists to rent that channel out. The verbatim AI assistant response left in a production overlay and API documentation describing credential theft as quiz answers complete the picture: the cost of producing bank-grade fraud infrastructure has fallen far enough that the people shipping it no longer read what they ship.

There is no patch to deploy; controls are app-origin and permission based. Enforce Play Protect and block installs from unknown sources through MDM, and treat any Accessibility Service grant to an app without an accessibility purpose as a compromise indicator, which matches Group-IB's guidance. The specific hunt: devices holding an active VPN service whose blocked-package list contains com.android.vending; recurring signing certificates under subject CN=Android Debug, O=Android, C=US paired with hashes that change per install; and accessibility back-navigation events firing the moment a user opens app management, accessibility settings, or factory reset screens. On the network side, block and alert on tvtap-hd[.]app, tvtap-liveapp[.]com, bnbnhura[.]top, definatelynoone[.]com and 157.90.179.116, and keep the dead-drop Telegram channels telegram[.]me/ftestera and telegram[.]me/+Psyt04xu-cRjMTg0 plus Meta Pixel IDs 997470916598588 and 1909605966397328 as pivots for campaign tracking. File hashes rot within days under per-install re-signing, so weight behavioral and infrastructure indicators; the threadlinqs entry TL-2026-2625 carries 25 IoCs and 9 detections worth pulling.

Two developments would move this assessment. Evidence that affiliates beyond UNKK are building payloads, visible in new affiliate tags in the wild, would convert a tracked single-operator campaign into a distributed one, and the build system for that is already in place. Campaign tags or overlays appearing outside the observed Italy, France, Spain, Poland, Portugal, Canada and GCC footprint would confirm the dropper's 30-plus language support is being exercised, and wider adoption of VPN-permission Play Protect suppression across malware families, already shared with ToxicPanda's droppers, would signal a broader drop in the cost of defeating store-side scanning.
