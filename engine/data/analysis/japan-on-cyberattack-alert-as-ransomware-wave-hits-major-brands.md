Japan is facing a mounting cybersecurity crisis with a ransomware wave disrupting hundreds of organizations and separate breaches exposing millions of customer records. Reports indicate stolen Japanese driver license images could be sold on dark-web markets for as little as 25 yen. The wave includes major brands coming forward with breach disclosures, adding to public exposure.

The scale of record exposure and the low estimated price for stolen identity documents underline significant data protection gaps. Organizations in Japan should review breach notification timelines, assess identity document handling practices, and monitor for follow-on fraud that uses leaked license images. The combination of ransomware disruption and mass breach disclosure suggests sustained pressure on the region.
