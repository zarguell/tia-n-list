malware.news is tracking what may be the first physical robbery linked to the Revolut data breach. On October 13 a cryptocurrency investor in the United Kingdom reported a home invasion in which they were held at knifepoint, taped up and robbed of about $800,000 in cryptocurrency along with physical valuables. In a follow-up reply the victim confirmed they banked with Revolut. That confirms the banking relationship, not the cause of the robbery.

A Golem report carried on November 20 says criminals obtained data belonging to Revolut customers and raises the risk of violent attacks against them, with a Netherlands development noted in the available text. Watch for further robberies with the same profile, any link between the leaked records and the victims, and confirmation from authorities in the affected countries. The connection remains a hypothesis until investigators establish how the attackers selected the victim.
