Kyle William Spitze, 27, of Friendsville Tennessee, was sentenced to 77 years in federal prison on Wednesday, the longest term ever imposed on a nihilistic violent extremist. Spitze was an original member of the 764 network and led one of its offshoots. He pleaded guilty in December 2024 to two counts of producing child sexual abuse material, possession of CSAM, and distributing animal crush videos. The judge in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Tennessee imposed the maximum penalty on each count, ordering them served consecutively.

Spitze victimized dozens of girls, coercing multiple victims under threats of doxing and swatting to produce CSAM of themselves, self-mutilate, and produce content for his online channels. The 764 network is a nihilistic violent extremist group that operates across online platforms, using coercion and exploitation to build influence and produce violent content. The sentence reflects the growing federal focus on dismantling these networks and holding individual operators accountable.

The case signals that law enforcement is treating 764 and its affiliates as serious organized threats rather than isolated online deviance. The consecutive sentencing structure, with the maximum for each count, sets a precedent for future prosecutions of similar actors in these networks.
