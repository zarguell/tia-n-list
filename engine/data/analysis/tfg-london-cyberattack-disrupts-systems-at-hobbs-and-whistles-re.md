TFG London, the UK subsidiary of South Africa's The Foschini Group, detected a cyberattack on October 11 that took down IT systems across its Phase Eight, Hobbs, and Whistles premium fashion brands. Physical stores remain open, but several systems are still unavailable. The group's other brands, Inside Story and White Stuff, and its operations in Australia and Africa are unaffected.

Scale context: as of mid-2026 the group ran 649 retail locations globally with annual sales of £488 million ($646 million). TFG London activated incident response and business continuity plans and brought in external cybersecurity experts. It is still investigating whether customer data was compromised and has committed to notifying affected individuals if so. No threat actor has publicly claimed the incident.

Two things to watch. First, whether this stays an outage or becomes a data breach, since the investigation into customer information is open and UK disclosure rules would force detail within 72 hours of a confirmed personal-data impact. Second, the pattern: this follows hard on ASOS confirming attackers social-engineered an employee's credentials and abused app push notifications to reach customer data, so retail, not finance, is where UK consumer brands are currently getting hit.
