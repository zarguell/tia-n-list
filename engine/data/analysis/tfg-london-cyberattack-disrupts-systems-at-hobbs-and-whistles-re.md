TFG London, UK subsidiary of South Africa’s The Foschini Group, disclosed a cybersecurity incident detected October 11 affecting IT systems for the Phase Eight, Hobbs, and Whistles brands. Several systems remain unavailable; physical stores stay open. The company engaged external cybersecurity experts and activated business continuity plans. TFG London is investigating whether customer data was compromised and will notify affected parties. No threat actor has claimed responsibility. The group operates 649 retail locations globally with annual sales of £488 million ($646M).
