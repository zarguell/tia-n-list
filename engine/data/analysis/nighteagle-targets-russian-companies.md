Kaspersky's Global Emergency Response Team has documented NightEagle, tracked as APT-Q-95, expanding its targeting from Asian organizations to Russian businesses. The group, active since at least 2023, gains initial access through compromised corporate VPN credentials, with connections originating from Cloudflare WARP tunnels and European VPS infrastructure.

Once inside, NightEagle deploys the GhostContainer backdoor on Microsoft Exchange servers. GhostContainer is a .NET-based malware that repurposes open-source components: the Neo-reGeorg tunnel, a CVE-2020-0688 exploit, and the GhostWebShell class from ysoserial. The group blends legitimate tools with public offensive utilities to avoid detection while moving through networks.

This expansion into Russian targets suggests NightEagle is broadening its operational scope beyond its original Asian focus. Organizations running Exchange servers should monitor for GhostContainer indicators and verify that CVE-2020-0688 is patched across all Exchange deployments.
