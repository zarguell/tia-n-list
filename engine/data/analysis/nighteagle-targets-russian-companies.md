NightEagle (APT-Q-95) is a threat actor active since at least 2023 that previously focused on Asian targets. Qihoo 360's Global Emergency Response Team documented the group's shift to targeting Russian businesses, using compromised VPN credentials sourced from Cloudflare WARP tunnels and European virtual infrastructure providers for initial access.

The group's signature tool is the GhostContainer backdoor deployed on Microsoft Exchange servers. It combines the Neo-reGeorg tunnel, an exploit for CVE-2020-0688 (an Exchange validation key vulnerability with a CVSS score of 9.8), and the GhostWebShell class from the ysoserial utility. All components are publicly available on GitHub, making attribution dependent on operational patterns rather than tooling novelty.

CVE-2020-0688 was patched in February 2020, yet NightEagle continues to find unpatched Exchange servers in the wild. The use of valid credentials for initial access means traditional perimeter defenses may not flag the intrusion. Russian organizations, and any entity running unpatched Exchange, should prioritize remediation of this six-year-old flaw. The shift in targeting from Asia to Russia may indicate either a broadening mandate or a contractor repurposing existing tooling against new victims.
