Quest Diagnostics disclosed unauthorized access to its Quanum Lab Services Manager (LSM) platform, which certain clients use to place test orders and look up results. The company learned of the incident on October 21, 2026 and reported it in an SEC Form 8-K, stating that certain patient records were accessed while the full scope remains under investigation. Quest deployed its incident response protocol to contain the activity and assess the nature and scope.

Because the Quanum LSM system sits between a major clinical lab and its healthcare clients, the exposed data may include patient records beyond basic demographics. Affected clients should monitor for unusual order activity and await Quest's individual notifications as the investigation proceeds.

Watch for updates on the number of affected patients and whether any data has been published or used for fraud, which would escalate the incident.
