Quest Diagnostics reported unauthorized access to its Quanum Lab Services Manager (LSM) system in a Form 8-K disclosure filed this week. The company became aware of the intrusion on October 21, 2026, and confirmed that an unidentified party may have accessed certain patient records through the platform, which healthcare clients use to place laboratory test orders and receive results. The full scope of affected patients and specific records exposed remains under investigation, with Quest stating it is working with external cybersecurity experts and federal law enforcement.

The disclosure adds to a history of security incidents at Quest, including a 2016 breach that exposed data for roughly 34,000 people and a 2019 incident involving about 11.9 million patients through a billing vendor. There is no CVE tied to this incident and no evidence yet of exploitation beyond unauthorized access. Watch for updates on patient notification timelines and any forensic confirmation of data types accessed.
