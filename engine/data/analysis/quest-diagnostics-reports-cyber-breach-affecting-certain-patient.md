Quest Diagnostics reported unauthorized access to its Quanum Lab Services Manager system on October 21, 2026, disclosing that certain patient records may have been accessed. The company filed a regulatory disclosure and notified federal law enforcement. It said core operations remain running, but the full scope of affected records remains under investigation. Quest has not yet published the number of affected patients or the specific data types compromised, though it confirmed that personal information and patient records were involved.

The breach affects a platform used by healthcare providers to place lab orders, access results, and manage services. Quest serves one in three US adults and operates 7,700 points, generating over $11 billion annually, which means even a partial data exposure could affect a significant population. The company will send notification letters once the investigation completes. Healthcare partners should monitor for unusual access to lab ordering systems and review provider authentication controls on the Quanum LSM portal.
