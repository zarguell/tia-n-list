Quest Diagnostics disclosed unauthorized access to its Quanum Lab Services Manager (Quanum LSM) platform in a regulatory filing dated October 21, 2026. The company discovered the intrusion on October 21 and notified federal law enforcement. The Quanum LSM is used by healthcare clients to place laboratory test orders and retrieve results. Quest believes certain patient records were accessed but has not yet confirmed the full scope or the specific data types affected. The company employs 65,000 people, serves roughly one in three U.S. adults annually, and operates 7,700 points across North America.

Previous Quest breaches include a 2016 incident affecting about 34,000 people and a 2019 incident through vendor American Medical Collection Agency affecting 11.9 million patients. The current incident is under active investigation with external cybersecurity experts engaged. Healthcare organizations using Quanum should monitor for further disclosures and review access logs for the affected platform.
