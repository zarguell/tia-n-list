Trezor received credible reports of a suspected hardware implant in a Safe 5 wallet sold by Southeast Asian reseller CryptoBilis. The user who bought the device through Tokopedia in August 2026 found an extra circuit board between the main board and screen, equipped with a cellular module and antenna. Based on unconfirmed customer information, the implant appears capable of reading the wallet recovery phrase from the screen and transmitting it over mobile networks when plugged in. Trezor has not yet received the device for independent confirmation, and no affected customers have reported fund losses so far. The company has warned users to move private keys and assets to known safe wallets.

The incident connects to a broader supply-chain pattern: CryptoBilis was also implicated in a Ledger implant incident earlier this month. Ledger confirmed that compromise and asked resellers and distributors in Southeast Asia and Hong Kong to halt sales pending investigation. The two implants appear similar in form and function. This points to targeted hardware tampering in the cryptocurrency wallet supply chain rather than an isolated event. Watch for independent verification of the Trezor device, further reseller confirmations, and any confirmed fund losses tied to the suspected implant.
