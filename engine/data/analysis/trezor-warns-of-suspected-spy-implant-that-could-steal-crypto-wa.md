Hardware wallet implants represent one of the most serious threats to cryptocurrency holdings because they bypass all software-level protections. If recovery phrases are stolen, attackers gain full control over wallets regardless of PIN or passphrase. Owners of affected Trezor devices should verify firmware integrity and consider migrating assets to new hardware if implant confirmation is verified.
