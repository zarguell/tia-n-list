Trezor issued an urgent warning after receiving a credible report of a suspected hardware implant in a Safe 5 cryptocurrency wallet purchased from former reseller CryptoBilis. The company has not independently verified the device, and so far no CryptoBilis customers have reported losing funds to Trezor. The advisory flags a supply-chain implant risk in the reseller channel rather than a confirmed compromise.

Trezor later confirmed crypto-stealing implants in three wallets and released a fix, per BleepingComputer. Defenders and users should treat hardware wallets sourced from resellers as higher risk, verify device integrity through the vendor's official verification tools, and avoid entering recovery phrases on any device whose origin is uncertain.
