Japanese media company Nikkei disclosed two separate email breaches. A Google Workspace account was accessed in late July, exposing data of 1,646 employees and partners; the company discovered it in early August. More recently, threat actors accessed a Microsoft 365 account in September and sent about 9,000 phishing emails to staff and interviewees. Nikkei changed passwords and confirmed no further unauthorized logins, but has not attributed the attacks or confirmed a link between the two incidents.
