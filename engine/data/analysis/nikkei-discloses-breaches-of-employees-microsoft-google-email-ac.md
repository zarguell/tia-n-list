Nikkei disclosed two separate breaches of employee email accounts: a Google Workspace account accessed in late July exposed names and email addresses of 1,646 individuals, and a Microsoft 365 account compromised in September was used to send 9,000 phishing emails. The company changed passwords and contacted recipients. No reader or interviewee data was involved in the first incident; the second targeted staff and interviewees. The attacks have not been attributed.
