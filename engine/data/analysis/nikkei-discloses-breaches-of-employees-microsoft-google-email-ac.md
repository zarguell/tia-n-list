Nikkei disclosed two separate breaches of employee email accounts over the weekend. The first, in late July, involved an employee's Google Workspace account accessed without authorization; Nikkei discovered it in early August via a Google notification, changed the password, and confirmed exposure of names and email addresses for 1,646 employees and business partners. No reader or interviewee data was involved. The second incident hit a different employee's Microsoft 365 account in September; attackers used it to send approximately 9,000 phishing emails to staff and interviewees on September 30th, linking to malicious websites. The company changed passwords again and says no further unauthorized logins have been observed since. Nikkei has not attributed either incident to a specific threat actor and has not said whether the two breaches are connected. The disclosure is primary reporting from Nikkei itself; it carries no CVEs and no confirmed exploitation beyond the account compromises. Watch for attribution updates or confirmation of connection between the two incidents.
