Overnight on November 2, freezers in supermarkets across Sweden, Belgium, Luxembourg and Ireland climbed to around 50 degrees C, spoiling stock. This is the second wave within a month, following a similar attack on hundreds of French stores in October. No actor has claimed either attack and the attack vector has not been confirmed.

The incidents highlight weak security in embedded refrigeration and building-management systems. Operators should segment these systems from corporate networks and monitor for unauthorized temperature changes, since the vector remains unknown.
