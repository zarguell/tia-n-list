Attackers distribute malicious Terraform providers through the HashiCorp Registry to deliver a Go RAT. The original reporting noted malicious providers and indicators extracted from public sources. A follow-up update from threat-intelligence feed threadlinqs links the campaign to the Lazarus group, names a typosquatted Docker provider, and records 9 detections and 28 IOCs. Watch for additional provider names being blocked in the registry and for any confirmed victim reports tied to the 28 IOCs.
