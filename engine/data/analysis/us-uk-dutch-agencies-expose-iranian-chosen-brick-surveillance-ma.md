The US, UK, and Netherlands have issued a joint advisory on Chosen Brick, a Windows malware family deployed by Iranian state cyber actors to target dissidents, activists, and journalists. Active since at least 2025, the malware harvests contacts, emails, social media messages, and location data to support state-sponsored repression. Stolen personal information is occasionally posted to pro-Iranian leak sites to harass targets.

Operators typically initiate contact on WhatsApp or Telegram, researching targets to build rapport by posing as acquaintances or platform support representatives. They first attempt delivery through corporate devices, then shift to personal devices if enterprise security controls block the payload. Malicious installers are disguised as utility software or fake medical documentation, including MRI scan results. When opened, the file displays a decoy while the malware executes in the background.

A second advisory from Threadlinqs linked Chosen Brick to the same Iranian campaign using fake MRI scans to plant Windows spyware that records microphone audio and screen captures, with 9 detections and 18 IOCs identified. The targeting pattern, social engineering tradecraft, and infrastructure overlap with known Iranian APT operations. Organizations with Iranian dissident or activist users should ensure messaging platform security controls are in place and that users are warned about unsolicited file transfers from unknown contacts.
