Layer3 Intel's Residential Proxy Observatory recorded 236 proxy exits from six IP addresses on AS2650, the network reserved for the Executive Office of the President, between October 5 and November 3, 2026, passing through 28 residential proxy services. Synthient traced the traffic to Windows 11 machines running the InfaticaSDK; Infatica claims more than 45 million residential, mobile and datacenter IP addresses, and its founder Vladimir Fomenko also owns the hosting provider King Servers. About half the activity fell between 5 p.m. and midnight Eastern Time, with almost none in the early morning, which Layer3 says indicates one infected device on the network rather than guest Wi-Fi. This is not a CVE or a product bug; it is live abuse of a residential proxy SDK, and anyone can sign up, pay a few dollars, and send requests out through a White House address. The finding is now circulating in weekly security roundups, which extends its shelf life but adds no new technical detail.

A working abuse gives an attacker an exit point inside one of the most sensitive networks on the internet: six addresses on a government autonomous system used on 26 of the 30 days in the observation window. Any control that grants implicit trust to traffic sourced from White House IP space, such as allowlists, reputation blocks or geo-based easing, is bypassable by renting one of those addresses. This story rates the desk over others because the exposure is an active, rentable foothold rather than a patchable flaw, and the events name no infected device and no remediation; nothing in the record confirms the proxy host has been found.

A separate November 6 report adds a second thread: infonewscyber.fr reports that the Etablissement francais du sang, the French national blood service, was hit by a cyberattack involving a data leak. The relay in this store carries only the French headline claim with a link, so scope, attacker and data types are unconfirmed. Treat it as an early claim, not an established breach: a blood service holds donor identity and health-adjacent data, which would make confirmation significant, but no second source or official statement is in the events yet.

Defenders should treat the proxy exits as an endpoint-hygiene problem because no patch version or CVE exists. Inventory Windows 11 hosts for unapproved proxy and VPN clients, remove any InfaticaSDK or similar proxy SDK not explicitly approved, and block egress to residential-proxy and anonymization services at the perimeter. Hunt proxy and VPN telemetry for sessions to the six AS2650 addresses and check those addresses on Spur and Synthient IP context before trusting traffic sourced from them. Watch whether Layer3 or Synthient attributes the exits to a specific device, whether new exits appear after November 4, and whether the French blood service confirms or denies the leak claim with scope details.
