Layer3 Intel's Residential Proxy Observatory recorded 236 proxy exits from six IP addresses on AS2650, the network reserved for the Executive Office of the President, between October 5 and November 3, 2026, passing through 28 residential proxy services. Synthient traced the traffic to Windows 11 machines running the InfaticaSDK; Infatica claims more than 45 million residential, mobile and datacenter IP addresses, and its founder Vladimir Fomenko also owns the hosting provider King Servers. About half the activity fell between 5 p.m. and midnight Eastern Time, with almost none in the early morning, which Layer3 says indicates one infected device on the network rather than guest Wi-Fi. This is not a CVE or a product bug; it is live abuse of a residential proxy SDK, and anyone can sign up, pay a few dollars, and send requests out through a White House address.

A working abuse gives an attacker an exit point inside one of the most sensitive networks on the internet: six addresses on a government autonomous system used on 26 of the 30 days in the observation window. Any control that grants implicit trust to traffic sourced from White House IP space - allowlists, reputation blocks, geo-based easing - is bypassable by renting one of those addresses. This story rates the desk over others because the exposure is an active, rentable foothold rather than a patchable flaw, and the events name no infected device and no remediation; nothing in the record confirms the proxy host has been found.

Defenders should treat this as an endpoint-hygiene problem because no patch version or CVE exists. Inventory Windows 11 hosts for unapproved proxy and VPN clients, remove any InfaticaSDK or similar proxy SDK not explicitly approved, and block egress to residential-proxy and anonymization services at the perimeter. Hunt proxy and VPN telemetry for sessions to the six AS2650 addresses and check those addresses on Spur and Synthient IP context before trusting any traffic sourced from them. Review allowlists and reputation rules that exempt Executive Office of the President IP space, and re-image any host flagged by residential-proxy detection services.

Watch whether Layer3 or Synthient attributes the exits to a specific device or user, whether Infatica or King Servers acts on the SDK abuse, and whether the observatory records new exits from AS2650 after November 4 - continued exits would mean the infected device was never remediated.
