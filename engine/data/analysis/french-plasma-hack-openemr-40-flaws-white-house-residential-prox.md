Layer3 Intel's Residential Proxy Observatory found residential proxy exit traffic on AS2650, the network reserved for the Executive Office of the President, between October 5 and November 3, 2026. The firm recorded 236 exits through 28 public proxy services, and cross-checks against Spur and Synthient independently confirmed the addresses as residential proxy exits. Six IP addresses on the White House network were involved, and the disclosure was published on November 4, 2026.

The timing pattern points to a single infected device rather than guest Wi-Fi. Almost all activity happened in the evening, between 5 p.m. and midnight Eastern Time, with almost none in the early morning. Layer3 Intel says that pattern suggests a person regularly on the White House network late into the evening whose device is infected with a proxy SDK.

Anyone could sign up to a public residential proxy service for a few dollars and route requests through these addresses. Cybersecurity startup Synthient tied the traffic to Windows 11 machines running InfaticaSDK. Infatica is based in Singapore and claims more than 45 million ethically sourced residential, mobile, and datacenter IP addresses; its founder Vladimir Fomenko also owns web hosting provider King Servers.
