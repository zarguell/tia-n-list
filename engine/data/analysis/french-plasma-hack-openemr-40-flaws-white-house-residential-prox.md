Security researchers found multiple computers on the White House network being used as residential proxy exit nodes. According to Layer3 Intel, six IP addresses on AS2650, the network reserved for the Executive Office of the President, were used by 28 residential proxy services between October 5 and November 3. Layer3 Intel's residential proxy enumeration pipeline exited through the White House network 236 separate times.

Those exits can be used by anyone signing up for a public residential proxy service to route requests through them. Most of the observed activity happened in the evening between 5 p.m. and midnight Eastern Time, with almost none in the early morning. Layer3 Intel said that pattern suggests a person rather than a public guest Wi-Fi network: someone regularly on the White House network late into the evening whose device is infected with a proxy SDK.

Cybersecurity startup Synthient traced the traffic to Windows 11 machines running the InfaticaSDK. Infatica, based in Singapore, claims access to more than 45 million residential, mobile and datacenter IP addresses worldwide. Its founder, Vladimir Fomenko, also owns web hosting and infrastructure provider King Servers. An Infosecurity Magazine summary of the findings cites 236 proxy exits, 28 services and six White House addresses.
