Within eight days of TypeSafe AI launching its Jev decision model on September 15, roughly 670 lookalike domains with "jev" branding obtained TLS certificates. The first two, jev-ai[.]pro and jevtypesafeai[.]com, were registered three days after launch, ranked above TypeSafe's official site in Google results for Jev queries, and presented full playgrounds, documentation, pricing, and checkout pages, with their non-affiliation disclosed only in footers or legal pages.

The sites are not classic phishing: they proxy requests to an upstream API that mirrors TypeSafe's request and response format, so customers get a working service. The costs are that prompts flow through an untrusted third party and prices run six to 11.5 times the official rate. TypeSafe lists Jev input tokens at $0.042 per million with no output-token charge; the lookalikes charge about $0.247 to $0.483 per million monthly, $0.124 to $0.242 per million on prepaid annual plans on jev-ai[.]pro, and up to about $0.42 per million in starter packs on jevtypesafeai[.]com.

This is the AI-era version of brandjacking, optimized by SEO and automated domain registration, and it turns every new model launch into a race. Watch for takedowns or registrar action, for TypeSafe to publish an official-domain warning, and for evidence that proxied prompts are being logged or resold, since developers sending proprietary data through these storefronts would leak it directly to the operators.
