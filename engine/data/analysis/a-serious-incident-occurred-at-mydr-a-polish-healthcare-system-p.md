MyDr, one of Poland's largest healthcare system providers, is investigating a serious security incident on its network. The alleged attackers contacted the breach-notification outlet before public disclosure and claimed to have access to patient data from numerous Polish clinics, including access to 18,814,422 unique PESEL numbers, Poland's national identification numbers.

If the claim is accurate, the incident would rank among the largest healthcare data exposures in Europe: 18.8 million PESEL numbers is more than half of Poland's population, and the numbers are usable for identity fraud and benefit abuse. The pattern matches the recent wave of attacks on healthcare and insurance providers holding concentrated identity data. Organizations in the Polish healthcare ecosystem should treat the breach as a signal to rotate access and watch for follow-on phishing targeting affected clinics and patients.
