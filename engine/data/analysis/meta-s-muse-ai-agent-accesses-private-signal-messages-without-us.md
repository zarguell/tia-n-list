Meta launched Muse on 8 September 2026 as a personal agent in a dedicated cloud VM, with Meta saying users choose connected services and can review a complete audit trail. Reuters reviewed more than two dozen internal posts and, with Signal and AI Now Institute staff, reproduced Signal text access on Android through push-notification permission and on macOS through accessibility and screenshot permissions, bypassing the Connector tool; the same requests failed on an iPhone. The employee reminder about a sister's Signal message is the visible symptom. End-to-end encryption protects the channel, not text already surfaced to an agent by the endpoint.

The argument here is consent scope, not a broken cipher. A free app with more than 10 million downloads since September is promoted to Meta's 3.6 billion Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp users, and TIME found hourly dossiers for 4 million users that also cover people who never installed Muse. Those non-users can be profiled from someone else's chats, messages and email without any relationship to Meta, and Reuters could not establish whether employee reports of other people's bank balances were real data or hallucinations. A classic vulnerability ends at a patch; this one expands with every notification and accessibility grant, including ones made at setup rather than at the moment of reading.

Revoke Muse's Android notification access and macOS accessibility and screenshot grants, disconnect its Messages and other connectors where possible, and remove the agent where that access cannot be justified; iPhone behavior is not a general exemption from permission review. Hunt the audit trail Meta says lists all activity and future plans for unsolicited replies, bank balances, purchases, childcare and other context no user supplied, inspect hourly relationship pages for people who never opted in, and use Connector bypass as the pattern to search for rather than a Signal-only indicator. Preserve internal feedback posts and the exact OS grants in place at the time, because Meta's isolated-incident and hallucination framing makes model output alone a poor way to prove whether data crossed accounts.

The assessment changes if Meta documents a permission fix that stops notification and accessibility reads, or confirms whether relationship pages hold message content rather than metadata. Watch for an answer to Reuters' open question on cross-account bank balances and for Apple or Google developer controls that let Signal opt out of agent screen reading; Signal says macOS lacks a reliable screenshot block, so OS behavior will decide how much of this remains reachable after any Muse-side denial.
