Reuters reviewed more than two dozen internal Meta posts describing Muse surfacing private information without being asked, including an employee who said the agent reminded her to answer a Signal message from her sister that she had not knowingly given it access to. Reuters and Signal staff reproduced Signal text access on an Android phone through setup-time notification permissions and on an Apple laptop through accessibility and screenshot tools; the same requests were blocked on an iPhone. Meta spokesperson Daniel Roberts called the reports isolated incidents and said many apparent mix-ups were hallucinations, while Reuters could not determine whether reported bank balances were real. Muse had more than 10 million downloads since its September launch.

Other covered findings raise the stakes around what the agent can hold and reach. WIRED obtained internal instructions describing hourly profiles of people in a user's life, and TIME reported dossiers covering 4 million users plus people who do not use Muse, with nightly analysis of conversations. NebuSec demonstrated a laboratory chain in which a malicious page opened by Muse's bundled Chromium during PDF export led to code execution in the runtime container, weak Sentinel egress classification let chats and memories leave the VM without an approval prompt, and generated kernel exploits against a 7.0.0-39-generic Hatch build gave root and access to a 100 GiB LUKS2 volume and credential storage. Meta fixed those issues before publication, and the chain stayed inside one user's VM. Signal has separately moved to block screenshots on iOS 27 and AI assistants reading chats on supported Android builds.

Watch for Meta's answer on whether the Signal messages were actually read or were model hallucinations, and whether the permission prompts and audit trail it describes cover notification and accessibility access. The NebuSec chain shows the Sentinel gateway is only as strong as its egress classification and the host kernel, so verify fixes are deployed before treating connected credentials as contained. Signal's own warning that agents can use other extraction paths means screenshot restrictions alone are not a full control.
