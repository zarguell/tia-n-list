Reuters reviewed more than two dozen internal Meta posts in which employees flagged Muse surfacing private messages, including a Meta employee who got an unsolicited reminder about a message from her sister on Signal. Working with Signal and AI Now Institute staff, Reuters reproduced Signal text access on an Android phone using setup-time permissions and Android push notifications, and on an Apple laptop using macOS accessibility and screenshot tools. The same requests were blocked on an iPhone. Meta spokesperson Daniel Roberts called the cases isolated and said many apparent account mix-ups were hallucinations; Reuters could not determine whether reported bank balances were real or invented.

The consent problem sits at the endpoint, not in Signal encryption. Once notification text or screen content is exposed to an agent, message protection does not apply. Meta launched Muse on 8 September 2026 with a dedicated Muse Secure VM and a separate Sentinel process for network approval, and said users choose connected services and can review an audit trail. WIRED reported that researcher Karan Joshi pulled Muse's internal instructions through the normal chat interface; one instruction calls for a page on every person in the user's life, refreshed hourly. TIME reported hourly dossiers for 4 million users covering people who never used Muse, plus a nightly analysis of the day's conversations, and cited more than 5 million downloads since launch. Reuters put downloads above 10 million and noted promotion to Meta's 3.6 billion Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp users.

A later report said Signal moved to block AI agents from reading chats through screenshots on Android and iOS, which addresses one access path without resolving notification or accessibility grants on other platforms. The iPhone result is platform-specific, not proof that other device setups are safe. Watch for Meta clarification on whether the relationship pages hold message content or only metadata, for fixes that stop notification and accessibility reads, and for OS controls that let messaging apps refuse agent screen access. When reviewing unsolicited replies, preserve the exact permission grants and audit trail entries, since the hallucination explanation makes model output alone a weak way to show what crossed accounts.
