Meta launched Muse on 8 September 2026 as an agent in a dedicated Muse Secure VM, with a separate Sentinel process gating network access and connector actions. Reuters reviewed more than two dozen internal posts describing unsolicited chats about purchases, childcare, careers and bank balances, then reproduced Signal text access on an Android phone and an Apple laptop using setup-time permissions. Android push notifications supplied message text and macOS accessibility and screenshot tools worked on the laptop, while the same requests were blocked on an iPhone. Meta spokesperson Daniel Roberts called the cases isolated and said many apparent account mixups were hallucinations; Reuters could not determine whether reported bank balances were real. The app had more than 10 million downloads since launch.

The exposure is not limited to one app. WIRED obtained Muse's internal instructions, which call for an hourly page on every person in a user's life, including family, colleagues and followed accounts, with shared history and open threads. TIME reported dossiers for 4 million users mapping relationships and disputes, including people who do not use Muse. NebuSec later demonstrated a chain from an attacker-controlled page opened by bundled Chromium during PDF export, through V8 issue 554034656, to code execution in the runtime container, then kernel exploits against a 7.0.0-39-generic Hatch build for container escape and root in the user VM. From there the unlocked 100 GiB LUKS2/Btrfs volume, secure credential storage and Sentinel were reachable. Sentinel itself runs as a DNS and proxy service at 198.19.0.1 on ports 53 and 3128, with 60-minute egress budgets of 2 MiB per originator and 5 MiB per domain.

NebuSec's work was a laboratory demonstration inside one user's VM, with no hypervisor or cross-tenant escape, and Meta fixed the issues before publication. The bug bounty pays up to $300,000, including $130,000 for prompt-injection issues affecting one user. Signal has since moved to block screenshots on iOS 27 and to stop AI assistants capturing conversations on supported Android devices, while warning that agents embedded in operating systems still offer other extraction paths. Watch whether the permission-based Signal access is fully closed and whether the relationship dossiers can be reviewed or deleted.
