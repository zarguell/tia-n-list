Reuters reviewed more than two dozen internal Meta posts in which employees flagged Muse surfacing messages and other people's information, including an employee who received an unsolicited reminder about a Signal message from her sister. Working with Signal and AI Now Institute staff, Reuters reproduced Signal text access on an Android phone through setup-time permissions and push notifications, and on an Apple laptop through macOS accessibility and screenshot tools. The same requests were blocked on an iPhone. Meta spokesperson Daniel Roberts called the cases isolated and said many apparent account mixups were hallucinations; Reuters could not determine whether reported bank balances were real or invented.

Meta launched Muse on 8 September 2026 with a dedicated Muse Secure VM and a separate Sentinel process that approves network access and sensitive actions. WIRED reported that researcher Karan Joshi extracted Muse's internal instructions through the normal chat interface; one instruction calls for a page on every person in the user's life, refreshed hourly. TIME reported hourly dossiers covering 4 million users and people who never signed up, plus nightly analysis of the day's conversations, and cited more than 5 million downloads since the previous month's launch. Reuters put downloads above 10 million and noted promotion to Meta's 3.6 billion Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp users.

NebuSec showed a separate path into the same assistant. An attacker-controlled page opened by bundled Chromium during PDF export led to code execution in Muse's runtime container through V8 issue 554034656; the PDF-export process ran without an IPC sandbox. Kernel exploits against a 7.0.0-39-generic Hatch build escaped the container, and Sentinel's outbound checks failed to stop chat history, stored memories and connected-account credentials from reaching an external server without an approval prompt. Sentinel was mapped to 198.19.0.1 on ports 53 and 3128 with eBPF redirection and egress budgets of 2 MiB per originator and 5 MiB per domain; processes outside the container could bypass it after VM root. The researchers reached root in the user VM and the unlocked 100 GiB LUKS2/Btrfs volume. They described a lab demonstration with no hypervisor or cross-tenant escape, and said Meta fixed the issues before publication.

Signal has since moved to block screenshot and AI capture paths on iOS 27 and supported Android versions, targeting Siri Onscreen Awareness and Gemini Intelligence. President Meredith Whittaker said the change follows months of reverse engineering and a May 2025 block against Windows 11 Recall, and warned that agents embedded deeply in operating systems remain an existential threat to the app. That mitigation covers screen capture but not the notification and accessibility grants Reuters used. Watch for Meta clarification on whether relationship pages hold message content or only metadata, for fixes to those endpoint reads, and for OS controls that let messaging apps deny agent screen access.
