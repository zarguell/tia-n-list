Reuters reviewed more than two dozen internal Meta posts in which employees flagged Muse surfacing other people's information, including an employee who received an unsolicited reminder about a Signal message from her sister. Working with Signal and AI Now Institute staff, Reuters reproduced Signal text access on an Android phone through setup-time permissions and push notifications, and on an Apple laptop through macOS accessibility and screenshot tools. The same requests were blocked on an iPhone. Meta spokesperson Daniel Roberts called the cases isolated and said many apparent account mixups were hallucinations; Reuters could not determine whether reported bank balances were real or invented.

Muse launched on 8 September 2026 with a dedicated Muse Secure VM and a separate Sentinel process that approves network access and sensitive actions. WIRED reported that researcher Karan Joshi extracted Muse's internal instructions through the normal chat interface, including a requirement to refresh a page on every person in the user's life hourly. TIME described hourly dossiers covering 4 million users, including people who never signed up, plus nightly analysis of the day's conversations. Reuters put downloads above 10 million since launch, and Meta has promoted Muse to its 3.6 billion Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp users.

NebuSec showed a second path into the assistant. An attacker-controlled page opened by bundled Chromium during PDF export, with the export process running without an IPC sandbox, reached code execution in the runtime container through V8 issue 554034656. Generated kernel exploits against a 7.0.0-39-generic Hatch build escaped the container. Sentinel, mapped to 198.19.0.1 on ports 53 and 3128 with egress budgets of 2 MiB per originator and 5 MiB per domain, did not stop chat history, stored memories and connected-account credentials from reaching an external server without an approval prompt. After VM root, processes outside the container could bypass Sentinel and reach the unlocked 100 GiB LUKS2/Btrfs volume. The researchers called it a lab demonstration with no hypervisor or cross-tenant escape and said Meta fixed the issues before publication.

Signal has moved to block the screen-capture side of the problem, restricting screenshots on iOS 27 and AI assistants on supported Android versions to target Siri Onscreen Awareness and Gemini Intelligence. President Meredith Whittaker said the change follows months of reverse engineering and a May 2025 block on Windows 11 Recall, and called deeply embedded OS agents an existential threat. Signal warns that screenshot blocks do not cover an agent reading chat content through other paths, including the notification and accessibility grants Reuters used. Watch for Meta clarification on whether relationship pages hold message content or only metadata, fixes for those endpoint reads, and OS controls that let messaging apps deny agent screen access.
