CISA added CVE-2026-46300 (Fragnesia) to its KEV Catalog and ordered federal agencies to patch by November 1 under BOD 26-04. The Linux kernel privilege escalation bug, part of the Dirty Frag class, lets local attackers gain root privileges via a logic bug in the XFRM ESP-in-TCP subsystem. CISA noted it can be chained with PHP2Shell flaws CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 to deploy webshells. Dream Security reported the chain was used to breach more than two dozen servers. The story scores 9.5 with KEV status set. Watch for patch compliance timelines and any expansion of observed exploitation campaigns.
