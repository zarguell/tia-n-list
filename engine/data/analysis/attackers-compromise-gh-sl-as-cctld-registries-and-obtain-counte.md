Attackers compromised third-party ccTLD registries for Ghana (.gh), Sierra Leone (.sl), and American Samoa (.as), then modified authoritative DNS records to obtain unauthorized HTTPS certificates for Google domains and other organizations. Chrome's Secure Web and Networking Team disclosed the issue on October 6, 2026. The new event adds GBHackers coverage confirming the unauthorized SSL certificates. This remains an infrastructure-level supply-chain risk: any domain validated through these compromised registries could serve counterfeit certificates. Watch for additional affected domains and whether Chrome or other browsers revoke trust in the counterfeit certs.
