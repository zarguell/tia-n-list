A Chinese-speaking operator used autonomous AI agents to breach websites of over 100 companies, with more than 1,200 intrusion attempts. The campaign relied mainly on software rather than manual effort, and was reported by Reuters and NorForge research on October 19. This is a concrete example of AI agents being weaponized for mass-scale web compromise.

The story matters because it moves AI-assisted intrusion from theory to measured campaigns with company-level impact. The 1,200 attempts against 100+ targets show automation scale, not a one-off experiment. Security teams should treat AI-driven scanning and exploitation as an operational reality, not a future risk.

Watch for follow-up attribution: whether the actor is tied to a known group, whether any victim sectors are named, and whether similar campaigns appear in other reports. The Reuters and NorForge sources will likely carry updates.
