Broadcom's VMSA-2026-0011 covers 23 vulnerabilities across VMware ESX, vCenter, Workstation, Fusion and Avi Load Balancer. The two headline flaws are CVE-2026-123903, an authentication bypass in the VMware Directory Service (vmdir), and CVE-2026-123910, an ESXi remote code execution bug. Both are rated CVSS 9.8 in the coverage collected for this story. Reporting summarized in the story describes CVE-2026-123903 as patched after in-the-wild zero-day use, but the available event text gives no victim count, exploit chain or actor attribution.

Broadcom lists ESXi 8.0 Update 3m as build 25926933, dated November 19 and distributed as an x86_64 ISO and offline bundle. Applying it requires a host reboot and either migration or shutdown of virtual machines. The advisory was last updated November 24 and carries notification ID 39626. Administrators have to plan maintenance windows around the reboot requirement rather than treating this as a simple package update.

Watch for follow-up detail on the in-the-wild use of the vmdir bypass and on whether CVE-2026-123910 has any observed abuse. Until that lands, the concrete actions are to inventory ESX, vCenter, Workstation, Fusion and Avi Load Balancer versions against VMSA-2026-0011 and prioritize build 25926933 for ESXi hosts. The 23-fix scope means a single host upgrade may not cover every affected product in the environment.
