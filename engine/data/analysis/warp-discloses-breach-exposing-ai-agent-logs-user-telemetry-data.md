Warp disclosed that agent decision logs and user telemetry were exposed when an attacker breached a third-party vendor in Warp's infrastructure. CEO Zach Lloyd estimated roughly 1% of Warp users may have been impacted, and the company said it detected and remediated the operation within hours. The exposed data could include agent traces and telemetry, though Warp did not confirm whether source code, prompts, or credentials were involved.

The incident points to supply-chain exposure in agentic development environments, a growing attack surface. Watch for updates clarifying the vendor, the scope of exposed data, and whether external investigators confirm attacker access to sensitive agent outputs or user credentials.
