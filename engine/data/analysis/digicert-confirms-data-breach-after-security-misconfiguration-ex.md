DigiCert disclosed unauthorized access to an internal business system containing archived customer information after a February 2025 server migration mistakenly exposed it to the internet. Attackers abused a self-registration feature that did not require MFA to create a read-only account with access to archived data from June 27 to July 2, 2026.

Exposed data includes names, business email addresses, phone numbers and business addresses, and in some cases driver's licenses, passports, utility bills and other ID documents. CA systems, CertCentral, DigiCert ONE, login credentials and issued certificates were not affected, and the access could not write records, act as a validation approver or issue certificates.

DigiCert blocked the account on July 2, began suspecting archived data access on October 12, and is notifying affected customers while reviewing identification documents to determine the full scope. The incident was initially treated as an account-creation event before the archived-data access was confirmed.
