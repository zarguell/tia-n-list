Fortinet disclosed three high-severity vulnerabilities across FortiMonitor, FortiSandbox, and FortiPAM. CVE-2026-26084 in FortiSandbox carries a CVSS of 9.9 due to improper access control, allowing crafted HTTP requests to manipulate network rules and expose corporate data. CVE-2026-84390 affects FortiMonitor OnSight via a static key issue that lets attackers bypass authentication through forged or reused JWTs. A third flaw in the FortiPAM Chrome extension routes user browser traffic through attacker-controlled servers when visiting a malicious site.

Affected versions span FortiMonitor 7.2.0 through 7.2.7, FortiSandbox 4.4 and 5.0, and FortiPAM extension 7.4 and 8.0. No public PoC or confirmed exploitation has been reported. Patches are available: FortiMonitor to 7.2.8, FortiSandbox to 4.4.9 or 5.0.6, and the FortiPAM Chrome extension to 8.0.1.123 or higher.

Fortinet products sit at the network edge for thousands of enterprises. Unpatched FortiSandbox and FortiPAM installations could expose internal data or enable traffic interception. Organizations should prioritize these updates, particularly the FortiPAM extension where the attack requires only user interaction with a malicious site.
