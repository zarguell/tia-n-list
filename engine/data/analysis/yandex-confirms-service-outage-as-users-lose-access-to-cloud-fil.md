Yandex announced that drone attacks damaged three Russian data centers in Sasovo (October 8), Kaluga (October 9), and Vladimir (October 11). The Sasovo and Vladimir facilities stopped operating fully; Kaluga is degraded. Customers lost access to cloud files and services. The attacks are believed linked to Ukrainian forces targeting Russian infrastructure.

The event connects to an existing Yandex service outage story. Primary reporting comes from Yandex's own status updates and BleepingComputer, with a secondary Italian-language Mastodon update referencing Slashdot. There are no CVEs; this is a physical infrastructure and geopolitical event rather than a software vulnerability.

Organizations relying on Yandex cloud services should review continuity plans and expect ongoing degradation as repairs proceed.
