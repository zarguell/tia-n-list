Apple sent new mercenary-spyware threat notifications to targeted users across 99 countries, bringing the total number of countries alerted to over 150. The company described these attacks as among the most advanced digital threats in existence and deliberately avoided attributing them to any specific actors or regions. Researchers at Citizen Lab called the notifications significant, recommending Lockdown Mode and contact with Access Now for potential victims.

The October 30, 2026 CyberScoop report quotes Apple directly on the global scope. The notifications remain significant because they confirm sustained, worldwide targeting rather than isolated incidents, but Apple offers no attribution or victim counts. Watch for follow-on reporting that links the alerts to known spyware vendors or state-linked campaigns.
