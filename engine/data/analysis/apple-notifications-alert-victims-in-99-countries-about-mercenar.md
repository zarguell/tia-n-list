Apple sent mercenary spyware notifications to targeted users in 99 countries, bringing the total notified countries above 150. The company described mercenary spyware as some of the most advanced digital threats, noting extreme cost, sophistication, and global reach. Apple does not attribute the attacks to specific actors or regions.

Recipients include journalists and documentary filmmakers. Researchers from Citizen Lab emphasized the significance of this notification wave and recommended Lockdown Mode and contact with Access Now. Apple also warned that absence of a notification does not rule out infection. The scale and recurring nature of these notifications point to sustained, well-funded spyware campaigns against civil society and media workers.
