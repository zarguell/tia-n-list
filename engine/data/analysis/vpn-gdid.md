A Korean-language researcher write-up documents GDID telemetry being used to neutralize VPN protections, with a focus on anti-forensics countermeasures. The post ties the technique to activity associated with Scattered Spider and walks through how the telemetry can be abused to defeat the VPN boundary that most organizations rely on as their network perimeter.

The operational takeaway is that VPN sessions are not a reliable trust boundary when an endpoint can be manipulated into revealing or disabling the tunnel. Defenders should watch for telemetry tampering, unexpected VPN client behavior, and the anti-forensics steps described, which are designed to erase the traces of that manipulation. The write-up is a reminder that identity and perimeter controls are only as strong as the endpoint they run on.
