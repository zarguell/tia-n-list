Wilmington School District 209U in Illinois is investigating a ransomware attack and possible data breach, according to a November 18 report from WJOL relayed by DataBreaches. Superintendent Kevin Feeney told staff and student families that cybersecurity experts are trying to determine the extent of the attack and whether sensitive information was accessed. A follow-up report says the district suspended its Windows devices after confirming the ransomware incident.

The events name no ransomware group, ransom amount, data types, or restoration timeline, so the confirmed facts are the device suspension and the ongoing assessment of whether sensitive information was accessed. Watch for a district notice on student or staff records, which systems were isolated, and when Windows devices return to service. Until that assessment is published, this is a district-level ransomware incident with an open breach question, not a confirmed data theft.
