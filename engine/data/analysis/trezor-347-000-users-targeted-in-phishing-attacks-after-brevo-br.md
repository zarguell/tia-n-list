Trezor disclosed that approximately 347,000 of its users were targeted in phishing attacks following a breach at Brevo, an email service provider. The attack leveraged customer email addresses obtained through the Brevo compromise to send targeted phishing messages to Trezor hardware wallet users. The phishing emails impersonated Trezor communications to steal cryptocurrency credentials and wallet recovery phrases.

This is a supply chain phishing attack where the initial breach occurred at a third-party email platform, not at Trezor itself. The Brevo breach gave attackers access to Trezor's customer mailing list, enabling highly convincing phishing campaigns. Hardware wallet users are particularly valuable targets because they typically hold significant cryptocurrency balances and the recovery phrase theft enables permanent, irreversible fund theft.

Trezor users should be on heightened alert for phishing emails referencing their wallet or account. The sheer scale of 347,000 targeted users indicates a well-resourced campaign. This incident underscores the risk of customer data exposure through third-party marketing platforms and the persistent targeting of cryptocurrency holders by phishing operators.
