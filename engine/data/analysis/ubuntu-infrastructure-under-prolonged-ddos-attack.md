Ubuntu infrastructure has been under a prolonged DDoS attack. Canonical's IT team is working on defenses and monitoring the Canonical status page. The attack has caused service disruptions but no data breach or compromise of source repositories has been reported. Follow the Canonical status page and related LinuxMint coverage for updates on restoration progress.

This is an operational incident rather than a vulnerability. Impact is availability-focused; no immediate patching action is required beyond monitoring status pages.
