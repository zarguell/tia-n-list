Binance suffered a hot-wallet compromise on October 29, 2026, with the exchange confirming an estimated $6.3 billion stolen across roughly ten blockchains in about 20 minutes. Blockchain intelligence firm TRM Labs calculated about $6.36 billion siphoned. North Korean state-sponsored hackers are the likely culprits according to security reports. About $3 billion has been frozen on-chain, and Binance halted withdrawals and pledged to reimburse losses using company funds and its SAFU reserve.

The attacker may have gained access through a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop, then used Binance's own signing infrastructure to submit unauthorized withdrawals. Binance stated there is no evidence of a private key leak. Most of the stolen funds were moved quickly; the attackers spent at least a day and a half preparing and funded their operation wallet with small Tornado Cash withdrawals.
