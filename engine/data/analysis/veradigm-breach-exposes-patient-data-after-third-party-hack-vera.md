Veradigm, a healthcare technology company, disclosed a data breach exposing patient personal information after the Gentlemen ransomware group claimed an attack on the company. The breach originated through a third-party vendor compromise, not a direct intrusion into Veradigm's primary systems. Patient personal data was accessed during the incident. Veradigm is notifying affected patients directly. The Gentlemen group previously claimed responsibility, and the disclosure confirms patient data was exfiltrated. Healthcare organizations using Veradigm services should review third-party vendor agreements and monitor for unauthorized access to patient records. The incident continues the pattern of supply chain attacks targeting healthcare data intermediaries.
