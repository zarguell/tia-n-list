ActiveCloud, a Belarusian cloud provider, confirmed a ransomware attack on November 10, 2026. Hackers breached its systems and encrypted parts of its cloud infrastructure, leaving customers unable to access key services for several days, with cloud services and email disrupted across Belarus. The impact extends to downstream businesses that rely on ActiveCloud's hosted platform.

No CVEs or technical details have been published. Available reporting is limited to ActiveCloud's own statement and short aggregator summaries, with The Record carrying the initial report. There is no attribution to a named ransomware group and no confirmation of data exfiltration or ransom demands.

Watch for confirmation of which services and customer data were affected, whether a ransom was demanded or paid, how long restoration takes for affected tenants, and whether the incident is linked to a known group. Cloud providers are high-value targets because a single compromise can cascade to many downstream customers.
