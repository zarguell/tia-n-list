The Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles confirmed a breach of its DAVID driver database after the ShinyHunters extortion gang claimed to have stolen over 200,000 driver records. FLHSMV says the attacker used compromised credentials from a single Plant City Police Department employee whose credentials were improperly stored on a personal device. The agency says the breach was detected September 4, 2026, and quickly contained.

ShinyHunters told a different story about initial access, claiming they exploited a password reset flaw to compromise multiple DAVID accounts including those of DMV employees and an FBI agent. The discrepancy between the official account and the gang's claim is notable and may reflect ongoing investigation constraints or deliberate misdirection by the attackers.

The incident highlights the persistent risk of credential sprawl on personal devices and the downstream damage when law enforcement credentials are compromised. FLHSMV is working with the Florida Digital Service and the Florida Department of Law Enforcement. The breach is the latest in a string of ShinyHunters operations targeting government and healthcare databases in 2026.
