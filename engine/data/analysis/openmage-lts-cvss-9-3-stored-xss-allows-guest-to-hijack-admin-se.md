OpenMage LTS has a stored cross-site scripting flaw rated CVSS 9.3 that lets a guest shopper take over an administrator session through a crafted email. The social post reporting the fix names upgrades to 20.19.1 or 20.18.3. No CVE identifier, affected lower version boundary or vendor advisory text is present in the captured event, so the fix versions and the guest-to-admin session path are the grounded facts.

The severity makes sense if the stored payload is rendered in an administrative view: a shopper can place content without an account, and an administrator who opens the affected record can have the session captured. That crosses the usual boundary between unauthenticated storefront input and privileged back-office access. OpenMage sites are commonly internet-facing storefronts, and the crafted email detail points to a workflow where customer-submitted text is retained and reviewed.

Patch to 20.19.1 or 20.18.3, then review stored customer email fields and admin templates for unexpected script or markup. Watch for a published CVE, a vendor advisory describing the exact field and version range, and any report of active exploitation. Until those appear, avoid attributing the flaw to a specific plugin or claiming observed attacks; the report supports the CVSS score, the stored XSS class, the session-hijack impact and the two fixed builds.
