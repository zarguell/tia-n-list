Malware.news reported that Microsoft recorded 2,900 failed sign-in attempts per minute against a handful of organizations on October 11. The attempts came from ARTEX agents directed by a China state-aligned actor and continued at the same pace over the weekend. The report describes ARTEX as a self-hosted automated penetration-testing framework, typically run in Docker with a Go backend, a Next.js web interface, and an operator-supplied large language model endpoint.

A later TechRadar report says Microsoft now sees Chinese and North Korean hacking groups actively using ARTEX. The operational concern is sustained authentication pressure generated by software rather than a person typing credentials. Defenders should watch for high-rate failed sign-ins, repeated probing from the same infrastructure, and activity that continues outside normal working hours. The collected reports do not establish that every ARTEX campaign is autonomous or identify a single actor behind all use.
