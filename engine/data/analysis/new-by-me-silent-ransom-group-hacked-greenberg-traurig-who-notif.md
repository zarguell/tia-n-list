Silent Ransom Group (SRG) added the law firm Greenberg Traurig to its leak site, claiming a breach affecting approximately 126,000 individuals. Greenberg Traurig is one of the largest law firms in the United States. DataBreaches.net reported that the firm notified a small number of affected clients directly, while the broader notification responsibility remains unclear.

SRG has previously targeted prominent law firms, including Troutman Pepper Locke, which appeared on the group's leak site alongside 63 other firms as of August 21. The pattern of targeting large legal practices suggests SRG is focused on firms holding sensitive client data, litigation documents, and privileged communications that carry high extortion value.

The incident raises questions about notification obligations when a law firm is breached. Attorney-client privilege complicates standard breach disclosure, and the 126k affected individuals likely include clients, opposing parties, and third parties whose data was processed by the firm. Greenberg Traurig's decision to notify only a small subset of affected individuals may face scrutiny from regulators and affected parties. The breach adds to a growing list of legal sector compromises in 2026.
