The FBI arrested Edward Dubrovsky, co-founder of Canadian ransomware-negotiation firm Cypfer, in Pennsylvania during a cyber-insurance conference (Cyber Risk Summit, Oct 5-7). The arrest connects to the ShinyHunters investigation that recently exposed FBI agent data. Sources indicate Dubrovsky specialized in handling ransomware negotiations. KrebsOnSecurity reports the investigation is centralized at an FBI field office in Texas. Watch for official statements and any impact on ransomware-negotiation firm operations.
