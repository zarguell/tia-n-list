The FBI arrested a co-founder of a Canadian cybersecurity firm linked to the ShinyHunters investigation into stolen FBI agent data. Multiple sources tell KrebsOnSecurity that the Canadian man was visiting Pennsylvania for the Cyber Risk Summit at the Loews Philadelphia Hotel from October 5 to October 7, where the firm was a top sponsor. The suspect's company specialized in ransomware negotiations, and the arrest relates to suspected assistance to ShinyHunters. The New York Times reported the arrest but did not identify the man; FBI Director Kash Patel made a statement on X without naming him. One source said the investigation is centralized at an FBI Texas field office. The arrest raises questions about the overlap between ransomware-negotiation services and threat-actor cooperation. Watch for formal identification of the suspect, charges, and any link between the firm and the ShinyHunters breach of FBI data.
