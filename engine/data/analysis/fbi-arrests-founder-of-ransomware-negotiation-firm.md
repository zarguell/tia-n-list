Canadian cybersecurity executive Edward Dubrovsky, 54, was arrested Thursday in Pennsylvania on federal extortion charges and transferred to the Eastern District of Texas. The co-founder of Cypfer and CyberSteward, firms that help breach victims negotiate with ransomware groups, faces conspiracy to threaten information confidentiality to extort money and Hobbs Act extortion conspiracy. Multiple sources link the arrest to the FBI's ShinyHunters investigation, which recently exposed data on thousands of bureau employees. KrebsOnSecurity and court records confirm the charge details; The New York Times and Politico also reported the arrest. FBI Director Kash Patel announced another suspected co-conspirator was in custody without naming Dubrovsky. The complaint remains sealed. The case highlights the risk that ransomware negotiation firms may become entangled with the criminal groups they interact with. Watch for unsealing of the complaint, confirmation of ShinyHunters links, and whether Dubrovsky's firms face regulatory or legal fallout.
