Canadian cybersecurity executive Edward Dubrovsky, 54, co-founder of Cypfer and CyberSteward, was arrested in Pennsylvania on federal conspiracy and Hobbs Act extortion charges and transferred to the Eastern District of Texas. Multiple sources tie the arrest to the FBI's ShinyHunters investigation that exposed data on thousands of bureau employees. KrebsOnSecurity first identified Dubrovsky; The New York Times and court records confirm the arrest and charge details. The sealed complaint lists conspiracy to threaten information confidentiality and extortion. Watch for unsealing of the complaint, confirmation of ShinyHunters links, and potential fallout for ransomware-negotiation firms.
