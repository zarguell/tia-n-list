The FBI arrested a co-founder of a Canadian cybersecurity firm linked to the ShinyHunters hacking group, which recently exfiltrated sensitive data on thousands of FBI agents. The New York Times reported the arrest of a Canadian man in Pennsylvania, and KrebsOnSecurity identified the suspect as Edward Dubrovsky, co-founder of CyberSteward, who attended the Cyber Risk Summit in Philadelphia between October 5 and October 7. Sources say the suspect's firm specialized in ransomware negotiations and that control of the ShinyHunters investigation has been centralized at an FBI field office in Texas. The arrest connects a negotiation firm founder to a breach that compromised the FBI's own agent data.
