FBI agents arrested the co-founder of a Canadian cybersecurity firm linked to the ShinyHunters hacking group, which recently exfiltrated sensitive data on thousands of FBI agents. The suspect was arrested in Pennsylvania while attending the Cyber Risk Summit (Oct. 5-7) at the Loews Philadelphia Hotel. His company, Cypfer, specialized in ransomware negotiations. The New York Times confirmed the arrest but did not name the individual; sources identify the co-founder as Edward Dubrovsky, also associated with CyberSteward. The ShinyHunters investigation has been centralized at an FBI field office in Texas. The arrest connects to a growing pattern of ransomware-negotiation firms being used as fronts for extortion and data-theft operations.

Watch for further naming of the suspect and any disclosure of how ShinyHunters accessed FBI personnel data. The intersection of ransomware negotiation and threat-actor collaboration remains a critical supply-chain risk.
