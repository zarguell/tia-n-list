South Korean President Lee Jae Myung ordered an emergency probe after hackers breached top banks including Shinhan, KB Kookmin, Hana, and Woori. The Financial Services Commission confirmed attackers did not target one entity but broadly scanned the financial sector for vulnerabilities, tracing attack traffic to IP addresses spanning the US, Japan, Singapore, Vietnam, and Britain. No specific CVE or vulnerability identifier has been assigned; the breach mechanism appears to be network-level intrusion rather than a single software flaw, with regulators warning artificial intelligence may have aided the scanning and access attempts.
