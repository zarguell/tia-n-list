South Korean President Lee Jae Myung ordered an emergency probe after a string of data breaches at major banks including Shinhan and Woori, according to event reports. Attackers conducted broad scanning and the incidents are linked to AI-powered cyberattacks. Welcome Savings Bank separately confirmed a breach affecting 2,200 corporate clients. The existing candidate story covers the initial emergency order; the new event adds specific institutional confirmation without introducing new CVEs or actor attribution.
