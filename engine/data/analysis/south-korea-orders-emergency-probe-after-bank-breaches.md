South Korea's president ordered an emergency investigation after hackers breached major banks including Shinhan and Woori, exposing personal data. The government cited AI-powered cyberattacks as an increasing factor in the breaches. Reports also reference broader data leaks across financial and public institutions that triggered the probe.

The event signals a coordinated campaign targeting South Korea's financial sector rather than isolated incidents. With AI referenced as an attack enabler, detection and response timelines may be compressed. Organizations in the region should expect tighter regulatory scrutiny and faster disclosure mandates.

Watch for official attribution, victim counts, and whether the breaches tie to a known ransomware or data-theft group. The current reporting is preliminary; concrete technical indicators and full impact assessments are still pending.
