US disruption of Integrity Technology Group tools MicroScan and FishHub, announced Thursday with a joint advisory from US, UK, Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand and Spain. Integrity Tech built a Mirai-variant IoT botnet for MicroScan reconnaissance against a US power company, NGOs, Japanese and Polish airports, and Taiwanese universities. FishHub enabled remote access and file exfiltration; it was used against at least 20 Taiwanese universities. The US seized domains including c0cc[.]cc, 98aicai[.]com and outlook3650[.]com. Integrity Tech was sanctioned in 2025 for supplying Chinese state-sponsored APTs including Flax Typhoon, and the EU sanctioned it in March 2026. MicroScan has been active since at least 2017.

This is a concrete disruption rather than a new vulnerability advisory. The seized infrastructure limits the group’s scanning and intrusion capability, though past sanctions did not eliminate operations. Watch for replacement domains and any shift in Integrity Tech’s botnet rebuild using other Mirai variants or different hosting.
