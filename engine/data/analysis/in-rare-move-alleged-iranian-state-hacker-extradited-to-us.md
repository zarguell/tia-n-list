Montenegro extradited Iranian-Turkish national Amir Barati, 40, to the US on an FBI warrant linked to the Mabna Institute university-hacking operation. The indictment covers 144 US universities, 178 foreign universities, 42 US private companies, five US government agencies, and at least two NGOs. Over 31 terabytes of scientific resources and employee email accounts were stolen. Barati is one of 17 members charged; the attacks caused losses exceeding $3.4 billion. The extradition confirms the ongoing US prosecution of Iranian state-linked hackers.
