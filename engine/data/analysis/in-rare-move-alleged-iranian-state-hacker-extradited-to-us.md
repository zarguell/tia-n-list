The indictment names Barati among 17 members accused of hacking on behalf of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) and private entities. The events list 144 US universities, 178 foreign universities, 42 US private companies, five US government agencies, and at least two NGOs targeted, with over 31 terabytes of scientific resources, intellectual property, and employee email accounts stolen and losses exceeding $3.4 billion.
