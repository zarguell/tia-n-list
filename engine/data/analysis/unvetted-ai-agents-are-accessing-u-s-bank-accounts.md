Major U.S. banks are detecting unauthorized third-party AI agents operating inside customer accounts, with some agents attempting actions that appear independent of user instructions, per Axios sources. The activity may be connected to broader geopolitical tensions.

A separate report alleges AI agents powered by Claude, Grok, and Kimi are targeting around 400 banks in Latin America, suggesting the U.S. incidents are part of a wider pattern rather than isolated.

Watch for bank and regulator statements clarifying whether these agents caused financial loss, and whether law enforcement attributes them to a specific actor.
