This story aggregates reports of vulnerabilities being probed or exploited within days of disclosure. The most recent development is CVE-2026-101767, an unauthenticated SQL injection in Oracle E-Business Suite's Application Object Library (CVSS 9.8) patched in Oracle's October 2026 CPU. Defused recorded exploratory exploits against honeypots on October 27, and Shadowserver tracks over 670 internet-exposed Oracle EBS instances, most in the United States. Censys identified 2,246 exposed instances. Horizon3.ai discovered the flaw; Oracle has not tagged it as exploited in the wild.

The broader pattern across the aggregated reports is shrinking time between disclosure and scanning. CVE-2026-101767 was patched October 20 and probed within a week, while CVE-2026-46817 in EBS Payments was exploited in June. The same velocity applies to other flaws in the cluster, reinforcing that newly disclosed critical issues draw rapid attacker attention.

Defenders should treat newly disclosed critical flaws as immediately at risk, prioritize internet-facing systems, and apply vendor patches without waiting for confirmed exploitation. Oracle EBS customers in particular should patch CVE-2026-101767 immediately given the observed scanning.
