Cisco Expressway CVE-2026-76466 is the concrete exploitation thread in this material. The flaw is an unauthenticated remote code execution bug scored 9.8 that affects clustered Expressway Core and Edge deployments. Persistent cookies together with insufficient client certificate validation let an attacker reach the database service and invoke RPC commands that run as root on the underlying operating system. Cisco disclosed it on October 21 and fixed it in Expressway X15.5.20; there are no workarounds, and systems on X14 or earlier have to move to a fixed release.

The gap between public exploit code and real intrusion was short. Researcher Nicolas Krassas posted a PoC and a fleet detector on GitHub, and F6 reported that attackers breached a Russian organization with CVE-2026-76466 soon after that release. Cisco's advisory has not been revised and still says the company is unaware of malicious use, so the vendor text lags the incident reports. CISA's KEV catalog now lists the Expressway hard-coded credentials flaw, which turns it into a remediation deadline rather than a tracking item. Watch for further victim reports and for scans against exposed Expressway clusters.

Other material here describes separate problems rather than one campaign. The CISA and FBI SafePay advisory counts at least 600 organizations hit since August 2024 and calls out stolen VPN credentials and deliberate backup destruction. GitLab CVE-2026-110264 is a CVSS 10 integer overflow in the regular expression compiler fixed in 19.5.1, 19.4.3, 19.3.6, 19.2.9, 19.1.9, 19.0.10 and 18.11.13. These items share a defensive lesson but not an actor or an exploit chain, so any claim of one machine-speed campaign would overread them. Keep the tracking separate and prioritize the KEV-listed Expressway bug on exposure.
