This story aggregates multiple recent threat developments rather than a single event. The events include a multi-agent AI system called CARLIY0S coordinating attacks against over 400 financial institutions in Latin America, a newly uncovered DarkCore RAT with credential theft and browser session reuse, an Operation IXIM botnet involving millions of Popcorn Time users, a widespread credential-theft campaign targeting FortiGate, FortiWeb, and Palo Alto GlobalProtect appliances by an operator known as k0t with over 70,000 exposed credentials, and additional reports of automated attacks. The common theme is rapid, automated, or AI-assisted intrusion methods that outpace manual defense.

No single CVE defines the cluster; the coverage spans malware campaigns, device exploitation, and AI-coordinated attacks. Organizations should prioritize patching exposed firewall and VPN appliances, reviewing credentials for exposed network devices, and monitoring for dark web leaks. The presence of AI-coordinated multi-agent systems targeting banks indicates that attackers are scaling operations faster than traditional detection timelines, making automation in defense a practical requirement rather than an optional upgrade.
