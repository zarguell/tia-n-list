CERT Polska confirmed a second facility was hit during the destructive attacks on Poland's energy sector last year. An attacker used a dedicated mobile gateway and a private APN misconfigured to let arbitrary devices communicate, pivoting from a wind farm into the OT network of a combined heat-and-power plant. Siemens PLCs were sabotaged, shutting down a steam turbine and the process-water treatment system at a facility that supplies heat to around 50,000 residents.

The detail that matters for every other operator is the misconfiguration: the private APN allowed lateral movement between arbitrary devices. CERT Polska says the pattern is common in Poland and elsewhere and published hardening guidance for private APN deployments. Any organization running OT over cellular backhaul should review APN isolation rules, segment mobile gateways from control networks, and validate that an exposed gateway cannot become a pivot into the plant.
