Ransomware groups are systematically encrypting backups before hitting production systems. The ALPHV/BlackCat Change Healthcare incident in February 2024 showed backups not isolated enough for quick recovery, leading to $22 million ransom and $1.6 billion total recovery cost. BlackMatter made backup destruction standard operating procedure against farm cooperatives in 2021. The pattern is deliberate: without a reachable recovery path, victims face sharper payout pressure.
