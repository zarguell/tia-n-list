Epichealth Medical Clinics confirmed a data breach following a claim by the ransomware group TheGentlemen, according to a November 5, 2026 report from beyondmachines.net. The event record carries little beyond the confirmation itself, so the breach scope, patient impact, and access method remain unstated in the collected material.

TheGentlemen separately claimed an intrusion on November 2 against MegChem, a South African engineering consultancy founded in 1985 in Secunda, Mpumalanga, with over 350 engineers and offices in Secunda, Cape Town, Johannesburg, and Richards Bay, sited next to the Sasol Secunda petrochemical complex; the group threatened to leak sensitive data unless its demands were met, per malware.news on November 7.

Watch for statements from Epichealth or MegChem confirming the scope of the respective incidents, any leak-site publication of customer or project data, and whether TheGentlemen claims additional targets in the same window.
