CenterPoint Energy confirmed a breach that exposed personal information of over 1 million Texans, with dark-web claims referencing 7.49 million records. The utility serves roughly 7 million metered customers across Indiana, Minnesota, Ohio, and Texas. A threat actor using the alias "4d722e4d656f77" posted on a cybercrime forum on September 12, releasing a 2.5 GB archive. The attacker said data was exfiltrated via an unprotected public API lacking rate limiting and WAF controls. The company told the SEC that services were not impacted, but names, addresses, Social Security numbers, and driver license numbers were among the compromised data.

The new Mastodon update reinforces the scale: more than 1 million Texas residents affected, with notification letters apparently not yet sent. The breach highlights how external-facing APIs without basic protections become direct exfiltration paths. Organizations relying on public APIs should audit for missing rate limits and WAF coverage immediately, not after disclosure.
