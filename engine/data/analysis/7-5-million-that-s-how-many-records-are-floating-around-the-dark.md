CenterPoint Energy, a Texas-based utility provider serving approximately 7 million metered customers, confirmed a data breach affecting 7.49 million customer records. The stolen data appeared on dark web forums, initially reported by The Perimeter Site and subsequently covered by Beyond Machines. CenterPoint disclosed the breach following the dark web claims, though the company has not detailed the initial access vector or the specific data types exposed.

The breach ranks among the larger utility-sector incidents in recent years. CenterPoint provides electric transmission and distribution, natural gas distribution, and energy services operations across Texas, Indiana, Ohio, and Minnesota. The company serves approximately 7 million metered customers and operates roughly 238,000 miles of distribution lines. The scale of the breach suggests potential exposure of customer names, addresses, account numbers, and possibly usage or billing data.

The incident remains under investigation. CenterPoint has not disclosed whether the breach involved a third-party vendor, ransomware, or exfiltration-only activity. No threat actor group has publicly claimed responsibility. Customers should monitor for phishing attempts and unauthorized account changes.
