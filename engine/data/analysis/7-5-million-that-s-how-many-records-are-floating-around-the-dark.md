CenterPoint Energy confirmed to Texas authorities that over 1 million Texans were affected by a breach first disclosed in September. The company told the Texas Attorney General that 1,062,060 residents were impacted, with names, addresses, Social Security numbers, and driver license numbers exposed. The disclosure came one month after an SEC filing acknowledged stolen customer data, and days after a threat actor claimed nearly 7.5 million records on a cybercrime forum. A 2.5 GB archive reportedly containing about 3 million phone numbers appeared on September 12. CenterPoint delivers electricity and natural gas across Indiana, Minnesota, and Ohio as well, so impact beyond Texas remains unclear. No notification letters had been sent at the time of reporting.

This is a significant utility-sector data breach with identity-theft risk at scale. The gap between the threat actor's forum post and formal disclosure, plus the partial customer count relative to the 7.5 million claim, suggests the full scope is still being reconciled. Watch whether multi-state notifications expand and whether regulatory action follows the Texas filing.
