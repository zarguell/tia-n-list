SOCRadar's dark-web roundup records KEPCO confirming a breach of about 1.52 million users, split between 650,487 KEPCO ON accounts and 869,761 users of its SRM procurement system. The KEPCO ON data includes names, resident registration numbers, bank account details, credit card information, and billing records. KEPCO blocked external IPs, suspended affected services, and opened an investigation with the National Intelligence Service and outside experts, after an earlier October leak of data on about 24,000 employees that it said did not involve customer data.

The same roundup tracks unconfirmed claims rather than confirmed incidents: an actor advertising 560 million records against KEPCO's 1.52 million confirmed figure, 22 million alleged Aeroméxico records, a claimed 180 TB defense-industry leak, two zero-day sales, a 27-organization VPN access auction, and a new RaaS platform. Watch for corroboration of the larger KEPCO dataset and Aeroméxico listing, and for any link between the employee and customer leaks.
