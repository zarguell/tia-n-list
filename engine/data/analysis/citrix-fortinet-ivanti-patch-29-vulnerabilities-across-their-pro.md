Tuesday's enterprise patch batch covered 29 vulnerabilities across Citrix, Fortinet, and Ivanti products. Citrix issued three advisories spanning nine defects in Workspace App for Windows, XenServer 8.4 and 9, and UniconOS, with impacts from guest-to-host compromise to arbitrary code execution. Fortinet published ten advisories for 13 bugs, including critical unauthenticated code execution in FortiSIEM (CVE-2026-106173) and an authentication bypass in FortiSandbox GUI (CVE-2026-107656), plus SQL injection flaws across FortiAnalyzer, FortiManager, and FortiMail. Ivanti patched seven defects, headlined by the exploited Connect Secure zero-day CVE-2026-104935 alongside a critical Neurons Patch for Intune bug, CVE-2026-109412.

The Fortinet criticals and the exploited Ivanti zero-day are the patch-first items since they expose management and edge infrastructure. Watch for working exploits against the FortiSIEM and FortiSandbox flaws, KEV additions, and scanning telemetry showing mass targeting of any of the three vendors' edge boxes.
