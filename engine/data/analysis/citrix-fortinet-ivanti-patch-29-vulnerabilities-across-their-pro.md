Tuesday updates from Citrix, Fortinet, and Ivanti address 29 vulnerabilities across their product lines. Citrix patched nine defects in Workspace App for Windows, XenServer 8.4 and 9, and UniconOS, with impacts ranging from arbitrary code execution and DoS to information leaks and host compromise from within VMs. Fortinet resolved 13 bugs, including critical unauthenticated code execution in FortiSIEM (CVE-2026-106173) and authentication bypass in FortiSandbox GUI (CVE-2026-107656), plus four high-severity SQL injection flaws in FortiAnalyzer and FortiMail. Ivanti fixed seven defects in Connect Secure, Neurons Patch for Intune, and Xtraction; Connect Secure resolves CVE-2026-104935, an exploited zero-day combining two chained bugs to compromise patched VPN appliances.

The exploited Ivanti zero-day and the critical Fortinet authentication bypass make this a high-urgency patch cycle. Organizations running NetScaler, FortiSIEM, or Ivanti Connect Secure should prioritize those updates; the Citrix XenServer and Workspace App fixes are also significant given the breadth of potential exploitation paths.
