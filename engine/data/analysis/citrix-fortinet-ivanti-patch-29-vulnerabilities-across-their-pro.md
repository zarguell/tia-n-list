Tuesday's patch batch closed 29 vulnerabilities across Citrix, Fortinet and Ivanti. Citrix published three advisories covering nine defects in Workspace App for Windows, XenServer 8.4 and XenServer 9, and UniconOS, with the impact list running from denial of service and information leaks to privilege escalation, arbitrary command execution and compromise or crash of the host from inside the VM. Fortinet issued ten advisories for 13 bugs, two of them critical: improper path traversal in FortiSIEM across 7.0.x through 7.5.0, rated CVSS 3.1 9.8 in PSIRT advisory FG-IR-26-180 and allowing unauthenticated command execution over the network, plus an authentication bypass in the FortiSandbox GUI, CVE-2026-107656. Fortinet also closed five high severity SQL injection flaws in FortiAnalyzer, FortiAnalyzer Cloud, FortiManager, FortiManager Cloud and FortiMail webmail, and a medium issue in FortiWeb, CVE-2025-66178.

Ivanti patched seven defects across Connect Secure, Neurons Patch for Intune and Xtraction, and the Connect Secure fix is the one that matters most. CVE-2026-104935 covers two underlying bugs chained together and NVD records it as an authentication bypass by spoofing before version 25.1.3.0, letting remote unauthenticated attackers run arbitrary commands against affected 25.1.0.0 through 25.1.1.2 and 22.8 R2 deployments, with active exploitation status and a KEV listing, base score 8.6. Neurons Patch for Intune carries CVE-2026-109412, an exposed dangerous endpoint that leaks sensitive authentication information to a remote low privilege attacker, CVSS 3.1 9.9.

What to watch is exploit traffic rather than the advisory list. The Ivanti zero-day already had exploitation status when NVD published it on 13 October, so expect scanning against exposed Connect Secure portals before patching reaches the long tail of 22.8 R2 installations. For Fortinet, FortiSIEM and FortiSandbox GUI are management plane services and are the ones most likely to face working exploits, since both were reachable without credentials. Monitor for KEV additions on the Fortinet criticals, credential-stuffing against VPN portals that have not moved past 25.1.3.0, and any move of these flaws onto the Citrix and Ivanti edge appliances as an initial access path rather than a headline patch.
