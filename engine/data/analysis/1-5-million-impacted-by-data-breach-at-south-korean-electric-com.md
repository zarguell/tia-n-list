South Korean state-owned utility KEPCO disclosed a breach affecting about 1.5 million people. The systems involved were KEPCO ON, its integrated customer service portal, and the supplier relationship management platform used for procurement and service contracts. KEPCO attributed the incident to an external attacker and pointed to increasingly sophisticated AI-driven attacks, but did not say how access was gained or confirm that AI was involved in this breach.

On November 20 the company confirmed that data had been exfiltrated. KEPCO ON records for 650,487 people included names, addresses, phone numbers, unique ID numbers, customer numbers, card numbers and details, bank names, bank account numbers, account-holder names, card issuers, cardholder names and billing or payment details. SRM data covered 869,761 people, including names, unique ID numbers, email addresses and company addresses for 426,827 of them. KEPCO suspended the affected services, blocked external access and carried out remediation. Watch for the initial access path and for clarity on how much supplier-side data was taken.
