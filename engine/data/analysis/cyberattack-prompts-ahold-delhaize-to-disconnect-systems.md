Ahold Delhaize confirmed a new cyberattack that forced it to take some systems offline, impacting deliveries and online orders for Albert Heijn and Delhaize in Belgium and the Netherlands, with US stores possibly affected. The company said stores remain open and no customer-facing impact occurred, but did not identify the attacker or confirm a ransomware link. It is unclear whether this is related to the separate European supermarket refrigeration attack over the weekend. The incident follows previous cyberattacks in November 2024 (pharmacy and supermarket chains) and April 2025 (INC Ransom group). Watch for updates on attribution and whether delivery disruptions expand.
