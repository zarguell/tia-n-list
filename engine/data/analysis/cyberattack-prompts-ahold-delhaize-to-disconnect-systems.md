Ahold Delhaize, the Dutch-Belgian retail giant with more than 9,400 stores across Europe, the US, and Indonesia, disconnected some systems after identifying a security issue in its network. The company runs about 400,000 employees, serves around 60 million customers weekly, and reported over $104 billion in net sales for 2024.

Disruption is visible at the brand level. Preventive measures hit deliveries and online orders at Albert Heijn and Delhaize in Belgium and the Netherlands, US operations appear affected too, and Stop & Shop employees reported trouble accessing schedules and pay stubs. Stores remain open and the company says its ability to serve customers has not been affected. It is unclear whether this incident is related to the weekend cyberattack that defrosted supermarket refrigeration across Europe. The company has prior history here: a November 2024 cyberattack hit Giant Food pharmacies, Hannaford, Food Lion, The Giant Company, and Stop & Shop.

Grocery retail is attractive to extortionists because downtime hits perishable supply chains and consumer trust directly. Watch for ransomware claims or leak-site listings, whether US banners escalate beyond back-office disruptions, and any disclosure of data theft, which the company has not yet addressed.
