Tower Records Japan disclosed on 21 November 2026 that customer information was leaked after unauthorized access, according to Japan Times reporting on 23 November. Separate coverage dates the attack to 19 November and says approximately 11 million customer records may have been compromised. A later post frames the exposure as more than 10 million login records, so the count and the type of record still differ between reports.

The reported fields are names, dates of birth and delivery addresses. The company said no credit card information was leaked. Even without payment data, that combination supports impersonation and targeted follow-on contact against music retail customers. The incident sits in a run of Japanese retail breaches reported in the same period.

Watch for a finalized count from Tower Records, clarification of whether the exposed set is customer records or login records, and confirmation of when the unauthorized access began. Regulator notification and direct customer notices would also fix the scope. Until then, the 11 million figure remains an upper estimate of records that may have been compromised, not a confirmed number of affected people.
