Microsoft Teams is adding AI-powered, context-aware phishing detection. The feature uses contextual signals to flag suspicious messages inside Teams chats, aiming to catch social engineering that bypasses traditional email filters. No release timeline or CVE references accompany the announcement; it is a defensive product update from Microsoft.

The move fits a broader trend of AI-driven security layers in collaboration tools. Without observed exploitation data, this remains a vendor capability update rather than an incident. Watch for rollout details and any future efficacy reports.
