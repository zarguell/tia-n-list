Meta fixed VM escape vulnerabilities in Muse, its viral AI agent product, in the weeks before launch. The flaws could have allowed users to break out of the agent environment into Meta's internal systems. 404media.co and madrobot.blog reported the rushed fixes. No CVEs assigned, but the issue is a pre-launch containment failure in a high-visibility AI product. Watch for any post-launch disclosure of the specific escape vectors and whether Meta issues a security bulletin.
