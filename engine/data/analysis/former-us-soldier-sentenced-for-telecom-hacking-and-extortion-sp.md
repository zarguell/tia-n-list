The Justice Department announced the sentencing of a former U.S. Army soldier for a multi-year cybercrime spree involving telecommunications hacking and extortion. KrebsOnSecurity reports the sentence at 70 months in prison for extortion schemes against AT&T and Verizon, and ties the case to massive telecom data breaches involving Snowflake credential theft.

The case matters for two reasons. It shows insider-adjacent access being monetized through direct extortion of major telecoms, and it connects to the broader Snowflake-related credential theft wave that hit dozens of organizations, meaning the stolen data likely fed the same criminal ecosystem.

What to watch: court records for the intrusion method and the exact data volume taken, whether co-defendants or buyers of the data are charged, and any AT&T or Verizon breach notifications that follow from the documented exfiltration.
