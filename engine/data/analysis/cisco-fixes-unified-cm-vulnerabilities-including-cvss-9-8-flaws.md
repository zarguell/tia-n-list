Cisco's security hardening release for Unified CM and related voice products addresses six vulnerabilities: CVE-2026-76473 and CVE-2026-76474, both rated CVSS 9.8, plus CVE-2026-76475, CVE-2026-76490, CVE-2026-76491, and CVE-2026-76492. The critical advisories were first published on October 21. The update is delivered as a hardening release rather than point fixes.

The covered systems include Unified CM and Unified CM Session Management Edition voice infrastructure. No in-the-wild exploitation is reported in the available coverage; the risk is the severity of the two critical flaws in internet-facing call-control systems.

Administrators should apply the Cisco hardening release to affected Unified CM builds and review the advisory for version-specific guidance.
