The ransomware group N0n claimed responsibility for attacking Hunter Neurology (hunterneurology.com.au) in Australia, with the claim dated October 13, 2026. The attacker statement lists 15,252 patient images and scans, 11,819 letters and reports, 439 laboratory results, 839 patient records system files, and four complete weekly backups of the practice medical records. The data includes names, dates of birth, and clinical data. The claim is marked "NOT PAID - EXPOSED".

A follow-up social mention notes the hackers have not yet published the data but are expected to do so. If released, thousands of additional Australian patients would be affected. Monitor dark-web and healthcare breach channels for publication confirmation. Healthcare practices should review backup integrity and access controls given the stated exposure of full weekly backups.
