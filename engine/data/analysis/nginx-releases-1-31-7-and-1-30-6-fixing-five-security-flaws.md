NGINX released versions 1.31.7 and 1.30.6 to fix five memory-safety flaws. The update covers CVE-2026-15176 (buffer overflow via alias and rewrite), CVE-2026-93479 (use-after-free in async file I/O), CVE-2026-95101, and CVE-2026-97205. The buffer overflow and use-after-free paths are the most serious, as remote attackers could crash servers or potentially reach code execution. The releases are available through nginx.org security advisories.

Organizations running NGINX 1.30.x or 1.31.x should upgrade to 1.30.6 or 1.31.7 respectively. Verify that load balancer and reverse-proxy instances in your environment are on patched versions and that no unpatched instances remain exposed to untrusted traffic.
