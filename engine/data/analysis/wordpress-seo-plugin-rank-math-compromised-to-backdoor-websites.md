Rank Math SEO PRO was compromised in a supply-chain attack that delivered malware through official downloads and updates between October 15 at 20:02 UTC and October 16 at 07:12 UTC. Affected version 3.0.123 contained embedded malicious code tracked as CVE-2026-111862 with a CVSS 9.8 rating. The malware establishes persistent access, communicates with rank1cdn[.]com, and recreates hidden backdoors when a page is visited.

The vendor released a clean version 3.0.124 and advised users who activated 3.0.123 during the 11-hour window to treat their sites as fully compromised. Simply updating is not sufficient; the malware may have added hidden administrator accounts, scheduled tasks, database entries, or fake WordPress core files. The event content cites securityonline.info and the vendor advisory confirming the affected build numbers and cleanup steps.

Only the PRO version was impacted; the free WordPress.org release was not affected. Watch for further reports of exploitation in the wild or additional malicious builds, and verify any site that installed 3.0.123 during the window.
