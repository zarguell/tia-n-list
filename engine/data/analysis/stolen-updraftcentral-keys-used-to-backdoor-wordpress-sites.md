Stolen UpdraftCentral connection keys could let attackers send commands to connected WordPress sites. A threat actor breached TeamUpdraft's website over the weekend of October 17-18 and accessed the customer database; the vendor replaced affected keys and signed out all accounts. The company says plugin files and self-hosted instances are unaffected, but about 400,000 sites with UpdraftCentral or Migrator keys (roughly 10% of UpdraftPlus users) could be at risk. Site owners should rotate storage credentials and reauthorize connections.

The event from BleepingComputer confirms the breach and notes the potential exposure. No observed in-the-wild exploitation is reported yet, but the key exposure creates a concrete backdoor path. This is a supply-chain style threat against a widely deployed WordPress plugin ecosystem.
