Attackers exploited stolen UpdraftCentral connection keys to send trusted remote-management commands to WordPress sites and install malicious plugins, following a breach of TeamUpdraft's website over October 17-18, 2026. TeamUpdraft detected unauthorized access to its infrastructure and suspects the threat actor accessed its customer database, which contained connection keys and account details. The company said it did not store payment details and noted that UpdraftPlus plugin files and downloads remain unaffected; self-hosted UpdraftCentral instances are also not impacted.

An estimated 10% of sites using UpdraftPlus have an UpdraftCentral or Migrator key, potentially exposing around 400,000 websites. TeamUpdraft has replaced affected keys and credentials, signed out every account, and recommends users rotate storage credentials and reauthorize connections. The compromise follows the original breach disclosure from BleepingComputer covering the same incident.
