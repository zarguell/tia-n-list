ClickFix is delivery by self-inflicted execution: the victim copies a command from a web page and runs it personally in Windows Run, PowerShell, or macOS Terminal to fix a fake error, pass a fake CAPTCHA, or install software that does not exist. The story's clearest instance is PasteSwitch, where Hudson Rock and ADAMnetworks found the verified u/hbomax Reddit account hijacked to push 108 malicious advertisements in about 48 hours across five lure groups, funneling users to hbomaxx[.]us and a paste-and-run prompt: curl piped to zsh on macOS delivering MacSync, AMOS Helper, and fake wallet apps; MSHTA and PowerShell on Windows delivering Amatera Stealer, which validates stolen credentials manually and disguises its C2 as Facebook connections. Backfill shows this machinery was industrialized well before the Reddit incident: Hudson Rock disclosed the ErrTraffic ClickFix framework for sale on Exploit.IN in December 2025 at $800, and Sekoia's June 2026 report found it running as subscription malware-as-a-service at $380 a month, with C2 resolved through EtherHiding smart contracts on the Polygon blockchain.

The numbers argue this is not a niche trick. CTM360 cataloged roughly 17,000 ClickFix URLs living on otherwise trusted websites, BleepingComputer counted more than 5,400 hacked small-business sites serving blockchain-stored payloads on September 5, and the dashboard in Hudson Rock's original ErrTraffic disclosure recorded a 58.8 percent conversion rate, twenty downloads from thirty-four lure views. Malvertising is normally measured in fractions of a percent, so a lure that converts at tens of percent while executing inside system utilities the browser never observes changes the economics of stolen credentials, and it explains why one hijacked ad account is worth more to criminals than a farm of lookalike domains. The event list presents the HBO Max ads, the Brevo CDN compromise, and the hacked Ukrainian business sites as separate stories; the backfill shows they are one economy in which infostealers harvest CMS credentials, those credentials inject new ClickFix lures into trusted sites, and the new lures harvest again, a loop Hudson Rock described in December 2025 and Sekoia's WordPress forensics confirmed, including initial access through stolen admin credentials rather than any exploit.

Hunt at the paste boundary. Enable PowerShell ScriptBlock logging and alert on the ErrTraffic command shape Sekoia published: ScriptBlockText beginning with "<" and containing "#>", "-bxor[int][char]$", and "[convert]::ToInt32". Correlate browser-launched spawns of mshta.exe and powershell.exe, hunt curl piped into zsh on macOS, and flag egress to blockchain RPC endpoints such as Polygon or Quicknode followed immediately by a PowerShell launch. On WordPress and Ghost estates, check wp-content/mu-plugins/ for session-manager.php, inspect pages for /cf.js or /api/css.js injections, patch Ghost against CVE-2026-26980, and rotate administrator credentials. Turn on the friction that now exists: the Terminal paste warning in macOS Tahoe 26.4, Opera's Paste Protect, and the ACSC's May 2026 advisory on ClickFix-delivered Vidar as help-desk awareness material.

Two developments would change the assessment. Microsoft's August 31 warning about TerminalFix showed ClickFix payloads deploying reverse tunnels after the paste, which converts a one-shot stealer into durable interactive access; if that variant spreads, this story stops being a malvertising problem and becomes an intrusion problem. And if blockchain-resolved C2 keeps spreading past ErrTraffic and its copycat Exvicy into supply-chain positions like the Brevo CDN incident, domain takedowns and blocklists lose their grip, because the pointer lives on-chain and rotated daily in Sekoia's observation.
