ClickFix campaigns have escalated dramatically with the PasteSwitch operation exposed in September 2026. Attackers compromised HBO Max's verified Reddit account and pushed 108 distinct ClickFix advertisements over 48 hours, leveraging the platform's trust infrastructure to distribute macOS stealers, Windows loaders, and cryptocurrency clippers. The campaign was uncovered by Hudson Rock and ADAMnetworks researchers who traced it across multiple platforms.

The core technique remains social engineering users into executing malicious commands, but the scale and sophistication have grown. Previous waves used multi-hop Google redirects to evade filters, and attackers exploited email spam-confidence-level bypasses to deliver HTA payloads through mshta.exe. The PasteSwitch variant adds compromised high-profile social media accounts as a distribution channel, making detection harder since the ads appear legitimate.

Defenders should monitor for anomalous use of legitimate platforms for command-and-control infrastructure. The campaign targets both Windows and macOS users, and the abuse of verified accounts means traditional reputation-based filtering is insufficient. Organizations should implement application allowlisting and educate users about the ClickFix social engineering pattern.
