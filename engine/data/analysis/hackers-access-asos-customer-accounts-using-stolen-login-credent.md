ASOS notified U.S. customers of a data breach after detecting unauthorized account access on July 28, 2026. The company's investigation, completed the following day, confirmed that an external party accessed accounts using credentials sourced from outside ASOS systems, consistent with a credential-stuffing attack. ASOS did not disclose the attack volume, total affected accounts, or the origin of the stolen credentials.

Potentially exposed data includes names, email addresses, delivery and billing addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, linked social media account details, and partial payment card information (cardholder name, last four digits, and expiration date). Notably, social media login credentials and full payment card numbers were not compromised. The incident is a reminder that credential reuse across platforms remains one of the most common and effective attack vectors for account takeover, and that consumer retailers with large user bases are persistent targets for automated credential-stuffing tools.
