CrowdStrike OverWatch identified a VAPOR PANDA campaign using ARTEX, a Go-based agentic framework, alongside the Chinese-developed DeepSeek large language model. The operation targets government and organizational infrastructure globally. CrowdStrike's disclosure on October 30, 2026, links the campaign to Chinese state-backed actors and highlights how AI-agent frameworks lower the operational cost of reconnaissance and intrusion.

Earlier Elastic Security Labs reporting on BLOODALCHEMY (October 13, 2023) connects to VAPOR PANDA: the backdoor was deployed at a Middle East interior ministry and a Latin America foreign ministry. Independent researchers also documented exposed ARTEX infrastructure and disposable VPS patterns in October 2026. The combination of agentic AI tooling, signed binaries, and established backdoor payloads indicates a mature, multi-stage intrusion set.

What to watch: whether ARTEX exposure expands into additional sectors and if DeepSeek integration becomes standard across other Chinese-aligned campaigns. The blend of AI agents and traditional malware infrastructure suggests evolving tradecraft that defenders should track closely.
