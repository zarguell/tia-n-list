CrowdStrike attributed to VAPOR PANDA, a China-nexus threat actor, a campaign running since October 17, 2026 that used ARTEX, an open-source multi-agent AI penetration-testing framework written in Go with a Next.js frontend that can run on any model, paired with the Chinese-developed DeepSeek large language model. The campaign targeted more than 200 organizations across at least 14 countries and compromised roughly 140 sensitive targets in 11 days, with about 70 percent of targets confirmed compromised or showing moderate-confidence evidence of intrusion. Targets included foreign ministries, a European country's presidency and prime minister's office, defense and intelligence agencies, telecommunications providers in the United States, Belgium, and the Philippines, and 19 U.S.-based organizations in the pharmaceutical, semiconductor, defense, nuclear, and financial sectors. ARTEX-driven activity generated requests as often as every 10 seconds and pursued multiple attack paths concurrently, which CrowdStrike's Adam Meyers described as the first observed nation-state agentic adversary at this scale.

The same ARTEX tooling surfaced in related incidents. Microsoft attributed to Storm-0940 a supply-chain compromise of the ConnectWise Cloud Backup environment in which attackers gained access to application identities, disabled seven affected identities, revoked certificates, suspended the service, and attempted sign-ins to more than 2,250 tenants. Zscaler ThreatLabz documented a separate campaign abusing ARTEX against South Korean financial institutions first observed in late September 2026 that exfiltrated personally identifiable information belonging to approximately 67,000 individuals. Elastic Security Labs' BLOODALCHEMY backdoor, part of the REF5961 intrusion set, was also observed deployed by VAPOR PANDA at a Middle East interior ministry and a Latin America foreign ministry. Independent analysis noted exposed ARTEX infrastructure on disposable VPS and proxy egress, arguing AI agents cut reconnaissance costs while disposable infrastructure cuts egress rotation costs.

What to watch: service-principal and application-identity sign-in logs for anomalous automated access, increased tempo of reconnaissance against exposed services, and further nation-state use of agentic frameworks. Organizations using ConnectWise Cloud Backup or SSO providers should review affected application identities, and financial-sector defenders in particular should harden against AI-assisted credential and reconnaissance automation.
