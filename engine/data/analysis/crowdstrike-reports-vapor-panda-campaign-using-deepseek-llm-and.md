CrowdStrike tracks VAPOR PANDA, a China state-backed threat group, using ARTEX, an automated agentic AI penetration testing framework paired with the DeepSeek large language model, against more than 200 organizations in at least 14 countries since October 17, 2026. The campaign already compromised about 140 sensitive targets in 11 days, with roughly 70 percent of targets confirmed compromised or showing moderate-confidence evidence of intrusion. Targets include foreign ministries, a European country's presidency and prime minister's office, defense and intelligence agencies, 19 U.S.-based organizations across pharmaceutical, semiconductor, defense, nuclear, and financial sectors, and telecom providers in the United States, Belgium, and the Philippines. The tooling allowed requests as often as every 10 seconds and concurrent pursuit of multiple attack paths.

CrowdStrike's Adam Meyers described this as the first observed nation-state agentic adversary operating at this scale and warned similar activity is likely to increase. Defenders should expect rapid, automated reconnaissance and lateral movement and tune detection for high-frequency, multi-path intrusion patterns rather than single-threaded human-paced activity. The campaign is a signal that agentic AI tooling has moved from research into operational state-sponsored use.
