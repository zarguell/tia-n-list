SOCRadar's Threat Research Unit tracks Operation Conflict Compass, a spearphishing campaign against Ukraine-focused entities attributed with moderate confidence to Konni (TA406, Opal Sleet), the North Korea-linked group, running since early August 2026 and assessed as seeking intelligence on the direction of Russia's war in Ukraine. Lures arrive as ZIP attachments holding LNK files disguised as PDFs, with themes including Russia-Ukraine peace plans and a résumé styled as the CV of a real social researcher, Olesia Tsvientukh, delivered as OlesiaTsvientukh_SocialResearcher_Sociologist_Qualitative.lnk. The target profile points at diplomatic groups, think tanks, researchers, and NGOs, though no victim list is confirmed.

Opening the shortcut pulls staged files from GitHub: a VBScript that creates a scheduled task named OneDriveUpdateScheduler launching a PowerShell payload every minute as a persistence and tasking channel, and VelvetCake, the modular PowerShell family that retrieves and runs additional scripts for collection. The September 27 addition to the record is the named CV lure, which confirms the actors mine real professional identities for decoys rather than generating generic documents.

Defenders at Ukraine-adjacent organizations should block LNK attachments at the mail gateway, alert on scheduled tasks launching PowerShell per minute under a OneDrive-sounding name, and watch GitHub repositories used as staging. Additional VelvetCake IOCs and confirmed victims are the developments that would sharpen hunting.
