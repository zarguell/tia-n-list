Tyler Robert Buchanan, 24, from Dundee, Scotland, was sentenced to six years in a US federal prison for his role in Scattered Spider phishing and cryptocurrency theft, the final sentence among five defendants. He was arrested in Spain in May 2024, held by US authorities from April 2025, and pleaded guilty in April 2026 to conspiracy to commit wire fraud and aggravated identity theft. A second report says the November charges covered a dozen companies across telecommunications, technology, BPO, entertainment, IT supply, cloud communications, and virtual currency.

Between September 2021 and April 2023 the group sent hundreds of SMS lures to employees, directing them to credential-harvesting pages that mimicked their employers. Captured logins and personal data landed in a Telegram channel run by Buchanan and an accomplice, then enabled intrusions into employee accounts and company systems. The actors used SIM swaps to defeat two-factor authentication and reach victim crypto wallets. The theft total rose from the 8 million dollars cited at the plea to at least 11.6 million dollars; a restitution hearing is set for December 11.

The case shows the full chain from smishing to SIM swap to wallet drain, and it closes the US prosecution of this defendant group. Watch the December restitution hearing and any related charging or sentencing updates for the remaining figure on losses and for confirmed victim counts.
