Tyler Robert Buchanan, a 24-year-old from Dundee, received a six-year US federal prison sentence for his role in Scattered Spider intrusions. He pleaded guilty in April 2026 to conspiracy to commit wire fraud and aggravated identity theft. Court reporting describes SMS phishing against employees of at least a dozen companies between September 2021 and April 2023, covering telecommunications, technology, business process outsourcing, entertainment, IT suppliers and virtual currency providers. Phishing pages captured credentials and personal data, which the group used to enter company systems and identify individual cryptocurrency account holders.

The theft total in the sentencing reporting is more than 11.6 million dollars in virtual currency, up from the eight million cited at the time of the plea. The group defeated two-factor controls through SIM swaps, moving victims' phone numbers to devices it controlled. Articles name Coinbase, Twilio, Mailchimp and LastPass among the targets and connect the wider indictment to the MGM Resorts ransomware attack, estimated to have cost about 100 million dollars. Buchanan was arrested in Spain in 2024 and was the last of five defendants to be sentenced; co-defendant Noah Urban previously agreed to pay over 13.2 million dollars in restitution to 59 victims.

The case shows how a relatively simple SMS phishing and SIM-swap playbook produced large losses against well-resourced companies. Watch the restitution proceedings and any prosecution statements on the remaining Scattered Spider activity, since the sentence closes one case rather than the group's operations.
