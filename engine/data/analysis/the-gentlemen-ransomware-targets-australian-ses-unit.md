The Gentlemen ransomware group has hit an Australian State Emergency Service (SES) unit in Mandurah, with the local unit confirming operations were unaffected by the attack. This follows prior targeting of Australian students by the same group.

The incident is a reminder that ransomware operators continue to strike public-safety and education-adjacent organizations in Australia. Defenders should watch for follow-on data-leak claims and any expansion of the group's Australian targeting.
