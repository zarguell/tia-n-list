The FBI and Justice Department disrupted FaceStealer, a North Korean social engineering operation tied to more than $400 million in cryptocurrency theft over 18 months. The scheme lured victims into fake video calls with contacts they recognized, then pushed malicious software disguised as an audio fix, yielding account takeover, fund theft, and follow-on approaches to the victim's contacts. Authorities seized the cloud infrastructure behind the operation and more than 900 webcam videos of victims.

The operation also involved a compromise of a developer who maintains widely used software, with malicious code added to that project; the intrusion was detected and stopped before it spread. Separately, the Justice Department announced court authorization to seize or restrain more than $1.2 billion, which the FBI attributes to a separate theft of approximately $6 billion from Binance.

The case shows North Korean actors combining social engineering with supply chain reach. Defenders should verify software dependencies and treat unexpected requests to install "audio fixes" during video calls as a social engineering indicator; the Binance-related seizure indicates the same enforcement action touched a far larger theft.
