FIFA confirmed that hackers gained unauthorized access to a limited set of personal and business information during a recent cyberattack. The Switzerland-based governing body said it was working with relevant authorities and condemned the acquisition and publication of personal data. FIFA did not answer follow-up questions about the nature of the incident, and Associated Press reporting noted that the organization did not specify when the attack occurred or precisely what information was accessed.

The public timeline begins with extortion claims rather than a breach notice. OffSet7 recorded a Handsome Boy Hacking Club leak-site listing on November 20, 2026 with a countdown to 23:00 UTC, but explicitly marked the entry as the group's own unverified claim and did not host leaked data. Escudo Digital reported on November 21 that the group was demanding a ransom, while noting that it had published no screenshots, data categories or theft volume and that FIFA had not confirmed a breach. Later reporting described a screenshot posted by the group on X and a claim that more than 12 GB of internal documents had been leaked. The group allegedly claimed access to FIFA's Microsoft environment and described identity and immigration documents, financial records, legal documents and governance information among the stolen material. Those details remain actor claims, not FIFA-confirmed findings.

The incident is significant because a large claimed leak now sits alongside a much narrower official confirmation, and the alleged document categories would be sensitive if authentic. The important distinction for responders is between FIFA's confirmed access to limited personal and business information and the group's unverified 12 GB volume and file descriptions. Watch for FIFA or law enforcement to identify affected people, confirm the data categories and validate the published files. Organizations handling FIFA-related personal data should also prepare for targeted phishing that uses authentic-looking identity, immigration or business documents.
