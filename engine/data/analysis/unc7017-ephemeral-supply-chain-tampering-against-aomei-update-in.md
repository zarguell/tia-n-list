Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) and Mandiant, working with ManageEngine METAL, disclosed that threat cluster UNC7017 repeatedly tampered with AOMEI Backupper update infrastructure between April and July 2026. Attackers modified down_html.ini and Notifycfg.ini on AOMEI origin servers during brief five-to-ten-minute windows, redirecting clients to a malicious upgrade-now.zip delivering the CROSSROAD downloader and SHINYROAD backdoor before reverting files to authentic content.

Three confirmed tampering windows occurred on May 28, July 16, and July 20. Victims span IT services, telecom, and universities across 11 countries, including Spain and Switzerland. The supply-chain vector exploited a built-in promotional notification module rather than a code vulnerability, making detection harder. Organizations using AOMEI Backupper should verify installation integrity and check for indicators of compromise linked to the campaign.
