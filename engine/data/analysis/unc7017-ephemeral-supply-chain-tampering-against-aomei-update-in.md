Google Threat Intelligence Group (GTIG) and Mandiant, with Zoho ManageEngine's METAL lab, tracked UNC7017 conducting three short-burst tampering windows against AOMEI update infrastructure between May 28 and July 20, 2026. During each window of five to ten minutes the attacker modified down_html.ini and Notifycfg.ini on AOMEI's origin servers to redirect Backupper clients to a malicious upgrade-now.zip carrying the CROSSROAD downloader and SHINYROAD backdoor. The observed windows were May 28 05:40-05:50 UTC, July 16 08:33-08:39 UTC, and July 20 10:35-10:40 UTC, after which the files reverted to legitimate content.

The novelty is duration, not malware: GTIG describes this as the first Windows software supply chain compromise built on minutes-long tampering, "effectively evading traditional point-in-time triage and automated file scanning." Any client that polled during one of those windows became a victim regardless of installed version, because the injected 35,000-plus line [dummy_XXXXXX] bloat in Notifycfg.ini overrode client version checks and forced execution of upgrade-now.exe from the local html directory. Impacted organizations spanned IT services, telecom, higher education and related entities across twelve countries including the US, Australia, and Brazil, but victimology was opportunistic and likely broader than identified.

Defenders should inventory hosts where AOMEI Backupper or AnyViewer polled updates on May 28, July 16, or July 20 and inspect the Tmphtml and html directories for upgrade-now.exe plus scheduled task persistence for SHINYROAD. Hunt outbound traffic to promotion.storage-data.com, storage-data.com, aomei.storage-data.com, and the Google Cloud Storage path storage.googleapis.com/aomeiupdate/, plus Alibaba Cloud OSS staging from April-May at serv.oss-cn-hongkong.aliyuncs.com. AOMEI reported corrective action securing distribution servers; verify installed versions against your own integrity baseline since no affected version list was published.

Watch whether additional tampering windows from April through July 2026 surface in telemetry given GTIG's note that more likely occurred outside visibility, and whether IoCs or detection rules for CROSSROAD and SHINYROAD ships from Google or METAL's published analysis.
