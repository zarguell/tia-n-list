Apple released iOS and iPadOS 27.2.1 on November 9, 2026, fixing two bugs in CoreUtils, two in Messages, one in AirPlay, one in App Intents and three other vulnerabilities. The same release cycle shipped watchOS 27.2.1 with nine bugs fixed and visionOS 27.2.1 with nine bugs fixed.

Watch for Apple's full security advisories with CVE assignments and exploitability notes, and for any sign that the fixed bugs are under active exploitation. Users on supported devices should update to 27.2.1.
