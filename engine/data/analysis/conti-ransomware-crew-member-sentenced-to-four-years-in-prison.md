An Armenian man, Karen Serobovich Vardanyan, was sentenced to 24 months in prison for his role in Ryuk ransomware attacks that hit U.S. companies between March 2019 and June 2020. Court documents say he specialized in initial access, breached networks of multiple U.S. organizations, and helped deploy ransomware on hundreds of servers and workstations. Victims included a Michigan company that paid over 200 BTC (over $1.1 million at the time), a Texas school, and an Oregon technology firm. Prosecutors say the group received about 1,610 BTC valued over $15 million. The sentence reinforces that ransomware initial-access brokers face real legal risk, but the multi-year span between attacks and sentencing shows enforcement timelines remain slow.
