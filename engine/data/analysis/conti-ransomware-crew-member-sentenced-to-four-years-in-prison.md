Oleksii Oleksiyovych Lytvynenko, a 44-year-old Ukrainian national, was sentenced to four years in prison for his role in the Conti ransomware operation. He pleaded guilty in June to conspiracy to commit wire fraud, admitting he joined Conti in September 2021 as both an intruder and malware developer. He personally held data from 12 victims, eight of them in the United States, and continued ransomware operations even after Conti disbanded in 2022.

Conti attacked more than 1,000 organizations globally before its 2022 dissolution, targeting critical infrastructure and causing losses in the millions of dollars.
