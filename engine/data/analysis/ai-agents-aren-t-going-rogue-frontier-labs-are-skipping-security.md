A CSO Online article argues frontier labs are skipping basic security practices rather than AI agents going rogue. The piece highlights gaps in how frontier labs contain models, including inadequate access controls and insufficient isolation for high-capability systems. The claim aligns with broader industry concern that rapid capability development outpaces security investment.

This matters because model security failures can lead to unauthorized access, data exposure, or misuse of AI capabilities. Organizations relying on frontier AI services should verify vendor security practices and require transparent containment controls. Watch for follow-up disclosures or independent audits that confirm whether these labs have closed the identified gaps.
