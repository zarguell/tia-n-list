CrowdStrike said it agreed to acquire Zscaler in an all-stock deal valued at about $43.9 billion. The two vendors report more than 2,000 shared customers and 16 product integrations, and had announced in September a plan to let Falcon continuously assess user risk and feed that signal into Zscaler's access controls. CrowdStrike says it intends to merge the products onto one platform with a common data layer and a single customer contract after closing.

Zscaler had built out its own portfolio through purchases including SquareX in February 2026, SPLX in November 2025, Red Canary in August 2025, and Avalor, acquired for $350 million in 2024, plus Airgap Networks. The acquisition would join a large endpoint provider with a cloud-delivered zero-trust access vendor. Watch for closing conditions, integration plans for overlapping products, and any change to the Falcon/Zscaler risk-sharing integration.
