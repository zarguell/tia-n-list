The iRhythm breach affects more than 8 million people according to an HHS filing. The June 2026 incident involved sensitive data stolen from third-party-hosted business applications, including patient names, addresses, dates of birth, email addresses, phone numbers, and device/account data. Hackers attempted extortion by threatening to leak the data. iRhythm previously reported roughly 8.18 million affected individuals to the Oregon Attorney General, matching the HHS portal figure.

The company states it has no evidence that stolen information will be used for identity theft. Affected individuals received notification letters this month. The breach highlights the risk of third-party hosted applications in medical technology supply chains and the scale of exposure when wearable health device providers are targeted.
