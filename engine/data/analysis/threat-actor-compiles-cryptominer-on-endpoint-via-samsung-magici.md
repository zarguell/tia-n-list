Huntress documented an intrusion, detected early September 2026 on a Samsung MagicINFO Premium digital signage server, where the attacker used the year-old CVE-2025-4632 path traversal (CWE-22, CVSS 9.8, arbitrary file writes with SYSTEM authority, fixed in May 2025 in MagicINFO 9 Server 21.1052) and then did something unusual: instead of dropping a miner binary, they compiled one on the endpoint. Process evidence shows activity through tomcat9.exe, the Apache Tomcat service MagicINFO runs under. The attacker tried three times to fetch AnyDesk from 194.87.89[.]30, lost twice to Defender (certutil.exe, then PowerShell Invoke-WebRequest), succeeded on the third attempt, installed AnyDesk with a set password, created a local account named oldadministrator, and used SystemSettingsAdminFlows.exe to disable Defender. A second alert followed eight days after the first, tied to the same access route, with the Monero miner built via the SilentXMRMiner toolchain whose compile burst stands out in telemetry.

The chain's history explains why unpatched servers keep falling. An independent researcher via SSD Secure Disclosure disclosed the unauthenticated arbitrary file upload in the /MagicInfo/servlet/SWUpdateFileUploader servlet in early 2025 after Samsung missed the 90-day window, Huntress verified the public PoC still worked against 21.1050.0 and 21.1040.2, CISA added the CVE to KEV on May 22, 2025 with a June 12 federal deadline, and May 2025 incidents among Huntress customers saw actors deploy PHP service persistence (the PHP5.3.8 LocalSystem service) from 185.225.226[.]53 across more than 75 machines with MagicINFO installed.

The ongoing exploitation a full sixteen months after the May 2025 fix says internet-facing MagicINFO remains under continuous automated attack. Confirm version 21.1052 or later, take the server off the public internet, and hunt for the tells this case provides: unexpected compilers or build activity on signage hosts, AnyDesk fetches and installs, the oldadministrator account, SystemSettingsAdminFlows.exe Defender tampering, and Event IDs 7045 and 7000 for new services.
