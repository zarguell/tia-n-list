Revolut, the digital banking platform with over 40 million customers, is notifying customers of a significant data breach involving sensitive user data. The German technology news site Borncity reported that the bank is informing affected customers about the breach, which appears to involve the exposure of personal customer information to attackers. Details on the scope and method of the breach are still emerging.

The breach at Revolut follows a pattern of attacks targeting fintech companies, which hold vast amounts of customer financial data and personally identifiable information. Digital banks are attractive targets because they combine financial transaction capabilities with large customer databases. Revolut has experienced previous security incidents, including a 2022 social engineering attack that affected approximately 50,000 customers.

The notification to customers suggests the breach has been confirmed and classified under GDPR or similar data protection regulations, which require disclosure within 72 hours of discovery. The involvement of "sensitive user data" indicates the breach likely included personally identifiable information such as names, addresses, account details, or transaction histories.

Revolut customers should monitor their accounts for unauthorized activity, enable all available security features including transaction notifications, and be vigilant for phishing attempts that may leverage the disclosed breach information. The incident reinforces the importance of not relying solely on a single financial institution for all banking needs and maintaining awareness of data exposure through services like Have I Been Pwned.
