IDScan confirmed a breach of its IDScan.net cloud environment, exposing a database of 153 million driver's license scans. BleepingComputer reported that hackers gained access to the cloud infrastructure, with customer data confirmed compromised. The breach was disclosed after the company became aware of unauthorized access to its systems.

The scale of this breach is significant: 153 million driver's license records represent a substantial portion of the U.S. driving-age population. Driver's license data includes full names, addresses, dates of birth, license numbers, and in many states facial photographs, making it highly valuable for identity theft and synthetic identity fraud.

This incident follows a pattern of identity verification and document scanning services being targeted for their rich PII datasets. Organizations that use IDScan's services should assume their customer data was exposed and take appropriate notification and remediation steps. The breach also raises questions about data retention practices for biometric and identity document scans at scale.
