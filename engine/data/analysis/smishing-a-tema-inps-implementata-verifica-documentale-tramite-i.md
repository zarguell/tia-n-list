CERT-AGID reports a surge in smishing campaigns abusing the INPS name, logo, and branding in Italy. The new wave adds a layer of AI-based document verification to the files victims upload, meaning the campaign now checks submitted documents before accepting them. That is a step up from the usual static credential-harvesting pages.

INPS is the Italian social security agency, and phishing in its name is effective because victims expect official requests for identity documents and financial data. The AI verification layer suggests the operators are investing in quality control to filter out fake or low-value submissions, which points to a more organized operation rather than a spray-and-pray setup.

Watch for the scale of the campaign and whether it expands to other Italian government agencies using the same template. Organizations with users in Italy should warn staff that legitimate INPS requests do not arrive via SMS with document upload links.
