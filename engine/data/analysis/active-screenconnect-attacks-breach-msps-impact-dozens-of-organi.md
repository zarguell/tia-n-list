Huntress identified an active campaign starting around 17:47 UTC on November 11 that compromised six MSPs and 288 endpoints across 48 organizations through cloud-hosted ConnectWise ScreenConnect panels. Attackers abused ScreenConnect's Run Command functionality to deploy rogue clients and a self-replicating toolkit called SCKit that provides persistent backdoor access, intercepts technician session cookies, and can spread to other panels when a technician connects to an infected endpoint. All 288 hosts were hit within 90 minutes.

The campaign exploited a vulnerability in the third-party RMM+ extension, versions 1.0.31 and 1.0.32, where the access key defaults to blank if not configured, allowing authentication bypass. ConnectWise responded by disabling five third-party extensions across roughly 400 partner environments as a precaution.

MSPs should audit ScreenConnect panels and installed extensions, remove or upgrade RMM+ to a version with a configured access key, and inspect managed endpoints for rogue ScreenConnect clients and SCKit artifacts. Watch for ConnectWise's investigation results and any expansion of the victim count beyond the currently tracked 288 endpoints.
