On August 14, 2026, the Storm ransomware group claimed three victims in a 24-hour burst, posting on a data-leak site monitored by DeXpose. The targets span the US and Canada: Rood & Riddle Equine Hospital (roodandriddle.com), a US veterinary hospital; Integra Castings (thectdgroup.com), an ISO 9001:2015 certified Canadian iron foundry; and Tapper Cuddy LLP (tappercuddy.com), a Canadian law firm. Each post threatens to publish exfiltrated data unless the victim initiates contact.

The claims follow the group's standard playbook: named victims, a deadline-style leak warning, and no published sample yet. Tapper Cuddy's posting explicitly targets the legal sector's data-handling profile, while the hospital and foundry picks show Storm is not limiting itself to any one industry. No CVEs or initial-access vectors are named in any of the three postings, and no samples have been released as of writing.

What to watch: whether any of the three victims confirms the breach or negotiates, whether Storm follows through with leaks on its site, and whether the group adds more victims to this series. The pattern of same-day multi-victim claims matches Storm's recent cadence, so additional postings in the next several days would make this an active campaign to track rather than isolated claims.
