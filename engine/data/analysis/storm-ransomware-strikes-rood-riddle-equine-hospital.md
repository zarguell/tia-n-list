Storm ransomware has claimed five victims in four days across three countries, hitting Rood & Riddle Equine Hospital (USA veterinary), Tapper Cuddy LLP (Canada legal), Integra Castings (Canada iron foundry), Westco Motors Cairns (Australia automotive), and WindRose Health Network (USA healthcare). The group uses the same playbook on every target: post a leak notice, threaten data publication, and demand contact through provided channels. No CVEs or technical intrusion details have been disclosed.

The breadth is the story. Storm is operating across healthcare, legal, manufacturing, and automotive sectors in the US, Canada, and Australia simultaneously. This suggests either a broad initial access campaign (likely phishing or vulnerability exploitation at scale) or a affiliate model where multiple operators feed victims into the same leak site. The absence of technical details makes it hard to assess whether these are opportunistic or targeted, but the cadence (5 claims in 96 hours) points to an active push.

The healthcare targeting (WindRose Health Network) is the most concerning element. Healthcare ransomware carries patient safety implications and triggers HIPAA notification obligations. The Canadian law firm and iron foundry targets suggest Storm is not sector-specific. Monitor the leak site for actual data publication, which would confirm whether these are encryption-less extortion claims or full double-extortion operations.
