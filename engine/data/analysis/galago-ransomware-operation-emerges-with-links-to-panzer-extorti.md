A new ransomware operation tracked as Galago has emerged with apparent links to the Panzer extortion group. Researchers began monitoring Galago's dark leak site on 15 September 2026 and found it inactive with no listed victims. The shared pnzr prefix in Tor addresses (Galago: pnzr4del, Panzer: pnzruro) supports a naming link but is not independent proof of shared operators, malware, or access. Panzer has a documented record: 32 victims between 5 August and 23 September 2026, described as a ransomware-as-a-service operation recruiting affiliates for double extortion, with advertised builds for Windows, Linux, ESXi, and FreeBSD. Galago's self-claimed operational partnership with Panzer remains unverified by independent intrusion evidence. Watch for Galago victims appearing on its leak site or corroboration of shared infrastructure.
