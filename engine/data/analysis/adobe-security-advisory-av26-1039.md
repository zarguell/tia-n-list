Adobe's October 13 bulletins, aggregated by Canadian Centre advisory AV26-1039 on October 14, span Bridge 16.0.7, ColdFusion 2025 at 2025.0.13 and ColdFusion 2023 at 2023.0.25, Commerce and Magento, Experience Manager, FrameMaker, Photoshop 2026 at 27.10 and Photoshop 2025 at 26.11.7, plus Dimension and the Substance 3D trio. The sharp end is a six-CVE Experience Manager cluster: CVE-2026-48382 and CVE-2026-48383 are incorrect authorization flaws scored CVSS 10.0 that yield arbitrary code execution with no user interaction and changed scope, joined by stored XSS CVE-2026-106629 and CVE-2026-106630 plus reflected XSS CVE-2026-83662 at 9.3 and path traversal CVE-2026-106634 at 7.7. No active exploitation or public proof of concept was confirmed as of October 14, and none of the cluster is in CISA KEV.

The product count flatters the advisory but the risk concentrates hard. Photoshop, Bridge, Dimension and Substance are desktop tools patched on normal cycles, while Experience Manager, Commerce, Magento and ColdFusion are internet-facing revenue infrastructure, which is why this batch deserves priority over most Patch Tuesday noise. The detail the event list understates is how the flaws compose: the two 10.0 flaws need no credentials, and for targets where RCE is blocked at the edge, the stored XSS pair offers a second road into the same privileged author sessions, with the low-privilege file-write traversal then converting a foothold into persistence. A single AEM release handing out unauthenticated RCE, persistent session theft, and server-side file write is a full compromise kit, not three separate bugs.

Patch AEM first per bulletin APSB26-152, then ColdFusion past 2025.0.13 and 2023.0.25, then Commerce, Commerce B2B and Magento, which face the same unauthenticated internet pressure. Restrict which hosts can reach AEM author and admin interfaces, since the 10.0 flaws punish any exposed management surface. Hunt on the telemetry the reporting names: unexpected code execution on AEM hosts, anomalous content in form fields consistent with stored script injection, and unusual path traversal patterns against AEM endpoints.

Two developments change the assessment. A KEV listing, a vendor-confirmed exploitation report, or a public proof of concept for CVE-2026-48382 or CVE-2026-48383 would move this from patch-fast to assume-exposure and force log review back to October 13. Watch also for Adobe revising the bulletins with additional affected branches, since the October batch already touches ColdFusion, Commerce, and AEM at once and version coverage has shifted after publication before.
