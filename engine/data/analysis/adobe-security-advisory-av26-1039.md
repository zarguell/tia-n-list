Adobe's October patch batch is unusually broad. The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security advisory AV26-1039, dated October 14, lists vulnerable products as of October 13 spanning Adobe Bridge, ColdFusion 2025 and 2023, Commerce and Commerce B2B, the Credentials SDK, Dimension, Experience Manager, FrameMaker 2022 and 2026, Photoshop 2026 and 2025, the Substance 3D Designer, Sampler and Stager lineup, and Magento Open Source. That breadth means most Adobe shops will have at least one product to update.

The sharp end is Experience Manager. A follow-up report flags six AEM CVEs peaking at CVSS 10.0, with CVE-2026-48382 and CVE-2026-48383 described as incorrect authorization leading to remote code execution with no user interaction and changed scope, alongside CVE-2026-106629 and CVE-2026-106634. Unauthenticated RCE at maximum severity on an internet-facing content platform is the combination that draws exploit attempts fast.

Administrators should treat AEM and ColdFusion as patch-first given their exposure, then work through Commerce and Magento, which are equally attacker-facing. Desktop-only products like Photoshop, Bridge, Dimension and the Substance tools matter less urgently but should go out with normal patch cycles. Watch for Adobe's own bulletins confirming exploit status and for the Cyber Centre advisory to gain detail as the bulletins land.
