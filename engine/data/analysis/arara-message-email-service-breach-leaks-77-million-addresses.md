A breach at Japanese bulk email platform Arara Message, a subsidiary of PayCloud Holdings, exposed an estimated 77 million email addresses and triggered a phishing wave; the incident later expanded to a supply-chain compromise affecting SEGA, exposing roughly 81 million addresses. The story carries CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, and reporting frames the Arara compromise as the pivot point for downstream customer exposure.

The scale and the supply-chain angle are the substance: an email delivery platform holding tens of millions of addresses is a high-value target, and customers such as SEGA inherit the exposure without controlling the platform's security. The leaked address lists feed phishing and social engineering at scale, and the CVEs suggest a software flaw contributed to the compromise.

Watch items include patching and remediation for CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, notification obligations for Arara and affected customers, whether additional downstream brands surface, and any guidance on detecting phishing that leverages the leaked address sets.
