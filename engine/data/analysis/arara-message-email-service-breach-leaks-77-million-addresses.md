The Arara Message breach remains an email-address exposure at scale: 77 million addresses leaked from the Japanese service operated by arara inc., a PayCloud Holdings subsidiary, affecting roughly 380 client companies. The service was suspended after the incident, and reporting already notes a phishing wave built on the exposed addresses. A November 6 PerimeterSite assessment frames the count from the defender side, noting that a file of 77 million verified addresses is direct fuel for credential-harvesting and business email compromise lures.

For affected organizations the risk is inbound: lures referencing Arara or PayCloud branding aimed at staff and customers of the impacted companies. No new technical detail on intrusion method or additional data classes has surfaced since the suspension.

Watch for PayCloud or arara disclosure of root cause and scope confirmation, plus telemetry on phishing campaigns reusing the dataset.
