DreamHost confirmed a breach of its browser-based File Manager platform at files.dreamhost.com, which ran from approximately August 2026 until October 23, 2026, when the company discovered it. Attackers exploited a vulnerability in the File Manager software and stole customers' FTP/SFTP usernames and passwords. The intruder may also have accessed webmail systems, exposing session information including credentials for a limited number of users.

The attack appears deliberate, and DreamHost said it has blocked the attackers' access and addressed the vulnerability. The company found no evidence that the intruder reached control panel login information or customer billing and payment details, which are handled by an external provider. Affected customers were required to reset their email and FTP/SFTP passwords, and active sessions were stopped for potentially exposed accounts.

Customers who used the web-based File Manager between August and October should treat their FTP/SFTP credentials as compromised, rotate any reused passwords, and monitor mailboxes for unusual access.
