DreamHost confirmed that attackers exploited a vulnerability in its browser-based File Manager at files.dreamhost.com and maintained access from August 2026 until discovery on October 23, 2026, a two-month window. The intruder stole FTP/SFTP usernames and passwords of customers who logged into the File Manager during that period. DreamHost also says the intruder may have accessed webmail systems, exposing session information and credentials for a limited number of users, and possibly viewed the contents of some mailboxes.

The company found no evidence that the attackers reached control panel login information or customer billing and payment details, which an external provider handles. DreamHost blocked the attackers' access, addressed the exploited vulnerability, and stopped active sessions for potentially affected customers, who are being notified and asked to reset email and FTP passwords.

Watch for credential-stuffing and phishing campaigns built on the stolen FTP/SFTP credentials and webmail session data, and for any update on how many customers had mailbox contents viewed.
