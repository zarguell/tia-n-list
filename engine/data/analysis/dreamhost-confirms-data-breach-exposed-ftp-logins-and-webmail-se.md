DreamHost disclosed that an unauthorized third party accessed the server behind its web-based File Manager at files.dreamhost.com by exploiting a vulnerability in that software. The company discovered the intrusion on the afternoon of October 23, 2026 and said access may have existed from August 2026 through October 23.

The intruder obtained FTP/SFTP usernames and passwords of users who logged into File Manager in that window, and possibly webmail session information including credentials for a limited number of users. DreamHost said the affected group was well under about 10 percent of hosted email and FTP accounts and found no evidence of control panel or billing data exposure. During the outage DreamHost expired passwords for some email and FTP/SFTP users; affected users cannot sign in until the account owner sets a replacement password.

File Manager connectivity failed on October 23 and the service was restored October 26 after password resets. Watch for disclosure of affected account counts and any use of the stolen credentials.
