DreamHost suffered a breach of its web-based File Manager at files.dreamhost.com from approximately August 2026 to October 23, 2026, when it was discovered. An unauthorized third party exploited a vulnerability in the File Manager software to access FTP and SFTP usernames and passwords of users who logged in during that window, and may have accessed webmail systems, exposing session information including credentials for a limited number of users. DreamHost says it found no evidence of control panel login or billing and payment data exposure, since payments are handled by an external provider.

The company blocked the attackers' access, addressed the exploited vulnerability, and stopped active sessions for potentially affected accounts, requiring password resets. The two-month dwell time and the File Manager as the entry point underline the risk of web-based management tools holding reusable credentials.

Watch for DreamHost's follow-up on the vulnerability details and whether the same File Manager flaw affects other hosting providers, plus any credential-stuffing fallout from the exposed FTP and SFTP logins.
