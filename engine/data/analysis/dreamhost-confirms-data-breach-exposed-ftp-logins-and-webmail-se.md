DreamHost confirmed that an unauthorized third party exploited a vulnerability in its web-based File Manager at files.dreamhost.com and accessed FTP/SFTP usernames and passwords of customers who logged in between approximately August 2026 and October 23, 2026, when the intrusion was discovered. The attacker may also have reached webmail systems, exposing session information including credentials for a limited number of users. DreamHost says it found no evidence of control panel login or billing and payment data exposure, since billing is handled by an external provider, and the affected group is well under roughly 10 percent of hosted email and FTP/SFTP accounts.

The incident unfolded over several days before the breach was confirmed: File Manager connectivity problems began on the afternoon of October 23, 2026, followed by precautionary password resets for email and FTP/SFTP accounts, restoration of File Manager on October 26, and then the November 4 notification confirming the two-month access window. DreamHost has blocked the attacker's access, addressed the exploited vulnerability, and advised account owners to check mailboxes for unfamiliar forwarding rules.

Customers who used File Manager during the window should treat FTP/SFTP credentials as compromised and rotate them, along with any reused passwords, and review webmail forwarding rules and sessions. Watch for follow-on abuse of the stolen credentials against hosted sites and email accounts, and for whether DreamHost discloses the specific vulnerability exploited in the File Manager software.
