A Texas judge ruled that TikTok misled users about its child safety features, according to Reuters. The ruling addresses claims that the platform's age-gating and content restriction mechanisms did not function as advertised, particularly for minor users.

The decision adds to a growing body of litigation against TikTok over its handling of minor safety. Texas has been among the most aggressive states in pursuing legal action against the platform, and this ruling establishes a factual finding that could support further enforcement or damages claims. The case centers on deceptive practices rather than data privacy, focusing on whether TikTok's public representations about child protections matched the actual product behavior.

This ruling is significant for the broader social media regulatory landscape. If upheld on appeal, it could set precedent for holding platforms accountable for the gap between marketed safety features and actual implementation, particularly regarding minor users.
