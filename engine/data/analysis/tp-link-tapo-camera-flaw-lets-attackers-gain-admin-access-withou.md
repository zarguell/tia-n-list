OPSWAT Unit 515 researchers Khoi Tran and Thai Do discovered two vulnerabilities in TP-Link's Tapo C200 and C120 smart cameras: CVE-2026-15315 and CVE-2026-15316. The flaws enable nearby network attackers to bypass administrator authentication or disrupt the device's management service. TP-Link has released patches for both vulnerabilities.

The attack requires network proximity to the camera, making it relevant for both consumer and enterprise environments where IoT devices sit on shared networks. An attacker on the same network segment could gain admin access without credentials, potentially accessing video feeds or pivoting to other devices.

What to watch: IoT camera vulnerabilities continue to attract researcher attention. The OPWAT discovery came through a Critical Infrastructure Cybersecurity Graduate Fellowship, highlighting academic investment in IoT security research. Organizations with TP-Link camera deployments should verify firmware versions and network segmentation.
