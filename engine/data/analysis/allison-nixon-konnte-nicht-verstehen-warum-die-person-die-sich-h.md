Allison Nixon, research director at Unit 221B, was targeted by members of "The Com," a youth-driven cybercrime community linked to cryptocurrency theft, data theft, extortion, and physical violence. Beginning in spring 2024, individuals operating under the pseudonyms "Waifu" and "Judische" posted death threats against Nixon on Telegram and Discord, including threats of garrote execution and beheading. AI-generated nude images of Nixon were also circulated.

The threats escalated because Nixon's undercover work had become a direct threat to The Com's operations. She uses scraping tools to systematically search internet forums and leveraged 50 billion AT&T call records in investigations. FBI Special Agent Ryan Brogan stated that Nixon's ability to unmask these individuals is unparalleled. The criminal groups have increasingly adopted counterintelligence tactics against researchers, including "bricking" (throwing bricks through victims' windows) and swatting.

The story, originally reported by Kim Zetter for MIT Technology Review and covered by t3n.de, illustrates the growing physical danger facing cybersecurity researchers who investigate cybercrime communities. The Com's subgroups, including "764," have been linked to animal cruelty, knife attacks, and school shootings in addition to cybercrime.
