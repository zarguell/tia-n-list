Reuters, citing three people familiar with the matter, reported that hackers breached the US Air Force's Cloud One platform. The intrusion was discovered within roughly the past month during work to upgrade the system, and the report says tactical, personnel and logistics data could be at risk. The Independent repeated the core claim, but the supplied coverage does not identify an actor, an initial-access method or confirmed data theft.

The potential scope makes this more than a routine cloud incident: Cloud One is described as the Air Force's flagship computing platform. Watch for an official Air Force statement, the discovery and containment timeline, affected workloads and data categories, and any evidence of exfiltration. Until those details emerge, the data exposure should be treated as a risk identified by reporting, not a confirmed loss.
