Dragos researchers sent nearly two million prompts carrying unique URLs, DNS names and canary AWS credentials through AI routers and inference marketplaces between July and November 2026. HD Moore's report says three hosting providers were compromised and learned of it only when the researchers told them. Resellers read customer prompts through ordinary request logging, and some requests marked Zero Data Retention were still fetched or forwarded to Chinese labs. Ars Technica covered the findings as a leaky AI supply chain exposing secret user data, framing the failure at the routing layer rather than in any single model.

Seeded credentials were used, not merely observed. Attackers made tens of thousands of AWS API calls that included S3 listing, Secrets Manager and Parameter Store lookups, Bedrock model enablement and IAM user creation. A separate honeypot run by Lauren Proehl from 7 July to 7 November planted more than 500 canary AWS keys; 155 were used from 12,353 distinct IPs, 72 percent went to SES or SNS send-quota checks and 36 percent touched Bedrock. The median theft-to-use interval was 37 minutes, and one checker graded stolen AI keys across five providers from 20 residential connections in 14 countries.

OpenRouter, which routes customer requests to inference hosts, removed three providers, dropped 125 endpoints from Zero Data Retention eligibility and notified affected customers. The Hacker News and other outlets reported the same remediation on 22 November. That is the concrete response so far, and it treats prompt handling at the gateway as the controllable failure point.

Watch whether other routers publish provider audits or revise their ZDR claims, and whether the three unnamed hosts confirm the compromise timelines Dragos reported. The canary design measures theft and misuse of seeded secrets, so it does not produce a count of real customer prompts that were read. That gap is the open question for affected users, and it is why the ZDR endpoint removals matter more than the initial theft numbers.
