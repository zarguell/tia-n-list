Enea identified SilentAir, a previously unknown threat actor that intercepts phone calls and SMS messages through compromised mobile operator infrastructure, with Taiwan as its main target. Enea assesses with high confidence that SilentAir is a state-level actor and with low confidence that it aligns with China-nexus operations, citing interest in Taiwan, the absence of Chinese targets, and attack timing matching a Chinese workday. The group has been monitored since early 2025, with suspicious activity before that.

SilentAir runs signaling attacks, mostly over SS7 and sometimes over Diameter, from network nodes it has hijacked at operators in at least six Asian countries. The attacks route a target's incoming calls and SMS through the compromised node and harvest subscriber details; location tracking is a minor component. Silent SMS messages that do not appear on the recipient's phone are used, possibly to verify earlier interception attempts. Besides Taiwan, about a dozen other countries and territories have been targeted, including East Africa.

The practical exposure is to anyone whose mobile operator has a compromised node, particularly high-value targets in Taiwan. Mitigation rests with operators hardening SS7/Diameter signaling and subscribers using app-based authenticators rather than SMS for sensitive accounts, since SilentAir's interception can capture SMS-based one-time codes.
