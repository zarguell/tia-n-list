Enea tracks a previously unknown state-level actor, SilentAir, running signaling attacks from network nodes it has hijacked inside mobile operators in at least six Asian countries. The interception uses SS7 and, less often, Diameter: incoming calls and SMS for selected targets are routed through the compromised node and subscriber details are harvested. Location tracking occurs but is a small part of the activity. Silent SMS messages, invisible to the recipient, appear to be used to check whether earlier interception worked. Taiwan is the primary target, with a dozen other countries and territories affected and some targeting in East Africa.

Enea has monitored the activity since early 2025 and found suspicious traces from before that. Attacks arrived in waves ranging from a handful to several thousand subscribers in a month, and some targets had SMS two-factor codes intercepted. Silent SMS traffic skewed toward midnight in Taiwan. Enea assesses with high confidence that SilentAir is a state-level actor and with low confidence that it aligns with China-nexus operations, citing the Taiwan focus, the absence of Chinese targets and timing consistent with a Chinese workday.

The same report tracks LIMINAL PANDA through 2026 and notes overlapping activity at some of the same operators, though Enea could not confirm a link. CrowdStrike disclosed LIMINAL PANDA in November 2024 as a China-nexus actor targeting telecom providers since at least 2020, using compromised telecom servers, GSM protocol emulation for command and control, and access to subscriber data, call metadata and SMS. Watch for further operator-level reporting and for any evidence tying the two clusters together.
