Microsoft Edge 156 now automatically turns off website notifications for sites flagged by Microsoft Defender SmartScreen and the Scareware blocker, cutting off a delivery channel used by fake antivirus warnings and deceptive security alerts. The behavior arrived with Edge 156 in October and was detailed by Microsoft in November, tying the notification permission to the site that triggered the detection. Reports from GBHackers and secquill describe the change as automatic, requiring no user action to silence the notification requests.

Notification pop-ups are a common route for scareware campaigns that push users toward bogus support numbers or malicious installers, so disabling them at browser level removes a low-cost social engineering channel. Watch for whether other browsers copy the behavior and whether attackers shift to other permission-based tricks or direct-site lures instead.
