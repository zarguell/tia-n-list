On August 10 the ransomware group Unsafe claimed responsibility for an attack on Presentations.AI, an Indian presentation software company. The group's statement highlighted the company's revenue and implied further data exposure unless its demands are met. Presentations.AI has not publicly confirmed or denied the claim.

The revenue quote is a pressure tactic common to ransomware extortion: it signals that the group holds data it considers valuable and will leak or sell it if the victim does not pay. Claims of this kind are frequently exaggerated, but they should be treated as credible until disproven.

The reporting firm recommends continuous dark web and infostealer monitoring, a full compromise assessment to determine how the attackers got in and whether persistence remains, and validated, encrypted, isolated backups. These are the standard playbook for any suspected ransomware intrusion and apply regardless of whether the claim is accurate.
