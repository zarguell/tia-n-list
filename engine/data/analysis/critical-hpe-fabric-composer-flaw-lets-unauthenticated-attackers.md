HPE Networking Fabric Composer versions 7.3.3 and earlier contain two maximum-severity vulnerabilities, both rated CVSS 10.0. CVE-2026-76658 targets the product's SSH daemon and allows an unauthenticated remote attacker to execute arbitrary commands as a privileged operating-system user. CVE-2026-76657 bypasses API authentication entirely, granting administrative privileges without credentials. The advisory HPESBNW05133 covers52 total vulnerabilities in the product, but these two are the urgent ones.

The attack surface is straightforward: both flaws are network-reachable, require no authentication, and have low complexity. Successful exploitation of CVE-2026-76658 results in complete compromise of the Fabric Composer management host, which has privileged visibility and control over data-center network fabrics. That makes it a stepping stone into broader network infrastructure.

At disclosure, HPE said it was not aware of public exploit code or active targeting. However, the combination of pre-auth RCE at CVSS 10.0 on a network management platform means exploitation is a matter of when, not if. Administrators running Fabric Composer should treat this as an emergency patch cycle. The SSH daemon flaw in particular is the kind of vulnerability that gets folded into botnets and mass-scanning campaigns within days of public exploit availability.
