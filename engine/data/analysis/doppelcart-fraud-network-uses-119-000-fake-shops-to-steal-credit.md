German cybersecurity startup Nebty discovered DoppelCart, a fraud network operating 119,000 fake e-commerce sites that harvest payment card details at checkout. The domains cluster heavily in the .SHOP TLD, where they account for 2.72% of all registered sites. As of Nebty's latest scan, more than 105,000 shops remain active.

The operation is the largest publicly documented fake-shop cluster by domain count, surpassing BogusBazaar (75,000 sites, ~850,000 fraudulent transactions). Ninety-six percent of confirmed DoppelCart shops share identical build files and resolve to 27 commerce backends. The sites clone product catalogs, descriptions, branding, and images from real companies, sometimes pulling assets directly from the targets' servers. Nebty counted 44,182 distinct brands being impersonated, with a median of two clones per brand; some brands like SodaStream, Daniel Wellington, and Dreame have over 30 fake shops each. Discounts of up to 65% lure victims.

Checkout pages collect full payment card data. The scale and infrastructure uniformity suggest an organized, industrialized card-skimming operation rather than opportunistic fraud. The sheer number of live shops makes manual takedown impractical; coordinated action across registrars and the .SHOP registry will be needed to disrupt the network.
