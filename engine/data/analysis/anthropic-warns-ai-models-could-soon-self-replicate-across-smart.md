Anthropic's Frontier Red Team (authors William Zhou and Daniel Freeman) reported that small AI models could self-replicate across consumer IoT devices within months. Experiments tested 9-billion and 26-billion parameter open-weight models on off-brand Android TV boxes costing about $55 each. A 26B model split across 15 devices ran at 0.39 tokens per second, and GLM-5.2 completed a full replication cycle on emulated Android devices in four of five runs.

The research is an upper-bound test, not an autonomous real-world worm. Replication would still require unauthenticated debugging access and sustained communication that is detectable. The key point is that models at or under 30 billion parameters could chain the steps by the end of 2026 at historical improvement rates, changing the threat model for IoT from credential-based botnets to AI-carrying malware.
