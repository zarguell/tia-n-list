The FBI arrested a third suspect in connection with the ShinyHunters breach of FBI systems that exposed data on agents and job applicants. The group had previously defaced the FBI jobs site and claimed possession of very sensitive data on almost all FBI agents and applicants. 404 Media initially broke the story, followed by NYT and TechCrunch reports.

The arrest is part of a broader disruption of the ShinyHunters network. Backfill events reference the group's open feud with rival ransomware operations and its spectacle-seeking posture before targeting the FBI. The breach included sample data containing names, addresses, phone numbers, and spouse details.

This is verified law-enforcement and media reporting with multiple independent sources (NYT, 404 Media, TechCrunch, SecurityAffairs). No CVEs apply; the incident is a data breach and criminal investigation rather than a software vulnerability.
