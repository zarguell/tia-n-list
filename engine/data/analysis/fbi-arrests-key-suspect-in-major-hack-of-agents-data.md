A suspect linked to the ShinyHunters hack of FBI agent data was arrested. The breach, announced in September 2026, targeted the FBI jobs portal (fbijobs.gov) and exposed sample records matching names, addresses, Social Security numbers, and duty assignments; Reuters verified at least 10 matches including Director Kash Patel. The arrest follows an intelligence failure that allowed a criminal group to steal troves of internal agent data. Monitor for charges and any disclosure of the full dataset scope.
