Railway, a PaaS with roughly 4.8 million users and 16 million hosted services, disclosed an intrusion into systems storing customer environment variables. The company says an unauthorized third party may have accessed encrypted variables and, in some cases, obtained decrypted contents. Railway stated the incident was part of a broader series of attacks targeting AI inference providers, and it urged all users to rotate secrets including API keys, database credentials, and registry tokens.

The exposure is significant because environment variables in platform-as-a-service deployments frequently contain credentials for downstream systems rather than isolated app configuration. Railway advised all users to rotate variables regardless of whether their account was in the confirmed-affected subset. There is no CVE for the intrusion mechanism itself, but the incident demonstrates how PaaS infrastructure storing deployment secrets becomes a high-value target when attackers focus on AI provider supply chains.
