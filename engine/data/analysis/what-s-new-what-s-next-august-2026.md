CISA's Continuous Diagnostics and Mitigation (CDM) program is being pushed to move faster, according to Richard Grabowski, acting branch chief of service delivery and deputy program manager for CDM, speaking at an Elastic Federal Cyber Defense Breakfast. Grabowski framed velocity as one of three core goals for the program, alongside unification of data across deployments and data-driven risk management, and argued that past collaboration speeds are inadequate for current and future threats. He emphasized automating tasks at scale so security experts can focus on novel threats and tuning advanced technology rather than routine alert triage.

The program's unification goal centers on breaking down data silos so deployments connect meaningfully and produce reusable, actionable lessons learned. Data-driven risk management is intended to give agencies timely, accurate, and trustworthy data during crisis-level events so CDM serves as a tool of first response.
