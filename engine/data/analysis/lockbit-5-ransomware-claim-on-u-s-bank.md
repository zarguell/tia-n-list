LockBit 5 has claimed responsibility for a cyberattack against U.S. Bank, a major financial institution, and threatened to release stolen data unless negotiations begin by September 3. The ransomware group issued a warning stating that "the full leak will be published soon, unless a company representative contacts us via the channels provided." U.S. Bank is investigating the claims but has not confirmed a compromise.

Lee Henderson, U.S. Bank's vice president of public affairs, said the organization is reviewing the situation and monitoring for potential exposure. "At this time, there is no indication that our internal systems are impacted and no evidence of unauthorized access to our network," Henderson stated. The bank did not disclose whether it had engaged with LockBit or received a ransom demand.

This incident represents a significant escalation in LockBit's targeting of major financial institutions. The September 3 deadline creates urgency for the bank to respond. Organizations in the financial sector should monitor for potential data exposure and review their incident response procedures. The claim highlights the persistent threat posed by LockBit 5 despite previous law enforcement disruptions.
