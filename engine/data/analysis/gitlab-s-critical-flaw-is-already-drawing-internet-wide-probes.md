GitLab's CVE-2026-85706 (CVSS 10.0) continues to draw internet-wide scanning and exploitation activity ten days after disclosure. The path traversal vulnerability allows unauthenticated attackers to read any file on self-managed GitLab servers without login, and was exploited within hours of the initial patch. Censys data shows 86,000+ GitLab instances exposed on the internet. The story has accumulated coverage from 22 sources including BleepingComputer, The Hacker News, Rapid7, and Cyberscoop, making it one of the most widely reported vulnerabilities of September 2026. The latest update from diesec.com confirms ongoing exploitation. GitLab patched the flaw in self-managed versions, but the scale of exposed instances and the trivial exploitation path mean organizations that haven't patched remain at high risk. The vulnerability also affects CVE-2026-87719, a related flaw in the same disclosure. Any organization running self-managed GitLab should verify patching status immediately; the CVSS 10.0 score reflects the combination of no authentication required, full file read capability, and widespread deployment.
