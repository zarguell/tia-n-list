Advantest, a Japanese chip-testing equipment manufacturer, is notifying individuals that attackers stole personally identifiable information during the ransomware incident first disclosed in February 2026. The stolen data includes names, dates of birth, contact details, Social Security numbers, passport and driver license numbers, and medical and financial information. The company has filed breach notices with U.S. state attorneys general: more than 500 California residents, 14 in Massachusetts, and 8 in Vermont are affected. Advantest states there is no evidence the data has been publicly disclosed or misused, but affected individuals face increased identity-theft risk. No ransomware group has claimed responsibility. The disclosure confirms that the February intrusion included data exfiltration, not just encryption.
