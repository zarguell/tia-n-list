D-Link disclosed multiple unpatched zero-day flaws in DGS-3130 series managed switches in a November 6 advisory (SAP10523), tested on DGS-3130-30TS rev B1 hardware. The researcher, Giresh Kumar Enugu of Spectrum Community Solutions, found the issues in the D-Link Discovery Protocol (DDP) covering authentication, access control and information handling, plus components of the device firmware security architecture. D-Link has not published CVE IDs, patches, or a fix timeline.

Successful exploitation could expose sensitive device data, allow unauthorized credential or configuration changes, leak SNMP communities, or cause service disruptions and device resets. D-Link said it is still investigating the impact on other D-Link switching platforms and firmware security mechanisms, including SSH service configuration, operating-system credential safeguards, and firmware encryption keys.

Until patches appear, exposed DGS-3130 switches should be treated as carrying unpatched, exploitable weaknesses with no vendor mitigation yet published. Watch for CVE assignments and firmware updates on the D-Link support advisory page.
