AhnLab ASEC's October 2026 APT trends report, also carried by malware.news on November 17, describes state-sponsored actors moving beyond their usual spear-phishing focus. The reported targets included development environments, cloud services, virtual asset infrastructure and network equipment, with legitimate platforms such as GitHub, GitLab, OneDrive, Terraform and Visual Studio appearing in the attack activity described in the summary.

The shift matters because the listed platforms sit close to source code, build configuration and cloud administration, where a compromise can reach more systems than a single mailbox. The available reporting is a high-level monthly trend summary and does not name specific groups, incidents or indicators. Watch for the underlying ASEC report to confirm which actor sets drove each category and whether the platform references mean credential abuse, malicious repository content or infrastructure hosted on those services.
