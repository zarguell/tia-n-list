Google Threat Intelligence Group is describing a threshold in intent, not a new CVE: its forecast puts multi-agent attacks needing little or no human interaction within three months and sophisticated fully autonomous agentic attacks within six months. The observed inputs are named frameworks, not a product release. GTIG saw CAIRN used against the US aerospace sector and suspected North Korean operators using Claude Code to deliver malware, and it names ARTEX, CAIRN, CyberStrikeAI and HexStrike AI as open-source pentesting suites being run as intrusion tooling. The FBI's FLASH-20261113-001 says ARTEX has been weaponized since at least August 2026, with some intrusions moving from initial targeting to privileged access in under three hours.

The forecast understates what is already measurable. Google's own October 30 report records 27 events in 23 seconds during AWS permission probing and ARTEX execution bursts of roughly 2 to 25 seconds, while Censys counted 1,465 exposed ARTEX consoles on November 4 and the FBI cited more than 1,600 by early November. That puts this story above a routine vendor warning: the exposed population is thousands of operator consoles and peripheral portals, not one vulnerable version, and the ConnectWise case shows the blast radius when an agent pivots through trusted application identities into more than 2,250 downstream tenants. The same automation that compresses the intrusion also makes it forensically loud, as ARTEX rewrote its own failed scripts during observed operations, so static IOC blocking is the wrong primary response.

There is no patch to deploy against a framework in misuse, so defensive work is exposure and identity control: inventory partner and supplier portals, mobile backends, employee apps and legacy services; restrict Kudu/SCM and cloud administration endpoints to trusted networks; apply least privilege to application consent grants and review Entra audit records for credential additions. Hunt for the behavior the sources actually describe, including 2 to 25 second bursts of sub-second permission checks, sequential portal lookups, unexpected egress to api.deepseek.com and other LLM API endpoints, MySQL general log writes to web-accessible paths, `.jsp` or `.aspx` web shells in odd application directories, and `artex-probe-*` filenames. For ConnectWise-related activity the FBI says to search tenant records by application ID back to October 1, 2026, and to preserve Entra sign-in, Purview and Microsoft Graph telemetry before containment.

The assessment changes if fully autonomous intrusions appear with no human tasking, which is the six-month claim rather than the observed state. The nearer signal is whether ARTEX, CAIRN, HexStrike AI and CyberStrikeAI keep surfacing in incident response cases after the original ARTEX repository came down on October 8, and whether the noisy burst pattern gives way to quieter agent execution that defeats behavioral detection.
