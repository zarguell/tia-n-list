Google Threat Intelligence Group says agentic attack activity has crossed a significant threshold. Its report documents large-scale adoption of open-source offensive frameworks including ARTEX, CAIRN, CyberStrikeAI and HexStrike AI by both financially motivated and state-sponsored actors. CAIRN was used against the US aerospace sector and, in follow-on autonomous attacks, against advanced manufacturing, defense, and civil and human rights organizations, and suspected North Korean operators used Claude Code to deliver malware. GTIG expects attacks needing little or no human interaction within three months and sophisticated fully autonomous agentic attacks within six months.

One observed system, named exploit_agent on an exposed staging server, ran differential analysis of pre- and post-patch code, identified the likely vulnerability, drafted and executed an exploit automatically, and held exfiltrated data alongside scripts for reconnaissance, brute-forcing, webshell installation and removal of rival webshells. Victim telemetry pointed to French media, entertainment and education sites. A separate FBI FLASH from November 13 says ARTEX has been weaponized since at least August across finance, government, technology, defense and healthcare, with intrusions reaching privileged access in under three hours, theft of data on roughly 67,000 people in South Korea, and access to more than 2,250 downstream ConnectWise Cloud Backup tenants.

CrowdStrike's related case involved South Korean financial firms in late September and early October, with open directories holding Claude Code session histories and ARTEX configuration files, an ARTEX host at 38.244.50[.]120 using DeepSeek v4.1-flash via reseller xcai[.]pro, and WISP nodes at 199.19.95[.]66 through 199.19.95[.]68. BleepingComputer counted more than 1,600 publicly exposed ARTEX consoles by early November even though the original GitHub repository was removed on October 8. Watch for further exploitation of internet-exposed low-complexity flaws, exposed agent consoles and forks, compressed bursts of iterative activity, and any public confirmation of the fully autonomous attacks GTIG forecasts.
