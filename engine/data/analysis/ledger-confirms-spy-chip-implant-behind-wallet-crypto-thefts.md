Ledger confirmed that hardware wallets distributed through Southeast Asian reseller CryptoBilis contained an unauthorized implant that drained cryptocurrency. The company launched an investigation after user reports of losses and asked CryptoBilis to stop selling Ledger devices. By October 10, Ledger verified hardware tampering on at least one device, though the exact mechanism and timing remain unclear. Some researchers had shown the implant in Ledger X devices since mid-September, with tampered units sold at roughly half the original price. Impacted users are being contacted directly.

This is a supply-chain compromise with direct financial impact. Users who purchased Ledger devices from CryptoBilis should avoid initializing new wallets until the device is verified clean. Ledger advises contacting support and working with authorities, but the immediate defense is to treat any device from this reseller as potentially compromised until confirmed otherwise.
