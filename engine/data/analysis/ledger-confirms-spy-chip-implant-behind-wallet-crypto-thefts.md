Ledger confirmed an unauthorized hardware implant in at least one Ledger device sold through Southeast Asian reseller CryptoBilis, draining cryptocurrency wallets. The implant was found after reports of user losses; Ledger launched an investigation on October 9 and confirmed tampering by October 10. Researchers showed altered Ledger X devices sold at roughly half price since September 16. Ledger recommends buyers from CryptoBilis not set up unconfigured devices and advises contacting support. Watch for expanded victim reports and law-enforcement attribution, since Ledger is working with authorities but has not identified the perpetrator or provided a loss figure.
