Ledger confirmed that all confirmed fund losses linked to the CryptoBilis reseller incident involved signers sold through that channel with an embedded LTE implant. The implant intercepts the recovery phrase during setup and exfiltrates it wirelessly. The confirmation came on October 13, reinforcing that the attack was limited to that reseller's supply chain rather than a broader Ledger manufacturing compromise.
Why it matters: the distinction between a reseller-level supply-chain breach and a manufacturer-level breach affects which users must rotate keys. Watch for Ledger's final incident report and any detection of additional affected resellers.
