Ledger confirmed a hardware implant in devices sold through Malaysian reseller CryptoBilis that drained cryptocurrency wallets. The company launched an investigation after reports of user losses, then confirmed unauthorized hardware tampering on at least one device. Impacted devices were sold at steep discounts. Ledger advised buyers from this reseller to stop setup and is working with authorities. The implant targets the private keys that control wallet assets, making it a supply-chain hardware compromise rather than a software vulnerability. Watch for updates on how many devices were compromised, the attack mechanism, and whether other resellers are involved.
