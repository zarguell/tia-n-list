Ledger confirmed that a device sold through CryptoBilis, a Malaysia-based reseller operating across Southeast Asia, contained an unauthorized hardware implant that drained cryptocurrency from user wallets. The company opened an investigation on October 9 after reports of losses and asked the reseller to stop selling and shipping Ledger devices. On October 10 it confirmed hardware tampering on at least one impacted device. Researchers had shown the implant in Ledger X units since September 16, with tampered devices offered at nearly half the original price.

Ledger says it is reaching out to impacted users and working with authorities. It has not published a loss total, a cause, or a precise point in the supply chain where devices were altered. Its advice to buyers from CryptoBilis was not to initialize devices they had not yet set up.

Watch for additional confirmed devices, a loss figure, and whether other resellers or models appear. The concrete artifact remains the unauthorized implant confirmed in a CryptoBilis-supplied device.
