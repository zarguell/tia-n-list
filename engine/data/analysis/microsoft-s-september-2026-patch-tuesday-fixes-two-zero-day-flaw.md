Microsoft's September 2026 Patch Tuesday addressed two zero-day vulnerabilities confirmed exploited in the wild: CVE-2026-81963 and CVE-2026-85880. Both have been added to CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. The release covered vulnerabilities across the full Microsoft product stack, with multiple sources including Microsoft's own MSRC, Qualys, SecurityOnline, BleepingComputer, and The Verge reporting on the update.

The two KEV-listed zero-days are the priority. CISA's KEV listing means federal agencies under BOD 26-04 have binding remediation deadlines, and the in-the-wild confirmation means exploit code is in active use. Beyond the zero-days, the broader patch batch addresses additional vulnerabilities across Windows, Office, Exchange, and other Microsoft products, though details on all CVEs are still being assimilated by the research community.

Organizations should prioritize the two zero-day patches immediately, then work through the remaining updates. The breadth of this release, combined with confirmed exploitation of the two zero-days, makes this Patch Tuesday one that demands expedited treatment rather than the standard monthly rollout.
