Cleverbridge says it detected unauthorized activity on 4 November 2026, took systems offline to contain it, and brought them back the same day. Its investigation later found that a third party copied payment card data stored on the platform. The company began notifying affected customers on 20 November. Cleverbridge runs e-commerce and subscription billing for software vendors including Red Hat, SUSE, Tenable, Veeam, Jamf, Sony and Parallels, but has not said which of those vendors' customers are in scope.

The public disclosure does not itemize the stolen fields; a notification shared with BleepingComputer lists payment card numbers, expiration dates and CVV/CVC codes. That combination is sufficient for card-not-present transactions, so affected buyers should watch statements rather than rely on account monitoring alone. Watch for Cleverbridge to name the affected vendors, the number of records and how the platform was accessed.
