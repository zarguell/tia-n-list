Cleverbridge detected unauthorized activity on 4 November 2026 and took systems offline to contain it, bringing them back the same day. The company later determined that a third party copied payment card data stored on its platform and began notifying affected customers on 20 November. A notification shared with BleepingComputer says the stolen fields include full payment card numbers, expiration dates and CVV/CVC codes, although Cleverbridge's public incident page does not enumerate the fields and points affected customers to individual notices.

Cleverbridge sells e-commerce, payment processing and subscription management services to software and SaaS companies and lists Red Hat, SUSE, Tenable, Veeam, Jamf, Sony and Parallels among its customers. It has not said which businesses' customers were affected, how many cards were copied, or how the intruder reached the card-data store. Watch for customer-specific disclosures, card issuer fraud reporting and any later timeline update that places the initial intrusion earlier than the 4 November detection.
