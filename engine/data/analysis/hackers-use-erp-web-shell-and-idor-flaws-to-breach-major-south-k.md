Attackers breached two major South Korean churches (Yoido Full Gospel Church, Sarang Church) through ERP web shells, privileged database access, leaked credentials, and broken authorization controls. The incidents mix suspected AI-assisted techniques with classic IDOR and web-shell intrusion. Recovered attacker artifacts show collection of congregant, employee, financial, and administrative data. No CVEs cited; the breaches demonstrate compound vulnerability in church IT infrastructure rather than a single product flaw.
