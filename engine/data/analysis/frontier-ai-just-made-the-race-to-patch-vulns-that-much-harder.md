Frontier AI is compressing the vulnerability discovery cycle faster than vendors can ship fixes. An expert-perspective piece from Security.com argues the industry is entering an era of effectively endless zero days, where AI-assisted research and exploitation outpace the traditional patch cadence most organizations still depend on.

The stakes are operational, not theoretical. If AI can find and weaponize flaws faster than vendors triage and ship, the gap between disclosure and exploitation shrinks toward zero and patching becomes a race the defender loses by default. Organizations that cannot automate detection and response, or at least prioritize exposure aggressively, will carry the risk.

Watch for early evidence of the claim: shorter time-to-exploit statistics, AI-discovered vulnerabilities landing in public trackers, and any acceleration in CISA KEV additions. Patching speed and asset exposure visibility are the two levers that still matter.
