The British government's Department for Work and Pensions took some online services offline as a precaution after a cyber incident, without disclosing its nature. The department said regular benefit and pension payments were unaffected and would be paid as normal, and that it was working with the National Cyber Security Centre and Government Cyber Coordination Centre to investigate.

DWP administers the state pension and other benefits relied on by millions of people for essential bills, so disruption to its online services has broad public-service significance even without confirmed data loss. Watch for restoration updates, any change to payment guidance, and attribution. The record does not connect this incident to the NCSC's separate warning about a rise in AI-assisted attacks.
