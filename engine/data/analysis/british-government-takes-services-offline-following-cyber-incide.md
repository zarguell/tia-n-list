The UK's Department for Work and Pensions took several online services offline on November 27 after what it described as a cyber incident. Universal Credit accounts, Personal Independence Payment applications, State Pension online services and Tell Us Once were among the reported disruptions. The department said regular benefit and pension payments were not affected and would be paid as normal.

DWP said it was investigating with the National Cyber Security Centre and the Government Cyber Coordination Centre. It had not disclosed the nature of the incident, whether personal data was accessed, the affected-system scope or when services would return. The outage therefore remains an availability incident with an unknown cause, not a confirmed data breach.

The operational risk is that claimants cannot report changes, apply for benefits or notify deaths while the investigation continues. Watch for the service restoration list, any evidence of unauthorized access and guidance for claimants who missed online tasks. Payments continuing as scheduled reduces immediate financial disruption but does not answer the integrity or confidentiality questions surrounding the affected systems.
