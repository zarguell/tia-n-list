Dutch authorities arrested two suspects, ages 23 and 25, over an NFC relay fraud scheme that drained over €380,000 (~$425,600) from 170 victims. The attackers used AI-generated audio to impersonate banks during phone calls, convinced victims to download an app, and then extracted card data via NFC contactless chips. A co-conspirator waited at an ATM to withdraw funds immediately. The suspects were arrested on October 6; investigators seized servers and took their infrastructure offline. This is a concrete law-enforcement outcome with verified victim counts and damage figures, not a theoretical vulnerability. Watch for copycat NFC relay schemes and bank-side NFC safeguards.
