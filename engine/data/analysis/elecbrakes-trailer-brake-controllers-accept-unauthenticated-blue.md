Elecbrakes EB2 controllers through version 3.0.4.2.0, VM1 controllers below 1.3.4.2.1 and every ELBC2000 accept brake commands over Bluetooth Low Energy with no pairing, PIN or authentication. CISA advisory ICSA-26-323-02, released 19 November 2026, tracks the missing authentication as CVE-2026-96805 at CVSS 9.6, replayable unauthenticated commands as CVE-2026-125273 at 9.6 and an unprotected firmware-update channel as CVE-2026-124281 at 6.1, with no public exploitation reported to CISA at release. The researchers wrote four bytes to the brake control characteristic, used one byte to set force from 0x00 to 0xFF and read state notifications back, with no sequence numbers, nonces or session tokens anywhere on that path.

The notable failure is not just absent cryptography. The controller already rejected GATT subscriptions to its bonding and central-command characteristics with the error Not paired while accepting writes to brake control, so the access-control tier existed and was wired to the wrong function. A four-byte command sets deceleration on a 1.5 to 4 tonne trailer, where full force at highway speed invites a jackknife, and a persistent command register plus brake-state feedback turns a drive-by into a verified action with little forensic residue. Updates are user-initiated: Elecbrakes reported about 60 percent of daily active EB2 and VM1 users on fixed firmware by 30 October, leaving roughly four in ten active users plus every discontinued ELBC2000 exposed more than three months after the 6 July fix. This outranks the rest of today's queue because the output is torque on a public road rather than data loss, and the attacker needs only a Bluetooth-capable browser within the stated 10 to 100 metre range.

Owners should install EB2 firmware 3.0.4.4.3 or later and VM1 firmware 1.3.4.2.1 or later through the Elecbrakes app, confirm the reported version and claim the free V3 remote if a V2 remote is in use. No fix exists for the ELBC2000, which Elecbrakes says remains under review; treat powered units as unmitigated and check the app or contact the vendor for remediation. Hunt for unexpected writes to the user commands characteristic, identified in the research by UUID prefix 18315e96, especially opcode 0x14 with force bytes 0x00 through 0xFF, and correlate with state or warnings notifications on prefix 2f4d4806 changing between 0x00 and 0x03. Research logs show the pattern as a four-byte write, an OK response and a brake-state notification, for example 14 00 00 00 followed by 03 00 00 20. Fixed firmware gates update mode behind an authenticated bonded connection, so a controller still reachable through Just Works pairing needs the update.

The assessment changes if Elecbrakes ships an ELBC2000 remedy or delivers the promised signed firmware, rolling encryption keys and app attestation; the fixed firmware image reviewed on 17 November was still unsigned. Any field report of unexplained brake application or damage would move this from a safety hazard with no known exploitation to an incident, and CISA's no-known-exploitation statement is a snapshot rather than proof of absence, because brake writes release two to five seconds after the connection drops.
