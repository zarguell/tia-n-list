Octagon is a previously undocumented Android banking and cryptocurrency fraud platform marketed as malware-as-a-service by a Russian-speaking actor using the handle AndroidKitKat. First advertised on a Russian-language cybercrime forum on June 1, 2026, the toolkit charges buyers $1,400 per month and provides a Windows-based control panel for managing infected devices. The malware targets cryptocurrency wallets, banking apps, and messaging services through hidden remote control and accessibility abuse.

Once installed, Octagon abuses Android accessibility permissions to read screen content, monitor applications, capture credentials, and place fake login overlays over legitimate apps. The malware can intercept SMS messages for OTP theft, execute hidden VNC sessions for real-time device control, and perform device reconnaissance to identify high-value targets. The combination of accessibility abuse and hidden VNC gives attackers complete visibility and control over compromised devices.

The $1,400 monthly pricing and professional control panel indicate a mature criminal operation targeting financial institutions and cryptocurrency holders. Organizations with mobile workforce or BYOD policies should implement mobile threat defense solutions and monitor for accessibility service abuse. Cryptocurrency users should be wary of unsolicited app installation requests and verify app authenticity through official app stores only.
