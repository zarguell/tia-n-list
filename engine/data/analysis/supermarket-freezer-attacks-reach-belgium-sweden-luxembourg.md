Supermarket refrigeration attacks have spread beyond France to Belgium, Luxembourg, and Sweden. Overnight November 2, refrigeration systems at dozens of stores showed sudden temperature rises up to 50-55°C, forcing retailers to discard stock and delay openings. Delhaize reported about 40 affected stores in Belgium and Luxembourg, Carrefour Belgium cited 15, and Swedish chains ICA and Coop confirmed multiple store disruptions. No group has claimed responsibility and authorities have not named a suspect or entry point.

The Swedish national cybersecurity center is actively responding. The incidents follow a destructive October cyberattack against French supermarket refrigeration controllers that reset freezers to 50°C. The current reports link affected environments to remote management software used with older refrigeration controllers, but technical confirmation of the entry point remains pending.
