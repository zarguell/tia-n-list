CERT-UA has alerted on active remote hijacking of Dahua IP cameras and DVRs in Ukraine. Attackers are exploiting P2P access paths and authentication weaknesses to gain entry and pull live feeds. The campaign targets surveillance infrastructure with real-time access as the outcome, not just data theft. No CVEs are assigned in the event, but the attack vector relies on weak P2P configurations and poor credential controls rather than a single software flaw. Organizations running Dahua devices should audit P2P settings, disable unnecessary remote access, and enforce stronger authentication. Ukraine-facing networks should treat this as an active threat given CERT-UA's direct involvement and the operational value of live camera feeds to adversaries.
