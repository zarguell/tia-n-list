Google Chrome is adding Gemini-powered scam detection that warns users when they copy suspicious commands associated with ClickFix attacks, delivered through Enhanced Protection in Safe Browsing. The change targets the social-engineering pattern where users are tricked into pasting malicious commands.

Users on Enhanced Protection gain the new warnings automatically as the rollout progresses. Watch for attacker adaptation to the detection heuristics and any reported bypasses of the ClickFix warnings.
