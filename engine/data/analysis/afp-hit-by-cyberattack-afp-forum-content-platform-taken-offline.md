Agence France-Presse took its AFP Forum client platform offline after a cyberattack detected at about 01:30 Paris time overnight into November 26, 2026. AFP Forum allows media clients to browse and download the agency's dispatches, photographs and videos. AFP management said its teams moved immediately to contain the intrusion and took the platform offline as a precaution, with AFP Forum to remain unavailable until further notice. The agency offered subscribers alternative ways to obtain its content while the investigation continued with external cybersecurity experts.

The incident directly disrupted a global news distribution service rather than AFP's newsroom production. French reporting says the platform still showed a maintenance notice on November 27, with no restoration timeline from the agency. That makes the duration of the outage and the integrity of the client portal the immediate operational concerns, particularly for newsrooms that rely on AFP Forum to retrieve time-sensitive material.

AFP had not identified the attackers or established whether they accessed sensitive information or affected other systems. The concrete facts currently stop at intrusion detection, isolation of AFP Forum and the ongoing investigation. Watch for confirmation of the attack's entry point, the scope of access, evidence of data theft and a timetable for restoring the platform. A separate AFP delivery-service attack in September 2024 is historical context, not evidence about the actors or method in this incident.
