AFP took AFP Forum offline after an intrusion it described in an internal email to staff, quoted in the agency's own reporting and in Recorded Future News. Management said the attack came overnight into Thursday 26 November 2026 and that the target was the portal clients use to browse and download AFP dispatches, photographs and videos. Teams isolated the platform on detection and it stayed down through Friday, serving a maintenance notice in several languages. AFP says it is investigating with external cybersecurity experts, has offered subscribers alternative routes to the content, and has not identified who was behind the intrusion. It has also not said whether any information was accessed or whether other systems were touched.

What gives the incident weight is the position AFP Forum occupies in the syndication chain. The portal is the bulk distribution point between the agency and thousands of outlets that republish its text and images under an AFP byline with limited re-verification, so an outage is the visible cost while integrity and credential exposure are the durable ones. Anything scraped or substituted out of the portal before it was cut is already downstream of the agency. Subscriber accounts on a bulk-download platform are also the kind of credential material that reappears later in impersonation attempts against newsrooms, and French security outlets have already begun republishing the outage as a data-leak item, which is unsupported by anything AFP has said.

The public record still holds no entry point, no malware family, no ransomware claim and no group taking credit. Treat the absence of a breach statement as provisional rather than exculpatory. Rotate AFP Forum credentials and API tokens at restoration instead of waiting for confirmation, and review the portal's authentication, download and administration telemetry for scripted or bulk fetches, sessions from hosting and VPS address space, fresh API key issuance, permission changes, and accounts whose login pattern shifts after a password or mailbox change. On the receiving side, identify which internal systems ingest AFP content automatically and check what arrived in the window before the platform went dark. Two things would change the assessment: an AFP or investigator statement that customer data was accessed, or disclosure of the entry point with a restoration date and independent validation.
