AFP confirmed that a cyberattack targeted AFP Forum, the platform media customers use to browse and download its stories, photographs and videos. Teams detected the intrusion, contained it and took the forum offline as a precaution. The platform remained unavailable with a maintenance notice, while AFP offered subscribers alternative access. AFP said it was investigating with external cybersecurity experts.

The incident disrupted a major global news distribution channel, but the reporting does not establish data theft. AFP had not identified the attackers, given a restoration timeline or said whether other systems were affected. The agency also had not said whether sensitive information was accessed. The observed impact is therefore service availability, not a confirmed breach of customer or employee records.

Watch for AFP to identify the entry point, scope of access and evidence of data exposure, along with a phased restoration of AFP Forum. Customers should verify alternative distribution routes and remain alert for fraudulent messages exploiting the outage. Any claim of stolen content or wider compromise needs confirmation from AFP or the external investigators.
