Operation Jackal IV, a coordinated law enforcement action spanning 22 countries, resulted in 58 arrests and the identification of 263 suspects linked to West African organized crime networks, primarily Black Axe. The operation ran from November 2025 through June 2026 and targeted the financial infrastructure of cybercrime syndicates engaged in business email compromise, romance scams, cryptocurrency investment fraud, and money laundering.

Black Axe operates as a hierarchical criminal organization generating billions annually through small-scale operations across dozens of countries, with Nigerian nationals leading the group. The operation disrupted multiple criminal pipelines: Argentine authorities made 17 arrests and linked 196 suspects to a Crime-as-a-Service network providing web domains and money laundering support; South African police arrested 39 people, blocked 257 bank accounts, and seized $2.67 million from a syndicate targeting English-speaking retirees; Romanian authorities dismantled a call center operation running investment scams.

This represents the fourth iteration of Operation Jackal, indicating sustained international focus on dismantling Black Axe's financial networks. The breadth of arrests across continents and the seizure of assets from multiple scam typologies suggest meaningful disruption to the group's operational capacity, though the full impact on global BEC and investment fraud volumes remains to be seen.
