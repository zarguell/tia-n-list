The ransomware group "thegentlemen" posted a claim against the University of San Francisco on its leak site, alleging theft of financial records and NDA documents. The claim lists the target as "Undisclosed" in terms of scope, suggesting the attackers may not yet have specified the full extent of exfiltrated data or the number of affected individuals.

The source is a single threat intelligence account (yazoul.net), and no mainstream cybersecurity outlet has independently verified the claim as of this writing. Ransomware groups routinely exaggerate or pre-empt claims before negotiations conclude, so the actual scope of any breach remains unconfirmed.

USF has not publicly commented on the claim. If validated, this would add to a 2026 trend of higher-education institutions being targeted by mid-tier ransomware operations seeking financial and legal documents that carry high leverage in extortion negotiations.
