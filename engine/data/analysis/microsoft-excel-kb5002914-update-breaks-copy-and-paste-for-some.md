Microsoft has released update KB5002655 to address the copy-and-paste failures introduced by the September 2026 KB5002914 security update. The regression broke copy-and-paste, autofill, and formula dragging in Excel across multiple versions, with users reporting that pasting leaves the source selected and destination unmodified, accompanied by no error messages. Microsoft confirmed the issue as a code regression after customer reports flooded Q&A forums and Reddit.

The current fix only applies to the Microsoft Installer (.msi)-based edition of Excel 2016, not to Click-to-Run editions like Microsoft 365 Home. Excel Online, Excel 2024, Excel 2021, and Excel 2019 remain affected without resolution. Microsoft acknowledged that the fix also addresses conditional formatting scenarios impacted by the regression. Users running Office 365 or Click-to-Run installations should hold off on KB5002914 until Microsoft ships a broader fix, or manually uninstall the update. The lack of silent failure indicators means users may not realize data operations are failing until they notice missing content in their spreadsheets.
