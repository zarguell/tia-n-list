Cisco Talos identified UAT-11473, an APT whose infrastructure overlaps with Volt Typhoon's Silent Train network, exploiting CVE-2026-20127 (CVSS 10) in Cisco SD-WAN controllers. The vulnerability allows unauthenticated remote access past peering authentication to obtain admin rights. Talos reports historical but unconfirmed-timing attacks planting KeyDrift malware, which operates inside SD-WAN control-plane processes to intercept decrypted traffic and exfiltrate keying material.

KeyDrift's primary component is PolyFlame, a memory-resident backdoor injected through modified runit paths. The framework targets space, satellite, defense, aerospace, and policy research organizations. Because the flaw was patched in February but exploitation continues, defenders should audit SD-WAN controllers for unauthorized runit modifications and review any access from overlapping Silent Train infrastructure.
