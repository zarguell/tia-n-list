A Ukrainian national, Artem Aleksandrovych Stryzhak, was sentenced to 70 months in prison for conspiracy to commit computer fraud related to ransomware attacks that targeted hospitals, government entities, law firms, and businesses between 2018 and late 2021. He was involved with Nefilim and Hive ransomware groups, which attacked about 1,500 victims worldwide and caused over $100 million in losses. Stryzhak pleaded guilty in late 2025, was arrested in Spain in June 2024, and extradited to the United States in April 2025. Authorities are seeking co-conspirator Volodymyr Tymoshchuk and previously announced an $11 million reward for information.

The case highlights the long tail of ransomware affiliate operations and international law enforcement cooperation spanning Spain and the United States. Nefilim affiliates targeted companies with revenues exceeding $100 million, using custom-tailored malware and data-leak threats. The sentence reflects the scale of damage to hospitals and critical infrastructure, though it does not recover the losses.
