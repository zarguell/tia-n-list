Ukrainian national Artem Aleksandrovych Stryzhak was sentenced to 70 months in prison for his role in Nefilim and Hive ransomware operations that targeted hospitals, government entities, dentists, oil and gas companies, and law firms between 2018 and 2021. He pleaded guilty in late 2025 and was extradited from Spain in April 2025 after a June 2024 arrest. Authorities say the groups caused more than $100 million in losses and hit over 1,500 victims worldwide. A co-conspirator, Volodymyr Tymoshchuk, remains at large with an $11 million reward for information.

Stryzhak used custom-tailored Nefilim ransomware and paid 20 percent of ransom proceeds to administrators. His Hive activity targeted at least seven organizations, including healthcare providers and a dental office. The sentencing demonstrates international cooperation in tracking ransomware affiliates, but the remaining co-conspirator and the scale of victim impact show that ransomware groups continue to pose a persistent threat to critical infrastructure and healthcare sectors.
