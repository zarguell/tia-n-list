Artem Aleksandrovych Stryzhak, a 35-year-old Ukrainian national, was sentenced to 70 months in prison for his role in Nefilim and Hive ransomware attacks targeting hospitals, government entities, and high-revenue businesses worldwide between 2018 and 2021. He was extradited to the United States in April 2025 after arrest in Spain in June 2024. Court documents state he paid 20% of ransom payments for access to custom Nefilim ransomware and targeted corporations with revenues exceeding $100 million annually. The FBI notes the groups disrupted patient care and caused over $100 million in losses.

This sentencing confirms the global reach of ransomware affiliate networks and the scale of damage from multi-year campaigns. It also highlights how affiliates operated with custom malware and targeted high-value sectors, including healthcare. Organizations should review historical Hive and Nefilim IOCs and ensure backup and recovery plans account for the double-extortion tactics these groups used. The case reinforces the importance of international law enforcement cooperation in disrupting ransomware supply chains.
