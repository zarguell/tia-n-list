Rackspace is winding down its Rackspace Spot cloud platform after a breach that let attackers into customer Kubernetes environments. According to a customer notification obtained by BleepingComputer, the intrusion happened on November 17 using compromised credentials. The attackers could create unauthorized pods in customer clusters, read Kubernetes Secrets, and list nodes and volumes. Rackspace found the intrusion on November 18 after a customer reported it, then disabled access to the platform administration console and moved to secure its infrastructure.

The company told customers it will shut Spot down permanently on December 8, 2026, at 5 PM Central Time. Workloads still on the platform will be terminated at that point and customer data will no longer be accessible. Customers have been told to download their data and migrate workloads before the deadline. Rackspace framed the shutdown as a clear path forward following a review of the incident, rather than a restoration of the service.

The practical concern is the exposure of Kubernetes Secrets and the window in which they remained usable. Any credentials held in affected clusters should be treated as exposed and rotated during migration, along with a review of pod creation and node or volume enumeration in those environments. The fixed shutdown date also creates a hard migration deadline for teams still running workloads on Spot.
