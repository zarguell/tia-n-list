Method and limits

Sources

The vendor's advisory feed ({{ rel.source_label }} — scope, CVSS vectors, exploitability flags), FIRST EPSS (probability + percentile), CISA KEV catalog, CISA Vulnrichment (SSVC assessments, matched per CVE), and Tia's own story store for news uptake.

Derived, not official

Unless a row says CISA Vulnrichment, the SSVC-style decision points are derived locally from the CVSS vector: automatable = AV:N/AC:L/UI:N/PR:N or L; technical impact total = C, I and A all High. Vulnrichment is fetched for the 40 highest-CVSS CVEs per release, not all of them.

Known limits

EPSS is a next-30-days estimate, not proof of exploitation.{% if rel.severity_basis == 'cvss' %} {{ rel.vendor }} publishes no vendor severity ratings, so Critical/High/Medium/Low here are CVSS base-score bands.{% endif %} The vendor revises the document during the week; every tracking pass checks the document's revision date and folds in late CVEs, severity/CVSS changes and new exploit-status flags, and the timeline lists what appeared. Scope excludes Azure Linux and Edge/Chromium families (different cadence) — the vendor's stated total stays visible above.
