How this works

Scope

We mirror the vendor's own release document, then drop the product families with a different cadence: Azure Linux (Mariner) and Edge/Chromium (weekly release train). The vendor's stated total stays on every page next to our count, so the exclusion is visible, not hidden.

Signals

CVSS base score and vector (parsed, not just displayed), EPSS probability and percentile, CISA KEV membership, CISA Vulnrichment SSVC where published, a locally derived SSVC-style assessment where it is not, Microsoft's own exploit-status flags, and how many stories our newsroom has published on each CVE.

Tracking

A release is followed for seven days after publication. Each day captures KEV additions, EPSS moves of 0.02 or more, and new newsroom coverage. The score re-ranks on the refreshed signals; nothing is backdated.

Triage score is a ranking device (0–100), not a prediction: CVSS×4, EPSS percentile×20, KEV +25, vendor exploitation flag +20, public disclosure +6, Microsoft "exploitation more likely" +6, automatable +8, technical impact total +5, media mentions +1.2 each (max 5).
