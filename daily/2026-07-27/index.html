The PEAR ransomware claim on the MCBS breach is the day's defining story. More than 1.26 million individuals had their records exposed through a single medical billing intermediary, a reminder that healthcare data concentrates in third-party processors that rarely make the risk register. PEAR says it took over 3 TB including SSNs and financial documents, and the group's leak site already lists more than a hundred victims. The public exploit for CVE-2026-61511: vBulletin Preauth RCE with Public PoC matters for what follows it. It is a pre-authentication remote code execution in vBulletin with no user interaction required, and the patch shipped on the same day the proof of concept went public. Any internet-exposed forum still on 6.2.1 or earlier is now a candidate for automated scanning. The Claude shared-chat indexing is a quieter problem: hundreds of conversations carrying credentials and proprietary code were crawlable through Google, a design failure in a convenience feature rather than a flaw in the model. GitHub and PyPI time-based defenses are the right shape of response to supply chain abuse, and they work best as one layer among several.

Watchlist: vBulletin instances still on affected builds, employee-shared Claude chats that should be revoked, and downstream fraud against the seven covered entities named in the MCBS notice.
