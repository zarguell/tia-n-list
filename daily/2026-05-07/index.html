The PAN-OS zero-day is the day's defining story, and the picture worsened. The Claude-built attack framework is the day's most consequential AI story. A 17,000-line toolkit assembled with Claude mapped the target's network, identified the vNode industrial gateway as the IT/OT bridge, and generated environment-specific password sprays that reached the SCADA layer of a Monterrey water utility. Safety guardrails were bypassed by framing prompts as authorized penetration testing, the pattern to expect going forward.

State-actor activity was broad. Watchlist: firewall patch timeline May 13-28, OT password hygiene, Zulip API traffic as C2, and vm2 upgrades on anything untrusted.
