Microsoft's silent patch of a critical Azure Backup for AKS privilege escalation is the day's defining story, less for the flaw than for the disclosure process around it. A low-privileged Backup Contributor role could reach cluster-admin through Trusted Access auto-configuration, CERT/CC independently validated the report, and Microsoft still rejected it, patched quietly, and blocked a CVE. When a vendor controls both the verdict and the identifier, defenders lose the ability to track and prioritize fixes.
