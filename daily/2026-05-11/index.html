The DigiCert breach is the day's defining story. A social engineering attack on a support analyst netted 27 Extended Validation code-signing certificates, which were used to sign Zhong Stealer payloads, and a misconfigured EDR agent left a second endpoint running silently. EV certificates are the strongest trust anchors in the Windows ecosystem, so anything signed with them until revocation carries a veneer of legitimacy. Operation HookedWing shows the value of patience in credential harvesting. Active since 2022, the campaign has collected more than 2,000 credentials from 500-plus organizations across aviation, energy, and government, using GitHub-hosted Outlook lookalikes that pre-fill the victim's organization. GhostLock is a reminder that ransomware is not the only way to paralyze a business. Exclusive deny-share handles can lock 500,000 files in under three minutes without a single write event, leaving honeypots, canary files, and write-rate analytics blind; storage-management session telemetry is the only signal that sees it.
